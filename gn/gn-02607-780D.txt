D. Procedure – transferring concurrent PUPS records
Do not transfer concurrent PENDING PUPS records to the PC/OCO-ODO. The PC/OCO-ODO
does not work title XVI cases.
The FO will process the title XVI PUPS PENDING record according to the processing
instructions in GN 02607.705. After the title XVI PUPS PENDING record is closed, the title II PUPS PENDING record
can be transferred to the PC/OCO-ODO, if necessary. See GN 02607.465B. for instances where the FO can transfer title II PUPS PENDING records to the PC/OCO-ODO.
