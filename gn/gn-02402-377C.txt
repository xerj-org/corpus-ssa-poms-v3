SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.377 - Coding Romania Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/16/2024 - Present | TN 252 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402377 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for Romania has a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the Routing Transit
Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field has the
letter “Q” assigned for Romania.
