D. Ottawa
Although Ottawa may, in rare instances, require coverage information for the period
1952 to 1956 in order to give the worker residence credits for periods of U.S. work,
do not request proof of U.S. military service. It is is not needed to process the
U.S. portion of the claim. However, annotate the remarks section of Form SSA-2960-U3-CQ,
“Military service alleged from             to            (dates alleged, but no earlier than 1952) but not proven. Please advise if you require
coverage information for the period.” If Ottawa requests coverage information for the period, obtain proof of military
service.
