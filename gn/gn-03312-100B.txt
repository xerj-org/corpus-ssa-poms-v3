B. Examples of serious crimes
Serious crimes include, but are not limited to:
•
terrorism;
•
murder;
•
rape;
•
child exploitation;
•
kidnapping;
•
armed robbery;
•
hijacking;
•
carjacking;
•
drug trafficking or drug possession with intent to manufacture, import, export, distribute
or dispense;
•
gang activity;
•
burglary; and
•
arson.
