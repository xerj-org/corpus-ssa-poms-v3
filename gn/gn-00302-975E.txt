E. HIAS policy on release of information
HIAS will assist SSA in any way it can. There is no charge.
If a search is requested by mail, HIAS will not demand the claimant's authorization
for release of information.
HIAS does insist, however, that SSA limit its requests to those that are necessary
for an equitable determination of age for Jewish immigrants.
