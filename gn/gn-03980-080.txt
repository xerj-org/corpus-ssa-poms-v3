GN 03980.080 Possible Violation of Conflict-of-Interest Provisions
Refer suspected violations of the conflict-of-interest provisions to SSA's Ethics
Office. When referring, provide brief narrative summary, identifying all parties by
name, title, and office location. If you have a question about whether an individual
has violated a conflict of interest provision, please contact the Ethics Office at
ssa.ethics.questions@ssa.gov.
Violations of the Federal laws underlying the conflict-of-interest policies may result
in administrative discipline or criminal prosecution.
