A. Policy - When to locate claims folders
You may need to locate a claims folder to process a current action, such as an appeal,
a court case, a continuing disability review (CDR), or satisfy the request for folder
information from a component, such as the Office of the Inspector General, the Office
of Quality Review, or the Office of Quality Improvement.
