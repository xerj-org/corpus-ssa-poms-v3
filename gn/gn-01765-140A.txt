A. Applying ANP for workers
Section 202(t)(1) of the Social Security Act provides that the Social Security Administration
(SSA) will suspend the benefits of certain alien beneficiaries once they have been
outside the United States for more than six consecutive calendar months unless they
meet an exception.
As explained in GN 01701.150A.1., certain alien beneficiaries who would otherwise be subject to suspension under section
202(t)(1) are exempt from suspension if they reside in a country with which the United
States has a Totalization Agreement. This exemption applies to residents of Romania
who receive regular retirement, survivors, or disability insurance benefits, as well
as those who receive totalization benefits.
NOTE:
Romanian citizens are exempt from the Alien Nonpayment Provision (ANP), regardless
of where they reside. See RS 02610.015 for more information on ANP exceptions based on citizenship.
