B. Policy – Alabama affidavit method
As an alternative to delivering a copy of the judge’s order for summary distribution,
as described above, section 43-2-694 states that upon delivery of an affidavit executed
by any person having knowledge of the facts and alleging the concurrence of the conditions
listed in § 43-2-692(b), together with a copy of the decedent’s will if the claim
is under such will, a distributee will be entitled to receive the decedent’s property
from any person owing any money to the decedent’s estate.
