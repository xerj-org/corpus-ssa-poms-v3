C. Exception
Notifications of birth registration issued by Kansas City (MO) sometimes show “Special” in the file number space. These records are similar to DBC's (see GN 00302.540) but are often used for persons born in other cities or States (see GN 00302.500).
Such documents cannot be used as preferred evidence. Develop for preferred evidence or, if none exists,
convincing evidence. If no such evidence is obtainable, the Kansas City (MO) Downtown
BO can verify what evidence was presented to support the DB shown if a copy of the “Special” notification is sent to the BO.
