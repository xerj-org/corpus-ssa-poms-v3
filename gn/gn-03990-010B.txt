SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03990.010 - Payment Effectuation Under the Equal Access to Justice Act
Effective Dates: 02/08/2002 - Present | TN 1 (05-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203990010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Point of Payment
Payments under the provisions of the EAJA will be made from SSA's administrative expense
account. Office of Finance, upon receipt of the court's order from the Litigation
Staff, will effectuate payment, and will furnish copies of the payment documentation
(marked “Prong File” ) to the Litigation Staff for the appropriate PC, ODO or FO (SSI cases).
