E. SSA processing of CMP
OCIG forwards the case information to the DCO/OPSOS/CMP Headquarters coordinator for
all CMPs.
OGC may forward the case if the CMP became final at the HHS ALJ level or above.
SSA components cannot directly refer to OCIG to consider a CMP. To refer cases to
OIG for possible fraud investigation, see GN 04110.000.
1.
Operations is responsible for posting the CMP on the MBR and/or SSR and for collecting
the CMP.
2.
The DCO/OPSOS/CMP coordinator based on the information, forwards the case to the RO
or PSC contact that will be responsible for posting and for tracking the CMP case.
3.
See a list of the RO/PSC CMP contacts and the DCO/OPSOS/CMP coordinators in GN 02604.420E.
