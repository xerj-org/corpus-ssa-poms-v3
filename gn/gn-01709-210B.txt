B. SSA accepts applications for benefits
Under the Agreement with Switzerland, the Social Security Administration (SSA) will
take applications for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of Switzerland
and provide limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim.
The following instructions show the types of evidence SSA personnel may use to support
claims for Swiss benefits.
