J. Enclosures to the SSA-562-U3
When the request is unusual or complicated, enclose copies of background material
which will help the assisting DO handle the request. It is not necessary to enclose
blank or partially completed letters or forms to the SSA-562-U3 unless provided for
elsewhere in this section.
