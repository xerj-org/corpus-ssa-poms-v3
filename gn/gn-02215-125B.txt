B. PROCEDURE - COMPLETING THE CCLR
Prepare the CCLR in accordance with GN 02215.168 with the following exceptions:
•
Item 3 - Show the above Central Justice address.
•
Item 15 - Show the name and phone number of a PC contact to answer questions relating
to the claim.
•
Item 24 - Show “Not applicable.”
NOTE: Each PC will designate a contact person; individual PCs have the option of deciding
who that contact will be.
If adjustment (full or partial) has been initiated, do not stop recovery unless specifically
requested by the debtor.
