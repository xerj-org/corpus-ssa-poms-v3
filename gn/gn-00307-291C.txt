C. Procedure for applying the WEP Guarantee
Apply the WEP Guarantee to compute the PIA when:
•
Based on the NH's allegations, use of the WEP Guarantee would result in a higher primary
insurance amount (PIA) than the modified formula; and
•
We have verified the pension amount from the paying agency or employer.
If we cannot verify the pension amount, do not apply the WEP Guarantee.
Use the maximum reduction to compute the PIA. Explain to the NH we can recompute the
PIA using the WEP Guarantee when we receive the necessary verification of the pension
amount from the agency or employer.
Document all actions taken in the claims file.
Refer any questions on the WEP and foreign pensions to the Office of Earnings and
International Operations (OEIO) at:
