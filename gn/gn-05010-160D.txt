Effective Dates: 10/23/2023 - Present | TN 16 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. PROCEDURE
Advise beneficiaries who ask why RC, not SSA or IRS, makes the tax refunds for 1996
and 1997 that this authority was given to RC by the Treasury Department which negotiates
tax treaties. SSA does not have any jurisdiction in these matters.
Advise beneficiaries that questions about refunds or the refund process for 1996 and
1997 should be referred to any Revenue Canada office.
