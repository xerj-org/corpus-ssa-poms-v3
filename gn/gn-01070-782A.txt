A. Overview of the WSUs and their role
This section outlines the responsibilities of the WSUs in handling initial claim diaries
that they establish in partial adjudication cases. These instructions should help
ensure consistent handling of claims diaries across all Operations' components.
The WSUs process first-party English language iClaim Retirement and Medicare-only
applications where the claimant provides a domestic residence address and has proven
age and citizenship status.
