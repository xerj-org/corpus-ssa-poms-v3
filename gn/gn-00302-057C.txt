Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302057 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. LIST OF FORMS FOR DOCUMENTATION
If the claimant had previously tried to obtain a copy of his/her public or religious
record but was unsuccessful, or has some other basis for knowing that such records
are unavailable, document the file accordingly using
•
An RC, or
•
Form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other Person), or
•
Form SSA-553 (Special Determination), or
•
Form SSA-2038-F3 (Date of Birth Determination), or
•
The application for benefits (in either the question pertaining to this subject or
in “Remarks”).
