A. Description of the Belgian social insurance number
The Belgian social insurance number consists of 8 or 9 digits which are assigned as
follows:
•
Digits 1-2 represent the year of birth;
•
Digits 3-4 represent the month of birth;
•
Digits 5-9 are chronologically assigned.
