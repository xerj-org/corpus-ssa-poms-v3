D. Overview of IDD for Thailand
IDD payments sent to Thailand must be in Thai Baht (THB) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Thailand. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
