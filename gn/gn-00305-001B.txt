B. Definitions — determining family status
1. State
State includes the 50 States, the District of Columbia, the U. S. Virgin Islands (beginning
9/1/50), Puerto Rico (beginning 1/1/51), Guam (beginning 1/1/61), American Samoa (beginning
1/1/61), and the Northern Mariana Islands (beginning 1/9/78).
2. Domicile
Domicile is the place where a person has their true, fixed, and permanent home to which they
intend to return whenever away. Every person has a domicile and can only have one
at a time.
3. Intestate
Intestate means to die without a legal will.
