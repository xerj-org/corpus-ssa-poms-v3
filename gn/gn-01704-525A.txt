A. Italian agreement
If an allegation of a worker's military service (MS) is in file, obtain proof of that
service. Send the proof to INPS. DO NOT SEND THE DOCUMENTS TO CTS.
It is not necessary to obtain proof of any other type of Italian coverage. However,
if a claimant submits evidence of Italian work, send the evidence untranslated to
INPS.
