SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.310 - How OIO Processes Luxembourg Agency Requests for Medical Evidence and Examinations
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Medical Evidence
When a Luxembourg agency requests medical evidence, send the evidence from OIO files
as described in GN 01704.810 C.
2. Medical Examinations
Follow these steps when responding to a Luxembourg agency request for a medical examination.
Step
Action
1
Request DDB to schedule an examination or forward “pertinent medical evidence from files” as described in GN 01704.810.
2
Stamp the first and last page of each medical record being sent to Luxembourg with
“true and exact copy.” Sign and date the blocks on the stamp.
3
Request reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for the Luxembourg
agency using form SSA-1278-U3-LX, Request for payment of reimbursable expenses under
the US-Luxembourg agreement on Social Security. Refer to the instructions in GN 01704.810 for completing the form.
