A. Policy - General Tasks
The individual assigned responsibility for authorizing fees (e.g., reconsideration
reviewer or claims technical expert (CTE)) in the processing center (PC):
•
Resolves or instructs others (e.g., the benefit authorizer (BA), post-entitlement
technical expert (PETE), or claims specialist (CS)) about issues related to Title
II benefit withholding, fee petitions, and direct payment of authorized fees from
Title II past-due benefits;
•
Evaluates the fee petition and authorizes a reasonable fee for representation in cases
decided at initial or reconsideration level (including Title XVI only); and
•
Notifies the field office (FO) when they authorize a fee in a Title XVI claim or concurrent
Title II and Title XVI claims, and when they make a direct fee payment in concurrent
Title II and Title XVI claims.
