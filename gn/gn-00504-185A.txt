SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00504.185 - Follow up on Questionable Representative Payees
Effective Dates: 04/29/2025 - Present | TN 38 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200504185 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Follow up on a questionable representative payee
We must monitor questionable payees by personal contact (either by phone, or by personal
visit).
1. Mandatory follow up on a questionable representative payee
We require follow up contact(s) to assess the effectiveness of the existing representative
payee-to-beneficiary relationship in the following circumstances:
a.
We pay the beneficiary directly despite indications of incapability because a suitable
payee is not available. We require three follow up contacts per GN 00504.105B.2.
b.
The beneficiary lives in a non-complying board and care facility, which is the appointed
payee. For information regarding a Quick Response Check (site review) see GN 00605.440A.1.
c.
A child is in the temporary custody of a social agency, but the parent is the appointed
payee. Follow up after three months to ensure that the representative payee uses the
benefits properly.
d.
The representative payee is a friend or casual acquaintance and does not have custody
of the beneficiary. For title XVI, coordinate these follow ups with the redetermination
(RZ) process.
e.
The representative payee is a felon or fugitive, refer to GN 00502.133.
f.
The representative payee has poor past performances, refer to GN 00502.134.
2. Discretionary follow ups
Consider discretionary follow ups whenever there is a need.
3. Follow up intervals
Conduct your follow up within three-months.
