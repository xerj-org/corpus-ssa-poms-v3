SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.003 - Postadjudicative Development of Age — Advance Determinations
Effective Dates: 03/10/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
In the following situations, do not delay adjudication of a benefit claim. Develop
evidence of age postadjudicatively if:
•
B2 or BY claimant alleges he/she will be age 62 or older throughout the month in which
the youngest entitled child attains age 16.
•
B2 or BY claimant has an entitled disabled child in care.
•
E or E4 claimant alleges he/she will attain age 65 in or before the month the youngest
child attains age 16.
•
E or E4 claimant has an entitled disabled child in care.
•
Auxiliary or survivor claimant (e.g., B2 or E) submits evidence or age sufficient
to establish a date of birth (DB) under existing rules without further development.
