B. Process for filing for Portuguese benefits
Field Offices (FOs) do not take special Portuguese application forms. After receiving
a claim for Portuguese benefits that an applicant filed with SSA, the Portuguese agency
will mail the appropriate Portuguese special application form(s) to the applicant.
The Portuguese agency instructs the applicant to complete the forms and mail them
directly to the Portuguese agency with any required evidence needed in the application
process.
