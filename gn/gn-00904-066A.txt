SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.066 - Handling Inquiries in Nonservicing Office - Guide for Development Clerks
Effective Dates: 01/15/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904066 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
If you are in a nonservicing office and receive a written request for general information,
send it to the servicing office by route slip unless the inquiry shows the person
wants to contact your office. Telephone inquiries should normally be handled by the
receiving office.
