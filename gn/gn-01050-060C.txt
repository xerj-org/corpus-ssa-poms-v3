C. Claims involving foreign address or residence
A new claim with a foreign address or residence will be sent to INTPSC for review.
If the claim is cross-referred to a previously adjudicated RSI claim INTPSC will cross-refer
and/or obtain the prior or related folders, as necessary. If the previous claim is
in ODO the new claim will be sent to INTPSC. Cross-referral will be handled in INTPSC
in accordance with the procedure outlined in GN 01050.075.
