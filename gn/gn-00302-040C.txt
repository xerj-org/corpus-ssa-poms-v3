SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.040 - Evidence Required for Claimants Age 73 or Older
Effective Dates: 06/18/2015 - Present | TN 15 (02-92)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Rationale
Studies have shown that this evidence will be reliable in establishing that a person
is within 3 years of the age shown (rather than an exact DB). Since a 3-year age difference
normally is not material when a person files at age 73, this rule can generally be
used.
