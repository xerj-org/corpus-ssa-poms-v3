A. Medicare set-aside companies
Medicare set-aside companies request Medicare and benefit entitlement information
from our records to assist insurance carriers, third party administrators, self-insured
companies, and attorneys in complying with Medicare Secondary Payer (MSP) laws. MSP
laws protect Medicare’s interests in workers’ compensation settlement cases. Medicare
set-aside companies use the information they request to help Medicare determine settlement
amounts in cases involving future medical and drug-related expenses. We may only disclose
information to Medicare set-aside companies with proper consent.
