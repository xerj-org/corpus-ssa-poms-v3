SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01757.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Brazil Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 3 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201757225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Brazilian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Brazilian benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Brazilian agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Brazil
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Brazil, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Brazil
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Brazilian agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Brazilian agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Ministério da Previdência e
Assistência Social (Ministry of Social Security and Assistance)
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Brazilian claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Brazil. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims,
see GN 01702.520.
