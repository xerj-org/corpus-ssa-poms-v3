B. Policy for Illinois summary administration proceedings
755 Ill. Comp. Stat. 5/9-8 provides for summary administration of an estate where the gross value of the decedent’s
real and personal estate subject to administration does not exceed $100,000. Upon
the filing of a petition, a court may determine the rights of claimants and other
persons interested in the estate, and direct payment of claims and distribution of
the estate on summary administration, without issuing letters of office or appointing
a personal representative. Payment of funds in accordance with a court order for summary
administration releases us to the same extent as making the payment to a legally qualified
representative of the decedent. For delivery of personal estate on order of summary
administration, see 755 Ill. Comp. Stat. 5/9-9. If a person seeking legal representative status presents a court order for summary
administration pursuant to 755 Ill. Comp. Stat. 5/9-8, see GN 02301.035D.
