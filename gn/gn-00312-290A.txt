A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
1. Office to Contact
a. Event recorded in Agadez, Dosso, Niamey, Maradi, Tahoua, or Zinder
Office of the Mayor (Mairie), Office of Vital Statistics (Service d'Etat Civil), of the city of registration.
b. Others
Sousprefecture where the event was registered.
2. Fee
a.
Birth and Marriage Certificates: CFA 200.
b.
Death Certificate: No fee
