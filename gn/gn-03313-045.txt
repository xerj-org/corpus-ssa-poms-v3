GN 03313.045 Disclosure to the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from VA, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
