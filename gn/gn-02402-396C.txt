SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.396 - Coding Montenegro Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/18/2023 - Present | TN 244 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402396 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Montenegro will have a prefix of “70” at the beginning of the
Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “W” assigned for Montenegro.
