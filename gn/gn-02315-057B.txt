B. Estate $20,000 or less in Maine
Title 18-A, section 3-1201 of the Revised Statutes of Maine applies to any small estate
which does not exceed $20,000. It provides that 30 days after the date of death, SSA
can make payment of the indebtedness to a person claiming to be the successor of the
decedent upon receipt of his affidavit stating that:
•
the value of the estate, wherever located, less liens and encumbrances, does not exceed
$20,000;
•
30 days have elapsed since the decedent’s death;
•
no application or petition for the appointment of a personal representative is pending
or has been granted in any jurisdictions; and
•
the claiming successor is entitled to payment or delivery of the property.
