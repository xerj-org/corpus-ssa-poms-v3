SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.005 - How Modules Should Review Totalization Claims Filed in a Field Office (FO) or in the International benefit Office (IBO)
Effective Dates: 01/10/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
Follow these steps for reviewing claims in the totalization modules.
STEP
ACTION
1
Review the file for the required applications and evidence listed in GN 01725.205 - GN 01725.215. (See GN 01704.500 for additional instructions for developing and evaluating evidence).
2
Check the file for a U.S. totalization earnings record (ER) which can be sent to the
Dutch agency. If one is not in file, request it from Office of Earnings Operations
(OEO), following GN 01704.570.
3
Refer to GN 01726.315 to determine if a widow(er)'s, spouse's or partner's earnings record is needed.
4
When all of the material required above is available, refer to GN 01726.205 - GN 01726.210 for preparing a claims package for the Dutch agency.
