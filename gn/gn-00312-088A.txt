A. Introduction to vital statistics records in Chile
Chile has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration records. The original
remains at the local civil registration archive and a copy is sent to the General
Archive of Civil Registration. Individuals may request records online from the Chilean
Office of Civil Registration.
NOTE:
We do not accept copies printed online from the Chilean Office of Civil Registration.
unless the custodian subsequently affixes a signature, stamp, or seal with a statement
attesting to the accuracy.
