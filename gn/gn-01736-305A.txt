SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.305 - Life to Death Conversions - Irish Agreement
Effective Dates: 01/18/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Ireland does not pay life social security benefits directly to family members of retirement,
old-age or invalidity pensioners. (Supplements are paid to the insured person.) Therefore,
survivors should be advised to file an application if they think they can qualify
for Irish survivor benefits.
