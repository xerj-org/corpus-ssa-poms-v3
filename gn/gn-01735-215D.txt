D. Procedure – Entering Irish insurance number(s)
Enter the worker's “Social Insurance Number” (issued for work before 1979) or “Revenue
and Social Insurance Number” (issued for work after 1978) on Form SSA-2490. Enter
both Irish insurance numbers if the worker contributed to the Irish system both before
1979 and after 1978.
NOTE: Ireland changed its original social insurance numbering system in 1979. People who
worked under the Irish system before 1979 have a Social Insurance Number consisting
of 4 numeric digits. People who worked after 1978 have a Revenue and Social Insurance
Number consisting of 8 or 9 digits (7 numbers followed by 1 or 2 letters). As a result,
a worker may have two Irish insurance numbers.
