SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02602.057 - Procedure to Reinstate Medicare Advantage (MA) or Medicare Prescription Drug Plan (Part D) After Correcting an Incorrect Death Report
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 117 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202602057 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Reinstatement into a Medicare Advantage or Prescription Drug Plan
Take the following steps to reinstate MA or PDP benefits based on individual circumstances.
NOTE: Only a FO manager or a designated technician in the Payment Center (PC) may request
CMS to reinstate MA or PDP enrollment.
1. Individual continues to receive coverage through their MA or Part D plan
If an individual indicates that their plan will continue to provide coverage while
the incorrect death error is being corrected on their record, provide them with a
notice to give to the MA or PDP plan to inform the plan provider that the loss of
entitlement was erroneous and the record is being corrected. For samples, see GN 00304.115 Exhibits Notice Language for Erroneous Death Cases.
2. Individual needs life sustaining prescription drugs and is not receiving coverage
through their MA or Part D plan
If an individual indicates a need for life sustaining prescription drugs because they
are not receiving coverage from their plan provider after correcting the incorrect
death on their record, follow these steps:
•
Complete all information on the Medicare Parts C and D Priority Case Referral Tracking
Form at https://nynet.ny.ad.ssa.gov/cmsreferral/.
•
Enter Problem Type from the drop-down box as Part A/B erroneously terminated (now
reinstated), needs Part D reinstated.
•
Address all the issues in Problem Description including the number of days of medication
the beneficiary has left in their possession.
•
Provide individual with a notice to give to the MA or PDP plan to inform the plan
that the loss of entitlement was erroneous and the record is being corrected.
NOTE: CMS works with Medicare Part D and MA companies to assign priority to reinstatement
requests based on the need of the beneficiary for medication. Therefore, an individual
who is out of medication has priority over an individual with 3 weeks of medication
left. CMS notifies the requesting FO or PC by email when an individuals Part D or
MA is restored.
