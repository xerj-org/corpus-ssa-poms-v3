F. Belgian Agreement
Belgian totalization claims can be filed with the United States at a DO or with the
International Benefits Office (IBO) of DIO - there is no trained claims-taking post
in Belgium; or with Belgium at any of many private, semi-private or government offices
in Belgium or at any of the Belgian diplomatic posts in the United States.
