SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03905.030 - Forms SSA-1696 Appointment, Revocation, and Withdrawal
Effective Dates: 12/10/2019 - Present | TN 22 (11-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
A claimant must notify us in writing if he or she appoints an individual to represent
him or her in dealings with us. Claimants must use our standard Form SSA-1696 (Claimant’s Appointment of a Representative) for the appointment.
A claimant must also let us know in a signed and dated writing when he or she wants
to revoke a representative's appointment. The writing must indicate the name of the
representative whose appointment the claimant is revoking. Claimants may, but are
not required to use our standard Form SSA-1696-SUP1 (Claimant's Revocation of the Appointment of a Representative) to revoke the appointment
of a representative.
Likewise, an appointed representative must notify us in a signed and dated writing
when he or she wants to withdraw from an appointment. Representatives may, but are
not required to use our standard Form SSA-1696-SUP2 (Representative's Withdrawal of Acceptance of Appointment) to withdraw acceptance
of an appointment.
For more information on appointing a representative and completion of standard Form
SSA-1696, see GN 03910.040 Appointment of a Representative.
For more information on revoking or withdrawing from representation, and completion
of standard Forms SSA-1696-SUP1 and SSA-1696-SUP2, see GN 03910.060 Termination of a Representative's Appointment.
