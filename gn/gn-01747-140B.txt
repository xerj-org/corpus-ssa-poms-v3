B. Procedure — Applying Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provisions
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
appears to fall into one of the categories shown in GN 01747.140A. in this section and alleges residence in Denmark, verify that the exemption provided
by the agreement applies by developing the following:
•
residence status, and
•
citizenship, or refugee or stateless person status, if the applicant alleges such
status.
