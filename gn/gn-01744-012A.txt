A. How do you process a claim in Manila Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) ?
When a non-entitled person contacts Centrelink to file a claim for U.S. benefits,
that agency routes Form AUS187 (Liaison Form) to the Manila FBU . Evidence of working
life residence periods (AUS187) will be included if available. If Centrelink is aware
that the person is already receiving U.S. benefits, they will restrict the claim to
Australian benefits only and request a U.S. coverage record directly from DIO, if
one is needed.
