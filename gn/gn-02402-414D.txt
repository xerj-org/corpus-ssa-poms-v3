D. Overview of IDD for Nicaragua
IDD payments sent to Nicaragua must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Nicaragua. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
