A. General
As previously indicated, the provisions of the EAJA do not ordinarily apply to SSA
claims adjudicated below the court level (GN 03990.001B.). However, despite this it is possible that an EAJA application may be filed in such
cases. The following describes the action to be taken if an EAJA application is received
in an FO or PC.
