D. EXAMPLES
The following are examples of determinations of whether a payee is a conduit payee.
1. Payee is a Conduit Payee
The payee indicates that they receive their mother's benefits. The payee cashes the
checks and turns the full amount over to the nursing home (their mother's custodian).
The nursing home takes care of all his mother's needs and gives her $10 a week for
personal spending.
The payee is acting as a conduit for the nursing home. The payee is not exercising
control over the funds, cannot accurately account for their use and is in no way verifying
that the nursing home is providing for his mother's needs. Development is necessary
to question the use of the benefits and determine whether a change of payee is needed.
2. Payee is Not a Conduit Payee
The payee indicates that they receive their mother's benefits. The payee cashes the
checks and pays the nursing home the entire amount to cover the home's fee for the
care of his mother. The payee buys her clothing and gives her $40 per month for personal
needs from other income.
The payee is not acting as a conduit. They are exercising control over the benefits
in the best interest of the beneficiary.
