A. Introduction
Canadian Social Security benefits consist of two separate programs:
1. Old-Age Security (OAS)
The OAS program, which was effective January 1, 1952, pays old-age benefits to people
based on their years of residence in Canada.
2. Canada Pension Plan (CPP) and Quebec Pension Plan (QPP)
CPP and QPP, which were effective January 1, 1966, pay earnings-related retirement,
survivors and disability benefits that are payable in addition to the OAS benefit.
Applicants for benefits who have contributed to both CPP and QPP apply to QPP if they
live in Quebec and to CPP if they live in any other part of Canada. For applicants
living abroad, the last place of Canadian residence determines jurisdiction.
