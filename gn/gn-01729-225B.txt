SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01729.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Austrian Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 11 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201729225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of an Austrian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Austrian benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Austrian agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Austria
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Austria, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Austria
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Austrian agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Austrian agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Main Association of Austrian Social Insurance
Agencies (Hauptverband der österreichischen Sozialversicherungsträger)
NOTE: DIO is unable to determine the status of an Austrian benefits claim once a technician
forwards the claim to Austria. For more information about status requests in Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
