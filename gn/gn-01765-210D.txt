D. Entitlement factors for Romanian retirement, survivors, and disability benefits
In addition to insured status, evidence of the following it required to support the
application for Romanian benefits.
1. Required evidence when filing an application for retirement benefits
These entitlement factors must be established for Romanian retirement claims.
•
Age of worker
2. Required evidence when filing an application for survivors benefits
These entitlement factors must be established for Romanian survivors claims.
•
Death of worker
•
Romanian social security number
•
Age of claimant
•
Marriage if filing for surviving spouse benefits
•
Divorce if applicable
•
Age and relationship of child for child’s benefits
3. Required evidence when filing an application for disability benefits
These entitlement factors must be established for Romanian disability claims.
•
Age of worker
•
Degree of disability (medical evidence, if available)
