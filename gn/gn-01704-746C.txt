C. Claims filed in a U.S. designated border DO
Initial claims packages are sent by these offices (see GN 01715.320) directly to the appropriate Canadian agencies. These packages include a copy of
the U.S. earnings record. If the record initially provided is insufficient for Canada's
needs, the Canadian agency requests a complete earnings record from OIO.
