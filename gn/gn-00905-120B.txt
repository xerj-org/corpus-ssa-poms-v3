SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.120 - Claims and Inquiries from Residents of the People's Republic of China
Effective Dates: 03/15/2024 - Present | TN 25 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Field Office (FO)
Refer claims inquiries and applications for benefits from, or on behalf of, persons
in the PRC to the Division of International Operations (DIO). Include, if available,
the individual's PRC address in Chinese characters. Advise DIO if it is not available.
NOTE: Correspondence to the PRC cannot be delivered without a Chinese-character address.
2. DIO
Refer the inquiry to the FBU in Manila for PRC, together with the Chinese-character
address, if available.
3. Alien Nonpayment Provision inquiries
Refer inquiries about the resumption of benefits for PRC citizens to the Office of
Income Security Programs if the:
a.
Award was processed prior to July 1991; and
b.
Beneficiary was in suspense status.
