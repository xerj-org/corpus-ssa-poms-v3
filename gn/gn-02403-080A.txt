A. Introduction - SSA-1395-BK Receipt Books
Each office should maintain at least one SSA-1395-BK in the event that the automated
receipt functions in Debt Management System (DMS) and the Social Security Electronic
Remittance System (SERS) is unavailable. The Remittance Supervisor will control the
Field Office (FO) supply of all SSA-1395-BK Receipt Books.
