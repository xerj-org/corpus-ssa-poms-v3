SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01070.210 - Placing DOTEL Calls From The Field Office
Effective Dates: 04/04/2018 - Present | TN 19 (05-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Guidelines for contacting processing center modules or field offices
1.
Make all DOTEL telephone requests directly to the appropriate processing center module
or field office.
2.
Identify the call as a DOTEL contact request and indicate whether you wish to speak
to a CR or SR in a field office or a CA or BA in a processing center, depending on
the nature of the call.
3.
Furnish the following information when placing the call form the field office:
•
FO name and code
•
Name and position of caller
•
FO telephone number (if available, provide an administrative number on which to make
the return call)
•
Date of call (if you reach an electronic answering device)
•
SSN of claim involved (PCACS location)
•
Name of NH
•
Claimant's name and BIC if different from NH
•
Any cross-reference SSN's if applicable
•
Evidence / information being sought. Describe the requested evidence and provide all
material facts associated with the request.
