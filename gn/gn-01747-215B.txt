B. Process
1. When the Modernized Claims System (MCS) version may be used
The SSA-2490 is available through MCS, but the MCS version may only be used when the
number holder (NH) is filing for:
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits only, or
•
U.S. retirement or disability benefits (regular or Totalization) and concurrently
for Danish benefits.
2. When the MCS version may not be used
The MCS version of the SSA-2490 may not be used to file for:
•
Danish benefits only, or
•
U.S. or Danish survivors’ benefits.
A paper or electronic (e-form) SSA-2490 application form must be used in these cases.
Form SSA-2490 is also available through the Totalization
Benefits Resource
Kit.
NOTE: It is not necessary to take an SSA-2490 in claims for U.S. auxiliary benefits in life
cases or in survivor cases where there was a previously approved award in a life case
since this application would have been completed previously in connection with the
previous claim.
