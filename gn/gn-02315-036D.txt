D. Policy — Alabama good acquittance
Section 43-2-696 provides that a transfer made pursuant to the presentation of the
above affidavit will effectively discharge the debtor to the same extent as if payment
were made to a court-designated personal representative of the decedent. Therefore,
SSA is not required to inquire into the truth of any statement in the affidavit, or
to see to the proper disposition of the underpayment once the distributee takes possession
of it.
