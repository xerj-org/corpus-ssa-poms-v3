GN 03312.125 Foreign and International Cases
Refer all requests for information from a foreign or international law-enforcement
agency (e.g., Interpol) via the Department of Justice to the Office of Privacy and Disclosure for questions concerning disclosures in instances where there may be a possible violation
of another government agency's regulations.
