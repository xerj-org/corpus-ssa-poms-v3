GN 01722.230 How OIO Controls Requests Sent to the Spanish Agency on Form SSA-2960-U3-SP
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the Spanish agencies.
•
Diary initial requests and followups for 90 days.
•
Diary a request made by the FSP in Madrid for 90 days from the date the FSP signed
form SSA-2960-U3-SP.
•
Forward the folder to IOTS if no response has been received after two followups, i.e.,
after 9 months.
