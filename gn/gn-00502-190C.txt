C. Procedure for Title XVI Manual Payee Selections
The FO is responsible for manually updating the SSR for cases where the undocumented
alien parent payee or foreign payee applicant does not have an SSN. The FO must secure
the SSA-11-BK, make the payee selection determination, and generate (or manually prepare)
appropriate notices, and scan and retain the SSA-11-BK. The FO must manually input
the case using forms SSA-1719B – SSI Post-Eligibility Data Input or SSA-450 – Claims
Input Data.
IMPORTANT: When updating the SSR manually, be aware of situations where a beneficiary who has
a payee is also applying to serve as payee. By law, a beneficiary receiving title
II, VIII, or XVI benefits through a payee may NOT serve as a payee.
