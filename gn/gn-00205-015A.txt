SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00205.015 - Handling Changes and Additions on Applications
Effective Dates: 07/31/2023 - Present | TN 71 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200205015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for changes and additions on applications
1. Changes or additions before filing or attestation
The claimant, or proper applicant on the claimant’s behalf, may change entries on
their application up until the claimant, or proper applicant on the claimant’s behalf,
signs and delivers the application to us.
2. Changes or additions after filing
The claimant, or proper applicant on the claimant’s behalf, cannot make changes after
filing because the application is our property. We cannot void the application. We
must retain it since it is a Social Security Administration record. The only exception
is when a claimant, or proper applicant on the claimant’s behalf, files a retirement
application via the Internet, and mistakenly chooses Option C as the Internet month
of election (MOEL). For more information on MOEL, see GN 00204.055K.
