SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.035 - FO, TSC or PC Provides Replacement Accounting Form
Effective Dates: 11/17/2005 - Present | TN 24 (11-05)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure – General
Take the following action to ensure that WBDOC can clear the control on the record.
•
Use the eRPS, MBR or SSR to complete the top portion of the SSA-623-F6, SSA-6230-F6,
or SSA-6234-F6 with enough information to permit control clearance. See GN 00605.035D, for required information.
•
Provide the payee with the appropriate WBDOC address for control clearance. (These
addresses are provided in GN 00605.030B.2.).
