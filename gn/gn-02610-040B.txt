SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.040 - How To Manually Compute Title II Offset For Both Members of An SSI Couple Simultaneously
Effective Dates: 08/16/2023 - Present | TN 33 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Example
John Allen has been receiving SSI benefits of $368.00 for some time. John's MOET to
title II of $250.00 per month is 01/89 (due 02/89). John receives their first title
II benefit in 05/89. Ann Allen is entitled to $600.00 title II in December 1988 (due
in 01/89). Ann's CMA is paid in 05/89. Ann is determined eligible for SSI benefits
in January as a member of an eligible couple.
•
Title II offset applies because John has been receiving SSI for some time.
•
Ann's retroactive SSI benefits of $185.00 per month for January through April are
released without reduction.
