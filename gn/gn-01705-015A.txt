A. The Italian Social Security System
In general, the Italian social security system covers all people who work in Italy.
The Istituto Nazionale della Previdenza Sociale (INPS) administers Italian social
security, which includes several special systems for workers in specific occupational
categories. Under the agreement, SSA and INPS coordinate the activities and functions of the two
countries for benefits and coverage.
