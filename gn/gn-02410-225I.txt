I. Returned garnishment payments
When SSA receives a returned garnishment check or remittance, the returned amount
will be reissued through COGS to the beneficiary, court, or entity. For instructions
on processing returned garnishment payments, refer to SM 00832.540.
IMPORTANT: When a beneficiary has multiple active garnishment orders and we receive a returned
garnishment payment because one of the orders has been satisfied or terminated, apply
the returned amount to the other orders, subject to garnishment priority and limitation
rules outlined in GN 02410.215.
