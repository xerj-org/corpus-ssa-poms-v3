SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00312.328 - Romania – Vital Statistics Records
Effective Dates: 08/26/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200312328 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
All requests from individuals outside Romania must be made through diplomatic channels.
Only the Ministry of the Interior in Romania will issue certificates to non-residents
of Romania. In the case of direct contact between an applicant and a Romanian authority
(either the Ministry of the Interior or a local vital statistics office), a negative
reply is sent to the U.S. Embassy for transmittal to the applicant.
