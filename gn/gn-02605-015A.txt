SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02605.015 - Direct Input of COA by TSCs and FOs
Effective Dates: 12/07/2004 - Present | TN 23 (12-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202605015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. PROCEDURE
Direct Input
•
Input most title II COAs per MSOM T2PE 003.002, including those received from PCs for audit development.
•
Retain COA source documents for 3 months after input and acceptance for audit and
assessment purposes. Do not interfile source documents reporting other events, because
destruction dates may differ.
•
Input COA requests as soon as possible even though the input may not be in time to
redirect the next payment. Input changes to both the MBR and SSR, if necessary. Follow
GN 02605.035 if the individual is also receiving SSI.
