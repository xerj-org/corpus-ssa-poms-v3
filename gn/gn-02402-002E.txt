E. Title XVI EFT payment process
We follow these steps for the payment process:
1.
We send Title XVI EFT and check payments to Treasury in one file;
2.
Treasurys RFC transmits the payments electronically to the correct FRB;
3.
The FRBs transmit the EFT payments to the FIs through the ACH system, in time for
the payments to be posted to the recipients accounts and available at the opening
of business at the FI on the payment date; and
4.
We send a separate EFT payment for each recipient.
