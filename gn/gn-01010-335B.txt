B. Procedure for foreign claims
In the Office of International Operation (OIO), a FO adjudicator in the International
Benefits Office (IBO) will prepare the adverse claim or adverse adjustment notice
and include it (undated) in the claims file with the award. A CA in the appropriate
processing branch (PC8) will subsequently date and release the letter if we authorize
the award.
