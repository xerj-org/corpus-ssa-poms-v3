GN 01726.230 Controlling Requests Sent to Dutch Agencies on Form SSA-2960-NL
Procedure
•
Prepare a diary when information is requested from a Dutch agency.
•
Diary initial requests and follow-up requests for 60 days.
•
1st and 2nd follow- up conducted by DIO Benefit Earnings Technician (BET.)
•
3rd follow-up routed by DIO BET to DIO Deputy Module Manager to contact Foreign Service
Post (FSP) for assistance.
•
4th follow up routed by DIO BET to DIO Module Manager to contact FSP Supervisor.
•
After 4th follow up DIO Module Manager should route to DIO Management Program Analyst/Deputy
Division Director/Division Director to contact Regional Federal Benefits Officer for
assistance.
