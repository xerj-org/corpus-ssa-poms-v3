A. Title XVI benefits are used to compute windfall offset
Windfall offset applies to any title XVI benefit payable including the following payments
in the offset calculation.
•
Payments made to the State under interim assistance reimbursement (IAR)
•
Emergency advance payments
•
Immediate payments
•
Presumptive disability/blindness payments
