A. Automatic status notice for an application for Polish benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about the Polish claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of
the worker’s U.S. coverage record) to Poland, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET)
electronically stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC)
system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Polish agency. The system automatically
generates a notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs the applicant
that the claims material was forwarded to the Polish agency.
