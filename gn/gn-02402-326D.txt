D. Overview of IDD for Jamaica
IDD payments sent to Jamaica must be in Jamaican Dollar (JMD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Jamaica. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
