Effective Dates: 05/10/2017 - Present |

A. Procedure - Initial Claims Packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Japanese coverage records and to inform
the Japanese agency about claims for Japanese benefits. (See GN 01745.205 – GN 01745.220 for the applications and evidence required to prepare the packages.)
