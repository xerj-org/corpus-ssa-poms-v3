GN 01775.320 Romanian Medical Exam Reimbursement Form
The Romanian liaison agency National House of Public Pensions will use the SSA e2960
USA/RO 3 (U.S. - Romania Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification
Form) for reimbursement of medical expenses regarding requests for exams.
