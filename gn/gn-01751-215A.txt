A. Policy — Applications for benefits under the Polish agreement
An individual filing for benefits under the agreement with Poland must complete form
SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only from the United States,
only from Poland, or from both countries.
