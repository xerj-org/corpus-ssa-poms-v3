C. Procedure to determine if TOP applies
To determine whether a TOP offset was processed on the payment, check the MBR TRANS
field, or the Payment History Update System (PHUS). When TOP offset applies, the following
information is on the queries:
•
The TRANS field of the MBR shows LAP-ZT PHUS - TOP OFFSET.
•
The PHUS record shows ZT PHUS TOP OFFSET followed by a line that states the amount
of the offset and the month the offset occurred.
When you need to know the type of offset (BPO or FPLP), go to Online Retrieval System (ORS) IESI, and look for the notice.
