A. Qualification for benefits
Under the Agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least six U.S. quarters of coverage
(QCs) but not enough to receive regular U.S. benefits, the United States can count
the NH's QCs under the Swiss social security system to permit the NH (with any dependents
or survivors) to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
