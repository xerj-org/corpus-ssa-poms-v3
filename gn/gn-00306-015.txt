GN 00306.015 Use of a State Court Judgment or Order to Establish a Parent-Child Relationship Under
State Intestacy Law
A State court judgment or order establishing a parent-child relationship that provides
inheritance rights is generally sufficient evidence of a parent-child relationship
under State intestacy law. The judgment or order should set out the legal basis for
establishing the parent-child relationship.
