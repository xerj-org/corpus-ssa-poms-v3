A. Introduction to Internal Revenue Service (IRS) levy
Section 6331 of the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Code gives the authority to levy
wages for delinquent tax liability. Section 6334(c) of the IRS Code contains a specific
exception to constraints on the assignment of Social Security Administration (SSA)
benefits as provided in Sec. 207 (b) of the Act. Properly served IRS Notices of Levy
for the collection of delinquent Federal taxes are enforceable against Title II benefits.
Effective July 1989, the Taxpayer's Bill of Rights (P.L. 100-647) prohibits levies
against SSI payments.
