E. Notations on completed claims file
1. Hardship cases
Identify hardship cases by marking (in red) the uppermost paper underneath the SSA-790
IDP or at the extreme top center “HARDSHIP.”
2. Congressional inquiry
If there is an unanswered congressional inquiry in file, staple Form SSA-4400 to whatever
is immediately under the SSA-790 IDP.
