GN 02406.160 Description and When to Use Department of the Treasury Forms FS-1133 and FS-3858
This section informs the technicians about the purpose of the forms used or requested
by the Department of the Treasury (Treasury), Bureau of Fiscal Service (Fiscal Service).
We use these forms to request information or assistance and to resolve nonreceipt
check claims filed by beneficiaries, recipients and representative payees for payments
issued by the U.S. Government.
