A. Introduction when payee fails to respond to SSA-6233-BK
SSA is required to obtain an annual accounting on the use of benefits and to monitor
the activity of funds in a dedicated account. If the representative payee does not
respond to, or will not cooperate with efforts to obtain, a SSA-6233-BK (Representative
Payee Report of Benefits and Dedicated Account), a change of payee may be in the best
interests of the beneficiary.
