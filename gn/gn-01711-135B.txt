Effective Dates: 12/08/1994 - Present | TN 2 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The agreement guarantees that Norwegian disability and survivors benefits for which
a person is entitled without totalization will never be reduced on account of U.S.
benefits below the pro rata amount that would have been payable if eligibility had
been based on totalization.
