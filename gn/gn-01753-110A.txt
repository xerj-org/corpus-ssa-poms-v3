SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01753.110 - Scope of the Agreement with the Slovak Republic
Effective Dates: 07/28/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201753110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Benefit programs included under the Slovak Republic Totalization Agreement
The provisions of the agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and Slovak coverage apply to the following types of benefits:
1. U.S. Retirement, Survivors, and Disability benefits
The agreement applies to U.S. retirement, survivors, and disability benefits. The
agreement does not apply to Medicare benefits or Supplemental Security Income payments.
2. Slovak Retirement, Survivors, and Disability benefits
The agreement applies to the Slovak old age, early retirement, survivors, and disability
benefits under the first pillar system.
