A. Month of Election (MOEL)
The MOEL is the month the claimant elects for their benefits to begin. The MOEL can
be the month of filing, a month in the past (subject to the rules of retroactivity,
see GN 00204.030), or a future month. The claimant can elect to begin benefits at any time, but they
must meet all factors of entitlement before we can pay benefits. The MOEL selected
is considered conditional and subject to change through the earnings enforcement process
due to the change in material earnings.
Material earnings for MOEL purposes means that, in the year of entitlement, a claimant
is working and estimates their earnings to be over:
•
Annual Earnings Test (AET); and
•
Monthly Earnings Test (MET) Limit for at least one month in or after the selected
MOEL month but prior to attaining full retirement age (FRA).
For processing purposes, we code claims in the Modernized Claims System (MCS) with
a conditional option code of “A” or “B.” For MOEL options codes, see GN 00204.041.
