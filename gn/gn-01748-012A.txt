SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01748.012 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops U.S. Claims Filed With the Danish Agency
Effective Dates: 02/01/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201748012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a person contacts the Danish social insurance agency to file a claim for U.S.
benefits, that agency sends the following information to the Oslo, Norway FSP.
•
A liaison form certifying the filing date for the claim;
•
Danish earnings record if it is readily available;
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
