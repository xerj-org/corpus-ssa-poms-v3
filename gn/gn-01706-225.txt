GN 01706.225 Representative payees - Italian agreement
Claims authorizers must advise the BET of the name and address of the payee selected
so that the BET may include this information in part IV of the SSA-2960-U3-IT, Transmittal
Request Form. This will permit Italy to pay an Italian benefit on behalf of the incapable
beneficiary to the payee selected by SSA.
