SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.468 - Coding Republic of Congo Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/23/2023 - Present | TN 235 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402468 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Republic of Congo will have a prefix of “75” at the beginning
of the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account
Number (DAN) field has the letter “A” assigned for Republic of Congo.
