SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01739.001 - Overview of Agreement with Chile
Effective Dates: 06/26/2014 - Present | TN 1 (06-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201739001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Purpose of the subchapter
This subchapter provides:
•
a brief overview of the Chilean system;
•
the instructions to take and process claims for both U.S. and Chilean benefits filed
under the agreement; and
•
the provisions of the agreement.
NOTE: You can obtain additional information on the Chilean Social Security System by visiting
Social Security Online at http://www.ssa.gov/international/links.html.
