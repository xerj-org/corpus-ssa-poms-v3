Effective Dates: 04/09/2025 - Present | TN 65 (04-25)

G. Program Service Center instructions
When you receive a case with an incomplete notice, check the DW01 screen in MCS for
a “527” code in the last 3 positions of the Unite Code. If the “527” code is present
in the last 3 positions of the Unit Code on the DWO1 or if the A101 Benefit Continuity
Remarks/Notice (BCRN) or the EF101 remarks continued (EFBCRN) screens state that Ely is involved:
•
If the most recent RPOC rationale for the determination states that the couple qualifies
for benefits under Ely
v.
Saul, include UTIs CAS019, CAS020, and CAS031 in the notice for initial claims. For claims
that have been reopened, include UTIs CAS018, CAS019, CAS020, and CAS031 in the notice.
•
If the most recent RPOC rationale for determination states that the couple does not
qualify for benefits under Ely
v.
Saul, include UTIs CAS021 and CAS020 in the notice.
•
If request to reopen was for deceased claimant, send notice to the individual who
is eligible for an underpayment on the deceased claimant’s record who contacted SSA
on behalf of the deceased claimant. For example, send notice to Jessica Smith on behalf
of Troy Brown (Deceased).
For UTI language, see section GN 00210.805H in this section.
