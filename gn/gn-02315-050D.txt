D. Policy for Idaho good acquittance
Any person paying debts under the above affidavit procedure is discharged to the same
extent as if dealing with the personal representative. SSA is not required to inquire
into the truth of any statement in the affidavit or seek evidence of how the successor
applied the money received. Idaho Code Ann. § 15-3-1202.
