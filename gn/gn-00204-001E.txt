E. Policy – Department of Veterans Affairs (DVA) application
An application filed with the DVA on one of its forms for survivors’ dependency and
indemnity compensation is considered an application for Social Security dependents
and survivors’ benefits except the lump sum death payment (see RS 01702.424).
An application filed with SSA for survivor benefits (i.e., SSA-4, SSA-5, SSA-7, and
SSA-10) is also an application for survivor benefits with the DVA (see the Note in
RS
01702.410A when a SSA life claim is converted to a survivor benefit claim).
