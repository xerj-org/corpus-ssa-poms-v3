SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.010 - Beneficiaries Subject to the Withholding Tax
Effective Dates: 02/08/2024 - Present | TN 18 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
If the nonresident alien spouse of a U.S. citizen or resident asks SSA to stop withholding
tax because they are treated as a resident by IRS,
•
Tell the spouse that the withholding must continue; but a credit can be claimed against
other tax liabilities when their tax return is filed.
•
Refer the spouse to IRS Publication 54 for information on the election and its effects.
