B. Description of form IRL/USA 4
•
The upper part of the form contains identifying information i.e. the worker's name,
date of birth and Irish and U.S. Social Security numbers.
•
In the main part of the form, the “FROM” and “TO” columns contain periods of Irish coverage shown in the European style, i.e. day/month/
year.
•
The third column contains the number of weeks of coverage within each coverage period.
•
The name, address and signature of the Irish technician who completed the certification
as well as the official stamp of the Irish agency appear at the bottom of the form
