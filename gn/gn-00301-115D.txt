D. Procedure - SSA-533 (Translation Request)
•
All employees can use this form to route foreign language documents for translation
(see GN 00301.365.)
•
SSA authorized translators can use this form to certify/translate foreign language
documents (SSA-533-FC in OIO);
NOTE: SSA employees who are authorized translators can use the SSA-533 to certify foreign-language
documents even though the translator is not an authorized SSA certifier under GN 00301.090.
