D. Overview of IDD for Barbados
IDD payments sent to Barbados must be in Eastern Barbadian dollar (BBD) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Barbados.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
