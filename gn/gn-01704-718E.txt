E. Diary period
1.
Diary initial requests to German agencies for:
a.
Six months, if the claimant has not previously filed for German benefits; or
b.
three months, if the claimant has previously filed for German benefits.
2.
Diary all followups and status requests for 90 days. If no response has been received
at the end of the second followup, forward the case to TOAB.
