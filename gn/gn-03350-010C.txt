Effective Dates: 02/13/2019 - Present | TN 6 (02-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203350010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
•
Immediately send any FOIA appeal request (whether or not it appears to be timely)
to the Executive Director of OPD.
•
OPD will control and develop the appeal and obtain any necessary clearances.
•
Employees should not contact the requester about an appeal currently in litigation
without consulting the OGC attorney handling the case.
