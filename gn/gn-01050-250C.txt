SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.250 - Disposing of Material When No Application is Filed
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 10 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Material which should not be forwarded to DEBS
The following list, which is not all-inclusive, represent the majority of types of
material which has been determined to have no future value and should not be returned
to DEBS:
1.
NUMIDENT Printouts (formerly SS-5);
2.
SSA-450;
3.
SSA-794
4.
Photocopies of E/R's;
5.
SSA-3018 (formerly OAR-3018);
6.
SSA-555-U3, Request for Status Delayed;
7.
SSA-2339-U3, Request for Postentitlement;
8.
Routine Reports of Contact (RC's) which contain no essential entitlement information;
9.
Copies of DO come-in letters;
10.
Letters requesting general information about the social security law which do not
express an intent to file for benefits;
11.
Copies of letters explaining the general provisions of the law;
12.
Assorted questionnaires not related to DEBS activities;
13.
Disability material (including medical reports) that may affect an established period
of disability. This material is to be sent to ODO. Any disability determination and/or
other material received from ODO must be returned to ODO. See GN 01050.055 B. and GN 01050.110.
14.
Forms SSA-795, Statement of Claimant or Other Persons, or SSA-5002, Report of Contact,
which do not request a service that is performed by DEBS and do not state or imply
intent to file for benefits;
15.
Applications for benefits on which the NH failed to follow up (material of this nature
is retained by the DO for six months);
16.
Report of telephone contact relating to taking of an application or other DO activities
by the claims representative;
17.
Loose material which cannot be associated to a claim or work currently in progress
in DEBS;
18.
Primary and secondary evidence which has not been requested by DEBS;
19.
Copies of materials which DEBS already has on record (Numident, ERF printouts, etc.);
20.
DO records of interviews which do not involve DEBS;
21.
Irrational, incoherent material or reports of interviews that deal basically in personalities
and do not request a specific service of DEBS;
22.
Proof document(s) submitted more than 12 months before the anticipated filing date
for benefits. If any material is sent to DEBS that should have been retained by the
DO (e.g., questionnaires) or should have been sent to ODO (e.g., disability determinations
sent to DEBS), or should have been forwarded to another DO or processing center in
the field (e.g., claims folders), DEBS will return the material to the forwarding
DO.
