A. Policy for entitlement to other benefits
Applications under title II cover all classes of title II benefits, (e.g., the benefit
types described in 202(a)-202(l) and 223 of the Social Security Act for which the
claimant is eligible on all Social Security numbers (SSNs). In addition, a claimant
is "deemed" to have filed an application for both reduced retirement insurance benefits
(RIB) and reduced spouse's benefits if eligibility for both exists in the first month
of entitlement (MOET). Refer to GN 00204.020 for the scope of the application and GN 00203.002 for discussing filing options.
