SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.454 - Coding Vanuatu Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 12/28/2023 - Present | TN 249 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402454 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Vanuatu will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “G” assigned for Vanuatu.
