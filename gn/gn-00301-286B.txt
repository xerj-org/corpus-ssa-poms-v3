B. Policy for retention of evidence
We retain evidence electronically in the Modernized Claims System (MCS), Consolidated
Claims Experience (CCE), the Evidence (EVID) screen, or the Certified Electronic Folder
(CEF). For faxing documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), see DI 81010.090.
