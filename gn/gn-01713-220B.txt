B. Process
The special Belgian application forms are not taken in the field offices (FO). After
receiving a claim for Belgian benefits that was filed with SSA, the Belgian agency
will mail the appropriate Belgian application form to the applicant. The Belgian agency
will include instructions asking the claimant to complete the forms and mail them
directly to the Belgian agency with any additional evidence that may be needed.
