SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.245 - Waiver of Recovery of Overpayments
Effective Dates: 06/06/2024 - Present | TN 22 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
Do not include the administrative adjustment amount when showing the overpayment amount
on the SSA-635 (Waiver Determination).
If the amount posted to ROAR (Recovery of Overpayments, Accounting, and Reporting
System) includes an administrative adjustment, delete the overpayment amount shown
on the SSA-635 and the administrative adjustment.
