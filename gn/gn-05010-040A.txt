Effective Dates: 04/09/1997 - Present | TN 10 (04-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. INTRODUCTION
An individual who has not been lawfully admitted to the U.S. for permanent residence
can be considered a U.S. resident if he meets a substantial presence test.
Under this test, presence in the U.S. is determined under IRS law and policy and is
not necessarily a period of residence for SSA purposes.
