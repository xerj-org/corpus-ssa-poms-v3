SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01070.222 - Unable To Locate Prior Folder In PC Operations
Effective Dates: 05/21/2007 - Present | TN 19 (05-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070222 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Information needed does not involve a factor of entitlement
1. FO action
•
Redevelop the issue in question.
•
Partially adjudicate the claim (GN 01010.110); and
•
Diary for 60 days.
2. Action
When the diary matures:
•
request the prior folder, and
•
prepare an amended award if applicable.
If the folder still cannot be located, follow the “prior folder destroyed” procedure
in GN 01070.224.
