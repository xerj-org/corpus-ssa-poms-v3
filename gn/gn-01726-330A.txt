Effective Dates: 08/11/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726330 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
SVB has designed two application forms for Dutch benefits under the agreement. These
forms are the NL/VS 202 (for old age claims) and the NL/VS 203 (for survivors' claims).
SSA has no role in the taking, supplying or forwarding of these forms. However, SVB
often includes these forms, completed by the claimant, in their claims packages.
