D. Denying a request for different rate of recovery
If you deny the request for a different rate of recovery, send a notice providing
the basis for the denial and the manner in which we will recover the overpayment.
Do not include a reconsideration paragraph. The decision as to the amount of withholding
is an administrative action and not an initial determination. Document the Remarks
screen in DMS.
