GN 03103.270 New Legislation or Change of Position Hearing Case
New legislation or a change of position on an issue may affect a matter previously
decided by the Administrative Law Judge (ALJ). The processing center (PC) may be able
to implement the change itself rather than refer it to the ALJ to make the change.
