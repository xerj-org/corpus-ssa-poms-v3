A. Check intercept background
On rare occasions, a field office (FO) may request the United States Postal Service
(USPS) to intercept a Title II or Title XVI benefit check prior to its delivery. This process does not apply to a direct deposit payment.
1. When to request a check intercept
a. Title II checks
This applies if a check is not due or routine procedures cannot stop its issuance
(i.e., suspension or termination).
b. Title XI checks
Only if the FO employee has determined payee misuse as discussed in GN 00604.001.
2. Who can request a check intercept
a. Title II checks
The processing centers (PCs) may ask the FO to intercept monthly or prior month accrual
(PMA) checks with incorrect amounts.
b. Title XVI checks
An FO employee may intercept a Title XVI check if he or she has determined payee misuse.
See the misuse procedures in GN 00604.001 - GN 00604.075.
