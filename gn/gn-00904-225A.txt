A. Introduction
To aid in processing claims from individuals outside the U.S., some FSPs (as shown
in this section GN 00904.225C) have been trained in the taking and development of Social Security claims and related
issues.
For consular jurisdiction in countries having more than one claims-taking FSP, see
NL 01510.100.
The FSPs listed in subsection C accept and develop all types of Social Security claims.
When the development is complete, the claim is forwarded to OIO.
Although the FSPs in Japan stock some SSA forms and applications, they are not trained
in claims taking. Claims from Japan are developed in the same manner as those from
other non claims-taking countries.
