SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03990.020 - Informing Claimants and Attorneys About the Equal Access to Justice Act
Effective Dates: 03/31/2009 - Present | TN 1 (05-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203990020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. SSA Adjudications
Social Security claims below the court level ordinarily do not meet the EAJA's definition
of “adversary adjudication,” and thus are not subject to the provisions of the act. However, if an EAJA application
is received for a Social Security claim adjudicated below the court level, see GN 03990.030.
