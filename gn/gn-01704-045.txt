GN 01704.045 Domestic-to-Foreign Change of Address
(See GN 01704.050 when a bank address is involved)
Special care should be given to domestic to foreign changes of address—in addition
to developing alien exception codes, CA's and/or BA's may have to develop the beneficiary's
country of residency if the guaranteed minimum provision or alien exception “A” are factors. Refer all domestic to foreign changes of address to BA's (See RS 02610.020)
NOTE: If a beneficiary
reports a temporary departure; e.g., less than 6 months, from the
United States, Italy, Germany or Switzerland and payment of or the
amount of his benefit is based on his residence in that country,
give the case to a benefit authorizer for any necessary diary or
development action.
