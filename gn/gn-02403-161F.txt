F. DMS file
Every evening, SERS gathers the remittance data and creates a batch file to send to
DMS. DMS processes and posts the remittance to the correct record. Remittances processed
through SERS do not produce a FORT or CCFORT, and no action is necessary to mail remittances
to MATPSC’s Remittance and Accounting Unit (RAU).
NOTE: Remittances processed within SERS do not generate an unverified alert.
