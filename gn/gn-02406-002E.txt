E. Obtaining status of the original check
When a B-stop is input, Treasury investigates the status of the original check after
the issuance of the courtesy disbursement check. If the claimant cashes the original
check and the courtesy disbursement check, SSA treats this as an overpayment and starts
recovery action per GN 02210.000. The overpayment is subject to the SSA appeals process. For additional information
about appeals under Robinson-Reyf see GN 02406.310.
