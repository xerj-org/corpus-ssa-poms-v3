C. Policy - Child Does Not Retain Inheritance Rights
If a child has lost inheritance rights in the NH's estate because he/she was adopted
by another person but can meet the dependency requirements referred to in GN 00306.165A., the child may be entitled under section 216(h)(3) of the Act, if all other requirements
are met.
NOTE: See SSR 63-28 for a case in which the child did not retain inheritance rights.
