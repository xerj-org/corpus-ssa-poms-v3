B. Procedure - State Sources
Field offices may request a birth, death, or marriage record by mailing the request,
using the Certificate Request Forms provided on the website within this section, to
the State at the address shown immediately below:
1. Address and FO Code
Birth/death/marriage
Vital Registration
Room 165
350 Capitol Street
Charleston, WV 25301–3701
Divorce
Clerk of Circuit Court
Chancery Side
in county where divorce granted
FO Code 305
NOTE:
You can find the most up-to-date information from West Virginia Department of Health and
Human Resources. In addition, you can find EIN information here.
2. Cost of Copy:
Birth/death/marriage $12.00, Divorce varies in each county.
3. Type of Payment:
Cash, check, or money order, but sending cash is at your own risk.
4. Remarks:
DHHR, Vital Registration has an index of divorces granted beginning 1968. Copies of
the decrees must be requested from the county where the divorce was granted.
Do not contact the parallel office. Make all requests for birth, death, or marriage
directly to the DHHR.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No
