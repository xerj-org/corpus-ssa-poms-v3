L. Does SSA charge fees for testimony?
Unless the Commissioner or designee grants a waiver, the requester must pay a fee
for services for information, records or testimony. They must pay in accordance with
20 CFR Parts 401.95, and 402.155 - 402.185. In addition, there may be additional costs incurred, i.e., wages, salaries and travel
costs.
