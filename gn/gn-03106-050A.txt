SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03106.050 - Responsibilities of the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) for Court Orders
Effective Dates: 08/29/2003 - Present | TN 12 (09-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203106050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. OGC’s Process for Effectuating Court Orders
1. OGC receives court order from the United States (U.S.) Attorney's Office or from a
court
When OGC receives the court order from the U.S. Attorney's office or the Federal Judiciary’s
Case Management/Electronic Case Files (CM/ECF) system, OGC is responsible for the
following:
•
Screening and identifying the nature of the court order (for example, affirmance,
remand, reversal, or attorney fee);
•
Recording receipt of the order in the Legal Automated Workflow System (LAWS);
•
Forwarding orders to the Social Security Administration (SSA) component responsible
for effectuation (see GN 03106.060 for effectuating component responsibilities, as well as GN 03106.036 for court remand order instructions); and
•
Identifying court orders that may warrant further appeal.
2. OGC believes court order warrants further appeal
If OGC believes that the court order warrants further appeal, OGC submits an Appeal
Recommendation to the Department of Justice and does not send the decision to the
effectuating component.
3. OGC believes no further appeal of court order warranted
If OGC determines that the case does not warrant an appeal, OGC is then responsible
for forwarding the order to the appropriate SSA effectuating component (Operations
or the Office of Hearing Operations (OHO)).
