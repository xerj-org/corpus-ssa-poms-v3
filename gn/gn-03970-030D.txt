D. Policy for withdrawal by OGC of charges prior to the hearing
If the representative's answer or the available evidence establishes to the satisfaction
of OGC that the representative should not be suspended or disqualified from acting
as a representative before us or from serving as an entity POC, OGC may withdraw the
charges. OGC mails the notice of withdrawal of the charges to the representative at
the representative's last known address.
