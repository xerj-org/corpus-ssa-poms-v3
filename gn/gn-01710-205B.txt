SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Switzerland
Effective Dates: 02/07/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Potential Entitlement to U.S. Totalization Benefits
If there is potential entitlement to U.S. totalization benefits, prepare an SSA-e2960-U3-SZ
to request the Swiss coverage record.
2. Claim for Swiss Retirement or Survivor Benefits
If there is a claim for Swiss retirement or survivor benefits, prepare a claims package
including:
•
Form SSA-e2960-U3-SZ;
•
evidence submitted for the Swiss claim; and
•
application Form CH/USA 2.1 or 2.2 (Application for an old age (or survivors) pension
for citizens of the United States of America residing outside Switzerland) if required
under the instructions in GN 01710.315B.
3. Claim for Swiss Disability Benefits
If there is a claim for Swiss disability benefits, prepare a claims package including:
•
Form SSA-e2960-U3-SZ;
•
evidence submitted for the Swiss claim;
•
medical evidence from DIO's files; and
•
application Form CH/USA-2.3 (Application for disability insurance for citizens of
the United States of America residing outside Switzerland) if required under the instructions
in GN 01710.315B.
