SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.700 - Exclusion of Military Reserve Pensions from the GPO Provision
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608700 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
The GPO no longer applies to spousal benefits for those persons who receive a pension
based in whole, or in part, on noncovered military reserve service.
This provision applies to new claims filed with benefits payable effective January
1995 and to beneficiaries currently in GPO as of January 1995.
NOTE: There is no time limit to remove the GPO.
