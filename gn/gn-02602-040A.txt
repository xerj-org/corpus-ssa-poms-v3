SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02602.040 - Requirements for Resumption of Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/12/1994 - Present | TN 58 (02-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202602040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Requirements for resumption from suspension or termination
1. Required eligibility or entitlement
Do not resume or reinstate benefits following a suspension or termination until it
is determined that the beneficiary meets all of the requirements for eligibility or entitlement effective with the month of resumption
or reinstatement.
2. Resumption of benefits
Do not resume benefits without evidence showing that the beneficiary meets all of
the requirements for eligibility or entitlement. Refer to the specific policy related
to entitlement requirements in POMS to determine payment eligibility. Example: For policy related to student entitlement requirements, see RS 00205.400.
When a determination reverses or revises a suspension or termination of benefits do
not take a new claim. In these situations, resume benefits for all months from the
date of suspense or termination through the period that the beneficiary continues
to meet eligibility or entitlement requirements.
3. When a claim application is required
A new claim application is required to establish reentitlement to benefits except
in cases:
•
of a reversed or revised suspension or termination of benefits, or
•
resolution of an erroneous death record, see GN 02602.055.
4. Reversal or revision of a number holder’s termination
When a determination reverses or revises a termination of benefits for a number holder
(NH), also resume auxiliary benefits on that claim for all months from the termination
date through the period that the auxiliary continues to meet eligibility or entitlement
requirements on that claim.
5. Resumption or reentitlement notice
When we resume payment following a suspension or termination, notify the beneficiary
and their representative payee (as appropriate) in writing regarding the reinstatement
action. Include the expected payment date and appeal rights in the notice if the reinstatement
is an initial determination. See GN 03101.070 for actions considered initial determinations.
