A. Process
Divorce records are available from the County Clerk in the county where the divorce
was granted. The cost of copy varies. Marriage records are available from the County
Clerk in the county where the license was issued. The cost of copy varies.
For birth record requests, write to the County Clerk at the address shown below unless
otherwise indicated. The counties charge $5.00 for birth/death /marriage records except
as noted below.
IMPORTANT: For birth record verifications when the conditions in GN 00301.210 are met, see note in Remarks.
