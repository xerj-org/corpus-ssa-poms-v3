D. Overview of IDD for Cayman Islands
IDD payments sent to Cayman Islands must be in Cayman Islands dollar (KYD) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Cayman
Islands. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
