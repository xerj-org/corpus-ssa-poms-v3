SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.135 - Voluntary Contributions to the French System
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The agreement gives U.S. nationals who reside in France the right to make voluntary
contributions to the French system on the same basis as French nationals. It also
permits both U.S. and French nationals to have their prior U.S. coverage counted,
if necessary, to establish the right to make voluntary contributions.
NOTE: U.S. nationals who reside outside of France (e.g., in the United States) are not eligible
to make voluntary contributions to the French system.
