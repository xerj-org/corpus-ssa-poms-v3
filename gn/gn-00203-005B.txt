B. Policy
The applicant's clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities will result
in:
•
Fewer reporting delinquencies
•
Fewer overpayments
•
Fewer dissatisfied claimants
What, when, and how to report should be explained at every initial interview and again
at every pertinent post-entitlement interview.
