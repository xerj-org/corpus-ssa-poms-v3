B. Procedure - State Sources
1. Address and FO Code
a. State Birth or Death records and Marriage records for the City of New Orleans
Louisiana Office of Public Health and Hospitals
Vital Records Registry
P.O. Box 60630
New Orleans, LA 70160-0630
FO Code 800 New Orleans Downtown
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information, see the Birth Certificates and Vital Records site. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
b. Only archived birth records over 100 years old, death records over 50 years old,
or Orleans Parish marriage records over 50 years old:
Office of the Secretary of State
Louisiana State Archives
P.O. Box 94125
Baton Rouge, LA 70807-9125
FO Code 803 Baton Rouge LA
c. Divorce Records and Marriage Records other than for City of New Orleans
The Louisiana Vital Records Registry can verify most divorce and marriage records.
If the record is not on the State index, however, or GN 00305.020 or GN 00305.140B. requires a certified copy of a divorce or marriage record, send the request and the
appropriate fee to the appropriate parish clerk of the court.
2. Cost of Copy:
Full Birth Certificate $15.00
Short form birth card $ 9.00
(Birth certificates at local parish courthouse may have an additional surcharge)
Death $7.00
Marriage $5.00
Flat fee of $5.00 for death or marriage record from State Archives
NOTE: As of 10/01/2003, an additional State charge of $.50 per transaction must be added
to the above fees.
3. Type of Payment:
For a certified record, make the money order or check payable to Vital Records if
you send the request to the Vital Records Registry in New Orleans. Make the money
order or check payable to Secretary of State if you send the request to the State
Archives address, and make the money order or check payable to the specific parish
clerk of the court if you send the request to a parish (e.g., “Jefferson Parish Clerk
of Court”).
4. Remarks:
Can FO 800 verify birth/death for entire state and Orleans parish marriage record?
Yes. Consent and ID are not required. For birth records, use EVVE (GN 00302.980). If not successful, send e562 and be sure to click on "Help" to supply all necessary
information including the BC number.
Can FO 803 verify State archive record? Yes. Consent and ID are not required..
Can FO obtain free certified copy or photocopy of birth/death/marriage record? No.
If the claimant meets the criteria in GN 00301.210 for SSA purchase of certified evidence, prepare a Third Party Payment using your
FO CAN. On all requests you send directly to the State for a certified copy of a birth
or death certificate, include a copy of a picture ID for the registrant and a signed
consent form giving permission for the document’s release to SSA. Please note that
the State office does not provide certified copies of divorce and marriage records
for other parishes, only for City of New Orleans. Send requests for certified divorce
or marriage records outside the City of New Orleans to the Clerk of the Court in the
appropriate parish office.
