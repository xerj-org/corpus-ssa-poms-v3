C. Jurisdictional PC’s procedure for forwarding the folder to the servicing HO
For TII only non-medical hearings, the PC completes the eNDSS and sends it along with
the folder to the servicing HO within 30 business days of receiving the hearing request.
Contact the servicing HO if you are not able to forward the folder and eNDSS within
30 days. Suspend actions not related to the hearing request. For information on completing
the eNDSS, see GN 03103.130.
1.
Add the eNDSS, documentation (worksheet(s) for overpayments and waivers), and evidence
to the folder.
2.
Complete and attach the SSA-636 (Transmittal Notice Hearing Case) and Non-medical Folder Flag to the folder.

Complete Part A of the Form SSA-492 (PSC Case Folder) and attach it to the folder
under the Form SSA-559 (Transmittal Slip for Claims Folder). This form alerts the
servicing HO to return the folder to the PC when they complete their action. The Office
of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO) uses the SSA-385 (OIO Case Folder)
for this purpose.
3.
For some Retirement Survivor Insurance claims and non-claim earnings discrepancy cases,
the PC may see that a fully favorable determination is possible or that the reconsideration
determination is incorrect. However, prepare and send the case to the servicing HO
for processing. Add a remark to the file that states the PC could make a fully favorable
determination.
4.
Mail the folder to the servicing HO and set a diary to control the folder as follows:
a.
Prepare a 180-day diary from the date of sending the folder to the servicing HO, 165
days for the hearing and ALJ decision and 15 mail days.
b.
After 180 days, the PC obtains the location and status of the case and sets diary.
In general, set a 60-day diary. Increase or decrease the number of diary days depending
on the status inquiry.
