B. Procedure
Use the information below to determine the proper claims actions for taking U.S. totalization
and Norwegian claims.
1. U.S. Totalization Benefits Only
For U.S. totalization benefits only:
a.
Complete Part I of the SSA-2490-F4
b.
Complete MCS application screens (see MSOM MCS 009.011 for completing the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen on MCS) or appropriate RSDI application(s)
(e.g., SSA-1 for retirement benefits).
2. Norwegian Regular or Totalization Benefits Only
Complete Parts I and II of SSA-2490-F4 for Norwegian regular or totalization benefits
only.
3. Norwegian Benefits (Regular or Totalization) and U.S. Totalization
For Norwegian benefits (regular or totalization) and U.S. totalization:
a.
Complete Parts I and II of Form SSA-2490-F4
b.
Complete MCS application screens (see MSOM MCS 009.011 for completing the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen on MCS) or appropriate RSDI application(s)
(e.g., SSA-1 for retirement benefits)
