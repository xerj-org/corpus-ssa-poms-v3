F. Procedure – capping the pro rata PIA
To determine if the capping rule applies, compute a non-Totalization PIA and compare
the amount to the pro rata PIA. Establish the non-Totalization PIA as the capped pro
rata PIA if it is less than the regular pro rata PIA. To compute the non-Totalization
PIA:
•
Assume the NH is insured based on U.S. coverage only and compute a PIA based on actual
U.S. earnings.
•
Consider all computations that would be applicable if the NH were insured based on
U.S. coverage only.
•
Where a particular computation method requires a given number of QCs within a specific
time period, consider only actual U.S. QCs when determining if that requirement is
met.
