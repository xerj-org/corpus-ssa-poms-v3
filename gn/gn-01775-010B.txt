SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01775.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications (Claims) Filed in the Federal Benefits Unit in Naples, Italy under the U.S.- Romania Totalization Agreement
Effective Dates: 09/02/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201775010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Developing and routing of U.S. claims for U.S. benefits filed at the FBU in
Naples, Italy
The following development and routing instructions apply to U.S. claims for U.S. benefits
filed at the FBU in Naples under the U.S.- Romania Agreement.
•
1. Development actions taken by the Naples FBU
•
a. Retirement and
Disability
claims - worker not insured based solely on U.S. coverage
–
Contact the applicant to obtain Form
SSA-2490BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement ) and any appropriate U.S. applications.
•
Forward Form SSA e2960 USA-RO 3 (U.S. - Romania Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification ) and Part I and II of Form SSA-2490BK to the liaison agency in Romania to request
coverage record;
•
Complete the appropriate MCS application screens including the Totalization (TOTL)
Screen (see MS 03509.011 ) and SSA 2490 Screens (MS
03507.001 ); and
•
Develop for any evidence needed to complete the application process.
–
b. Survivor claims under the Agreement - worker not insured based solely on
U.S. coverage
•
Complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the Totalization (TOTL) Screen (see
MS 03509.011 );
•
Complete Part 1 of Form-2490BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S.
International Social Security Agreement ) if the number holder's MBR is not already established with the Totalization agreement;
•
Forward Form SSA e2960 USA-RO 3 (U.S. Romania Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification ) to the liaison agency in Romania to request a coverage record; and
•
Develop for any evidence needed to complete the application process.
•
2. Claim sent to the International Benefits and Processing (IBP) for
processing
When all documentation and Romanian foreign earnings record are received, forward
the claim to IBP and include a copy of the completed liaison form sent to the Romanian
liaison agency.
