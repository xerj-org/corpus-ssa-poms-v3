SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.220 - Completing the U.S./Korea Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification Form (SSA-2960-KOR)
Effective Dates: 08/29/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
The SSA-2960-KOR is completed in the totalization module in OIO or by VARO in Manila.
It is used to:
•
Transmit claims and related material to the NPC
•
Request information from the NPC, and
•
Respond to requests from the NPC.
