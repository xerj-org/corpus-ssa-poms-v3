C. IBO procedures for completing the Administrative Message
Send initial and followup requests to FBUs and the Veteran's Affars Regional Office
(VARO) in the Manila FBU via the administrative message.
Field
Enter
Transfer to field
Leave blank
Addressee routing indicators (RIs)
The alpha RI for each FBU listed in the “TO” field.
NOTE:
RIs are shown in the Foreign Service Post Directory in the Telecommunications Routing
Indicator Directory (TRID).
To
The rank of the FBU. Since DOS's communications software requires a precise format
and recognizes only two FBU acronyms, enter either:
•
“AMEMBASSY”; or
•
“AMCONSUL."
After the rank enter a space and show the city in which the FBU is located.
Enter ATTN FBU or ATTN SSA DIVISION (for VARO).
Use a single slash to separate the RI from the FBU rank and city and double slashes
to surround the attention portion of the “To” line.
If there is more than one FBU, repeat the RI for each office.
Show “INFO” before the RI for any FBU which is to receive an informational copy.
Examples of what to enter after the RI:
One FBU:
•
AMEMBASSY MANILA//ATTN SSA DIVISION//
•
AMCONSUL HERMOSILLO//ATTN FBU//
Multiple FBUs:
•
AMCONSUL GUADALAJARA//ATTN FBU//RUEHME/AMEMBASSY MEXICO CITY//ATTN FBU//
One FBU with an Information Copy to the RFBO:
•
(RFBO)RUEHMD/AMEMBASSY MADRID//ATTN FBU//INFO RUEHRO/AMEMBASSY ROME//ATTN RFBO//
From
SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTRATION BALTIMORE
Subject
The subject of the message (e.g., Request for Services, Followup to Request for Services,
Response to Your Request Dated (date), etc.).
Text
Enter the message, the SSN, name of the NH and of the claimant, and the name and telephone
number of the preparer in case the FBU needs to discuss the case.
Transmit
If another page is needed to complete the message, enter “N” in the “TRANSMIT” field
and press “enter” or “PF8” to page forward.
To send the message, enter “Y” in the “TRANSMIT” field and press “Enter.”
