SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with a U.K. Agency
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these instructions for processing claims.
If the claimant is...
then...
already entitled to U.S. benefits
forward material to OIO and annotate it "already entitled to U.S. benefits."
not entitled to U.S. benefits
obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part I) and the appropriate RSDI applications, and evidence,
AND forward the claim to OIO with form UK/USA 2 and the U.K. coverage record. (UK/USA3)
​
NOTE: Do not develop any eligibility factors which have already been verified by the U.K.
agency.
