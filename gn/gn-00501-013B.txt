B. Procedure for determining the relationship between the payee and beneficiary
Carefully determine the type of payee which best describes the relationship between
the payee and the beneficiary and code the proper Type of Payee.
Check the previous payee code. If the relationship code between the payee and beneficiary
is incorrect, make the necessary changes.
