SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.045 - Manual Calculation of Past-Due Benefits
Effective Dates: 02/05/2026 - Present | TN 80 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Use Form SSA-1129 (Representative Fee Case Past-Due Benefits Summary) (see exhibit
in GN 03905.040) to manually calculate the past-due benefits amount in the following situations:
•
court cases, when requested;
•
administrative review cases in which the reviewing official requests verification
of the past-due benefits amount; and
•
the Hearing Office (HO) cases when the Administrative Law Judge or Administrative
Appeals Judge specifically requests it.
