SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Korea
Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — U.S. and Korean or Korean claim only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-2960-KOR
•
The special Korean application KOR-USA 1
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from SSA's files, if the Korean claim is for disability benefits.
Note: SSA files include claims folders in OIO, in OCO's Megasite or in the caves in Kansas
City.
•
Evidence submitted for the Korean claim, and
•
A request for the Korean coverage record, if one is needed
