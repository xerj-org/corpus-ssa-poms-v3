A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Divorce and Death Certificates
1. Office to Contact
Civil Registrar’s Office of the Burgomaster of the community in which the event occurred.
See GN 00312.280B. for a list of the addresses of the civil registry offices in the Netherlands.
2. Fee
11 euros
