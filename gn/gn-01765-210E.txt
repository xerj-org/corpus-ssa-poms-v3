SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01765.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for U.S. and Romanian Claims
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201765210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Developing evidence in support of an application for retirement, survivors, or disability
benefits
Take the following actions when developing evidence to support claims for Romanian
benefits.
1. Researching the types of evidence
When necessary, you can find additional information regarding the following types
of evidence.
•
GN 00302.000 Proof of age
•
GN 00304.000 Proof of death
•
GN 00305.000 Proof of marriage
•
GN 00306.000 Proof of child relationship and dependency
•
RS 00205.350B.1 Proof of school attendance
•
RS 01301.000 Living with, contributions and one-half support
2. Accepting Evidence
The applicant must submit the evidence noted in GN
01702.200 - GN 01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are claimed from Romania.
•
SSA accepts any medical evidence the applicant wishes to submit.
•
If the applicant does not want to release an original document, photocopy the evidence
and certify the copy.
•
Do not attempt to secure evidence for the applicant or offer advice as to what types
of evidence Romania may need.
•
Send all submitted medical evidence and the claims file to Central Processing – International
Operations (CP/IO), International Benefit & Processing (IBP) at the following address:
Social Security Administration
CP/IO - IBP
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, MD 21235-7769
USA
NOTE:
If a courier service is used, mail to:
Social Security Administration
Central Processing – International Operations
International Benefit & Processing
6100 Wabash Avenue
Baltimore, MD 21215
•
Complete the Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the date the FO
sent the Romanian claim to International Benefit and Processing (IBP). Input "Totalization"
as the ISSUE.
•
Inform the applicant that the responsible Romanian agency will contact them directly
to obtain any additional documents or evidence needed to make a determination.
CAUTION:
Do not hold claims for Romanian benefits for prolonged periods pending receipt of
evidence. If an applicant is unable to obtain needed evidence within a reasonable
time, transmit the claim to the IBP without the evidence. Advise the applicant that
the responsible Romanian agency will contact them directly to obtain any needed evidence.
3. Documenting the File
Document the file to show when a factor of entitlement for a Romanian benefit has
already been verified for a U.S. claim.
a. Concurrent Title II claims
•
Send relevant evidence with the Romania claim to the Program Service Center (PSC)
of jurisdiction, and
•
Electronically send a certified photocopy of the relevant evidence with the Romanian
claim to: FAX number: 877-385-0645
b. Prior Title II claims
Attach a Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) printout with the Romanian claim sent to
IBP to reflect which factors, other than disability, have been verified in a prior
file.
4. Obtaining the number holder's (NH) U.S. earnings record
Obtain the NH's U.S. earnings record in all claims for Romanian benefits when the
applicant is filing for both U.S. and Romanian benefits. You can complete the Modernized
Claims System (MCS) screens. The MCS screens will automatically generate a separate
earnings record for the Romanian claim.
When a claimant files for Romanian benefits only, which is a MCS exclusion, request
an Informational/Certified Earnings Record (ICERS) per MS
02101.004 .
Obtain proof of lag earnings (unposted paid earnings in the current or preceding year
through the quarter of attainment of age 65 if the NH is a man or the quarter of attainment
of age 61 and 10 months if the NH is a woman). For proof of lag earnings, see RS
01404.005 and RS 01404.018 . For proof of lag self-employment earnings, see RS
01804.100 .
