SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.363 - Coding Nauru Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/29/2024 - Present | TN 264 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402363 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Nauru will have a prefix of “66” at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “N” assigned for Nauru.
