B. Premiums Paid by Enrollee
Where the enrollee paid the enrollee's own premiums, the unearned premiums will be
refunded to the enrollee. (See HI 01001.040 if the enrollee is a monthly benefit recipient.) No request for refund will be necessary
as the request for termination will be deemed an application for any excess premiums
on the enrollee's account.
