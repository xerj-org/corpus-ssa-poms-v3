GN 00203.014 Interviewing People who may be Victims of Abuse
When you become aware of allegations or evidence where children or vulnerable adults
may be the victims of abuse or neglect, follow the instructions in GN 00502.138.
To avoid duplicate reporting, inform the Disability Determination Services (DDS) if
someone reports abuse to another State agency.
