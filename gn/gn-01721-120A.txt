SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.120 - U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Spanish Agreement
Effective Dates: 04/22/2004 - Present | TN 7 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Under the agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least six U.S. quarters of coverage
(QCs) but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under the Spanish Social Security system
can be added to NH's U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents or survivors)
to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
