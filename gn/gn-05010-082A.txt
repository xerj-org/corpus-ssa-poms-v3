A. DEFINITIONS
1. Israel
For tax treaty purposes, Israel is Israel as it existed prior to the 1967 war. It does NOT include the West Bank, Gaza Strip, Golan Heights or East Jerusalem.
NOTE: Areas outside Israel can generally be identified by the address which may contain
the terms such as: “West Bank,” “East Jerusalem,” “via Israel,” “Ramallah,” or “Ramallah District.”
2. Resident of Israel
A resident of Israel is any person who is:
•
resident in Israel for the purposes of Israeli tax; or
•
an Israeli citizen and on whom taxes are imposed by Israel pursuant to the Income
Tax Ordinance on income from sources outside Israel.
