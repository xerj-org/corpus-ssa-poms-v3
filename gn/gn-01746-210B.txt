SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01746.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in Japan
Effective Dates: 05/10/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201746210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - U.S. and Japanese or Japanese Claim Only
Prepare an SSA-e2960-J to:
•
Request the Japanese coverage record if one has not been requested, include a copy
of form J/USA 4, or
•
Send the Japanese Agency the information or documentation they have requested.
NOTE: Under this agreement, most correspondence on liaison issues should take place between
the Japanese agencies and FSP-Manila.
