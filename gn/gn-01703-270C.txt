C. Procedure - disability cases
When a disabled beneficiary acquires nontotalization insured status:
Route folder to DRDD for resolution of cessation of DIB, trial work period, and related
issues. (DRDD will return the file to the module with a decision on continuing DIB
entitlement.)
Disability ceases - Terminate all entitled beneficiaries and retain the file in the module, if DIB entitlement
ceases due to NH's work.
Disability continues - Follow steps 1 - 3 in B above.
Transfer the folder to the appropriate module if at least one beneficiary is a foreign
resident. Transfer the folder to ODO, or if over age 59, to the PSC with a complete
explanation of the reason for the referral, if all beneficiaries are U.S. residents.
