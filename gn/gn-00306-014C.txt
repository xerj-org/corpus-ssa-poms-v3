C. Applying State intestacy laws to cases involving surrogacy
For all cases involving surrogacy, request a legal opinion using instructions in GN 01010.815.
EXCEPTION: Cases in which a court decree establishes a biological parent-child relationship
(see GN 00306.100) do not require a legal opinion unless the decree is questionable (see GN 00306.015).
