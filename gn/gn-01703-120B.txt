SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.120 - How to Process Amended Foreign Coverage Records
Effective Dates: 02/03/2017 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Action is taken on the amended coverage record only if the totalization claim is still
pending or if the totalization claim has been adjudicated and both of the following
conditions are met:
•
The additional or deleted foreign coverage would change SSA's earlier determination
of insured status or month of entitlement (MOEN), and
•
The rules of administrative finality permit reopening the initial decision. See GN 04001.010, GN 04010.001, GN 04020.070, and GN 04020.090 for further discussion of administrative finality.
