SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.225 - Requests for Status of Application for Spanish Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 6 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Spanish claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Spanish benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Spanish agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Spain
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Spain, advise the applicant to
write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Spanish
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Spanish agency, advise the applicant to
contact the Spanish agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Social
Security in Spain
•
Ministry of Labor, Migration,
and Social Security
DIO is unable to determine the status of Spanish claims after a technician forwards
the claim to Spain. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims,
see GN 01702.520.
