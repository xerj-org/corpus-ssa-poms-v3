A. Background of the state and territory digest of barred crimes
Using information provided by the Office of General Counsel (OGC), we have compiled
a digest of State and United States territory criminal law terminology to assist with
determining if an individual is barred from serving as a representative payee per
the policy in GN 00502.133A.4.
