GN 02406.425 Treasury Case Action Codes of Claims Disposition Notices (CDN)
U.S. Department of the Treasury (Treasury) sends us a systems-generated claims disposition
notice (CDN) that indicates Treasury's decision of a forgery determination on a handwriting
analysis claim.
A current list of the possible CDN messages from Treasury is in GN 02406.425B in this section.
