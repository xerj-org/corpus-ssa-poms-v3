A. Introduction
As explained in GN 01733.130, Luxembourg has recent coverage requirements for the payment of disability or survivors
benefits. The worker must have made contributions for at least 12 months during the
three years before the onset of disability or the date of death. The 3 year period
may be extended for periods of education, child-rearing and prior periods of disability.
