D. FO procedure to report improper use and misuse to VA
If you find that a VA fiduciary is not performing their duties in the veteran's best
interests, or if you are investigating or have found misuse by a payee who is also
a VA fiduciary, report the information to the VA Fiduciary Hub e-mail address and
your regional representative payee coordinator. To determine the proper hub address
depending on the state, see GN 00602.100E.
IMPORTANT: When you report to the VA, only disclose information that is relevant and necessary
to the investigation.
In some situations, it may be appropriate to incorporate paragraphs in GN 00602.090C.1.
The VA directs follow-up correspondence to you and the regional CPS mailbox.
