A. Introduction to the translation process
We adjudicate thousands of claims per year based upon foreign-language documents.
The majority of these documents are from other countries, but some are issued by churches,
temples and other organizations within the United States. If a person submits a foreign-language
document that we need to review to determine entitlement, we will have it translated.
