GN 01753.125 Eligibility for Slovak Benefits Under the Agreement with the Slovak Republic
Under the Totalization agreement with the Slovak Republic, SSA takes applications
for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of the Slovak Republic. SSA also
provides limited assistance in obtaining evidence needed to support the claim.
