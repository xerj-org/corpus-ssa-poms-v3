A. Representative payee and legal guardian
In cases where beneficiaries are unable to manage their own benefits, and they have
a legal guardian, the Italian social security program will generally make the guardian
the representative payee. If there is no appointed guardian, Italy accepts the Social
Security Administration’s recommendation concerning the payee.
