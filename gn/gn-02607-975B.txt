B. Inmate status codes
1. Codes effective 04/01/00
CODE
DEFINITION
DESCRIPTION
Y
Convicted
Individual convicted of a crime (felony or misdemeanor) and confined to a correctional
institution.
C
Confined but not convicted
Individual confined in a correctional institution but not convicted of a crime.
I
Institutionalized by court order (NGRI)
Individual found not guilty by reason of insanity or found incompetent to stand trial
and confined to a public institution as a result of a court order in connection with
a criminal case.
X
Sexually dangerous person
Individual confined in the public institution based on a determination that they are
a sexually dangerous person or a sexual predator.
P
Parole violation
Individual violated parole and returned to a correctional institution.
E
Escapee
Individual escaped from a correctional institution.
J
Confined juvenile
Inmate, under age 18, in the custody of juvenile authorities; will not be tried as
an adult for their crime.
A
Juvenile convicted as an adult
Inmate, under age 18, convicted of a crime as an adult.
2. Codes used before 04/01/00
CODE
DEFINITION
DESCRIPTION
N
Convicted, development of more than 1-year sentence criteria needed
Individual convicted. Sentence may be 12 months or less. Reporter unable to verify
length of sentence.
Y
Convicted, more than 1-year sentence criteria met
Individual convicted of a crime with a sentence of more than 1 year or crime carries
possible sentence of more than one year.
S
Convicted, more than 1-year sentence criteria not met
Reporter confirms crime carries a sentence of 12 months or less.
C
Not convicted, charge not punishable by sentence > 1 year
Crime carries possible sentence of one year or less.
D
Not convicted, charge potentially punishable by sentence > 1 year
Crime carries possible sentence of more than 1 year, but reporter unable to determine
the length of the sentence.
I
Institutionalized by court order (NGRI)
Individual found not guilty by reason of insanity or found incompetent to stand trial
and confined to a public institution as a result of a court order in connection with
a criminal case.
P
Parole violation
Individual violated parole and returned to a correctional institution.
J
Confined juvenile
Inmate, under age 18, in the custody of juvenile authorities; will not be tried as
an adult for their crime.
A
Juvenile convicted as an adult
Inmate, under age 18, convicted of a crime as an adult.
