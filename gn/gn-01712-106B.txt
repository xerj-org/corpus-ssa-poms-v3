SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01712.106 - How to Evaluate Norwegian Coverage Certifications
Effective Dates: 12/07/1993 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201712106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these rules to evaluate Norwegian “Sickness Pay”:
•
Do not develop substantial gainful activity just because a Norwegian coverage certification
shows earnings after onset.
•
Do not impose work deductions after entitlement to U.S. benefits just because of entries
shown on Norwegian coverage certifications.
NOTE: “Sickness Pay” does not represent foreign work.
