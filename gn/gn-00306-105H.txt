H. Dependency for NH's child based on written acknowledgment
If you establish the child's relationship to the NH based on the written acknowledgment
provision of section 216(h)(3), deem the child dependent on the NH unless someone
other than the NH adopts the child during the NH's lifetime (see GN 00306.165).
