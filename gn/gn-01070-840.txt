GN 01070.840 How to Associate Evidence When It Is Received
Apply the following procedure when evidence is received in the module:
Step
Action
1
Query EM 2.8 using the SSN located on the letter and/or evidence to determine the
current location of the case.
2
Associate evidence documents with case material located in the diary hold file (DHF).
3
Process per current processing instructions.
