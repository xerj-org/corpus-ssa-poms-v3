SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01729.210 - Developing Evidence in Claims for Austrian Benefits
Effective Dates: 09/27/2021 - Present | TN 12 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201729210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Using evidence submitted in the claim for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to submit additional evidence to file for Austrian benefits
since evidence used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits, both regular and Totalization, is sufficient for Austria to establish the
same factor for Austrian benefits.
