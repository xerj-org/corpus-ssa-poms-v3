E. Description of Lebanon IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Lebanon comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The full length of the IBAN for
Lebanon consists of 28 characters. However, the bank data on the MBR consists of 27
characters including Lebanon’s country code of C.
