SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00304.100 - Use of Numident Death Data
Effective Dates: 06/15/2022 - Present | TN 40 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200304100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Death data shown on the Numident can be used as preferred proof of death for initial
claims processing when
•
The data was established on the Numident by preferred proof of death, i.e., a death
certificate or Form SSA-721 (Statement of Death By Funeral Director); (see GN 02602.050A.); OR
•
The data was established on the Numident based on an Electronic Death Registration
(EDR) Report.
REFERENCE: For Numident coding that indicates that the Numident can be used as preferred proof
of death see the instructions in GN 00304.100B.3.
