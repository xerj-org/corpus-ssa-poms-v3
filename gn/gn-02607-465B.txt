SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02607.465 - Transferring Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS) Records
Effective Dates: 09/10/2015 - Present | TN 33 (09-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202607465 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to transfer PUPS records
1. FO transfers
Transfer a PUPS pending record when:
•
PUPS assigned the alert to the wrong servicing office; or
•
the alert involves a PUPS Title II automated processing exclusion or a T2R exception.
In these cases, the PC must suspend benefits manually. (For information on PUPS automated
processing exclusions, see GN 02607.740. For information on T2R exceptions, see GN 02607.750. )
NOTE: PCs do not work Title XVI cases. Do not transfer concurrent Title II and Title XVI
pending PUPS records to a PC. Process the Title XVI PUPS record according to the processing
instructions in GN 02607.705. After closing the Title XVI PUPS record, transfer the Title II PUPS pending record
to the servicing PC.
2. PC transfers
Transfer a PUPS pending record when:
•
PUPS assigned the alert to the wrong servicing location; or
•
the FO must develop identity, confinement, conviction, or other inmate information.
