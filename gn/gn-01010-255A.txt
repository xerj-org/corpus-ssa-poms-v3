A. Foreign claims jurisdiction
Border Field Offices (FOs) and the International Benefits Office (IBO) in the Office
of International Operations (OIO) have adjudicative authority for claimants with foreign
addresses. This includes adjudicative authority for technical denials in disability
claims. In addition, IBO and specific FOs have authority for totalization claims from
certain countries (see GN 01701.005).
