C. DIB-DWB claims by one Individual - Two SSN's
Prepare the DIB folder as explained in GN 01050.165, and the DWB as explained in A. above. Staple the back flap of the DIB folder to
the front flap of the DWB folder. If either folder doesn't require DDS review, prepare
a R/C to this effect and place it in the DIB folder.
