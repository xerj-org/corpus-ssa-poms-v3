A. Process
The Georgia State Office of Vital Records has divorce and marriage records recorded
since June 9, 1952 and no certified copies are available. Divorce records are available
from the Clerk of the Superior Court in the county where the divorce was granted.
Marriage records are available from the Probate Court in the county where the license
was issued. The cost of the copies varies. If you need a marriage, death or birth
record send your request to the County Clerk provided by the vital records office.
