SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.061 - Acquiescence Ruling On Offset (Dion and Fagner)
Effective Dates: 01/23/1999 - Present | TN 12 (10-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610061 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Dion
The Court of Appeals in the First Circuit ruled that offset does not apply to cases where the claimant:
a.
Resided in Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island or Puerto Rico at the
time a determination or decision is made at any administrative level (i.e., initial,
reconsideration, ALJ or AC), and
b.
Applied for concurrent claims under title II and title XVI, and
c.
Was awarded retroactive benefits for months prior to July 1981, and
d.
Was paid retroactive title II benefits before February 1985.
