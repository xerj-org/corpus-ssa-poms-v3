SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01732.012 - How the Regional Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with a Finnish Agency
Effective Dates: 06/24/1994 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Not already entitled to U.S. Benefits
If the claimant is not already entitled to U.S. benefits and completed U.S. applications
are:
a.
not submitted, then
•
obtain the appropriate RSDI applications and form SSA-2490 (Part I)
•
obtain any necessary evidence
•
forward the claim to OIO with the form SF/USA 2 and the Finnish coverage record
b.
submitted, then
•
review completed applications and resolve any missing or questionable answers
•
obtain any missing RSDI applications, including form SSA-2490 F4 (Part I)
•
obtain any necessary evidence
•
forward the claim to OIO with the form SF/USA 2 and the Finnish coverage record
2. Already entitled to U.S. Benefits
If the claimant is already entitled to U.S. benefits and completed U.S. applications
are or are not submitted, then forward material to OIO and annotate it “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
