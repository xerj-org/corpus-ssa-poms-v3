SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.041 - How To Apply Offset
Effective Dates: 01/06/2011 - Present | TN 33 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610041 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Example — couples case
Joe and Sue Smith are entitled to title II and title XVI benefits. Joe's offset amount
is $1250.00. Sue's offset amount is $400.00. Joe's retroactive title II benefits in
the offset period are $1000.00. Total retroactive title II benefits due Joe are $1100.00.
The $1250.00 in offset is first subtracted from the title II due in the offset period,
reducing it to zero. Joe has no NCI. The remaining $250.00 reduces the retroactive
title II due outside the offset period to zero leaving a balance of $150.00 offset
to be collected.
Sue is due $500.00 total retroactive benefits, $200.00 in the offset period and $300.00
outside the offset period. Sue's offset amount is $400.00. The $400.00 reduces the
title II due in the offset period to 0, and the title II due outside the offset period
to $100.00. This $100.00 is then reduced by the $150.00 remaining offset from Joe,
reducing Sue's NC1 to 0. The $50.00 remaining offset is excess offset and is not collected
as an overpayment.
