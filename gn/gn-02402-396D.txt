D. Overview of IDD for Montenegro
IDD payments sent to Montenegro must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefit Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Montenegro. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
