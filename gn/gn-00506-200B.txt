B. Procedure for DAA condition
To determine if a beneficiary meets the DAA condition, query the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR) and Supplemental Security Income Record (SSR) and look for the identifying
DAA coding shown in SM 01601.790C.19. (for Title XVI) and SM 00510.200 (for Title
II).
