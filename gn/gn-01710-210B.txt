SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in Switzerland
Effective Dates: 03/15/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to prepare a claims package.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Prepare a claims package if a Swiss coverage record is required and has not been requested
by the FSP in Switzerland.
•
Include Form SSA-2960-U3-SZ to SSA-e2960-U3-SZ in the package.
2. U.S. and Swiss or Swiss claim only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
the Swiss coverage record is required and has not been requested by the FSP; or
•
the Swiss agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-SZ to SSA-e2960-U3-SZ; and
•
information or documentation requested by the Swiss agency.
