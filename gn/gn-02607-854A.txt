A. Procedure – Reinstating benefits to sexually dangerous persons
The instructions in GN 02607.850A for determining whether you can reinstate title II benefits to an NGRI individual
also apply to sexually dangerous persons. See GN 02607.850 for the rules and procedures for reinstating benefits to individuals who are no longer
confined because they are sexually dangerous.
