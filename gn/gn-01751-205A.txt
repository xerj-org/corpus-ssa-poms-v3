A. Policy — evidence required under the Polish agreement
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with Poland,
the applicant should submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish
a claim for regular (non-Totalization) retirement, survivors or disability insurance
benefits. In addition, the applicant should submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 – GN
01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are claimed from the United States.
