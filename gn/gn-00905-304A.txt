SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.304 - Requests for Evidence or Other Information from Sources in Mexico
Effective Dates: 04/16/2018 - Present | TN 8 (07-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905304 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If there is no claimant or beneficiary in Mexico, FOs can request development directly
from the appropriate FBU. Except as explained in GN 00905.300C.1., if there is a claimant or beneficiary in Mexico, the request must be through DIO.
