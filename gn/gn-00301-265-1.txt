SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.265 - Safeguarding claimant's documents
Effective Dates: 05/15/2003 - Present | TN 9 (03-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301265 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Handling
a.
Do not fold documents if the claimant submits them unfolded.
b.
When forwarding documents for translation, use an envelope of sufficient size with
protective inserts or a mailing tube. If an envelope is used, type or stamp the following
legend on the face of the envelope: “Important Document - (Language) Translation Needed-Do not fold, bend or mutilate.”
c.
Do not staple, punch, or write on documents. An exception may be made in the case of the NH's name and SSN, and the resident FO.
This should be written in light pencil on the reverse of the document to reduce the
danger of loss or disassociation.
d.
Do not date stamp the document.
