C. Claimant or beneficiary returns to U.S.
If an RSDI claimant or beneficiary returns to the United States and the claim has
been adjudicated, the resident DO will receive a photocopy of the most recent award.
If a claim is in process, the resident DO will receive the incomplete claims file
from DIO.
