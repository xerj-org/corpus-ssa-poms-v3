C. Using evidence previously submitted in applications for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to resubmit the evidence previously submitted and verified
to establish factors of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits. The evidence
previously submitted for entitlement to U.S. Social Security benefits is sufficient
to establish the same factors of entitlement to Australian benefits.
