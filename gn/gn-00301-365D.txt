D. Procedure - Facsimile Transmission
1. General telefax transmission option
Follow the procedure in this section for facsimile transmission.
a.
Telefax transmission may be used as an option to request the translation of a foreign-language
document and to return the completed translation.
b.
Observe SSA's basic evidentiary principle that the claimant must always submit the
original of a foreign-language document or a photocopy properly certified by the custodian
of the record system to SSA for evaluation.
2. Requesting office
a.
Evaluate the document submitted by the claimant for alterations (additions, erasures,
etc.).
b.
Complete sections A. and B. of the SSA-533 “Translation Request” form.
c.
Telefax all printed faces of the foreign-language document and the face of the SSA-533
form to the translating office.
d.
Photocopy the completed translation when it is printed on thermal paper if it is being
used for legal entitlement purposes. (This is necessary because telefax processes
differ, and the permanency of the output degrades over a period of time. A photocopy
of the telefax will ensure the permanency of paper folder documentation for the entitlement
factor.)
3. Translator
a.
Complete sections C. and D. on the reverse side of an SSA-533 form and annotate item
C.1.b “telefax translation.”
b.
Telefax the completed translation and SSA-533 cover sheet back to the requesting office.
NOTE:
If, for any reason, the translator questions the authenticity of the telefax document,
they may telephone the requesting office and ask that the original document be provided
for evaluation.
