SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01714.310 - Belgian Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Effective Dates: 03/01/2017 - Present | TN 1 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201714310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If a Belgian liaison agency requests a medical examination for a U.S. resident, SSA
will schedule the examination and send the results to the Belgian agency.
Medical examinations for Belgium are obtained as follows.
•
Belgian agency requests for medical examinations are made on Form BE /USA 3.
•
DIO schedules an examination or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from DIO's files as described in GN 01704.810.
•
SSA requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for Belgium
using a reimbursement form. Since Belgium seldom requests a medical examination, a
Belgian reimbursement form has not been printed. If a form is needed, the case should
be routed to DIO program analysts.
