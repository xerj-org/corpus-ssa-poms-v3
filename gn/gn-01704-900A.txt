SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.900 - Handling German Life Certificates
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704900 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
The life certificate, Germany's version of a foreign enforcement form, is sent to
German pensioners residing in the United States. Its basic function is to verify that
the individual is still alive. Although the certificates vary in origin and format,
the same information is being requested.
