C. Reimbursement for travel
If claimants, their representative, or reasonably necessary unsubpoenaed witnesses
are required to travel, inform them of:
•
The right to travel reimbursement; and
•
The possibility of advance payment; and
•
The hearing office or DDS office, address information where a request for payment
is sent.
