SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01743.140 - Exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision (ANP) under the U.S. - Australian Totalization Agreement
Effective Dates: 02/02/2023 - Present | TN 3 (02-23)

B. Development requirements for certain non-U.S. citizen beneficiaries
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the ANP alleges residence in
Australia, develop residence status according to the instructions in GN 01702.230 to verify that the exemption provided by the Agreement applies.
