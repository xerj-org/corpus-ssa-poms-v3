B. List of required entries
•
The correct worker's name must be shown in Part A of Form KOR-USA 2.
•
A “FROM” – “TO” entry must be shown for each period in which there is an entry in
the “months of contribution” column.
•
A “months of contributions” entry must be shown for each “FROM” – “TO” period and
the number of months must be equal to or less than the number of months in the corresponding
“FROM” – “TO” period.
•
The form KOR-USA 2 must be properly certified, including the signature and official
stamp.
•
If any of the required information is missing, route the case to CPSI for evaluation.
