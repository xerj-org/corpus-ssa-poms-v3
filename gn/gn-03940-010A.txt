SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03940.010 - Receipt of the Fee Agreement in the Field Office and Processing Center
Effective Dates: 12/09/2024 - Present | TN 51 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203940010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Upon receipt of a fee agreement, document the date of receipt (date stamp, etc.) and
determine whether you have jurisdiction over the claim, post-entitlement, or post-eligibility
(PE) action. If you do not have jurisdiction, forward the fee agreement to the proper
component (preferably as an email attachment).
