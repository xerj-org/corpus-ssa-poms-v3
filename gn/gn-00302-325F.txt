SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.325 - Establishing the DB for Holocaust Survivors — General
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302325 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Policy
Special rules of evidence apply to claimants who were victims of Nazi persecution
during World War II. Under these procedures, the development emphasis is directed
toward establishing that the claimant is a Holocaust survivor, and assisting that
claimant in obtaining evidence when necessary. SSA thus avoids reliance on documents
based on the false DB which was adopted to enhance the claimant's chances for survival,
and establishes the DB based on the procedures in GN 00302.330.
