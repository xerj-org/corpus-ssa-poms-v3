SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.200 - Overpayments Involving Alien Withholding Tax
Effective Dates: 11/09/1990 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
These instructions apply to all overpayment cases involving alien taxation. Except
as provided in GN 05010.200 - 05010.270, overpayment policies and procedures in GN 02200.000 also apply to overpayments
for months in which alien tax was withheld.
