SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.730 - Evaluation of the 1910, 1920, and 1930 Census Record
Effective Dates: 01/07/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302730 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Comment
The Bureau of Census provides a 1920 census record when the 1910 record is requested
and the DB shown on the 1910 record is discrepant by more than one year (see GN 00302.810). This does not  imply that the 1920 record should be considered the best evidence obtainable or
that development should stop at this point.
If the 1920 census record and all other evidence in file except the 1910 census record
support the claimant's allegation, further development is not necessary. In other cases, it may be necessary to obtain other evidence to determine
the correct DB.
