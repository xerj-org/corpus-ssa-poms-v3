D. Overview of IDD for the British Virgin Islands
IDD payments sent to the British Virgin Islands must be in U.S. dollar (USD) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to IBP or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. IBP faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves the
British Virgin Islands. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
