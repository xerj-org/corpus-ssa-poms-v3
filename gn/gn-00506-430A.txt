A. BACKGROUND
Based on information obtained by the Office of General Counsel (OGC), a digest of
State licensing laws was compiled which summarizes the rules and regulations in place
for businesses and occupational licensing. This information was compiled to assist
the field in informing prospective fee for service organizational payees and volume
payees of the licensing requirements imposed by each State in which the organizational
payee provide services. This digest will help you determine if state and local regulations
require an operating business license or similar to start, operate, or expand a fee
for service payee business.
There are different criteria for determining whether a license is required. In some
states, a specific business license is not required for businesses that manage other
people's money. However, depending on how the state classifies the fee for service
organization, a business license may be required. For example, some states determine
that a fee for service organization that provides fiduciary services is considered
a trust company, and operating licenses are required. Most states issue licenses specific
to the business or occupation seeking the license.
The information in this digest is provided as a consultational guide only, and its
content may be subject to change at any time. All State listings contain the website
address as well as the appropriate licensing office or bureau's phone number. If you
need more detailed information, please contact your Regional Office for clarification
from OGC.
