B. Refund submitted and waiver is pending
While the waiver request is pending, if the beneficiary refunds the overpayment (either
in full or in part), consider the entire amount of the overpayment when making your
determination. This includes the amount of the refund. Process the waiver request
as normal by following the instructions in GN 02250.000
