SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.215 - Application for Benefits Under the Dutch Agreement
Effective Dates: 03/05/2013 - Present | TN 1 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with the Netherlands must complete
Form SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits Under A U.S. International Social Security
Agreement). This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only from the
United States, only from the Netherlands or from both countries.
