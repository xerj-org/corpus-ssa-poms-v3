B. Procedure — estoppel - general
1. Development
Generally do not develop the validity of the divorce in determining the status of
a spouse who is estopped (i.e., precluded) from denying the validity of a divorce
from the worker. However, it may be necessary if the status of the worker's spouse
by a later marriage is involved.
2. Legal Opinion
If the estoppel principle applies, check existing precedents. If a new opinion is
needed, follow the procedures in GN 01010.800 ff. for possible submittal to the Office of General Counsel (OGC).
