B. Policy - Taxes included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Dutch Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors and disability benefits,
family allowances, health and sickness insurance benefits, and unemployment benefits
under the NIS, EPIS, and SPIS.
