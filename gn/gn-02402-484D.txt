D. Overview of IDD for Burkina Faso
IDD payments sent to Burkina Faso must be in West African CFA Franc (XOF) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to OEIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Burkina
Faso. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
