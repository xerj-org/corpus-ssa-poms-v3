SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.366 - Coding South Korea Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/12/2022 - Present | TN 255 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402366 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for South Korea has a prefix of "68" at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “B” assigned for South Korea.
