SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.150 - Adversely Affected Parties
Effective Dates: 07/12/2023 - Present | TN 42 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. FO action
•
If the person receives a notice to appear at the hearing inquires, advise the person about the possible adverse effect
of the ALJ's decision upon their rights; and, why their evidence and testimony are
material. Tell the person that they may appear either in person or by counsel, or
may file a written brief. Give the person a copy of the appeals pamphlets.
•
If a person did not receive a notice from the ALJ asks to be a party to the hearing, help the person prepare a letter or
statement to that effect and send it to the ALJ.
