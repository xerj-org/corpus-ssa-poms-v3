SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01744.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Australia
Effective Dates: 09/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201744205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure – U.S. and Australian or Australian claim only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-2960-AUS. If the claimant is receiving a U.S. benefit, include the date of
entitlement and monthly benefit amounts. Include all changes in the monthly benefit
amount from the date of entitlement or October 2002, whichever is later.
•
The special Australian application, AUS/USA 4, and form Mod (i)
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
The Australian Work Ability Form (form WA) if the claim is for disability benefits.
•
Medical evidence from SSA's files, if the Australian claim is for disability benefits.
Note: SSA files include claims folders in OIO, in OCO's Megasite or in the caves in Kansas
City.
•
Evidence submitted for the Australian claim, and
•
A request for the Australian working life residence record, if one is needed.
