C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor bank for IDD to several Caribbean countries, including Grenada.
All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix of
"70" at the beginning of the Routing Transit Number (RTN) and an assigned letter in
the first position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for
Grenada is “K.”
