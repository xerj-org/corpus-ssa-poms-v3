Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302960 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Use of forms as evidence of age
IF...
Then...
The I-151 or I-551 was established before age 31
Use it as automatically convincing evidence of age as long as all of the other conditions
in GN 00302.115 are met.
NOTE: The form need not be the earliest INS record to be used as automatically convincing evidence.
Any other situation exists
•
Follow the development guidelines provided elsewhere in this subchapter, and
•
Use judgment in determining whether it is necessary to ascertain the existence of
an earlier INS record.
