SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.055 - Determining Processing Center Jurisdiction
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 14 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Claims where a beneficiary resides outside the U.S.
The Division of International Operations (DIO) has jurisdiction over all claims where
at least one beneficiary:
•
Uses a foreign mailing address; or
•
Resides in a country to which payment of benefits is barred or restricted; or
•
Uses an APO/FPO address specific to a foreign country, or
•
Uses a U.S. address because their representative payee lives in the U.S. as described
in GN 02605.220.
Note:
If at least one claimant or beneficiary resides in a country to which payment of benefits
is barred by the Treasury Department restrictions described in RS 02650.001B., DIO (PC8) has jurisdiction of the claim, regardless of SSN, mailing address, or
whether the claim is disability or non-disability.
