A. Process
The Louisiana Vital Records Registry in New Orleans is the repository for all Louisiana
birth certificates less than 101 years old and all death certificates less than 51
years old. However, a recent change now allows for issuance of birth certificates
at the parish level by clerks of court in most parish courthouses for an additional
surcharge. Check with the local clerk of court or the State website to determine if
a parish is participating in this new service.
