Effective Dates: 03/12/2009 - Present |

A. Using evidence submitted in claim for U.S. benefits
Evidence establishing factors of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits are
used by the Czech Republic to establish the same factors for Czech benefits. The applicant
needs to submit additional evidence to document a factor of entitlement for Czech
benefits.
