SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.400 - How to Maintain Folders Involving Totalization
Effective Dates: 06/02/2014 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Follow these policies when maintaining folders involving totalization.
1. Totalization folders
Totalization folders are blue claims folders with the legend “Totalization Claims Folder” printed on the front. The following are guidelines for using them:
•
Totalization applications, SSA liaison forms, documentation of SSA development, and
any notices are filed on the right side of the folder in chronological order.
•
Case control printouts, MBRs, material provided by foreign agencies, and adjudicative
forms are filed on the left side of the folder.
•
When they are maintained in conjunction with a nontotalization folder, the totalization
folder is always stapled on top.
•
If dual entitlement exists and both benefits involve totalization, the male's folder
is always stapled on top. In survivor cases, the folder of the live wage earner is
stapled on top.
•
If dual entitlement exists between a totalization benefit and a nontotalization benefit,
and both folders are OIO's jurisdiction (foreign beneficiary), both folders are maintained
in the module. The totalization folder is always stapled on top.
•
If dual entitlement exists between a totalization benefit and a nontotalization benefit,
and only one folder is within OIO's jurisdiction, the folder that is within the jurisdiction
of a domestic PSC or ODO is not requested or retained. That folder is maintained in
the PSC or ODO.
•
Totalization folders are maintained in the modules. Totalization folders are sent
to DRDD only when required by procedure. Totalization folders are never sent to another
PSC, ODO, or ODAR.
2. RSDI folder
The following are guidelines for maintaining nontotalization folders in the modules:
•
The RSDI application and related documentation are filed on the right side of the
folder.
•
All award and disallowance forms, as well as any documentation concerning payment
(SSA-2417-C1, SSA-3925-C1, etc.), are filed on the left side of the folder.
•
The nontotalization folder is retained after the final totalization decision, if totalization
benefits have been awarded or both totalization and nontotalization benefits have
been denied.
•
A nontotalization folder is never retained unless it is stapled to the back of the
related totalization folder.
•
Previously existing RSDI folders are obtained from other components if totalization
benefits have been awarded.
