A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
Requests must include the following information.
Birth Certificates
•
Full name at birth;
•
Date and place of birth; and
•
Parents' full names.
Marriage Certificates
•
Full names of both parties; and
•
Date and place of marriage.
Death Certificates
•
Full name of the deceased; and
•
Date and place of death
1. Office to Contact
Registrar General
P.O. Box 2226
Suva, Fiji
2. Fee
a.
Birth Certificate: Fiji $2.25;
b.
Marriage Certificate: Fiji $5.63;
c.
Death Certificate: Fiji $2.35.
