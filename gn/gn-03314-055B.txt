B. Procedure for handling requests
State agencies that administer the TANF program receive certain tax return and non-tax
return information from us primarily through electronic data exchanges, as authorized
under data exchange agreements with us. Consult with the regional office Data Exchange
Coordinator or Privacy Act Coordinator to determine if we have a data exchange in
effect with a particular State agency for its administration of the TANF program.
For more information regarding the handling of requests from State and local agencies,
see GN 03314.001E.
