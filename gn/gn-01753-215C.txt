C. Determining the required application screens on MCS for Totalization benefits
Use the following information to determine the application screens to complete when
taking claims under the agreement with the Slovak Republic for U.S. Totalization benefits
or Slovak benefits.
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
a. Retirement and disability claims
Complete the following in retirement or disability claims:
•
MCS application screens;
•
Totalization (TOTL) screen (You can view this screen in MSOM MCS 009.011); and
•
SSA-2490-BK screens (You can view this screen in MSOM MCS 007.001).
b. Survivors claims
Complete the following in survivors claims:
•
MCS application screens;
•
Totalization (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011); and
•
Part I of the paper or electronic (eform) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
2. Slovak benefits only (regular or Totalization)
Claims for Slovak benefits only (regular or Totalization) are MCS exclusions. Complete
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic (eform) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
3. Slovak benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. (regular or Totalization) benefits
a. Retirement or Disability claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the following:
•
MCS application screens;
•
TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011); and
•
SSA-2490-BK screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
b. Survivors claims
In survivors claims, complete the appropriate
•
MCS application screens;
•
TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011); and
•
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic (eform) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
