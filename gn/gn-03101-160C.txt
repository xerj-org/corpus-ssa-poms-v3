C. Postadjudicative issues
Res judicata can apply to post adjudicative issues as well as issues involved with
the filing of subsequent applications.
EXAMPLE: We denied the claimant’s waiver of recovery of an overpayment based on “not without fault.” Six
months later the claimant files another waiver request. When the adjudicator reviews
it, there is no new evidence. A second waiver determination is not required. The adjudicator
dismisses the request based on res judicata.
