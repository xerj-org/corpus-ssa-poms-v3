D. Field office applicant status request
When DIO forwards the completed application form and the certified copy of the proof
of citizenship to Korea, DIO annotates the MDW to show the type of claim, the date
the claim was forwarded and that a notice was sent to the applicant.
Take the following steps if the applicant contacts the FO for the status of the applicant's
claim:
1.
Check the MDW to see if DIO has sent the claim to Korea.
2.
If DIO hasn't completed the MDW, advise the applicant to write directly to DIO at
the address shown in GN 01741.130C.6.
3.
If DIO has completed the MDW, advise the applicant to write directly to the Korean
agency shown on the notice the applicant received from DIO when the claim was forwarded.
