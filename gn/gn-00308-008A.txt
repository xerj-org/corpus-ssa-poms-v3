A. Process
Divorce decrees are available from the Clerk of the Superior Court in the judicial
district where the divorce was granted: Juneau and Ketchikan (First District); Nome
(Second District); Anchorage (Third District); Fairbanks (Fourth District). The cost
of copy varies. The State office has divorce records since 1950.
