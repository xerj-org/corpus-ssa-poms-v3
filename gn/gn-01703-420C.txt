C. Procedure - misrouted claim for U.S. benefits
CA -Handle a misrouted claim for U.S. benefits as follows:
1. RSI claim - Worker insured based only on U.S. coverage
If the rerouted claim is a retirement or survivor claim, and the worker is insured based only on U.S. coverage only, and
•
it is detected in screening - route the claim to module 1 - 5 or the servicing PSC,
as appropriate
•
it is not detected in screening - process the RSI award and include the appropriate
totalization denial paragraph. Transfer the RSI folder to module 1 - 5 or the servicing
PSC, as appropriate
2. RSDI claim - Worker has less than six QCs
If the misrouted claim is a retirement, survivor, or disability claim, for which the
worker has less than six QCs, and it is detected in screening or later transfer the
RSDI folder to module 1 - 5 or the servicing PSC, as appropriate
3. Disability claim - Worker meets status based on U.S. coverage only
If the misrouted claims is a disability claim in which the worker meets disability insured status based on U.S. coverage only and:
•
at least one claimant has a foreign address, send the folder to CDB or module 1 -
5 for review and processing
•
no claimant has a foregin address, return the folder to the FO for proper routing
if a disability determination has not been made. Send it to ODO or the PSC if a disability
determination has been made.
