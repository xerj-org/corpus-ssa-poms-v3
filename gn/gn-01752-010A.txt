A. Introduction – Warsaw Foreign Service Post
The Warsaw Foreign Service Post (FSP) takes claims from individuals residing in Poland
who wish to file for U.S. benefits.
When processing claims under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
