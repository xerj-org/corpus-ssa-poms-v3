SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed in Korea
Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure — U.S. and Korean or Korean claim only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
A Korean coverage record is required and has not been requested, or
•
The NPC has requested information or documentation.
Include form SSA-2960-KOR and the information or documentation requested by the NPC.
