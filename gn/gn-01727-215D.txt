D. Entering the worker's name
Enter the worker's complete name in item 1(a) of Form SSA-2490-BK. If the worker is
Portuguese, the name will be shown on an official Portuguese document such as a passport
or identity card. In the event the worker's name will not fit in item 1(a) of Form
SSA-2490-BK, use the “Remarks” section.
