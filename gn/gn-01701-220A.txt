SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.220 - Lump-Sum Death Payment (LSDP) in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A Totalization LSDP may be paid only if the number holder (NH) died on or after the
effective date of the Totalization agreement under which insured status is established.
When entitlement to a LSDP is established based on combined U.S. and foreign coverage,
the Totalization LSDP amount is three times the pro rata primary insurance amount
(PIA) or $255, whichever is less.
