SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.220 - Additional Applications for Dutch Benefits
Effective Dates: 02/23/1999 - Present | TN 1 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
In addition to application Form SSA-2490 (paper or MCS version), a person applying
for Dutch benefits must complete a separate Dutch application form. Claimants filing
for Dutch retirement benefits must normally complete Dutch form NL/VS 202, while claimants
filing for survivors benefits must complete Dutch form NL/VS 203.
