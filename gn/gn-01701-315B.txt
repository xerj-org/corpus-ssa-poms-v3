SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.315 - Evidence of Foreign Pensions Based on a Totalization Agreement
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Request the beneficiary to submit the foreign award notice if it shows that the foreign
pension is based on an agreement with the United States. If an award notice is not
available, or if it does not indicate the pension is based on an agreement with the
United States, ask the beneficiary to write to the agency paying the foreign pension
to request a letter clearly stating whether the foreign pension is based on an agreement
with the United States. If the beneficiary does not know the address of the paying
agency, provide the address of the foreign agency as shown in the specific country
subchapter.
