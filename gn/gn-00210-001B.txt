Effective Dates: 06/12/2017 - Present | TN 27 (02-16)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200210001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Instructions for processing claims, appeals, post-eligibility and post-entitlement
actions involving a determination of marital status
1. Title II
To determine whether a same-sex relationship is a same-sex marriage or non-marital
legal relationship (NMLR), follow instructions in GN
00210.002, GN 00210.004, and GN 00210.006.
2. Title XVI
To determine marital status for Title XVI, follow instructions in GN 00210.800.
3. Medicare
To process Medicare-related claims involving same-sex couples, follow instructions
in GN 00210.700 through HI 01120.000.
