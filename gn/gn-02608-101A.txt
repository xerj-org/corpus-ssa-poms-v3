SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.101 - Exemption Due to Application Filed Before December 1, 1977
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608101 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Application filed before December 1, 1977
The Government Pension Offset (GPO) does not apply to spouse's benefits if the spouse's
application:
•
was filed before December 1, 1977; and
•
the first month of entitlement was before December 1, 1977.
