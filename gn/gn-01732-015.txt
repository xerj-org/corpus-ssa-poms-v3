GN 01732.015 How OIO Reviews and Completes Claims Taken by the Regional Foreign Service Post or
the Finnish Agency- Procedure
Step
Action
1
Review the file for the required applications and evidence listed in GN 01731.205 - .220. Additional instructions for developing and evaluating evidence are located
in GN 01704.570.
​
NOTE: All claims taken by the Finnish Agency are routed to the RFSP.
2
If additional information is needed, request the RFSP to obtain it.
3
Check the file for a U.S. totalization earnings record.
4
If there is no totalization earnings record in file, request it.
