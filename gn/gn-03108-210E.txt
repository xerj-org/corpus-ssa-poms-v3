E. Protesting an Administrative Law Judge decision (SEPSC only)
If there is reason to believe the ALJ decision is incorrect, SSA will protest the
decision. The I&E CTE prepare a protest memo for SEPSC OAS review and send the ACR
to SEPSC OAS. SEPSC Operations Analysis Section (OAS) forwards the protest memo to
Program Policy and Data Exchange (LP PPDX) and Program Litigation (LP PL) for concurrence.
SEPSC OAS analyst must control, on a weekly basis, to ensure SEPSC submits the protest
to the MAC no later than the 40th day after the ALJ decision. Upon concurrence from LP PPDX and LP PL, OAS returns
the ACR to the I&E CTE. The I&E CTE sends the ALJ protest memo to the MAC at:
Department of Health and Human Services
Departmental Appeals Board
Medicare Appeals Council, MS 6127
Cohen Building Room G-644
330 Independence Ave., S.W.
Washington, D.C. 20201
SEPSC must send a copy of the protest letter to the beneficiary with a cover notice
explaining our action.
