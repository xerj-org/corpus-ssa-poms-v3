GN 01771.105 Slovenian Certified Coverage Record
Forwarding certified coverage records
The Slovenian certified coverage record is the Form SI-USA 5 (Certificate of
Insurance Periods). The SI-USA 5 is the only acceptable form for the certification of the Slovenian
coverage record. Forward certified coverage records received in any other format to
the Division of Training and Program Support (DTPS) for approval.
