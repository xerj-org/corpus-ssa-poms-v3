B. Policy - Taxes included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
French Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors and disability benefits
and several other benefit programs including national health insurance, worker's compensation,
family benefits, sickness and maternity benefits.
