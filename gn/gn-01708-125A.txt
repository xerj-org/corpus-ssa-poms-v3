Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The German liaison agencies usually do not send a coverage certification until they
have conducted a thorough search and can provide a complete record. However, on occasion,
they furnish an interim or incomplete record. Sometimes, these interim records contain
sufficient German coverage to process the U.S. totalization claim. (See GN 01708.230 for when to request an interim record.)
