SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.222 - Debt Contact Representative (DCR) Use of Telephone in Debt Collection
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 81 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210222 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
These instructions provide a Debt Contact Representative (DCR) with actions needed
when calling a Title II or Title XVI debtor.
NOTE: Telephone calls may also be made when trying to identify a proper address for billing
and debt collection.
