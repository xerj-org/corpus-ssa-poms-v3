F. EDR Delete transactions
Completing an EDR Delete transaction is the same as completing a Delete transaction
in section (E), except that deleting the death information on the Numident when the
source is EDR requires 2-PIN clearance. You must alert your management that their action is required to complete the EDR Delete transaction.
If you do not obtain a 2-PIN clearance for your EDR Delete transaction on the same day, the record will not process and you will have to resubmit the entry into DIPS.
