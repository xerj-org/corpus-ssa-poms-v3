A. Process
When Luxembourg centralized their coverage records in 1970, everyone was assigned
a “registration number”. Since then, a registration number is assigned when an individual starts working.
If someone files for Luxembourg benefits and doesn't have a registration number, Luxembourg
assigns a number.
