SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.790 - Request for Search of Deceased Person's Record
Effective Dates: 01/07/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302790 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Who must sign request
The Bureau of the Census will honor a request for a census record for a deceased person
only if the SSA-1535-U3 or BC-600 is signed by one of the following:
•
A blood relative in the immediate family (i.e., parent, brother, sister or child),
or
•
The surviving wife or husband, or
•
The administrator or executor of the estate, or
•
A beneficiary by will, intestacy, or insurance.
