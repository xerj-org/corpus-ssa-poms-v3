B. When to Use Form SSA-4345-U2
1. General
The PC completes section I to record the title II data necessary for the FO to do
an offset computation. This data is transmitted to the FO by phone call, fax, e-mail
etc. from the PC.
2. Number of Forms
a.
D/E cases — only one form is required even if computing title II offset for both benefits
at once.
b.
Couple's cases — complete one if only one member is entitled to retroactive benefits;
complete two if both members are entitled to retroactive title II benefits.
