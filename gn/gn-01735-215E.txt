E. Procedure – Irish insurance number(s) unknown
If the Irish insurance number(s) is unknown, indicate in the “Remarks” section of
the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK all of the worker's prior home addresses
in Ireland. If the claim is taken on MCS, the FOREIGN RESIDENCE (FRES) screen should
be completed showing all of the worker's prior home addresses in Ireland. The Irish
agency needs this information to complete the NH's Irish coverage record.
