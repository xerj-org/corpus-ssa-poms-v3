A. Disclosures to assist us in administering our Social Security Programs
The Department of Veterans Affairs, Regional Office (VARO) assists us in administering
the Social Security Act in the Philippines and in other parts of the Asia-Pacific
region. We may disclose certain tax and non-tax return information to VARO when VARO
assists us in administering the Social Security Act for these purposes.
