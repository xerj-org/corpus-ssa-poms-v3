Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
The Dutch application forms are not taken in Field Offices (FO). After receiving a
claim for Dutch benefits that was filed with SSA, the Dutch agency will mail the appropriate
form to the applicant. The Dutch agency will include instructions asking the claimant
to complete the forms and mail them directly to the Dutch agency along with any additional
evidence that may be needed.
