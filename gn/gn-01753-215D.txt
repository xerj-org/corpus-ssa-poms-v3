D. Procedure for entering the Slovak birth registration numbers
Enter the worker's and each claimant's Birth Registration Number (BRN) on all claims.
In Part I of the Form SSA-2490-BK, enter all the following information if the BRN
is unknown. The Slovak agency will need this information to transmit the NH’s Slovak
coverage record:
•
The worker’s full name, including both the paternal and maternal last names (see GN
00307.160).
•
The worker's employment history, including names of employers and dates of employment;
and
•
The name of the agency to which the worker last contributed.
