B. Description of form D/USA 5
•
The year for which coverage exists appears in the first column followed by the “from - to” period expressed in day/month format.
•
The “from” date is never earlier than January 1, 1937.
•
All coverage periods are wholly within a calendar year but more than one period may
be reported in a calendar year.
•
The number of months in a period are shown in either the contribution period or substitute
period columns.
•
The total coverage months for the period from 1937 through the last year of coverage,
are displayed in the box following the list of coverage periods.
