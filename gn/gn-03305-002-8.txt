8. Fees for supplying information
Section 1106 of the Social Security Act and our implementing regulations authorize
us to charge the full cost of supplying information to first and third-party requesters
where the information requested is for a non-program purpose. For more information
about fees for non-program purposes, see GN 03311.005E.
