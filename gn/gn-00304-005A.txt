A. Preferred order for evidence of death
Obtain preferred order of evidence of death for initial claims processing, if it is
available.
Preferred evidence is a:
•
Death certificate; or
•
Form SSA-721 (Statement of Death By Funeral Director); or
•
Master Beneficiary Record (only when the date of death is prior to December 5, 2015);
or
•
Numident record that shows death data based on an Electronic Death Registration (EDR)
report (GN 00304.100); or
•
Numident record that shows a "proven" (POD:P) Date of Death, see GN 00304.100B.3.b.
NOTE:
An obituary is not considered proof of death. We can use the obituary only as a
lead to obtain other acceptable evidence of death.
