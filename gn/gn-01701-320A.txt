SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.320 - Types of Pensions in Totalization Agreement Countries Not Subject to the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP)
Effective Dates: 10/19/2022 - Present | TN 17 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy on the types of Totalization agreement countries and applicability of WEP
Some agreement countries pay pensions that are based entirely on non-work-related
factors such as residence or citizenship. Other Totalization agreement countries pay
pensions based entirely on work or on a combination of work and non-work factors (e.g.,
citizenship and voluntary contributions).
•
Foreign pensions that are based on work or a combination of work and non-work factors
may be subject to WEP. However, when a foreign pension is based on a combination of
work and non-work factors, only the portion of the foreign pension attributable to
work is considered for purposes of applying the WEP Guarantee Provision. For information
on the WEP Guarantee see RS 00605.370.
•
Foreign pensions that are based entirely on non-work-related criteria are not subject
to WEP.
REMINDER:
The Social Security Fairness Act of 2023 (SSFA) repealed the WEP for benefits payable
for January 2024 and later. Therefore, beginning with benefits due for January 2024
(paid in February 2024), SSA no longer reduces or offsets Social Security benefits
for individuals receiving any pension (domestic or foreign) based on non-covered earnings
(i.e., employment that is not subject to Social Security payroll taxes).
