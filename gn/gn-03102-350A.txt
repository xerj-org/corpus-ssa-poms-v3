SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03102.350 - Claimant Sends Reconsideration Request to Processing Center (PC) Procedure
Effective Dates: 12/07/2006 - Present | TN 8 (07-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203102350 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Acknowledge Request
See GN 03102.325B.1 for acknowledging request. Inform field office (FO) of request. Ask the FO to interview
the claimant if the basis for the request is not clear or to send a copy of the report
of interview if the FO had an interview with the claimant regarding the issue after
the claimant received the notice of initial determination.
