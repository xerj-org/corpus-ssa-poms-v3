SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
•
If an applicant contacts the FSP in Santiago, Chile to file for U.S. benefits, the
FSP forwards the claims lead to the FSP in Buenos Aires, Argentina. The FSP in Buenos
Aires contacts the applicant and develops the claim for U.S. benefits. If the claimant
requires Chilean coverage to qualify for U.S. benefits, an agreement claim is developed.
When taking claims under the agreement, the FSP follows the same general procedures
and obtains the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
•
The FSP in Santiago refers persons who wish to file for Chilean benefits to the appropriate
Chilean agency office.
