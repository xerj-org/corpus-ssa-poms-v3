SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.360 - Action on Claims Involving a Claims Helper in Philippines
Effective Dates: 04/17/2018 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905360 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
The charging of excess fees by third parties in the Philippines for assistance with
claims is a serious problem. Therefore, the appointment of a representative in the
Philippines is not recognized in developing a claim or in notifying a claimant of
the final action.
