B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to Contact
Contact the local civil registration offices of the district where the event took
place, or the Registro Civil for birth, marriage, divorce, and death records.
For birth, marriage, and death records contact:
Registro Civil,
Plaza Venezuela No.23
Direccion Departamental de
Registro Civil
Sala Murillo La Paz, Bolivia
Another office has records from consular offices in other countries.
Direccion Nacional de Registro Civil
Av. 6 de Agosto esq Cordero
Plaza
Avaroa Sanchez Lima
La Paz, Bolivia
For divorce records contact:
Plaza Venezuela No.23
Direccion Departmental de Registro Civil
Sala
Murillo La Paz, Bolivia
