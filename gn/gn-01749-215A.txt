SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01749.215 - Application for Benefits under the Czech Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 4 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201749215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Filing for U.S. Totalization benefits
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
An individual filing for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with the Czech
Republic must complete Part 1 of Form SSA-2490-BK, “Application for Benefits under
a U.S. International Social Security Agreement,” and the appropriate retirement, survivors
or disability insurance application.
2. U.S. Totalization and Czech benefits
An individual filing for both U.S. Totalization benefits and Czech benefits must complete
the SSA-2490-BK (Part 1 only), the appropriate retirement, survivors or disability
insurance application, and the special Czech application form (CZ/USA 202, “Claim
for a Pension from the Czech Republic”) discussed in GN 01749.220.
3. Czech benefits only
An individual filing for Czech benefits only, completes the Czech application form
(CZ/USA 202, Claim for a Pension from the Czech Republic). The individual does not
complete Form SSA-2490-BK.
When an applicant files for fully insured (non-totalization) U.S. benefits and Czech
Republic benefits (fully insured or Totalization), do not complete the Form SSA-2490-BK.
The Modernized Claims System (MCS) does not allow clearance of the claim for U.S.
benefits until the CR enters the path for the SSA-2490. To clear the U.S. claim, the
CR completes only the first (FINF) screen in the SSA-2490 path. This action releases
the claim for U.S. benefits for award or denial. The CR completes the claim for Czech
benefits using the Czech application form.
