GN 01740.230 Controlling Requests Sent to a Chilean Agency on Form SSA-2960-CHI
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from a Chilean agency.
•
Diary initial and follow-up requests for 3 months.
•
Forward the folder to CPSI if no response has been received after two follow-ups;
i.e., after 9 months.
