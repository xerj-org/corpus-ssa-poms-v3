B. Disclosures to audit SSA’s compliance with the safeguard provisions of the Internal
Revenue Code (IRC)
These disclosures allow IRS to access our files and SOR containing tax return information
to determine if we comply with IRS’ safeguard standards and requirements for tax return
information. Requests from IRS for access to tax return information, for purposes
of auditing SSA’s compliance with the safeguard requirements of the IRC, are ordinarily
coordinated at the executive level in our Central Office.
