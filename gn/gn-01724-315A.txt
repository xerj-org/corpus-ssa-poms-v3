SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01724.315 - Obtaining Coverage Records for Persons Who Want to Make Voluntary Contributions to France
Effective Dates: 03/08/2017 - Present | TN 1 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201724315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
To make voluntary contributions to the French system, U.S. nationals living in France
must establish that they have at least 6 months of compulsory coverage under a general
or special social insurance system. U.S. coverage may be used to meet this 6-month
requirement.
Because this is not a claim for benefits under the agreement, the French liaison agency
needs the worker's written consent to request a U.S. coverage record directly. Therefore,
France instructs the worker to contact the FSP in Paris or DIO directly for a copy
of his/her earnings record.
