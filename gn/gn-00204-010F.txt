F. Protective filing closeout
A protective filing date must be closed out. Failure to close out a protective filing
results in filing protection that will remain open indefinitely. For Title II and
Title XVI closeout instructions see GN 00204.012.
NOTE: For Title XVI oral inquiry instructions refer to SI 00601.025.
