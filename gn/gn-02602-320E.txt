SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02602.320 - Whereabouts Unknown for Retirement and Survivor (RSI) insurance beneficiaries

Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202602320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Other whereabouts unknown cases
If the "whereabouts unknown" case involves disability or representative payees, refer
to the appropriate policy below:
1. Title II disability cases
Follow the procedures in DI 11010.045, DI
13010.160 and SM
03020.050.
2. Disability - special CDR issues
In those situations where "whereabouts unknown" issues arise during the continuing
disability review (CDR) process, follow the instructions in DI
13015.001 through DI
13015.025 and DI
40525.001.
3. Whereabouts of the representative payee are unknown
Do not suspend benefits when the whereabouts of the representative payee are unknown.
Initiate development for a new payee or develop for direct payment following the guidelines
in GN
00504.110.
