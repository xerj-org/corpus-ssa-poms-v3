B. Description of form CHI/USA 3
The Form CHI/USA 3 has the same format and entries as the SSA-2960-CHI. Following
are the two main differences:
•
The address blocks in the “TO” and “FROM” sections are reversed, and
•
The item for status of the U.S. claim is not shown.
