SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.010 - Use of State Intestacy Laws to Make a Parent-Child Relationship Determination - Overview
Effective Dates: 08/23/2011 - Present | TN 43 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Individual questions constitutionality of the Social Security Administration's (SSA)
interpretation of State intestacy law
If a child claimant, or an individual acting on the child’s behalf, questions the
constitutionality of SSA’s interpretation of State intestacy law at the initial claim
or reconsideration level, request a legal opinion using instructions in GN 01010.815.
