Effective Dates: 04/24/2001 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302470 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Supporting evidence shows a discrepant DB
A document with discrepant DB information which was meant to resolve a name discrepancy
on another document submitted as evidence of age does not establish that the latter record refers to the claimant. Follow the procedure below
in this situation.
IF the document meant to be corroborated by the discrepant evidence is...
THEN...
Preferred evidence
Obtain another document recorded at least 5 years before the current filing date.
Other than preferred evidence
•
Develop as a material discrepancy case (see GN 00302.170)
•
Resolve the DB discrepancy as well as the identity question, and
•
Include the rationale in the claims file (e.g., on an RC or special determination)
NOTE: If the explanations are unconvincing or the evidence questionable, assign low probative
value to the discrepant document.
