G. NOTE; SOUNDEX RECORDS
The 1900, 1920, and part of the 1910 census records are on soundex. Soundex permits
a much quicker and more comprehensive search on the basis of less identifying information.
Ordinarily, a successful search may be completed if the following information is provided:
•
Name of the individual, and
•
Head of the household and
•
State of residence.
