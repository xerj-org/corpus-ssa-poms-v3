D. Comment
The information concerning DHS records contained in the several following sections
should apply in most cases. However, you may sometimes have to modify the procedures
indicated bcecause of:
•
Complexities and changes in the laws which DHS administers, and
•
Slightly different record keeping practices in different DHS offices.
Additional information and instructions for obtaining DHS information are contained
on Form G-639 .)
