A. Background for New Jersey small estate statute
The New Jersey statutes relating to small estates provide the procedures that individuals
may use to settle small estates without formal administration. For purposes of receiving
Title II or Black Lung payments, we consider an individual authorized to act pursuant
to these procedures an estate “legal representative”.
