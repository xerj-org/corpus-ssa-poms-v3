SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.202 - Functions of the Division of International Operations
Effective Dates: 03/12/2024 - Present | TN 21 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904202 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. FO function
DIO is the servicing office if the principal beneficiary or claimant:
•
resides in a foreign country;
•
uses an address in a foreign country; or
•
uses a foreign army post office (APO) or fleet post office (FPO) address (i.e., one
which is not listed in the Detailed Office Organization Resource System (DOORS)).
2. PC function
DIO has PC jurisdiction if at least one beneficiary:
•
resides in a country to which the payment of benefits is barred (as shown in RS 02650.001 - RS 02650.020);
•
will be outside the United States for at least 6 months, even if the beneficiary uses
a U.S. mailing address;
•
uses a foreign mailing address, including a foreign APO or FPO address;
•
resides in the United States and is entitled to totalization benefits; or
•
resides in the United States, is not insured for Retirement, Survivors, and Disability
Insurance (RSDI) benefits but has at least 6 U.S. quarters of coverage, and their
claim for totalization benefits has been denied.
