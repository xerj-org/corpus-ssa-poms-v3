SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00303.440 - Determining LPR Status
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 67 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200303440 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
DHS has jurisdiction for the determination of Lawful Permanent Resident (LPR) status
and the issuance of documentation of lawful permanent resident status to aliens determined
to be LPR.
If no DHS determination has been made, a determination of LPR is made for SSA purposes
if certain limited situations described in GN 00303.440B.2.-3. apply.
