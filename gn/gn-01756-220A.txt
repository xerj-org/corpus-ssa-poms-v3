A. Process for the SSA e2960 USA-HU3
The Division of International Operations (DIO) and the Kraków, Poland Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) complete the SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3 via the Totalization Data Collection Program
(TDCP). Use the SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3 to:
•
transmit claims and related material to the Hungarian liaison agency;
•
request information from the liaison agency; and,
•
respond to requests from the Hungarian liaison agency.
