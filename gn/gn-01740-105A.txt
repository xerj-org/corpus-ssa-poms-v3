SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.105 - Chilean Coverage Certification
Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Form CHI/USA 5 is the only acceptable form for the certification of Chilean coverage.
Certifications in any other format should be forwarded to CPSI for a determination
as to their acceptability. The form is designed to certify coverage under both the
old and new Chilean systems. The AFP Superintendency (new system) sends the record
of Chilean coverage for anyone with coverage under both systems.
