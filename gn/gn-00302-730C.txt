C. When not to apply the above instruction
The instruction in B. above can not be used where the age on the census record is
shown in completed months (rather than completed years) as was done for children under
certain ages in various census (e.g., 1 and 7/12 years). If the age is shown in completed
months and is discrepant, follow GN 00302.170.
