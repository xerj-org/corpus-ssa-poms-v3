SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.620 - State Census Records
Effective Dates: 01/07/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302620 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to request
Request State census information only if
•
A Federal census record has been secured and additional evidence is needed to resolve
a material discrepancy in DB (see GN 00302.160ff.), or
•
A Federal census record and other evidence of acceptable probative value (see GN 00302.110ff)
is not available.
