SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.310 - Swiss Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Effective Dates: 03/15/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If the Swiss liaison agency requests a medical examination for a U.S. resident, SSA
will schedule the examination and send the results to the Swiss agency.
Medical examinations for Switzerland are obtained as follows.
•
Swiss agency requests for medical examinations are made in item III.D. of Form CH/USA
3.
•
DIO schedules an examination or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from DIO's files as described in GN 01704.810.
•
DIO requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for Switzerland
using Form SSA-1278-U3-SZ (Request for Payment of Reimbursable Expenses Under the
U.S.-Swiss Agreement on Social Security).
