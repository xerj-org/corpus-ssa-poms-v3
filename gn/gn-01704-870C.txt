Effective Dates: 08/27/2014 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704870 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. From Claimants
Answer inquiries about the status of totalization claims when they are received directly
from claimants.
Occasionally, BETs may prepare dictated paragraphs to fully answer the inquirer. However,
most inquiries can be answered with appropriate paragraphs in NL 00711.000 ff. Consult a CA or BA if there are questions about the preparation of a correct
response.
