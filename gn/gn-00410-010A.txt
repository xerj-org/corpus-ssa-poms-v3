A. Requesting authorization for use
The Office of the Regional Commissioner (ORC) or delegate, decides to invoke the disaster
procedures. If it is apparent that the event will affect the entire country the Associate
Commissioner, Office of Income Security Programs (OISP) may authorize the implementation
of emergency evidentiary procedures.
