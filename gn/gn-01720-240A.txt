SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.240 - Responding to Requests that Are Not on a Liaison Form
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The majority of assistance requests from the U.K. agencies are submitted on the approved
liaison forms described in GN 01720.235. However, some requests are received in the form of correspondence, both standardized
form letters as well as dictated letters. The U.K. most often uses correspondence
to request medical examinations, to send follow up requests and to ask our assistance
in unusual situations.
