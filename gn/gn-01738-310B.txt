B. Process
The following process is used for obtaining medical examinations for Greece (See GN 01704.810 for general information on processing requests for medical examinations.)
•
IKA requests for medical examinations are made in item IV.C. on Form GR/USA 3 which
is accompanied by a Form GR/USA 6, Disability Liaison Form and, if laboratory tests
or x-rays are needed, a Form GR/USA 6a, Disability Liaison Form Attachment.
•
SSA schedules an examination or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from files as described in GN 01704.810.
•
SSA requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for Greece
using Form SSA-1278-U3-GR.
