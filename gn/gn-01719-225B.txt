SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01719.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for United Kingdom (U.K) Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/13/2021 - Present | TN 3 (07-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201719225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a U.K. claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for the status of
his or her claim for U.K. benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the U.K. agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Paperless ROQS (Read
Only Query System), accessible via iMain.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the U.K.
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the U.K., advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore,
Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the U.K.
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the U.K. agency, advise the applicant to contact
the U.K. agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information online
at:
Pension
Service
Note: DIO is unable to determine the status of a U.K. claim after a technician forwards
the claim to the U.K. For more information about status requests related to Totalization
claims see GN 01702.520.
