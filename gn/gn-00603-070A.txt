Effective Dates: 06/04/2007 - Present | TN 14 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200603070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Accumulated funds may include some or both of the following:
•
Benefits which accrued prior to an award (retroactive benefits) or while payment was
suspended for selection of a new payee.
•
Conserved benefit funds returned to SSA by a former payee for reissuance to a new
payee.
