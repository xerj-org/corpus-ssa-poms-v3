B. Procedure – special development and routing for claims filed in Warsaw
This procedure describes special development and routing instructions that apply to
claims under the agreement filed in Warsaw, Poland.
1. U.S. Claim
a. Retirement, Survivors, and Disability Claims
The Foreign Benefits Unit (FBU) in Warsaw will contact the applicant and obtain form
SSA-2490BK and any appropriate U.S. applications. Warsaw FBU will forward a liaison
Form SSA-e2960 (U3–PL) and Part I and II of Form SSA-2490 to ZUS or KRUS, to request
a coverage record.
For retirement and disability claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens
and the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) Screen (see, MSOM MCS 009.011) and SSA-2490 screens (MSOM
MCS 007.001).
b. Survivor Claims under the Polish agreement
For survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the Totalization
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and Part 1 of the paper or electronic (e-form)
application Form SSA-2490-BK.
c. Worker Appears Not to be Insured Based On U.S. Coverage Alone- Requesting Polish Coverage
Record
Forward a liaison form SSA-e2960 –U3–PL), to ZUS or KRUS to request the Polish certified
coverage record if it appears that the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage
alone.
d. Claim to OIO for processing
Forward the claim to DIO and include a copy of the completed liaison form sent to
ZUS or KRUS.
