SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.288 - What Information is Needed on EVID and the Report of Contact page in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) in PE Situations to Identify the Type of Evidence Submitted
Effective Dates: 08/15/2023 - Present | TN 105 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301288 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Recording Specific Evidence Types
1. W-2 Forms
When entering the event date in the “DOCUMENT EVENT DATE” field on EVID or “REPORT
TEXT” on the Report of Contact page in CCE, enter the last day of the year as stated
on the W-2.
Information about the amount of income may be shown in the “COMMENTS” field on EVID
or the “REPORT TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in CCE. The amount of income
must be entered in the system for which the substantive information is being captured (e.g.,
MCS, MSSICS, etc.). Record the employer's EIN in the “RIN” field on EVID or the “REPORT
TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in CCE.
The date recorded and the date issued are not needed.
In an initial claims situation, when a claimant brings in two or more W-2s from different
employers as proof of wages, complete two different EVID screens. Show all W-2's on
one Report of Contact page in CCE. (If proof of prelag wages is presented, completion of the Earnings Modernization screens is sufficient
to capture an audit trail about the employer.)
2. Tax Return Information
When coding tax return information on EVID or the Report of Contact page in CCE, show
as the event date the last day of the tax year as stated on the tax return.
Show information about the amount of income in the “COMMENTS” or “REPORT TEXT” field.
However, the amount of income must be entered in the system for which the substantive information is being captured (e.g.,
MCS, MSSICS, etc.).
The date the tax return was signed by the taxpayer (if shown) should be recorded in
the “DATE RECORDED” field or “REPORT TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in
CCE.
3. Monthly Pay Stub Information
In title XVI PE situations where the local requirements call for SSI recipients to
provide proof of income on a monthly basis, the FO can annotate EVID or the Report
of Contact page in CCE with the first month reporting; do not code subsequent months. Record/store subsequent months' information according to SI 00820.141C.2.b. In the “COMMENTS” field of EVID or “REPORT TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page
in CCE, include a statement “Future monthly income stored locally.”
4. Military Service
When coding military service (DD-214) information, show the discharge date in the
“DOCUMENT EVENT DATE” field on EVID or “REPORT TEXT” field on the Report of Contact
page in CCE. Show the active duty start date in the “COMMENTS” field on EVID or “REPORT
TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in CCE. If there are several periods of
active military service with a break in service, show the very last discharge date
as the event date. Enter the other periods in the “COMMENTS” field on EVID or “REPORT
TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in CCE.
If a person has multiple periods of active military service with no break in service,
show as one period of service. You may annotate the fact that there were several DD-214s
covering this period in the “COMMENTS” field on EVID or the “REPORT TEXT” field on
the Report of Contact page in CCE. However, the military service periods must be entered
in the system for which the substantive information is being captured (e.g., MCS).
Neither the date recorded nor date issued is needed.
5. Veteran Administration Benefit Query
If VBAQ is used to prove a period of military service, annotate on EVID/Report of
Contact page in CCE. When VBAQ is an issue, see MS 05203.020.
6. Online Evidence
When evidence certified by the custodian of record that is received via Online Data
Exchange is being recorded, show that it is “Online” evidence. Record the date of
access, name of the agency whose records were accessed, the name or number of screens
accessed and data element used as evidence.
