SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.407 - Coding Chile Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 227 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402407 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Chile have a prefix of “68” at the beginning of the Routing and
Transit Number (RTN).
The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field has the letter “U” assigned
for Chile.
