SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01714.305 - Life to Death Conversions - Belgium Agreement
Effective Dates: 06/12/2017 - Present | TN 1 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201714305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Belgium automatically converts dependent spouse's benefits to survivors' benefits
upon the death of the worker. There is no need for additional survivors' applications,
and DIO does not need to inform the Belgian agencies of the worker's death.
