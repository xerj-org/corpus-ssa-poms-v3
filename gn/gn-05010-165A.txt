Effective Dates: 03/27/2018 - Present | TN 11 (11-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010165 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
The tax treaty with India was effective January 1, 1991. It exempted the Social Security
benefits paid to certain beneficiaries residing in India from the nonresident alien
withholding tax.
Because of the very limited applicability of the tax treaty provisions, no action
was taken to identify beneficiaries who might be affected.
