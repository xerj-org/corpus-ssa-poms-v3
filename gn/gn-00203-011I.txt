SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00203.011 - Special Interviewing Situations: Limited English Proficiency or Language Assistance Required
Effective Dates: 09/18/2024 - Present | TN 80 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200203011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Procedure when the Social Security Administration interviewer is not satisfied with
the interpreter provided by the limited English proficiency individual
1.
The interviewer should take the following steps when the interpreter:
•
Is not acting in the individual's best interest;
•
Is not providing accurate information to SSA;
•
Does not agree to sign the SSA-795; or
•
Is engaging in fraudulent activity or possible interpreter fraud.
2.
Advise the individual needing language assistance that SSA will provide free interpreter
service to ensure the individual is not disadvantaged.
3.
Offer to continue the interview, but only with the additional assistance of an interpreter
SSA provides.
4.
If the services of a qualified in-office interpreter are necessary:
•
Terminate the interview;
•
Inform the individual that SSA will provide a qualified in-office interpreter at the
time of their rescheduled appointment date and time;
•
Document the file giving the reason the interview was terminated; and
•
Protect the individual's filing date as outlined in GN 00204.010 and SI 00601.015 through SI 00601.025 for title XVI.
5.
If the services of a telephone interpreter would be suitable, explain that the TIS
must be called before the interview can continue.
•
Tell the individual that their interpreter can remain during the interview, if they
request it, but that you will direct all questions to the individual being interviewed
and you must receive all responses from that individual.
•
Call the TIS and obtain an interpreter before continuing the interview.
6.
Follow the procedures in GN 04111.000 if fraud is suspected at any time during the
interview or development of the case.
