A. Filing for U.S. Totalization benefits
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with Slovenia must complete Form
SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
The applicant must complete this form whether the application is for benefits from:
•
the United States only
•
Slovenia only; or
•
from both countries.
