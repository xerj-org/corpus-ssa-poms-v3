A. Disclosure policy
Disclosures to State legislators can only be made with the written consent of the individual, or someone who can consent for
him or her. For information on our consent requirements, see GN 03305.003.
NOTE: We may make disclosures without consent to Members of Congress under a routine use.
This routine use permits disclosure to Congressional offices when the individual makes
a request to that office for assistance related to a matter that requires disclosure
of certain information in his or her Social Security records.
