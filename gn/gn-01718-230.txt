GN 01718.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the SLAFU on Form SSA-2960-U3-SW - Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested on Form SSA-2960-U3-SW.
•
Diary all initial and followup requests for 90 days.
•
Diary a request made by the FSP Stockholm for 90 days from the date the FSP signed
the request.
•
Forward the folder to an OIO analyst if no response is received after two followups,
i.e., after 270 days.
