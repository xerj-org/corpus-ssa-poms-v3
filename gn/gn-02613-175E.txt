E. Procedure - developing representative payee suitability
Check RPS in each case where the beneficiary/claimant has an outstanding felony warrant
to determine if they are serving as a representative payee.
Effective 4/2005, persons having an outstanding felony warrant are prohibited from
serving as a payee. There are no exceptions to this prohibition. Refer to GN 00502.133.
