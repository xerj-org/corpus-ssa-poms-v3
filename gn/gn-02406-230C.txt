C. Instructions when the United States Postal Service (USPS) returns undeliverable mutilated
Title II and Title XVI check to Treasury
There will be situations when checks are mutilated and undeliverable, and returned
directly to Treasury by the USPS.
•
The RFC will cancel the check immediately.
•
If a stop payment action is in the RFC, Treasury will hold the check for 90 days and
then shred it.
•
If no stop payment action is in the RFC and correspondence attached, the RFC will
forward the correspondence to the appropriate PC for Title II or the Office of Benefit
Accounting and Systems Analysis (OBASA) " for Title XVI with an annotation, “Check
Cancellation Due to Mutilation.” SSA will receive the funds as a returned check credit.
•
If no correspondence is attached, the RFC will prepare correspondence for the PC or
OBASA stating, “Check Cancelled Due to Mutilation.” SSA will get a credit in the form
of a returned check from Treasury. For Title II, REACT will process the returned check
and it will appear on PHUS. Title XVI credit will post to the Supplemental Security
Record (SSR).
