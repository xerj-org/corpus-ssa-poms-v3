A. Types of Public Records
For purposes of this section, treat the following types of records as public records
of birth:
a.
A record of birth maintained by a governmental entity charged to maintain the record
system, including States, counties, parishes, and boroughs
b.
A hospital birth record (see GN 00302.530). This does not include souvenir hospital certificates (see GN 00301.050D. for instructions about souvenir certificates of age and marriage).
c.
A Notification of Birth Registration (see GN 00302.515)
d.
Seneca Indian tribal census records and Bureau of Indian Affairs tribal census records
of the Navajo Indians (see GN 00302.630 - GN 00302.640).
e.
Forms FS-240 (Report of Birth Abroad of a Citizen of the United States) and FS-545
(Certification of Birth).
