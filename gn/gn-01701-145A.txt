SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.145 - Entitlement to Supplementary Medical Insurance (SMI) in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Totalization beneficiaries not entitled to premium-free hospital insurance (HI) (see
GN 01701.140) must meet certain residence and citizenship or alien status requirements
in order to qualify for SMI.
NOTE: Since a beneficiary must be entitled to premium-free HI or be age 65 to qualify for
SMI, Totalization beneficiaries cannot be entitled to SMI until age 65 unless they
are entitled to premium-free HI on a different Social Security number (SSN).
