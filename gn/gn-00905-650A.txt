A. Introduction
There are no FSPs in U.S. possessions. They are administered by either the Department
of the Interior, the Department of the Navy, the Federal Aviation Agency or the U.S.
Weather Bureau.
The U.S. possessions are:
•
American Samoa (including Swains Island),
•
Baker Island,
•
Guam,
•
Howland Island,
•
Jarvis Island,
•
Johnson Island (including Sand Island),
•
Kingman Reef,
•
Midway Islands
•
Navassa,
•
U.S. Virgin Islands, and
•
Wake (including Walker and Peale).
The Federally-appointed district administrators or governors of these areas are required
by their positions to assist other Federal agencies as the need arises. They are empowered
to attest to the authenticity and reliability of proofs originating within their jurisdiction.
