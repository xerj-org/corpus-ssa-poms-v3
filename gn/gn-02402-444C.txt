SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.444 - Coding Tonga Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/28/2019 - Present | TN 164 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402444 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Tonga will have a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “T” assigned for Tonga.
