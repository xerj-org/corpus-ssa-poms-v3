SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.080 - Forwarding the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and Folder to the Servicing Hearing Office
Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure for requesting a folder from the PC, Office of Central Operations (OCO),
Federal Records Center (FRC), or Disability Determination Services (DDS)
1.
Initial folder request
The servicing FO or jurisdictional PC requests the folder through Processing Center
Action Control System (PCACS) within one business day of receiving the hearing request.
If you are unable to obtain the folder through PCACS, send an email message using
the message format in GN 03103.080F.3.
If the folder is in the Disability Quality Branch (DQB), request it by telephone.
Consider the following situations when you request a folder.
•
If you need multiple or related folders and the folders are in different jurisdictions,
prepare a separate email for each folder;
•
If the folder is at DDS or if the FO sends the folder to DDS under informal remand
criteria, see DI 12010.015 and DI 12010.020.
2.
Folder request follow-up procedures
If you do not receive the folder within 15 business days of the initial folder request,
take the following actions. Allow 21 business days if folder is in FRC.
a.
Send a follow-up email and on the subject line, add “Priority Follow-up” and the date
of the first request.
b.
You should receive the folder or an email stating the reason for delay and the expected
release date within 10 calendar days after the follow-up request.
c.
If you do not receive the folder within 10 days after the follow-up, request, or the
expected release date, transfer responsibility for obtaining the folder to the ORC
per GN 03103.080J in this section.
3.
Exhibit email message requests for claim folder(s)
Find the email address of the PC by using the Detailed Office Organization Resource
System (DOORS).
Include the following information in the email.
a.
Email to PC
Requesting Office’s: Office Code, Address, City, State, Name of office contact
•
Claimant Name, Social Security number (SSN), filed a hearing on day/month/year
•
Send the claim type (disabled wage earner (HA), disabled adult child (DAC) claim folder(s))
to: office code, address, city, state, zip code.
If the folder(s) is not in the reconsideration or Disability Processing Branch, check
all other PC sections housing inactive folders - Dormant, FRC.
b.
Email request to Office of Disability Operations (ODO)
Requesting Office’s: Office Code, City, State, Name of office contact
•
Claimant name, SSN, filed a hearing on day/month/year.
•
Send the claim type (HA, DAC) claim folder(s) to: office code, address, city, state,
zip code.
Verify location through off-line query. If off-line query location for prior folder
is ODO FRC, request the folder.
