A. Automatic status notice for an application for Finnish benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards information about a Finnish
claim, certified copies of evidence, and a copy of the worker's U.S. coverage record
to Finland, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically stores the claims
material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Finnish agency. The system automatically
generates a notice that the BET sends to the applicant.
