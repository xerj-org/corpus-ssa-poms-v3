SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.120 - Eligibility for U.S. Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
Under the agreement, if a NH has at least 6 U.S. QC's but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under the French Social Security system
can be added to his/her U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents or survivors)
to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
