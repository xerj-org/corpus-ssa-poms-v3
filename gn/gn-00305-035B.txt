B. Procedure — presumption of validity of last marriage —general
Follow the instructions below to determine the presumption of validity of the last
marriage.
1. General Procedure
a. One Claimant
The spouse must submit information which will enable SSA to provide assistance in
getting evidence concerning continuation or dissolution of a former marriage.
b. More than one claimant
Each spouse must submit as much information as possible; generally neither spouse
has a greater burden than another to prove their case.
c. Development
•
Exhaust all leads.
•
Consult legal precedent opinions in POMS Part 15 (PR) for the involved State.
•
If, after complete development, sufficient evidence cannot be obtained to determine if the prior marriage was or
was not terminated, apply the presumption applicable in the particular State.
2. Legal opinion
If no precedent opinion exists, follow the procedures in GN 01010.815 for possible submittal to the Office of General Counsel (OGC).
