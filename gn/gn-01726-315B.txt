SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.315 - Earnings Records for Spouses/Partners or Widow(er)s - Netherlands Agreement
Effective Dates: 12/12/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedure below which describes the actions needed to provide a widow's,
spouse's or partner's earnings record for the SVB.
IF..
THEN. ..
A widow(er) files for Dutch survivor's benefits under the agreement
Obtain a totalization earnings record on the widow(er)'s own SSN and include it in
the claims package.
A claimant for Dutch old-age benefits indicates that he/she has a spouse or shares
a household with a partner
Obtain the spouse's or partner's consent to release the earnings record to Dutch agency.
Diary 30 days for a response. When the consent is received, obtain the spouse's or
partner's totalization earnings record and include it in the claims package. Retain
the consent statement in file.EXCEPTION: A spouse's consent to release his/her earnings information is not necessary if the
spouse has filed for U.S. benefits under the Dutch agreement.
