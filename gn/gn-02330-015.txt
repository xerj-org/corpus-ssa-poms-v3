GN 02330.015 Refund of Excess Medicare Premiums—Voluntary Termination
For beneficiaries whose benefits will not be payable because of a suspension event
and for nonbeneficiaries, the bulk of the premiums will be collected quarterly. Some
of these enrollees pay premiums for as much as a year in advance. If an enrollee voluntarily
terminates the enrollee's entitlement so that coverage ends before the last month
for which the enrollee has paid premiums, the unearned premiums received may be refunded
only as indicated below, with priority in the order shown.
