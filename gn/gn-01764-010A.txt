A. Process for applications filed in the Oslo, Norway Federal Benefits Unit
The Oslo, Norway Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) takes claims from persons residing in
Iceland who wish to file for U.S. Social Security benefits.
In processing claims for U.S. benefits under the U.S. - Icelandic Totalization Agreement
the FBU should follow the same general procedures as Social Security Administration
(SSA) field offices when processing domestic claims.
