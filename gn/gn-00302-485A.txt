SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.485 - Evidence Establishes Only Age
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302485 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
If the evidence shows only the individual's age at a given time and does not show the DB or at least the year of birth, we must ascertain whether the age was
given as of the last, next, or nearest birthday to determine the individual's DB.
The age is usually given as of the last birthday in documents other than U.S. census
records and insurance policies. Use the following chart to aid in determining when
the age was given.
