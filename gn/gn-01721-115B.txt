B. Definition
For purposes of the agreement, the territory of Spain includes:
•
The Spanish territory on the Iberian peninsula,
•
The Canary Islands,
•
The Balearic Islands,
•
The Island of Alboran, and
•
The North African territories of Ceuta and Melilla.
