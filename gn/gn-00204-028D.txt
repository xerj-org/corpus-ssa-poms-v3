D. Procedure - Auxiliary Application Filed - DIB Claim Denied
Use the language in DI 11010.030 as a guide for preparing the notice when an auxiliary applicant files a duplicate
application on behalf of a NH and also files an auxiliary application on behalf of
self and others, and the original claim has been or is being denied. This letter is
a combination of the Guide Letter, Acknowledging Multiple Applications Filed by the
Secondary Applicant and Form Letter SSA-L823 (Denial Notice to Auxiliary Applicant)
and should be substituted for them under the circumstances described above.
