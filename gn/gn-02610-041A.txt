A. Procedure — FO Title XVI offset
•
Count the retroactive title II benefit as though paid when due.
NOTE:
Prior to 9/08/95 the MBR/SSR interface automatically posts the title II benefits as
type G income if no SSI was paid. Beginning 09/08/95 the system will not post type
G income when the title II CMA is paid unless the Title XVI CR inputs a win code of
D when there is no SSI eligibility that can be established. See REMINDER.
•
See SM 01320.040 when the interface did not post income.
•
Apply title XVI offset before determining any reimbursement due the State under IAR.
REMINDER: Title II offset will apply to claims processed 9/08 /95 or later. For information
about recomputation of windfall offset in situations with or without installment payments,
see GN 02610.050C.2.
