SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.125 - Eligibility for French Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
The agreement provides that France will count a worker's U.S. coverage when determining
eligibility for French benefits if the worker:
•
is not insured based on French coverage alone, and
•
has at least one year of French coverage.
