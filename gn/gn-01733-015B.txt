B. Types of coverage
1. Compulsory contributions
Covered employees and their employers, as well as self-employed workers, including
public sector workers who entered the labor force after December 31, 1998, must make
compulsory contributions based on a percentage of the worker’s earnings.
2. Voluntary contributions
Luxembourg permits workers who are not subject to compulsory contributions, and who
meet certain qualifying conditions, to make voluntary contributions to build future
benefit rights.
NOTE: Separate systems cover public sector workers who entered the labor force before January
1, 1999.
3. Retroactive contributions
Retroactive contributions are possible for periods of no work or reduced work for
family responsibilities if the insured:
•
Is a resident of Luxembourg; and
•
Is younger than age 65 with at least 12 months of contributions; and
•
Is not entitled to any pension.
