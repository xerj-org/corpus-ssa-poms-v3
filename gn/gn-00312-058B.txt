B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the local civil registration offices or district where the event took place
or state civil archives for birth, marriage, and death records. Contact the district
court where the divorce was granted.
Write a brief request to the proper office in Portuguese. Use this address guide below,
replacing the information in parentheses. Find the Brazilian postal code at http://postalcode.globefeed.com/Brazil_Postal_Code.asp.
Cartório de Registro Civil
(postal code), (city), (state)
BRASIL
2. Fee
Varies.
•
Birth and death certificates - free (in San Pualo), additional copies R $14,52
•
Divorce decrees - unknown
•
Marriage certificates - $158,86, additional copies are R $14,52
