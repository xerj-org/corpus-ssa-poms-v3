GN 01701.155 Overpayments Involving Totalization Benefits
Procedure
Process overpayments involving U.S. Totalization benefits following the normal procedures
that apply to regular (non-Totalization) retirement, survivors, and disability insurance
benefits. Overpayments of foreign benefits will not be recovered by the Social Security
Administration from U.S. Social Security benefits.
