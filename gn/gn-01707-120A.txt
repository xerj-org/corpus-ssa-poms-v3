SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.120 - U.S. Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 05/28/1997 - Present | TN 10 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Under the agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least
six
quarters of U.S. coverage but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's coverage credits under the German Social Security
system can be added to the NH's U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents
or survivors) to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
