A. Overpayment waiver reviews
Certain waiver approvals require a second pin (2-PIN) review before effectuating the
waiver determination. The amount of an overpayment waiver approval determines the
level of the Decision Maker and Reviewer that is required. For the applicable level
of review, including required management and Risk & Quality (RQ) review, refer to
the charts in GN 02250.301C. When a second pin (2-PIN) review is required, the person who completes the second
pin (2-PIN) is the final approver. The person who completes a second pin (2PIN) review
must be different than the person who completes the first review and proposes a waiver
approval.
NOTE: If you can approve the waiver under the administrative waiver tolerance provision,
you do not need a second pin (2-PIN) review. For information about approving a waiver
under the administrative waiver tolerance provision, refer to GN 02250.350.
