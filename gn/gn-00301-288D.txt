D. Foreign Countries Whose Evidentiary Documents Can Be Certified Electronically For
Domestic Claims
1. Procedure
Use the codes appearing in the charts in GN 00301.288D.2. and GN 00301.288D.3. to record the foreign country on EVID or Report of Contact page in CCE in PE situations.
See GN 00301.286E.4. for foreign documents that do not appear in the charts.
