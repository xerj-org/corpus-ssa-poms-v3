SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.068 - How To Evaluate Payee Responses About Conserved Funds
Effective Dates: 11/14/2013 - Present | TN 16 (10-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605068 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy
SSA recommends that conserved funds over $500 be held in U.S. savings bonds or in
an interest yielding checking or savings account that is insured under either federal
or state law. Since payees are investing the beneficiary's funds, the rules governing
conserved funds are very conservative to avoid any loss to the beneficiary. The investment
must clearly show that the beneficiary is the owner and any interest earned belongs
to the beneficiary.
