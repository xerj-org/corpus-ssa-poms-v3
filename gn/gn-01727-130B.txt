B. Special minimum benefits for Portuguese residents
If a Portuguese resident qualifies for a Totalization benefit from Portugal, as well
as a U.S. benefit, and the amount of the combined benefits would be less than the
minimum benefit payable under Portuguese law, Portugal will pay a supplement sufficient
to raise the combined benefits to the minimum benefit amount.
