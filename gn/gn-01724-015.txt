GN 01724.015 How the Totalization Modules Review and Complete Claims Taken by the FSP
Procedure
When you receive a claim from the FSP, check the file for the forms and evidence listed
in GN 01724.010.B. If any are missing, request the FSP to obtain them. (See GN 01724.010, How FSPs develop claims under the agreement)
Since all claims taken in a French local agency are routed to the FSP in Paris, follow
the guidelines above when evaluating these claims. Direct all requests for missing
information, other than the French coverage record, to the FSP in Paris.
