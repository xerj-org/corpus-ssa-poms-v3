B. Definitions
1. Representative
For the purpose of this section, the term “representative” does not include a representative
payee.
2. Category 1 Issue
An issue that does not affect entitlement is a Category 1 issue.
3. Category 2 Issue
An issue that does affect entitlement is a Category 2 issue.
