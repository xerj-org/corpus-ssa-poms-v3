B. Procedure — adoption after entitlement or death
Do not routinely volunteer information about the provisions of the Act concerning
such adoptions. You may give such information only if:
•
Someone requests it,
•
There is an indication that adoption proceedings have been started or are contemplated,
or
•
If the NH is deceased, evidence shows that the child was placed in the NH's home for
adoption, or that the NH and spouse had intended to adopt the child.
