SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01741.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for U.S. and Korean Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 8 (02-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201741210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence requirements
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Agreement with Korea,
the person must submit the same evidence required to file a claim for retirement,
survivors or disability insurance (non-Totalization) benefits. The applicant must
also submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States.
