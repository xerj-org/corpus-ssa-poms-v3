A. Introduction
The FSPs in Paris and Nice take claims from individuals who wish to file for benefits
under the agreement, and also assist in processing certain types of claims filed with
the French Social Security Agency. In general, FSP staff follow the same procedures
as SSA FOs when developing these claims. Special FSP routing and development procedures
are explained below.
