B. Residents of Finland under the section 202(t)(11) exception to the Alien Nonpayment
Provision
Under the terms of the Totalization Agreement with Finland, non-citizen dependent
and survivor beneficiaries residing in Finland can receive U.S. Social Security benefits
for which they qualify even if they do not meet the 5-year U.S. residency requirement.
This applies to both ordinary and totalized U.S. benefits.
