SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02403.160 - Writing Off the Cost of Stop Payment Orders for Lost Remittances
Effective Dates: 05/10/2010 - Present | TN 34 (10-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202403160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Stop payment order is $45 or less, Title XVI
Use these steps to 'write off' those stop payment orders that are less than $45 and involve title XVI overpayments.
1.
'Write off' the amount of the stop payment charge by posting to the individual's record
an uncollectible decision for the amount of the stop payment. See SM 01311.280 for
how to post an uncollectible decision to the SSR.
2.
Enter the replacement remittance according to current procedures, using the Social
Security Electronic Remittances System (SERS) when possible.
3.
Annotate the original FORT accordingly and attach the new automated FO receipt to
it.
4.
Scan proof of the stop payment charge into NDRED. For information on sending documents
to NDRED and viewing them in CFUI, see GN 00301.310, GN 00301.313, and SM 00801.000.
