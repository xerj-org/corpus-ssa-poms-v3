D. Reviewing the foreign garnishment order
Foreign garnishment orders are subject to the same review as domestic orders.
Check each foreign garnishment order for the following:
•
Does the garnishment order appear legitimate?
•
Is the garnishment order for child support?
•
If the reciprocity agreement requires specific forms, did the foreign country use
them?
Forward foreign garnishment questions to the OISP, OSSIRP address in GN 02410.211G in this section. When you send the question to OISP for review, input COGS to withhold
benefits and code the payover field as “N” pending receipt of the reply. For procedures
on annotating the COGS screen, see SM 00832.250.
