SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01763.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Agreement with Iceland
Effective Dates: 04/25/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201763205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for required evidence
When an applicant files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with Iceland,
he or she should submit the same evidence required to file a claim for regular (non-Totalization)
retirement, disability, or survivors insurance benefits. The applicant must also submit
the evidence when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States as noted in
GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230.
