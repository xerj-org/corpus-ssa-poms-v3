E. SSA Requests from DHS Information
SSA requests for DHS information should not be made on Form G-639. (If the Form G-639 were used, the request might be returned
because of the absence of a fee.)
To avoid delays or problems SSA requests to DHS should:
•
Be submitted directly to the proper DHS office (see GN 00302.910) on the DO letterhead over the district manager's signature.
•
State that the request is being made “pursuant to a 'Conditional' or "Routine Use' disclosure under the provisions of
Section 552a(b) of the Privacy Act.”
•
Contain sufficient identifying information so that DHS can locate the desired record,
e.g.,
–
Full name
–
Maiden name (if married)
–
Date and place of birth
–
Alien registration number (if known)
–
Date and place of entry
•
Identify the particular information desired.
•
State why disclosure to SSA will benefit the government, e.g., detection of a fraudulent
document or resolution of conflicting evidence in our possession. (Sufficient details
must be given to support the statement.)
•
Be accompanied by a self-addressed, franked envelope.
