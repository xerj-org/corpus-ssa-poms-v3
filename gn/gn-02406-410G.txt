G. CCB actions for undeliverable FS-1133
If CCB sends out a claims package to the payee for a Title II check and it is returned
to CCB as undeliverable (for address purposes), CCB will send the claims package to
the PSC for Title II. The PSC will determine the correct address and forward the package
to the payee.
For a Title XVI check, CCB returns undeliverable FS-1133's to the FO of jurisdiction.
The FO will correct the address on the FMS-3858.
