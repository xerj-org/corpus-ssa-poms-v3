SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01711.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Norwegian Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 5 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Norwegian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for the status of
his or her claim for Norwegian benefits and states that he or she has not received
notification that DIO forwarded a claim to the Norwegian agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP)
application. See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access
the EP User Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Norway
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Norway, advise applicant to write
directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO-DIO
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded claim to Norway
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Norwegian agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Norwegian agency directly. Applicants can find additional contact and
other information online at:
NAV National Office for
Social Insurance Abroad
NOTE: DIO is unable to determine the status of a Norwegian claim after a technician
forwards the claim to Norway. For more information about status requests in Totalization
claims see GN 01702.520.
