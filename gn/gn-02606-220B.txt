SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02606.220 - Accessing/Initiating the PC Reply
Effective Dates: 08/17/1999 - Present | TN 6 (08-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202606220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
Access/initiate the PC reply to the FO as shown below.
1. 2568 Screen
To access/prepare the 2568/256C screen:
a.
select #16 on the SSA Menu Main;
b.
select #18 on the Administrative Applications Menu;
c.
complete screen entries;
d.
transmit the reply;
e.
print a copy of the transmitted reply;
f.
attach supporting material (if applicable), under cover of a printed copy of the transmitted
2568 annotating in the "CASE INFORMATION" field "SUPPORTING MATERIAL - PRIOR WIRE
MESSAGE SENT," and release to the mailroom.
2. Form SSA-2568-U3
To prepare the Form SSA-2568-U3:
a.
complete the entries on the form;
b.
send the original to the mailroom;
c.
attach supporting material (if applicable) to the original and send to the mailroom.
