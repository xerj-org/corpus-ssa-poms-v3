C. Procedures for scheduling the personal conference
If you cannot fully approve the waiver request after initial review, do not send a waiver determination
notice. Instead, prepare the Personal Conference & Folder Review (Waiver/Recon) notice
found in the Document Processing System (DPS), under the national and overpayment
tabs.
NOTE: For Title XVI, do not input a determination on the Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS) Direct SSR Update
screens.
