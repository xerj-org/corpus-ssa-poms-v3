GN 00301.100 Certifying Photocopies of Evidence Already in SSA's Possession
Procedure for certifying photocopies of evidence in SSA’s possession
If one SSA certified photocopy of an evidentiary document exists and we need another
(e.g. in a concurrent TII/TXVI claim);
•
Photocopy the copy.
•
Do not certify the second copy.
•
Place the properly certified copy with the TII claims folder.
