A. Policy - General
An applicant's concern and responsibility for the beneficiary must be verified with
the beneficiary or custodian. It is essential to verify concern when the applicant
does not have custody of the beneficiary. An applicant who does not demonstrate concern
for the beneficiary is a very poor choice for a payee, and every effort should be
made to find a more suitable candidate.
