SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with an Austrian Agency
Effective Dates: 12/16/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
When a claimant contacts an Austrian agency to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the
agency routes Liaison Form A/USA 2 to the FSP in Vienna. If the Austrian coverage
has been established, they will include the information on form A/ USA 4 Certificate
of Insurance Record in Austria.
