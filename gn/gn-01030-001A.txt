SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01030.001 - Awards to Foreign Students and Childhood Disability Beneficiaries Previously Entitled to Child's Benefits
Effective Dates: 01/24/2024 - Present | TN 5 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201030001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Adjudicating foreign student child beneficiaries (SCB) and childhood disability beneficiaries
(CDB) awards for beneficiaries previously entitled to child's benefits
Claims Specialists (CS) in the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO)
use Form SSA 1077-FC, Determination of Entitlement as a SCB or CDB, or Form SSA-559, Claims Folder/Material Transmittal, to process awards or reinstatements for a foreign SCB or a CDB.
