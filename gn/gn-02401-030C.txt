SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02401.030 - Combining or Separating Payments from Two Different Trust Funds
Effective Dates: 03/28/2011 - Present | TN 24 (03-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Currently combined payments
For previously combined payments that met one of the exceptions in GN 02401.030B, the T2 Dual Entitlement Automation Release 10, effective May 27, 2008, began separating
existing combined payments that involved separate trust funds. Systems completed the
process in December 2008.
