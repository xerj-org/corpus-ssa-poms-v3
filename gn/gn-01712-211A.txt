SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01712.211 - Providing Benefit Information for the Norwegian Agency
Effective Dates: 12/07/1993 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201712211 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Norway needs to know about entitlement to and the amount of U.S. benefits for persons
receiving Norwegian disability or survivors benefits. This information is supplied
upon request and only if the beneficiary receives the same type of benefit from the
U.S. that he receives from Norway. For example, if a person is receiving retirement
benefits from the U.S. and widow's benefits from Norway, we will not provide information
about the retirement benefit.
