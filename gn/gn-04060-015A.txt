SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04060.015 - Who May Reopen an Initial, Revised, Reconsidered, or Reviewed Determination or a Hearing Officers Decision
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204060015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Part A Claims
The Health Care Financing Administration or an intermediary may reopen a Part A initial,
revised, or reconsidered determination. However, an intermediary may reopen such a
determination only if the last action on the determination was taken by the intermediary
(i.e., a determination has not been made by an ALJ, Appeals Council, or a court).
