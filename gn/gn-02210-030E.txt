E. Fraud conviction
If an individual has a fraud conviction or if we establish that similar fault exists
for the debt, do not approve a request to lower the rate of withholding. The individual
must pay the overpayment through full withholding. Notify the individual that we will
not change the repayment rate because fraud or similar fault exists. Annotate the
Remarks screen in DMS.
Example: Documenting a request for a lower rate of withholding when an individual has a fraud
conviction on the Remarks screen in DMS:
Luke Brown requested partial withholding on April 4, 2018. On January 5, 2018, we
determined that Luke’s overpayment resulted from fraud; therefore, I cannot approve
their request. I sent a them a notice on April 27, 2018. – John Jones FO A12.
