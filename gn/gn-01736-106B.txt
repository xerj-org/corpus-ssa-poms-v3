SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.106 - How to Evaluate Irish Coverage Certifications
Effective Dates: 01/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to evaluate Irish coverage certifications, on form IRL/ USA 4.
Step
Action
1
Is the correct worker's name shown on the coverage record?
•
If yes, go to Step 2
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
2
Is a “FROM - TO” entry shown for each period in which there is an entry in the number of weeks column?
•
If yes, go to Step 3
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
3
Is there a number of weeks entry for each “FROM - TO” period and is the weeks entry less than or equal to the number of weeks in the corresponding
“FROM - TO” period?
•
If yes, go to Step 4
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
4
Is the form IRL/USA 4 properly certified including the signature and official stamp?
•
If yes, see GN 01736.110 to credit the Irish coverage.
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
