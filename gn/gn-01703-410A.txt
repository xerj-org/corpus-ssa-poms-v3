SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.410 - Disposition of Totalization Folders
Effective Dates: 02/15/1995 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Totalization folders (TF) are not automatically selected for Federal Records Center
(FRC) inactivation because often the wage earner is receiving nontotalization benefits
and his/her MBR is in active status.
TF folders containing a U.S. denial are listed for future destruction.
TF folders containing no U.S. claim (no attached RSDI folder) are either destroyed
immediately after all actions are completed or held for a set period of time and then
destroyed. See GN 01703.410.C.4 for instructions to determine destruction date.
