SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.015 - How the FSP Processes and Develops Claims Filed with the Swiss Agency
Effective Dates: 02/06/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
the Swiss agency.
1. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications and forward
the claim to DIO with the Form CH/USA 3 and the Swiss coverage record.
2. Claimant already entitled to U.S. benefits
Destroy the Form CH/USA 3, unless it contains a request for information from SSA.
In that case, forward the form to DIO.
