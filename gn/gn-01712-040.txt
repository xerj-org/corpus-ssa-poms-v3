GN 01712.040 Procedure for Reviewing and Completing Claims Taken by the Norwegian Agency
Direct all requests for missing information, other than the Norwegian coverage record,
to an FSP or to the beneficiary, as appropriate, depending upon the country of residence.
Accept certifications of vital statistics from the FFU unless the dates certified
cause a material discrepancy. See GN 01701.320 for a discussion of verification of eligibility factors of foreign agencies.
