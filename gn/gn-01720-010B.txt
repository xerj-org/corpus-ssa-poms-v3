SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these instructions to develop and process applications filed in the FSP.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2960-F4, Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement (Part 1), and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-UK to the appropriate U.K. agency (See GN 01720.025) to request a U.K. coverage record, (if it appears that the worker is not insured
based only on U.S. coverage) and in disability cases, to request medical evidence
from the agency's files.
•
Forward the claim to OIO and include the file and follow-up copies of the liaison
form sent to the U.K. agency.
2. U.K. Claim
•
Take a protective statement on form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other Person)
indicating a desire to file for U.K. benefits and send it to the U.K. agency under
cover of form SSA-2960-U3-UK.
3. U.S. and U.K. Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Parts I and II) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-UK to the U.K. agency to request a U.K. coverage
record (if it appears that the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone),
to certify the protective filing date, and, in disability cases, to request medical
evidence from the agency's files.
•
Forward the claim to OIO, and include the file and follow-up copies of the liaison
form sent to the U.K. agency.
