SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.005 - How the Totalization Module Reviews Claims Filed in a Field Office (FO)
Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
Follow these steps for reviewing claims in the totalization module:
•
Review the file for the required applications and evidence listed in GN 01739.205 – GN 01739.220.
•
If the person is filing for Chilean benefits, mail the Chilean application Form CHI/USA
1 (Pension Request – Worker) or CHI/USA 2 (Survivor Pension Request) to the applicant.
Instruct the applicant to complete the application form and send it directly to the
appropriate Chilean agency. Refer to the guidelines in GN 01740.025 to determine the correct Chilean agency and provide a pre-addressed envelope for
the applicant.
Note: The Chilean applications may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section
of the Totalization Resource Kit.
•
Check the file for a U.S. totalization earnings record that can be sent to the Chilean
agency. If one is not in file, request it from OEO following the instructions in MSOM
ICERS 001.001.
•
When all required material is in file, refer to the guidelines in GN 01740.205 – GN 01740.210 for preparing a claims package for the Chilean agency.
