B. Check-intercept policy
The FO uses the check-intercept procedures only for Title II checks, not direct deposits. Because of special due process considerations, do not intercept
Title XVI checks.
EXCEPTION: An FO employee may intercept a Title XVI check if he or she has determined payee
misuse. See the misuse procedures in GN 00604.001 - GN 00604.075.
In addition, SSA must reissue the Title XVI intercepted check to the new payee (or
directly to the recipient) without a significant delay.
However, technicians must not reissue Title II checks intercepted because they were
not due. Also, technicians must correct the amount on checks intercepted because they
were for an incorrect amount before reissuance.
Do not request a check intercept to force a beneficiary to contact the FO.
