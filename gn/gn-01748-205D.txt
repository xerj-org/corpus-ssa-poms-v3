D. Procedure - Claim for Denmark benefits only (U.S. resident)
DIO takes the following action.
Sends the Danish agency the following:
•
A liaison form to certify the filing date for Danish benefits and to certify established
factors of entitlement such as date of birth
•
Part I and II of the SSA-2490 used to apply for Danish benefits (Totalization or non
totalization)
•
A U.S. earnings record
•
Available supporting evidence including medical evidence, if the claim is for disability
benefits.
DIO will send a letter to the claimant advising them that his/her claim was forwarded
to the Danish Agency.
