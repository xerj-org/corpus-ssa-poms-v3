A. Definition of void marriages
A void marriage is a marriage that is legally nonexistent from the beginning under
state law. The parties to a void marriage are considered never to have been validly
married. Therefore, a void marriage requires no formality to terminate. However, in
some states, under certain circumstances, a court may issue an order affirming that
a marriage is void (e.g., an annulment decree).
