Effective Dates: 04/18/2014 - Present | TN 25 (04-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401915 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for right to review Limited Payability (LP) decision
The beneficiary or recipient has the right to request that Fiscal Service (Treasury)
provide a second handwriting analysis as long as he or she has additional information
to support the disagreement with the original decision. Someone other than the person
who made the original decision must perform the second review. Additional proofs are
required from the beneficiary or recipient supporting the second request.
