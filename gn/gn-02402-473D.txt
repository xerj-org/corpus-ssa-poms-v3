D. Overview of IDD for Madagascar
IDD payments sent to Madagascar must be in Malagasy ariary (MGA) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Madagascar.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
