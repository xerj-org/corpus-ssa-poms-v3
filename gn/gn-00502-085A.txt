SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.085 - Advance Designation of Representative Payee
Effective Dates: 08/12/2024 - Present | TN 100 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Section 201 of Public
Law
115-165, the Strengthening Protections for Social Security Beneficiaries Act of 2018 (Strengthening
Protections Act), amends Section 205(j)(1) of the Social Security Act to allow for
advance designation of representative payees.
