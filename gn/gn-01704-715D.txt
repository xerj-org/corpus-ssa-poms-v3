D. Swiss liaison certification forms (SSA-1279-U3-SZ, SSA-1280-U3-SZ and SSA-1281-U3-SZ)
Follow the preceding general instructions. In addition, Part IV, 1.c. of Form SSA-1281-U3-SZ
requests information about the relationship of the child to the worker. If the child
is or was an RSDI beneficiary and the RSDI folder is available, determine the child's
relationship from the SSA-101-U3 or SSA-2417 on the left side of the folder. If the
RSDI folder is not available and the source of information is an MBR, or if the child
is or was not an RSDI beneficiary, show the child's relationship as shown on the totalization
application and check “alleged.” If Switzerland needs more information, they will request it. If evidence of birth
has been submitted with the totalization claim, include it in the claims package.
