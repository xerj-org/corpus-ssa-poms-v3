SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.520 - Status Requests and Post-Adjudicative Actions in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 10/03/2022 - Present | TN 7 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702520 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy
Do not follow up on claims for U.S. Totalization benefits or foreign benefits unless
at least 9 months have elapsed since the claim was sent to DIO.
If a claim for foreign benefits has already been forwarded to the foreign agency,
the applicant must follow up directly with the foreign agency.
