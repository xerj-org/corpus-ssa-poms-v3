E. Description of Sierra Leone IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Sierra Leone comes from banks determined by the FRBKC.
The bank data for Sierra Leone consists of 29 characters. However, the bank data on
the MBR will reflect 27 characters including the country code of H.
