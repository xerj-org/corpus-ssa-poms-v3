SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.112 - Collection of Unpaid Tax Liabilities in Cases Involving Determinations Under Section 202(t)(2) of the Social Security Act
Effective Dates: 09/15/2004 - Present | TN 13 (09-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010112 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. PROCESS
When a new or revised 202(t)(2) determination is made which permits payment to citizens
of a country, SSA identifies the beneficiaries that this determination may affect
and verifies their continuing entitlement to benefits. SSA then examines the file,
master beneficiary record (MBR) and, if needed, Payment History Update System (PHUS)
to determine whether the beneficiary has an unpaid tax liability.
If an unpaid tax liability exists, action is taken as explained in GN 05010.112D.
