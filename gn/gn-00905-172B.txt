B. Process
FBU will:
•
Forward the original Greek proofs, when possible;
•
Certify the contents of the record on an RC when the original document (e.g., source
records) cannot be forwarded; and
•
Provide translations of Greek documents.
Note: Claims representatives in the FBU are authorized translators.
