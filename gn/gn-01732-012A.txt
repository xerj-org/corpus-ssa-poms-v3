SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01732.012 - How the Regional Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with a Finnish Agency
Effective Dates: 06/24/1994 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
When a claimant contacts a Finnish agency to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the CPSI
routes form SF/USA 2 and a Finnish coverage record to the RFSP. If the claimant has
insisted on completing U.S. applications with the help of the SII, those completed
applications will be sent under cover of the same SF/ USA 2.
