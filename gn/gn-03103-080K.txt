K. Procedure for responding to a folder request from another office
Send the folder(s) to the FO, PC, or the servicing HO within five business days of
the folder request and control the folder per the current folder control instructions.
If the folder is not in the office or the folder cannot be sent within 25 days of
receipt of the folder request, send an email to the requestor within one business
day of when you receipt the folder request. If the folder is in another office or
another office has jurisdiction of it, provide the office’s contact information and
a contact person.
