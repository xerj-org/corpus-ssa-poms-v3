GN 04030.090 Reopening When Government Pension Offset (GPO) is Involved
NOTE: Due to the Social Security Fairness Act (SSFA) of 2023, December 2023 is the last
month that Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) and Government Pension Offset (GPO)
will apply. This means that those rules no longer apply to benefits payable for January
2024 and later.
