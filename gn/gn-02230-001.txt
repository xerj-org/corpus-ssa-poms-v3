GN 02230.001 General — Special Overpayment
AUDIENCE: BA, BATA, RECOVR, CA
The procedures outlined in the preceding sections for handling overpayments are applicable
for most cases. However, there are certain types of overpayments which, because of
the manner in which they arose, require somewhat different processing. Guidelines
for handling these extra-sensitive overpayments are outlined below.
