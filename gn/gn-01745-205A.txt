SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01745.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Japanese Agreement
Effective Dates: 09/26/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201745205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with Japan,
the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish a
claim for regular (non-Totalization) retirement, survivors or disability insurance
benefits. In addition, the applicant must submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are claimed from the United States.
