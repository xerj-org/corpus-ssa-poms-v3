B. Court Review
1. United States (U.S.) District Court
A requester may seek further review of the agency decision in a U.S. District Court.
2. Jurisdiction and Venue
The requester may file in the District Court:
•
where complainant resides, or has principal place of business;
•
where agency records are situated; or
•
at the District of Columbia.
3. Court Requirements
•
Courts are no longer required to expedite FOIA lawsuits.
•
Courts may retain jurisdiction and grant the agency additional time to process requests
in exceptional circumstances.
•
Courts may deny the agency request for additional time, if the requester shows exceptional
need or urgency.
•
Courts may require agencies to submit withheld documents for review (in camera inspection)
or to prepare an itemized list of withheld documents (Vaughan Index).
