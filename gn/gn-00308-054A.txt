A. Process
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the District Court in the county where
the divorce was granted. The cost of copy varies. Marriage records are available from
the Clerk of the District Court in the county where the license was issued. The cost
of copy varies.
Send birth/death record requests directly to the State Vital Records at the address
shown below. Unless otherwise noted, the cost of copy is $12.00 for a birth/death
record. The FO cannot certify, verify or obtain a free copy of the record.
