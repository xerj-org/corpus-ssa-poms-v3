C. Procedure
Use the following procedure to determine the proper Belgian agency.
1.
Claim for Belgian Benefits or Request for Belgian Coverage Record
•
If you are sending a claim for Belgian retirement or survivor benefits or requesting
a Belgian coverage record for a retirement or survivor claim, then send the claim
or request to the Rijksdienst voor Pensioenen (RVP).
•
If you are sending a claim for Belgian disability benefits or requesting a Belgian
coverage record for a disability claim, then send the claim to the Rijksinstituut
voor Ziekte-en Invaliditeitsverzekering (RIZIV).
2.
Response to Assistance Request from a Belgian Agency
If you are responding to an assistance request from a Belgian agency, then write to
the branch of the agency which requested the information.
