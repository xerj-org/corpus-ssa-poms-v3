B. Process
The special application forms are not taken in the Field Offices (FO). After receiving
a claim for Norwegian benefits that was filed with an FO, OIO mails the appropriate
Norwegian application to the applicant with instructions to the applicant to complete
the form and mail it directly to Norway. (These application forms are identified as
N-USA-1.1 (application for old-age pension), N-USA-1.2 (application for disability
pension), and N-USA-1.3 (application for survivor pension)).
