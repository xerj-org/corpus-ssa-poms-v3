SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.352 - Coding Singapore Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/22/2026 - Present | TN 325 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402352 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Singapore will have a prefix of "66" at the beginning of the
Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN)
field has the letter S assigned for Singapore.
