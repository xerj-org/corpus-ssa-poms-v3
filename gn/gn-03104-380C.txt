C. Procedure for Title XVI
1.
Include the following remark on the Supplemental Security Income Record (SSR): PRIOR CLAIM AWAITING AC DECISION.
2.
Include the following remarks on the Development Worksheet:
•
ISSUE of “AC APPEAL.”
•
The SSR record number, e.g., “RECORD 1" in the remarks for that issue.
•
Set an appropriate tickle date for the AC's decision.
3.
If the subsequent claim is not a Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS) claim, control
the prior claim with the appeal pending at the AC with a Modernized Development Worksheet
(MDW) record, using the same ISSUE and REMARKS as shown above, or control it manually.
4.
Terminate the SSR and lock the claim iteration on the MSSICS pending file for the
claim with the appeal pending at the AC. Follow procedures in SM 01010.060, SM 01010.061,
and MS 04404.011.
5.
Process the subsequent claim by following existing new claim procedures as if the
prior claim had not been filed:
a.
The date of entitlement/eligibility can be no earlier than the day after the date
of the ALJ decision.
b.
If the subsequent claim is an allowance or denial, process through normal systems
procedures.
c.
If the subsequent claim is denied and the claimant appeals, process though normal
systems procedures.
