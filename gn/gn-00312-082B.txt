B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
For birth, death, and marriage records, individuals may order records through the
Cayman Islands Government General Registry website or by submitting an online application
at https://vitals.ky. They may also visit or request by mail at:
Ground Floor, Government Administration Building
133 Elgin Avenue
Box 123
Grand Cayman KY1-9000
Cayman Islands
NOTE:
We do not accept copies printed online from the Chilean Office of Civil Registration.
unless the custodian subsequently affixes a signature, stamp, or seal with a statement
attesting to the accuracy.
For divorce records, contact the court that granted the divorce. Pursuant to Order
63 r.8 of the Grand Court Rules, certified copies of the register(s) may be obtained
through the Office of the Clerk of Court by emailing civilregistry@judicial.ky.
2. Information to include on applications
a. Birth records
•
Full name of the individual; and
•
Date and place of birth.
b. Death records
•
Full name of the deceased;
•
Date and place of birth; and
•
Date and place of death.
c. Divorce records
•
Full name of the parties;
•
Dates and places of birth of the parties;
•
Date and place of marriage; and
•
Date of divorce.
d. Marriage records
•
Full names of the parties;
•
Dates and places of birth of both parties,;and
•
Date and place of marriage.
3. Fee
•
Birth or death records - $10.00 (KYD) or $12.20 (USD)
•
Divorce records - Unknown
•
Marriage records - $15.00 (KYD) or $18.30 (USD)
