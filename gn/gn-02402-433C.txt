SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.433 - Coding Palau Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/06/2019 - Present | TN 140 (06-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402433 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Palau will have a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “G” assigned for Palau.
