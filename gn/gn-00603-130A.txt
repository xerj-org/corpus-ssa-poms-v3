SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00603.130 - When Former Payee May Administer Conserved Benefits
Effective Dates: 08/11/2023 - Present | TN 14 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200603130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A former payee may be permitted to continue administering conserved benefits in exceptional
circumstances where the arrangement serves the best interests of the beneficiary.
The former payee will have the same responsibilities as the current representative
payee for complying with regulations governing use of benefits. If the benefits being
held by the former payee are not needed for the current maintenance of the beneficiary,
they are to be conserved or invested in accordance with the instructions in this subchapter.
