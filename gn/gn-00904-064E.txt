Effective Dates: 04/11/2016 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904064 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Written lead or inquiries
When received by a nonresident DO, and transmit to the resident office unless the
lead or inquiry contains a clear preference to contact the nonresident office. In
the latter case, the nonresident office will handle the matter as in C. above. Do
not contact the inquirer solely for the purpose of determining his/her preference.
