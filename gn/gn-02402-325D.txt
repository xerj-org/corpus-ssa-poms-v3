D. Overview of IDD for Hong Kong
IDD payments sent to Hong Kong must be in Hong Kong dollar (HKD) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Hong Kong.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
