GN 00210.505 Same-Sex Relationships – Child’s Benefits Based on a Stepchild Relationship
Follow these instructions when entitlement to benefits as a stepchild depends on the
number holder's (NH) same-sex marriage or non-marital legal relationship (NMLR) with
the child’s parent or adoptive parent.
When you are ready to process the claim as an award or denial, follow the instructions
in GN 00210.020.
IMPORTANT: Do not use these instructions if the NH is the biological or adoptive parent of
the child seeking benefits. Child benefits can be awarded on that NH’s record. For
more information about how a child may qualify as the NH's child for purposes of entitlement
to benefits, refer to GN 00306.001.
