A. Interviewing guidelines for Medicare claimants
When interviewing claimants in regards to Medicare, provide a general explanation
of Medicare. To help explain Medicare provisions, refer to GN 00203.050. For procedures on how to handle special situations that are likely to arise during
the interview that may require additional explanations or actions, see GN 00203.025C in this section.
