E. Procedure for debtor inquiries, requests, and protests
NED payees have the same due process rights as beneficiaries who are overpaid. In
addition, the payees make the same requests and have the same questions as overpaid
beneficiaries.
SSA’s existing overpayment policies and procedures serve to address the inquiries,
requests, and protests made by NED payees.
