D. Assembly and Disposition of Documents
Assemble the below documents, in electronic format, and send them (as an attachment)
via email to OGC. Each document should be in its own separate PDF file:
•
Notice of Bankruptcy,
•
Proof of Claim (i.e. Official Form 410), and
•
Certificate of Indebtedness.
CAUTION: Do not send a copy of the electronic claims folder, unless specifically requested
by OGC.
