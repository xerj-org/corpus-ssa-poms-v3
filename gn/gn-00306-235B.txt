B. Policy - Definition of Grandchild
An applicant is the grandchild or stepgrandchild of the NH or his/her spouse if the
child is the natural child, legally adopted child, or stepchild of a parent who is
a child of the NH or his spouse. A great-grandchild cannot qualify under this provision.
NOTE: If the grandchild is legally adopted by the NH, follow GN 00306.135
