C. Process when DNE is sent to the FI
On the day after a death termination is displayed on a Title II or Title XVI record,
SSA passes a DNE to the Richmond Federal Reserve Bank (RFRB) if the record was in
current payment status before the death termination was applied to the record.
RFRB then passes the information through the Automated Clearing House (ACH) to the
U.S. FI to which the beneficiary/recipient had direct deposit of benefits.
