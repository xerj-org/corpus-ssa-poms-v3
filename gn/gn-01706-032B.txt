B. Requesting Partial Certifications
When a coverage certification is requested, CAs must determine the number of weeks
of Italian coverage required to establish insured status. To determine the number
of weeks required, multiply the number of QCs needed for insured status by 13.
If Italian coverage is needed to establish disability insured status, CAs also must
determine the beginning and ending dates (month/year) for the period in which coverage
is required.
