B. Procedure for adjudicating awards to SCBs not previously entitled to child's benefits
1. Non-District Office Final Adjudicative (DOFA) cases
Include a Form SSA-1383-FC (Report of Social Security Administration by Student Outside
the United States) with the award notice. In Philippine cases, prepare a student contact
diary (RS 00205.755) for the Check Claims and Recovery Examiner (CCRE).
2. DOFA cases
Annotate the routing form to show we must include an SSA-1383-FC with the award notice.
