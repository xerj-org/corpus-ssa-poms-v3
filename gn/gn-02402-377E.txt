E. Description of Romania IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Romania will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The IBAN for Romania consists
of up to 24 characters. The bank data for Romania reflected on the MBR consists of
up to 27 characters including the country code of Q for Romania.
