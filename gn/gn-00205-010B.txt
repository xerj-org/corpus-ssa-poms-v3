B. Policy for simultaneous entitlement
We have enough information to apply the simultaneous entitlement provisions of section
202(k) of the Act if the claimant answers, “Yes,” to having ever filed an application
for Social Security benefits and gives the identifying information requested, such
as the number holder’s (NH) SSN.
