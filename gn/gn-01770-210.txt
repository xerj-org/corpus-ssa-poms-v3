GN 01770.210 Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Slovenian Benefits
Under the agreement with Slovenia, the Social Security Administration (SSA) takes
applications for both regular and totalization benefits on behalf of Slovenia. SSA
also provides limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim
(see GN 01755.125).
