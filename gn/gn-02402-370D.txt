D. Overview of IDD for Bolivia
IDD payments sent to Bolivia must be in U.S. dollar accounts (USD) at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bolivia. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
