GN 03313.025 Disclosure to the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from HHS, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
Prior to becoming independent of HHS, we provided certain information to HHS components
based on the Privacy Act’s “need to know” provision (5 U.S.C. § 552a(b)(1)). Under
provisions of the Social Security Independence and Program Improvements Act of 1994,
we must continue to disclose certain information when requested in writing by the
Secretary of HHS, if records or information of such type were disclosed under applicable
rules, regulations, and procedures in effect before the enactment of this statute.
See 42 U.S.C. §904(e)(1)(B).
