A. Title II beneficiary living abroad requests direct deposit to a United States FI
Title II beneficiaries residing outside the United States can request direct deposit
to a U.S. Financial Institution (FI).
If you receive this type of request, follow the same procedures used for beneficiaries
residing within the United States.
