Effective Dates: 01/11/2017 - Present | TN 45 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When not to use a U.S. mailing address
A beneficiary may not use a mailing address in the U.S. to receive payment when they:
•
travel abroad for more than 3 months;
•
reside in a Treasury-restricted country listed in RS 02650.001C; or
•
reside in a barred country listed in RS 02650.040.
NOTE: Technicians must never send checks to relatives or friends in the U.S. for beneficiaries
who are abroad more than 3 months except during interim periods while developing a
proper mailing address.
