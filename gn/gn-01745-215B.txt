B. Procedure – Required Applications for U.S. Totalization Benefits
Take the following steps when taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits under the
agreement with Japan:
1.
Complete the appropriate MCS application screens for retirement, disability or survivors’
benefits.
2.
Complete the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011).
3.
Access the Totalization Benefits Resource Kit on the Intranet at Totalization
Benefits Resource
Kit.
4.
Print form J/USA 4 and give it to the applicant to complete.
5.
Provide any necessary assistance in completing the Japanese form.
NOTE: Most individuals filing for Japanese benefits should be able to complete the Japanese
form without assistance.
6.
Photocopy and certify any documents (evidence) submitted by the applicant.
7.
Process an initial insured-status denial (see GN 01702.315).
8.
Forward the completed U.S. Totalization claim (with certified copies of evidence)
to the Office of International Operations (OIO) in Baltimore at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OIO - Totalization
P.O. Box 17049
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7049
9.
Complete the Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the date the claim
is forwarded to OIO. Show “Totalization” as the ISSUE when completing the MDW screen.
