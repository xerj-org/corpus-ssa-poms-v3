Effective Dates: 04/07/2023 - Present | TN 49 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating Policy
Unlike waiver of recovery which precludes further recovery efforts, an overpayment
for which collection efforts are discontinued remains subject to recovery (e.g., from
future benefits payable to the debtor or to any other person on the same earnings
record). If benefits are subsequently payable to the debtor on that record, the system
will automatically initiate adjustment to recover the overpayment.
