Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
This procedure describes special development and routing instructions that apply to
claims under the agreement filed in the FSP in Buenos Aires.
•
Have the applicant complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
NOTE: Make certain that the applicant enters the name of the Chilean agency or Pension
Fund Administrator to which the worker last contributed in item 5 on the first page
of Form SSA-2490-BK.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-2960-CHI and the first page of Form SSA-2490-BK to the
appropriate Chilean liaison agency to request a Chilean coverage record.
•
Forward the claim to DIO with a copy of the Form SSA-2960-CHI sent to the Chilean
agency.
