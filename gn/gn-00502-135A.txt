A. Definition of Creditor
A creditor is an individual or organization who provides the beneficiary with goods
or services for monetary consideration. Some examples of a creditor payee may be:
•
Nursing Homes
•
Assisted Living Facilities
•
Group Homes
•
Legal Guardian
