Effective Dates: 10/13/2017 - Present | TN 3 (08-89)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202315005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If someone seeking legal representative status appears to meet the requirements of
the small estate statutes, but has not yet complied with those requirements, advise
him/her to contact the appropriate civil authority for information on satisfying that
State's requirements.
