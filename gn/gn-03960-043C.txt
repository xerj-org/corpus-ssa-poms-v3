SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.043 - Administrative Review of the Fee Authorized Under the Fee Agreement Process - Concurrent Titles II and XVI Cases or Affected Auxiliary Beneficiary(ies) Involved
Effective Dates: 01/08/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960043 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. EXAMPLE
In a concurrent claims case favorably decided at the reconsideration level, the claimant
timely requested administrative review of the title II portion of the fee. The FO
has not yet authorized the title XVI portion of the fee. The reviewer contacts the
FO and asks that it authorize the title XVI portion of the fee, notify the claimant
and representative of the fee, and send him the claim(s) file with a copy of the Fee
Agreement Notice. The reviewer acknowledges receipt and notifies the representative
and the title II decision maker of the request, explaining that he will continue the
review once the FO has authorized the title XVI portion of the fee.
