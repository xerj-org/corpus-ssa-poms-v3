C. Principal claimant does not file
When it becomes apparent that a potential principal claimant will not file a claim,
the person next in line will become the principal claimant. Prepare an R /C or include
other documentation in the file explaining why a claim was not obtained from the potential
principal claimant. If the next person in line lives in another service area, forward
the development to the appropriate office without delay.
