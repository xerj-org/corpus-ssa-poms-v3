C. Procedure for sending RSDI material to DIO when it is the resident office
1.
If the beneficiary notifies the DO of change of address to a country for which the
DIO is resident office (that is, there are no claimants or beneficiaries on the same
SSN residing in the 50 States, D.C., Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa,
Canada, or the Philippines), send the notification to the servicing PSC or ODO as
appropriate. Upon receipt of the change of address notice, the servicing PSC or ODO
will forward the claims file to DIO.
2.
When a beneficiary or claimant returns to the United States from a foreign country
serviced by DIO and a claim is in process, DIO will send the complete claims file
to the new resident office. The DO will handle the claim as the resident office and
send all material to the appropriate reviewing office upon completion of all remaining
development.
