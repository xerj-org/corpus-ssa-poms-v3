SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.230 - Evidence of Residence
Effective Dates: 10/13/2022 - Present | TN 8 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702230 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy
1. General
A period of residence cannot begin earlier than the day a person is physically present
in the country with the intention of establishing at least a temporary home there.
For example, arrival as a visitor or tourist with no intention of establishing a home
in that country would not begin a period of residence. A period of residence ends
with the departure from a country with the intention to reside elsewhere.
2. Temporary absence
Residence in a country may continue even though a person is temporarily absent from
that country. An absence of six months or less is considered temporary. If an absence
is for more than six months, residence may be considered to continue only if there
is sufficient evidence to show the person intends to maintain the residence. Sufficient
evidence includes the maintenance of a home, the existence of business or family associations
sufficient to warrant the person’s return, or other similar acts or factors.
