SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.215 - Family Maximums – Totalization and Non-Totalization Involved
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 13 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When Totalization and non-Totalization benefits are payable on the same record for
the same period:
•
All Totalization beneficiaries will be subject to a family maximum based on the pro
rata primary insurance amount (PIA).
•
All non-Totalization beneficiaries will be subject to a family maximum based on the
non-Totalization PIA.
•
In no case may the total benefit payable to all beneficiaries exceed the non-Totalization
maximum.
