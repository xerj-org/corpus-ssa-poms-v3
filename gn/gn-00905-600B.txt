B. Policy for protecting the filing dates for residents of Treasury barred countries
In situations where an inquiry meets the requirements for protective writing as outlined
in GN 00204.010, make the same effort to protect filing dates for residents of countries to which
payment is barred as is made for residents of any other country.
However, because payment to these individuals is precluded by Treasury Circular 655,
do not make extensive efforts to determine entitlement to benefits.
