SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside the U.K.
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

3. U.S. and U.K. or U.K. Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-UK
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from OIO files, if the U.K. claim is for disability benefits
•
Evidence submitted for the U.K. claim, and
•
A request for the U.K. coverage record, if one is needed
