D. Overview of IDD for Andorra
IDD payments sent to Andorra must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that territory. The FBU contacts
the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
