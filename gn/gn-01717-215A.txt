Effective Dates: 05/27/2010 - Present | TN 3 (02-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201717215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An individual who wishes to file for benefits under the agreement with Sweden must
complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social
Security Agreement). This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only
from the United States, only from Sweden or from both countries.
