SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01715.340 - Applications for U.S. Benefits Filed in Quebec Province
Effective Dates: 06/15/2004 - Present | TN 7 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201715340 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Persons in Quebec Province may file for U.S. benefits with the Quebec liaison agency.
When a person filing for a pension from the Quebec Pension Plan (QPP) indicates that
he or she has U.S. coverage but is not old enough to file for U.S. benefits, the Quebec
liaison agency sends the person a letter advising him or her to recontact the liaison
agency within 4 months of reaching the U.S. age requirement. The Quebec liaison agency
also telephones the person within 1 year of reaching the U.S. age requirement to remind
him or her about applying for the U.S. benefit.
