GN 02607.420 Incarceration Reporting and Control System (IRCS)
The IRCS is a series of automated screens and queries used to record, maintain, and
display information about all Federal, State, and local correctional and mental institutions
(both participating inmate reporting institutions and non-participating institutions).
IRCS maintains information about individual reporters and facilities, including:
•
reporting agreement data,
•
details of reports received, and
•
incentive payments for timely reports of confinement.
Field office (FO) personnel establish and update agreement, reporter, and facility
information on IRCS as discussed in this section.
For more information on the IRCS, see GN 02607.410.
