Alabama
Fiduciary Services
Direct Fiduciary Services are not required to hold a license. Court appointed conservators do not have to be
licensed.
Type of license
Health Care Providers
Community health and mental health centers are state or county owned; they are certified
but not licensed. Privately owned medical facilities are licensed as such, not as
Fiduciary
Services. Individuals within these facilities are state licensed only as required due to their
profession. (MD, RN)
Respite or Long Term Care Facilities
Group homes, nursing homes, and psychiatric facilities are licensed. Other facilities
are certified but not licensed. None are licensed as Fiduciary Services, but many serve as rep payee for their clients.
Other Licensing Requirements
State and counties require business licenses. They are specific to the type of business
seeking licensing.
Procedures for obtaining a license
Apply at the corresponding county courthouse for both state and county licenses. Corporations
must also apply with the Secretary of State for a corporate license.

American Samoa
Fiduciary Services
Every business operating in American Samoa, unless exempted, must maintain an annual
business license. Only businesses carried by, or directly and solely for, the American
Samoa Government or U.S. Government, and farmers marketing their own produce in its
natural state, are exempted. Any person, partnership or corporation that operates
a business venture in two or more locations in American Samoa must obtain a business
license for each location. Similarly, any person, partnership or corporation that
operates a business venture that is engaged in two or more activities must obtain
a separate business license for each activity. Corporations must submit a copy of
the Article of Incorporation, certified by the Treasurer of American Samoa. Partnerships
must submit a notarized partnership agreement.
Type of license
Health Care Providers
No data available
Respite or Long Term Care Facilities
No data available
Other Licensing Requirements
The business license year begins in January 1; most business license fees are $25.00
per year. A business license certificate, regardless of when issued or renewed, expires
on December 31st of the year in which it was issued or renewed. A licensee may renew their business
license by filing a Business License Renewal Application with the Economic Development
Division and pay the appropriate business license fee at the Department of Treasury,
Revenue Branch.
Procedures for obtaining a license
The same application process is required for all types of businesses; including, but
not limited to, general business applicants, fiduciary licensees, social service entities,
etc. If the business is introducing a new service to American Samoa, the Territory
Planning Commission must first approve it before applying for a business license.

California
Fiduciary Services
A license is required for persons engaging in the business, for compensation, of receiving
money as an agent of another for the purpose of paying bills, invoices or accounts.
A license is also required for persons who, for compensation, engage in whole or in
part in the business of receiving money or evidences thereof for the purpose of distributing
the money or evidences thereof among creditors in payment or partial payment of the
obligations of the debtor. Licenses for such business will only be issued to corporations

of the business and payment of the applicable fees.

Delaware
Fiduciary Services
No specific licenses are issued for businesses that manage other people's money.
Nonprofit Corporations in Delaware are those that have an exemption under Section
501(c) of the Internal Revenue Code. They are exempt from obtaining a business license
and paying gross receipt taxes on the sales of most goods and services. Nonprofit
corporations are required to register with the Delaware Division of Revenue and withhold
Delaware State income taxes on employees performing services within Delaware. Registration
is also required with the Delaware Department of Labor. Additionally, if the nonprofits
activities are conducted in Delaware, a copy of Federal Form 990 PH must be filed
with the State of Delaware, Attorney General. Unincorporated Nonprofit Associations
must appoint an agent and register with the Secretary of State's Office. Business
must satisfy any local license and zoning requirements. (A for-profit occupational
professional or person service provider is required to obtain a business license --
$75.00 for the first location and $25.00 for each additional location – which must
be renewed annually on or before December 31st of each year).
Type of license
Health Care Providers
Community mental and health centers must be licensed.
Respite and Long Term Care Facilities
Licenses are required for health care facilities, rest homes, and boarding homes.
Other Licensing Requirements
The business license issued by the Division of Revenue is not a regulatory license
and the issuance of such a license does not attest to the qualifications of the applicant
to perform the activity described on such license.
Procedures for obtaining a license
Complete appropriate application forms and pay scheduled fees.

Fiduciary Services
A business that provides Fiduciary Services and is considered a trust company must be licensed in this state, with some exceptions.
These are: attorneys, trustees under a deed of trust, a court appointed fiduciary
or a person who acts as a fiduciary if such relationship is not one of their principal
occupation, or who serves as a fiduciary for a relative by blood or marriage.

Respite and Long Term Care Facilities

license. Some other specific licenses are required in addition to a general business
license; among them are group foster homes and trust companies.
Procedures for obtaining a license

to the Nevada Dept. of Taxation. Foster home license: Submit the application to the
Division of Child and Family Services of the State Dept. of Human Resources. Trust
