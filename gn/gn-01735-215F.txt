F. Procedure – Irish survivors claims
If the applicant is claiming widow(er) or divorced widow(er) benefits from Ireland,
enter the widow(er)'s or divorced widow(er)'s Irish insurance number(s) in the “Remarks”
section of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK. Since the coverage requirements
for Irish widow(er) or divorced widow(er) benefits can be met using either the NH's
or the widow(er)'s or divorced widow(er)'s coverage record (see GN 01735.020B.4.a), Ireland needs the widow(er)'s or divorced widow(er)'s number(s) as well as the
worker's.
