B. Policy – situations when U.S. Residency determinations are not necessary
1. SSI For Children Of Military Personnel Abroad
No determination of U.S. residency is needed for a child who:
•
Is a U.S. citizen; and
•
Is living with a parent who is a member of the U.S. Armed Forces assigned to permanent
duty ashore outside the United States.
(See SI 00501.415 ff. for instructions regarding this provision.)
2. SSI For Students Temporarily Abroad
No determination of U.S. residency is needed for certain individuals who are temporarily
abroad for study purposes. (See SI 00501.411 ff. for instructions regarding this provision.
