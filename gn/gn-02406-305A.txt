A. Background
Effective March 22, 2008, SSI Systems began receiving an electronic file that contains
Claims Disposition Notices (CDN) from the Department of the Treasury. CDNs inform
SSA about a check claims’ status (i.e., forgery claims). The CDN file posts a forgery
indicator in the PMTH Segment Display in Column F for checks on which a claim exists.
