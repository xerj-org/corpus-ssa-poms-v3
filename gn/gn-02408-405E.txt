E. PC procedure for follow-ups on reclamations involving BPO
Direct requests for the status of an FMS-133 and FMS-135 with BPO involved, by email, to Fiscal Service at PFC-Reclamations@fiscal.treasury.gov.
Note:
Treasury no longer receives requests by mail.
