SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.198 - Assembly of Material Where Claims Folder Received From DDS or ODO
Effective Dates: 01/15/1999 - Present | TN 1 (06-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050198 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Folder from ODO (new claim filed)
1.
Place new application and related material above previous application material on
the right side of the folder in the order specified in GN 01050.190 or GN 01050.195.
2.
Prepare Form SSA-831-U5.
Note: If the current claim is DDS jurisdiction, the current application and current nondisability
material would be retained in the DO and not sent with the prior folder to the DDS.
