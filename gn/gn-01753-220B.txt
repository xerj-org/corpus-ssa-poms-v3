B. Application process for Slovak benefits
The special Slovak application is Form SK/USA 2 (Claim for Pension Benefits from the
Pension Insurance System of the Slovak Republic).
The bilingual Slovak special application Form SK/USA 2 is available on the Division
of International Operations (DIO) Totalization Forms
Intranet website.
NOTE: Do not share this Form publicly unless claimants are filling an application or claiming
benefits.
