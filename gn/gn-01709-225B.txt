SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01709.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Swiss Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 8 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201709225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Swiss claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for the status of
his or her claim for Swiss benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded a claim to the Swiss agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP)
application. See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access
the EP User Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Switzerland
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Switzerland, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC Shows DIO forwarded the claim to Switzerland
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Swiss agency, advise the applicant to
contact the Swiss agency directly. Applicants can find additional contact and other
information online at:
NAV National Office for
Social Insurance Abroad
NOTE:
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Swiss benefits claim after a technician
forwards the claim to Switzerland. For more information about status requests in Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
