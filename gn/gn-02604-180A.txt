A. Description of the automated job stream-3 (AJS-3) penalty process
For automated enforcement actions and late annual reports directly entered into the
Master Beneficiary Record (MBR), the AJS-3 will:
1.
calculate the penalty;
2.
prepare a notice advising of the overpayment, penalty, proposed adjustment, and the
opportunity to establish good cause;
3.
control the penalty for collection; and
4.
withhold the penalty from benefits due (unless we revoke the penalty or the beneficiary
requests a partial withholding).
