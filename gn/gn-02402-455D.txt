D. Overview of IDD for Bhutan
IDD payments sent to Bhutan must be in Bhutanese ngultrum (BTN) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bhutan. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
