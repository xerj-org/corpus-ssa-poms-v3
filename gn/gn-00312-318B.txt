B. Policy for obtaining birth, marriage, divorce, and death records from the Philippines
The Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) in Manila requests CRDs from the LCRO and PSA.  Ask
the FBU to request CRDs by following GN 00904.220B.3.  Obtain (or ask the FBU to obtain) written authorization as discussed in GN 00904.204B.7.b Include information needed for birth records described in GN 00307.001C.1.
