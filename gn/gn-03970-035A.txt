A. Disability Adjudication’s designation of a hearing officer
Upon receiving a request from Law & Policy (LP) for the designation of a hearing officer,
Disability Adjudication (DA) will assign an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) to serve
as the hearing officer for the case following instructions in Hearings, Appeals, and
Litigation Law (HALLEX) manual HA
01110.02B.
If the hearing officer holds a hearing, follow policy and procedure in GN 03970.035B through GN 03970.050.
