SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.210 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed In Luxembourg
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use these instructions for preparing claims packages.
1. U.S. Claim
Prepare a claims package if a Luxembourg coverage record is needed and has not been
forwarded by the Luxembourg agency. Include the liaison form SSA-2960-U3-LX in the
package.
2. U.S. and Luxembourg or Luxembourg Claim Only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
a Luxembourg coverage record is needed and has not been forwarded by the Luxembourg
agency; or
•
a Luxembourg agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-LX
•
Information or documentation requested by the Luxembourg agency
