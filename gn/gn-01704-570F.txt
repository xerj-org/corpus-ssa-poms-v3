F. Railroad earnings involved
1. Determining need to identify railroad service months
If railroad (RR) involvement exists, benefit earnings technicians (BETs) must first
determine if there is a need to identify the RR service months (SMs). BETs need not
identify SMs if:
a.
Additional QCs cannot be credited because of the RR SMs. (It is known that the SMs
are for a period for which QCs are already posted;
b.
There are 12 SMs shown for a calendar year;
c.
The NH has 120 or more SMs or is an Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) annuitant (SSA
may not use the RR earnings hence the foreign agency may not); or
d.
The NH is insured for RSDI benefits and the claimant has:
1.
Restricted his or her claim to U.S. benefits;
2.
The NH's ER displays at least 13-1/2 years (54 QCs) without the SMs and the claimant
is filing under the German agreement;
3.
The claim was filed under the Swiss agreement and it is neither a claim for disability
benefits, nor for orphan's benefits filed on a deceased mother's account;
4.
The claim is a disability claim or an orphan's claim on a deceased mother's account,
which is filed under the Swiss agreement, but the year shown in the “YLW” (year last
worked) box of block 4 of the ER is 4 years prior to the year of filing (disability
claim), or 4 years prior to the mother's death (orphan's claim);
5.
The claim is for Norwegian disability or survivor benefits and the SMs are not needed
to fulfill the recent coverage requirement;
6.
The claim is for a Norwegian old-age pension and the ER displays at least 8 QCs without
SMs; or
7.
The claim is for Belgian benefits and a Belgian agency has not requested coverage
information for the period in which the SMs are showing.
2. Service month identification necessary
The information in the Disability, Railroad, Alien, and Military Service (DRAMS) record
is accurate and reliable to identify or verify Railroad SMs. The RRB automatically
updates information to the DRAMS record on a weekly basis according to a Computer
Matching Agreement (CMA) between SSA and the RRB.
