D. How arrival records are arranged
Most of these records are arranged by soundex code. The reason that an arrival record
has not been located in a significant number of cases is that the individual failed
to furnish correct or sufficient information regarding his/ her arrival. It is getting
more difficult to locate arrival records because many current claimant's were children
at the time of arrival and they, of course, may not be as sure of the facts of their
arrival as therr parents were.
