SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.136 - Dependency Requirements for Child Adopted Prior to Entitlement or Death
Effective Dates: 11/23/1998 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306136 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy — life cases
1. General
A child legally adopted by the NH prior to or in the month in which the NH became
entitled to a primary benefit is deemed dependent on the NH at any of the points listed
in GN 00306.007 at which the relationship existed.
NOTE:The month in which a person becomes entitled is the month in which he/she files the
necessary application, if all other requirements are met in that month.
2. Special Rule — NH Entitled to DIB
The following policy applies only for purposes of determining whether a child was
adopted before or after a NH's DIB entitlement. It is effective with respect to applications
for child's benefits filed 6/89 or later: If a NH's entitlement to DIB was terminated
due to cessation of disability, and he/she successfully appealed the termination (i.e.,
benefits were reinstated retroactively), he/she is not considered “entitled” to benefits during the period between the termination and reinstatement. Thus, a
child adopted by the NH during that period would be deemed dependent at any of the
points listed in GN 00306.007 at which the relationship existed.
NOTE: The child's application receives full retroactivity, even if it is retroactive to
a point before 6/89.
