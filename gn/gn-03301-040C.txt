C. Policy - Right to Financial Privacy Act of 1978
The Right to Financial Privacy Act (RFPA) of 1978, P.L. 95-630, governs SSA's obtaining
financial records or information about a customer of a financial institution.
The RFPA:
•
requires that requests for information from financial institutions must be made with
the full authorization of the individual, and
•
restricts SSA's redisclosure of financial information.
(See GN 03360.050 and AIMS GAM Chapter 14 for a full explanation of the requirements of the RFPA.)
