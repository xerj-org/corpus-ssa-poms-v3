A. Introduction
When an applicant files a claim for U.S. Totalization or foreign benefits, special
evidence may be required to support the claim. The following sections describe the
evidence requirements common to all agreements. Other special evidence required under
a particular agreement is described in the POMS subchapter describing that agreement.
