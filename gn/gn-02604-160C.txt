C. Procedure for good cause approval
Find good cause for filing a late report if the beneficiary alleges any of the circumstances
listed in this section. Accept a good cause explanation under these circumstances
only for the first violation. We require corroborating evidence for all subsequent
violations and for good cause reasons not specified in this section. For guidance on acceptable miscellaneous good cause see
GN 02604.160D.11. (in this section).
