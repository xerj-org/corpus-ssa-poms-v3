SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.041 - How To Apply Offset
Effective Dates: 01/06/2011 - Present | TN 33 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610041 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure — PC Title II offset
1. Non-couples cases
a.
Subtract the title II offset amount first from the retroactive benefits due in the
offset period.
b.
If any offset remains, subtract it from the retroactive title II due for months outside
the offset period.
c.
Subtract any collectible SSI Overpayment, SI 02220.021
2. Couples cases
a.
Offset previously computed for one member
Subtract the additional offset amount from the retroactive title II benefits due the
newly eligible member (GN 02610.037).
b.
Offset computed for both members of a couple simultaneously
•
Follow GN 02610.040 to compute offset amount and determine each member's prorated share.
•
Subtract this from the retroactive title II benefits due each member.
•
If the offset for member A exceeds the total retroactive title II benefits due, subtract
the remaining offset from the retroactive title II benefits due member B after member
B's offset has been subtracted.
•
Subtract any collectible SSI Overpayment, SI 02220.021
