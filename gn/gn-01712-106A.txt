A. Policy
In Norway a disabled worker receives “Sickness Pay” for at least one year after a disability has been established. The FFU treats “Sickness Pay” exactly the same as covered earnings and it is shown on the worker's earnings record.
Norwegian Coverage which is based on sickness pay and certified by Norway may be used
to establish insured status for totalization benefits from the U.S.
