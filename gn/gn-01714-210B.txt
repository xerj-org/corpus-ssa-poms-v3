SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01714.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in Belgium
Effective Dates: 02/17/2017 - Present | TN 1 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201714210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to prepare a claims package.
1.
U.S. claim
•
Prepare a claims package if a Belgian coverage record is required and has not been
requested by the FSP in Brussels.
•
Include Form SSA-e2960-U2-BE in the package.
2.
U.S. and Belgian or Belgian claim only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
the Belgian coverage record is required and has not been requested by the FSP; or
•
the Belgian agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Form SSA-e2960-U2-BE; and
•
information or documentation requested by the Belgian agency.
