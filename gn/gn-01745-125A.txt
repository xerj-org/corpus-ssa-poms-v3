SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01745.125 - Eligibility for Japanese Benefits under the Agreement
Effective Dates: 09/26/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201745125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
The agreement provides that Japan will count a worker’s U.S. coverage when determining
eligibility for Japanese benefits if the worker:
•
is not insured based on Japanese coverage alone, and
•
has at least 1 month of Japanese coverage.
