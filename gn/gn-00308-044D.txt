D. City of Boston Record
Information on fees and methods of claimant contact can be found at the City of Boston Registry Division. SSA field office requests for City of Boston records must be made through the parallel
field office (FO Code 030, Boston, MA). The Boston FO prefers that requests be made
via e562 with the Birth Certificate verification screen completed fully. Title the
request “Boston City Hall Request” and include all pertinent information. Can parallel
FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record from Boston City Hall?
Only verifications are free. The FO must pay for all other services. Limit requests
to those detailed in GN 00904.080.
