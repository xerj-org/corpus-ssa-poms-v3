SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00608.042 - Evaluation of SSA 7161-OCR-SM — Item 7-Custody of the Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 08/15/2023 - Present | TN 7 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200608042 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
Compare the response to item 7 on the form with the preprinted custody code on the
top of the form. (See RS 02655.005C.3. and/or other information available for information on the meaning of the custody
codes.) If this information shows that the payee has custody, but the response to
this item is “YES,” resolve the discrepancy.
Develop any indication of a change in custody, unless it is a temporary, short-term
arrangement and the beneficiary has returned to the payee's custody.
Depending on other information furnished in the report and/or in the file, consider
the need for a new payee if the payee reports they are not living with the beneficiary.
