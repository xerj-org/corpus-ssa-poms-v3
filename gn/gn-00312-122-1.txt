1. Office to Contact
Records are maintained at the local level and centrally in Quito. Request certifications
from:
•
Direccion General del Registro Civil
Teniente Hugo Ortiz
Turubamba, Quito
or
•
Civil Registry Office in the district or parish in which the event occurred
If the event occurred in Guayaquil, send the request to:
Registro Civil
Avenida 25 de Julio
Guayaquil.
