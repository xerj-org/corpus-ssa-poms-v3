B. Procedure for handling requests
State agencies that administer LIHEAP receive certain non-tax return information from
us primarily through electronic data exchanges, as authorized under data exchange
agreements with us. Consult with the regional office Data Exchange Coordinator or
Privacy Act Coordinator to determine if we have a data exchange in effect with a particular
State agency for its administration of LIHEAP. For more information regarding the
handling of requests from State and local agencies, see GN 03314.001E.
