SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.312 - Coding Antigua and Barbuda Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/05/2021 - Present | TN 268 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402312 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Antigua and Barbuda has a prefix of “74” at the beginning of
the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “D” assigned for Antigua and Barbuda.
