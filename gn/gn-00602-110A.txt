A. INTRODUCTION
The Social Security Act precludes compensating an individual or entity out of benefits
for serving as representative payee; that is, compensating a payee for their time
and effort or administrative expenses. The Act provides an exception and permits a
qualified organization, authorized by SSA, to collect a fee from a beneficiary for
expenses (including overhead) incurred by the organization in providing services performed
as the beneficiary's representative payee. (See GN 00506.001 for Payment for Representative Payee Services)
