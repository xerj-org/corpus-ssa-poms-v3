A. Procedure fugitive felon processing FAX instructions
Complete FAX requests for fugitive felon suspension cases as outlined in the following
instructions.. Appropriate PSC FAX numbers appear on the form. Make sure that all
entries are legible.
1. Explanation of entries
•
CAN: Show the wage earner's SSN.
•
BIC: Provide the beneficiary identification code for the individual subject to suspension.
•
BOAN: This is the beneficiary's own social security number.
•
NAME: Show the first name, middle initial, and last name of the individual subject to suspension.
•
DATE OF SUSPENSE: Show the first month for which suspension is appropriate in MM/YY format.
•
SUSPENSE TYPE: Suspension code S9 with an RFST of FUGFEL.
•
NOTICE: the FO should send advance notification whenever required; i.e.,
all suspensions EXCEPT: first-party reports, beneficiary in non-pay status and closed
period of suspension, require advance notification. Enter the date you sent the due
process notice, and check the due process period or check that no due process notice
is required as appropriate.
•
EXCLUSION CATEGORY: Check the appropriate exclusion category. Use the OTHER category only when directed
in the "EXCEPTION" below or after consultation with your Field Operations Fugitive
Felon Coordinator (FOFFC) or Regional Systems Staff. If OTHER is checked, explain
the reason in the REMARKS section of the form.
2. Field operations fugitive felon coordinator (FOFFC) and payment service center (PSC)
contact information
a. Field operations fugitive felon coordinators (FOFFCs)
Direct policy questions for Title II fugitive felon suspensions to vHelp. See GN 02613.990 for the list of FOFFC.
b. PSC contact information
See GN 02613.995 for the telephone numbers for contacting the PSC about fugitive issues.
