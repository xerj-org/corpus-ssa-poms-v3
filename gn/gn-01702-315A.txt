A. Introduction
When an applicant files a claim for U.S. benefits and alleges coverage in a Totalization
country, the modernized claim system (MCS) will produce a notice (award or disallowance)
containing language that explains the decision on the Totalization aspects of the
claim. The following subsections explain the actions necessary to produce a notice,
the type of notice that is generated and what information is provided on the notice
relative to the Totalization aspects of the claim.
