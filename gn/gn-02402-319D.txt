D. Overview of IDD for Cyprus
IDD payments sent to Cyprus must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The data for coding
IDD for Cyprus will come from the International Bank Account Number (IBAN) provided
to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN will be 28 characters (see Section
F). Technicians will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD
request.
