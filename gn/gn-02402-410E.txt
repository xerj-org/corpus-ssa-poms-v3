E. Description of Georgia IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Georgia comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The full length of the IBAN for
Georgia consists of 22 characters. However, the bank data on the MBR consists of 27
characters including the country code “X” designated for Georgia.
