SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed in Portugal
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to prepare a claims package.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Prepare a claims package if a Portuguese coverage certification is required and has
not been requested by the FSP in Lisbon.
•
Include Form SSA-2960-U3-PE in the package.
2. U.S. and Portuguese or Portuguese Claim Only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
the Portuguese coverage certification is required and has not been requested by the
FSP; or
•
the Portuguese agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-PE; and
•
information or documentation requested by the Portuguese agency.
