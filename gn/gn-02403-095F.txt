F. Procedure - Action to Take When SSA-1395-BK Is Determined To Be Lost
If an FO determines a receipt book cannot be located, take the following steps:
1.
Annotate the Master Control Roster and the Receipt Book Log with the date that the
receipt book was discovered missing.
2.
Attach a write-up of the attempts taken to locate the receipt book.
