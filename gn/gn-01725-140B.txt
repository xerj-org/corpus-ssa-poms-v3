SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.140 - Exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision Under the Dutch Agreement
Effective Dates: 02/23/1999 - Present | TN 1 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
alleges residence in the Netherlands, develop residence status to verify that the
exemption provided by the agreement applies.
