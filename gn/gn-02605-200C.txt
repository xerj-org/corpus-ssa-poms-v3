C. Procedure for change of address from domestic to foreign
1. Instructions for entering the change of address from domestic to foreign
Use the Post Entitlement System (POS) Address/Direct Deposit/Phone (PEAD) screen for
current post-entitlement (PE) changes of address, direct deposit, and telephone information.
The following screens may come into the PEAD path if the beneficiary is not a U.S.
citizen:
•
PELF (Alien Tax Update) - MS 06303.014;
•
NUSC (Non U.S. Citizen Coding) - MS 00705.026;
•
LEUS (Left U.S./Outside of US) - MS 00705.024;
•
DPRT (Deportation) if the beneficiary was removed (deported) - MS 00705.019;
•
BARD (Barred Country) if the beneficiary moves to a barred or restricted country -
MS 00705.006.
Use future POS Address/Direct Deposit/Phone (PEAF) to enter future payment address,
direct deposit and telephone changes up to three months in the future.
2. Instructions for providing publications and collecting forms for change of address
from domestic to foreign for a beneficiary who is not a U.S. citizen
•
Provide the SSA Publication 05-10137, Your Payments While You are
Outside the United States.
•
Secure Form SSA-21, Supplement to Claim of Person Outside the United States. For information about the SSA-21, see GN 00205.180.
3. Instructions for providing publications and collecting forms for change of address
from domestic to foreign for a beneficiary who is a U.S. citizen
•
Provide SSA Publication 05-10137, Your Payments While You are Outside the United
States.
•
If the beneficiary is under full retirement age, provide Form SSA-7163, Questionnaire
About Employment or Self-Employment Outside the United States.
•
If the beneficiary is approaching an enrollment period for any Medicare program, provide SSA
Publication 05-10043, Medicare and CMS 11037, Medicare Coverage
Outside the United States, available at www.medicare.gov/publications.
•
If the beneficiary is already enrolled in Medicare, provide SMI cancellation Form
CMS-1763, Request for Cancellation of Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance). For the form see HI 00820.901C.
For additional references with information about Medicare benefits outside the United
States see GN 02605.200E.
Note:
Beneficiaries cannot use most Medicare benefits outside the United States.
