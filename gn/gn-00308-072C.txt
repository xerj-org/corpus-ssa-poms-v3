C. PA DVR branch offices
You may apply in person at one of the following offices. Same-day processing varies
by office. Office hours are 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Monday through Friday, excluding
State holidays. The DVR requires valid government-issued photo identification.
Make payment for walk-in services with a check or money order only ($10.00 per certified
copy of birth and $9.00 per certified copy of death). The DVR may waive this fee for
individuals who served or are currently serving in the Armed Forces or their dependents.
