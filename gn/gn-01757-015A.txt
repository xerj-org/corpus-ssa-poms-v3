A. The General Regime of Social Security (RGPS)
The General Regime of Social Security (RGPS) is a basic contributory program that
covers residents of Brazil. Brazil pays RGPS benefits in amounts that it bases primarily
on the number of years of contribution and amount of lifetime earnings.
