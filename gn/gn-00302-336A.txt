SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.336 - Evidence of Survivor Status - Residence in Certain Countries
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302336 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
If a claimant meets the definition of a Holocaust survivor (see GN 00302.327) and can provide proof that he/she resided in a Nazi controlled country during specified
times, this will be sufficient evidence of Holocaust survivor status (see GN 00302.335B).
