B. Documenting the Fee Agreement Determination
1. Initial and Reconsideration Levels
At the initial and reconsideration level, document the fee agreement determination
on an SSA-553 or report of contact (e.g., RPOC). See GN
03905.065B.1 for sample language.
2. Hearing and AC Levels
The decision maker documents the fee agreement determination on a Form HA-L15 (Fee
Agreement Approval) at the hearing level, a Determination of Fee Agreement in the
Appeals Council Decision, or other fee agreement determination document.
