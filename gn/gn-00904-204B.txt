B. Procedure for managing inquiries from individuals abroad
1. Procedure for making telephone or direct mail contact
Do not make telephone or direct mail contact with any individuals abroad except claimants
or beneficiaries (including their appointed representatives and representative payees).
Unless specifically instructed to do so, do not telephone or write directly to foreign
employers, churches, civil authorities, etc.
