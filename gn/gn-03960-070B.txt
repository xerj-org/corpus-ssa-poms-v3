SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.070 - Administrative Review Notice Preparation - Fee Agreement Process
Effective Dates: 05/16/2011 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Requesting the Letter(s)
Prepare requests for letter(s) as follows:
a.
Make requests on Form SSA-573 (Special Typing Instructions) or SSA-559 (Transmittal
Slip for Claims Folders).
b.
Prepare dictated letters patterned after the model letters contained in GN 03960.075 (acknowledgment), GN 03960.080 (interim), and GN 03960.085 (final).
2. Typing the Letter(s)
Prepare letter(s) as follows:
a.
Paper
Use the Form SSA-L2000-C2 (Universal Notice) or SSA letterhead, showing the return
address.
b.
Enclosure Paragraphs
Place “Enclosure” or “Enclosures” at the left margin on the first page.
If the letter is:
•
a single page, place “Enclosure(s)” two lines below the title of the signatory; or
•
more than one page, place “Enclosure(s)” two lines below the text.
c.
Copies
Handle copies as follows:
•
Produce an extra copy or copies to send notice to the other party or parties and the
FO (in concurrent titles II and XVI or title XVI only claims).
•
Return a file box copy for review by the employee who requested the letter before
placing it in the claim(s) file.
•
Place a copy of each letter sent in the claim(s) file.
