B. Procedure -sending requests
Follow this procedure for requesting Spanish application forms.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Send the claimant a form E/USA 2 with a preaddressed return envelope.
•
Annotate the claims folder to show that form E/USA 2 has been sent to the claimant.
2. U.S. and Spanish or Spanish Claim Only
•
Send the claimant a form E/USA with a preaddressed return envelope.
•
Annotate the claims folder to show that form E/USA has been sent to the claimant.
