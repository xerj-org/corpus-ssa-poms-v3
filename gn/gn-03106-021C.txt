C. DESCRIPTION OF COMPONENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The component that has the information necessary to respond to the order/subpoena:
•
Prepares a document(s) containing the information that responds to the order/subpoena.
(See GN 03106.099, Exhibit B for an example.)
•
Insures that the Certification Seal is affixed. If the Certification Seal cannot be
affixed, contact OALC. See GN
03106.020E. for a hyperlink to the OALC website for contacts and telephone numbers).
•
Forwards the documents to OALC.
