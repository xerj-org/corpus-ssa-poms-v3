B. Procedure
In order to respond to Form N/USA 3:
•
Obtain the requested information.
•
Obtain a consent statement, if no claim has been filed under the Agreement.
NOTE: Norway has agreed to provide a consent statement (N/USA 7) when they need information
about a person who resides in Norway and has not filed a claim under the Agreement.
•
Complete Form SSA-2960-U2-NE, showing the information being sent.
