C. Requests for medical evidence from the records of SSA
1.
If there is an RSDI folder in OIO, obtain and review the folder to determine whether
or not “pertinent medical evidence” is present.
a.
If “pertinent medical evidence” is present, BETs photocopy it and route the photocopies to the foreign agency via
a Form SSA-2960.
b.
If pertinent evidence is not in the folder, inform the agency via a Form SSA-2960.
2.
If there is no RSDI folder in OIO, inform the agency with an appropriate remark via
a Form SSA-2960.
