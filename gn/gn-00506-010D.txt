D. Form SSA-445
Use the following chart to determine what information is expected in response to specific
questions on the form SSA-445 and how to evaluate those answers.
QUESTION
EXPLANATION
1
The organization’s full name (abbreviations acceptable).
2
The organization’s Employer Identification Number.
3
See GN 00506.100B. (Determines whether the organization is State/Local government agency or Community
based, non-profit social service agency. Community based non-profit orgs. require
bonding and licensing).
4
Address of the organization. If the org. uses a different address for mail, then list
both.
5
City, State, Zip Code & Telephone number of organization.
6
All non-governmental agencies must complete this section. This will supply information about the license the organization
holds and the State in which issued. Licensing information should be current. (If
information appears questionable refer to GN 00506.110B.4.).
7
All non-governmental agencies must complete this section. This will supply information about the bond/insurance company,
its name, address and telephone number; the bond/policy type (e.g., Surety Bond, Commercial
Coverage or Fidelity Bond); expiration date (future dates only); serial/policy number
and; the full amount of the bond coverage. (If information appears questionable refer
to GN 00506.110B.4.).
8
This may help in assessing the applicant’s ability to handle additional clients, if
ever needed.
9
This may help in determining the suitability of the payee. A “Yes” response must be
fully investigated, as they are not yet authorized to collect a fee.
10
This may provide the client-payee ratio and whether there is sufficient staff to handle
our beneficiaries.
11
This may help in assessing the applicant’s ability to handle additional clients, if
ever needed.

Helps to determine if the payee is a Creditor. See GN 00506.100B.3.b.
