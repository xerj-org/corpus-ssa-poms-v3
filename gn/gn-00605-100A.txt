SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.100 - When a Payee Evaluation Report (SSA-624-F5) Is Required
Effective Dates: 10/29/2001 - Present | TN 16 (10-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
The FO must conduct face-to-face interviews and complete an SSA-624-F5 when:
•
A change in custody has taken place.
•
The payee gave unacceptable responses on the accounting report which cannot be resolved
(GN 00605.065 through GN 00605.068).
•
No response is received after two requests for accounting (GN 00605.085).
EXCEPTION: In limited nonresponse situations, telephone contact is sufficient to document a Report of Contact
(SSA-5002). (See GN 00605.085B., Step 3, for specific situations).
