SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.110 - Automatically Convincing Evidence of Age if No Material Discrepancy in File
Effective Dates: 09/30/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Exception to consistency rule
Certain discrepant evidence may be ignored (see GN 00302.180). In those special situations, the discrepant document does not reduce the probative
value of the automatically convincing evidence.
In all other situations where a discrepancy exists, the documents in the convincing
evidence lists are no longer of any greater reliability than any other document and
must be individually evaluated (see GN
00302.165).
