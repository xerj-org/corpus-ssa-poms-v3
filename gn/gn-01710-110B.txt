SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.110 - How to Credit Swiss Coverage
Effective Dates: 06/25/1996 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Take these actions to assign Swiss coverage.
1. Determining the amount of coverage required
If the claimant is filing for disability benefits on the record, credit all possible
F quarters of coverage to the ER.
If the claimant is not filing for disability benefits on the record, credit only the
number of quarters required for insured status.
2. Determining maximum FQCs
Determine the maximum possible number of FQCs by dividing the number of months of
coverage in each year by 3 (the number of months in a calendar quarter) to determine
the maximum possible number of FQCs for that year. Any remainder is considered 1 QC.
3. Assigning FQCs
Assign FQCs as follows:
•
Assign FQCs for each year shown on the Swiss coverage certification starting with
the first calendar quarter of the year and continuing consecutively until all FQCs
are assigned.
•
Do not assign an FQC in a calendar quarter where a U.S. QC is already assigned. When
a U.S. QC is assigned within a Swiss period, credit FQCs up to that quarter and begin
again in the next available quarter.
•
For years after 1977, assign existing U.S. QCs using the flexible crediting provision
so as to permit assigning the maximum number of FQCs. (See RS 00301.230 regarding the assignment of QCs.)
4. Insured Status After Assigning Swiss Coverage
If the worker is insured for U.S. totalization benefits after assigning Swiss coverage,
process the award (assuming all other entitlement factors are met).
5. Claim for disability or currently insured survivor benefits
If the claim is for disability benefits or for survivor benefits which could be paid
based on currently insured status, recredit coverage per GN 01710.110B.3. However, assign FQCs starting with the last available calendar quarter of each period and
continuing backwards consecutively until all FQCs are assigned.
Note: For years which contain 12 months of Swiss coverage and no coinciding U.S. QCs this
step is not necessary.
6. Insured after crediting coverage
If the worker is insured for U.S. totalization benefits after crediting coverage,
, process the award (assuming all other entitlement factors are met).
If the worker is not insured for U.S. totalization benfits after crediting coverage, deny the claim.
