SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Chile
Effective Dates: 01/17/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure – U.S. and Chilean or Chilean claim only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-2960-CHI. Request a Chilean coverage record, if one is needed.
•
The first page of Form SSA-2490-BK (Work History)
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from SSA's files, if the Chilean claim is for disability benefits.
Also include a copy of Form SSA-831 to provide the U.S. disability decision if a decision
has been made.
Note: SSA files include claims folders in DIO, a PSC, a FO, OCO's Megasite or the caves
in Kansas City. However, it does not include folders in ODAR.
•
Evidence submitted for the Chilean claim
•
A request for the Chilean coverage record, if one is needed.
