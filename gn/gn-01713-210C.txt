C. Policy — Using evidence submitted in claim for U.S. benefits
Evidence that is used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits may also be used by Belgium to establish the same factor for Belgian benefits.
Consequently, it is unnecessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence to
document a factor of entitlement for Belgian benefits when that factor has already
been verified for SSA purposes.
