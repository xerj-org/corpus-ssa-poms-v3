A. Types of Uruguayan social security benefits
This section describes old-age retirement, disability, and survivors benefits. These
are the only Uruguayan benefits included within the scope of the agreement. This information corresponds to the second and third pillars of the Uruguayan social
security system discussed in GN 01760.010.
