B. Sample notice
IMPORTANT NOTICE
IF YOU WANT TO OBTAIN A PRIVATE ATTORNEY to represent you and you need assistance in obtaining an attorney's services, you
may contact one of the organizations shown below. They can refer you to a private
attorney who is familiar with representing claimants before the Social Security Administration.
Some private attorneys may be willing to take your case under an arrangement in which
he or she will not charge a fee unless your claim is allowed. The attorney must obtain
the Social Security Administration's authorization for any fee he or she wants to
charge and collect.
These organizations have indicated that they want to be included on this list. The Social Security Administration has made no attempt to evaluate their
services.
