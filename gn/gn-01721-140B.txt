Effective Dates: 04/22/2004 - Present | TN 3 (04-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
alleges residence in Spain, develop residence status to verify that the exemption
provided by the agreement applies.
