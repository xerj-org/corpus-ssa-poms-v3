B. Crediting Belgian coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. QC for every three months or remaining fraction of Belgian
coverage in a calendar year. (Belgian coverage is measured in months.) However, no
credit will be given for:
1.
any month in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC, or
2.
more than four QC's for any calendar year.
