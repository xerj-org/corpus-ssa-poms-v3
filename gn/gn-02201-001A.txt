SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02201.001 - What is a Title II Overpayment
Effective Dates: 10/13/2023 - Present | TN 72 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202201001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What is an overpayment
An overpayment is the amount we paid to an individual that was more than the amount
we should have paid the individual. An overpayment may result from the failure to:
•
Impose deductions;
•
Suspend benefits;
•
Reduce benefits; or
•
Terminate benefits.
An overpayment may also result from technician error. For example, Jessica James primary
insurance amount (PIA) is $525. When we adjudicated their award, we coded their monthly
benefit amount (MBA) as $552. Therefore, we paid Ms. James more than the amount we
should have paid them.
Once we determine that we overpaid an individual, the overpaid amount becomes a debt
owed to the United States Government.
