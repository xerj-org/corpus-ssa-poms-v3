B. Background for using the website
This website lists all beneficiaries chosen for MNUP. Use the MNUP website to document all contacts with the selected beneficiaries. Once you enter the interview
information into the MNUP website , it maintains historical summary level data. The
case remains on the list, but appears as complete. The worksheets are also located
on this website.
For more information on the worksheets, see GN 02602.660.
