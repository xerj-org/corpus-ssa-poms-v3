SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.335 - Evidence of Holocaust Survivor Status
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302335 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Rule
When a claimant alleges a DB which is discrepant with the evidence of age submitted,
and states that he/she is a survivor of the Holocaust who changed the DB in order
to survive, ask the claimant to submit evidence of survivor status. Such evidence
will constitute proof of survivor status for individuals who meet the definition of
Holocaust survivor in GN 00302.327.
