SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02403.161 - Social Security Electronic Remittance System (SERS) for Programmatic and Conserved Funds Remittances
Effective Dates: 11/18/2024 - Present | TN 60 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202403161 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. How to determine which store to use
1.
Check the SERS Programmatic store for debts or conserved funds. To process a remittance in the SERS Programmatic
store, an overpayment or debt must exist on the Master Beneficiary record (MBR) or
Supplemental Security Record (SSR). If there is an established overpayment or debt
on the MBR, SSR, Recovery of Overpayments, Accounting, and Reporting (ROAR), or External
Collection Operation (ECO), SERS displays the overpayment or debt.
2.
If there is no established overpayment or debt on the MBR, SSR, ROAR or ECO, SERS
will not display the overpayment or debt. If SERS does not identify and display overpayments
or debts, follow the current procedures in GN 02403.001 through GN 02403.160, to process the non-Conserved Funds remittance through the Debt Management System
(DMS). If this is a Conserved Funds remittance with no associated debt on the record,
go to step 3.
3.
If this is a Conserved Funds remittance and no debt is found in the SERS Programmatic
store, the Conserved Funds remittance should be entered in the SERS Conserved Funds
store.
