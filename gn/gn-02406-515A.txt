SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02406.515 - Processing Nonreceipt Reports with Foreign Involvement
Effective Dates: 03/28/2000 - Present | TN 55 (04-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406515 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Guidelines for processing Title II nonreceipt reports with foreign involvement
Enter nonreceipt reports into the Post Entitlement Online System (POS) data input
screen. For more information on the POS data input screen, see SM 00609.210. For a
list if exclusions to the nonreceipt process, see GN 02406.175.
1. Recurring Title II payments
If a beneficiary alleges nonreceipt of a regular payment check, input via POS for
transmission to the Department of the Treasury.
