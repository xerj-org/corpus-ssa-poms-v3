SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00602.090 - Exchange of Information with VA
Effective Dates: 04/21/2023 - Present | TN 26 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200602090 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. POLICY
To aid VA in assuring that no overpayment of VA payments occurs, we have agreed to
supply appropriate VA officials, upon request, information as to the amount of Social
Security benefit savings reported by the representative payee on the most recent accounting
report.
