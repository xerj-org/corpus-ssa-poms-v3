SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.012 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed With a Spanish Agency
Effective Dates: 03/22/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following procedure for developing the claim.
1. Claimant is not already entitled
If the claimant is not already entitled to U.S. benefits, obtain a statement that
the claimant does not want to file, or develop as follows:
•
Obtain the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Obtain form SSA-2490 (Part I) if the claimant will not be insured with U.S. coverage.
After development, forward the statement or claim to OIO.
2. Claimant is already entitled
If the claimant is already entitled to U.S. benefits, forward form E/USA 2 to OIO.
Request OIO to close out the protective filing.
