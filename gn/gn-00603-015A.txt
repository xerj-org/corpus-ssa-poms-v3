SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00603.015 - Foreign Bank Account Legends
Effective Dates: 08/11/2023 - Present | TN 14 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200603015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Funds not needed for the day-to-day expenses of a beneficiary living outside of the
United States (U.S.) must be conserved for their future use by the representative
payee. The funds may be deposited in a bank account. That account must show that the
payee has only a fiduciary interest in the funds. For more information, see GN 02402.055.
