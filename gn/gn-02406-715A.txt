A. Process for mass loss non-receipts
When mass loss non-receipts are input, the system reads the inputs as non-receipt
type “A.” The system then:
•
Sends a B-stop to Treasury, so that Treasury can issue a courtesy disbursement check;
and
•
Posts an “A-stop” on PHUS for title II or the Treasury Status Query for title XVI.
