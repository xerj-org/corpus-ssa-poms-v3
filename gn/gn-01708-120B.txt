SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.120 - How to Determine Months of German Coverage
Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - Years with one period of coverage
Determine the number of months of coverage as follows:
•
If there is no entry in the months column, consider the “from - to” period to be continuous with coverage for all of the months.
•
If the number of months is less than or equal to the actual months in the “from - to” period, accept the number of months entry.
•
If the number of months is greater than the actual months in the “from - to” period, refer to GN 01708.110B.5.
