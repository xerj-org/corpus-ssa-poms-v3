B. Procedure - State sources

Department of Public Health
Center for Health Statistics

Montgomery, AL 36103-5625
FO Code 623
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from Vital Records . In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
$15.00
3. Type of Payment:
Money order, certified check or personal check
4. Remarks:
The FO should write directly to BVS for all records. The fee is $15.00 each and $6.00
for each additional copy; use SSA-L706 to request. Make check payable to Alabama State
Board of Health. Send requests for expedited search by overnight express mail service
and add $15.00 to fee.
Can FO verify, certify, or obtain free copy death record? No. Decatur General Hospital
will not issue certified hospital birth records beginning March, 1997.
