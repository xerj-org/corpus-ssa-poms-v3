B. Procedure for disability referrals
Refer the folder to the Disability Examiner (DE) in the appropriate program service
center (PSC) after all other actions are taken, including the release of post adjudicative
notices.
Do not refer if the folder contains Form SSA-833-U3 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability),
or Form SSA-899-U2 (Continuing Disability Review) prepared within the previous 4 weeks
reflecting the change, or if a Childhood Disability Beneficiary (CDB) will attain
age 62 or a surviving spouse with disability will attain age 60 by the time all other
actions are completed.
