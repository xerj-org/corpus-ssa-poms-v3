GN 01729.120 U.S. Totalization Benefits under the U.S. - Austrian Agreement
Under the agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least six U.S. quarters of coverage
(QCs) but not enough to receive regular U.S. benefits, the United States can count
the NH's QCs under the Austrian social security system to permit the NH (with any
dependents or survivors) to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
