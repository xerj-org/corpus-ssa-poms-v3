SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01716.315 - Telephone Communication With the Canadian Liaison Agencies
Effective Dates: 01/26/2015 - Present | TN 1 (04-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201716315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
OIO technicians use telephone communication with Canada to:
•
request coverage and other information from Ottawa or Quebec records,
•
request the status of pending claims or development requests, and
•
clarify development requests received from Ottawa/Quebec.
