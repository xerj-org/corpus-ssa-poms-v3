B. Procedure to apply the exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision
Develop citizenship status to verify that the exception provided by Brazilian citizenship
applies if a claimant alleges citizenship of Brazil and does not meet another exception
to the ANP as described in RS 02610.010.
For more information about exceptions under the U.S. and Brazilian agreement regarding
additional U.S. residency requirements for alien dependents and survivors, see GN 01757.145.
