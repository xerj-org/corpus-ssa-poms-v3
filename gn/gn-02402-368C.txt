SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.368 - Coding San Marino Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/16/2024 - Present | TN 251 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402368 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in San Marino has a prefix of "71" at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “C” assigned for San Marino.
