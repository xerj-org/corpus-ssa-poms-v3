SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.370 - Title XVI and Concurrent Handling of Conserved Funds When a Payee Changes
Effective Dates: 06/26/2023 - Present | TN 71 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605370 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A former payee is generally required to return any conserved funds to SSA for recertification
to the new payee or to the beneficiary when the beneficiary becomes their own payee.
NOTE: In some rare instances, the former payee may be permitted to continue administering
conserved benefits. This arrangement is allowed only when it serves the best interests
of the beneficiary. For instructions on allowing the former payee to administer conserved
funds, see GN 00603.130.
