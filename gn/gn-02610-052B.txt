SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.052 - How To Process Title II Offset Cases When An Attorney Fee Is Involved - Determinations Made Before July 1, 1991
Effective Dates: 08/29/1995 - Present | TN 16 (09-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610052 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the applicable table below:
1. Case A
For Case A the PC will:
STEP
ACTION
1
Deduct the title II offset from the retroactive title II benefits in the offset period.
2
Multiply the result of Step 1 by 25 percent.
​
RESULT: This is the amount to withhold toward the AF.
2. Case B
For Case B the PC will:
STEP
ACTION
1
Determine the title II benefits due in the AF period only (title II MOE up through
the month before the month of adjudication of the title II claim).
2
Compute the title II offset amount for the AF period by adding all WSO and WFO amounts
from WCMP segment of SSI2, starting with the offset start date (WSD) and ending with
the last month of the AF withholding period plus 3 months.
​
If the last 2 months of the offset period reflect zero offset in WCMP segment of SSI2,
use all title II offset amounts from the WSD through the last month of the AF period
plus one month in this step.
3
Subtract results of Step 2 from Step 1.
​
RESULT: Title II payable in AF period.
4
Multiply results of Step 3 by 25 percent.
​
RESULT: Amount to withhold towards AF.
​
If the case requires a manual offset computation, advise the FO that an attorney is
involved and the FO will provide the offset amount for the AF period with the offset
computation.
