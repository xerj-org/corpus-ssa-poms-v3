A. Background on resolving policy and legal issues
The Social Security Act as amended, Regulations, Social Security Rulings (SSR), and
the POMS provide the basic guides for adjudication and review of Social Security claims.
Policy and legal issues that arise in the adjudication process are usually resolved
by reference to POMS guidelines. Issues not addressed in POMS are resolved through
use of SSA's precedent process or SSA's policy resolution process.
