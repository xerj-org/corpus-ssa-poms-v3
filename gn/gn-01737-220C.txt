Effective Dates: 02/20/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
If an applicant brings a Greek application form to the FO:
•
provide any necessary assistance in completing the form, and
•
return the application(s) to the applicant and advise him or her to return it directly
to Greece at the address shown on the application form.
Reminder: Do not mail the forms to OIO.
