SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.441 - Coding Brunei Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/28/2019 - Present | TN 164 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402441 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Brunei will have a prefix of “71 ” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “ Y ” assigned for Brunei .
