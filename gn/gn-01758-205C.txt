C. Procedure – U.S. Claims Brazilian totalization benefits
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 to request a Brazilian certified coverage record if needed,
to certify the filing date for Brazilian benefits, establish factors of entitlement,
and U.S. benefits information;
•
U.S. totalization earnings record;
•
Medical evidence from SSA's files, if the claim is for disability benefits; and,
•
Evidence submitted for non-disability claim.
