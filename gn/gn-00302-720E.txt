E. Example 3
In the same situation as Example 2, the claimant actually presents a 1930 census record
at the initial interview which indicates a DB of 2/2/22. Given the one-year variance
in some 1930 census records (see GN 00302.730), the consistency of the other proofs of relatively high probative value, and the
claimant's explanation of the discrepant DB shown on the SS-5, it is reasonable to
determine 2/2/21 as the DB.
