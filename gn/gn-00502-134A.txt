SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.134 - Payee Applicant Has Poor Past Performance
Effective Dates: 05/23/2023 - Present | TN 84 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502134 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
History of past poor performance as a payee raises serious doubt about a person's
ability to perform suitably as payee. While poor performance is not an absolute bar
to serving as payee, you should not appoint a person with poor past performance unless
you determine there is some compelling reason for doing so. On those rare occasions
when you make such an appointment, you must fully document the reasons for your decision.
