A. Policy for applying ANP for workers
Section 202(t)(1) of the Social Security Act (ANP) generally provides that the Social
Security Administration (SSA) does not pay benefits to a beneficiary who:
•
Is not a U.S. citizen; and
•
Has been outside the United States for six consecutive calendar months.
Swiss citizen number holders (NHs) were already exempt from the ANP prior to the effective
date of this Agreement, as explained in RS 02610.015. Under the Agreement, non-U.S. citizen beneficiaries who are residents of Switzerland
are exempt from the ANP if they are Swiss citizens, refugees or stateless persons,
or if they are family members of Swiss citizens, refugees or stateless persons.
