F. Estate requests a waiver
Although waiver of recovery of an overpayment from an estate is possible under other
provisions, recovery from an estate can never defeat the purpose because an estate
does not have ordinary and necessary living expenses. If the estate has been closed
and the distributee(s) request a waiver, the waiver may be approved under the defeats
the purpose provision. For more information on closed estates, refer to GN 02215.070.
