SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.338 - Evidence of Survivor Status - Information From West German Diplomatic Office
Effective Dates: 04/23/2001 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302338 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Information may be available from the consulate which served the area in which the
claimant lived if the claimant ever
•
Filed for West German Social Insurance Benefits, or
•
Filed for indemnity payment, or
•
Was asked to testify about atrocities committed by the Nazis.
