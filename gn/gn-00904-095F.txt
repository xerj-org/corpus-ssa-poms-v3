F. Requests for medical evidence
Enclose an authorization for release of medical information signed by the claimant.
In DAA cases, the medical release should also be signed on the reverse of the authorization
form. Complete the identifying information section of the appropriate medical report
form and enclose this with the SSA-562-U3. Be sure the date of onset is entered at
the top of the form.
