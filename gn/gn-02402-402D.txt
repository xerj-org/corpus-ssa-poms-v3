D. Overview of IDD for Suriname
IDD payments sent to Suriname must be in Suriname dollar SRD accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Suriname. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
