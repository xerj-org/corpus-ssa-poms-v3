Effective Dates: 03/25/2014 - Present | TN 65 (08-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406805 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Reinstatements
If you receive a nonreceipt of payment input alert 'RAILROAD CASE' or you determine
from the claimant interview the involvement of RRB benefit certification and the beneficiary previously reported a reinstatement event, examine the MBR. If the
MBR reflects:
1.
Nonreceipt for known reasons (e.g., work suspension with LAF S2), and
2.
Indicates these benefits should resume, then
3.
Send an MDW referral to the PC.
NOTE: If nonreceipt of payment exclusion exists, see GN 02406.175. The Office of the General Counsel (OGC) opinion prevents RRB from making determinations
with SSA post-adjudicative issues.
