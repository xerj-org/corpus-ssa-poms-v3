A. Forms to complete for Slovenian Benefits
An applicant for Slovenian benefits must complete one or more special Slovenian application
forms, in addition to Form SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S. International
Agreement). The form(s) will vary depending on the type of benefit.
