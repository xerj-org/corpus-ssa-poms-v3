SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01762.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO), Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed Outside of Uruguay
Effective Dates: 04/30/2020 - Present |

B. Procedure – Claims for U.S. benefits
Prepare Form SSA e2960 USA/UY 3 (U.S. - Uruguay Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
and forward to the Uruguayan liaison agency if the Uruguayan certified coverage record
is not in the claims file.
