F. Example
Title II aged claimant, Franz Deuringer, submits a family bible with entries written
in German as proof of age for a domestic RIB claim. The CR is a certified German translator.
The document shows that the claimant was born on 4/3/33. The claimant's DOB allegation
on the APPL screen in MCS is 4/2/33. (A personal letter was also presented. A second
Evidence Screen can be used to code information concerning the letter.)
