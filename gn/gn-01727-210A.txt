A. Evidence requirements for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Portuguese Agreement
When a person files a claim for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Agreement with
Portugal, that person must submit the same evidence required to file a claim for regular
retirement, survivors, or disability insurance (non-Totalization) benefits. The applicant
must also submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States. The following subsections
show the types of evidence that the Social Security Administration (SSA) may use to
support claims for benefits.
