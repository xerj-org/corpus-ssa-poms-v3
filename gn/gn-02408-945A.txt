SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.945 - Partial Recovery or No Recovery of International Direct Deposit (IDD) Payments
Effective Dates: 02/08/2016 - Present | TN 89 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408945 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process for the Federal Reserve Bank of New York (FRBNY) after partial recovery
If the reclamation request results in a partial recovery, FRBNY compares the returned
payments with the amounts sent to the beneficiaries. For payments that were converted
to local currency and returned in local currency, FRBNY converts the amounts to U.S.
dollars. If this is a partial payment return, FRBNY uses a pseudo trace number to
return the amount and notifies the Office of Earnings and International Operations
(OEIO) of the return. FRBNY forwards all returned payments and partial payments to
SSA through the Department of the Treasury (Treasury).
