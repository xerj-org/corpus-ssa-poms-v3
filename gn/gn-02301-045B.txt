SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02301.045 - Handling Inquiries for Title II Underpayment Due Deceased Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 09/14/2017 - Present | TN 3 (09-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202301045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Inquiry when underpayment may be due
If the FO, PC, or N8NN agent receiving the inquiry knows an underpayment is due, explain
how and on what basis we may issue the underpayment. If the inquirer wants to file
for the underpayment, take the request and any necessary proof. Follow instructions
for the order of priority and taking the application in GN 02301.030 and GN 02301.050.
