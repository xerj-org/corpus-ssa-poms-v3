A. Introduction
Many claims filed under the Canadian agreement in the United States are filed in field
offices (FO’s) with service areas near the Canadian border or with a substantial Canadian
population. To provide quicker service, these offices (referred to as “designated
border offices”) perform some of the claims-related liaison functions with Canadian
agencies that would normally be handled by the Office of International Operations
(OIO).
