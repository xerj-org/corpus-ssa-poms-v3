A. Process
1. Obtaining Verification of Birth, Death, or Marriage Information
To obtain verification of birth information, send the request via e562 to the Hartford
Field Office (FO) (080), which is parallel to the State Vital Records Section in Hartford
CT. The Hartford FO obtains the information one day a week. There is no fee to SSA
for this service. Requests to local city and town clerks require no fee, either, but
the response time will be significantly longer.
NOTE:  The electronic 562 is available in Regions I through X and the Foreign Service Posts.
Click here
for instructions for the completion and use of the electronic E562.
2. Obtaining a Certified Record of Birth, Death, or Marriage
If you require an actual certified birth, death, or marriage document, you should
send the request to the Hartford FO (080). The Hartford FO will assist you with this
request, and the State Vital Records Section will issue the document. There is no
fee to SSA for this service. A fee is required for requests to local city and town
clerks.
3. Obtaining Divorce Information
Please note that divorce records are not covered by this agreement. These records
are available from the Clerk of the Superior Court where the divorce was granted.
The FO parallel to the court can verify the existence of a divorce record on Form
SSA-4385. Click here
for a list of CT court addresses.
