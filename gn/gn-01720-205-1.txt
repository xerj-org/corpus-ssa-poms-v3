SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside the U.K.
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Initial Claims Package
Prepare initial claims packages to request U.K. coverage records and to inform the
U.K. agencies about claims for U.K. benefits. (See GN 01719.205 - GN 01719.215 for the applications and the evidence required to prepare the packages.)
