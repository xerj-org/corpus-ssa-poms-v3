Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

L. Legal marital child
A "legal marital child," often referred to as a child "born in wedlock" or a "legitimate
child," is a child who is born of a valid marriage, including common-law (where recognized),
void, voidable, and same-sex marriages, and certain non-marital legal relationships
(NMLR).
NOTE: Refer all cases involving children born to NMLRs for a legal opinion (see GN 00306.013A).
