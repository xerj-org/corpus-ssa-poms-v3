NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from Nebraska Vital

2. Cost of Copy:
Birth $17.00 (additional copies -- $17.00), Death/marriage/divorce $16.00 (additional
copies -- $16.00)
3. Type of Payment:
Cash, check or money order. Shipping and handling fees apply for VISA, MasterCard,
American Express or Discover.
4. Remarks:
Make all requests directly to the Dept. of Health. Do not contact the parallel office.
Include SSA L-706, return envelope and check or third party draft. Requests processed
within 21 days.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No
Electronic request for birth certificate is available using VISA or MasterCard --
$17.00.
