SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.930 - Claimant is an Alien Who Arrived After 6/30/1924 — Sources of Immigration and Alien Registration Records
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302930 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Locations of records
Visas and birth records or other supporting evidence were required for all arrivals
after 6/30/1924. The INS district office having jurisdiction over immigration matters
at the alien's place of residence maintains the:
•
Visa (which contains month, day, and year of birth), and
•
Supporting evidence, and
•
Alien registration form.
