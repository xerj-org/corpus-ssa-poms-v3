SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.366 - WW II Military Service of Filipinos
Effective Dates: 04/17/1990 - Present | TN 7 (03-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905366 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. General
Usually, RS 01702.000 ff. is used to determine if military service is creditable for Social Security purposes.
However, experience has shown that there are some situations which confuse beneficiaries
and claimants, and background information is needed to clarify them for such individuals.
