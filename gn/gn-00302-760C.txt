C. SSA-1535-U3: GENERAL
Upon receipt of an SSA-1535-U3, the Bureau of the Census will search its records and
provide age information, billing SSA on a monthly basis.
This form is for internal use only and can not be given to claimants or their representatives.
EXCEPTION:  The form can be completed by a DO employee during a personal or telephone interview
with the claimant, and the form mailed to the claimant for signature.
