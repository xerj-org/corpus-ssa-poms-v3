GN 03316.115 Disclosure Without Consent Concerning Adverse Claims or Adverse Actions
These instructions below in GN 03316.115A.1. and GN 03316.115A.2. do not apply to hearing cases. The administrative law judge should decide what is
proper to disclose to parties who are or may be adversely affected. Field offices
(FO) or regional offices should consult the Office of Hearings Operations (OHO), Regional
Office (RO) for questions concerning hearing case disclosures.
