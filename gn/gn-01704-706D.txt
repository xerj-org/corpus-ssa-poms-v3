D. Certification section completed by an unauthorized person
The certification sections on the CAN/USA 1 and QUE/USA 1 are for use by DIO and designated
border DOs only. However, occasionally, these sections may be completed and signed
by unauthorized persons, usually nondesignated DO personnel. When this happens, carefully
check the entries. Then make the following entries using a different color ink. If
correct, add your initials to the “Verified by” block of the CAN/USA 1. DO NOT place an additional “X” in the “Verified” block of the QUE/USA 1. If an entry is incorrect, line it out, enter the correct
date and add your initials to the “Verified by” block on the CAN/USA 1. Place an additional “X” in the “Verified” block of the QUE/USA 1. Enter the current date, your signature, title and DIO in
the appropriate spaces. Affix the DIO stamp.
