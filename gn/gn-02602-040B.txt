B. Address and direct deposit verification for resumption or reinstatement of benefits
Verify the payment address and direct deposit information prior to resumption or reinstatement
of benefits if:
•
the report is received from a third party, or
•
the beneficiary has been in nonpayment status for 3 months or more.
