B. Policy for completing the application
1. Military service (MS) or railroad retirement (RR) service questions
It may be possible to get evidence of disability from the Railroad Retirement Board
(RRB) if the claimant filed a disability claim with RRB. Evidence may be available
from a service department or from a Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) facility
if the NH was in the armed forces. Both questions are leads to possible additional
quarters of coverage (QC).
2. Disclosing medical evidence
Most medical sources require authorization from the claimant before releasing information
to us.
3. Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) question
The WEP question applies to NHs entitled to a pension or an annuity from non-covered
employment, eligible for RIB or DIB after December 1985, and have a month of entitlement
prior to January 2024. For more information, see RS 00605.360.
