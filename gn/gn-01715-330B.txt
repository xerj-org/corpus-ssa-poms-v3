B. Policy - Transmittal form SSA-2960
Designated border offices must complete the appropriate transmittal form SSA-2960:
•
to route applications for Canadian benefits (the CDN-USA 1 or QUE/USA-1) and associated
material to the Canadian agencies in Ottawa or Quebec; or
•
if an applicant has filed for U.S. Totalization benefits and it is necessary to request
a Canadian coverage record from Ottawa or Quebec because the worker has at least 6
U.S. credits but not enough to be insured.
