D. Overview of IDD for Vanuatu
IDD payments sent to Vanuatu must be in Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Vanuatu. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
