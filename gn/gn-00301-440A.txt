A. Procedure for translating documents submitted as evidence of death
1. Information we require as evidence of death
When translating evidence of death, show at least the following information from the
document:
•
name of deceased,
•
date of death, and
•
place of death.
If material, show the age and marital status of the deceased, name of the surviving
spouse, and cause of death.
2. Information we require when a claimant submits circumstantial evidence of death
For information on circumstantial evidence of death, see GN
00304.025.
