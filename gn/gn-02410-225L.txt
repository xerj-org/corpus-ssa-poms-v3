L. Garnishment termination case number
When terminating a case number, follow “How to Terminate a Garnishment Case” in SM
00832.440. Always rename the case number by adding a “T” at the end of the Case ID.
If a case number already exists with a T at the end, add a numerical value corresponding
to the number of occurrences such as T2, T3, etc. The addition of a T, T2, T3, etc.,
by the technician will allow SSA to automatically process any new eIWO sent by the
agency upon receipt.
Refer to SM 00832.370 for processing a case number correction.
