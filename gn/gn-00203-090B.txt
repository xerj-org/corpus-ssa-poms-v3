B. SNAP benefits
The term “SNAP benefits” means the value of supplemental nutrition assistance provided
to a household by means of an Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT) under the Food and
Nutrition Act of 2008 or other means of provided assistance, as determined by the
United States Department of Agriculture (USDA).
