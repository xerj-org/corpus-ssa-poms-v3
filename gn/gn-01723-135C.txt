SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.135 - Voluntary Contributions to the French System
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. General Inquiry
Refer persons who want general information about French voluntary contributions to
the Social Security Center for Migrant Workers at the address in GN 01723.020C.
2. Coverage Record Needed
Advise individuals who need a record of their U.S. coverage to establish the right
to make voluntary contributions to complete Form SSA-7004 (Request for Earnings and
Benefit Estimate Statement) requesting a QC breakdown.
