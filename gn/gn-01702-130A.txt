SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.130 - Prospective Life of Applications for Foreign Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The Social Security Administration (SSA) will take an application and effectuate it
as an allowance up to 4 months before the applicant meets all the entitlement factors
(see GN 00204.007C.). However, some countries permit an application to be filed much further in advance
of the entitlement date, particularly if the applicant is living outside that country.
Further information concerning the prospective life of an application for a foreign
benefit, where available, is contained in the subchapter covering the specific agreement.
