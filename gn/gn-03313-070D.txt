D. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)
With a valid law enforcement request, we may disclose non-tax information to the FBI:
•
to investigate or prosecute fraud or other abuse in an SSA program;
•
to investigate fraud or abuse in income maintenance or health maintenance programs
in Native American Territories; or
•
in cases involving national security or threats to the lives of government officials.
For more information about disclosures for national security situations, see GN 03312.085.
