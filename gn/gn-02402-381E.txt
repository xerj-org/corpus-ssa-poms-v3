E. Description of Tunisia IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Tunisia comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The IBAN for Tunisia consists
of 24 characters. The bank data reflected on the MBR also consists of 24 characters
including Tunisia’s country code of F.
