D. Overview of IDD for Greece
IDD payments sent to Greece must be in Euro (EUR) accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Greece. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for the additional information.
