GN 01770.120 U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Agreement with Slovenia
Under the agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least six U.S. quarters of coverage
(QCs) but not enough to receive regular U.S. benefits, the United States can count the NH's QCs under the Slovenian
social security system to permit the NH to qualify for U.S. totalization benefits.
If the NH has enough QCs under the U.S. system to receive regular U.S. benefits, the
United States will not count Slovenian credits.
