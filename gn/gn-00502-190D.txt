D. Other Manual Payee Actions
1. T2R Output
The PC is responsible for manually processing fallout from T2R (alert and exception
processing). Process T2R outputs in accordance with SM 03005.195. Process exceptions
using MACADE. The payee application in eRPS remains in a “ready to process” status
until the MACADE action processes. You can view the status of the payee application
under “Search/View/Update Applications” in eRPS.
2. Notice Output
When you receive a “Review Notice” alert, review the alert and query eRPS to determine
the proper recipient of the notice. If required, print a copy of the SG-SSA-11 from
the Online Retrieval System to obtain necessary information.
