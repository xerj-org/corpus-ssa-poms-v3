B. Handling direct deposit requests with potential assignment of benefits
Upon receiving a request for direct deposit that raises a question about possible
assignment, perform the following actions:
•
Obtain an SF-1199A (Direct Deposit Sign-Up Form),
•
Ensure the SF-1199A form does not reflect an assignment of benefits and that proper
account titling requirements are met (see GN 02402.050).
If there is no assignment, then approve the request.
