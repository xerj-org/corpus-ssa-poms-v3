SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.710 - Age Information in Census Records
Effective Dates: 04/24/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302710 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
In most years the census taker asked individuals their age in terms of “age at last birthday” as of the Census Day. The person's age was written in whole years, except as noted
in the last column of the table below. At no time has the exact day of birth ever
been asked.
Except for the 1950 census (see “Note” in F. below), the census transcript shows complete years and months.
