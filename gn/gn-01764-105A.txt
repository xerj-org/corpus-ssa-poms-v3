A. Policy for forwarding certified coverage records
The Icelandic certified coverage record is the Form IS-205 (Icelandic Coverage Certification).
It is the only acceptable form for the certification of an Iceland coverage record.
Forward certified coverage records in any other format to the Office of Earnings and
International Operations, Division of Training and Program Support for approval.
