D. Procedure - Disposition of transmittal form SSA-2960
If a U.S. claim (regular or Totalization) is filed, print two copies of the completed
Form SSA-2960. Send one copy of the completed form to Ottawa and/or Quebec and keep
the other copy of the completed form with the U.S. claim.
