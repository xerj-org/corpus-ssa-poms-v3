SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.150 - Preparation and Assembly of RSHI Claims Material for Transmittal to the Processing Centers
Effective Dates: 10/18/2005 - Present | TN 1 (06-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Material to include:
1.
Include all essential material—this includes more than evidence that establishes facts
or tends to back up statements. It means material required or suggested by POMS instructions
including any material that conflicts or disagrees with the application or other evidence.
(See also GN 01050.240)
2.
Ask the CR if you have any question as to whether or not material should be included.
