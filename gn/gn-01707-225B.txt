SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.225 - Requests for Status of Application for German Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 9 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a German claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for German benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded a claim to the German agency, check the PPC system. NOTE:
Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Germany
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Germany, advise the applicant
to contact DIO directly at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Germany
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the German agency, advise the applicant
to contact the German agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Bundesministerium für
Arbeit und Soziales (Federal Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs)
•
Deutsche
Rentenversicherung Bund (German Federal Pension Insurance)
NOTE::
DIO is unable to determine the status of a German claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Germany. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
