Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Chile does not pay social security benefits directly to family members of retirement
or disability pensioners. (Family subsidies are paid to the insured person under the
old system.) Therefore, survivors should be advised to file an application in order
to receive Chilean survivor benefits.
