SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02405.300 - Limited Payability - Voiding Returned Benefit Checks More Than a Year Old
Effective Dates: 04/25/2014 - Present | TN 15 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202405300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Remittance Supervisor Procedure
The remittance supervisor must follow the procedures in the following table when processing
endorsed and unendorsed benefit checks returned to the Field Office (FO) that Treasury
issued and are more than one year old.
STEP
ACTION
1
Review each line of the FODRL to ensure that each item matches the listed remittances.
2
Review any mailroom or reception area logs completed that day to ensure that any returned
checks annotated on the logs are included on the FODRL.
REFERENCE: Remittance Handling in the FO Mailroom and Reception Area, GN 02403.004.
3
Resolve any discrepancies between the FODRL, reception area logs, and returned checks
submitted by the remittance clerk and interviewers.
4
Take appropriate action for the other items on the FODRL as identified in GN 02403.021.
5
Sign and date the FODRL after completing all actions required for the listed items.
Shred the limited payability checks for which there are LP credits (For Title II,
EVENT 169 in the TRANS field on PHUS. For Title XVI, review the unnegotiated check
indicator (UCI) code in the PMTH segment of the SSR to determine the status of Limited
Payability Credit) and file the FODRL. Give the checks for which SSA is waiting LP
credits to the Office Manager for safekeeping.
