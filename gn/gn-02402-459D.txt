D. Overview of IDD for Botswana
IDD payments sent to Botswana must be in Botswanan Pula (BWP) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Botswana. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
