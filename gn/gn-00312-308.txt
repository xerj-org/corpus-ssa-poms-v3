GN 00312.308 Panama – Vital Statistics Records
Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Divorce and Death Records
When requesting a birth certificate, ask for a copia
integra (long form).
Divorce decrees contain many pages; thus, if a divorce action was annotated on the
marriage record, consider requesting the marriage record for proof of divorce rather
than a copy of the divorce decree.
