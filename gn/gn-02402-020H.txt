H. Instructions for Handling Protests of Overpayments Resulting from Incorrectly Processed
Redirected Payments, After the Overpayment Recovery Process
If the beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee protests the overpayment and
alleges fraud, after SSA begun recovery efforts for the redirected payment, refer
the case to Office of the Inspector General (OIG) via the Allegation Referral Intake
System (ARIS).
1.
For Title XVI, follow SM
01311.220.
2.
For Title II, reestablish the overpayment back on the Non-Entitled Debtors (NED) record
and follow the steps in SM
01311.225.B.
If the account holder failed to respond to the EFT Claim Form and protests the overpayment
posted, then take the following applicable action:
1.
For Title XVI, follow SI
02220.017
2.
For Title II, follow GN
02201.025
NOTE:
If a FO receives a case involving Title II, they must refer the case to the PC via
MDW or RQFA.
