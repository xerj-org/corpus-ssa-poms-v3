A. Policy for managing inquiries from individuals abroad
Border field offices (FOs), domestic FOs, Payment Centers (PC), and the International
Benefits Office (IBO) in the Division of International Operations (DIO) receive inquiries
from persons abroad.
Refer to the chart in GN 00904.200 for when these offices have jurisdiction of foreign claims and issues.
