A. Policy – General
Under the agreement, Canada will count a worker’s U.S. coverage, where necessary,
in determining eligibility for Canadian benefits under both the Old-Age Security (OAS)
and the Canada Pension Plan (CPP) or the Quebec Pension Plan (QPP) programs.
