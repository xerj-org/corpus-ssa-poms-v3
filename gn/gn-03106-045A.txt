A. DESCRIPTION - MAGISTRATE'S REPORT AND/OR RECOMMENDATION
A Magistrate's report and/or recommendation contain a summary of a court case and
a recommendation of how the court should decide the case. A Magistrate's report and/or
recommendation is not a final order, but the information contained in the Magistrate's Report is often
needed for effectuation after a final order/judgment of the court is entered. Magistrate's
reports and/or recommendations are forwarded by the OGC to the effectuating components.
