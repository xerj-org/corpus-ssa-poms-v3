A. Policy for adjudicating foreign death underpayments
Benefit Authorizers (BA) and Claims Specialists (CS) in the Division of International
Operations' (DIO) Payment Center (PC-8) process inquiries and claims for foreign death
underpayments.
NOTE: For the difference between a foreign death underpayment inquiry and claim, see GN 02301.080.
