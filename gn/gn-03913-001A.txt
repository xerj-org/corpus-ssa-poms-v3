A. Registration of representatives, entities, and entity points of contact
Effective September 30, 2024, all individuals who wish to serve as an appointed representative
must register with us before we can process their appointment in a case. Effective
December 9, 2024, all entities that wish to receive direct payment of fees authorized
to an affiliated representative must register with us and name a point of contact
(POC) before we can process an assignment of direct payment.  Any individual who intends
to act as the POC for an entity also must register with us before being named as a
POC on the entity’s registration.
