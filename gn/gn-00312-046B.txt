B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Attorney General’s Ministry (AGM) or local civil registration offices
or municipality where the event took place or state civil archives for birth, marriage,
and death records. Contact the district court where the divorce was granted. Individuals
may also visit the Registry on the ground floor of the Supreme Court Building at the
corner of Regent Street and Treasury Lane, Belize City.
AGM
6120 Racoon Avenue
Belmopan, Belize
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Names of the parents, including the maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Address at the time of birth
3. Fee
•
Birth and adoption - $3.50 U.S.
•
Death records - $3.50 U.S.
•
Divorce records - $7.25 U.S.
•
Marriage records - $6.00 U.S.
