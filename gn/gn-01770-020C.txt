C. Administrative organization for Slovenian benefits
The Institute for Pension and Invalidity Insurance of Slovenia (IPII) operates the
pension system of Slovenia. If inquirers request additional information about Slovenian
benefits, advise them that the Social Security Administration (SSA) cannot provide
official information about Slovenian benefits. Refer all inquirers seeking additional
information about the Slovenian pension system to the following address:
Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia
Kolodvorska 15
SI-1518 Ljubljana
Slovenia
