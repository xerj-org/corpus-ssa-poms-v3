Effective Dates: 04/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The Foreign Service Post (FSP) in Warsaw, Poland will forward requests to the Czech
Republic agency, CSSZ and CSSZ will send the FSP the required documents needed to
process the claim
When processing claims under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
