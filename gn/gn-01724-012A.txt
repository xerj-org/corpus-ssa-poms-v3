SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01724.012 - How the Totalization Modules Process Claims for French Benefits Filed with a French Agency by U.S. Residents
Effective Dates: 03/20/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201724012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The French agencies require that any claim for French benefits be filed in the claimant's
country of residence. Sometimes, U.S. residents file a claim for French benefits while
visiting in France. These claimants must still file for French benefits through SSA
when they return to the U.S. France may notify DIO of these claimants or the claimant
may contact DIO themselves.
