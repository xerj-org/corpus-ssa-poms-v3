SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.227 - Bankruptcy Petition Pending — Subsequent Debt - PC Procedure
Effective Dates: 03/23/2023 - Present | TN 47 (03-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215227 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If a new debt is identified and a bankruptcy petition for a prior debt(s) is pending,
recovery of the new debt is appropriate if the debt is post-petition -- that
is, the payments comprising the debt occur after the bankruptcy
petition was filed. Recovery is appropriate whether or not SSA filed a Proof of Claim on the prior debt(s).
