D. Overview of IDD for Malawi
IDD payments sent to Malawi must be in Malawi Kwacha (MWK) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Malawi. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
