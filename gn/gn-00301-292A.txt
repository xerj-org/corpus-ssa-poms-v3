Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301292 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
One of the primary purposes of regional instructions is to explain procedures pertaining
to situations peculiar to a particular region or State. Of necessity, development
can be different from region to region, particularly when developing Title XVI (SSI)
claims. Regional instructions tailor general national instructions to situations unique
to the region.
All regional instructions must be reviewed by Central Office (CO).
