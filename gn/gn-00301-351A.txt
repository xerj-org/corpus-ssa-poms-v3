A. When should you purchase a non-Social Security Administration (SSA) translator
Use non-SSA translators who charge a fee for their services only when there are no
other reasonable alternatives. The purchase of a non-SSA translator is subject to
regional budget resources. (See GN 00301.330D for alternative translator choices.)
