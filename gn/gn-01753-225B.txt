B. Applicant requests status of a Slovak claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of the
applicant's claim for Slovak benefits and states that notification that DIO forwarded
the claim to the Slovak agency was not received, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovak Republic
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovak Republic, advise the
applicant to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovak Republic
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovak agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Slovak agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Ministerstvo práce,
sociálnych vecí a rodiny (Ministry of Labour, Social Affairs and Family)
•
Sociálna poisťovňa (Social
Insurance Agency)
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Slovak claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Slovak Republic. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
