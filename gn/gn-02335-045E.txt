E. Payment to Minors or Incompetent Adults
If a relative who qualifies for payment is incompetent or a minor, the claim may be
made on such person's behalf by the legal guardian or other individual who has assumed
responsibility for the relative who is incompetent or a minor. The representative
payee for monthly social security benefits will also qualify as payee for health insurance
purposes. If there is no legal guardian or representative payee, the claimant's statement
of authority to act for the qualified individual may be accepted in the absence of
evidence to the contrary.
