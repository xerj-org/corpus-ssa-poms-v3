C. Responding to denied direct deposit requests made in the Field Office (FO) or via
the National 800 Number (N8NN)
FO or N8NN technicians denying direct deposit requests may provide the requesting
beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee either an oral or written explanation
of denial. This response may highlight the specific requirements not met by the beneficiary,
recipient, or the representative payee.
NOTE: It is not necessary to retain any record of the denial.
