GN 01771.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Slovenian Liaison Agency on Form SSA e2960 USA/SI
3 (U.S. - Slovenia Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
Preparing a diary
Prepare a diary for information requested from the Slovenian liaison agency Pension
and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia
•
Diary initial request for 60 days;
•
Diary follow-up requests for 15 days; and
•
Notify your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) or Module Manager (MM) if there is no response
after 2 follow-ups.
