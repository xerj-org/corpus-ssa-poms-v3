2. Notices to individuals in Turkey
Send all correspondence and notices to individuals in Turkey to the servicing FBU
for forwarding to the addressees.
Show “For transmission to the addressee” and that the notices are from SSA, DIO.
Exception: See GN 00905.130 if the Turkish address contains a reference to Mersin 10, Turkey or Lefkosa, Turkey.
