SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03905.070 - Form SSA-1560 Petition to Obtain Approval of a Fee for Representing a Claimant
Effective Dates: 09/16/2020 - Present | TN 6 (09-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Appointed representatives, who want to charge and collect a fee for services before
us must use one of two mutually exclusive fee processes: the fee agreement process
or the fee petition process. Representatives have the option to use this form when
they want to charge a fee under the fee petition process. The representative also
sends a copy to the claimant.
