D. Overview of IDD for Norway
IDD payments sent to Norway must be in Norwegian Krone (NOK) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Norway. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
