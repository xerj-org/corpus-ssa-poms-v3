SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02335.025 - Services Paid For—Who May Claim Payment
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 2 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202335025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Services Paid by Deceased Beneficiary or Funds of the Deceased Beneficiary's Estate
1. Legal representative
If the services were paid for by the deceased beneficiary or from funds of the deceased
beneficiary's estate, payment will be made to the legal representative, if any, of
the individual's estate (see GN 02335.040).
2. No legal representative
If the services were paid for by the deceased beneficiary prior to death and no legal
representative has been appointed, payment will be made to certain survivors of the
beneficiary in the following order of priority:
a.
To the surviving spouse who was either living in the same household with the beneficiary
at the time of death, or entitled to monthly social security or railroad retirement
benefits on the same earnings record as the beneficiary for the month of death (see
GN 02335.050 B.);
b.
To the child(ren) of the beneficiary who was entitled to monthly social security or
railroad retirement benefits on the same earnings record as the beneficiary for the
month of death (see GN 02335.050 C.);
c.
To the parent(s) of the beneficiary who was entitled to monthly social security or
railroad retirement benefits on the same earnings record as the beneficiary for the
month of death (see GN 02335.050 D.);
d.
To the surviving spouse of the beneficiary who was not living in the same household
with the beneficiary at the time of death, and was not entitled to monthly social
security or railroad retirement benefits on the same earnings for the month of death;
e.
To the child(ren) of the beneficiary who was not entitled to monthly social security
or railroad retirement benefits on the same earnings record for the month of death;
f.
To the parent(s) of the beneficiary who was not entitled to monthly social security
or railroad retirement benefits on the same earnings record for the month of death.
