SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Ireland
Effective Dates: 01/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

3. U.S. and Irish or Irish Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-IR
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from OIO files, if the Irish claim is for disability benefits
•
Evidence submitted for the Irish claim, and
•
A request for the Irish coverage record, if one is needed
