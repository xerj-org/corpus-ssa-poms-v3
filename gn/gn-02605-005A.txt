A. POLICY
Generally, the individual's mailing address should be the address where he/ she resides
or his/her own post office (PO) box (or the residence address or PO box of his/her
representative payee). Any other address is questionable and will not be acceptable
if it:
•
Facilitates an assignment of benefits (see GN 02410.001), or
•
Directs checks to a location where the check payee cannot readily negotiate them,
or
•
Permits the check payee to conceal information which, if known to SSA, would result
in nonpayment of benefits, substitution of payee, etc.
