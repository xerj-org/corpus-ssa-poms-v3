A. Exemption for certain non-U.S. citizen beneficiaries residing in Korea
Section 202(t)(1) of the Social Security Act provides that certain non-U.S. citizen
beneficiaries will have their benefits suspended once they have been outside the U.S.
for more than six months, unless they meet an exception.
One such exception is in section 233(c)(2) of the Act. As explained in GN 01701.150A.1., certain non-U.S. citizen beneficiaries who would otherwise be subject to suspension
under section 202(t)(1) are exempt from suspension if they reside in a country with
which the United States has a Totalization Agreement. This exemption applies to citizens
or residents of Korea who receive regular retirement, survivors or disability benefits,
as well as those who receive Totalization benefits.
Note: Citizens of the Republic of Korea are exempt from the alien nonpayment provision
of section 202(t)(1), regardless of where they reside (see RS 02610.015).
