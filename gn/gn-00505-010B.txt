B. Policy for determining the need for and selecting a Representative Payee (RP) in foreign
claims
The policies for determining the need for and selecting a RP for a beneficiary living
outside the United States are the same as the domestic policies. For information about
making, a capability determination see GN 00502.060. Except as described below, you must interview the RP applicant face-to-face unless
an exception applies. For a list of foreign country and regional service information,
see https://www.ssa.gov/foreign/foreign.htm.
For information on face-to face interviews see GN 00502.050A.1, GN 00502.107A.2, and GN 00502.110D. For information on interviewing the RP applicant and selecting a qualified payee
see GN 00502.113 and GN 00502.132.
