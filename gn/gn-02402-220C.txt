C. Process - routing of edits
All rejects relating to direct deposit actions transmitted through ITS.gov are routed
to Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Edits relating to IDD actions transmitted by
a Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) are sent to the Office of International Operations (OIO).
If the FBU has access to IWS-LAN, the edits are routed to the transmitting office.
Edits relating to IDD actions transmitted by a field office (FO) are sent to that
office.
