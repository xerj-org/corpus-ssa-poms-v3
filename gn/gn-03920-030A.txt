SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.030 - Representative's Fee - Title II Past-Due Benefits
Effective Dates: 11/05/2015 - Present | TN 89 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - Purpose
SSA uses title II past-due benefits that result from a claim(s) or post entitlement
(PE) action(s) to do the following:
•
Calculate the 25 percent amount of title II past-due benefits to withhold for direct
payment of a representative's fee, when the representative is eligible for direct
fee payment, whether SSA will authorize a fee for administrative proceedings under
the fee petition or fee agreement process. (See GN 03920.016, for Payment of Representatives Fee.)
•
Authorize a fee under the fee agreement process for services a representative provided
in proceedings before SSA in connection with the title II claim.
•
Calculate the 25 percent amount of title II past-due benefits to withhold for direct
payment of an attorney's fee in a court case. For the definition of past-due benefits
in court cases, see GN 03920.060C.1.
