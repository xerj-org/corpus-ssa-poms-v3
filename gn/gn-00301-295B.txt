B. Procedure — forwarding claims materials
Review and remove all extraneous material before either initial or subsequent claims
folders or materials are forwarded from:
•
The FO to the SSA National Records Center (SSANRC)
•
The FO to the PC; and
•
PC's to their respective storage facilities.
