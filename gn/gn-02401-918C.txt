Effective Dates: 04/01/2024 - Present | TN 46 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401918 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Request for repayment of incorrect payment
When we issue incorrect checks after the death of the beneficiary or recipient, release
the notice requesting repayment of funds from the spouse or other endorser. The “Lim
Pay – Request for Repayment – Initial & FU” notice is available in the Document Processing
System (DPS).
