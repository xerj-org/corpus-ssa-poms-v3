A. Automatic status notice for an application for Romanian benefits
When the International Benefit & Processing (IBP) forwards information about a Romanian
claim, certified copies of evidence, and a copy of the worker’s U.S. coverage record
to Romania, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically stores the claims
material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system in IBP before forwarding
the claim to the Romanian agency. The system automatically generates a notice that
the BET sends to the applicant.
