SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed With a German Agency
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
a German agency.
1. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part I) and the appropriate RSDI applications and evidence
and forward the claim to DIO with the forms D/USA 3 and D/USA 4 and the German coverage
record.
2. Claimant already entitled to U.S. benefits
Destroy the forms D/USA 3 and D/USA 4, unless they contain a request for information
from SSA. In that case, forward the forms to DIO, annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
