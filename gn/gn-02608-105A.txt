SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.105 - Exemption Due to Pension Eligibility Before July 1983 and One-Half Support Met
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Eligible or entitled to pension before July 1983
GPO does not apply to benefits payable for December 1982 and later, if the spouse:
•
is eligible for or entitled to a government pension before July 1, 1983 (If pension
eligibility began in June 1983, see GN 02608.106), and
•
meets the one-half support requirement. For an explanation of the one-half support
requirement, see RS 01301.010 and RS 01301.015.
