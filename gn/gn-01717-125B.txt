B. Policy- Crediting U.S. coverage
Sweden will credit 1 year of Swedish coverage (1 pension point year) for every 4 U.S.
quarters of coverage. However, Sweden will not credit any QC's in calendar years which
are already credited as pension point years under the Swedish system.
