B. Procedure for the FBU to process referrals from the Uruguayan Liaison Agency
This procedure explains how the Buenos Aires, Argentina FBU processes referrals from
the Uruguayan liaison agency.
1.
Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits: Forward all material to the Division of International
Operations (DIO) annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits."
2.
Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits: Process the claim in the exact same manner
shown in GN 01762.010B.
