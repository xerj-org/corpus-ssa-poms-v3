SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.157 - Processing Data in the Electronic Representative Payee System for Beneficiaries in Foster Care
Effective Dates: 06/20/2019 - Present | TN 53 (06-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502157 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
This section provides guidance for using the electronic Representative Payee System
(eRPS) to process foster care work issues involving beneficiaries who have entered,
exited, or changed foster care placement.
