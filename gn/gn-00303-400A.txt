A. Policy - DHS Authorization of Aliens
An individual who is not a U.S. citizen or a U.S. national and is present in the U.S.
must have authorization from DHS to lawfully remain in the U.S.
SSA accepts evidence of alien status, in support of a claim for benefits, based on
established legal principles governing the rules of evidence. Only original, unexpired
DHS documents are acceptable.
