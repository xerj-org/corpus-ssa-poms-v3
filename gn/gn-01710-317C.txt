C. Policy-persons from whom rights can be derived
The persons listed in GN 01710.317B. can derive rights from:
•
nationals of either the United States or Switzerland;
•
refugees who reside (or resided at death) in either the United States or Switzerland;
or
•
stateless persons who reside (or resided at death) in either the United States or
Switzerland.
