C. Procedure FO forwarding documentation material to the PSC
Once a suspension action is on the MBR, forward copies of the advance notification
letter (if sent) and any other documentation to the jurisdictional PSC. Do not fax this material. Annotate the material "Fugitive Felon (Suspension/Reinstatement/Resumption) Processed
Drop-file Material." Mail the documentation to the jurisdictional PSC address shown
below.
