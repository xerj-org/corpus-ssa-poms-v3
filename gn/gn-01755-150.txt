GN 01755.150 Filing Appeals of Decisions under the Agreement with Hungary
The agreement provides that applicants who wish to appeal a decision on a claim for
U.S. or Hungarian benefits may file the appeal with either country.
For more information about appealing a decision on a claim, see GN 01702.500.
For information about representation of applicants in totalization claims, see GN 01702.505.
