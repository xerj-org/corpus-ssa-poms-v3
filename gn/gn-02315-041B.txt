B. Policy of good acquittance and the effect of a California affidavit or declaration
under the small estate procedure
If the requirements of sections 13100 to 13104 of the California Probate Code, as
listed in this section are satisfied, receipt by the holder of the decedent’s property
of the affidavit or declaration constitutes sufficient acquittance for the payment
of money, delivery of property, or changing of ownership and discharges the holder
from any further liability with respect to the money or property. The holder may rely
in good faith on the statements in the affidavit or declaration and has no duty to
inquire into the truth of any statement in the affidavit or declaration.
Reference: CAL. Prob. Code § 13106 (effective July 1, 1991, to present)
