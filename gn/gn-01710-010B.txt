SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When taking applications under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices. (See
GN 01702.001 through GN 01702.525 for FO and FSP development and GN 01709.205 through GN 01709.215 for special evidence and application requirements for the agreement with Switzerland.)
