A. Policy — Adoption Proceedings Invalid
Where adoption proceedings were defective and invalid under State law, some States
grant inheritance rights to the child under a theory of estoppel. The child's foster
parents and their heirs may be barred from asserting the adoption proceeding was invalid.
Submit the question to the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) per GN 01010.815 ff. in the absence of a precedent opinion.
