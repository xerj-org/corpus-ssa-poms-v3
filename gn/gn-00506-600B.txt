B. Procedure to request a credit report for FFS applicants
The field office (FO) must verify that the potential new non-governmental FFS applicant
has proper documentation prior to requesting a credit report analysis, see GN 00506.010. When all the documentation listed in GN 00506.010 is received, send an email with the organization’s name, the name of the organization's
representative, the organization's address and the organization's telephone number
to the Fee for Service Coordinator at LP ISP FFS
Coordinator with a cc to your RO Program Support Team. The RO is responsible for ensuring that
the FO has the complete FFS application package.
