GN 00210.020 Processing Instructions for Title II Same-Sex Relationship Claims, Reconsiderations,
and Hearing Requests
The instructions in this section apply to awards and disallowances for claims and
appeals involving same-sex marriages and non-marital legal relationships (NMLRs).
To determine claimant eligibility for benefits based on a same-sex marriage or NMLR,
refer to GN 00210.000.
