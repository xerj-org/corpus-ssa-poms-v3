GN 01755.010 Overview of the Hungarian System
The Hungarian social insurance system provides old-age retirement, disability, and
survivor benefits.
The Hungarian statutory pension system functions as a single-pillar social insurance
structure financed on a pay-as-you-go basis and supplemented by voluntary pension
funds.
