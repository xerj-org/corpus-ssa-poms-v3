SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03105.010 - Reconstruction of Destroyed or Missing Paper Claims Folders
Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present | TN 7 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203105010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Missing paper folder located during redevelopment
After initiating folder reconstruction, we may find the original folder. If found
by the:
a.
Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) -- OHO should telephone the FO immediately to stop reconstruction and, if appropriate,
notify the DDS.
b.
FO – The FO should telephone the requesting office immediately and send the located
folder and any materials already reconstructed to the requesting office.
