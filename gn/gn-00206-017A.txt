A. Policy for canceling a WD request
A claimant has 60 days from the date of the WD approval notice to request a cancellation
of the WD. The cancellation request must:
•
be in writing;
•
be signed by the claimant or proper applicant;
•
provide the reason for the cancellation; and
•
state that the claimant wants a determination on their eligibility and entitlement
for the original filed application.
