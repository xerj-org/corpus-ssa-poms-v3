SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Veterans Administration Regional Office (VARO) in Manila
Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The procedure below describes special development and routing instructions that apply
to claims under the agreement filed in VARO.
1. U.S. Claim
•
In retirement and disability claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens,
the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and the SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
•
In survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and Part I of the paper or electronic (e-form) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-KOR, U.S./Korea Agreement on Social Security Request/Transmittal/Certification
Form, to the NPC to request a Korean coverage record if it appears the worker is not
insured based on U.S. coverage alone.
Note: This form may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section of the Totalization
Resource Kit.
•
Forward the claim to OIO and include a copy of the completed liaison form sent to
the NPC.
2. Korean Claim
•
Have the applicant complete the application for Korean benefits (KOR-USA 1) using
the Guide for Completing an Application for Korean Benefits Under the Agreement.
Note: The Korean application and Guide may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section
of the Totalization Resource Kit.
•
Mail the Korean application, a copy of the U.S. coverage record (ICERS) and any evidence
the applicant wants to submit to the NPC under cover of Form SSA-2960-KOR. Be sure
to enter the protective filing date on the form.
Note: Form SSA-2960-KOR may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section of the Totalization
Resource Kit.
•
If the claim is for Korean disability benefits, give the applicant the Korean Medical
Report (KOR-USA 1M) and instruct them to have their physician complete it and return
it to the NPC. Provide a return envelope pre-addressed to the NPC.
Note: The Korean Medical Report Form may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section
of the Totalization Resource Kit.
•
Inform the applicant that the NPC will contact him/her directly if they need any additional
information.
3. U.S. and Korean Claim
•
In retirement and disability claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens,
the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and the SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
•
In survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and Part I of the paper or electronic (e-form) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
•
Have the applicant complete the application for Korean benefits (KOR-USA 1) using
the Guide for Completing an Application for Korean Benefits Under the Agreement.
Note: The Korean application and Guide may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section
of the Totalization Resource Kit.
•
Mail the Korean application, a copy of the U.S. coverage record (ICERS or MCS) and
any evidence the applicant wants to submit to the NPC under cover of Form SSA-2960-KOR.
Be sure to enter the protective filing date on the form and request a Korean coverage
record if it appears the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage only.
Note: Form SSA-2960 KOR may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section of the Totalization
Resource Kit.
•
If the claim is for Korean disability benefits, give the applicant the Korean Medical
Report (KOR-USA 1M) and instruct them to have their physician complete it and return
it to the NPC. Provide a return envelope pre-addressed to the NPC.
Note: The Korean Medical Report Form may be printed from the SSA Totalization Forms section
of the Totalization Resource Kit.
•
Forward the U.S. claim to OIO and include a copy of the completed liaison form sent
to the NPC.
•
Inform the applicant that the NPC will contact him/her directly if they need any additional
information regarding the claim for Korean benefits.
