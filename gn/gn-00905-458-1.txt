SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.458 - Correspondence With and From Beneficiaries or Claimants in Turkey
Effective Dates: 05/08/2018 - Present | TN 7 (03-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905458 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Correspondence from Turkey
Examine postadjudicative correspondence and inquiries carefully for any indications
of the diversion of funds, unreported termination events, etc.
A report of death of a beneficiary in the Elazig- Hozat area must be supported by
documentary evidence.
