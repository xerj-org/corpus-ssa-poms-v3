A. What is an alert?
A death alert is a notification to the field that action is required to resolve an
issue related to a death report. Alerts are generated to the field office (FO), and
displayed on the Death Alerts
Tracking System (DATS). DATS is an Intranet application that provides daily listings of death alerts at
the FO, district, or area level.
