SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.400 - Developing Medical Evidence in Claims for U.S. Totalization Disability Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
In order to avoid unnecessary development, field offices (FOs) should generally develop
medical evidence in claims for U.S. Totalization disability benefits only when it
appears the number holder (NH) can meet insured status requirements based on combined
U.S. and foreign coverage.
