C. Organization
If the claimant cannot provide any lead to foreign or domestic evidence of survivor status, write directly
to the following organizations. The DO (or the claimant) may write simultaneously
to all the organizations or to only those which appear very likely to be productive.
EXAMPLE: If the claimant is sure that he/she was awarded or denied indemnification payments,
the Bundeszentralkartei will most likely be able to provide the requisite evidence
of survivor status.
The organizations are listed in the order of greatest likelihood of finding a record.
