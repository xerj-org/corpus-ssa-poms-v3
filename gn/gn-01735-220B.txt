Effective Dates: 01/23/2003 - Present | TN 1 (01-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201735220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
The special Irish application forms are not taken in the field offices (FO's). After
receiving a claim for Irish benefits that was filed with SSA, the Irish agency will
mail the appropriate Irish application forms to the applicant. The Irish agency will
include instructions asking the claimant to complete the forms and mail them directly
to the Irish agency with any additional evidence that may be needed.
