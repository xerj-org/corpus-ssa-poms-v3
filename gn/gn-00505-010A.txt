A. Introduction to Representative Payees (RPs) in foreign claims
The different laws, customs, languages, and conditions abroad can often complicate
the administration of the Social Security programs outside the United States. This
section provides general information on determining the need for and selecting Representative
Payees (RPs) for beneficiaries living outside the United States.
