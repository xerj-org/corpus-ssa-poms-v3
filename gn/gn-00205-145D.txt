D. Marital history
We collect marital history, per GN 00205.070, to determine eligibility to benefits. Use the responses to determine if proof of
termination of prior marriage(s) is required. Get all the information in this item.
For developing proof of termination, see GN 00305.120 and GN 00305.140. For deemed spouse development, see GN 00305.055.
