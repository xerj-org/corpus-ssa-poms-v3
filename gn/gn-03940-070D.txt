D. Procedure - Concurrent Titles II and XVI Claims - Favorable Decision - No Spouse Involvement
- Fee Agreement Disapproved - Past-Due Benefits Withheld for Possible Direct Payment
For concurrent titles II and XVI claims, do not prepare a fee notice of the disapproval:
the title II CR, ALJ or AAJ notifies the claimant and representative of the disapproved
fee agreement. Because SSA is withholding the claimant’s past-due benefits for possible
direct payment to the representative, it is necessary to manually notify the claimant
and the representative that the representative should file a fee petition within 60
days if he/she wishes SSA to pay the fee directly to him/her.
Use UTI FEE058; caption ATYC10 and UTI ATY050.
Use caption ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035, FEE107, FEE108, FEE085 and FEE087.
NOTE: In this situation, the representative will receive a title II and a title XVI notice
advising him/her to file the fee petition.
