A. Procedure — Translation and Priority Workload Unit (TPWU)
SSA
Translators
In addition to the normal rules, use the following guides in translating evidence
of age, marriage, legitimation or acknowledgment.
1. Age, legitimation or acknowledgment
Show the names (if given) of the parents, grandparents and godparents in addition
to the normal identifying information.
Translate the document entirely if the individual was illegitimate and was acknowledged
or legitimated.
2. Marriage
Provide the usual translation if both parties were present before the person who performed
the marriage. Translate the document entirely if one of the parties was married by
proxy.
Translate the document entirely if any children were legitimated by the marriage.
