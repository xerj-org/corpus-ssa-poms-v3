GN 01758.235 Facts about the Brazil Liaison Agency on Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 (Transmittal/Request/Certification)
Procedure – When to use Form SSA-2960 USA/BR 3
The Brazilian liaison agency the INSS uses Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 to:
•
Transmit material to the Division of International Operations (DIO) or the Lisbon,
Portugal Federal Benefits Unit (FBU);
•
Send information to DIO or Lisbon. Portugal FBU; and
•
Request information from DIO.
