SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Portugal
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Initial claims packages are prepared to request Portuguese coverage certifications
and to inform the Portuguese agency about claims for Portuguese benefits. (See GN 01727.205 - GN 01727.215, for the application and evidence required for the package.)
