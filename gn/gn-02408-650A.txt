SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.650 - Title II Beneficiary Receiving EFT Payments Dies After the End of the Month but Before the Payment Date
Effective Dates: 04/19/2022 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408650 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Social Security employees must not state that a financial institutions (FI) should
not have returned a Title II direct deposit payment received on the 3rd of the month or a cycled payment date, when the beneficiary died before the payment
date but after the close of the month for which the payment was due; i.e., the beneficiary
was entitled to the payment. Statements regarding payments after a beneficiary's death
must take into account the Department of the Treasury (Treasury) “Green
Book ” instructions.
