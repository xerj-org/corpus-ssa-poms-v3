A. Process to file a claim for U.S. benefits
When a person contacts the Icelandic Liaison Agency (Social Insurance Administration
- SIA) to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the Agency sends the following information
to the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) in Oslo, Norway:
•
Form SSA-e2960 USA-IS3, (U.S.- Iceland Transmittal/Request/Certification) certifying
the filing date of the claim;
•
Icelandic certified coverage record IS-205;
•
supporting evidence for non-disability claims; and,
•
available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
