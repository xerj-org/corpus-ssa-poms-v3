A. Introduction
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Japan:
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Japanese
benefits, even though they don’t meet the normal insured status requirements.
