SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.387 - Coding Macedonia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 11/06/2024 - Present | TN 279 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402387 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Macedonia will have a prefix of "68" at the beginning of the
Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN)
field has the letter “L” assigned for Macedonia.
