SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 10/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedure below which describes special development and routing instructions
that apply to claims under the agreement filed in the FSP.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-e2960-U3-GR to IKA to request a Greek coverage record if
it appears the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone.
•
Forward the claim to DIO, and include the file and follow-up copies of the liaison
form sent to IKA.
2. Greek Claim
•
Refer individuals who want to file claims for Greek benefits to the IKA local office.
Notify IKA via the USA/GR 3 (SSA-e2960-U3-GR) of the claims lead.
3. U.S. and Greek Claim
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Parts 1 and II) and the appropriate RSDI application.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-e2960-U3-GR to IKA to request a Greek coverage record (if
it appears the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone) and to certify
the protective filing date.
•
Forward the claim to DIO, and include the file and follow-up copies of the liaison
form sent to IKA.
•
Inform the applicant that IKA will contact him/her directly to complete the required
Greek applications.
