B. Taxes under the Totalization Agreement with the Slovak Republic
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to:
1.
U.S. Social Security taxes under the Federal Income Contributions Act (FICA) and Self-Employment
Contributions Act (SECA), including the Medicare portions of those acts; and
2.
Slovak social security taxes that finance retirement, survivors, disability, health
insurance, maternity and sickness, work injury, and unemployment programs.
