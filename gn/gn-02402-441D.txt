D. Overview of IDD for Brunei
IDD payments sent to Brunei must be in Brunei Dollar (BND) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Brunei. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
