GN 03910.020 Qualifications for and Recognition of Representatives
If a claimant would like to appoint a representative to help him or her in pursuing
a pending claim, matter, or issue, we require the representative:
•
meet certain qualifications, as explained in GN 03910.020A; and
•
complete the appointment process, as explained in GN 03910.040.
If the representative meets these requirements, we recognize a representative's appointment
on a pending claim, matter, or issue. See 20 CFR 404.1703 and 416.1503 for a definition of "representational services."
Any individual can provide general assistance to a claimant on a pending claim, matter,
or issue without being appointed as a "representative."
We recognize representatives who live outside of the United States to the extent they
meet the qualifications explained in GN 03910.020A and complete the appointment process explained in GN 03910.040.
