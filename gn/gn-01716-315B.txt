SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01716.315 - Telephone Communication With the Canadian Liaison Agencies
Effective Dates: 01/26/2015 - Present | TN 1 (04-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201716315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following instructions for telephone communication with Canada.
1. Placing Calls to Ottawa
Use the following guidelines when placing calls to Ottawa:
•
Complete a Record of Phone Call Documentation Form when you initiate a call to Ottawa.
•
Make no more than one call per day, per technician to each of the 6 sections.
•
Call between 8:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.
•
Be prepared to provide the NHs full name and Canadian social insurance number (SIN).
•
If the Canadian SIN is unknown, be prepared to provide full identifying information;
i.e., name at birth, if different, father's name, mother's maiden name and date and
place of birth, and
•
Relate calls to a specific case. Refer general to an OIO program analyst or policy
questions to CPS.
2. Placing Calls to Quebec
Use the following guidelines when placing calls to Quebec:
•
Complete a Record of Phone Call Documentation Form when you initiate a call to Quebec,
•
Make no more than one call per day per technician,
•
Call between 8:30 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.
•
Be prepared to provide the NHs full name and Canadian SIN.
•
If the Canadian SIN is unknown, be prepared to provide full identifying information;
i.e., name at birth, if different, father's name, mother's maiden name and date and
place of birth, and
•
Relate calls to a specific case. Refer general to an OIO program analyst or policy
questions to CPS.
