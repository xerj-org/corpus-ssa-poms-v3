SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02403.014 - Processing Non-Programmatic Fees Received in the Field Office Using the Social Security Electronic Remittance System
Effective Dates: 03/10/2026 - Present | TN 63 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202403014 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Fees received in the FO
Using SERS, the FO may receive non-programmatic fee payments for the following:
•
Copying an electronic folder to a disc,
•
Copying a paper folder,
•
Third Party Social Security Number (SSN) verification (no charge if issued directly
to the number holder),
•
Detailed Earnings Query (DEQY),
•
Regional office certification of records, or
•
Record extracts (entitlement, benefit, or status information).
NOTE: The Social Security Administration (SSA) requires payment before providing the requested service.
Do not use FeeBayNet to process these standard administrative fees. FeeBayNet is only
used to invoice for payment of non-programmatic fees.
