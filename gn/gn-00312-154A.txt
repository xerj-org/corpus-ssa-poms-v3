A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Adoption and Death Records
1. Office to Contact
The Civil Status & Registration Office
Joshua Hassan House
3 Secretary's Lane
Gibraltar
Telephone: 011-350-51725
2. Fee
a.
Birth Certificates: Full copy – 7.00 pounds; Short form - 3.50 pounds
b.
Marriage and Death Certificates: 7.00 pounds
c.
Adoption Records: Unknown
Include a handling charge of 5.00 pounds with each request regardless of the number
or type of certificates requested.
Payment must be by international money order or banker's draft in pounds sterling
payable to “Government General Account.”
