C. PROCEDURE - PUPS PROCESSING BENEFICIARY NOT CONVICTED OR COMMITTED OR IN AN INSTITUTION
30 DAYS OR LESS
If the beneficiary was not convicted of a crime or committed as an NGRI, incompetent
to stand trial individual or a sexually dangerous person, or was in a correctional
or mental institution 30 days or less, process as indicated in the chart below:
ACTION
PUPS PROCESSING ACTION
INPUT DATA
SELECT
Type of Action
2 - CLEAR CONTROL--NO SUSPENSION NECESSARY
POST
Conviction Date
Post conviction date if beneficiary was convicted and released before remaining in
jail 30 continuous days or less or given time already served.
Do not post a conviction date for individuals not convicted of a crime; e.g., NGRIs,
etc.
POST
Release Date
Date beneficiary was released from the institution.
POST
Remarks
Explain the reason for the "NO SUSPENSION" clearance in the PRMK screen on the PUPS
record.
