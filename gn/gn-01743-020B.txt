B. Eligibility requirements for workers
1. Retirement Benefits (Age Pension)
To qualify for Australia's Age Pension, a person must:
•
be at least age 66 years and six months (increased to age 67 on July 1, 2023); and
•
have at least 10 years residence in Australia between age 16 and minimum retirement
age, including a continuous period of at least five years (some exceptions apply).
2. Disability Benefits (Disability Support Pension)
To qualify for Australia's Disability Support Pension, a person must:
•
be between age 16 and the minimum retirement age;
•
be permanently blind or have a physical, intellectual, or psychiatric impairment;
•
be (1) unable to work or (2) unable to be retrained for work at least 15 hours per
week within two years due to the impairment; and
•
be an Australian resident for at least 10 years, including a continuous period of
five years; there is no minimum residence requirement if the disability occurs while
the person is a permanent resident of Australia (some exceptions apply).
