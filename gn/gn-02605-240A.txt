A. Use of direct deposit – general
Direct deposit is the preferred manner of payment to all Title II beneficiaries. It
is a cost effective and secure manner to disburse Social Security payments to beneficiaries
both within and outside the United States.
Beneficiaries outside the United States may receive their benefits via a form of electronic
funds transfer (EFT) when they use:
•
Direct deposit to a Financial Institution (FI) within the U.S.;
•
Direct deposit in any International Direct Deposit (IDD) country; or
•
Payment through correspondent banking.
