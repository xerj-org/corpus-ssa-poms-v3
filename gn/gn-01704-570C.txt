C. Requesting certified earnings record for totalization
Follow the steps below when requesting certified ERs for totalization:
1.
SSA Main Menu -  Enter “09” (Master File Query) press enter
2.
Master File Query Menu (MFQM) – Enter “23” ICERS (Info/Cert Earnings Records), press
enter
3.
Informational/Certified Earnings Record Menu (ICMN) – Enter NH’s SSN and “1” (Certified
Earnings Record for Totalization) press enter
