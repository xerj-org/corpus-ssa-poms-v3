A. Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request)
1. When to use the Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request)
Field offices (FOs) use the Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) to transmit Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS) prisoner
case information to the payment centers or program service centers (PSC/OCO-ODO) when
prisoner-processing issues occur. These processing issues include:
•
PUPS prisoner suspension exclusions,
•
PUPS/Post-entitlement Online System (POS) reinstatement exclusions, or
•
Title II Redesign (T2R) PUPS suspension exceptions.
Prepare a Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) to refer a Title II beneficiary’s prisoner
suspension or reinstatement processing action to PSC/OCO-ODO for the associated systems
issues listed on the chart.

2. PSC/OCO-ODO responds to SSA-2790
When the PSC/OCO-ODO technician receives the Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request),
they review the completed information on the SSA-2790, the beneficiary’s PUPS record,
and the PUPS T2R systems exception. For PSC/OCO-ODO SSA-2790 exclusion and exception
processing instructions, see GN 02607.995C in this section.
