G. Federal court previously decided fugitive felon status issue
If you learn of a Federal court decision on a class member case concerning payment
of SSI and a PPV warrant, refer the case to your Regional Fugitive Felon Coordinator
(RFFC) who will contact the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) for advice on whether
it precludes giving Clark relief. For a list of RFFCs, refer to GN 02613.990.
