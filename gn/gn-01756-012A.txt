A. Process to file a claim for U.S. benefits
When a person contacts the Hungarian liaison agency to file a claim for U.S. benefits,
the liaison agency sends the following information to the Foreign Benefits Unit (FBU)
in Kraków, Poland:
•
Form SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3, (U.S.-Hungary Transmittal/Request/Certification) certifying
the filing date for the claim;
•
Hungarian certified coverage record HU-205;
•
supporting evidence for non-disability claims; and,
•
available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
