D. Policy Principle — Refusal to Reopen Is Not an Initial Determination
A denial of a request to reopen a determination or decision under the rules of administrative
finality is not an initial determination and does not give the claimant the right
to appeal. The appeal rights paragraph should not be included in the notice to the
claimant.
