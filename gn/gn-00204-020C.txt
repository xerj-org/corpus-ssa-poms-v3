C. Scope of the application examples
1. Application for Retirement Insurance Benefits (RIB) (Form SSA-1-BK)
John, age 64, files an application for RIB. They states that their spouse died. The
RIB application covers widower's benefits and the LSDP on their spouse's SSN.
2. Application for Lump Sum Death Payment (LSDP) (Form SSA-8-F4)
James, age 62, files an application for the LSDP on their deceased spouse's account.
The LSDP application covers widower's benefits on the deceased NH’s record but does
not cover RIB on their own SSN.
