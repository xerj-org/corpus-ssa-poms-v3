SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03905.010 - Form SSA-1699 Representative Registration
Effective Dates: 11/01/2024 - Present | TN 16 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Representatives use Form SSA-1699 to register with us. Starting September 30, 2024,
anyone who has not previously registered with us and who wants to be appointed as
a representative or serve as a Point of Contact (POC) to an entity must register with
us using the Form SSA-1699 before we will recognize a new appointment request or allow
the entity to register.
For more information on the registration process, see Social Security
Online - SSA-1699 Representative Registration. For more information on the POC, see GN 03913.005. For more information on processing Form SSA-1699, technicians should see GN 03913.012
Processing Form SSA-1699, Representative Registration.
