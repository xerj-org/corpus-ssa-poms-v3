D. Charges Paid by More Than One Person
If the services have been paid for by more than one person, each payer is entitled
only to the person's own proportionate share of the Medicare reimbursement. Where
one (or more) of the group has died, the individual's share of the payment is settled
under GN 02335.025 B.
