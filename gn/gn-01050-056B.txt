B. Background on processing dually entitled paper and fully electronic disability claims
Dual entitlement claims (paper and electronic) are currently split by OHO and sent
to the PC of jurisdiction, as reflected in the OHO Case Processing Management System
(CPMS), for each claim. The instructions given below should be followed when OHO issues
hearing decisions on dual entitlement claims and sends the claims to separate PCs.
There is no need to send the paper folder to the other PC unless the technician in
that PC requests it.
