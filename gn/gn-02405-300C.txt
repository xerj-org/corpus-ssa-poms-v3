C. Remittance Clerk Procedure
The remittance clerk must follow the procedures for endorsed and unendorsed benefit
checks returned to the Field Office (FO) that Treasury issued and are more than one
year old in accordance with the following table.
STEP
ACTION
1
Request the Field Office Daily Receipt Listing (FODRL) at the end of the day and associate
the returned check along with the other remittances. (See MS DMS 003.002, MS DMS 004.006,
and MS DMS 004.009 for how to obtain the FODRL.)
2
Ensure that the information on the FODRL matches the attached returned checks and
other remittance items.
3
Sign the FODRL on the Remittance Clerk line, and hand carry the FODRL, related remittances,
and queries to the remittance supervisor.
