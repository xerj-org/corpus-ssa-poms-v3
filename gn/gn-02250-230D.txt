D. Authorization to obtain financial account information
On page 8, we included the Authorization for the Social Security Administration to
obtain Account Records from a Financial Institution and Request for Records. In most
situations, the overpaid individual must provide their authorization for us to obtain
financial account information whenever we need the information to make a defeats the
purpose determination, or in some circumstances, to make a deemed to defeat the purpose
determination. To determine when someone other than the overpaid individual must provide
their authorization, refer to GN 02250.100E.2.
