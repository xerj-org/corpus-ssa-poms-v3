SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.110 - How to Credit Chilean Coverage
Effective Dates: 02/27/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Chilean coverage certification is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage
to the U.S. coverage record.
•
There is no minimum amount of Chilean coverage that must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
•
All periods of coverage certified by Chile are creditable unless a remark on the Form
CHI/USA 5 brings the creditability into question.
•
Refer any questionable Chilean coverage records to CPSI for evaluation.
