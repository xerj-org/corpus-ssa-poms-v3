SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.165 - Entitlement Requirements - Child Adopted by Someone Other Than NH
Effective Dates: 10/13/2005 - Present | TN 29 (10-05)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306165 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
A natural or legally adopted child of the NH who was adopted by another person during
the NH's lifetime (see GN 00306.165B.) is the NH's child for benefit purposes only if:
•
The adoption did not cut off the child's inheritance rights in the NH's estate under
applicable State law; and
•
The NH was living with or contributing to the child's support at one of the points
set forth in GN 00306.007 or GN 00306.008.
This provision applies even if the adoption is terminated before the child applies
for benefits. The provision would not apply if the adoption has been revoked, i.e., treated under State law as if it had
not occurred.
NOTE: Adoption by someone other than the NH does not terminate a child's entitlement. If
the adoption occurred before the child's application is filed, but the application
has retroactivity to a point before the adoption at which all entitlement requirements
are met, the adoption has no effect on determining the child's entitlement.
