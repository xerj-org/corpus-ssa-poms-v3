SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.205 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Luxembourg
Effective Dates: 09/27/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Claims packages are prepared to request Luxembourg coverage records and to inform
the Luxembourg agencies about claims for Luxembourg benefits. The application and
evidence required for the packages are described in GN 01733.205 - 01733.215.
