C. Procedure for verifying posting of a limited payability credit on SSA’s record
Treasury sends SSA a limited payability credit when they have no record that the beneficiary
or recipient cashed the check. SSA then posts the credit to the record. When a beneficiary
or recipient indicates that they have not received the check, the technician will
verify the posting of the limited payability (LP) credit.
For instructions on how to proceed when credits are involved, see details in this
subsection.
NOTE: A LP credit can only post to an SSA record at least 13 months after the check’s issue
date.
