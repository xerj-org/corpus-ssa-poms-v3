D. Requesting BC - Claimant Resides Outside the U.S.
These cases will be handled in much the same manner as those in which the claimant
resides within the U.S.
OIO will request the Foreign Service Post (FSP) servicing the area in which the birth
record may be located to obtain a certification of the claimant's record.
NOTE: It is assumed that few, if any, of these claimants will be residing in the country
of their birth. However, if the claimant is so residing, ask him/her to obtain the
birth record.
