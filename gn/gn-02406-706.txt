GN 02406.706 Regional Financial Centers (RFCs) Issuing SSI Payments
Determining the issuing RFC for Title XVI checks
The Payment Integrity and Resolution Services, formerly the National Payment Integrity
and Resolution Center (NPIRC), is the consolidated nationwide service center for all
misdirected payment activities and the backup site to the Federal Disbursement Services,
formerly the National Payment Center of Excellence (NPCE), for payment disbursement
and print production.
The Payment Integrity and Resolution Services and the Federal Disbursement Services
both print and mail Title XVI checks. The first digit of the recipient’s mailing address
ZIP code printed on the check will help determine the issuing Regional Financial Center
(RFC). If the ZIP code begins with "4", then the first two digits will be considered.
NOTE: Forward any returned checks to the Payment Integrity and Resolution Services regardless
of the ZIP code displayed. The Federal Disbursement Services no longer processes returned
checks.
