SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.245 - Waiver of Recovery of Overpayments
Effective Dates: 06/06/2024 - Present | TN 22 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. EXAMPLE
A has been overpaid. The amount ($500.00) posted to ROAR includes the $372.50 which
he has been asked to repay and an administrative adjustment of $127.50.
A determination is made to waive recovery of the $372.50. The SSA-635 shows this as
the overpayment amount for which recovery is waived. When action is taken to delete
the $372.50 overpayment from ROAR, the $127.50 administrative adjustment is also deleted.
