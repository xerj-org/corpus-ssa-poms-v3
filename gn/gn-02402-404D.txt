D. Overview of IDD for Ecuador
IDD payments sent to Ecuador must be in U.S. dollar (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Ecuador. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
