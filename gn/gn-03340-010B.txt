B. Procedure for obtaining guidance on access requests
If you receive an individual's request for access to his or her records and you need
additional guidance, request assistance as shown in the following chart:
Component
Contact
Field offices (FO)
Regional Office Privacy Act Coordinator (RO PAC)
Program Service Centers (PSC)
PSC or RO PAC
Office of Central Operations (OCO)
Operations Analysis (OA) staff
Central Office (CO)
Office of Privacy and Disclosure (OPD)
NOTE: The RO PAC, PSC, or OA should consult with OPD on any concerns with Privacy Act requests,
or if the request contains a high risk of litigation.
