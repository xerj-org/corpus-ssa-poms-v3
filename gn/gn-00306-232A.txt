A. Policy — entitlement for July 1996 or later
A stepchild becoming initially entitled for July 1996 or later must have been receiving
at least one-half support from the NH at one of the points listed in GN 00306.007 or GN 00306.008.
“Living-with” will not be an option for proving dependency. This change results from
section 104 of P.L. 104-121. See RS 01301.010 for how to determine one-half support for a stepchild.
NOTE: The one-half support requirement does not apply to stepchild reentitlements actions
when the reentitlement criteria in RS 00203.015A.1. are met.
