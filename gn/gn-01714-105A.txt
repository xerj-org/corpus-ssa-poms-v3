A. Policy
Belgian coverage certifications are issued by the Belgian agency that has jurisdiction
of the claim. Coverage certifications sent to DIO by any of the Belgian agencies contain
a complete record of an individual's work in Belgium.
Each of the agencies and its branches uses a slightly different coverage form. The
forms may be in Flemish or in French. However, the basic coverage information on all
of the forms is the same.
In invalidity (disability cases), Belgium credits periods of coverage for years after
the onset of disability up to retirement age. This is shown on the coverage certification
as “nonwork” coverage, and it may be used by SSA.
