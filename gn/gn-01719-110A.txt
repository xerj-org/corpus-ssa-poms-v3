A. Benefit programs included under the U.S.-U.K. Agreement
The provisions of the Agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and U.K. coverage credits apply to the following types of benefits:
•
U.S. retirement, survivors, and disability benefits. The Agreement does not apply
to U.S. Medicare benefits, or Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments.
•
U.K. retirement, survivors, and disability benefits. The Agreement does not apply
to other contributory benefit programs such as national health insurance.
