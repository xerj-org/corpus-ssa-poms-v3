E. Procedure – Death Certificates
1. Office to Contact
a. Death Before 7/1/91
Parish of residence at the time of death.
b. Death After 6/30/91
Local tax office of the tax authority (Skatteverket) where the death occurred. (See NOTE in GN 00312.382B.1.)
2. Fee
No fee.
