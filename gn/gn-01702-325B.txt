SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.325 - Summary of Field Office Development and Processing of U.S. Totalization Claims and Foreign Claims (Regular and Totalization)
Effective Dates: 03/03/2025 - Present | TN 11 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702325 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Filing for foreign benefits only
•
Take the appropriate application form(s) (see GN 01702.120).
•
Request a certified U.S. earnings record for the foreign country (see GN 01702.215C.3.).
•
Accept any evidence the applicant wishes to submit.
•
Forward the claim to the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO) at
the address in GN 01702.310B.4.
•
Complete the Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the date the claim
was forwarded to OEIO. Show “Totalizatio” as the ISSUE when completing the MDW screen.
2. Filing for U.S. benefits only
•
Take the appropriate application form(s) (see GN 01702.120).
•
Develop evidence as required.
•
Process award or denial (see GN 01702.310 — GN 01702.315).
•
Forward claim to OEIO or domestic program service center (PSC) as appropriate (see
GN 01702.310).
3. Filing for both U.S. and foreign benefits
•
Take the appropriate application forms (see GN 01702.120).
•
Develop evidence as required.
•
Request a certified U.S. earnings record for the foreign country (see GN 01702.215C.3.).
•
Process award or denial of the U.S. claim (see GN 01702.310 — GN 01702.315).
•
Forward the claim for foreign benefits to OEIO at the address in GN 01702.310B.4.
•
Complete the MDW screen to show the date the claim was forwarded to OEIO. Show “Totalizatio”
as the ISSUE when completing the MDW screen.
•
Forward the claim for U.S. benefits to the domestic PSC or OEIO at the address in
GN 01702.310B.4 as appropriate.
