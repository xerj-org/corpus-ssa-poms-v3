SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.030 - Child Born of Voidable Marriage
Effective Dates: 12/08/2023 - Present | TN 108 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Marriage Not Annulled
A child born of a voidable marriage which has not been annulled is legitimate in all
States.
2. Marriage Annulled
If a child was born of a voidable marriage which has been annulled, the invalid ceremonial
marriage provision or section 216(h)(3) of the Act may apply. If not, and you have
no precedent opinion, submit the case to the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) per
GN 01010.815 ff. for an opinion on the child's legitimacy.
