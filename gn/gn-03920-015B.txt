B. Policy – fee petition - PC recommends fee over $15,000 - Attorney Fee Officer review
required
If a PC proposes to authorize a fee (or total of fees) of more than $15,000, based
on a fee petition(s) or on a request for administrative review of the fee amount authorized
based on a fee petition, the Attorney Fee Officer (AFO) must review the recommendation
(Form SSA-1178 - Evaluation of Fee Petition for Representation).
