Effective Dates: 02/01/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201748210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and Danish or Danish claim only
Prepare an SSA-2960 (USA/DK3) to:
•
Request the Danish coverage record if one has not been requested, include a copy of
the SSA- 2960 (USA/DK3).
•
Send the Danish Agency the information or documentation they have requested.
