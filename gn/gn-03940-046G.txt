G. Procedure — Fee Agreement Disapproved
If the decision on the claim is favorable and the decision maker (Title XVI CR at
the initial or reconsideration level, or the OHO decision maker at the hearing level,
or OAO decision maker at the AC level) disapproved the fee agreement, take no further
action on the fee agreement.
Refer to  MS 00302.014  for fee agreement disapproval coding procedures. Also, because
the representative may file a fee petition, refer to GN 03930.030.
