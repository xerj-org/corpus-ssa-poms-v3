C. Procedure if DCN alert displays
The DCN alert message at the bottom of the screen is not proof of DCN abuse. The system
does not contain enough information to make a fully automated DCN abuse determination.
Based on the limited information available, it can only alert you to the possibility of DCN abuse. The DCN decision is that of the interviewer, and, if applicable the
second reviewer.
When the system displays the alert, investigate the possibility of DCN abuse before
selecting the action. For more information on DCN abuse, see GN 02406.150.
