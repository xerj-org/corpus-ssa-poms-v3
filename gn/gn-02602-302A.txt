A. Procedure for child’s marriage termination
If a payee or beneficiary contacts SSA in response to a termination for marriage,
Code T-3:
•
obtain a statement of the child's marital status.
•
advise the Payment Center (PC) to reinstate benefits if the explanation is sufficient
to establish that continuing entitlement exists.
