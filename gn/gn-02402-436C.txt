SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.436 - Coding French Polynesia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 02/21/2023 - Present | TN 232 (02-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402436 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in French Polynesia has a prefix of 72 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “M” assigned for French Polynesia.
