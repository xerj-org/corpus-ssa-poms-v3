SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.070 - Requirements if the Claimant Has a Representative
Effective Dates: 11/09/2011 - Present | TN 17 (11-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Information a representative may furnish
A representative may furnish and be furnished information to the same extent as the
claimant.
•
Always contact the representative, rather than the claimant, unless we have the representative's
permission to contact the claimant directly.
•
Mail the notice of award or disallowance directly to the claimant with a copy to the
representative.
