SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.015 - Receipt and Routing of Requests for Administrative Review Under the Fee Agreement Process
Effective Dates: 12/09/2024 - Present | TN 13 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A party is encouraged to send a request for administrative review to the component
with jurisdiction to review (POMS GN 03960.005). However, all components will promptly route erroneously filed requests for administrative
review to the component with proper jurisdiction to process the review.
