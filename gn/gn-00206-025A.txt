A. Policy on advising claimants on VA or RRB benefits
When discussing Social Security Administration (SSA) programs with the claimant, do
not:
•
Advise the claimant about VA or RRB benefits, or explain the provisions of any benefit
program we do not administer.
•
Comment on or question a claimant's understanding about information received from
the VA or RRB unless it involves incorrect, incomplete or misleading information about
our program(s).
