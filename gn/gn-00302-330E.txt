E. OIO Handling of Requests for Birth Record
Step
OIO Actions
1
Upon receipt of an SSA-562-U3, ask the appropriate FSP to obtain a BC.
2
Show the following information on each request to the FSP:
•
“The cost for securing this document should be charged in accordance with 4 FAM 335.,”
and
•
“Holocaust survivor.”
3
Continue normal development procedures until either the certification or negative
response is received.
NOTE: Refer to International Program Policy and Totalization Staff, International Policy
Staff, Office of Policy (IPPTS, IPS, OP) any case where it appears impossible to get
either a birth record recertification or a negative response after 180 days.
4
Forward the certification or negative response to the requesting FO upon receipt.
