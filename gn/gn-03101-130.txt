GN 03101.130 Appeals of Effect of Appellate Process on Prospectivity of Application
An application for monthly benefits filed before the first month that meets all the
requirements for entitlement remains valid if the applicant meets those requirements
before we make a hearing decision. (For information on application filing date, see
GN 00204.007.)
