SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02413.002 - Determining the Acceptability of Gifts to the Government
Effective Dates: 05/13/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202413002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating Procedure
EVENT
ACTION
Case 1
Donation due to rumors concerning the lack of soundness of trust funds.
Explain trust funds are secure. Accept if individual insists.
Case 2
Donation by representative payee.
Develop payee suitability. Accept if suitable.
Case 3
Donor is an SSI recipient.
Make personal contact. Advise consequences of excess resources. Accept if individual
insists. Do a redetermination per SI 02305.001 ff. (Also consider transfer of resources below fair market value).
Case 4
Donation for a State supplement.
Explain that SSA administers State supplements but does not accept gifts for them.
