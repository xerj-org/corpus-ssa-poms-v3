A. Compulsory coverage
In general, Chilean Social Security covers all employees working in Chile, including
foreign nationals. Currently, self-employed individuals may contribute on a voluntary
basis. However, Chile will gradually extend mandatory coverage to some self-employed
persons from 2012 to 2015. Separate, special systems cover the armed forces and the
national police.
