B. Description of eligibility requirements
1. Old-age retirement benefit
To qualify for an old-age benefit, a worker must:
a.
be age 63 with 20 years of service (age 62 with at least 20 years of service if born before January 1, 1952); or
b.
be age 62 with 15 years of service for a partial benefit.
NOTE: Women with 40 years of service may be eligible for an old-age retirement benefit
regardless of age.
2. Disability benefits
To qualify for disability benefits, a worker must have a loss of at least 40 percent
of working capacity, and must:
a.
have been insured for at least:
•
1,095 days (3 years) within 5 years;
•
2,555 days (7 years) within 10 years; or
•
3,650 days (10 years) within 15 years before submitting a claim;
b.
not have performed any gainful activity in Hungary or abroad; and
c.
not receive any regular cash benefits.
3. Survivor benefits
Hungary pays survivor benefits for the following:
•
surviving spouse's benefit (including divorced spouse and domestic partner);
•
parental allowance; or
•
orphan’s allowance.
