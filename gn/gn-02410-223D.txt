D. Procedure for garnishment due to court ordered victim restitution
Garnish for court ordered victim restitution through COGS. See Garnishment System
Table of Contents, SM 00832.000.
1.
The Consumer Credit Protection Act (CCPA) only permits garnishment at a maximum of
25% of a beneficiary’s monthly benefit amount (MBA) for court ordered victim restitution
cases. This will require a review for possible adjustment to COGS whenever the continuing
benefits change to a lower or higher rate. Manually enter the withholding amount in
COGS. See How Garnishment Withholding Is Calculated,GN 02410.215.
2.
In the Garnishment Payment Data (OGPD) screen (see SM 00832.260 COGS Processing of
the Garnishment Payment Data (OGPD) Screen) enter the following.
a.
Enter an “N” in the “ARREARS 12 WEEKS OR GREATER (Y/N)” field.
b.
Enter the withholding amount (not to exceed 25% of the MBA) in the “OTHER” entry field.
NOTE: When you must apply the 25% limit (court ordered restitution garnishment exceeds 25%
of the MBA), round down to the next dime.
EXAMPLE: MBA = $ 974.60, divided by 4 = $243.65 (25%), round down to $243.60.
c.
Enter “Victim Restitution” in the “SPECIFY (OTHER)” field.
3.
Add a Special Message to the MBR to state, “Garnishment Order Involves Court Ordered
Victims Restitution.”
4.
COGS issues all notices for court ordered victim restitution garnishment.
