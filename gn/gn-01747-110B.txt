B. Policy — Taxes Included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Danish Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors and disability benefits,
unemployment, sickness, maternity, medical, work accident, and family allowance benefits.
