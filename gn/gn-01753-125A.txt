A. Minimum coverage requirement for Slovak benefits
The agreement modifies the insured status requirements of the Slovak social insurance
system. Under the agreement, if a worker has at least 12 months of Slovak coverage,
but not enough to receive an old-age benefit, the Slovak Republic will credit 90 days
of coverage for each quarter of U.S. coverage SSA certifies.
