E. Policy — When not to reopen based on new and material evidence
A written request received from the claimant within 4 years does not constitute new
and material evidence for reopening unless it indicates that there is a basis for
disagreement. The supporting evidence need not necessarily accompany the written request.
The decision could be reopened if evidence which is later presented or uncovered as
a result of additional development meets the definition of “new” and “material” as explained above.
