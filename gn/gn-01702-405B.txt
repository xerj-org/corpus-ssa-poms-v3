SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.405 - Associating Evidence From Prior Disability Folders
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702405 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Associate a prior title II folder or photocopies of medical evidence from a prior
title XVI folder where the applicant is filing for U.S. Totalization disability benefits
and;
•
the number holder (NH) has at least 6 quarters of coverage (QCs), and
•
the prior folder is in the servicing field office (FO) or another FO.
Do not associate a prior title II folder or photocopies of medical evidence from a
prior title XVI folder if the NH has fewer than 6 QCs, the folder is not in an FO,
or the applicant is filing for foreign benefits only.
