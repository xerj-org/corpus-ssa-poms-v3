SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01749.001 - Overview of Agreement with the Czech Republic
Effective Dates: 03/12/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201749001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Purpose of this subchapter
This subchapter:
•
gives a brief overview of the Czech system,
•
explains the provisions of the agreement, and
•
provides instruction on how to take and process claims for both U.S. and Czech benefits
filed under the agreement.
Additional information on the Czech Social Security system can be found at the Czech
Social Security web site which is accessible through Social Security Online at http://www.socialsecurity.gov/international/links.html
