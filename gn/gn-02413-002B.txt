SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02413.002 - Determining the Acceptability of Gifts to the Government
Effective Dates: 05/13/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202413002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Related Operating Procedure
1. QUESTIONABLE OR UNRESOLVED SITUATION
Refer a questionable situation or unresolved issue, e.g., an unacceptable anonymous
gift, to the ARC, Programs.
2. OUT OF SERVICE AREA
Refer an out-of-service-area donor to his/her servicing FO for development and processing
of the gift.
3. A BEQUEST
Refer a donor who offers a bequest to his/her attorney to have a will drawn up.
4. GIFT TO GENERAL FUND
Refer a donor who offers a gift to the General fund to the Treasury Department.
