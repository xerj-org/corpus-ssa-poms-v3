SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01713.140 - Exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision Under the Belgian Agreement
Effective Dates: 02/12/2003 - Present | TN 3 (01-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201713140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
appears to fall into one of the above categories and alleges residence in Belgium,
verify that the exemption provided by the agreement applies by developing the following:
1. Residence status,
2. Refugee or stateless person status, if the claimant alleges such status, or in
the case of an auxiliary or survivor it is alleged that the worker is (or was at the
time of death) a refugee or stateless person, or
3. Citizenship of the worker, if a dependent or survivor alleges that the worker is
(or was at the time of death) a U.S. or Belgian citizen.
