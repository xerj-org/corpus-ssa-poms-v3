C. Policy — Dependents of retired or disabled workers
There is no provision under either the Social Insurance Institution (Zaklad Ubezpieczen
Spolecznych-ZUS) or the Agricultural Social Insurance Fund (Kasa Rolniczego Ubezpiecenia
Spolecznego-KRUS) for benefits for family members or children of the insured person.
These benefits are granted and paid by municipal institutions.
