GN 01706.315 Italian Liaison Form (IT/USA 5)
When a claim is filed outside Italy, OIO initiates communications by sending INPS
the U.S. liaison form (USA/IT 3), the U.S. earnings record, the application and any
evidence. INPS may respond by completing the liaison form IT/USA 5 which provides
information about the entitlement or nonentitlement of all claimants to regular and
totalization benefits from Italy and an Italian coverage certification if it was requested
by the United States.
