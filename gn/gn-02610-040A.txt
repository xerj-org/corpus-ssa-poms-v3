Effective Dates: 08/16/2023 - Present | TN 33 (08-23)

A. Introduction
After computing title II offset for a couple using the couple's SSI rate per GN 02610.035, determine the fraction of each member's retroactive benefits in the offset period
to the couple's total retroactive benefits to compute the individual's share of the
offset amount.
