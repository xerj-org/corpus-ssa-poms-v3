G. Use of SSA's appointment scheduling systems to protect date of written acknowledgment
An initial claim appointment record established for the child in SSA's appointment
scheduling system, e.g., eLAS, can protect the date of written acknowledgment if:
•
the NH is shown as the person who contacted SSA to schedule the appointment to apply
for benefits for the child;
•
an intent to file for the child is shown, e.g., child is listed by name; and
•
there is no question that the child or children for whom the application is filed
is the same as the child or children referred to on the appointment record.
