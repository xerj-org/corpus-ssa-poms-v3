E. Description of Oman IDD bank data
The data to code IDD for Oman comes from the International Bank Account Number (IBAN)
provided to the beneficiary from their FI. The IBAN for Oman consists of up to 23
characters. The bank data on the MBR reflects 27 characters including the country
code of H..
