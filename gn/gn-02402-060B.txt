B. Policy for direct deposit to Totten trusts or similar accounts
Direct deposit of a beneficiary’s or recipient’s benefits into a Totten trust or accounts
similar to Totten trusts, as described in Part A of this section, does not constitute
an assignment of benefits because the depositor (grantor) retains legal ownership
and control of the benefits after they are deposited into the account. Therefore,
direct deposit into this kind of account may be approved.
