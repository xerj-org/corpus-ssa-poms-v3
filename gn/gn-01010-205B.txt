B. MCS EC processing claim types
Refer to MS MCSEC 001.001C. for a complete list of the types of claims that can be
fully processed to payment, denial, disallowance, abatement or pre-effectuated withdrawal
using MCS EC screens.
For a list of EC processing limitations and messages, see MS MCSEC 001.036 and MS
MCSEC 001.001.
