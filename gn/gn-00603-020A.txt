A. Collective account overview
We may allow representative payees (payees) to “collect” Social Security benefits
or Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments for multiple beneficiaries and recipients
in one deposit account. We call this a collective account. Organizations, agencies,
and individual payees establish and use this “collective” checking or savings account
to minimize costs and streamline maintenance. Some payees may have more than one collective
account. All collective accounts must comply with our policies, and the payee must
obtain our approval before using the account.
