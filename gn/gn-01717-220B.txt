B. Process
The special application forms are not taken in the field offices (FO’s). After receiving
a claim for Swedish benefits that was filed with SSA, the Swedish agency will mail
the appropriate Swedish application to the applicant. The Swedish agency will include
instructions asking the claimant to complete the forms and mail them directly to the
Swedish agency with any additional evidence that may be needed.
