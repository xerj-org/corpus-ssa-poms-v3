SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.413 - Coding Uruguay Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/13/2019 - Present | TN 136 (05-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402413 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Uruguay will have a prefix of “71” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “V” assigned for Uruguay.
