A. Filing appeals of decisions on U.S. or Portuguese claims
The agreement provides that persons who wants to appeal a decision on a claim for
U.S. or Portuguese benefits may file the appeal with either country. See GN 01702.500 - GN 01702.505 for instructions on filing an appeal of a decision on U.S. or Portuguese claims.
