SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.555 - Obtaining Evidence from District Offices (DOs)
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704555 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
If the source of the required documentation is in the United States and direct contact
does not apply (see GN 01070.100), request the assistance of the appropriate DO by completing a Form SSA-4766-U3 as
follows.
Determine which office(s) is (are) in a position to be most responsive to the request,
considering the residence of the claimant and the location of the source of the record.
