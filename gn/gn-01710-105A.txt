A. Policy - Coverage certification
Swiss coverage is generally certified on the computer produced format shown in GN 01710.105E. below in the French language. Sometimes, certifications in German are received. Although
the Swiss agreed to certify only in French, we can accept the German language certifications
if they appear to be complete and authentic.
