SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.060 - Transitional Rules for Applying the Substantial Presence Test in 1985 and 1986
Effective Dates: 08/01/2008 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definition
A U.S. resident for tax purposes prior to 1985 was an alien actually present in U.S. (50 States, D.C.,Guam and NMI), not as a transient
or sojourner.
NOTE:  Lawful admission for permanent residence (or even lawful admission) was not necessary.
However,the alien must have resided in U.S. with an intention of establishing at least
a temporary home in this country.
