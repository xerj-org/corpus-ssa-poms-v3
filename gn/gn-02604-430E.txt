E. Preparing administrative sanction notices
1. What actions to take when preparing notices for Category 2 cases
•
prepare the appropriate notice(s);
•
send copies of the notice(s) to the CTE or other designated employee for review;
•
send the administrative sanction notice(s) and saves the notice(s) to the electronic
folder only after the CTE completes their review;
•
prepare separate notices for Title II and Title XVI benefits in concurrent cases;
and
•
update the AS Tool accordingly.
2. What actions to take when preparing notices for Category 3 cases and cases with a
combination of Category 2 and Category 3
•
prepare the appropriate notice(s);
•
send copies of the notice(s) to the RSC for review;
•
send the administrative sanction notice(s) and saves the notice(s) to the electronic
folder only after the RSC completes their review; and
•
update the AS Tool accordingly.
