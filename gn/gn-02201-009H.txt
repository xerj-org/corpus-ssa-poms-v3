SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02201.009 - Notification of a Title II Overpayment
Effective Dates: 09/30/2026 - Present | TN 86 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202201009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Failure to give adequate notice
If we did not send a notice, the content of the notice did not comply with subsection
GN 02201.009B, or the individual did not receive notice at least 30 days before adjustment action,
send a new notice with all of the required information specified in subsection GN 02201.009B. Repay the individual any money that we withheld to recover the overpayment. Update
the overpayment balance and the recovery date another COM plus 2 months.
