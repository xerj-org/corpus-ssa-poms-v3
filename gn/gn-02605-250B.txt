B. Foreign to Foreign COA – Same Foreign Country
No change will be made to the alien nonpayment coding or nonresident alien tax withholding
when the original address and the new address are within the same foreign country
unless the beneficiary alleges that the information has changed.
Secure supporting documentation for all changes alleged.
