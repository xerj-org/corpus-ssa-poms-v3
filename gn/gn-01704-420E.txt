E. U.S.-Belgian Agreement
Only one application has been devised in connection with claims filed under the U.S.-Belgian
agreement. (See GN 01713.215.)
1.
SSA-799 (Application for Benefits Under the U.S./Belgian Agreement)—This form is not
needed by the Belgian agencies.
In addition to the SSA-799, the following Belgian forms have been devised to process
claims under the U.S.-Belgian agreement.
2.
BE/USA 3 (Transmittal/Request/Certification Form)
3.
BE/USA 5 (Belgian Coverage Certification Form (Manual Certifications Only))
4.
BE/USA 7 (Consent Form)
