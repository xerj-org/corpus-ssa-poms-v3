SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.335 - Coding Trinidad and Tobago Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/19/2016 - Present | TN 260 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402335 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contact
The processing FI in Trinidad and Tobago will have a prefix of “70” at the beginning
of the Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “R” assigned to Trinidad and Tobago.
