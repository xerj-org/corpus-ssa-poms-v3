GN 02315.091 Saipan, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands
There does not appear to be any authority for SSA to make payments other than pursuant
to a court order (8 N. Mar. I Code § 2501 et seq.). The small estates statue does not contain a provision for collection of debts by
use of affidavits.
