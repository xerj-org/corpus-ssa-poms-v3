E. Description of Martinique IDD bank data
The data to code IDD for Martinique comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by their FI. The IBAN will be 27 characters. However,
the bank data reflected on the MBR will consist of 26 characters. Technicians will
use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
