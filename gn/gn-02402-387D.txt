D. Overview of IDD for Macedonia
IDD payments sent to Macedonia must be in U.S. dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Macedonia. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
