SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.449 - Coding Papua New Guinea Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 03/03/2021 - Present | TN 185 (03-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402449 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Papua New Guinea will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning
of the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account
Number (DAN) field has the letter “A” assigned for Papua New Guinea.
