D. Procedure – How to Retrieve an Accounting Report from WBDOC
Copies of imaged accounting reports are available from WBDOC. For example, you can
telephone, fax or email your request to WBDOC and you must provide them with certain
identifying information such as CAN/SSN, BIC, Payee Name, etc. This same identifying
information is required when requesting the “paper” report (see GN
00605.055B for retention of paper accounting reports).
