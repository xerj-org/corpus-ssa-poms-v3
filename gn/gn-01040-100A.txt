A. Introduction to listing codes
We provided the listing code chart as a convenience and general aid to field office
(FO) and processing center (PC) personnel. Do not use this chart as the sole source
to determine when a case should be listed. Instructions in Program Operations Manual
System (POMS), or other materials, are the basis for preparing a listing. Be sure
to refer to those instructions whenever a listing is prepared.
It is not possible to update this chart immediately after the addition of new codes
or the deletion of obsolete codes. If you use the chart as a complete reference, you
must update it as changes are issued in POMS or other material. We will update this
chart periodically.
