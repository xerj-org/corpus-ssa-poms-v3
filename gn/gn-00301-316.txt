GN 00301.316 The Purpose of Non Disability Repository
•
The purpose of the NDR is to store documents and forms (referred to as artifacts)
in that cannot be stored or recorded in existing systems such as MCS, MSSICS, or Shared
EVID.
•
Artifacts placed in the NDR will be viewable through CFUI using the Evidence Portal.
•
The NDR in the Evidence Portal is a repository in DMA created specifically to house
descriptive information about a particular artifact as well as to retain a picture
of the artifact. The required information will be:
–
Claim filing: The claim file record the artifact belongs to.
–
Document type: The specific document type (form or non-form) that describes the artifact
and an optional document description.
