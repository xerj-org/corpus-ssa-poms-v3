D. Documenting the Fee Authorization
The fee authorizer will complete Form SSA-1178 (Evaluation of Fee Petition for Representation)
or an equivalent document at the hearing and Appeals Council levels.
On the Form SSA-1178, or equivalent, the fee authorizer will record the amount of
the authorized fee and provide a detailed explanation of how they arrived at the fee
amount. The explanation will reflect their decision based on each of the factors described
in GN 03930.010B. in this section.
