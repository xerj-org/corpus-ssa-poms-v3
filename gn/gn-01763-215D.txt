D. Procedure for entering the Icelandic Social Security Identification Number
Enter the worker and each claimant's Icelandic Kennitalia Number on all claims. If
the number is unknown, enter all of the following information under Part I of Form
SSA-2490-BK:
•
worker’s full name, including both the paternal and maternal last names (see GN 00302.460 and GN 00302.470): and
•
worker’s date of birth;
NOTE: The Icelandic agency will need this information to transmit the NH’s Icelandic coverage
record.
