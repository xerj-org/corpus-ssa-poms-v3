SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.485 - Evidence Establishes Only Age
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302485 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Rules
IF the age shown on the document is...
AND...
THEN...
Consistent with the DB alleged
There is no evidence to the contrary
Consider the age to have been given as of the last birthday.
Consistent with the DB alleged only if it was given as of the next birthday
The difference between the 2 possible years indicated by the document is not material
Do not undertake further development to resolve the difference.
There appears to be a reasonable explanation as to why the age might have been given
as of the next birthday in the particular document involved
Do not undertake further development to resolve the difference.
NOTE: If the difference is material, write a determination showing this explanation.
