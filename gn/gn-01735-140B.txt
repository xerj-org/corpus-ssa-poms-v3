SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01735.140 - Exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision under the Irish Agreement
Effective Dates: 01/23/2003 - Present | TN 1 (01-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201735140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
alleges residence in Ireland, develop residence status to verify that the exemption
provided by the agreement applies.
