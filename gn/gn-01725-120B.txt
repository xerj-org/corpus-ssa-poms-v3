B. Policy - Crediting dutch coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. quarter of coverage for every 3 months of Dutch coverage
in a calendar year. (Dutch coverage is measured in months.) However, no credit will
be given for:
•
any month in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. quarter of coverage,
or
•
more than 4 quarters of coverage for any calendar year.
