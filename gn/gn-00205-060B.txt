B. Procedure when auxiliary or survivors claimant is near age 62 or older
If the claimant has a spouse with 120 or more months of RR service, or effective January
1, 2002, has less than 120 months of RR service but has at least 60 months of RR service
after December 31, 1995, get the spouse's Social Security Number (SSN) for certification
purposes. These questions are part of the ADDB screen on MCS claims and propagate
to the CLRR screen.
