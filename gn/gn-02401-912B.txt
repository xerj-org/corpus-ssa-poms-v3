SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02401.912 - Forwarding the Handwriting Analysis Package to the Questioned Documents Branch (QDB) for Review
Effective Dates: 01/15/2025 - Present | TN 52 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401912 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to forward for handwriting analysis
Forward the letter to Treasury for handwriting analysis if the responses on the CIPS
letter indicate the beneficiary, recipient, or payee did not:
1.
receive the check,
2.
endorse the check (i.e., not the beneficiary or recipient signature),
3.
cash the check,
4.
give permission for another person to cash the check,
5.
benefit from proceeds of check (not a misuse issue), or
6.
have access to the account where the check was deposited.
If the beneficiary, recipient or representative payee answers yes to any of the statements
in 1 through 6 as shown in GN 02401.912B in this section and on the CIPS letter, follow instructions in subsection GN 02401.912C in this section.
