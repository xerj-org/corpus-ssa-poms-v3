SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01733.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Luxembourg Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 4 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201733225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of Luxembourg claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Luxembourg benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded a claim to the Luxembourg agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show DIO forwarded the claim to Luxembourg
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Luxembourg agency, advise
the applicant to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO _ DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Luxembourg
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Luxembourg agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Luxembourg agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Ministère de la Sécurité Sociale
(Ministry of Social Security)
•
Sécurité Sociale (Social Security)
NOTE: DIO is unable to determine the status of a Luxembourg benefits claim after a
technician forwards the claim to Luxembourg. For more information about status requests
for Totalization claims, see GN 01702.520.
