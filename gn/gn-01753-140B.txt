B. Procedure for applying the exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the Alien Nonpayment Provision
in RS 02610.010, alleges citizenship of or residence in, the Slovak Republic, the FO must develop
citizenship or residency status to verify that the exception provided by Slovak citizenship
or residency applies.
NOTE: For exceptions to the additional U.S. residency requirements for alien dependents
and survivors under the Slovak Agreement see GN 01753.145.
