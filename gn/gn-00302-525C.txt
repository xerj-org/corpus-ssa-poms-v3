C. How to assist claimant in securing religious records
Step
Action
1
Ask the claimant if he/she ever participated in a recorded religious ceremony.
​
• If yes, go to step 2.
​
• If no, use another source of evidence.
2
Ask the claimant at approximately what age the ceremony occurred.
3
Explain that we are requesting this information only to allow us to assist in securing
evidence of age.
4
•
Use Form SSA-L706, which specifically asks for the date the record was established,
to assist the claimant in requesting a certificate of the religious record (see GN 00302.070).
•
In the absence of information to the contrary, assume that a domestic religious record
of birth or baptism was registered shortly after the event or ceremony.
•
See GN 00307.100 regarding foreign documents.
