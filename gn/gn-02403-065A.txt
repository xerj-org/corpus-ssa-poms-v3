A. Process - Resolving FORT Discrepancies
When a FORT/CCFORT and corresponding refunds are received in the MATPSC, the items
are compared for discrepancies. If all information on those items agrees and is correct,
no FO action is required. If the data on the FORT/CCFORT does not agree with the remittance,
see GN 02403.065B.
