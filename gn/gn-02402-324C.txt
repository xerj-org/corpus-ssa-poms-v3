SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.324 - Coding Haiti Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/22/2026 - Present | TN 325 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402324 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix of
"70" at the beginning of the routing number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor
Account Number (DAN) field has the letter L assigned for Haiti.
