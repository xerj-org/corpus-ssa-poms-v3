B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
Contact the Bermuda Registry General for birth, marriage, and death records. Contact
the Registry of the Supreme Court for divorce records.
1. Office to Contact
Bermuda Registry General
Government Administration Building
30
Parliament St.
Hamilton HM12
Registry of the Supreme Court
Old Town Hall
113 Front
St.
Hamilton HM11
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Names of the parents, including the maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Certificate Registration Number (optional)
•
Whether the birth was a maritime birth (i.e., occurred on-board a Bermuda registered
ship)
3. Fee
•
Birth Certificates (BCs), Short Form - $29.00 U.S.
•
Long Form BCs - $34.00 U.S.
•
Death Certificates - $34.00 U.S.
•
Divorce - $15.00 U.S.
•
Marriage Certificates - $36.00 U.S.
