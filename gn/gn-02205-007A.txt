A. Joint and several liability
Joint and several liability is the equal responsibility (shared liability) for repayment
of an overpayment by two or more people. Each person also has to be independently
primarily liable for the overpayment. If two or more people are jointly liable for
an overpayment, then each person has full responsibility for the overpayment. This
situation may arise when we pay a beneficiary’s payments to a representative payee.
