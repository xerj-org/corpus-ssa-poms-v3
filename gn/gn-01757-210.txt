GN 01757.210 Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Brazilian Benefits
Under the agreement with Brazil, we accept applications for both regular and totalization
benefits on behalf of Brazil and provide limited assistance to obtain evidence to
support the claim (see GN 01757.125).
