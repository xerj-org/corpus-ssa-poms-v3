B. Surviving spouse information and selection
Each month the Regular Transcript Attainment and Selection Pass (RETAP) program selects
young surviving spouses eligible for the Recontact process based on their date of
birth (DOB), date of current entitlement (DOEC), and NOTICE indicator in the Recontact
(RECNTACT) data line on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR). Surviving spouses receive
the initial mailing in the same month in which they first became entitled and in year
1, 2, 3, 6, and 9 of their entitlement.
The Recontact (RECNTACT) Data Line on the MBR controls the automatic RETAP selection
and records the status of the recontact process. For more detailed information about
this process, see SM 03020.230.
