SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01713.215 - Application for Benefits Under the Belgian Agreement
Effective Dates: 04/24/2014 - Present | TN 3 (01-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201713215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An individual who wishes to file for benefits under the agreement with Belgium must
complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social
Security Agreement). This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only
from the United States, only from Belgium or from both countries.
