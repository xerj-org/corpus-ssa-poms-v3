F. Processing foreign garnishment orders
We will make a determination on all garnishment orders served on SSA by foreign courts.
Garnishment orders issued by foreign courts do not generally constitute legal process
under the Act unless the U.S. has entered into an agreement with a foreign country
requiring the U.S. to honor that country's legal process. See GN
02410.211.C for the list of countries with agreements with the U.S. Because additional agreements
could be made at some future date, send a copy of all foreign court orders not currently
listed to:
OISP,
