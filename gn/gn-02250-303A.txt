A. Introduction
This section provides guidance on when to make a new waiver determination after an
individual makes a subsequent waiver request of an overpayment for which we previously
made a waiver determination. This section also provides guidance on when to apply
res judicata to deny a subsequent waiver request after we previously made a waiver
determination on a prior waiver request.
