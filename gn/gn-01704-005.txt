GN 01704.005 Processing Cases Involving Totalization
Totalization impacts several areas which are the responsibility of the direct action
clerk. These areas are discussed in greater detail in the sections which follow. However,
unless special processing instructions are provided in this chapter, all cases should
be processed in the normal way.
