SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03104.380 - Processing Subsequent Application (FO/PC)
Effective Dates: 06/18/2025 - Present | TN 18 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203104380 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure for both titles
1.
If the subsequent application results in a favorable initial or reconsideration determination,
immediately send an email to the Office of Appellate Operations (OAO) at ^DCDA
OAO. The subject line should read FAVORABLE SUBSEQUENT APPLICATION. Include any relevant
information in the text of the message including the claimant's name and Social Security
Number and attach a copy of the SSA-831, if any.
For a subsequent favorable claim that is electronic, see DI 12045.027D.2.b.
This will provide the Appeals Council (AC) with advance notice that a subsequent favorable
determination is on the way.
2.
Route claims file material to OAO electronically (via email or by uploading material
in eView, Evidence Portal (EP), or Electronic Non-Medical (ENM), whichever is appropriate).
Paper claims folders should only be mailed to OAO upon request. Questions regarding
proper procedures regarding routing can be forwarded to ^DCDA
OAO.
3.
If the subsequent claim is denied and the claimant appeals, process through normal
appeal procedures, see GN 03104.380D in this section.
4.
If the determination on the subsequent claim was favorable, the date of eligibility/entitlement
can be no earlier than the day after the date of the administrative law judge (ALJ)
decision.
5.
If the AC takes favorable action on the prior claim, notify the appropriate adjudicating
component to stop processing the subsequent claim.
If the AC remands the prior claim, notify the appropriate adjudicating component to
stop processing the subsequent claim and to forward the claim file to the hearing
office (HO).
Forward any new evidence sent from the AC to the appropriate adjudicating component
when the AC denies or dismisses the request for review on the prior claim.
