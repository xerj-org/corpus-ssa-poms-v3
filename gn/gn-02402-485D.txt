D. Overview of IDD for Zambia
IDD payments sent to Zambia must be in Zambian Kwacha (ZMV) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to OEIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Zambia. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
