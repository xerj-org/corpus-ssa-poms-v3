1. Office to Contact
Bureau de l'Etat Civil of the locality where the event occurred
The address for the civil registry office of each city/commune can usually be found
on its website. Type in the name of the city commune followed by “.lu” (e.g., http://www.luxembourg-city.lu or http://www.diekirch.lu )
