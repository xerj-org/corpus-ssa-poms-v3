SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.320 - Routing of Claims for Foreign Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Develop and route claims for benefits from a foreign country as follows:
•
Take application(s) and develop evidence as appropriate.
•
Request a certified U.S. earnings record for the foreign country as explained in GN 01702.215C.3.
•
Forward claim for foreign benefits to OIO at the address in GN 01702.310C.
Complete the Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the date the claim
was forwarded to OIO. This is for informational purposes only—no Tickle date is necessary.
Show the local field office unit designation as the UNIT and “Totalizatio” as the
ISSUE when completing the MDW screen.
