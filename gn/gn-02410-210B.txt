B. Responding to garnishment inquiries
Beneficiaries may contact SSA for information regarding garnishment cases.
1.
For general inquiries by the garnished beneficiary to verify a garnishment order (e.g.,
the amount of withholding, the effective date), FO or PC technicians and National
800 Number Network (N8NN) agents can answer questions by accessing the EP. Technicians
can print or access the garnishment documents for review in EP.
2.
For inquiries questioning a garnishment order, (i.e., the garnished person denies
knowledge of a garnishment order), refer those questions to the issuing court or SDU.
3.
Refer any inquiries involving access to personally identifiable information (PII)
to the issuing court or SDU.
