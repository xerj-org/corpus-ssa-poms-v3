A. Appeals (Paper)
If an appeal is pending on a paper application, the artifact can be stored in the
Non-Disability Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRed) using the Evidence Portal
(EP). This will ensure that the artifact is preserved in the appropriate claim filing.
The artifact must then be sent to the appropriate appeal office to associate with
the pending appeal. This will ensure that the artifact is exhibited properly.
