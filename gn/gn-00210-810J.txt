J. Regional Office (RO) Instructions for Handling Inquiries
RO Support Staff should contact the Office of Public Service and Operations Support
(OPSOS) for any additional guidance.
Direct all program-related and technical questions to your RO Support Staff or Program
Service Center (PSC) Operations Analysis (OA) staff. RO Support Staff or PSC OA staff
may refer questions, concerns or problems to their Central Office contacts.
