M. Accuracy of the correction is questionable
Use the following procedure to establish the DB when you have determined that the
evidence supporting the “correction” is insufficient to determine that the correction was accurate.
Step
Action
1
Treat the BC as originally recorded and the amended BC as separate documents
NOTE: The “corrected” record will be considered to have been established as of the date the correction
was made.
2
Was the original record established before age 5?
​
• If yes, go to step 3.
​
• If no, go to step 4.
3
Treat the original record as preferred evidence and stop.
4
Resolve the material discrepancy between the original record and the amended record.
NOTE: Best evidence (see GN00302.160ff.) may be:
•
BC as originally recorded, or
•
BC as amended, or
•
Other evidence of age.
If a document listed on the BC as supporting evidence for the corrected entry appears
to be the best evidence, obtain the document.
5
Prepare a written DB determination.
