B. Types of coverage
1. Compulsory contributions
In general, Austrian social security covers all persons who work in Austria. However,
not all workers contribute to every program. For example, students who work contribute
only to the Worker's Compensation program and people who receive a social insurance
pension contribute only to health insurance.
Covered employees, their employers, and covered self-employed persons are subject
to compulsory contributions that are based on a percentage of their earnings.
2. Voluntary contributions
Austria permits people who are not subject to compulsory contributions and who meet
certain qualifying conditions to make voluntary contributions to build future benefit
rights (e.g., self-employed persons).
