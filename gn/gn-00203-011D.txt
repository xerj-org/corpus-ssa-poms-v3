SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00203.011 - Special Interviewing Situations: Limited English Proficiency or Language Assistance Required
Effective Dates: 09/18/2024 - Present | TN 80 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200203011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure for identifying the need for an interpreter
Interviewers should follow these steps.
1.
Be alert to the language needs of any individual having difficulty understanding or
speaking English and be sensitive to the cultural differences that could affect the
interview.
2.
Determine whether the individual wishes to conduct the interview in English or what
language other than English they prefer to use;
•
If available, offer to obtain the services of an in-office interpreter prior to an
interview if an appointment is being scheduled for a future in-office interview.
•
Offer to obtain an interpreter by telephone through the nation-wide TIS if the future
appointment is a telephone interview, or you need immediate service.
3.
Provide an interpreter when the individual requests one, whenever it is difficult
to understand the individual, or when it is evident that language assistance is needed
to ensure that the individual is not disadvantaged, even if the individual does not
request an interpreter.
4.
Use the following option when you cannot identify the individual's preferred language.
•
For in-office interviews, use the interpreter poster or language card available in
all field offices and at the Multilanguage Gateway Internet. The interpreter poster
and language card explain SSA's interpreter policy in many languages and may be helpful
in identifying the individual's preferred language.
5.
For information inquiries, provide available pamphlets and fact sheets in the language
that the individual prefers. See the Multilanguage Gateway on the SSA website. These sites are updated frequently and provide expanded information
about SSA's interpreter services and materials available in languages other than English.
6.
If the individual has access to the Internet and materials are available for their
language, refer the individual to the Multilanguage Gateway on the SSA website.
7.
Assure the individual that SSA will provide them with quality service.
8.
Inform them that SSA will provide interpreter services free of charge.
9.
Follow instructions in GN 00203.011H if the individual prefers to use their own interpreter.
