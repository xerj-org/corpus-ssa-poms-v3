GN 01758.105 Brazilian Certified Coverage Record
Policy for forwarding certified coverage records
The Brazilian certified coverage record is the Form BR/USA 6 (Certificate Concerning
the History of Insurance in Brazil). It is the only acceptable form for the certification
of the Brazilian coverage record. Forward certified coverage records in any other
format to the Division of Training and Program Support for approval.
