SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03980.002 - Bribery of Public Officials and Witnesses
Effective Dates: 09/25/2018 - Present | TN 1 (09-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203980002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Responsibilities of employees
1.
All SSA officials and persons who have been selected to be SSA officials are prohibited
from, directly or indirectly, demanding, seeking, receiving, accepting, or agreeing
to receive or accept anything of value, personally or for any other person or entity,
in return for:
a.
Being influenced in the performance of any official act; or
b.
Being influenced to commit or aid in committing, or to collude in, or allow, any fraud,
or make opportunity for the commission of any fraud, on the SSA or the United States;
or
c.
Being induced to do or omit to do any act in violation of the official duty of such
official or person.
2.
Otherwise than as provided by SSA as compensation for performing official duties,
SSA officials, former SSA officials, and persons who have been selected to be SSA
officials are prohibited from, directly or indirectly, demanding, seeking, receiving,
accepting, or agreeing to receive or accept anything of value for or because of any
official act performed or to be performed by such official or person.
