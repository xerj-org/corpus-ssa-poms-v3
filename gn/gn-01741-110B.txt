B. Social security taxes under the Agreement
The provisions of the Agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to the following:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA), including the Medicare portions of those
taxes; and
•
Korean social security taxes that finance retirement, survivors, and disability programs;
and
•
The Korean industrial accident program.
