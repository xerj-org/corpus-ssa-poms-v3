E. Description of IDD bank data for Chile
The FRBKC relies on us to provide the complete bank data to ensure correct posting
of payments to a customer’s account.
If the information is incomplete, the FRBKC rejects the payment and returns the funds
to us. The bank data on the MBR reflects 27 characters including the country code
of U.
