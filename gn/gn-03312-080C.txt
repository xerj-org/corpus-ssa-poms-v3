C. Request for name, when only the SSN is provided
We restrict providing names to match an SSN to the most sensitive law enforcement
situations or health and safety situations when the requester provides no other identifying
information (e.g., date of birth, place of birth, mother’s maiden name, or father’s
first and last name). Refer law enforcement requests of this type to the component Privacy Act Coordinator for handling and copy the Office of
Privacy and Disclosure .
