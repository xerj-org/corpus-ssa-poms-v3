3. Alcoholism and Drug Abuse Prevention (ADAP) patient records
The Public Health Service (PHS) regulations (42 CFR 2.31) prohibit us from disclosing ADAP records without specific written consent from the
individual to whom the records pertain. For more information about ADAP records and
the related consent requirements, see GN 03305.030.
