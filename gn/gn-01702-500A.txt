SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.500 - Filing Appeals of Decisions on U.S. or Foreign Claims
Effective Dates: 04/08/2011 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702500 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Each agreement provides that individuals who wish to appeal a decision on a claim
for U.S. or foreign benefits may file the appeal with either country. Thus, an individual
may file an appeal of a decision on a claim for foreign benefits with the Social Security
Administration (SSA), or file an appeal of a decision on a claim for U.S. benefits
with the foreign agency.
