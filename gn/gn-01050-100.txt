GN 01050.100 General - Transfer of Claims Folders
Several situations can cause a change in the jurisdiction of the claim and result
in the transfer of that folder to another office. Some of these are as follows: A
change in the beneficiary's mailing address and/or place of residence; the attainment
of age 54 of a disabled beneficiary; the subsequent entitlement of another beneficiary;
or subsequent entitlement of a beneficiary to another type of benefit. The actual
transfer procedure is as outlined below.
An overpayment or a request for reconsideration will be handled by the office having
jurisdiction. If jurisdiction changes prior to completion of all actions, the office
assuming jurisdiction will be responsible for the completion thereof. The transmittal
form should be appropriately annotated to ensure that the office receiving the claims
folder takes all proper action on the case.
