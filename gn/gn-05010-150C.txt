SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.150 - Protests on the Withholding Tax Status or the Tax Amount
Effective Dates: 08/01/2011 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Process
OIO reviews the claims file and “Alien Tax Protest” material. It changes the AWSC if the evidence supports a change and notifies the
beneficiary.
If the evidence is not sufficient to change his withholding status, OIO advises the
beneficiary of this and his right to go to IRS for a ruling on his status.
