A. Old social insurance system
The Superintendence of Social Security oversees the social insurance system. This
traditional government-administered system financed by employee and employer contributions
pays retirement, survivors and disability benefits. Chile bases these benefits on
a worker’s average earnings and the length of coverage. For the purposes of the agreement,
Chile classifies workers according to three categories:
1.
salaried employees;
2.
wage-earners; and
3.
public employees and journalists.
