SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.205 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Spain
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use these instructions for preparing claims packages.
1. U.S. Claim
Prepare a claims package only if a Spanish coverage record is needed. Include the
following:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP.
•
Form E/USA 2 (Statement of Working Life).
•
Spanish Navigation Book (if it was submitted with form E/USA 2).
2. U.S. and Spanish or Spanish Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP.
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
NOTE: Enter “form USA/E4” in the upper right hand corner of the earnings record.
•
Form E/USA (Spanish Application Form)
•
Spanish Navigation Book (if it was submitted with form E/USA).
•
Medical evidence from OIO files if the Spanish claim is for disability benefits.
•
Any additional evidence that the claimant has submitted for the Spanish claim.
