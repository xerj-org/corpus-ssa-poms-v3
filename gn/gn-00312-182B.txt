B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Office of the Mayor where the event took place for birth, marriage, and
death records. Contact the district court where the divorce was granted.
For birth, marriage, and death records:
•Identify the correct district.
•Address the request to the mayor of the city.
•Include a self-addressed envelope.
Persons residing outside the United States contact the Hungarian Embassy (or the designated
representative) in the country of their residence.
In the United States, these are the:
Embassy of the Republic of Hungary
3910 Shoemaker St., N.W.
Washington D.C. 20008
Consulate General of the Republic of Hungary
223 East 52nd St.
New
York, NY 10022
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth, including the district number if born in Budapest
•
Names of the parents, including the parent's maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Address at the time of birth
3. Marriage records
Include the following information in requests for marriage certificates:
•
Names of both parties (including the spouse's maiden name)
•
Date and place of marriage (including the district number if married in Budapest)
4. Death records
Include the following information in requests for death certificates:
•
Name of deceased
•
Date and place of death (including the district number if death was in Budapest)
5. Fee
None.
