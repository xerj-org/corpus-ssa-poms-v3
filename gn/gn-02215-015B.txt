B. Operating Procedure
1. LIFE CASES
For the $30 or more debt:
a.
Provide the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) with a complete explanation of the overpayment
and any other pertinent information, including a report of our efforts to collect.
b.
In representative payee situations, where both the payee and the beneficiary are in
the same FBU jurisdiction, furnish the FBU with forms for both persons and ask the
FBU to obtain all information requested from each person.
c.
If appropriate, use Sample Letter for Request for State Department's Assistance to
Recover an Overpayment on Form SSA-1320-U4 (GN 02280.700).
d.
Enclose the SSA-3560, Instructions Regarding Overpayments of Social Security Benefits
(GN 02280.030). Advise typist to enter appropriate claim number(s) in item three of the SSA-3560.
e.
Diary and follow-up in accordance with GN 00904.245.
If the FBU's efforts to recover are unsuccessful, terminate collection action (ROAR
TC 71).
2. DEATH CASES
a.
Request FBU assistance where the overpayment is $3,000.00 or more, unless there is
information available to establish there is no estate.
b.
If the representative of the estate has made refund reducing the overpayment to less
than $3,000.00, make no further collection efforts to recover from the estate. However,
do not terminate efforts to recover by adjustment from benefits payable to other persons
on the same earnings record (GN 02210.015).
c.
In known estate cases, the request for FBU assistance should contain:
•
A letter of explanation of the overpayment (including a report of our efforts to obtain
refund), and
•
A request that the FBU contact the representative of the estate to obtain refund.
d.
Include a complete explanation of the overpayment in those letters in which we are
seeking information about the existence of an estate.
e.
Request the FBU to make a demand for refund of the overpayment if they find there
is an estate and the representative is identified.
