A. Explanation of Box 2 and how it differs from Box 8
The SSN shown in Box 2 is the beneficiary’s and is a mandatory entry. It is possible
that the SSN in Box 2 will differ from the claim number shown in Box 8 of the benefit
statement. This is because the claim number in Box 8 is the number from which benefits
are paid.
