A. Process
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the District Court in the county where
the parties filed for divorce for $10.00 a certified copy. Marriage records are available
from the Clerk of the District Court in the county where the license was issued for
$9.00 a certified copy.
Copies of birth or death records may be obtained from the Clerk of the District Court
of the county where the event occurred. For birth or death records in Minneapolis
or St. Paul, write to the Vital Records Office of the city where the event occurred.
All counties charge $11.00 for a birth certificate and $8.00 for a death certificate.
NOTE:
Minnesota Statutes restrict access to a certified birth or death certificate to a
person who has a tangible interest in the requested certificate. A document request,
including from a federal or state agency, must be notarized. For more information,
use the link in GN 00308.048B.1.
