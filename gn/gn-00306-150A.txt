SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.150 - Advice About Adoption
Effective Dates: 11/23/1998 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure — general
Follow these guidelines:
•
Use great care in giving advice on adoptions so you do not imply that SSA encourages
them. No one can be sure that adoption in a specific case will be granted, and adoption
has legal consequences other than entitlement to Social Security benefits.
•
Explain only the provisions of the Act. You can make a final determination as to a
child's entitlement only after adoption is complete.
•
Do not give advice on adoption laws in specific States even upon request.
