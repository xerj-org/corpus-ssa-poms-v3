A. Introduction
GN 01702.310 and GN 01702.320 provides routing instructions for claims for U.S. totalization benefits filed by
persons who are either insured based on U.S. coverage only or who have less than 6
U.S. QCs. These claims are rightfully the jurisdiction of a PSC, ODO, or modules 1
- 5 and not the totalization modules. The following procedure covers situations in
which these types of claims are routed to the totalization modules in error.
