SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.560 - Family Bible or Other Family Record
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302560 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A Bible or other family record of age or DB made at or near birth has high probative
value. However, study has indicated that it is often difficult to determine when a
person's age or DB was entered into such a record. Therefore, there are special documentation
requirements to assist the DO in evaluating such records.
