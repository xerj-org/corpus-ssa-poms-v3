SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.030 - Developing Lay Evidence of Capability
Effective Dates: 05/10/2023 - Present | TN 98 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. If you obtain a signed statement from a third-party
•
Store a copy of the court order in NDRed under the beneficiary's claim number using
EP or in eView under the BOAN using EP or the eView application; and
•
Note in the “Report of Contact” in eRPS, MCS, or CCE that a third-party signed statement
used in your capability determination, per GN 00502.065, is in NDRed or eView. This includes if you obtain the SSA-788 per GN 00502.030C.
