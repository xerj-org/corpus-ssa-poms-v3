B. Residence exemption from alien nonpayment under the agreement
In addition, under section 233(c) (2) of the Act, certain beneficiaries are exempt
from the payment restrictions described in section 202(t)(1) of the Act when they
reside in a country with which the United States has a totalization agreement. See
GN 01701.150A.1.
The exemption under the Chilean agreement applies to residents of Chile who receive
regular or totalized retirement, survivors, or disability insurance benefits.
