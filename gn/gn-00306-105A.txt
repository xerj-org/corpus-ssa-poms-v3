SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.105 - Section 216(h)(3) – Federal Standard for a Parent-Child Relationship Based on Written Acknowledgment
Effective Dates: 06/11/2026 - Present | TN 145 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for a section 216(h)(3) child based on number holder’s (NH) written acknowledgment
When a child is the NH’s biological child, the child may satisfy the relationship
requirement under section 216(h)(3) of the Social Security Act (Act) if, during the
NH’s lifetime, the NH acknowledged the child as their child in a written statement.
The child must be conceived (in utero) or born at the time of the acknowledgment.
NOTE: Section 216(h)(3) requires that the child must be the NH's son or daughter. Even
if the NH acknowledged the child in writing, you must consider evidence showing that
the NH is not the child's biological parent. For reasons to doubt biological relationship,
see GN 00306.105E.
