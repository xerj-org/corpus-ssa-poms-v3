SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02335.040 - Special Provisions Regarding Development of Underpayment Claims from Legal Representative
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 2 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202335040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Evidence Requirement
In addition to the general provisions in GN 02335.030, a copy of the order of appointment should be submitted with the request for payment
filed by a legal representative under the following circumstances:
1.
Where the physician has died before an SMI payment due to the physician is completed,
and the legal representative of the estate is claiming payment (see GN 02335.020 D.);
2.
Where services have been paid for by the beneficiary before death or with funds of
the beneficiary's estate, and the legal representative of the beneficiary's estate
is claiming payment (see GN 02335.025 A.l). A copy is not required if the proof has already been submitted to the carrier
or DO in connection with another claim (including a claim for monthly benefits). If
a copy of the appointment was previously received in the DO and receipt of the proof
annotated on an SSA-250, this fact shown over the signature of a staff member on a
report of contact will suffice in lieu of a copy of the appointment papers.
