H. Appeals and MOEL changes
A beneficiary receives a notice informing them about the MOEL change. This notice
contains appeal language and provides 60 days to submit a written request. Accept
a Form SSA-561-U2 (Request for Reconsideration) or any other written request (e.g.,
letter, facsimile, or email) if it clearly implies disagreement with the new MOEL
date.
Process the appeal request as follows:
1.
Review the information provided in the beneficiary’s appeal to determine if the MOEL
appeal can be approved (e.g., the AUX MOEL is determined by the NH’s new MOEL).
2.
If we approve the appeal, change the MOEL as requested.
3.
Document the beneficiary’s MOEL decision in the Paperless Action Control Record (ACR)
and set it to “long-term retention”
4.
If we cannot approve the MOEL change request, deny the appeal and document the rational
in the ACR.
5.
Prepare a reconsideration notice of determination (see GN 03102.425).
