C. Individuals who have access rights
Only the following individuals have access rights, or are legally authorized to act
on behalf of the subject of the record, under the Privacy Act and our regulations:
•
The NH who is the subject of the record (i.e., whose record we retrieve by their personal
identifier);
•
A birth or adoptive parent or legal guardian acting on behalf of a minor child, who
is the subject of the record; and
•
A legal guardian acting on behalf of an individual, who is the subject of the record,
who has been declared to be incompetent due to physical or mental incapacity or age,
by a court of competent jurisdiction.
