SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.420 - Coding Fiji Islands Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/12/2024 - Present | TN 250 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402420 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Fiji Islands has a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “J” assigned for Fiji Islands.
