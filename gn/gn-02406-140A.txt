A. When to obtain a non-certified photocopy
You may obtain a non-certified photocopy of a check from the Treasury Check Information
System (TCIS) when a claimant alleges nonreceipt for a check that is more than 13
months old. For more information on limited payability, see GN 02401.905 through GN 02401.917.
