SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01715.145 - Exception to Section 202(t)(11) Alien Nonpayment Provision for Dependents and Survivors Under the Canadian Agreement
Effective Dates: 04/05/2004 - Present | TN 6 (10-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201715145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If an alien dependent or survivor, who does not meet the 5-year residency requirement,
alleges he or she is a citizen or resident of Canada, develop citizenship or residence
status.
