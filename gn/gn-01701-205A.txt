SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.205 - Determining Benchmark Year in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 09/22/2022 - Present | TN 12 (09-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The benchmark year in Totalization claims is determined in the same manner as for
non-Totalization claims. When a Totalization benefit is payable and, subsequently,
a non-Totalization benefit becomes payable, the period of entitlement to Totalization
benefits must be considered when determining the benchmark year for the non-Totalization
benefit.
