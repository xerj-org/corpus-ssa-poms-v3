SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in Chile
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and CHILEAN or Chilean claim only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
A Chilean coverage record is required and has not been requested, or
•
The Chilean agency has requested information or documentation.
Include Form SSA-2960-CHI and the information or documentation requested by the Chilean
agency.
