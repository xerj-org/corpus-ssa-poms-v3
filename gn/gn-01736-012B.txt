SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.012 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with the Irish Agency
Effective Dates: 01/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
SWS.
1. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications and forward
the claim to OIO with the form IRL/USA 3 and the Irish coverage record, if available.
2. Claimant already entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all material to OIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
