A. Policy for appointed representatives
If the claimant has executed Form SSA-1696-U4, or equivalent statement, appointing
either an attorney or a non-attorney representative, all contacts must normally be
made through the representative (see GN 03910.050) until such time as the representative submits a fee petition or otherwise terminates
their services.
