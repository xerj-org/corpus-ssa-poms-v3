D. Definition — Recalculation
A Recalculation:
•
Is any change to earnings for the years used in the initial base years (1951-year
before month/year of entitlement) resulting in a change to the PIA.
•
Is NOT an initial determination used to count the time for reopening. It is ALWAYS
a revised initial determination. The time for reopening a revised initial determination is
counted from the date of the notice of the initial determination (see GN 04001.040).
•
Can result in an increase or decrease in the PIA.
•
Can be effective in any month of the year.
•
Can include an increase in DRCs.
See RS 00605.401 for additional information on Recalculations.
NOTE: If a recalculation is based on a change in date of birth for a beneficiary, follow
RM 10212.200 – Changing Numident Data-Other than Name Change, to develop evidence of age and process
an SS-5 to correct the date of birth shown on the beneficiary’s Numident record.
