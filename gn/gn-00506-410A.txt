A. Policy
Sections 205(j)(1)(B) and 1631(a)(2)(A)(ii)(II) provides that the specified policies
apply if the individual is entitled to title II benefits or eligible for title XVI
benefits "by reason of disability" and the individual also has a DAA condition. As
explained in DI 90070.060, in order for the "special" policies concerning DAA condition individuals to apply,
including the higher representative payee fees, the individual must meet the following
three conditions. They must be entitled to disability benefits, must have a DAA "condition",
and must be incapable of managing their benefits.
