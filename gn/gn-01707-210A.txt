A. Policy
1. General
Under the agreement with Germany, SSA will take applications for both regular and
Totalization benefits on behalf of Germany and provide limited assistance in obtaining
evidence needed to support the claim (see GN 01707.210A.3. and GN 01707.210A.4.).
2. Using U.S. evidence
Evidence which is used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits may also be used by Germany to establish the same factor for German benefits.
Consequently, it is unnecessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence to
document a factor of entitlement for German benefits when that factor has already
been verified for SSA purposes.
