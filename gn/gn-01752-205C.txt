C. Procedure – Claims packages for U.S. Claims/Republic of Poland Totalization Benefits
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-e2960–U3–PL to request a Polish certified coverage record (PL/USA 6) if needed
and to certify the filing date for Polish benefits, establish factors of entitlement,
and U.S. benefit information.
•
A U.S. totalization earnings record.
•
Medical evidence from SSA’s files, if the Polish claim is for disability benefits.
In addition, if a completed physician’s medical evaluation report (PL/USA 7) is not
included, follow up with the claimant 7 days after receipt of application. Tickle
for 53 days and mail package if not received when tickle is due.
•
Any necessary proofs and evidence.
•
Letter to claimant advising them that their claim was forwarded to the ZUS /KRUS Agency
if claim is for Polish benefits only.
