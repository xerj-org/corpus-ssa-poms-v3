SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01753.020 - Slovak Old-Age Benefits Eligibility Requirements
Effective Dates: 07/28/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201753020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Types of Slovak retirement benefits
1. Old-age
To qualify for old-age retirement benefits, a worker must meet certain age requirements:
•
men must be age 62; for women, the age varies depending on birth year and number of
children; and
•
have 15 years of contributions.
2. Early retirement
To qualify for early retirement benefits, a worker must:
•
be no more than 2 years from retirement age;
•
have at least 15 years of coverage under the first pillar system;
•
cease all work activity; and
•
qualify for an early retirement pension that is at least 1.2 times the legally defined
subsistence wage.
Additionally, from September 1, 2012, contributors to the second pillar fully funded
contributory system must:
•
have contributed to that system for at least 5 years; and
•
qualify for an early old age pension that is greater than 0.6 times the legally defined
subsistence wage.
