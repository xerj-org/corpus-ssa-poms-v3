SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.295 - Nonessential Material to be Omitted from Title II and Title XVI Claims Folders
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | TN 15 (01-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301295 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure — material to be excluded /purged from claims folders
Purge and exclude the following:
1. Notes, drafts, etc.
a.
Initial notes or rough drafts made for the convenience of the FO.
b.
Route slips, such as Form SSA-408, etc.
c.
RC's recording follow-up efforts made for evidence that is now in the claims folder
or letters requesting evidence now in file.
d.
Signed authorization forms (such as the SSA-827, SSA-4641-U2, SSA-8510, etc.) which
have not been used.
2. Duplicate copies
a.
Multiple photocopies of proofs.
b.
Carbon copies of multiple-page forms.
c.
Photocopies of material already in file.
d.
Computer output material on the right side of the folder which has no future documentation
value. In Title II claims retain only the latest computer output (such as MBR, ROAR,
etc.) in file.
e.
Extra copies of RC's.
3. Mailings
a.
Blank forms or blank questionnaires returned to the FO.
b.
Envelopes not required to establish a filing date or necessary for other documentation
purposes.
c.
Copies of routine development correspondence with claimants or third parties. (This
does not include correspondence which might be considered an application or might
be important to a determination.)
