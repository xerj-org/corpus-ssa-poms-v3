B. Procedure – field office requests
When an applicant expresses a desire to withdraw an application for foreign benefits,
obtain a signed statement giving the reason for the request and forward it to the
Division of International Operations (DIO) at the address in GN 01702.310C.
Advise the applicant that either DIO or the foreign agency will notify the applicant
about the disposition of the request. DIO will send the notification unless it has
already sent the application to the foreign agency, in which case that agency will
notify the applicant.
Exception: When a request for withdrawal of a claim for Canadian benefits is filed in a designated
border office (see GN 01715.320) and the claim has already been forwarded to a Canadian agency, forward the request
to the same agency rather than DIO.
