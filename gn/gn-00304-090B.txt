B. Procedure for completing form letter SSA-L4159
1. Preparing the SSA-L4159
To prepare and forward the form letter
a.
Enter the originating office's address and telephone number in the upper right hand
corner;
b.
Enter the name and address of the law enforcement agency or official in the normal
letterhead address space;
c.
Enter the name of the deceased, the date and place of death, the decedent's Social
Security number and the claimant's name in the space provided; and
d.
Sign the name of the originating claims representative.
2. Mailing form letter SSA-L4159
Take the following actions to mail the form letter SSA-L4159:
a.
Complete identifying information on the form and send it by SSA-562 U3 (Request for
Assistance/Certification of Document(s) or Record(s)) to the parallel FO if the law
enforcement official is outside the service area; and
b.
Mail the form in a window envelope directly to the law enforcement official. Enclose
a postage-paid self-addressed return envelope.
