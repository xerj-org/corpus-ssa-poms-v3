D. Definition of Parent
A person will qualify as a parent of the deceased beneficiary if the individual:
1.
Would have the status of parent with respect to the taking of intestate personal property
under the law of the State in which the beneficiary lived at the time of death, or
2.
Is a stepparent of the beneficiary, or
3.
Is an adoptive parent. (See GN 00306.000ff.)
