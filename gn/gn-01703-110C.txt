C. Procedure - Observation Method (Retirement and Survivor claims only)
Use the observation method when it is obvious from a quick review of the foreign coverage
record that totalization insured status is met; i.e., the record shows far more foreign
coverage than is needed.
Example: A NH requires 33 QCs to meet fully insured status. He has 30 QCs of U.S. coverage
and the foreign coverage record displays 5 years of non-coinciding coverage.
