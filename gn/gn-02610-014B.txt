B. Applying windfall offset when Interim Assistance Reimbursement (IAR) is involved
1. IAR involved
Compute the Windfall offset using all title XVI benefits including the amount paid
to the State for IAR. For processing eIAR cases, see SI 02003.001.
2. Eligible claimant dies before receiving title XVI benefits
Offset the title II benefits using only the title XVI retroactive benefits that we
actually paid or will pay. If the balance of the title XVI retroactive benefits is
not payable to a survivor, then only offset the amount paid to the claimant, to the
State for IAR, and any fee paid to a representative. Always pay the IAR before the
representative fee. For procedures to determine the windfall offset period when the
recipient dies, see GN 02610.050C.1. For instructions on when to pay the fee in this situation, see SI 02101.003 and SI
02101.004.
