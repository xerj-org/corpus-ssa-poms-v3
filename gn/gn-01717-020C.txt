C. Policy – Dependents of retired or disabled workers
The benefit eligibility requirements for dependents of retired or disabled workers
under the old Swedish system are shown below. Information about the new Swedish system
is available from the National Social Insurance Board at the address shown in GN 01717.020E.
1. Basic Pension
a. Wife
There is no provision for a separate wife’s benefit. However, a means-tested supplement
may be added to the retired or disabled worker’s pension for a wife who is at least
age 60.
NOTE: Certain residence requirements may apply.
b. Husband
There is no provision for a husband’s benefit.
c. Divorced Spouse
There is no provision for a divorced spouse’s benefit.
d. Children
There is no provision for children’s benefits.
2. ATP Pension
There are no separate benefits for spouses, divorced spouses or children of retired
or disabled workers.
