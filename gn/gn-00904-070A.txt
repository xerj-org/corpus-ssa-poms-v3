SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.070 - Requesting Lag Wages from Employers in Other Districts
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Conditions Necessary for Direct Requests
You may request lag wages information directly from an employer outside your district
if all of these conditions are met:
1.
You have the full name and mailing address of the employer,
2.
You have sufficient information to identify the employee, and
3.
You have not been notified previously that the employer request must be channeled
through the servicing office.
