GN 00312.402 Tunisia – Vital Statistics Records
Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
Marriage records are complete from August 1, 1957; records exist for marriages prior
to this time if performed before a civil authority.
The French (from 1920) and the Italian (from 1873) Consulates in Tunis have records
of the births of French and Italian citizens reported to them.
The Prelate of Tunis keeps records for all persons of the Catholic faith baptized
in Tunisia.
