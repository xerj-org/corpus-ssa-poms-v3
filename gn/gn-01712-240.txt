GN 01712.240 Description of the Norwegian Personal Insurance Number (PIN)
A Norwegian PIN is an 11-digit number. The first six digits represent the date of
birth of the PIN holder in DD/MM/YY order. The ninth digit indicates sex — odd numbers
for males, even for females.
