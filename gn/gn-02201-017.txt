GN 02201.017 Explanation of a Title II Overpayment
The overpaid person may still have questions about their overpayment and request an
explanation, despite the information they receive in the initial overpayment notice.
We must stop recovery efforts until we provide the overpaid person an explanation,
and give them an opportunity to decide how to respond to the explanation.
