SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.245 - Determining Relationship of NH or Spouse to Grandchild's Parent
Effective Dates: 12/14/1998 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Develop relationship according to the appropriate section(s) in this subchapter. For
a section 216(h)(3) child, all factors apply, including living with or contributions
when relationship is based on other evidence of paternity.
In addition, for a legally adopted child, accept an amended BC showing NH or spouse
as parent as proof that adoption took place. If no BC is available, follow GN 00306.155. No dependency is required.
