B. Procedure – Marriage and Divorce Records
1. Office to Contact
a. Moslems
Sharia Court in Amman or in the principal cities in Jordan.
b. Christians
Religious authorities where the event occurred.
2. Fee
a. Moslems
First marriage: 27,100 Jordanian dinars.
Subsequent marriage: 78,100 Jordanian dinars.
b. Christians
Donation only.
