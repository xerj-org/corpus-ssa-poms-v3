SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01724.110 - How to Credit French Coverage
Effective Dates: 11/23/1993 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201724110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The French coverage record is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage to the
FCER.
•
All periods of coverage certified by France are creditable. Do not question their
validity.
•
There is no minimum amount of French coverage which must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
