B. Minimum coverage requirement for Brazilian benefits
To meet the requirements to establish entitlement to Brazilian old-age, disability,
or survivor benefits under the agreement, Brazil credits three months of coverage
for each quarter of coverage we certify. For more information about eligibility requirements
for Brazilian benefits, see GN 01757.020.
