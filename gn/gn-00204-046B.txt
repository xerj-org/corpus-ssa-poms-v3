SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.046 - Dual Entitlement Diary System
Effective Dates: 11/05/2008 - Present | TN 124 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204046 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Field Office Responsibility
a.
Give the claimant the monthly benefit amount information they need to determine the
month of election for the second benefit.
b.
Diary for 5 months earlier than the month of election (MOE).
•
See MSOM MCS 009.013 and MSOM MCS 009.015 for how to enter codes on DECI and complete
DIAR.
•
Annotate the routing screen/routing form that a diary is being established.
•
See GN 01040.010 for diary codes.
2. Processing Center Responsibility
a.
When the diary matures, query the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR), Modernized Claims
System (MCS) screens, Numident, etc., to determine if the claimant is alive and has
not filed for (or become entitled to) the class of benefits for which the diary was
prepared.
b.
No action is necessary if the claimant has filed for the other benefit or died. Otherwise,
establish a Processing Center Action Control System (PCACS) control for the field
office to develop for the other claim.
