B. Procedure for paper applications
If
Then
The claimant is uncertain about employment periods.
Use “Unknown.”
The NH was self-employed during an employment period.
Use “self” or “self-employed.”
The claimant alleges agricultural employment.
Ask for employer information for the years of alleged work.
A part of the work for an individual employer is within the requested period but begins
before the period.
Two acceptable answers are the first month of the period or an indication that the
employment began before the period without any specific dates.
