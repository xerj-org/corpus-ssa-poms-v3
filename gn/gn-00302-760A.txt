SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.760 - Completing Requests for Census Search
Effective Dates: 10/25/2004 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302760 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. GENERAL
There are two forms which may be used to request a census search by the Bureau of
the Census. Specifically, use:
Form
When
BC-600
Claimant will pay for the census search
SSA-1535-U3
SSA will pay for the census search
Do not send requests for a search of census records to the parallel FO in New Albany, Indiana.
