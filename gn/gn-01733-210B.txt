SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01733.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for U.S. and Luxembourg Benefits
Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present | TN 7 (02-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201733210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Using evidence submitted in the claim for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to submit additional evidence to file for Luxembourg benefits
since evidence used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits, both regular and Totalization, is sufficient for Luxembourg to establish
the same factor for Luxembourg benefits.
