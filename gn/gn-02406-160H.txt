H. Procedure for mailing completed forms
When a beneficiary, recipient, or payee comes into the field office for assistance
with completing the FS-1133, take the following actions
STEP
ACTION
1
After providing assistance, instruct the beneficiary, recipient, or payee to mail
the original FS-1133 to Treasury at the address provided. Do not mail to the PC or
CO.
2
Instruct the payee to use the envelope Fiscal Service provides or use the following
address:
Department of the Treasury
Bureau of the Fiscal Service
P.O. Box 51318
Philadelphia, PA 19115.
3
Diary for 90 days for return of the CDN from Treasury
4
Obtain status of the case through TCIS or call Treasury at 855-868-0151.
