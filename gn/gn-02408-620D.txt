D. Process for Title XVI
1. When to input a manual reclamation
The Field Office (FO) manually inputs an E-stop in the following situations:
•
A death termination has been on the Supplemental Security Record (SSR) for more than
30 days. The SSR shows that one or more incorrect payments were issued. However, the
Treasury Status Query (UPTQ) does not show E-stops for one or more of these payments.
For information on the Treasury Status Query, see MS
00303.020.
•
An incorrect month or year of death has been discovered on the SSR, and the actual
date of death was earlier. An E-stop is required for any incorrect payment that was
not included in the original reclamation request.
•
The date of death is earlier than the start date (SD) on the current record.
NOTE: Unlike Title II, there is no systems limitation on the number of payments that can be included in a Title XVI automated or manual
reclamation request.
2. Examples
•
The recipient's date of death was August 9, 2008, but on September 10, 2008, a date
of death (DOD) of September 8, 2008, is input. When the technician discovers the error,
he corrects the DOD and sends a request to the FO to input an E-stop for the September
payment, which was dated September 3, 2008.
•
On September 30, 2008, a date of death for Ms. Day is input as July 10, 2008. When
Ms. Day's daughter calls to correct the DOD to July 25, 2008, the technician corrects
the date. However, the technician does not send a reclamation request, as the correct
DOD did not change the months for which an automated reclamation had already been
sent.
•
After the technician terminates Mr. Cheer's Title XVI record and establishes a new
record with a start date of October 1, 2008, the technician learns that Mr. Cheer
died on September 30, 2008. In addition to making the death input, the technician
inputs an E-stop for the October 1, 2008, payment.
