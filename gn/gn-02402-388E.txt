E. Description of Bosnia Herzegovina IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Bosnia Herzegovina will come from the International Bank
Account Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by their FI. The full length of
the IBAN for Bosnia Herzegovina consists of 20 characters. However, the bank data
on the MBR consists of 19 characters including Bosnia Herzegovina country code of
M.
