B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Office of the Mayor where the event took place for birth, marriage, and
death records. Contact the district court where the divorce was granted.
For birth, marriage, and death records:
•
Identify the correct department or district
•
Address the request to the mayor of the city
•
Include a self-addressed envelope
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Names of the parents, including the maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Address at the time of birth
