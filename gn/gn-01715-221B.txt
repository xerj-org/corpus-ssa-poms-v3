SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01715.221 - Requests for Status of Application for Canadian Benefits
Effective Dates: 06/15/2004 - Present | TN 7 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201715221 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Take the following steps if the applicant contacts an SSA field office for the status
of his or her claim for Canadian benefits:
1.
Check the MDW screen to see if OIO has sent the claim to Canada.
2.
If OIO hasn’t completed the MDW screen, advise the applicant to write directly to
OIO at the address shown in GN
01715.220C.
3.
If OIO has completed the MDW screen, advise the applicant to write directly to the
Canadian agency shown on the notice he or she received from OIO when the claim was
forwarded. (Canadian agency addresses can be found in GN 01716.220D.)
