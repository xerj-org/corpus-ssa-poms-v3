SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.185 - Overpaid medicare individual wishes to refund by installments
Effective Dates: 09/30/2005 - Present | TN 17 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210185 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If an individual informs a FO that he/she cannot comply with the installment schedule
established by the carrier or the intermediary,obtain a written statement from the person as to how much can be paid per month. Forward this information to the appropriate carrier or intermediary to make the determination
on whether the offer is acceptable and to notify the person accordingly.
Exercise care in distinguishing between a request for repayment by installments and
a request for waiver of recovery
(GN
02250.002).
If a person who has received a refund request from an RSDI Processing Center informs
the FO that he/she is unable to refund the full amount of an overpayment at one time,
but is willing to refund by installments, see GN 02210.180.
