GN 00904.075 Requesting Information on When Wages were Earned from Employers in Other Districts
(See OS 15010.347 for form SSA-L725)
Use Form SSA-L725 (Letter to Employer Requesting Him to Furnish Wages Earned by Beneficiary)
to reconcile material discrepancies between wages earned and wages paid in deduction
cases. The information requested by Form SSA-L725 is necessary in postadjudicative
earnings investigations when the beneficiary is unable to secure information regarding
his/her earnings or is unable to support his/her allegations of wages earned. In some
instances, completion of the form may be necessary during the development of an initial
claim when the imposition of deductions is pertinent or when there is reason to question
the allegations.
Send SSA-L725 directly to an employer outside your district following instructions
in GN 00904.070 and see GN 00904.082, Exceptions to Use of Form SSA-562-U3. (The use of this form is not restricted to
lag earning information.) Make the initial request with Form SSA-562-U3 through the
servicing office if special wage payments are involved or incorrect reporting is likely.
Send a Form SSA-562-U3 to the servicing office if you do not receive a reply to an
initial direct request, informing the servicing office that a previous direct mail
request was unproductive, or if the reply requires additional information or clarification.
Attach copies of requests and replies to aid the servicing office in its contact.
