SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.010 - Providing Information to Prospective Organizations and FFS Application Requirements
Effective Dates: 03/31/2021 - Present | TN 17 (03-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Follow these procedures when responding to inquiries about fee for services:
•
Explain that organizations must meet certain requirements to qualify and that fees cannot be collected until written authorization is obtained from SSA.
•
Obtain the name and daytime telephone number of the inquirer and the name and address
of the organization involved.
•
Inform the inquirer that information regarding collecting fees for representative
payee services will be mailed.
•
Explain that records must be kept and are subject to oversight.
•
Prepare a notice using the language supplied in NL 00703.940. Include the appropriate FO contact name, FO address, and telephone number and fax
number on FO letterhead.
•
Include the necessary exhibits supplied in NL 00703.941 and NL 00703.942 as attachments.
•
Include a form SSA-445, Application to Collect a Fee for Payee Services, as an attachment.
