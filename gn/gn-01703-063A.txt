A. Introduction
Most of the liaison forms that DIO and FSPs receive from foreign countries do not
contain a block or statement to indicate whether or not there is a U.S. claims lead.
In addition, DIO and FSPs receive liaison forms with available blocks that are not
checked. The following instructions apply to these two situations.
