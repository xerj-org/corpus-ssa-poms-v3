D. Overview of IDD for Fiji Islands
IDD payments sent to Fiji Islands must be in Fijian Dollars (FJD) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Fiji Islands.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
