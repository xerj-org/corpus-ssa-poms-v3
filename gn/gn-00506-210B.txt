SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.210 - When Fees for Service Can Be Collected
Effective Dates: 04/20/2023 - Present | TN 24 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. EXAMPLES
1. Check issued before the end-of-month or holiday weekend
A beneficiary has had a social service agency, Local Help Inc. as their representative
payee since 1995. On January 1, 2002, the payee applies for status as a fee for service
organization. SSA approves the request on January 15, 2002. The beneficiary receives
their March check on February 28. SSA will consider the payment received in March
for the purpose of charging the permitted fees.
2. Suspension and Reinstatement
A beneficiary moved to a different town and did not notify their payee right away;
the payee notified SSA, who suspended payment; when the beneficiary finally told the
payee of their move, (due to miscommunication) the payee failed to report the move
to SSA.
The agency (SSA) suspended benefits for two months while developing whereabouts unknown.
The payee continued to act in that capacity and handle all the affairs of the beneficiary,
including paying their rent, utilities, groceries, keeping up with medical appointments,
etc. When the payee finally reported the move and the beneficiary’s checks were reinstated,
the payee was entitled to collect a fee for the two months in question.
