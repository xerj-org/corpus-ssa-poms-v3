A. Background
This section explains the procedures to follow if a remittance is lost and therefore
not credited to the overpaid record.
If a Field Office (FO) cannot find a remittance by following GN 02403.050D steps 1 through 9 and it has been more than 60 days since the FO received the remittance,
the FO should start the lost in transit procedures in this section to determine if
the check was ever negotiated.
These instructions will address the following types of remittances:
•
Personal check or money order
•
Cash converted to money order or cashier's check
•
Endorsed government check
•
Cashier's check, bank draft, or bank money order
For instructions to determine acceptable proof, see GN 02403.150B.2.
