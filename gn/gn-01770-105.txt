GN 01770.105 Effective Date of the Agreement with Slovenia
The agreement with Slovenia became effective February 1, 2019. The United States and
Slovenia can use periods of U.S. and Slovenian social security coverage earned in
the United States or Slovenia before February 1, 2019 to establish entitlement to
U.S. or Slovenian benefits. However, the earliest possible date of entitlement to
benefits under the agreement is February 1, 2019.
