B. Records in multiple locations
The requested information may reside in multiple locations. For example, part of the
requested information may be in a claim file located in the Office of Disability Operations
while the other part of the requested information may be in a hearing file located
in the Office of Disability Adjudication. In this instance, the processing office
should send a copy of the consent document to each location.
