B. Procedure - Manual Worksheet Method (Retirement and Survivor claims only)
Follow the guidelines below for the manual worksheet method.
1. When to use
Complete a totalization insured status worksheet in the following situations:
•
Claims in which the deceased NH is currently insured.
•
Claims where insured status is not met after foreign coverage is credited.
•
Claims where it is too close to call; i.e., where insured status is met using combined
coverage, but there are fewer than 5 foreign quarters of coverage (QCs) in excess
of the number needed for totalization insured status.
2. How to complete
For each year in which there is a U.S. and/or a foreign QC:
•
Display a U.S. QC as “C.”
•
Display a foreign QC as “F.”
•
Display no QC as “N.”
•
Show QCs in proper order.
Example: In 1968 there are 4 F QCs. In 1969, the first quarter is an “F” and the last quarter is a “C.” Enter, “1968 FFFF 1969 FNNC.”
•
Sign and date the form.
