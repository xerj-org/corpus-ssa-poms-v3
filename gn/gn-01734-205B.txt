SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.205 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Luxembourg
Effective Dates: 09/27/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use these instructions for preparing claims packages.
1. U.S. Claim
Prepare a claims package only if a Luxembourg coverage record is needed. Include Liaison
Form SSA-2960-U3-LX in the package.
2. U.S. and Luxembourg or Luxembourg Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-LX
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from OIO files if the Luxembourg claim is for disability benefits
•
Any additional evidence that the claimant has submitted for the Luxembourg claim
