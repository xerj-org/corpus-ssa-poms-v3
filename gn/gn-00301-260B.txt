SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.260 - Annotating or Altering Documents or Writings
Effective Dates: 05/15/2003 - Present | TN 9 (03-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301260 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Identify claim
Show the NH's SSN on every document in the claim file. See GN 01050.003.
2. Clarify illegible entries
Use overwriting to enter on the copy information from the document which the photocopy machine failed
to reproduce clearly. Initial any overwriting as provided in GN 00301.280.
3. Highlight entries for data input
For data input purposes, the FO may wish to highlight  (e.g with a highlighter) certain entries on a document for ease of input. This is
permitted providing the highlighting is done only on:
a.
Photocopies and is never used on original records which must be returned to the claimant;
b.
Reports of contact; or
c.
Other written statements.
