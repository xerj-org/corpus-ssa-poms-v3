A. Introduction
Some agreements provide for exemption from the alien nonpayment provisions of the
law for beneficiaries who reside in the agreement country, even though they are not
citizens of the United States or the agreement country. The subchapters describing
the individual agreements provide information for determining who is exempt from the
alien nonpayment provisions based on residence in that agreement country.
