C. Distribution of Payment to Children
If the claim is filed by a surviving child, explain to the claimant that if there
is more than one child, the Medicare payment will be divided equally among all children
in the highest priority (those entitled to monthly benefits on the same earnings record
as the deceased beneficiary), subject to the $200 rule in GN 02335.030 G.
