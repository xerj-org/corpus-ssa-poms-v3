SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02335.035 - Special Provisions Regarding Development of Underpayment Claims From Person Who Paid for the Services
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 2 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202335035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Identification of Person or Persons Who Paid for Services
Any person who paid part or all of the charges with the person's own funds may apply
for the underpayment by submitting evidence of payment (see GN 02335.030 D.) which identifies the individual as the "payer." If more than one person paid the
charges and the evidence of payment does not clearly show the identity of all “payers” and the amount each paid, this information should be obtained on an SSA-795.
