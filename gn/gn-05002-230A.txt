A. Policy for fiscal year requests
Some beneficiaries who file their Federal tax returns on a fiscal year basis may request
a benefit statement based on their taxable year. The law requires SSA to issue benefit
statements only for calendar years; accordingly, SSA will not issue benefit statements
for fiscal years. Persons who do not file tax returns on a calendar year basis must
rely on their own records although FOs must explain how we compute the benefit amount,
as it may be subject to tax.
