D. Procedure – Marriage Certificates
1. Office to Contact
a. Marriage Before 7/1/91
Parish or civil authority which performed the marriage.
b. Marriage After 6/30/91
Local tax office of the tax authority (Skatteverket where either spouse resides. If residing outside Sweden, the local tax office from
where either spouse emigrated. (See NOTE in GN 00312.382B1.)
2. Fee
No fee.
