B. Jurisdictional PC and Servicing FO completes the eNDSS
The jurisdictional PC’s or servicing FO’s technicians complete the eNDSS for non-disability
hearing requests. Technicians take the following actions when they receive a hearing
request for a non-disability issue.
1.
To complete the eNDSS, access the Office of Quality Assurance (OQA) Toolbar and select
Menu, Programs, and eNDSS. The eNDSS should provide a summary of the relevant facts
associated with the case. See the eNDSS Processing Guide for help with completing the eNDSS.

2.
Fax the eNDSS into the Non-Disability Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRed)
per GN 00301.313 and place a copy in the paper folder.
3.
Send the completed eNDSS, HA-501, documentation, any additional evidence and folder
to the servicing hearing office (HO) per GN 03103.080C (TII only) and SI 04030.030 (TXVI only and TII and TXVI concurrent common issue).
