SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02205.007 - Joint and Several Liability for Title II Overpayment Recovery
Effective Dates: 12/20/2012 - Present | TN 14 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202205007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Determining liability for the overpayment
Presume joint and several liability when making an overpayment determination unless:
•
The available facts at the time we discover the overpayment negate such a presumption;
or
•
The beneficiary or other jointly liable person appeals an overpayment determination,
protests his or her liability, or requests waiver.
Make a formal determination of liability for repayment if there is an indication that
the representative payee did not use the overpaid funds for the support and maintenance
of the beneficiary.
1. Effect of the determination
If the beneficiary or representative payee requests a waiver or an appeal and, after
review, there is no liability, we will approve the waiver or appeal.
2. Preparing a formal liability determination
Use an SSA-553 (Special Determination), or equivalent, for a determination that a
payee or beneficiary is not liable for the overpayment:
•
the field office (FO) prepares the SSA-553 when making the determination in connection
with a waiver request and notifies the person.
•
the Program Service Center (PSC) prepares the determination if the person questions
liability but does not request waiver.
•
retain a copy of the liability determination using Evidence Portal.
3. Misuse
When we determine misuse, only the representative payee is liable for repayment of
any misused funds. For more information about misuse of benefits, see GN 00604.001.
