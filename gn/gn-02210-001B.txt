B. How to withhold the payment
When we have established an overpayment on an individual’s record, and the 90-day
due process recovery period has elapsed without a due process request from the individual,
the automated system will begin withholding 10% of the monthly benefit payment until
we have fully recovered the overpayment (see SM 00610.715C). The automated system
will withhold any payment (as applicable) listed in GN 02210.001A from the overpaid individual, the liable representative payee, or a contingently
liable individual as applicable.
