SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.240 - Accounting for Fees for Service
Effective Dates: 02/27/2025 - Present | TN 36 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
When the organization receives a Representative Payee Report, form SSA-6234, to complete
for the beneficiary, the total amount of the fees collected should be included in
the figure entered on line 2.B. of the accounting form. (See GN 00605.065 through GN 00605.069 for information about the completion of the annual accounting form)
