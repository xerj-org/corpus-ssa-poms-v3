SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.100 - When an Application for U.S. Benefits Establishes a Protective Filing for Foreign Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 8 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When an applicant indicates on a title II application that the NH has coverage in
an agreement country, it establishes a protective filing for benefits from that agreement
country unless the applicant states explicitly that the applicant does not wish to
file for benefits from that country. Dispose of such protective filings in accordance
with GN 01702.100C.
