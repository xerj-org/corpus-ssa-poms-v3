SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.222 - How to Evaluate Payee Response That A Dedicated Account Was Never Established
Effective Dates: 01/15/1999 - Present | TN 15 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605222 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. INTRODUCTION
This section explains that FO personnel must first determine how the system identified
the dedicated account as they review the payee's response that a dedicated account
was never established. It also provides instructions when SSA released past-due benefits
to a non-dedicated account in error.
