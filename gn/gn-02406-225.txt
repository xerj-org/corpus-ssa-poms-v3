GN 02406.225 Nonreceipt of a Dual Entitlement Check
When a person alleges nonreceipt of a dual entitlement paper check, verify the acceptable
reporter (e.g. beneficiary) of nonreceipt through the interview and screening process.
If the beneficiary alleges dire need, process the nonreceipt using the immediate payment
criteria.
For more information on verifying reporters and dire need, see
•
GN 02406.130 Nonreceipt Interview and Screening
•
RS 02801.010 Immediate Payment (IP) Criteria and Process.
If the allegation does not meet the dire need criteria, take appropriate steps to
process the nonreceipt per GN 02406.000.
