A. Definition of adverse adjustment
An adverse adjustment results when a claim meets the following criteria:
a.
The family maximum provision results in a reduction in the past or present benefit payment rates of an earlier entitled beneficiary due to:
•
Entitlement of a new beneficiary, see GN 01010.310B.1.; or
•
Re-entitlement or resumption of a prior beneficiary (see GN 01010.310B.2. or GN 01010.310B.3.; and
b.
The claim involves a “late filer” who is not part of the same household as the adversely
affected beneficiary.
For more information about adverse adjustments, see GN 01010.310.
