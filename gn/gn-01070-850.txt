GN 01070.850 Follow-up Procedure—No Evidence Or Documents Received
Follow-up on a successful DIRCON contact by direct mail. A follow-up contact is necessary
when the evidence and/or documents are not received in the module after 20 days of
the successful DIRCON contact from Division of Earnings and Business Services (DEBS).
Step
Action
1
Prepare follow-up letter to individual. See NL 00901.140B.2 (ML400) and NL
00901.140B.6 (ML402).
2
Annotate the DEBS internal cover sheet as follows:
•
Show location of 1+(2 digit MOD number)+DHF+DC
(ex. 1_DHFDC)
•
Diary for 15 calendar days from the date of the letter.
3
Prepare an Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) requesting the information and annotate
the diary via EM 2.8.
