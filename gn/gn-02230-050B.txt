B. Policy regarding CMP
We do not initiate CMP actions. OCIG receives a referral of suspected fraud or other
misconduct from within SSA or from the 1-800 Fraud Hotline and initiates action.
The SSA Commissioner delegated the authority to pursue and impose CMPs to OIG, and
OIG re-delegated the authority to OCIG. SSA Operations is responsible for posting
the CMP on the MBR and/or SSR and for collecting CMPs.
