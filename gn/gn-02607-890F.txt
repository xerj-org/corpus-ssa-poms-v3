SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02607.890 - Requests for Prisoner Record Changes, Updates, or Deletions to the Prisoner Update Processing Systems (PUPS)
Effective Dates: 08/15/2023 - Present | TN 97 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202607890 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. ORPDP approves the request for PUPS record deletion
If ORPDP approves the PUPS deletion request, ORPDP will:
•
Notify the Office of Systems to delete the erroneous PUPS record.
•
Send the requestor a notice informing them of ORPDP’s decision on the PUPS deletion
request. ORPDP will file the requestor’s written letter demanding the PUPS deletion
in the requestor’s electronic file, along with their identity proof and supporting
evidence for the deletion.
•
Send a notice indicating that ORPDP made a PUPS record deletion correction to any
other agency previously given a copy of the incorrect PUPS record based on the requestor’s
claim, if required, and approved by the Office of the General Counsel.
