SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03990.030 - Action to be Taken if an Equal Access to Justice Act Application is Received in an FO or PC
Effective Dates: 04/21/2009 - Present | TN 1 (05-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203990030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. SSA Claim Decided at the Hearing or Appeals Council Level
If an EAJA application is received for a claim adjudicated at the hearing level, it
should be forwarded immediately to the ALJ who rendered the decision. Similarly, if
an EAJA application is received for a claim adjudicated at the Appeals Council level,
it should also be forwarded immediately to the ALJ who heard the case. In such instances
the ALJ will dispose of the EAJA application.
