B. Responding to e-IWO inquiries
Beneficiaries may contact SSA for information regarding e-IWO garnishment cases.
1.
For inquiries to verify information in an e-IWO (e.g., the amount of withholding,
the effective date), FO or PC technicians and National 800 Number Network (N8NN) agents
can answer questions by accessing the e-IWO in ORS or the Evidence Portal (EP).
•
On the ORS home screen, select “ACCESS BY SSN” under “FOLDER LIST.” Find your case
using the claim Social Security Number (SSN) for the garnished beneficiary. For the
selected case, choose the notice showing EIWNOT in the REPORT ID field. Access ORS
at ORS Production.
•
On the EP home screen, select “ORS-Load” under “Search Results." Choose the notice
showing EIWNOT in the REPORT ID field. For more information on EP, see MS 09701.000.
2.
Refer questions to the issuing court or State Distribution Unit (SDU) that involve:
•
access to personally identifiable information (PII); or
•
questions about withholding under an e-IWO (e.g., the garnished beneficiary denies
knowledge of a garnishment order).
