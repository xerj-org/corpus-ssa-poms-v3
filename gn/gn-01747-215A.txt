SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01747.215 - Application for Benefits under the Danish Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 3 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201747215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An individual who wishes to file for benefits under the agreement with Denmark must
complete form SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement). This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only
from the United States, only from Denmark, or from both countries.
NOTE: Applications for Danish benefits may be filed up to 12 months in advance of the entitlement
date.
