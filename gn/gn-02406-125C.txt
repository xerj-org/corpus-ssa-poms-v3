C. Procedure - DIRE NEED - TITLE II (If criteria in RS 02801.010 are met)
1. TSC:
Do not input nonreceipt or CPS. Refer to FO via PRIORITY MDW.
2. FO:
•
Issue an IP for up to $400 ($999 effective 8/21/99) and a CPS payment for the balance,
if any.
•
Code TPT of C43 and in the NPF field on the CPAY transaction enter: ”INPUT STOP PMT
SFTF FOR ORIG PMY DTD MM/DD/YY.REPLCMT PMT MADE BY IP/CPS.” (MSOM CPS 001.005 and RS 02801.001)
•
Send a priority MDW to the PC to input F-stop on missing recurring payment.
3. PC:
•
Input an F-stop on the missing recurring payment.
Unlike title XVI, the title II IP is not deducted automatically from the next month's
check; therefore, the IP and CPS take the place of the original check and no additional
benefits are due.
