GN 01760.210 Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Uruguayan Benefits
Under the agreement with Uruguay, the Social Security Administration (SSA) takes applications
for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of Uruguay. SSA also provides
limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim (see GN 01755.125).
