SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.120 - Hearing Filed Before Receipt of Reconsideration Determination
Effective Dates: 01/28/2021 - Present | TN 42 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure — FO Action
FO action:
•
If a claimant wants to file a request for hearing without filing for a reconsideration
or before the reconsideration determination has been completed, explain that reconsideration
is the next step in the appeals process and that the hearing will be dismissed (see
GN 03103.220 on dismissal).
•
Assist the claimant in requesting a reconsideration if that is the claimant's decision
and the claimant has not previously filed a reconsideration. (See GN 03102.225.)
•
If claimant still insists on filing for a hearing, complete an HA-501-U5 and attach
to all copies (except the claimant's) an RC containing a brief statement of the explanation
given the claimant and indicate why claimant insists on filing for a hearing before
having a reconsideration. Follow
GN 03103.080 for processing HA-501-U5.
•
If FO receives a letter requesting a hearing, contact claimant and explain the above
points. Prepare an RC and, if necessary, complete either an SSA-561-U2 (see GN 03102.225) or HA-501-U5 (see GN 03103.020 F.).
