B. Disclosure to administer DOS programs for issuance of passports
DOS collects the name, Social Security Number (SSN), date of birth, sex, and other
identity information of passport applicants to verify proof of their US citizenship
and identity. We have established a centralized data exchange to help verify SSNs
and other identifying information for DOS. Refer any requests from DOS concerning
the issuance of passports to Privacy and Disclosure Policy at ^LP PDP Controls.
