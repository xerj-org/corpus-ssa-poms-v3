SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside the Netherlands
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the appropriate procedures below for preparing claims packages
1. Initial Claims Packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Dutch coverage records and to inform the
Dutch agencies about claims for Dutch benefits. (See GN 01725.205 - GN 01725.215 for the applications and the evidence required to prepare the packages.)
2. U.S. Claims
Prepare form SSA-2960-NL to request the Dutch coverage record, if one is needed. (See
GN 01726.320 for disability claims).
3. U.S. and Dutch or Dutch Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-296-NL
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from DIO files, if the Dutch claim is for disability benefits
•
Evidence submitted for the Dutch claim, and
•
A request for the Dutch coverage record, if one is needed
