A. INTRODUCTION
SSA has a responsibility to make sure that each representative payee outside the United
States applies the benefits they receive in a reasonable and prudent manner. The payee
must also account annually for the use of the benefits.
These instructions provide guidelines for obtaining and evaluating accountings from
representative payees outside the United States.
