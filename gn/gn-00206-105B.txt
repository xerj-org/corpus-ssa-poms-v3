B. PC instructions for a WD request after a claim is disallowed
Upon receiving the WD request, review it to ensure it meets the conditions listed
in GN 00206.005. If it does, approve the WD by annotating:
•
The Manual Adjustment, Credit, and Award Process (MADCAP)/Manual Adjustment, Credit,
and Award Data Entry (MACADE) or equivalent form.
•
The Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) with a special message stating that we approved
the WD.
If the evidence in the file supports denying the WD request without further development,
(e.g., request submitted more than 12 months after the entitlement), deny the WD request.
For notice of WD and appeal rights, see GN 00206.145.
