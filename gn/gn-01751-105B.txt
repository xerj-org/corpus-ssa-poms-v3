B. Earliest month of entitlement
Periods of U.S. and Polish Social Security coverage a worker earned prior to March
1, 2009 may be used to establish entitlement to U.S. or Polish Totalization benefits.
However, the earliest possible month of entitlement to such benefits is March 2009.
