Effective Dates: 09/04/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201756205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - U.S. claims / Hungarian totalization benefits
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3 to request a Hungarian certified coverage record if needed.
Use this form to certify the filing date for Hungarian benefits, to establish factors
of entitlement, and to give U.S. benefit information;
•
U.S. totalization earnings record;
•
Medical evidence from SSA’s files, if the claim is for disability benefits; and,
•
Evidence submitted for non-disability claim.
