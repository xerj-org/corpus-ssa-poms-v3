C. Procedure - Exempt Person
Examine the individual's visa or other admission papers to ascertain his status if
there is an indication he may be an exempt person.
If the individual is an exempt person, advise him he cannot be considered a U.S. resident
under the substantial presence test and is subject to tax withholding.
