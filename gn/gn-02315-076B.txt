B. Procedure — Pennsylvania underpayments
To support his or her claim to the underpayment as a legal representative of the estate
by virtue of rights conferred on him or her under the above statute, have the claimant
present a certified copy of the decree entered by the orphan's court of the county
wherein the decedent was domiciled at the time of his death. Pay underpayment based
on the decree. Retain the certified copy if possible, as evidence that the underpayment
was paid in accordance with the foregoing authority. If, however, the claimant requests
the return of the certified copy, prepare a photocopy for the files.
