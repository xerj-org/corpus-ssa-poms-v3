D. Nonresident Alien Tax Withholding
The withholding tax applies to Title II benefits paid to nonresident
aliens both inside and outside the United States.
Nonresident alien tax withholding does not apply to:
•
U.S. citizens;
•
noncitizens lawfully admitted to the U.S. for permanent residence, or;
•
nonresident aliens who meet the substantial presence test.
Determine if nonresident alien tax withholding should remain in effect once the beneficiary
returns to the United States.
Review the MBR and verify that this information is present, current and accurate before initiating the change
of address via the post entitlement PEAD screen.
Process any changes to nonresident alien tax withholding via the PELF (Alien Tax Update)
screen.
