A. Process to file a claim for U.S. benefits
When a person contacts the Brazilian liaison agency the National Social Insurance
Institute (INSS) to file a claim for U.S. benefits, INSS sends the following information
to the Foreign Benefit Unit(FBU) in Lisbon, Portugal:
•
Form SSA 2960 USA/BRA 3 (U.S. Brazil Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
certifying the filing date for the claim;
•
Brazilian certified coverage record;
•
Supporting evidence of non-disability claims; and
•
Available medical evidence (if the claim is for disability benefits).
