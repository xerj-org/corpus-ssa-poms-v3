GN 01050.185 Preparation of Folder for Transmittal of End Stage Renal Disease Claims (ESRD)
Prepare the folder in accordance with GN 01050.165 except that it will not be necessary to enter the claimant's name on the reverse
of the folder tab. Assemble the material in the folder following the guidelines in
GN 01050.190. Enter “ESRD” below the upper right edge of the front face of the folder for identification purposes.
Send all CRD claims to:
SSA
