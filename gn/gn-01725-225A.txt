A. Automatic status notice for an application for Dutch benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about a Dutch claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of the
worker’s U.S. coverage record) to the Netherlands, the Benefit Earnings Technician
(BET) electronically stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center
(PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Dutch agency. The system automatically
generates a status notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs
the applicant that DIO forwarded the claims material to the Netherlands.
