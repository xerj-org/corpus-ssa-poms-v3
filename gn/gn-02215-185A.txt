A. Introduction
To obtain relief from liability for repayment of their debts, an individual or spouses
together may petition the bankruptcy court. Depending on the type of bankruptcy, before
a debtor (overpaid individual) receives a discharge from the court, their assets (if
any) may be liquidated to pay their debts or there may be a repayment plan where the
debtor makes payments to a bankruptcy trustee who then distributes payments to creditors,
such as SSA.
Any questions about a bankruptcy case should be sent, via email, to SSA's Office of
the General Counsel (OGC) at ^SSA Bankruptcy.
If a bankruptcy attorney or trustee contacts SSA about a bankruptcy case, refer them
to OGC and immediately notify OGC of their contact.
