SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01732.205 - Preparing Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Finland
Effective Dates: 02/08/1995 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Initial Claims Packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Finnish coverage records and to inform
the Finnish agencies about claims for Finnish benefits. See GN 01731.205 - GN 01731.220 for the application and the evidence required to prepare the packages.
