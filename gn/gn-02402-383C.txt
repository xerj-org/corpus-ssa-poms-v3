SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.383 - Coding Sierra Leone Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/18/2023 - Present | TN 243 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402383 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Sierra Leone has a prefix of "68" at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN).The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “H” assigned for Sierra Leone.
