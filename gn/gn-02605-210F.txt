F. Related Publication
The Social Security Publication, 10137 Social Security—Your Payments While You Are Outside The United States, addresses all the information cited above and should be provided to any beneficiary
who is planning to reside outside the United States. It is available in a booklet
format and on the official website http://www.socialsecurity.gov.
