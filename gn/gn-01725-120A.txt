SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.120 - U.S. Totalization Benefits Under the Dutch Agreement
Effective Dates: 02/23/1999 - Present | TN 1 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
Under the agreement, if an NH has at least 6 quarters of U.S. coverage but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under the Dutch Social Security
system can be added to his or her U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents
or survivors) to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
