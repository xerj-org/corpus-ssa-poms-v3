SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.215 - Application for Benefits under the Spanish Agreement
Effective Dates: 04/22/2004 - Present | TN 3 (04-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. U.S. Totalization Benefits Only
An individual who wishes to file for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement
with Spain must complete the appropriate modernized claim system (MCS) application
screens as well as the SSA-2490 screens. If the SSA-2490 cannot be completed on MCS
(GN 01721.215B.), the individual must complete Part I only of either a paper or electronic (e-form)
SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
2. U.S. Totalization and Spanish Benefits
An individual who wishes to file for both U.S. Totalization benefits and Spanish benefits
must complete the appropriate MCS screens (including the SSA-2490 screens) and the
special Spanish application form (E/USA) discussed in GN 01721.220. If the SSA-2490 cannot be completed on MCS (GN 01721.215B.), the individual must complete Part I only of either a paper or electronic (e-form)
SSA-2490-BK.
3. Spanish Benefits Only
An individual who wishes to file only for Spanish benefits must complete the Spanish
application form (E/USA) discussed in GN 01721.220 but does not need to complete Form SSA-2490-BK.
NOTE: When an applicant is filing for regular (nontotalization) benefits from the U.S.
and benefits from Spain (regular or Totalization), it is not necessary to complete
the SSA-2490. However, MCS will not allow clearance of the claim for U.S. benefits
until the path for the SSA-2490 is entered. To clear the U.S. claim, complete only
the first (FINF) screen in the SSA-2490 path. This will release the claim for U.S.
benefits for award or denial. The claim for Spanish benefits should then be completed
using the Spanish application form (E/USA) described in GN 01721.220.
