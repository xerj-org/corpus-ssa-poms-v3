SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.800 - Followup on Census Record Requests
Effective Dates: 10/31/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302800 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use this procedure in following up on census requests.
Step
Action
1
Was the census record request made on an SSA-1535-U3
•
If yes, go to step 4
•
If no, go to step 2
2
Contact the claimant to determine:
•
When the BC-600 was mailed, and
•
The amount of the fee submitted, and
•
Whether the claimant canceled the authorization for the DO to receive a copy.
3
Does the information obtained in step 2 show that followup is needed?
•
If yes, go to step 4
•
If no take whatever action is required by the situation (e.g., wait for the end of the
45 day period of followup).
4
Include the following with the followup request:
•
The claimant's name and address, or
•
A photocopy of the SSA-1535-U3 or BC-600
5
Send the following by SSA-562-U3 to the FO at:
2656 Charlestown Rd.
New Albany, IN 47150
(FO code is 465)
NOTE:  Do not  attempt to contact the Bureau of the Census directly. Only the parallel DO in New
Albany, Indiana is authorized to do so, at the request of the FO which requested the
record.
