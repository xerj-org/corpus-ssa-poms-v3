C. Procedure for the enhanced Leads and Appointment System FO Informational/Referral
Screen
Since TSC staff handle many requests for Information and Referral (I&R) services,
FOs must ensure that their information on the Field Office Information/Referral screen
in the enhanced Leads and Appointment System (eLAS) provides as much information as
possible about community resources in the FO service area.
For more information, refer to MS 00601.003.
