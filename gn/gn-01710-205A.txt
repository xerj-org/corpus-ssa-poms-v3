SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Switzerland
Effective Dates: 02/07/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Initial claims packages are prepared to request Swiss coverage records and to inform
the Swiss agency about claims for Swiss benefits. (See GN 01709.205 - GN 01709.215 for the application and evidence required for the package.)
