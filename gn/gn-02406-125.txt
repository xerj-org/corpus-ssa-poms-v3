XVI -- Recurring Checks and PMA Checks -- Normal and Dire Need Situations
VERY IMPORTANT: Under NO circumstances should a photocopy request be transmitted
when a nonreceipt (as described below) or SF-1184 stop payment action is appropriate.
See GN 02406.140B. for the situations where a photocopy request should be transmitted.
