SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.452 - Claims from Residents of Türkiye (formerly Turkey)
Effective Dates: 11/01/2023 - Present | TN 21 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905452 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Handle claims inquiries as follows:
•
Send responses to the claimant through the FBU servicing their address; and
•
Provide the FBU with the necessary forms and complete instructions if it is requested
to develop a claim for benefits. Do not send the forms to the claimant.
