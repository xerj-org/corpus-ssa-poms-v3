B. Procedure - establishing an onset date for the new title ii disability application
If the new title II disability application is allowed, the disability onset date cannot
be earlier than one day after the date of the final decision of the Commissioner on
the prior application; and
The date of the final decision of the Commissioner is:
•
The date of the ALJ decision if the AC denied review; or
•
The date of the AC decision if the AC granted review or reviewed on its own motion;
or
•
The date of the ALJ decision after court remand unless the AC assumed jurisdiction
and issued a decision; or
•
Deemed to be the date disability ceased as affirmed by the ALJ or AC in cessation
cases. (See DI 27510.001B., Exhibit 3).
NOTE: In cessation cases from the Sixth Judicial Circuit, i.e., Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio,
and Tennessee, the date of the final decision is the actual decision date and not
the date disability ceased (see AR 92-2(6)).
