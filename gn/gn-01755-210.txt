GN 01755.210 Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Hungarian Benefits
Under the agreement with Hungary, the Social Security Administration (SSA) takes applications
for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of Hungary. SSA also provides
limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim (see GN 01755.125).
