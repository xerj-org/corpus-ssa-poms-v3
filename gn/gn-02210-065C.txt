C. Procedure — subsequent overpayment
If a new overpayment is determined while a prior overpayment is being recovered by
partial withholding, propose
10% withholding of the new overpayment and the balance of the old overpayment.
If the individual subsequently requests continuance of the prior withholding rate,
grant request without development.
