C. Data minimization policy
When making a disclosure of our data to State and local agencies, we must ensure that
we disclose only the minimal amount of information that is relevant and necessary
to satisfy the purpose of the request. Consider the following before making a disclosure:
•
if it is not necessary to disclose certain information, do not disclose it. Consider
what uses the requesting entity might make of any information we disclose. For example,
if there is no indication that the requesting entity maintains information by SSN
or otherwise needs to know the SSN, do not disclose it.
•
when deciding how much information to disclose, consider whether the requester can
obtain the information elsewhere.
