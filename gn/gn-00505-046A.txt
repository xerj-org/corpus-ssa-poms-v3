A. DO Approved Cases
Output Form SSA-3926-C2FC, the MBR printout, and the folder will be received for each
processed DO item input. CA's should work these cases in screening; the cases should
not be backlogged. The CA will review the Form SSA-3926-C2FC, prepare any necessary
listings and diaries, and prepare a Form SSA-573 for the typist to send the notices.
If a 400 series alert is shown on the Form SSA-3926-C2FC, a Form SSA-567 will be prepared
routing the case to the benefit authorizer after typing. In addition, the CA should
prepare a Form SSA-5015-U2 diary for 30 days to control receipt of the payee development
from the DO. The folder will be returned to files after the notices are released.
When the DO payee development is received, the folder will be routed to the CA for
payee review.
