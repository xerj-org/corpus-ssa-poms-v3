B. Procedure — Processing Title II/XVI Claims 09/08/95 or Later
NOTE: Effective with claims processed 9/08/95 or later, the interface of the MBR/SSR will
control the release of retroactive title II benefits after the title XVI has been
paid.
Process the cases normally — that is, input the title II claim with the WF FAC code
and allow the system to process payment of the retroactive benefits. These cases will
no longer be SPORT exclusions.
