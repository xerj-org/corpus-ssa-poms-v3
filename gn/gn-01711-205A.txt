SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01711.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 2 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a person files for U.S. totalization benefits under the agreement with Norway,
the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish a
claim for regular RSDI (nontotalization) benefits. In addition, the applicant must
submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 - GN 01702.230 whenever totalization benefits are being claimed from the United States.
