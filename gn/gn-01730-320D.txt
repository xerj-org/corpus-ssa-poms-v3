SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.320 - Austrian Agency Requests for Citizenship or Civil Status
Effective Dates: 04/04/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure for civil status requests
Use the following procedure to respond to an Austrian agency's request for civil status
(marital status).
1. Obtain MBR
Obtain an MBR to try to ascertain the person's civil status. If the MBR is NIF, advise
the Austrian agency that the civil status is unknown, unless there is evidence of
marriage or divorce in file. In that case attach a photocopy to the liaison form,
SSA-e2960-U3-AU.
2. Worker's Status
If the request is for the worker and the MBR shows that there is a spouse currently
entitled on the worker's SSN or the worker is currently entitled as a spouse on another
SSN inform the Austrian agency that the worker is married but that the date is unknown.
3. Widow's Status
If the request is for the worker's widow(er) and the MBR shows that he/she is currently
entitled on the worker's SSN, advise the Austrian agency that the worker and widow(er)
were married but that the date is unknown.
4. Other Situations
In all other situations inform the Austrian agency that the civil status is unknown.
5. Response
Since there is no space on form SSA-e2960-U3-AU for the civil status, enter your response
in the remarks section.
