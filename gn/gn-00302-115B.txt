Effective Dates: 07/01/1996 - Present |

B. Rule
Ask for records from the first priority list. If any such record is readily available
(i.e., within 10 working days of the request), you must obtain it. Note that, except
for the Canadian delayed birth record, all documents in C. below must be domestic
records.
