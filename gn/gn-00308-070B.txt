B. Procedure - state sources
1. Address and FO Code
Oregon Vital Records
P.O. Box 14050
Portland, OR 97293-0050
FO code 932
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information at the Oregon
Health Authority website. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
$25.00, additional copies $25.00 each (effective 01/01/2018)
See Remarks, below, for information about verifications.
3. Type of Payment:
Check, money order or third party draft.
4. Remarks:
Send mail requests directly to the Division of Vital Records. Include an SSA L-706,
a return envelope and payment. Telephone requests for all types of records, using
VISA or Mastercharge, can be made by telephoning (971) 673-1190. Fax requests can
be made to (971) 673-1203. The fee for a record request by phone or fax is $32.50.
Verification only is $8.00. Contact Oregon Center for Health Statistics directly using
the State of Oregon’s form CHS45–110. Oregon does not accept credit cards for verifications.
NOTE: Birth verifications are preferred evidence only if the birth occurred in 1939 or
later and the record is not marked delayed.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No – effective 12/01/03.
