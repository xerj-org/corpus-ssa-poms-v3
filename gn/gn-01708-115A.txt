SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.115 - How to Credit German Coverage
Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The German coverage certification is used to assign (F) quarters of coverage to the
U.S. earnings record (ER).
•
All periods of coverage certified by a German liaison agency on a form D/USA 5 are
creditable including periods of coverage after disability onset.
•
Except for periods identified as AUSFALLZEIT (excused periods) or periods that have
been crossed out, all periods of coverage certified on computer printouts are creditable,
including periods of coverage after disability onset.
•
The presence of German coverage after onset is not necessarily an indication of work,
so development of “substantial gainful activity” (SGA) should not be initiated based only on the German coverage.
•
There is no minimum amount of German coverage which must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
