C. Procedure
Follow these guidelines to process U.K. liaison forms:
•
Determine if a U.S. claim's lead exists.
•
If a U.S. claim's lead exists, respond to the U.K. request (if any) via a form SSA-2960-U3-UK
and forward the case to a CA.
•
If there is no U.S. claim's lead and the response consists only of a single attachment,
respond to the U.K. request either by SSA-2960-U3-UK or by attaching the response
to the U.K. liaison form and reversing the addresses on the form.
