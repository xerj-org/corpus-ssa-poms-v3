SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02604.110 - Determining if a Report is Late
Effective Dates: 03/23/2011 - Present | TN 53 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202604110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Filing date of report
The date of filing a report or deemed filing, determines if the report is late.
“Filed,” for these purposes, means one of the following offices received the report:
•
processing center (PC),
•
field office (FO),
•
Foreign Service Post (FSP), or
•
Department of Veterans Affairs (DVA) Regional Office (VARO).
