C. Procedure – determining family status
1. Developing questionable domicile
a.
Get all possible evidence.
b.
Get a complete statement regarding the worker's domicile and include the following
points:
•
If the worker maintained homes in two jurisdictions, where did they spend the most
time?
•
If the worker traveled constantly or moved often, where did the worker last have a
permanent home?
•
What State did the worker regard as his/her home State?
•
Where did the worker say his/her home was? (Letters and/or oral statements may establish
this.)
•
Where did the worker vote?
•
Where did the worker pay personal property, real property, and State income taxes?
•
Where did the worker's family live?
•
Where was the worker buried?
c.
Based on all information, make a determination on domicile.
d.
Apply the appropriate State law.
2. Legal opinion needed
If a case needs a legal opinion, follow the procedures in GN 01010.815 for possible submittal to OGC.
3. Special determination
Prepare and record on the RPOC screen an informal special determination in all spouse
relationship disallowances. (See GN 01010.360B.3. and GN 01010.365.) Incorporate an analysis of the evidence, resolving any conflicts, and affirm that
there are no other entitlement options.
4. References
See GN 01010.410 for follow-up and closeout.
