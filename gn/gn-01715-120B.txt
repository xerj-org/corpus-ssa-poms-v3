B. Policy — crediting Canadian coverage
In determining eligibility for U.S. Totalization benefits, SSA counts periods of coverage
under the CPP/QPP but not periods of residence under the Old-Age Security (OAS) program.
SSA will credit four U.S. QCs for every calendar year of CPP/QPP coverage. (Canadian
coverage is measured in terms of calendar years.) However, no credit will be given
for:
•
any calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC, or
•
more than 4 QC's for any calendar year.
