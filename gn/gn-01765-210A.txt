A. Evidence requirements for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Romanian Agreement
When a person files a claim for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Agreement with
Romania, that person must submit the same evidence required to file a claim for regular
retirement, survivors, or disability insurance (non-Totalization) benefits. The applicant
must also submit the evidence noted in GN
01702.200 through GN
01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States.
