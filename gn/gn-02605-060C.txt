C. Procedures
1. Attempt to find correct address or locate claimant or recipient
•
If returned mail is addressed to a claimant or recipient, attempt to locate the individual
by following the instructions in SI 02301.240D.
•
If the envelope is marked "Deceased," follow the procedures in GN 02602.050B.
2. Attempt to find correct address or locate representative payee (payee)
•
If the returned mail is a request for information material to develop or redevelop
eligibility (such as the SSA-L8009-U3, SSA-L8003-U2, SSA-L8004-U2, SSA-L3074-U2, SSA-L3075-U2,
etc.), do not immediately deny or suspend benefits (N18 or N20).
•
Take reasonable steps to locate the payee per SI 02301.240D. If you are unable to locate the payee, follow instructions in GN 00504.100.
•
Exception: In IC situations a field visit to the claimant's last known address is not required.

b. Notice does not contain appeal rights
If the notice does not contain appeal rights and does not require a response, re-mail
the notice as is. Do not use the UCL.
c. Written request for information,appointment, or protective filing closeout letter
If the letter is a written request for information, appointment, or a protective filing
closeout letter, retype and re-mail the notice with the current date and update the
contents as necessary. Do not use the UCL. Apply this rule to the SSA-L305-U3, SSA-L991-U3, SSA-L8003-U2, SSA-L8004-U2,
SSA-L8009-U3, SSA- L8024-U3, SSA-L3074-U2, SSA-L3075-U2, and any other similar requests
that require a response.
d. Change of address (COA)
If the address has changed, input the COA into the system and develop for living arrangement
changes, if appropriate. For living arrangement development, refer to SI 00835.001. For IC systems input instruction, refer to SM 01005.400 through SM 01005.430. Determine
if any other events occurred that may require an unscheduled redetermination, per
SI 02305.022.
4. Efforts to locate individual fail
To deny benefits based on whereabouts unknown in IC situations, refer to SI 02301.240.
For PE situations, follow instructions in SI 02301.240E. Check for suspending payments for whereabouts unknown (S06). The system will generate
a Notice of Planned Action that explains that we will not send any more payments until
the recipient or payee contacts a Social Security office.
5. Fee for returned mail
If a FO receives any documents from the USPS regarding charging a fee for returned
mail follow these steps:
•
The FO should send the letter in Exhibit F to the Post Office, and
•
USPS will charge the fee for returned mail to Central Office (G11 Permit), and not
to the FO.
