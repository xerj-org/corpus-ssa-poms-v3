C. Payee Legend
It will not be necessary to establish the name of the official since the application
will frequently be filed by a deputy. The official's name will not be used in the
payee legend. Instead, the payee legend should show, for example, “Public Trustee for . . .,” this eliminates the need to process a change of payee when a new official is appointed
to office.
