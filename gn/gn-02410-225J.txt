J. Waiver of benefits
If a garnished beneficiary requests waiver of benefits, do not stop garnishment. A
waiver of benefits can only apply when the full monthly benefit is being waived. For
instructions on conditions required for waiver of benefits, refer to GN 02409.001B.1.
