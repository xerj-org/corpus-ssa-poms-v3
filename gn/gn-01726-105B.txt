B. Description of form NL/VS 205
The following is a description of Form NL/VS 205.
•
The upper part of the NL/VS 205 contains a space for entering the U.S. SSN, if known.
•
Part 1 contains all necessary identifying information about the insured person and
is self-explanatory.
•
Part 2 contains periods of creditable Dutch coverage in the European style, i.e. day,
month, year.
•
“From-to” periods will often span many years.
•
The number of months of coverage in a period are not reflected. However, all periods
of Dutch coverage are continuous periods, i.e. all months within a given “from-to” period are coverage months.
•
If there is a break in coverage, a new period identified by a new beginning date is
shown.
•
Records certified by SVB contain coverage information beginning with January, 1957
or the first month of coverage, if later.
•
Records certified by UWV contain coverage information beginning with July, 1967 or
the first month of coverage, if later.
•
A provisional record can be recognized by the word “provisional” written somewhere on the form or by the appearance of coverage periods which are
later than the date the form was completed. (See GN 01726.115 for a discussion of provisional records.)
•
The agency which issued the record is indicated at the bottom of the form, in the
event that additional information or explanation are required.
