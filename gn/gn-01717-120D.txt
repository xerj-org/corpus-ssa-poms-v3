D. Policy — Adjudication responsibility
1. Field Office Responsibilities
Field offices (FOs) are responsible for processing an initial insured status denial
(see GN 01702.315 - GN 01702.120) when the worker has at least 6 QC's but is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone.
This denial will produce a notice advising the applicant that insured status is not
met based on U.S. coverage alone and that the Swedish coverage record is being requested.
2. Office of International Operations Responsibilities
The Office of International Operations (OIO) will request the Swedish coverage record.
When the record is received, OIO will convert the Swedish coverage to QC's and finally
adjudicate the claim.
