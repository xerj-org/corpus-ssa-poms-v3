SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.055 - Retaining Completed Representative Payee Reports
Effective Dates: 07/10/2024 - Present | TN 74 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The accounting report must be retrievable. This is necessary in the event an accounting
report must be reviewed for a misuse allegation/investigation. (See GN 00605.085 for
the handling of the SSA-624-F5)
WBDOC retains imaged title 2 and title 16 report forms received there. Paper title
2 and title 16 report forms received in FOs and PCs should be stored in the Non Disability
Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRED) using the Evidence Portal (EP). See MS
09701.001 Evidence Portal (EP) – Overview.
