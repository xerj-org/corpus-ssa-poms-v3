A. Policy on incompetent adult access rights
An incompetent adult is an individual who has been the subject of a judicial finding
of incompetency. An incompetent adult may exercise the right of access on his or her
own behalf. The legal guardian of an incompetent adult may also request access to
the individual’s records when acting on behalf of the individual.
