B. LIST OF EXCLUSIONS
Exclusions from the Address Change/Name Correction process are:
•
Certain changes to or from a foreign address, for SSI purposes this includes Puerto
Rico and the Virgin Islands;
•
Requests where no entitlement exists;
•
Change of payee;
•
Prouty beneficiaries/recipients to an address outside the 50 states or the District
of Columbia.
