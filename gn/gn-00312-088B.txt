B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
For birth, marriage, and death records, individuals can contact the local civil registration
archive (Servicio de Registro Civil ) in the town, municipality, and cities. Include
the approximate year in which the birth, marriage, or death occurred. Individuals
may also write to the General Archive of Civil Registration. Write the request in
Spanish whenever possible.
Archivo General del Registro Civil
Calle Huérfanos 1570
Santiago de Chile
Chile
For divorce records, contact the Family Judicial System where the divorce was granted.
2. Fee
•
Birth record - CH $520.00
•
Marriage record - CH $720.00
