GN 01757.150 Filing Appeals of Decisions under the Agreement with Brazil
The agreement provides that persons who wish to appeal a decision on a claim for U.S.
or Brazilian benefits may file the appeal with either country.
For more information about appealing a decision on a claim, see GN 01702.500.
For information about representation of applicants in totalization claims, see GN 01702.505.
