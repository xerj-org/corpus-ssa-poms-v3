SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.635 - Tennessee Intestacy Laws
Effective Dates: 01/11/2002 - Present | TN 28 (01-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306635 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Father acquires status of parent if parents intermarry and after 03/30/49, father
recognizes child, or paternity is established under A.1., 3., 4., or 5. above but,
if under 5., the father must have openly treated the child as his and must not have
refused to support the child.
