SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.505 - Recordation Date Tolerance for Public Records of Birth
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302505 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Rule
To avoid unnecessary development to resolve this discrepancy, assume that the year
of recordation was actually one year later than the recordation date shown if:
•
The year of birth and the year of recordation shown on the BC are the same, and
•
The recordation date precedes the DB, and
•
The recordation month is January, and
•
The alleged DB agrees with the DB shown on the BC.
No documentation or further development is required.
