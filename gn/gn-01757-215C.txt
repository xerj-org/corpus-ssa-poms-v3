C. Determine the application screens on MCS for Totalization benefits
Determine the application screens to complete when taking claims under the agreement
with Brazil for United States totalization or Brazilian benefits.
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
When taking retirement or disability claims, complete the following screens:
•
MCS application;
•
TOTL, see MS MCS 009.011; and
•
SSA-2490-BK, see MS MCS 007.001 (for survivor claims complete Part I of the paper
or electronic SSA-2490-BK).
2. Brazilian benefits only (regular or totalization)
Claims for Brazilian benefits only (regular or tTotalization) are MCS exclusions.
Complete Parts I and II of the paper or electronic form SSA-2490-BK
3. Brazilian benefits (regular or totalization) and U.S. benefits (regular or totalization)
When taking retirement or disability claims, complete the following screens:
•
MCS application;
•
Totalization (TOTL), see MS MCS 009.011; and
•
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic form SSA-2490-BK
When taking survivor claims, complete:
•
the appropriate MCS screens;
•
Totalization (TOTL), see MS MCS 009.011; and
•
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic SSA-2490-BK.
NOTE: If the claimant presents the Brazilian Social Security card, document that the
claimant submitted this evidence under remarks.
