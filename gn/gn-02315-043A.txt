A. Background for Connecticut small estates
The Connecticut Small Estate Statute and the Connecticut General Statutes 45a-273
to 45a-274, outlines two alternate procedures for underpayments. These provisions
apply only to estates for which no will has been presented for probate or application
for administration filed within 30 days after death.
