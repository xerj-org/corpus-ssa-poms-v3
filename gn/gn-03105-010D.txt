SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03105.010 - Reconstruction of Destroyed or Missing Paper Claims Folders
Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present | TN 7 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203105010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. FO action after completion of reconstruction
When completing reconstruction of all necessary documents:
1.
annotate "Reconstructed" in bold red letters across the front of the folder jacket;
2.
mark each piece of reconstructed evidence "Reconstructed;"
3.
annotate any new evidence the claimant submits as "New Evidence;"
4.
fax copies of all evidence and information into the CEF. If a CEF is not available,
fax the information into EP; and
5.
route the reconstructed paper folder immediately to the requesting office, or as directed
by regional instructions.
