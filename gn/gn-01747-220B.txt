B. Process
The Danish application forms are not taken in the field office (FO). After receiving
a claim for Danish benefits that was filed with SSA, the Danish agency will mail the
appropriate Danish applications, if necessary. The Danish agency will include instructions
asking the applicant to complete the forms and mail them directly to the Danish agency
with any additional evidence that may be needed.
