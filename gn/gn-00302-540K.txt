K. DBC based on affidavits
If the affiant is the claimant's parent, assume that he/she had sufficient basis for
knowing when the individual was born, unless the claims file suggests otherwise. A
DBC based on a parent's affidavit and recorded several years ago carries more weight,
as a rule, than registrations based on affidavits of other individuals. (Thus, a DBC
recorded in the 1940's based on a parent's affidavit would have high probative value.
