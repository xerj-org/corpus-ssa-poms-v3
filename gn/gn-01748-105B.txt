B. Description of the Danish earnings record
The Danish earnings record is a computer-generated form which lists a person’s coverage.
The form has been converted for SSA to show quarters of coverage (QC) in column 7.
The fields are as follows:
1.
Name
2.
Year -Year worker made contributions
3.
SATS – Kinds of Contribution code (A, B, C or D) does not affect SSA
4.
Contributions - amount of contribution
5.
Contributions for Calculation – allowable contributions to be used for calculation
6.
Monthly Employment Factor
7.
QC Employment Factor – Conversion has been done for SSA (use this field) for quarters
of coverage
8.
Year Employment Factor
9.
Monthly Standard Contribution
10.
Quarterly Standard Contribution
11.
Yearly Standard Contribution
12.
FRI Number – Contribution Codes
a.
SSA does not accept code 600 (cash sickness benefits from municipality)
b.
SSA does not accept code 610 (cash unemployment benefit)
