SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04030.070 - Administrative Finality - Effect of Administrative Finality on Subsequent and Prior Claims
Effective Dates: 05/28/2015 - Present | TN 11 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204030070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Terms such as “prior,” “earlier,” “existing,” “new,” “current,” “subsequent,” and
“later claims,” etc., refer to claims filed in both of the following situations:
•
Claims filed by the same or different person for 2 or more different periods of entitlement on the same Social Security Number (SSN).
EXAMPLE 1: Claimant was entitled to disability insurance benefits (DIB) 06/1997. Their benefits
terminated 06/2002. (Prior or earlier claim.) Claimant files new (subsequent, current
or later) claim for DIB on the same SSN in 08/2006. Claimant is found to be entitled
to DIB with an onset of 07/2006.
EXAMPLE 2:
Number holder (NH), wife, and children were entitled to benefits. When all children
had turned age 16, wife's benefits were terminated. (Prior or earlier claim.) NH later
died. When wife turns age 60 they filed for widow's benefits on the same SSN. (New,
subsequent, current or later claim.)
•
One period of entitlement with one or more different claims filed on behalf of different
beneficiaries at different times.
EXAMPLE 3: NH and 2 auxiliaries are entitled to benefits. (Prior, earlier or existing claims.)
A third auxiliary later files for benefits. (New, subsequent, current or later claim.)
