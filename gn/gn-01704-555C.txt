C. Dispatch and Diarying
1.
Dispatch the original copy with any attachments in the appropriate bin.
2.
File remaining copies on right side of folder.
3.
Diary for 60 days via a Form SSA-382-U2.
4.
When response is received, cancel diary.
5.
If no response is received and diary comes due, prepare another Form SSA-4766-U3 requesting
status. Attach a photocopy of original request. Diary for 60 days.
6.
If no response is received and diary comes due, telephone DO. Take appropriate action
based on DO response.
