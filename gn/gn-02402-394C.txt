Effective Dates: 10/18/2023 - Present | TN 270 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402394 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Sri Lanka will have a prefix of 68 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “S” assigned for Sri Lanka .
