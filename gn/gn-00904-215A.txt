A. Introduction to correspondence with foreign officials
Designated border offices (DBOs), field offices (FOs), processing centers (PCs), and
the Division of International Operations (DIO) receive routine correspondence and
diplomatic notes from foreign diplomatic and consular officials. Both types of correspondence
are first sent to the Department of State (DOS) and then forwarded to our agency.
DOS serves as the official channel for diplomatic communications between the U.S.
Government and a foreign government, except for certain military activities. For more
information on the types of routine correspondence and diplomatic notes, including
exhibits visit DOS' Foreign Affairs Manual Section 5,
Chapter 612.
