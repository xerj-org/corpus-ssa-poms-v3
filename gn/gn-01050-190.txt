GN 01050.190 Assembly of Disability Claims Material—ODO or INTPSC Jurisdiction for Determination
of Disability
To expedite processing of disability claims the following instructions should be carefully
followed. Punch holes in the left margin of all file materials, not already prepunched,
in accordance with GN 01050.150C. Enter NH's name and SSN on each piece of material not containing this information
and remove any staples which will impede review of the case material in the DDS or
ODO. Assemble the claims material on prongs in the following order from bottom to
top. Excluded material is described in GN 01050.150B.
