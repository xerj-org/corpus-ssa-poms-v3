A. Introduction
In certain cases, both Totalization and non-Totalization benefits may be payable on
the same record for the same period of time. This could occur in survivor claims where
the number holder is currently insured based on coverage under the U.S. system but
is fully insured only when coverage under a foreign system is counted.
