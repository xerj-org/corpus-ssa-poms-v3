SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.080 - Forwarding the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and Folder to the Servicing Hearing Office
Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General information about forwarding the HA-501-U5 and folder
Use the instructions in this section to send the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and folder to the servicing hearing
office (HO). Field offices (FO) also use these instructions to forward the HA-501-U5
and folder to the jurisdictional processing center (PC) when the case involves a Title
II (TII) only non-medical issue per GN 03103.080B of this section. We use the words hearing request and HA-501-U5 interchangeably throughout
these instructions. These instructions presume:
•
The claimant received a reconsideration determination; or
•
The claimant insists on filing a hearing request before receipt of a reconsideration
determination per GN 03103.120; and
•
There is a completed HA-501-U5 and when applicable, a completed Electronic Non-disability
Summary Sheet (eNDSS). For information on the HA-501-U5 see GN 03103.010 and GN 03103.020. For information on the eNDSS, see GN 03103.130.
Other Program Operations Manual System sections include instructions for processing
hearing requests on the following issues.
1.
Medicare Part A (Hospital Insurance (HI)), Part B (Supplementary Medical Insurance
(SMI)), and Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount (IRMAA)
Process hearing requests for Medicare Part A and Part B per GN 03108.210.
Process hearing requests for Medicare IRMAA per HI 01140.001.
2.
TII exceptions
The FO does not send TII exceptions to the PC. TII exceptions include:
•
Substantial gainful activity;
•
Res judicata (previously determined claim for disability benefits);
•
Work issue continuing disability review (dispute of earnings and work activity - does
not include appeals on overpayments if beneficiary agrees with the work continuing
disability review decision);
•
Benefit Offset National Demonstration (BOND) overpayments; and
•
Administrative Sanctions.
For TII exceptions, process the HA-501-U5 per GN 03103.080B.1 in this section and GN 03103.080B.4 in this section; and forward it, additional evidence, and the folder to servicing
HO.
3.
Title XVI (TXVI) only, TII and TXVI concurrent common issue – non-medical
Process hearing requests for TXVI only and TII and TXVI concurrent common issues cases
per SI 04030.030 and GN 03103.130.
4.
TII, TXVI, and TII and TXVI concurrent – disability
Process hearing requests for disability issues per DI 12010.010, DI 81010.085, and DI 81010.150.
5.
Representative Payee (RP) Misuse
Process hearing requests for RP misuse per the instructions in GN 00604.064G
