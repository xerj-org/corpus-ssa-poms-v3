D. Overview of IDD for South Korea
IDD payments sent to South Korea must be in South Korean Won (KRW) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves South Korea.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
