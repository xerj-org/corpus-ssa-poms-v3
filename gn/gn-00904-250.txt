GN 00904.250 Furnishing case information to FBUs
Procedure
Send copies of the following to the FBU servicing the address of the claimant or beneficiary:
•
SSA replies to congressional, Commissioner and White House referrals;
•
SSA replies to inquiries from foreign officials on specific cases;
•
approved Forms SSA-553 (Special Determination); and
•
reconsideration Determinations.
Annotate the copies with the name of the servicing FBU, or if unknown, the name and
address of the claimant.
