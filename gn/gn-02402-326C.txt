C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor FI for IDD to several Caribbean countries, including Jamaica.
All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor FI have a prefix of "70"
at the beginning of the routing transit number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the
first position of the depositor account number (DAN). The letter assigned for Jamaica
is “M.”
