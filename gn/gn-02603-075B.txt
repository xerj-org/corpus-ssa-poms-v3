SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02603.075 - Facility of Payment Provision
Effective Dates: 12/16/2011 - Present | TN 7 (12-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202603075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Dates of application
1. For benefits payable for months prior to 01/1996
•
If the deduction before reduction provision causes no change in the total benefit
amount payable to the household, do not make the deduction.
•
If the deduction causes a change in the total payable amount to the household, the
deduction should be no less than the reduced amount payable to the household.
•
Do not pay any resultant increase in the benefits to auxiliaries and survivors who
are not subject to the deduction. Instead, pay it to the beneficiary subject to the
deduction.
•
This payment to the beneficiary subject to the deduction is facility of payment provision.
•
The facility of payment provision applies to all of the deduction before reduction
events listed in GN 02603.040 except the suspension of auxiliary or survivor benefits because of noncompliance
with the drug addiction and alcoholism (DA&A) treatment provisions of P.L. 103-296
and the prisoner suspension provisions.
2. Separate households
In applying the facility of payment provision, beneficiaries who do not live in the
same household with the beneficiary subject to a deduction receive the readjusted
rate. However, delay payment of the readjusted rate resulting from work suspension
or temporary work deductions under the annual earnings test until after receipt of
the annual report of earnings and the deductions become permanent.
3. For Months after 12/1995
After 12/1995, the facility of payment provision no longer applies.
