SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.040 - Evidence Required for Claimants Age 73 or Older
Effective Dates: 06/18/2015 - Present | TN 15 (02-92)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
If a claimant filed an application alleging to be age 73 or older, his/her DB may
be determined on the basis of two documents provided both records
•
Were recorded at least 5 years prior to the filing date of the current application,
and
•
Indicate that he/she is at least age 73.
A public or religious record of birth established before age 5 need not be requested
and no written DB determination need be prepared. Use the alleged DB.
For instructions about proof of age tolerances that may apply, see "Evidence of Age
- Tolerances" in GN 00302.030.
