E. Description of Monaco IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Monaco will come from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN is 27 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR consists of 25 characters. Technicians
will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
