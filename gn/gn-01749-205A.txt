SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01749.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefit under the Czech Agreement
Effective Dates: 03/12/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201749205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with the Czech
Republic, the applicant should submit the same evidence necessary to establish a claim
for regular (non-Totalization) retirement, survivors or disability insurance benefits.
In addition, the applicant must submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are claimed from the United States.
