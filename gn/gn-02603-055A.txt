A. General information on deductions and WC and PDB offset
Apply the reduction under the family maximum provision before withholding under the
offset provision. This step provides a real benefits reduction due to the WC or PDB
offset. For WC or PDB procedures, see DI 52150.070 Procedure for applying WC or PDB offset
Follow these steps when WC or PDB offset applies:
Step
Action
Step 1
Reduce benefits for the family maximum.
Step 2
Apply WC or PDB offset to determine the amount of offset that applies to each beneficiary.
Step 3
Apply events subject to deductions listed in GN 02603.040.
Step 4
Apply deduction before reduction procedure in GN 02603.050.
Subtract WC or PDB offset amount from step 2 above, for the deductee from the PTAP
or TTAP.
Subtract WC or PDB offset amount for non-deductees from the benefit amount determined
using the formula.
