SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.313 - Coding Austria Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/12/2024 - Present | TN 250 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402313 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processor bank for Austria will have a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “A” assigned for Austria.
