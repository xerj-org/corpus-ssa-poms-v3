D. Process - Manual Adjustment Credit and Award Processes (MADCAP)
When designating the proper paragraphs and fill-ins for notices in claims processed
through MADCAP, the CS or other PC technician fills in the "representative" line.
The BA/BTE or PETE enters the fill-in showing the proper withholding amount, e.g.,
fill-in (1) in paragraph ATY057.
The charts below in GN 03930.040E and GN 03930.040F outline the most common situations and circumstances.
