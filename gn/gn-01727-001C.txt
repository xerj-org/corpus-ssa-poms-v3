C. Periods of coverage earned before the effective date of the Totalization Agreement
The United States and Portugal can use periods of U.S. and Portuguese social security
coverage a worker earned prior to August 1, 1989 to establish entitlement to U.S.
or
Portuguese Totalization benefits. However, the earliest possible month of entitlement
to such benefits is August 1989.
