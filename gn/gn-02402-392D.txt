D. Overview of IDD for Russia
IDD payments sent to Russia must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that territory.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
