GN 01706.305 Italian Transmittal/Request Form (IT/USA 2)
The IT/USA 2 is used by INPS to transmit material and request information. The IT/USA
2 will be used to transmit the application form IT/USA 1 when it is sent by INPS to
an FSP in Italy. It also will be used to transmit the Italian coverage certification
form IT/USA 3 bis to OIO. INPS completes part III of the form when information is
requested from OIO.
