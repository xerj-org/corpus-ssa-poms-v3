SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.650 - Title II Beneficiary Receiving EFT Payments Dies After the End of the Month but Before the Payment Date
Effective Dates: 04/19/2022 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408650 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Examples
1. Example 1
Ms. Jessica Smith was receiving benefits in Cycle 2 as a spouse. They died on July
4, 2008. When the FO developed for the underpayment for the month of June, they discovered
they had received a final divorce from the wage earner on June 3, 2008, after only
5 years of marriage. Ms. Jessica Smith's benefits were terminated as of June. The
payment for July 9, 2008, was not due.
2. Example 2
Lisa Beneficiary, a widow, received benefits in Cycle 1. They died February 29, 2008.
The March 3, 2008, payment for February 2008 was not
due.
3. Example 3
Sara Tee received retirement benefits in Cycle 1. They died on August 1, 2008, the
payment date for July 2008 benefits. This meets the early delivery exception, so their
August 1, 2008, payment was due.
4. Example 4
Morgan Payman received retirement benefits in Cycle 4. They died on June 1, 2008.
Their June 25, 2008 payment for May 2008 was due.
5. Example 5
Melvin Hearty was receiving Cycle 3 disability benefits before their death on February 29,
2008. Melvin Hearty's March 19, 2008 payment for February 2008 was not due.
