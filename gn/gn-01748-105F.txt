F. How ATP credits Danish contributions
a.
Contributions
Total contributions are paid per year. One line per type of contribution per year.
b.
Deductions
If the contribution includes type 600 or 610 it has to be divided by 2 (these contributions
are paid in double – SSA does not recognize type 600 or 610).
c.
Contribution for calculation
This is the “cleared” contribution which the calculation is based upon.
d.
Employment factor
Months – amount of months (contribution for calculation is divided by the standard contribution
per citizen)
QCs – amount of quarters (number of months is divided by 4
Year - amount of years (contribution for calculation is divided by 12 months.
NOTE: ATP will do all calculations for SSA and convert all contributions to the “QC Employment
Factor”.
