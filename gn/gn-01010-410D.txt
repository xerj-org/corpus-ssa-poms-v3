D. Failure to submit evidence chart
Step
T2 Initial Claims Disposition Process – Failure to Submit Essential Evidence
1
At the initial interview, if needed, we provide a notice and request that the claimant
return the information or evidence to us within 30-days.
The initial notice provides for a 15-day return period to indicate the sense of urgency.
We will deny the claim at 30 days.
2
If we do not receive the information or evidence within 15 days, we send a final request
notice explaining that we will deny the claim 30 days from the date of the original
request.
3
If after 30 days, we do not receive the requested information or evidence, we make
a determination on the initial claim. Technically deny the initial claim the day after
the 30th day.
