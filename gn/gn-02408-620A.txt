A. Overview of manual EFT reclamation requests
The Field Office (FO) or Processing Center (PC) must input a manual reclamation request
(E-stop) when:
•
The system has failed to send an automated reclamation request 20 days after a Death
Notification Entry (DNE) has gone out for Title II, or 30 days after a DNE has gone
out for Title XVI;
•
The FO/PC receives a reject message that the system's attempt to send an automated
reclamation request rejected;
•
An automated or manual E-stop has been sent with an incorrect date of death, and not
all of the incorrect payments were requested;
•
More than 50 Title II payments need to be reclaimed; or
•
A Title II Prior Month Accruals (PMA) payment sent after death needs to be reclaimed
because the beneficiary was not due the payment. A manual reclamation request is not
needed if the payment was due.
