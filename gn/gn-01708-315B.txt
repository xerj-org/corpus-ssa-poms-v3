B. Process
The following process is used for obtaining medical examinations for Germany. (See
GN 01704.810 for general information on processing requests for medical examinations.)
•
German agency requests for medical examinations are made in the bottom block of Item
III.C. of form D/USA 3. The type of examination should be shown in the space provided.
•
SSA schedules an examination or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from files as described in GN 01704.810.
•
SSA requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for the German
agency using form SSA-1278-U3-GE.
