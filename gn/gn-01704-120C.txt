SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.120 - Totalization Status Code (TSC)
Effective Dates: 07/30/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Pending
P1 - Used to indicate that a totalization claimant is not insured but has at least
six U.S. QC's and that a final decision on his totalization claim is awaiting completion
of totalization development.
P2 - Used to indicate that a U.S. totalization claim is pending and that the claimant
is entitled to RSDI benefits.
