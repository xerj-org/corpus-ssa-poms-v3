B. Policy
For purposes of the agreement, the “territory of the Netherlands” means Dutch territory on the continent of Europe. Although the Netherlands Antilles
are a part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, the islands have a separate Social Security
system which is not covered by the agreement.
