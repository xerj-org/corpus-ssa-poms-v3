E. Description of Costa Rica IDD bank data
The data to code IDD for Costa Rica comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The IBAN for Costa Rica consists
of 22 characters. However, the bank data reflected on the MBR consists of 25 characters
including Costa Rica’s country code of “E.”
