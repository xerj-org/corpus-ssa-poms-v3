B. Procedure — examining evidence
There is no list of exact rules as to the evidence required to establish equitable
adoption. Because State laws vary and development may be complex, handle each case
on an individual basis. If there is a written agreement, the agreement or copy thereof
that you have obtained, together with answers to the questions in GN 00306.220A.2. above, should provide sufficient evidence for a determination.
