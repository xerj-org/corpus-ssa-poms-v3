SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00505.018 - Legal Guardian in Scandinavia
Effective Dates: 05/07/2018 - Present | TN 6 (09-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200505018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Denmark, Finland and Norway
In these countries, the practices of the individual courts vary and the type of legal
guardians appointed is not always based on a finding of legal incompetence. Therefore,
where the appointment papers from these countries do not show a court finding of legal
incompetence, other evidence of incompetence is required.
