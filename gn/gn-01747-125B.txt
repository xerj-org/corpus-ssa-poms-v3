B. Policy — Using Evidence Submitted in Claim for U.S. Benefits
Evidence that is used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits may also be used by Denmark to establish the same factor for Danish benefits.
However, it may be necessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence to document
a factor of entitlement for Danish benefits if requested by the Danish authorities.
