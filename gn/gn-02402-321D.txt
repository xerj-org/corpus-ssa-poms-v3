D. Overview of IDD for Dominican Republic
IDD payments sent to Dominican Republic must be in U.S. Dollar (USD) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Dominican
Republic. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
