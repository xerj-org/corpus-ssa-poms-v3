SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.090 - Payee Fails To Complete Annual Accounting Report
Effective Dates: 06/19/2013 - Present | TN 82 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605090 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Evaluating the current payee’s suitability
1. Attempt to contact the payee
Use the following steps to contact and evaluate the current payee:
a.
Make every reasonable effort to contact the payee by phone or mail before considering
appointing a successor payee. Advise the payee that failure to complete and return
the accounting report may result in paying benefits directly to the beneficiary, or
to another individual.
b.
Document your contacts with the payee, using the Make Notes screen, as described in
MS INTRANETERPS 015.002 and MS INTRANETERPS 015.003B.
c.
Develop for a successor payee, if you determine the current payee is no longer suitable,
as described in GN 00605.085D.
NOTE: Do not suspend benefits as a tool for achieving contact with a representative payee. If
you cannot contact the current payee, begin developing for a successor payee or begin
paying the beneficiary directly, as described in GN 00605.090C.
2. Decision factors
Consider a change of payee in these situations:
•
Undeliverable mail may indicate the payee’s whereabouts are unknown, refer to GN 00605.045.
•
Unacceptable responses on the accounting form may indicate poor payee performance,
refer to GN 00605.066 through GN 00605.069.
•
Not responding to accounting requests and additional attempts at contact may indicate
poor performance, misuse or fraud, refer to GN 00605.085.
