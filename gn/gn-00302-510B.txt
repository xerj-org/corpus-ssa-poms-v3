B. How to recognize corrected BC's
Examine BC's for one of the following features commonly used by registrars to indicate
corrected records:
•
Incorrect entry is lined through and the change is typed or written in (sometimes
along with the date of the change)
•
Explanation of the corrected item(s) is attached or entered on the document itself
(often on the reverse), or
•
Document is marked “Corrected,” “Altered,” or some similar notation.
