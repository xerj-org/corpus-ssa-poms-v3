SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.960 - Use of Forms I-151 and I-551 as Proof of Age
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302960 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Example
A RIB claimant presents an I-151 with the revision date of 7/1/46. She entered the
U.S. on 9/10/47. Her alleged DB, 12/7/21, agrees with the DB on the I-151.
We assume that the I-151 was recorded between 9/10/47 and 7/14/49 (6 months after
the next revision, 1/14/49).
NOTE: Provided the other requirements of GN 00302.115 are met, the SSA record plus the I-151 constitute first priority automatically convincing
evidence since the entire possible recordation period was prior to the claimant's
attainment of age 31. (If the WE had attained age 31 between 9/10 /47 and 7/14/49,
the automatically convincing evidence guidelines could not be used unless INS is contacted for the actual recordation date, and that date was
before the claimant became age 31).
