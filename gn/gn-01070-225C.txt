SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01070.225 - Prior Folder In Federal Archives And Records Center (FARC)
Effective Dates: 05/21/2007 - Present | TN 19 (05-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Folder not retrieved timely from FARC
1. Open application involved
If immediate payment is due to the claimant, and the prior claims folder has not been
retrieved from the FARC within 15 working days (the amount of elapsed time before
the FO's second follow-up request):
•
FO Action--Partially adjudicate the current claim and prepare a diary for 60 days
for PC follow-up of the open application issue.
•
PC Action--When the diary matures, follow the instructions in GN 01070.222A.1.
2. All other issues
If the prior claims folder has not been retrieved from the FARC within 15 working
days of the initial FO telephone request, the FO should proceed in accordance with
GN 01070.222B. & C.
