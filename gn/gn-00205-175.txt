GN 00205.175 Claimant Outside U.S. - Procedure
Send the applicant all the needed forms when the application is sent.
To avoid any benefit loss, suggest claimants whose claims are being developed by direct
mail return completed applications by airmail.
See GN 00905.000 for special instructions on correspondence with claimants outside the U.S.
