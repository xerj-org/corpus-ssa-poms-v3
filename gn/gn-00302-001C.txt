SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.001 - Situations Where Age Must be Immediately Developed
Effective Dates: 11/14/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Note
In addition, the following individuals must submit evidence of age:
•
Applicant for a Social Security number (SSN) (see RM 10205.020)
•
Miner filing for black lung benefits who is, by any allegation in file, age 70 as
of the date the claim was filed and who is working.
