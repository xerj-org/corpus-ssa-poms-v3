SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02315.042 - Colorado – Small Estates
Effective Dates: 10/09/2014 - Present | TN 14 (10-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202315042 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Contents of Affidavit
The affidavit must state that:
1.
The value of the entire estate, wherever located, less liens and encumbrances, does
not exceed, for the Decedent’s year of death (Y.O.D.) 2014 $64,000, Y.O.D. 2013 $63,000,
Y.O.D. 2012 $61,000, and for Y.O.D. 2011 and 21010, $60,000, and there is no real
estate;
2.
At least ten (10) days have elapsed since the death of the decedent;
3.
No application or petition for the appointment of a personal representative is pending
or has been granted in any jurisdiction; and
4.
Each claiming successor is entitled to payment in the respective proportion set forth
in the affidavit.
