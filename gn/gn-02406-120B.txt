SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02406.120 - When to Use the Nonreceipt of Payment Process — General
Effective Dates: 03/15/2017 - Present | TN 36 (03-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy - Criteria
The nonreceipt of payment process is used:
•
if the payment is not listed as an exclusion in GN
02406.175,
•
if the nonreceipt allegation is “valid and timely”, as defined in GN 02406.120A, and
Note: “Timely” is defined as no more than 12 months from the issue date of the paper check.
•
if the Payment History Update System (PHUS), Supplemental Security Income Detail (SSID)
or TCIS indicates that the payment was issued.
