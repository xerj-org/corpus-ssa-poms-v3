Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
When a U.S. claim is filed with a Luxembourg agency, the agency forwards Form LUX/USA
3 certifying the date of filing. In addition, the appropriate block in Item I.D. should
be checked to indicate that a U.S. claim has been filed.
