B. Procedures
Take the following actions when a claimant states that he/she contacted the West German
Embassy or Consulate in connection with the Holocaust:
Step
Action
1
Ask the claimant where he/she lived at the time of the contact.
2
Consult the list of consulates below to determine to determine which one to contact.
3
Request authorization from the claimant to contact the consulate.
4
Request the consulate to verify that the claimant is a survivor and to certify the
age shown in the consulate's record.
