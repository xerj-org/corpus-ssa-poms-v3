B. Processing
Send the forms directly to the individual for completion. Enclose a totalization-addressed,
blue, return envelope. When the completed form is received, ensure it is signed but
do not attempt to verify the accuracy of the information. Forward the form to the
appropriate Belgium agency using Form SSA-2960-U2-BE.
