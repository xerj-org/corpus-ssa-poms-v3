C. Related Policy
1. Domicile
The laws that apply in determining if the legal representative can qualify under the
small estate statutes are the laws of the State of domicile of the deceased at the
time of his/her death. See GN 02301.035C.
2. Good Acquittance
See GN 02301.030C for a discussion of good acquittance.
NOTE: An individual does not give good acquittance under a small estate statute of a State
other than the domicile of the deceased at the time of his/her death.
3. Payment of LSDP to Legal Representative
See RS 00210.010A. for instances where payment of a LSDP can be made to the legal representative of
an estate.
