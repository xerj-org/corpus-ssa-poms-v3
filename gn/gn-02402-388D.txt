D. Overview of IDD for Bosnia Herzegovina
IDD payments sent to Bosnia Herzegovina must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bosnia
Herzegovina. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
