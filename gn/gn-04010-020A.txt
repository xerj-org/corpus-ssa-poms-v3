A. Error on the Face of the Evidence
Error on the Face of the Evidence exists when it is absolutely clear that the determination or decision was incorrect based on the evidence that the Social Security Administration (SSA) had, anywhere
within SSA's records, when it made the determination or decision.
In view of the interrelationship between Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) compensation
records and SSA earnings records, SSA considers RRB compensation records as a part
of SSA earnings records for the purpose of the topic described in this section. SSA
does not consider other records (such as Veterans Affairs records) to be a part of
SSA records.
