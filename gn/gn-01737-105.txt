GN 01737.105 Effective Date of the Greek Agreement
Policy
The agreement with Greece became effective September 1, 1994. Periods of Greek and
U.S. Social Security coverage a worker earned prior to September 1, 1994, may be used
to establish entitlement to U.S. or Greek totalization benefits. However, the earliest
possible month of entitlement to such benefits is September 1994.
