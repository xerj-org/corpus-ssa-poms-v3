Effective Dates: 04/10/2019 - Present | TN 2 (04-19)

B. Procedure – U.S. claims
Prepare Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 (U.S. Brazil Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
and forward to the Brazilian liaison agency the INSS if the Brazilian certified record
is not in claims file.
