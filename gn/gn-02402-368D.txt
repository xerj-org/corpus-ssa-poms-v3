D. Overview of IDD for San Marino
IDD payments sent to San Marino must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves San Marino. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for additional information.
