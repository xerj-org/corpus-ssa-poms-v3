A. Introduction
Claims for U.S. Totalization benefits will take significantly longer to process than
regular claims. This is due to the need to obtain the number holder’s (NH’s) foreign
coverage record from the agreement country. In addition, due to systems limitations,
all Totalization awards are processed manually.
