D. How the DO may obtain information from records
Step
Action
1
Obtain the Petition for Naturalization or Declaration of Intention number, if available
2
Using this number, obtain the information from the naturalization records (or arrival
or alien registration records) maintained by INS (see E. and F. below).
NOTE:   Alternatively, you may examine the naturalization record at the U.S. District Court
or other court of record where they are filed.
These records may contain information not only regarding the individual's DB and
place of birth, but also that of his/her spouse and children.
