C. Determining the required application screens on MCS for Totalization benefits
Use the following information to determine which application screens to complete when
taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with the U.K.
1. Retirement or disability claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the appropriate retirement or disability
benefit MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MS 03509.011),
and the SSA-2490 screens (see MS 03507.001).
2. Survivors claims
In survivors claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MS 03509.011), and Part I and Part II of the paper or electronic
Form SSA-2490-BK.
