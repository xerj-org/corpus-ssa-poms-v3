C. Procedure — Required applications under the Polish agreement
Use the following information to determine which applications must be completed when
taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits and/or Polish benefits under the agreement
with Poland.
1. U.S. Totalization Benefits Only
•
Retirement or disability claims under U.S. Totalization benefits
In retirement or disability claims, complete the appropriate retirement or disability
MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and
the SSA-2490-BK screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
•
Survivor claims under U.S. Totalization benefits
In survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and Part I of the paper or electronic (e-form)
application Form SSA-2490-BK.
2. Polish Benefits Only (Regular or Totalization)
Claims for Polish benefits only (regular or Totalization) are MCS exclusions. Complete
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic (e-form application Form SSA-2490-BK.
3. Polish Benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. Benefits (regular or Totalization)
•
Retirement or disability claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the appropriate retirement or disability
benefits MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011),
and the SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
•
Survivor Claims (regular or Totalization)
In survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and Parts I and II of the paper or electronic
(e-form) application form SSA-2490.
NOTE: Form SSA-2490-BK is available through the Totalization Benefits Resource
Kit.
