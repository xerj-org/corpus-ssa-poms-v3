D. Development for dependents and survivors
If a non-U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the five-year residency
requirement and alleges Korean citizenship or residency, the Field Office (FO) should
develop for Korean citizenship and residence status to verify that an exemption provided
by the Agreement applies (Section 202(t)(1) of the Act).
