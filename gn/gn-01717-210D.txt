D. Policy — Entitlement factors
The following indicates what factors of entitlement, in addition to insured status,
must be established in claims for Swedish benefits.
1. Retirement Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Swedish retirement claims are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Marriage, if a wife's supplement to the basic pension is claimed.
2. Survivors Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Swedish survivors' claims are:
a.
Death of worker
b.
Age of all claimants
c.
Marriage, if a widow(er)'s benefit is claimed
d.
Relationship of child, if a child's benefit is claimed.
3. Disability Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Swedish disability claims are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Degree of disability (medical evidence)
c.
Marriage, if a wife's supplement to the basic pension is claimed.
