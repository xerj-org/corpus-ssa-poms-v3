B. Policy-taxes included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social
Security coverage and taxes apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Swedish Social Security taxes that finance old-age,
survivors and disability benefits under both the basic and ATP programs (but not
taxes that finance other Swedish benefits such as unemployment, work accident and
occupational illness, family cash allowances, and health insurance).
