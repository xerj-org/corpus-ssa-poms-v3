SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02605.040 - Notice of COA and Another Event
Effective Dates: 09/20/2021 - Present | TN 39 (09-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202605040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Development
•
If there are multiple events, input immediately those events that do not require development,
and continue development on the other events. See MSOM T2PE 003.001. (Title II)
•
If the report must be transferred to another office for additional development, annotate
the referral as to what action has already been taken.
