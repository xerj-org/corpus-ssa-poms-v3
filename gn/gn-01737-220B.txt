Effective Dates: 02/20/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
The special application forms are not taken in the FO. After receiving a claim for
Greek benefits that was filed with SSA, the Greek agency will mail the appropriate
Greek applications, if necessary, to the applicant with instructions requesting that
he or she complete the forms and mail them directly to the Greek agency with any necessary
additional evidence.
