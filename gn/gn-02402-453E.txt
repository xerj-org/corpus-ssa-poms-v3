E. Description of Mongolia IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Mongolia will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN is 20 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR consists of 12 characters. Technicians
will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
