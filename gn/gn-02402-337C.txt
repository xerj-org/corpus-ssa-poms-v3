SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.337 - Coding Israel Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 08/25/2026 - Present | TN 327 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402337 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processor bank in Israel will have a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the routing
number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the first position of the Depositor Account
Number (DAN). The letter assigned for Israel is R.
