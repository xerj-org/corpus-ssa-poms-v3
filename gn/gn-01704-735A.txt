A. General
The CSC does not require U.S. earnings information.
However, in two situations, the CSC does require U.S. QC information for specific
years. Those situations are:
1.
Disability claims; and
2.
Claims for orphan benefits on the record of a deceased mother.
If either of these situations apply, complete part I, item 3 on Form SSA-1279-U3-SZ.
