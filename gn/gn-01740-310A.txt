SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.310 - Chilean Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
If the Chilean agencies need additional medical evidence to process a claim for a
U.S. resident, they will request SSA to schedule a medical examination. SSA will schedule
the examination and send the results to the Chilean agencies.
Chilean agency requests for medical examinations will be made on Form CHI/USA 3. The
Chileans will use a separate form or letter to specify the type of examination or
medical tests that they need.
