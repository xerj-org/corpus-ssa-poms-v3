Effective Dates: 01/27/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302370 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Extreme difficulty in obtaining evidence
Sometimes it may be very difficult to obtain evidence of the worker's age from the
claimant due to a lack of communication or cooperation between the worker and the
claimant. This is most likely to occur when the worker and spouse do not live together, but may also happen when they do.
Follow the procedure below if evidence (e.g., a civil birth record) is clearly unobtainable
due to reasons beyond either SSA's or the claimant's control (e.g., the worker refuses
to cooperate).
