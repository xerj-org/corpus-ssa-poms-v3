B. Policy — taxes included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
1.
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
2.
Belgium’s Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors and disability
benefits, sickness insurance, unemployment and work accident insurance and cash family
allowances.
