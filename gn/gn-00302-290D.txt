SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.290 - Proof of Age Requirements for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
Effective Dates: 08/26/2009 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302290 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Assistance
Because of the age of many of these claimants, and since many of them may be physically
or mentally handicapped, the FO must be alert to the need to assist them in securing
the necessary proofs (see GN 01010.410 and GN 00301.280 - GN 00301.285). This may include the purchase of necessary evidence (e.g., a BC) if the claimant
cannot reasonably be expected to obtain the evidence without payment of a fee.
