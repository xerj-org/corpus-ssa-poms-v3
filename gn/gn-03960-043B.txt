SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.043 - Administrative Review of the Fee Authorized Under the Fee Agreement Process - Concurrent Titles II and XVI Cases or Affected Auxiliary Beneficiary(ies) Involved
Effective Dates: 01/08/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960043 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
If a party requests administrative review and SSA has not authorized the total fee
under the agreement, take the following actions if you are the PC reviewer.
1. Concurrent Titles II and XVI Cases
a.
Call the FO.
•
Ask the title XVI CR to: estimate when he/she will make a fee determination; notify
each party as quickly as possible; and send a copy of the Fee Agreement Notice with
the claim(s) file to you.
•
Advise the CR that because of the pending request, he/she should not adjust the title
II income as SI 00830.100 and SI 02006.210 require, until receiving the claim(s) file and a copy of the determination you make
on administrative review.
b.
Explain that you cannot work on the request until SSA authorizes the total fee under
the agreement, when acknowledging receipt and notifying the parties.
c.
Diary the case for 30 days for receipt of the copy of the Fee Agreement Notice(s),
unless the estimate for completion is greater than 30 days.
2. Affected Auxiliary Beneficiary
a.
Call the PC module. Ask the Benefit Authorizer (BA) or CA to:
•
estimate the date of completion of the auxiliary claim(s) and fee determination(s);
•
notify all parties of the amount of the representative's fee as quickly as possible;
and
•
send a copy of the fee determination(s) to you.
b.
Explain that you cannot work on the request until SSA authorizes the total fee under
the agreement, when acknowledging receipt and notifying the parties.
c.
Diary the case for 30 days for receipt of a copy of the notice(s) of the outstanding
fee amount(s), unless the estimate for completion is greater than 30 days.
