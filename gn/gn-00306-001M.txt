SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.001 - Definitions of Terms Used in Determining Parent-Child Relationship for Title II Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/24/2014 - Present | TN 43 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

M. Legitimate child
A "legitimate child," often referred to as a child "born in wedlock" or a "legal marital
child," is a child who is born of a valid marriage, including common-law (where recognized),
void, voidable, and same-sex, and certain NMLRs.
Section 202(d)(3) of the Act references legitimacy with respect to the dependency
requirement for child's benefits (see GN 00306.002C).
