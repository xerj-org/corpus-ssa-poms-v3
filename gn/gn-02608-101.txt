GN 02608.101 Exemption Due to Application Filed Before December 1, 1977
NOTE: This POMS section applies to months of entitlement prior to January 2024 only. The
Social Security Fairness Act (SSFA) of 2023 eliminated the reduction of Social Security
benefits under GPO as of January 2024. For more information, see GN 02608.100.
