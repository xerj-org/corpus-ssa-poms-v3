SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02605.250 - Change of Address (COA) – Foreign to Foreign
Effective Dates: 02/07/2024 - Present | TN 45 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202605250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Initiate a change from one foreign address to another when:
•
the beneficiary notifies SSA that they will be moving, or
•
SSA is notified of the change in some other manner (i.e. response on Foreign Enforcement
Query).
If the notification is made by someone other than the beneficiary, verification of
the information provided may be necessary before initiating the change of address
action via the post entitlement system PEAD screen.
