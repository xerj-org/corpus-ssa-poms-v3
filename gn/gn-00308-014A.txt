A. Process
Divorce and marriage records are available from County Clerk, in the county where
the divorce was granted or the license was issued in addition to the State (as indicated
below). The cost of copy varies at the County level. Birth and death records are available
from the counties listed below. The cost of a copy is $20.00 unless otherwise indicated
and, unless indicated, all counties are “YES.”
