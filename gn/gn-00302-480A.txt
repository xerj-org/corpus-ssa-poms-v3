SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.480 - Missing Element in DB Found Only in SSA Records
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302480 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Occasionally, the evidence submitted by the claimant (i.e., evidence other than the
NUMIDENT or other SSA records) will not show the complete DB. Documents such as census
records or insurance policies may show only an age. Other documents may show only
a year of birth.
