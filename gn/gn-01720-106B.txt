SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.106 - How to Evaluate U.K. Coverage Certifications
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to evaluate a U.K. coverage certification.
Step
Action
1
Ensure that the record has been certified directly from the U.K. agency to OIO or
FSP, not through the claimant or third party.
•
If yes, go to step 2
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
2
Check all identifying data (name, DOB etc.) to ensure, to your satisfaction, that
the U.K. certification belongs to the proper claimant.
•
If yes, go to step 3
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
3
Verify that there is a number of weeks entry for each "from-to" period and that the
number does not exceed the actual number of weeks in the period.
Exception: For the period 1 Jan 37 - 4 July 48, the number of weeks entry may exceed the 599
actual weeks. However, the maximum number of QCs that can be credited for that period
is 47.
•
If yes, see GN 01720.110 to credit the U.K. coverage.
•
If no, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
