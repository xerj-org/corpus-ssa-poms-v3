E. Procedure - Determining “Substantially All of the Period”
1.
Determine the number of days in the period.
2.
Determine the number of days in the period that the child was both living with and
receiving one- half support from the NH.
3.
Find the appropriate length of period in the chart below. If the number of days meets
or exceeds the minimum required in the right hand column of the chart, the dependency
requirement is met.
Length of Period from Child's DB to Point for Determining Dependency
Minimum Number of Days Where Living With and One-Half Support Must Be Met
183 - 365 Days
Length of period minus 91 days
1 - 182 Days
One-half of days in period (round fractions up to higher number)
