SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.510 - Corrected Birth Certificates (BC)
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | TN 9 (02-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302510 (fetched 2026-10-04)

N. Accuracy of the correction is confirmed
If you determine that the correction was accurate, follow this procedure to establish
the DB.
Step
Action
1
Explain briefly on an RC why you believe the accuracy of the corrected item has been
established.
2
Treat the BC as if the original document reflected the corrected item(s).
3
Was the original document recorded before age 5?
​
• If yes, treat the BC as preferred evidence.
​
• If no, go to step 4.
4
Treat the BC as a DBC for purposes of:
•
Convincing evidence (see GN 00302.110-GN 00302.118), or
•
Best evidence, if a discrepancy exists (see GN 00302.165-GN 00302.170)
•
Go to step 5.
5
Establish the DB under normal proof-of-age rules.
