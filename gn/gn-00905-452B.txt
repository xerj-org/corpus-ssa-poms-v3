SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.452 - Claims from Residents of Türkiye (formerly Turkey)
Effective Dates: 11/01/2023 - Present | TN 21 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905452 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
When a claimant appears at an FBU or FSP (with prior RFBO authorization) to file for
benefits, FBU or FSP staff must:
•
Secure a statement concerning their claim for benefits;
•
Verify the claimant's identity;
•
Certify the claimant's identification book (see GN 00905.452D in this section); and
•
Forward this material to the Division of International Operations (DIO), with any
evidence the individual submits.
