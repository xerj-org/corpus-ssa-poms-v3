SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.130 - Crediting U.S. Quarters of Coverage (QCs) During the Year the Totalization Agreement Becomes Effective
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 12 (09-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Example
A U.S. citizen who is permanently employed in France by an American employer earns
$15,000 during the period 1/88-6/88. Four QCs would be credited to the U.S. record
for 1988 even though the agreement with France assigns coverage only to France beginning
7/1/88.
