A. Introduction
There are over 100 compensation funds and commissions which administer the decentralized
Swiss old-age, survivors and disability insurance program. However for purposes of
the agreement, the Swiss Compensation Fund (Caisse Suisse de Compensation) located
in Geneva is the sole liaison agency.
