A. Introduction to the Field Office (FO) Remittance Receipt Process
Field Offices can process programmatic remittances through the Social Security Electronic
Remittance System (SERS) see GN 02403.161. However, remittances that cannot be processed through SERS require the interviewer
to enter the remittance information into the Debt Management system (DMS) in order
to acknowledge receipt of an acceptable remittance received in the FO.
