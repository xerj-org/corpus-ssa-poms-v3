C. Procedure
If a Chilean liaison agency requests information, make sure that the requirements
of the Privacy Act are met. Return any request (without the requested information)
unless:
•
The request indicates that the information is needed to process a claim under the
agreement,
•
The agency has provided the claimant's written consent for SSA to disclose information,
or
•
The file contains a Form SSA-2490-BK that is signed by the claimant.
