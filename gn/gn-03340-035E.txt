E. Access to Alcohol and Drug Abuse Patients (ADAP) medical records
ADAP records are defined in 42 U.S.C.§ 290dd-2(a) as records of the identity, diagnosis,
prognosis, or treatment of any patient which are maintained in connection with the
performance of any program or activity relating to substance abuse education, prevention,
training, treatment, rehabilitation, or research, which is conducted, regulated, or
directly or indirectly assisted by any department or agency of the United States.
With limited exceptions, the agency is prohibited from disclosing ADAP records, without
the individual’s consent. Thus, when ADAP records are likely to have an adverse effect
on an individual, the consent to disclose the records to the person’s designated representative
must meet both SSA requirements and the requirements in 42 C.F.R. § 2.31. See GN 03305.030 for further guidance on disclosure of ADAP records and the form of consent.
