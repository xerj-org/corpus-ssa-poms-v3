A. Overview of methods for responding to request for assistance
1. Electronic response
SSA offices are permitted to provide a response to another office solely by electronic
means (e.g., annotating the MDW or EVID screen), without any further action from the
assisting office, if an authorized SSA employee (see GN 00301.090 for list of authorized employees):
a.
Saw and authenticated the original document or a certified copy of the original or
extract; and
b.
The information was:
•
Certified to one of SSA's electronic records (e.g., MCS, MSSICS, etc.) (see GN 00301.286) and retrieved from there; or
•
Retrieved from a copy of the document already in SSA's possession.
2. Paper response
A paper response may be required if the issue is too complex to respond to electronically,
or if attachments are needed. If a paper response is provided, the FO will send:
•
The actual document; or
•
A certified copy
