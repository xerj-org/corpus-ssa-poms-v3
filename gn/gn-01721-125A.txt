SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.125 - Eligibility for Spanish Benefits under the Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/13/2022 - Present | TN 7 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
The agreement provides that Spain will count a worker's U.S. coverage when determining
eligibility for Spanish benefits under the general system or special occupational
systems if the worker:
•
is not insured based on Spanish coverage alone, and
•
has at least one year of Spanish coverage (See GN 01721.125D. for exception.)
