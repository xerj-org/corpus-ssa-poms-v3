A. Introduction
The Greek Social Security agency maintains a computerized record for each person who
works under the Greek system. To help ensure that these records are correct, each
worker is required to keep a separate “insurance booklet” which also shows his or
her periods of Greek coverage. The worker's employer (or the worker in case of a self-employed
person) places stamps purchased from the Government in the insurance booklet to record
periods of coverage.
