SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01735.130 - Payment of Irish Invalidity (Disability) Benefits in the United States
Effective Dates: 01/23/2003 - Present | TN 1 (01-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201735130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Under the agreement, a worker who has been incapacitated for at least 12 months (as
defined by Irish law) while a resident of the United States will satisfy the residence
requirement. Thus, the agreement permits U.S. residents who meet the other eligibility
requirements for Irish invalidity (disability) benefits to qualify even though they
did not reside in Ireland during the 12-month period prior to entitlement.
