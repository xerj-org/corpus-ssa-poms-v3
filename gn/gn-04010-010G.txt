SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04010.010 - Reopenings - Clerical Error
Effective Dates: 10/21/1996 - Present | TN 7 (02-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204010010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Documentation Requirements
SSA employees must document, per GN 04001.080:
•
The rationale used to reopen a determination or decision; or
•
If SSA only considered reopening a determination or decision, but determined the rules
of administrative finality prohibit reopening.
