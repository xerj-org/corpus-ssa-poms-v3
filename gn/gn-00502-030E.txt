SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.030 - Developing Lay Evidence of Capability
Effective Dates: 05/10/2023 - Present | TN 98 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Court order as lay evidence
If the court order does not establish legal incompetence as described in GN 00502.023, but provides insight into the beneficiary's ability or lack of ability to manage
or direct the management of benefits, use the court order as lay evidence, along with
the other evidence required in your capability determination (GN 00502.030 through GN 00502.050). Weigh the information in the court order along with all the other evidence in your
capability determination.
