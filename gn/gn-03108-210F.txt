F. Effectuation of a Medicare Appeals Council (MAC) decision (SEPSC only)
The SEPSC I&E CTE is responsible for updating the Special Message on the MBR reflecting
the MAC decision.
If the MAC affirms the ALJ decision, the SEPSC I&E CTE is responsible for the effectuation
of the decision.
