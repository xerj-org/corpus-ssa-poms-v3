C. Who may be an independent decisionmaker for a personal conference
If you are unable to locate the pertinent overpayment documents in the Evidence Portal,
or from the IRG, request the paper folder using instructions in GN 03340.010B.1.
1. The independent decisionmaker
The independent decisionmaker must be a technician who:
•
Did not participate in the initial review and preliminary recommendation to deny the
waiver;
•
Did not participate in any prior waiver determination for the same overpayment; and
•
Is the same grade level as, or a higher grade level than, the technician proposing
to deny the waiver request.
In the rare situation when all employees at the appropriate grade levels have been
involved with the initial waiver review, management may need to arrange for an authorized
employee from another office to conduct the conference by telephone or video teleconference.
In situations where the individual wants an in-office conference, the manager may
arrange for an employee from another office to travel to the servicing field office
(FO).
