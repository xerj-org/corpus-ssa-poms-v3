A. Stopping overpayment recovery
Stop recovery using these instructions:
•
When an individual requests a waiver, stop recovery the month that we receive Form
SSA-632, Request for Waiver of Overpayment Recovery.
•
Input the initial waiver protest reason using the Protest/Stop Recovery Request (PC)
(DRPR) screen in the Debt Management System (DMS), refer to MS DMS 006.003.
•
Annotate DMS remarks to document your actions.
NOTE:
To determine if an individual must submit the SSA-632 form to request a waiver, refer
to GN 02250.001C.
