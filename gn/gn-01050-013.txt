GN 01050.013 Supplementary Medical Insurance Only
000-00-0000
M-Supplementary Medical Insurance.—This claim symbol is used only when the individual
is not eligible for any type of RSI monthly benefit or HIB (Deemed Insured) and is
not a “qualified RR beneficiary.” Includes individuals entitled to Premium HI.
000-00-0000
M1-Supplementary Medical Insurance.—This claim symbol is used when the individual
elects to file for SMI only but appears eligible for HIB either through entitlement to monthly benefits or under
the deemed insured provision.
