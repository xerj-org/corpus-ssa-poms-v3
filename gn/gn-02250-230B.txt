B. Information on the SSA-632-BK
At the top of the SSA-632, we inform respondents when they should complete the form.
They can complete the form if they think they are not at fault in causing the overpayment
and:
•
they cannot afford to pay the money back; and
•
they think the overpayment is unfair for some other reason.
We also inform them when they should not complete the form. An individual should not
complete the SSA-632 if they:
•
are requesting a waiver for an overpayment that is $2,000 or less.
•
think we made a mistake when we decided they were overpaid, or they disagree with
the amount of the overpayment.
•
want to request a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge.
•
only want to change the amount of money they pay back each month.
•
have been convicted of fraud relating to the overpayment they want us to waive.
NOTE:
If the overpayment was the result of fraud or similar fault on the part of the overpaid
individual, we will not make a waiver determination, refer to GN 02250.001G.
