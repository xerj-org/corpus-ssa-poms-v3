C. Periods of coverage
Periods of U.S. and Swiss social security coverage are usable to establish entitlement
to U.S. or Swiss Totalization benefits. This includes coverage a worker earned prior
to November 1, 1980. However, the earliest possible month of entitlement to such benefits
is November 1, 1980, the effective date of the original Agreement.
