GN 01716.001 Geographic Terms - Definitions - Canada and Quebec Understanding
LIST OF GEOGRAPHIC TERMS
The listed geographic descriptions are defined as follows for the purposes of this
subchapter:
•
Canada or Canadian - The information being presented applies equally to all of Canada including Quebec;
e.g., “Crediting Canadian Coverage,”
•
Quebec - The information being presented is applicable only to Quebec,
•
Ottawa - The information being presented is applicable to all of Canada except Quebec.
