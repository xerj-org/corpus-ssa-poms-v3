A. Process
The Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics has birth and death records since January 1, 1911. For earlier birth and death records,
contact the Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives.
The Kentucky OVS has marriage and divorce records since June 1, 1958. Marriage records are also available
from the County Court Clerk in the county in which the license was issued. Divorce
records are also available from the Circuit Court Clerk in the county in which the
divorce was granted. These Clerks have the records prior to June 1958 as well.
A certificate is available for purchase on the Internet or by phone, mail, or visit
to the OVS office.
