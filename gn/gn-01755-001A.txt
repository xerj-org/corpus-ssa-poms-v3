A. Introduction to the U.S.-Hungary Totalization Agreement
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Hungary:
•
eliminates dual social security coverage and taxation of the same work; and
•
permits workers (and their dependents and survivors) to qualify for partial U.S. or
Hungarian benefits, even if they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
