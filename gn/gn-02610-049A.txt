A. Introduction
The FO completes section II of SSA- 4345-U2, Record of Manual Title II Offset Computation,
to display the title II offset computed, the noncountable income and the trust funds
from which the offset amount must be subtracted for transfer to the general fund.
NOTE: The e4345 was developed for the PC and FO to be able to exchange windfall-offset
data electronically. It is a web-based application of the paper SSA-4345. For instructions
on completing the e4345, see the Field Office User's Guide and the Processing Centers User's
Guide.
