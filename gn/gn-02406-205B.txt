SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02406.205 - Nonreceipt of Title XVI Underpayment Check/Type 2 and One-Time Payment/Type 4 Payment -- Field Office (FO) and Teleservice Center (TSC) Instructions
Effective Dates: 01/09/2023 - Present | TN 101 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Non-receipt alleged and MOTP is not on SSR
•
TSC: Refer the inquiry to the servicing FO for action via the MDW or a Development Worksheet
, as appropriate.
•
FO: If the recipient alleges a nonreceipt, but there is no evidence of a payment on the
SSR, check the FO manual OTP control file to determine if we issued an MOTP but it
was not posted to the SSR. (See SM 01901.420)
•
FO: If the OTP action never occurred, and is still appropriate, initiate an OTP immediately.
EXCEPTION: If the ineligible spouse or parent contacts the FO or TSC and alleges nonreceipt
of an OTP, input the nonreceipt. The TSC will send an MDW to the FO for possible immediate
payment action.
