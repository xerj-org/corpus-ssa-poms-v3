N. Can SSA certify records?
SSA can certify the authenticity of copies of records we disclose pursuant to 20 CFR
Parts 401 and 402. We provide this service only in response to a written request.
If we certify, we do so at the time of the disclosure and will not certify copies
of records that have left our custody. A request for certified copies of records previously
released is considered a new request for records. Fees for certification are found
in 20 CFR 402.165(e). See also GN 03311.000, Privacy Act and FOIA Fees.
