A. Adjudicating claims under the Agreement
1. Field office responsibilities for processing claims under the agreement
Take an initial claim to process the insured status denial when an NH does not have
enough U.S. coverage to meet fully insured status, but the NH has at least six QCs.
The initial denial automatically generates a notice that informs the NH that he or
she does not meet the insured status requirement based on U.S. coverage alone, and
we will request the applicant’s Brazilian coverage record.
2. Division of International Operations (DIO) responsibilities
Request the applicant’s Brazilian coverage record and convert Brazilian periods of
coverage on the Brazilian record to United States QCs before adjudicating the claim.
