C. Policy - disclosure to others (3rd party)
When a request for information from a person's records is received from a third party
(including attorneys, pension experts, etc.), the person about whom the information
is sought must authorize the disclosure. In order to be acceptable to SSA, the authorization
must meet the following criteria.
•
be written, dated and signed by the individual who is the subject of the record,
•
be received by SSA not more than 60 days after it has been signed,
•
specify that SSA may disclose the information,
•
specify what information may be disclosed,
•
indicate the individual or organization to receive the information, and
•
indicate a time frame during which the information may be disclosed. If no time frame
is given, assume that the authorization is for one disclosure only.
•
be requested on an SSA-7004-SM-OP1 (Request for Earnings and Benefit Estimate Statement)
if annual earnings, credits and/or a benefit estimate is needed. The certified earnings
record is not the proper vehicle for providing this information.
•
be requested on a SSA-7050-F3 (Request for Social Security Earnings Information) if
detailed earnings information or a certified copy of earnings information is needed.
