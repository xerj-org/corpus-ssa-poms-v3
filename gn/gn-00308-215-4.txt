4. Remarks:
Birth/death records are generally available since November 21, 1952, divorce since
1969 and marriage records since 1987 when marriage records were first required for
local citizens. Roman Catholic Church records of birth/baptism /death/marriage since
1900 are available from individual parishes.
The Saipan RS (670-236-8717) can certify documents without charge, but cannot obtain
free certified copies.
