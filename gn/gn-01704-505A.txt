SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.505 - Military Service (MS)
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704505 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
1. WHEN TO DEVELOP
Develop a NH's U.S. MS if it is needed by a foreign agency, if it is alleged and if
it is not precluded. Allegations of MS can be found on most applications; however,
if a claimant's statement on any other form alleging MS is in the file, this also
is an allegation. MS is precluded if it is being used by another U.S. federal agency,
except the Veterans Administration, to pay a monthly benefit. If there is an application,
SSA-1 or SSA-16-FC, and the question concerning entitlement to a pension from any
other federal agency is checked “no,” assume the MS is not precluded and, therefore, could be used by a foreign agency.
Consult a CA or a CATA if there is any question concerning whether or not the NH's
MS is precluded.
2. WHAT TO REQUEST
a.
If additional information about the NH's MS is needed; e.g., service number, branch
of service, dates of service, whether MS is precluded, etc., ask the claimant to complete
a Form SSA-2512 (Pre-1957 Military Service—Federal Benefit Questionnaire).
b.
Request proof of the NH's MS. Acceptable proofs are:
1.
Certificate of Discharge
2.
Certificate of Statement of Services (DD-13 or DD-14)
3.
Report of Casualty (DD-1300)
4.
Form SSA-654-U4—(Refer cases requiring this form to a CA or CATA for advice and assistance.)
3. PROOF OF MS IN FILE
Refer case to a CA or CATA for posting the MS to the SSA-794TR.
