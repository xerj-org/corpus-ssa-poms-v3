A. Background for coding British Virgin Islands bank data
This section describes how to code bank data for direct deposit of Title II benefits
to a financial institution (FI) in British Virgin Islands. The bank data will be stored
on the MBR in the same fields as are used for U.S. direct deposit.
Special coding identifies the data as direct deposit for the British Virgin Islands.
This allows us to route benefit payments through the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas
City (FRBKC) and the processor bank in British Virgin Islands to the beneficiary's
local FI.
If you receive an IDD request in a U.S. domestic (i.e, non-border) field office (FO),
forward all appropriate information to International Operations (IO), International
Benefits and Processing (IBP) to process.
