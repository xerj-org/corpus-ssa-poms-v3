B. Background
In September 1999, a U.S. District Court in New York held that SSI notices do not
provide adequate explanations of SSI financial eligibility determinations. In accordance
with the court's decision in Ford, et al. v. Apfel, SSA is developing numerous enhancements to automated SSI notices. One of the enhancements
is the statement on SSI notices that claimants and beneficiaries can get copies of
the law, regulations, and POMS pertinent to determinations about their SSI eligibility
and benefit amount from their local field office.
Claimants, beneficiaries, and their representatives have always been able to get copies
of the law, regulations, and POMS. But because of the revised language in SSI notices,
FOs may see an increase in the frequency of these requests.
