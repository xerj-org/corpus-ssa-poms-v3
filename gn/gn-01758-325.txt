GN 01758.325 Social Security Administration (SSA) Medical Exam Reimbursement Form
SSA uses the Form SSA 1278 USA/BR 9 (Request for Payments of Reimbursable Expenses
under U.S. Brazil Agreement on Social Security) for reimbursement of medical expenses
regarding requests for exams. The Form SSA 1278 USA/BR 9 (Request for Payments of
Reimbursable Expenses under U.S. Brazil Agreement on Social Security) is located on
the Division of International Operations website.
