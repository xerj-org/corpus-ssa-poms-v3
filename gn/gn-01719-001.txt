GN 01719.001 Overview and Effective Date of the Totalization Agreement with the United Kingdom
(U.K.)
The U.K. social security system, known as “National Insurance”, operates in England,
Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.  In addition to these U.K. areas, the Agreement
applies to the social security systems of the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands.
