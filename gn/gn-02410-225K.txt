K. Requests for benefit history on the non-custodial parent’s record
State Child Support Service agencies may request the benefit history of dependents
for payments received on the non-custodial parent's record in order to calculate balances
for lump sum arrears.
When State Child Support Service agencies request benefit history for dependent children,
supply the requested benefit history. For instructions on disclosure of monthly benefit
information to state agencies, refer to GN 03314.002.
