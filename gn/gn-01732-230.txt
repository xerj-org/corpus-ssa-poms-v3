GN 01732.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Finnish Agency on Form SSA-2960-U3-FI-Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the CPSI.
•
Diary initial requests for 120 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 60 days.
•
Forward the folder to IOTS if no response has been received after two followups, i.e.;
after 240 days.
