A. Policy – North Dakota Small Estates
North Dakota law permits collection and distribution of small estates under $50,000
by affidavit. Section 30.1-23-01 of the North Dakota Century Code provides that after
30 days have elapsed since the decedent’s death, any person indebted to the decedent
shall make payment of the indebtedness to a person claiming to be the successor of
the decedent upon being presented an affidavit made by or on behalf of the successor.
