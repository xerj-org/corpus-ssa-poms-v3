A. Background on SERS
SERS is an automated payment solution that uses check scanners to electronically process
domestic remittances paid by check or money order... In 2014, the Social Security
Administration (SSA) implemented the SERS application in the field offices (FO) to
collect standard fees for non-programmatic requests for information. In 2017, SSA
implemented SERS for certain Title II and Title XVI programmatic debts. In 2021, SSA
implemented SERS to accept conserved funds remittances.
SERS:
•
Provides management information required to manage the workload;
•
Assists the Agency’s compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards;
•
Supports timely collection of programmatic and conserved funds remittances;
•
Promptly updates applicable debt balances;
•
Enhances customer service to the public; and
•
Helps streamline and modernize the remittance handling process.
