B. Discrimination Policies
It is not discrimination for SSA to apply the provisions of the Social Security Act,
the regulations, Social Security Rulings (SSRs), and similar materials (like the POMS)
in the conduct of benefit determinations, applications and other activities conducted
by the Agency. Furthermore, filing a complaint is not a means to:
•
appeal an unfavorable decision on a claim for benefits.
•
obtain an investigation of alleged discrimination by an SSA employee that occurred
while the employee was off-duty and was unrelated to the employee's duties. EXAMPLE:
Employee is alleged to have uttered an offensive remark in the course of a dispute
over a parking spot at a shopping mall.
•
request an SSA employee to withdraw from handling a specific claim or matter.
