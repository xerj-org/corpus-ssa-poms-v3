A. Introduction - Discrimination Complaints Against SSA and/or SSA Employees
The Commissioner of the Social Security Administration (SSA) has taken steps to ensure
that no person is discriminated against on the bases of disability, race, color, national
origin, sex, age, religion or retaliation (for filing a previous complaint) in any
program or activity conducted by SSA. The Commissioner has delegated to SSA's Deputy
Commissioner for Civil Rights and Equal Opportunity (OCREO) the authority to accept,
investigate and resolve such complaints.
