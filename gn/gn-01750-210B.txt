SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01750.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed in the Czech Republic
Effective Dates: 04/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and Czech or Czech claim only
Prepare a SSA-2960 (Transmittal/Request/Certification Form - USA/CZ3) to:
•
Request the Czech Republic certified coverage record and include a copy of the SSA-2960
(Transmittal/Request/Certification Form- USA/CZ3).
•
Send CSSZ the information or documentation they have requested.
