Effective Dates: 07/14/2026 - Present | TN 177 (07-26)

B. Procedure - how to complete the notice
1. Identifying information
•
Addressee: Enter the claimant's name and address
•
Office Address: Enter the field office (FO) address
•
Date: Enter the date you issue the notice
•
BNC#: Enter the Beneficiary Notice Control Number (BNC#)
2. First paragraph
•
Fill-in 1: Enter the date of the interview (mm/dd/yyyy)
•
Fill-in 2: Enter as appropriate: you, or name of applicant
•
Fill-in 3: Enter as appropriate: your, or name of applicant (possessive)
3. What you need to do
Fill-in 4: Select "or bring us the form(s) and requested information with this letter"
or "the form to us at the address shown at the top of this letter"
4. If we don't hear from you
Fill-in 5: Enter as appropriate: you are, or name of claimant (possessive)
a. Option 1
Check option 1 if no previous closeout notice was issued or one was issued, but the
protective filing period has expired.
•
Fill-in 6: Enter the protective filing closeout date in format (mm/dd/yyyy) that is
6 months from the date of the notice.
•
Fill-in 7: Enter the protective filing date in format (mm/dd/yyyy).
b. Option 2
Check option 2 if a closeout notice was issued based on a prior protective filing
date and the closeout period has not expired.
•
Fill-in 8: Enter the protective filing closeout date shown in the prior closeout notice
in format (mm/dd/yyyy).
•
Fill-in 9: Enter the protective filing closeout date in format (mm/dd/yyyy) that is
6 months from the date of the notice.
•
Fill-in 10: Enter the date of the application interview in format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Things we need
Select the appropriate option(s) for the information or evidence needed. As an option,
you may enter "for" followed by the individual's name for whom you need the evidence
and the year(s) needed, as appropriate.
6. If you have any questions
Fill-in 11: Enter the contact person's telephone number in the space provided.
Fill-in 12: Enter the name of the contact person in the space provided.
