D. Overview of IDD for Czech Republic
IDD payments sent to Czech Republic must be in Czech Koruna (CZK) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Czech Republic.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
