B. Earliest month of entitlement
January 2009 is the earliest possible month of entitlement to Czech Totalization benefits.
Both the United States and the Czech Republic may use periods of U.S. and Czech Social
Security coverage earned prior to January 1, 2009 to establish entitlement to U.S.
or Czech Totalization benefits.
