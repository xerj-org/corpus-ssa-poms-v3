B. LIST OF DIFFERENCES IN THE BENEFIT PART OF THE SSA-1042S
The following list shows information shown on the SSA-1042S in addition to that shown
on the SSA-1099 and expands on the explanation of the material included under other
items which are common to both forms.
1. Box 3, Benefits
a. Paid by check or direct deposit
This shows all payments made to the beneficiary, both taxable and nontaxable income.
b. Deductions for work or other adjustments
This includes any amounts withheld for work or other deductions or to recover overpayments.
Where a benefit which was subject to the nonresident alien tax was withheld to recover
an overpayment from which alien tax was withheld, the amount shown includes the administrative
adjustment (as explained in GN 05010.200B.3.)
c. Amount withheld for taxes
This is the net amount of nonresident alien tax withheld (as shown in Box 9).
2. Box 4, Benefits Repaid to SSA
The amount shown in this box and in the description under “Deductions for work or other adjustments” includes the administrative adjustment in overpayment cases involving alien tax withholding.
3. Box 6, Rate of Tax
This shows the nonresident alien tax withholding rate in effect at the time the annual
statement was prepared. This rate is shown even if the beneficiary was subject to
more than one rate during that year. The rate applies to 85 percent of the benefit
amount.
4. Box 7, Amount of Tax Withheld
This shows the total amount of nonresident alien tax withheld in the statement year.
5. Box 8, Amount of Tax Refunded
This shows the amount of nonresident alien tax refunded to the beneficiary in the
statement year. The refund may have been paid to the beneficiary or used to repay
an overpayment.
6. Box 9, Net Tax Withheld (Box 7 Minus Box 8)
This shows the difference between Box 7 and Box 8. Due to rounding and other factors,
the amount shown is not necessarily the same as the amount derived by applying the
tax rate to the amount in Box 5.
