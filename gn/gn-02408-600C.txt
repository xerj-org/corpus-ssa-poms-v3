C. Policy for EFT payments issued after death that are not due
For Title II, a payment for the month of death or later is not due and must be reclaimed.
For Title XVI, a payment for any month later than the month of death is not due and
must be reclaimed.
For a description of the exception of early payment delivery dates for Title II and
Title XVI, see GN
02408.650E.
