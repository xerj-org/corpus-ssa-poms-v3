E. Routine requests
Routine requests are any situations not defined in GN 01070.200B. Routine requests are sent via Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW). (Refer to MSOM
MDW-001-001 for instructions on this procedure or here for MDW instructions for inter-component development requests (see GN 01070.228A.3).
