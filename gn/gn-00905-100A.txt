SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.100 - FO Contacts with Residents of Canada
Effective Dates: 04/16/2018 - Present | TN 7 (03-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. General
Contact Canadian residents by telephone, mail or in person. Personal contacts can
take place in the U.S. or within Canada, as explained in 2.
2. Personal contact
Contacts must be at approved contact points under the conditions specified in agreements
worked out with the Canadian Government. No other personal contacts can be made in
Canada by FO employees.
