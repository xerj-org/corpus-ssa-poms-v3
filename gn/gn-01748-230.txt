GN 01748.230 Controlling Requests to the Danish Agency on Form SSA-2960 (USA/DK3)
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested for a Danish agency:
•
Diary initial request for 60 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 15 days.
Forward the folder to your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) Module if no response has been
received after two follow-ups.
