SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00602.075 - Transfer of Benefits to a Trust
Effective Dates: 04/26/2023 - Present | TN 32 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200602075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A representative payee is permitted to transfer Title II and/or Title XVI benefits
to establish a trust, or fund an existing trust, provided the terms of the trust are
not contrary with use of benefits policies (see GN 00602.001). However, for Title XVI eligibility, additional requirements may have to be met
(refer to GN 00602.075C.4.).
Exception: A representative payee cannot use Title XVI past-due benefits which meet dedicated
account requirements as described in SI 02101.010 to establish a trust or fund an existing trust. These benefits must be held in a
savings account, a checking account, or a money market account established in a financial
institution as described in GN 00603.025.
