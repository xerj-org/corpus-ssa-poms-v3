SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00307.015 - Office of International Operations (OIO) Translation Requests
Effective Dates: 12/11/2015 - Present | TN 26 (11-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200307015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. General
Prepare a separate Form SSA-533-FC (Translation Request) for each document to be translated.
See GN 00301.355 and GN 00301.365 for information on the SSA-533.
Show the total number of documents at the top of each SSA-533-FC if more than one
document is being translated on a claim or letter. Use carbons for additional copies
of the SSA-533-FC if they are legible.
