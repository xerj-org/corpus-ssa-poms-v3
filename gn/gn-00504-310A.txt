A. Policy for referring a case with specific representative payee issues
The Processing Center (PC) or Field Office (FO) refers representative payee issues
by memorandum to the appropriate component as noted in GN 00504.310C., in this section. The memorandum must identify the problem; cite POMS policy, and
other pertinent references.
