A. Introduction
Field Office (FO) technicians should use the SSI
eComputations (eComps) application and the associated SSI eComputations User Guide to perform manual
Title II and Title XVI windfall offset computations.
If the SSI eComps application is not available or the windfall offset computations
cannot be completed in eComps due to limitations of the application (see Chapter 10
of the User Guide), FO technicians will use the form SSA-4346-U2 (SSI Offset Summary)
to compute the windfall offset amounts manually. Form SSA-4346-U2 is the equivalent
of the Windfall Results, Windfall Offset, and Windfall Offset Summary tabs in eComps.
FO technicians will also need to fill out the form SSA-4968 (SSI RMA Payment Worksheet)
to complete the SSA-4346-U2 if they cannot complete any computations using eComps.
Form SSA-4968 is the equivalent of the Computational Results tab in eComps.
