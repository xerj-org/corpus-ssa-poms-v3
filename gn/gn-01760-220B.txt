B. Application process for Uruguayan benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) field offices (FO) will provide the applicant
with the special Uruguayan Application Form (UY-US/01) but will not assist the applicant
with completing the form. After receiving a claim for Uruguayan benefits filed with
SSA, the Uruguayan agency will contact the applicant directly if necessary to provide
instructions for the applicant to complete and mail any other form(s) directly to
the Uruguayan agency along with any additional required evidence.
