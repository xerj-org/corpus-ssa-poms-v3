GN 01775.235 Facts about the Romanian Liaison Form SSA e2960 USA/RO 3 (U.S. – Romania Agreement
on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
When to use Form SSA e2960 USA/RO 3
The Romanian liaison agency National House of Public Pensions uses Form SSA e2960
USA/RO 3 to:
•
Transmit material to the International Benefits and Processing (IBP) or to the Naples,
Italy Federal Benefits Unit (FBU);
•
Send information to IBP or to the Naples FBU; and
•
Request information from IBP.
