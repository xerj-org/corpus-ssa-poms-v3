SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.195 - Debtor Involved in Bankruptcy Proceedings - PC Procedure
Effective Dates: 03/23/2023 - Present | TN 47 (03-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215195 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. General
The PC has the overall responsibility of assuring that the bankruptcy petition is
handled properly and expeditiously.
2. Chapter 7
A Chapter 7 petition applies to all existing debts whether or not specifically included in the petition. For the definition of an existing debt, see GN 02215.185C.1.
3. Chapter 13 or 12
A Chapter 13 petition applies only to those debts listed in the petition (GN 02215.185C.4.). However, SSA is precluded from further collection efforts on any debt that arose before
the filing of the bankruptcy petition, whether listed or not. A repayment plan usually runs for 3 years but 5 year plans are allowed. Therefore,
recovery must be stopped where necessary. Also, if the time limit for filing a Proof of Claim has not expired, a claim must
be filed on any existing debt, listed or unlisted, unless an exception to filing applies
(see GN 02215.195F.).
