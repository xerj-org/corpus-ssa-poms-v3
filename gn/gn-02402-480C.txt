SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.480 - Coding Seychelles Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 11/12/2024 - Present | TN 280 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402480 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Seychelles will have a prefix of “75” at the beginning of the
Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “I” assigned for Seychelles.
