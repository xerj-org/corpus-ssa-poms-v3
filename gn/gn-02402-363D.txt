D. Overview of IDD for Nauru
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Nauru. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
