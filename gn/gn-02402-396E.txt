E. Description of Montenegro IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Montenegro comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI. The full length of the IBAN for
Montenegro consists of 22 characters. The bank data on the MBR consists of 24 characters
including the country code of W.
