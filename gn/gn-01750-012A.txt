SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01750.012 - How the Foreign Service Post in Warsaw, Poland Processes and Develops Claims Filed With the Czech Republic Agency
Effective Dates: 04/25/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a person not eligible for Czech benefits contacts the Czech Republic agency,
CSSZ to file a claim for U.S. benefits, CSSZ sends the following information to the
FSP in Warsaw, Poland:
•
SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits under the U.S. International Social Security
Agreement) and Supplemental Questionnaire (This form can be located on the Division
of International Operations website).
•
A liaison form, SSA-e2960-U3-CZ(Transmittal/Request/Certification Form-CZ/USA3), certifying
the filing date for the claim.
•
Czech certified coverage record (CZ/USA205)
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
