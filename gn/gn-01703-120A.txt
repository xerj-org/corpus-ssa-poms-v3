Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Occasionally DIO receives an amended foreign coverage record, which periods of coverage
that are different from the coverage shown on an earlier record. This is not a record
showing additional recent coverage. Rather it is a record, which shows additional
or deleted coverage which would cause the initial determination to change.
