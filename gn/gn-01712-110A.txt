SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01712.110 - How to Credit Norwegian Coverage
Effective Dates: 12/07/1993 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201712110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Norwegian coverage certification is used to assign foreign quarters of coverage
(FQCs) to the FCER.
•
All periods of coverage certified by Norway are creditable provided there is an entry
other than “0.00” in the pension point (POENG) column.
•
There is no minimum amount of Norwegian coverage which must be earned before the United
States can use it.
