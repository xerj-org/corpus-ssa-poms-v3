B. Procedure for adding funeral director reports of death to the NUMIDENT
1. Input
Add the death record to the NUMIDENT using the Death Information Processing System
(DIPS).
2. Documentation
•
For a
non-beneficiary
Update the NUMIDENT with the death information, once the record is updated destroy
the SSA-721.
NOTE: When the SSA-721 includes the name and address of the surviving spouse or children,
send the Survivor letter in the General folder in Document Processing System (DPS)
to provide awareness of potential entitlement to survivor benefits.
•
For
a
beneficiary
If a beneficiary filed a claim, annotate the Evidence (EVID) screen with 721. For
more information on the EVID screen, see MSOM EVID 001.003.
Process the SSA-721 per GN 02602.050 to update the Numident, and keep until we update SSA records. Then destroy the SSA-721.
