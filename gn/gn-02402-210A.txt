A. When a beneficiary moves from a country with International Direct Deposit (IDD) to
the United States (foreign to domestic)
NOTE: U.S. domestic (non-border) field offices (FOs) should not attempt to process an IDD
request for any country. U.S. domestic FOs must forward all appropriate information
to the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO), Division of International
Operations (DIO) for processing. For more information on how to forward the appropriate
IDD information, see GN 02402.205C, GN 01010.255, and GN 01702.310C.
