B. Process
The special application form(s) are not taken in the Field Offices (FO). After receiving
a claim for German benefits that was filed with an FO, the German agency with jurisdiction
of the claim will mail the appropriate German application to the applicant with instructions
to complete the form and mail it directly to Germany along with any necessary additional
evidence that may be needed.
