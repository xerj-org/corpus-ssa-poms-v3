D. Overview of IDD for Romania
IDD payments sent to Romania must be in Romanian Leu (ROM) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Romania. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
