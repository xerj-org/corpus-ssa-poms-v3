A. Background on Fee-for-Service Payees
The servicing field office (FO) authorizes qualified representative payee organizations
to collect a fee from the beneficiary. State and local government agencies and community-based,
nonprofit social service payee organizations must meet specific requirements (see
GN 00506.001C) in order to be authorized to collect a fee, and must continue to satisfy those requirements
to remain eligible to collect a fee. SSA will verify that organizational payees continue
to meet the requirements as part of our monitoring program.
