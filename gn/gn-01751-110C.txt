C. Policy — U.S. Social Security and Polish Social Security taxation
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (Federal Insurance Contributions Act [FICA] and Self-Employment
Contributions Act [SECA]) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Polish Social Security taxes that finance old-age, survivors and disability benefits;
farmer’s pension and social insurance; one-time indemnity payments and benefits awarded
as a result of work accidents and occupational diseases, and funeral grants.
