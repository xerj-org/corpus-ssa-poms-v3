G. What is a legal proceeding?
A legal proceeding includes any pretrial, trial, and post-trial stage of any existing
or reasonably anticipated judicial or administrative action, hearing, investigation,
or similar proceeding before a court, commission, board, agency, or other tribunal,
authority or entity, foreign or domestic. Legal proceedings also include any deposition
or other pretrial proceeding, including a formal or informal request for testimony
by an attorney or any other person.
