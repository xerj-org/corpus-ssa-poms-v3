SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.230 - Death of an International Direct Deposit (IDD) Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 06/07/2022 - Present | TN 277 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402230 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. FO/TSC receives report of death of an IDD beneficiary
When you receive a report of the death and the routing number for direct deposit begins
with 5, 6, or 7, to indicate the beneficiary was receiving IDD benefits, follow these
steps.
1.
Ask for the date of death.
2.
Input the date of death.
3.
Ask OEIO to code the death to ITS.gov.
