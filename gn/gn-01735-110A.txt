A. Policy — Benefit programs included
The provisions of the agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and Irish coverage credits apply to the following types of benefits:
1. U.S. Retirement, Survivors and Disability Benefits
The agreement does not apply to Medicare benefits or Supplemental Security Income
payments.
2. Irish Retirement/Old-Age, Survivors and Disability Benefits
The agreement does not apply to other contributory benefit programs such as unemployment,
maternity, occupational injuries, health insurance, etc.
