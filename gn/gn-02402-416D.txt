D. Overview of IDD for Bermuda
IDD payments sent to Bermuda must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bermuda. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
