C. Process - Fowlkes AR Relief Case Suspension Processing
1. Use of the Fugitive Felon Processing Exclusion Fax form for Fowlkes AR Relief Case
Suspension Processing
See GN 02613.850C.3.a. for information on when the Fugitive Felon Fax form is necessary for re-suspension
case processing where fully favorable relief under the Fowlkes AR was granted but
the beneficiary moved out of the Second Circuit.
See GN 02613.840C.3.a. for information on when the Fugitive Felon Fax form is necessary for resumption and
suspension case processing after partially favorable relief under the Fowlkes AR granted.
See GN 02613.840C.3.b. for information on when the Fugitive Felon Fax form is necessary for Fowlkes AR relief
case processing when the POS/FFEL screen does not work correctly.
2. Completion of the Fugitive Felon Processing Exclusion Fax Request for Fowlkes AR Relief
Cases
Send the FAX form to the appropriate PC as indicated on the FAX form. In addition,
annotate the subsequent blocks on the form with the appropriate case information as
instructed below:
•
CAN
•
BOAN
•
NAME
•
BIC
Check the OTHER block on the EXCLUSION CATEGORY.
