GN 01719.120 Totalization Benefits under the U.S. - United Kingdom (U.K.) Agreement
Under the Agreement, if a Number Holder (NH) has at least six quarters of U.S. coverage, but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under the U.K. Social Security
system can be added to his or her U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents
or survivors) to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
