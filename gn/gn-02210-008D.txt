D. Recovery of title XVI overpayments from monthly title II benefits
The Recovery and Collection of Overpayments (RECOOP) system automatically selects
title XVI debts for CPR from title II benefits. For the automated process, see SI 02220.020B. Process Non-RECOOP cases manually per SI 02220.020C.
