2. Preadjudicative development
IBO is responsible for:
•
Developing all claims leads and inquiries;
•
Undertaking all development required to make a determination on a claim—including
termination, suspension and deduction information; and
•
Handling preclaims earnings discrepancies involving NH's in foreign countries not
listed in 1.
