C. Procedure - Protests
Resolve any inquiries arising from the advance notice.
Review the information provided with the inquiry along with the evidence used in the
initial determination.
Request a face-to-face interview with the beneficiary to resolve any problems.
Record the protest on the Protest Details screen on eRPS per MS 07412.004 to prevent
the selection from being processed while you are resolving it.
Document the rationale for your decision on the Make Note screen, see MS 07415.002
and MS 07415.003.
1. Change in initial determination
The FO is responsible for sending all notices.
a. Capability issue
If the protest involves the need for a payee and you decide that the beneficiary is
capable, initiate direct payment immediately. The system will send the correct notice
to the beneficiary if they had a payee.
If the beneficiary is in direct pay, contact the beneficiary and advise them of the
decision. Record the non-selection issue on eRPS and process the SSA-11 normally (“SSA-11”
represents both the SSA-11-SG and SSA-11BK in this subchapter).
b. Selection of payee issue
If the issue is the selection of a specific payee and you decide to select a different
one, process the new SSA-11 in the normal procedure. The eRPS will send the beneficiary
an advance notice of the revised selection.
Allow the beneficiary time to appeal the revised determination.
Record the non-selection issue for the first applicant on eRPS. The T2R notice system
will send the notices to the new payee, beneficiary, and former payee.
The FO is responsible for sending the notices to the new payee and beneficiary in
title XVI change of payee situations. The title XVI system will send these notices
if the beneficiary was in direct pay. Always send the former payee notice manually
on title XVI cases.
2. No change in initial determination
If, after a thorough review, you decide that the original decision is correct, contact
the beneficiary and advise them accordingly.
If the beneficiary understands and agrees, document the decision on the Protest Decision
Details screen. No further action is required. Follow MS 07412.002 to initiate selection.
If the beneficiary still disagrees and wants to exercise their right to a formal appeal,
obtain an SSA-561-U2 and process as indicated below.
