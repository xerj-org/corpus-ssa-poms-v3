SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.580 - Assigning Correct Type-of-Event (TOE) Codes to Requests for Totalization Development
Effective Dates: 01/18/2024 - Present | TN 7 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704580 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. TOE code 320
Show TOE code 320 on requests for assistance needed for claims for totalization benefits
from the United States, if the claimant is entitled to regular RSDI benefits (P2 cases
or fictitious E1 cases). For example:
1.
Requests for U.S. and/or foreign earnings records for P2 (Totalization Pending) cases.
2.
Requests to DOs or FSPs for statements of restriction or development (e.g., medical
evidence for entitlement to DIB for a RIB beneficiary) needed to adjudicate a P2 case.
