SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00205.085 - Retirement, Survivor, Disability Insurance (RSDI) Applications for Claimant's Earnings During the Year of Filing (YOF) and Preceding Year
Effective Dates: 01/19/2012 - Present | TN 45 (11-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200205085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. General procedure for collecting information on earnings during YOF and the preceding
year
1. Fill-ins (paper application)
Fill in the appropriate monthly exempt amounts before completing an application where
there are blank spaces.
2. Explanation
During the first interview, fully explain the applicable deduction provision to every
claimant who is under full retirement age for at least one month in the YOF.
3. Use of answers
Use the earnings information to:
•
assist the claimant in choosing the best month of election (MOEL) based on the claimant’s
personal decision. See GN 00204.040B for more detail on MOEL.
•
determine if work suspensions or deductions are applicable under the retirement test.
•
determine if it is necessary to develop lag earnings for computation purposes.
4. Further development
Develop the question of substantial services per RS 02505.065 if a SE person who had or expects to have earnings over the exempt amount in the
year.
