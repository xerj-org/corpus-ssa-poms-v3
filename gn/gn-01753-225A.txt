A. Automatic status notice for an application for Slovak benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about a Slovak claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of the
worker’s U.S. coverage record) to the Slovak Republic, the Benefit Earnings Technician
(BET) electronically stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center
(PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Slovak agency. The system automatically
generates a notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs the applicant
that the claims material was forwarded to the Slovak Republic.
