A. Requesting Texas birth and death records
Direct requests for birth and death records to the Texas Department of State Health
Services Vital Statistics Unit. For the address of the Texas Vital Statistics Unit,
forms, fees, payment methods, expedited process, and request requirements, go to the
Texas Department of State Health Services Texas Vital Statistics website . Also see the Texas.gov: Frequently Asked
Questions page.
