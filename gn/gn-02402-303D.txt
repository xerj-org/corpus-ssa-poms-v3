D. Overview of IDD for Ireland
IDD payments sent to Ireland must be in Euro (EUR) accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU). DIO will
fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Ireland. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for additional information.
