GN 01750.315 Czech Republic Birth Number
The Czech Republic Birth Number is a 10-digit number. The first two numbers represent
the year of birth. The next two numbers represent the month of birth, followed by
two more digits that represents the sex (male/female). The last four numbers are in
sequential order.
