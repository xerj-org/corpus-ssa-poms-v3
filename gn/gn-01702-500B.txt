SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.500 - Filing Appeals of Decisions on U.S. or Foreign Claims
Effective Dates: 04/08/2011 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702500 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The date an appeal is filed with one country’s agency is recognized as the date of
filing by the other. Regardless of where a person files an appeal, the country whose
decision is being appealed will process the appeal in accordance with its own laws.
