A. Process
Use the Tennessee
Vital Records website for the most up-to-date information including cost of copy. In addition, you can find EIN information here.
FO code 565
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No.
Effective October 1, 2005, SSA offices will be responsible for contacting the TN Division
of Vital Records directly to purchase required records. Offices must pay the full purchase price for each record
requested. Funds for the records will come from the budget of the office requesting
the record. If the conditions in GN 00301.210 are not met for SSA purchase of evidence, individuals must request records directly from the DVS either in person, online, or by mail.
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the Court where the divorce was granted.
The cost of copy varies. Marriage records are available from the County Clerk in the
county where the license was issued. The cost of copy varies.
