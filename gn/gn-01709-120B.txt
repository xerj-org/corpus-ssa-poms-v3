B. Crediting Swiss coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. QC for every three months of Swiss coverage in a year.
However, SSA will not give credit for:
•
Any month in a calendar quarter that SSA already credited as a U.S. QC; or
•
More than four QCs for any calendar year; or
•
Any period of coverage not based on contributions.
