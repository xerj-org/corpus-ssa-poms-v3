Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
The SSA-2960-CHI is completed by the totalization module in DIO or by the FSP in Buenos
Aires. It is used to:
•
Transmit claims and related material to a Chilean agency,
•
Request information from a Chilean agency, and
•
Respond to requests from a Chilean agency.
