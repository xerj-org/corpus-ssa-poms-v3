G. Retroactivity for Hospital Insurance (HI)
We can allow up to 6 months retroactivity for HI when the claimant files for RSI benefits.
When a NH files for DIB, we can allow up to 12 months of retroactivity for HI. For
more information on HI retroactivity, see HI 00801.022.
