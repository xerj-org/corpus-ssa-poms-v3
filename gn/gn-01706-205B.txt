B. Italian coverage certification
INTPSC will request the Italian coverage certification form IT/USA 3 bis. Under the
provisions of the Supplementary Agreement, Italian coverage is used only to establish
insured status. Therefore, when requesting a coverage certification, INTPSC will specifiy
the number of weeks of Italian coverage required. (See GN 01706.032.)
