GN 01085.025 Exhibit - Retention Periods For Program-Related Forms
Exhibit
The following exhibit is to be used as a guide for discarding forms in a claims folder.
Although many forms are displayed, this is not an all-inclusive list. Additional forms and any changes to current retention periods will be added in future
updates. The retention information was extracted from pertinent sections of the Administrative
Instructions Manual System (AIMS), the Materiel Resources Manual - Chapter 07, and
the Records Management Handbook, and from a policy review of the program-related forms currently mentioned in certain
sections of the POMS. The exhibit provides form numbers in numerical order with titles
and retention periods. Note that, unless otherwise annotated, all forms with a multiple
suffix (e.g., F6, SP, U2, etc.) have the same retention disposition as the basic numeric
form.
