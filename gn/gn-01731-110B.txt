B. Taxes under the Agreement
The provisions of the Agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) and Self-Employed
Contributions Act (SECA)), including the Medicare portion; and
•
Finnish social security taxes that finance retirement, survivors, disability, sickness
and maternity insurance, and child allowances.
