Effective Dates: 09/30/2026 - Present | TN 87 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202201023 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Overpayment amount is $2,000 or less
If the original overpayment amount is $2,000 or less, determine if you can approve
the waiver request by following the instructions in GN 02250.350.
If the overpayment is solely due to dollar down rounding, waive the overpayment following
the instructions in GN 02201.013. Annotate DMS Remarks to document your actions.
