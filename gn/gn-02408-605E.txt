E. Erroneous DNE
When a beneficiary’s death is posted erroneously, contact the FI's Automated Clearing
House (ACH) department to request they remove the death “flag” from the beneficiary’s
account. When necessary, fax a statement on SSA letterhead to the FI stating the death
was input erroneously and payments are resuming to the account because the beneficiary
is alive and entitled. If the death flag is not removed, the bank rejects and returns
future deposits with a reason code of "death" which posts the death to SSA's records.
