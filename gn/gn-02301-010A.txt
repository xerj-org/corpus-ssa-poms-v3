A. Underpayment notification requirement
Any time we take an action that affects a beneficiary’s payment amount, we must notify
them of the change and give the beneficiary their due process rights.
In situations where the system does not have the capability to issue an appropriate
or complete automated underpayment notice, we must prepare a manual notice.
