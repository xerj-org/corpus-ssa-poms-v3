SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03345.020 - Statements of Disagreement With SSA Records
Effective Dates: 12/06/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203345020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Statements filed by individuals disagreeing with SSA's decison not to correct or amend
his or her record will be:
•
Made part of the disputed record.
•
Photocopied and sent to any agency or person to whom SSA accountings show the record
was disclosed.
•
Provided to anyone to whom the record is subsequently disclosed.
