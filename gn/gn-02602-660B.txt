B. Procedure for completing the worksheets
1. Telephone interview worksheet
Use this worksheet for interviews conducted by telephone. Because a telephone interview
is the preferred contact method, you will use this worksheet more often than the other
worksheets.
If you determine during the telephone interview that the beneficiary is deceased,
use the telephone worksheet to document your development of the beneficiary’s death.
You are still required to update Shared Processes (GN 00301.287) with the necessary information pertaining to the beneficiary’s death. For a sample
of the worksheet, see GN 02602.571.
2. Face-to-face interview worksheet
Use this worksheet for face-to-face interviews conducted at the field office or at
the beneficiary’s residence. If during the face-to-face interview you determine that
the beneficiary is deceased, use the face-to-face worksheet to document your development
of the beneficiary’s death. You must still update Shared Processes with the necessary
information pertaining to the beneficiary’s death. For a sample of the worksheet,
see GN 02602.570.
3. Unable to locate the beneficiary
Use this worksheet when you are unsuccessful in locating the beneficiary. Prior to
completing this worksheet, make sure you complete the failure to respond procedures
in GN 02602.630. For a sample of the worksheet, see GN 02602.573.
