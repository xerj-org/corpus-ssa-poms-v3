C. Form SSA-2960-SZ
The address of the Caisse Suisse de Compensation (CSC) is preprinted on the form.
Part II is self-explanatory. Complete Part III by checking item A or at least one
of the other items. If item C is checked requesting Swiss earnings and coverage information,
request the information for all years beginning 1948 for new claims; for recomputations,
request the information from the date of the last certification after conferring with
a claims authorizer.
