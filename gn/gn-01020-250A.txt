A. Introduction
The Form SSA-3601 is used to notify the PC/OCO that additional actions are needed
on a claim. For DOFA claims, the claims representative prepares all required notices,
diaries and listings.
This form has been redesigned to facilitate the entry of its attached claims material
into PCACS. The information within the large block at the top of the form corresponds
to PCACS data needed to ensure proper routing within the PC/ OCO.
