E. Remittance Logs
The intent of the remittance logs maintained in the mailroom and reception area are
to establish control over remittances that enter the FO.
A designated member of management should review each log and account for all remittances
weekly. The Remittance Supervisor should review the logs daily to reconcile remittances
with that day’s Field Office Daily Receipt Listing (FODRL) per GN 02403.006B.3. Management should review any items that are still in the office to ensure that
they are appropriately stamped and housed in a secure location. The designated member
of management should determine and discuss the reason why the interviewer did not
enter a logged item into the Debt Management System. Management has the option to
confirm this with the remitter.
