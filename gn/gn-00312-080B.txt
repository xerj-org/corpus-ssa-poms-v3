B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
For birth, death, and marriage records, individuals can request them online at http://www.portondinosilha.cv/ or in person or by mail at the National Institute of Statistics or the Civil State
or Civil Registry Office of Cape Verde on the island on which the event occurred.
Note:
Birth certificates show marginal notes about parental recognitions, marriages and
divorces of the registrants.
