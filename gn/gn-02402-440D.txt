D. Overview of IDD for Turks and Caicos Islands
IDD payments sent to Turks and Caicos Islands must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Turks
and Caicos Islands. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
