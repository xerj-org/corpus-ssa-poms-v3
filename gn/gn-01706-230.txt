GN 01706.230 Handling Italian Award and Disallowance Notices
Occasionally, INPS will send to SSA Form IT/USA/8, Italian Award Notice, to be forwarded
to a U.S. resident. Since there is no provision in the agreement for forwarding INPS'
award or disallowance notices, return the notice to INPS via SSA-2960-U3-IT and advise
them of this fact.
