B. Evidence requirements for applying the WEP Guarantee
We require proof of the pension when the alleged pension amount based only on the
earnings from non-covered employment, after currency conversion, is low enough for
the WEP Guarantee to apply. Only accept original and certified photocopies of foreign
pension evidence when applying the WEP Guarantee. Evidence of entitlement to a foreign
pension will generally be a letter or award notice issued by the paying agency in
the foreign country.
Ensure the proof includes the amount of the pension in the first month of concurrent
entitlement to both the pension and the Social Security benefit, as well as the months
of employment (after 1956) and any months or amounts that are not based on earnings
from that employment.
