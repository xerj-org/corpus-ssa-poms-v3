C. Slovak survivors benefits eligibility requirements
To qualify for survivors benefits, the survivor and the deceased worker must meet
certain conditions.
1.
For a person to qualify for survivors benefits, the deceased worker:
•
must have been receiving a benefit at the time of death; or
•
must have met all factors of entitlement at the time of death (except for age attainment);
or
•
must have died as the result of a work related injury or disease.
2.
The following persons may be eligible for survivors benefits:
•
unmarried surviving spouse; and
•
children under age 26.
