B. Using the Modernized Claims System (MCS) version of Form SSA-2490-BK
1. When to use the MCS SSA-2490-BK
The SSA-2490-BK is available through MCS. Use the MCS version only when the number
holder (NH) is files for:
•
U.S. retirement or disability totalization benefits; or
•
United States retirement or disability benefits (regular or totalization) and Brazilian
benefits concurrently.
2. When not to use the MCS SSA-2490-BK
Do not use the MCS version of the SSA-2490-BK when the applicant files for:
•
Brazilian benefits only; or
•
U.S. or Brazilian survivors benefits.
Use a paper or electronic (eform) SSA-2490-BK application form for totalization survivor
claims.
For instructions on how to complete the SSA-2490-BK, see GN 01702.110. NOTE: If there is an existing U.S. totalization life claim award, do not take an
SSA-2490-BK in claims for U.S. auxiliary benefits or survivors benefits.
