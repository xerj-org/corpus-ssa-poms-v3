B. Description of form A/USA 4
•
Part 1 of the A/USA 4 contains the name and date of birth of the worker.
•
Part 2, columns 1 and 2, contains periods of creditable Austrian coverage in day,
month, year format. Column 3 shows the number of months within a “from-to” period.
•
Any breaks in coverage or changes in the type of coverage (e.g. from compulsory to
voluntary) are identifiable by a new “from-to” period.
•
Part 3 identifies which one of the six Austrian agencies certified the record and
the address of the specific office of that agency.
