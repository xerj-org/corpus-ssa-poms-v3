Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
When an individual files a claim for U.S. benefits with the social security agency
in an agreement country, that agency transmits information about the claim to the
Social Security Administration (SSA) using liaison forms developed for this purpose.
Many of these forms include check blocks or spaces which the foreign agency can use
to inform SSA that a factor of entitlement for U.S. benefits (age, death, etc.) has
already been established by the foreign agency in connection with a claim for foreign
benefits.
