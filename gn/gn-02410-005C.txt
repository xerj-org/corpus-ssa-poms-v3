SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02410.005 - Bankruptcy Court Orders
Effective Dates: 03/27/2023 - Present | TN 63 (03-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202410005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
If any SSA office is served with a court order directing SSA to pay all or part of
a benefit to a bankruptcy trustee, immediately forward the order and a current MBR/SSR
to the ARC, Programs in the region responsible for the State in which the issuing
court is located. The ARC, Programs will:
•
Forward the order to the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) by email for preparation
of a response to the court; and
•
Store the order to the Non-Disability repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRed)
using the Evidence Portal (EP), see MS 09701.001.
•
If there is an electronic folder (EF), fax the order into the EF, see DI 81010.090.
