A. Policy for priority handling
All requests received between January 31 and April 15 should be given priority handling.
Give requests priority handling in the following order:
•
Requesters with tax liability. These cases are given priority as the April 15 tax
filing deadline nears.
•
Third Requests
•
Second Requests
The PSC will strive to answer all inquiries prior to April 15.
