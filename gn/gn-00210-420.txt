GN 00210.420 Same-Sex Relationships - Mother’s and Father’s Benefits
Follow these instructions to determine entitlement to benefits for a same-sex surviving
spouse (or surviving divorced spouse) who has the child of the deceased number holder
(NH) in their care. The surviving spouse must meet all other factors for entitlement
in RS 00208.000.
When you are ready to process the claim as an award or denial, follow the instructions
in GN 00210.020.
