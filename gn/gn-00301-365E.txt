E. Procedure - Control and Follow-up
Take the following actions for control and follow-up:
1.
Annotate the SSA-533 with the SSN and keep one copy for control and follow-up purposes.
2.
For requests sent to regional translators, follow-up after 20 days.
3.
For requests sent directly to TPWU, follow-up after 30 days using the following contacts:
•
DOTEL Number (410) 965-0160
•
TPWU Manager (410) 966-5083
