SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03314.015 - Disclosure Without Consent to State and Local Agencies for Law Enforcement Purposes
Effective Dates: 09/13/2005 - Present | TN 3 (12-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203314015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Information we can disclose
In both non-violent and violent crime situations, we may disclose non-tax return information,
such as:
•
benefit data (benefit amount, date of entitlement, date of termination or suspension);
•
date of birth;
•
medical records;
•
health insurance data;
•
enumeration data; and
•
addresses.
For specific instructions on disclosing Social Security numbers and tax return information
for law enforcement purposes, see GN 03312.075 and GN 03320.015.
