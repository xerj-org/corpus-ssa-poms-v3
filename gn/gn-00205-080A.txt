SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00205.080 - Questions About the Number Holder’s (NH) Employment and Self-Employment (SE) for Retirement, Survivor, Disability Insurance (RSDI) Applications
Effective Dates: 07/26/2023 - Present | TN 70 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200205080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Questions on most applications request the name and address of the NH's recent or
present employer and the dates work began and ended. The Modernized Claims System
(MCS) propagates this information only if the claimant states that they worked in
the last four years. For years after 1978, request a SEQY or DEQY for all earnings
and employer information.
