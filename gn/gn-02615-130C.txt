C. Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) appeal
If there is an administrative appeal pending in Office of Hearings Operations (OHO),
notify the appropriate administrative appeal organization of the Clark relief. For cases pending at the hearing level, notify the hearing office. When a
case is pending before the Appeals Council, notify the Office of Appellate Operations
(OAO). For ALJ appeals case processing instructions, see GN 02615.130C.1. in this section. For OAO appeals case processing instructions, see GN 02615.130D.1.

2. Pending ALJ appeals with multiple issues
If the ALJ pending appeal request includes issues other than those related to the
PPV suspension or overpayment decision, the case will remain with the HO, even if
the beneficiary is due Clark relief.
Provide the HO with information on the beneficiary’s eligible Clark relief. For information on how to notify the ALJ about the beneficiary’s Clark relief and how to process the relief, see GN 02615.130C.1. in this section.
