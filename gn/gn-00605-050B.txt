B. Procedure
Title II, Title XVI, and Concurrent
•
Use the eRPA Representative Payee Accounting link and input the SSN to update the
form status. Click on the Update Form Status to clear or update the system control
for the Account Number and BIC (see MSOM INTRANETERPA 005.012 ).
Before deleting a control from the system, be certain it is for the correct BIC and 12-month report
period.
•
Use the Update Conserved Funds to input reported conserved funds (see MSOM INTRANETERPA 005.013 ).
