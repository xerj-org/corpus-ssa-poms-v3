E. Missing cashier's check, bank draft, or bank money order
This subsection explains the procedure for investigating bank drafts, cashier's checks,
or bank money orders received in the FO and subsequently lost. Proceed as follows:
1.
Did SSA negotiate the remittance?
•
If yes,
request a copy of the remittance from the beneficiary then notify the MATPSC to credit
the beneficiary or recipient's account at ||PHI ARC PCO RDDB
DMS. STOP.
•
If no, ask the individual to secure a duplicate remittance.
2.
Is a duplicate provided?
•
If yes, process as a regular remittance and issue a new receipt, see MS 011 03.006. When
possible, use SERS to submit the new remittance, see GN 02403.161.
•
If no, follow the instructions in GN 02403.160.
3.
If the original check, draft, or money order is located later, notify the MATPSC at
||PHI ARC PCO RDDB
DMS, Attention: Debt Management Section Chief. When notifying the MATPSC, forward the
check, draft, or money order.
4.
Document and clear the alert per GN 02403.155B.4 in this section.
