SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01737.120 - U.S. Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/05/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy -General
Under the agreement, if an NH has at least 6 quarters of U.S. coverage but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under the Greek system can
be added to his or her U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any dependents or survivors)
to qualify for U.S. totalization benefits.
