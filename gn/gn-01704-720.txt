GN 01704.720 Assembling claims packages
Assemble claims packages for foreign agencies
placing the material in the order listed below—the Form SSA-2960 will always be on
the top.
A.
Transmittal/Request Form or Transmittal/Request/Certification (SSA-2960)
B.
Liaison Certification Form(s) (SSA-1279, SSA-1280 and SSA-1281)
C.
United States Earnings Record (SSA-794TR)
D.
Application
E.
Additional Evidence; i.e., any material submitted by claimant solely in support of
the claim for foreign benefits.
