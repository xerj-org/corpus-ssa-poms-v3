B. Application process for Italian benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) Field Offices (FO) will not provide the applicant
with any special Italian application forms. After receiving an Italian benefits claim
filed with SSA, the Italian agency will contact the applicant directly, if necessary,
and provide instructions for the applicant to complete and mail any special Italian
forms directly to the Italian agency along with any additional required evidence.
