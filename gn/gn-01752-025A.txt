A. Policy – Role of ZUS Liaison agency
Zaklad Ubezpieczen Spolecznych (ZUS) or Kasa Rolniczego Ubezpieczenia Spolecznego
(KRUS) are the only Republic of Poland insurance agencies that will be used to communicate
and process the agreement workload. ZUS is responsible for the Social Insurance Institution
and KRUS is responsible for the Agricultural Social Insurance Institution of Poland.
If periods of agriculture are alleged all applications go to KRUS for determination.
