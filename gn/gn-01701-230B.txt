B. Policy – family maximums
IF
THEN
Eligibility is before 1979 or, regardless of eligibility date, the pro rata PIA was
derived from a theoretical special minimum and the new pro rata PIA is at or above
the table minimum
The family maximum is the table maximum corresponding to the new pro rata PIA.
Eligibility is before 1979 and the new pro rata PIA is below the table minimum or,
eligibility is after 1978
The COL increase is applied to the old maximum to determine the new maximum.
