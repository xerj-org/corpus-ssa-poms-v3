C. Procedure - controlling requests
Follow this procedure for controlling requests for Spanish application forms.
Step
Action
1
Prepare a diary for 60 days.
2
•
If no response is received, send a second request including a preaddressed return
envelope, and
•
Prepare a diary for 30 days.
3
If no response is received after 30 days, route the folder to the International Operations
and Totalization Staff (IOTS).
