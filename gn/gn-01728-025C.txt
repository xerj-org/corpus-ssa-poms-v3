C. PROCEDURE
Use the following procedure to determine the proper Portuguese agency.
1. Claim for Portuguese Benefits or Request for Portuguese Coverage Record
•
If you are sending a claim for Portuguese benefits or requesting a Portuguese coverage
record, check the application Form SSA-2490-F4 to determine if the worker's last residence
in Portugal was in the Azores.
•
If the worker resided in the Azores, then send the claim or request for a Portuguese
coverage record to the Nucleo Coord. de Prest. Diferidas.
•
If the worker did not reside in the Azores, send the material to the NCP in Lisbon.
2. Response to Assistance Request from a Portuguese Agency
If you are responding to an assistance request from a Portuguese agency, then write
to the agency which requested the information; either the NCP or the Nucleo Coord
de Prest. Diferidas.
