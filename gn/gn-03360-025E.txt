E. Policy - DIB certifications received in DDS
Requests for disability insurance benefit (DIB) certifications are sometimes received
in the DDS.
a.
Kinds of Requests
•
verify disability insurance benefits;
•
verify date disability began.
b.
Routing of requests
•
DDS sends to parallel FO;
•
parallel FO determines folder location, and sends request to office having folder;
i.e., PSCs or ODO/DIO.
Certification of documents must be performed by the office having custody of the record.
