SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.453 - Coding Mongolia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 04/15/2022 - Present | TN 291 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402453 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Mongolia has a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN).
The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field has the letter “U” assigned
for Mongolia.
