E. Policy for verifying foreign pensions
It is not necessary to verify a foreign pension when the alleged amount of the pension
based only on earnings from the non-covered employment, after currency conversion,
is large enough to preclude the WEP Guarantee (the WEP Guarantee does not apply).
When the alleged amount of the pension based only on earnings from non-covered employment,
after currency conversion, is low enough for the WEP Guarantee to apply, we need evidence
of the pension amount. Evidence of entitlement to a foreign pension will generally
be a letter or award notice issued by the paying agency in the foreign country. For
applying the WEP Guarantee, see GN 00307.291.
