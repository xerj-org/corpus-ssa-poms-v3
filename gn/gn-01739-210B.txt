B. Using evidence previously submitted for claims for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to resubmit the evidence used to establish factors of entitlement
for U.S. Social Security benefits. The evidence previously submitted is sufficient
for Chile to establish the same factor for Chilean benefits.
