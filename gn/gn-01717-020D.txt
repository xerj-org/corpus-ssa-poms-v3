D. Policy – Survivors
The eligibility requirements for survivors’ benefits under the old Swedish system
are shown below. Information about the new Swedish system is available from the National
Social Insurance Board at the address shown in GN 01717.020E.
1. Widow(er)
Two types of benefits are payable if the worker died 1/1/1990 or later.
a. Readjustment Grant
A readjustment grant is payable for 1 year if the widow(er) was:
•
Under age 65.
•
Permanently living with the worker at the time of death.
•
Married to the worker or had a child with the worker (or was expecting a child of
the worker).
b. Special Survivor Pension
A special survivor pension may be paid after 1 year if the widow(er) has custody of
and is living with a child under age 12 or is unable to support herself or himself.
NOTE: Different eligibility rules apply if the worker died before 1/1/1990 or a widow was
born in 1944 or earlier.
2. Divorced Widow(er)
There is no provision for divorced widow(er)’s benefits.
3. Children
The eligibility requirements for children to receive Swedish survivors’ benefits are
as follows:
a. Basic Pension
•
Under age 18, or age 20 if a student.
•
Certain residence requirements may apply.
b. ATP Pension
•
Under age 18, or age 20 if a student.
•
Deceased parent had at least 3 years of ATP coverage.
•
No nationality or residence requirements.
