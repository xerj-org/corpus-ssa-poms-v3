SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02604.415 - Referring a Potential Administrative Sanction Case to the Office of the Inspector General (OIG) - Field Office (FO) Procedure
Effective Dates: 05/04/2020 - Present | TN 73 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202604415 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Processing initial claim, post-entitlement, or post-eligibility actions
Consult with the OIG prior to completing any pending adjudicative or post-adjudicative
actions. In addition, communicate with the OIG to determine case status and when to
take administrative action. For more information on completing actions for potential
administrative sanction cases, see GN 04111.010.
