C. No Person Meets the Above Requirements
If no person survives who meets the above requirements, payment may not be made. Furthermore,
if a person higher on the priority listing is unavailable or the whereabouts are unknown,
payment may not be made to a person in a lower priority. Under the same principle,
a person may not waive the right to claim the benefit in favor of a person in a lower
priority.
