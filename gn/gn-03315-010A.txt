A. Disclosure of tax return information
Section 6103 of the Internal Revenue Code (IRC), 26 U.S.C. 6103, prohibits disclosure
of return information (any information taken from a Federal tax return), unless specifically
authorized by that section. The IRC protects the confidentiality of tax return information
after death. For more information about the disclosure of tax return information of
deceased individuals, see GN 03320.020. For information about the definition of tax return information, see GN 03320.001D.1.
