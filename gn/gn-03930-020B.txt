SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03930.020 - Petitioning for a Fee for Services Provided in Proceedings Before Us
Effective Dates: 12/09/2024 - Present | TN 35 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203930020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Multiple Individuals Provided Services
1. Representative's Unappointed Associate Provided Services
A representative may delegate certain tasks to an unappointed associate whom the representative
supervises and directs, as explained in GN 03910.025B. The representative files a single fee petition that may include the services of the
unappointed associate. Refer to GN 03910.025B and GN 03305.025 for instructions about disclosing a claimant's record to such associates.
2. Co-Representation
A claimant may appoint multiple representatives (refer to GN 03910.040). An agreement between the representatives about the fee does not control our fee
authorization under the fee petition process. Each representative must file a separate
fee petition to obtain our approval of any fee for services performed.
