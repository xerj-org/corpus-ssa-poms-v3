GN 00302.430 The SSA-2038-F3 (Date of Birth Determination)
A.
Use of
Form
The SSA-2038-F3 may be used whenever a DB determination is required (see GN 00302.405). The form is designed to help guide the adjudicator in determining the DB and providing
explanations when required by POMS instructions (see GN 00302.170).
Where fewer details and less explanation are required (e.g., in cases where no material
discrepancy exists), only part of the SSA-2038-F3 need be completed, as instructed
on the form itself.
