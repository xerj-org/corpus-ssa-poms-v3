A. Disclosure policy
1. Non-violent crimes
Disclosures to State and local agencies for non-violent crimes are generally limited
to cases involving targeted investigation or prosecution of fraud and abuse in health
maintenance and income maintenance programs. For specific processing instructions
related to non-violent crimes, see GN 03312.110.
2. Violent crimes
For processing instructions related to violent crimes, see GN 03312.100.
