D. Procedure — canceling an ssa-562-u3
If you receive the requested evidence from a source other than the assisting FO or
when you determine that the requested evidence is not necessary, cancel the SSA-562-U3
immediately. The SSA-562-U3 can be canceled by memorandum, the third copy (or photocopy)
of the SSA-562-U3, marked “CANCEL,” or telephone when appropriate with FO procedures.
