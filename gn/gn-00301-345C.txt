C. Procedure – translator authorization checklist and Form SSA-1014
Provided below is a checklist managers may use for evaluating the qualifications of
employees who have foreign language skills and are willing to serve as translators.
Also provided is the SSA 1014 (Official Translator Signature Card) which can be used
to submit the form to the RO electronically.
