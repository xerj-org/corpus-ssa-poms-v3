SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.635 - Reclamation Documentation Request Process for Title II Reclamations - Processing Center (PC) Instructions
Effective Dates: 02/15/2024 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408635 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
This policy provides information and guidance for PC technicians requesting reclamation
documentation needed for the reclamation process. PSC technicians will request reclamation
documentation from the Department of the Treasury's (Treasury) Bureau of the Fiscal
Service (BFS). Requests to BFS for reclamation documentation must only occur if the
documentation is not available in the Treasury Check Information System (TCIS) and/or
Paperless.
This policy does not apply to Field Office technicians and Title XVI reclamation actions.
