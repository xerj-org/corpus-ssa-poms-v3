B. PROCEDURE - OCO SPECIAL APPEALS AND EXAMINING SECTION (SAES)
If an adverse order is issued against SSA in a case that is the jurisdiction of PC7,
OALC or OPL will work with OCO, SAES to insure prompt compliance with the implementation
requirements of the court order.
