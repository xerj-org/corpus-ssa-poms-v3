GN 00302.240 Non-Material Discrepancy — Age
Policy
When there is evidence in the file which reflects a different DB from that alleged
by the claimant, follow GN 00302.160 to determine whether the discrepancy is material.
If the discrepancy is not material, establish the DB based on the evidence which,
in the judgment of the adjudicator, has the highest probative value. See GN 00302.165 for evaluation guides.
No written determination is needed.
