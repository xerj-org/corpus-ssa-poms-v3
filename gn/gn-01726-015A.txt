SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with a Dutch Agency
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a claimant contacts a Dutch agency to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the agency
routes form NL/VS 3 (Netherlands-U.S. Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
to the FSP in Dublin. The Dutch coverage record, form NL/VS 205, will be included.
