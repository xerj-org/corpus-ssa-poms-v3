F. Effect of a bankruptcy judgement on SSA
If the bankruptcy court does not dismiss the case, it will ultimately rule on the
extent to which debts are discharged, refer to GN 02215.230. OGC transmits dismissal, discharge, and other relevant court orders to the appropriate
component for specific action.
If SSA was first notified of a bankruptcy case after discharge, immediately contact OGC (^SSA Bankruptcy) and wait for a response before processing the discharge
order.
