SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03316.105 - Disclosure Without Consent to Primary Claimant/Beneficiary about Auxiliary Claimant/Beneficiary Filing on Their Record
Effective Dates: 07/27/2020 - Present | TN 6 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203316105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Content of disclosure
We may disclose the following information about auxiliaries to a primary claimant,
beneficiary, or NH:
•
first and last name;
•
collective benefit amount, (i.e., the amount of the benefit, the effective date of
that benefit amount, and any changes in the benefit amount and the effective dates
of those changes); and
NOTE: This disclosure does not include the amount of any monthly check or the amount of any retroactive benefit
check. The benefit amount does not mean the payment amount. The actual check amount
could be different from the benefit amount due to a number of factors, such as a deduction
for a Medicare premium, work deduction, or a deduction for an overpayment.
•
date(s) and period(s) of entitlement, including date(s) benefits were terminated.
