D. Assignment of appeal requests submitted through iAppeals non-medical
IAppeals Non-Medical completes the following actions.
•
Sends appeals to the Non-Medical iAppeals Tracking System that is available on the
IMAIN Menu.
•
Assigns appeals to FOs as detailed in GN 03101.125. An FO manager views and assigns appeal requests in the Non-Medical iAppeals Tracking
System.
