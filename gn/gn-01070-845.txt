GN 01070.845 How To Process Material After Initial Diary Period Matures
Follow the procedures below when the diary control matures and the evidence and/or
documents have not been received.
Step
Action
1
Check pending file daily for diary due dates
2
Pull the case material if the diary due date has been met.
3
Move through the control system.
4
Assign to the technician shown on the diary to make a follow-up request using the
DIRCON mail procedure in GN 01070.835.
