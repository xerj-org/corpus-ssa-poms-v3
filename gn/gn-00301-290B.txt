SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.290 - Material Essential to Claims Folder Documentation
Effective Dates: 09/21/2010 - Present | TN 18 (05-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301290 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy – claims documentation
1. Title II claims
a Application for Title II benefits, which is generally captured on MCS.
b. Application support forms captured in paper, see GN 00301.286
D.4.d., and on MCS and EVID.
a.
Entitlement proofs (age, relationship, death, etc.), which are captured on MCS or
EVID. See GN 00301.286.
b.
Statements signed by claimant or other third party regarding an MCS entitlement factor,
which, unless another POMS section requires retaining the paper, are captured on MCS
or EVID.
c.
Earnings Record and any supporting proof or determinations.
d.
Medical evidence.
e.
Notices of initial and reconsideration determinations, medical and non-medical; e.g.,
Document Processing System (DPS).
f.
Appellate determinations and decisions.
g.
Material relating to resolution of overpayment and underpayment issues, which is captured
on EVID.
h.
Manually prepared notices which form the basis for an unfavorable or partially favorable
determination.
2. Title XVI claims
a.
SSI applications and redeterminations, which are generally captured on MSSICS.
b.
Application support forms which are captured in paper, see GN 00301.286 D.4.d., or on MSSICS.
c.
Proofs (age, citizenship, residency, etc.), which are captured on MSSICS or EVID.
d.
Statements signed by recipient; e.g., statements documenting citizenship, residency,
earnings, other income, living arrangements, resources, etc., which are captured on
MSSICS or paper per GN 00301.286
D.4.d.
e.
Statements signed by other persons which are used as evidence to support the determination
of benefit amount (e.g., statement relating to living arrangements by the householder
or other knowledgeable persons), which, unless another POMS requires retaining the
paper, are captured on MSSICS.
f.
Verification of earnings, bank accounts, living arrangements, citizenship, etc., including
SSA-5002's prepared as a result of telephone contacts, which are captured on MSSICS
or EVID.
g.
Medical evidence and disability development forms.
h.
Notices of initial and reconsideration determinations, medical and nonmedical, including
payment calculation sheets and overpayment determinations, e.g., DPS.
i.
Appellate determinations and decisions including hearing cassettes.
j.
Material relating to resolution of overpayment and underpayment issues, which is captured
on MSSICS and EVID.
k.
Manually prepared notices which form the basis for an unfavorable or partially favorable
determination.
