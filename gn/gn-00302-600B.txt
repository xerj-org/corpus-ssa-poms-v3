B. Requesting evidence from CCC records
Request age information on Form SSA-562-U3 through the St. Louis (South Side) D0,
4365 Chippewa, St Louis, Missouri 63116
The request should contain as much of the following information as possible.
•
The claimant's exact full name at the time of entry into the CCC, and
•
Alleged DB, and
•
The home address at the time of entry and discharge, and
•
The claimant's parents' names, and
•
The rate of pay, and
•
The CCC Company name, number and location, and
•
The claimant's position title
