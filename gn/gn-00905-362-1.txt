SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.362 - Requests for Delayed Payment of Retroactive Benefits - Philippine Claims
Effective Dates: 04/17/2018 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905362 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. The Federal Benefits Units (FBU) in Manila, Philippines
If the possibility of excessive fee charging is noted during the claims interview,
discuss the possibility of special handling of the first check with the beneficiary.
If the beneficiary agrees, flag the claim for special processing in DIO by attaching
an authorization signed by the beneficiary which requests that:
a.
Any retroactive benefits be paid by separate check after the regular monthly checks
have begun; and
b.
No mention be made of retroactive benefits on the award notice.
Note: An SSA-795 or other form can be used.
