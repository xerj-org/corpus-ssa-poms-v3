D. Entitlement factors
The following indicates what factors of entitlement, in addition to insured status,
must be established in claims for Spanish benefits.
1. Retirement Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Spanish retirement claims are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Marriage, if a spouse's supplement is claimed
c.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's supplement is claimed
d.
Type of work in the U.S., if benefits are claimed under a special occupational system
(see GN 01721.010).
2. Survivors Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Spanish survivors claims are:
a.
Death of worker
b.
Age of surviving spouse and date of marriage, if surviving spouse or divorced surviving
spouse benefit is claimed
c.
Date of divorce, if divorced surviving spouse's benefit is claimed
d.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's benefit is claimed
e.
Type of work in the U.S., if benefits are claimed under a special occupational system
(see GN 01721.010).
3. Disability Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Spanish disability claims are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Degree of disability (medical evidence)
c.
Marriage, if a spouse's supplement is claimed
d.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's supplement is claimed
e.
Type of work in the U.S., if benefits are claimed under a special occupational system
(see GN 01721.010).
