GN 01736.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Irish Agency on Form SSA-2960-U3-IR
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the Irish agency.
•
Diary initial requests for 180 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 60 days.
•
Forward the folder to IOTS if no response has been received after two followups, i.e.;
after 300 days.
