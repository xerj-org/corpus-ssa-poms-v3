B. Using evidence previously submitted for claims for U.S. Title II benefits
Applicants do not need to resubmit the evidence previously used and verified to establish
factors of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits. The evidence previously
submitted is sufficient for the Slovak Republic to establish the same factor for Slovak
benefits.
