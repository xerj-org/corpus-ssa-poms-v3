SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00210.701 - Same-Sex Marriage - Premium Surcharge Rollback
Effective Dates: 02/07/2025 - Present | TN 63 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200210701 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
As a result of the Windsor and Obergefell decisions, we are able to process premium
surcharge rollback request actions for individuals who enrolled in the General Enrollment
Period, but had Group Health Plan (GHP) or Large Group Health Plan (LGHP) coverage
based on current employment of a same-sex spouse.
