A. Policy for completing the U.S./Greece Agreement
Technicians in the Office of International Operations (OIO) and personnel in the American
Embassy in Athens communicate with the Greek agencies via Form SSA-2960-USA-GR3. Greek
agencies’ personnel use a similar form to communicate with SSA.
