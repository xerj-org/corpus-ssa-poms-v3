A. Policy Principle
Collateral estoppel provides that an issue which has already been decided in a previous
determination or decision in a claim involving the same parties but arising under
a different title of the Act or under the Federal Coal Mine Health and Safety Act,
will not be considered again unless there are reasons to believe that it was wrong.
For collateral estoppel in disability claims, see DI 11011.001 and DI 27515.000.
EXAMPLE:If a claimant is found disabled for title II, based on collateral estoppel, we may
find the claimant disabled for title XVI.
