SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03360.025 - Certification of Copies of Documents and Extracts from Records
Effective Dates: 06/25/2008 - Present | TN 15 (11-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203360025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure - PSC'S and ODO/DIO
PSC's and ODO/DIO should determine when it is appropriate to furnish certified information,
prepare a certification form and affix the Department Seal.
1. Forms to be prepared
a.
Prepare a cover letter to the requester in accordance with current correspondence
policies.
b.
Prepare an original certification (Form SSA-1841) (see GN 03360.999, Exhibit 2), yellow filebox (Form SSA-1841A) (see GN 03360.999, Exhibit 3), and two copies of the filebox.
c.
Prepare two copies of the requested evidence.
2. How to assemble
Assemble as follows:
a.
Clip one copy (do not staple) of the requested evidence to the original certification
form and staple the other copy to one of the filebox copies.
b.
Place the certification forms (the original and appropriate copies) and the cover
letter (the original and copies) on top of the claims folder. Ensure that the filebox
copies are signed in accordance with unit instructions.
3. How to route
Route the material as follows:
a.
Handcarry the package to an individual delegated the authority to certify documents
in the servicing PSC or ODO/DIO. See GN 03360.025B.3. above for signature.
b.
Send the original certification form and filebox copy with attachments to the Office
of the Director of the servicing PSC or ODO/DIO where the ribbon and Department Seal
will be affixed.
c.
Attach route slip (Optional Form 41) to the material to request the necessary action
and to identify the requester.
d.
Send the original certification form (with attachments) and cover letter to the requester.
e.
Retain the remaining filebox copy of the certification form and the filebox copy of
the cover letter in the file.
