Effective Dates: 02/20/1996 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010190 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
All beneficiaries with a foreign address entitled in July 1983 were sent information
about the alien tax provision and asked to furnish information about their alien status.
This was used to code them as subject/not subject to the tax withholding. Nonresponders
were coded as subject to withholding.
