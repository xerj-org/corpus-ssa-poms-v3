SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.110 - Automatically Convincing Evidence of Age if No Material Discrepancy in File
Effective Dates: 09/30/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. DB on first or second day of month
When using automatically convincing evidence to establish the DB, if the claimant
alleges that he/she was born on the first or second day of a month, and the month
of entitlement or the amount of benefits is or may be affected (i.e., would be different
if the claimant were not born on the first or second day of the month), at least one
of the documents must reflect the complete DB.
