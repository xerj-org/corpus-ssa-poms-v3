E. Procedure – PC/OCO-ODO processing of reinstatement actions
1. Instructions for reinstating benefits to prisoners
See GN 02607.860B.2. for instructions that the PC/OCO-ODO will use when benefit reinstatement did not
occur through POS/PUPS.
2. Instructions for reinstating benefits to Federal Bureau of Prisons Inmates
See GN 02607.792E. for instructions that the PC/OCO-ODO should use when reinstating benefits to released
Bureau of Prisons (3BOP) Inmates.
3. INstructions for reinstating benefits to NGRI inmates
See GN 02607.850E.2. for instructions that the PC/OCO-ODO should use when reinstating benefits to released
NGRI inmates.
4. Instructions for reinstating benefits to incompetent to stand trial inmates
See GN 02607.852B.2. instructions that the PC/OCO-ODO should use when reinstating benefits to released
Incompetent to Stand Trial inmates.
5. Instructions for reinstating benefits to sexually dangerous person inmates
See GN 02607.854B.2. for instructions that the PC/OCO-ODO should use when reinstating benefits to released
sexually dangerous person inmates.
6. PC/OCO-ODO processing of reinstatement exceptions
See GN 02607.860B.2. for PC/OCO-ODO instructions for processing reinstatement exceptions.
