B. Description of coverage record entries
The following is a description of the coverage record entries on the liaison form.
•
Ottawa
Coverage information from Ottawa is found in Section 2f of Form CDN/USA 2. Ottawa
lists the beginning and ending years of each period of coverage and the total number
of years in each period. The total of all years of coverage is shown at the bottom
of the section. If the worker had no Canadian coverage, a zero is shown.
•
Quebec
Coverage information from Quebec is found in Section 3 of form QUE/USA 2. Quebec lists
the beginning and ending years of each period of coverage and the total number of
years in each period. The total of all years of coverage is shown at the end of the
section. If the worker had no Canadian coverage, a zero is shown.
