G. Requests for Benefit Verification (BEVE) letter
If a beneficiary or recipient is receiving title II or XVI benefits or payments, or
has Medicare, he or she may request a BEVE letter from our internet website at my
Social Security. The online BEVE application automatically mails the BEVE letter to
the address of record.
In all other instances, use the Inquiry Response Benefit Verification screen or the
paper Form SSA-2458 (Report of Confidential Social Security Benefit Information) to
manually generate a BEVE letter or respond to benefit and payment questions.
