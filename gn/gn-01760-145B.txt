Effective Dates: 01/10/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201760145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Dependent or survivor does not meet U.S. residency requirement under the agreement
If a non - U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the 5-year residency requirement
and alleges Uruguayan citizenship or residence, develop for Uruguayan citizenship
and residence status.
