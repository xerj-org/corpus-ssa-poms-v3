SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01711.215 - Application for Benefits Under the Norwegian Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/07/1998 - Present | TN 2 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with Norway must complete Form
SSA-2490-F4 (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only from the United States,
only from Norway or from both countries.
