A. Introduction and Jurisdiction
The Division of International Operations (DIO), Payment Center (PC)-8, has jurisdiction
when a beneficiary resided outside the United States at their time of death. DIO handles
underpayment inquiries and claims related to the deceased beneficiary.
For information about foreign death adjudication, see GN 02301.095.
