B. U.S./German Agreement
Three application forms have been devised for claiming totalization benefits under
the U.S./German agreement. (See GN 01707.215.
1.
The SSA-3957-F6 (Application for Benefits Under the United States-Federal Republic
of Germany International Social Security Agreement)
2.
The D/USA 1 (Application for Insurance Benefits from the German Pension Insurance)—This
is an application for use by U.S. residents who apply for German retirement, disability
and/or the child's supplement. This form is stocked in DOs and OIO; FSPs in Germany
and elsewhere do not stock or use this application. Persons filing for German benefits
outside the United States must complete regular German applications.
3.
The D/USA 2 (Application for Survivors Benefits from the German Pension Insurance)—This
is an application for U.S. residents who apply for survivors benefits from Germany.
This form is stocked in DOs and OIO; FSPs in Germany and elsewhere do not stock or
use this application. Persons filing for German benefits outside the United States
must complete regular German applications.
In addition to the applications, several forms have been devised to enable the German
agencies to communicate with OIO. They are:
4.
D/USA 3—Transmittal/Request Form.
5.
D/USA 4—Liaison Certification Form. May be used to certify no German earnings in Part
II, 2d), in lieu of D/USA 5.
6.
D/USA 5—German Earnings Record.
7.
D/USA 6—Medical Evaluation (No longer in use.)
8.
D/USA 6A—Disability Liaison Form—Request for medical information or examination.
9.
D/USA 7—Declaration of Consent.
10.
D/USA 8—Information needed for issuance of German work record. Completed if claimant
has restricted application to U.S. benefits and has not previously filed a claim with
Germany.
