D. Overview of IDD for Togo
IDD payments sent to Togo must be in West African CFA Franc (XOF) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Togo. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
