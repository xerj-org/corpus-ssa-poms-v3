SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01775.012 - How the Federal Benefits Unit in Naples, Italy Processes and Develops Claims Filled with the Romanian Liaison Agency
Effective Dates: 09/02/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201775012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Romanian liaison agency processing of claim referrals for U.S. benefits to the
FBU
When an individual contacts the Romanian liaison agency National House of Public Pensions
to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the liaison agency sends the following information
to the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) in Naples, Italy:
•
Form SSA e2960 USA/RO 3 (U.S.- Romania Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification) certifying the filing date for the claim;
•
Romanian certified coverage record;
•
Available medical evidence (if the claim is for disability benefits).
