Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707155 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
SSA has agreed to help German beneficiaries who live in the United States complete
an annual enforcement questionnaire they receive from Germany each year. The questionnaire
serves to verify the beneficiary's identity and continuing eligibility for German
benefits.
Before the form is returned to Germany, it must be certified by an SSA employee or
other public official (e.g., a German consular employee, notary public, etc.).
