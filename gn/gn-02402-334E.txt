E. Description of Switzerland IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Switzerland will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN for Switzerland
consists of 21 characters. However, the bank data reflected on the MBR will reflect
23 characters including the country code of N for Switzerland. Technicians will use
the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
