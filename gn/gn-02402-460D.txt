D. Overview of IDD for Cote d’Ivoire
IDD payments sent to Cote d’Ivoire must be in West African CFA franc (XOF) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Cote
d’Ivoire. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
