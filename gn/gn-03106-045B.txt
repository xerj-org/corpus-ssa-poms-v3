B. PROCEDURE - MAGISTRATE'S REPORT AND/OR RECOMMENDATION
Upon receipt of a Magistrate's report and/or recommendation recommending reversal
pre-effectuation development should begin. However, objections to the Magistrate's
recommended decision are often filed. Consequently, a Magistrate's report and/or recommendation
should not be effectuated until a final court order is issued and received from the
OGC.
