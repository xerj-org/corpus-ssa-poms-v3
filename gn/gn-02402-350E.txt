E. Description of Slovenian IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Slovenia will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary. The IBAN for Slovenia consists of up to
19 characters. However, the bank data reflected on the MBR will reflect 20 characters.
Technicians will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
