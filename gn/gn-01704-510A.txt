SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.510 - Lag Earnings
Effective Dates: 08/08/2014 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704510 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
1. WHAT TO DEVELOP
BETs are responsible for developing an NH's lag earnings if alleged and if needed
by a foreign agency. Allegations of lag earnings can be found on all applications.
In addition, a claimant's statement alleging lag can occasionally be found on other
forms in the file.
2. WHAT TO REQUEST
a.
Acceptable proof of lag wages are:
1.
W-2 forms;
2.
Computer-prepared wage statement;
3.
Form SSA-1002 (Statement of Agricultural Employer);
4.
Form SSA-7011 (Statement of Employer);
5.
Pay envelopes, vouchers, pay checks, tax receipts or wage statements;
6.
Form SSA-768 (Certification of Evidence of Wages).
b.
Acceptable proofs of self-employment income (SEI) are tax returns (Schedules SE, Schedule
C, Schedule F, Form 1065 or Form 1040).
NOTE: A self-employed individual must have earned at least $400 in a year for SEI to be
credited.
When proof of lag earnings is in file, refer the claim to a CA for posting to the
SSA-794TR. In addition, any questions concerning the validity of evidence of lag earnings
should be referred to a CA or CATA.
