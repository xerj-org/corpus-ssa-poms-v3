C. Process when FRB debits the FI account
After determining the amount to be debited, Treasury sends a Request for Debit (FMS-135)
to the FI servicing the FRB. FRB does the following:
•
Takes the money from the FI reserve account (or its correspondent’s reserve account)
at FRB;
•
Notifies the FI of the action; and
•
Returns the reclaimed funds to Treasury.
