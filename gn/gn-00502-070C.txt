C. Referral to a social service agency
If the child is incapable and you cannot find a qualified payee,
•
refer the case to the state social service
agency for a special investigation into the child's welfare and recommendation for a payee
per GN 00504.300 and GN 03316.065H;
•
pending this recommendation, take action to either pay the child directly (if permitted)
or suspend benefits per GN 00504.110. For DAA cases and substantial harm determination, see GN 00504.105C.
You must document the social agency's recommendation or findings:
1.
Cases established in the electronic Representative Payee System (eRPS) (a payee application
is taken or will be taken, whether the application is denied or approved):
–
See the Beneficiary Details page, using the Add Report of Contact link, complete the
RPOC.
–
Write in the “Report” section “CAPA-SOCIAL AGY CONTACT” before adding your details,
see MS 07416.002.
2.
Cases not established in eRPS (e.g., the payee does not have an SSN):
–
Title 2 or Concurrent - Complete the Report of Contact (RPOC). Write in the “Report”
section “CAPA-SOCIAL AGY CONTACT” before adding your details, see MS 03508.007.
–
Title 16 - Complete the Report of Contact page. Write in the “Report ” section “CAPA-SOCIAL
AGY CONTACT” before adding your details, see MS 07416.002.
If you are unable to establish an RPOC in MCS and Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE),
use the paper Form SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) for your documentation and store it
in NDRED using EP or in eView using EP or the eView application.
