SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.417 - Coding Curacao Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 288 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402417 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Curacao have a prefix of “73” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “P” assigned for Curacao.
