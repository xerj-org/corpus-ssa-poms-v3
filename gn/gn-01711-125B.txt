B. Policy - Crediting U.S. Coverage
Norway will generally credit 1 year of Norwegian coverage for each 4 quarters of U.S.
coverage. When making the conversion, the Norwegian agency divides the worker's total
QC's by 4 with the result representing the number of years of coverage to be credited
for eligibility purposes under both the basic and supplementary programs. However,
Norway will not credit any U.S. QC's in a calendar year which is already credited
as a year of coverage under the Norwegian system.
