F. Combining claims files
1.
Auxiliary (or survivor) claimant also eligible on own SSN.
a.
Assemble the material separately as listed in GN 01050.150
D. but staple the two files together with SSA-101-U3s or SSA-1000 TRs on top.
b.
All proofs relative to the RIB claim (A) and all proofs pertaining to both claims
(i.e., proof of age, etc.) will, in all situations, be associated with the RIB claim
(A).
2.
If the files are going to different processing centers, a copy of all necessary proofs
should be with each file.
