A. Policy
Besides governing the use and disclosure of personal information, the Privacy Act
requires SSA to inform individuals of their rights when collecting information from
them. The information which must be included is summarized in GN 03301.020B.3.
This information is printed on most of SSA's applications and forms. You must ensure
that individuals are aware of their rights in situations where the data collection
form does not include this information.
