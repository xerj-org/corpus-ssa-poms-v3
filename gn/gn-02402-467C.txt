SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.467 - Coding Democratic Republic of Congo Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/23/2023 - Present | TN 235 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402467 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Democratic Republic of Congo will have a prefix of “75” at the
beginning of the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor
Account Number (DAN) field has the letter “D” assigned for Democratic Republic of
Congo.
