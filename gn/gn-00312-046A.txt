A. Policy for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
Belize has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration record under the
Attorney General’s Ministry. Records are also held at the local level in municipalities.
Belize has a website for forms individuals may use at https://mfa.gov.bz/vital-statistics-forms/.
