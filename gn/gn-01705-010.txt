GN 01705.010 Overview of the Italian Social Security System
Italy has an extensive social security (previdenza sociale) system that covers most
people. The Italian social security system provides a wide range of benefits including
retirement, survivor, and disability benefits.
To qualify for Italian benefits, a worker must meet minimum coverage or current work
requirements that vary depending on the type of benefit.
Benefit amounts are based on:
•
The length of time the worker contributed to the Italian system; and
•
The level of the worker's earnings.
