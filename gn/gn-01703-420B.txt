SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.420 - Processing Misrouted Claims in the Totalization Modules
Effective Dates: 05/30/1996 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703420 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - all cases
Claims Authorizer - Always take the following steps:
•
If there is a pending foreign agency assistance request with the U.S. claim or a claim
for foreign benefits, retain that request/claim and any related material in a totalization
folder.
•
Route the totalization folder to the LC to obtain the information requested by the
foreign agency or prepare a claims package. (Return the totalization folder to files
if the LC has already taken all necessary actions.)
•
If there is no pending foreign agency assistance request or claim for foreign benefits,
instruct the RAC to delete the "TF" case control record and destroy the totalization
folder.
•
Place the totalization application and any material related to that claim received
from the foreign agency on the right side of the RSI folder.
•
Complete, sign, and reverse file a folder documentation notice (see Exhibit GN 01703.270.E) on the right side of the RSI or DIB folder.
