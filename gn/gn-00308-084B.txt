B. Requesting Texas marriage and divorce records
Direct requests for certified copies of marriage licenses to the county clerk’s office
that issued the license.
Direct requests for certified copies of divorce decrees to the district clerk’s office
where the divorce decree was granted.
For county and district clerk’s office addresses, forms, fees, payment methods, and
request requirements, go to the Texas Vital Statistics Unit County and District Clerks, Local Registrars, and
Remote Access Issuance Listings.
