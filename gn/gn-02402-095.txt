GN 02402.095 Questions from Financial Institutions (FIs)
For general operating instructions about direct deposit and related topics, FIs have
the Department of the Treasury's (Treasury) Green Book, which is online at https://www.fiscal.treasury.gov/fsreports/ref/greenBook/greenbook_home.htm. An FI or field office (FO) may obtain a copy by downloading it from the website.
If the FI contacts SSA with questions concerning direct deposit, determine who should
reply and direct the inquiry to the appropriate agency.
