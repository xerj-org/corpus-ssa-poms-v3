B. Policy
1. Compulsory Contributions
Covered employees and their employers as well as self-employed persons are subject
to compulsory contributions which are based on a percentage of the worker's earnings.
2. Voluntary Contributions
Greece permits people who are not subject to compulsory contributions and who meet
certain qualifying conditions to make voluntary contributions to build future benefit
rights.
