SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.021 - Assignment of Direct Payment of Authorized Fees to an Entity
Effective Dates: 12/12/2024 - Present | TN 71 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Effective December 9, 2024, we will permit representatives who are eligible for direct
payment to assign direct payment of the fees we authorize for representational services
performed before us to an eligible affiliated entity.
