GN 02315.059 Massachusetts Small Estates
Massachusetts’ small estates law is found in its civil code under chapter 190B, article
III, part 12, sections 3-1201 to 3-1204.
COMMENT: Massachusetts changed its probate laws in 2012 and repealed chapter 195, among other
parts of the code.
