A. Process
The State Bureau of Vital Statistics (BVS) maintains birth, marriage and death records
starting with 1911.
Divorce records are available from the Superior Court or Probate Court where the divorce
was granted. The cost of copy varies.
IMPORTANT: Instructions for obtaining records through the state BVS are included in GN 00308.044B. Instructions for obtaining City of Boston and other cities and towns' records are
found in GN 00308.044C.
