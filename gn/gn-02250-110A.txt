SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02250.110 - Deemed to Defeat the Purpose (Ability to Repay) of Title II and Title XVI
Effective Dates: 11/22/2024 - Present | TN 68 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202250110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
We will waive overpayment recovery when:
•
An individual requests a waiver,
•
We find the overpaid individual is not at fault, and
•
Recovery would deprive the overpaid individual of income and resources required for
ordinary and necessary living expenses.
We will approve the waiver request under the deemed to defeat the purpose provision,
if the overpaid individual meets the criteria outlined in GN 02250.110B.
