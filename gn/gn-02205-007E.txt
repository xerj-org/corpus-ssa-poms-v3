The priority order of recovery depends on the payment status of the beneficiary. First,
we will recover from the beneficiary. Afterward, we will recover from the representative

2. Representative payee solely liable
If the overpaid beneficiary establishes that he or she is not liable for the overpayment
or he or she meets the criteria for an approved waiver, then only the representative
payee is primarily liable. The PSC will post the overpayment to the representative
payee using the overpaid beneficiary’s SSN, the record where the overpayment occurred,
using the BIC of “_X” (i.e., AX, CX), see SM 00610.772B.2.
Effective December 4, 2008, a representative payee (or his or her estate) is solely
liable for repayment if he or she received any benefits on behalf of a deceased beneficiary
after the death of the beneficiary.
