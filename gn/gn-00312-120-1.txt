1. Office to Contact
a. Event Occurred Prior to 1945
Official de Estado Civil in the district in which the event took place.
b. Event Occurred After 1944
Oficina Central de Registro Civil de la Republica Dominicana;
or
Official del Estado Civil in the district in which the event took place.
