SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.215 - Application for Benefits Under the German Agreement
Effective Dates: 08/15/2011 - Present | TN 5 (04-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following information to determine which applications must be completed when
taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits and/or German benefits under the agreement
with Germany.
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
For U.S. Totalization benefits only:
a.
For claims taken on MCS, in addition to completing the appropriate RSDI application
screens, see MSOM MCS 009.011 for completing the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen on MCS and MSOM MCS 007.001 for completing the SSA-2490 on MCS.
b.
For claims taken using the paper SSA-2490-BK application form, complete Part I only
and the appropriate RSDI application(s).
2. German regular or Totalization benefits only
These claims are MCS exclusions. Complete Parts I and II of the paper SSA-2490-BK
application form for German regular or Totalization benefits.
3. German benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. Totalization
For German benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. Totalization benefits:
a.
For claims taken on MCS, in addition to completing the appropriate RSDI application
screens, see MSOM MCS 009.011 for completing the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen on MCS and MSOM MCS 007.001 for completing the SSA-2490 on MCS.
b.
For claims taken using the paper SSA-2490-BK application form, complete Parts I and
II and the appropriate RSDI application(s).
