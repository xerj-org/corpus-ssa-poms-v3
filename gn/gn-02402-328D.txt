D. Overview of IDD for Netherlands
IDD payments sent to the Netherlands must be in Euro (EUR) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Netherlands.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
