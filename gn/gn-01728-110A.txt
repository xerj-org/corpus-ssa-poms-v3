SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.110 - How to Credit Portuguese Coverage
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Portuguese coverage certification is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage
to the earnings record (ER).
•
All periods of coverage certified by Portugal are creditable unless they are already
credited as U.S. quarters of coverage.
•
There is no minimum amount of Portuguese coverage which must be earned before the
U.S. can use it.
