A. Introduction to the Slovenian liaison agency
The Slovenian liaison agency Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia
is the only Slovenian social insurance agency that SSA will use to communicate and
process the claims under the U.S. - Slovenia Totalization Agreement.
