SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.427 - Coding Gambia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 01/25/2023 - Present | TN 253 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402427 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Gambia has a prefix of “73” at the beginning of the Routing and
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “B” assigned for Gambia.
