SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.220 - Completing the U.S.-Austria Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/ Request/Certification Form (SSA-2960-U3-AU)
Effective Dates: 12/16/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
The SSA-2960-U3-AU is completed in the totalization modules in OIO and by the FSP
in Vienna. It is used to:
•
transmit claims and related material to the Austrian agencies.
•
request information from the Austrian agencies, and
•
respond to requests from the Austrian agencies.
(Instructions for determining where to send the form are located in GN 01730.025.)
