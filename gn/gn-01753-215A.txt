A. Filing for U.S. Totalization benefits
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with the Slovak Republic must
complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement). The applicant must complete this form whether the application
is for benefits from the United States only, the Slovak Republic only, or from both
countries.
