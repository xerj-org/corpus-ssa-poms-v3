A. When to intercept a check sent to a foreign address
The following instructions are for checks only, not electronic fund transfers. To intercept a direct deposit payment issued
to an account in a financial institution outside the United States, see GN 02402.235.
You should attempt to intercept a check to a beneficiary residing in a foreign country
only if the situation meets all of the following criteria:
•
delivery of the check will create an overpayment of at least $100.00;
•
no more than 15 days have elapsed from the date of the computer run to the date the
incorrect payment was discovered;
•
direct deposit is not involved; and
•
we directed the check to a country listed in GN 02401.075C in this section.
