B. Crediting Spanish coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. QC for each 91 days (or remainder thereof) of Spanish contributions
in a calendar year. (Spanish coverage is measured in days of contributions.) However,
no credit will be given for:
•
any day of Spanish contributions prior to 1937, or
•
any day in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC, or
•
more than four QCs for any calendar year.
