GN 03313.035 Disclosure to the Department of Labor (DOL)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from DOL, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
DOL administers programs such as:
•
Title IV of the Federal Coal Mine Health and Safety Act (FCMHSA),
•
certain workers compensation benefits programs under the Longshore and Harbor Workers
Compensation Act, the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act, and Part C of the FCMHSA,
and
•
the Work Opportunity Tax Credit program.
These programs meet our criteria for income maintenance programs. We may disclose
certain non-tax return information to DOL for administration of these programs.
