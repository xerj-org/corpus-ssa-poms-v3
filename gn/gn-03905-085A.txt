Effective Dates: 12/10/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Processing centers, the Office of Hearing Operations, and the Office of Appellate
Operations use this form to notify claimants and their representatives of the authorized
fee amount for the representatives' services under the fee petition process.
