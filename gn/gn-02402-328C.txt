SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.328 - Coding Netherlands Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/19/2016 - Present | TN 262 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402328 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Netherlands will have a prefix of "71" at the beginning of the
Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN)
field has the letter “K” assigned for the Netherlands.
