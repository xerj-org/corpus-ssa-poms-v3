SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Follow the procedure below which describes special development and routing instructions
that apply to claims filed in the FSP.
1. U.S. Claim
If this is a U.S. claim, do the following:
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4, Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Request a Swiss coverage record from the Swiss agency.
•
In disability claims, if the claimant has previously filed for Swiss disability benefits,
request a copy of the medical evidence from the Swiss agency's records.
•
Forward the claim to DIO along with the Swiss coverage record.
2. Swiss claim
If this is a Swiss claim, do the following:
•
Refer the claimant to the appropriate Swiss local office to file a claim.
•
If the claimant insists on filing a claim for Swiss benefits at the FSP, take a protective
statement on Form SSA-795 and send it to the Swiss agency.
3. U.S. and Swiss claim
If this is a concurrent U.S./Swiss claim, do the following:
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Parts I & II) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Request a Swiss coverage record and inform the Swiss agency of the filing date.
•
In disability claims, if the claimant has previously filed for Swiss disability benefits,
request a copy of the medical evidence from the Swiss agency's records.
•
Forward the claim to DIO, along with the Swiss coverage record.
•
Refer the claimant to the appropriate Swiss local office to complete the required
Swiss applications.
