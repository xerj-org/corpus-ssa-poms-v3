D. Overview of IDD for Mauritania
IDD payments sent to Mauritania must be in Mauritanian ouguiya (MRU) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Mauritania.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
