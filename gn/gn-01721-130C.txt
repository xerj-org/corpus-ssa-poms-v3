SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.130 - Voluntary Contributions to the Spanish System
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 2 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Advise people who inquire about making voluntary contributions to the Spanish system
to write directly to the National Institute of Social Security at the address in GN 01721.020.
If the Spanish agency needs the person's U.S. coverage record, they will request it
directly from OIO.
