Effective Dates: 08/29/2003 - Present | TN 9 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203106061 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
If the OALC and/or OGC inform you by telephone that a case involves possible contempt,
follow the OALC/OGC instructions given over the telephone for expedited processing.
If a claimant or a representative contacts the effectuating component and is distressed
about the status of payment on a court order, take the following action:
•
OCO notify OGC, OPL;
•
FOs should contact the ARC-MOS or other designated component;
•
PSCs/ARC-MOS, notify Regional Chief Counsel;
immediately. Such a sensitive inquiry may indicate that a contempt action is being
contemplated. (See GN 03106.020B. for a hyperlink to OGC addresses and telephone numbers.)
