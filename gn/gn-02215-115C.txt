SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.115 - FO handling of compromise offers
Effective Dates: 08/04/2026 - Present | TN 63 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Overpayment $2,000 or less
The CR has the authority to accept or reject the offer without further review.
a.
Accept an offer of 80 percent of the debt amount (without financial development).
b.
Accept an offer which is at least 60 percent of the debt if:
•
The full debt cannot be recovered within 1 year by benefit withholding, and
•
Financial circumstances support the 60 percent offer.
c.
Accept an offer of less than 60 percent of the debt when financial evidence establishes that the debtor
is unable to pay more and the amount offered is reasonable.
When financial information is needed in the above situations, obtain the information
from the debtor in accordance with GN
02210.030B Document the Debt Management System (DMS) REMARKS (RMKS) screen.
2. Overpayment exceeds $2,000 but is not over $20,000
The CR, with the concurrence of an operations supervisor (or higher), has the authority to accept or reject the offer.
a.
Accept an offer which is at least 80 percent of the debt amount.
b.
Accept an offer which is at least 60 percent of the debt if:
•
The full debt cannot be recovered by benefit withholding within 3 years, and
•
Financial circumstances support the 60 percent offer.
c.
Accept an offer of less than 60 percent when:
•
The financial evidence establishes that the individual is financially unable to repay
more than the amount offered, and
•
The amount offered is reasonable.
Obtain financial information in accordance with GN 02215.115C.1. and document the
RMKS screen accordingly.
3. Overpayment exceeds $20,000
Develop and document financial circumstances (see above) and forward the offer to the appropriate
PC (the PCOCS on the MBR). If the debtor refuses to provide financial information,
advise the PC accordingly. The PC will prepare all notices.
4. Effectuating the FO decision
Notify the person in writing of the decision and, if the offer is accepted, when the
compromise amount is due. (See NL 00703.140 or NL 00703.141 for sample notice language.)
Generally, the compromise amount is due in full within 30 days from the date of the
notice. However, there could be circumstances which would require a different due
date (e.g., the debtor has a certificate of deposit which will mature in 90 days.)
a. When the FO Accepts a compromise offer:
•
Input the decision to the Record Compromise Decision (DRCD- MSOM DMS 006.012) screen.
•
If the debt is greater than $2,000, a second level review is necessary via the Review
Compromise Approval (DRCW - MSOM DMS 006.013)
NOTE: Input to the above screen(s) will generate a TC53 to post the write-off amount to
ROAR and set a PC diary for 30 days or the diary date if one is input. Once the decision
is final, send the proper notice.
b. When the FO rejects a compromise offer
•
Input the decision to the DRCD.
•
Send the proper notice.
c. When payment is received in the FO
•
Follow GN 02403.003 to input the remittance into DMS.
•
Follow GN 02403.020 to transfer monies to MATPSC.
