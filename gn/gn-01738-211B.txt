SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.211 - Preparing Final Claim Packages for the Greek Agency
Effective Dates: 09/08/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738211 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Claims Authorizers
Prepare a routing slip, SSA-559, after awarding benefits to a Greek resident.
a.
Enter:
•
the first month of entitlement (MOEN) that a full benefit is payable.
IMPORTANT: If a beneficiary's MOEN is prior to the effective date of the Agreement, enter “09/01/94” as the MOEN.
•
the MBA for the first month of a full payment.
b.
Route the folder to a Liaison Clerk.
2. Liaison Clerks
Complete Form SSA-2960-U3-GR Part II with “Benefit Data” information.
Note: This information cannot be released without a consent form unless a claim has been
filed under the agreement.
