E. Description of San Marino IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for San Marino comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN is 27 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR consists of 26 characters. Technicians
will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
