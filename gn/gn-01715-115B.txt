B. Definition
Canadian territory includes:
•
the Canadian provinces: Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland
(including Labrador), Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, and Saskatchewan,
and
•
the Canadian territories: the Northwest and Yukon territories
