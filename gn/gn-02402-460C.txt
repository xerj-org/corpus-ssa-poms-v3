SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.460 - Coding Cote d'Ivoire Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 04/05/2023 - Present | TN 233 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402460 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Cote d’Ivoire will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning of
the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “M” assigned for Cote d’Ivoire.
