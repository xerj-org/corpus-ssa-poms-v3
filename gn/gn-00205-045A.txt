A. Introduction
A question about foreign coverage is included on selected RSI and Supplemental Security
Income (SSI) applications to identify cases that interviewers must develop possible
entitlement to benefits under an international agreement. Also, inquire about eligibility
to any foreign benefits from another country. These questions are part of the ADDB
screen on Modernized Claims System (MCS) claims.
