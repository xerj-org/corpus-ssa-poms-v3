GN 00210.800 Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Same-Sex Marriages and Same-Sex Couples
CITATIONS: Social Security Act 1614(b) and 1614(d) 20 CFR 416.1801 through 416.1835
Following the 2013 U.S. v. Windsor Supreme Court decision, we began recognizing some same-sex marriages for Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) purposes.
On June 26, 2015, the Supreme Court found in Obergefell v. Hodges that all states must permit same-sex marriage and recognize valid same-sex marriages
from other states.
Because of the Obergefell decision, we recognize same-sex couples as married in all states for purposes of
determining eligibility or payment amount for SSI, including some same-sex couples
we earlier determined were unmarried.
