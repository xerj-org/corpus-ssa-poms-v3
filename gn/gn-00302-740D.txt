SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.740 - SSA Payment for Census Records
Effective Dates: 09/09/2021 - Present | TN 32 (09-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302740 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. IMPORTANT
When SSA pays for the census search, the claim can not  be adjudicated until the census record or a negative response has been received.
EXCEPTION:  In those rare instances in which preferred evidence is received in the meantime,
the claim may be adjudicated before receipt of a response from the Bureau of the Census.
