A. Process
Divorce records since April 1949 are available from the Clerk of the county where
the petition was filed. The cost of copy varies. Marriage records since July 1911
are available from the Probate Judge in the county where the license was issued. Cost
varies.
Birth and death records are available from all county health departments. The cost
is $12.00.
