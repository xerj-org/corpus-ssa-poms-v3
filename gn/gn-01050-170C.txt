C. CDB and DIB Applications Filed for Different Individuals—One SSN
In DIB cases where there is also a CDB application based on the DIB claimant's E/R,
prepare separate folders. Prepare the DIB folder as in GN 01050.165 and the CDB folder as in GN 01050.170A or GN 01050.170B in this section. If both claims are sent simultaneously to the same office, staple
the back flap of the DIB folder to the front flap of the CDB folder. Do not transmit
the CDB claim before the DIB claim unless the NH has also filed for a Retirement Insurance
Benefits (RIB). Cross-refer in remarks section of SSA-831-U5.
