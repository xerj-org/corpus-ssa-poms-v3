B. Description of form SSA-2960-U3-PE
The form consists of three pages.
•
Page 1 (Blue File copy) is printed in English and used for folder documentation.
•
Page 2 (White First Request) is printed in Portuguese and is the initial copy sent
to the Portuguese agencies.
•
Page 3 (Yellow Follow-up) is printed in Portuguese and is used as a follow-up request
when necessary.
