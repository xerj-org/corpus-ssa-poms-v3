SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.473 - Coding Madagascar Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 11/02/2023 - Present | TN 245 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402473 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Madagascar will have a prefix of “74 ” at the beginning of the
Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “ X ” assigned for Madagascar .
