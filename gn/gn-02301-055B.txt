B. When we need proof of death
If proof of the beneficiary’s death was not submitted previously, or is not annotated
to our systems, we require proof of their death. Proof is required regardless of the
amount of the underpayment.
We require proof of death of claimants in a higher or same order of priority before
payment of the underpayment only when there is conflicting evidence that the claimant
may be alive.
We require proof of death of other persons if it is needed to establish the claimant’s
relationship to the deceased beneficiary (for example, to establish that a grandchild
meets the definition of a child of the deceased beneficiary under GN 00306.235). For more information, see GN 02301.055D.
For information on preferred proofs of death, see GN 00304.005.
NOTE: Before issuing a death underpayment, check the Numident to determine if the potential
recipient is deceased. If an individual in the order of priority list dies before
receiving payment, see GN 02301.030B.2.
