D. Procedure — Required Remarks on the Totalization remarks screen (FRMK)
1. Coverage under the Polish Zaklad Ubezpieczen Spolecznych-ZUS
In all claims for Polish benefits, ask the applicant if the applicant has worked in
Poland and if they are applying for the ZUS pension. The response to this question
should be entered on the Totalization Remarks (FRMK) screen in MCS (see MSOM MCS 007.009)
or in the “Remarks” section (#19) on the paper or electronic (e-form) application
form SSA-2490.
2. Coverage under the Polish Kasa Rolniczego Ubezpiecenia Spolecznego- KRUS
In all claims for Polish benefits, ask the applicant if the applicant has worked on
a farm in Poland and if they are applying for the KRUS pension. The response to this
question should be entered on the Totalization Remarks (FRMK) screen in MCS (see MSOM
MCS 007.009) or in the “Remarks” section (#19) on the paper or electronic (e-form)
application form SSA-2490.
