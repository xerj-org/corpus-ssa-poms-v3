C. IF THE BENEFICIARY PROTESTS THE OVERPAYMENT
1.
If the beneficiary thinks he should not have to pay back the overpayment, then develop
for waiver.
SSA can waive the recovery of the overpayment if:
•
the overpayment wasn't the person's fault in any way, AND
•
the person couldn't meet his necessary living expenses if we recovered the overpayment,
or recovery would be unfair for some other reason.
If the above conditions seem to apply, have the person complete form SSA-632–BK(enclosed).
All amounts on the SSA-632–BK should be shown in local currency, and expenses and
income must be verified as explained under Step 3 of “What to Do to Collect the Overpayment.”
2.
If the person disagrees with the overpayment decision (rather than saying he can't
repay the overpayment), obtain a signed statement from the person requesting a reconsideration
and explaining why he thinks the overpayment is not correct. Also, have the person
submit any additional information about the overpayment.
