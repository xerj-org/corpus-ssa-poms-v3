A. Procedure for reinstatement at age 70
The Program Service Centers (PSC) use automated systems to process voluntary delayed
retirement credit (VDRC) reinstatements at age 70. For requests for voluntary suspension
received April 30, 2016 and later, unless requested at an earlier point, we deem a
beneficiary to have submitted a request for reinstatement of benefits the month before
the month of attainment of age 70, with benefits reinstated the following month at
age 70. When we reinstate benefits at age 70, we credit all VDRCs immediately. For
an explanation of the PSCs’ automated processes for voluntary suspension reinstatements,
see SM 03020.250.
