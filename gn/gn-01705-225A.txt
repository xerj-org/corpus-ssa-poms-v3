A. Automatic status notice for an application for Italian benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards information about an
Italian claim, certified copies of evidence, and a copy of the worker’s U.S. coverage
record to Italy, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically stores the claims
material in the Paperless
Processing Center (PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Italian agency. The system automatically
generates a notice that the BET sends to the applicant.
