B. Procedure – FSP process for handling claimants entitled and not entitled to U.S. benefits
This procedure explains how the FSP in Warsaw, Poland should process referrals from
ZUS or KRUS.
1. Claimant Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Forward all material to OIO annotated “already entitled to U.S.” benefits.
2. Claimant Not Entitled to US Benefits
Process the claim in the same manner shown in GN 01752.010B.
