SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02401.030 - Combining or Separating Payments from Two Different Trust Funds
Effective Dates: 03/28/2011 - Present | TN 24 (03-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When payments must be separated
Effective July 23, 2007, we must not combine benefits payable to the same individual from both the disability insurance benefits
(DIB) and retirement and survivors’ insurance (RSI) trust funds into one check or
direct deposit payment. Issue two separate payments to the individual.
