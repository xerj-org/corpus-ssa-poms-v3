SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.225 - Bankruptcy Petition Pending — Refund Received or Underpayment Determined
Effective Dates: 03/23/2023 - Present | TN 47 (03-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The automatic stay of collection when a bankruptcy petition is pending precludes the
withholding of any part of an underpayment accruing for a month that a bankruptcy
petition is filed (as in a pending waiver situation, GN 02210.006 2.c.) and even prevents accepting and crediting an unsolicited voluntary refund.
