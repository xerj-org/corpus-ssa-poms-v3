C. Procedure - Completion of SSA-533
Follow the procedure in this section when completing the SSA-533.
1. Request to translator in region
a.
Complete appropriate items in part A. If the document pertains to a person other than
the number holder, show that person's name as well as the maiden name if the person
is a married woman.
NOTE:
This information can be of material assistance to the translator in determining what
information is required.
b.
Annotate the form “claim pending,” if appropriate, to ensure the request is given
priority.
c.
Complete return address and telephone number on the SSA-533. (Failure to show this
can result in unnecessary delays or the loss of documents.)
d.
Use a separate form for each document to be translated.
e.
Ask the translating office to prepare a photocopy of the claimant's document if you
cannot photocopy it. Indicate if the translator should return the claimant's document.
f.
If the translator is expected to return the claimant's document, forward a label showing
the claimant's address to the translating office (if a mailing tube or other reusable
mailing package is being used), or forward an envelope addressed to the claimant.
EXCEPTION: If a document is sent to a non-SSA translator, the document will be returned to the
requesting office for return to the claimant.
g.
Exercise care to avoid defacing or damaging documents and to assure their safe handling
by the use of mailing tubes or other protective devices when indicated, see (GN 00301.265).
2. Translation and Priority Workload Unit (TPWU) translation requests
a.
Prepare an SSA-533 form as in GN 00301.365C.1.
b.
Email to: OCO.OEIO.DIO.TPWU@ssa.gov
3. Request to non-SSA translator
a.
Prepare Form SSA-533 as in GN 00301.365C.1.a. through GN 00301.365C.1.d. in this section to mail or fax the request.
NOTE:
Due to privacy considerations do not include claimant’s entire SSN when completing
form SSA-533.
b.
Check verbatim translation.
c.
Delete section D of the form, since the non-SSA translator prepares a verbatim rather
than an extract translation.
4. Translator
a.
If translating from an original document, complete item C.1.a. on the SSA-533.
b.
If translating from a photocopy, complete C.1.b.
