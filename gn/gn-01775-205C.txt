SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01775.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in the International Benefits and Processing (IBP) for Claims Filed Outside of Romania<EndLine/>
Effective Dates: 09/02/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201775205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. U.S. and Romanian claims or Romanian claim only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA e2960 RO/USA 3 (U.S. - Romania Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification ) to request a Romanian certified coverage record if needed. Use this form to certify
the filing date for Romanian benefits, to establish factors of entitlement, and to
give U.S. benefits information;
•
U.S. totalization earnings record;
•
Medical evidence from SSA’s files, if the claim is for disability benefits; and,
•
Any other relevant evidence that Romania requires to take action on the claim.
