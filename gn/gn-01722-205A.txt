SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.205 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Spain
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Claims packages are prepared to request Spanish coverage records and to inform the
Spanish agencies about claims for Spanish benefits. (The application and evidence
required for the packages are described in GN 01721.205 - GN 01721.215 and GN 01722.006).
