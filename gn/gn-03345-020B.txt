SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03345.020 - Statements of Disagreement With SSA Records
Effective Dates: 12/06/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203345020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Disputed Paper Record
Handle disputed records that are part of a paper file (such as a claims file) as follows:
a.
File copy of statement of disagreement in appropriate record.
b.
Send copy of statement, disputed record, and letter of explanation to any agency or
person to whom a copy of the record has previously been provided.
c.
Include statement of disagreement when making future disclosures of the record.
2. Disputed Automated Record
Handle disputed automated records (such as the MBR) as follows:
a.
Make statement of disagreement part of the supporting paper file (e.g., claims folder).
b.
Annotate automated record to indicate it has been disputed.
