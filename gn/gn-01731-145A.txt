A. Exceptions for Dependents and Survivors
Section 202(t)(11) of the Social Security Act requires certain non-U.S. citizen dependent
and survivor beneficiaries who have been outside of the United States for more than
six consecutive calendar months to satisfy a 5-year U.S. residency requirement to
receive benefits. During this 5-year U.S. residency, the dependent or survivor must
have been in a family relationship with the number holder (NH).
