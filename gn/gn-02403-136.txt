GN 02403.136 Disposing of Completed SSA-1395-BK Receipt Books
POLICY - SSA-1395-BK RECEIPT BOOKS
Since it will take individual FOs a long time to accumulate enough complete books
to make a shipment for the Federal Records Center--a shipment must be one cubic foot,
per AIMS MRM Chapter 07--FOs within a district or area should consolidate the SSA-1395
books that are eligible for shipment to make up complete boxes.
The FOs should annotate the Master Control Roster (in the Date Shipped to AD/FRC box)
that the SSA-1395 book was transferred to the designated FO. That FO should then transfer
the SSA-1395-BK Receipt Books to the FRC 1 year after the date in the “DATE BOOK COMPLETED”
block and record the transfer in the “DATE
SHIPPED TO AD/FRC” block.
NOTE: See GN 02403.095 for complete instructions for completing the SSA-3944 Master Control Roster.
