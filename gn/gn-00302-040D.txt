SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.040 - Evidence Required for Claimants Age 73 or Older
Effective Dates: 06/18/2015 - Present | TN 15 (02-92)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Exception
Follow GN 00302.050 if:
•
Any evidence shows the claimant may be under age 73, and the tolerance in GN 00302.030
does not apply, or
•
An age discrepancy affects the computation of insured status, benefit amount, or SMI
premium amount.
