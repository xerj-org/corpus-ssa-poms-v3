C. Actions to take when C-stop present
If a C-Stop was input, inform the individual that SSA will not issue a replacement
check until Treasury has determined the status of the original check. If no one has
cashed the original check, Treasury issues a replacement check. If someone cashed
the original check, the beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee may make a
forgery claim (if check was issued 12 months ago or less).
NOTE: For further explanation of the forgery claim process see GN 02406.160 and GN 02406.310. If the claim does not involve forgery, Treasury sends the CDN information to SSA
and notifies the beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee . The technician
does not issue a settlement check.
