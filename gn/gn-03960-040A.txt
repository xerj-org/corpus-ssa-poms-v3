SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.040 - Administrative Review of the Fee Authorized Under the Fee Agreement Process
Effective Dates: 11/30/2022 - Present | TN 10 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Parties to a fee authorized under the fee agreement process include:
•
the claimant;
•
any auxiliary beneficiary(ies) who would share in payment of the fee, as explained
in GN 03920.035B.;
•
the representative and any co-representative or co-counsel who signed the fee agreement;
and
•
the decision maker.
