SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.360 - Action on Claims Involving a Claims Helper in Philippines
Effective Dates: 04/17/2018 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905360 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy
Do not notify a representative of a claimant of the need for evidence or information
or send him a copy of the final notice when the claim has been completed.
Any deviation from this policy requires the written approval of the Office of International
Programs (OIP) or the Division of Training and Program Support (DTPS) in the Division
of International Operations (DIO).
EXCEPTION: This procedure does not apply to a U.S. attorney who meets the qualification in GN 03910.020B, or to a Congressman or a representative of a foreign government.
