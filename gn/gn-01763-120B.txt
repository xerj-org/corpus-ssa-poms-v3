B. Crediting Icelandic coverage under the agreement
The Social Security Administration (SSA) credits one U.S. QC for every three months
of coverage certified by the agency of Iceland. SSA does not provide credit for the
following:
•
Icelandic coverage credited for periods prior to 1937;
•
any month in the calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC;
•
more than four QCs for any calendar year; or
•
any period of coverage not based on work under Icelandic laws.
