2. Costs
Certified copies of birth/death/marriage/divorce documents $15.00. The cost of verifying
a document is $15.00 for certified and $10.00 for non-certified. Payment must accompany
request for verification.
3. Type of Payment:
Money order or personal check.
4. Remarks:
Please enclose a long, self-addressed, STAMPED envelope with your request.
Requests for any record, using a bank card, can be made via telephone to (207) 287-3181.
Maine Vital Records does not accept credit cards or online orders. Additional certified
copies of the same record requested at the same time are $6.00. A certified copy of
a delayed birth certificate (BC), new BC as a result of an adoption or legitimation
of registrant or amended BC for processing court ordered legal name changes is $60.00
each. Make all personal checks or money orders payable to “Treasurer of State.”
