Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 2 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201760225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Uruguayan claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Uruguayan benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Uruguayan agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Uruguay
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Uruguay, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Uruguay
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Uruguayan agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Uruguayan agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Banco de Previsión
Social--Social Insurance Bank
DIO is unable to determine the status of a claim after a technician forwards the claim
to Uruguay. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims, see
GN 01702.520.
