A. Background information for TSC and TCC
The TSCs A1, A2, A3, E2, and E3 reflect totalization payments and we must post them
to the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) along with the appropriate TCC. Claims Authorizers
are responsible for determining the TSCs. However, if the TSC is not present on the
MBR, he or she should route the case to a Benefit Authorizer for the necessary action.
The TSCs are available via the Totalization Data Collection Program e4962. These codes
show the status of or the decision on U.S. totalization claims.
