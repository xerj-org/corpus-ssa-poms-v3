B. Operating Policy—When We Can Have Different Determinations Under Different Titles
•
If in processing the later claim on one title we discover that the determination on
the earlier claim under a different title was incorrect, we do not have to adopt the
erroneous determination when we process the new claim.
•
We can only change the incorrect determination on the first claim if it can be reopened
and revised under the rules of administrative finality.
