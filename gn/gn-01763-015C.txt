C. Third Pillar: Voluntary Pension Contributions
The third pillar is based on voluntary individual pension savings with tax incentives.
Third pillar contributions are managed by the same pension funds, banks, and insurance
companies that manage the occupational pension system (second pillar). Pension funds
must make payments in equal installments over at least seven years.
