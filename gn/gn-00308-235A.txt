4. Remarks
Can FO obtain a free copy of birth/death record? Yes, but only if the conditions in
GN 00301.210 are met for SSA purchase of evidence. The request must be on SSA letterhead, and
the requestor should specify in their request letter they are requesting a For Government
Use Only record. In addition, an employee identification letter (see GN 00308.001B.3) must be provided with the request. The document received from the Department of
State (DOS) will be marked as Government Use Only or For SSA Use Only.
The public must write directly to the address in GN 00308.235(1) and pay the required fee. The public may call DOS at (877) 487-2778. The State
Department requires a copy of a valid photo identification and a signed, notarized
request.
