A. Forms to complete for Chilean benefits
Along with completing the SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S. International
Agreement), an applicant for Chilean benefits must complete a special Chilean application
form. The form will vary depending on whether the applicant files for a life or survivor
benefit.
