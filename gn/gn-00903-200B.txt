B. Field office management responsibilities
FO managers should ensure that the FO's I&R information on the Field Office Informational/Referral
screen in the enhanced Leads and Appointment System (eLAS) is complete and up-to-date.
Managers should also ensure that employees in co-located Teleservice Centers (TSCs)
have access to the FO’s Informational/Referral Screen.
