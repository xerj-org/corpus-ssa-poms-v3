SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03930.105 - Determining the Fee for Services Provided Below the Hearing Level
Effective Dates: 08/31/2023 - Present | TN 34 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203930105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - completing the SSA-1178
Complete the Form SSA-1178 (Evaluation of Fee Petition for Representation). Use this
form to show the amount of the fee you find reasonable, and to explain and document
your rationale.
1. Fee $15,000 or less
If the amount of the fee you believe is reasonable is $15,000 or less, follow GN 03930.110 to notify the representative and the claimant of the fee.
2. Fee would exceed $15,000
If the amount of the fee (or total of fees) you believe is reasonable exceeds $15,000,
take the following actions.
a.
Send the recommendation you prepared on the SSA-1178 and the claim file to the Attorney
Fee Branch (AFB):
Social Security Administration
Office of Appellate Operations
ATTN:
Attorney Fee Branch
6401 Security Blvd
Baltimore, MD
21235-6401
NOTE:
The AFB will authorize the fee and prepare and distribute Form SSA-1560A-U4.
b.
If SSA is withholding for direct payment of a representative's fee, the PC diaries
the case for 45 days. See GN 03920.015C if the PC has not received the fee authorization when the diary matures.
