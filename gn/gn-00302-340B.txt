SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.340 - Evidence of Survivor Status - Foreign Sources
Effective Dates: 08/26/2005 - Present | TN 16 (09-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302340 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow this procedure in attempting to obtain evidence of survivor status from foreign
sources if there is no domestic evidence.
Step
Action
1
Ask the claimant is comparable evidence exists in foreign countries.
2
Does such evidence exits?
•
If yes, go to step 3, and skip 4.
•
If no, go to step 4
3
Obtain the name and address of the organization from the claimant.
4
Use the list of organizations following this procedure short as source of evidence.
5
Obtain the claimant's authorization to contact the source.
NOTE: If the claimant prefers, he/she, may contact the organization directly.
6
Provide with the request identifying information which is as complete as possible
EXAMPLE: The following should be provided
•
Foreign spelling of anglicized name
•
Place of birth
•
Where the claimant was located during the war
•
Name of any camp where located.
•
Dates present at any camp
•
Names of other family member interned at the same camp.
7
Ask for verification that the claimant was residing in one of the countries listed
above during the appropriate period (see GN 00302.336B.)
