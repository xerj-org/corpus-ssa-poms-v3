Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Finland does not pay life social security benefits directly to family members of old-age
or disability pensioners. (Supplements are paid to the insured person.) Therefore,
survivors should be advised to file an application if they think they can qualify
for Finnish survivor benefits.
