SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Portugal
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Potential Entitlement to U.S. Totalization Benefits
If there is potential entitlement to U.S. totalization benefits, prepare an SSA-2960-U3-PE
to request the Portuguese coverage certification.
2. Claim for Portuguese Benefits
If there is a claim for Portuguese benefits, prepare a claims package including:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-PE;
•
U.S. totalization earnings record;
•
medical evidence from OIO's files, if the Portuguese claim is for disability benefits;
and
•
evidence submitted for the Portuguese claim.
