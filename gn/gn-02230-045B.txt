SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02230.045 - Remittance process for repayments of Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments paid to a sponsored alien
Effective Dates: 01/06/2023 - Present | TN 25 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202230045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Collection procedures
Operations is responsible for posting the sponsor debt on the Recovery of Overpayments
Accounts and Reporting (ROAR) and for collecting it once imposed. SSA will pursue
recovery by:
•
Billing and follow-up. For more information on repayment of SSI paid to the sponsored
alien, see SI 02220.065G.
IMPORTANT: SSA may also apply the following external collection methods for delinquent sponsored
alien debts:
•
Reducing Federal and State tax refunds to which the person is entitled via the Treasury
Offset Program (TOP);
•
Using Administrative Wage Garnishment (AWG), Administrative Offset (AO), Federal Salary
Offset (FSO), and credit bureau reporting;
