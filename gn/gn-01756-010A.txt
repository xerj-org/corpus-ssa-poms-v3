A. Process for applications filed in the Kraków, Poland Foreign Benefits Unit
The Kraków Foreign Benefits Unit (FBU) takes claims from individuals residing in Hungary
who wish to file for U.S. benefits.
The FBU should follow the same general procedures and obtain the same applications
and evidence as SSA field offices when processing claims under the agreement.
Photocopies of original documents must contain a reference to the number of pages
of the original documents and have Hungarian authentication stamps on them. Copies
must be made of all pages of working documents (blank pages included).
