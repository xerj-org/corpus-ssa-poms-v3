C. Documentation for payee selection and non-selection for cases not established in the
eRPS
Complete the Report of Contact (RPOC):
•
if a T2 or Concurrent: Complete the Report to add documentation details (see MS MCS
07408.007); or
•
if a T16: Complete the Report to add your documentation details (see MS MSSICS 04422.010).
If you are unable to establish a Report of Contact in MCS and MSSICS, document the
SSA-11-BK and scan into the NDRed using the Evidence Portal (EP) or Electronic Disability
Claims Folder (eDIB).
