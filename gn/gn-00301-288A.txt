SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.288 - What Information is Needed on EVID and the Report of Contact page in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) in PE Situations to Identify the Type of Evidence Submitted
Effective Dates: 08/15/2023 - Present | TN 105 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301288 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Use EVID or the Report of Contact page in CCE to record identifying information about
documents presented to prove an issue or a factor of entitlement/eligibility.
Refer to MS 01701.003 for instructions on how to complete the EVID and MS 08122.010
for the Report of Contact page in CCE.
When establishing EVID, be extremely careful about keying the client's name and all SSNs. There is no SSN interface with the NUMI to ensure that the information entered is
correct.
