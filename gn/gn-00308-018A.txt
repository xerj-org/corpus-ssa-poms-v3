A. Process
Certified divorce records are available for $4.00–uncertified copy $1.00–from the
Family Court in the county where the divorce was granted for years after 1975. For
years prior to 1976, divorce decrees must be obtained from the Prothonotary's office
in each county. The cost is $6.00.
