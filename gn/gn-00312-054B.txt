B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to Contact
Contact the local civil registration offices in the government local office where
the event took place or state civil archives for birth, marriage, and death records.
Contact the municipal court of the town where the divorce was granted.
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Names of the parents, including the maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Address at the time of birth
3. Fee
•
Birth certificate - 4km
•
Slovene tolars - 420 (The certificate is issued on an international form in 8 languages
including English)
•
Divorce - Varies
