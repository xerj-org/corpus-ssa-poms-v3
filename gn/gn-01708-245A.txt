A. When to use Form SSA-1281-U2-GE
The SSA-1281-U2-GE is completed only by DIO. It is used to respond to German agencies'
requests:
•
for certification of data pertaining to children's claims, and
•
for information concerning the decisions made on children's claims for U.S. benefits.
The SSA-1281-U2-GE should not be included routinely in initial claims packages. It
is intended only to be used to respond to requests for assistance.
