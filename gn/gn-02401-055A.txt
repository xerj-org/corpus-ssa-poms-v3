A. Negotiability of Department of the Treasury (Treasury) checks
Prior to October 1, 1989, there was no time limit on the negotiability of a Treasury
check. Effective October 1, 1989, the period for the negotiation of checks changed
to 12 months from the issue date. For limited payability information, see GN 02401.901. For nonreceipt claims with limited payability involved, see GN 02401.902. To determine how to process allegations of nonreceipt involving limited payability,
see GN 02401.903.
