A. Purpose of SNAP
On October 1, 2008, Congress renamed the Food Stamp program, created by the Food Stamp
Act of 1977, the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP). SNAP is intended
to safeguard the health and well-being of the nation’s population by raising levels
of nutrition among low-income households.
