A. Registration Process
Entities must register with us to be eligible to receive direct payment of any fees
we authorize for representational services performed by representatives who affiliate
with them through the individual registration process. Registration also allows us
to report income to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) using the IRS Forms 1099-NEC
and 1099-MISC.
NOTE: Registration is optional if an entity does not wish to receive direct payment:
•
Of any fees we authorize for representational services performed by representatives;
or
•
tax documents from us.
