SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.311 - Coding Anguilla Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 06/07/2021 - Present | TN 268 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402311 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor bank for IDD to several countries, including Anguilla. All
Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix of "70"
at the beginning of the Routing Transit Number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the
first position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for Anguilla
is “A.”
