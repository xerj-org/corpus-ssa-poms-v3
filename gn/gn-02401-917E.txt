E. PC and FO workload processing
Title II cases are processed by the PC and title XVI cases by the FO. The technician
will process the workload as outlined below:
•
attempt to contact the person by mail who allegedly endorsed the checks and recover
the money;
•
refer to OIG via the ARIS as outlined in GN 04111.010;
•
remove the incorrect payments from the deceased's record;
•
annotate the record to provide an audit trail;
•
examine information found in the deceased’s file, on the master beneficiary record,
supplemental security record, or the back of the check, and
•
send out a letter to the best lead possible.
