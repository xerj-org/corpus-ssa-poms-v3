B. Procedure for requesting access to records from hearing cases
1. Hearing pending
For access requests received while the hearing is pending, request Hearings send an
electronic copy of the folder (via a compact disc) or a photocopy of the material
needed to satisfy the access request. Do not request the entire claims file unless
it is necessary to fulfill the individuals request.
2. Hearing recordings and transcripts
We record all hearings on audio recordings. The claimant or representative can receive
a duplicate recording via a compact disc. We generally prepare transcripts only when
an Administrative Law Judge needs a transcript to issue a decision or dismissal. See
HA 01250.077 for more information on requests for duplicate audio recordings and hearing transcripts
from claimants and their authorized representatives.
