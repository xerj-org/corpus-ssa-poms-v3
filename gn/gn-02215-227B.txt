Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215227 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Send an overpayment notice for the new debt and follow normal recovery procedures.
Notify the Office of General Counsel (OGC), via email, of the new debt.
•
Indicate whether or not a Proof of Claim was filed for the prior debt(s).
•
If a Proof of Claim was not filed, explain the reason for not filing.
•
Attach a copy of the new overpayment notice to the email.
