C. Periods of coverage
Periods of U.S. and Korean social security coverage a worker earned prior to April
1, 2001, may be used to establish entitlement to U.S. or Korean Totalization benefits.
However, the earliest possible date of entitlement to such benefits is April 1, 2001.
