B. List of required entries
The required entries are as follows:
•
Show the name of the receiving agency in item 1.1 on Insurance Periods Certificate
(CZ/USA 205).
•
Show an entry in item 1.2 reflecting the address of the office that is receiving the
coverage record.
•
Show an entry in item 2.1 to reflect the surname of the applicant.
•
Show an entry in item 2.2 to reflect applicant’s first name.
•
Show an entry in item 2.3 to reflect applicant’s date of birth.
•
Show an entry in item 2.4 to reflect the address of the applicant.
•
Show an entry in item 2.5 to reflect the U.S. SSN.
•
Show an entry in item 2.5 to reflect the Czech Republic (Birth Certificate Number)
•
Show an entry in item 3.1 to reflect the agency completing the form.
•
Show an entry in item 3.2 to reflect the agency’s address.
•
Show an entry in item 3.3, 3.4 and 3.5 to reflect that it is properly certified.
•
If any of the required information is missing, contact your DIO Division Program Analyst.
