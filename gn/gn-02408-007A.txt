SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.007 - Title II Paper Check Payment Issued to a Representative Payee after the Death of a Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 04/22/2011 - Present | TN 78 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Any payment issued to a representative payee after the death of a beneficiary is an
overpayment that becomes the responsibility of the payee. A paper check payment issued
to a representative payee after the death of a beneficiary is automatically posted
to the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) of the deceased beneficiary as an overpayment
when the beneficiary’s death termination is input, even though the paper check may
be unnegotiated.
