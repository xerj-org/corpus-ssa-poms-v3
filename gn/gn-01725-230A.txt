A. Introduction
Under the agreement, SSA will help Dutch beneficiaries who live in the United States
complete a life certificate form. This bilingual form must be completed each year by persons receiving Dutch benefits
to verify the person's identity and continuing eligibility. The person must have it
authorized by a certifying authority. SSA is shown on the form as one of the acceptable
certifying authorities.
