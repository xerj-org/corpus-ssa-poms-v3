SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.250 - Disposing of Material When No Application is Filed
Effective Dates: 10/28/2022 - Present | TN 10 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Material which should be fowarded to DEBS
1.
Original SS-5, OAAN-7003 (except those used internally and are not signed by the NH).
2.
SSA-7011 (Statement of Employer), Civil Service Commission requests for eligibility
to RSI benefits (BR 146-289), RR-90 (Railroad Retirement Board Certification of Compensation).
3.
Document(s) that are accompanied by earnings discrepancy case material;
4.
Document(s) solicited by DEBS in an effort to develop earnings or obtain additional
evidence necessary whenever coverage is questionable;
5.
Certification of an original document(s) solicited by DEBS. This type of material
is to be returned to DEBS with their request attached;
6.
SSA-553 (Special Determination). Whenever a covered wage or SEI determination is submitted,
an RC should be completed calling to DEBS's attention that there is a determination
to be reviewed and that an adjustment to the earnings record may be required.
7.
Form 4361, Application for Exemption from Self-Employment Tax for Use by Ministers,
Members of Religious Orders and Christian Science Practitioners, and Form 4029, Application
for Exemption from Social Security and Medicare Taxes and Waiver of Benefits. These
are IRS forms.
8.
United States Civil Service Commission Form, Request for Verification of OASI Entitlement.
9.
Form DD-13, Statement of Service and Form DD 1300, Report of Casualty.
