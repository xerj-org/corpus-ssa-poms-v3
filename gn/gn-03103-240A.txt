SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.240 - Routing the decision by the Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) After a Hearing
Effective Dates: 03/12/2024 - Present | TN 47 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process for routing the decision
The ALJ sends a copy of the ALJ decision to the claimant, the claimant’s appointed
representative, and field office (FO) and places a copy in the folder. The ALJ routes
the case as follows:
•
If the ALJ affirms the reconsideration determination or dismisses the request for
hearing, the ALJ routes the folder to Social Security Administration National Records
Center (SSANRC).
•
If the ALJ decision reverses the reconsideration determination, (favorable to claimant),
the ALJ sends the folder to the Processing Center (PC) for effectuation. In a disability
claim disallowed on non-disability factors, if the ALJ renders a favorable decision
on the non-disability factors they return the case to the State Disability Determination
Service (DDS) for a determination on the disability factors.
•
For a partially affirmed and partially reversed reconsideration determination, the
ALJ determines whether to send the folder to Office of Analytics, Review, and Oversight,
Office of Appellate Operations (OAO) or to the PC, depending on the situation.
EXAMPLE: If there is a $1,000 overpayment and the ALJ waives recovery of $200 only, the ALJ
routes the claim to OAO .
