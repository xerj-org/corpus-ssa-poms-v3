SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.064 - Handling Claims and Claims Inquiries in Non-Resident Office
Effective Dates: 04/11/2016 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904064 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Applicant (or one of the applicants) is the principal claimant
When a claim is filed, or inquiry made about filing a claim in person or by telephone
at a nonresident office (or one of its contact stations) ask whether it will be more
convenient for the person to contact that office rather than the resident office.
If the person was merely confused about the location of the DO servicing his/her address
and would prefer to contact that office follow GN 00904.064C. If the person prefers to contact the nonresident office, follow D. below. Any administrative
advantage a given DO might gain or lose is NOT a factor to be considered in resolving
jurisdictional issues. The individual's own preference and convenience are controlling.
Do not discourage the person from contacting the nonresident office if he/she so prefers.
