SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in Germany
Effective Dates: 01/24/2017 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - U.S. and German or German claim only
Prepare a claims package on Form SSA-2960-U3-GE if:
•
a German coverage record is required and has not been requested by the FSP; or
•
the German agency has requested information or documentation.
