SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.110 - How to Credit Spanish Coverage
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Spanish coverage certification is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage
to the FCER.
•
All periods of coverage certified by Spain are creditable unless they are already
credited as U.S. quarters of coverage.
•
There is no minimum amount of Spanish coverage which must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
