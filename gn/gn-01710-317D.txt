SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.317 - Derived Rights
Effective Dates: 06/25/1996 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710317 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Example
Maria is an Italian citizen. Her husband, Fred, is a citizen of Switzerland. As a
citizen of a third country, Maria is not eligible for Swiss benefits under the agreement
on her own record. However, since she derives rights as the wife of a Swiss citizen,
she might be eligible for Swiss dependent's or survivor's benefits on Fred's record.
