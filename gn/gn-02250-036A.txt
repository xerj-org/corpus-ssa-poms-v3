A. Introduction
This section provides guidance for making a not at fault determination for a waiver
request only for the portion of an overpayment resulting from a period of benefit continuation.
We will find an overpaid individual not at fault for an overpayment due to benefit
continuation if they appealed the medical cessation in good faith. We assume an individual
appealed in good
faith unless they did not cooperate during the appeal and did not have a good reason for
the failure to cooperate.
