3. Regulation No. 2 (20 CFR Part 402)
Regulation No. 2 (20 CFR Part 402) sets out procedures for making and processing requests for records. It deals with
records such as:
•
administrative staff manuals, and
•
instructional materials that contain policies, procedures and interpretations that
affect the public.
The Regulations also provide fee schedules for providing information under the FOIA,
PA and Social Security Act.
