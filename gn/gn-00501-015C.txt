C. The role of Program Service Center (PSC)
The PSC has responsibility for Title II payee actions that the FO cannot process.
PSC responsibilities include:
•
processing any Title II payee determinations that cannot be processed in RPS (i.e.,
undocumented alien) and the fallout from T2R (alert and exemption processing) (see
GN 00502.190),
•
sending Title II manual notices designated by the FO,
•
resolving the PSC S8 suspension alerts,
•
resolving discrepancies and omissions on the annual and final accounting reports,
and
•
processing Title II non-responder accounting cases (third alerts).
