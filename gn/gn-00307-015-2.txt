2. Philippine documents
Do not return Spanish-language documents to the Foreign Service Post in Manila, Philippines
(FSP) for translation. Return documents in a Philippine dialect which are not translated
to the FSP for translation.
Refer Philippine documents received from other sources to the FSP for translation
if they are in one of the following dialects:
•
Tagalog;
•
Ilocano;
•
Pampango;
•
Major Visayan dialects (e.g., Cebuano, Ilongo, Waray).
NOTE: The FSP cannot translate Moslem dialects such as Tausog, Maranao and Magundanao.
