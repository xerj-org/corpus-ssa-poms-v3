D. Overview of IDD for Gabon
IDD payments sent to Gabon must be in Central African CFA Franc (XAF) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to OEIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Gabon.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
