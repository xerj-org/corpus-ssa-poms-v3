D. Overview of IDD for Tanzania
IDD payments sent to Tanzania must be in Tanzanian shilling (TZS) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Tanzania.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
