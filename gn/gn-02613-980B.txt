B. Procedure FO follow-ups on suspensions or reinstatements or resumptions
Tickle the MDW for 7 days to give time for the suspension action to show on the MBR
for T2 exception cases. Call the PSC using the PSC contact information in GN 02613.995
when the diary matures and there is no suspension.
