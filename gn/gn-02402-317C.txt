SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.317 - Coding British Virgin Islands Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 08/25/2026 - Present | TN 327 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402317 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
Citibank serves as the processor bank for IDD to several countries, including British
Virgin Islands. All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have
a prefix of 70 at the beginning of the routing number (RTN), and an assigned letter
in the first position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for
British Virgin Islands is "G".
