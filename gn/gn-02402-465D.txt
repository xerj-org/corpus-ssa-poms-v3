D. Overview of IDD for Rwanda
IDD payments sent to Rwanda must be in Rwandan franc (RWF) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Rwanda. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
