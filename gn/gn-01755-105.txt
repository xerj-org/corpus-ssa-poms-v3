GN 01755.105 Effective Date of the Agreement with Hungary
The agreement with Hungary became effective on September 1, 2016. The United States
and Hungary can use periods of U.S. and Hungarian Social Security coverage earned
in the United States or Hungary before September 1, 2016 to establish entitlement
to U.S. or Hungarian benefits. However, the earliest possible date of entitlement
to benefits under the agreement is September 1, 2016.
