B. Procedure for payment of benefits if homicide is an issue
1. Payment options
a.
If claimant elects delayed payment
•
Follow the delayed claim procedure in GN 01010.140.
•
Obtain a signed statement from the claimant.
•
Inform the claimant of their responsibility to notify us if:
–
they are no longer under suspicion;
–
the court having jurisdiction reaches a decision about their involvement; or
–
They subsequently wish to start benefits.
•
Prepare the appropriate listing and diary.
b.
If claimant elects payment
•
Obtain a signed statement from the claimant.
•
Prepare the appropriate listing and diary.
•
Verify any allegations regarding the homicide and insure that the claimant continues
to meet requirements for entitlement in any case where we previously withheld benefits.
c.
Combined family maximum
•
Follow the delayed claim procedure on both earnings records and withhold payments
at claimant requests.
•
Regardless of the claimant's decision concerning payment, contact any other beneficiaries
whose benefit is affected, advise them of the situation (including possibility of
overpayment) and of the option to have the increase withheld.
•
Obtain signed statements documenting the claimant and beneficiary decisions.
2. LSDP
If there is a delay in payment, follow the listing and diary instructions in GN 00304.075B.5. and GN 00304.075B.6.
LSDP not payable to a person who killed NH:
•
Reopen any other LSDP claim that was either only partially paid or disallowed based
on the entitlement of the claimant convicted of intentionally killing the NH.
•
Advise any claimant identified in the file with potential eligibility to file a claim
(If needed, find good cause if the reason for not filing was because the claimant
states they intentionally killed the NH ).
•
Document the file that there are no other potential claimants. NOTE: You do not need to continue investigating the claimant’s location.
