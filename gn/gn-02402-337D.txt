D. Overview of IDD for Israel
IDD payments sent to Israel must be in Israeli new shekel (ILS) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to IBP or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. IBP faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Israel. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
