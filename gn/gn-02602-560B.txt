B. Procedure for completing the worksheets
1. Telephone interview worksheet
Use this worksheet for interviews conducted by telephone. Because a telephone interview
is the preferred contact method, you will use this worksheet more often than the other
worksheets.
If you determine during the telephone interview that the centenarian is deceased,
use the telephone worksheet to document your development of the centenarian’s death.
You are still required to update Shared Processes with the necessary information pertaining
to the centenarian’s death. For a sample of the worksheet, see GN 02602.571.
2. Face-to-face interview worksheet
Use this worksheet for face-to-face interviews conducted at the field office or at
the centenarian’s residence. If you determine during the face-to-face interview that
the centenarian is deceased, use the face-to-face worksheet to document your development
of the centenarian’s death. You must still update Shared Processes with the necessary
information pertaining to the centenarians death. For a sample of the worksheet, see
GN 02602.570.
3. Unable to locate the centenarian
Use this worksheet when you are unsuccessful in locating the centenarian. Prior to
completing this worksheet, make sure you complete the Unable to Locate Centenarian
Procedures in GN 02602.530. For a sample of the worksheet, see GN 02602.573
