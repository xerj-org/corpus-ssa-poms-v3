GN 01704.540 Canadian Residence Evidence
Although evidence of Canadian residence is required for persons filing for old-age
security pensions, assume DOs have requested this evidence for claims filed in DOs.
If, however, you are requesting Forms CAN/USA 1 from persons who may be eligible for
old-age security pensions, request evidence of Canadian residence also.
If the requested evidence has not been submitted by the time the claims package is
ready to be transmitted, send the package to Ottawa without the evidence. Do not followup
with the claimant.
