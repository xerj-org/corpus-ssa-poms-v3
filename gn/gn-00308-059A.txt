A. Process
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the Superior Court that issued the
decree. The cost of copy $15.00, and additional certified copies are $10.00. Birth,
death, and marriage records are available from the City or Town Clerk where the event
occurred for $15.00. Additional certified copies are $10.00.
