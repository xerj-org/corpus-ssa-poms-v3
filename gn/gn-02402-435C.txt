SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.435 - Coding Saudi Arabia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/06/2019 - Present | TN 157 (02-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402435 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Saudi Arabia has a prefix of 72 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “E” assigned for Saudi Arabia.
