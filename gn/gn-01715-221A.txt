A. Introduction
When the Office of International Operations (OIO) forwards the completed Canadian
application form(s), certified copies of evidence and a copy of the applicant’s U.S.
coverage record (if necessary) to Canada, OIO annotates the Modernized Development
Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the type of claim, the date the claim was forwarded
and that a notice was sent to the applicant.
