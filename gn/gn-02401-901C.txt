C. Title XVI Limited Payability (LP) alert process
1. Six-month alert
If a Title XVI check is uncashed 6 months after the issue date, the SSI system generates
an L5 or L8 alert to the FO for investigation. For more information on the limited
payability alert process, see GN 02401.904.
2. Twelve-month Treasury notification
If the Title XVI check is still uncashed 12 months after the issue date, the check
is no longer valid. Treasury informs SSA that a credit is forthcoming, and the SSI
system updates the UCI field on the SSR to show the status.
3. Thirteenth-month alert
In the 13th month after the issue date, Treasury will credit SSA with the funds. The
Title XVI system generates a K5 alert to the FO for investigation, updates the UCI
field on the SSR and sets a K5 diary.
