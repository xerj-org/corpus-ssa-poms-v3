C. Location of records
•
The records are maintained by the clerk of the Seneca Nation. The clerk is changed
every 2 years when the headquarters of the Nation is rotated between the Allegheny
reservation in Salamanca, N.Y. (serviced by the Olean FO) and the Cattaraugus reservation
in Irving, N.Y. (serviced by the Dunkirk FO).
•
The records will be in Salamanca from November 1982 to November 1984; they will then
return to Irving for 2 years and continue to rotate in November of even-numbered years.
