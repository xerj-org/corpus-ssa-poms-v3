GN 02602.057 Procedure to Reinstate Medicare Advantage (MA) or Medicare Prescription Drug Plan
(Part D) After Correcting an Incorrect Death Report
Individuals are disenrolled from their Medicare Advantage (MA) or Medicare Prescription
Drug Plan (PDP) when Social Security Administration (SSA) reports a death to the Centers
for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). When a disenrolled individual notifies their
plan provider of an erroneous disenrollment, CMS instructs the plan provider to direct
the individual to SSA to correct the record and to continue to provide benefits until
the record is corrected. Disenrollments resulting from incorrect death information
are considered invalid and affected individuals must be reinstated into their previous
plan, regardless of when the record is corrected.
Follow the procedures in this section when a beneficiary was enrolled in an MA or
Part D plan at the time of the incorrect death termination. CMS will reinstate the
MA plan or Part D enrollment; however, SSA must reinstate Title II benefits and Medicare
Part A and B, and correct SSAs records before requesting CMS to reinstate Part D and
MA. (See GN 02602.056, Procedure to Correct an Incorrect Death Record.)
