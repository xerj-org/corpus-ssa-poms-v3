E. Procedure for good cause denials
Deny good cause for late filing if the beneficiary does not meet any of the examples
of good cause reasons in GN 02604.160D (in this section). Assume that the beneficiary knows to report their annual earnings
timely because we explain reporting requirements in:
•
application interviews
•
reporting instructions supplied at time of filing
•
periodic check stuffers
•
award notices or other letters
•
periodic questionnaires
•
mass media announcements, and
•
information posted on Socialsecurity.gov
