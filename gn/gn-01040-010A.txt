SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01040.010 - Diary Codes
Effective Dates: 05/20/2011 - Present | TN 6 (10-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201040010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
All diaries established will call the claims up for review 60 days after the diary
is established unless a later due date is entered into the system. Adjudicators should
modify the systems generated diary date if a due date other than 60 days is needed
or required. Adjudicators should also delete systems propagated diaries if they are
unnecessary. For example, a 016 diary for post 1967 military service should be deleted
prior to adjudication.
The list of diary codes is included in this chapter as a convenience and general aid
to FO and PC personnel. This list cannot be used as the sole source to determine when
a diary should be coded onto a claim. That decision is an adjudicative decision based
on the type of issue being diaried, and the policy relating to that issue. Diaries
should be entered based upon careful review of the various pertinent POMS sections
to which the codes apply.
Note that when the diaried action is completed prior to the maturing of a DO/BO or
PC initiated diary or if an established diary is unnecessary, the FO should instruct
PSC to cancel the diary by faxing the SSA-409 into PSC paperless at the time the diaried
action has been completed if it has not yet matured or at adjudication if the diary
is unnecessary. See GN 01040.005.
