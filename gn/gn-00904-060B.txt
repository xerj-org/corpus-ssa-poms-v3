SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.060 - Forwarding Claims And Related Materials To The New Resident Office - Guide For Development Clerks
Effective Dates: 06/13/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Claims pending in DO
a.
Transfer the entire claims file to the new resident office immediately.
b.
For RSDHI claims the transferring office will prepare the SSA-450 for the receiving
office to transmit immediately upon receipt to notify central office of the transfer
of the certified earnings record. This is done by the DRT. The SSA-450 should be the
topmost form of the file being transferred.
Exception:
When the only remaining claim development necessary is in the area serviced by your
office, the claim should be retained until adjudication action has been completed,
including preparation of the award form and transmittal of award to the PSC or ODO.
c.
If an SSI claim is being transferred, case control input is required.
2. Claim no longer pending in DO
a.
Give change of address notice to a service representative for processing unless the
notice has been referred to the CR for handling.
b.
If a claimant or beneficiary notifies us he/she will change address at some future
date, the file will not be transferred until the effective date of the change of address.
