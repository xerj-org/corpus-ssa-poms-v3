SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03106.075 - Applicability of Decision to Other Cases
Effective Dates: 08/28/2003 - Present | TN 9 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203106075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
1. District Court
If a district court ruling for one case is adopted for other cases, POMS and/or Social
Security Rulings (SSR) will be issued providing specific guidance.
2. Circuit Court
Circuit court decisions that conflict with SSA policy are not applied to claimants
who are not named plaintiffs (or class members in class action lawsuits), unless SSA
issues an Acquiescence Ruling (AR). (See GN 03501.001.)
3. Supreme Court
An interpretation of a provision of the Social Security Act by the Supreme Court of
the United States must be applied as a precedent in all similar cases. When the Supreme
Court issues a decision adverse to SSA, instructions regarding implementation of the
decision are issued as soon as possible. Until instructions are issued, direct questions
regarding the decision to OALC through the PSC or the ARC-MOS to coordinate litigation
activities. See GN 03106.020F. for a hyperlink to OALC telephone numbers.
