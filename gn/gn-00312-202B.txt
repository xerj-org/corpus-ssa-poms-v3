B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the municipality, town, or village where the head of the family had their
legal residence at the time the event occurred. Obtain records for non-Japanese citizens
from the ward or city where the report was filed. Contact the district court where
the divorce was granted.
For birth, marriage, and death records:
•Identify the correct municipality, town, or village
•Address the request to the office of the municipality, town, or village
•Include a self-addressed envelope
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Family Name (show the name of the head of the household and their legal residence
at the time of the event
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Current address
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Date the birth was reported (if available)
•
Names of the parents, including the parent's maiden surname
If transferred from another Family Register, the former Family Register
•
Purpose of the request, i.e., how you plan to use the requested document
•Registered residence
3. Other records
Include the following information:
•
Family name (show the name of the head of the household and their legal residence
at the time of the event
•
Given name
•
Date of records and causes (marriage, death, adoption, etc.
•
Names of the parents and the relation to them
•
If adopted, names of the adoptive parents
•
If married, which spouse is making the request
•
If transferred from another Family Register, the former Family Register
•
Registered residence
4. Fee
450 Yen.
