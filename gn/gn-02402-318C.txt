SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.318 - Coding Cayman Islands Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/12/2022 - Present | TN 223 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402318 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Cayman Islands will have a prefix of “70” at the beginning of
the Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “H” assigned for Cayman Islands.
