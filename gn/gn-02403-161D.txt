SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02403.161 - Social Security Electronic Remittance System (SERS) for Programmatic and Conserved Funds Remittances
Effective Dates: 11/18/2024 - Present | TN 60 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202403161 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure for handling SERS remittances
When we receive remittances in the FO, continue to follow the procedures for logging
the remittances in the daily log when received per GN 02403.004. In addition, examine the remittances to determine if the remittances are acceptable,
per GN 02403.005.
A technician, remittance clerk, remittance supervisor, or an office manager can process
remittances via SERS, as long as they ensure that they process all SERS remittances
upon receipt and take proper safeguards, as applicable.
The instructions provide the procedures on how to process:
•
Mail-in checks or money orders (see, GN 02403.161D.1.); and
•
Walk-in checks or money orders (see, GN 02403.161D.2.)
1. Mail-in check or money order transactions procedures
a.
Verify the controlling SSN or Beneficiary’s Own Account Number (BOAN) of the account
that the remitter wants to pay.
b.
Ensure that the remitter provided the correct SSN and there is an active debt on the
MBR or SSR. This verifies that the remittance should be processed in the Programmatic
store.
•
If there is no active debt on the MBR or SSR,and the payment is for conserved funds,
process the remittance in the Conserved Funds store.
•
In the Conserved Funds store, enter Number Holder's SSN for Disability or Retirement;
enter BOAN for Supplemental Security Income (SSI).
•
Select Trust Fund for Conserved Funds Remittance. Proceed to step "d".
c.
Input the controlling SSN into SERS and review the returned programmatic debt(s) per
MS 09301.001 through MS 09301.012.
d.
Examine the check or money order to determine if it is acceptable per GN 02403.005.
1.
Verify the date and signature, and ensure that the check or money order is made payable
to SSA,
2.
Verify the amount on the check is equal to the amount to be paid in SERS;
3.
Scan the remitter’s check or money order using the SERS check scanner;
4.
View the check or money order image to ensure it is legible and upright and if it
is not, rescan the check or money order; and
5.
Enter the amount of the check or money order in the Check Amount box.
e.
Place the SERS order.
f.
Print the SSA receipt.
g.
Mail the receipt to the remitter.
h.
Stamp non-negotiable on the front of the check or money order.
i.
Retain the check or money order in a locked, secured location for five days and then
shred it.
IMPORTANT:
Do not send checks or money orders processed through SERS to MATPSC. This will result
in
duplicate processing.
2. Walk-in check or money order transaction procedures
a.
Request the controlling SSN or BOAN of the account that the remitter wants to pay.
b.
Ensure that the remitter provided the correct SSN and that there is an active debt
on the MBR or SSR.
1.
If there is no active debt on the MBR or SSR, and the payment is for conserved funds,process
the remittance in the Conserved Funds store.
2.
In the Conserved Funds store, enter Number Holder's SSN for Disability or Retirement;
enter BOAN for Supplemental Security Income (SSI).
3.
Select Trust Fund for Conserved Funds Remittance. Proceed to "d" below.
c.
Input the controlling SSN into SERS and review the returned programmatic debt(s) per
MS
09301.001 through MS
09301.012.
d.
Request the check or money order from the remitter.
e.
Examine the check or money order to determine if it is acceptable. For more information
regarding examining remittances, see GN 02403.005.
1.
Verify the date and signature, and ensure that the check or money order is made payable
to SSA;
2.
Verify the amount on the check is equal to the amount to be paid in SERS; then
3.
Scan the remitter's check or money order using the SERS check scanner.
4.
View the check or money order image to ensure it is legible and upright. If it is
not, rescan the check or money order.
5.
Enter the check amount in the Check Amount box.
f.
Verbally inform the remitter, that the transaction is NON-REFUNDABLE and the completed
transaction occurs immediately.
g.
Place the order in SERS.
h.
Print the receipt.
i.
Give the remitter the receipt.
j.
Stamp non-negotiable on the front of the check or money order.
k.
Retain check or money order in a locked, secured location for five days and then shred
it.
IMPORTANT:
Do not send checks or money orders processed through SERS to MATPSC. This will result
in
duplicate processing.
