H. Procedure for annotating the title II and title XVI payment records
1. Title II special message field
Annotate the special message field of the MBR: “Limited Payability Death Case for
BIC __ mm/dd/yyyy paid via CPS” (Use the date of the check that the LP credit was
issued against and the payment system used to repay the check)
2. Title XVI remarks field
Annotate the Remarks field of the SSR: “Limited Payability Death Case mm/yyyy” (Use
the date of the check the LP credit represents.)
REMINDER: Avoid overlaying message fields with any subsequent actions.
