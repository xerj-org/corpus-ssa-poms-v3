B. Process for INSS to obtain medical examinations
To obtain medical examinations for INSS, use the following process:
1. INSS request for medical examinations
INSS requests medical examinations using the Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 (U.S. Brazil Agreement
on the Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification).
2. SSA Schedules an examination or forwards pertinent medical evidence
Pertinent medical evidence is medical evidence that SSA obtained in connection with
a continuing disability review or a disability claim filed or adjudicated within a
year of the date for which INSS has specifically requested medical evidence. SSA schedules
an examination, or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from SSA files to INSS. SSA’s
files include a paper claims folder and electronic files.
3. SSA requests reimbursement for cost of medical examinations
SSA requests reimbursements for the cost of medical examinations obtained for INSS
using form 1278 USA/BR 9 Request for Payments of Reimbursable Expenses under U.S.
Brazil Agreement on Social Security. Once the form is completed, scan it into electronic
