D. Claims filed with U.S. FSP in Italy
The following chart indicates how claims for U.S. benefits are processed when they
are filed with a U.S. FSP in Italy:
1. U.S. foreign service post
The Foreign Service post takes the U.S. national application and the SSA-3954-BK-IT.
A liaison form USA/IT 2 is forwarded to the appropriate INPS Regional Office, certifying
the filing date. The applicant is advised to contact the appropriate INPS provincial
office to complete the required Italian applications. The Foreign Service post forwards
the form SSA-3954-BK-IT and the U.S. national application to INTPSC, Baltimore.
2. INTPSC, Baltimore
INTPSC forwards the U.S. coverage certification with the liaison form USA/IT 2 to
the INPS Regional Office. On the USA/IT 2, INTPSC indicates whether or not Italian
coverage record is needed and, if so, specifies how much coverage is needed for insured
status.
3. INPS regional office
The regional office sends the Italian coverage certification on form IT/USA 3 bis
with the liaison form IT/USA 2 to INTPSC, Baltimore.
4. INTPSC, Baltimore
If an Italian coverage certification was requested from INPS, INTPSC sends form USA/IT
2 to advise the INPS regional office whether or not U.S. benefits were awarded.
