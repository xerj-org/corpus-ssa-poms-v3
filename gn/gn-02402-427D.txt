D. Overview of IDD for Gambia
IDD payments sent to Gambia must be in Gambian Dalasi (GMD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Gambia. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
