Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306166 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy — Submittal to RCC
Do not submit a case to the Office of General Counsel (OGC) if the dependency test
is not met. Submit the case per GN 01010.815 ff. in the absence of a precedent opinion if:
•
The child was adopted in a State in which he/she kept the right to inherit from the
natural parent; and
•
The natural parent died domiciled in a State where an adopted child did not retain
inheritance rights from a natural parent or vice versa.
NOTE: See SSR 63-28 for an example of this situation.
