A. Introduction to name and address of individuals outside the United States (U.S.)
We make errors on our records to names and addresses of individuals outside the U.S.
because they are not written clearly or consistently on applications or forms. When
establishing the name or address for individuals outside the U.S. based on a form,
application, or correspondence the Federal Benefit Unit, field office, or Division
of International Operations, handling the material prints the name and address in
all capital letters on the application as shown below.
