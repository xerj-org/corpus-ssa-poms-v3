D. Overview of IDD for Uganda
IDD payments sent to Uganda must be in Uganda Shillings (UGX) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Uganda. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
