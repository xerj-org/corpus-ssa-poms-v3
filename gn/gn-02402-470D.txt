D. Overview of IDD for Guinea
IDD payments sent to Guinea must be in Guinea Franc (GNF) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Guinea. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
