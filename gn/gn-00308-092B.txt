B. Procedure - State Sources
1. Address and FO Code
WA State Dept. of Health
Center for Health Statistics
P.O. Box 9709
Olympia, WA 98507-9709
FO Code 918
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from the Washington State Department of Health. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
$20.00
Add an additional $11.00 to the $20.00 cost for payment by credit card for phone requests.
3. Type of Payment:
Check or money order payable to Department of Health.
4. Remarks:
If a birth certificate issued by the State office does not contain the recordation
date, assume, absent information to the contrary, that the record was made soon after
birth. Document requests can take up to eight weeks by mail. Make follow-ups directly
with the Center for Health Statistics at (360) 236-4300.
Each 10 year search for death/marriage/divorce records costs $20.00.
