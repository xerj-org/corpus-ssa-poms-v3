SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.270 - Processing Cases Where Insured Status is Acquired Based on U.S. Coverage Only
Effective Dates: 06/02/2014 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703270 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Some NHs engage in work covered by Social Security after entitlement to U.S. totalization
benefits. These NHs may earn enough QCs to become fully insured without foreign coverage.
If this occurs, CAs must consider adjusting the entitlement record.
