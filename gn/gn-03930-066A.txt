A. Introduction
We are required to charge an assessment, also called a “user fee,” when directly paying
fees from past-due benefits. The Social Security Protection Act of 2004 (SSPA) amended
section 206(d)(2)(A) of the Social Security Act by:
•
Capping the assessment at a flat-rate dollar amount or 6.3% of the fee payment, whichever
is lower, effective September 1, 2004; and
•
Adjusting the flat-rate cap based on annual cost-of-living adjustments rounded down
to the next lower $1.
The current flat-rate assessment amount is $120.00, effective December 1, 2024.
Refer to GN 03920.019, Assessment on Representatives Who Receive Direct Payment, for assessment policy.
For information about the assignment of direct payment of fees to an entity, see GN 03920.021.
