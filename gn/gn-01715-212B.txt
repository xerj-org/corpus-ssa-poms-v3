B. Description of types of evidence
1. Legal Status
The following types of evidence may be used to establish that a claimant was legally
present in Canada.
•
proof of birth in Canada
•
Canadian citizenship card
•
Minister's permit
•
Canadian immigration documents
2. Periods Of Residence
If the applicant has not resided continuously in Canada, the following types of evidence
should be submitted to establish the beginning and ending dates of absences from Canada:
•
passports
•
visas
•
U.S. alien receipt card
•
airline, train, bus tickets, etc.
Note: All of above must show entry/departure dates.
