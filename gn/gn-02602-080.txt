GN 02602.080 Suspending Other Beneficiaries on the Payment Record in the PSC due to Reports of
Death
Procedure when an allegedly deceased beneficiary is payee for auxiliaries
The Program Service Center (PSC) should take the following actions when resolving
the death alert and the allegedly deceased beneficiary is the payee for an auxiliary
(or auxiliaries):
•
Notify the FO immediately to develop for a representative payee for the auxiliary
or to determine if we should consider direct payment of benefits to the auxiliary.
For determining whether we need to appoint a new payee, see GN 00504.100.
•
Do not substitute a minor child’s or incapable childhood disability beneficiary’s
surviving parent who lived in the same household as the beneficiary’s payee when the
current parent payee dies without first completing payee development. For determining
whether we should appoint a new payee, see GN 00504.100. The system suspends the child’s benefits until the FO takes and approves a new payee
application.
IMPORTANT: Current law and policy require a face-to-face interview and investigation before
selecting a new representative payee.
