B. Process for reclamations from foreign FIs
Reclamations of direct deposit payments sent through International Direct Deposit
(IDD) to accounts at FIs outside the country cannot be processed automatically or
through manual input of E-stops. These funds must be reclaimed through manual actions.
Information about the reclamation of payments made through IDD starts at Recovery
of Title II IDD Payments Improperly Issued After Death of Beneficiary GN
02408.900.
