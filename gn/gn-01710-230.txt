GN 01710.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Swiss Agency on Form SSA-e2960-U3-SZ-Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the Swiss agency.
•
Diary initial requests for 180 days.
•
Diary follow up requests for 90 days.
•
Forward the folder to DIO Analysts if no response has been received after two follow-ups
(i.e., after 1 year).
