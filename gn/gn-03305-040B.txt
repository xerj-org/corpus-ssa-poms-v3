B. Requests received from the Mexican Government
The Mexican Government requires evidence of a set income level before granting an
alien special immigrant status as a financially independent immigrant. The Ministry
of the Interior (Secretaria de Gobernación) will not accept evidence from U.S. Government
sources unless translated into Spanish with the authenticated signature of the issuing
Mexican consular official in the U.S. The Mexican Government will also accept, as
evidence of Social Security income, letters from the following:
1.
U.S. consular official in Mexico;
2.
Legally Engaged Staff or officer in the Federal Benefits Unit; and
3.
American Citizens Services in the U.S. Embassy.
