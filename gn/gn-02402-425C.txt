SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.425 - Coding Senegal Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 12/07/2018 - Present | TN 119 (01-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402425 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Senegal will have a prefix of “73” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “M” assigned for Senegal.
