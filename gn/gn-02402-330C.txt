Effective Dates: 01/29/2016 - Present | TN 260 (03-24)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in New Zealand will have a prefix of “71” at the beginning of the
Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN)
field has the letter “L” assigned for New Zealand.
