C. The role of ZUS under the Polish agreement
This office investigates claims for benefits from ZUS under the Agreement for claimants
residing in Poland, in the USA or in third country.
This office is responsible for PL/USA 4 (Retirement/Disability Application), PL/USA
5 (Survivor Benefit Application), PL/USA 7 (Request for Medical Evaluation), USA/PL
6 (ICERS), PL/USA 8 (Reimbursement for Medical Evaluation Expense) or PL/USA 10 (Burial/Funeral
Benefit Application).
