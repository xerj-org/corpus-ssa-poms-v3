B. PROCEDURE — APPEARANCE NECESSARY
If OGC determines that personal appearance is necessary, then:
•
Refer to the Financial Management Manual for instructions about the travel authority
to be used in claiming expenses in connection with court appearances; and
•
Send reimbursement checks for testimony given in court to:
Social Security Administration
Office of the Chief Financial Officer
Office of Financial Policy and Operations
6401 Security Boulevard
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-6401
