Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)

A. Introduction
Initial claim packages are prepared to request German coverage records and to inform
the German agencies about claims for German benefits. (See GN 01708.205 for the applications and evidence required for the packages.)
