A. Procedure – When to use Form SSA-e2960 (HU-USA 3)
The Hungarian liaison agency uses the Form SSA e2960 HU-USA 3 to:
•
transmit material to the Division of International Operations (DIO) or the Kraków,
Poland Federal Benefits Unit (FBU);
•
send information to DIO or the Kraków, Poland FBU, and
•
request information from DIO.
