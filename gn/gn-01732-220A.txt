Effective Dates: 06/24/1994 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
The SSA-2960-U3-FI is completed in the totalization modules in OIO and by the RFSP
in Stockholm. It is used to:
•
transmit claims and related material to the CPSI or the SII;
•
request information from the CPSI; and
•
respond to requests from the Finnish agencies.
NOTE: Blocks for verifying dates and certifying monthly benefits are completed only by OIO.
