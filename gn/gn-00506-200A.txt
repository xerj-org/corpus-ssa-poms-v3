A. Policy for organizations to collect fees
Organizations can collect the lesser of 10% of the combined Title II and Title XVI monthly payment, or the amounts listed
in the chart. The field office (FO) provides the Fee-for-Service (FFS) organizational
payee with a list of beneficiaries who meet the Drug Alcohol Addiction (DAA) condition
for a higher fee.
NOTE:
DAA condition means the beneficiary or recipient is entitled to disability benefits
and has a medically determinable substance abuse disorder listed as a secondary diagnosis.
DAA cannot be material to the disability determination.
