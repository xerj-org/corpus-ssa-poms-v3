C. Title XVI inquiry about Clark relief
We identified Title XVI Clark class members as explained in GN 02615.100A.2. We added the identified cases to CATS with a CCID entry of C3, C4, or C5, but we
did not add a CO MSG to the class member’s SSR. CATS is no longer available to technicians
based on changes made by the Office of General Counsel (OGC). Only Central Office
staff has access to the information on the Clark CATS records.
1. N8NN instructions
If you receive an inquiry, provide the following explanation to the inquirer:
“We need to investigate your case further. Please allow us 60 days to respond to your
inquiry. No further action is required from you at this time. If further action is
later required, we will contact you.”
Refer the case to the FO for investigation via:
•
Development Worksheet page in CCE for MSSICS cases. Enter the following on the Development
Worksheet page:
o   ADM as the ISSUE; and
o   CLARK INQUIRY in the REMARKS field.
In addition, on the Development Notes page in CCE, explain why the inquirer believes
they are a class member;
Or
•
MDW for non-MSSICS cases, enter the following on the MMDW screen:
o   CLARK in the ISSUE field;
o   ADM in the CATEGORY field; and
o   INQUIRY in the REMARK field.
In addition, on the MRMK screen explain why the inquirer believes they are a class
member.
2. FO instructions
If you receive a Clark inquiry or a N8NN referral with CLARK as the MDW ISSUE, or CLARK INQUIRY in the REMARKS
field on the Development Worksheet page, review the SSR to determine if we already
processed Clark relief. If we provided relief, inform the recipient that we already provided relief
and send them a copy of the reinstatement or award notice. If the SSR shows we did
not provide relief, handle the inquiry following instructions about reviewing FFSCF
in GN 02615.190B.2.b in this section. Send the inquirer the Title XVI Clark Court Case Notice of Inquiry located in the general folder in DPS.
