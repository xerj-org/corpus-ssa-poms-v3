SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01030.005 - Awards to Foreign Students Not Previously Entitled to Child's Benefits
Effective Dates: 01/24/2024 - Present | TN 5 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201030005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Adjudicating awards to foreign students not previously entitled to child's benefits
Claims Specialists (CS) in border field offices or in the Office of Earnings and International
Operations (OEIO) use Form SSA 1383-FC (Report of Social Security Administration by
Student Outside the United States) when processing awards to a foreign student child
beneficiary (SCB) or a child disability beneficiary (CDB).
