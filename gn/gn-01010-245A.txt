A. Policy on concurrent claims
Generally, applications under title II cover all classes of benefits for which the
claimant is eligible on all social security numbers (SSNs) regardless of the title
of the application unless the claimant specifically restricts the claim. Therefore,
an applicant can file an application for more than one type of benefit at the same
time (i.e. concurrent filing or claims) However, the claimant must meet the entitlement
requirements for each benefit. If the claimant meets the entitlement factors for one
type of benefits but not for another then dual entitlement does not apply.
