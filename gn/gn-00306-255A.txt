SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.255 - Adoption by NH's Surviving Spouse -Parent Not Living With and Contributing
Effective Dates: 12/14/1998 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306255 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The child does not qualify under this provision if his/her natural or adopting parent
or stepparent was making regular contributions to the support of the child and living in the NH's household. If a parent is doing only one of these, it will not
bar the child's entitlement. However, consider any contributions by a parent in determining
whether the child was receiving at least one-half of his/her support from the NH.
