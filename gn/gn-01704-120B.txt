SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.120 - Totalization Status Code (TSC)
Effective Dates: 07/30/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disallowance
DI - Used to indicate that a totalization claimant is not insured and that his U.S.
totalization claim has been denied because he has less than six U.S. QC's.
D2 - Used to indicate that a totalization claimant is not insured and that his U.S.
totalization claim has been denied because he has at least six U.S. QC's but is still
not insured considering foreign periods of coverage.
D3 - Used to indicate that a totalization claimant is denied because he is insured
without considering his foreign earnings. (This code is not applicable under the Italian
agreement.)
D4 - Used to indicate that a totalization claimant is insured but is denied for some
other reason; e.g., person is not retirement age or cannot prove relationship to the
NH.
