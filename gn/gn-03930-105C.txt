C. Procedure - concurrent Titles II and XVI
When evaluating the fee petition to determine a reasonable fee for representation
in concurrent titles II and XVI cases that involved a common substantive issue, consider:
•
the purposes of the programs, in GN 03930.105A;
•
the factors listed in GN 03930.105B.1. through GN 03930.105B.7.; and
•
whether the other criteria in GN 03930.010B are met. If those criteria are met and SSA is withholding for possible direct payment
of a representative's fee, decide whether any services were so unique to the supplemental
security income program that you must designate a portion of the fee amount as attributable
to title XVI exclusively.
