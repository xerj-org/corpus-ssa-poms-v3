SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.010 - Transfer of Overpayment Jurisdiction to DIO
Effective Dates: 05/21/2024 - Present | TN 57 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Operating procedure
1. Debtor alive
a.
If follow-up refund efforts are necessary and a payment center (PC) other than DIO
had jurisdiction for the initial overpayment notice, transfer debts of $30 or more
to DIO for special handling.
b.
DIO will:
•
Obtain full refund or installment payments, or
•
Make compromise decisions (GN 02215.100), or
•
Make a waiver determination if the debtor's address is outside the United States,
and
•
Return the folder to the servicing PC after the collection issue is resolved.
2. Debtor deceased
Transfer debts to DIO only if:
a.
The debt is $3,000.00 or more, and
b.
An estate is known to exist, or
c.
State Department assistance is needed to ascertain an estate's existence.
Discontinue collection efforts (GN 02215.235) for debts not referred to DIO under the instructions in GN 02215.010B.1. or GN 02215.010B.2. Be sure, however, that recovery from any contingent liable individual has been explored
(GN 02210.015).
