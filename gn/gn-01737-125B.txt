B. Policy -Crediting U.S. coverage
Greece will credit 75 days of Greek coverage for each quarter of U.S. coverage. For
example, where a person has 4 quarters of U.S. coverage in a year, Greece will credit
300 days of coverage for that year under the Greek system. (Under Greek law, 300 days
of coverage is equivalent to one year.) However, Greece will not give credit for any
days of coverage based on U.S. QC's if they are already days of coverage under Greek
law.
