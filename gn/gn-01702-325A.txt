SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.325 - Summary of Field Office Development and Processing of U.S. Totalization Claims and Foreign Claims (Regular and Totalization)
Effective Dates: 03/03/2025 - Present | TN 11 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702325 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Field office (FO) development of claims filed under Totalization agreements will vary
slightly depending upon whether the applicant is filing for foreign benefits only,
U.S. benefits only, or both. The following subsection provides a general summary of
FO development and routing procedures in nondisability claims. Instructions for developing
medical evidence and routing in disability claims is found in GN 01702.400 — GN 01702.415.
