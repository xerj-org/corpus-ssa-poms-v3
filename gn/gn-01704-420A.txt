A. U.S./Italian Agreement
Two application forms have been devised for claiming totalization benefits under the
U.S./Italian agreement. (See GN 01705.215 and GN
01705.220.)
1.
The Form SSA-3954-BK-IT (Application for Benefits Under the Italy/ United States International
Social Security Agreement)—This application is filed in the United States and claims-taking
FSPs.
2.
The IT/USA 1 (Italy/U.S.A. Agreement on Social Security—Application for Benefits)—This
application is filed with INPS.
In addition to the application IT/USA 1, INPS uses the following forms:
3.
IT/USA 2—Transmittal/request form used by INPS to transmit information and material
to OIO and to request information and material from OIO.
4.
IT/USA 3—A liaison form used by INPS to convey information to OIO and request earnings
information from INTPSC. This form is usually included with the IT USA 1.
5.
IT/USA 3 bis - (“bis” means attachment)—An Italian earnings certificate.
6.
IT/USA SS-3—Medical Report
7.
IT/USA 4—An Italian coverage certificate which relays information about the worker
and his or her employer.
8.
IT/USA 5—INPS' response to OIO's request for Italian earnings.
9.
IT/USA 6—Request for claimant's choice of benefit.
10.
IT/USA 7—Statement of interest to file for U.S. benefits in the future.
11.
IT/USA 8—Notice to claimant of INPS determination on a totalization claim.
12.
IT/USA 9—Transmittal/request form.
13.
IT/USA 10—Italian agency development request form.
14.
IT/USA 11—Request for Medical Examination.
