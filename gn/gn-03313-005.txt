GN 03313.005 Disclosure to the Department of Defense (DOD)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our records to other
Federal agencies and officials and a link to the corresponding systems of records
and their applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from DOD, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
