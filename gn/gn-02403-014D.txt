D. Processing fees paid by check or money order
1. FO Technician
Follow these instructions to process the non-programmatic standard fee payment.
a.
Begin the transaction in SERS,
b.
Ensure that the check or money order is legible,payable to SSA, for exact amount of
fee, dated and signed.
c.
Process the check or money order through the SERS check scanner:
d.
View the check or money order image on the screen to ensure it is legible and upright.
If it is not, rescan the check or money order.
e.
Complete the transaction in SERS.
f.
Print a receipt and provide it to the customer.
g.
Annotate the front of the check with Non-Negotiable" and retain in a locked, secured
location for five days then shred it.
