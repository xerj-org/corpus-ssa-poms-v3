B. CDN code descriptions of appeals to Treasury
The numerical code does not appear on the CDN but is a means of identification for
Treasury's use.
297
Appeal has been received for denied claim.
327
Upon review of all evidence presented, the previous decision to deny claim is upheld.
329
The previous decision to deny the claim is overturned. Settlement will be authorized
in a subsequent action.
330
Immediate settlement requested prior to adjudication of claim. This pertains to NY
residents only.
