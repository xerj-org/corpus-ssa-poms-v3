Effective Dates: 02/10/2024 - Present | TN 66 (08-23)

A. Procedure in Modernized Claims System (MCS) applications
In MCS claims, answer the Medicare question on the ADDB screen if the claimant is
64 years and 9 months old. This information will propagate to the HIHI screen.
