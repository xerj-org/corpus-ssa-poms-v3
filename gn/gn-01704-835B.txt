B. Processing claims leads (except under Belgian agreement)
If a claims lead exists:
1.
Obtain an MBR and a case control read to be sure that development is not already in
progress; and
2.
If the individual lives in a country with a claims-taking post, ask the FSP to take
claims lead action; or
3.
If the individual lives in the U.S. or Canada, send claims lead letter; or
4.
If the individual lives in a country without a claims-taking post, except Canada,
ask IBO to take claims lead actions; or
5.
If you are not sure which office to contact, request assistance from a CA or CATA.
