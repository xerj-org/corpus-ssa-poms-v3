A. Claimant change of address notification
We may be notified that the claimant is or will be moving during the development of
the initial claim by one of the following:
1.
SSA-612-EV (Report of New Information in Disability Cases), or
2.
SSA-1625-EV (Report by Person Entitled to Special Payments), or
3.
SSA-8150-EV (Reporting Events—SSI)
4.
Other appropriate signed statements.
