SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.500 - Totalization and HI/SMI Entitlement
Effective Dates: 04/27/2016 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703500 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - initial award
Claims Authorizer -Follow this procedure when awarding totalization benefits.
1. Claimant under age 65 or entitled to disability benefits for less than 23 months
If the claimant is under age 65 or entitled to disability benefits for less than 23
moths:
a.
prepare an SSA-101-U3 award.
b.
inform the claimant that he/she will not be eligible for free HI because foreign coverage
was used to establish insured status.
c.
inform the claimant that he/she will not be automatically enrolled in supplemental
medical insurance (SMI) when he/she attains age 65, but must file an application within
an enrollment period.
d.
refer to GN 01701.140 for alternate ways a claimant may establish HI entitlement.
e.
inform a disabled beneficiary that he/she cannot be entitled to premium HI because
a worker must have attained age 65 and be entitled to SMI to be entitled to premium
HI.
2. Claimant within 2 months of age 65 or older or entitled to disability benefits for
23 months or more
If the claimant is within 2 months of age 65 or older or entitled to disability benefits
for 23 months or more:
a.
Prepare an SSA-101-U3 award.
b.
Consider the following alternatives for HI entitlement:
•
premium HI (See HI 00801.131)
•
reduced premium HI. (See HI 01005.007.)
•
end stage renal disease (ESRD). (See HI 00801.186.)
•
Medicare qualified government employee (MQGE). (See HI 00801.405.)
•
qualified railroad beneficiary (QRRB). (See HI 00801.006.)
•
entitlement to free HI as a nontotalization beneficiary.
•
State “buy-in” for SMI. (See HI 00815.030.)
c.
Enroll the claimant for HI if he/she meets the criteria for any of the above alternatives.
d.
If not, inform the claimant that he/she is not eligible for free HI because foreign
coverage was used to establish insured status.
NOTE: Beneficiaries within 3 months of age 65 at the time of filing should have made their
SMI election at the time of filing.
e.
Enroll the claimant for SMI if he/she elected coverage and meets the criteria for
SMI entitlement. (See HI 00805.005 for these criteria.)
