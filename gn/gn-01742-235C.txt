C. Description of form KOR-USA 2
•
Part A contains identifying information about the worker and space to enter both the
U.S. social security number and Korean national pension number. If the NPC is verifying
the worker's date of birth, they will show that in this section.
•
Part B contains information about the applicant and will only be completed if the
applicant is not the worker.
•
Part C contains information provided by Korea, including the protective filing date
and the record of Korean coverage.
•
Part D contains a list of the types of information that the NPC might request from
OIO along with the remark that the information is needed to process a claim under
the agreement.
