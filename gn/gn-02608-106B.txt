SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.106 - Exemption Due to Pension Eligibility In November 1982 or June 1983
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Eligible for pension in June 1983
GPO does not apply to benefits payable for December 1984 or later if the spouse:
•
was eligible for the government pension in June 1983, however the benefit payment
was delayed for that month until the following month,
and
•
meets the 1983 one-half support requirement (both men and women).
