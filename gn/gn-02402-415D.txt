D. Overview of IDD for Honduras
IDD payments sent to Honduras must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Honduras. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
