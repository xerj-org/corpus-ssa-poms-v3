SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03990.030 - Action to be Taken if an Equal Access to Justice Act Application is Received in an FO or PC
Effective Dates: 04/21/2009 - Present | TN 1 (05-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203990030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. SSA Claim Decided Below the Hearing Level
If an EAJA application is received in an FO or PC for a claim adjudicated below the
hearing level, the FO or PC should immediately telephone the Regional Office. The
Regional Office should then contact the OGC Regional Office for guidance on the disposition
of the EAJA application.
