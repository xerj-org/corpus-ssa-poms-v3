GN 01763.210 Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Icelandic Benefits
Under the agreement with Iceland, the Social Security Administration (SSA) takes applications
for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of Iceland. SSA also provides
limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim (see GN 01763.125).
