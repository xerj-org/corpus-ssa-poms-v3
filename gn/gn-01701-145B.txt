SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.145 - Entitlement to Supplementary Medical Insurance (SMI) in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
When a Totalization beneficiary not entitled to premium-free HI wishes to file for
SMI, take and process through the modernized claims system (MCS) an uninsured Medicare
claim. As an alternative, obtain and process a CMS-18F (Application for Hospital Insurance),
CMS-4040 (Request for Enrollment in Supplementary Medical Insurance), or a statement
providing similar information.
