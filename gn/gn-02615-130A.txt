A. Appeals case processing
Beneficiaries who were suspended for probation or parole violation (PPV) outstanding
warrants (i.e., 5011, 5012, 8101 or 8102) prior to 10/24/2006, but who had a pending
appeal or an administrative appeal determination related to the PPV suspension issue
(or PPV suspension-related overpayment issue) on or after 10/24/2006, are included
in the Clark Court Order. For more information on the terms of the Clark Court order
regarding administrative appeals, see GN 02615.100.
1. Identifying Class Members
We sent the Clark administrative appeal cases along with all the other Clark manual
cases directly to the processing centers (PCs) for processing. We listed these Class
Members on the Civil Action Tracking System (CATS) with a CCID of C2 Clark Court Order
Manual cases. For information on Clark Court Order manual case processing, see GN 02615.120A.
2. Queries on PPV suspension or overpayment appeal issues

For information on the MBR data items, see GN 02615.120A.3.

c. Review the Case Processing and Management System (CPMS) or Appeals Review Processing
System (ARPS) Query
Obtain a CPMS query or an ARPS query via the Intranet. Review the beneficiary’s CPMS or ARPS
query to determine if the beneficiary requested an administrative appeal of a PPV
suspension or overpayment. If the administrative appeal information on the query does
not relate to the PPV suspension or overpayment, do not use this information. Evaluate
the next lower level of appeal associated with the PPV suspension or overpayment issue.
If the ALJ or AC administrative appeal request is associated with a Clark PPV suspension or overpayment issue, determine if the appeal is closed or pending
using the chart below:
Status of Appeals Decision
Action on Appeals Decision
