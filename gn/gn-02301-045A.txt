SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02301.045 - Handling Inquiries for Title II Underpayment Due Deceased Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 09/14/2017 - Present | TN 3 (09-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202301045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Inquiry when no underpayment is due
If the field office (FO), processing center (PC), or a National 800 Number Network
(N8NN) agent receives an inquiry regarding a possible underpaid deceased beneficiary,
review the beneficiary’s master benefit record (MBR) or payment history update system
(PHUS)) to determine if an underpayment is due. If the office determines that there
is no underpayment, explain why no underpayment is due.
