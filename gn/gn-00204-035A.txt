SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.035 - Deemed Filing
Effective Dates: 01/04/2017 - Present | TN 166 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Since 1956, the Social Security Act required claimants to file for both reduced retirement
insurance benefits (RIB) and spouses, divorced spouses, and independently entitled
divorced spouses (AUXSPO) benefits when eligibility for both benefits existed in the
claimants first month of entitlement to either benefit. We refer to this requirement
as deemed filing because when a claimant applies for one benefit, we deem them to
have filed for the other benefit.
On November 2, 2015, the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015 (BBA) Section 831(a) changed
the policy for deemed filing for claimants who have a date of birth (DOB) of January
2, 1954 or later.
