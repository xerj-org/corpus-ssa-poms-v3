A. First pillar coverage
Slovak social security covers almost all residents of the Slovak Republic. Covered
employees, their employers, and self-employed workers must make required contributions.
The Slovak Republic pays benefits based on the number of years of contributions, the
amount of lifetime earnings and an assessed amount designed to guarantee a constant
replacement rate.
