Effective Dates: 05/19/2017 - Present | TN 13 (09-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202205003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Recovery policy for an overpayment caused by a garnishment
The garnished beneficiary has primary liability for the entire overpayment even though
the garnisher may have received a portion of the overpaid amount. For more information
on other garnishment situations, see #4 in GN 02410.225.
EXAMPLE: John Brown is overpaid $300 for October 2010. Of that $300, we paid $150 to the court
for garnishment. John Brown is liable for the entire $300 overpayment.
If the garnished beneficiary is deceased, and we issue a garnishment payment for the
month of death or later, the payment is an incorrect payment (or unjust enrichment),
not an overpayment. We will request a refund from the garnisher. If the deceased garnished
beneficiary had a representative payee or surviving joint owner of a direct deposit
account, that person is not liable for repaying the portion of the overpayment that
we paid to the garnisher. For more information about garnishment payments paid after
the beneficiary dies, see #3a in GN 02410.225.
Supplemental Security Income (SSI) (title XVI) payments are not subject to garnishment.
