A. Automatic status notice for an application for Japanese benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about a Japanese claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of
the worker's U.S. coverage record) to Japan, a Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically
stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system in DIO
before forwarding the claim to the Japanese agency. The PPC system automatically generates
a notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs the applicant that
DIO forwarded the claims material to Japan.
