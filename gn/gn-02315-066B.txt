B. Policy for Nevada good acquittance
Upon receipt of an affidavit that contains the information described listed above,
SSA may rely on such information without inquiring as to the truthfulness of any matter
contained therein. Accordingly, SSA receives good acquittance when an underpayment
is paid in good faith to the person who has complied with the above requirements.
Nev. Rev. Stat. § 146.080(4)
