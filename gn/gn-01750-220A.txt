A. Process for the SSA-e2960–U3–CZ
The Division of International Operations, and the Warsaw, Poland Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) completes the SSA-e2960–U3-CZ via the Totalization Data Collection Program
(TDCP). Use the SSA-e2960–U3–CZ to:
•
Transmit claims and related material to Ceske Spravy Socialnlho Zabezpeceni (CSSZ).
•
Request information from CSSZ.
•
Respond to requests from CSSZ.
