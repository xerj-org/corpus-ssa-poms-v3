B. Flagging of Delayed Disability Cases
Use of Form SSA-441 to Identify Delayed Cases
Attach a Form SSA-441 to any initial disability claim still pending 70 days after
the application was filed or deferred medical development is begun. When a claim is
returned to the DO from a DDS or ODO, attach an SSA-441 to any unflagged case which
has been delayed more than 70 days. Affix the SSA-441 to the upper edge of the front
face of the folder opposite the SSN entry as shown in GN 01050.165 C.
NOTE: Your region may require that you flag cases earlier than 70 days.
