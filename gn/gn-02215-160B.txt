SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.160 - Estate is Involved - Overpayment
Effective Dates: 05/09/1996 - Present | TN 17 (02-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Secure as much detailed information as possible about the estate's assets, and any
unpaid administrative expenses or unsatisfied liens upon assets of the estate.
Do not report an overpayment claim, regardless of amount, to the ARC, P0S where a
deceased debtor left no estate.
