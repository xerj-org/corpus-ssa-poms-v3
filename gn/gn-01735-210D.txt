D. Policy — Entitlement factors
The following indicates what factors of entitlement, in addition to insured status,
must be established in claims for Irish benefits.
1. Retirement or Old-Age Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Irish retirement or old-age claims
are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Marriage, if a spouse's supplement is claimed
c.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's supplement is claimed.
2. Survivors Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Irish survivors claims are:
a.
Date of death of worker
b.
Marriage, if a widow(er)'s benefit is claimed
c.
Marriage and divorce, if a divorced widow(er)'s benefit is claimed
d.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's benefit is claimed.
3. Disability Claims
The entitlement factors that must be established for Irish disability claims are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Degree of disability (medical evidence).
