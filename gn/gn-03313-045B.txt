B. Disclosures to administer VA programs
VA administers entitlement, compensation, and pension programs that meet our criteria
for income and health maintenance programs. We may disclose certain tax return and
non-tax return information to VA for the administration of their income and health
maintenance programs. As a reminder, field offices may provide copies of medical documentation
to VA upon request for VA’s use in administration of its programs. For more information
concerning disclosure of tax return information to VA, see GN 03320.015C.2 Disclosure of Tax Return Information Without Consent.
