C. Procedure — benefit summary chart
As noted in GN 00204.025B.1.d., an application is only valid for classes of benefits or conditions of entitlement
that existed at the time the application was filed. Use the following summary list
to determine if entitlement for a particular class of benefits could have existed
at the time of the application. If the application date is later than the date shown
in the “Effective” column, entitlement could have existed. Conversely, if the application
date is earlier than the date shown in the “Effective” column, entitlement could not
have existed so there would not be an open application for that class of benefits.
