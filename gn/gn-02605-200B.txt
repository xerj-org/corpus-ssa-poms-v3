B. Policy for change of address from domestic to foreign
Change a domestic address to a foreign address when the beneficiary:
•
Tells us they are leaving the United States (U.S.) for more than 3 months; or
•
Has already been outside the United States for more than 3 months.
We use the 3-month timeframe to identify individuals who may become subject to suspension
under the Alien Nonpayment Provisions (ANP) if outside the United States for more
than 6 months. For more information about the ANP, see RS 02610.001.
For processing instructions when a beneficiary returns to the United States, see GN 02605.260.
