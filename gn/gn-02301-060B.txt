B. Locating the proper recipient if the underpayment is under $50
If the underpayment is less than $50, pay the underpayment if the file contains sufficient
information to determine who is entitled to receive it. Do not develop to locate the
proper recipient, however. Post any unissued underpayment to the Special Payment Amount
(SPA) field on the master beneficiary record (MBR).
NOTE: If the underpayment is under $1, do not pay the underpayment unless specifically requested.
