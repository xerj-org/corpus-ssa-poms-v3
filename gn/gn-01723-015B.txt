B. Policy - Compulsory contributions
Covered employees and their employers are subject to compulsory contributions which
are based on a percentage of their earnings. Self-employed persons contribute at either
a fixed rate or rates that vary with earnings depending on the type of work. Contributions
for retirement insurance are subject to an earnings ceiling. Contributions for survivors
and disability insurance are assessed on total salary.
