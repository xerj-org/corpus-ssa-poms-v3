B. Introduction
The misinformation provision allows SSA to rectify situations where misinformation
deterred a claimant or proper applicant from filing for benefits. Policies have been
developed which:
•
Define misinformation;
•
Provide that the issue of misinformation may be raised at any time;
•
Include related aspects of entitlement within the scope of the provisions, such as
the amount of benefits; and
•
Resolve the issue in favor of the claimant, unless there is reasonable doubt or contradictory
evidence exists.
