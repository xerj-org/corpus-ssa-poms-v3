Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070224 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure for open application when prior folder destroyed
1. Open application — FO/PC action
•
Adjudicate the current claims. Do not initiate development to rebut the above assumptions
unless the claimant submits evidence to the contrary (e.g., the actual notice sent
on the prior claim containing close-out language).
•
Document the RPOC screen with the rationale.
2. Information needed does not involve a factor of entitlement
a. FO action
•
Redevelop the issue in question; and
•
Partially adjudicate the claim, if appropriate, and prepare a 60 day diary for PC
follow-up (see GN 01010.110).
b. PC action
When the information is received in the PC:
•
Prepare an amended award; and
•
Cancel the diary, if necessary.
3. Information needed involves a factor of entitlement
a. FO action
•
Redevelop the issue in question; and
•
Delay payment to the affected claimant, if appropriate, and diary for 60 days for
PC follow-up (see GN 01010.140 on processing delayed claims).
b. PC action
When the information is received in the PC:
•
Adjudicate the delayed claim accordingly; and
•
Cancel the diary, if necessary.
