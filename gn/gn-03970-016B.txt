B. Procedure for suspected criminal violation referrals
Follow the instructions below to refer potential criminal violations.
1.
Complete the ARIS referral.
2.
Email a copy of the ARIS referral to the appropriate regional Center for Program Support (CPS) using the usual
CPS email address, and to Law & Policy via email at ^LP Representative
Conduct with a subject line IMMEDIATE ACTION NECESSARY. REPRESENTATIVE SANCTION REFERRAL.
3.
Although electronic referrals are preferred, you may use a physical address when an
electronic referral is not possible due to a prolonged systems outage or for other
reasons. You can find Law & Policy's physical address in GN 03970.070B.
