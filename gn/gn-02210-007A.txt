SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.007 - Benefit Adjustment from Someone Other than the Overpaid individual
Effective Dates: 06/25/2024 - Present | TN 87 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to consider adjustment
When our efforts to recover an overpayment from a primarily liable individual (i.e.
original overpaid individual) are unsuccessful, propose adjustment of benefits payable
to any other individual currently receiving benefits on the same earnings record as the primarily liable individual.
We call this contingent liability (see GN 02205.005). For more information regarding a waiver and a reconsideration (appeal) request,
see GN 02250.001 and GN 02201.025.
