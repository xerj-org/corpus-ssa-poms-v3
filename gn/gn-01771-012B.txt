B. FBU processing referrals from the Slovenian liaison agency
This procedure explains how the Rome FBU processes referrals from the Slovenian liaison
agency Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia.
1. Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits
Upload all material in Evidence Portal annotating “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Follow the instructions in GN 01771.010B to process the claim.
