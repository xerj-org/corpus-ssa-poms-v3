SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.365 - Coding Indonesia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/22/2026 - Present | TN 325 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402365 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for Indonesia has a prefix of 68 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter A assigned for Indonesia.
