SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02406.700 - General Information on Mass Loss of Checks and the Payment Delivery Alert System (PDAS)
Effective Dates: 06/05/2026 - Present | TN 136 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406700 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Description of mass loss
The term mass loss describes situations when all checks in a specific geographic location
or zip code are undeliverable because they are permanently lost, stolen or otherwise
unavailable for delivery. A mass loss of checks can occur in the following situations:
•
Mail is destroyed or damaged (e.g., postal truck fire);
•
Mail is stolen from a distribution box or mail truck; or
•
Mail never arrived at a postal station and the United States Postal Service (USPS),
the Department of the Treasury (Treasury), and SSA cannot locate the misplaced mail.
NOTE: Mass loss instructions do not apply to a situation when a Financial Institution (FI) does not receive an expected
direct deposit payment file. If an FI reports non-receipt of a direct deposit payment
file, see GN 02406.007D.
IMPORTANT: Similar to non-receipt situations, a beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee
may cash the original check and the replacement check resulting from the mass loss
declaration. This is a Double Check Negotiation (DCN) and must be resolved through
the same manner as other DCNs (For more information refer to GN 02406.300).
Mass loss instructions do not apply to checks the USPS holds due to natural disasters. See GN 02406.705B and GN 00410.020C.
