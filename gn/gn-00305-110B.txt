B. Procedure — death in the uniformed services — marital relationship
Obtain a casualty report from the Armed Forces branch. Unless the military casualty
report clearly indicates to the contrary, assume an in-service death occurred in line
of duty. If the report clearly indicates that this requirement is not met, consider
whether the requirements pertaining to accidental death can be met; if necessary,
get a certified copy of the death certificate if death occurred in the U.S.
