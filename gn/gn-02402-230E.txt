SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.230 - Death of an International Direct Deposit (IDD) Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 06/07/2022 - Present | TN 277 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402230 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. FBU receives report of death of an IDD beneficiary
The FBU must take the following actions when they learn of the death of an IDD beneficiary:
1.
Ask for the date of death.
2.
Process the death termination through DIPS.
3.
Intercept any payment(s) that might go out after death. This will prevent any further
payments from reaching the FI.
4.
Code the death on ITS.gov.
5.
Does the routing number through which the deceased individual's payments were being
sent begin with 51, 61, 62, 70, or 71?
•
If yes, code the death on ITS.gov.
•
If no, go to step 5.
6.
Is your FBU in the country where the FI is located?
•
If yes, code the death to ITS.gov, and also send a reclamation request to the FI for
any payments already paid after death and not returned.
•
If no, notify the appropriate FBU of the death.
•
Is this a joint bank account or rep-payee involved? - if yes stop
•
If no, initiate reclamation in ITS for countries listed in GN 02408.920F
7.
Follow GN 02408.900 to GN 02408.950 to reclaim any incorrect payments that went out after death.
