B. Process for requests for policy interpretation
The ARC and MOS staff has access to other applicable policy components within the
Central Office, which provides support when needed. Do not call the Office of General
Counsel (OGC), the Office of Operations Support (OPSOS) or any other policy component
directly unless other established instructions ask to do so. If the issue cannot be
resolved, follow the applicable instructions for initiating a precedent in GN 01010.810 thru GN 01010.820.
