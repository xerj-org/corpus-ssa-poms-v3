SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01746.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Japan
Effective Dates: 05/10/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201746205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - U.S. and Japanese or Japanese Claim Only
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-e2960-J to certify the filing date for Japanese benefits, established factors
of entitlement, and U.S. benefit information
•
The special Japanese application form (either J/USA 1 or J/USA 2)
•
A U.S. totalization earnings record
•
Medical evidence from SSA's files, if the Japanese claim is for disability benefits.
NOTE: SSA files include claims folders in DIO, in the caves in Kansas City.
•
Evidence submitted for the Japanese claim, and
•
A request for the Japanese coverage record, including form J/USA 4, if one is needed
