SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.120 - Hearing Filed Before Receipt of Reconsideration Determination
Effective Dates: 01/28/2021 - Present | TN 42 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — PC Action
PC action:
•
When PC is advised a hearing has been filed, the PC immediately prepares the folder for transmittal to the servicing FO per GN
03103.080K
.
•
If PC receives a letter directly from claimant requesting a hearing, contact claimant
and explain the points in B. above. PC prepares an RC and, if necessary, completes
either an SSA-561-U2 (see GN 03102.225) or HA-501-U5 (see GN 03103.020F.).
•
Do not take any final related action pending the ALJ action on the hearing request.
Upon receipt of notice of the ALJ action, the PC may complete any pending action and/or
consider any additional evidence or information submitted.
EXCEPTION: In a dual determination situation, where SSA makes a reconsideration determination
and an initial determination on the same case, the ALJ will not dismiss the case merely
because the initial determination has not been reconsidered, since a hearing is proper
with respect to the reconsideration determination. Therefore, the entire claims file
is sent to the ALJ. (See GN 03101.001 and GN 03103.140 concerning multiple levels of appeal on one case and GN 03103.220 on dismissal.)
