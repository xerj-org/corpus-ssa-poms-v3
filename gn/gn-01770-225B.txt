Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 2 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201770225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Slovenian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Slovenian benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovenian agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP)
application. See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access
the EP User Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Slovenia
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Slovenia, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Slovenia
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Slovenian agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Slovenian agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Zavod za pokojninsko in invalidsko
zavarovanje Sloveniji (Institute for Pension and Disability Insurance Slovenia)
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Slovenian claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Slovenia. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
