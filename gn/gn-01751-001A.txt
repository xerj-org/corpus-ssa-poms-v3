A. Introduction — Social Security agreement with Poland
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Poland:
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Polish benefits,
even if they do not meet the normal insured-status requirements.
