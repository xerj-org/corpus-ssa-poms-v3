C. Alternative appeals process
1. Expedited appeals process (EAP)
The claimant or appointed representative may request an EAP, but only after appealing
through the reconsideration step. The claimant or representative can use the EAP in
cases where they do not dispute SSA's version of the facts in the claim. Rather, they
may challenge the constitutionality of the law, underlying the determination. See
GN 03107.000, Expedited Appeals Process.
NOTE: SSA and all parties to the determination must agree to use EAP.
2. Federal court review
The AC review completes the administrative review process. If the claimant or appointed
representative is still dissatisfied, they may request a judicial review by filing
a civil action in a federal district court.
