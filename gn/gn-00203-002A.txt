SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00203.002 - Discussing Title II and Title XVI Filing Options
Effective Dates: 01/21/2011 - Present | TN 88 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200203002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
If someone contacts SSA to apply for a specific benefit, they may not be aware of
other benefits they could get based on their age or relationships. Technicians must
inform them about all benefits they may qualify for, and explain program policies
so the individual can make an informed decision about applying.
NOTE:
For information on interviewing Medicare claimants, see GN 00203.025.
