B. Dependency for child under State intestacy law
When we establish a child’s relationship to the NH under State intestacy law, we deem
the child dependent on the NH unless someone else adopts the child during the NH’s
lifetime (see GN 00306.165). For more information on dependency requirements, see the chart in GN 00306.002F.
