B. Medical evidence from Germany, Switzerland, Norway and Belgium
If a disability claimant is uninsured for SSA disability benefits, but has at least
six U.S. QCs and the claimant has already filed a disability claim with a foreign
agency, request copies of any medical evidence in the records of the foreign agency
at the same time earnings information is requested.
