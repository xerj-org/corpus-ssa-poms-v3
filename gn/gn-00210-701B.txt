SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00210.701 - Same-Sex Marriage - Premium Surcharge Rollback
Effective Dates: 02/07/2025 - Present | TN 63 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200210701 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Same-sex marriages are treated the same as opposite-sex marriages for purposes of
the premium surcharge rollback. The sex of the married individuals, domicile and date
of marriage are not factors in determining eligibility for the premium surcharge rollback.
For instructions regarding premium surcharge rollback, see HI 00805.280 for SMI and HI 00805.285 for Premium-HI.
NOTE: Individuals age 65 and over who are, at the time of the premium surcharge rollback
request, in non-marital legal same-sex relationships, such as civil unions and domestic
partnerships are not considered spouses and do not meet eligibility requirements for
the premium surcharge rollback.
