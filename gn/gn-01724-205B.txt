SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01724.205 - Preparing Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside France
Effective Dates: 03/06/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201724205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Include the following information in claims packages:
1. U.S. Claim
•
SSA-e2960-U3-FR (check item VI—coverage record to request a French coverage record).
•
A work history statement if the worker is not a French beneficiary.
2. U.S. and French Claim
Include the following information in claims packages:
•
SSA-e2960-U3-FR (check item VI—coverage record to request a French coverage record).
•
U.S. Earnings Record.
•
Parts I and II of the Disability Annex (USA/FR 3a) if the French claim is for disability
benefits.
•
Medical evidence from the DIO file if the French claim is for disability benefits.
•
Medical Report Form (SE 404-4) if there is no pertinent evidence in DIO file to be
included in the package.
•
A work history statement if the worker is not a French beneficiary.
3. French Claim
Include the following information in claims packages:
•
SSA-e2960-U3-FR.
•
U.S. Earnings Record.
•
Parts I and II of the Disability Annex (USA/FR 3a) if the French claim is for disability
benefits.
•
Medical evidence from the DIO file if the French claim is for disability benefits.
•
Medical Report From (SE 404-4) if there is no pertinent medical evidence in DIO files
to be included in the package.
