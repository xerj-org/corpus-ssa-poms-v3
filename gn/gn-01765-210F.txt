F. Developing medical evidence for disability claims
In addition to taking the actions in GN
01719.210E. , take the following actions when developing medical evidence for Romanian disability
claims.
1. Romanian benefits only
•
Accept any medical evidence the applicant wishes to submit. Do not attempt to secure
evidence for the claimant or offer advice as to what types of evidence are needed
by the Romanian.
•
Send all medical evidence along with the claims file to: Central Processing – International
Operations (CP/IO), International Benefit & Processing (IBP) at the following address:
Social Security Administration
CP/IO - IBP
P.O Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
See GN 01702.310 for more information on routing claims for U.S. Totalization benefits.
•
Complete the Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) screen to show the date the field
office sent the Romanian claim to International Benefit & Processing (IBP). Show "Totalization"
as the ISSUE.
•
Transmit the file without medical evidence if it is not readily available; and
•
Advise the claimant that the Romanian agency will contact him or her, if necessary,
to obtain required evidence
2. Romanian benefits and concurrent Title II claim
Follow the instructions in GN
01702.400 - GN 01702.415 to process claims for Romanian disability benefits and concurrent Title II disability
claims.
