C. Procedure for established DB indicators
Refer to the entries shown in the chart to determine whether a claimant's DB has previously
been established for SSA purposes. Follow this chart for needed development relating
to these entries. For the proof code to show on the current claim, see GN 00302.011.
NOTE: The general requirement that the SSA record be established at least 5 years prior
to the current filing date does not apply to the records shown in the chart.
