SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.080 - Forwarding the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and Folder to the Servicing Hearing Office
Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Procedure for forwarding the HA-501-U5 in foreign claim and non-claim earnings discrepancy
cases
Do not request the folder for foreign claim and non-claim earnings discrepancy cases.
In block 17 of the HA-501-U5 check “Other Attached,” and add a remark: “folder is
not attached because the case involves a foreign claim or non-claim earnings discrepancy.”
Mail the HO copy of the HA-501-U5 along with any additional evidence to the servicing
HO within five business days.
1.
Foreign claims
In foreign claim situations, mail a copy of the HA-501-U5 to:
Foreign Claims
Social Security Administration
Office of International Operations
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, MD 21235
2.
Non-claim earnings discrepancy cases
For non-claim earnings discrepancy cases, mail a copy of the HA-501-U5 to:
Social Security Administration
Office of Earnings and International Operations
6100 Wabash Ave
Baltimore, MD 21215
