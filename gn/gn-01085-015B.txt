SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01085.015 - Nonessential Material Omitted From Title II and Title XVI Claims
Effective Dates: 06/16/2026 - Present | TN 4 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201085015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
To reduce the accumulation of nonessential paper, all claims processing personnel
will:
•
Use available electronic remarks or report of contact screens (e.g., RMKS and RPOC
in MCS, and "Report of Contact page in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE)) to
record statements and other information instead of preparing separate forms (see GN 01085.010A.).
•
Use claims transmittal forms for alerts and special instructions to the PC, whenever
possible, instead of preparing separate report of contact (RC).
•
Avoid recording routine development notes on RCs when an electronic screen is available.
•
Evaluate and remove all extraneous material as part of the current action.
