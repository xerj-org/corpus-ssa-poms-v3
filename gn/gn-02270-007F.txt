F. When the individual requests to conduct the personal conference on the same day as
the file review
We must schedule the personal conference at least 5 workdays after the date of the
file review. However, we can hold the personal conference on the same day if the individual
requests to conduct the personal conference on the same day as the file review. You
must document their request on the DMS RMKS screen for Title II cases and on the Report
of Contact for Title XVI cases.
