D. Appeal rights
Any claimant affected by the withdrawal of the NH’s claim will receive a notice with
appeal rights notifying them of the benefit changes. Appeal rights apply only to those
claimants whose benefits are nullified or reduced by WD request. The withdrawing claimant
cannot appeal the notice of approval for the WD request.
NOTE:
The auxiliary (or proper applicant for the auxiliary) may withdraw consent anytime
within the 60-day appeal period.
