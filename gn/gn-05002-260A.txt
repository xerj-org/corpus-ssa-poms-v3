A. Policy on appeals
The benefit statement is not a determination made under the Social Security Act. Therefore,
the benefit statement is not subject to appeal.
A request for an “amended” or “corrected” benefit statement or a question concerning
the accuracy of the benefit statement does not constitute an administrative appeal.
Do not use the SSA-561-U2, Request for Reconsideration, to request correction of the
statement.
