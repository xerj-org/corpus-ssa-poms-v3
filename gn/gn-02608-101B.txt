Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608101 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applying GPO for prior entitlement
1. Entitlement is continuous
Do not apply GPO under the following conditions:
•
We based the spouse's prior entitlement upon an application the claimant filed before
December 1, 1977 and
•
We do not require a new application for current entitlement (i.e., conversion cases).
2. Prior entitlement terminated
Apply GPO under the following conditions:
•
We based a spouse's prior entitlement upon an application the claimant filed before
December 1, 1977, entitlement terminated and
•
We require a new application (filed after November 30, 1977) for current entitlement
on the same or different social security number.
