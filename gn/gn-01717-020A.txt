SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01717.020 - Swedish Social Security Benefits – Eligibility Requirements
Effective Dates: 02/24/2003 - Present | TN 3 (02-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201717020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Sweden has a new pension system that covers people born in 1938 or later. People born
in 1937 or earlier will continue to receive their benefits only under the old system.
People born between 1938 and 1953 will receive part of their benefits under the old
system and part under the new system. People born in 1954 or later will receive benefits
exclusively under the new system.
1. Old Swedish System
Benefits under the old Swedish system consist of two separate components:
a. Basic Pension
The basic pension is a flat-rate benefit payable to Swedish residents when they reach
retirement age or become disabled and to certain survivors of Swedish residents. It
is also payable to Swedish nationals outside Sweden under certain conditions.
b. Supplementary “ATP” Pension
The supplementary, or “ATP,” pension is a benefit based on the level of earnings and
the length of covered work without regard to residence or nationality.
2. New Swedish System
Sweden’s new pension system replaces the basic and ATP pensions with a pension that
includes a component related to a person’s prior earned and unearned income and another
component based on individual investment accounts. The Totalization agreement with
Sweden currently refers only to the basic and ATP pensions under the old system, although
it is expected that the agreement will be renegotiated to take account of the new
system.
