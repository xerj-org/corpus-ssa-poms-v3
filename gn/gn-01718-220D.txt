SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01718.220 - Completing the U.S.A./Sweden Transmittal/Request/Certification Form (SSA-2960-U3-SW)
Effective Dates: 09/05/2013 - Present | TN 2 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201718220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
1. General
To complete the electronic Form SSA-2960-U3-SW, access the electronic form via the
TDCP and complete the following information.
2. Part I —Information About the Claim
a.
ALWAYS complete A., B. and C.
•
If you don't know the SSN, enter “UNKNOWN” in B.,
•
If you don't know the PIN, enter “UNKNOWN” in C. and complete Part II A.1 and A.2.
b.
Complete D., E. and F. to inform the SLAFU about a claim for Swedish benefits.
3. Part II —Certification of Data
a.
Complete the appropriate section(s) to:
•
inform the SLAFU about claimants for Swedish benefits.
•
respond to requests for information from the SLAFU, and
•
inform the SLAFU about U.S. social security benefits sent to Swedish residents.
b.
Mark “X” in the verified block(s) if the data has been used by SSA to adjudicate claims.
4. Part III —Transmittal of Information by U.S.
Mark “X” in the appropriate block(s).
5. Part IV —Request for Information from Sweden
Mark “X” in the appropriate block(s).
6. Part V —Remarks
Enter clarifying or additional information. Be concise and do not use jargon.
