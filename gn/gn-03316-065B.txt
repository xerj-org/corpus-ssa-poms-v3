B. Disclosure to addiction treatment facilities
Our regulations (20
C.F.R. § 416.936(a)) provide that in order to receive Supplemental Security Income (SSI), individuals
with drug addiction or alcoholism as a material factor in the determination of disability
must avail themselves of appropriate treatment for drug addiction or alcoholism at
a facility approved by SSA. In these cases, we may disclose information to approved
facilities when the information is necessary to determine the best course of treatment
for the individual.
See GN 03301.040B for limitations on the disclosure of drug abuse and alcoholism records.
