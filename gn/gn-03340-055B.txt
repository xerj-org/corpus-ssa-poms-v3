B. Policy for when a NH does not have the right of access
An NH never has a right of access to records pertaining to other individuals that
are not retrieved under the NH’s own PII. The only exception is when the NH is the
parent or legal guardian and acting on behalf of a minor or legally incompetent person
to request access to those persons’ records.
