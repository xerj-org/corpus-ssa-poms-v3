D. Overview of IDD for Morocco
IDD payments sent to Morocco must be in Moroccan Dirham (MAD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Morocco. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
