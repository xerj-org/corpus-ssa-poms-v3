B. Policy on who was eligible for the stimulus payment
Individuals (or couples) who filed a 2007 or 2008 tax return were eligible for the
stimulus payment of up to $600 per taxpayer ($1200 for a joint tax return) and $300
for each dependent minor child in the household.
