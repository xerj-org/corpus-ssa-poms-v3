B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, or death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Family Registry Office for birth, marriage, and death records.
Embassy of Turkey
Consular Section
2525 Massachusetts Ave, NW
Washington, D.C. 20008
or
Turkish Consular office having jurisdiction over the claimant's residence.
Parents can also go to the Directorate of Civil Affairs in the area in which they
live for birth records. Contact the district court where the divorce was granted.
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
The Family Register
•
Full name at birth, including the correct spelling of their Turkish name
•
Date of birth
•
Place of birth
•
Parent's Turkish name
•
Original registration place by
–
Province
–
County (District)
–
Township (Sub district)
–
Village
–
Street (very important in large cities, e.g., Istanbul)
–
House number (also very important)
–
The volume and page number of the original nufus entry
The original registration and volume and page number of the original nufus entry is
available on the Turkish identity card issued to each Turkish citizen. If the individual
needing the birth record does not have this information, ask them whether they have
any nufus (census) papers, Turkish identification card or other papers issued to them or their
parents. Examine these for the identifying information noted above. If the information
is not completely available, assume no record is available and develop for other evidence.
3. Fee
None.
