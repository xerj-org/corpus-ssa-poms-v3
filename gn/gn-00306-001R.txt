SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.001 - Definitions of Terms Used in Determining Parent-Child Relationship for Title II Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/24/2014 - Present | TN 43 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

R. State
For purposes of parent-child relationship determinations, "State" includes the 50
States, the District of Columbia, the U.S. Virgin Islands (beginning 9/1/1950), the
Commonwealth of Puerto Rico (beginning 1/1/1951), Guam (beginning 1/1/1961), American
Samoa (beginning 1/1/1961), and the Northern Mariana Islands (beginning 1/9/1978).
