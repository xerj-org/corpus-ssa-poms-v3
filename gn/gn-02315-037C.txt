C. Policy for Alaska good acquittance
Any person paying debts under the above affidavit procedure is discharged to the same
extent as if dealing with the personal representative. The Social Security Administration
(SSA) is not required to inquire into the truth of any statement in the affidavit
or seek evidence of how the successor applied the money received. Alaska Stat. § 13.16.685.
