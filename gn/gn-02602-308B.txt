B. PSC RETAP development alert processing
Take the following actions to process the RETAP Alert if we have not reinstated the
young surviving spouse:
1.
Develop to determine if there could have been an earlier date of marriage in order
to determine the correct overpayment.
2.
Attempt DIRCON and ask the young surviving spouse when they married and whether or
not they have a child-in-care.
3.
Input any correction to the date of marriage on the MBR via the Postentitlement Online
System (POS).
4.
Send a Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) or a Processing Center Action Control
System (PCACS) Request for Assistance to request to the field office (FO) to contact
the young surviving spouse for the actual date of marriage as a last resort when DIRCON
is unsuccessful.
5.
Reinstate benefits through the Manual Adjustment, Credit, and Award Data Entry (MACADE)
system if the young surviving spouse reports there has been no marriage.
