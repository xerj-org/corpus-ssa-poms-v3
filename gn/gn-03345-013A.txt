SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03345.013 - Making the Requested Correction or Amendment - Procedure
Effective Dates: 09/19/1991 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203345013 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Advise by Mail
When a decision is reached, advise the individual in writing of any correction or
amendment made in response to his or her request. See GN 03345.015 if all or any part of change requested cannot be made.
NOTE: If a correction or amendment to a record is made which has a bearing on a disability
determination or benefit decision, it may be necessary to consider review of the determination
or decision in regard to the correction or amendment made.
