B. Procedure – Birth Certificates
1. Office to Contact
Local tax office of the tax authority (Skatteverket) where the individual is residing or last resided.
NOTE: In response to a request for a birth, marriage or death certificate, the tax authority
will issue a Personbevis that is normally neither stamped nor signed. Therefore, requests should specifically
ask that the document be stamped.
2. Fee
No fee
