SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.354 - Assisting Claimants in the Philippines
Effective Dates: 01/16/1998 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905354 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
In developing claims from the Philippines:
•
Be certain the claimant understands what information and/or evidence is needed and
where and how to obtain it.
•
Document the file to show what information and/or evidence was requested and an explanation
of any missing material.
•
Be certain the claimant understands that his claim cannot be held open indefinitely.
Failure to submit the information and/or evidence within the specified time could
result in an unfavorable determination on the claim.
•
Use established diary periods unless the claimant justifies the need for additional
time (e.g., a birth certificate has been requested from the U.S. and no reply yet
received).
•
Document the file by RC or statement from the claimant to show the cause for the delay
and the expected completion date.
