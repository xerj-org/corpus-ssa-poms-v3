E. Process for handling requests for tax return information
If the request is . . .
Then . . .
1.
An access request or a request with written consent for summary earnings
information (e.g. SEQY or ICERS) when the person states that they have an urgent need for disclosure
of the information …
Advise the individual that they may obtain the information by visiting an FO. The
FO should provide the requested information via a SEQY, or any other online query
to which the FO has access once the FO has explained the entries on the query in writing
or in a face-to-face interview.
Accept the person’s allegation that they have an urgent need for the information.
There is no fee for summary earnings when the information is provided to the wage
earner, their legal representative or survivor, or the legal representative of the
wage earner’s estate as required under section 205(c)(2)(A) of the Social Security
Act.
See the instructions about fee charging for non-program purposes information in GN 03311.005E.
For the restrictions that may apply to releasing tax return information over the telephone
see GN 03320.005A.4. and GN 03360.005A.2.
2.
An access request or a request with written consent for detail earnings information …
Advise the requester to complete and submit Form SSA-7050-F4 (Request for Social Security
Earnings Information) or other written request to the following address:
Social Security Administration
Division of
Earnings and Business Services
PO Box 33011
Baltimore, MD 21290-3003
The SSA-7050-F4 is available from our Internet website at www.socialsecurity.gov and in RM 01498.027.
Apply the fee schedule on the SSA-7050-F4 to requests for detailed earnings.
If a person is inquiring about the online Social Security Statement, advise the following:
“In light of the current budget situation, we have suspended the Request a Social
Security Statement service. You may be able to estimate your retirement benefit using
our online Retirement Estimator at www.socialsecurity.gov/estimator .”
3.
An access request for detailed earnings is received in an FO and the person states
that they have an urgent need for disclosure of the information and cannot wait to
receive the information via form SSA-7050-F4 . . .
Advise the person that they may obtain the information by visiting an FO. The FO should
provide the requested information via a DEQY, or any other online query to which the
FO has access if the FO has explained the entries on the query in writing or a face-to-face
or telephone interview.
Accept the person’s allegation that they have an urgent need for the information.
In cases where a DEQY is furnished for a non-program purpose, the fee schedule found
on the SSA-7050-F4 should be followed (see GN 03320.001E.2. For instructions about fee remittance, see GN 02403.012, MSOM DMS 003.006, MSOM DMS 003.007, and MSOM DMS 004.009.
When providing a DEQY in lieu of processing an SSA-7050-F4, shred the SSA-7050-F4.
Do not forward or retain the SSA-7050-F4 when a DEQY was instead provided to the individual.
For the restrictions that may apply to releasing tax return information over the telephone
see GN 03320.005A.4. and GN 03360.005A.2.
4.
A third-party request without consent and the disclosure is authorized in accordance
with the instructions in GN 03320.015 or GN 03320.020 . . .
Forward the request to:
Social Security Administration
Division of
Earnings and Business Services
PO Box 33011
Baltimore, MD 21290-3003
The Division of Earnings and Business Services and other SSA components, as necessary,
should consult with the Office of Privacy
and Disclosure (OPD), Office of the General Counsel (OGC), concerning any questions about the appropriateness of making a requested disclosure
For requests from employers that may be received in the regions, see GN 03320.015C.
5.
A third party request without consent and is not authorized in GN 03320.015 or GN 03320.025 . . .
Refer the request to OPD, OGC, for action.
6.
A subpoena or court order . . .
Regional offices and FOs should follow regional procedures.
Central office components should consult with OPD, OGC, for advice.
