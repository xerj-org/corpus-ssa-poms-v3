SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.370 - Developing Evidence of Age of Eligible Worker When Uninsured Spouse Applies for HI
Effective Dates: 01/27/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302370 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Procedure: Worker and uninsured spouse live in same household
If the worker and uninsured spouse live in the same household, develop evidence of
age under normal proof-of-age procedures. You can generally expect cooperation from
the worker where both spouses live together.
