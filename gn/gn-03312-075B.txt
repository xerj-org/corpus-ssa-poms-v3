B. Non-tax return information
Non-tax return information are records we maintain that are not tax return information,
as defined in the Internal Revenue Code. This may include program or non-program records.
Program records include information about our clients that we keep to administer benefit
programs. This includes requests for Social Security numbers (SSN) and SSN verifications.
Non-program records include administrative and personnel records which contain information
about our activities as a government agency and employer.
