SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.240 - Responding to Requests that Are Not on a Liaison Form
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Before responding to a request submitted in a letter, be sure that the following criteria
are met:
•
the letter is on letterhead paper of one of the U.K. liaison agencies listed in GN 01720.025.A.,
•
the person about whom the request pertains is clearly identified by name and SSN,
and
•
the person's consent to disclose information is attached or you are satisfied that
the information is needed to process a claim under the agreement either because the
U.K. agency states it or because there is evidence in file like an SSA-2490, etc.
If any of these criteria are not met, submit the request to IOTS for instructions.
