SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.080 - Forwarding the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and Folder to the Servicing Hearing Office
Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Procedure for processing hearing requests when the folder is at the HO
If you obtain a query that shows that the folder is at the servicing HO or the Appeals
Council (AC), contact the office indicated on the query to determine if the current
HA-501-U5 is a duplicate or if the folder shown on the query is a prior claims file.
1.
Prepare a Form SSA-5002 (Report of Contact (ROC) if applicable, indicate that the hearing request is a “possible
duplicate;” and add the address and contact information for the office that has the
folder.
2.
Send the ROC and a copy of the HA-501-U5 to the servicing HO.
