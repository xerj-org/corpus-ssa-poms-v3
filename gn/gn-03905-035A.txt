SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03905.035 - Registration, Appointment, and Services for Representatives (RASR) Appointment, Revocation, and Withdrawal Notices
Effective Dates: 12/16/2024 - Present | TN 21 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
The claimant or the representative must submit a completed, current Form SSA-1696
(Claimant's
Appointment
of a Representative) to appoint a representative. When we process Form SSA-1696 in RASR, the system automatically
the sends appointed representatives and their claimants relevant notices. This section
contains the notices related to appointment, withdrawal, and revocation of a representative.
