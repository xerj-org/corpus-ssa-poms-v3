C. Court orders as DBC's
In certain States (e.g., Indiana and Michigan), State or county courts have sometimes
issued DBC's in the form of court decrees or orders. These vary greatly in format
(some show supporting evidence and some do not). Treat these records as DBC's and
evaluate them accordingly.
