E. Procedure - Recalling the SSA-1395 from the FRC
If a FO needs to retrieve a book from the FRC, contact the designated office to formally
make the request. The designated office will request the needed receipt(s) per AIMS,
MRM Chapter 07, Records Management Handbook, and will ship the SSA-1395-BK to the
FO upon receipt.
