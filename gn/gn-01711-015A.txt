A. POLICY — COMPULSORY COVERAGE
Coverage under the basic pension program is based on residence in Norway, while coverage
under the supplementary pension program is credited based on a worker's earnings.
1. Basic Program
In general, all persons who reside in Norway are covered under the basic pension program.
No contributions are required.
2. Supplementary Pension Program
Persons ages 17 through 69 who work in Norway are covered under the supplementary
pension program for years in which their earnings exceed a base amount. Covered employees
and their employers, as well as self-employed persons, are subject to compulsory contributions.
