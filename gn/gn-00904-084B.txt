SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.084 - FO Contact with Individuals Living Outside FO Service Area
Effective Dates: 09/18/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904084 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Restrictions
Contacts outside the service area are restricted to individuals; present restrictions
on contacts with organizations and institutions such as employers, hospital, school,
funeral directors, etc., are not affected. Direct contact with these groups is already
permitted in many situations, e.g., employer contacts for Forms SSA-7011 or SSA-L725
(see OS 15010.347 for the form SSA-L725), etc.
