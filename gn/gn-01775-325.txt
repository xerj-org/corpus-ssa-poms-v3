GN 01775.325 Social Security Administration (SSA) Medical Reimbursement Form
SSA uses the Form SSA 1278 USA/RO 5 (Request for Payments of Reimbursable Expenses
under U.S. - Romania Agreement on Social Security) for reimbursement of medical expenses regarding requests for exams.
The Form SSA 1278 USA/RO 5 (Request for Payment of Reimbursable Expenses under U.S.A.-
Romania Agreement on Social Security) is available on the located on the SSA
Totalization Formssection of the International Operations, International Benefits and Processing, Totalization
Resource Kit.
