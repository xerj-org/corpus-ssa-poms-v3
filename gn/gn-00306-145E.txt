SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.145 - Child Legally Adopted by NH's Surviving Spouse (Benefits for 1/91 or Later)
Effective Dates: 01/04/2017 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure- development
1. Proceedings to Adopt
Look for evidence of proceedings to adopt on adoption certificates, contracts, affidavits,
etc. In some cases you may need to contact appropriate officials for verification.
2. Living with or One-Half Support
See RS 01301.002 for development of living with, and RS 01301.010 for development of one-half support. “Living with” or “one-half support” may be found in utero situations (see GN
00306.006).
3. Marriage
Develop the marital relationship of the NH and surviving spouse as discussed in
GN 00305.001 ff.
