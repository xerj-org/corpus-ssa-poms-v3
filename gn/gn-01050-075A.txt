A. PC Name/SSN Area Number Range/PC Claims Jurisdiction
Use this chart as a guide to determine PC jurisdiction of a claim. In most instances,
determine PC jurisdiction by SSN area number (the first three digits of an SSN.) However,
in some instances, determine PC jurisdiction by the type of claim as indicated in
the column labelled ‘PC Claims Jurisdiction.’
In these cases, the claim type supersedes the SSN area number when determining PC
jurisdiction:
