SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.002 - DIO International Benefits Office (IBO) Jurisdiction
Effective Dates: 04/16/2018 - Present | TN 7 (03-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. General
IBO has jurisdiction over all cases in which at least one claimant or beneficiary
resides, or uses a mailing address, in a foreign country other than:
•
The British Virgin Islands;
•
Canada;
•
The Philippines; and
•
Western Samoa.
