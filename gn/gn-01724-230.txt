GN 01724.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the French Liaison Agency on Form SSA-2960-U3-FR
Procedure
Prepare a diary when requesting information from the Center for Social Security for
Migrant Workers.
•
Diary all initial and follow-up requests for 90 days.
•
Diary a request made by the FSP Paris for 90 days from the date the FSP signed Form
SSA-2960-U3-FR.
•
Forward the folder to IOTS if no response has been received after two followups, i.e.,
after 270 days.
