D. Overview of IDD for the Philippines
IDD payments sent to the Philippines must be in Philippines Peso (PHP) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves the
Philippines. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
