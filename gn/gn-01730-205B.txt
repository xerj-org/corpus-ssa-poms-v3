SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Austria
Effective Dates: 12/10/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Potential Entitlement to U.S. Totalization Benefits
If there is potential entitlement to U.S. totalization benefits, prepare an SSA-2960-U3-AU
to request the Austrian coverage record.
2. Claim for Austrian Benefits
If there is a claim for Austrian benefits, prepare a claims package including:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-AU
•
U.S. totalization earnings record
•
evidence submitted for the Austrian claim including medical evidence if the claim
is for disability benefits.
