Effective Dates: 08/10/1994 - Present | TN 8 (05-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201705020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Contact Information
SSA cannot provide official information about Italian social security benefits. Italian
social security information is available online at: https://www.inps.it.
The Italian website also provides contact information for further explanation of benefits.
GN
01705.215-GN 01705.225 provides information on how to file claims in the United States for Italian benefits.
