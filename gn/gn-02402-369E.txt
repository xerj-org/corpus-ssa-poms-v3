E. Description of Albanian IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Albania will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN for Albania
consists of up to 28 characters. However, the bank data reflected on the MBR will
reflect up to 27 characters. Technicians will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN
when processing an IDD request.
