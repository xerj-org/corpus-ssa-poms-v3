A. Automatic status notice for an application for Korean benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards information about a Korean
claim, certified copies of evidence, and a copy of the worker’s U.S. coverage record
to Korea, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically stores the claims material
in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim
to the Korean agency. The system automatically generates a notice that the BET sends
to the applicant.
