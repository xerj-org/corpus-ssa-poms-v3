A. Background
SSA now prints the servicing field office (FO) address as the return address for all
SSI Notices, instead of printing central office's address. In the past, any undeliverable
notices were returned to central office and then sent to the servicing FO for development
and re-mailing whenever possible. The change was made in order to speed up the processing
time for address corrections and notice delivery.
