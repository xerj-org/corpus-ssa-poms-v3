GN 01757.125 Application Process for Benefits under the Agreement with Brazil
Under the agreement with Brazil, we accept applications for both regular and totalization
benefits on behalf of Brazil. We provide limited assistance to obtain evidence we
need to support the claim. See GN 01757.210 for the types of evidence needed to support the claim.
