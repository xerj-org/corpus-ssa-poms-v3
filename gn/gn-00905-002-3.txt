3. Postadjudicative development
IBO is responsible for:
•
Developing and controlling unresolved issues on claims which can be partially adjudicated,
even if the claim has been authorized and forwarded to the module;
•
Developing student claims where an entitled child files for student or disabled child
benefits; and
•
Developing representative payee changes.
