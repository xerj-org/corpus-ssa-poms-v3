B. PROCEDURE - INVESTIGATING VIOLATIONS OF THE PA
When there is a suspected or alleged criminal violation of the PA involving an SSA
employee, route a report of the incident to the employee's supervisor.
The supervisor will review the SSA AIMS, GAM Chapter 15, for further instructions
on Personally Identifiable Information Loss and Remediation, and consult OGC as needed.
