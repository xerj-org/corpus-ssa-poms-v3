B. Description of the Luxembourg registration number
The Luxembourg registration number consists of 11 digits which are assigned as follows:
•
Digits 1-4 represent the year of birth;
•
Digits 5-6, month of birth;
•
Digits 7-8, day of birth; and
•
Digits 9-11 are a control number that is assigned to identify persons born on the
same day.
