F. Procedure notices
Request incomplete notices when processing actions through EAM and MADCAP.
Send the original notice and one copy by OF-41 to the U.S. Embassy in Bern as explained
in GN00905.210C.
Show the beneficiary's address and “For transmission to the addressee” on the OF-41.
