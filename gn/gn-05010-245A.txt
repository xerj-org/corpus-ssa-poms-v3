SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.245 - Waiver of Recovery of Overpayments
Effective Dates: 06/06/2024 - Present | TN 22 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
As shown in GN 05010.220, we waive recovery of the tax portion of the overpayment when the overpayment is
computed. Thus, waiver development undertaken in connection with the overpayment notice
involves only the amount the beneficiary is asked to repay.
The administrative adjustment is not part of the overpayment and is not subject to
waiver.
