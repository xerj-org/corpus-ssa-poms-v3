A. Process
A resident of Luxembourg may inquire about filing for benefits under the agreement:
•
through the Foreign Service Post (FSP) in Luxembourg, or
•
directly with OIO.
Since the FSP in Luxembourg is not a claims taking FSP, any inquires made there are
forwarded to OIO and then to CDB. Claims inquiries mailed directly to OIO are also
routed to CDB for claims development.
