Dear:
This office will no longer honor high volume, Third Party SSN printouts request for
individuals who reside outside of our service area.
The Social Security field office is staffed to assist the local population base.
Each office services a certain number of Zip Codes and is to assist individuals and
third party requesters in that office’s designated service area.  While a beneficiary
may visit any office of his/her choosing and receive service, a third party requester
(public or private) should seek service from the office servicing the inquired-about
individual’s mailing Zip Code.
Third-party requesters should visit our website at www.socialsecurity.gov and enter the zip codes for their clients. They will get the address of the servicing
SSA office.
Please note that SSA is now charging a standard fee for SSN printouts. The fee for
a match/no match response on multiple requests from a single requestor will be $46
for the first SSN and $20 for each additional SSN in that particular request.
We are returning the enclosed requests because they are for individuals residing outside
of my service area.
