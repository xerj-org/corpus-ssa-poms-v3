B. DEFINITION
A conduit payee is a payee who merely signs the check and turns it over to the beneficiary or other
person or who cashes the check and turns the full amount over to the beneficiary or
other person. These payees do not exercise control over the funds and cannot accurately
account for how the funds are spent.
