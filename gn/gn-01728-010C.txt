SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Follow the procedure below which describes development and routing instructions that
apply to claims filed in the FSP.
1. U.S. Claim
If this is a U.S. claim, do the following:
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4, Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement (Part I), and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-2960-U3-PE to the appropriate Portuguese agency to request
a Portuguese coverage certification, if it appears that the worker is not insured
based only on U.S. coverage. See GN 01728.025 for determining which Portuguese agency office has jurisdiction of the claim.
•
Forward the claim to OIO along with copies of the liaison form sent to the Portuguese
agency.
2. Portuguese Claims
If this is a Portuguese claim, do the following:
•
Refer the claimant to the appropriate local office of the Centro Nacional de Pensoes
or the Nucleo Coord. de Prest. Diferidas to file a Portuguese claim.
•
If the claimant insists on filing a claim for Portuguese benefits at the FSP, take
a protective statement on Form SSA-795 and send it to the Portuguese agency under
cover of Form SSA-2960-U3-PE.
3. U.S. and Portuguese Claim
If this is a concurrent U.S./Portuguese claim, do the following:
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Parts I and II) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-2960-U3-PE to the appropriate Portuguese agency office
to request a Portuguese coverage certification (if it appears that the worker is not
insured based only on U.S. coverage) and to inform the Portuguese agency of the filing
date.
•
Forward the claim to OIO along with copies of the liaison form sent to the Portuguese
agency.
•
Refer the claimant to the appropriate local office of the Centro Nacional de Pensoes
or the Nucleo Coord. de Prest. Diferidas to file a Portuguese claim.
