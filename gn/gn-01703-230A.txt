Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703230 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
TSCs are two-digit codes which describe the status of claims for U.S. totalization
benefits. These codes have also been developed to collect information from the MBR
for accounting and statistical purposes. The presence of some TSC codes on the MBR
prevents automatic HI entitlement and PSC jurisdiction transfers.
