A. Policy on disclosing information from a hearing record
An individual may request access to and examine all evidence that is to be a part
of the hearing record, either before or during the hearing. An individuals appointed
representative may also request the same evidence during the course of the representation.
For information about handling cases while at the hearing level, see GN 03103.190 through GN 03103.230.
