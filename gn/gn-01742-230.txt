GN 01742.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the NPC on Form SSA-2960-KOR
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the NPC.
•
Diary initial requests for 90 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 90 days.
•
Forward the folder to CPSI if no response has been received after two follow-ups;
i.e., after 9 months.
