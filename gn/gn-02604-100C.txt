SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02604.100 - When to Assess a Penalty for Late Reports
Effective Dates: 03/23/2011 - Present | TN 54 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202604100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Foreign work test and no child-in-care
Assess a penalty when all the following factors are present:
1.
The beneficiary received full or partial benefits for a month subject to the deduction.
(For penalties regarding receipt and acceptance of benefits, see GN 02604.115.)
2.
The report is late. (For penalties regarding late reports, see GN 02604.110B.1.).
3.
There is an overpayment due to an unwaived penalty. However, if we deem the beneficiary
“without fault” in causing the overpayment, revoke the penalty (even though recovery
may not be waived).
