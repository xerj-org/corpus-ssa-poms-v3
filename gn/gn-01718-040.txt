GN 01718.040 How to Review and Complete Claims Taken by the Swedish Agency - Procedure
Direct all requests for missing information, other than the Swedish coverage record,
to a FSP or to the beneficiary, as appropriate, depending upon the country of residence.
Accept verifications of vital statistics from the SLAFU unless the dates verified
cause a material discrepancy. See GN 01701.210 for a discussion of verification of eligibility factors by foreign agencies.
