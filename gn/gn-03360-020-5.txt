5. FAXing Documents
Individuals requesting access to their records may ask that copies of the records
be sent to them by FAX. This request may be honored if the office which receives it
has the capability of FAXing material.
Information may also be FAXed to a third party requester, if the written authorization
of the subject individual specifies that FAX may be used to send the requested records.
