Effective Dates: 01/11/2017 - Present | TN 45 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use a U.S. mailing address
A beneficiary abroad may use a U.S. mailing address when they:
•
have an Army Post Office, Fleet Post Office or Diplomatic Post Office address,
•
have a representative payee in the U.S., or
•
expect to be abroad for 3 months or less.
NOTE: The beneficiary (or their representative payee) must always keep SSA advised of their
residence address for contact and foreign enforcement purposes.
