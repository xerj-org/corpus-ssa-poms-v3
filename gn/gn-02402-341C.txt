SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.341 - Coding Hungary Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/01/2024 - Present | TN 286 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402341 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy for IDD to Hungary
1. Currency
IDD payments to Hungary can go only to U.S.
dollar
accounts. At this time, IDD payments
cannot go to Hungary currency (forint) accounts.
2. Coding Hungary IDD data
Any IDD for Hungary that is coded directly to the MBR must also be coded in ITS.gov. Because the MBR cannot accept all 26 digits for Hungary banking, complete bank information
must be on ITS.gov for each beneficiary.
