D. Procedure for handling requests
State CSE agencies should obtain information for child support enforcement purposes
through FPLS, or through electronic data exchanges with us, as discussed in GN 03314.040A and GN 03314.040B in this section. However, an FO may provide information to the CSE agency or another
State or local agency in emergency or sensitive situations. For information regarding
the handling of requests from State and local agencies, see GN 03314.001E.
