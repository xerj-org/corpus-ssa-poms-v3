A. BACKGROUND
Alien residents of the U.S. Virgin Islands are not subject to alien tax withholding.
IRS assumes they satisfy their U.S. tax obligations under section 28(a) of the Revised
Organic Action of the Virgin Islands. This law permits an individual to meet their
U.S. tax obligation by payment to the Virgin Island Treasury, rather than IRS.
