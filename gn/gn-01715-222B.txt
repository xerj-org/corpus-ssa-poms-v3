B. Process
Periodically, the Quebec Pension Board mails a life certificate form to each QPP beneficiary
residing in the United States with instructions on how to complete the form. After
completing the form, the beneficiary must take it to an FO, or one of several other
approved authorities, to have his or her identity verified. The beneficiary then returns
the certified form to the Quebec Pension Board by mail within the time period specified
by the Board.
