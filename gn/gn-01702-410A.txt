Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The routing of the claims file in claims for U.S. Totalization disability benefits
will vary depending on whether the worker is, or appears to be, insured. These procedures
are designed to eliminate unnecessary development when the number holder (NH) is unlikely
to meet insured status requirements even taking into account the NH's alleged foreign
coverage. Section GN 01702.410B. provides the routing procedures based on whether the NH meets or will probably meet
insured status requirements.
