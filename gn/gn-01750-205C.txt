SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01750.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside of the Czech Republic
Effective Dates: 04/26/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - U.S. claims/Czech totalization benefits
Prepare a claims package that includes:
•
Form SSA-e2960-U3-CZ USA/CZ3 to request a Czech Republic certified coverage record
if needed and to certify the filing date for Czech benefits, establish factors of
entitlement, and U.S. benefit information.
•
A U.S. totalization earnings record.
•
Medical evidence from SSA’s files, if the Czech claim is for disability benefits.
•
CZ/USA 202 and any necessary proofs and any evidence
•
Letter to claimant advising them that their claim was forwarded to the Czech Republic
Agency if claim is for Czech benefits only.
