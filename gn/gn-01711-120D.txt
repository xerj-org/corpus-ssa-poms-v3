D. Policy - Adjudication responsibility
The Office of International Operations (OIO) is responsible for converting Norwegian
coverage to U.S. QC's and adjudicating claims for U.S. totalization benefits when
the NH has at least 6 QC's but is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone.
