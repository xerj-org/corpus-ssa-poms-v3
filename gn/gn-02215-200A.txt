SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.200 - Preparation of the Bankruptcy Proof of Claim
Effective Dates: 03/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. List of Documents
The following documents are required for filing a Proof of Claim:
•
Notice of bankruptcy (from the bankruptcy court),
•
Proof of Claim (Official Form 410), and
•
Certificate of Indebtedness.
Prepare the documents electronically, with electronic signatures where applicable.
Email the documents to the Office of General Counsel (OGC) at SSA.Bankruptcy@ssa.gov,
and cc the assigned attorney if known.
