2. Disability
The ALJ will follow the procedures in HALLEX I-2–8–30 in disability cases if disallowance
was based on non-disability factors and no initial disability determination was made.
If the disallowance was based on a disability factor and the ALJ makes a favorable
disability decision, the ALJ can also decide on non-disability factors (after sending
the notice in 1. above). If the ALJ reverses an insured status denial and an SSA-7010-U5
or SSA-7000-U5 is needed, the folder is first sent to the PC to correct the E/R and
determine when insured status is first and last met; then it will be sent to the DDS
for the medical decision.
