SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01770.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for Slovenian Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/01/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201770210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Using evidence previously submitted for claims for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to resubmit the evidence previously used and verified to establish
factors of entitlement for U.S. social security benefits. The evidence previously
submitted is sufficient for Slovenia to establish the same factor for Slovenian benefits.
