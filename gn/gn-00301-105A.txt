SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.105 - Certification of Evidence for Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 05/01/2003 - Present | TN 40 (05-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Under the requirements of U.S. International Social Security agreements, also known
as totalization agreements (see GN 01702.200 - GN 01702.215), claimants who are unwilling to part with evidentiary documents, may request FOs to certify the evidence for their totalization claim.
