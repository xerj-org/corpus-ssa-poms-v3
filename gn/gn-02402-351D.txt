D. Overview of IDD for Iceland
IDD payments sent to Iceland must be in U.S. dollar (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Iceland. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
