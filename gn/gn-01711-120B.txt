B. Policy - Crediting Norwegian coverage
SSA will credit 4 U.S. quarters of coverage for each year of Norwegian coverage under
the supplementary pension program. (Norwegian coverage is measured in years known
as “pension point years.”) However, no credit will be given for:
•
any calendar quarter within a pension point year that is already credited as a U.S.
quarter of coverage, or
•
more than 4 quarters of coverage for any calendar year.
