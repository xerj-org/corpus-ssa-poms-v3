B. Procedure for evaluating foreign records and documents
When evaluating foreign records and documents:
•
Consider information about the issuance, maintenance, availability, and certification
of foreign records. Refer to GN 00307.000 for country-specific policies and procedures.
•
If evidence appears questionable or of unknown value, request assistance from the
Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) assigned to that location.  Provide any necessary information
to the FBU (including a photocopy of the document).
