SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01748.012 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops U.S. Claims Filed With the Danish Agency
Effective Dates: 02/01/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201748012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The Oslo, Norway FSP should process referrals from the Danish agency if:
1. The claimant is entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all material to DIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. The claimant is not entitled to U.S. benefits
Process the claim in the same manner shown in GN 01748.010B.
