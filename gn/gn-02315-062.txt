GN 02315.062 Mississippi — Small Estates
Mississippi has a small estates statute, but the statute is not necessary for SSA
to determine who would be the legal representative of such an estate because SSA would
pay any debt owed to a decedent to individuals with higher priority than the estate.
See Miss. Code Ann. § 91-7-322(1) (2014).
