B. Taxes under the Agreement
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to the following:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes, Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) and Self-Employment
Contributions Act (SECA), including the Medicare portions of those Acts; and
•
Hungarian social security taxes that finance old-age retirement, survivor, disability,
health insurance, sickness and maternity, work injury and unemployment programs.
