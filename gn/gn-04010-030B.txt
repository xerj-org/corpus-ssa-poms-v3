B. Policy — When to reopen based on new and material evidence
A final determination or decision may be reopened within 4 years of the notice of
the initial determination on the basis of new and material evidence even though the
determination or decision, when made, was reasonable based on the evidence then in
file. (See GN 04030.010 and GN 04030.050E. for special situations involving new and material evidence received after 4 years.)
