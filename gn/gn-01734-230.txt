GN 01734.230 How OIO Controls Requests Sent to the Luxembourg Agency on Form SSA-2960-U3-LX
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the Luxembourg agencies.
•
Diary the initial request for 6 months.
•
If no response is received, prepare a followup and diary for 90 days.
•
If no response is received, forward the folder to the International Operations and
Totalization Staff.
