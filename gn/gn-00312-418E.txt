E. Procedure – Northern Ireland
1. Birth, Marriage, Adoption and Death Records
Website: http://www.groni.gov.uk/
Original records for persons born in what is now Northern Ireland before 1922 are
maintained in Belfast. The General Register Office in Dublin contains duplicates of
these records. Certificates from either source are acceptable primary evidence of
age for persons born before 1922 for Social Security purposes.
a. Office to Contact - Event Occurred Before 1997
Registrar General's Office
Oxford House
49-55 Chichester Street
Belfast BT1 4HL
Northern Ireland
Telephone number: 011-44-2890-270274
Telephone number (Adoption records): 011-44-2890-252000
b. Office to Contact - Event Occurred After 1996
Council office where event occurred
If the council office is not known, the Registrar General's office can assist in identifying
it.
c. Fee
•
Birth and Adoption Records: Full and short copy: 9.00 Pounds.
•
Marriage and Death Certificates: 9.00 Pounds;
Include an additional 1 pound for postage and handling.
Payment must be by international money order payable to “Registrar General.”
2. Divorce Records
Include the court reference number with the request for divorce records. If this number
is not known, provide as much information as possible about the parties and the date
and place of marriage.
a. Office to Contact
Probate and Matrimonial Office
The Family Division
Royal Courts of Justice
Belfast BT1 3JF
Northern Ireland
Telephone number: 011-44-2890-235111
b. Fee
5.50 pounds per record if the request includes the court reference number.
If the court reference number is not known, the cost is 5.50 pounds for the search
and 5.50 pounds for the certificate.
Include 5.50 pounds for postage and handling for each certificate requested.
Payment must be by international money order payable to “Inland Revenue.”
