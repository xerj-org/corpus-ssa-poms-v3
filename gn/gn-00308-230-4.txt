4. Remarks:
The above addresses are for records from St. Thomas and St. John. Birth and death
records for St. Croix may be obtained from Registrar of Vital Statistics, Charles
Harwood Memorial Hospital, Christiansted, St. Croix, VI 00820. Divorce records may
be obtained from the Deputy Clerk of District Court and marriage records from the
Judge of Police Court, Christiansted, St Croix, VI 00820.
