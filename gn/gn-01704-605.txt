GN 01704.605 Foreign Language Documents Not Requiring Translation
Evidence is frequently required to satisfy only the requirements of foreign agencies.
This evidence will usually be foreign language documents. When such evidence is identified,
forward it untranslated to the foreign agency. Use the following lists to identify
foreign language documents which are not needed for RSDI purposes and, therefore,
need not be translated.
