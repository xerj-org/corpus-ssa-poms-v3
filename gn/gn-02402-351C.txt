SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.351 - Coding Iceland Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 02/13/2018 - Present | TN 257 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402351 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Iceland has a prefix of "66" at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “L” assigned for Iceland.
