SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.032 - Restricting Retroactivity for Title II Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/18/2023 - Present | TN 117 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204032 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Beneficiary requested restricted retroactivity
A claimant may choose to restrict any retroactive benefits. If a claimant does not
wish to receive retroactive benefits, we must honor their decision.
EXAMPLE: John files for retirement benefits 11 months after he reached full retirement age.
Based on the retroactivity rules, he can receive 6 months of retroactivity payments
from the date he files. However, John restricts the retroactivity of the application
to their date of filing in order to receive delayed retirement credits.
EXCEPTION: A claimant filing or receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) cannot restrict
retroactivity. For more information on the SSI requirement to file for other benefits,
see SI 00510.001D.1.a.
