SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.198 - Assembly of Material Where Claims Folder Received From DDS or ODO
Effective Dates: 01/15/1999 - Present | TN 1 (06-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050198 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Folder from ODOto be returned to ODO (SSA-5526-u3)
1.
Put new material above prior material on right side of folder in chronological order
with the most recent material on top.
2.
Forward material per instructions on the SSA-5526-U3 or using the reverse side as
the transmittal sheet placed on the outside of the folder.
