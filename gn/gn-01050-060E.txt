E. Technical entitlement on one SSN and payment on another SSN
In instances involving one or more claimants who will be technically entitled on one
SSN but paid on another SSN (e.g., children entitled on two SSN's involving the combining
of family maximums) the following rules apply. If both claims are initial life or
subsequent life claims or if both claims are initial death or subsequent death claims,
send both claims to the processing center having jurisdiction of the SSN on which
payment will be made. If one claim is a subsequent life claim and the other is a subsequent
death claim, send the claims to the processing center having jurisdiction over the
SSN for the life claim. If one of the claims is ODO-jurisdiction, cross-refer the
claims, include a microfiche, MBR extract, or RC with information about the other
claim, and route the claims separately.
