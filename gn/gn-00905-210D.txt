D. Procedure development requests
Make sure the request is clear and complete. Do not use technical terms or abbreviations
since the Swiss are not trained in U.S. Social Security matters and terms.
Attach all the necessary forms to the development request.
REMINDER:The Swiss do not have the authority to certify evidence for Social Security purposes.
However, we can accept photocopies of evidence that have been properly certified by
the Department of State, per the exception in GN 00301.095A.
Diary development requests for 120 days, with followups as explained in GN 00904.245.
