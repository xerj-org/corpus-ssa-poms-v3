A. Filing for benefits using multiple application forms
Form SSA-2490-BK
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with Italy must complete Form
SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
The applicant must complete this form whether the application is for benefits from
the following:
•
United States; or
•
Italy; or
•
Both countries.
