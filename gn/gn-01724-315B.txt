SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01724.315 - Obtaining Coverage Records for Persons Who Want to Make Voluntary Contributions to France
Effective Dates: 03/08/2017 - Present | TN 1 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201724315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
CAUTION: The SSA-794 is not the proper vehicle for providing coverage information. This form
is designed for internal SSA use and is not intended for distribution to the public.
Ask the requester to complete an SSA-7004-SM-OPI (Request for Earnings and Benefit
Estimate Statement).
NOTE: This form is designed as a postage paid mailer with the Commissioner's explanation
and the SSA-7004-SM-OPI already sealed inside. It can be sent by simply writing the
name and address of the requester in the appropriate area of the envelope.
When a request for earnings information is received from other than a recognized liaison
agency, send an SSA-7004-SM-OPI instead of an SSA-794.
