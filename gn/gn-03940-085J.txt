1. Description - DPS UTI FEE085 - Time limit for Filing Fee Petition and Be Paid Directly
If (1) (2) wants us to pay the fee we approve from (3) withheld benefits, he or she
must ask us to approve the fee within 60 days of the date of this letter.
•
If all the work on this case is finished, and the (4) wants to charge a fee, he or
she should send us a request to approve it right away.
•
If all work is not finished in this case, the (5) should let us know within 60 days
of the date of this letter that he or she will ask for a fee.
•
If the (6) does not want to charge a fee or does not want us to pay the fee from the
benefits we withheld, he or she should send us a signed and dated statement saying
so right away.
