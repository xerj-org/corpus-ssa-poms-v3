A. Introduction to vital statistics records in Costa Rica
Costa Rica has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration records. Records
are held by the National Registry in the Vital Registry. The registry is part of the Tribunal
Supremo de Elecciones (the supreme electoral authority of Costa Rica). In addition,
the National Registry has several physical offices (local Registro Civil offices)
in municipalities throughout the country where individuals can visit in person.
