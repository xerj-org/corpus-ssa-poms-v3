SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.336 - Coding St. Kitts and Nevis Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/05/2021 - Present | TN 194 (06-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402336 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in St. Kitts and Nevis will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning
of the Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account
Number (DAN) field has the letter “E” assigned for St. Kitts and Nevis.
