B. Procedure for referring claim inquiries to the FBUs
Refer claims inquiries to the FBU servicing the individual's address for development
of the claim and contact, as explained in GN 00905.302. See GN 00904.220 to determine which FBU serves the claimant’s address in Mexico. Visit the Foreign Country Service
Information website to locate the address of the appropriate FBU in Mexico.
