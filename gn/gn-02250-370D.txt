D. Title XVI procedures
When you approve a waiver request and input the determination, the system sends an
SSA-L8166 to the individual and their representative payee.  The SSA-L8166 includes
the title “Our Decision On Your Waiver Request.” For more information on who receives
an SSI automated notice, refer to NL 00801.001B.3.
NOTE:When you approve a waiver request and input the determination, the system sends an
SSA-L8166 to the individual and their representative payee.  The SSA-L8166 includes
the title “Our Decision On Your Waiver Request.” For more information on who receives
an SSI automated notice, refer to NL 00801.001B.3.
If you issue a manual waiver approval notice, use the “Waiver Approval” notice located
in the overpayment section in the DPS.  Prepare and issue the notice, including appeal
rights.  For more information on appealing a waiver, refer to GN 02250.380. Store all waiver notices in ORS. For more information about Title XVI manually prepared
notices, refer to NL 00801.010.
