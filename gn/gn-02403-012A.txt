Effective Dates: 04/01/2016 - Present | TN 58 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202403012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Fees received in the FO
The FO may receive fee payments for:
•
copies of earnings records,
•
photocopies of documents requested under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA),
•
material in claims files, or
•
providing non-program-related benefit information or verifications. For information
on Privacy Act and FOIA fees, see GN 03311.005.
NOTE: The Social Security Administration (SSA) requires payment before providing the requested
record or service.
