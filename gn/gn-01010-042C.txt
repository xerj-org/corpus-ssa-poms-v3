SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01010.042 - Prior uncollected annual earnings test (AET) penalties
Effective Dates: 11/10/2011 - Present | TN 13 (11-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201010042 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. AET penalty exists
If the PENALTY data line of the MBR shows that an uncollected AET penalty exists for
a current claimant, do not process the subsequent claim thru MCS EC and take the following
actions:
•
Process the claim manually by preparing an Automated 101 (A101) or an Electronic Form
(EF101); and,
•
Annotate the Benefit Continuity Remarks/Notice (BCRN) screen with the remark “UNCOLLECTED
AET PENALTY EXISTS - INITIATE RECOVERY FROM BIC     ” to alert the processing center
(PC).
