SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03501.017 - Beneficiary Moves Out of Circuit After Receiving Benefit of AR
Effective Dates: 08/23/1991 - Present | TN 2 (08-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203501017 (fetched 2026-10-04)

2. Title II
When a title II beneficiary moves out of the circuit where he/she benefited from an
AR, he/she will continue to get the benefit of the AR (i.e., there is no reduction
or termination of benefits) unless or until his/her benefits are terminated or reduced
because such action is called for by the Social Security Act and Regulations. Movement
out of a circuit is not a terminating event.
