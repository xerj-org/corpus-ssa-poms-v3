C. Recomputations
When performing a recomputation of a windfall offset, check to see if the Title II
monthly amounts currently provided by the PC are different than the Title II monthly
amounts used in the initial windfall offset computation. If the Title II monthly amounts
differ by less than $1.00 due to HI/SMI rounding:
1.
Use the Title II monthly amounts from the original windfall offset in your computations
in eComps so you do not have to account for the $1.00 tolerance found in SM 03045.910
when calculating any difference in the original windfall offset amount and the recomputed
windfall offset amount.
2.
Annotate that you used the Title II monthly amounts from the original windfall offset
in the e4345 application to account for the $1.00 tolerance found in SM 03045.910.
