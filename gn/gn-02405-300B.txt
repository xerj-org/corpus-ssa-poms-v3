SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02405.300 - Limited Payability - Voiding Returned Benefit Checks More Than a Year Old
Effective Dates: 04/25/2014 - Present | TN 15 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202405300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Interviewer Procedure
Process endorsed and unendorsed benefit checks returned to the Field Office (FO) that
Treasury issued and are more than one year old in accordance with the following table.
STEP
ACTION
1
Access the Treasury Check Information System (TCIS) or the Treasury Check Verification
Query (TCVQ) screen to determine the claim number. (See MS QUERIES 003.022 for instructions
on how to access the TCVQ screen).
Enter “NOT NEGOTIABLE” below the payee address on the returned check, but above the
solid line.
REMINDER: Do not write or stamp below the solid line, which extends the full length of the
check. Doing so causes problems with Treasury's optical scanning equipment.
2
Enter the returned check into the Debt Management System (DMS) as an unendorsed returned
benefit check with limited payability, with Code L as the reason for the return of
the check (see MS DMS 003.006).
3
Give or mail the automated receipt to the remitter. (see MS DMS 003.007).
4
Review the MBR and PHUS or the SSR to determine whether the payment was due, and then
follow the instructions in SM 00624.420 for Title II, and SM 01315.011 for Title XVI.
5
Give the check and the supporting MBR and PHUS, or SSR queries, to the remittance
clerk.
