B. Title II inquiry about Clark relief
We identified Title II class members as explained in GN 02615.100A.2. We added identified Title II cases to the Civil Action Tracking System (CATS) with
a Court Case Identifier (CCID) of C1 or C2. We also posted one of the two following
Central Office Messages (CO MSG) to the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR):
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – CASE SELECTED FOR CLARK REVIEW; or
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – CASE SELECTED FOR CLARK RELIEF AND MEDICARE REVIEW
After processing Clark relief, we sent a class review, resumption, or reinstatement notice, and we posted
one of the following Special Messages (SP MSG) to the MBR:
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – AUTOMATED RELIEF PROCESSED;
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – CLARK RELIEF PROCESSED;
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – CLARK RELIEF AND MEDICARE RELIEF PROCESSED; or
•
CLARK COURT ORDER – CLARK RELIEF PROCESSED, NOT ELIGIBLE FOR MEDICARE RELIEF.

The following chart provides N8NN, FO, and PC instructions for handling Title II inquiries
about Clark relief based on the Clark messages on the MBR.

2. Inquirer’s MBR contains no Clark messages
If there is no Clark CO MSG on the inquirer’s MBR, then we did not identify the inquirer as a Title II
Clark class member.
a. N8NN instructions
If you receive an inquiry and there is no Clark CO MSG on the MBR, provide the following explanation to the inquirer:
“We need to investigate your case further. Please allow us 60 days to respond to your
inquiry. No further action is required from you at this time. If further action is
later required, we will contact you.”
Refer the case to the FO for investigation via Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW),
entering the following on the MDW Worksheet (MMDW) screen:
•
CLARK in the ISSUE field;
•
ADM in the CATEGORY field; and
•
INQUIRY in the REMARK field.
In addition, on the MDW Remarks (MRMK) screen explain why the inquirer believes they
are a class member.
b. FO and PC instructions
If you receive an inquiry or a N8NN MDW referral with “Clark” as the ISSUE, and there is no Clark CO MSG on the MBR:
•
Check the Fugitive Felon System Control File (FFSCF) to determine if the inquirer
has any warrants subject to Clark relief. Follow the instructions for using FFSCF in MSOM PRISON 006.001 through MSOM
PRISON 006.014.
•
Review the FFSCF warrant information using the instructions in this chart.
