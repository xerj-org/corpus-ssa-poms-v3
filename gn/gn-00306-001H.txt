H. Illegitimate child
An "illegitimate child" is a child who is born to parents who are not married to each
other, i.e., born out of wedlock or a non-marital child.
Section 202(d)(3) of the Social Security Act (Act) references legitimacy with respect
to the dependency requirement for child's benefits (see GN 00306.002C).
