A. Description of my Social Security Change of Address (myCOA)
myCOA is an online application that allows Title II users with electronic access to
my Social Security, to update their address or telephone number. Title XVI users with
payment status codes of C01, N01, S08, and E01 and concurrent users, can use myCOA
to update their telephone number. The change will take place without the user having
to contact an SSA employee. The user will receive a notification via email and mail
to inform them of recent changes. In addition, the user will receive a receipt confirmation
of the change in their Message Center inbox. myCOA processes the new information through
existing batches and posts it to the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) for Title II
and to the Supplemental Security Record (SSR) for Title XVI.
NOTE: Title II U.S. Citizens can make Domestic address changes and Foreign address changes
to countries that are not barred or restricted.
