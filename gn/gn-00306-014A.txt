SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.014 - Use of State Intestacy Laws to Develop Title II Parent-Child Relationship
Effective Dates: 09/13/2019 - Present | TN 43 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306014 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to apply State intestacy laws to develop child status
Apply State intestacy laws in GN 00306.405 through GN 00306.680 when using State law to determine child status and you cannot find child status based
on the general rules in GN 00306.011.
IMPORTANT: For cases involving surrogacy or posthumous conception, see GN 00306.014C and GN 00306.014D, respectively.
