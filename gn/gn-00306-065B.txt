B. Policy - Standards of Proof
The standard of proof for establishing paternity under State intestacy laws is a matter
of State law. Generally, clear and convincing proof or evidence is an intermediate
standard, requiring more certainty on the part of the fact finder than a preponderance
of the evidence standard, but less certainty than the beyond a reasonable doubt standard.
Standards of proof under each State's law are described in GN 00306.400 - GN 00306.680. If you have a question as to what standard of proof a specific State law requires,
or whether the evidence in a particular case meets the State law standard, submit
it to OGC under GN 01010.815 ff. in the absence of a precedent opinion.
