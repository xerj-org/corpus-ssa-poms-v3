H. DBC as best evidence
A DBC can be used as best evidence even if one of the documents on which the DBC is
based is the earliest available (assuming the DB on that document agrees with the
DB on the DBC). However, the adjudicator must explain in the DB determination why
obtaining the earliest document (as well as any earlier records) would not alter the
DB determination (see GN 00302.170).
