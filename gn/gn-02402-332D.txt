D. Overview of IDD for St. Lucia
IDD payments sent to St. Lucia must be in Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves St.
Lucia. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
