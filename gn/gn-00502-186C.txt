C. Procedure – Substance Abuse
Benefits to individuals who were entitled because drug addiction or alcoholism was
a contributing factor material to their disability were terminated effective 1/1/97.
Installment payments to their representative payees continued until all remaining
benefits were paid. See MS INTRANETCPS 001.001 and SM 00635.000 for CPS payment instructions.
