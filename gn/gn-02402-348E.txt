E. Description of Slovakia IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Slovakia will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from FI. The IBAN will be 24 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR will be comprised of 26 characters. Technicians
will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and DAN when processing an IDD request.
