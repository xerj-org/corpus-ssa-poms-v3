A. Prohibited Representation by Former SSA Employees and Officials
1.
A former SSA employee or official may never communicate to or appear before SSA on
behalf of any person or entity in any matter in which the former employee had participated
personally and substantially as an employee of SSA.
2.
Personal and substantial participation is defined as directly influencing by official
recommendation, decision or adjudicative action, a claimant's eligibility to a benefit,
payment or other right to which he may be entitled under the Social Security Act.
