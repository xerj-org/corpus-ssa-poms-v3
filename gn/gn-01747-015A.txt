A. Introduction
Denmark has a two-tier program. Coverage under the Folkepension (FP) or “Social” pension is based on residence in Denmark, while coverage under the
Labour Market Supplementary Pension (ATP) is based on a worker’s contributions and the total years of coverage under this
plan.
