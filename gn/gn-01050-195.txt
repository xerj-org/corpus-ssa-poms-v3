GN 01050.195 Assembly of Material to be Forwarded to DDS
File in the folder (in order from bottom to top) the SSA-3368-BK, SSA-3369-BK, SSA-821-F4
(when necessary), any medical reports brought in by the claimant, authorization release
forms, RC information about a prior application, and the SSA-831-U5. Also include
any related prior disability denial material that is in the DO. Do not make routine
subsequent transmittals to the DDS (e.g., SSA-16-F6, SSA-794-C1, SS-5). Hold these
for association with the disability folder on return from the DDS.
