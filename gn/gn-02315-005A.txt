A. Operating Policy
If the claimant seeking payment of an underpayment can qualify only as the legal representative
of the deceased's estate and there is no appointed executor or administrator of the
estate, he/she may qualify to receive the underpayment as legal representative under
the “small estate” statutes.
NOTE: An individual can be a legal representative even though that individual is not an
executor or administrator.
