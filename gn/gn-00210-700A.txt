Effective Dates: 02/07/2025 - Present | TN 63 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200210700 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
As a result of the Windsor and Obergefell decisions, we are able to process Special
Enrollment Period (SEP) requests for individuals with Group Health Plan (GHP) or Large
Group Health Plan (LGHP) coverage based on the current employment of a same-sex spouse.
