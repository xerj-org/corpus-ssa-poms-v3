SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01715.140 - Exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision Under the Canadian Agreement
Effective Dates: 11/07/2003 - Present | TN 6 (10-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201715140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
alleges residence in Canada, develop residence status to verify that the exemption
provided by the agreement applies.
