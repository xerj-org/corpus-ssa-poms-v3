B. Limitations of referrals
•
Although SSA field offices (FO) provide information about existing community services,
we will provide direct referrals only in those situations where there is a clearly
expressed need for services or where the need is readily identifiable or clearly observable.
•
The FO will not provide detailed or in-depth counseling beyond that required to give
advice about services provided by agencies sufficient for an appropriate referral.
In addition, it will not probe for hidden or undetected needs. We will assist such
individuals as necessary in making contact with the State or local agency.
