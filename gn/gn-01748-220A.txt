A. Process for the SSA-2960 USA/DN3
The Division of International Operations and the Oslo Norway Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) completes the SSA-2960 USA/DN3 via the Totalization Data collection Program
(TDCP). Use the SSA-2960 USA/DN3 to:
•
Transmit claims and related material to the Danish agency.
•
Request information from the Danish agency.
•
Respond to requests from the Danish agency.
