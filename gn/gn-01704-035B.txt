SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.035 - Foreign-to-Foreign Change of Address
Effective Dates: 08/08/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Beneficiary CURRENTLY living in a country other than a country of agreement
1.
Beneficiary reports a change of address to another country with which we have no agreement—Process
according to GN 02605.250.
2.
Beneficiary reports a change of address to a country with which we have an agreement.
a.
Beneficiary is an alien who does not meet an exception to the alien nonpayment provision—Give
the case to a benefit authorizer for a determination of residence and possible payment
of benefits.
b.
All other situations—Process according to GN
02605.250.
