SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.302 - Windfall Elimination Provisions (WEP) Exceptions Based on Years of Coverage for Totalization Benefits Payable for Months Prior to January 1995
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 15 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701302 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure for determining YOCs for the WEP exception
Determine total YOCs when deciding if an exception to WEP applies as follows:
1. Determine the total years of U.S. Coverage
Determine total years of U.S. coverage in the same way they are determined for the
special minimum PIA (see RS 00605.070B).
2. Determine the total years of foreign coverage
Determine total YOCs from the Totalization agreement country as follows:
•
Convert foreign coverage to quarters of coverage (QCs) following the rules of the
Totalization agreement.
•
Delete any foreign QCs which coincide with actual U.S. QCs.
•
Divide remaining foreign QCs by four, dropping any remainder, to determine YOCS based
on foreign coverage.
3. Combining U.S. and foreign coverage
Add the total years of U.S. coverage to the total years of foreign coverage and use
the combined total to determine whether the YOC exception applies.
NOTE: Do not use YOCs based on attributed earnings as shown in the NH’s theoretical earnings
record (see GN 01701.200B.2) in determining whether WEP applies.
