A. The Death Master File (DMF)
SSA maintains the Death Master File (DMF), which includes deaths reported to SSA from
various sources. We provide this information to the U.S. Department of Commerce, National
Technical Information Service (NTIS). NTIS sells this information to the public. However,
State death information is not included in the publicly-available DMF since this information
is restricted from disclosure.
