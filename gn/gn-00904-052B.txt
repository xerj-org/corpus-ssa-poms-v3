B. Foreign claimants
1.
A foreign claimant is a claimant not listed in A.1. above.
2.
If there is a domestic claimant on the same social security number—the DO servicing
the domestic claimant is the resident office even if the foreign claimant is the principal
claimant.
3.
If there is NO domestic claimant—see Service Area Directory, Part C, to determine
resident office.
Note: For RSDI claims, DIO is resident office for all foreign countries (other than Canada,
and the Philippines) when there are no domestic claimants
