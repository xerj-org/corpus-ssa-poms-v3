GN 05002.016 Social Security Statement-Box 6, Voluntary Federal Income Tax Withheld
Section 3402(P) of the Internal Revenue Code has been amended to permit voluntary
withholding of Federal income tax from specified Federal payments when the recipient
of such payment requests such withholding.
