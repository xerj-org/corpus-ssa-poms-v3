SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.303 - Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) Computations and the Guarantee for Totalization Benefits Payable for Months Prior to January 1995
Effective Dates: 03/24/2026 - Present | TN 19 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701303 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The guarantee explained in RS 00605.370 applies to Totalization benefits payable for months before 1995 as well as regular
U.S. benefits. When determining if the guarantee is applicable, it is the amount of
the reduction in the theoretical primary insurance amount that must be considered
in applying the rules in RS
00605.370B.
