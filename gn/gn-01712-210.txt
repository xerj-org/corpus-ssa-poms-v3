GN 01712.210 Procedure for Preparing Initial Claims Packages For Claims Filed in Norway
When claims are filed in Norway, do not prepare a package to request a Norwegian coverage
record or to inform the FFU about a claim for Norwegian benefits.
Prepare a claims package only to respond to requests from the FFU. Include the SSA-2960-U2-NE
and any material requested by the FFU.
