D. Overview of IDD for Indonesia
IDD payments sent to Indonesia must be in Indonesia Rupiah (IDR) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Indonesia.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
