A. Introduction
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Germany
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work, and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or German benefits,
called totalization benefits, based on combined work credits from both countries.
