B. Second pillar coverage
Employers only contribute under this system to individual investment funds. These
contributions come from the employer portion of the first pillar contribution. The
Slovak Republic introduced the second pillar system in 2005. Since 2005, contributions
to the second pillar have been compulsory at some points and voluntary at others.
This pillar also permits fund members to make tax-exempt contributions from their
own resources.
