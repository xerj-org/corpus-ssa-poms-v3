D. Dual entitlement (DE) and VTW systems instructions
1. Basic rules for DE
When the request involves DE:
•
Apply VTW requests to any record on which the beneficiary is or will be receiving
Title II payments (LAF codes C, D, AD or E).
•
Keep DE payments combined on one record if the beneficiary requests us to tax each
benefit at the same tax rate.
•
Split combined DE payments to their individual records if the beneficiary requests
that we withhold taxes from each record at a different tax rate.
NOTE: MCS will process a DE claim as long as the requested VTW percentages are the same.
