D. Post Transaction Record Updates
US Bank will include an Individual Reference Number (IRN) in the daily file for all
remittances processed by the lockbox. This will assist us to process check returns,
and check adjustments, etc. An IRN is a unique number generated by US Bank sent to
Treasury’s CIR. As a result, the IRN ‘LCK########’ will appear in the 'Receipt Number’
field on the daily detail file and in the Receipt Number field of the REQD screen
in DMS. The number #will be the last 8 digits of the IRN.
