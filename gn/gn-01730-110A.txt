SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.110 - How to Credit Austrian Coverage
Effective Dates: 03/24/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Austrian coverage certification is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage
to the earnings record (ER).
•
All periods of coverage certified by Austria are creditable.
•
The presence of Austrian coverage after onset is not necessarily an indication of
work. Development of substantial gainful activity should not be initiated based only
on the presence of Austrian coverage.
•
There is no minimum amount of Austrian coverage which must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
