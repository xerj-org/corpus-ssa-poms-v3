D. Overview of IDD for Saudi Arabia
IDD payments sent to Saudi Arabia must be in Saudi Arabian Riyal (SAR) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that
territory. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
