SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01727.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Portuguese Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 5 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201727225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Portuguese claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of their
claim for Portuguese benefits and states that they have not received notification
that DIO forwarded a claim to the Portuguese agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show DIO forwarded the claim to Portugal
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Portugal, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235 - 7769
USA
2. PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to Portugal
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Portuguese agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Portuguese agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
about Portuguese benefits online at:
Institute of Solidarity
and Social Security
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Portuguese benefits claim after a technician
forwards the claim to Portugal. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, GN 01702.520.
