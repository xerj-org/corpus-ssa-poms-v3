D. First vs. second priority convincing evidence
Study data also indicate that the records in the first priority list in GN 00302.115 are relatively more reliable and available than those listed as second priority in
GN 00302.118.
Records other than those shown in these two lists are either known to be unreliable
or so few have been studied that the probative value cannot be statistically determined.
