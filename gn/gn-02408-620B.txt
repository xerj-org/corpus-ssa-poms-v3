SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.620 - Manual Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Reclamation Requests and Exclusions
Effective Dates: 12/04/2015 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408620 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. 120-day rule
It is important to input the manual reclamation as soon as the responsible component
learns of the need for a manual input. The Social Security Administration (SSA) is
required by the Code of Federal Regulations to send the reclamation request within
120 days of the date the agency learned of the death.
2. Manual reclamation exclusions
Do not input a manual reclamation request (E-stop) for a case listed in GN
02408.620E in this section.
3. Three-year rule
The Department of the Treasury (Treasury) imposes a three (3) year time limit for
federal agencies to submit final debits (i.e., Automated Clearing House (ACH) reclaims)
of a Financial Institution's (FI) Federal Reserve account via the FS-135, Request
for Debit. An ACH reclaim represents a debit against an FI for certain liability on
payments disbursed after the death of a beneficiary. Agencies have three years from
the date the FS-133, Notice of Reclamation, is dated to submit ACH reclaims via the
FS-135 to Treasury's Regional Financial Centers (RFC). Items older than three years
are rejected and not processed (see GN02408.626).
4. Six-year rule
Generally, the FI is not liable for any money received more than six years before
the most recent payment. However, if the account contains more than that amount, the
FI is responsible for the total amount of all payments made after death, up to the
amount in the account when the FI receives the notice of reclamation.
The responsible SSA component must assist the FI by doing a manual reclamation input
or providing a reclamation request letter when an FI asks for documentation. (See
GN 02408.750)
