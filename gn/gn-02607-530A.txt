A. Procedure - correcting PUPS records with blank receipt dates
If you receive a PUPS alert/record and the receipt date is blank, you should enter
the date shown in the REPORT DATE field of the alert as the RECEIPT DATE on the PU02
screen.
EXAMPLE:
(Inmate Data Recorded on the PUPS ALERT)
CONFINEMENT DATE: 03131998
RECEIPT DATE: REPORT DATE: 03141998
(Correction to PUPS Record):
CONFINEMENT DATE: 03131998
RECEIPT DATE: 03141998 REPORT DATE: 03141998
