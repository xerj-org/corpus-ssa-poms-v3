D. Overview of IDD for French Polynesia
IDD payments sent to French Polynesia must be in CFP Franc (XPF) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that territory.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
