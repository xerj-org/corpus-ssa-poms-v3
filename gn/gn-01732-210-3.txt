SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01732.210 - Preparing Claims Packages for Claims Filed in Finland
Effective Dates: 02/08/1995 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

3. U.S. and Finnish or Finnish claim only
Prepare a claims package for a U.S. and Finnish or Finnish claim only if:
•
A Finnish coverage record is required and has not been requested by the RFSP; OR
•
A Finnish agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Form SSA-2960-U3-FI
•
Information or documentation requested by the Finnish agency.
