SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.001 - Overpayment Notice Modifications - Debtor Outside U.S.
Effective Dates: 04/25/2018 - Present | TN 15 (08-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure-Notice
Send all overpayment notices by direct mail to the person from whom recovery is sought
with the following exceptions:
1. GREECE
If adjustment is proposed:
a.
Send copy of notice via Form OF-41 to:
Federal Benefit Unit
American Embassy
APO New York 09255-0006
b.
Annotate Form OF-41, “For Your Information.”
If refund is the sole means of recovery, see 8. below.
2. PHILIPPINES
If refund is the sole means of recovery:
a.
Send original and one copy of notice via OF-41 to:
American Embassy
1201 Roxas Boulevard
Ermita, Manila 0930
Philippines
b.
Annotate OF-41, “For Transmission to Addressee.”
NOTE: The American Embassy will add a footnote explaining the equivalent amount in pesos
(at the current exchange rate) to be refunded before mailing the notice to (or personally
contacting) the debtor.
3. HONG KONG AND MACAO
Send any overpayment notice via Form OF-41 to:
Federal Benefits Unit
Hong Kong
Box 30
FPO San Francisco, CA 96659-0002
Annotate Form OF-41 “For Transmission to Addressee.”
4. TURKEY
Depending on the servicing Federal Benefits Unit (FBU), send an overpayment notice
via Form OF-41 to:
Consular Section
Adana
APO New York, NY 09289,
or
Consular Section
Ankara
APO New York, NY 09257-0006,
or
Consular Section
Istanbul
APO New York, NY 09380,
or
Consular Section
Izmir
APO New York, NY 09224
Annotate Form OF-41 “For Transmission to Addressee.”
5. THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA (PRC)
Depending on the servicing FBU, send an overpayment notice via Form OF-41 to:
Consular Section
Beijing
Box 50
FPO San Francisco, CA 96655
Consular Section
Guangzhou
Box 100
FPO San Francisco, CA 96655-0002
Consular Section
Shanghai
Box 200
FPO San Francisco, CA 96655
6. NICARAGUA
Send notice via Form OF-41 through the FBU addressed to:
Consular Section
American Embassy
APO Miami 34021
7. IRAN
Send notice through the FBU in Germany. See GN 00905.210.
8. COUNTRIES TRAINED IN CLAIMS-TAKING AND DEVELOPMENT
If refund is the sole method of recovery:
a.
Send the overpayment notice directly to the (GN 00904.225) debtor.
b.
Send a copy (via Form OF-41) to the FBU, with a copy of Form SSA-3560, Instructions
Regarding Overpayments of Social Security Benefits.
The FBU, based on workload and/or clientele, will determine the appropriate followup
contact.
