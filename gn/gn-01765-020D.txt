SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01765.020 - Romanian Social Security Benefits – Eligibility Requirements
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201765020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Survivor benefits
Survivors may receive benefits provided the deceased person had been entitled to an
old-age or disability benefit at the time of death or met all the requirements for
entitlement, except for age attainment, at the time of death
1. Surviving spouse
To receive benefits, a surviving spouse must have been married to the deceased worker
for at least ten years at the time of death and have reached the standard retirement
age or have an income that is less than a statutorily defined amount.
2. Disabled surviving spouse
A disabled surviving spouse must have been married to the deceased worker for at least
one year.
3. Children
The following categories of children may receive benefits.
•
Children of the deceased worker under age 16
•
Children of the deceased worker ages 16-26 and attending school or an apprenticeship
•
Disabled children of the deceased worker at any age, provided their disability began
prior to age 16 or age 26 if in school or an apprenticeship.
4. Death grants
Romania will pay a death grant to an eligible family member of a deceased worker if
the deceased worker was receiving a benefit or was covered under the Romanian system
at the time of death.
