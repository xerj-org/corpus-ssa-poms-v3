C. Policy – Dependents of retired or disabled workers
1. Spouse
There is no provision for spouses’ benefits. However, a supplement may be paid to
the worker if the worker receives the minimum benefit and has a spouse.
2. Divorced Spouse
There is no provision for divorced spouses’ benefits.
3. Children
There is no provision for children’s benefits. However, workers with limited income
may receive a supplement for each child under age 18 or disabled.
