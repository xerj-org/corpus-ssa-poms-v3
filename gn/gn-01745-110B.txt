B. Policy - Taxes Included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Japanese Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors and disability benefits
and, in some cases, the health insurance program. The agreement doesn’t apply to the
National Pension Fund or the Employees’ Pension Fund, which are corporate funds under
which both participation and contributions are voluntary (see NOTE in GN 01745.010).
