E. Procedure for recording opinions
During the interview, a claimant or other person may express a relevant opinion.
1. Signed statement obtained
a.
If possible, include the opinion in the form of a statement signed by that person.
Procedure for evaluating statements, see GN 00301.305C (in this section). The signed statement should include an explanation of the basis
for the opinion.
b.
Fax the SSA-795 into Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) or the Evidence Portal
(EP) to retain the signature in an electronic format. Do not retain the paper document.
For more information on retention of paper material after faxing into either the eDIB
folder or claims folder in the EP, refer to POMS GN 00301.322.
2. Signed statement not obtained
a.
Include the information on a report of contact (RC).
b.
Take care to record, as accurately as possible, what the interviewed claimant or person
said. For accuracy, we should quote the claimant or other person’s exact words on
the RC.
