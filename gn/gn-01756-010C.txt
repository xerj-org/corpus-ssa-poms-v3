SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01756.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Benefits Unit in Kraków, Poland under the U.S. - Hungary Totalization Agreement
Effective Dates: 09/04/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201756010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Hungarian Claim
Refer all inquiries for Hungarian benefits from a resident of the Hungarian Republic
received in the FBU in Kraków, Poland to the Hungarian liaison agency.
If an applicant contacts the Kraków, Poland FBU to file for Hungarian benefits, the
FBU will send the Hungarian liaison agency the following:
•
Form SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3 (U.S-Hungary Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
to certify the filing date for the Hungarian benefits;
•
U.S. coverage record;
•
Supporting evidence for non-disability claims; and,
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
For Hungarian disability claims submitted in the U.S., claimants must obtain medical
evidence from U.S. health care providers and submit the evidence to the appropriate
Hungarian authorities.
If the Hungary liaison agency needs a U.S. certified earnings record, entitlement
information, or both to process a claim for Hungarian benefits, it will request the
information directly from the Kraków, Poland FBU using a Form SSA-e2960 HU-USA 3 (U.S.-Hungary
Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification form).
