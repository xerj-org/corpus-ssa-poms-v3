B. Slovak partial disability eligibility requirements
To qualify for partial disability benefits, a worker must:
•
have between a 41-69 percent reduction in the capacity to work; and
•
meet the length of coverage requirement based on age at time of disability.
NOTE: A person whose disability began prior to age 20 needs less than 1 year of coverage,
while a person aged 45 or over needs at least 15 years of coverage.
