D. Handling mature IBCs
If the IBC comes due; i.e., the CA has not authorized the RSDI claim nor sent the
teletype to the FBU in Rome, the LC obtains the folder and refers it to the claims
authorizer technical assistant (CATA) for action. The LC must annotate and date the
IBC with “Referred to CATA on (current date),” and file it in the back of the IBC box.
