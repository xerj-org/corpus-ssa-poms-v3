A. Definition of Lawful Permanent Resident
An alien is a lawful permanent resident (for IRS purposes) if he/she has been lawfully admitted to the U.S. for permanent
resident in accordance with U.S. immigration laws and that status has not been administratively
or judicially determined to have been abandoned.
