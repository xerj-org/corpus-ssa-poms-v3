SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.060 - Claims Leads Based on Liaison Forms from Foreign Agencies
Effective Dates: 01/24/2017 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Preview
The following sections provide instructions on how to identify and process claims
leads when the foreign agencies forward:
•
a signed statement of intent to file for benefits,
•
a liaison form with a checkblock which indicates the claimant wants to file, or
•
a liaison form with no checkblock or a checkblock that is not completed.
Reminder: BETs should consider the possibility of a U.S. claim or claims lead on every assistance request received in DIO.
