GN 01758.320 Brazilian Medical Exam Reimbursement Form
The Brazilian liaison agency the National Social Insurance Institute (INSS) will use
the USA/BR 3 (Application on Recovery of Incurred Cost Based on Agreement on Social
Security between Brazil and the United States of America) for reimbursement of medical
expenses regarding requests for exams.
