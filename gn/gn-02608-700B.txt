SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.700 - Exclusion of Military Reserve Pensions from the GPO Provision
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608700 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
1. New Claims
For new claims (including advance filed claims), the FO should:
a.
Determine (during the interview) that the pension is based on military reserve service.
b.
Answer question (16-M) on the ADDB screen “NO”.
c.
Document the RMKS in the application screen path “claimant exempt from GPO - receives
military reserve pension.”
d.
Obtain a statement from the employer (or pension paying agency) or a pay stub showing
the pension is based on military reserve duty.
2. Postentitlement Cases
For postentitlement cases:
a.
The FO should:
•
Follow evidence requirements in GN 02608.300.
•
Send all documentation to the PC and annotate on the routing slip that “GPO does not
apply - receives military reserve pension.”
b.
The PC should:
•
Remove the GPO and adjust the benefit.
•
Send a notice that the benefit has been adjusted (include additional paragraphs as
necessary)
