2. Regulation No. 1 (20 CFR Part 401)
Regulation No. 1 (20 CFR Part 401) is SSA's regulatory authority for disclosure of personal information. The basic
policies for disclosure discussed in GN 03301.004B. have been taken into account in the regulation, including the provisions of the PA
and FOIA.
