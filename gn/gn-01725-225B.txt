SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.225 - Requests for Status of Application for Dutch benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 3 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Dutch claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Dutch benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Dutch agency, check the PPC system. NOTE:
Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Netherlands
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Netherlands, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Netherlands
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Dutch agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Dutch agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Ministerie van Sociale Zaken en
Werkgelegenheid (Ministry of Social Affairs and Labor)
•
Sociale
Zekerheid (Social Security)
•
Sociale
Verzekeringsbank (The Social Insurance Bank)
•
Uitvoering
Werknemersverzekeringen (National Institute for Social Security)
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Dutch claim after a technician forwards
the claim to the Netherlands. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
