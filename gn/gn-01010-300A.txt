A. Policy on handling unknown address
When a benefit entitlement is established but the claimant’s whereabouts become unknown,
make every effort to locate them. Query the Master Earnings File (MEF), the Supplemental
Security Record (SSR) or contact employers, friends, neighbors, etc., for leads to
the claimant's whereabouts. If after all the development you cannot determine the
claimant's address, follow the instructions below.
