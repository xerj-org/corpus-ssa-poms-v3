SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.015 - Voluntary Coverage Under the German System
Effective Dates: 05/28/1997 - Present | TN 4 (07-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
Persons who are not subject to compulsory contributions and who meet certain qualifying
conditions may make voluntary Social Security contributions to build future benefit
rights. As a general rule, a person must be a German citizen or resident to be permitted
to make voluntary contributions.
