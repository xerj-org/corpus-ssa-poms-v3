B. Application process for Slovenian benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) field offices (FO) will accept applications
for Slovenian benefits with or without the special Slovenian application forms. After
receiving a claim for Slovenian benefits filed with SSA, the Slovenian agency will
mail the appropriate Slovenian forms to the applicant, if necessary. The Slovenian
agency will include instructions for the applicant to complete the forms and mail
directly to the Slovenian agency along with any additional required evidence.
