B. Process
Currently, TPWU holds a contract with a translation company. Languages not listed
in Section GN 00301.355D. are contracted out. Field offices (FOs) and processing centers (PCs) should use
local translators when available and follow the translator priority lists shown in
GN 00301.330D.1. or GN 00301.330D.2. before sending translations to TPWU. If a translator higher on the priority lists
is not available for a particular language, send the request to TPWU.
