B. Process
Luxembourg may ask OIO for evidence to verify these periods that have occurred while
the worker was in the United States.
When the Luxembourg agencies need verification of periods of education, child-rearing
and prior periods of disability, they request specific information in the “Remarks” section of form LUX/USA 3. For example, when the agencies want to establish periods
of child-rearing, they request the birth certificates of the children.
