C. Description of coverage record
The following is a description of the items on the Luxembourg coverage record:
1. Identifying Information
The top of the form contains the worker's name and Luxembourg social insurance number.
2. Periods of Coverage
•
The column titled “ANNEE” shows the first year in which there is coverage and every year thereafter.
•
The column headed “JRS” shows days of coverage for the year shown.
•
The column headed “MOIS” shows months of coverage for the year shown.
