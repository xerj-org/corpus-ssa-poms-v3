SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.456 - Coding Maldives Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/03/2022 - Present | TN 213 (05-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402456 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Maldives will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “L” assigned for Maldives.
