SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.005 - Representative's Fees Subject to SSA's Authorization
Effective Dates: 07/29/2004 - Present | TN 11 (07-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy - Fee Authorization Required
A representative, attorney or non-attorney, must obtain SSA's authorization to charge
and collect a fee for services provided in proceedings before SSA irrespective of
whether (among other things):
•
the services result in an allowance, reinstatement, or disallowance action by SSA;
•
the attorney/non-attorney was ever recognized by SSA as a claimant's representative,
or the individual did not deal directly with or actually contact SSA; or
•
the fee is charged to or collected from the claimant or a third party (e.g., an insurance
company), unless the requirements in GN 03920.010B. are met.
