B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Registrar General’s Department where the event took place for birth, marriage,
and death records in person or online at http://agla.gov.tt. Visit http://agla.gov.tt.home-contact-us/ for the addresses of the Registrar General's Department. For divorce records individuals
contact the Registrar of the Supreme Court, Halls of Justice, Knox Street, Port of
Spain, Trinidad.
NOTE:
While, Trinidad and Tobago considers these documents as certified evidence, we do
not accept the documents unless the custodian subsequently affixes a signature, stamp,
or seal with a statement attesting to the accuracy of document.
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name at birth
•
Date and place (including the district) of birth
•
Parent's maiden name
•
Parents' full names
3. Fees
•
Birth, death, marriage - TT $25.00
•
Divorce - TT $10.00 if for use in Trinidad and Tobago, $20.00 if for use outside Trinidad
and Tobago
Online services have convenience fees, one certificate and registered post - $3.18
TTD and delivery via registered post within Trinidad and Tobago - $25.00 TTD.
