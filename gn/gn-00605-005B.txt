SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.005 - PC and FO Responsibilities For Accounting
Effective Dates: 05/26/2023 - Present | TN 72 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
1. PC Duties and Responsibilities
The PCs:
a.
Receive actionable accounting reports from WBDOC to resolve blank, discrepant and
unacceptable responses through DIRCON or FO development.
b.
Input title II conserved funds (see GN 00605.050B.1.) if the amount changed after PC development or WBDOC indicates that they did not
enter (see GN 00605.040B).
c.
Request the FO input (or delete) title XVI (and concurrent) conserved funds as appropriate.
EXAMPLE: The PC develops the “use of benefit” questions on the SSA-623-OCR-SM accounting report.
The payee reports they used all ($4,000) of the benefits (i.e., $3,900 = total benefits
paid plus $100 = conserved funds as reported on last year's accounting form) for the
beneficiary's current needs; there are no (zero) conserved funds. The FO must delete
the $100 from the CF field in the REPY segment of the SSR so that the accounting report
for the next accounting cycle shows “zero” conserved funds in Item 3.
d.
Clear the title II system control if the accounting report was not processed by WBDOC
(see GN 00605.050B.1.).
e.
“Approve” completely processed accounting reports (e.g., unacceptable response was
resolved). Stamp/mark the report APPROVED, sign and date.
f.
Retain “approved” accounting reports (see GN 00605.055B).
g.
Correct payee coding and/or mailing address on the MBR and eRPS.
h.
Review the collective account precedent file in eRPS (see MS 07418.004 and MS 07418.006).
FO development is required if the payee has a collective savings/checking account
but the payee is not listed on the precedent file.
2. FO Duties and Responsibilities
The FOs:
a.
Respond to PC requests for assistance to resolve unacceptable responses, etc. on the
accounting reports.
b.
Input title II conserved funds (see GN 00605.050B) if the amount changed after FO development.
c.
Input (or delete) title XVI (and concurrent) conserved funds (see GN 00605.050B).
EXAMPLE: The PC develops the “use of benefit” questions on the SSA-623-OCR-SM accounting report.
The payee reports they used all ($4,000) of the benefits (i.e., $3,900 = total benefits
paid plus $100 = conserved funds as reported on last year's accounting form) for the
beneficiary's current needs; there are no (zero) conserved funds. The FO must delete
the $100 from the CF field in the REPY segment of the SSR so that the accounting report
for the next accounting cycle shows “zero” conserved funds in Item 3 (see SM 01301.475).
d.
Process the accounting reports to completion when the payee comes into the FO and
requests assistance (see GN 00605.030B.3.). Clear the title II system and title XVI (and concurrent) AR diary control and
input reported conserved funds (see GN 00605.050B).
e.
Process the nonresponder alerts to completion (see GN 00605.085). Clear the title
II system and title XVI (and concurrent) AR diary control and input reported conserved
funds (see GN 00605.050B.).
f.
Conduct face-to-face interviews with the payee, beneficiary and custodian (if appropriate)
to complete a Representative Payee Evaluation Report, SSA-624-F5, when necessary (see
GN 00605.100).
g.
“Approve” completely processed accounting reports (e.g., unacceptable response was
resolved). Stamp/mark the report APPROVED, sign and date.
h.
Forward “approved” accounting reports to the appropriate PC for retention (see GN 00605.055B).
i.
Correct payee coding and/or payee mailing address and/or beneficiary residence address
on the SSR/MBR/eRPS.
j.
Inspect the records of institutions, agencies and individual payees with approved
collective savings/checking accounts every 3 years (e.g., in conjunction with a scheduled
site visit or random review under the Expanded Monitoring Program) to ensure compliance
with the criteria described in GN 00603.020. Establish new payees with approved collective savings/checking accounts to the collective
account precedent file in RPS, update existing data via an alert process, delete and
query as appropriate (see MS 07418.002).
