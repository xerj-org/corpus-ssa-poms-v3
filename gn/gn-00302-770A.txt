A. Normal Time for Census Requests
If the claimant furnishes sufficient information to allow a successful search of the
census records, the result will be furnished in most cases within 14 days after receipt
by the Personal Census Services Branch. It will rarely be more than 4 weeks before
the record (or notice that it cannot be located) is received. To avoid a delay, be
sure there is an actual signature, not a typed name or a blank, authorizing payment.
