B. Claims Representative use of the SSA-2490-BK
Use the MCS version of the SSA-2490-BK only when the number holder (NH) is filing
for
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits only, or
•
U.S. retirement or disability benefits (regular or Totalization) and concurrently
for Czech benefits.
You must use a paper or electronic (e-form) SSA-2490-BK for Totalization survivor
claims. Do not use the MCS version. When there is a previously approved Totalization
life case award, it is not necessary to take an SSA-2490-BK in claims for U.S. auxiliary
benefits in life cases or survivor cases, since this application would have been completed
with the NH’s claim.
