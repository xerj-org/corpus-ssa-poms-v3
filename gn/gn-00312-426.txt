GN 00312.426 Venezuela – Vital Statistics Records
Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
Each state has a Principal Registry. However, since it can take 5 to 10 years for
events to be passed to that source, documents are not generally requested from that
source. The number, book and year the document was filed is needed to obtain a copy
of the registration.
