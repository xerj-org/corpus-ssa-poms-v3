SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01747.145 - Exception to Section 202(t)(11) Alien Nonpayment Provision under the Danish Agreement for Dependents and Survivors
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 3 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201747145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a non-U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the 5-year U.S. residency
requirements, develop citizenship or residence status as follows.
If The Person Alleges
Then
The person is a resident of Denmark
Develop residence status.
The person is a citizen of Denmark residing in another Totalization country
Develop residence status.
The person is a citizen of a Totalization country other than Denmark or Australia
Develop citizenship status.
The person is a citizen of Australia
Develop citizenship and residence status for purposes of the 5-year U.S. residence
rule.
NOTE: The 5-year U.S. residence rule applies to Danish and Australian citizen dependent
and survivor beneficiaries’ residing outside the United States, Denmark, Australia,
or another Totalization country.
