C. Waiver of Fee
The fee which normally accompanies the Form G-639 may be waived by DHS. The decision
to waive the fee appears largely judgmental. For this reason, it is important for
the applicant to explain as clearly as possible why the fee should be waived; e.g.,
simply stating “I am an SSI claimant” may be sufficient.
