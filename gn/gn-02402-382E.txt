E. Description of Bulgaria IDD bank data
The data to code IDD for Bulgaria comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary by his or her FI.
The full length of the IBAN for Bulgaria consists of 22 characters.
However, the bank data on the MBR consists of 19 characters including Bulgaria’s country
code of G.
