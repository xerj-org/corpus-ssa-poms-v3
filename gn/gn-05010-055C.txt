C. Policy - Last Year of Residency
An alien is not a U.S. resident during any part of a calendar year if that:
•
Part is after the last day on which he was present in the U.S. (or was a lawful permanent
resident);
•
Individual had a closer connection to a foreign country than the U.S. during that
part of the year. (up to 10 days of actual presence in U.S. can be disregarded for
which it is established he had a closer connection to another country); and
•
Individual was not a U.S. resident at any time during the next calendar year.
Assume an individual leaving the U.S. and not claiming resident alien status is a
nonresident alien effective with the day after the day of departure.
