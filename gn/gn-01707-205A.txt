SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits
Effective Dates: 05/16/1991 - Present | TN 3 (04-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. General
When a person files for U.S. totalization benefits under the agreement with Germany,
the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish a
claim for regular RSDI (nontotalization) benefits. In addition, the applicant must
submit the evidence in GN
01702.200 - GN 01702.230 and may wish to submit the evidence described in GN 01707.205A.2.
2. Unverified periods of German coverage
Although most German coverage records are complete, some workers may be entitled to
credit for certain unverified periods which are not shown on their German records.
In order to receive credit for any unverified periods, the claimant must submit evidence
that the German agency can use to correct its records.
