E. Additional coverage record information
The National Social Security Agency (NSSA) forwards all request for Danish earnings
to the Labour Market Supplementary Pension (ATP) agency. This agency retrieves the
earnings records requested and will forward to SSA only twice a month with any other
information that was requested.
If a person living in the U.S. needs their own data from ATP it is still possible
for the individual to contact ATP directly through www.atp.dk (direct secure mail) or by phone at + 011-45-4820-4923. Questions from the U.S. must
include name and Danish CPR.NR (or date of birth). Information from ATP will be sent
by mail including datasheet (Excel) which will include the name and the CPR number.
