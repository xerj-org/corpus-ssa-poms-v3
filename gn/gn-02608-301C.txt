SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.301 - Alerts from OPM/MBR Match (GPO)
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608301 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - development
1. FO action
a.
If the spouse protests the action, secure evidence to substantiate an allegation that the action to be taken is incorrect.
b.
If the spouse is uncooperative:
•
Assume the OPM data are correct and apply the offset.
•
Document the facts and send them to the PC.
2. Contacting OPM
Contact OPM if information is needed to resolve the alert (e.g., historical data)
or the pension data must be corrected.
a.
Send correspondence to the Office of Personnel Management, Employee Service and Records
Center, Claims Correspondence, Boyers, PA 16017. Include the person's CSA #, DOB,
the person's SSN and the exact nature of the request.
b.
Control for 60 days. When the information is received, forward all development to
the PC with a determination substantiating the imposition of GPO or that GPO does
not apply.
