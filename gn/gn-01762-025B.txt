B. Procedure for requesting information from the Uruguayan Liaison Agency
Transmit to, or request information from, the Uruguayan liaison agency using the Totalization
Data Collection Program (TDCP) Form pre-addressed to:
Banco de Prevision Social
Colonia 1881 Piso 2
C.P. 11200
Montevideo, Uruguay
