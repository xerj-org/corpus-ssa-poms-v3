Effective Dates: 02/08/2023 - Present | TN 6 (02-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201739145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. No U.S. residency requirement under the agreement
Dependents and survivors are not required to meet the 5-year U.S. residency requirement
if they:
•
are citizens or nationals of Chile regardless of where they reside; or
•
are residents of Chile regardless of their citizenship or nationality.
