B. Application process for Icelandic benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) field offices (FO) will not provide the applicant
with the special Icelandic Application Form. After receiving a claim for Icelandic
benefits filed with SSA, the Icelandic agency will contact the applicant directly,
if necessary, to provide instructions for the applicant to complete and mail any other
form(s) directly to the Icelandic agency along with any additional required evidence.
