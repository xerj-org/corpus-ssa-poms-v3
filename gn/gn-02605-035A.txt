A. INTRODUCTION
A COA for a SSI recipient may mean a change in circumstances that can affect eligibility
and payment amount. Therefore, when an SSI recipient or a dually entitled SSI/RSDI
beneficiary requests a COA, there are special considerations in addition to the requirements
in GN 02605.001. (Also see SI 02306.010 (SSI policy and procedures for SSI COA) and SM 01301.385C. (the effects of death
and COA in SSI program).)
