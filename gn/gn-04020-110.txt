GN 04020.110 Unrestricted Reopening - Criminal Conviction Affecting Benefit Rights — Policy Principle
We can reopen at any time if:
A claimant previously entitled to benefits is convicted of a crime that affects their
right to receive benefits or their entitlement to a period of disability; or
A claimant's conviction of a crime that affected their right to receive benefits or
their entitlement to a period of disability is overturned.
See DI 27510.005.
