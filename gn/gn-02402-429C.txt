SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.429 - Coding Uganda Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 06/12/2019 - Present | TN 183 (01-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402429 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Uganda has a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the Routing and
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “Z” assigned for Uganda.
