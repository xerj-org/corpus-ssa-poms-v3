B. Definitions related to name discrepancies involving evidence
1.
Given name: All, or any part, of a name (including a middle name) except the surname.
2.
Immaterial (minor) discrepancy: The name discrepancy is obvious, easily explainable, and raises no doubt that the
record pertains to the claimant.
3.
Material (major) discrepancy: The name on the record is so different from the one the claimant now uses that it
raises doubt about whether the document pertains to the claimant.
4.
Surname: The family name or last name that distinguishes members of one family from members
of another.
