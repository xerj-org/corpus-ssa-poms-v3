GN 01732.310 Finnish Requests for Medical Examinations
OIO will forward whatever medical evidence is in its possession or is submitted by
the claimant. OIO will not request medical evidence from other PSC's. If requested,
the claimant will have to submit new evidence himself to the Finnish agency. Finland
is unlikely to request OIO to arrange for consultative examinations (CE) on their
behalf because of the need to reimburse. If you receive a request for a CE, forward
the case to IOTS.
