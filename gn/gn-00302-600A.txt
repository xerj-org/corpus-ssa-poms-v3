SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.600 - Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) Records
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
During the 1930's and early 1940's, the CCC (a Federal agency) employed 2 1/ 2-3 million
men (primarily young men) to perform public work related to conservation and the development
of natural resources. CCC records generally show the specific DB of former employees.
These records maintained by the Natural Civilian Personnel Records Center, 111 Winnebago
Street, St. Louis, Missouri 63119.
