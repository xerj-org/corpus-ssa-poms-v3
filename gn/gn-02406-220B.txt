B. Handling Form FMS-1133 if received in the FO, TSC or Processing Center (PC)
The FO, TSC or PC will immediately scan all pages of the form into the Evidence Portal
and forward the completed FMS-1133 claims package to Treasury for review.
•
Treasury makes a forgery determination when the beneficiary, recipient or representative
payee files non-receipt timely and the check is 12 months or less.
•
Treasury makes a handwriting analysis determination when the beneficiary, recipient
or representative payees non-receipt claim is not timely filed and the payment is
over 12-months old (limited payability involved).
If a beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee inquires about the status of
a forgery or handwriting analysis determination, review TCIS.
