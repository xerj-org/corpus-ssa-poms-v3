D. Processing remittances by an entity
If an FO or PC technician receives a remittance from an entity the technician:
1.
Checks the record to verify the full excess fee amount that we assessed and the amount
that has been refunded.
2.
Processes the remittance. See the references below for remittance and returned fee
payment processing instructions.
3.
Documents the pending status of the remittance in a RASR ROC for each related representative,
if the Mid-Atlantic Program Service Center (MATPSC) Remittance and Accounting Unit
(RAU) has not processed it.
4.
After MATPSC RAU processes the remittance for Title II claims, queries the remittance
for manual DMS and PHUS annotation if an automated interface does not occur.
5.
Checks the IDPE list in RASR to determine whether there is an IDPE entry related to
this excess fee. If the excess fee appears on the IDPE list, email the IDPE Coordinator
Group and notify them of the amount remitted. Include proof of payment as an email
attachment.
6.
If the entity does not have an active entry on the IDPE list, the technician creates
an ROC in RASR for each related representative documenting the receipt and processing
of the remittance.
