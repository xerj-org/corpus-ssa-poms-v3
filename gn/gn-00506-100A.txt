A. Introduction
Only qualified organizations serving as payee may participate in the Fee-for-Service
(FFS) program. The field office (FO) authorizes the organization to collect a fee
for providing representative payee services. Authorized organizations must meet all
the criteria defined in the Social Security Act and SSA regulations.
