B. USDC important facts
The transition of paper checks to electronic payments created a unique need for an
alternative electronic payment method to provide IP or EAP payments. The issuance
and use of the USDC differs from paper checks. The following list provides some important
USDC facts:
•
USDCs are Treasury-sponsored, prepaid debit cards intended for one-time use.
•
No changes were made to the eligibility criteria when determining if a beneficiary
or recipient is entitled to an IP or EAP. The monetary limits for IPs and EAPs outlined
in policy have not changed.
•
IPs/EAPs will no longer be allowed without first establishing the payment within CPS
or MSSICS.
•
All post-issuance issues with a USDC will be handled through USDC customer services
(i.e. lost or stolen cards, fraud,). The customer service phone number is 1-800-341-6700.
•
USDCs are used exclusively for IPs and EAPs.
Administrative Payments will NOT be issued via USDC. They will be issued via Electronic
Funds Transfer (EFT). Please refer to guidance on Administrative Payments in the TPPS website.
