A. Policy for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
Japan has no single, nationwide repository of civil registration records. Records
are held at the local level in municipalilities, towns, or villages where the head
of the family had their legal residence at the time the event occurred. For most Japanese,
the Family Register is a single document serves as a birth, marriage, divorce and
death certificate, in addition to recording the names of one’s children.
Non-Japanese citizens records are maintained by the ward or city office where the
reports were filed.
