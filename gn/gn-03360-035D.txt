D. Procedure - Nonclaims Situations
For disclosures without consent in nonclaims situations (third party contacts in connection
with a wage record item or earnings discrepancy or disclosure without consent of an
SSN to an employer) take the following actions:
•
Annotate the wage record item or earnings discrepancy developments with the accounting
information listed in item A. above for such disclosures.
•
Annotate materials forwarded to DEBS for pre-entitlement filing, if appropriate.
EXCEPTION: For correspondence sent to DEBS, if no claims file exists, file the record of accounting
in the FO.
