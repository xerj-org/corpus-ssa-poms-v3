SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.106 - How to Evaluate Portuguese Coverage Certifications
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Check the coverage certification form for the following entries:
•
Worker's name
•
“FROM - TO” entry for each period in which there is an entry for months.
•
“MONTHS” entry for each “FROM - TO” period and “MONTHS” entry that is less than or equal to the number of months in the corresponding “FROM - TO” period.
•
Portuguese agency stamp, signature and date.
If any of the entries is not shown, route the case to IOTS for evaluation.
