A. Determining eligibility
The Agreement provides that Portugal will count a worker's U.S. coverage, where necessary,
when determining eligibility for Portuguese benefits under the general system or special
occupational systems if the worker:
1. Does not meet insured status based on Portuguese coverage alone; and
2. Has at least 12 months of Portuguese coverage.
