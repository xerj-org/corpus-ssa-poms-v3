A. Introduction - FODRL
The FODRL controls the receipt of fees, savings bonds as conserved funds, CMS lockbox
premium payments, unendorsed returned benefit checks, returned benefit checks voided
under limited payability, returned Medicare contractor's checks and Medicare overpayment
refunds.
