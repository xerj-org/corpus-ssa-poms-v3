C. Policy – SSA Payments Excluded from Tax Levy
IRS excludes some payments under the Social Security Act from the tax levy process.
IRS excludes the following payments:
1.
Title XVI payments;
2.
From the FPLP, Title II Disability payments;
NOTE: IRS and Fiscal Service excluded these payments from the FPLP in October 2015.
3.
Title II payments undergoing partial withholding;
•
Title II payments undergoing partial withholding to recover a debt owed to SSA;
•
Title II payments that IRS is already levying through the Form 668W or paper levy
process; and (For more information on the paper levy process, see GN 02410.100.)
4.
Title II payments with a PIC C, G, J, or K, see SM 00550.010.
