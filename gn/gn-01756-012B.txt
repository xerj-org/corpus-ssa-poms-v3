B. Procedure for FBU to process referrals from the Hungarian Liaison Agency.
This procedure explains how the Kraków, Poland FBU processes referrals from the Hungarian
liaison agency.
1. Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all material to the Division of International Operations (DIO) annotated “already
entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Process the claim in the same manner shown in GN 01756.010B.
