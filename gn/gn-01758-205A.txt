SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01758.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed Outside of Brazil
Effective Dates: 04/10/2019 - Present | TN 2 (04-19)

A. Procedure – Initial Claims Packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request a Brazilian certified coverage record and
to inform the Brazilian liaison agency the National Social Insurance Institute (INSS)
about claims for Brazilian benefits.
