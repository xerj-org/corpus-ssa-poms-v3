A. Introduction to special Polish application forms
In addition to Form SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under a U.S. International
Social Security Agreement), an applicant for Polish benefits may need to complete
one or more special Polish application forms. The form(s) to be completed vary depending
on the type of benefit claimed.
