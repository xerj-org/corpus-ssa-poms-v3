B. No CMA payable due to temporary development
1. Claims representatives or authorizers
The Manual Adjustment, Credit, and Award Process (MADCAP) and Modernized Claims System
(MCS) can process initial award cases with no CMA payable due to temporary development.
Post the Windfall (WF) code to the Benefit Continuity Factors (BECF) screen. MCS will
identify the situation based on other data and post WIN ‘Z’ when required. For a list
of WIN codes posted to the MBR, see SM 03005.330A and SM 00510.150C. For a description
of WIN codes posted to the Supplemental Security Income Data (SSID), see SM 00815.430.
2. Benefit Authorizers (BA)
For instructions on processing windfall offset when no CMA is payable due to temporary
development, see SM 00815.000.
