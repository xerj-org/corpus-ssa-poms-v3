SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.110 - How to Credit Swiss Coverage
Effective Dates: 06/25/1996 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The Swiss coverage certification is used to assign foreign (F) quarters of coverage
to the earnings record (ER).
•
Only the types of coverage listed in GN 01710.105C. are creditable.
•
Periods for which contributions were refunded under Swiss law prior to 1975 are not
creditable.
•
There is no minimum amount of Swiss coverage which must be earned before the U.S.
can use it.
