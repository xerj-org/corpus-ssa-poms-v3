SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.030 - Request for Change in Overpayment Recovery Rate, Form SSA-634
Effective Dates: 10/08/2024 - Present | TN 85 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Effective date of lower recovery rate
1. Request made prior to any adjustment
Initiate withholding effective with the Current Operating Month (COM).
2. Request made after full adjustment initiated
Process the request in the month the individual requests the rate change. If you process
the action later than the month the individual requested the rate change, pay the
difference between what we withheld and what we should have withheld based on the
request.
3. Lesser request made after partial adjustment initiated
Process the lesser amount effective with the COM.
