B. Crediting Finnish coverage
The Social Security Administration (SSA) will credit one U.S. Quarter of Coverage
(QC) for every 3 months of Finnish coverage in a year. Finland measures coverage in
months. However, SSA will not credit coverage for:
•
Any month in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC; or
•
More than 4 QCs for any calendar year.
