SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.349 - Coding Luxembourg Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 02/13/2018 - Present | TN 259 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402349 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Luxembourg will have a prefix of 66 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “M” assigned for Luxembourg.
