GN 03313.050 Disclosure to the Office of Personnel Management (OPM)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from OPM, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
OPM administers retirement and pension programs for Federal employees. These programs
meet our criteria for income maintenance programs and health maintenance programs.
We may disclose certain tax return and non-tax return information to OPM for the administration
of these programs. For more information concerning disclosure of tax return information
without consent to OPM, see GN 03320.015C.2.
