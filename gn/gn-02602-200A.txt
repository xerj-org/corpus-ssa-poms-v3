A. Introduction
The “HI System DOD” alert generates when the date of death (DOD) on the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR) differs from the DOD on the Health Insurance (HI) System. These alerts
require immediate resolution to ensure prompt and accurate payment of the HI Part
A medical bill. Medicare does not pay these bills unless the charges are for services
provided on or before the month, day, and year of death shown on the “HI” system.
