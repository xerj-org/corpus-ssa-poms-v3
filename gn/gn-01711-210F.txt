Effective Dates: 12/08/1994 - Present | TN 2 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure - Disability claims
In addition to taking the actions in GN 01711.210D., take the following actions when developing medical evidence for Norwegian disability
claims.
1. Norwegian Benefits Only
•
Accept any medical evidence the applicant wishes to submit. Do not attempt to secure
evidence for the claimant or offer advice as to what types of evidence are needed
by Norway.
•
Send any medical evidence that is submitted along with the claims file to OIO at the
following address,
Social Security Adminstration
OIO - Totalization
P.O. Box 17049
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7039
•
Transmit the file without medical evidence if it is not readily available. Advise
the claimant that the Norwegian agency will contact him or her, if necessary, to obtain
needed evidence.
2. Norwegian Benefits and Concurrent Title II Claim
Follow the instructions in GN 01702.400 - GN 01702.415 to process claims for Norwegian disability benefits and concurrent title II disability
claims.
