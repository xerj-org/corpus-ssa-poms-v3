C. Medical evidence from Ottawa/Quebec
Do not routinely request copies of medical evidence contained in Canadian agency files
even when you know that the claimant has filed for or is receiving CPP or QPP disability
benefits. The U.S. and Canada have agreed to develop evidence of disability independently
and not to request evidence contained in each other's files.
