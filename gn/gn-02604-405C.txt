C. Length of the administrative sanction period
A sanction period lasts for a specified period of consecutive months. The sanction
period is:
•
6 months for the first occurrence;
•
12 months for the second occurrence; and
•
24 months for each subsequent occurrence.
Once the sanction begins, it runs for the full term, even if there are intermittent
changes to the payment status during that sanction period. The only exception to this
nonpayment period is that the sanction ends with the month of death if a person dies
while in a sanction period.
