SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01716.006 - How the Totalization Modules Determine if Canada Needs a U.S. Earnings Record
Effective Dates: 05/17/2017 - Present | TN 1 (04-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201716006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A U.S. earnings record is NOT needed in the following situations:
•
the applicant is requesting CPP or QPP retirement benefits only,
•
the applicant is requesting death benefits and the worker died before August 1, 1984,
•
the applicant is requesting Old Age Security (OAS) benefits and alleges 22 or more
years of Canadian residence after he/she attained age 18,
•
the claim originated with Ottawa or Quebec. In this situation the foreign agency requests
the U.S. earnings record directly from DIO if they need it, or
•
the designated border FO has already sent a claims package which included a U.S. earnings
record.
