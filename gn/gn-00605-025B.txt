SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.025 - Changing The Month of Selection For Accounting
Effective Dates: 04/15/2016 - Present | TN 16 (10-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
You can change the Negotiated Accountability Month (NAM) following these procedures:
•
Title II initial claim, MS INTRANETERPS 020.001.
•
Title II postadjudicative changes, SM 01301.635 and SM 01301.647.
•
Input or change the NM in title XVI cases, SM 01301.635.
