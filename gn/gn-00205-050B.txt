B. Completing the military service questions on the Modernized Claims System (MCS)
Until the MCS ADDB screens are changed, follow these operating procedures.
1. Active U.S. military service after Sept 7, 1939 (Y/N)
This question now means “DID YOU HAVE ACTIVE MILITARY SERVICE PRIOR TO 1968?”
a. No military service requiring documentation
If the NH had no military service prior to 1968, answer, “NO” (N), to the ACTIVE MILITARY
SERVICE AFTER SEPT 7, 1939 question.
b. Military service requiring documentation
If the NH had military service prior to 1968, answer, “YES” (Y), to the ACTIVE MILITARY
SERVICE AFTER SEPT 7, 1939 question.
2. NHMS and CLMS screens
Document only military service prior to1968 on the MCS NHMS AND CLMS screens.
3. DWO3 screen remarks
A message appears on the DWO3 screen if military service is not material to the claim.
4. Changes to the MCS printed application summary not required.
a.
No paragraph is added to the application summary if the ACTIVE U.S. MILITARY SERVICE
AFTER SEPT 7, 1939 question is answered, “NO” (N).
b.
If the ACTIVE U.S. MILITARY SERVICE AFTER SEPT 7, 1939 question is answered, “YES”
(Y) and only dates of military service prior to 1968 are documented on the NHMS or
CLMS screens, the paragraph on the application summary reflects the claimant’s actual
response.
NOTE: See GN 00204.055D for the downstream operating process as it applies to MCS applications
initiated via the Internet.
