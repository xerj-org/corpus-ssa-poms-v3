SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01750.110 - How to Credit the Czech Republic Certified Coverage Record
Effective Dates: 04/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Use the Czech Republic certified coverage record to assign foreign “F” quarters of
coverage to the U.S. coverage record.
•
There is no minimum amount of Czech coverage earned before the U.S. can use it.
•
SSA will credit one quarter of coverage in a calendar year for every 90 days of coverage
certified for that year by CSSZ.
•
All periods of coverage certified by CSSZ are creditable unless they are “Substitute Periods” – do not use these periods of coverage.
