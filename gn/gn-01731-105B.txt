B. Month of Entitlement
Periods of Finnish and U.S. social security coverage a worker earns, including those
earned prior to November 1, 1992, may be used to establish entitlement to U.S. or
Finnish Totalization benefits. However, the earliest possible month of entitlement
to such benefits is November 1, 1992.
