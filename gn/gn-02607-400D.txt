D. Negotiating reporting agreements
FOPCs are responsible for overseeing the reporting agreement negotiation process.
Depending upon regional practices, the FOPC or the parallel field office (FO) manager
negotiates reporting agreements with the appropriate representatives of correctional
and mental health institutions.
