GN 01070.300 Payment Center (PC) Contact With Public (Direct Contact--DIRCON)
This section explains a development procedure, Direct Contact (DIRCON), which permits
PCs to contact the public directly either by telephone or mail in initial claims or
post-entitlement situations.
