GN 01706.200 Applications Under Italian Agreement
Review the application form SSA-3954-BK-IT to ensure all questions are answered and
the application is signed. Enter the filing date in the block FOR SSA USE ONLY on
page 6.
It is important that all questions be answered and that the information, particularly
concerning Italian coverage periods, is complete because the SSA-3954-BK-IT sent to
INPS is the only application INPS will have in file. INPS will not obtain their national
application unless the claimant resides in Italy.
