B. Taxes under the Agreement
The provisions of the Agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to the following:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes, FICA and SECA, including the Medicare portion of those
taxes; and
•
Australia's Superannuation Guarantee employer contributions.
