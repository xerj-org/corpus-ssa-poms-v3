GN 01717.010 Overview of the Swedish Social Security System
Introduction
Sweden provides old-age, survivors and disability benefits through two separate programs:
•
a basic pension program that began in 1914 pays residence-based benefits, and
•
a supplementary pension program (ATP)implemented in 1960 pays earnings-related benefits.
