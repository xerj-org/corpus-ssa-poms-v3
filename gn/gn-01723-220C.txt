SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.220 - Additional Applications for French Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
If an applicant brings a French application form to the FO:
•
provide any necessary assistance in completing the form(s),
•
photocopy and certify any documents (evidence) the French agency has requested the
applicant to include with French claim, and
•
return the forms (and certified photocopies) to the applicant and advise him/her to
mail them directly to the French agency that sent them.
Reminder: Do not mail the French application forms to OIO.
