SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps for developing and processing applications.
1. U.S. Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1), Form E/USA 2 and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP and form E/USA 2 to the Instituto Social de
la Marina (ISM) or to the Instituto Nacional de Seguridad Social (INSS) to request
a Spanish coverage record.
(See GN 01722.045 for determining which Spanish agency office has jurisdiction of the claim).
Attach a copy of the worker's Spanish Navigation Book (If the worker was employed
at sea), and a copy of the applicant's DNI (National Identity Card).
•
Forward the claim to OIO with the file copy of the liaison form sent to the Spanish
agency.
2. Spanish Claim
Advise the applicant to file a Spanish claim at the local or central office of the
ISM or at the appropriate provincial office of the INSS.
3. U.S. and Spanish Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Parts I and II) and the appropriate RSDI application.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP to the ISM or to the appropriate provincial
office of the INSS to certify the date of filing and to request a Spanish coverage
record.
•
Forward the claim to OIO with the file copy of the liaison form sent to the Spanish
agency.
•
Advise the applicant to file a Spanish claim at the local or central office of the
ISM or at the appropriate provincial office of the INSS.
