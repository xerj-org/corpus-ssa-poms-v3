B. Purpose of the U.S. - Italian Totalization agreement instructions
This subchapter provides:
•
Information about the Italian social security system; and
•
Eligibility requirements for benefits under the Italian social security system and
under the agreement; and
•
Instructions on how to take and process claims for both U.S. and Italian benefits
filed under the agreement.
NOTE: For more information about how the agreement eliminates dual coverage, see sections
RS 02001.050 through RS 02001.055.
