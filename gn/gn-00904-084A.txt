SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.084 - FO Contact with Individuals Living Outside FO Service Area
Effective Dates: 09/18/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904084 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
The servicing FO may, in certain instances, contact individuals living outside the
FO service area by telephone or mail. This applies only to contacts within regional
boundaries. Contacts outside regional boundaries are to be made only as already provided
in specific instructions unless expanded by regional offices arranging reciprocal
agreements permitting direct contacts across regional boundaries.
