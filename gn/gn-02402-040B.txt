SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.040 - Where to Find Routing Transit Numbers (RTNs) and Depositor Account Numbers (DANs) on Account Documentation
Effective Dates: 02/08/2018 - Present | TN 94 (02-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Exhibits
1. MICR coding symbols
MICR is a special type font printed with magnetic ink on the bottom line of checks.
Dash symbol – this symbol will be found inside the DAN field and should be treated
as a dash (-) when you input the DAN to the system
2. Check with check serial number inside the DAN field
Look for the following information:
a.
RTN - In exhibit B.2, input “12345678”.
b.
Check serial number - In exhibit B.2, the check serial number, found in the upper right corner, is also
coded inside the DAN field. Do not input this number, if you find it.
c.
DAN - In exhibit B.2, input “12312345” for a title II case, and “123 1234 5” for a Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) case. Be sure to include the blank spaces in the number for
SSI. Do not put dashes in the DAN unless the dash symbol is shown (see exhibit B.3.).
d.
Account title - The name shown on the check should be preprinted or verified with the FI and must
meet the account title requirements in GN 02402.050.
3. Check with check serial number outside the DAN field
Look for the following information:
a.
RTN - In exhibit B.3, input “123456789”.
b.
DAN - In exhibit B.3, input “123-456-789”.
c.
Check serial number - In exhibit B.3, the check serial number is outside the DAN field, and you can ignore
it.
4. Payable through check
Look for the following information:
a.
Payable through notice.
b.
RTN - In exhibit 4, you would not input the RTN shown in the field because it does not
belong to the ODECO Federal Credit Union. Use other means of finding the correct RTN
for the ODECO Federal Credit Union (see GN 02402.035A.2.d.).
c.
DAN - In exhibit 4, you would input “123456789012”.
d.
Check serial number - In exhibit B.4, the check serial number is outside the DAN field, and you can ignore
it.
5. Account statement
Look for DAN in exhibit B.5, you would input “100349750-00-8”. The RTN is not shown
on an account statement, so use other means of finding the RTN (see GN 02402.035A.2.d.)
6. Savings account passbook
Look for DAN in exhibit B.6, you would input “25-00130-6”. The RTN is not shown on
a savings account passbook, so use other means of finding the RTN (see GN 02402.035A.2.d.).
