A. Definition of multiple entitlement
Multiple entitlements refer to claimants who are entitled to more than one Social
Security benefit at the same time. For example, a claimant may be entitled to Retirement
Insurance Benefits (RIB) on their own Social Security number (SSN) and as a spouse
on another SSN (dual-entitlement). In some situations, a claimant may be entitled
to benefits on more than two SSNs (for example, triple-entitlement).
