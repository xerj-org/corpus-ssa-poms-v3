Effective Dates: 01/23/2017 - Present | TN 2 (02-95)

A. When to use
Totalization technicians in DIO and FSP personnel in the American Embassy in Oslo
communicate with the FFU via Form SSA-2960-U2-NE. FFU personnel use a similar form
to communicate with SSA. Form SSA- 2960-U2-NE is used to:
•
transmit claims and related material to the FFU.
•
request information from the FFU, and
•
send information requested by the FFU.
