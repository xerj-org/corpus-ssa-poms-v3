SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01731.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits Under the Finnish Agreement
Effective Dates: 12/09/1997 - Present | TN 1 (12-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201731205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Agreement with Finland,
the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish a
claim for regular RSDI (nontotalization) benefits. In addition, the applicant must
submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 - GN 01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are being claimed from the United States.
