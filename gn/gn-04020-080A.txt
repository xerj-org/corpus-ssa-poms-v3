SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04020.080 - Unrestricted Reopening - Clerical Error or Error on the face of the Evidence
Effective Dates: 10/21/1996 - Present | TN 9 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204020080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principle
A final determination or decision unfavorable in whole or in part to the claimant may be reopened at any time when it is based on clerical error or error on the face
of the evidence. (A determination or decision is unfavorable where benefits are denied, benefits are
awarded in a lesser amount, deductions are imposed, etc.)
