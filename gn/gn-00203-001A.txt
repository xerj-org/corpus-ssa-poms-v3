A. Introduction
The information described in this subchapter provides general guidelines for technicians
to follow when discussing Title II and Title XVI benefit eligibility (GN 00203.002) or conducting an application interview (GN 00203.004). These guidelines ensure technicians:
•
Provide the information potential applicants require to make an informed filing decision,
and
•
Conduct an efficient application interview.
