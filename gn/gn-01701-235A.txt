Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701235 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. INTRODUCTION
Although unlikely, it is possible for an established savings clause maximum to affect
the amount of a Totalization benefit. This could occur where prior entitlement was
based on disability or currently insured status, but benefits are now payable based
on fully insured status that is met only by counting foreign coverage.
