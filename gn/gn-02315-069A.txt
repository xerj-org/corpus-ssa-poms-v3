A. Background on the New Mexico small estate statute
Sections 45-3-1201 and 45-3-1202 of the New Mexico Uniform Probate Code contain provisions
for the collection of property by affidavit. Sections 45-3-1203 and 45-3-1204 provide
a summary administration procedure for small estates.
