B. Procedure - Discussion during an in-person or telephone claims interview
The instructions regarding discussing the claimants complete marital history to identify benefit entitlement and situations where additional documentation
and evidence is required is unchanged. For iClaims situations, please see GN 00204.055L.
We ask marital information to determine eligibility for various benefits (i.e. spouses,
widow(er)s, etc.). See also instructions in GN 00205.145 through GN 00205.160. For a full discussion of proof of marital relationship, see GN 00305.000.
