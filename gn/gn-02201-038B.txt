B. Guidelines for handling problem cases
IRS has strict guidelines for handling problem cases. The most important guideline
is that contact between referring agencies and IRS is done through a single contact
point. SSAs Division of Collections (Collections), located in Financie and Management
(Accounting), is the contact point with the IRS for tax refund problem cases. Collections
is responsible for:
•
Referring problem cases to IRS. Collections may need to contact Treasury first for
an up to date Treasury Offset Program (TOP) contact.
•
Conducting regular case status follow-ups with IRS on the status of cases.
•
Advising our agency of resolutions and actions.
IMPORTANT: For problem cases, do not reverse the offset by refunding the amount to the debtor.
Collections will advise the payment center when any refunding or other corrective
action is necessary.
