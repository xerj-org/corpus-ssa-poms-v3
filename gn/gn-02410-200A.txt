A. Policy - Section 459 of the Act
Section 459 of the Act contains a specific exception to Section 207 so that title
II benefits are subject to legal process brought by an individual in a State court
for the enforcement of a legal obligation to provide child support and/or make alimony
payments.
NOTE: SSI payments are not subject to garnishment.
