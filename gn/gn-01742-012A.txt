SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.012 - How VARO Processes and Develops Claims Filed With the Korean Agency
Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a non-entitled person contacts the NPC to file a claim for U.S. benefits, that
agency routes form KOR-USA 2 (Liaison Form) to VARO. Periods of Korean coverage are
shown on this form.
If the NPC is aware that the person is already receiving U.S. benefits, they will
restrict the claim to Korean benefits only and request a U.S. coverage record directly
from OIO, if one is needed.
