SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.270 - Processing Cases Where Insured Status is Acquired Based on U.S. Coverage Only
Effective Dates: 06/02/2014 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703270 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - retirement cases
Use the following guidelines to determine the proper course of action when an NH acquires
nontotalization insured status.
1. Highest MBA
Determine the highest MBA:
a.
Compute the PIA and benefit payable based on U.S. coverage only.
b.
Compare the result of this calculation with the current totalization benefit. Do not
recompute the totalization benefit using earnings and quarters of coverage from the
year insured status is met or subsequent years.
2. Nontotalization MBA highest
If the nontotalization MBA is highest:
a.
Prepare amended award to pay nontotalization benefits effective with the month that
nontotalization insured status is met.
b.
Change TSC to T1.
c.
Obtain SMI election, if beneficiary is within four months of age 65.
d.
Notify beneficiary(ies) of new benefit amount and HI entitlement if age 65 or older.
e.
If all beneficiaries are U.S. residents, transfer RSDI folder, including all totalization
related material, and MBR to PSC. Delete TF folder.
f.
Complete, sign, and reverse file a folder documentation notice (see exhibit below
in GN 01703.270D) on the right side of the RSI or DIB folder. Reproduce these forms locally. Use the
remarks portion of this form to provide explanations/instructions.
g.
If any beneficiary is a foreign resident, transfer the RSDI folder to the appropriate
module.
3. Current Totalization MBA highest
If the current totalization MBA is highest:
a.
Change TSC to E2.
b.
Obtain SMI election, if beneficiary is within four months of age 65.
c.
Notify the beneficiary(ies) of automatic HI enrollment if age 65 or older.
d.
Send a notice only to the beneficiary who initiated the recomputation.
