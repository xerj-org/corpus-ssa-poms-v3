A. Process for requesting birth, death, marriage, and divorce verifications and certifications
from Pennsylvania (PA)
You can find the most up-to-date information from the Department of Health. In addition, you can find Employer Identification Number (EIN) information here.
The Division of Vital Records (DVR) branch offices accept walk-in requests for birth
($20.00) and death ($9.00) records. For a list of PA DVR branch offices, see GN 00308.072C (in this section).
There is no charge for field office (FO) verifications of birth and death records
or for certifications of death records. Certifications of birth records are $20.00.
