B. Requesting evidence from the NPRC
Contact the appropriate FO, depending on the individual’s date of separation, via
Form SSA-562-U2 or e562.
1. Military Records for 1973 and earlier
Request age information through the Florissant MO FO (FO Code E76):
Social Security
11753 West Florissant
Florissant, Missouri 63033
2. Non-Military Records for 1973 and earlier and all (Military and Non–Military) Records
for 1974 and later
Request age information through the East St. Louis IL FO (FO Code 486):
Social Security
Room 104 Federal Bldg.
650 Missouri Ave.
East St. Louis, Illinois 62201.
