D. Subsequent FO actions when adverse claim is allowed
1. Claimant Protests Adverse Claim
If after receiving the advance due process notice, the adversely affected beneficiary
contacts the FO within the 30-day protest period, advise the beneficiary of the following:
•
The right to make a formal written protest;
•
The right to have benefits continuing; and
•
The possibility of an overpayment (O/P) resulting if the adverse claim is sustained.
2. FO receipt of notification of protest
Upon receipt of the written protest, take the following action:
•
Immediately inform the PC by faxing it to the paperless unit. Include a report of contact (SSA-5200)
indicating whether evidence has been or will be submitted; and
•
Store all supporting evidence into the NDR.
3. Additional FO Action
If a formal determination results in an adverse decision, take any additional actions
necessary (e.g., accept a request for reconsideration, undertake O/P recovery development,
etc.).
