B. Procedure — State sources
1. Address and FO code
Office of Vital Statistics
Box 210
Jacksonville, FL 32231
FO Code 655
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from Vital Records. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of copy:
Birth $9.00, Death/divorce/marriage $5.00, additional copies of all records are $4.00.
3. Type of payment:
Money order or check.
4. Remarks:
The State office has divorce and marriage records since June 6, 1927. Additional year
searches are $2.00 per year. This fee includes a copy of the record, if found.
Can FO verify/certify the birth/death record? Yes
Can FO obtain a free copy of the birth/death record? No
