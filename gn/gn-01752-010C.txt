C. Polish Claim
Any inquiries for Polish benefits from a resident of the Republic of Poland received
in the FSP in Warsaw, Poland will be referred to either ZUS or KRUS.
If ZUS or KRUS needs U.S. certified earnings record and/or entitlement information
to process a claim for Polish benefits, it will request the information directly from
the FSP in Warsaw, Poland using a liaison form SSA-e2960-U3-PL.
