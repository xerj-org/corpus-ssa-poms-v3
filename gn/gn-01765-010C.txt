C. Periods of coverage earned before the effective date of the Totalization Agreement
The United States and Romania can use periods of U.S. and Romanian social security
coverage a worker earned prior to September 01, 2026 to establish entitlement to U.S.
or Romanian Totalization benefits. However, the earliest possible month of entitlement
to such benefits is September 01, 2026.
Additional information on the Romanian social security system can be found at the
Romanian social security web site, which is accessible through at Social
Security Online.
