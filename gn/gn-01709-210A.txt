SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01709.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for U.S. and Swiss Benefits
Effective Dates: 02/09/2022 - Present | TN 10 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201709210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence requirements
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the Agreement with Switzerland,
that person must submit the same evidence required to file a claim for regular retirement,
survivors, or disability insurance (non-Totalization) benefits. That person must also
submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States.
