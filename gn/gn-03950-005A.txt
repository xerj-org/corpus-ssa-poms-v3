A. Policy - How jurisdiction is determined
Jurisdiction to conduct administrative review under the fee petition process for claims
under Title II, Title XVI, or Title XVIII of the Act; or Title IV of the Black Lung
Benefits Act is determined by the office that made the initial fee authorization.
