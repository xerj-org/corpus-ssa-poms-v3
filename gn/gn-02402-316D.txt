D. Overview of IDD for Belgium
IDD payments sent to Belgium must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Belgium. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for additional information.
