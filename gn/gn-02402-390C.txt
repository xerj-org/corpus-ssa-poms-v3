SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.390 - Coding Bangladesh Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/20/2026 - Present | TN 324 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402390 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Bangladesh has a prefix of 68 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter O assigned for Bangladesh.
