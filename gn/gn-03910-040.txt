GN 03910.040 Appointment of a Representative
CITATIONS:
20 CFR 404.1710, 416.1507, and 416.1510
At any time during the administrative process, an individual who has a claim, post-entitlement
issue, or other interest in a matter pending before us can appoint a registered individual
to represent them. Claimants must use our standard form to appoint a representative,
see SSA-1696 (Claimant's Appointment of a Representative).
NOTE 1: In this section, we refer to individuals with claims, post-entitlement issues, or
other interests in matters pending before us as "claimants" unless context requires
a different specification.
NOTE 2: We refer to matters pending before us (e.g., claims and post-entitlement issues)
as "cases."
•
Before a representative may be appointed, they must register with us. For details
about registration for all representatives, see GN 03913.005.
•
For more information about the scope of an appointment, see GN 03910.040A.3.
•
For more information about who can be an appointed representative, and the authority
and responsibilities of a representative, see GN 03910.025.
IMPORTANT: Family members, friends, or other individuals do not need to be appointed or registered
as a representative when they provide only general assistance, such as interpreting,
filing forms, or transporting claimants to and from our offices. However, they must
register as a representative and be appointed if they want to take on the role of
a representative for the claimant, a spouse, or auxiliary beneficiaries, and gain
access to our electronic records. For information about access to electronic records,
see GN 03913.005.
If family members or friends want information about a case but do not want to represent
the claimant, they must have the claimant's consent and submit a properly completed
Form SSA-3288 (Social Security Administration Consent for Release of Information).
