A. Background on Title II paper folder storage
Title II paper claims folders are stored in the Social Security Administration National
Records Center (SSANRC).
1. Information Retrieval Groups (IRG)
The SSANRC and Mid-America Program Service Center (MAMPSC) operate the Information
Retrieval Group (IRG), which assists field offices (FO), Workload Support Units (WSU),
and Processing Centers (PC) by retrieving and supplying information and documents
from Title II paper claims folders.
The IRG is not
responsible for:
•
processing claims or post entitlement (PE) actions,
•
annotating or updating screens,
•
obtaining folders for the FO, WSU, or PC, or
•
providing voluminous copies of medical records.
Within five business days, the IRG responds to the requester by relaying the requested
information in the file by telephone, fax, email, or by uploading the material to
the electronic claims folder.
2. FO, WSU, and PC Instructions
FOs, WSUs, and PCs consider the following when requesting information through the
IRG process:
•
If the needed information is electronically stored (i.e., Evidence Portal (EP), Electronic
Folder, Master Beneficiary Record (MBR), Modernized Claims System (MCS), Evidence
(EVID) screens, etc.), do not contact the IRG.
•
Always obtain a Processing Center Action Control System (PCACS) query or Evidence
Portal (EP) query to confirm that an action is not pending in the PC. For example,
the Type Of Event Level (TOEL) may show that an exception to an FO input is pending
in the PC.
•
Review the PCACS query for the current folder location. Do not use the Folder Location Indicator (FLI) shown on the MBR, as this is not always accurate.
Do not rely on an old PCACS query as the folder may have moved.
•
If you need information from a stored folder, request the information from the IRG
prior to requesting the folder.
•
Use PCACS when you must obtain the paper claims folder.
•
If the PCACS response shows a folder location of "QUES", do not request information.
This indicator means that the folder is not in the SSANRC.
•
If you receive a query, letter, document, etc., that requires action by another office,
forward it to the appropriate office for processing. (If necessary, that office contacts
the IRG).
