A. Automatic status notice for an application for Austrian benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about an Austrian claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of
the worker’s U.S. coverage record) to Austria, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET)
electronically stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC)
system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Austrian agency. The system automatically
generates a claim status notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs
the applicant that the claims material was forwarded to Austria.
