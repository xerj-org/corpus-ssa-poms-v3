SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.011 - How the Luxembourg Agencies Develop Applications
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present |

B. Process
The following instruction discusses the applications and evidence obtained by the
Luxembourg agency offices that is forwarded to OIO.
1. U.S. Claim
If the claimant contacts a Luxembourg agency office to apply for U.S. benefits, the
agency obtains a signed statement of intent to file for U.S. benefits and forwards
it to OIO with a liaison form LUX/USA 3 and a Luxembourg coverage record.
2. Luxembourg Claim
If the Luxembourg agency needs a U.S. coverage record and/or entitlement data to complete
the Luxembourg claim, the agency requests the information from OIO using form LUX/USA
3.
3. U.S. and Luxembourg Claim
If the claimant contacts a Luxembourg agency to apply for both U.S. and Luxembourg
benefits, the agency obtains the required Luxembourg application form. The form asks
for an indication of work in another country. If the claimant indicates work in the
U.S., the Luxembourg agency notifies OIO using a liaison form.
The Luxembourg agency prepares and forwards the following material to OIO:
•
Liaison form LUX/USA 3, certifying the filing date and requesting a U.S. coverage
record and/or entitlement data if it is needed.
•
Luxembourg coverage record.
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
