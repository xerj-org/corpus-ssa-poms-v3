SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.105 - German Coverage Certifications
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The German agencies certify their coverage on either form D/USA 5 (Agreement Between
the FRG and the U.S. on Social Security) or on a computer printout, or both. A statement
that no German coverage exists is acceptable if it is on a liaison form or on the
official stationery of one of the German liaison agencies listed in GN 01708.025.
