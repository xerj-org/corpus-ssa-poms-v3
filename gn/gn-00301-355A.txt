A. Policy
The Translation and Priority Workload Unit (TPWU), formerly known as Central Translation
Section translates documents in-house in the languages shown in subsection D. below.
Documents in languages not listed below and documents that cannot be translated in
TPWU due to workloads are referred to an authorized contract translator. Translations
completed by a contractor are returned to TPWU for return to the requesting office.
