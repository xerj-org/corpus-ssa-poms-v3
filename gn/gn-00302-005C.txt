SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.005 - When a Deceased WE's Age Must be Established
Effective Dates: 03/10/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Claimant does not submit evidence
When the claimant is not able or does not wish to submit proof of age for the deceased
WE, do the following:
•
Use the DB in file which yields the lowest benefit, provided the WE is insured by
the DBs in file.
•
Document the file (with a Report of Contact (RC) or SSA-795) that the claimant does
not wish to furnish proof.
