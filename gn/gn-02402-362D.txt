D. Overview of IDD for Belize
IDD payments sent to Belize must be in Belize Dollars (BZD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Belize. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
