D. Overview of IDD for East Timor
IDD payments sent to East Timor must be in U.S. Dollar (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves East Timor. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
