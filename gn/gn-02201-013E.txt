E. $2,000 Administrative waiver tolerance
For information on how to process a waiver request when the overpayment is $2,000
or less and it is clear the overpaid beneficiary is without fault, see GN 02250.350. In addition, the Kansas City Region created a decision tree to assist in determining
when it is appropriate to apply this waiver tolerance. Use this link to access the
decision tree: Administrative Waiver Decision Tree.
