D. Policy — entitlement factors
The following indicates what factors of entitlement must be established in claims
for Greek benefits.
1. All Claims
The worker's coverage under the Greek system must be established for all Greek claims.
(See GN 01737.212.)
2. Retirement Claims
The additional entitlement factors that must be established for Greek retirement claims
are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Marriage, if a wife's supplement is claimed
c.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's supplement is claimed.
3. Survivors Claims
The additional entitlement factors that must be established for Greek survivors claims
are:
a.
Death of worker
b.
Marriage, if a widow(er)'s benefit is claimed
c.
Degree of disability if a widower's benefit is claimed
d.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's benefit is claimed.
4. Disability Claims
The additional entitlement factors that must be established for Greek disability claims
are:
a.
Age of worker
b.
Degree of disability (medical evidence)
c.
Marriage, if a wife's supplement is claimed
d.
Age and relationship of child, if a child's supplement is claimed.
