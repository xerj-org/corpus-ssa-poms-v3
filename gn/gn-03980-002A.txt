SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03980.002 - Bribery of Public Officials and Witnesses
Effective Dates: 09/25/2018 - Present | TN 1 (09-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203980002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions for terms used in this section
1.
“SSA official” means an officer or employee of SSA or a person acting for or on behalf of SSA.
2.
“Person who has been selected to be an SSA official” means any person who has been nominated or appointed to be an SSA official, or has
been officially informed that she or he will be so nominated or appointed.
3.
“Official act” means any decision or action on any question, matter, cause, suit, proceeding, or
controversy, which may be pending at any time, in such SSA official’s official capacity.
