A. General
Evidence of age for separated or deceased Federal Government civilian employees is usually available on their official personnel
records that the National Personnel Records Center (NPRC) maintains. Request age information
through the appropriate FO, and include the following information about the claimant
in the request:
•
Full name, and
•
SSN, and
•
Alleged DB, and
•
Most recent Federal employer (including the Department or Bureau and the Agency involved,
and
•
The most recent dates of Federal employment.
