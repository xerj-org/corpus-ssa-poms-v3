F. Description of Achieving a Better Life Experience (ABLE) Accounts
The ABLE account is a tax-advantaged savings depository account that individuals may
use to pay for qualified disability expenses. An eligible individual may establish
an ABLE account provided that the individual is blind or disabled by a condition that
began before the individual’s 26th birthday. For Title XVI, because we can exclude
up to $100,000 in the account, recipients remains eligible for payment (see SI 01130.740 for information on ABLE accounts). We permit direct deposit of both Title II and
Title XVI into ABLE accounts, as well as, Title II and Title XVI beneficiaries and
recipients that have representative payees. For more information on Direct Deposit
to representative payee cases, please see GN 02402.055.
NOTE: Title II and Title XVI payments are considered first-party contributions to an ABLE
account. The total annual amount of contributions to an ABLE account from all sources
is limited to the amount of the per-donor gift-tax exclusion in effect for a given
calendar year (Refer to SI 01130.740B.2
to obtain the current year’s contribution cap). ABLE account holders should be mindful
of the contribution cap if commingling Title II and Title XVI payments with other
contributions.
