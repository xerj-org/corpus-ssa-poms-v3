F. Name changes
If, at any stage of development, it is clear that the name of the claimant is the only corrected item, refer to GN 00302.460 instead of the procedures below. Thus, if the BC indicates on its face, or a subsequent
contact with the custodian reveals, that the claimant's name was the only corrected
item, follow GN 00302.460 to resolve the name discrepancy.
