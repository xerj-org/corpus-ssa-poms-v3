B. Procedure for checks mailed to a Foreign Service Post
The Foreign Service Post follows this process when receiving checks:
Step
Action
1
Annotate the check listing with the date of receipt.
2
Mail the checks and annotate the listing with the mailing date.
3
Code any undeliverable checks to indicate the reason for non-delivery.
4
Return the (coded) undeliverable checks to the Kansas City Regional Financial Center
(KFC).
5
In change of address cases, redirect checks for up to 3 months and ensure processing
of a change of address action. After 3 months, if the check still bears the incorrect
address despite inputs to correct it, contact the Office of Earnings and International
Operations (OEIO) for assistance. Ask OEIO to clear any exception and to input the
correct check address.
