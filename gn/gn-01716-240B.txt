B. Process — Form QUE/USA 2
Most entries on the form are self explanatory with the exception of those specifically
listed below:
1. Part 2
CERTIFICATION OF DATA - A) FAMILY MEMBERS, B) EVENTS
•
Quebec uses this section to certify data taken from their records pertaining to the
worker and his family. Also contained in this section is the date a disability onset
was established under QPP regulations.
•
These dates will be used without further verification unless there is a material discrepancy
with other evidence in file. (See GN 01701.210 regarding verification of eligibility factors by foreign agencies.)
2. Part 3
INFORMATION PROVIDED BY D.A.E.S.S.
•
Quebec checks the appropriate blocks to show information that they are providing with
this liaison form.
•
Periods of coverage - The periods of Canadian coverage are listed on the right hand
side of this section when someone has filed for U.S. benefits.
•
When coverage appears in this section for a year after the year of alleged disability
onset, RRQ should be contacted to determine if the coverage is based on work.
•
When a person has no coverage in Canada, Quebec enters a zero in the total block.
•
The filing date of the current application is hand written in the space to the right
of the heading, “Information Provided by D.A.E.S.S.”
3. Part 4
REMARKS
•
Quebec enters an earlier protective filing date in this section if the claimant had
previously filed for QPP benefits and indicated U.S. coverage on that application.
•
If the two filing dates would result in different months of entitlement, the date
which provides the greatest advantage to the claimant should be used.
4. Part 5
INFORMATION REQUIRED BY REGIE DES RENTES DU QUEBEC
•
Quebec has provided a statement at the beginning of this section which indicates whether
the information requested below is needed to process a claim under the agreement.
If “YES” is checked, this eliminates the need for a copy of the individual's consent before
we can release the information.
•
If no is checked, be sure a consent statement is obtained before releasing the information.
