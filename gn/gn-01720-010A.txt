SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01720.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 01/20/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201720010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The FSP in London take claims from individuals who wish to file for benefits under
the agreement and also assists in processing certain types of claims filed with the
U.K. social security agencies.
When taking applications under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices (See
GN 01702.001 - GN 01702.525 for FO and FSP development and GN 01719.205 - GN 01719.215 for specific evidence and application requirements for the agreement with the U.K.)
