C. Introduction
When a determination or decision is made with respect to entitlement, amount, or the
actual payment of benefits (GN 03101.070) the individual to whom that determination or decision applies should be able to
rely on its correctness. The regulations prescribe that determinations and decisions
made by SSA can be reopened and revised only for certain reasons and/or within certain
time periods.
