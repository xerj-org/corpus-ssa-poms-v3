SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.288 - What Information is Needed on EVID and the Report of Contact page in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) in PE Situations to Identify the Type of Evidence Submitted
Effective Dates: 08/15/2023 - Present | TN 105 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301288 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Types of Information to Be Coded In The “Comments” Field On EVID Or Recorded In The
“Report Text” Field On the Report of Contact Page in CCE
The “COMMENTS” field on EVID is a two-line free form field that appears at the bottom
of EVID. This field and the “REPORT TEXT” field on the Report of Contact page in CCE
should be used to record information about evidentiary documents that will be useful
to subsequent reviewers.
The adjudicator should use their discretion in deciding what information should be
captured. The following is a list of possible uses:
1.
To note the purpose for which the document is presented when “OTHER” is selected in
the “DOCUMENT SUBMITTED AS PROOF OF THE FOLLOWING EVENTS/ISSUES:” field.
2.
To annotate the name(s) appearing on the document if different from the claimant's
name:
•
in the “NAME” field on EVID;
•
in the “CLAIMANT/PERSON“ field on the Report of Contact page in CCE; or
•
used in the MCS or MSSICS claim (see GN 00301.288B.).
3.
To indicate whether other documents were presented to prove the same allegation/issue.
4.
To annotate that the allegation(s)/issue(s) was not proven by the document.
5.
To indicate that a material discrepancy exists between the allegation and the document.
6.
To indicate that the claim was taken on paper.
7.
To indicate, if needed, the time period covered by the document.
8.
To indicate if the document had a Raised seal.
9.
To indicate the month and year reduced spouse's or widow(er)'s benefits were elected.
EXAMPLE: Claimant signed (Form) dated (MM/DD/YY) electing to accept reduced benefits beginning
(MM/YY).
