C. What is NOT a FOIA request
The following types of requests are not FOIA requests:
•
requests for explanations of policies and procedures, the status of claims, and publicly
available information about Social Security programs (such as public information material);
•
requests by individuals (or persons authorized to act on their behalf, such as a parent
or legal guardian) for access to their own records (we process these requests as Privacy
Act requests - for more information regarding Privacy Act requests) see GN 03340.000; and
•
requests from other Federal agencies, or from Federal or State courts.
