D. Release of liability in Virginia
A person paying or delivering a small asset in accordance with GN 02315.085B or GN 02315.085C (in this section) is discharged and released to the same extent as if the person
dealt with the personal representative of the decedent. The person is not required
to see the application of the small asset or inquire into the truth of any statement
in any affidavit. See VA Code § 64.2-603.
