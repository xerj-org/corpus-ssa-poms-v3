B. Swiss agreement alien exception provisions - Listing code 994
The agreement with Switzerland excepts certain beneficiaries from suspension under
the alien nonpayment provisions. This includes all totalization and RSDI beneficiaries
who are:
1.
Nationals of Switzerland, or
2.
Refugees who reside in Switzerland, or
3.
Stateless persons who reside in Switzerland, or
4.
Swiss residents who are:
a.
Entitled as auxiliaries on the record of NH's in categories 1 through 3, or
b.
Entitled as survivors of United States or Swiss nationals, or
c.
Entitled as survivors of refugees or stateless persons who were residents of Switzerland
at the time of death.
NOTE: Swiss nationals meet alien exception 6. When residence in Switzerland is established
for an alien beneficiary who meets no other exception to 202(t), the authorizer lists
the case under listing code 994 (Alien Exception A - International Agreement Exception)
. The listing also shows the country of residence.
