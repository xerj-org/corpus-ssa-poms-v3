B. Policy - Domicile in Minnesota, Montana, South Carolina, Virginia or Washington
1. General Policy
If the worker was domiciled in one of these States at the time of death or when the
spouse's application (in a life case) was filed, and the parties to the divorce are
estopped, the second spouse is given the right to inherit the worker's intestate personal
property (the alternative to valid marriage under section 216(h)(1)(A) of the SS Act)
and may qualify as a spouse, widow, or widower.
