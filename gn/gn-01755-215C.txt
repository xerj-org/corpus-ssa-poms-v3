C. Determining which application screens are required on MCS for Totalization benefits
Use the following information to determine the application screens to complete when
taking claims under the Agreement with Hungary for U.S. Totalization benefits or Hungarian
benefits:
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
When taking retirement or disability claims:
Complete the following screens:
•
MCS application;
•
TOTL (see MS MCS 009.011); and
•
SSA-2490 (see MS MCS 007.001).
a. When taking survivor claims:
Complete the following screens:
•
MCS application;
•
TOTL (see MS MCS 009.011); and
•
Part I of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK.
2. Hungarian Benefits only (regular or Totalization)
Claims for Hungarian benefits only (regular or Totalization) are MCS exclusions. Complete
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK.
3. Hungarian benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. (regular or Totalization)
a. Retirement or disability claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the following screens:
•
MCS application;
•
Totalization (TOTL) (see MS MCS 009.011); and
•
SSA-2490 (see MS MCS 007.001).
b. Survivor claims
In survivor claims, complete the following screens:
•
Appropriate MCS;
•
Totalization (TOTL) (see MS MCS 009.011); and
•
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK.
