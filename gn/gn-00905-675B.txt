B. Policy for paying beneficiaries in countries formerly part of the Soviet Union
We can pay benefits to, or on behalf of, beneficiaries living in a country that was
formerly part of the Soviet Union unless the country remains on the SSA restricted
countries list in RS 02650.040B.
For countries that are still on the SSA restricted countries list, we generally cannot.
For exceptions, see RS 02650.040.
