SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.510 - Lag Earnings
Effective Dates: 08/08/2014 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704510 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Norway
Develop lag earnings for Norway as follows:
1.
For disability claims, obtain the NH's earnings through the quarter of filing.
2.
For survivor claims, obtain the NH's earnings through the quarter of death and the
surviving spouse's earnings through quarter of filing.
NOTE: If the claims material contains no information about the surviving spouse's lag earnings,
contact him/her to determine whether lag development is necessary.
3.
Do not obtain lag earnings for retirement (old-age) claims if at least 2 years (eight
U.S. QCs) are shown on the SSA-794TR.
