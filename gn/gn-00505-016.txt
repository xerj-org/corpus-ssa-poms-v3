GN 00505.016 Legal Guardian Appointed - Foreign Claims
Where the recommended payee is a legal guardian of the beneficiary's estate, obtain
a certified copy of the appointment papers. It will ordinarily be sent by the consular
officer with the application for change in payee. If, however, proof of appointment
is not readily available, payment to the guardian may be started before receipt of
the letters of appointment.
