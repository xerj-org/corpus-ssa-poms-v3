H. Important expiration dates
As stated in the initial notice of bankruptcy, SSA generally has 180 days from the
date of the bankruptcy petition to file a Proof of Claim (refer to GN 02215.195F and GN 2215.195G), and 60 days from the date of the first scheduled meeting of the
creditors to file a complaint to determine whether a debt is a dischargeable debt.
NOTE:
The court may shorten or lengthen these deadlines. OGC monitors relevant deadlines
and includes the Proof of Claim deadline when notifying the appropriate component
of each new bankruptcy case.
