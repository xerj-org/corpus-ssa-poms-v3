B. Application process for Hungarian benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) field offices (FO) do not take the special
Hungarian application forms. After receiving a claim for Hungarian benefits filed
with SSA, the Hungarian agency will mail the appropriate Hungarian forms to the applicant.
The Hungarian agency will include instructions for the applicant to complete the forms
and mail directly to the Hungarian agency along with any additional required evidence.
