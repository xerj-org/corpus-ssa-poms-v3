D. List of Proceedings Before SSA
Proceedings that require SSA's fee authorization include, but are not limited to,
services in connection with:
•
an application for Social Security monthly benefits, supplemental security income
(SSI) payments, or a lump-sum death payment;
•
an application for hospital insurance benefits or supplemental medical insurance benefits;
•
a request to establish or continue a period of disability;
•
a request to modify the amount of benefits;
•
a request to reinstate benefits;
•
a request to waive recovery of an overpayment, or an appeal of an overpayment waiver
denial determination; and
•
a request to revise an earnings record.
