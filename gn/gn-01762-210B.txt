SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01762.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO), Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed in Uruguay
Effective Dates: 04/30/2020 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201762210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – Claims for both U.S. and Uruguayan benefits or Uruguayan benefits only
Prepare a Form SSA e2960 USA/UY 3 to:
1.
Request the Uruguayan certified coverage record and include a copy of the Form SSA
e2960 USA/UY 3; and
2.
Send the information or documentation the Uruguayan liaison agency has requested.
