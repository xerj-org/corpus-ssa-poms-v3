D. PC responsibility when partial award diary matures
When the diary for an outstanding issue on a partially adjudicated claim matures,
the PC will first review the Workload Management System (WMS) query to ascertain which
office originated the diary. If a WSU FO code is present, the PC will forward the
follow up to the WSU rather than the servicing FO shown on the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR).
