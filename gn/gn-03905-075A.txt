A. When to use
When a representative submits a fee petition before completing his or her services
on a pending claim, we return the fee petition and advise the representative, using
the notice in exhibit 1 below, that we cannot accept the requests until after representation
has ended.
If there is no evidence to show the claimant received a copy of the fee petition,
we use the notice in exhibit 2 below to advise the claimant that the representative
filed a fee petition.
