B. Purpose of the U.S.-Hungary Totalization Agreement
This subchapter provides:
•
a brief overview of the Hungarian system;
•
eligibility requirements for Hungarian benefits; and
•
an explanation of the provisions of the agreement.
For more information on the Hungarian social security system and how to issue a certificate
of coverage under the agreement, see sections RS 02002.320 through RS 02002.380. Also, click here to visit the International Programs webpage.
