B. Description of the UK Coverage Certifications
The following information is applicable to both formats:
•
“From-to” periods are expressed in day/month/year format.
•
The periods are continuous and may overlap many years.
•
The “from” date will never be earlier than January 1, 1937.
•
Coverage prior to July 5, 1948 will always be shown in one continuous entry beginning
with the later of the first date of coverage or January 1, 1937 and ending with July
4, 1948 (or earlier).
•
“From-to” periods can never be outside the person's “working life” . The working life generally runs from age 16 to age 60 for women or age 65 for men
or to the year of death or the year of disability onset.
•
The “number of weeks of insurance” represents the number of weeks within the respective “from-to” period.
