C. Overview of IDD for Panama
IDD payments sent to Panama must be in U.S. dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Panama. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
NOTE: The Panamanian balboa is on a par with the U.S. dollar and is available only in
coins. The balboa is mainly for use in vending machines.
