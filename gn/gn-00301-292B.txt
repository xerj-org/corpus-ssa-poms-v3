SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.292 - Use of regional instructions
Effective Dates: 01/13/2000 - Present | TN 15 (01-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301292 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
•
Use only those regional instructions approved by CO (i.e., pre- or post-review in
accordance with appropriate AIMS guides) in adjudicating claims.
•
When used to resolve a material issue in Title II claims, include a reference to the regional instruction in the claims file with sufficient identifying information to distinquish which regional instruction
was used. (See GN 01010.053D.)
EXAMPLE: Kansas City Regional Supplement, March 1989, TN 1.
