SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.375 - Coding Pakistan Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/14/2022 - Present | TN 263 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402375 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for Pakistan has a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “P” assigned for Pakistan.
