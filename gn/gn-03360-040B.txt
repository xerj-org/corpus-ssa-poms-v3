B. Policy - FO Responsibilities
FOs must keep up-to-date copies of the materials which the public may access. These
materials are listed at GN 03360.040H.1. and GN
03360.040H.2. below. It is not necessary to keep a special copy for the public to inspect. A working
copy may be used.
See GN 03360.020 above for instructions on copying records.
