D. Overview of IDD for Trinidad and Tobago
IDD payments sent to Trinidad and Tobago must be in Trinidad and Tobago Dollar (TTD)
accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or
the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that
serves Trinidad and Tobago. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional
information.
