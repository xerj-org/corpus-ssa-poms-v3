B. List of required applications
The following chart lists the application forms that must be completed when the applicant
is filing in a U.S. Field Office (FO). Foreign Service Posts should follow the instructions
in the subchapters dealing with the specific Agreements when determining which application
form to take.
NOTE:
All application forms listed in the chart are available through the Totalization Benefits
Resource Kit on the Intranet here.
