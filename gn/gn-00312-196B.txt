B. Procedure – Marriage and Divorce Records
1. Office to Contact
a. Jewish Persons
Chief Rabbinate in the district in which the event occurred.
b. Moslems
Sharia Court in which the event occurred.
c. Others
Religious authority in the place in which the event occurred.
2. Fee
a.
Marriage Certificate for Jewish Persons: 50 New Israeli shekels (NIS)
b.
Divorce Records for Jewish Persons: 217 NIS.
c.
Marriage Certificates for Moslems: 46 NIS.
d.
Divorce Records for Moslems: 46 NIS.
e.
Other Marriage and Divorce Records: Token fee charged in some jurisdictions.
