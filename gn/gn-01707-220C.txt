Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
If an applicant brings one of the German application forms to the FO:
•
provide any necessary assistance in completing the form, and
•
return the application(s) to the applicant and advise the applicant to return it directly
to the German agency that sent the application form.
Reminder: Do not mail the German application form(s) to the Division of International Operations
(DIO).
