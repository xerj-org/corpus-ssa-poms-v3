SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.025 - Determining the Tax Status of a Claimant/Beneficiary Who Is (or Will Be) Outside the U.S.
Effective Dates: 08/01/2008 - Present | TN 20 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The SSA-21 (Supplement to Claim of Person Outside the United States) usually contains
enough information to determine whether a claimant/beneficiary who is, or will soon
be, outside the U.S. is subject to nonresident alien tax withholding. (See GN 00205.170 for information on obtaining an SSA-21.)
