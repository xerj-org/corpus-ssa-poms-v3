B. Taxes under the Agreement
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual social security coverage and taxes
apply to the following:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes under the Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) and
Self-Employment Contributions Act (SECA), including the Medicare portions of those
Acts; and
•
Uruguayan laws governing social security contributions for old-age retirement, survivors,
and disability benefits.
