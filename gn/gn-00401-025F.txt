SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00401.025 - Office Recovery Procedures by Role
Effective Dates: 06/14/2010 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200401025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. OPSOS
•
Serve as the focal point in CO for recovery effort.
•
Formulate any action plan(s) needed.
•
Through contact with the RO Recovery Team, determine what CO information and support
is necessary and arrange for that support.
•
Bring significant and sensitive issues to the attention of the Commissioner through
the DCO.
Reference
Administrative Instructions Manual System (AIMS), General Administration Chapter 10.03
