Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Servicing FO’s procedure for forwarding the HA-501-U5 and folder to the servicing
HO and the jurisdictional PC
Within five business days of receipt of the HA-501-U5, take the following actions
and suspend all actions not related to the hearing request.
Note:
Process hearing requests for disability issues per DI 12010.010, DI
81010.085, and DI 81010.150.
1.
Fax the HA-501-U5 to the servicing HO undercover of the 501 Case Flag. For instructions
on how to determine the servicing HO, see GN 03103.040.

2.
Fax the HA-501-U5 into Paperless using the HA-501-U5 NONDIB-ALJ Hearing Req (NON-DIB)
barcode. Fax the HA-501-U5 to the fax number on the barcode coversheet.
3.
If you have the paper folder, complete and attach the Form SSA-409 (Post Adjudicative Routing) to the front of the paper folder. Complete the appropriate
fields and check Action Required. Check box “27. Other” and write, “non-medical hearing
and electronic documents,” and use TOEL Appeal RHEAR. To determine the jurisdictional
PC, see GN 01070.245.
4.
Fax the HA-501-U5 and additional evidence into the Non-Disability Repository for Evidentiary
Documents (NDRed) or eView. See GN 00301.328.
5.
To ensure that the HA-501-U5 is legible, review the Claims File Records Management
System (CFRMS) and take one of the following actions.
•
If the folder is in the office, place the HA-501-U5 and additional evidence into the
original paper folder. Mail the folder to the jurisdictional PC.
•
If the folder is not in the office and the HA-501-U5 is legible, shred it. Handle
other evidence per GN 00301.275 and GN 00301.322.
