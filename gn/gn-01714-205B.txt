SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01714.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Belgium
Effective Dates: 02/22/2017 - Present | TN 1 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201714205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1.
Potential Entitlement to U.S. Totalization Benefits
If there is potential entitlement to U.S. totalization benefits, prepare an SSA-e2960-U2-BE
to request the Belgian coverage record.
2.
Claim for Belgian Benefits
If there is a claim for Belgian benefits, prepare a claims package including:
•
Form SSA-e2960-U2-BE;
•
U.S. totalization earnings record;
•
medical evidence from DIO's files, if the Belgian claim is for disability benefits;
and
•
evidence submitted for the Belgian claim.
