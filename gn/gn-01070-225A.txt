SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01070.225 - Prior Folder In Federal Archives And Records Center (FARC)
Effective Dates: 05/21/2007 - Present | TN 19 (05-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Prior folder request
1. PC action
•
Request the folder immediately.
•
Request only the most recently stored folder if there are multiple prior claims folders.
However, see GN 01010.032D.2.b. when disability is involved.
•
Advise the FO of the approximate time needed to obtain the folder from the FARC -
usually 10 working days in the PC's and OIO, 30 days in ODO.
2. FO action
Adjust follow-up timeframes accordingly.
