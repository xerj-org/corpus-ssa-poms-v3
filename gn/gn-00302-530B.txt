B. PROCEDURE - WHO MUST CERTIFY
Copies of, or excerpts from, hospital birth records must be certified by the custodian
of these records in his/her official capacity, or by an SSA employee.
The custodian must indicate his/her official status by the use of the organization's
letterhead or seal or his/her own seal of office. He/she must identify the organization
and his/her relationship to the records. Do not develop the date of recordation unless
there is doubt about the record's authenticity.
See GN 00301.035 for instructions about accepting certifications of evidence from other government
or state agencies.
Excerpts from hospital birth records made by an SSA employee or a letter from the
custodian of the record should include the child’s name and date of birth and the
mother’s name in order to compare this data with data on other documents.
