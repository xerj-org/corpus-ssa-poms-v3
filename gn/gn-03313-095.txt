GN 03313.095 Disclosure to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from DHS, please forward the request directly to the Office
of Privacy and Disclosure at OGC.OPD.LE.Requests@ssa.gov.
