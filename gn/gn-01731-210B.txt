B. SSA takes applications for benefits
Under the Agreement with Finland, the Social Security Administration (SSA) will take
applications for both regular and Totalization benefits on behalf of Finland and provide
limited assistance in obtaining evidence required to support the claim.
The following subsections show the types of evidence SSA personnel may use to support
claims for Finnish benefits.
