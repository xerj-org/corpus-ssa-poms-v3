GN 00312.194 Ireland – Vital Statistics Records
Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Adoption and Death Certificates
Website: http://www.groireland.ie
The General Register Office in Northern Ireland has the original records of birth
for persons born prior to1922 in what is now Northern Ireland. Duplicates of these
records are maintained in the General Register Office in Dublin. Certifications from
either source are acceptable primary evidence of age for persons born before 1922
for Social Security purposes.
