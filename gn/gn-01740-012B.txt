SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.012 - How the FSP in Buenos Aires Processes and Develops Claims for U.S. Benefits Filed with a Chilean Agency
Effective Dates: 01/17/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
This procedure explains how the FSP should process referrals from a Chilean agency.
1. Claimant Already Entitled to U.S. Benefits
•
Take no claims action if the claimant is already entitled to U.S. benefits and no
other benefits are payable on the same or any other SSN.
•
If the Chilean agency has requested information, forward the liaison form to the totalization
module in DIO. If no information has been requested, destroy the liaison form.
2. Determination of Eligibility
Determine if the claimant is eligible for U.S. benefits following the guidelines in
GN 01703.061B.
3. Claimant is Eligible for U.S. Benefits and is Not Already Entitled
•
Have the claimant complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward the claim to DIO.
4. Claimant is Not Eligible for U.S. Benefits
Forward the liaison form to the totalization module in DIO for an informal denial.
