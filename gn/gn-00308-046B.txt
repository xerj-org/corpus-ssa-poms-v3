B. Procedure - State Sources
1. Address and FO Code
Vital Records Request
Dept. of Public Community Health
P.O. Box 30195
Lansing, MI 48906
FO Code 354
NOTE: You can order birth, death, marriage and divorce records online from the Michigan State Department of Community Health site. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
Birth/death/divorce/marriage $34.00 for a search of up to three years. An expedited
or rush fee is an additional $12.00. Extra copies are $16.00 each if ordered at the
same time. Persons 65 or older who apply for a certified copy of their own birth record
are charged a reduced fee of $14.00.
3. Type of Payment:
Cashier's or personal check, or money order.
4. Remarks:
The State office has marriage records beginning April 1867. Additional copies of the
same birth or death record requested at the same time are $5.00 each. Except for Detroit,
a recent birth record can more quickly be obtained from the appropriate city or county
clerk. However, records for adopted children before 1980 or illegitimate children
before 1979 are available only from the Vital Statistics Section.
Additional year searches over the initial three years cost $12.00 and if expeditious
handling is needed, an extra $10.00 is charged.
There is a six-month waiting period before an event is recorded and verification of
paternity affidavits are not available for approximately four months.
A written application is required to request a Michigan vital record. To obtain an
application or to obtain information on requesting a Michigan vital record via the
Internet, call (517) 335-8656.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? FO 354 has a procedure
for verifying birth, death, marriage, and divorce records and for obtaining certified
copies of vital records other than birth records. Click here for the procedure to verify, certify or obtain a free copy of birth/death record.
The parallel field office has a budget allocation to obtain verifications of records
when conditions in GN 00301.210 are met.
