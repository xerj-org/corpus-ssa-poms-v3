B. Overpayment waiver approval policy
The amount of a waiver approval determines the level of decision making and review.
1.
Waiver approval amounts over $2,000 require a review and a second pin (2-PIN). After
the Decision Maker determines they want to approve the waiver, the Decision Maker
refers the case to the applicable reviewer for the second pin (2-PIN) review. The
Reviewer ensures the determination adheres to documentation requirements, policy,
and procedures. The Reviewer must make sure that the Decision Maker recommends approving
the waiver under the correct waiver provision. For more information about the required
level of review and required inputs, refer to the Title II and Title XVI charts in
GN 02250.301C.
2.
If the Reviewer does not have enough information to concur with the determination,
they will notify the Decision Maker that the determination requires additional development
or documentation. The Reviewer must document the Debt Management System (DMS) Remarks
(Title II) or a Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) Report of Contact (Title XVI)
with the reason for returning the case (e.g., no SSA-635, no proof of income, etc.).
After the Decision Maker provides a thorough assessment of their waiver determination,
they will return the case to the second pin (2-PIN) Reviewer for a subsequent review
if the determination remains an approval after the corrections. If the Reviewer concurs
with the determination, the Reviewer completes the required inputs. After receiving
the second pin (2-PIN) approval, the Decision Maker releases the waiver approval notice
to the individual.
3.
If the Reviewer disagrees with the waiver approval because it does not meet one of
the waiver provisions, they will notify the Decision Maker. The Reviewer must document
DMS Remarks (Title II) or a CCE Report of Contact (Title XVI) with the rationale for
non-concurrence. For instance, if the Decision Maker's makes a not at fault determination
that is not supported by the policy in POMS GN 02250.005 and GN 02250.016 or an against equity and good conscience determination that is not supported by the
policy in GN 02250.150, the Reviewer will notify the Decision Maker. The Decision Maker must refer the case
to an independent decision-maker to schedule the folder review and personal conference,
if a personal conference was not previously scheduled, according to GN 02270.000. If the personal conference was scheduled and held or if the individual did not appear
or declined the personal conference for reasons provided in GN 02270.007G, the Decision Maker must revise the waiver determination to a denial and update the
determination on the SSA-635 form.
NOTE: We do not require review and concurrence for waiver approvals made by a federal court,
an administrative law judge (ALJ), or the Appeals Council. Since, the second pin (2-PIN)
process is a systems requirement for waiver approvals over $2,000, the Reviewer must
still provide their second pin (2-PIN) approval in the system.
