SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.310 - How OIO Processes Luxembourg Agency Requests for Medical Evidence and Examinations
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
•
If a Luxembourg agency requests medical evidence, OIO will forward medical evidence
from OIO files. OIO will not request medical evidence from other PSCs. The Luxembourg
agency request is submitted on form
LUX/USA 6.
•
If a Luxembourg agency requests a medical examination from a U.S. resident, SSA will
schedule the examination and send the results to the Luxembourg agency. The Luxembourg
agency request is submitted on form LUX/ USA 6.
