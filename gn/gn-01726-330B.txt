SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.330 - Information About Dutch Application Forms NL/VS 202 and NL/VS 203
Effective Dates: 08/11/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726330 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
•
Both forms contain space for the worker or survivor claimant to list the worker's
periods of residence and employment from age 15 to the present. An indication in this
space that the worker was employed in the U.S. constitutes protective filing for U.S. benefits and must be developed
or closed out, as appropriate, by a CA. However, an indication that the worker merely
resided in the U.S. does not constitute protective filing under the agreement since coverage
under the U.S. system is not residence based like it is in the Netherlands.
•
Both forms also contain a statement authorizing SSA to release information to SVB.
Thus in the absence of an application form SSA-2490 or SVB's statement on their liaison
form that there is a claim under the agreement, the NL/VS 202 or NL/VS 203 constitutes
the claimant's authorization to disclose if it is properly signed by the claimant.
