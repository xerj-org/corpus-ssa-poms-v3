SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04001.001 - Reopenings - General Statement
Effective Dates: 09/09/2011 - Present | TN 3 (01-89)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204001001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Definitions
1. Determination
Initial or reconsideration level of adjudication.
2. Decision
The hearings and Appeals Council (AC) level of adjudication.
3. Reopen
Reopen means to take another look, even if the determination or decision is not revised.
NOTE: Reopening is at the discretion of SSA and is not a right afforded to the claimant upon request, even if the claimant makes
the request within one year of the notice of the initial determination. However, in all cases it is SSA’s policy to honor the request to review the case
to see if reopening applies. SSA informs the claimant if we decide not to reopen and why (GN
04001.090).
4. Revision
Revision of a determination or decision occurs when:
a.
The end result is changed (e.g., disallowance changed to an award); or
b.
Based on new and material evidence, the basis for the determination is changed (even
though the end result may not differ).
EXAMPLE: SSA revises a determination as to the quarters of coverage a claimant has even though
it is determined that the claimant is still not insured and, thus, not entitled.
NOTE: It is SSA’s policy to revise a determination if reopening applies and the evidence
shows the prior determination was incorrect. (See GN 04020.080 for a discussion of reopening and revising when it would have a dual effect.)
5. Hearing
Personal appearance before the administrative law judge (ALJ), not the face-to-face
hearings which are held at the reconsideration level in disability medical cessation
cases.
