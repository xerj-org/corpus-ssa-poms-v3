Effective Dates: 08/28/2003 - Present | TN 9 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203106021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. BACKGROUND
The court issues subpoenas or orders requiring the production of documents because
a party has requested information, generally under the Freedom of Information Act.
See GN 03330.001 when a subpoena for production of information or records is served on your office.
OPL forwards any subpoena or court order (if Regulation would permit disclosure) that
they receive to OALC.
