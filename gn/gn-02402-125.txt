GN 02402.125 Direct Deposit for Railroad Retirement Board Cases
The Social Security Administration (SSA) Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) rewrite in
2004 made it possible to include direct deposit data when a Railroad Board Certification
claim is going to Railroad Retirement Board (RRB).
