SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.320 - Providing Monthly Benefit Data to German Agencies
Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
SSA has agreed to routinely certify to the German liaison agencies only the amount
of RIB (A) or DIB (HA) benefits that a person is receiving. The amounts of other U.S.
benefits that a person receives such as spouses, widows and children's benefits, may
be certified if specifically requested by the German agency.
