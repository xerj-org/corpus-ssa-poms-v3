SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.300 - Processing Payee Applications from Organizations Approved To Collect a Fee for Service
Effective Dates: 06/20/2025 - Present | TN 38 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Organization applies - Determining DAA and creditor status
When an authorized Fee-for-Service (FFS) organization files to be payee for additional
beneficiaries, and we select them as payee, we must determine whether the beneficiaries
have a Drug Addiction or Alcoholism (DAA) condition and whether a creditor relationship
exists between the organization and the beneficiaries.
If not a creditor, the organization can begin to collect a fee in the first month
for which it is appointed payee and receives payment on behalf of the beneficiary,
see subsection B.1. in this section.
If a creditor, the organization must wait for the FO's determination and follow the
procedure in subsection B.2. in this section.
2. Systems input
The following information about the authorized organization should be collected in
the remarks section of the SSA-11-SG in eRPS, using the Remarks for the Application
(SSA-11) screen in accordance with MS 07409.020. On the SSA-11-BK, use the Remarks
section to show:
•
that the organization is authorized to collect fees for services;
•
if a fee will be collected from the beneficiary; and
•
whether the organization is a creditor of the individual.
3. More than one organization applies
If more than one FFS organization applies to serve as payee for the same beneficiary,
determine which payee applicant would best serve the interests of the beneficiary.
For factors to consider when evaluating payee applicants, see GN 00502.130.
Appoint one payee for all benefits to which the beneficiary is entitled. For additional
information on this subject, see GN 00502.183B.3.
When the beneficiary has a DAA condition give preference to an organizational payee,
GN 00502.105C.
