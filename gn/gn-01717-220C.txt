C. Procedure
If an applicant brings one of the Swedish application forms to the FO, the FO should:
1.
Provide any necessary assistance in completing the Swedish application form.
2.
Photocopy and certify any documents (evidence) the Swedish agency has requested the
applicant to include with the Swedish claim.
3.
Return the application form(s) and certified photocopies to the applicant and advise
him or her to return them directly to Sweden at the address shown on the application
form.
4.
Advise the applicant to check with the U.S. Postal Service for the international rate
and/or fee for mailing materials to Sweden.
Reminder: Don’t mail the Swedish application forms to OIO.
