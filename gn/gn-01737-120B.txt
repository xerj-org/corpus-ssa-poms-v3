B. Policy -Crediting greek coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. quarter of coverage for every 75 days of Greek coverage in
a calendar year. (Greek coverage is measured in days.) However, no credit will be
given for:
•
any day in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. quarter of coverage,
or
•
more than 4 quarters of coverage for any calendar year.
