SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00502.075 - Child Beneficiary Becomes Entitled Based on Disability
Effective Dates: 12/30/2022 - Present | TN 81 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200502075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Certain age attainment alerts will result in the filing of disability claims for minor
children. The need for payee development may come to your attention at this time.
Whenever possible you should take advantage of these early opportunities to initiate
any necessary payee development.
