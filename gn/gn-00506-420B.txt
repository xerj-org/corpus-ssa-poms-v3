B. Policy for certifying Fee-for-Service Payees
The servicing FO is responsible for annually certifying the continuing eligibility
of each Fee-for-Service (FFS) payee. Generally, the Regional Offices (RO) will create
a list of organizations to be certified and forward that list to the Area Director
Offices (ADO) and/or the servicing FOs.
