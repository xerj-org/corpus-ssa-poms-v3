B. Procedure for intercepting a check sent to a foreign address
This procedure is for the Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO) only.
1. Intercepting regular monthly checks
Step
Action
1
Before you attempt to intercept a regular monthly check, review the list of countries
in GN 02401.075C in this section. For countries with an asterisk, follow Step 2. For countries with no asterisk, follow Step 3.
2
If there is an asterisk beside the name of the country, do not attempt to intercept
the regular monthly check. Treasury ships these checks to countries by bulk mail through
International Priority Airmail (IPA) directly to the beneficiaries’ mailing address.
3
If there is no asterisk beside the country's name, telephone or fax the intercept
request to the appropriate check distributing office listed in GN 02401.075C in this section. Obtain the phone or fax number from the Detailed Office/Organization
Resource System (DOORS).
2. Intercepting prior month accrual (PMA) checks
If you want to intercept a PMA check, telephone or fax the intercept request to the
appropriate check distributing office listed in GN
02401.075C in this section. Obtain the phone or fax number from the DOORS.
