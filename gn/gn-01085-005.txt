GN 01085.005 Document Retention Under the Modernized Systems
Background
A photocopy of a public domestic evidentiary document is no longer retained when claims
are taken on a modernized system (see GN 00301.286). Instead, sufficient data to identify the type of evidence submitted is recorded
on the system.
