SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04001.050 - Reopening Must Be In Writing
Effective Dates: 10/04/1994 - Present | TN 3 (01-89)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204001050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
To reopen a determination or decision:
A written request (i.e., affirmative action in writing) must be filed by
the party to the determination or decision within the applicable time limit (i.e., one or four
years from the date of notice of initial determination); or
SSA must take some affirmative action in writing within the applicable time limit questioning the correctness of the determination
or decision.
