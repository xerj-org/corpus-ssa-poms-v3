SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01712.110 - How to Credit Norwegian Coverage
Effective Dates: 12/07/1993 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201712110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following guidelines when assigning Norwegian coverage:
Step
Action
1
Is the claimant filing for disability benefits on this record?
•
If yes, credit all possible FQCs to the earnings record.
•
If no, credit only the number of FQCs required for insured status.
2
Consider each year separately.
3
Assign FQCs as follows:
•
If there are zero U.S. credits posted for a year of Norwegian coverage, credit 4 FQCs.
•
If there are 1-3 U.S. credits posted for a year of Norwegian coverage, credit FQCs
in the blank U.S. quarters.
•
If there are 4 U.S. credits posted for a year of Norwegian coverage, credit no FQCs.
