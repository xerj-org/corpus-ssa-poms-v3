Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The procedures below explain how VARO should process referrals from the NPC.
1. Claimant Already Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Forward all material to OIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
2. Claimant Not Already Entitled to U.S. Benefits
•
In retirement and disability claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens,
the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and the SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
•
In survivor claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011) and Part I of the paper or electronic (e-form) application Form SSA-2490-BK.
•
Forward the claim to OIO and include the form KOR-USA 2.
