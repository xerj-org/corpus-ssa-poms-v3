A. Procedure – Birth and Death Certificates
In requesting a birth or death certificate, provide full identifying information about
the individual concerned, the date and place of the event and the individual's Israeli
identification or passport number.
1. Office to Contact
District Office of the Ministry of the Interior in the district in which the event
occurred. If the event occurred after 1948, a record may also be obtained from the
Head Registry Office, Ministry of the Interior, Jerusalem.
2. Fee
No fee.
