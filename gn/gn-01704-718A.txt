SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.718 - Transmittal/Request/Certification Form (SSA-2960-U3-GE)
Effective Dates: 08/22/2014 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704718 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Description
1.
This is a 3-page snapout form.
a.
Page 1 is blue and printed in English. It is completed and filed in the folder as
documentation.
b.
Page 2 is white and printed in German. The copy is initially sent to the appropriate
German agency.
c.
Page 3 is yellow and printed in German. If necessary it is used as a followup request.
2.
The information and verification entered on page 1 corresponds to the information
that is forwarded to Germany on pages 2 and 3.
