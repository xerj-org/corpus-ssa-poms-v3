A. Applications for Benefits under the Agreement with Brazil
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with Brazil must complete Form
SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
The applicant must complete this form whether applying for benefits:
•
from the United States only;
•
from Brazil only; or
•
from both countries.
Note: Applicants filing for benefits from Brazil require additional applications.
See GN 01757.220.
