If the Berlin district of birth is not known, send the request to:
Standesamt Friedrichschain- Kreuzberg von Berlin
Frankfurter Allee 35/37
10247 Berlin
This office will initiate a search of all the districts in Berlin (i.e., the former
East and West Berlin). This search takes at least 6 months. If the district of birth
is not known, the parents' residence at the time of birth may help in locating the
record.
