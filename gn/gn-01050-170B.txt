SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.170 - Preparation of Folder for Transmittal of Childhood Disability Claims
Effective Dates: 08/10/2023 - Present | TN 11 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050170 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Two or More Disabled Children—NO DIB
Where two or more disabled children are filing on the same NH's SSN and all material
is being sent simultaneously to the same office (Office of Disability Operations (ODO)
or Disability Determination Services (DDS), prepare one folder for each Childhood
Disability Beneficiary (CDB) per GN 01050.170A in this section. Staple the back flap of one CDB folder to the front flap of the
second CDB folder and so on.
