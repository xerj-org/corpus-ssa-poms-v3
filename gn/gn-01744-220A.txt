Effective Dates: 08/29/2013 - Present |

A. When to use
The SSA-2960-AUS is completed in the totalization module in OIO or by VARO in Manila.
It is used to:
•
Transmit claims and related material to Centrelink
•
Request information from Centrelink, and
•
Respond to requests from Centrelink.
