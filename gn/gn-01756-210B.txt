SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01756.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed in Hungary
Effective Dates: 09/04/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201756210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and Hungary or Hungary claim only
Prepare a form SSA-e2960 USA-HU 3 (Transmittal/Request/Certification) to:
1.
request the Hungarian certified coverage record and include a copy of the Form SSA-e2960
USA-HU 3; and
2.
send the Hungarian liaison agency information or documentation they requested.
