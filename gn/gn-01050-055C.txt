SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.055 - Determining Processing Center Jurisdiction
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 14 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Claimant's mailing address in Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands, or Alaska, Hawaii, Guam,
American Samoa
Claims bearing SSN's within a servicing PSC's jurisdiction will be handled by that
PSC. Therefore, if GN 01050.055A. does not apply, all PSC's will handle both Puerto Rican and Pacific claims based
on the first three digits (area code) of the SSN serviced by them.
