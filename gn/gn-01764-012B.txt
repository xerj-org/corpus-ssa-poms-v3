B. Procedure for FBU to process referrals from the Icelandic Liaison Agency
This procedure explains how the Oslo, Norway FBU processes referrals from the Icelandic
Liaison Agency.
1. Claimant already entitled to U.S. benefits
If the claimant is already entitled to the type of U.S. benefit for which he or she
is applying, forward all material to the Division of International Operations with
the annotation “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not already entitled to U.S. benefits
If the claimant is not already entitled to the type of U.S. benefit for which he is
she is applying, process the claim in the same manner described in GN 01772.010B.
