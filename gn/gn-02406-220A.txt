A. Failure to receive Form FMS-1133 (Claims Against the United States for the Proceeds
of a Government Check)
1.
Background
The Department of the Treasury (Treasury) generates Form FMS-1133 when the Social
Security Administration (SSA) inputs a non-receipt or stop payment request for Title
II or Title XVI checks. The form contains questions the beneficiary, recipient or
representative payee must complete to assist Treasury in determining if the check
in question is forged.
2.
Failure to Receive Form FMS-1133 Report
•
Verify
Stop
Code
If the beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee does not receive the form from
Treasury, access the Payment History Update System (PHUS) for Title II or the Treasury
Status Query for Title XVI to ensure the appropriate stop code is input. If the appropriate
stop code is not present, complete the input.
•
Stop
Code
Present
If the appropriate stop code is input, do a one-time print of the FMS-1133 from the
Treasury Check Information System (TCIS) and send it to the beneficiary, recipient,
or representative payee.
•
Printing
the
FMS-1133
–
Field Office (FO) or Program Service Center (PSC) staff:
FO and PSC staff may access TCIS to complete a one-time print of the FMS-1133 from
the Treasury Check Information System (TCIS) and send it to the beneficiary, recipient,
or representative payee.
–
Telephone Summary Conversation (TSC) Customer Service Representative:
Since the TSC staff cannot print from TCIS, they will send an MDW to the FO instructing
them to print the form from TCIS.
•
Unable
to
Print
If the form cannot be printed:
–
FO and TSC will request assistance through vHelp (vHelp analysts, only, see GN 02406.220E, this section)
–
PSC personnel will contact their Operations Analysis Staff (OAS).
NOTE: The form is available for printing until Treasury receives the completed FMS-1133.
NOTE: An L-stop (certified photocopy request) will only produce the FMS-3858 and a copy
of the check. Additionally, Treasury will not process a photocopy of an FMS-1133 for
a non-receipt claim. For more information about an L-stop and the FMS-1133, see GN 02406.145 and GN 02406.160
