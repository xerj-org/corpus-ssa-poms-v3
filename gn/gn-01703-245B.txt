SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.245 - Adjudicating Claims for U.S. Totalization Benefits After Foreign Coverage Record is Received
Effective Dates: 01/27/2017 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the steps below once the insured status decision is made.
1. Totalization insured status is met
Proceed as follows if totalization insured status is met.
a. Development
Develop for any evidence still missing.
If the claim is for disability benefits, send the medical jacket to IDU for a disability
determination. Be sure to show the date insured status is last met on the SSA-559
(Claims Folder Material Transmittal).
b. Award
If all other factors of entitlement are met, prepare a totalization award using the
guidelines in MS MCS 014.001. Designate the appropriate paragraphs (See NL 00711.305).
Send the file to a BA for processing through MADCAP.
c. Denial
See GN 01703.310 if you must deny the claim for a reason other than lack of insured status; e.g.,
not disabled, relationship not established, etc.
d. Notification of foreign agency
If required by the individual country, send the file to the BET to prepare a final
package informing the foreign agency of our decision. (See individual DIO agreement
subchapters to determine if a final package is required.)
2. Totalization insured status not met
If totalization insured status is not met:
a.
Deny the claim using the guidelines in GN 01703.310.
b.
If required by the individual country, send the file to the BET to prepare a final
package informing the foreign agency of our decision. (See individual DIO agreement
subchapters to determine if a final package is required.)
