GN 01706.300 Italian Liaison Forms and Transmittal/Request Forms
Italy has agreed to begin using only one transmittal/request form, the IT/ USA 2,
to exchange claims and earnings information. However, until this procedure is adopted
by all INPS offices, OIO may continue to receive the Italian liaison forms IT/USA
3 and IT/USA 5.
