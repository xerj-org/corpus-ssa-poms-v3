C. Dictated notices
If information must be sent to an individual and the RSDI notice was sent without
it and the preprinted notice does not contain all the information necessary, send
a dictated letter. Most of the paragraphs which should be included in the letter can
be found in NL 00711.000 ff. Be sure to show the numbers of and fill-ins for these paragraphs.
