Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201709210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Using evidence previously submitted in claim for U.S. benefits
Applicants do not need to resubmit the evidence previously used and verified to establish
factors of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits. The evidence previously
submitted is sufficient for Switzerland to establish the same factors of entitlement
for Swiss benefits.
