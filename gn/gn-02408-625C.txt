C. Process when an FI has full liability
If the FI has full liability, the FI must complete all of the following:
•
Mail the “Notice to Account Holders” (Part 5 of the Notice of Reclamation) to the
account holder(s);
•
Notify the account holder(s) of any action the FI has taken or intends to take against
the account;
•
Respond to Treasury so the response arrives at the Government Disbursing Office within
60 days of the date of the Notice of Reclamation; and
•
Return all payments listed on the Notice of Reclamation, FS-133, and any payments
received after receipt of the Notice.
