GN 01753.105 Effective Date of the Agreement with the Slovak Republic
May 1, 2014 is the effective date of the agreement with the Slovak Republic.
Earliest month of entitlement of the totalization agreement with the Slovak Republic
The United States and the Slovak Republic can use periods of U.S. and Slovak social
security coverage earned in the United States or the Slovak Republic before May 1,
2014 to establish entitlement to U.S. or Slovak Totalization benefits. However, the
earliest possible date of entitlement to benefits under the agreement is May 1, 2014.
