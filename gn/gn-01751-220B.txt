B. Process for forms from ZUS or KRUS (PL/USA 4)
The Polish application forms from ZUS or KRUS (PL/USA 4) can be printed in the field
office (FO) from the SSA Totalization Forms section of the Totalization Resource Kit
and given to or mailed to the applicant for completion. Completion and mailing instructions
are provided with the forms. The forms are to be sent to either ZUS or KRUS. If the
claim for Polish benefits was filed with SSA and additional information is needed
by the Polish agency, the Polish agency will mail the applicant any additional forms
for completion and instruct the applicant to mail the additional evidence directly
to the Polish agency.
