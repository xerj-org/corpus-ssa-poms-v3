SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.305 - Recomputations Involving Totalization and the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP)
Effective Dates: 04/18/2025 - Present | TN 17 (04-25)

B. Policy on recomputations involving Totalization benefits
1. General policy on recomputations when the Totalization benefit is greater than the
non-Totalization benefit subject to WEP
Continue to pay the Totalization benefit if the non-Totalization benefit with WEP
is less than the Totalization benefit. However, do not use earnings and QCs in the
year the NH meets insured status based on U.S. coverage alone or in subsequent years,
to recompute the Totalization primary insurance amount (PIA). For information on how
to process cases where insured status is acquired based on U.S. coverage only, see
GN 01703.270
2. Effect on premium-free hospital insurance when the Totalization benefit is greater
than the non-Totalization benefit subject to WEP
If the NH receiving a Totalization benefit would be entitled to premium-free hospital
insurance (HI) by gaining insured status based on U.S. coverage alone but continues
to receive a Totalization benefit because it is greater than the non-Totalization
benefit, technically entitle the beneficiary to the non-Totalization benefit for entitlement
to premium-free HI. For information on Hi in Totalization claims see GN 01701.140. Pay the Totalization benefit, so WEP does not apply.
