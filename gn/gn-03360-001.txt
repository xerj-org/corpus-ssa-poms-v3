GN 03360.001 Privacy Act Administration, Communication and Other Procedures - Overview
The purpose of this subchapter is to provide guidance concerning several matters related
to protecting an individual's right to privacy. It addresses SSA's basic policy and
procedures on:
•
Disclosing information by telephone
•
Acquiring or collecting personal information under the PA
•
Photocopying
•
Certifying records for legal purposes
•
Releasing records by FOs
•
Deleting personal detail from records prior to disclosure
•
Handling requests for nonpersonal information, staff manuals and instructional material
under the FOIA
•
Administering the Right to Financial Privacy Act
