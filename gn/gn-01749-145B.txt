SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01749.145 - Exception to Section 202(t)(11) Alien Nonpayment Provision under the Czech Agreement for Dependents and Survivors
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 4 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201749145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Does not meet 5 year residency
If a non-U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the 5-year U.S. residency
requirements and alleges citizenship or residency of the Czech Republic, the Claims
Representative develops citizenship or residence status.
