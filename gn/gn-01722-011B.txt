Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present |

B. Process
The following section contains a description of the applications and evidence obtained
by the Spanish agency offices and forwarded to the FSP and OIO.
1. U.S. Claim
If the claimant contacts an ISM or INSS office to apply for U.S. benefits, the agency
obtains form USA/E2 (Statement of Intent to File for U.S. Benefits). The form is forwarded
to the FSP, Madrid for claims development.
The Spanish agency prepares and forwards the following material to OIO:
•
Liaison form E/USA 3, certifying the filing date.
•
Spanish coverage record.
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
2. Spanish Claim
If the Spanish agency needs a U.S. coverage record and/or entitlement data to complete
the Spanish claim, the agency requests the information from OIO using form E/USA 3.
3. U.S. and Spanish Claim
If the claimant contacts an ISM or INSS office to apply for both U.S. and Spanish
benefits, the agency obtains the required Spanish forms and form USA/ E2 (Statement
of Intent to File for U.S. Benefits). Form USA/E2 is forwarded to the FSP, Madrid
for claims development.
The Spanish agency prepares and forwards the following material to OIO:
•
Liaison form E/USA 3, certifying the filing date and requesting a U.S. coverage record
and/or entitlement data if it is needed.
•
Spanish coverage record.
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
