D. Overview of IDD for Bahrain
IDD payments sent to Bahrain must be in U.S dollar (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that territory.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
