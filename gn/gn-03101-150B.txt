B. Policy - Special Situations
1. Office of Disability Operations (ODO)
ODO reconsiders initial Part A and Part B entitlement determinations on domestic end
stage renal disease (ESRD) claims. For jurisdiction if the claimant's spouse has filed,
see Priority of Jurisdiction-Two Processing Centers Involved, GN 01050.060.
2. Railroad Retirement Board (RRB)
RRB is responsible for Part A and Part B entitlement determinations for, and appeals
by, qualified RRB annuitants (QRRB's) who are not entitled to Social Security benefits.
If the QRRB is entitled to Social Security benefits, SSA processes the appeal through
its regular appeals process.
3. OHO and the Office Analytics, Review, and Oversight, Office of Appellate Operations
(OAO)
OHO is responsible for hearings and OAO is responsible for Appeals Council review
requests.
