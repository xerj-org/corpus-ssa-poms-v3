B. Disclosure to protect the safety of SSA employees and facilities
We may disclose information to appropriate Federal, State, or local law enforcement
agencies, or contract security entities, when necessary, to protect the safety of
SSA employees and customers; the security of the SSA workplace and the operation of
SSA facilities; or to assist investigation or prosecution activities that affect such
safety and security or disrupt the operations of our facilities. Many of our systems
of records contain a routine use allowing disclosure of relevant information to protect
SSA employees and facilities. You can view a full listing of all of our systems of
records and their routine uses at https://www.ssa.gov/privacy/sorn.html.
