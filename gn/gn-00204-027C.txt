C. Procedure – Obtaining and Adjudicating Title II Applications -- Title XVI Claim Not
Yet Adjudicated
If the Title XVI claim has not been adjudicated, determine the claimant's potential
entitlement to any Title II benefit (including benefits on another SSN such as a widow,
child (e.g. DAC) or parent) and process the Title II claim per SI 00601.035. For adjudicative responsibilities, see GN 01010.007 and GN 01010.008.
