D. Overview of IDD for Peru
IDD payments sent to Peru must be in Peruvian sol (PEN) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Peru. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional info.
