SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.052 - Pursuing Recovery of Excess Fees Paid to Representatives or Entities and Referrals for Ineligible for Direct Payment Entity (IDPE) List
Effective Dates: 01/29/2025 - Present | TN 66 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920052 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. FO and PC instructions for recovering excess fee payments made to an entity
1. Confirm that the excess fee meets the conditions for pursuing recovery from an entity
When FO or PC technicians identify a potential excess fee issue, they must first ensure
that the excess fee is one for which we will pursue recovery and that the excess fee was paid to the entity.
For more information on the circumstances under which we will pursue recovery of an
excess fee, see GN 03920.051B.
2. Issue a notification of collection action to the entity’s POC, document the situation,
and related procedures.
If the excess fee issue meets the criteria for pursuing recovery and the fee in question
was paid to an entity, the technician issues a Notification of Collection Action to
Entity (MAO104). This notice can be created using the Document Processing System (DPS)
or AURORA, and it should be addressed to the entity’s POC.
The technician then documents the situation in RASR by recording an ROC in the Appointment-Related
Report of Contact (ROCD) screen for each appointment associated with the excess fee
issue. In cases involving multiple representatives, technicians must ensure that an
ROC is created for each representative associated with the excess fee payment, even
if they all assigned direct payment to a single entity.
The ROC should include the following information:
•
The name and Employer Identification Number (EIN) of the entity involved;
•
The name and Rep ID of the entity’s POC;
•
The names and Rep IDs of any representatives involved, their dates of appointment,
and the date(s) they assigned the right to direct payment of their fee to the entity,
if applicable;
•
The amount of the excess fee payment;
•
The fee process involved (fee petition or fee agreement);
•
The past-due benefit period and amount (include amounts for the claimant and any affected
auxiliaries);
•
The date of the fee authorization; and
•
A brief description of the excess fee issue.
The technician then establishes the excess fee in the appropriate system and sets
a 30-day diary to follow up on the refund request. For more information on establishing
an excess fee and setting a diary, see GN 03920.052.B.6. and GN 03920.052.B.7.
REMINDER: When following the instructions in GN 03920.052.B.7. to set a diary for entity-related fee issues, technicians must set a 30-day
diary, not a 45-day one.
3. Follow-up after the initial diary expires
Once the initial 30-day diary has expired, the technician queries RASR and any other
relevant system (e.g., DMS, MBR, MDW, PCACS) to determine whether the entity has refunded
the entire excess fee.
•
If the entity has refunded the entire excess fee payment, the technician creates a
new ROC in RASR for each related representative to reflect that full payment of the
excess fee has been received.
•
If the entity has not refunded the entire excess fee payment, the technician proceeds
to step 4.
4. Issue a final notification of collection action to the entity’s POC and establish
a second diary
If the entity has not refunded the full excess fee payment by the expiration of the
first 30-day diary, the technician issues a second, final recovery notice, Final Notification
of Collection Action to Entity (MAO105). This notice can be created using DPS or AURORA,
and it should be addressed to the entity’s POC.
The technician then creates a new ROC in RASR for each related representative and
sets a new 30-day diary to follow-up on the refund request. The technician reviews
the prior ROC and ensures that the information listed in GN 03920.052C.2. was included in that ROC. If any information is absent, or if there is no prior
ROC, the technician will include that information in the new ROC.
In addition, the technician adds the following information:
•
The amount of the excess fee payment that has been refunded, if applicable;
•
The amount that has not yet been remitted.
•
The date the initial notification of collection action was issued; and
•
Any other relevant information related to the fee recovery process, including whether
the entity’s POC has contacted us.
5. Follow-up after the second diary expires
Once the final 30-day diary has expired, the technician queries RASR and any other
relevant system (e.g., DMS, MBR, MDW, PCACS) to determine whether the entity has refunded
the entire excess fee.
•
If the entity has refunded the entire excess fee payment and RASR already contains
ROCs documenting this for each related representative, the technician clears any pending
diaries and takes no further action.
•
If the entity has refunded the entire excess fee payment, but RASR does not contain
ROCs for each related representative documenting this, the technician creates an ROC
for each related representative to reflect that the full payment of the excess fee
has been received. The technician then clears any pending diaries.
•
If the entity has not refunded the entire excess fee payment, or has refunded only
part of it, the technician proceeds to step 6.
6. Refer the Entity-related Fee Issue to the IDPE Coordinator Group
If the entity does not refund the full excess fee by the expiration of the second
diary, the FO or PC technician should consult their manager for a potential referral
to the IDPE Coordinator Group.
The manager will confirm that:
•
We issued to the entity's POC both the "Notification Action to the Entity" and the
"Final Notification of Collection Action to the Entity";
•
At least 30 days have passed since we issued the "Final Notification of Collection
Action to the Entity"; and
•
We have not received a full refund of the excess fee.
If the conditions above are met and the manager approves, the manager will:
•
Refers the entity to the IDPE Coordinator Group by sending an email to the IDPE Coordinator
Group Mailbox. See the example email below.
•
Creates an ROC in RASR for each related representative with the following message
“IDPE Referral made on [insert date].”
•
Closes any open diaries.
The subject line of the referral email should be “IDPE Referral for [Entity name and
EIN].
The body of the email may be formatted as follows:
Re: [Claimant Name and SSN]
Good [Morning/Afternoon],
We are referring [Entity Name, EIN] for potential action by the IDPE Coordinator Group.
An excess fee of [Full Excess Fee] was paid to this entity on [Fee Payment Date].
Based on the criteria in [Cite the applicable recoverable excess fee provision in
GN 03920.051B], we identified this payment as an excess fee requiring recovery.
We sent collection notices to the entity’s POC on [Date of First Collection Notice]
and [Date of Final Collection Notice], but as of today, [Remaining Excess Fee] remains
unremitted. Please see the information below for more details.
[Include any information that is relevant to recovering the excess fee. This includes
but is not limited to:
•
The claimant’s SSN and name;
•
The name and EIN of the entity involved;
•
An explanation of the excess fee payment;
•
The dates of the first and final notice collections notice; and
•
Any information that should have been documented in RASR ROCs, but was not. For more
information, see subsection GN 03920.052C.2 and GN 03920.052C.4., above.
Once the referral has been submitted, the IDPE Coordinator Group will not generally
provide updates to the referring office. However, the IDPE Coordinator Group may contact
the referring office as needed to resolve any question related to the referral.
7. Responding to excess fee-related inquiries received after the issue has been referred
to the IDPE Coordinator Group
If an FO or PC technician receives an inquiry from an entity’s POC about an issue
that relates to the IDPE list or other excess fee-related matters, the technician
may access the IDPE list in RASR for information on an entity’s status and any unresolved
fee issues.
Technicians may provide any necessary information needed to resolve the excess fee
matter, including but not limited to:
•
Claimants’ SSN;
•
IDPE status;
•
Ineligibility Start or End Date; and
•
Amount Owed.
