B. Procedure for verifying records and obtaining a certified copy.
1. Birth and death records
The Atlanta Field Office (FO) can verify a birth or death record. You must send the
electronic form SSA-562 (E-562) to FO code 600 requesting the verification. However,
you must use the Electronic Verifications of Vital Events (EVVE) system for births
from 1985 and continuing before contacting the FO. If you need a certified copy, the
FO cannot obtain a free copy. You can request a copy by directly submitting a request
to the Georgia Vital Records located at:
Vital Records
Atlanta, GA 30349
(404) 679-4701
You can find the most up-to-date cost information from the Georgia Vital Records website. In addition, you can find EIN information at the Office of Financial Policy
and Operations TPPS Vital Records EINs page.
2. Marriage and divorce records
FOs should send requests for marriage and divorce records directly to the State. Information
on this can be found at Georgia Department of Public Health.
