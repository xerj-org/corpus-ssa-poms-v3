GN 02406.207 Nonreceipt of Courtesy Disbursement or Replacement Check/ Type 9 Payment -- Field
Office and Teleservice Center Instructions -- Title XVI
IMPORTANT: Specific notations are made for action the TSC should take and when; once 10 calendar
days have passed since the nonreceipt was input, the TSR should input an A-stop and
send an MDW to the FO requesting that an AOTP be issued, unless abuse of the nonreceipt
reporting system is a factor. If abuse is present, see GN 02406.207B. Refer to the TSCOG if there are further questions.
