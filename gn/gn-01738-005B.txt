SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.005 - How to Review and Complete Claims Filed in a Field Office (FO) or the Claims Development Branch (CDB)
Effective Dates: 02/21/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
To review claims filed in the FO or processed through CDB, take the following steps.
Step
Action
1
Have all applications and evidence listed in
GN 01737.215 been obtained?
•
If yes, go to step 2.
•
If no, request the FO, CDB, or FSP to obtain missing applications or evidence. If
appropriate, depending on the country of residence, request information directly from
the claimant.
2
Is a current, complete U.S. earnings record in file?
•
If yes, include it in any claim package sent to Greece.
•
If no, request a totalization earnings record from OCRO (See GN 01704.570).
3
Are lag earnings alleged on the U.S. application?
•
If yes, develop lag earnings.
•
If no, go to step 4.
4
Is there a claim for disability benefits from Greece?
•
If yes, did the claimant submit medical evidence or is medical evidence of record
available in OIO?
–
If yes, include copies of medical evidence in the claim package.
–
If no, go to step 5.
•
If no, go to step 5.
5
Did claimant submit any additional evidence, including original insurance booklets,
in support of the Greek claim?
•
If yes, prepare a claim package for Greece and include this evidence. Keep a certified
photocopy of the insurance booklet in the OIO files.
•
If no, prepare a claim package for Greece.
6
Send a preprinted notice to the claimant informing him/her that:
•
you sent information concerning his/her claim to:
Idryma Kinonikon Asfaliseon (IKA)
8, Agiou Konstantinou Str
10241 Athens
GREECE
•
IKA will only contact him/her if further information is necessary.
•
if he/she is not contacted or if he/she has any questions concerning the Greek claim,
contact IKA directly.
7
Determine if the Greek coverage record (GR/USA-5) has been requested by the FSP for
claims processed through CDB.
•
If no, or if claim was filed in a FO, prepare an SSA-2960-U3-GR (U.S. - Greece Agreement
on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification Form) requesting a Greek coverage
record. Diary for 180 days.
•
If yes and the coverage record is in file, adjudicate the claim.
•
If yes, and the coverage record is not in file, diary for 180 days from the date of
the FSP request.
•
If more than 180 days have elapsed, prepare an SSA-2960-U3-GR status request showing
the FSP request date on the top line. Diary for 90 days.
Note: See GN 01701.210 for a discussion of verification of eligibility factors by foreign agencies.
