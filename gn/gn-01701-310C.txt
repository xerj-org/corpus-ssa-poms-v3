SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.310 - Foreign Pensions Based on a Totalization Agreement With the United States – Effect on the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP)
Effective Dates: 03/24/2026 - Present | TN 19 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Example
Under Swiss law, citizens or residents of Switzerland can normally qualify for a Swiss
retirement pension with as little as one year of Swiss coverage. However, a non-Swiss
citizen who does not reside in Switzerland would normally need 10 years of Swiss coverage
to qualify for a Swiss retirement pension. The U.S. - Swiss agreement modifies Swiss
law so that U.S. citizens can receive a Swiss Pension with as little as one year of
Swiss coverage even if they don’t reside in Switzerland. Thus a U.S. citizen living
in the United States who qualifies for a Swiss pension with less than 10 years of
Swiss coverage would receive a Swiss pension that is based on the agreement. A U.S.
citizen who resides in Switzerland or a U.S. citizen with 10 or more years of Swiss
coverage, regardless of where the U.S. citizen resides, would not receive a Swiss
pension that is based on the agreement.
NOTE:
Effective January 2024, the Social Security Fairness Act (SSFA) of 2023 eliminated
the reduction of Social Security benefits under the Windfall Elimination Provision
(WEP). For more information, please refer to RS 00605.360.
