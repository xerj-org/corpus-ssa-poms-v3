SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01725.210 - Developing Evidence in Claims for Dutch Benefits
Effective Dates: 02/23/1999 - Present | TN 1 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Procedure - Disability claims
In addition to taking the actions in GN 01725.210E., take the following actions when developing medical evidence for Dutch disability
claims.
1. Dutch Benefits Only
Accept any medical evidence the applicant wishes to submit. Do not attempt to secure
evidence for the claimant or offer advice as to what types of evidence may be needed
by the Netherlands.
•
Send any medical evidence that is submitted along with the claims file to OIO.
•
Transmit the file without medical evidence if it is not readily available. Advise
the claimant that the Dutch agency will contact him/her directly, if necessary, to
obtain additional evidence.
2. Dutch Benefits and Concurrent Title II Claim
Follow the instructions in GN 01702.200 - GN 01702.215 to process claims for Dutch disability benefits and concurrent title II disability
claims.
