SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01711.125 - Eligibility for Norwegian Benefits Under the Agreement
Effective Dates: 12/08/1994 - Present | TN 2 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201711125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
The agreement provides that Norway will count a worker's U.S. coverage, where necessary,
when determining eligibility for Norwegian benefits under both the basic pension program
and the supplementary pension program.
1. Basic pension
If a person has resided in Norway for at least 1 year but not long enough to meet
the applicable coverage requirement for a basic pension, the person's U.S. and Norwegian
coverage can be combined to meet the requirement. Thus, U.S. QC's can be used to meet
the 3-year minimum coverage requirement for a basic old-age pension, or to meet the
recent-coverage requirements that apply to the basic disability and survivors pension.
2. Supplementary pension
If a person has at least 1 year of coverage under Norway's supplementary pension system,
but not enough to meet the 3-year minimum requirement for a pension, the person's
U.S. QC's can be added to his/her supplementary system coverage credits to meet the
requirement. In addition, U.S. QC's can be used to meet the recent-coverage requirement
for the surviving spouse's pension.
