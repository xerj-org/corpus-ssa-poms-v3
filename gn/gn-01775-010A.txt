A. Processing applications filed in the Naples, Italy Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU)
The Naples Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) takes claims from applicants residing in Romania
who file for U.S. - Romania totalization benefits. The FBU follows the same general
procedures and obtains the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices (FO)
when processing claims under the Agreement, but they cannot adjudicate claims.
