A. Policy
The primary liaison form used by the U.K. agencies is a computer produced form which
includes the U.K. coverage certification. It is designated as “Forms UK /USA 2 and 3”. However, the U.K. agencies may also use the traditional form UK/USA 2.
Both of the forms mentioned above are acceptable for the U.K. agency to:
•
transmit material to OIO or the FSP,
•
send information to OIO or the FSP, and
•
request information from OIO.
