SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.330 - Providing Monthly Benefit Data to Austrian Agencies
Effective Dates: 04/10/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730330 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
•
Month of Entitlement - for purposes of this instruction, month of entitlement pertains
to the first month for which the beneficiary received his /her full monthly benefit
amount (but no earlier than November 1991).
•
Monthly Benefit Amount - defined as the monthly benefit credited (MBC), i.e. the monthly
amount before SMI premiums or taxes are withheld.
