A. Introduction to the U.S.-Iceland Totalization Agreement
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Iceland:
•
eliminates dual social security coverage and taxation of the same work; and
•
permits entitlement to benefits based upon a combination of periods of coverage earned
in the United States and Iceland.
