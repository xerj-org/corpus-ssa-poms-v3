A. Background
Effective May 15, 2000, the Department of the Treasury (DT), in cooperation with the
Richmond Federal Reserve Bank (RFRB) in Richmond, Virginia, implemented the Check
Operations Re-Engineering Effort (CORE) process for deceased payee reclamations. This
process is now handled by Kansas City Federal Reserve Bank (KFRB).
CORE
•
reduces payment over cancellations for checks cashed after death of the beneficiary
or recipient or representative payee.
•
eliminates the need for reclamation of checks cashed after death of a beneficiary
or recipient.
