C. Procedure - Title XVI Only - Favorable Decision - Spouse’s Past-Due Benefits May Be/Are
Used to Calculate and/or Pay Fee
1. Fee Agreement Disapproved - Past-Due Benefits Withheld for Possible Direct Payment
a. CR Disapproves Fee Agreement
When the CR disapproves the fee agreement at the initial or reconsideration level,
the CR prepares the disapproval notice manually through DPS.
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC08 and UTI FEE060; caption ATYC02, UTI ATY817, caption
ATYC09, and UTIFEE093; caption ATYC10 and UTI ATY045; and caption ATYC03 and UTIs
FEE035, FEE107, FEE108, FEE067, FEE085, and FEE087 to notify the claimant and representative
that:
•
the fee agreement is disapproved and why;
•
SSA is withholding the individual’s past-due benefits and the individual’s spouse’s
past-due benefits; and
•
the representative should file a fee petition within 60 days if he/she wishes SSA
to pay the fee directly to him/her.
b. ALJ or AAJ Disapproves Fee Agreement
Do not prepare a fee notice of the disapproval because you are not the decision maker.
In those claims, the ALJ or AAJ notifies the claimant and representative of the disapproved
fee agreement.
Use UTI FEE058, caption ATYC10 and UTI ATY045; caption ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035, FEE107,
FEE108, FEE067, FEE085 and FEE087, to manually notify the claimant and the representative
that:
•
SSA is withholding the individual’s and the individual spouse’s past-due benefits,
and
•
the representative should file a fee petition within 60 days if he/she wishes SSA
to pay the fee directly to him/her.
2. Fee Agreement Approved - Fee Authorized - No IAR
a. CR Approves Fee Agreement
When the CR approves the fee agreement at the initial or reconsideration level and
authorizes a fee, send a manual notice.
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC06 and UTI ATY020; and caption ATYC10 and UTI FEE089.
•
If the representative is not eligible for direct payment, use FEE022.
•
If the representative is eligible for, but waived direct payment, use FEE033.
Use UTI FEE077.
•
If the representative is eligible for and receiving direct payment, use caption ATYC03
and UTIs FEE035, FEE078, FEE080, and FEE047.
Also send a manual notice to the spouse. Refer to GN03940.070F.
b. ALJ or AAJ Approves Fee Agreement
When an ALJ or AAJ approves the fee agreement, the CR authorizes the fee and advises
about fee payment through a manual notice.
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC10 and UTI FEE051.
•
If the representative is not eligible for direct payment, use FEE022.
•
If the representative is eligible for, but waived direct payment, use FEE033.
Use UTI FEE077.
•
If the representative is eligible for and receiving direct payment, use caption ATYC03
and UTIs FEE035, FEE078, FEE080, and FEE047.
Also send a manual notice to the spouse. Refer to GN03940.070F.
3. Fee Agreement Approved - Fee Authorized Includes Spouse’s Past-Due Benefits - IAR
- Direct Payment to Representative
Use UTI FEE038.
If the CR approves the fee agreement, use caption ATYC06 and UTI ATY020.
Use caption ATYC10.
•
If the CR approves the fee agreement, use UTI FEE089.
•
If an ALJ or AAJ approved the fee agreement, use UTI FEE051.
Use UTI FEE077 and caption ATYC03.
•
If SSA is authorizing and paying the fee to the representative and paying the IAR
at the same time, use UTIs FEE035, FEE078 and FEE041. Use UTI FEE043 (partial fee
paid to representative), FEE044 (fee paid to representative, nothing left to pay claimant)
or FEE045 (fee paid, remainder to claimant).
NOTE: UTI FEE042 is not an option because a portion of the fee is being paid from the
spouse’s past-due benefits.
•
If fee payment from the recipient’s withheld past-due benefits is delayed pending
payment of the IAR, use FEE040 and FEE079.
Use UTI FEE047.
4. Fee Agreement Approved - Fee Authorization Based on Spouse’s Past-Due Benefits Delayed
- No Interim Assistance Reimbursement (IAR)
a. CR Approves Fee Agreement
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC06 and UTI ATY020; caption ATYC10 and UTI FEE094.
•
If the representative is not eligible for direct payment, use UTI FEE022.
•
If the representative is eligible for, but waived direct payment, use UTI FEE033.
Use captions and UTIs ATYC02, FEE052, FEEC09, and FEE056.
•
If the representative is eligible for direct payment, use caption ATYC03 and UTIs
FEE035, FEE080 and FEE047.
b. ALJ or AAJ Approves Fee Agreement
Use UTI FEE038, caption ATYC10 and UTIs FEE003 and FEE094.
•
If the representative is not eligible for direct payment, use UTI FEE022.
•
If the representative is eligible for, but waived direct payment, use UTI FEE033.
Use captions and UTIs ATYC02, FEE052, FEEC09, and FEE056.
•
If the representative is eligible for direct payment, use ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035,
FEE080 and FEE047.
