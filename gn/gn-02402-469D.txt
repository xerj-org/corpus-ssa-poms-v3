D. Overview of IDD for Djibouti
IDD payments sent to Djibouti must be in Djibouti Franc (DJF) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Djibouti. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
