Effective Dates: 10/05/2018 - Present | TN 12 (03-24)

D. Exceptions – When do the rules in 20 C.F.R. Part 403 not apply to a request for SSA
employee testimony?
1.
In an SSA administrative proceeding;
2.
In a legal proceeding that SSA is a party ("SSA" here includes the Commissioner and
any employee acting in his or her official capacity);
3.
When the information is requested by the U.S. Department of Justice;
4.
In a criminal proceeding that the U.S. is a party;
5.
In legal proceedings initiated by State or local authorities arising from an investigation
or audit initiated by, or conducted in cooperation with, SSA's Office of the Inspector
General (OIG);
6.
When the information is requested by either house of Congress;
7.
In a law enforcement proceeding related to threats or acts against SSA, its employees,
or its operations ("SSA" here includes the Commissioner and any employee acting in
his or her official capacity); or
8.
Where Federal law or regulations expressly requires a Federal employee to provide
testimony.
