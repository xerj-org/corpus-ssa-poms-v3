SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.900 - Recovery of Title II International Direct Deposit (IDD) Payments Incorrectly Issued After Death of Beneficiary
Effective Dates: 02/23/2016 - Present | TN 85 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408900 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for recovery of incorrect IDD payments
International Direct Deposit (IDD) payments issued after the death of a beneficiary
must be recovered using procedures outlined in GN 02408.901 through GN 02408.920. These procedures are only for IDD payments in which the Routing and Transit Number
(RTN) begins with the number 5, 6, or 7.
NOTE: The Department of Treasury has no authority over financial institutions (FI) in other
countries.
