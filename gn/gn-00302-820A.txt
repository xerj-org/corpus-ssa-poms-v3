A. Receipt of interim correspondence
Occasionally, the Bureau of the Census may have a question about whether a particular
entry refers to the claimant because of a difference in name or other information.
In such cases, it may write to the individual asking for additional information before
issuing a transcript.
Copies of this interim correspondence will also be sent to the DO if the individual
had authorized the Bureau to send the census information to the DO.
