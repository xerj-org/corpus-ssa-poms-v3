SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.310 - Portuguese Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If a Portuguese liaison agency requests a medical examination for a U.S. resident,
SSA will schedule the examination and send the results to the Portuguese agency.
Medical examinations for Portugal are obtained as follows:
•
Portuguese agency requests for medical examinations are made on Forms P/USA 6 and
P/USA 6a.
•
OIO schedules an examination or forwards “pertinent medical evidence” from OIO's files as described in GN 01704.810.
•
SSA requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for Portugal
using a reimbursement form. Since Portugal seldom requests a medical examination,
a Portuguese reimbursement form has not been printed. If a form is needed, the case
should be routed to IOTS.
