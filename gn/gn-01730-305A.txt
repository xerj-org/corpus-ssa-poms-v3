Effective Dates: 12/10/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Austria does not pay life social security benefits directly to family members of old-age
or disability pensioners. (Supplements for children are paid to the insured person.)
Therefore, survivors should be advised to file an application if they think they can
qualify for Austrian survivor benefits.
