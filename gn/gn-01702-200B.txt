B. Policy
Evidence to support claims for U.S. Totalization benefits must be documented the same
as claims for regular title II benefits. If the applicant is unable or unwilling to
provide the required evidence, see GN 01010.410 and document the file regarding efforts
to secure available evidence.
Evidence required to support claims for foreign benefits should be developed in accordance
with GN 01702.215.
