A. Purpose of the Slovak Republic Totalization Agreement
The social security agreement between the United States and the Slovak Republic:
•
eliminates dual social security coverage and taxation of the same work; and
•
permits workers (and their dependents and survivors) to qualify for partial U.S. or
Slovak benefits, even though they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
