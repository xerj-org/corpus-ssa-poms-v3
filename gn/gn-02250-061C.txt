C. Procedure for handling waiver requests in misinformation cases
Obtain a full explanation on Form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other) for the
individual’s reliance on incorrect information in their own words.  Have the individual
describe the information received, when it was received (if known), and the official
source who provided the information. Review the individual's record to determine if
there is support for the individual's allegations of misinformation.  If development
does not preclude a finding of misinformation and the detailed allegation is consistent
with the facts of the case, resolve any doubts in favor of finding that the beneficiary
did receive misinformation.  You may find the individual was not misinformed if there
have been previous allegations for the same reason (refer to GN 02250.021B.2).
Document the misinformation finding in section 18 of the SSA-635.
