SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.086 - Request for Assistance, Federal Agencies
Effective Dates: 06/30/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904086 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
If the needed proof is in the custody of a Federal agency and if the rules in GN 00904.082 do not apply, send the SSA-562-U3 to the parallel DO. A request for assistance involving
contacts with Federal agencies must show that the claimant cannot successfully obtain
the proof, unless operating instructions specify that the contact must be made through
official channels.
