SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00303.440 - Determining LPR Status
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 67 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200303440 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - Documentation
Examine all DHS documents submitted for current validity, referring to GN 00303.400 and the Administrative Confidential
Memorandum as applicable. Scan both sides of the immigration document and a printout of the
SAVE response into the electronic folder (e.g., Evidence Portal, eView). If the document
includes an Alien Registration Number (ARN or A#), enter the ARN in Person Information.
1. MCS Claims
Follow GN 00301.285 and GN 00301.286 for documenting acceptable evidence of LPR status for the claim.
2. All Other Claims
a. Alien Granted LPR Status
Place in the file a copy of the DHS document.
b. American Indian Born in Canada
Place in the file a copy of evidence establishing birth in Canada and that the individual
has “one-half” American Indian blood.
c. Kickapoo Indian Born in Mexico
Place in the file a copy of evidence establishing birth in Mexico and a tribal enrollment
card or American Indian Card verifying LPR status.
d. SAWs
Place in the file a copy of the alien's I-688.
e. Alien Status Adjustment from LTR to LPR
Place in the file a copy of the alien's I-688EXT or I-551.
f. Alien Status Previously Established
•
Place in the file a copy of the SSR or MBR, annotated “LPR
evidence.”
•
For CCE SSI claims: Annotate the file documentation notes on the Alien Status page:
CITZ , e.g., CITZ MBR.
