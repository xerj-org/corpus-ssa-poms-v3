B. Procedure — twenty day followup
Tickle the pending file for 20 days with the following exceptions:
•
Requests for information from VA facilities—35 days; subsequent followups, 35 days.
•
Request to VARO in Manila — 60 days; subsequent followups — 30 days.
•
Requests for information from military service departments or record centers — 90
days.
•
Requests to FBU in Athens — 60 days; subsequent followups — 30 days.
•
Request to FSP in Mexico — 45 days; subsequent followups — 30 days.
•
For diary periods on requests in OIO for foreign contacts, refer to GN 00904.245.
•
See GN 00904.105A. for a complete listing of diary periods.Additional time for transmitting may be added
where the requesting and assisting FO's are widely separated.
