GN 03340.001 Policy on Access to SSA Records Under the Privacy Act
This section describes (1) the policy requirements for individuals' requests for access
to records we maintain about them that are contained in agency systems of records
under the Privacy Act of 1974, and (2) the ability of certain individuals to access
records on another's behalf.
The Privacy Act defines a "record" as any item, collection, or grouping of information
about an individual that is maintained by an agency and that contains the individual's
name or other identifying particular assigned to the individual. The Privacy Act defines
a "system of records" as a group of any records under the control of an agency from
which the agency retrieves information by the name of the individual or by a personal
identifier, such as a Social Security number (SSN).
