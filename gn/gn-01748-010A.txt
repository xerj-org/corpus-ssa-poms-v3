A. Introduction
The Oslo Foreign Service Post takes claims from individuals in its service area who
wish to file for U.S. and/or Danish benefits under the agreement with Denmark. The
Oslo FSP also assists in processing the claims filed with the National Social Security
Agency (NSSA).
When taking claims under the agreement, Oslo should follow the same general procedures
and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
