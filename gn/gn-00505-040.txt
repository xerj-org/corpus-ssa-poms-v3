GN 00505.040 Change of Payee for Beneficiaries Living Abroad - General
From time to time U.S. consular officers report that a beneficiary who is in payment
status is unable to properly manage their benefits or protect their own interests,
or that a representative payee has died or is unsuitable. In some of these cases,
because of knowledge that suspension of payments will result in hardship, the consular
officer will recommend that direct payment to the beneficiary-payee be continued or
that payment be immediately made to another person, pending complete development.
Payment will be made temporarily to a substitute recommended by the consular office,
provided the recommended payee completed an SSA-11-F6 (Application To Be Selected
As Payee). Refer to the instructions in GN 00205.180 to determine if a form SSA-21
is needed.
