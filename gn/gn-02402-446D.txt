D. Overview of IDD for Guyana
IDD payments sent to Guyana must be in Guyanese dollar (GYD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Guyana. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
