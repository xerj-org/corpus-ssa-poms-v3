Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203930025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A representative can file a fee petition only after completion of representational
services, which may be before or after we make a determination or decision.
EXAMPLE: A claimant discharged a representative before we made a final determination on their
claim. The representative may file a fee petition before we make a final determination.
The field office (FO) or processing center (PC) action varies depending on whether
the claim, or post-entitlement or post-eligibility (PE) action has been decided and,
if so, who decided the case.
