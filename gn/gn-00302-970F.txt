SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.970 - Chinese <Quote>Confession</Quote> Cases
Effective Dates: 10/26/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302970 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Note
INS' acceptance of the “confession” statement as sufficient evidence for the administration of immigration laws based
on:
•
All information available in INS files, and
•
Interviews with the individuals and others involved, and
•
Information from other agencies, and
•
INS' long experience with Chinese immigrants.
How other agencies, including SSA, evaluate the statement is not of concern to INS.
