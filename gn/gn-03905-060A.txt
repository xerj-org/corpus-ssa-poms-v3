Effective Dates: 12/10/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Appointed representatives, who want to charge and collect a fee for services before
us can use one of the two mutually exclusive fee authorization processes: the fee
agreement process or the fee petition process. Representatives have the option to
use this form when they want to charge a fee under the fee agreement process.
