D. Appeals of subsequent claims
If the subsequent claim is denied at the initial level, the claimant may appeal to
the reconsideration level and, if necessary, to the hearing level. Follow normal procedures
for taking these requests, including routing the claim to the Processing Center (PC)
or HO as appropriate. The ALJ will hold the subsequent application until the AC acts
on the prior claim.
