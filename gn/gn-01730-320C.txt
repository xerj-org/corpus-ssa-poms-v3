C. Procedure for citizenship requests
Use the following procedure to respond to an Austrian agency's request for citizenship.
1. Check for Citizenship Allegation
Check item 3(f) or 13(e) of form SSA-2490 for a worker's or a widow(er)'s allegation
of citizenship.
2. Enter alleged Citizenship
Enter the alleged citizenship in item II.A.8 or II.B.8. of liaison form SSA-e2960-U3-AU.
3. Attach Evidence
Attach to the SSA-e2960-U3-AU a photocopy of any evidence of citizenship contained
in the file (e.g. a naturalization certificate, passport, etc.)
4. No allegation or evidence
Enter “unknown” in block II.A.8 or II.B.8 of the SSA-e2960-U3-AU if the file does not contain any
allegation or evidence of citizenship (e.g. form SSA-2490 is not in file or the appropriate
question is unanswered).
