SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02610.036 - Recomputations of Title II Offset
Effective Dates: 05/14/2013 - Present | TN 25 (06-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202610036 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. PC procedure for processing recomputations
Process recomputations according to instructions in the following table:
STEP
ACTION
1
Record in Section II. on the SSA-e4345, the revised offset data provided by the FO.
2
Compare the original offset amount to the newly computed offset amount. When comparing
the two windfall offset amounts, remember to apply the $1.00 tolerance for changes
to the monthly benefit credited (MBC) due to rounding, which can be found in SM 03045.910.
3
If the newly computed offset amount is less than the additional retroactive title II benefits payable due to the PE event:
•
Subtract the difference from any retroactive benefits due because of the PE event.
•
Subtract any collectible overpayment. For cross-program recovery of title XVI overpayment
from title II underpayments, see SI 02220.021.
NOTE: After subtracting these amounts, release the remaining title II benefits to the beneficiary.
If the newly computed offset amount exceeds the retroactive title II benefits due as a result of the PE action:
•
Subtract the new offset amount from the title II overpayment.
•
Notify the claimant of any remaining overpayment.
4
Computing non-countable income (NCI) when recomputing the offset:
a.
Compute the amount of additional title II benefits withheld for months in the offset
period.
b.
If the results of step 2 exceed the results of step 4.a., there is no additional NCI.
Enter the NCI from the prior SSA-e4345 in Section II.C. of the new SSA-e4345.
c.
If the result of step 2 is less than the result of step 4.a., the difference is additional
NCI. Add the new NCI to the NCI from the prior computation and enter in Section II.C.
on the SSA-e4345.
d.
If there is no additional offset, add the additional title II benefits payable for
months in the offset period to the NCI from the last offset computation. Enter this
amount in Section II.C. on the SSA-e4345.
