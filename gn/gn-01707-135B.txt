B. Procedure
Because German laws on voluntary contributions are very complex, SSA staff must not
attempt to give advice on this subject. Refer individuals who have questions about
voluntary contributions to the Salaried Employees' Pension Insurance at the address
in GN 01707.020C., or to the German Embassy in Washington or one of the German consulates located around
the country.
