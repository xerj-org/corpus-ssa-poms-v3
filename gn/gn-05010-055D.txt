SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.055 - Special Rules for the First and Last Year of Residency
Effective Dates: 07/30/1994 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
For the purposes of B.2 and C., disregard nominal presences in the U.S. when applying
the rules for the first and last years of residency.
Do not suggest an individual elect to be treated as a resident alien for his first
year of presence. Refer any individual to IRS who asks if he can be treated as a resident
alien for that year or requests a tax refund because he has made such an election.
