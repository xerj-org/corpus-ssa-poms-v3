SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.255 - Recomputations in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/17/2026 - Present | TN 18 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701255 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Recompute the benefit as described in the order given below, using the computation
method described in GN 01701.200.
•
Establish a new relative earnings position (REP) using the additional earnings.
•
Establish a new theoretical earnings record using the new REP.
•
Attribute earnings to years after the benchmark year only if they have actual earnings
and at least 1 quarter of coverage (QC).
•
Establish a new pro rata PIA, including in the pro rata fraction any additional QCs
credited as the result of the additional earnings.
