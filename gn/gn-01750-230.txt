GN 01750.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Czech Republic Agency on Form SSA-e2960–U3–CZ (Transmittal/Request/Certification
Form- USA/CZ3)
Procedure
Prepare a diary for information requested from CSSZ:
•
Diary initial request for 60 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 15 days.
•
Forward the folder to your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) or Module Manager if there
is no response after two follow-ups.
