A. Automatic status notice for an application for Czech benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards claims material (such
as information about a Czech claim, certified copies of evidence, or a copy of the
worker’s U.S. coverage record) to the Czech Republic, the Benefit Earnings Technician
(BET) electronically stores the claims material in the Paperless Processing Center
(PPC) system in DIO before forwarding the claim to the Czech agency. The system automatically
generates a notice that the BET sends to the applicant. The notice informs the applicant
that DIO forwarded the claims material to the Czech Republic.
