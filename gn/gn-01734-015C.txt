C. Procedure - Totalization modules
Use this instruction for processing form LUX/USA 3 if:
•
the block is checked in item I.D to indicate the claimant is filing a U.S. claim,
or
•
a statement of intent to file a U.S. claim is attached.
Step
Action
1
Provide information and/or material that the Luxembourg agency requested on the form
LUX/USA 3.
2
Determine if the claimant is eligible for U.S. benefits.
•
If yes, route all material to CDB to develop a claim.
•
If no, go to step 3.
3
If the claimant is not eligible or is already entitled to U.S. benefits, send an informal
denial notice.
