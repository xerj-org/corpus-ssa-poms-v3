SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.340 - Authorized Translators
Effective Dates: 08/11/2023 - Present | TN 103 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301340 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions of authorized translators:
1. SSA translators
SSA translators consist of two groups:
a.
Field Office (FO) and Processing Center (PC) employees authorized per the instructions
in GN 00301.345. Disability Determination Services (DDS) employees and DDS contract medical employees
translating foreign language documents refer to DI 23045.001.
b.
Translation and Priority Workload Unit (TPWU) translators who hold actual translator
positions. To request TPWU services refer to the procedures in GN 00301.390.
2. Non-SSA translators
Non-SSA translators are translators who are not SSA employees. For guidance on authorizing
non-SSA translators, refer to GN 00301.350.
NOTE:
SSA employees should NOT use any internet translator websites. Employees who attempt
to access one of these sites will receive a warning when attempting to access internet
based websites.
