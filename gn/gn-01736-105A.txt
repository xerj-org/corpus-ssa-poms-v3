SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.105 - Irish Coverage Certification
Effective Dates: 01/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Form IRL/USA 4 (Agreement between Ireland and the United States of America on Social
Security) is the only acceptable form for the certification of Irish coverage. Certifications
in any other format should be forwarded to IOTS for a determination as to their acceptability.
