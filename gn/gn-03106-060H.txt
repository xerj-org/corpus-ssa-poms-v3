H. PROCEDURE - PREPARATION OF SSA-1129-U3 IN TITLE II CASES
In title II cases where the claimant is represented by an attorney and there are past-due
benefits, send an SSA-1129-U3, Statement of Past Due Benefits, to notify OGC of the
amount of benefits payable and the amount withheld for possible payment of attorney
fees.
