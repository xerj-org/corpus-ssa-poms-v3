H. ​SERS limitations
1. Display errors
SERS will not display debt data for the following scenarios
•
The date of birth fields on the MBR and Numident do not match;
•
The Title XVI debtor is deceased (applies to Programmatic Remittances only); or
•
The web service, or any of the other internal data services, encounter an error.
2. Dollar amount thresholds
•
SERS will not allow a payment greater than the total overpayment or debt amount due.
Example: The SERS Programmatic store displays a debt of $400.00; however, you entered a remittance
check for $500.00. You must request a new check for the correct overpayment amount.
