SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.010 - Providing Information to Prospective Organizations and FFS Application Requirements
Effective Dates: 03/31/2021 - Present | TN 17 (03-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
SSA should assist prospective applicants with the fee-for-service (FFS) application
process and explain the program requirements.
Inquiries from a prospective FFS applicant must be forwarded to the servicing FO.
The payee should be advised that a fee cannot be collected unless SSA authorizes them
to do so.
