B. Policy
A religious record of age is preferred evidence only if it was established before age 5.
Follow the instructions in GN 00302.057 and GN 00302.058 to determine the availability of birth records from U.S. states and foreign countries
and whether to ask the claimant to obtain his/her religious record of age. See GN
00307.190 for instructions about the acceptability of foreign evidence and whether
to request it.
NOTE: A souvenir (or keepsake, ornate, ceremonial, complimentary, goodwill, memento, heirloom,
or other alternative) certificate is NOT acceptable as preferred evidence of age.
See GN 00302.052B. for the policy about when you must obtain preferred evidence of age. See GN 00302.056D. and GN 00301.050D. for instructions about souvenir and other alternative certificates.
