B. Kinds of Luxembourg coverage
There are several types of Luxembourg coverage in addition to coverage based on work.
The following periods of coverage may be certified on the record:
•
Mandatory coverage based on work
•
Voluntary coverage based on contributions
•
Noncontributory coverage for periods of child rearing
•
Noncontributory coverage for periods of sickness
