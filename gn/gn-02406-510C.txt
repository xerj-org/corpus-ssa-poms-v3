C. Request for a signed statement of nonreceipt
When forwarding FSP reports of nonreceipt to the Department of the Treasury (Treasury)
for investigation, do not request that FSP secure a signed statement of nonreceipt
from the beneficiary. In this situation, Treasury will accept a signature sample in
lieu of a signed statement.
