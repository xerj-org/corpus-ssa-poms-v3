SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.305 - Life to Death Conversions - Spanish Agreement
Effective Dates: 12/12/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Spain does not pay life Social Security benefits directly to family members of old-age
or disability pensioners. (Supplements may be paid to the worker.) Therefore, survivors
should be advised to file an application if they think they can qualify for Spanish
survivor benefits.
