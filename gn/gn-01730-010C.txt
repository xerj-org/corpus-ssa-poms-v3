SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 12/16/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Follow the procedure below which describes special development and routing instructions
that apply to claims under the agreement filed in the FSP.
1. U.S. Claim
If this is a U.S. claim, do the following:
•
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4, Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social
Security Agreement (Part I) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison Form SSA-e2960-U3-AU to the Hauptverband der Osterreichischen Sozialversicherungstrager
(HOS) to request an Austrian coverage record if it appears the worker is not insured
based on U.S. coverage alone.
•
Forward the claim to OIO, and include the file and follow-up copies of the liaison
form sent to HOS.
2. Austrian Claim
If this is an Austrian claim, do the following:
•
Take a protective statement on Form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other Person)
indicating a desire to file for Austrian benefits and send it to HOS under cover of
Form SSA-e2960-U3-AU.
•
Inform the claimant that an Austrian agency will contact him/her directly to complete
the required Austrian applications.
3. Concurrent U.S./Austrian Claim
If this is a concurrent U.S. and Austrian claim, do the following:
•
Obtain form SSA-2490 (Parts I & II) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-e2960-U3-AU to HOS to request an Austrian coverage record
(if it appears the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone) and to certify
the protective filing date.
•
Forward the claim to OIO, and include the file and follow up copies of the liaison
form sent to HOS.
•
Inform the claimant that an Austrian agency will contact him/her to complete the required
Austrian applications.
