C. Procedure - FBU
When an IDD payment for Germany, Ireland, Italy, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden,
or the United Kingdom is returned because of an incorrect bank or branch
number, do the following:
•
Determine the correct information by referring to the bank routing numbers guide or
documentation in the FBU, or by contacting the beneficiary or the beneficiary's FI.
•
Advise the beneficiary that the payment may be delayed a few days.
•
Make corrective input to avoid further unprocessable transactions.
