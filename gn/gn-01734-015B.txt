SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.015 - How OIO Totalization Modules and CDB Process and Develop Claims Filed With a Luxembourg Agency
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When a Luxembourg agency certifies factors of entitlement as "verified" in Part II
of the form LUX/USA 3, the certification may be accepted as proof of the eligibility
factor. Further development is not required unless there is a material discrepancy.
