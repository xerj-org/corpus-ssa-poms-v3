C. Procedure for when to use the Spanish Language HA-501-U5-SP
When a Spanish-speaking claimant is unable to or has problems with speaking or reading
English,
•
have the claimant complete the HA-501-U5 –SP, and
•
translate and transcribe the information provided on the HA-501-U5-SP to the HA-501-U5
and staple the forms together.
The HA-501-U5-SP is available at the inForms Library or OS 15030.018.
