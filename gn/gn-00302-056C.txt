C. Procedure - Who Must Certify
Religious records must be certified by the custodian of these records in his/her official
capacity, or by an SSA employee. The custodian must indicate his/her official status
by the use of the organization’s letterhead or seal or his/her own seal of office.
He/she must identify the organization and his/her relationship to the records. Do
not develop the date of recordation unless there is doubt about the record’s authenticity.
See GN 00301.035 for instructions about accepting certifications of evidence from other government
or state agencies.
