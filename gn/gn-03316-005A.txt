A. Disclosure within SSA
The Privacy Act (5 U.S.C.
552a(b)(1)) allows disclosures of personal information within SSA to officers and employees
who need the information to perform their duties.
SSA officers and employees must adhere to established standards of conduct when accessing,
using, and disclosing personal information in our records. Section 1.5 of the Overview
of Information Security and our disclosure regulations, 20 C.F.R. Part 401,
Appendix
A, explain the rules of behavior for users and managers of information resource.
