C. Recording the Document Type on EVID/Report of Contact page in CCE
1. Procedure
Use the codes appearing in the charts in GN 00301.288C.2. and GN 00301.288C.3. to record the type of document. However, the type of document may be spelled out,
if it is uncommon or does not appear in the chart.
In addition to the type of document, always code the name(s) shown on the document
if different from the name shown in the “NAME” field on EVID or the “CLAIMANT/PERSON”
field on the Report of Contact page in CCE. If the name is too long for the space
provided, code the name(s) in the “COMMENTS” field on EVID or in the “REPORT TEXT”
field on the Report of Contact page in CCE.
For SSA forms other than the ones listed in GN 00301.286D.4.d., show the form number.
