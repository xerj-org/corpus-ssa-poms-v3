B. Procedure — State Sources
1. Address and FO Code
Office of Vital Statistics
Jesse S. Cooper Building
417
Federal Street
Dover, DE 19901
FO Code 268
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from the Birth,
Marriage, Death, Divorce, and Veterans Certificates website. In addition, you can find EIN information on the Division of
Administrative Payment Services website. Use the address for the Office of Vital Statistics for birth records that
are less than 72 years of age and for death and marriage records that are less than
40 years of age. For older records, contact the Delaware Public Archives; contact
information is available at the Office of Vital Statistics link.
2. Cost of Copy:
$25.00 except divorce.
3. Type of Payment:
Money order or check.
4. Remarks:
Delaware requires a copy of the requester's official photo identification (driver's
license, State ID, or work ID) for vital record requests. A parent's identification
is needed for a child. If an FO employee is the requester, see GN 00308.001B.3. for acceptable forms of identification.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? Yes, but attempt
to verify through the Electronic Verification of Vital Events (EVVE) before contacting
the FO.
FO will verify FAX request accompanied with a photocopy of the birth certificate.
Requests are returned by mail. The FAX number is 833-912-2301. Verifications are made
every Friday.
