GN 00302.410 When a Written DB Determination is Not Required
When not required
A written DB determination is not required when the DB is established based on:
•
A public or religious record established before age 5 which agrees with the claimant's
allegation, regardless of other discrepancies which may exist
NOTE: If the allegation disagrees with such a record or the record is not being used to establish the DB, see GN 00302.440.
•
An SSA record showing an established DB (see GN 00302.010).
•
Automatically convincing evidence guides in GN 00302.110ff.
•
A combination of documents (see GN 00302.125).
•
Two documents, both of which are at least 30 years old (see GN 00302.125)
•
Discrepant documents but the discrepancy is not material (see GN 00302.240).
•
Exceptions to material discrepancy development (see GN00302.180).
