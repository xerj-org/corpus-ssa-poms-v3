D. Overview of IDD for Sint Maarten
IDD payments sent to Sint Maarten must be in Caribbean Guilder (XCG). The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Sint Maarten. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for additional information.
