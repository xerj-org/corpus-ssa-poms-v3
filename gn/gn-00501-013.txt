GN 00501.013 Explanations for Types of Payees
NOTE: The descriptions for types of payees and types of custodians are the same.
In the Electronic Representative Payee System, we no longer use the custody code “PYE”
(payee is custodian). An explanation of the various types of payees are in GN 00501.013A.
