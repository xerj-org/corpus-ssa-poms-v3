B. Official Form 410
Prepare the Official Form 410 electronically, using this link to obtain the form:
Form 410
B. This form is authorized by the Court and used to file a Proof of Claim. In Part
1, the form has separate entries for where to send notices and where to send payment.
In Part 3, check the second box that says, "I am the creditor's attorney or authorized
agent."
