B. Policy - Jurisdiction Of Requests
Generally, the operational component that has the requested material should respond
to the request. For example, if the request is for claims folder material and the
folder is in ODIO, that component should provide the information. Likewise, if the
request is for earnings information, OCRO is responsible for the reply. However, the
component that receives the initial request may retain jurisdiction.
