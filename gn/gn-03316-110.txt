GN 03316.110 Disclosure Without Consent to Auxiliary Claimants/Beneficiaries about a Primary Claimant/Beneficiary
or Number Holder (NH)
Auxiliary claimants or beneficiaries are entitled to information from the record of
the NH or primary beneficiary, without his or her written consent, when the auxiliary
needs the information to pursue his or her claim for benefits on the NH’s or primary
beneficiary’s record.
