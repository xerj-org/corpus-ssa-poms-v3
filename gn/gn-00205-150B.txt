B. Marital history
We collect marital history per GN 00205.070 to determine eligibility to benefits. The claimant must establish if they are currently
married. If they had intervening marriages since the death of the NH, obtain proof
of termination and consider possible entitlement on each Social Security number (SSN).
For developing proof of termination, see GN 00305.140.
