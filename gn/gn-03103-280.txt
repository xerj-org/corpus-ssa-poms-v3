GN 03103.280 Processing Hearing Requests through Modernized Claims System (MCS)
Completing hearing requests through the Modernized Claims System (MCS) does not change
policy and procedure for processing a hearing request. For instructions on how to
complete hearing screens and process hearing requests through MCS, refer to MSOM MCS
010.001.
