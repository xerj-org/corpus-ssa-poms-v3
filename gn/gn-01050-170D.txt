D. One Individual Claiming CDB and DIB—Two SSN's
When an individual files a CDB application on their parent's or grandparent's SSN
and a DIB application on their own SSN, transmit the two applications simultaneously
in separate folders. Prepare a DIB folder in accordance with GN 01050.165 and the CDB folder in accordance with GN 01050.170A in this section. In transmitting a combined CDB and DIB claims, staple the back flap
of the DIB folder to the front flap of the CDB folder.
