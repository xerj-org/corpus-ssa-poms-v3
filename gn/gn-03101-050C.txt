C. Policy for cost of living adjustment (COLA) notice
A COLA is an initial determination subject to administrative and judicial review.
Therefore, the claimant can question our calculation. Direct depositors receive a
printed notice. For non-direct depositors who get a check stuffer, use the first check
issued in the increased amount as the notice of the COLA for counting the period for
appeal purposes.
NOTE: For information on COLA's and administrative finality, see GN 04030.060.
