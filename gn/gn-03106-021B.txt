B. PROCEDURE - OALC
The OALC obtains case control queries to either respond to the order/subpoena or locate
the folder.
1. Systems Queries Contain Enough Information to Comply With Court Order/Subpoena
If systems queries, obtained on the SSN of the individual identified in the court
documents, contain enough information to comply with the court order/subpoena request,
OALC:
•
Prepares the response to the request for information.
•
Prepares a certification attesting to the authenticity of the information provided
in response to the court order/subpoena.
•
Insures that the Certification Seal is affixed.
•
Forwards the sealed documents to OPL for release. Be sure to provide OPL with OALC
contact name and telephone number.
2. Systems Queries Do Not Contain Enough Information to Comply With Court Order/Subpoena
If systems queries, obtained on the SSN of the individual identified in the court
documents, do not contain the information needed to comply with the court order/subpoena,
OALC:
•
Contacts the component/office that has the information necessary to comply with the
order/subpoena and faxes a copy of the subpoena request to that office.
•
Monitors component compliance to insure that a timely response to the order/subpoena
is released.
