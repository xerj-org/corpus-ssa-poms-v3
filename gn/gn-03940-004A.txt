A. Partially Favorable Decisions
If we issue a partially favorable determination or decision (hereinafter, we generally
refer to both as a "decision"), the decision maker will approve the fee agreement,
subject to the conditions in GN 03940.003, and if approved, we will authorize a fee under the terms of the agreement. Refer
to DI 25501.280 for additional information on what constitutes a partially favorable decision.
NOTE: Do not confuse partially favorable decisions or decisions revised on appeal with
partial adjudication. Partial adjudication, described in GN 01010.110, is our method of awarding benefits to a claimant before completing all development
on a claim. When we partially adjudicate a claim, the decision is an interim action
subject to revision. As such, we defer authorizing a fee in these claims until we
have completed all development. GN 03940.036 provides procedures for partial adjudication involving deferred development.
