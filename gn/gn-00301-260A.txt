SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.260 - Annotating or Altering Documents or Writings
Effective Dates: 05/15/2003 - Present | TN 9 (03-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301260 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Show NH's SSN
The NH's social security number (SSN) must be shown on all documents retained by SSA
so that the documents are associated with the proper claim.
2. Writing comments on evidentiary documents
Never alter or annotate documents or writings (including letters or statements from
the public, reports of contact, etc.) to be used as evidence. The alteration may prejudice
a fair hearing and subject SSA to public criticism.
