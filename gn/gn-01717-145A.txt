SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01717.145 - Exception to Section 202(t)(11) Alien Nonpayment Provision Under Swedish Agreement
Effective Dates: 02/07/1996 - Present | TN 1 (09-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201717145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Benefits generally may not be paid to aliens outside the United States who first became
eligible for dependents or survivors benefits after 1984, unless they satisfy certain
U.S. residency requirements.
As explained in GN 01701.150A.2., citizens or residents of a country with which the U.S. has a totalization agreement
are exempt from these requirements. Therefore, residents of Sweden or Swedish citizens,
regardless of where they reside, are exempt from these requirements.
