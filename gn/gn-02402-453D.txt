D. Overview of IDD for Mongolia
IDD payments sent to Mongolia must be in Mongolian tugrik (MNT) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Mongolia.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
