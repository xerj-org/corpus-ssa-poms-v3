SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.320 - How to Use the Overprinted SSA-3428-U2 (Determination of Disallowance Coding Sheet)
Effective Dates: 06/02/2014 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
When preparing an overprinted SSA-3428-U2:
•
Complete all information in the areas that are not blocked out.
•
Designate the appropriate disallowance letter and appropriate totalization denial
and reconsideration paragraphs.
•
Enter the most advantageous date claim filed for totalization benefits based on information
in file to allow the BETs to certify the most advantageous date to foreign agencies.
•
Route the folder to a BA to post the TSC D2 when insured status is not met after combining
coverage from both countries.
