SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.205 - Requesting Help from Other Field Offices (FOs)
Effective Dates: 05/22/2012 - Present | TN 39 (05-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. Obtaining evidence from another FO
If the claimant needs help securing evidence and another FO has more access to obtain
it, request assistance from the servicing FO (FO you are requesting service from)
to assist in obtaining the necessary evidence or information.
Example: The number holder (NH) needs five to six weeks to obtain evidence, but the servicing
FO (field office that we are asking for assistance) can obtain the same evidence in
one week. The NH’s local FO should request assistance from the servicing FO that has
the capability to obtain the evidence quicker.
2. How to request evidence
When requesting evidence from another FO, use form SSA-562-U3 Request for Assistance
Certification or the Electronic e562 Request for Assistance/Certification of Documents
or Records.
NOTE: When necessary, the SSA-562-U3 assistance request should include a check or money
order for payment of any fees associated with obtaining evidence.
