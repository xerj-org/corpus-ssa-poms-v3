E. Evidence of Services Rendered Where Bill Not Paid by Beneficiary Before Death
If a bill was paid by the beneficiary before death, evidence that the services were
rendered is not needed because the payment is evidence that the services were rendered.
However, in cases where the beneficiary did not pay the bill before death, each claim
must be substantiated by evidence submitted to the carrier that the services were
rendered to the decedent enrollee.
