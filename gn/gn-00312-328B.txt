B. Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Divorce and Death Certificates
See GN 00307.784 for information on obtaining birth certificates from Romania.
Since it takes four to eight months to get a reply from the Romanian authorities,
do not follow up until 120 days have elapsed from the date the initial request was
made.
1. Office to Contact
Consular Section
U.S. Embassy, Bucharest
Department of State
Washington, D.C. 20521-5260
2. Fee
a.
$36.00 U.S. if the request is made through a Romanian Embassy
b.
$8.00 U.S. if the request is made through the American Embassy
Payment must be by postal money order payable to “American Embassy, Bucharest.”
