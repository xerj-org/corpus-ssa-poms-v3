SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for French Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 4 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a French claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for French benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the French agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP)
application. See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access
the EP User Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarding the claim to France
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to France, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to France
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to France, advise applicant to contact the French
agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information online at:
Central Agency of Social
Security Agencies (ACOSS)
NOTE: DIO is unable to determine the status of a French benefits claim after a technician
forwards the claim to France. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
