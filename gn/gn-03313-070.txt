GN 03313.070 Disclosure to the Department of Justice (DOJ)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from DOJ, contact Privacy and Disclosure Policy (PDP).
