SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.001 - Overpayment Recovery by Benefit Adjustment
Effective Dates: 06/25/2024 - Present | TN 84 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What payments to withhold
To recover an overpayment, we withhold the following payments from the overpaid individual,
representative payee, or any other individual currently receiving benefits on the
same earnings record on which the overpayment occurred (contingent liability), as
applicable:
•
Ten percent of (10%)any current and future monthly benefit payment(s);
•
Any underpayment (see GN 02301.001A);
•
Any lump-sum death payment (LSDP);
•
Any misused funds (from the representative payee) that we reissue to an overpaid individual;
or
•
Any other Social Security administered program payment(s) (i.e. cross program recovery).
