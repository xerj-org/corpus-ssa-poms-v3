B. Compulsory Coverage
The following provides information about compulsory coverage under the Danish system.
1. Folkepension (FP)
In general, all persons who reside in Denmark from age 15 up to age 65 are covered
under the FP or “Social” pension program. The FP program is non-contributory and funded
from general tax revenues.
2. Labour Market Supplementary Pension (ATP)
Employees in Denmark ages 16 to full retirement age who are permanently employed at
least 9 hours a week pay ATP mandatory contributions.
