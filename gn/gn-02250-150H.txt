SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02250.150 - Against Equity and Good Conscience - Title II and Title XVI
Effective Dates: 12/19/2024 - Present | TN 74 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202250150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Examples of an adult individual overpaid as a minor child
Example 1:
Xaden received SSI as a child. We determined that Xaden was overpaid $3,000 due to
deemed resources when they were still a child.  At that time, we notified Xaden and
Xaden’s representative payee of the overpayment.  We recovered a portion of the overpayment
by payment adjustment until Xaden’s benefits ceased at age 18.  At age 19, Xaden requested
a waiver of the entire original overpayment.  We presume Xaden is not at fault because
Xaden was a child and had a representative payee at the time of the overpayment and
there was no evidence of fault.  We deem recovery of the remaining amount at the time
that Xaden turned 18 years old, as against equity and good conscience because Xaden
was a child with a representative payee when they were overpaid. We will waive only
the portion of the outstanding overpayment while Xaden was an adult.  We will not
waive the overpayment amount that was recovered when Xaden was a child as against
equity and good conscience.
Example 2:
Violet received child’s benefits. We determined that they were overpaid $4,000 at
age 13 due to their parent’s wages. We notified Violet and the representative payee
of the overpayment.  Violet is now an adult and receives benefits on the same record
as a DAC. Violet requested a waiver.  We presume that Violet is not at fault because
Violet was a minor child and had a representative payee at the time of the overpayment.
We deem recovery is against equity and good conscience under this provision because
Violet is now an adult. We will waive only the portion of the outstanding overpayment
while Violet was an adult.  We will not waive the overpayment amount that was recovered
when Violet was a child as against equity and good conscience.
