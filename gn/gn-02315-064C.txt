C. Effect of Affidavit
Section 72-3-1102 of the Montana Code provides that the person paying the successor
pursuant to an affidavit is discharged and released to the same extent as if the person
dealt with a personal representative of decedent. The person is not required to inquire
into the truth of any statement in the affidavit. If any person to whom an affidavit
is delivered refuses to pay, payment may be compelled by judicial process. The person
to whom the payment is made is answerable and accountable for the property to any
personal representative of the estate or any other person having a superior right.
