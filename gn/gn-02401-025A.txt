A. Operating procedure for combining payments from the same trust fund
1. General information
We authorize each benefit award separately, but combine the payments.
2. Requirement for combining benefits
•
A person entitled to retirement insurance benefits (RIB) or disability insurance benefits
(DIB) is also entitled to a wife's or husband's benefit payable from the same trust
fund; or
•
A person is simultaneously entitled to a RIB and a survivor benefit.
