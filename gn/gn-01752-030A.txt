A. Introduction - KRUS
Zaklad Ubezpieczen Spolecznych (ZUS) or Kasa Rolniczego Ubezpieczenia Spolecznego
(KRUS) will be the only Republic of Poland insurance agencies that will be used to
communicate and process the agreement workload. If periods of agriculture are alleged
all applications go to KRUS for a determination.
