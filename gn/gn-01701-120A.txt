SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.120 - Use of Simplified Method For Determining U.S. Quarters of Coverage (QCs) in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Simplified method may be used
Where applicable, the simplified method may be used when determining the number of
U.S. QCs to be added to foreign coverage to determine whether fully insured status is met based on combined coverage.
2. Simplified method may Not be used
The simplified method may not be used to:
•
determine if the number holder meets the 6 QCs minimum requirement for Totalization
benefits,
•
determine the pro rata fraction when computing Totalization benefits (see GN 01701.200D), or
•
determine the number of U.S. QCs to be certified to the foreign country.
