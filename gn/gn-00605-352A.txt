A. Policy - When a final accounting is required
Representative payees (payee) who are exempt from annual representative payee accounting
are not selected for final accounting, see GN 00605.015 for a list of exempted payees. For all other Title XVI (or concurrent Title II/Title
XVI) cases, the field office (FO) must obtain a final accounting report when a payee’s
appointment terminates and the former payee has served 12 months or less.
