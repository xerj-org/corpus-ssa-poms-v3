GN 03313.065 Disclosure to the United States Postal Service (USPS)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from USPS, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
We disclose certain non-tax return information to USPS to investigate lost, stolen,
or forged Social Security or Medicare checks.
