E. Procedure - Authorized Fee Revised/Recomputed - Direct Payment Not Involved
If SSA recalculates or changes the authorized fee amount after notifying the claimant
of the authorized amount and direct payment is not involved, use UTI XVI720 (FEE038
in DPS), the caption UTI XVI717 (ATYC10 in DPS), and UTIs XVI536 (FEE123 in DPS) and
FEE008 (FEE008 in DPS).
NOTE: If the fee recalculation involves direct payment or any action that requires manual
adjustment, a manual notice is required. Begin with the above sequence of paragraphs
and add the appropriate UTIs or prepare case specific language to address the direct
payment situation.
