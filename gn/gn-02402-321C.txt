SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.321 - Coding Dominican Republic Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/20/2026 - Present | TN 324 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402321 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor bank for IDD to several countries, including the Dominican
Republic. All countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix of
70 at the beginning of the routing number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the first
position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for the Dominican
Republic is J.
