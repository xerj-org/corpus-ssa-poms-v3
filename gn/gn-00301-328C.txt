C. Residual Trailer Medical Information
At times, Field Offices may receive residual trailer medical information from the
DDS. This material (if received) should be sent by mail to associate with the current
paper folder. If an electronic file exists, the material should be returned to the
DDS for processing. (See DI 22520.001 through DI 22520.010 and DI 81020.140)
