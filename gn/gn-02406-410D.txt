D. Treasury action for original check negotiated after courtesy disbursement or replacement
issued
If someone cashes the original check and the courtesy disbursement (or replacement)
checks, Treasury sends out claims packages for both checks. For more information on
where Treasury sends the claims package, see GN 02406.410 C in this section.
NOTE: For information on Payment Over Cancellations (Treasury credit reversals) see GN 02406.245.
