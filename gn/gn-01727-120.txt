GN 01727.120 U.S. Totalization Benefits under the U.S. - Portuguese Agreement
Under the Agreement, if a Number Holder (NH) has at least six U.S. Quarters of Coverage
(QCs), but not enough to meet insured status requirements for regular U.S. benefits,
the Social Security Administration (SSA) can add the NH's credits under the Portuguese
social security system to the NH's U.S. coverage to permit the NH (or any survivors)
to qualify for U.S. Totalization benefits.
