E. Totalization claims
All claims for totalization benefits, including those in which at least one beneficiary
resides in the U.S. and entitled to totalization benefits, are under DIO (PC8)'s jurisdiction
(see GN 01702.310).
Claimants who reside in the U.S. and who are not insured for RSDI benefits but have
at least 6 quarters of coverage and received a claim denial for totalization benefits
are under DIO (PC8)'s jurisdiction. Refer to GN 02605.200 and GN 02605.230.
