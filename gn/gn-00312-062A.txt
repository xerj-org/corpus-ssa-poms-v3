A. Policy for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
The British Virgin Islands has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration
records. Records are held at the Vital Records and Statistics (VRS). Forms for records
are located at https://doh.vi.gov/programs/vital-records-statistics/.
