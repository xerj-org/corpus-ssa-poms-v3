A. Policy on requests for policy interpretation
The Assistant Regional Commissioner (ARC), Management, Operations, and Support (MOS)
provides the Field Office (FO) and Processing Centers (PCs) with guidance in general
policy questions, Hospital and Supplemental Insurance (HI/SMI) entitlement, non-claim
questions and questions on existing POMS instructions. Consider issues involving more
than one claim general policy questions.
NOTE: Refer all Medicare coverage issues to the Medicare Carrier or Intermediary.
