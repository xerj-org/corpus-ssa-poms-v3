GN 00306.001 Definitions of Terms Used in Determining Parent-Child Relationship for Title II Benefits
This section provides the Social Security Administration's definitions of terms used
to determine a parent-child relationship for Title II benefits.
NOTE: State law may use a term differently. When used in the context of State law, apply
the State's definition unless otherwise indicated.
