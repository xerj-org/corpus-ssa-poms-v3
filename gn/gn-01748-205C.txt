SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01748.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed Outside Denmark
Effective Dates: 02/03/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201748205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. U.S. claims/Danish totalization benefits
The Division of International Operations (DIO) takes the following action.
Sends the Danish agency the following:
•
A liaison form to request a Danish Coverage record to certify the date for the Danish
benefits and to certify established factors of entitlement, such as date of birth.
•
A copy of SSA-2490 (Part 1), SSA -2960 and ICERS record used to apply Danish Totalization
benefits.
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
