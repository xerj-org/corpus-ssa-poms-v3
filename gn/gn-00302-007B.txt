SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.007 - When Age Must be Established for a Disallowed Claimant
Effective Dates: 03/10/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Rule: Claimant Insured
Use the following to determine the proof-of-age development required for a disallowance
where the claimant is insured by some or all DBs in the file:
If the claimant was...
Then...
​
Cooperative in exploring and obtaining all known existing records of age
Establish the DB regardless of the number of discrepancies or scarcity of evidence.
​
​
Uncooperative in obtaining required evidence
Document the file to show:
•
What existing evidence was not submitted;
•
That all necessary assistance was give the claimant per GN 01010.410; and
•
Why the adjudicator believes that he/she cannot make a DB determination without further
evidence.
NOTE:In the case of a minor or incapable adult beneficiary see GN 00502.001.
