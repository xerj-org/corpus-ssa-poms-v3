GN 03970.011 Potentially Unqualified Attorney and Non-Attorney Representatives
The Social Security Act (Act) requires certain qualifications for attorney and non-attorney
representatives (see GN 03910.020A). If a representative does not meet these qualifications, we may (1) not recognize
the appointment, or (2) take action to disqualify the representative based on the
guidance in this section.
