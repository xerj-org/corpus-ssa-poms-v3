SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03970.030 - Action to Suspend, Disqualify, or Withdraw Charges Against a Representative by the Office of the General Counsel (OGC)
Effective Dates: 09/30/2024 - Present | TN 22 (10-24)

C. Policy for representative’s answer
The representative's answer must be in writing, signed under oath (or affirmation),
and filed within 14 business days from the date of the notice, or from the date the
notice was delivered personally to the representative. The representative may request
an extension to file an answer and present good cause as to why the representative
needs the extension. Upon a finding of good cause, OGC may extend the representative’s
time for filing an answer. The representative must file the answer with OGC at the
address indicated in the notice. If the representative fails to file an answer, files
an untimely answer without good cause, or files a defective answer, the representative
will not have the right to present evidence at a hearing. However, even if the representative
loses the right to present evidence, the hearing officer may still permit the representative
to present a statement about the sufficiency of the evidence or the validity of the
proceedings.
