SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01758.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed in Brazil
Effective Dates: 04/10/2019 - Present | TN 2 (04-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201758210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and Brazilian or Brazilian claim only
Prepare a Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 to:
1.
Request the Brazilian certified coverage record and include a copy of the Form SSA
2960 USA/BR 3; and
2.
Send the the National Social Insurance Institute (INSS) the information or documentation
they requested.
