SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.190 - Claimant Submits Best Evidence or No Numident or Other SSA Record Exists
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302190 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Rule
Best evidence is evidence that establishes a person's DB regardless of other evidence which may
exist. Therefore, in a material discrepancy case when best evidence is submitted by
a claimant for whom there is no NUMIDENT or other SSA record of age, another record
of age need not be obtained. This is an exception to the rule that at least two documents must be
obtained when a DB determination is based on other than preferred evidence.
