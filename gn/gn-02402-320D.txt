D. Overview of IDD for Denmark
IDD payments sent to Denmark must be in Danish Kroner (DKK) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Denmark. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
