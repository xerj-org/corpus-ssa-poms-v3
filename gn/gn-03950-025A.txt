A. Introduction
The second step in the administrative review process is a preliminary examination
of the claim(s) file, including the fee petition and the initial fee authorization.
The purpose of this preliminary review is to:
•
Determine if more information is needed to make the administrative review decision;
and
•
Identify issues, if any, which we did not resolve when we initially authorized the
fee.
