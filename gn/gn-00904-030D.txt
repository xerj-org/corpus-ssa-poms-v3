D. Principal claimant not eligible
Even though the principal claimant may not be eligible, if he or she has filed a claim,
he or she will remain the principal claimant until his or her claim is forwarded and
the processing center completes adjudication of the claim. Such resident office jurisdiction
is extended to ensure orderly processing if other claims are filed.
