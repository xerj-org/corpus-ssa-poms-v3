B. Process - MATPSC
The following occurs when the FORT/CCFORT and remittances are sent to MATPSC.
•
Each FORT/CCFORT is reviewed in the MATPSC. Since the Remittance Supervisor has already
checked the remittance against the FORT/CCFORT, MATPSC should be able to verify the
FORT/CCFORT without encountering discrepancies. However, when there is a discrepancy,
such as no money amount shown on a check, or a check is missing, further action will
be necessary.
