A. POLICY - BENEFIT PROGRAMS INCLUDED
The provisions of the agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and Norwegian coverage credits apply only to retirement, survivors and
disability benefits under each country's system. The agreement does not apply to Medicare
benefits or Supplemental Security Income payments.
