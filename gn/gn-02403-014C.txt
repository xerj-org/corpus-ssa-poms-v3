C. Forms of payment accepted in SERS
•
SSA accepts checks (drawn on domestic financial institutions) or money orders payable
to SSA.
NOTE: Send fees paid by checks drawn on foreign financial institutions, either in foreign
amount or in United States dollars, to the Office of Financial Statements (FS) at
this address:
Attention: SERS
Office of Financial Statements
P.O. Box 17042
Baltimore, Maryland 21235
SSA does not accept cash to pay fees.
