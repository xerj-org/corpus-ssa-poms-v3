D. What is the procedure for handling foreign agency liaison forms?
In addition to the procedures for handling foreign agency liaison forms contained
in GN 01704.400 through GN 01704.990, return to Centrelink any request that does not indicate that it is needed to process
a claim under the agreement unless
•
written consent to disclose in attached or
•
the file contains an SSA-2490 signed by the claimant.
