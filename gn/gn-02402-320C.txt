Effective Dates: 01/29/2016 - Present | TN 263 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processor bank in Denmark will have a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the routing
number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the first position of the Depositor Account
Number (DAN). The letter assigned for Denmark is “F.”
