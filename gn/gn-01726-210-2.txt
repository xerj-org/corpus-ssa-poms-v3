SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in DIO for Claims Filed in the Netherlands
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

2. U.S. and Dutch or Dutch Claim Only
Prepare a claims package for U.S. and Dutch or Dutch Claim only if:
•
Dutch coverage record is required and has not been requested by the FSP; or
•
A Dutch agency has requested information or documentation, and
•
include Form SSA-2960-NL, and
•
information or documentation requested by Dutch agency.
