SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01763.145 - Exceptions to Section 202(t)(11) U.S. Residency Requirements for Certain Dependents and Survivors under the Agreement with Iceland
Effective Dates: 04/25/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201763145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Dependent or survivor does not meet U.S. residency requirement under the agreement
If a non-U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the 5-year residency requirement
and alleges citizenship of or residence in Iceland, develop for Icelandic citizenship
and residence status.
