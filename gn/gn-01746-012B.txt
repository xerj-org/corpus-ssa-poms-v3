SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01746.012 - How VARO Processes and Develops Claims Filed With Japanese Agencies
Effective Dates: 10/06/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201746012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
This procedure explains how VARO should process referrals from Japanese agencies.
1. Claimant Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Forward all material to OIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
2. Claimant Not Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Process the claim in the same manner shown in GN 01746.010B.
