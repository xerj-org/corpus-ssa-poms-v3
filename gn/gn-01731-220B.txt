B. Filing additional Finnish special application forms
Social Security Administration (SSA) personnel do not take the special
application forms in the Field Offices (FO). After the Finnish agency receives a claim
for Finnish benefits filed with SSA, the Finnish agency with jurisdiction will mail
the appropriate Finnish application forms to the applicant. The Finnish agency will
include instructions asking the claimant to complete the forms and mail them directly
to the Finnish agency at the address shown on the application form along with any
needed additional evidence.
