C. Effective date of parent-child relationship based on other satisfactory evidence and
living with or support
When establishing parentage using the other satisfactory evidence provision of section
216(h)(3), the parent-child relationship can begin no earlier than the date of the
evidence, unless the evidence points to an earlier date. The living with or contributions
requirement must also be met.
EXCEPTION: If a signed statement attesting to a person's knowledge about a child's paternity
is being used as evidence, the date to which the statement points may be used as the
effective date of the relationship.
