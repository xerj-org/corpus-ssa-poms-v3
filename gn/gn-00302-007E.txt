SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.007 - When Age Must be Established for a Disallowed Claimant
Effective Dates: 03/10/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Exception
If the claimant has submitted sufficient evidence to establish a DB, the adjudicator
will establish the DB. If you establish a different DB than the claimant alleged,
inform him/her of the DB established (even if DB is not the reason for disallowance).
