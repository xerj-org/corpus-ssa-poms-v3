SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.540 - Delayed Birth Certificates
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302540 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
A DBC (i.e., a BC established at age 5 or later) is usually considered to be one piece
of evidence. Even if the documents on which the DBC was based are described in great
detail, the DBC can not normally be “dismantled” and used as more than one document, except as explained in subsection E. below. However,
if the supporting documents shown on the DBC are actually presented (in addition to
the DBC), consider them as separate pieces of evidence.
