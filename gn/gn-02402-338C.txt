C. Description of Mexico Bank Data
There are two possibilities for Mexico bank data: the 18-digit CLABE for a regular
bank account, or the 3-digit bank code plus the 16-digit Debit Card information, totaling
19 digits.
The 18-digit CLABE is constructed as follows:
•
3-digit ABM Bank Number (#ABM de Banco),
•
3-digit Locality (Plaza),
•
11-digit Account Number (Número de Cuenta), and
•
1-digit Check Digit (Digito Verificador).
If the beneficiary requests direct deposit to a Debit Card Account (Tarjeta de
Débito), the necessary information is as follows:
•
3-digit ABM Bank Number (#ABM de Banco),
•
16-digit Debit Card Number (Tarjeta de Débito). The Debit Card Number is shown on
the Debit Card.
