SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.320 - Providing Monthly Benefit Data to German Agencies
Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Month of entitlement
Month of Entitlement - for purposes of this instruction, month of entitlement pertains to the first month
for which the beneficiary received his /her full monthly benefit amount (but no earlier than December 1979, the effective month of
the German Agreement).
2. Monthly benefit amount
Monthly benefit amount (MBA - for purposes of this instruction, the MBA is defined as the monthly benefit
credited (MBC), i.e., the monthly amount before SMI premiums or taxes are actually
withheld, but after the appropriate dollar rounding (considering any deductible SMI
premium).
