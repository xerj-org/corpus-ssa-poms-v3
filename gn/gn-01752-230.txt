GN 01752.230 Controlling Requests to the ZUS or KRUS Agency on Form SSA-2960 USA/PL3 — Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from ZUS or KRUS:
•
Diary initial request for 180 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 90 days.
•
Forward the folder to your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) or Module Manager if no response
has been received after two follow-ups (i.e., after 365 days).
