A. Procedure for processing access requests
Individuals can make access requests by mailing their request to the system of records
manager for the system of record the individual is seeking access to or by visiting
their local Social Security office. The receiving component should process an individual’s
request for records, if the access request was properly made and the information is
available. If the request was properly made but the information is not available to
the receiving component, forward the request to the appropriate component for processing.
If the request was not properly made, direct the requester on how to make a proper
request.
If we are unable to provide the requested information within 30 days, send a notice
to the requester to:
•
acknowledge receipt of the request;
•
explain their reason for the delay; and
•
advise of an approximate date when we may be able to send the requested information.
