B. Incorrect Box 2 SSN
Since Box 2 of the benefit statement displays the Beneficiary's Own Account Number
(BOAN) as shown on the MBR, it is possible the MBR could show no BOAN or an incorrect
one. The beneficiary may inquire about an incorrect or missing SSN on the benefit
statement.
