3. Type of Payment:
Money order payable to Treasurer of Guam for requests for birth, death, and marriage
records. Money order, cashier's check, personal check, and business check payable
to the Superior Court of Guam for divorce documents.
There is a $50.00 nonsufficient funds (NSF) fee for any overdraft checks. They do
accept payment via credit card, but there is a 3.1% convenience fee. Please reference
the case number or invoice number when sending payment via mail.
Note:
For an off-island request from a SSA field office, a check issued by SSA is acceptable.
