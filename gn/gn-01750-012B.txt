SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01750.012 - How the Foreign Service Post in Warsaw, Poland Processes and Develops Claims Filed With the Czech Republic Agency
Effective Dates: 04/25/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201750012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
This procedure explains how the FSP in Warsaw, Poland should process referrals from
CSSZ.
1. Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all material to DIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not entitled to US benefits
Process the claim in the same manner shown in GN 01750.010B.
