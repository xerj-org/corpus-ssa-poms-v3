SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.021 - Separate HI Filing
Effective Dates: 07/29/2026 - Present | TN 179 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. General eligibility to HI entitlement
Eligibility to HI entitlement is tied to monthly benefits. Any individual establishing
entitlement to monthly benefits cannot decline HI. HI entitlement is a necessary result
of entitlement to monthly benefits for claimants age 65, see GN 00204.020D.1.a.
Generally, the 6 month limitation on retroactivity applies. However, instructions
outline the guidelines for Deemed Insured HI and explain when an exception to the
retroactivity provision applies, see HI 00801.047 and HI 00801.052.
2. Requirements for filing for HI-only
a. Person may qualify for HI-only benefits
A person may qualify for HI-only benefits if:
•
they are age 65 or older; and
•
meet all entitlement factors for monthly benefits (except for filing an application).
b. Auxiliary may qualify for HI-only benefits
An auxiliary may qualify for HI-only benefits if:
•
they are age 65 or older; and
•
the NH meets all entitlement factors (except for filing an application).
