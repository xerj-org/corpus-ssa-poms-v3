D. Kinds of Danish coverage
The types of Danish coverage that may be on the Danish coverage record are:
•
Mandatory coverage based on work
•
Cash Sickness benefits from employer
•
Cash Sickness benefits from municipality (SSA does not accept code 610)
•
Cash Unemployment benefits (SSA does not accept code 600)
