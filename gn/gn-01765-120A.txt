A. Crediting Romanian coverage
Social Security Administration (SSA) will credit one U.S. quarter of coverage (QC)
for every three months of Romanian coverage (or remaining fraction thereof) in a calendar
year. (Romanian coverage is measured in months.)
However, no credit will be given for:
1.
Any month in a calendar quarter that SSA has already credited as a U.S. QC.
2.
Any period of coverage not based on contributions.
3.
More than four QCs for any calendar year.
