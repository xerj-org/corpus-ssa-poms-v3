SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.210 - How OIO Prepares Claims Packages for Claims Filed In Spain
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use these instructions for preparing claims packages.
1. U.S. Claim
Prepare a claims package if a Spanish coverage record is needed and has not been requested
by the FSP. Include the following:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP.
•
Form E/USA 2 (Statement of Working Life).
2. U.S. and Spanish or Spanish Claim Only
Prepare a claims package if:
•
A Spanish coverage record is needed and has not been requested by the FSP; OR
•
A Spanish agency has requested information or documentation.
Include the following in the claims package:
•
Liaison form SSA-2960-U3-SP
•
Information or documentation requested by the Spanish agency.
