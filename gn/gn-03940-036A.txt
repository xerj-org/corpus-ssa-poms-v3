A. Introduction
We defer development of certain non-medical factors such as WC and PDB offset until
after we issue a favorable decision. Further, we frequently partially adjudicate claims
based on unverified WC/PDB amounts, military service credits, date of birth evidence,
or other deferred or delayed development that affects the past-due benefits amount.
Therefore, special handling is required for claims involving a fee agreement and partial
adjudication based on deferred development.
NOTE: The proof of age tolerance procedure in GN 00302.030 does not involve deferred date
of birth development or partial adjudication because the tolerance procedure, when
it applies, establishes a claimant’s date of birth.
