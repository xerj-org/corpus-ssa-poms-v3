GN 03313.060 Disclosure to the Department of the Treasury (DT)
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
(SOR) to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records
and their applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. If you receive a request from DT, contact your regional Privacy Act Coordinator.
