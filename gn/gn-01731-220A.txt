A. Additional Finnish Application Forms
In addition to the Form SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International
Social Security Agreement), an applicant for Finnish benefits must complete one or
more special Finnish application forms. The forms that are completed vary depending on the type
of benefit claimed.
•
Applicants submit Form SF/USA 3A to claim National Pension retirement benefits.
•
Applicants submit Form SF/USA 3B to claim National Pension survivor benefits.
•
Applicants submit Form SF/USA 3C to claim earnings-related benefits.
