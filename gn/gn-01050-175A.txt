SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.175 - Preparation of Folder for Transmittal of Disabled Widow or Widower (DWB) Claims
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | TN 1 (06-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050175 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DWB claims
Enter the NH's social security number on the face of the folder tab, as described
in GN 01050.165A.1. DO NOT enter the DWB claimant's SSN. Print the surname of the widow or widower
and the first initial on the reverse of the folder tab with the surname first.
