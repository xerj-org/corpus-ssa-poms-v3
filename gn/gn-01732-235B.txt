B. Description of the SF/USA 2
•
This form is printed on both sides of one page and is self-explanatory in most respects.
•
There are no “verification blocks” on the form because all dates that are entered on the form have been verified by
Finland.
•
There is no blanket statement on the SF/USA2 which provides Privacy Act Authorization
to release information to Finland. When requesting data/ information from SSA, Finland
will always send along a separate signed authorization to disclose.
