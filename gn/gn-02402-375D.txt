D. Overview of IDD for Pakistan
IDD payments sent to Pakistan must be in Pakistani Rupee (PKR) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves that territory.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
