B. Claims filed in the United States or directly with INTPSC
If a claim is filed in a U.S. DO or directly with INTPSC, the totalization application
form SSA-3954-BK-IT and national applications are forwarded to INTPSC. The following
chart indicates how these claims are processed:
1. District office
The district office forwards the totalization application form SSA-3954-BK-IT and
the U.S. national application to INTPSC, Baltimore.
2. INTPSC, Baltimore
INTPSC forwards a claims package to the INPS Regional Office following the instructions
in GN 01704.ff. On the liaison form USA/IT 2, INTPSC indicates whether or not an Italian
coverage record is needed and, if so, specifies how much coverage is needed for insured
status.
3. INPS Regional Office
If requested, the INPS Regional Office sends the Italian coverage certification on
form IT/USA 3 bis with the liaison form IT/USA 2 to INTPSC, Baltimore.
4. INTPSC, Baltimore
If an Italian coverage certification was requested from INPS, INTPSC sends form USA/IT2
to advise the INPS Regional Office whether or not U.S. benefits were awarded.
