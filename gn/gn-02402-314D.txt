D. Overview of IDD for Bahama Islands
IDD payments sent to Bahama Islands must be in Eastern Bahamian dollar (BSD) accounts
at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bahama
Islands. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
