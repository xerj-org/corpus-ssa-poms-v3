A. Finding space for the hearing
The agency sets the time and place for the hearing and may ask the FO manager on an
HA-506 (Request to Arrange Place for Hearing) to arrange for space for the hearing.
The space should be private and quiet. The following list indicates the approved types
of space, in the order of preference:
•
Post Office;
•
Other Federal building;
•
Public or semipublic building, such as county or municipal office;
•
Rental space;
•
State employment security office; or
•
District or branch office.
When the agency makes the arrangements for hearing space, the agency sends the servicing
FO a list of the hearings scheduled.
