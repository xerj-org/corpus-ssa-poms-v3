SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01705.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for U.S and Italian Benefits
Effective Dates: 04/19/1997 - Present | TN 10 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201705210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence requirements
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with Italy,
the person must submit the same evidence required to file a claim for regular retirement,
survivors, or disability insurance (non-Totalization) benefits. The applicant must
also submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 through GN 01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States.
