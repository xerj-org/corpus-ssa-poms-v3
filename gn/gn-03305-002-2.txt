2. Information about other individuals contained in the requested record
Personal information about other individuals for whom we do not have a written consent
document authorizing the release of their information may be contained in the number
holder’s (NH) record. The NH may not provide consent authorizing the release of their
information from their record. Therefore, we must redact any such information about
other individuals before releasing the requested information about the NH.
