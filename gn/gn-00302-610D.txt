D. Evaluation
If possible, the DO will contact the custodian of the newspaper records to authenticate
the clipping presented by the applicant. If these records are no longer available,
careful analysis and evaluation of the claimant's newspaper clipping is necessary
to ensure that it is genuine, unaltered, and made at the time purported.
