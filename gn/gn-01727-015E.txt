E. Portuguese voluntary coverage inquiries
General inquiries:
If there is a general inquiry about voluntary contributions, direct the person to
the Departamento de Relacoes Internacionais e Convencoes de Seguranca Social at the
address in GN 01727.020E.
If the U.S. Coverage Record is needed:
If a worker needs a U.S. coverage record to qualify for voluntary contributions, instruct
the worker to get a coverage record by completing Form SSA-7004 (Request for Social
Security Statement). (See RM 01301.014 and RM 01301.015).
