SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02409.010 - Effect of Waiver of Benefits on Other Eligibility
Effective Dates: 10/17/2011 - Present | TN 4 (10-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202409010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When a beneficiary asks to waive payment
A beneficiary may ask to waive payment in order to receive or have increased benefits
from another Federal or State agency. Do not approve a waiver, unless the other agency
recognizes the validity of the waiver, and provides the expected benefit for the period
of waiver. If there will be no benefit from the other agency, but the beneficiary
insists on waiving the payment, document that this is an informed decision and accept
the waiver request.
