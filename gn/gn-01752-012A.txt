SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01752.012 - How the Foreign Service Post in Warsaw, Poland Processes and Develops Claims Filed With the ZUS or KRUS Agency
Effective Dates: 07/16/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201752012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process – person is not eligible for Polish benefits
When a person is not eligible for Polish benefits and contacts the ZUS or KRUS agency
to file a claim for U.S. benefits, ZUS or KRUS sends the following information to
the FSP in Warsaw, Poland:
•
A liaison form PL/USA3 certifying the filing date for the claim
•
Polish certified coverage record (PL/USA 6).
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits
