SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01747.120 - U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Danish Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/24/2008 - Present | TN 3 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201747120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Adjudication responsibility
1. Field office (FO) responsibilities
FOs are responsible for processing an initial insured status denial (see GN 01702.315) when the worker has at least six QCs but is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone.
This denial will produce a notice advising the applicant that insured status is not
met based on U.S. coverage alone and that the foreign coverage record is being requested.
2. Division of International Operations (DIO) responsibilities
DIO will request the Danish coverage record. When the record is received, DIO will
convert the Danish coverage to QCs and finally adjudicate the claim.
