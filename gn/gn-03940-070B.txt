B. Procedure - Title XVI Only - Favorable Decision - No Spouse Involvement
1. Fee Agreement Disapproved - Representative Eligible for Direct Payment - Past-Due
Benefits Withheld for Possible Direct Payment
a. CR Disapproves Fee Agreement
When the CR disapproves the fee agreement at the initial or reconsideration level
and the representative is eligible for direct fee payment, the CR prepares the disapproval
notice.
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC08 and UTI FEE060; caption ATYC02, UTI ATY817, caption
ATYC09, and UTI FEE093; caption ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035, FEE107, FEE108, FEE085 and
FEE087 to notify the claimant and representative that:
•
the fee agreement is disapproved and why;
•
SSA is withholding the individual’s past-due benefits; and
•
the representative should file a fee petition within 60 days if he/she wishes SSA
to pay the fee directly to him/her.
b. ALJ or AAJ Disapproves Fee Agreement
When an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) or an Administrative Appeals Judge (AAJ) disapproves
a fee agreement at the hearing or Appeals Council review level, the CR does not prepare
a disapproval fee notice because the CR is not the decision maker. In those claims,
the ALJ or AAJ notifies the claimant and representative of the disapproved fee agreement.
The CR does prepare a notice about withholding past-due benefits.
Use UTI FEE058; caption ATYC10 and UTI ATY045; caption ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035, FEE107,
FEE108, FEE085 and FEE087, to notify the claimant and the representative that:
•
SSA is withholding past-due benefits, and
•
the representative should file a fee petition within 60 days if he/she wishes SSA
to pay the fee directly to him/her.
2. Fee Agreement Approved - More than One Representative Receiving Direct Payment
When SSA is paying a fee to more than one representative, process the fee payments
and the notice manually. Follow the procedures in GN 03940.055C.2. and GN 03940.055C.3., as the situation warrants, to prepare the notice, except replace the fee assessment
UTI FEE047 with UTI FEE081.
