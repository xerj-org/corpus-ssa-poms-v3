C. No evidence from any source
If there is no evidence of Holocaust survivor status available from any source, telephone
the Regional Office, Insurance Programs Branch for assistance and possible referral
to the Office of retirement and Survivors Insurance, Division of Entitlement, Eligibility
and Relationship Branch.
