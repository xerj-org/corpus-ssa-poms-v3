SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01736.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Ireland
Effective Dates: 01/12/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201736205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Initial Claims Packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Irish coverage records and to inform the
Irish agency about claims for Irish benefits. (See GN 01735.205 - GN 01735.220 for the applications and the evidence required to prepare the packages.)
