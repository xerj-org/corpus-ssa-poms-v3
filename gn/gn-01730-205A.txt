SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.205 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed Outside Austria
Effective Dates: 12/10/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Initial Claims packages are prepared to request Austrian coverage records and to inform
the Austrian agency about claims for Austrian benefits. (See GN 01729.205 - 01729.215, for the applications and evidence required for the packages.)
