SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.045 - Recalculation of the Amount of Past-Due Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/05/2005 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Policy - Determination Already Revised
SSA may have increased or decreased the amount of past-due benefits or past-due benefits
payable after notifying the claimant and the representative of the amount of the fee
under the agreement, e.g., in amending an award or reducing benefits. As GN 03920.040 explains, such increases or decreases do not affect the amount of the representative's
fee unless a proper party has timely requested administrative review. In that event,
SSA uses the past-due benefits amount at the time of administrative review to derive
the amount of the fee which results from the agreement. (GN 03920.040 also explains the effect of interim increases or decreases on withholding.)
EXCEPTION: In cases involving an approved fee agreement and offset based on unverified workers'
compensation (WC) or public disability benefit (PDB) payments, the PC will normally
delay calculating the past-due benefits amount and the amount of the representative's
fee until after they have verified the WC or PDB amounts (See GN 03920.040 and GN 03940.036.) However, if the PC calculates the past-due benefits amount and the amount of the
representative's fee under the fee agreement process using unverified WC or PDB amounts,
it will recalculate the amount of the past-due benefits and recalculate the amount
of the representative's fee based on the revised amount of past-due benefits.
