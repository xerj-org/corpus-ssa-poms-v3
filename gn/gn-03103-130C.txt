C. FO completes development upon request by an ALJ
Promptly complete the development request made by an ALJ.
Follow these procedures:
1.
Obtain and send the requested information to the ALJ.
2.
If you are unable to obtain the evidence or information within 45 days, advise the
ALJ of the status, and ask whether they want to continue with development.
3.
If you are unable to obtain the information, return the request to the ALJ and use
the Form SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) to provide an explanation of the efforts to
try to obtain the information.
