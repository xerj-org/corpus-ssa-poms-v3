A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
1. Office to Contact
Registry Office at the Municipal Office (Opstina) for the place in which the event occurred.
2. Fee
500 dinars if the certificate is issued on an international form (8 languages, including
English).
300 dinars if the certificate is issued on a Serbian-language form.
