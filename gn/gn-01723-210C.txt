C. Policy - Using U.S. evidence
Evidence which is used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits may also be used by France to establish the same factor for French benefits.
Consequently, it is unnecessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence to
document a factor of entitlement for French benefits when that factor has already
been verified for SSA purposes.
