SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01737.225 - Requests for Status of Application for Greek Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/02/2022 - Present | TN 4 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Greek claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Greek benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Greek agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Greece
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Greece, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Greece
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Greek agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Greek agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Idryma Koinonikon Asfaliseon
(IKS) ( Social Insurance Institute)
•
Organismos Asfalisis Eleftheron
Epangelmation (Social Security Organization for the Self-employed)
•
Hellenic Republic Ministry of Labor
and Social Affairs
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Greek claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Greece. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims,
see GN 01702.520.
