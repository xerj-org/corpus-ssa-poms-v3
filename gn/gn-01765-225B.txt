SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01765.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Romanian Benefits
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201765225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Romanian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of their
claim for Romanian benefits and states that they have not received notification that
IBP forwarded a claim to the Romanian agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE:
Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User Guide.
1. PPC does not show IBP forwarded the claim to Romania
If PPC does not show that International Benefit & Processing (IBP) forwarded the claim
to Romania, advise the applicant to write directly to IBP at the following address:
Social Security Administration
International Benefit & Processing
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235 - 7769
USA
2. PPC shows IBP forwarded the claim to Romania
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Romanian agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Romanian agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
about Romanian benefits online at:
National House of Public
Pensions
IBP is unable to determine the status of a Romanian benefits claim after a technician
forwards the claim to Romania. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, GN 01702.520 .
