C. PROCEDURE - ARC-MOS
The ARC-MOS or other designated component will notify the FOs, PCs and affected regional
component(s) of the commencement of significant civil actions instituted by claimants
in their service areas. If an adverse order is issued against SSA that is not appealed,
other than cases that are the jurisdiction of PCs, the designated regional component
will act as liaison between OALC, FOs, PCs and regional component(s) to insure prompt
compliance with implementation requirements of the court order.
