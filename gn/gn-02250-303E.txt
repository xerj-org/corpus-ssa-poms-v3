E. Policy-Reopening prior waiver determination
Before denying a subsequent waiver request based on res judicata, we must consider
whether we may reopen the prior waiver determination (refer to GN 03101.160A). In general, a final waiver determination that is later found to have been incorrect
when made may be reopened and revised under the rules of administrative finality,
if the relevant conditions are satisfied. For more information regarding administrative
finality, refer to GN 04001.010 through GN 04001.130 and SI 0407.010.
If reopening is not possible but the individual was previously found not at fault
and meets the defeat the purpose provision based on a change in financial circumstances,
we will make a new waiver determination.
