B. Policy
SSA helps claimants who are unable to pursue their claims and obtain needed evidentiary
documents through their own efforts. We do this by preparing letters for claimants
to custodians of records. The FO uses Form SSA-L106-F3 (Letter to Custodian of School
Records) to assist the claimant in securing a school record in these situations.
