Effective Dates: 01/14/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302575 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Evidence of the individual's age at a given time or of his/her exact DB may be available
on an insurance policy in the claimant's possession. In most cases, the policy itself
will show only the claimant's age in relations to the date the policy was issued.
