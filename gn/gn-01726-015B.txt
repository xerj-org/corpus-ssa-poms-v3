SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with a Dutch Agency
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
a Dutch agency.
1. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part I) and the appropriate RSDI applications and evidence
and forward the claim to DIO with the form NL/VS 3A and the coverage record, NL/VS
205.
2. Claimant already entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all material to DIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
