SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01712.205 - Preparing Claims Packages for Claims Filed Outside Norway
Effective Dates: 12/07/1993 - Present | TN 1 (12-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201712205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
To prepare claims packages to request Norwegian coverage and medical evidence and
to inform FFU about claims for Norwegian benefits, follow these guidelines:
1. U.S. Claim Only
Complete form SSA-2960-U2-NE. Check item V.B to request a Norwegian coverage record.
Check item V.C. to request medical evidence from FFU files if claimant has filed for
U.S. disability benefits.
2. U.S. and Norwegian Claim
Include the following information in claims packages:
•
SSA-2960-U2-NE. Check item V.B. to request a Norwegian coverage record. Check item
V.C. to request medical evidence from FFU files if claimant has filed for U.S. disability
benefits.
•
U.S. earnings record
•
Any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit.
(See GN 01712.005B.7)
Be sure Norwegian applications were sent to U.S. residents.
3. Norwegian Claim Only
Include the following information in a claims package:
•
SSA-2960-U2-NE
•
U.S. earnings record
•
Any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit
Be sure Norwegian applications were sent to U.S. residents.
(See GN 01712.005.B.7)
