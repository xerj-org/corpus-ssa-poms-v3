C. Appeals for representative payee actions
•
determinations that a representative payee is required for a legally incompetent beneficiary
(For information regarding representative payee and appeal rights, see GN 00503.110);
•
determinations that a minor needs a representative payee when the court finds the
minor is still under parental control;
•
determinations not to appoint a payee applicant cannot be appealed by the applicant;
•
a determination regarding whether a representative payee misused benefits paid on
behalf of a claimant (This includes a determination of “misuse” as well as “no misuse.”);
•
determinations to pay a large conserved or accrued benefit to a representative payee
in installments;
•
suspension of benefits pending the appointment of a representative payee.
