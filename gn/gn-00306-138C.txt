C. Procedure — determining grandchild/great-grandchild relationship
1. Grandchild
Apply the basic definition of a grandchild and the development in GN 00306.240 - GN 00306.245, except that a step-relationship cannot be used to establish either of the required
relationships.
2. Great-Grandchild
Follow GN 00306.240 - GN 00306.245 except that you must establish the following three relationships for the great-grandchild:
•
The great-grandchild applicant to his/her parent;
•
The great-grandchild applicant's parent to his/her parent (the applicant's grandparent);
and
•
The great-grandchild applicant's grandparent as a child of the NH or his/her spouse
(the applicant's great-grandparent).
NOTE: As in GN 00306.138C.1., you may not use a step-relationship to establish any of the required relationships.
