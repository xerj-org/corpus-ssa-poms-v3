H. Administrative sanctions in reconsiderations and appeals cases
1.
QUESTION: Who is responsible for the reconsideration decision on sanctions?
ANSWER: An employee in the FO who did not make the original sanction determination will
make the reconsideration decision.
2.
QUESTION: If a claimant requests a reconsideration of the sanction after 60 days, alleging
good cause for late filing, do we stop the sanction?
ANSWER: If we find that good cause applies, we will stop the sanction and pay any benefits
withheld until we issue a determination on the reconsideration. However, we do not delay imposing administrative sanctions if a hearing is pending.
