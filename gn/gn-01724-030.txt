GN 01724.030 How the French Agency Develops Applications
Process
There are two ways in which the French local office may handle claims for U.S. benefits.
•
If the request for U.S. benefits is not in writing, the office refers the claimant
to the FSP in Paris.
•
If the request for U.S. benefits is in writing, the office prepares a liaison form
(SE 404-3), certifies the filing date, and sends the form, written statement, and
a copy of the French coverage record to the FSP in Paris.
If the French agency needs a U.S. coverage record in connection with a claim for French
benefits they request it:
•
directly from DIO if they are not referring a claim to the FSP.
•
through the FSP if they are sending a claim for U.S. benefits to the FSP.
