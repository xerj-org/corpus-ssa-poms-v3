SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.240 - How OIO Processes Spanish Agency Requests for Information Submitted on Form E/USA 3
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Before releasing information from SSA records, OIO must make certain that:
•
the information is needed to process a claim under the agreement, or
•
the claimant has submitted a signed authorization which permits the release of information
to the Spanish agency.
