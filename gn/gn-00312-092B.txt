B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
For birth, marriage, and death records, contact the municipality or the office of
the mayor, depending on if there is a public notary in the municipality where the
event occurred. For divorce records, contact the Family Court in which the event took
place.
2. Fee
•
Birth, death, marriage records - $5,000.00 Col.
•
Divorce records - $300.00 Col. per page.
