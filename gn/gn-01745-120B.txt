B. Policy - Crediting Japanese Contributory Coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. QC for every 3 months or remaining fraction of Japanese coverage
in a calendar year. (Japanese coverage is measured in months.) However, no credit
will be given for:
•
any month in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC, or
•
more than 4 QCs for any calendar year.
