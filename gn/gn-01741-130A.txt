SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01741.130 - Refunds of Korean Contributions
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 7 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201741130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Lump-sum benefit
U.S. nationals (by International Agreement), Korean citizens, and former Korean citizens
can obtain a refund of contributions to the Korean Social Security system, referred
to as a lump-sum benefit, if they have less than 10 years of Korean coverage and have:
•
Attained age 60 (except for the Special Old-age Pension beneficiaries); or
•
Lost Korean nationality; or
•
Permanently emigrated from Korea; or
•
Died (in which case survivors could file).
