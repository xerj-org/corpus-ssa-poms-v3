GN 01739.105 Effective Date of the Agreement
Earliest month of entitlement
The U.S. and Chile can use periods of U.S. and Chilean Social Security coverage earned
in the U.S. or Chile before December 1, 2001, to establish entitlement to U.S. or
Chilean Totalization benefits. However, the earliest possible date of entitlement
to benefits under the agreement is December 1, 2001.
