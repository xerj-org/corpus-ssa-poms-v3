A. Background
The Brazilian liaison agency the National Social Insurance Institute (INSS) uses two
registration numbers for an individuals filing for Brazilian benefits. Both the Brazilian
Workers Identification Number (NIT) and the Brazilian Individual Taxpayer Registration
(CPF) number need to be obtained when a beneficiary is filing for a U.S. Brazil Totalization
benefit.
