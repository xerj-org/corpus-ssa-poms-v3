GN 01775.105 Romanian Certificate of Insurance Periods
Forwarding certificate of insurance periods
Romania will send the certificate of insurance with the RO-USA 3. The RO-USA 3 is
the only acceptable form for the certification of the Romanian certificate of insurance
periods. Forward certificate of insurance periods received in any other format to
the International Support Unit (ISU) for approval.
