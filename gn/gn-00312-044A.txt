A. Policy for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
Belgium has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration records at the State
Archives. Individuals can visit over 20 repositories and review records online in
a reading room. For more information, visit https://search.arch.be/en/. Records are also held at the local level in municipalities.
NOTE:
We do not accept copies printed from the State Archives unless the custodian subsequently
affixes a signature, stamp, or seal with a statement attesting to the accuracy.
