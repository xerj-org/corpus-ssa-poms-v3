SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02406.505 - Nonreceipt of a check Outside the United States
Effective Dates: 08/02/2024 - Present | TN 126 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202406505 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Nonreceipt reported by or on behalf of a beneficiary residing outside the United States
When handling a nonreceipt report for beneficiaries residing abroad or third parties
acting on their behalf, be mindful of :
•
the way we issue a payment to the beneficiary; and
•
the original date when beneficiary receives their payment in their country or area.
The U.S. Department of State delivers most checks to beneficiaries in foreign countries
by Army Post Office (APO) and Fleet Post Office (FPO) facilities. The checks for APO
and FPO are mailed directly to the APO and FPO facilities. They are not sent through
the consulates.
However, SSA sends checks directly to beneficiaries residing in Canada and Mexico.
