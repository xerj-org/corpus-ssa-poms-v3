C. Procedure – England and Wales
Website: http://www.statistics.gov.uk/registration/n/
1. Birth, Marriage, Adoption and Death Records
Separate registers are maintained of the births of adopted children. Thus, if the
individual whose birth certificate is being requested is adopted, this must be shown
in the request.
a. Office to Contact
General Register Office
P.O. Box 2
Southport
Merseyside PR8 2JD
England
Telephone number: 011-44-151-471-4800
Certificates can also be requested by telephone, fax or e-mail with payment by credit
or debit card. Only VISA, MASTER CARD, SWITCH, SOLO AND DELTA are accepted.
Telephone requests can be made by calling: 011-44-870-243-7788. Fax requests can be
made to: 011-44-1704-550013.
b. Fee
•
Birth, Marriage and Death Certificate: Long or Short Form, 11.50 pounds (3 week processing
time); 27.50 pounds for urgent requests (processed in 2 days).
•
Adoption Records: Long Form - 11.50 Pounds
Payment must be by international money order payable to “ONS.”
2. Divorce Records
The Divorce Registry recommends that all requests be sent in writing. Include the
following identifying information with the request:
•
Names of both parties;
•
Date of decree;
•
Place of divorce; and
•
Case number.
a. Office to Contact
Divorce Registry
First Avenue House
42-49 High Holborn
London WC1V 6NP
England
Telephone: 011-44-20-7947-6000
b. Fee
1 Pound - If all the identifying data is not known and a search is necessary, there
is an additional 20.00 pound fee for each 10-year period searched.
Payment must be by international money order in pounds sterling payable to “H.M. Paymaster
General.”
