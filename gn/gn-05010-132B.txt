SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 05010.132 - Tax Refunds by SSA
Effective Dates: 11/09/1990 - Present | TN 6 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0205010132 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If a beneficiary due a tax refund has died and the file shows there is a surviving
spouse, advise the spouse of the amount due and that the estate must file with IRS
at the end of year to get the refund.
If there is no surviving spouse, send the notice to the deceased's estate at the deceased's
last residence address.
