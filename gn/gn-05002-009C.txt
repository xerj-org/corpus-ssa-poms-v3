C. Policy for missing or incorrect Box 2 SSN
1. Beneficiary has SSN
If the beneficiary has an SSN, obtain it and correct the MBR via the MONET Beneficiary's
Reference Data (BREF) screen for input. Instructions for updating the Beneficiary
Own Account Number (BOAN) are in MSOM (See MSOM T2PE 007.038)
2. Beneficiary has no SSN
If the beneficiary has no SSN, obtain an SS-5 using the instructions in RM 10205.000. (Regardless of age, anyone entitled to benefits after June 1989, must have an SSN.
See RM 10205.090.)
