C. Extending the time limit due to Military service
SSA excludes the amount of time that a claimant or their heirs spent in active military
service when deciding whether the claimant filed an appeal timely.

RS 00210.025 Soldiers and Sailors Civil Relief Act (SSCRA) and the Lump-Sum death payment (LSDP)
