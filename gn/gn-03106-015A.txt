A. PROCEDURE - OALC or OPL
The OALC or OPL will advise the ARC-MOS or other designated component or OCO, the
Special Appeals and Examining Section (SAES) of any significant civil actions that
arise. A significant civil action is a:
•
Threatened or certified class action;
•
Constitutional challenge;
•
Policy, regulatory or statutory challenge; or
•
Case that has the potential for significant adverse effects on SSA.
