C. Description of the form CZ/USA3
1. Part 1
Part 1 contains information about the claim and the claimant.
2. Part 2
Part 2 contains certification of data. If verified, the appropriate block is checked.
3. Part 3
Part 3 contains information provided by the Czech agency, including medical evidence
in disability claims.
4. Part 4
Part 4 contains a list of the type of information that the Czech agency might request
from OIO and/or FSP in Warsaw Poland along with the remark that the information is
needed to process a claim under the agreement or that a consent statement is attached.
5. Part 5
Part 5 contains any necessary remarks.
