A. Procedure
1. Address and FO Code
Registrar of Vital Statistics
Pago Pago, American Samoa 96799
FO Code 025
Phone: (684) 633-1405 or (684) 633–1406
NOTE: You can also find information at the National Center for Health Statistics .
2. Cost of Copy:
•
Verification of all records from the Office of Vital Statistics cost $5.00 each.
•
Certified copy of birth or death certificate cost $7.00 each.
3. Type of Payment:
Cash, check or money order.
4. Remarks:
Birth records from 1930 on are on computer. Prior to 1930 some records may not be
recorded due to the manner in which birth information was recorded. SSA offices should
submit requests for birth or death verification directly to the Office of Vital Statistics.
