2. Cost of Copy:
The cost for a birth record is $20.00. Death - $20.00, Marriage /Divorce - varies.
3. Type of Payment:
Money order, traveler’s check, cashier’s check, credit/debit card
4. Remarks:
The claimant needs a current State-issued picture identification with signature (or
a photocopy of same) to acquire a birth or death record. (There is no change in certification
requirements for SSA.) If the picture ID has no signature, then a notarized signature
must be presented also. State individual's relationship to the requestor (e.g., self,
spouse, parent) as well as the need for the birth record.
Can FO verify, certify, or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No.
