A. Introduction
Section 233 of the Social Security Act authorizes the President to enter into bilateral
agreements with foreign countries to provide for limited coordination of the U.S.
Social Security program with certain social insurance programs of foreign countries.
These international social security agreements are often referred to as Totalization
agreements.
