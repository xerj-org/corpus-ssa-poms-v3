C. When RFC rejects a nonreceipt claim
The RFC does not forward a nonreceipt claim to CCB if:
•
there is no issued check that matches the nonreceipt claim;
•
someone returned the check; or
•
for any reason, the RFC cannot process the claim.
The RFC transmits rejected items to the originating Field Office (FO), Teleservice
Center (TSC), or Processing Center (PC) via our Central Office (CO) for reconciliation.
The RFC annotates responses (both interim and final dispositions) to the PHUS record
for Title II and to the Query Control Record (Q query) for Title XVI. The FO, TSC,
or PC may access PHUS and the Q query to obtain the RFC responses whenever a claimant
makes a follow-up inquiry.
