C. Claims filed with INPS
The following chart indicates how claims for U.S. benefits are processed when they
are filed with INPS:
1. INPS Regional Office
The regional office takes the totalization application form IT/USA 1 and routes the
application form under cover of the liaison form IT/USA 2 to the appropriate U.S.
Foreign Service post. The Italian coverage certification should not be forwarded with
this material.
2. U.S. Foreign Service Post
If this is a new claim for U.S. benefits, the Foreign Service post obtains the U.S.
national applications and develops the required evidence. No development is needed
if the claimant already is entitled to U.S. benefits. After all development, the Foreign
Service post routes the IT/USA 1 and national applications to INTPSC in Baltimore.
3. INTPSC, Baltimore
INTPSC forwards the U.S. coverage certification with a liaison form USA/IT2 directly
to the INPS Regional Office. On the USA/IT 2, INTPSC indicates whether or not an Italian
coverage record is needed and, if so, specifies how much coverage is needed for insured
status.
4. INPS Regional Office
The regional office sends the Italian coverage certification on form IT/USA 3 bis
with the liaison form IT/USA 2 to INTPSC, Baltimore.
5. NTPSC, Baltimore
If an Italian coverage certification was requested from INPS, INTPSC sends form USA/IT
2 to advise the INPS Regional Office whether or not U.S. benefits were awarded.
