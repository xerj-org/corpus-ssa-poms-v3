SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.370 - Developing Evidence of Age of Eligible Worker When Uninsured Spouse Applies for HI
Effective Dates: 01/27/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302370 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Procedure: Worker and uninsured spouse do not live in same household
If the worker and uninsured spouse do not live in the same household, use the following procedure:
Step
Action
1
Is preferred evidence of the worker's age in the claimant's possession or obtainable
within 10 days?
•
If yes, obtain preferred evidence and stop.
•
If no, go to step 2.
2
•
Document the file with an explanation (on an RC or SSA-795) of the unavailability
of preferred evidence, and
•
Request automatically convincing evidence (GN 00302.110ff.).
3
Is automatically convincing evidence in the claimant's possession or obtainable within
10 days?
•
If yes, obtain automatically convincing evidence and stop.
•
If no, go to step 4.
4
•
Document the file with an explanation (on an RC or SSA-795) of the unavailability
of automatically convincing evidence, and
•
Obtain at least 3 documents (one of which may be an SSA record) recorded at least
5 years prior to the current filing date that show the worker's DB or age.
5
Do all documents obtained in step 4 show the worker to be at least age 62?
•
If yes, go to step 6.
•
If no, develop evidence of age under normal proof-of-age procedures (GN 00302.010-GN 00302.240) and stop.
6
Does the evidence presented satisfy all requirements of step 4 (i.e., at least 3 documents
recorded at least 5 years prior to filing)?
•
If yes, go to step 7.
•
If no, go to step 8.
7
•
Use the DB shown on the oldest record available as the worker's DB and stop.
8
•
Develop more extensively to get additional evidence, regardless of delay.
9
Does the evidence obtained after more extensive development satisfy all the requirements
of step 4.
•
If yes, go to step 7.
•
If no, go to step 10.
10
•
Use the DB shown on the oldest record available and stop, provided:
—
All efforts to obtain additional evidence have been exhausted,
—
At least one record (in addition to an SSA record) is presented, and
—
Each record shows the worker to be age 62.
NOTE: If the minimal evidence required in this step is not obtainable, disallow the claim
due to failure to establish the worker's eligibility for benefits. However, see the
procedure in subsection I. below.
