SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03930.040 - Title II - Providing Proper Notice in Claims Involving Representatives Who May Petition for Fees
Effective Dates: 12/09/2024 - Present | TN 35 (12-24)

C. Process - Modernized Claims System (MCS)
Properly coding MCS claims will produce correct payment, a correct notice to the claimant,
and a copy of the notice for the representative.
1. Check/Notice 2 Data
MCS Check/Notice 2 (NOT2) screen provides the data that notifies the system whether
to withhold from past-due benefits. The system also uses the data to generate correct
notices and payments.
2. Windfall Offset
If the representative (and the entity, if there is a valid assignment per GN 03920.021) is eligible for direct payment, and the representative has not waived a fee or direct
payment, entering both the representative fee NOT2 data and the windfall (Title II
or Title XVI) offset data by coding a “6” in the Miscellaneous Factors field on the
BECF screen will produce:
•
paragraphs ATY002, ATY003, ATY004, ATY005, ATY006, ATY007, ATY 011 (Attorney/Representative Fee Has Not Yet Been Authorized); and
•
paragraph WFO002 (SSI Offset-Representative Involved).
Because we calculate the amount to withhold (25 percent of past-due benefits for fee
petitions) before imposing offset, we can notify the claimant and the representative
of the amount withheld through the award notice.
Refer to MCS notice paragraphs about representation, GN 03930.032 and NL 00725.005.
