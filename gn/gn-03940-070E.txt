E. Procedure - Concurrent Titles II and XVI Claims - Favorable Decision - Fee Agreement
Approved - Spouse Involvement
1. Fee Authorized - Title II Fee Amount Known - Representative Not Eligible for Direct
Payment
When the SSR interface posts the title II fee information and the claimant’s and the
spouse’s title XVI past-due benefits are known, authorize the title XVI fee and send
a manual notice.
Use UTI FEE038, caption ATYC10 and UTIs ATY029 and FEE092.
•
If the title II portion of the fee amount reduces the title XVI fee amount, use UTI
ATY027.
Use UTI FEE022.
•
If SSA is sending the claimant's first check to a State/subdivision based on an IAR
agreement, use caption CHKC07 and UTI CHK002.
Use UTI FEE077.
2. Fee Authorized - Title II Fee Amount Known - Representative Eligible for Direct Payment
When the SSR interface posts the title II information, authorize the title XVI fee
and send a manual notice.
Use UTI FEE038; caption ATYC10 and UTIs ATY029 and FEE092.
•
If the title II portion of the fee amount reduces the title XVI fee amount, use UTI
ATY027.
•
If a representative is eligible for, but waives direct payment, use UTI FEE033.
Use UTI FEE077.
•
If SSA has withheld past-due benefits for direct payment, use caption ATYC03 and UTIs
FEE035 and FEE078.
•
If SSA is making direct fee payment and there is no IAR involved, use UTI FEE080.
•
If SSA is processing the fee payment after reimbursing the State agency for IAR, use
UTIs FEE041 and FEE043 (partial fee paid to representative), FEE044 (fee paid to representative,
nothing left to pay claimant) or FEE045 (fee paid, remainder to claimant).
Use the appropriate assessment paragraph.
–
If the assessment deducted from the title II direct payment reduced the assessment
on the title XVI payment, use UTI FEE048.
–
If the maximum assessment was deducted from the title II direct payment, use UTI FEE049.
(See GN 03920.019K. for further information about the assessment language in the notice.)
Also send a manual notice to the spouse. See GN 03940.070F.
3. Title II Fee Amount Unknown - Representative Eligible for Direct Payment
When the SSA decision maker approves the fee agreement, the title II fee is not known,
and SSA is withholding title XVI past-due benefits pending fee authorization, send
a manual notice.
Use UTI FEE038 and caption XVI719.
•
If the title II CR approved the fee agreement, use UTI FEE062.
•
If an ALJ or AAJ approved the fee agreement, use UTI ATY024.
Use caption ATYC03 and UTIs FEE035, FEE107, FEE108, FEE067 and FEE039.
