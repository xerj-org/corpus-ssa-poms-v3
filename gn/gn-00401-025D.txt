SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00401.025 - Office Recovery Procedures by Role
Effective Dates: 06/14/2010 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200401025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Regional disaster recovery team
•
Work with the AD to develop an action plan for recovery and identify any unique situations.
•
Serve as a source of information and support to the AD and affected office(s).
•
Make all necessary contacts within the RO and with other agencies (e.g., Federal Emergency
Management Agency (FEMA), General Services Administration (GSA)).
•
Establish contact with the Office of Public Service and Operation Support (OPSOS)
in Central Office (CO) at ||DCO OPSOS.
•
Determine what assistance other SSA offices can and should provide in accordance with
any Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP).
