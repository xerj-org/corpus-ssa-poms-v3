B. Purpose of the U.S.-Iceland Totalization Agreement
This subchapter provides:
•
a brief overview of the Icelandic social security system;
•
eligibility requirements for Icelandic benefits; and
•
an explanation of the provisions of the agreement.
For more information on the Icelandic social security system, visit the International
Programs webpage. To find out how the agreement eliminates dual coverage, see sections
RS 02002.500 through RS 02002.555.
