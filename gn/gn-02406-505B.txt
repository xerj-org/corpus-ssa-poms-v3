B. Processing reports of nonreceipts from outside the United States
Follow these steps when processing reports of nonreceipt by a beneficiary residing
outside the United States.
Step
Action
1
Obtain the required information from the beneficiary who reports the nonreceipt:
•
claim number,
•
payee name,
•
payment date,
•
payment amount, and
•
Signature.
If the beneficiary reports a nonreceipt claim by telephone, establish the identity
of the caller. For more information on establishing the identity of the caller, see
GN 03001.012.
Once you obtain the required information, go to step 2.
If you do not have, the required information go to step 3.
2
Input the nonreceipt. For more information on inputting nonrecipt reports, see GN 02406.515.
STOP.
3
Forward the report of nonreceipt to the Office of Earnings and International Operations
(OEIO), P.O. Box 17769, Baltimore, Maryland, 21235-7769.
NOTE: Canadian border offices may make direct contact with beneficiaries in Canada.
