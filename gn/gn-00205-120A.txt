SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00205.120 - Retirement, Survivor, Disability Insurance (RSDI) Applications Tear-Off Sheet
Effective Dates: 11/24/2009 - Present | TN 45 (11-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200205120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
1. Pre-printed paper applications
All pre-printed RSDI applications (i.e., SSA-1-BK, SSA-2-BK, etc.) have a tear-off
sheet that has the Collection and Use of Information From Your Application – Privacy
and Paperwork Reduction Act notice (with the computer matching statement) and Reporting
Instructions. Provide claimants with the sheet upon receipt of their application(s).
2. Modernized Claims System (MCS) applications
Claimants filing applications via MCS must be given the appropriate instruction form
(i.e., SSA-1-INST, SSA-2-INST, etc.) containing the Privacy Act Notice/Paperwork Reduction
Act (with the computer matching statement) and Reporting Instructions.
3. Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System (MSSICS) applications
Give claimants filing through MSSICS form SSA-5000 (Collection and Use of Information
by the Social Security Administration) and Form SSA-3157 (Facts About Computer Matching).
4. Internet applications
Privacy Act Notice information is presented on the Internet application's security
page (i.e., the page that follows the Benefit Application Home Page). Reporting Instructions
are included on the printed Internet application.
