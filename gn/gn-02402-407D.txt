D. Overview of IDD for Chile
IDD payments sent to Chile must be in Chile Peso (CLP) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Chile. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for the additional information.
