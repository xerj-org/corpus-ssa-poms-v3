SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01751.215 - Application for Benefits under the Polish Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 3 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201751215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applications for Polish benefits may be filed 1 month in advance of the entitlement
date.
1. When the Modernized Claims System (MCS) Version May Be Used
The SSA-2490-BK is available through MCS, but the MCS version may be used only when
the number holder (NH) is filing for:
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits only, or
•
U.S. retirement or disability benefits (regular or Totalization) and concurrently
for Polish benefits.
2. When the MCS Version May Not Be Used
The MCS version of the SSA-2490-BK may not be used to file for:
•
Polish benefits only, or
•
U.S. or Polish survivor’s benefits.
A paper or electronic (e-form) SSA-2490 application form must be used in these cases.
Form SSA-2490-BK is also available through the Totalization Benefits Resource
Kit.
NOTE: It is not necessary to take an SSA-2490-BK in claims for U.S. auxiliary benefits in
life cases or in survivor cases where there was a previously approved award in a life
case since this application would have been completed previously in connection with
the NH’s claim.
