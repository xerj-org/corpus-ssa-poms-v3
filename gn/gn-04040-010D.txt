D. Operating Policy-Notice and Appeal
SSA does provide a notice on the second claim with the appropriate appeal paragraph but simply tells the individual that the decision reached on the first application
applies to this application; i.e., SSA applies res judicata. SSA advises the claimant
that
their
appeal is limited to whether we have the same issue, the same facts and the same person.
