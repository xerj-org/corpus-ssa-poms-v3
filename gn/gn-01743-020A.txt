A. Benefits included in the Agreement
Australia's social security program pays flat-rate retirement, survivors, and disability
benefits to qualified individuals. Persons must meet income and assets tests to qualify.
Australia generally considers the income and assets of both members of a couple who
are married or in a registered or de facto relationship.
