A. Filing for U.S. totalization benefits
An applicant filing for benefits must complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Application for Benefits
under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement). The applicant must complete
this form whether the application is for benefits from the United States only, Chile
only, or from both countries.
