C. Mailing Form SSA-1588-SM
The automated program sends an initial mailing of the form SSA-1588-SM to all eligible
young surviving spouses. The young surviving spouse has 90 days to complete and return
the form to the Wilkes-Barre Direct Operations Center (WBDOC) at:
WBDOC
PO Box 5888
Wilkes Barre PA 18767-5888
