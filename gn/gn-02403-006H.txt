H. When to use the Office of Earnings and International Operations’ (OEIO) refund address
Do not forward remittances to OEIO. If needed, use OEIO's address to answer phone inquiries
from debtors who want to mail a refund directly to OEIO. OEIO’s mailing address is
as follows:
Office of Earnings and International Operations
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, MD 21235–7769
