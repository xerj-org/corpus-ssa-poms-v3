D. Payment Legend
The "Payment Legend" should accurately reflect whether the payee is an individual
or an organization. When the payee is an individual, the legend should include the
individual's name. When the payee is an organization, the legend must include the
business name. If the individual or organization has established a Doing Business
As (DBA) name, include that in the legend.
If a sole proprietor or partner is an individual payee, and the individual insists
that the payments be issued to the business name, honor the request by recording the
business name on the eRPS Payment Legend screen (MS 07411.006). Enter all other data
as for an individual payee (use the owner's SSN and individual Rep Payee Type).
