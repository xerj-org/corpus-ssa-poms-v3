E. Instructions for updating the Development Worksheet (DW01) or Modernized Development
Worksheet (MDW)
1.
Record the date we received the Form HA-520-U5 on the DW01 or MDW.
2.
Record all subsequent actions including all contacts with the claimant or representative
on the DW01 or MDW.
3.
Post the AC decision and date on the DW01 or MDW.
