A. Procedure – Birth and Death Certificates
1. Office to Contact
Civil Registry Office (Al-Ahwal Al Madamiah) where the event occurred.
2. Fee
a.
Birth Certificate: 1.00 Jordanian dinar if the birth was reported within 1 month;
11.00 Jordanian dinars if the birth was reported later.
b.
Death Certificate:1.00 Jordanian dinar if the death was reported within 1 week; 11.00
Jordanian dinars if death was reported later.
