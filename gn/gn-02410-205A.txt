SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02410.205 - Service of a Garnishment Order
Effective Dates: 01/20/2010 - Present | TN 14 (06-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202410205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
Legal process must be “served” (i.e., delivered in a manner prescribed by law) on
the party named in the process. Proper service of a garnishment order on SSA is in
a manner provided by the applicable State law on the manager (or designated representative)
of any Social Security Field Office (FO) or on any Program Center (PC).
NOTE: To terminate garnishment withholding, a fax from the State Office of Child Support
Enforcement is acceptable. If sent from any other entity, it must be delivered in
the manner provided by the applicable State law as outlined above.
