SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.105 - Portuguese Coverage Certification
Effective Dates: 08/13/1998 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Portuguese coverage is certified on thebilingual Form P/USA 5. Coverage certifications
are issued by the office with jurisdiction, either the NCP or Nucleo Coord. de Prest.
Diferidas. Coverage certifications sent to OIO by either of the Portuguese agencies
contain a complete record of an individual's work in Portugal.
