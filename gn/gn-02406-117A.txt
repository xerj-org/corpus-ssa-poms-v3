A. Introduction
This section provides a general explanation of SSA procedures used by the FO, PC,
and TSC when a failure to receive a payment by paper check is reported. An explanation
of the Department of Treasury (DT) processing is also included. Specific instructions
on how to process non-receipts are in GN 02406.125.
