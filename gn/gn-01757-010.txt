GN 01757.010 Overview of the Brazilian Social Security System
The Brazilian social security system provides old-age, disability, and survivor benefits.
The agreement applies to two branches of the Brazilian system:
•
General Regime of Social Security (RGPS)
•
Regime of Social Security of Public Servants (RPPS)
