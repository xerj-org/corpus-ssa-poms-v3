C. Procedure – Divorce Records
Divorce may be obtained either administratively or by judgment.
•
An Administrative Decree (Bevilling) is granted when both parties agree to the divorce. Copies can be obtained from the
local Government Office (Statsamt) granting the divorce.
•
A Judicial Divorce is granted by local courts by judgment (dom) when the parties cannot mutually agree on the conditions of divorce.
1. Office to Contact
Court that granted the divorce.
2. Fee
No fee.
