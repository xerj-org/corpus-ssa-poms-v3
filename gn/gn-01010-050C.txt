SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01010.050 - Adjudicating Claims Involving Multiple Entitlement
Effective Dates: 01/05/2017 - Present | TN 62 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201010050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Documenting multiple entitlement
1. Concurrent claims
If you establish multiple entitlements based on concurrently filed claims, include
the following information in each Modernized Claim System (MCS) file involved:
•
The award for all claims involved under each SSN;
•
Annotate the reason for any suspension, termination or adjustment that concurrent
entitlement caused on both SSNs in the:
a.
Benefit Continuity Remarks/Notice (BCRN) screen,
b.
Report of Contact (RPOC) screen, or
c.
Earnings Comp Print (DAPR) Screen, from which can generate a routing form (similar
to the SSA-3601) that you will fax to the appropriate Processing Center (PC) via the
paperless process.
•
Prepare an award or amended award to document both SSNs when the CFM applies. For
simultaneous entitlement of children on more than one SSN policy, see RS 00615.770.
2. Non-concurrent claims
When the new claim affects the benefits already established on another SSN and it
requires an Automated SSA-101 (A101) or Electronic Form 101 (EF101) indicate the reason
for any resulting suspension, termination, or adjustment on the RPOC, BCRN or routing
sheet of the new award.
NOTE: Always obtain the current Full Master Beneficiary Record (FACT) for previously established
claims.
3. Document the adjudicative decision
When documenting the adjudicative decision in multiple entitlement situations, follow
the instructions in GN 01010.053.
