SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.580 - Assigning Correct Type-of-Event (TOE) Codes to Requests for Totalization Development
Effective Dates: 01/18/2024 - Present | TN 7 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704580 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. TOE code 190
Show TOE code 190 on requests for assistance needed for claims for totalization benefits
from the United States if the claimant has been disallowed for regular RSDI benefits
because the worker does not meet insured status but does have at least six U.S. quarters
of coverage (P1 cases). For example:
1.
Requests for U.S. and/or foreign earnings records for P1 (Totalization Pending) cases.
2.
Requests to DOs, FSPs or foreign agencies for information or evidence needed to adjudicate
a P1 case.
