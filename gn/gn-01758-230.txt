GN 01758.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Brazil Liaison Agency on Form SSA 2960 USA/BR 3 (Transmittal/Request/Certification)
Procedure to prepare a diary
Prepare a diary for information requested from the Brazilian liaison agency the National
Social Insurance Institute (INSS):
•
Diary initial request for 60 days;
•
Diary follow-up requests for 15 days; and
•
Notify your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) or Module Manager (MM) if there is no response
after 2 follow-ups.
