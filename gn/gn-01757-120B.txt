B. Crediting Brazilian coverage under the agreement
Credit one U.S. QC for every three months of Brazilian coverage in a calendar year.
Do not credit:
•
Brazilian coverage credited for periods prior to 1937;
•
Any month in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC;
•
More than four QCs for any calendar year; or
•
Any period of coverage not based on contributions
