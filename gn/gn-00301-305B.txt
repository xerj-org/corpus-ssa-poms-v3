B. Definitions of statement of fact and opinion
1. Statement of fact
A statement of fact is a statement made concerning a matter, which is susceptible
to exact knowledge, by one having personal knowledge of the matter.
2. Opinion
An opinion is a belief arrived at because of an inference on the part of the person
forming the opinion.
3. Example
If a witness states that the claimant and number holder (NH) were ceremonially married,
it is a statement of fact if the witness was present at the ceremony. It is an opinion
if the witness was not present but based their statement on the couple having referred
to themselves as each other's spouse.
