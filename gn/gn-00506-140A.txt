SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.140 - Fee for Service Notification - Beneficiaries
Effective Dates: 03/27/2003 - Present | TN 5 (03-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
Beneficiaries receiving payments through an authorized Fee for Service organization
must be notified that fees can be collected. Since the decision to allow an organization
to collect a fee is not an initial determination, the notice to the beneficiary does
not include formal appeal rights. However, the beneficiary may make an informal protest
of SSA's decision. The beneficiary also may request the appointment of an alternate
payee or request a new capability determination. (See GN 00506.150B.1. when a beneficiary requests a review of our decision).
