A. Introduction to vital statistics records in Cabo Verde
Cabo Verde has a single, nationwide repository of civil registration records. Records
are held at the National Institute of Statistics and the Civil State (Arquivo Nacional
de Cabo Verde). Records can also be obtained at the Civil Registry Office of Cape
Verde (Conservatoria do Registo Civil) on the island on which the event occurred.
