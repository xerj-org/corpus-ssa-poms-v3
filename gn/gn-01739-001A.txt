A. Purpose of the agreement
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Chile:
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work; and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Chilean
benefits, even though they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
