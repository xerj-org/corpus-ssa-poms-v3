D. Overview of IDD for Kenya
IDD payments sent to Kenya must be in Kenya Shillings (KES) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Kenya. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
