B. Procedure - State Records
See GN 00308.044D. for City of Boston Records
1. Address and FO Code
Massachusetts Department of Public Health
Bureau of Vital Statistics
150 Mt. Vernon Street
Dorchester, MA 02125
FO Code 048
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from the Massachusetts Registry of Vital Records and Statistics . In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
$20.00 if the request is made in person at the Bureau of Vital Records in Dorchester;
$50.00 if the request is made by internet, fax or telephone; $32.00 if the request
is made by mail. Expedited service is available. The fee for expedited service varies
depending on the method of request. Specific information on fees, various downloadable
forms, phone and fax numbers is available at the above website.
3. Type of Payment:
Cash, money order, personal check or credit card.
4. Remarks:
To obtain birth records prior to 1911 contact the Massachusetts State Archives, 220
Morrisey Boulevard, Boston MA 02125. FO Code is 048. The cost of a certified record
is $3.00. Personal checks are accepted. The phone number is 617-727-2816.
The State BVS office has an index of divorce records since 1952. The inquirer will
be directed where to forward the request.
Can parallel FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record from the
central BVS? Verifications are free only when requested via the parallel FO (048,
Dorchester, MA). The preferred method to submit requests is via e562. The FO must
pay for all other services. Limit requests to those detailed in GN 00904.080.
