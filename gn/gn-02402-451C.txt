SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.451 - Coding East Timor Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 04/15/2022 - Present | TN 212 (04-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402451 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in East Timor will have a prefix of “74” at the beginning of the
Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “I” assigned for East Timor.
