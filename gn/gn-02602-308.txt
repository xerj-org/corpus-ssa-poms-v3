GN 02602.308 Processing Form SSA-1588-SM (Beneficiary Recontact Report) Nonresponders and RETAP
Alerts
The Regular Transcript Attainment and Selection Pass (RETAP) automated process selects
a young surviving spouse and sends a follow-up mailing (form SSA-1588-SM and notice)
when the beneficiary does not respond within 90 days of the initial mailing. When
the young surviving spouse does not respond to the follow-up mailing within an additional
90 days, RETAP either suspends or terminates benefits.
