B. Overpayment processing on terminated records
Review the current and prior records to determine if Martinez related post-2006 overpayment
months exist. If such overpayment months exist, take the following action:
•
Compute the fugitive felon overpayment and remove that amount using an N TAC. Annotate
the SSR with remark Overpayment Removed Martinez Relief.
•
If necessary, hold the underpayment using a U TAC and apply the collected amounts
from the fugitive felon overpayment to any outstanding Interim Assistance Reimbursement
(IAR) agreements and attorney fees that may be due. Apply any remaining funds to overpayments
caused by a suspension other than that of fugitive felon.
•
Release any remaining underpayment, subject to installment processing, and applicable
underpayment priority.
