H. Claim for constant attendance supplement
In addition to taking the actions in GN 01727.210F., if the applicant alleges they are severely disabled and requires the constant care
of a third party, enter in the “Remarks” section of Form SSA-2490-BK, the severity of the impairment and why it requires third-party
assistance. This will allow the Portuguese agency to solicit an application for a
constant attendance supplement at the same time it requests the other application.
