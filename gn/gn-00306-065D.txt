D. Procedure - Statements Concerning Paternity
If you obtain statements from individuals (e.g., the NH's relatives or friends) that
are being considered as evidence of paternity for purposes of State intestacy law,
make sure that the statements show:
•
The relationship of the individual to the NH; and
•
How the individual knows that the child was the NH's, e.g., the specific words used
by the NH in orally acknowledging the child.
