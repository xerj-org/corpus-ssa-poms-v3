GN 03313.100 Disclosure to the Office of the President
We provide general instructions for disclosing information from our systems of records
to other Federal agencies and officials and a link to our systems of records and their
applicable routine uses in GN 03313.001. Refer all requests from the Office of the President to the Office of Privacy and
Disclosure at ^OGC OPD Controls.
We established a routine use under the Privacy Act that permits disclosure of non-tax
return information to the Office of the President without the written consent of the
subject of a record. The routine use allows the Office of the President to respond
to an individual who has made an oral or written inquiry to that Office seeking assistance
in a Social Security matter.
We may disclose non-tax return information that the individual is entitled to receive
and that is relevant to the individual's inquiry. Inquiries involving non-tax return
information need not be in writing, but the Office of the President must indicate
that there was an inquiry.
We disclose tax return information only with the individual's written authorization.
We must receive the written authorization within 120 days from when the individual
signs and dates it. For further instructions concerning the disclosure of tax return
information with written consent, see GN 03305.003E and GN 03320.010B.2.
