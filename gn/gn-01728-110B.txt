SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01728.110 - How to Credit Portuguese Coverage
Effective Dates: 06/04/1997 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201728110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these steps to assign Portuguese coverage.
1. Determining the Amount of Coverage Required
Is the claimant filing for disability benefits on the record?
•
If yes, credit all possible F quarters of coverage (FQCs) to the ER.
•
If no, credit only the number of quarters required for insured status.
2. Determining Maximum FQCs
Determine the maximum possible number of FQCs as follows:
•
Divide the number of months of coverage shown for each period of coverage by 3 (the
number of months in a calendar quarter) to determine the maximum possible number of
FQCs for that period. Any remainder is considered 1 FQC.
•
If there is a “FROM - TO” period which begins or ends in the same calendar quarter as another “FROM - TO” period, determine FQCs for each period separately.
3. Assigning FQCs to Calendar Quarters
•
Assign FQCs for each period shown on the Portuguese coverage certification starting
with the calendar quarter inwhich the period begins and continuing consecutively until
all FQCs are assigned.
•
Do not assign FQCs after the calendar quarter in which the Portuguese period ends.
•
Do not assign an FQC in a calendar quarter where a U.S. QC is already assigned. When
a U.S. QC is assigned within a Portuguese period, credit FQCs up to that quarter and
begin again in the next available quarter.
•
For years after 1977, assign existing U.S. QCs using the flexible crediting provision
so as to permit assigning the maximum number of FQCs. See RS 00301.230 regarding the assignment of quarters of coverage.
•
Do not skip over an FQC that was assigned based on another Portuguese period. If FQCs
based on separate Portuguese periods fall within the same calendar quarter, cancel
one of the overlapping FQCs.
4. Alternate Method of Assigning FQCs to Calendar Quarters
•
Use this method of assigning FQCs if insured status is not met using the previous
method and recent coverage is required. (The claim is for disability benefits or currently
insured survivors benefits.)
•
Credit coverage following the method in #3 above except assign FQCs starting with
the last available calendar quarter of each period and continuing backwards consecutively
until all FQCs are assigned.
5. Determining Insured Status
•
After assigning FQCs by one of the methods shown above, determine if insured status
is met and process the award or denial.
NOTE: Before denying a claim, determine if any periods of Portuguese coverage overlap or
coincide so that FQCs for two periods fall in the same calendar quarter. If so, forward
the case to IOTS for evaluation before processing a denial.
