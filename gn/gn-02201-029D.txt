SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02201.029 - The Treasury Offset Program (TOP)
Effective Dates: 05/08/2025 - Present | TN 78 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202201029 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Actions Treasury may take with TOP
1. Conduct the debtor and payment matching process
If the TIN and name of the debtor matches the TIN and name on the payment, TOP determines
if an offset is allowed. If offset is allowed, TOP determines the amount of the offset
and sends a notice of offset to the debtor.
2. Notify debtor of the offset
Treasury notifies the debtor of the offset on or before the date the payment is normally
due. This notice tells the debtor that Treasury offset the payment (in whole or in
part) to repay a delinquent debt owed to SSA. Treasury's notice also provides the
following information:
a.
SSA's claim account number on which the offset occurred,
b.
SSA's contact point and address, and
c.
SSA's telephone number, which is either the national 800 number or the phone number
of the Debt Management Section handling the debt.
3. Notify SSA of the offset
Treasury also notifies SSA of the offset. This is an automated notification update
to the ROAR system for Title II.
4. Update the TOP delinquent debtor database
Treasury updates and maintains the delinquent debtor database to show the most current
debt balance and other important information.
NOTE: Due to confidentiality concerns, Treasury does not divulge the source of the collection
to the creditor agency.
