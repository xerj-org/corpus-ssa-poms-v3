D. Overview of IDD for Monaco
IDD payments sent to Monaco must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Monaco. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for additional information.
