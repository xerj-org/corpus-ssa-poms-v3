C. Signature requirements
The adjudicator must properly sign all adjudicative decisions (electronic or paper)
before final processing. The adjudicator's PIN serves as the electronic signature
when they establish, adjudicate, or effectuate a claim, via MCS, the Automated 101
(A101), the Electronic Form 101 (EF101) process or the applicable adjudicative direct
input screens. For instructions about signatures on adjudicative forms, see GN 01010.275.
Properly store any related paper documentation or adjudicative decisions in the Claims
File Records Management System (CRFMS).
