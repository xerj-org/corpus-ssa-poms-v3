D. Procedure
In addition to the procedures for handling foreign agency liaison forms contained
in GN 01704.400 - GN 01704.990, return to the NPC any request that does not indicate that it is needed to process
a claim under the agreement unless
•
written consent to disclose is attached or
•
the file contains an SSA-2490 signed by the claimant.
