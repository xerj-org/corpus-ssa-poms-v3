SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01775.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in International Benefits and Processing (IBP) for Claims Filed in Romania
Effective Dates: 09/02/2026 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201775210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. U.S. and Romanian claims or Romanian claim only
Prepare a Form SSA e2960 USA/RO 3 (U.S. - Romania Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification) to:
1.
Request the Romanian certified coverage record; and
2.
Send the liaison agency National House of Public Pensions any information or documentation
they may have requested.
