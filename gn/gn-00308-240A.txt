A. Procedures — state sources
These instructions are to be followed to request a search, copy, or certification
of passport files via form DS-1173. Any federal agency can make a request directly
on agency letterhead (see attached copy of sample letter). Attach three copies of
your letter to the request (DS-1173). The normal response time is 60 - 90 days. The
Washington (“M” Street), DC field office (DOC 270) will follow up on requests when they are received
with a copy of the original requesting letter and any other pertinent information.
Claimants can write directly to obtain a faster response for a fee. Their response
time will be 30-45 days. Claimants can call (202) 955-0291 to follow up on their request.
