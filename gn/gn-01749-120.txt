GN 01749.120 U.S. Totalization Benefits under the Czech Agreement
Under the agreement, if a number holder (NH) has at least 6 U.S. quarters of coverage
(QCs) but not enough to be insured for regular U.S. benefits, the NH's credits under
the Czech Social Security system can be added to the NH's U.S. coverage to determine
insured status.
