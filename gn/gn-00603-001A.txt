A. Introduction
Title II and title XVI benefits must be used for the immediate or reasonably foreseeable
needs of the beneficiary. Any remaining benefits must be conserved or invested for
the beneficiary.
The term “beneficiary” is used generically in this subchapter to refer to both title
II beneficiaries and title XVI recipients. Title II and title XVI (or SSI) are specified
when an instruction applies to only one type of benefit.
