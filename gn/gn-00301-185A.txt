SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.185 - Helping Claimants Obtain Evidence
Effective Dates: 06/17/1996 - Present | TN 13 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301185 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. FO assistance
•
Prepare special form letters addressed to the custodian of the records (see GN 00301.195);
•
Enlist the help of third parties (see GN 00301.200);
•
Request the help of other FO's (see GN 00301.205).
2. Claimant authorization
Have claimant sign Form SSA-8510 (Authorization for the Social Security Administration
to Obtain Personal Information) to enable SSA to obtain evidence /information on the
claimant's behalf.
