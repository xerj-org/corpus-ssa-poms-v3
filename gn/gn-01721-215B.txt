B. Process
1. When the MCS Version of SSA-2490 May Be Used
The SSA-2490-BK is available through MCS, but the MCS version may only be used when
the number holder (NH) is filing for:
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits only, or
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits and concurrently for Spanish benefits.
2. When the MCS Version of SSA-2490 May Not Be Used
The MCS version of the SSA-2490-BK may not be used to file for:
•
Spanish benefits only, or
•
U.S. survivors Totalization benefits or Spanish survivors benefits.
A paper or electronic (e-form) SSA-2490-BK application form must be used to file for
U.S. survivors benefits under the agreement. Form SSA-2490-BK is also available through
the Totalization Benefits Resource
Kit.
NOTE: It is not necessary to take an SSA-2490-BK in claims for U.S. auxiliary benefits
in life cases or in survivor cases where there was a previously approved award in
a life case since this application would have been completed previously in connection
with the NH's claim.
