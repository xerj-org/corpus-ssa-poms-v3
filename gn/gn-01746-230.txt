GN 01746.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the Japanese Agencies on Form SSA-2960-J
Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from a Japanese agency:
•
Diary initial requests for 90 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 90 days.
•
Forward the folder to your Deputy Module Manager (DMM) or Module Operations Team Leader
(MOTL) if no response has been received after two follow-ups; i.e., after 9 months.
