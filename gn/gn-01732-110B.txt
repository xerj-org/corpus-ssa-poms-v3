B. Example
The first period of employment under TEL shows a beginning date of 27.04.1948. Although
the TEL system did not begin until 1962, the worker receives credit for work in the
period 4/27/48-12/31/61 because his work was for the same employer and was continuous
from 4/48 until the system began.
