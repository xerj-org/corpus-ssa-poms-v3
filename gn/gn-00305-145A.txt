A. Introduction — divorce and remarriage - general
The effect of a judgment of divorce depends on the State in which it is issued. It
can:
•
immediately restore the parties to the status of single persons;
•
impose a restriction on remarriage against one or both parties for a stated period,
even though the divorce was final; or
•
postpone the dissolution of the marriage for a stated period following the judgment
of divorce and the divorce will not become final until the parties take some additional
action or the court makes the divorce final.
