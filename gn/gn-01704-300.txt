GN 01704.300 Processing Totalization-Related Inquiries
Inquiries regarding totalization benefits or international agreements will be received
from claimants, beneficiaries, employers, DO's, foreign consular officials, and others.
Special processing instructions will depend on whether the inquiry is of a general
nature or claims related.
