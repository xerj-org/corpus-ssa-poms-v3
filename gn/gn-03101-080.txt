GN 03101.080 Appeals of Actions Which Are Not Initial Determinations
We may review administrative actions that are not initial determinations, but they
are not subject to administrative or judicial review. These actions include, but are
not limited to, actions about:
•
Non-payment of benefits (GN 03101.080A in this section)
•
Appeals and reopening a prior determination (GN 03101.080B in this section)
•
Appeals for representative payee actions (GN 03101.080C in this section)
•
Attorneys and representatives (GN 03101.080D in this section)
•
Earnings (GN 03101.080E in this section)
•
Other appeals actions (GN 03101.080F in this section)
