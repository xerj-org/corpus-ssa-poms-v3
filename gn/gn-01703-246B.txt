B. Policy
The Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) transfers to SSA the coverage of Railroad (RR)
workers who have less than 120 months of RR service. This coverage and the resulting
QCs are combined with Title II coverage and will be considered by the foreign country
if, under the agreement, it uses U.S. QCs.
