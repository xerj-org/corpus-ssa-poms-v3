F. Policy - Forwarding FEES
If forwarding of receipts and fees on a weekly basis is impractical because the amounts
to be submitted are so small, they may be forwarded at the end of the month, rather
than weekly. In no case should the fees collected by held in the FO longer than 1
month, or more than 1 week after $10 has been collected.
