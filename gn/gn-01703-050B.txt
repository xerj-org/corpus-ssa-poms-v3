SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.050 - How DIO Responds to Totalization Inquiries
Effective Dates: 01/27/2017 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow these procedures when responding to totalization inquiries.
1. Claims inquiry
Forward claims inquiries received in the modules or International Benefit Office (IBO)
from persons residing in countries serviced by trained FSPs to the FSP via an OF-41.
Include QC information to facilitate the claims interview.
Forward claims inquiries from persons residing in the U.S. or Canada to the servicing
FO via an OF-41.
Send claims inquiries from persons not living in a country serviced by a trained FSP
to IBO via an OF-41 for routine claims development and completion of a totalization
application (SSA-2490-F4).
2. Request for earnings information
If a request is for earnings quarters of coverage, benefit estimate, earnings record
or detailed earnings information, send an SSA-7004-SM-OP1 (Request for Earnings and
Benefit Estimate Statement) or an SSA-7050-F3 (Request for Social Security Earnings
Information). Never send a certified earnings record.
3. General information about totalization or an agreement
Refer simple inquiries regarding general information about totalization or an agreement
to the Benefit Earnings Technicians (BETs). Refer complex inquiries to the claims
specialists (CSs). See the paragraphs in NL 00711.310.B for help in replying.
4. Request for more than one totalization pamphlet
If a request is for more than one totalization pamphlet, refer to the Office of International
Programs (OIP), Office of Foreign Program Policy (OFPP).
5. Request for coverage certificate or certificate of the applicable legislation
If a request is for a coverage certificate or certificate of the applicable legislation,
refer to OIP, OFPP.
6. Coverage or taxation inquiry
Refer coverage or taxation inquiries to OIP, Office of Agreements, Negotiations and
Analysis (OANA).
7. Request from foreign court, welfare agency, consulate, etc.
Send a request from a foreign court, welfare agency, consulate, etc., to the Office
of Public Inquiries, 4100 Annex.
