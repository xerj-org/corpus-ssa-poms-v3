D. Exhibit of Title II and Title XVI Martinez Notice of Inquiry
The new Title II and Title XVI Martinez Notice of Inquiry will inform non-class members
who inquired about possible Martinez relief that they are not eligible to receive
Martinez settlement relief. The notice offers the non-class members two ways they
may obtain more information about the settlement.
You can locate the notices in the Document Processing System (DPS), Post-Entitlement
Folder, and search for Martinez Notice of Inquiry. Send a copy of the completed notice
to Online Retrieval System (ORS).
