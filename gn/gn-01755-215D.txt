D. Procedure for entering the Hungarian Social Security Identification Number (TAJ)
Enter the worker and each claimant's Hungarian Social Insurance Number (TAJ) on all
claims. If the TAJ is unknown, enter all of the following information under Part I
of Form SSA-2490-BK:
•
worker’s full name, including both the paternal and maternal last names (see GN 00302.460 and GN 00302.470);
•
worker’s employment history, including names of employers and dates of employment;
and
•
name of the agency to which the worker last contributed.
NOTE: The Hungarian agency will need this information to transmit the NH’s Hungarian coverage
record.
