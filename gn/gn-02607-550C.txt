SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02607.550 - Payments to Prisoners and Correctional Institutions
Effective Dates: 09/12/2014 - Present | TN 86 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202607550 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Correctional institution receives a paper check
If an institution reports receiving an inmate’s check, follow the steps in the following
chart:
Step
Action
1
Obtain the inmate’s name and SSN and ask the institution to report any additional
checks the inmate may receive.
2
Does a Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS) record exist for this inmate?
a.
If yes, go to step 3.
b.
If no, establish a PUPS record following instructions in GN 02607.460 and go to step 4.
3
Did we send the inmate a suspension notice containing their appeal rights?
a.
If yes, ask the institution to intercept the check and send it to the field office
(FO) for coding and return to Treasury.
b.
If no, send the inmate a notice containing appeal rights and go to step 4.
4
Ask the institution to photocopy the check, write the date of incarceration on the
photocopy, and send the photocopy to the FO to facilitate our inmate status development.
Then, advise the institution to handle the check following its own check cashing policies.
