C. Remittance Clerk Procedure
The remittance clerk must follow the procedures when processing unendorsed returned
Treasury checks:
Step
Action
1
Request the FODRL at the end of the business day.
Reference: MSOM DMS 004.006 and MSOM DMS 004.009 for instructions on how to obtain an FODRL
2
Gather the returned check(s) and the other miscellaneous refunds, and ensure that
they match the remittance information on the FODRL.
3
Sign the FODRL on the remittance clerk line, and hand carry all documents to the remittance
supervisor.
