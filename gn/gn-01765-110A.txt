A. Benefit programs included under the Agreement
The provisions of the Agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and Romanian coverage credits apply to the following types of benefits:
•
For the United States, this Agreement applies to retirement, survivors, and disability
benefits. The Agreement does not apply to Medicare benefits or Supplemental Security
Income payments.
•
For Romania, this Agreement applies to the laws governing the old‑age, survivors,
and disability insurance (OASDI) Benefits programs of the social security system,
as well as to the death grant provided to the family of deceased workers under Romanian
national laws.
