SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.118 - Second Priority Automatically Convincing Evidence of Age
Effective Dates: 12/14/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302118 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Rule
If records from the first priority list (see GN 00302.115) are not readily available (i.e., within 10 working days of the request), request
evidence from the second priority list. Note that all documents in GN 00302.118D. must be domestic records.
