B. Procedure for obtaining vital records in person from Pennsylvania State sources
1. Address and FO code
a. Birth and death records
Division of Vital Records
101 S. Mercer St., Room 401
New Castle, PA 16101
FO code 210
b. Marriage and divorce records
Obtain marriage or divorce records from the courthouse or from the county that granted
the divorce or issued the marriage license.
2. Cost of copy for birth or death records
Birth records cost $20.00 and death records cost $9.00 if an individual requests the
record. For FO requests, see instructions in GN 00308.072C.5. (in this section). The cost for marriage and divorce records varies in each county.
3. Type of payment for birth, death, marriage, and divorce records
The DVR accepts checks or money orders payable to Vital Records.
4. Remarks about requesting vital records by fax or mail
Fax individual requests that do not require payment to the DVR to 724-656-3224. Mail requests requiring payment with the check or money order to:
Division of Vital Records
P.O. Box 1528
New Castle, PA 16101-1528
The DVR requires photo identification for all requests (i.e., in-person, fax, and
mail). The DVR does not accept telephone requests. Examples of acceptable forms of
identification are State-issued driver's licenses and state-issued non-driver photo
identifications. The DVR office has a special form for individuals who do not have
a photo ID (e.g., the Amish).
5. Instructions for requesting birth and death certificate verifications and certifications
You may verify a Pennsylvania (PA) Division of Vital Records birth or death certificate;
however, you must attach a copy of the PA Division of Vital Recordsissued birth or death certificate to the request for verification.
a. Instructions for FOs in the Philadelphia Region
For birth certificate verifications, use the Electronic Verification of Vital Events
(EVVE) system. For instructions about EVVE, see GN 00302.980. If the EVVE search is not successful,
•
attach the birth certificate to the Request for Verification of Birth Record and, for death certificate verifications, attach the death certificate to the Request for Verification of Death Record; and
•
fax the request to PA Division of Vital Records at 724656-3224.
There is no charge for verifications of either birth or death certificates.
There is a $10.00 charge for a Request for Certification of Birth Record. FOs within the Philadelphia Region should mail the form with the check, money order,
or third party draft payable to Vital Records to the:
Division of Vital Records
P.O. Box 1528
New Castle, PA 16101-1528
There is no charge for a Request for Certification of Death Record. Fax requests to PA Division of Vital Records at 724656-3224.
Prepare a written request including the employee identification information (see GN 00308.001B.3).The Agency Approval Number, which all Philadelphia Region FOs received, must accompany
all requests.
b. Instructions for FOs outside the Philadelphia Region
Fax copies of the following items to the New Castle, PA, FO. See DOORS for fax and telephone information. :
•
an e562, which you can also use as your fax coversheet, and
•
one of the appropriate request forms mentioned in GN 00308.072B.5.a, and
•
a copy of the employee identification letter in GN 00308.001B.3 (of the employee submitting the request), and
•
a copy of the birth or death certificate for all verifications.
If requesting a birth certification, do not send a third party draft. New Castle will
be responsible for payment.
