C. myCOA exclusions
1.
The myCOA application cannot be used for:
•
SSI eligible and concurrent users who want to update their mailing address (eligible
SSI users can update their telephone number only);
•
Individuals with a representative payee;
•
Prisoners; or
•
Individuals with a pending initial claim or appeal.
2.
A user with electronic access to my Social Security can only change their own address and telephone number via the internet.
The user cannot make changes for anyone else (e.g., family members); even if they
are paid benefits under the same account number.
