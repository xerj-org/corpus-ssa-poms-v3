GN 00312.398 Tonga – Vital Statistics Records
Procedure – Birth, Marriage, Divorce and Death Certificates
Include the following information on certificate requests:
Birth Certificates
•
Full name at birth;
•
Date and place of birth; and
•
Parent's full names
Marriage Certificates
•
Full names of both parties; and
•
Date and place of marriage
Death Certificates
•
Full name of the deceased; and
•
Date and place of death
