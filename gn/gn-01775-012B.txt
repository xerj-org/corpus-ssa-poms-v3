B. FBU processing referrals from the Romanian liaison agency
This procedure explains how the Naples, Italy FBU processes referrals from the Romanian
liaison agency National House of Public Pensions.
1. Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits
Upload all material in Evidence Portal (EP) annotating “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Follow the instructions in GN
01775.010B to process the claim.
