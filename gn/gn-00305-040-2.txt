2. Prior spouse's whereabouts unknown
a.
Have the worker or claimant submit:
•
The dates of prior marriage and separation.
•
The places where the prior spouse has lived, including dates of residence, from date
of separation to present (or the date of death if known to be deceased).
•
Names and addresses of the prior spouse's relatives and friends.
b.
If the prior spouse is not located from the information in a. above, get a DEQY and
ascertain the most recent employer's name and address. If the prior spouse's SSN is
unknown, to get the name and address of an employer use identifying information from
the marriage record to locate the SSN. Next, take the action in the following chart.

Then . . .
