SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.110 - How to Document Insured Status Decisions
Effective Dates: 03/23/2022 - Present | TN 8 (03-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
If a record of foreign coverage was obtained to adjudicate a claim, document the claims
file to show that the foreign coverage was considered. Foreign periods of coverage
should be credited only to the extent they are needed to provide totalization insured
status. There are two methods (manual method and observation method) used for determining
and documenting that insured status is met for retirement and survivor claims. When
determining and documenting insured status for disability benefits use the Disability
Insured Status Calculator (DISCO).
