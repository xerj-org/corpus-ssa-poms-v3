SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02301.065 - Overpayment Owed by Deceased Beneficiary Due Title II Underpayment
Effective Dates: 12/10/2024 - Present | TN 16 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202301065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Process underpayments due deceased beneficiaries depending on the status of the overpayment
protest:
•
If the deceased beneficiary has an unresolved overpayment protest (waiver or reconsideration
resolution pending), do not release the underpayment until we resolve the protest.
For information on overpayment waivers, see GN 02250.001 through GN 02250.410. For information on a pending reconsideration request for a deceased beneficiary,
see GN 03102.200B.5 and GN 03102.325B.7.
•
If the deceased beneficiary has an outstanding overpayment and there is no protest
pending, withhold the amount of the overpayment from the underpayment. Pay any remaining
underpayment to the proper recipient(s).
