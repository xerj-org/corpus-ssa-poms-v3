A. POLICY
Benefits not required to meet the needs of the beneficiary must be conserved or invested
for the beneficiary. See GN 00603.001 for more information about the conservation of benefits.
Conserved or invested funds must be held in a form of account which shows that the
payee holds the benefits in trust for the beneficiary.
