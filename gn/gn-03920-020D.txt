D. Rescinding a fee waiver
A representative who has waived their fee or direct payment of their fee may rescind
the waiver and change the fee arrangement before the date we notify the claimant of
our first favorable determination or decision. To do so, the representative must submit
an updated Form SSA-1696 with a different fee arrangement selected or submit an equivalent
written statement unequivocally stating the intent to charge and collect a fee in
the case. The representative and the claimant must both sign the Form SSA-1696 or
equivalent written statement.
