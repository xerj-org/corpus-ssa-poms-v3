A. Purpose of the U.S. - Romania Totalization Agreement
The social security Agreement between the United States and Romania:
•
Eliminates dual social security coverage and taxation of the same work in the two
countries, and
•
May permit workers (and their dependents and survivors) to qualify for partial U.S.
or Romanian benefits, even if they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
