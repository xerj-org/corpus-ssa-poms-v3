C. USDC inventory process and inventory log
The USDC Inventory Log helps the field office (FO) track the disbursement of USDC
cards to beneficiaries and recipients. Please follow the process when utilizing the
USDC inventory log:
1.
The USDC Inventory Log must be stored together with the USDCs in a safe with a combination
lock, an electronic or mechanical keypad, or in a lock-bar cabinet with a combination
lock. The template for the required Inventory Log can be accessed on the TPPS
website.
2.
Upon receipt of the USDCs, management will complete the “Date USDC received in the
FO” and “Card Number (Last 4 Digits)” fields and place the log in the locked safe
or cabinet.
3.
When a cashier accesses the safe to retrieve a debit card, they must enter the “USDC
Issued to Recipient” date and sign the log. Management must also sign the log at the
time of debit card retrieval.
4.
Ensure that the FO Code is completed at the top of the Inventory Log.
