SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.300 - Application of the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) in Totalization Benefit Computations – General
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 10 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The WEP does not apply when computing Totalization benefits payable for months after December 1994. The WEP may apply when computing the theoretical PIA for months prior to January 1995 for a worker entitled to both a Totalization benefit and a pension based on noncovered
employment.
