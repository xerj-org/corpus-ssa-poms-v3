SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.388 - Coding Bosnia Herzegovina Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 11/02/2023 - Present | TN 245 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402388 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Bosnia Herzegovina has a prefix of “68” at the beginning of the
Routing Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN)
field has the letter “M” assigned for Bosnia Herzegovina.
