D. Procedure – required remarks
If a claimant, other than the worker, was born in Greece but doesn't have a certification
of the Greek register record, enter the city and nomarchy (province) of birth in the
Remarks section of the Form SSA-2490-BK. (This information is already requested in
item 12(b) of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK for the worker.)
