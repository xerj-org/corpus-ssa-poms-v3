3. Remarks:
Birth, marriage, and death records are indexed for 1948 through the most recent closed
year. Records for 1896-1947 are also on file at BVS but are not indexed.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of a birth/death/marriage record? Yes.
Contact the Hartford FO if you need verification of birth, death, or marriage information
or a certified birth, death, or marriage record.
