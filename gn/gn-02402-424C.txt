SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.424 - Coding Micronesia Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 12/07/2018 - Present | TN 121 (02-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402424 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Micronesia will have a prefix of “72” at the beginning of the
Routing and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number
(DAN) field has the letter “C” assigned for Micronesia.
