D. Requests for medical evidence from records of U.S. physicians and U.S. hospitals
1.
Obtain medical evidence from the records of U.S. physicians and hospitals via Forms
SSA-4766-U3 requesting assistance from the appropriate DOs. The applicant's consent
is necessary for the physician or hospital to release the medical records to SSA.
If the claimant resides in the U.S.A., obtain a completed Form SSA-827. If the claimant
resides outside the U.S.A., obtain a completed Form SSA-827F. Either of these forms
authorizes physicians and hospitals to release medical information to the Social Security
Administration. If no claim has been filed under an agreement, consent statements
allowing SSA to release the information to a foreign agency must also be obtained.
Note: Consent forms are not required if the person is scheduled for a consultative medical
examination requested by a foreign agency.
2.
Send medical evidence to the foreign agency via a Form SSA-2960.
