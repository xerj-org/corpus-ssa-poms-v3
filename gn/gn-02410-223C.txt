SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02410.223 - Garnishment for Court Ordered Victim Restitution
Effective Dates: 05/30/2012 - Present | TN 67 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202410223 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. If there is no MBR for the beneficiary and there is no MCS claim or appeal pending
Do not input to COGS. Return the order using the notice in NL 00703.724.Garnish for court ordered victim restitution against current or prior month accrual
(CMA or PMA) payments.
Do not garnish retroactively. Applying garnishment cannot cause an overpayment.
See Processing Garnishment Orders in the Field Office or Processing Center, GN 02410.210A.2.
