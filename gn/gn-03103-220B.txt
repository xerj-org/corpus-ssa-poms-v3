B. Reopening after a hearing dismissal
The dismissal does not preclude reopening and revising the reconsideration determination
(see GN 04001.000). If the claimant submits new evidence after a dismissed hearing, send it to the
PC to see if reopening applies. Relative to the reopening, handle the case as if no
hearing had ever been requested (For reopening and appeal rights, see GN 04001.090).
