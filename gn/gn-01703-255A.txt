SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.255 - Totalization Claims Filed by RSDI Beneficiaries
Effective Dates: 02/27/1995 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703255 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The United States will not consider foreign coverage if a NH is fully insured based
on U.S. coverage only unless foreign coverage might yield either an earlier MOEN or
DIB insured status. These claims are often received as unrestricted claims from the
foreign agencies.
