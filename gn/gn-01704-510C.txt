SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.510 - Lag Earnings
Effective Dates: 08/08/2014 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704510 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Germany
Do not request proof of lag earnings for Germany; however, if lag earnings are alleged,
annotate part IV of the Form SSA-2960-U2-GE, “Additional earnings alleged for (alleged period). Please advise if you need quarter
of coverage information for this period.” If the German agency requests lag information, develop it.
