B. Applying Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provisions
If a claimant does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provision and
alleges residence in the Czech Republic, the Claims Representative develops residence
status to verify that the exemption provided by the agreement applies.
