D. Procedure — Agreement to Report
As pointed out above, some pensioners may have worked in more than one country during
their lives and therefore may be eligible for pensions from more than one country.
Therefore, be careful to point out to the claimant completing the SSA-308 that, in
completing the form, they agree to report promptly to SSA if they subsequently become entitled to another pension prior to January 2024
after the cessation of the pension currently received (see the “Important
Information” block on the reverse of the form).
