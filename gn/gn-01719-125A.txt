Effective Dates: 01/12/2023 - Present | TN 4 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201719125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Determining eligibility
The Agreement provides that the U.K. will count a worker's U.S. coverage when determining
eligibility for U.K. benefits if the worker:
•
is not insured for a basic U.K. benefit based on U.K. coverage only; and
•
has at least 52 weeks (1 year) of U.K. coverage.
