A. Process
Divorce records are available from the County Clerk in the county where the divorce
was granted. The cost of copy varies. Marriage records are available from the Clerk
of the Court of the county where the license was issued. The cost of copy varies.
The FO can certify, verify or obtain a free copy unless otherwise indicated below.
