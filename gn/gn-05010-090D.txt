D. PROCEDURE — SUMMARY CHART

changes of address. It does not eliminate the need to undertake other development,
such as getting an SSA-21.
NOTE: “NAN” means no action is taken to change the AWSC unless there is evidence of a change
in beneficiary's alien status.
