SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.245 - Determining Relationship of NH or Spouse to Grandchild's Parent
Effective Dates: 12/14/1998 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306245 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
All of the provisions as to State laws on inheritance, child (or stepchild) of a deemed
marriage, and deemed child under section 216(h)(3) of the Act apply. In establishing
the relationship to the NH's spouse, consider the spouse as the “insured individual” (NH) in applying those provisions.
NOTE: It is not necessary to establish the dependency of such a person on the NH or spouse.
However, dependency-type factors are a condition of relationship in some cases.
