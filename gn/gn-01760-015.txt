GN 01760.015 Coverage and Contributions under the Uruguayan Social Security System
Uruguayan social security extends coverage to every citizen who has income from gainful
employment, including employees and self-employed persons. The entire Uruguayan population
at retirement age is entitled to a pension or social benefit.
The amount of a Uruguayan benefit is set by the:
•
worker's average earnings; and
•
number of years of contributions; and
•
age of retirement
