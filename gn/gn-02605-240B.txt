B. International direct deposit
IDD service is a joint effort between SSA and its fiscal agent, the Federal Reserve
Bank of New York (FRBNY). The process also involves the assistance of a processor
bank. This may be either the central bank of the foreign country (e.g., the Bank of
England) or a large commercial bank.
