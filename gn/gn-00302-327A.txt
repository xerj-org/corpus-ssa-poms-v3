SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.327 - Definition of Holocaust Survivor
Effective Dates: 12/21/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302327 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
This policy applies to survivors of the Holocaust. However, not everyone who was caught
up in the events preceding and during World War II qualifies to use these procedures.
Only those who meet the definition below may have their DB established under the special
procedures.
