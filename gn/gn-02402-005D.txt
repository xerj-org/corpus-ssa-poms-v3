D. Handling iClaims when the applicant does not elect direct deposit
Currently iClaim filers are not offered Direct Express. If you are processing an iClaim
and the applicant did not select direct deposit as the method of payment, please contact
the applicant via phone and follow the instructions outlined in subsection GN 02402.005E.
