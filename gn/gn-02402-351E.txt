E. Description of Iceland IDD bank data
The data for coding IDD for Iceland comes from the International Bank Account Number
(IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from his or her FI. The IBAN will be 26 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR consist of 27 characters, including the
country code of “L” for Iceland. Technicians will use the IBAN to derive the RTN and
DAN when processing an IDD request.
