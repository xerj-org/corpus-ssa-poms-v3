B. Operating Procedure
Where collection efforts were previously discontinued (TC 71 or TC 73) and the PC
is on notice of a substantial change in the debtor's financial circumstances, or a
TC 73 diary matures, the PC will update ROAR (TC 77) and resume refund efforts, see
GN 02210.006 C.3.
