B. When the U.S. Embassy in Tokyo processes an IDD reclamation
The U.S. Embassy in Tokyo processes reclamations for Japan that are at least $30.
The U.S. Embassy in Tokyo also processes reclamation requests from the Office of Earnings
and International Operations (OEIO). This occurs when an incorrect payment transmits
to a bank in the Embassy’s assigned region and the payment did not return.
