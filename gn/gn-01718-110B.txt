SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01718.110 - How to Credit Swedish Coverage
Effective Dates: 10/05/1993 - Present | TN 1 (09-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201718110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
When assigning Swedish coverage:
a.
Use only years with entries other than “000” in the “PPA” or “PPB” column, and in the “SA” column.
b.
Is the claimant filing for disability benefits on this record?
•
If yes, credit all possible FQCs to the earnings record.
•
If no, credit only the number of quarters required for insured status.
c.
Consider each year separately.
d.
Assign FQCs as follows:
•
If there are zero U.S. quarters posted for a year of
Swedish coverage, credit 4 FQCs.
•
If there are 1-3 U.S. quarters posted for a year of Swedish coverage, credit FQCs
in the blank quarters.
•
If there are 4 U.S. quarters posted for a year of Swedish coverage, credit no FQCs.
