B. Procedure for reviewing special determinations
•
If the original determination requires substantial modifications, you must prepare
a new determination. However, do not indicate on the new determination that it is
a reversal or modification.
•
Do not prepare a new determination if the FO determination is correct and it only
requires an additional statement to strengthen the conclusion. In this situation,
write “COMMENT” in the bottom of the determination and add the additional statement.
•
If a determination is revised or modified, write “REVISED” or “MODIFIED” on the original
FO determination.
•
Maintain the original of the revised determination in the claims file either the “paper”
or the “electronic”;
•
Fax a copy of the new determination to the Non Disability Repository (NDR) or electronic
DIB folder (eDIB) as per GN 00301.322 and inform the FO of the modification using the Modernized Development Worksheet
(MDW).
