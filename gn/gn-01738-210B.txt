SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.210 - Preparing Claim Packages in OIO for Claims Filed in Greece
Effective Dates: 09/08/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. U.S. Claim
•
Prepare a claims package if a Greek coverage record is required and has not been requested
by the FSP in Athens.
•
Include Form SSA-2960-U3-GR in the package.
2. U.S. and Greek or Greek Claim Only
•
Prepare a claims package if a Greek coverage record is required and has not been requested
by the FSP or IKA has requested information or documentation.
•
Include Form SSA-2960-U3-GR and information or documentation requested by IKA.
