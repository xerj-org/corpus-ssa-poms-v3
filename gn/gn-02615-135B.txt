B. Federal court previously decided PPV issue
If you receive information that a beneficiary’s record shows a final federal court
PPV suspension or overpayment issue disposition, take no action on the case based
on the Clark Court Order. Refer these cases via email to the Office of General Counsel (OGC) at:
^OGC OPL Controls @ssa.gov. In the email subject line type, “Clark Court Order Case
– final federal court disposition.” Provide the beneficiary’s name and social security
number (SSN) in the body of the email.
