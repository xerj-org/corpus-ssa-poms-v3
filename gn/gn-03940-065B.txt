B. Procedure - Title II - Delayed Auxiliary Claim(S)
As GN 03940.035 explains, do not defer authorizing a fee when there are one or more delayed auxiliary
claims. Use paragraphs shown in GN 03940.080 to notify the primary claimant and any non-delayed auxiliary(ies) of the authorized
fee.
