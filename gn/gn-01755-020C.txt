C. Administrative organization for Hungarian benefits
The Central Administration of National Pension Insurance (ONYF) operates the public
pension system of Hungary. If inquirers request additional information about Hungarian
benefits, advise that the Social Security Administration (SSA) cannot provide official
information about Hungarian benefits. Refer all inquiries about the Hungarian pension
system to the following address:
Budapest, VIII. district, Fiumei út 19/A.
Országos Nyugdíjbiztosítási
Fõigazgatóság
1916 Budapest, Hungary
