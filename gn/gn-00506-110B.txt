SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00506.110 - Reviewing Evidence in a Fee for Service Application
Effective Dates: 04/15/2016 - Present | TN 25 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Applications for fee for service status should be referred to the SSA reviewing official
in the servicing FO along with all pertinent documentation. The SSA official will
make a decision within 30 calendar days of receipt by the servicing FO.
NOTE: Applications received in a non-servicing FO should be mailed to the servicing FO
for a decision. The non-servicing FO should notify the organization that the application
was transferred. The reviewing official will determine if all qualification factors
are met based on the information received.
1. SSA Reviewing Official
The form SSA-445, Application to Collect a Fee for Payee Services, will be reviewed
in the servicing FO by a member of management (OS, MSS or above). The reviewing official
will:
•
Decide if the organization submitted all information required to make a determination
(refer to GN 00506.010B.1. and GN 00506.010B.2. for a list of all the documentation required);
•
Request CO to obtain a credit report, only if the organization is on eRPS and currently
serving five or more beneficiaries (refer to GN 00506.600);
•
Perform a follow-up (if needed) to obtain additional information and clarify any pending
issues with the organizational applicant, including an explanation of the FFS rules
and responsibilities (in addition of fulfilling the regular payee duties); and,
•
Determine whether the organization will be approved or denied to collect a fee, mail
the appropriate notices, and enter appropriate data on RFEE screen.
2. Timeframe for Decisions
The income generated from fees for payee services may be important to the overall
economic stability of the nonprofit organization and may determine the extent of its
services. Once the servicing FO receives form SSA-445, it is SSA policy that a decision
be made on an organization’s application and proper notice be issued within 30 calendar
days.
a. Required Documents Not Received After Initial Request
If the organization fails to send a copy of any required document (e.g., IRS letter,
bonding agreement, etc.) which must accompany the request, proceed as follows:
•
Contact the applicant or its designee in writing, indicating that this is the second
request and remind them of the additional documentation needed.
•
Give a 15-day deadline (including mailing time) from the date of the second request. This 15-day deadline counts towards the 30 calendar days.
•
Document the contact on the eRPS Report of Contact Details screen and control the
document’s return in eRPS by using the Add New Issue screen. Enter “ORGCON” in the
issue field.
•
Deny the request if the information is not received by the specified date on the basis
that the qualification factor has not been established due to the lack of evidence
(GN 00506.100B.). Issue the appropriate denial notice per NL 00703.944.
b. Documents Received
Upon receipt of a completed and signed SSA-445, along with all other evidence proving
that the organization meets the required qualifications to become a Fee for Service
organization:
•
Process the FFS application as soon as possible not to exceed the 30 calendar days
from receipt of the application.
•
Issue the appropriate notice (i.e., approval or denial notice) and file the documentation
in the paper precedent file to be kept in the FO. Send copies of the approval notice
to your RO and to Central Office at the address listed in GN 00506.170B.
•
Complete the Fee For Service screens (MS INTRANTERPS 014.008 and 015.004) (if applicable)
and clear the issue on the Add New Issue screen in eRPS.
•
Notify the beneficiaries (if applicable) using the notice found in NL 00703.770 Representative Payee — Authorized Fee Notice to Beneficiary or Person Standing in
Place of Beneficiary
3. Improper Signature
The only person authorized to sign a form SSA-445, Application to Collect a Fee for
Payee Services, is the principal operating officer or its designee. If the signature
is not that of the authorized official, follow this procedure:
•
contact the head of the organization if the application does not indicate that the
applicant has the authority to enter into an agreement.
•
Ascertain if the person submitting the request can act on behalf of the organization.
•
ask that the proper individual in the organization to submit an application, if the
person submitting the original application cannot act on behalf of the organization.
•
if the authorized individual does not sign the form SSA-445, Application to Collect
a Fee for Payee Services, or you are unable to obtain proper signature within the
specified 30 calendar days, deny the request and document the eRPS Fee For Service
screen. Issue the appropriate denial letter per NL 00703.944.
4. Questionable Bonding or Licensing
a. Unable to determine proper bonding or licensing
If it cannot be determined that a non-governmental organization is bonded and licensed
in the state in which it serves as payee:
•
Contact a representative of the organization or State agency which issued the agreement
or license to obtain further explanation; and/or
•
Obtain the necessary information, such as the protection given under a bonding agreement
or the purpose of a license, and document the eRPS Fee For Service screen with the
information obtained.
•
Document the contact on an SSA-5002 and photocopy the proofs for the FO paper precedent
file.
b. Documentation provided is not acceptable
If it is determined that the evidence submitted by a non-governmental organization
is inappropriate:
•
Deny the request if the applicant organization does not present an acceptable license
or the bonding/insurance agreement does not provide for adequate protection against
losses caused by the organization.
•
See GN 00506.105C. for bonding/licensing characteristics.
•
Document the eRPS Fee For Service screens (MS INTRANTERPS 014.008 and 015.004) accordingly.
Send denial letter per NL 00703.944.
5. Questionable Government Agency
If it cannot be determined that an organization is a government agency:
•
contact the person who applied and determine whether the organization was created
as a part of state or local government for the benefit of the public at large.
•
determine if there is a parent agency and/or a subsidiary that is not questionable.
Contact that office for verification of the questionable agency’s status.
•
document the eRPS Fee for Service screen (MS INTRANTERPS 015.004) with the information
obtained and input your determination.
