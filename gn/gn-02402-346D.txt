D. Overview of IDD for Turkey
IDD payments sent to Turkey must be in U.S. Dollar (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) to input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Turkey. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
