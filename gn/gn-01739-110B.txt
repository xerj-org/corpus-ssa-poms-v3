B. Taxes under the agreement
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes, Federal Income Contributions Act (FICA) and Self-Employment
Contributions Act (SECA) including the Medicare portion, and
•
Chilean Social Security taxes that finance retirement, survivors, and disability benefits,
health insurance, work accident and occupational illness programs.
