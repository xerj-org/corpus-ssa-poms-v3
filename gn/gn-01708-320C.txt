SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.320 - Providing Monthly Benefit Data to German Agencies
Effective Dates: 03/18/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. When to certify benefit data
•
Certify the amount of RIB or DIB, i.e., A or HA benefits, that a person is receiving
in all initial claims packages sent to a German liaison agency or in response to a
German liaison agency's request.
•
Certify the amounts of other types of benefits only in response to specific German
liaison agency requests.
