B. Policy
When we approve a waiver request, we relieve the individual from the obligation to
repay the overpayment. We must send a waiver approval notice. The waiver approval
notice must include:
•
That we approved the individual’s waiver request;
•
The overpayment amount;
•
The reasons that we approved the waiver request, which includes finding the individual
not at fault and a determination that the individual meets one of the other waiver
provisions (e.g., cannot pay their bills or paying us back would be unfair for some
other reason);
•
That the individual does not have to repay the overpayment;
•
We will resume the individual’s benefits or payments, if applicable;
•
We will refund any benefits or payments that we withheld or recovered while the waiver
determination was pending, if applicable. For more information on refunding monies
after a waiver approval, refer to GN 02250.310; and
•
The appropriate appeal language. To determine the correct level of appeal, refer to
GN 02250.380.
