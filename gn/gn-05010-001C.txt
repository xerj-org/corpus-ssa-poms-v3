C. DEFINITIONS - UNDER THE IRC
1. United States
The United States consists of the 50 States, District of Columbia (D.C.), Guam and Northern Mariana
Islands (NMI).
2. Citizen of a Possession of the U.S.
A citizen of a possession of the U.S. is an individual who is not otherwise a citizen of the U.S. and who has not become
a citizen by naturalization in a State, Territory or D.C.
