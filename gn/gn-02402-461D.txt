D. Overview of IDD for Lesotho
IDD payments sent to Lesotho must be in Lesotho Loti (LSL) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Lesotho. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
