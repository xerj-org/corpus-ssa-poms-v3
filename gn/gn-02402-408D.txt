D. Overview of IDD for Vietnam
IDD payments sent to Vietnam must be in Vietnam Dong (VND) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Vietnam. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
