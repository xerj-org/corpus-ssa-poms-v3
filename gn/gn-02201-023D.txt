D. Individual requests waiver and reconsideration
If an individual requests a waiver and reconsideration, take these actions:
•
Stop recovery (if applicable) using the Protest/Stop Recovery Request (PC) (DRPR)
screen in DMS using #5, Waiver and Recon Fact/Amount, as the protest reason.
•
Fax a copy of the request for reconsideration and the SSA-632 form into the Evidence
Portal (EP).
•
Before forwarding the waiver request to the FO, make a formal determination or dismissal
of the reconsideration following the instructions in GN 02250.002.
•
Annotate DMS remarks to document your actions.
