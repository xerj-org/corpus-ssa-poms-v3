C. Dispatching and diarying
1.
Dispatch the first and second copies of the form (any attachments should be stapled
to these copies). File third and fourth copies in the folder.
2.
Prepare an SSA-382-U2 to control the reply to the SSA-1320-U4. Diary the request for
90 days; exceptions: 120 days for requests to Syria, Cape Verde Islands, Madeira,
the Azores, Canary Islands, Mallorca and Ibiza; 180 days for requests to Czechoslovakia,
Yemen and Southern Yemen. Followups should be diaried for 60 days.
