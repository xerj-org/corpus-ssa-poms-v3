SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00303.400 - Authorized Alien Status
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 67 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200303400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Use this procedure to verify alien status.
1. Reviewing DHS Documents
Review the DHS document as follows:
a.
Carefully examine any document presented as evidence of alien status for agreement
with the guidelines in the Administrative-Confidential
Memorandum.
b.
If needed, view the document under the black light for conformation to the special
identification check points and fluorescence.
NOTE:
Do not accept any:
•
DHS document which SSA does not use to establish lawful alien status (refer to GN 00303.440B.1. for list of acceptable documents to establish lawful alien status);
•
Expired DHS document;
•
DHS receipt; or
•
Photocopy of a document.
2. Which Documents To Verify With DHS
To ensure program integrity, all non-U.S. citizen applicants must have their immigration
status verified through DHS's Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE)
program, regardless of the type or appearance of the immigration document presented.
For SSI claimants and recipients, this requirement applies to all initial applications,
status changes, and redeterminations.
For information on how to verify documents with DHS for SSI PRUCOL determination purposes,
see SI 00501.430.
3. Verifying Documents with DHS
To verify documents with DHS (via the SAVE program), see GN 00303.600A
a.
Photocopy both sides of the DHS document and attach legible photocopies electronically to the SAVE
initial case verification. Send the completed SAVE case verification with attached
photocopies to DHS via SAVE and return the immigration document to the claimant (see
GN 00303.600 for how to request the SAVE case verification and interpret DHS's response).
b.
Scan both sides of the immigration document and a printout of the SAVE response into
the electronic folder (e.g., Evidence Portal, eView).
c.
If the document includes an Alien Registration Number (ARN or A#), enter the ARN in
Person Information.
