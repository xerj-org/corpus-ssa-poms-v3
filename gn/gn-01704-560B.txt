Effective Dates: 02/24/1999 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)

B. Completion of items on form SSA-1320-U4 (See exhibit)
1.
Always enter the correct SPR code: SPR558-Module 7, SPR559-Module 8.
2.
Show the SSN of the NH and include the claim symbol(s) for the individual(s) whose
case is being developed.
3.
Date—Enter today's date plus 3 days.
4.
Enter the NH's name.
5.
Show the name of the claimant/beneficiary about whom the development is being requested.
6.
Do not complete this item.
7.
Show the name of the individual (or institution). Provide the complete address, as
well as the telephone number (if available).
8.
LCs should never have to complete this item.
9.
List the forms to be completed. (If the request is sent to a claims-taking post, the
forms need not be attached unless it is a form not regularly stocked by the FSP.)
The FSPs in the following countries are claims-taking posts: Austria, Denmark, France,
Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Mexico, Norway, Philippines (VARO),
Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom and Yugoslavia.
10.
Always check this block when referring to development instructions in “Remarks.” Always include the following remarks, “Reply to Totalization, P.O. Box 17049, Baltimore, MD 21235.”
11.
State the request as simply and concisely as possible. Do not use technical abbreviations.
If the request is to a claims-taking post, cite POMS references. (The FSPs do not
have any manual other than POMS.)
12.
Always indicate the total number of enclosures.
