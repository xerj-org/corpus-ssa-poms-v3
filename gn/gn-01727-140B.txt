B. Procedure for applying ANP for workers
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the alien nonpayment provisions
alleges residence in Portugal, develop residence status to verify that the exemption
provided by the Agreement applies by developing the following:
•
Residence status;
•
Refugee or stateless person status, if the claimant alleges such status, or in the
case of an auxiliary or survivor it is alleged that the worker is (or was at the time
of death) a refugee or stateless person; or
•
Citizenship of the worker, if a dependent or survivor alleges that the worker is (or
was at the time of death) a U.S. or Portuguese citizen.
