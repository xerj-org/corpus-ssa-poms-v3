SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03920.025 - Representative's Fee - Trust or Escrow Accounts
Effective Dates: 12/01/2017 - Present | TN 15 (11-05)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203920025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Policy - Disclosure and Documentation
If SSA has a question about a trust or escrow agreement or about a representative's
refund to a claimant, SSA will request and a representative must provide SSA a copy
of the trust or escrow agreement and/or proof that the representative returned to
the claimant funds in the account in excess of the authorized fee.
