Effective Dates: 09/18/1991 - Present | TN 1 (09-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203360050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. References
1. Use of SSA-4641
A financial institution will not release records or information to SSA without Form
SSA-4641-U2 (Authorization for the Social Security Administration to Obtain Account
Records from a Financial Institution). See SI 01140.200B.5. for instructions on completing this form.
2. SSA's Reporting Requirements
SSA's reporting requirements under the RFPA are explained in the AIMS Guide, General
Administration, Chapter 14.07.09.
