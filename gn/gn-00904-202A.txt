A. Introduction
The Division of International Operations (DIO), within the Office of Earnings and
International Operations, coordinates foreign claims and related issues. DIO has processing
center (PC), disability determination service (through its International Disability
Unit (IDU)) and field office (FO) functions.
The Office of Income Security Programs' Office of Strategic Planning, Notices, and
International Policy has program and policy responsibility for administering the Social
Security program outside the United States.
