SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.045 - Assisting Title II Surviving Spouse or Surviving Divorced Spouse Claimants with Month of Election (MOEL) Decisions when the Retirement Insurance Benefit – Limitation (RIB LIM) Applies
Effective Dates: 11/05/2008 - Present | TN 129 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition - RIB LIM
When a deceased number holder (NH) received reduced retirement insurance benefits
(RIB), that benefit amount is the maximum the surviving spouse or surviving divorced
spouse may receive. This limitation is referred to as the “RIB LIM.” For more information
about the RIB LIM, see RS 00615.320.
