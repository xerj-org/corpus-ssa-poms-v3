Effective Dates: 05/10/2024 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)

E. Process for FI’s under limited liability
1. Outstanding total has been repaid
If the total of all payments issued after death has already been returned by a survivor,
the FI must do the following:
•
Complete the Notice of Reclamation;
•
Attach proof of the survivor’s repayment; and
•
Send to Treasury so it arrives within 60 days of the Notice.
2. Account balance is equal to or greater than the total reclamation amount
If the account contains the full amount listed in the Notice of Reclamation, the FI
must do the following:
•
Return the exact amount of the outstanding total within 60 days of the Notice; and
•
Return the completed Notice within 60 days.
3. Account balance is less than the total reclamation amount
If the account contains less than the amount listed in the Notice of Reclamation,
the FI must do all of the following:
•
Return the amount of the account balance;
•
Title II only -- Provide the names and addresses of any persons who withdrew funds
(i.e., last withdrawers) from the account after the death on the FS-133. If that is
not possible, the FI must explain in writing why this information is not available;
and
•
Complete and return the FS-133.
