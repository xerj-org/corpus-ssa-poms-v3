Some software programs in use in FOs, PCs, and TSCs, that are being piloted and tested,
have an authorized support procedure. Contact should be made according to the procedures
appropriate to the program.
An alternate method of contact for questions would be an email to the Component’s
Outlook mailbox. This allows for distribution of questions to program experts by subject
matter and expertise.
