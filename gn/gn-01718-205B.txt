SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01718.205 - Preparing Claim Packages for Claims Filed Outside Sweden
Effective Dates: 12/23/1996 - Present | TN 1 (09-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201718205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
1. U.S. Claim
Complete Form SSA-2960-U3-SW. Check item IV.B to request a Swedish coverage record.
Check item IV.D to request medical evidence from SLAFU files if claimant has filed
for U.S.
disability benefits.
2. U.S. and Swedish Claim
Include the following information in claim packages.
•
SSA-2960-U3-SW. Check item IV.B. to request a Swedish coverage record. Check item
IV.D. to request medical
evidence from SLAFU files if claimant has filed for U.S. disability benefits.
•
U.S. earnings record.
•
Medical evidence from OIO files if the Swedish claim is for disability benefits.
•
Any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit.
3. Swedish Claim
Include the following information in claim packages.
•
SSA-2960-U3-SW.
•
U.S. earnings record.
•
Medical evidence from OIO file if the Swedish claim is for disability benefits.
•
Any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit.
