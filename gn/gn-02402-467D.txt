D. Overview of IDD for Democratic Republic of Congo
IDD payments sent to Democratic Republic of Congo must be in Congolese franc (CDF)
accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or
the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that
serves Democratic Republic of Congo. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the
additional information.
