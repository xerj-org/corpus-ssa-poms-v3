B. Procedure for changes in hearing arrangements
1. Change in arrangements
The agency may change the time or place of the hearing if necessary, or in response
to a written request from a party to the hearing. Forward any such request to the
agency immediately, and by email, if necessary. If the request is made orally, help
prepare a letter to the agency stating reasons for the request. If advisable, send
the agency a memorandum stating whether the request seems justified.
If the agency grants the request, cancel the former arrangements and make new arrangements
as the agency directs. Notify the agency promptly of the new arrangements, so the
agency may issue Form HA-508 (Amended Notice of Hearing) to all parties immediately.
2. Interpreter availability
In some instances, an interpreter may not be available to the ALJ. The ALJ may then
contact the FO closest to the hearing site to see if an employee or other person is
available to act as an interpreter at the hearing. If so, the ALJ sends a memorandum
providing pertinent information and requesting the services of the interpreter to
the FO through the OHO Regional Office, with a copy to the Regional Commissioner.
The FO arranges for the employee's (or other person's) travel. (For information on
interpreters, see GN 03103.020D).
