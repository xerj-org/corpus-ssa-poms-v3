GN 03345.016 Privacy Act Appeal of Decision Not to Correct or Amend A Record - Policy
This section describes the Privacy Act appeal review when a subject individual requests
further consideration of SSA's initial decision to refuse a request for correction
or amendment of a record or information about him or her.
