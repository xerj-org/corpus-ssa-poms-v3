A. Adverse claims
The administrative law judge (ALJ) may notify a beneficiary or other persons who did
not request the hearing, to appear at the hearing and protect their interests, if
the ALJ’s decision adversely affected their rights. (For information on adverse claims,
see GN 01010.100 and GN 04020.020.)
