B. Policy-persons who can derive rights
The following persons can derive rights:
•
Family members: includes any persons who could be entitled to U.S. or Swiss benefits
on the record of a living worker.
•
Survivors: includes any persons who could be entitled to U.S. or Swiss benefits on
the record of a deceased worker.
