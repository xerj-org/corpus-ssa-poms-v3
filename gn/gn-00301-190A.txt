A. Obtaining foreign evidence through Office of International Operations (OIO)
If a claimant needs help in obtaining evidence from a foreign country, route the request
through OIO. OIO will make the necessary contact with the Foreign Service Post (FSP).
For more information on FSP, see GN 00307.005 Roles of FSPs—Process and GN 00307.010 Retention of Documents Issued Outside the
U.S.
