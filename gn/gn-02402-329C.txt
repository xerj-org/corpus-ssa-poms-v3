SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.329 - Coding Sint Maarten Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/12/2022 - Present | TN 288 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402329 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for Sint Maarten has a prefix of “70” at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “N” assigned for Sint Maarten.
