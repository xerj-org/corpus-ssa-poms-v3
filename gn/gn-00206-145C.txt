C. Notice for disapproved WD request
The disapproval of a WD is an appealable determination for the claimant or others
on the record whose entitlement the WD would affect. Include the reconsideration paragraph
in any notice of disapproval and explain the reason for the disapproval.
If the disapproval of the WD occurs after we disallowed the claim, use the E3705 notice
in NL 00703.705.
IMPORTANT:
If we deny a WD request for Retirement Insurance Benefits (RIB) because it is more
than 12 months after the current date of entitlement (DOEC), or the claimant has previously
withdrawn a RIB claim, prepare a manual notice using Exhibit E4019 (Withdrawal denial)
found in NL 00703.703.
