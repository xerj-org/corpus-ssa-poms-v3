GN 01760.105 Effective Date of the Agreement with Uruguay
The agreement with Uruguay became effective November 1, 2018. The United States and
Uruguay can use periods of U.S. and Uruguayan social security coverage earned in the
United States or Uruguay before November 1, 2018 to establish entitlement to U.S.
or Uruguayan benefits. However, the earliest possible date of entitlement to benefits
under the agreement is November 1, 2018.
