D. Overview of IDD for Aruba
IDD payments sent to Aruba must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Aruba. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
