SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01734.320 - How OIO Processes Luxembourg Agency Requests for Special Evidence
Effective Dates: 02/17/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201734320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Use the following procedure for responding to Luxembourg agency requests for special
evidence:
If the Luxembourg agency requests OIO to verify periods of...
then...
education
•
request the claimant to submit a statement signed by a school official that verifies
dates of attendance; and
•
forward the statement to the requesting agency with the liaison form SSA-2960-U3 LX.
child-rearing
•
request the claimant to submit original or certified photocopies of the birth certificates
of the children (if they are not already in OIO files); and
•
forward the documents to the requesting agency with the liaison form SSA-2960-U3 LX.
prior periods of
disability
•
check the MBR to determine if the claimant has been entitled to disability benefits;
and
•
complete the appropriate item in Section II.B. on the liaison form SSA-2960-U3-LX.
Refer to the instructions in GN 01734.220C. for completing the form.
