C. Procedure - Years with two or more periods of coverage
Determine the number of months of coverage as follows:
•
If there are no entries in the months column for any periods, consider each period
to be continuous with coverage for all of the months.
•
If the number of months is shown for only one period, accept that number as the total
for that year. (If the total exceeds the sum of the actual calendar months in all
the “from - to” periods for the year, refer to GN 01708.110B.5.
•
If months are shown for more than one period, ensure that they are shown for each
period and that the number is less than or equal to the actual number of months in
the corresponding period.
•
If periods overlap, apply the above rules but do not credit more than the actual calendar
months listed in the “from - to” periods for a year.
