A. Policy for developing claims from residents of Mexico
Except as shown in GN 00905.300C.1 in this section, the Federal Benefit Units (FBUs) in Mexico handle claims and inquiries
from residents of Mexico. The field offices (FOs) along the Mexico - U.S. border are not servicing offices for contacts or claimants in Mexico.
