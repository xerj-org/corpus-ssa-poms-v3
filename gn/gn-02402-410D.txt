D. Overview of IDD for Georgia
IDD payments sent to Georgia must be in Georgian Lari (GEL) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the federal benefits unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Georgia. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
