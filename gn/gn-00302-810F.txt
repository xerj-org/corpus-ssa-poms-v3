F. Complex Requests
In the few cases where the request for specific information is very complex, the parallel
FO in New Albany, Indiana may assist.
Use the SSA-562-U3 for further explanation of the required data. When the SSA-562-U3
is sent subsequent to receipt of correspondence from the Bureau of the Census, show
the Census Case Number on the SSA-562-U3.
