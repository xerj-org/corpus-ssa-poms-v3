GN 02408.004 General Information on Stop-Payments for Checks
SSA uses a stop-payment to request that Fiscal Service (Treasury) determine the status
of a check payment that was issued but not due. Non-entitlement occurs when checks
are issued after the death of the beneficiary/recipient or for reasons other than
death (see Stop Payment Non-Entitlement Due To Death Checks GN 02408.005 and Stop Payment Non-Entitlement for Reasons Other Than Death F-stops Checks GN 02408.020).
NOTE: Stop-payment actions only apply to checks that are still negotiable; i.e., within
12 months of the issue date of the check. For checks that are 13 months or older,
only a photocopy request (K or L-stop) or a status request (M-stop) processes. For
photocopy instructions, see GN 02406.140B.
