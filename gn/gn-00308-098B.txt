2. Cost of Copy:
Birth $13.00, Death $10.00, Marriage and Divorce $13.00. If using a credit card, the
cost is $30.25 with additional copies for $12.00.
3. Type of Payment:
Money order or personal check or credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express and
Discover).
4. Remarks:
Requests should be sent to the FO.
Fax requests to the Vital Records Section may be made on an emergency basis for birth
and death certificates. The fax number is (307) 635-4103.
Divorce and marriage records are available from the State office since May 1941.
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record?
Yes, for cases that meet the requirements in GN 00301.210, SSA Purchase of Evidence.
