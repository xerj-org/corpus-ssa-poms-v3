A. INTRODUCTION
The DI diary will be controlled on the Post Entitlement Operational Data Store (PE
ODS) system. This section tells you how to identify cases requiring representative
payee annual accounting and dedicated account monitoring, and what to do when the beneficiary continues to have
a dedicated account but there is no longer a representative payee.
