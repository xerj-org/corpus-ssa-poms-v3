Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201725220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
If an applicant brings one of the Dutch application forms to the FO:
•
provide any necessary assistance in completing the form,
•
photocopy and certify any documents (evidence) the Dutch agency has requested the
applicant to include with the Dutch claim, and
•
return the forms (and certified photocopies) to the applicant and advise him/her to
mail them directly to the Dutch agency that sent them.
REMINDER: Do not mail the Dutch application forms to OIO.
