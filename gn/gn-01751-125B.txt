B. Policy — Using Evidence Submitted in Claim for U.S. Benefits
Evidence establishing factors of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits may
be used by Poland to establish the same factor required for entitlement to Polish
benefits. However, it may be necessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence
to document a factor required for entitlement for Polish benefits if requested by
the Polish authorities.
