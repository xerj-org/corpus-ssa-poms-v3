C. Policy - Title XVI
The appeal following an initial waiver determination for a Title XVI overpayment is
always reconsideration. After we issue an initial waiver determination for a Title
XVI overpayment, the individual may appeal only by requesting reconsideration.  The
individual may request a hearing before an ALJ after they have requested reconsideration
and we have made a decision on the request for reconsideration.  This is the appeal
process for a Title XVI waiver determination regardless of whether the initial waiver
determination is an approval or denial, regardless of whether the individual attended
the personal conference, and regardless of whether the individual also requested reconsideration
of the overpayment determination.
