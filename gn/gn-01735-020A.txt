A. Introduction
Although Ireland pays many different categories of benefits, this section describes
only the benefits to which the agreement applies; retirement, old-age, disability
and survivors benefits. (A retirement benefit is paid a year earlier than an old-age
benefit, but requires that the worker retire.)
