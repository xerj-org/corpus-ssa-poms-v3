SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01718.220 - Completing the U.S.A./Sweden Transmittal/Request/Certification Form (SSA-2960-U3-SW)
Effective Dates: 09/05/2013 - Present | TN 2 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201718220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to use
The SSA-2960-U3-SW is used to:
•
transmit claims and related material to the Swedish agencies,
•
request information from the Swedish agencies, and
•
send information requested by the Swedish agencies.
