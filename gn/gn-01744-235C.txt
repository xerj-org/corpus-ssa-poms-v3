C. What is the description of Form AUS187?
1. Part 1
Part 1 contains information about the claimant.
2. Part 2
Part 2 contains information about the worker. All dates provided by Centrelink are
verified..
3. Part 3
Part 3 contains information about the spouse or former spouse. All dated provided
by Centrelink are verified.
4. Part 4
Part 4 contains information provided by Australia, including the protective filing
date (lodgement date).
5. Part 5
Part 5 contains a list of the type of information that Centrelink might request from
DIO along with the remark that the information is needed to process a claim under
the agreement. Centrelink needs all benefit rates from the date of entitlement but
not prior to the beginning date of the agreement, October 1, 2002.
6. Part 6
Part 6 contains remarks.
7. Part 7
Part 7 contains the signature and date.
