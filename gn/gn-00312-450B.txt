B. Procedure - Death Records
The following website provides information about requesting a Report of Death of an
American Citizen from the Department of State: http://travel.state.gov/family/family_issues/death/death_600.html
1. Office to Contact
U.S. Department of State
Vital Statistics Service
Passport Services
1111 19th St., N.W., Suite 510
Washington, D.C. 20522-1705
Response time can be 4 to 8 weeks.
2. Fee
$30.00 for the first copy and $20.00 for each additional copy
Payable to the “Department of State.”
