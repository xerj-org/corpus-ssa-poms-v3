A. Policy - Form J/USA 5 Certificate for Periods of Coverage
Form J/USA 5 is the only acceptable form for the certification of Japanese coverage.
Certifications in any other format should be forwarded to the Division of Training
and Program Support (DTPS) for a determination as to their acceptability.
