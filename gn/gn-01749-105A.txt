A. The current agreement and supplementary agreement
January 1, 2009 is the effective date of the agreement with the Czech Republic.
On May 1, 2016, a supplementary agreement entered into force. The supplementary agreement
exempts workers who, by virtue of the original agreement, are not responsible for
contribution to the Czech old-age, survivors, disability, and unemployment insurance
programs, from paying taxes that fund the Czech health insurance program.
