SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.315 - Disallowing a Title II Child Claim Based on Failure to Establish Relationship
Effective Dates: 06/21/2019 - Present | TN 41 (06-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Obtain Form SSA-2519 Child Relationship Statement
Before disallowing a claim for not meeting the relationship requirement (disallowance
code 32):
•
assist the claimant (as needed) to complete the SSA-2519 (see GN 00306.300); and
•
follow up on information listed on the SSA-2519 that may have a bearing on the child's
entitlement.
Fax the signed SSA-2519 into the Non-Disability Repository for Evidentiary Documents
(NDRed).
