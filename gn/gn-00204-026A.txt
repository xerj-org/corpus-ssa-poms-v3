SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.026 - When Not to Pursue an Open Application
Effective Dates: 07/31/2023 - Present | TN 132 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204026 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Listed below are instances where the issue of an open application is not developed.
These exceptions to pursuing the question of an open application are based on the
assumption that the prior claim was handled properly. The possibility of open applications
need not be pursued unless some payment would be due; e.g., work deductions do not
preclude payment, or Medicare entitlement may be involved.
