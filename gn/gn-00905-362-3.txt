SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.362 - Requests for Delayed Payment of Retroactive Benefits - Philippine Claims
Effective Dates: 04/17/2018 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905362 (fetched 2026-10-04)

3. BA
Pay the retroactive benefits by a one-check-only action, outside the MADCAP system.
Send this payment to the beneficiary in care of the USVA Regional Office, SSA Division,
APO San Francisco CA 96528.
Process the award to pay current benefits directly to the beneficiary at his residence
address. Show the beneficiary's residence address on the award certificate and send
it to the FBU by OF-41, for personal delivery of the beneficiary.
Prepare a special notice to the beneficiary showing only the current benefit amount
and send it to his home address.
Refer inquiries from the beneficiary about the special procedure to the FBU for reply.
