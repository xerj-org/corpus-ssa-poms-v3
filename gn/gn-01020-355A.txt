A. INTRODUCTION
The items on the form identify actions that must be taken by the PSCs.  In order to
alert the PSCs to the needed action, the FO must check all applicable items.  The
appropriate block identifying the pending action must be checked to facilitate proper
routing of the material in the PSCs through PCACS.
