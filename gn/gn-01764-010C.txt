SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01764.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Federal Benefits Unit in Oslo, Norway under the U.S. – Iceland Totalization Agreement
Effective Dates: 03/12/2025 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201764010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Iceland Claim
Refer all inquiries for Icelandic benefits received in the FBU in Oslo, Norway to
the Icelandic Liaison Agency SIA.
If an applicant contacts the Oslo, Norway FBU to file for Icelandic benefits, the
FBU will send the Iceland Liaison Agency SIA the following:
• Form SSA-e2960 USA-IS3 (U.S-Iceland Agreement on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification)
to certify the filing date for Icelandic benefits;
• U.S. coverage record;
• Supporting evidence for non-disability claims; and,
•
Available medical evidence if the claim is for disability benefits.
