Message codes
Explanation of message codes

Courtesy settlement check authorized from your
appropriation at your request. Treasury will credit
your account when refund is received.
02
Check cancelled by the Regional Finance Center
by deposit ticket #[999999999] dated [MM/DD/YY].

Settlement not recommended at this time pending
investigation.
2b
Settlement not recommended at this time pending
refund from the presenting bank.

Claim valid but settlement not appropriate, based on
your recertified or non-entitlement stop. Treasury
will credit your account when refund is received.

4a

05
Treasury is pursuing collection and your account
will be credited when refund is received.
5a
BFS is unable to recover the proceeds of the check due to the expiration of the 1
year statute of limitations for deceased payee cases. [REF 31 USC 3172 (A) (1)]
6a
Settlement not recommended and case closed.
The examiner of questioned documents is of the opinion
that the payee endorsed the check(s).
6b
Settlement not recommended and case closed.
Claimant never lived at or received mail at the check
address. If check negotiated by person of same name at
check address, there is no legal basis for recovery.
6c
Settlement not recommended and case closed.
Both co-payees of check must sign claim form. Return
claim form and copy of signatures from applicable IRS return.
Submit statement of division of check proceeds if applicable.
6d
Settlement not recommended and case closed. Determination
made that payee partially benefitted from check proceeds;
therefore, recovery is legally barred.
6e
Settlement not recommended and case closed. Statute of
limitations has expired.
6f
Settlement not recommended and case closed. Payee endorsed
the check prior to it being cashed; therefore recovery is
legally barred.
6g
Settlement not recommended and case closed: based on the
results of USSS investigation.
6h
Payee died [MM/DD/YY], not MM/DD/YY]; case closed.
6i
We have been advised that an adjustment was made by your office.  Case closed.
6j
We have been advised that the payee is still alive.  Case closed.
6k
Reclamation action has been abandoned since the check was negotiated by the agency.
See reverse of the attached check copy.  Case closed.
6l
Investigation determined that proceeds were deposited to the payee's account.
Case closed.
6m
Investigation determined that the payee authorized the negotiation of the check.
Case closed.
6n
The U.S. Secret Service determined that the payee was involved in the negotiation.
Case closed.
6o
Additional evidence supplied by the financial institution shows that the
payee was involved in the negotiation; or benefited from the
proceeds of the check.  Case closed.
7a
When check is drawn to alternate payees, endorsement by either
constitutes proper negotiation.  The document analyst is of the opinion the
endorsement on the check was written by ______________.  Case closed.
7b
When a check is drawn to alternate payees, endorsement by either constitutes proper
negotiation.  Provide specimen signature of alternate payee.
08
Treasury has exhausted all available means to obtain refund; no further action will
be taken.
8a
Forgery established; a settlement check is being issued to the payee.
8b
Forgery established; Treasury is crediting your account.
8c
Alleged forgery being investigated; settlement check has not been issued to the payee
pending investigation.
8d
Alleged forgery being investigated; more information to follow.
8e
Forgery established.  Treasury will not pursue reclamation action because the cost
of collection exceeds the check amount. Case closed.
