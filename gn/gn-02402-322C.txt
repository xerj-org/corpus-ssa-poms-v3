SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.322 - Coding Finland Bank Data for the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 07/19/2016 - Present | TN 263 (03-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402322 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processor bank in Finland will have a prefix of 71 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “G” assigned for Finland.
