SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02201.009 - Notification of a Title II Overpayment
Effective Dates: 09/30/2026 - Present | TN 86 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202201009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Individual has a representative
If the overpaid individual has a representative (e.g., an attorney or non-attorney)
to act on their behalf, send a copy of the overpayment notice to the representative.
For instructions on who is a representative, refer to, GN 03910.020.
For instructions on contacting a representative, refer to, GN 03910.050.
