D. Overview of IDD for Ghana
IDD payments sent to Ghana must be in Ghanaian Cedi (GHS) accounts at local FIs. The
beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Ghana. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
