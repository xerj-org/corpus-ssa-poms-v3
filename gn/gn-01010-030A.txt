A. Policy for identifying prior claims
The adjudicator is responsible for identifying any prior claims filed when adjudicating
an initial or subsequent claim. If the prior claim is archived, you must retrieve
the claim to review it and to determine if the information is material to the new
claim. This is necessary to:
•
Prevent multiple incorrect benefits or payments;
•
Detect unresolved overpayments for which the claimant is liable or uncollected Annual
Earnings Test (AET) penalties the claimant is responsible on this or any other Social
Security number (SSN);
•
Identify prior underpayments and determine eligibility for payment to new claimant(s);
•
Discover open applications based on an earlier filing;
•
Determine if the Res Judicata rule applies (e.g., technical denial code of 0S1 on
the MBR) per GN 03101.160 and GN 04040.010; and
•
Identify related claim files that may contain evidence previously submitted to SSA.
NOTE: Query the evidence (EVID) screen and the Evidence Portal (EP) for required evidence
before attempting to retrieve prior claims file (see GN 00301.286 and MSOM EVID 001.001).
