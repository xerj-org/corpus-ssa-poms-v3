B. Procedure correcting PUPS records with blank report date and blank receipt date
If the receipt date and the report date are missing on the alert; then enter the last day of the confinement
month as both the RECEIPT DATE and the REPORT DATE on the PU02 and PU16 screens. (You can extract this information from
the DATE OF CONFINEMENT entry on the PUPS record/alert).
EXAMPLE:
(Inmate Data Shown on PUPS Alert/Record)
CONFINEMENT DATE: 03131998
RECEIPT DATE: REPORT DATE:
(Correction to PUPS Record):
CONFINEMENT DATE: 03131998
RECEIPT DATE: 03311998 REPORT DATE: 03311998
