A. Determining eligibility
The Agreement provides that Romania will count a worker's U.S. periods of coverage,
where necessary, when determining eligibility for Romanian benefits if a worker does
not meet insured status based on Romanian coverage alone and the worker has at least
one year of Romanian coverage.
