E. Policy for OGC’s action initiating a hearing on the violation
If OGC does not receive an answer within the time allowed, or withdraw the charges,
OGC will ask the Office of Hearings Operations (OHO), Office of the Chief Administrative
Law Judge, to designate an administrative law judge to serve as the hearing officer
to adjudicate the case.
