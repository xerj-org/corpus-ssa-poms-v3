A. Definition of a non-entitled debtor
A Non-Entitled Debtor (NED) is a person or entity that owes a debt to the Social Security
Administration (SSA) but is not entitled to Social Security benefits or Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) payments. Generally, the NED does not have a Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR) or a Supplemental Security Record (SSR) of his or her own.
