SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed With a German Agency
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a claimant contacts a German agency to file a claim for U.S. benefits, the agency
routes forms D/USA 3 (Agreement Between the FRG and the U.S.A. on Social Security)
and D/USA 4 (Agreement Between the FRG and the U.S.A. on Social Security-Liaison-Certification
Form) to the FSP. The German coverage record will be included if it is readily available.
