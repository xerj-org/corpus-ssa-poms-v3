B. Procedure
The liaison Clerk should:
•
Complete Form SSA-2960-U2-NE Part III. The “Name”,
“Type of Benefit,” and “Awarded/Denied” blocks are self explanatory: If the claim was awarded, enter the date of entitlement
to the first month a full benefit is payable in MM/YY format.
•
Enter the CURRENT monthly benefit amount.
•
If the Claimant has been terminated, no entry can be made in current monthly benefit
amount, however, show the date of termination in MM/YY format.
