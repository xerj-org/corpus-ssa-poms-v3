D. Process – Controlling AWG Update Actions
When AWG is active and employers are making payments, a variety of debt- and debtor-related
events requiring AWG update can occur. For example, the debtor can request waiver,
thus requiring AWG to stop. To the maximum extent possible, the ECO system automatically
controls and monitors the need for AWG updates.
The ECO system reacts to overpayment-related codes on the MBR/ROAR, SSR and within
ECO to determine the appropriate update action. The system generates notices that
either tell the employer what action to take or follow-up with the employer about
a previous communication. The system generates notices intended to accomplish four
purposes: (1) Stop AWG; (2) Resume AWG; (3) Change AWG amount; and (4) Follow-up with
the employer.
In addition, the ECO system automatically sends a copy of the Stop AWG, Resume AWG
and the Change AWG amount notice which was sent to the employer.
See GN 02201.042D.3. for instructions on situations where the employer does not stop, change, or resume
garnishment payments.
1. Stop AWG
The Stop AWG notice is generic in that it just tells the employer to stop the garnishment.
Due to disclosure policy, SSA does not inform the employer why the cessation is necessary.
The ECO system generates a Stop AWG notice when the debtor:
•
Requests waiver;
•
Requests reconsideration of the overpayment determination;
•
Requests review of the debt;
•
Alleges hardship (and development of income/expenses is necessary);
•
Becomes subject to mandatory cross program recovery;
•
Files for bankruptcy or the debt is discharged in bankruptcy;
•
Pays debt in full;
•
Pays compromise amount;
•
Goes back into current pay;
•
Incurs a new debt that is in due process status;
•
Reduces the debt balance to less than $75;
•
Participates in a Ticket to Work Program;
•
Becomes subject to a higher priority garnishment order.
If AWG payments continue after the Stop AWG notice is sent, SSA sends another Stop
AWG notice to the employer. If payments continue after the second Stop AWG notice,
the ECO system creates an alert for the PC to contact the employer.
2. Resume AWG
Due to disclosure policy, the Resume AWG notice is also generic. It tells the employer
to resume garnishing the wages of an employee for whom SSA previously sent a Stop
AWG notice. The ECO system automatically recognizes when all conditions are clear
for the resumption of garnishment. The system then generates a Resume AWG notice.
The ECO system monitors the case for subsequent garnishment payments after the Resume
AWG notice is sent. If none are received, the system sends follow-up notices to the
employer. If non-payment continues after the follow-up notices, the system generates
an alert for the PC to contact the employer. (See GN 02201.042D.3. for instructions on contacting the employer.)
NOTE:
SSA will send a new AWG Employer Package when sending the Resume AWG notice to the
employer.
3. Change AWG Amount
SSA changes the AWG amount when the debtor demonstrates hardship and when the debtor
asks for a higher AWG amount. In both cases, the ECO system automatically recognizes
the need for the Change AWG Amount notice based on inputs to DMS screens.
The ECO system will send a follow up notice to the employer when the amount is changed
after garnishment has started and garnishment payments are received for more than
the agreed upon amount. If a second payment is received for more than the amount,
an alert will be generated to the PC.
See SM 00610.080B.2. and SM 01311.840B.2. for instructions on processing AWG alerts when the garnishment amount is greater
than the agreed upon garnishment amount.
4. Follow-Up Notice to Employer
The ECO system automatically generates follow-up notices to the employer when the
employer fails to resume AWG or change the AWG amount or stops sending garnishment
payments with no explanation.
If the employer does not respond to the AWG Employer Package, the system generates
a notice telling the employer we have not received a response. The follow-up notice
will contain another copy of the employer package.
