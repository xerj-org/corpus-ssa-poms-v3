A. Process — Puerto Rico vital records
You can find information about requesting vital records from Puerto Rico (PR) at the
National Center for Health
Statistics or Departamento De Salud for information in Spanish.
Field Offices (FOs) in PR do not have direct access to the vital statistics database
and are not able to verify or certify vital records events that occurred in their
service areas.
Where applicable, include claimant's maternal and paternal surnames (e.g., Gonzalez
Gomez) on all requests for vital records.
NOTE: For enumeration and claims purposes, SSA will only accept birth certificates
from Puerto Rico with an issue date of July 1, 2010 or later. For acceptance of Puerto
Rico birth certificates, see GN 00301.065.
