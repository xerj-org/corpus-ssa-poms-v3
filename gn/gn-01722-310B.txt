SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.310 - Processing Spanish Agency Requests for Medical Evidence and Examinations
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Medical Evidence
When a Spanish agency requests medical evidence, send the evidence from OIO files
as described in GN 01704.810C.
2. Medical Examinations
Follow these steps when responding to a Spanish agency request for a medical examination.
Step
Action
1
Request a foreign disability examiner to schedule an examination, or forward “pertinent medical evidence from files” as described in GN 01704.810
2
Stamp the first and last page of each medical record being sent to Spain with “true and exact copy.” Sign and date the blocks on the stamp.
3
Request reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for the Spanish
agency using form SSA-1278-U3-SP. Refer to the instructions in GN 01704.810B. for completing the form.
