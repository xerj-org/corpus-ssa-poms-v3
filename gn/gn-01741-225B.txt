B. Applicant requests status of a Korean claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Korean benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded a claim to the Korean agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show DIO forwarded the claim to Korea
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Korea, advise the applicant to
write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Korea
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Korean agency, advise the applicant to
contact the Korean agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
National Pension Service of
Korea
NOTE::
DIO is unable to determine the status of Korean claims after a technician forwards
the claim to Korea. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims,
see GN 01702.520.
