Effective Dates: 12/01/1995 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201703600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Reference
To determine if reported work is covered by the United States and therefore subject
to the annual earnings test, or not covered by the United States and therefore subject
to the foreign work test, consult the instructions in RS 02605.030.
For a detailed explanation of the coverage provisions of a specific agreement, refer
to RS 02001.000.
