SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01706.020 - Crediting Italian periods of coverage
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | TN 2 (07-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201706020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Method of crediting coverage
Italian periods of coverage are credited to the U.S. earnings record using the following
criteria:
1.
The number of weeks of coverage reported for each “From-To” period is divided by 13 (the number of weeks in a calendar quarter) to determine
the number of QCs which may be credited in that period based on Italian coverage.
Any remainder is considered one QC.
2.
The Italian QCs derived in this manner are credited starting with the quarter in which
the period begins and continuing consecutively until all QCs are credited. However,
no QC is credited after the quarter in which the Italian period ends and no Italian
QC is credited in any calendar quarter where a QC is already credited based on U.S.
coverage.
3.
When a U.S. QC is credited within a “From-To” period, Italian QCs are credited up to that quarter and begin again in the next available
quarter. U.S. QCs between the beginning and ending dates of a period of Italian coverage
may preclude the crediting of any or all Italian QCs during the period.
4.
Where, after 1977, there are both U.S. and Italian QCs to be credited in the same
year, the U.S. QCs are assigned using the flexible crediting provision of the 1977
amendments so as to permit the crediting of the maximum number of Italian QCs.
5.
In cases where DIB insured status or currently insured status cannot be met by using
the above method of crediting QCs, the Italian QCs are assigned using the alternate
method described in GN
01706.030.
6.
When insured status cannot be met by using the above methods of crediting QCs, the
case must be reviewed according to the instructions in GN 01706.035.
