D. Voluntary credit coverage
Under the
Agreement,
Portugal will:
•
Give U.S. nationals who reside in Portugal the right to make voluntary contributions
to the Portuguese system on the same basis as Portuguese nationals; and
•
Permit both U.S. and Portuguese nationals to have their prior U.S. coverage counted,
if necessary, to establish the right to make voluntary contributions.
NOTE: U.S. nationals who reside outside of Portugal (e.g., in the United States) are not
eligible to make voluntary contributions to the Portuguese system.
