SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01010.040 - Underpayments (U/P) and Overpayments (O/P) on Prior Claims
Effective Dates: 08/29/2014 - Present | TN 96 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Processing claims with a prior O/P
When processing a new claim involving a prior O/P, you must always resolve the O/P
issue during the interview and before the adjudication. Discuss the prior O/P amount
and recovery options with the claimant; see GN 02210.000 for Overpayment Recovery policy.
1.
If a prior title II O/P exist and you determine payment liability, do not process
the new approved claim through EC. Use the A101 or EF101 process if:
•
The O/P is on the same or a related (cross-reference) SSN and the claimant is the
overpaid beneficiary or was the representative payee for the overpaid beneficiary
and is liable for repayment;
•
The O/P is on the same SSN, the current claimant is not the overpaid person but was
previously entitled on that SSN and is due payment on the current claim for the month
following the current month or any earlier month; or
•
The overpaid beneficiary died and a Lump Sum Death Payment (LSDP) is due. See GN 02210.001 for more information about Adjustments of Overpayments.
2.
Thoroughly explain that by default we will withhold 10% of their monthly benefit amount
to recover the overpayment, unless another recovery rate is agreed to.
3.
If the claimant agrees to a monthly amount deduction from the current payment to repay
the prior O/P, do the following:
•
Complete and have the claimant sign a SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or other person);
and
•
Post the BCRN screen of the A101 or EF101 with O/P information and rate of withholding.
4.
If the claimant does not agree with the O/P or the recovery exceeds 60 months, do
the following:
•
Explain the options of filing an appeal (e.g. SSA 561), or a request for waiver (SSA-632-BK),
or request for a lower withholding (SSA-634) GN 02210.030; and
•
Annotate the A101 or EF101 BCRN screen with the remark “PRIOR O/P - INITIATE RECOVERY
FROM BIC__”.
