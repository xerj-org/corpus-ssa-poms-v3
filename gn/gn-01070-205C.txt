Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Prior information was destroyed
Assume the folder was destroyed if:
a.
All entitlements on the SSN were terminated 5 years ago (10 years ago in DIB claims)
and no overpayment was pending; or
b.
The prior claim was a lump sum, disallowance, or a withdrawal, processed 10 or more
years ago; or
c.
The MBR shows “NIF”.
