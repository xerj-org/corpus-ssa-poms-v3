E. Effect of WD requests when Medicare benefits are involved
If the claimant requests to WD all benefits (i.e., Retirement Survivor’s Insurance
(RSI) and Medicare), they must also repay Hospital Insurance (HI) expenses and Supplementary
Medical Insurance (SMI) premiums.
For guidance on processing withdrawals of Medicare benefits, see GN 00206.020.
For policy and procedures when the claim involves HI only (no cash benefits), see
withdrawal considerations in HI 00801.034.
For information on withdrawing from Premium-HI for the working disabled, see HI 00801.174.
For information on withdrawal of Medicare based on ESRD, see HI 00801.197.
