SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.260 - How to Protest Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Decisions
Effective Dates: 07/23/2026 - Present | TN 55 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103260 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure - notices
Do not send a notice of referral or a copy of the protest memo to the claimant/representative
in any of the cases referred under these procedures, regardless of whether the referral
is timely for own motion review or if reopening may be utilized. In the protest cases,
it is preferable for the AC staff to send the claimant's first notice that own motion
review or reopening is being taken. The protest memo will be included by the AC when
they notify the claimant they are taking action. In payment cases, follow GN 03103.250C. notice procedures without mentioning the referral.
