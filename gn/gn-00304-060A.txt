A. Policy for forfeiture of entitlement
1. Adult
An adult convicted of the felonious and intentional homicide of another person cannot
be entitled to monthly benefits or the Lump Sum Death Payment (LSDP) on the earnings
record of that person, or receive any underpayment due the deceased person.
Consider the convicted person as nonexistent in determining the entitlement of other
survivors, or the amount of their benefits.
2. Juvenile
Beginning 03/1982, the following applies to survivors subject to the juvenile justice
system:
If...
Then...
Found by a court of competent jurisdiction to have intentionally caused the Number
Holder's (NH) death by an act which, if committed by an adult, would be considered
a felony or an act in the nature of felony
•
We cannot pay survivor's benefits on the worker's earnings record beginning 03/1982,
or the month of the NH's death, whichever is later.
•
We cannot pay underpayments or LSDP based on the NH's earnings record.
The conviction is post-adjudicative
Terminate benefits retroactively to 02/1982, or the date of the NH's death, whichever
is later.
NOTE: This applies even if the NH's death or the juvenile justice proceedings occurred
prior to March 30, 1982.
