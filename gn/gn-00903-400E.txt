SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00903.400 - Complaints of Discrimination Against SSA and/or SSA Employees by Members of the Public
Effective Dates: 02/26/2025 - Present | TN 22 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200903400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Where must a complaint be filed
Complaints received in any component other than OCREO must be sent immediately to
Social Security Administration, Deputy Commissioner for Civil Rights and Equal Opportunity,
Center for Compliance Management (CCM), with all supporting documentation, via email
to Civil.Rights.Program.Complaint.Intake@ssa.gov. “Immediately” means no later than the next business day.
