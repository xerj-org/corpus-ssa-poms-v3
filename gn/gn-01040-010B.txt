B. Kinds Of Diary Codes
Here are the diary codes.
DIARY CODE
DESCRIPTION
11
Wages
​
Requests for information outlined in RS 01401.000 including quarterly breakdown, employer's failure to report properly, and discrepancies
between alleged or reported amounts.
12
Coverage
​
Questions arising in employment or deduction cases, relating to wages, and rules discussed
in RS 02101.000, and RS 01901.000 and RS 01505.000. (For State and local Statute of Limitations, use Code 17.)
13
Employment History
​
Questions relating to a NH's allegations and employment history.
14
Self-Employment
​
All SE questions relative to trade or business, net earnings exclusions and computations
of net earnings (except code 86 cases). See RS01801.001 - RS 01804.000
15
Railroad Compensation
​
For requests pertaining to railroad compensation, see RS 01601.000.
16
Veterans Provisions
​
For veterans credits or deemed insured status, see RS 01702.000 ff.
17
Statute of Limitations
​
(Including State and local; RS 02201.001 and RS 01505.000.
18
Computations and Recomputations
​
For questions relating to computations and recomputations, see RS 00601.001 ff.
21
Completion of Application Forms
​
Requests as a result of improper or ambiguous completion or omissions from application
forms not covered under specific development codes.
22
Proper Address
​
Requests to obtain proper address for NH or auxiliaries.
23
Auxiliary, Survivor or LSDP Application
​
Requests for development of potential auxiliary, survivor or LSDP claims. (A code
23 diary must be entered if an application for a delayed claimant is needed. If the
application has been taken, enter the diary code for the outstanding evidence, e.g.,
31 - proof of relationship, 33 - dependency and support, 51 - proof of age, etc.)
24
Any Development Regarding Month of Election
25
Suspension, Deduction or Termination of Benefits
​
Requests relating to the suspension or termination of benefits or deduction items.
26
Deportation
​
Requests relating to payment of benefits because of deportation.
27
Filing Dates
​
Requests about the use of a written document other than a formal application to establish
a filing date
31
Relationship
​
Requests about relationship of any auxiliary to the NH include children, wife, widow,
husband, etc.
32
Child in Care
​
Requests about whether a wife under retirement age, a surviving father, widow or former
divorced wife has in care a child of the NH.
33
Dependency and Support
​
Requests about whether a wife under retirement age, a surviving father, widow or former
divorced wife has in care a child of the NH.
34
Living in Same Household
​
Development of whether a widow or widower was living in the same household with NH
to determine whether LS is payable.
41
HIB-SMIB Eligibility
​
Any request relating to the hospital insurance benefits provisions, including supplementary
medical insurance.
42
Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB)
​
Any request relating to the receipt of WC/PDB.
45
School Attendance
​
Development of whether child is in full-time attendance at an educational institution.
51
Date of Birth
​
Requests regarding the date of birth of any claimant.
52
Proof of Death
​
All requests regarding proof of and date of death.
71
Selection of Payee
​
Requests regarding the selection of representative payee
72
Use of Benefits
​
Requests regarding the use of benefits and personal contact diaries.
78
Other
​
Development of any cases not included in any of the above series.
81
Referrals to the Central Office, ODO, INTPSC or a PSC
82
Requests to Office of Central Records Operations
84
Requests to Another Government Agency
85
Diary cases not covered by any other code
86
Diary Awaiting Availability of SE Lag Period Postings
87
Diary Pending First Month of Entitlement
88
Diary Awaiting Lag Period Wage Postings
91
Development in Reconsideration Cases Entered on the Reconsideration and Appeals Report
92
Other Cases Diaried by Reconsideration Reviewers
98
Attorney Fee Petition - Initial/Reconsideration
99
Attorney Fee Petition - Administrative Law Judge/Appeals Council, see GN 03930.045B.
