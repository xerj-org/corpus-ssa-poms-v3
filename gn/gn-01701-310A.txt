SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.310 - Foreign Pensions Based on a Totalization Agreement With the United States – Effect on the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP)
Effective Dates: 03/24/2026 - Present | TN 19 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Beginning January 1995, a foreign pension that is based on a Totalization agreement
with the United States will not cause the WEP to apply in the computation of a non-Totalization
U.S. benefit. This section provides instructions for determining whether a foreign
pension is based on a Totalization agreement with the United States.
