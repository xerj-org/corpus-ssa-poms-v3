C. PROCEDURE-PART I
REFERENCE: Follow GN 00605.061C. to complete identifying data preceding the interview questions.
1. Interview the Payee
While interviewing the payee:
a.
Obtain and record the payee's response to every question.
b.
Note in Remarks (item 8) or in appropriate response blocks what records, if any, the
payee used to support responses.
c.
Obtain the payee's signature following item 8.
2. Determine the Amount of Conserved Funds
Compute the amount of conserved funds as instructed in Part I, item 5 of the SSA-624-F5.
