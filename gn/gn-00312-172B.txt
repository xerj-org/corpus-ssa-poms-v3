B. Procedure – Divorce Records
1. Office to Contact
Vara Civil
Ministerio do Justica
Caixa Postal 17
Bissau, Guinea-Bissau
2. Fee
No fee.
NOTE: Although there is no charge for a divorce record, no record will be issued until the
fees have been paid for the judicial proceedings resulting in the divorce. These can
range from 20,000 to 60,000 G-B pesos.
