C. Federal Court Previously Decided Fugitive Felon Status Issue
If you receive information that an individual’s record shows a final federal court
disposition about payments due to fugitive felon status, take no action on that case
under the Martinez settlement. Refer these cases to The Office of General Counsel
(OGC). If OGC confirms a final federal court disposition, remove the special message
on the MBR. For the special message, see GN 02613.860C.1.a.
