A. The Manila Federal Benefits Unit (FBU)
The Manila FBU takes claims from individuals in their service area who wish to file
for U.S. and/or Australian benefits under the Australian agreement and also assists
in processing certain types of claims filed with Centrelink.
When taking claims under the agreement, the Manila FBU should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
