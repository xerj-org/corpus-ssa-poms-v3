SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.905 - Belgian Requests for Income Statements
Effective Dates: 09/26/1995 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704905 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Belgian benefits are subject to reduction if certain family income levels are exceeded.
Family income includes income from the spouse's work and income from other social
benefits. Therefore, Belgium may require a claimant and spouse to sign a declaration
about income. The United States will obtain an income statement from the claimant
and spouse only upon specific request from Belgium. The Belgian agencies will provide
English language forms with their requests.
