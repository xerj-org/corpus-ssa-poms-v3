SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00605.080 - Combining The Accounting and SSI Redetermination Interviews
Effective Dates: 10/29/2001 - Present | TN 16 (10-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. POLICY
When the FO is in the process of conducting an SSA-623, SSA-6230 or SSA-624-F5 development
interview and a SSI redetermination is pending, the FO can schedule the interviews simultaneously
provided:
•
The forms and information are not combined.
•
The SSA-623, SSA-6230 or SSA-624-F5 is completed and processed according to the accounting
instructions in this subchapter.
•
The SSA-623, SSA-6230 and SSA-624-F5 are not subject to any redetermination constraints;
e.g., profiling (See SI 02305.014).
