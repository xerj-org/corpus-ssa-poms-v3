SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.110 - Claims from RSDI Beneficiaries
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Generally, where an RSDI beneficiary files for totalization benefits from the United
States, the claim is disallowed unless the claim was filed under the Italian agreement
(see B below). This is because all other agreements provide that the United States
will not consider foreign work if the worker is insured based on U.S. work alone with
rare exceptions.
