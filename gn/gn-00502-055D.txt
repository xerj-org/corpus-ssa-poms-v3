D. Identifying beneficiaries in need of a payee
Field Offices should establish and maintain relationships with community social service
organizations to help identify beneficiaries who may need assistance in managing benefits.
SSA does not have routine touch points (e.g., CDRs) with beneficiaries who receive
retirement and survivors benefits. Therefore, we may not receive information through
our normal business processes about beneficiaries in this population who undergo changes
in their ability to manage benefits over time. FO technicians should be vigilant and
proactively address capability issues in all of our beneficiary programs; this helps
prevent instances of misuse and fraud. For information on SSA’s referrals to other
agencies, see Types of Referral Services SSA Provides in GN 00903.100. Referrals specific to the elderly population may include Area Agencies on Aging
or Long Term Care Ombudsmen.
