[Attorney at Law]
Street Address
City/State/ZIP Code
We are writing about your letter dated January 25, 1995. You asked us to review the
decision we made on the fee agreement between you and Harry Henderson.
What Will Happen
Someone who did not make the decision will review it and make a new one. We will give
you that decision in writing. If we approve the fee agreement, we will tell you the
most you can charge under the agreement for your work on the Social Security claim.
If we do not approve the fee agreement and you want to charge a fee, you must ask
us in writing to approve the fee amount.
Because the claimant and the person who disapproved the fee agreement can comment
on your request for administrative review, we are sending them copies. They also can
provide more information to the person reviewing your request.
If we need more information, we will contact you. We will give you our decision in
writing. We will also send copies of our letter to Harry Henderson and James Kelly
(claims representative).

AR-3
Acknowledgment Letter - Request for Review - Fee Resulting From Agreement - To Requester
Bruce Smith
Street Address
City/State/ZIP Code
We are writing about your letter dated January 25, 1995. You asked us to review the
fee amount we said your representative, Marvin Levy, can charge for work under your
fee agreement.
What Will Happen
Someone who did not decide the fee amount will review the decision and make a new
one. Based on that review we will raise, lower, or not change the fee amount your
representative can charge.
If we lower the fee amount, the lower fee is the most your representative can charge
under the fee agreement for work on your Social Security claim. If we raise the fee
amount, your representative can charge you the higher amount.
Because Marvin Levy, Susy Smith, Joey Smith, and James Kelly (claims representative)
can comment on your request for administrative review, we are sending them a copy
of your request. They also can provide more information to the person reviewing your
request.
If we need more information we will contact you. We will send you and the other people
listed above, a letter telling what we decide.
If You Have Any Question
See Referral Paragraphs in Model Acknowledgment Letter AR-1

AR-6
Acknowledgment Letter - Request for Review - Fee Resulting From Agreement - Good
Cause Development
Jimmy Johnson
Street Address
City/State/ZIP Code
We are writing about your letter dated January 1, 1995. You asked us to review the
fee amount we said your representative, Charles Yeager, can charge for work under
your fee agreement.
What You Should Do
In our letter dated November 15, 1995, we told you to write to us within 15 days of
the day you got the letter if you wanted a review. We assume you got our letter within
5 days after the date on it, unless you show us that you did not get it within the
5-day period.
You did not write us within 15 days, so we will not review our decision unless we
decide you had good reason for not asking for review on time. If you think you have
a good reason, write us within 15 days from the day you get this letter. Tell us why you did not ask for the review on time. Also, give us any information
that might support what you say.
If You Have Any Questions
See Referral Paragraphs in Model Acknowledgment Letter AR-1
