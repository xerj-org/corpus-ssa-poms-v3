Effective Dates: 11/09/2023 - Present | TN 15 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203305005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Consent by competent adults
Generally, the individual who is the subject of the record and a competent adult may
give us written consent to disclose information about themselves to a third party.
Written consent can be provided on paper, so long as it contains the individual’s
valid signature and meets the consent requirements listed in GN 03305.003. See GN 03305.001C for more information. We will only accept electronic signatures
for the purpose of Privacy Act consents on the Form SSA-3288-OP1 submitted through
our website, www.ssa.gov/privacy.
