SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01740.310 - Chilean Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201740310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following procedure to respond to Chilean agency requests for medical examinations:
•
Schedule an examination or forward “pertinent medical evidence” from SSA files.
Pertinent medical evidence is evidence that was obtained by SSA within one year of the filing date shown on
the foreign agency request or within one year of the date for which the Chilean agency
has specifically requested medical evidence.
SSA files include claims folders in DIO, a PSC, a FO, OCO's Megasite or the caves in Kansas
City. However, it does not include folders in ODAR.
•
Request reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for Chile using
Form SSA-1278-CHI (USA/CHI 9). Prepare three copies of this form.
NOTE: This is an electronic form and can be located by selecting “TOTALIZATION FORMS” from
the Table of Contents in the Totalization Resource Kit. The form is completed on the
screen.
