SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.020 - Reimbursement Process Due to Alleged Fraud and Other Unauthorized Redirection of Reoccurring Payments
Effective Dates: 08/25/2025 - Present | TN 308 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Instructions for Handling Reports of Incorrect SSA-1099 Forms (Social Security Benefit
Statement) Due to Unauthorized Redirected Payments Initiated Through Auto-Enrollments
(ENR)
1.
If a beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee alleges the inflation of payments
on the SSA-1099, because of nonreceipt of one or more payments due to alleged fraud,
then issue a corrected benefit statement.
2.
If the beneficiary did not receive payments during the related tax year in question,
then the PC will follow the process to prepare a manual SSA-1099, showing $0.00 in
all boxes. (For the processing of the manual SSA-1099, see SM
00630.070.)
3.
If the real beneficiary filed for cash benefits during the related tax year, the SSA-1099
will reflect the amount of benefits that the real beneficiary received.
NOTE:
The FO must contact the PC via MDW or RQFA regarding requests for a manual SSA-1099.
Direct all program–related and technical questions to your RO support staff or PSC
OA staff. RO support staff or PSC OA staff may refer questions or problems to their
CO contacts.
