C. Procedure - Required applications
Use the following information to determine which applications must be completed when
taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits and/or French benefits under the agreement
with France.
1. U.S. Totalization Benefits Only
a. Retirement or Disability Claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the appropriate retirement or disability
benefit MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
b. Survivors Claims
In survivors claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and Part I of the paper application Form
SSA-2490-BK.
2. French Benefits Only (Regular or Totalization)
Claims for French benefits only are MCS exclusions. Complete Parts I and II of paper application Form SSA-2490-BK.
3. French Benefits (Regular or Totalization) and U.S. (Regular or Totalization Benefits
a. Retirement or Disability Claims
In retirement or disability claims, complete the appropriate retirement or disability
benefit MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen, (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and the SSA-2490 screens (see MSOM MCS 007.001).
b. Survivors Claims
In survivors claims, complete the appropriate MCS application screens, the TOTALIZATION
(TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 009.011), and Parts I and II of the paper application Form SSA-2490-BK.
