SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02409.010 - Effect of Waiver of Benefits on Other Eligibility
Effective Dates: 10/17/2011 - Present | TN 4 (10-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202409010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Verification of contact with another agency
Unless the beneficiary requires SSA assistance, ask the beneficiary to verify that
the agency administering the other benefit recognizes the validity of the waiver.
Obtain a signed statement that the contacted agency understands the effects of this
action over the beneficiary’s signature.
If the beneficiary insists on waiving the payment, and this appears to be disadvantageous,
refer the signed request to the ARC, MOS, for review.
