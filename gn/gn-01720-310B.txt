B. Process
The following process is used for obtaining medical examinations for the U.K. (See
GN 01704.810 for general information on processing requests for medical examinations.)
•
U.K. agency requests for medical examinations are often made in a letter and may or
may not be accompanied by a liaison form.
•
SSA schedules an examination or forwards "pertinent medical evidence" from files as
described in GN 01704.810.
•
SSA requests reimbursement for the cost of medical examinations obtained for the U.K.
using form
SSA-1278-U3-UK.
