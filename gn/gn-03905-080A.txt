Effective Dates: 12/10/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Processing centers and the Office of Hearings Operations, and the Office of Appellate
Operations may use this form, primarily in the fee petition process, to explain and
document the rationale for authorizing a reasonable fee in the specified amount.
