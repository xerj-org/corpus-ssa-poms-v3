D. Overview of IDD for Tonga
IDD payments sent to Tonga must be in Tongan pa’anga (TOP) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Tonga. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
