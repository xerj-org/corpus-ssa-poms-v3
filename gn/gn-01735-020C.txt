C. Policy – Dependents of retired or disabled workers
1. Spouse
There is no provision for benefits paid to spouses. However, a spouse’s supplement
may be paid to a retired or disabled worker depending on the income of the spouse.
2. Divorced Spouse
There is no provision for divorced spouses’ benefits.
3. Children
There is no provision for benefits paid to children. However, a child’s supplement
may be paid to a retired or disabled worker for each child under age 18 (age 21 if
in school full time).
