A. Background
The Paperless Processing Center System (PPC) is a document imaging and management
system that SSA employs in the payment centers (PCs) to manage the work.
This section provides guidance for the retention and subsequent disposition of program-related
documents once an action has been successfully imaged; i.e., it is clearly readable.
The following retention policy applies only to paper forms (and other paper documents)
that are scanned into the paperless imaging equipment in the PSCs.
