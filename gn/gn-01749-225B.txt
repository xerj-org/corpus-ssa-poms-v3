SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01749.225 - Requests for Status of Applications for Czech Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 4 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201749225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Czech claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of the
applicant's claim for Czech benefits and states that the applicant has not received
notification that DIO forwarded the claim to the Czech agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Czech Republic
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to the Czech Republic, advise the
applicant to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Czech Republic
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Czech Republic, advise the applicant
to contact the Czech agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Ministerstvo práce a sociálních
věcí (Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs)
•
Česká správa sociálního
zabezpečení (The Czech Social Security Administration)
DIO is unable to determine the status a Czech claim after a technician forwards the
claim to the Czech Republic. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
