Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070505 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Guidelines for PC to PC telephone development
Contact with another PC should only be made if the following conditions are met:
1.
Based on the PCACS or Evidence Portal (EP) query, the claims folder is physically
located in the other PC or the other PC has a pending action in question; and
2.
One of the “high priority” criteria listed below is met:
•
award and disallowance situations;
•
start and stop payment actions;
•
appeal situations;
•
congressional inquiries; or
•
public relations problems; and
3.
All systems records for the related claim lack information which is needed to take
a current claims or post-adjudicative action; and
4.
The adjudicator believes that the examination of the related folder or action will:
•
Permit the completion of the current claims action; or
•
Substantially reduce efforts to obtain the needed evidence; or
•
Avoid the need to redevelop evidence involving sensitive issues or public relations
concerns.
Note: Follow the instructions in GN 01070.320 if information is needed from a folder that
is housed in the SSA National Records Center (SSANRC) or in the Office of Central
Operations (OCO) Megasite in Baltimore, Maryland.
