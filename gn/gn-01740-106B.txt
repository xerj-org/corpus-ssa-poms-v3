B. Description of required entries
•
The correct worker's name must be shown in Part 1 of Form CHI/USA 5. (Completion of
the other items in Part I is not required.)
•
A “FROM” – “TO” entry must be shown for each period in which there is an entry in
the “months” column unless the entry is for the special noncontributory coverage described
above.
•
A “months” entry must be shown for each “FROM” – “TO” period and the number of months
must be equal to or less than the number of months in the corresponding “FROM” – “TO”
period. (Entries should not be shown for “days” and “years.”)
•
The Form CHI/USA 5 must be properly certified, including the signature and official
stamp.
•
If any of the required information is missing, route the case to CPSI for evaluation.
