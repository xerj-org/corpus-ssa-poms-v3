C. Procedure — DIO requests for FBU assistance
Make initial and followup requests to FBUs by administrative message, SSA-1320-U4
(Request for Services), telefax or telephone.
When preparing written messages, block print, using black ink for telefax transmission,
or type the form. All copies must be clear and legible.
