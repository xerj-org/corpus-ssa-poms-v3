A. Introduction
There are two liaison agencies in Spain that are responsible for implementing the
Spanish agreement. The Instituto Social de la Marina (ISM) has jurisdiction of the
earnings records and claims of Maritime Workers. The Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad
Social (INSS) has jurisdiction of claims filed by all other workers.
The INSS and ISM both have central offices in Madrid and local offices in the Spanish
provinces.
