C. Documentation requirements
The adjudicator is responsible for documenting the claim with all the required information
when adjudicating a claim involving a precedent of fact or SSR. Formal determinations
are required to cite authority in the Act or regulations along with details of facts
that lead to the conclusion. Refer to GN 01010.365 for formal determinations instructions.
