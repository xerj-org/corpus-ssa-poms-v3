SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.800 - Foreign Agency Requests for Evidence and Information
Effective Dates: 12/22/1989 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704800 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Foreign agencies may request a wide variety of information, and every effort should
be made to comply with their requests. If the requested information is not in file
or obtainable from a current MBR, obtain the requested evidence and information in
the most efficient way possible.
