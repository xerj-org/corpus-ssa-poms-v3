2. Pending OAO AC appeal case, multiple appeal issues
If the pending request for review before the AC includes issues other than those related
to a PPV suspension or overpayment, the case remains with the AC, even if the beneficiary
is due Clark relief.
Provide the AC with information on the Clark relief due the beneficiary. For instructions on emailing the Clark Court Order relief information to the AC, see GN 02615.130D.1. in this section. When the AC takes an action on all appeal issues, process the AC
action. For relief processing instructions, see GN 02615.120.
3. HA-505 Transmittal by OHO for Clark Court Order Cases
The OAO will use a transmittal form to return the case to the FO or PC after OHO makes
an appeal decision and Clark relief applies to the case. The OAO Clark Court Order transmittal form will look similar to the exhibit below.
