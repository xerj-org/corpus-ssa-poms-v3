SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03315.025 - Disclosure to Law Enforcement Entities Involving Deceased or Missing Individuals
Effective Dates: 01/06/2017 - Present | TN 3 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203315025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Request for information involving missing individuals
We may disclose information from a missing individual’s Social Security record when
the purpose of the request meets the requirements for law enforcement-related disclosures.
We treat the record as the person is living and apply the same requirements for permissible
disclosures for law enforcement-related purposes. For more information concerning
valid law enforcement requests and the requirements for disclosure, see GN 03312.000.
