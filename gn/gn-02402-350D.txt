D. Overview of IDD for Slovenia
IDD payments sent to Slovenia must be in Euro (EUR) accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Slovenia. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for additional information.
