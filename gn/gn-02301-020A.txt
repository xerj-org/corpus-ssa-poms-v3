A. How we discover underpayments
SSA’s systems detect most underpayments due a living beneficiary. If the system cannot
pay an underpayment automatically, the office that detects the underpayment is responsible
for issuing the payment and notice. For more information on Title II underpayment
notices, see GN 02301.010.
