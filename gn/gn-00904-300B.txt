SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.300 - SSI applications for individuals who move to Puerto Rico or the U.S. Virgin islands
Effective Dates: 11/14/2008 - Present | TN 10 (05-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
For claims in which the SSI applicant moves to Puerto Rico or the U.S. Virgin Islands,
the mainland SSA FO with jurisdiction of the applicant/recipient's last known address
in the U.S. prior to their move to Puerto Rico or the U.S. Virgin Islands will retain
jurisdiction of the claim until it is paid or denied. (See DI 11010.265 and DI
20101.035E.)
Note: The Puerto Rico and U.S. Virgin Island FO's will still be responsible for such postentitlement
items as overpayments, nonreceipt, etc.
