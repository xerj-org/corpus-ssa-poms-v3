A. Introduction
In general, Spanish Social Security covers all persons at least 16 years of age who
work in Spain or who work outside the country but maintain their residence in Spain.
A worker's occupation determines whether the worker is covered under the general system
or one of the special systems mentioned in GN 01721.010.
