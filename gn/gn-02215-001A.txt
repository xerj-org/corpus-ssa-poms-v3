SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.001 - Overpayment Notice Modifications - Debtor Outside U.S.
Effective Dates: 04/25/2018 - Present | TN 15 (08-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a debtor from whom recovery is sought is outside the U.S., jurisdiction for the
initial overpayment determination and notice may or may not be the Division of International
Operations (DIO). See GN 02215.010.
Regardless of the PSC having jurisdiction, certain changes in the preparation and
delivery of the overpayment notice are necessary. When a MADCAP system notice is not
used for a debtor outside the U.S., an overpayment notice must be prepared using exhibit
notices in NL
00703.000.
