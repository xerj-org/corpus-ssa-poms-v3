Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402332 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor bank for IDD to several countries, including St. Lucia.
All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix of
70 at the beginning of the routing number (RTN) and an assigned letter in the first
position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for St. Lucia
is “S.”
