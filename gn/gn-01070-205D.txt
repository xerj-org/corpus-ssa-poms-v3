SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01070.205 - When The Field Office (FO) Is Not To Make DOTEL Contact
Effective Dates: 04/04/2018 - Present | TN 19 (05-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201070205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Open applications
If the prior claims folder was destroyed, and the notice of the initial determination
on the prior claim was before February 1, 1979, assume that the prior application
is open. (See GN 00204.025 and GN 01070.224). If all information available is online and accessible a telephone contact will
not be necessary.
