B. Procedure – notice action on the S9 suspension
The PSC will send appropriate suspension and overpayment notices to the beneficiary
to explain the reason SSA suspended benefits and to provide overpayment, reconsideration
language to the beneficiary. See the notices for fugitive felon suspensions and overpayments
in NL 00755.400 and NL 00755.500.
