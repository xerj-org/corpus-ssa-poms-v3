C. Administrative sanction case with incarceration
1.
QUESTION: If a jailed claimant released during the sanction period is due a partial month payment
on Title XVI, does the partial month count as a sanction month?
ANSWER: Yes, the partial month payment is for a sanction month. Once the sanction period
begins, it continues for 6, 12, or 24 months regardless of intermittent months of
ineligibility or partial payment.
2.
QUESTION: If we sanction a claimant for 6 months on Title II and Title XVI and they are in
jail and not yet convicted of a felony, do we begin sanctions on Title II before the
conviction and sanction the Title XVI benefits after their release?
ANSWER: When the claimant is eligible and entitled to receive payment under either title,
the sanction period will begin. The sanction will apply for the same 6 months under
both titles, even though the claimant is due payment on only one title.
