SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.035 - Foreign-to-Foreign Change of Address
Effective Dates: 08/08/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Beneficiary CURRENTLY living in Italy, Germany or Switzerland
1.
If a beneficiary who meets exception A to the alien nonpayment provision because he
or she is receiving checks based on residence in Italy, Germany or Switzerland (listing
under code 994 and folder flash are in file) reports a change of address to another
foreign country, give the case to a benefit authorizer for possible suspension of
benefits.
2.
If a beneficiary meets any other alien exception, process according to GN 02605.250.
