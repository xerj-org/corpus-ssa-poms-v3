B. Description of the Paper or Electronic Form SSA-2490-BK
The paper and electronic (e-form) form is divided into two parts. As explained below,
persons filing for U.S. benefits under any of the Agreements must always complete
Part I.
Persons filing for foreign benefits under any Agreement except Australia, Canada,
Japan, South Korea or Spain must complete Parts I and II. Persons claiming benefits
from Australia, Canada, Japan, South Korea or Spain under the Agreements with those
countries do not complete Form SSA-2490-BK since there are separate Australian, Canadian,
Japanese, Korean and Spanish application forms for that purpose (see summary chart
in GN 01702.120).
