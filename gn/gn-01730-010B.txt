SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 12/16/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When taking applications under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices (See
GN 01702.001 - 01702.525 for FO and FSP development and GN 01729.205 -01729.215 for specific evidence and application requirements for the agreement with
Austria).
