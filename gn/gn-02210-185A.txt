SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02210.185 - Overpaid medicare individual wishes to refund by installments
Effective Dates: 09/30/2005 - Present | TN 17 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202210185 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Each request for refund sent to an individual by a carrier or intermediary advises
the individual that he/she may refund the overpayment by installments. It also specifies
a minimum $10 monthly amount and the number of installments required if he/she chooses
to repay through installments.
