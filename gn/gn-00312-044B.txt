B. Procedure for requesting birth, marriage, divorce, and death records
1. Office to contact
Contact the Office of the Civil Register in the municipality where the event took
place for birth, marriage, and death records. Contact the Office of the Civil Register
in the municipality where the marriage occurred or the municipality of last residence
in the case of a foreign marriage. The address of the civil registry for a municipality
can usually be found on its website. To find the website, enter the name of the municipality
and “be” for Belgium (e.g. http://www.antwerpen.be/).
2. Birth records
Include the following information in requests for birth records:
•
Full name of child at birth
•
Sex of child
•
Date and place of birth
•
Names of the parents, including the maiden surname
•
Names of the parents of a single parent
•
Address at the time of birth
3. Fee
Local tax, varying per municipality.
