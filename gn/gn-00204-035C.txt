C. Procedure for applying deemed filing for DOB January 2, 1954 or later
When a claimant files for benefits, you must explore their eligibility for other benefits
and determine if deemed filing applies to any current or past due benefits, for the
claimant or the spouse or ex-spouse named on the claimants application.
IMPORTANT: When a spouse or divorced spouse becomes eligible for benefits based on a claimant’s
filing and entitlement to retirement or disability benefits, determine if the spouse
or ex-spouse is a Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipient and eligible for spouse’s
or divorced spouse’s benefits. If eligible, follow SI 00510.001 Filing for other Benefits as the spouse or ex-spouse is required to file an application
for spouse’s or divorced spouse’s benefits to maintain his or her SSI eligibility.
Follow the procedures outlined in SI 00510.021 and SI 00510.025 for issuing an SSA-L8051 to the spouse and monitoring for compliance. If the spouse
or ex-spouse is not an SSI recipient, no further action is required.
1. Application for RIB or AUXSPO benefits
When a claimant files for RIB or AUXSPO benefits and eligibility exists for both benefits:
•
Secure an application for both benefits;
•
Determine the month of election for the first benefit; and
•
Process the second benefit with a MOET equal to the first month the claimant becomes
eligible for the second benefit.
NOTE: If the claimant filed for RIB or AUXSPO benefits online, you must determine if another
application is required.
2. Application for Widow(er)s Insurance Benefits (WIB)
When a claimant entitled to RIB files for WIB and you determine that deemed filing
applies to past due AUXSPO benefits:
•
Use the WIB application to award the past due AUXSPO benefits when the benefits are
under the same earnings record; or
•
Secure an AUXSPO application to award past due AUXSPO benefits when the benefits are
under a different earnings record.
•
Select a MOET that equals the first month the claimant was eligible for the additional
AUXSPO benefits.
3. Deemed filing date
When applying deemed filing, the MOET for the deemed filers claim must be the first
month they are eligible for the second benefit. When adjudicating the claim, MCS may
generate an adjudicative edit message because the month of election does not correspond
with the filing date. In this situation, use the deemed filing date as the protective
filing date for processing purposes. The deemed filing date is the first day of the
MOET. For example, if the MOET is 05/2016, the deemed filing date would be 05/01/2016.
For more information on adjudicative messages, see MS 03511.007. For information on
using the protective filing date in MCS, see MS 03509.013 Decision Input (DECI) and
MS 03509.014 File Date Determination Screen (FDDS).
4. Deemed filing documentation
An adjudicator is responsible for documenting the claim to clarify aspects of a claim
that may not be apparent to others reviewing the file. Use the RPOC screen in MCS
to document aspects of deemed filing that may not be evident when reviewing the claim,
such as:
•
Reason(s) for denying a RIB or AUXSPO claim when the claimant is deemed to file for
that benefit,
•
Reason for using the deemed filing date for a RIB or AUXSPO claim.
