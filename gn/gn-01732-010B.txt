SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01732.010 - How the Regional Foreign Service Post (RFSP) Develops and Processes Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 08/24/1993 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201732010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. U.S. Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1) and the appropriate RSDI applications.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-FI to the Central Pension Security Institute (CPSI)
to request a Finnish coverage record if it appears the worker is not insured based
on U.S. coverage alone.
•
Forward the claim to OIO, and include the English language copy of the liaison form
sent to the CPSI.
2. Finnish Claim
•
Take a protective statement on form SSA-795 indicating a desire to file for Finnish
benefits and forward it, under cover of form SSA-2960-U3-FI, to the CPSI.
•
Inform the applicant that the NPS or CPSI will contact him/her directly to complete
the required Finnish applications.
3. U.S. and Finnish Claim
•
Obtain form SSA-2490 (Parts I and II) and the appropriate RSDI application.
•
Forward a liaison form SSA-2960-U3-FI to the CPSI to request a Finnish coverage record
(if it appears the worker is not insured based on U.S. coverage alone) and to certify
the protective filing date.
•
Forward the claim to OIO, and include the English language copy of the liaison form
sent to the CPSI.
•
Inform the applicant that the NPS or CPSI will contact him/her directly to complete
the required Finnish applications.
