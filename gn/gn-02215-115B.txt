SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02215.115 - FO handling of compromise offers
Effective Dates: 08/04/2026 - Present | TN 63 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202215115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Develop financial circumstances when:
•
The debt is $20,000 or less but the offer is less than 80% of the debt amount, or
•
The debt exceeds $20,000.
If unusual circumstances exist, develop with respect to the point involved.
For example, financial information from the debtor indicates expenses exceed income
and there are no cash assets, yet the debtor offers to compromise a $7,500 debt by
repaying $2,000. Explain how the debtor will get the money.
