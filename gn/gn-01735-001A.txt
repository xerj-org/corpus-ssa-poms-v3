A. Introduction
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Ireland:
1.
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work, and
2.
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Irish benefits,
even though they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
