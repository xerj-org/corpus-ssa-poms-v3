SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.580 - Assigning Correct Type-of-Event (TOE) Codes to Requests for Totalization Development
Effective Dates: 01/18/2024 - Present | TN 7 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704580 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. TOE code 520
Show TOE code 520 on requests made to obtain information or evidence needed to respond
to a foreign agency request. For example:
1.
A request to the International Disability Unit (IDU) for a medical examination requested
by a foreign agency.
2.
A request for a U.S. earnings record requested by a foreign agency.
NOTE:
TOE codes 120 and 155 apply to requests for development needed to authorize regular
RSDI claims. BETs should have no reason to assign these codes to their requests.
