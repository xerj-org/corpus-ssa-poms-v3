SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00201.015 - Signature Methods for Benefit Applications
Effective Dates: 07/22/2026 - Present | TN 46 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200201015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
SSA accepts the following signature methods for benefit applications:
•
Wet signature,
•
Attestation,
•
Electronic signature via Click-and-sign,
•
Electronic signature using Upload Documents, and
•
Commercial Product Alternative Signature (CPAS)
Only certain individuals can sign benefit applications (e.g., SSA-1, SSA-16, SSA-8000.
See Proper Applicant GN 00204.003.
For non-benefit application forms, follow the applicable guidelines for each specific
form to ensure signature compliance.
For example, refer to the specific signature policy related to Form SSA-827 (Authorization
to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration), DI 11005.056.
NOTE: For SSI redetermination (RZ) signature methods, refer to SI 02305.003.
