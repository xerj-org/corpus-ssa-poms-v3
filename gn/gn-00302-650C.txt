C. Quarterly Reports of Indian Schools, 1910 - 1939
Quarterly Reports of Indian Schools (1910-1939) involve both Federal Government -
operated and private contract schools.
They list students and their ages.
The records are not complete in that they do not list all schools or even all students
in a given school.
