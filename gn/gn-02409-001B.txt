Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202409001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Field Office (FO) operating policy
1. Conditions for waiver
a.
The waiver applies only to the requester's own payments; and
b.
Waiver does not apply to a period for which benefits were paid unless full refund
for the period is made; and
c.
The full monthly benefit is being waived (see GN 02409.001C.1. if the beneficiary elected SMI); and
d.
SSI is not involved.
2. Waiver on behalf of child or incapable adult by a representative payee
Develop waiver of benefits requests per GN 00504.260, and submit the requests to the Assistant Regional Commissioner (ARC), Management,
and Operations Support (MOS), for a determination on approval of the request.
3. Withdrawal of a waiver
Withdrawal of the waiver must be in writing. The withdrawal is not retroactive, and
will be effective only for the month of signing and subsequent months. For information
on Revocation of Waiver Benefits, see GN 02409.030.
