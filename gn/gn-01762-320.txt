GN 01762.320 Uruguayan Medical Exam Reimbursement Form
The Uruguayan liaison agency will use Form SSA-e2960 USA/UY 3 (U.S.A. - Uruguay Agreement
on Social Security Transmittal/Request/Certification Form) for reimbursement of medical
expenses regarding requests for exams.
