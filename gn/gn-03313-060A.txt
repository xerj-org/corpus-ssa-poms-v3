A. Disclosures to assist us in administering our Social Security programs
We disclose information to Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to help detect unreported
resources and non-wage income of Supplemental Security Income recipients and to assist
IRS with investigations of lost, stolen, or forged Social Security checks.
