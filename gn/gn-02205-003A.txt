SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02205.003 - Primary Liability for Title II Overpayment Recovery
Effective Dates: 05/19/2017 - Present | TN 13 (09-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202205003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What is primary liability?
The person who receives the overpayment is primarily (directly) responsible for the
overpayment. Other persons may be primarily liable for the overpayment. If the person
who receives the overpayment is the only person with primary liability, that person
is solely liable for the overpayment.
