SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00204.001 - Valid Application
Effective Dates: 06/30/2015 - Present | TN 176 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200204001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy - requirements
1. Valid application
To be valid an application must be:
a.
On a prescribed application form approved by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB)
such as:
•
A specific benefit application form (e.g., SSA-1, SSA-8000),
•
A computer printout generated from one of SSA’s application systems (e.g., that includes
information SSA employees obtained and recorded in one of SSA’s application during
interviews from applicants applying for Title II, XVI or XVIII benefits, or
•
An internet application (i.e., Universal Benefit Application, internet claim).
b.
Signed beneath the penalty clause by a proper applicant (GN 00204.003); and
c.
Filed with SSA (GN 00204.006);
d.
The claimant must be alive when the application is filed unless:
•
Before death, there was a written statement of intent to claim benefits (GN 00204.005A );
•
Before death, a Supplemental Security Income (SSI) oral inquiry was received (SI 00601.025); or
•
Within 3 months after the month of death, a title II disability application is filed
(GN
00204.005A)
2. Disposition
We will adjudicate a valid application and notify the claimant of the determination.
Do not adjudicate invalid applications, see GN
01010.475B.1. If the claimant files but dies before we effectuate the application, we will process
it per GN 00204.005F. For instructions on handling SSI applications, which are pending at the time of the
claimant's death see SI
02101.003B.3.
NOTE: For retroactivity of disability benefits refer to DI 10105.015 and for specific instructions for processing disability applications DI 40101.001 - DI 40105.025.
