SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.043 - Administrative Review of the Fee Authorized Under the Fee Agreement Process - Concurrent Titles II and XVI Cases or Affected Auxiliary Beneficiary(ies) Involved
Effective Dates: 01/08/1997 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960043 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
When a party requests administrative review of a determination of the amount of the
fee under a properly approved agreement, the reviewer must consider the total fee
for all administrative level services.
1. Concurrent Titles II and XVI Cases
In concurrent claims involving a common issue, e.g., disability, this means considering
the total fee amount based on the past-due benefits resulting from both the titles
II and XVI claims. (See GN 03960.050C.4. for the criteria.)
2. Affected Auxiliary Beneficiary
In title II claims involving one or more affected auxiliary beneficiaries, this means
considering the total fee amount based on the past-due benefits of the primary claimant
and the auxiliary beneficiary(ies). (See GN 03920.035.)
