C. Process
The following subsection describes how the Spanish agencies determine which office
has jurisdiction of a claim.
1. Maritime Workers
Claims filed by maritime workers are processed by the central office of the ISM. If
a claimant files an application in one of the local ISM offices, the claim is forwarded
to the central office in Madrid where the processing is completed.
2. All Other Workers
Claims filed by all other workers are processed by the appropriate provincial office
of the INSS.
•
If the claimant resides in Spain, the claim is processed by the provincial office
in the province where the claimant resides.
•
If the claimant resides outside Spain, the INSS central office determines which provincial
office has jurisdiction of a claim. When OIO sends a claim to the central office,
that office routes it to the correct provincial office and sends a referral letter
to SSA. Refer to GN 01722.035 for a sample of the referral letter in Spanish and in English.
