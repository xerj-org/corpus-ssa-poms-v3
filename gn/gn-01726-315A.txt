SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01726.315 - Earnings Records for Spouses/Partners or Widow(er)s - Netherlands Agreement
Effective Dates: 12/12/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201726315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
To determine the amount of their spouse's or partner's supplement or the amount of
their widow(er)'s benefits, the SVB requires a copy of the spouse's, partner's or
widow(er)'s own earnings record. This presents no problem with widow(er)'s claims or when a spouse
files a claim in his/her own right because his/her application permits disclosure
of the earnings under the “routine use” provisions. However, when the insured person might be eligible for a spouse's or
a partner's supplement and the spouse has not filed an application, his/her consent
to release earnings information is required.
