D. Procedure for entering the Slovenian social security identification number
Enter the worker and each claimant's Slovenian EMSO number or the Slovenian File number
on all claims. If the EMSO or File number is unknown, enter all of the following information
under Part I of Form SSA-2490-BK:
•
worker’s full name, including both the paternal and maternal last names (see GN 00302.460 and GN 00302.470);
•
worker’s employment history, including names of employers and dates of employment;
and
•
name of the agency to which the worker last contributed. NOTE: The Slovenian agency
will need this information to transmit the NH’s Slovenian coverage record.
