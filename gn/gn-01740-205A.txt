Effective Dates: 01/17/2017 - Present |

A. Procedure – Initial claims packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Chilean coverage records and to inform
the Chilean agencies about claims for Chilean benefits. (See GN 01739.205 – GN 01739.220 for the applications and evidence required in the packages.)
