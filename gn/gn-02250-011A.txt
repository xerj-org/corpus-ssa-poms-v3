A. Introduction
This section provides guidance on how to process a waiver request when we are unable
to locate and review the information necessary to verify an overpayment. In some instances,
you may not be able to locate and review paper or electronic records to determine:
•
the amount of the overpayment; or
•
the overpayment period; or
•
the cause of the overpayment.
