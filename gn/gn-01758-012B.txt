B. Procedure for the FBU to process referrals from the Brazilian Liaison Agency
This procedure explains how the Lisbon, Portugal FBU processes referrals from the
Brazilian Liaison agency.
1. Claimant entitled to U.S. benefits
Forward all materials to the Division of International Operations (DIO) annotated
“already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
2. Claimant not entitled to U.S. benefits
Process the claim in the exact same manner shown in GN 01758.010B
