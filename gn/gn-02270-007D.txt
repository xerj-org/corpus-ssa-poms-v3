D. When USPS returns the notice as undeliverable
If the United States Postal Service (USPS) returns the notice scheduling the file
review and personal conference as undeliverable, develop for a new address, refer
to GN 02605.055 and GN 02605.060.
If you obtain a new address, send a new notice to the individual. If you cannot obtain
a new address, document your efforts on the DMS Remarks (DMS RMKS) screen for Title
II cases and on the Report of Contact for Title XVI cases. Process the waiver as if
the individual declined the personal conference and follow the steps in GN 02270.007G.1.
