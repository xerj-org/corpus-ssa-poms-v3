A. Procedure – Birth, Marriage and Death Certificates
If the event for which the document(s) is being requested occurred on an island, show
the name of the town and the island. Include the name of the parish (Freguesia) in which the event occurred.
1. Office to Contact
Office of the Civil Registrar (Conservatoria do Registo
Civil) in the town in which the event occurred or the nearest town.
2. Fee
4.99 euros
