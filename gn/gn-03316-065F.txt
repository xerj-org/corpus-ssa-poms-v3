Effective Dates: 10/08/2010 - Present | TN 2 (02-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203316065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. To establish or verify information provided by representative payees and payee applicants
We may disclose information to third parties, where necessary, to establish or verify
information provided by representative payees or payee applicants. We may disclose
relevant information from the following systems of records, including but not limited
to:
•
60-0089—Claims Folders System;
•
60-0090—Master Beneficiary Record;
•
60-0103—Supplemental Security Income Record and Special Veterans Benefits;
•
60-0222—Master Representative Payee File;
•
60-0269—Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS); and
•
60-0320—eDIB Claim File.
NOTE: Do not disclose the address of a former payee to the current payee, as this is not
necessary to perform his or her duties.
