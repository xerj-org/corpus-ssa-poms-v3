C. Policy - Using U.S. evidence
Evidence which is used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security
benefits may also be used by the Netherlands to establish the same factor for Dutch
benefits. Consequently, it is unnecessary for the applicant to submit additional evidence
to document a factor of entitlement for Dutch benefits when that factor has already
been verified for SSA purposes.
