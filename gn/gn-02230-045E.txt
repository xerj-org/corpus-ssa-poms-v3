SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02230.045 - Remittance process for repayments of Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments paid to a sponsored alien
Effective Dates: 01/06/2023 - Present | TN 25 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202230045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Address where sponsor sends remittance
Instruct the sponsor to send remittances to this address:
MATPSC
P.O. Box 3430
Philadelphia, PA 19122
Instruct the sponsor to include on their remittances the sponsor’s SSN
and the letters “SAR” (sponsor alien remittance) to ensure proper crediting to the sponsor’s debt balance.
