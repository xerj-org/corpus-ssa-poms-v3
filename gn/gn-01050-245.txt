GN 01050.245 Material Not Pertinent
Do not include material that is clearly nonessential and without present or probable
future value or significance. As a general rule, do not forward:
A.
Initial notes or rough drafts made for the convenience of the DO.
B.
Blank forms or blank questionnaires returned to the DO.
C.
Extra copies of RC's.
D.
Envelopes not required to establish a filing date.
E.
Copies of routine development correspondence with claimants or employers. (This does
not include correspondence which might be considered an application under.)
