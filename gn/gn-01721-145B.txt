SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01721.145 - Exception to Section 202(t)(11) Alien Nonpayment Provision for Dependents and Survivors under the Spanish Agreement
Effective Dates: 10/13/2022 - Present | TN 7 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201721145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If an alien dependent or survivor, who does not meet the 5-year residency requirement,
alleges they are a citizen or resident of Spain, develop citizenship or residence
status.
