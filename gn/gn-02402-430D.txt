D. Overview of IDD for United Arab Emirates
IDD payments sent to United Arab Emirates must be in United Arab Emirates Dirham (AED)
accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or
the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that
serves United Arab Emirates. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional
information.
