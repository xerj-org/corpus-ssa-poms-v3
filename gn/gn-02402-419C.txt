SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.419 - Coding Thailand Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 03/01/2019 - Present | TN 128 (03-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402419 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Thailand has a prefix of “73” at the beginning of the Routing
and Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “T” assigned for Thailand.
