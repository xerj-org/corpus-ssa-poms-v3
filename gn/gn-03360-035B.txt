B. Policy - When to Account
1. Disclosures to Federal Agencies and Federal or State Courts
Disclosures to Federal agencies and Federal or State courts are made under the provisions
of the PA and not under the FOIA. (See GN 03301.015.B.2.) Thus, an accounting for disclosure is required.
2. SSA-Initiated Disclosures
SSA-initiated disclosures are made from a system of records under provisions of the
PA:
•
if a notice of routine use has been published which designates an agency to which
disclosure is to be made (see GN 03301.020.B.4.), or
•
under compelling circumstances affecting the health or safety of an individual (see
GN 03316.135).
SSA-initiated disclosures require an accounting.
