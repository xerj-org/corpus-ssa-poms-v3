B. Procedure for applying the exception to Section 202(t)(1) Alien Nonpayment Provision
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the Alien Nonpayment Provision
as described in RS 02610.010 alleges citizenship of Iceland, then develop citizenship status to verify that the
exception provided by Icelandic citizenship applies. All residents of Iceland are
exempt from the Alien Nonpayment Provision.
For more information about exceptions to the additional U.S. residency requirements
for alien dependents and survivors under the U.S-Iceland Agreement, see GN 01763.145.
