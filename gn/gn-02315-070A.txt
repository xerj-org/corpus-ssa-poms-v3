A. Background for New York small estates
The New York
Surrogate’s Court Procedure Act (SCPA) provides two different procedures that individuals may use to settle small estates
without formal administration. For purposes of receiving Title II or Black Lung payments,
we consider an individual who is authorized to act pursuant to either of these procedures
a “legal representative” of the estate.
