SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03103.080 - Forwarding the Form HA-501-U5 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) and Folder to the Servicing Hearing Office
Effective Dates: 12/19/2018 - Present | TN 36 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure for requesting the folder from an FO
1.
Initial folder request
Call the FO that has the folder within one business day of receiving the hearing request.
Ask the FO to send the folder as appropriate, to the servicing FO, jurisdictional
PC (TII non-medical), or the servicing HO within five business days.
2.
Folder request follow-up procedures
If you do not receive the folder within:
a.
15 business days of the initial request, call the FO for a status;
b.
10 business days of the follow-up request, transfer responsibility for obtaining the
folder to the Office of the Regional Commissioner (ORC) per GN 03103.080J.
