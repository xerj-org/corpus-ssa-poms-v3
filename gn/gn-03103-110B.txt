Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203103110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
1. PURPOSE
The SSA-636-U3 is used to transmit hearing folders to the ALJ. It can also be used
by the FO or PC to inform the ALJ of things they should know for the hearing.
2. WHEN USED
a.
PSC cases (both RSI and DI);
b.
OCRO non-claims;
c.
SSI cases.
NOTE: For ODO cases, use SSA-3583-U2 (see DI 42010.005).
3. WHO COMPLETES
Normally, servicing FO or PC, whichever has folder when hearing is requested.
