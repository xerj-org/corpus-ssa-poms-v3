A. Introduction
Danish Social Security benefits consist of two separate components:
1. Folkepension (FP)
The FP program, or first tier, is a non-contributory program that covers all residents
of Denmark. The FP or “Social” benefit is paid in flat-rate amounts to people who
meet certain residence requirements.
2. Labour Market Supplementary Pension (ATP)
The ATP program, or second tier, provides old-age benefits based on the length of
time a worker contributes to the program but does not provide for disability benefits.
