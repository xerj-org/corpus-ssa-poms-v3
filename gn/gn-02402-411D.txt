D. Overview of IDD for Guatemala
IDD payments sent to Guatemala must be in Guatemalan quetzal (GTQ) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Guatemala.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
