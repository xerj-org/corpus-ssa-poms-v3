B. Minimization
The Privacy Act requires us to employ a policy of minimization when we disclose information
to third parties. Thus, to the extent possible, we disclose only the minimal information
that is relevant and necessary to accomplish the purpose for which the third party
requests the information. Further, in determining how much information we may disclose
to a third party, we should consider whether the requested information can be obtained
from some other source (e.g., outside of the Social Security Administration) as well
as what uses the third party might make of the information we disclose.
