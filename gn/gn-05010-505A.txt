A. DESCRIPTION OF THE SSA-1042S
The SSA-1042S consists of three parts:
•
a benefit statement part which is the same as that on the SSA-1099, except as shown
in GN 05010.505B.;
•
an explanation of the form and the items in the benefit portion of the form; and
•
an explanation of computer matching programs.
The SSA-1042S is revised each year to show the tax year for which it is issued and
to include any changes which have been made since the prior year.
