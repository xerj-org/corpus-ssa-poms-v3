E. Access Disputes
If we deny the NH access to information concerning other individuals on their record,
the NH may choose to dispute our denial. The NH can file a Privacy Act appeal with
us or file a Freedom of Information Act request for the information they are seeking.
Please see GN 03340.075 for more information on Privacy Act denials and appeals.
NOTE: Determining the NH’s right of access to information concerning other individuals on
their record can be difficult in certain circumstances. Consult with the regional
office Privacy Act Coordinator when you have concerns about the NH’s right of access.
