B. Using the Modernized Claims System (MCS) version of Form SSA-2490-BK
Use the following information to determine which applications require completion when
taking claims for U.S. Totalization benefits and Finnish benefits under the Agreement
with Finland.
1. U.S. Totalization benefits only
a. Retirement or disability claims
•
Complete the appropriate retirement or disability benefit MCS application screens;
and
•
The TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 03509.011); and
•
The Form SSA-2490-BK screens (see MSOM MCS 03507.001).
b. Survivors claims
•
Complete the appropriate MCS application screens; and
•
The TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 03509.011); and
•
Part I of the paper or electronic Form SSA-2490-BK.
2. Finnish regular or Totalization benefits only
These claims are MCS exclusions. Complete Parts I and II of the paper or electronic
appliaction Form SSA-2490-BK.
3. Finnish benefits (regular or Totalization) and U.S. (regular or Totalization) benefits
a. Retirement or disability claims
•
Complete the appropriate retirement or disability benefit MCS application screens;
and
•
The TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM ), MCS 03509.011; and
•
The Form SSA-2490-BK screens (see MSOM MCS 03507.001).
b. Survivors claims
•
Complete the appropriate MCS application screens; and
•
The TOTALIZATION (TOTL) screen (see MSOM MCS 03509.011); and
•
Parts I and II of the paper or electronic form SSA-2490-BK.
