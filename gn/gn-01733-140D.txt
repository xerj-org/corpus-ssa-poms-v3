D. Development for dependents and survivors
If a non-U.S. citizen dependent or survivor does not meet the five-year U.S. residency
requirement and alleges citizenship or residency of Luxembourg, the Field Office (FO)
should develop for Luxembourg citizenship and residence status to verify that an exemption
provided by the Agreement applies (Section 202(t)(1) of the Act).
