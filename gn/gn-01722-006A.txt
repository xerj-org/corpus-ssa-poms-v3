SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.006 - Requesting Spanish Application Forms from U.S. Residents
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. background
Since the FOs do not stock the application forms required by Spain, OIO must send
these forms to the claimants when a claim is filed under the agreement. The form E/USA
2 (Statement of Working Life) provides the information that Spain needs to locate
a worker's coverage. The form E/USA is an application for Spanish benefits, and it
also provides the work history.
