SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.150 - Preparation and Assembly of RSHI Claims Material for Transmittal to the Processing Centers
Effective Dates: 10/18/2005 - Present | TN 1 (06-86)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201050150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Material not To be sent to PSC
1.
Notes, carbon copies of R/C's or rough drafts made for the convenience of the DO.
2.
Blank forms or questionnaires and routine correspondence with claimants or employers.
3.
Extra copies of RC's.
4.
Envelopes not required to establish a filing date or necessary for other documentation
purposes.
5.
Copies of routine development correspondence with claimants or third parties. (This
does not include correspondence which might be considered an application or important
to any determination).
6.
Route slips, such as forms SSA-408, etc.
7.
Multiple photocopies of proofs.
8.
RCs recording follow-up efforts made for evidence that is now in the claims folder
or letters requesting evidence now in file.
9.
Carbon copies of multiple-page forms.
10.
Photocopies of material already in file.
11.
Computer output material on the right side of the folder which has no future documentation
value. In title II claims retain only the latest computer output (such as MBR, ROAR,
etc.) in file.
12.
Signed authorization forms (such as the SSA-827, SSA-4641-U2, SSA-8510, etc.), which
have not been used.
