GN 03340.050 Access to Records in Exempt Systems of Records
Some systems of records are exempt from individuals’ access because we compiled them
for investigative or law enforcement purposes or the records otherwise meet the requirements
for exemption under the Privacy Act. Claims records at the Field Offices are generally
not exempt. However, claims-related investigatory records for developing fraud or
other cases may be exempt.
