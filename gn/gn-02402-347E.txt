E. Description of Lithuania direct deposit data
The data for coding IDD for Lithuania will come from the International Bank Account
Number (IBAN) provided to the beneficiary from FI. The IBAN will be 20 characters.
However, the IDD coding for Lithuania bank data consists of 21 characters, including
the country code of H for Lithuania.
