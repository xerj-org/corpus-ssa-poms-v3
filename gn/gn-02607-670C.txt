C. Procedure - PC/OCO-ODO use of FID codes to respond to inquiries
Use the facility code, located on IRCS, to help respond to inquiries about a beneficiary's
benefits. You may also use the name of the correctional facility when preparing notices
to the beneficiary to inform them of the reported place of incarceration. To locate
a FID code on IRCS, see MSOM PRISON 002.016
