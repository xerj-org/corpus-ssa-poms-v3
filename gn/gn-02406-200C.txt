C. Procedure for second follow-up
The beneficiary/recipient may inquire for a second time concerning the status of the
initial nonreceipt claim.
•
Obtain a PHUS to determine, based on Treasury disposition codes, whether the corrective
action taken after the first inquiry transmitted properly.
•
If the nonreceipt input was transmitted properly and the courtesy disbursement, replacement
check, or settlement check was issued but not received, follow instructions in GN 02406.200D to correct the specific problem. Follow this section also if the delay is causing
a hardship or a public relations problem.
