D. Servicing FO’s and jurisdictional PC’s procedure when the documents are stored electronically
or the paper folder is destroyed or lost
If the servicing FO (TXVI only and TII and TXVI concurrent common issue cases – non-medical)
or the jurisdictional PC (TII only – non-medical) is unable to obtain a paper folder
because the documents are stored electronically or the paper folder is lost or destroyed,
create or reconstruct a paper folder.
1.
Electronically stored documents
In non-medical situations, when there is no folder because we store the documents
electronically, create a paper folder. Include the following documents in the paper
folder when applicable:
•
Completed eNDSS (non-medical cases);
•
Copy of the award notice(s);
•
Copies of letters and notices pertaining to the issue before the administrative law
judge (ALJ);
•
Evidence used to make the initial and reconsideration determinations; and
•
Additional evidence submitted with the hearing request.
2.
Destroyed or lost folder
Before you reconstruct a destroyed or lost folder (disability or non-medical), see
GN 03105.010 and GN 03105.040.
