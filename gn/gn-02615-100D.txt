D. Clark class members processing categories in CATS
We added all identified Title II and Title XVI class members potentially eligible
for Clark relief into CATS with CCIDs.
The following five CCIDs indicate the possible processing categories:

General Counsel (OGC). Only Central Office staff has access to Clark CATS records. If a beneficiary or recipient inquirers about Clark relief, follow the instructions in GN 02615.190.
