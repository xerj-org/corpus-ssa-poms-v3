SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.306 - Recomputations Involving Totalization and the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) – Additional Foreign Coverage for Months Prior to January 1995
Effective Dates: 03/24/2026 - Present | TN 19 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701306 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
When WEP applies to Totalization benefits payable for months prior to January 1995
and additional foreign coverage is alleged, the Office of International Operations
will obtain a certified record of the foreign coverage directly from the foreign country.
NOTE:
Effective January 2024, the Social Security Fairness Act (SSFA) of 2023 eliminated the reduction of Social
Security benefits under the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP). For more information,
please refer toRS
00605.360.
