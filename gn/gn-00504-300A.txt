A. Referrals to State or local social agencies for payee assistance
The Regional Commissioners (RCs) are the focal point for Social Security Administrations
(SSAs) interaction with State social agencies. RC’s are responsible for establishing
rules and procedures for field office’s (FOs) in their regions to follow in referring
beneficiaries to public or private community social agencies.
