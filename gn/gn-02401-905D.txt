SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02401.905 - Beneficiary or Recipient Contacts SSA Concerning a Limited Payability Notice
Effective Dates: 07/16/2026 - Present | TN 53 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202401905 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Beneficiary returns incorrectly completed notice to PC
NOTE: The beneficiary must sign the LP notice before SSA releases a new payment.
When the PC technician receives the LP notice, take the following steps:
•
Review the notice for completeness.
•
If the beneficiary:
–
circles a check and returns the notice un-signed, return the notice to the beneficiary
with a self-addressed envelope enclosed and a note asking the individual to sign the
notice and mail it back to the PC;
–
does not circle a check on the signed notice, contact the beneficiary directly to
resolve the discrepancy;
–
does not circle a check on the unsigned notice, file the notice in paperless. Take
no action to repay the check;
–
indicates the individual is unsure if the check was cashed, the notice is signed,
and the payment is still due, repay the check. (For more information, see GN 02401.905B.2.); or
–
indicates the individual is unsure if the check was not cashed and there is no signature
on the notice, return the notice to the beneficiary with a self-addressed envelope
enclosed and a note asking the individual to sign the notice and mail it back to the
PC. Repay the check if the beneficiary returns the signed notice and the payment is
still due.
