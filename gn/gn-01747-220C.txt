C. Procedure
If an applicant brings one of the Danish application forms to the FO, the FO should:
1.
Provide any necessary assistance in completing the form(s).
2.
Photocopy and certify any documents (evidence) the Danish agency has requested the
applicant to include with the Danish claim.
3.
Return the form(s) and certified photocopies to the applicant and advise the applicant
to mail them directly to Denmark at the address shown in the instructions or on the
form.
4.
Advise the applicant to check with the U.S. Postal Service for the international rate
and/or fee for mailing materials to Denmark.
REMINDER: Do not mail the forms to the Division of International Operations..
