GN 01738.230 Controlling Requests Sent to IKA on Form SSA-2960-U3-GR - Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested.
•
Diary all initial requests for 180 days.
•
Diary follow-up requests for 90 days.
•
Diary 2nd follow-up request for 90 days.
•
Diary a request made by the FSP Athens for 180 days from the date the FSP signed the
request.
If information is not received send file to CTE. The CTE should process the case according
to instructions listed in GN 01010.410B.1.- GN 01010.410B.5.
