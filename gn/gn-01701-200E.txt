E. Policy – capping the pro rata PIA
1. Background
In certain rare cases involving disability computations, it is possible for the pro
rata PIA to exceed the non-Totalization PIA (i.e., the PIA that would result if the
NH were insured without the foreign coverage). This could occur, for example, in cases
where the actual number of U.S. QCs exceeds the number of quarters in the computation
years but the foreign coverage is needed to meet the recency-of-work test for disability
benefits. Capping only needs to be considered in claims involving disability computations.
2. The Capping Rule
When the pro rata PIA is greater than the non-Totalization PIA, the pro rata PIA will
be capped and established as the lesser non-Totalization PIA. All benefits are then
based on the capped pro rata PIA.
