A. Introduction
Claimants for Belgian disability benefits are not required to complete application
forms. The claimant submits a medical certificate and is examined by a Belgian agency
doctor. If found to be disabled, the claimant is paid short term (sickness) benefits
for a year. If the beneficiary is still disabled after a year, Belgium will begin
paying long term (invalidity) benefits.
