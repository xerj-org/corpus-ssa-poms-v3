Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present |

A. Procedure — Initial claims packages
Prepare initial claims packages to request Korean coverage records and to inform the
NPC about claims for Korean benefits. (See GN 01741.205 – GN 01741.220 for the applications and evidence required to prepare the packages.)
