D. Overview of IDD for Curacao
IDD payments sent to Curacao must be in Caribbean Guilder (XCG) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Curacao. The
FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
