E. Procedure for processing a non-DOFA claim in the FO
When you process a non-DOFA claim in the FO, annotate the appropriate non-DOFA reason
on the DECI screen in MCS and transfer the claim to the PC of jurisdiction by entering
a PROC code of N on the INTE screen. This action creates a record request for the
PC.
