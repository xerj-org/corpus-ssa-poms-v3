A. Disclosure for adverse claim situations
An adverse claim is an award of benefits to a subsequent claimant, or the reinstatement
or resumption of benefits to a prior beneficiary that results in the nonentitlement
or termination of an earlier beneficiary.
We may disclose a copy of the relevant evidence to the adversely affected beneficiary.
EXAMPLE: A woman (X) is entitled, but another woman (Y) submits evidence of an earlier marriage
to the beneficiary that appears to be still valid. You may show X the evidence establishing
Y's earlier marriage to the beneficiary, so X may try to refute it. You may also disclose
to Y information relevant to the issue of who is the valid spouse.
