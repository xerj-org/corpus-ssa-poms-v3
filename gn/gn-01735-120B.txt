B. Policy — crediting Irish coverage
SSA will credit one U.S. QC for every 13 weeks or remaining fraction of Irish coverage
in a calendar year. (Irish coverage is measured in weeks.) However, no credit will
be given for:
1.
any week in a calendar quarter that is already credited as a U.S. QC, or
2.
more than 4 QC's for any calendar year.
