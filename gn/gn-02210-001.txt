GN 02210.001 Overpayment Recovery by Benefit Adjustment
CITATIONS:
Section 204(a) of the Social Security Act
20 CFR
404.502
When we determine that an individual is overpaid, we send a notice requesting full
and immediate refund and informing the individual of the right to request waiver and
reconsideration (known as a “due process request”) (see GN 02201.009).
If the 90-day due process recovery period expires without a due process request from
the individual, and the individual does not refund the overpayment, we recover the
overpayment by withholding the 10% of the individual’s benefit payment(s) until we
fully recover the overpayment. The 90-day due process recovery period begins with
the current operating month (COM), or in other words, the month we establish the overpayment.
For cases where there has been a due process request, see GN 02250.001 and GN 02201.025.
