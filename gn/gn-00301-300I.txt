I. Example
Marylin Wheeler, a SSI recipient, comes to the FO to submit their pay stubs (dated
3/3/2004 and 3/16/2004) for 3/2004 as proof of their monthly earnings. They report
their earnings on a monthly basis. Ms. Wheeler works for Shannon Moving Co. (EIN 37-8755123)
in Boise, Idaho.

CROSS REFERENCE SSN: 999999999 999999999 999999999

- AGE - LAWFUL ALIEN STATUS - RELATIONSHIP - IDENTITY
- CITIZENSHIP - LIVING ARRANGEMENT - RESOURCES X WAGES
- DEATH - MARRIAGE - SELF-EMPLOYMENT - OTHER
