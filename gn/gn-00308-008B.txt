B. Procedure - State Sources
1. Address and FO Code
Bureau of Vital Statistics
State Dept. of Health & Social Services
P.O. Box 110675
Juneau, AK 99811-0675
FO Code 996
NOTE: You can find the most up-to-date information from the Alaska Department of Health. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
Birth/Death/Marriage/Divorce $30.00, $25.00 for each additional copy requested at
the same time.
3. Type of payment:
Money order or certified check made payable to “State of Alaska”.
4. Remarks:
Can FO verify, certify, or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No.
Divorce records are available since 1950. The State office has some records beginning
1913. State office also has some records for the following cities and towns beginning

Assume that births of Alaska natives, except in the towns and cities listed above,
were not recorded until 1930 or later. The BVS will accept credit cards for all record
requests made by fax (907-465-3618) or mail. There is an $11.00 fee for this service
in addition to the $25.00 fee for each record. The $11.00 credit card fee is not for
expedited service.
ID Requirements: All requests must include a copy of a picture ID of the requester, and a signature
under the copied ID is also required. If SSA is the requester, proper completion of
the SSA-L706 will satisfy the State’s identity requirement.
