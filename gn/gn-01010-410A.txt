A. Failure to submit essential evidence
It is the claimant’s responsibility to submit evidence to establish eligibility and
entitlement for benefits. Some claimants may need assistance obtaining evidence; for
instructions on how to provide assistance, refer to GN 00301.180. Prior to disallowing or denying the claim, make every reasonable effort to assist
the claimant to obtain the necessary evidence.
