A. Process
Divorce and marriage records are available from the Clerk of the Circuit Court in
the county where the divorce was granted. The cost varies. Marriage records are available
from the Judge of the Probate Court in the county where the license was issued. The
cost varies. Field office employees can certify records in all counties.
