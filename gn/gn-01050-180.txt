GN 01050.180 Preparation of Folder for Transmittal of Parent Determination Claims
In the case of a parent filing for a disability determination in connection with a
child filing for benefits on a grandparent's earnings record, enter the NH's (i.e.,
the grandparent) SSN on the face of the folder tab and print the parent's surname
and the first initial on the reverse of the folder tab with the surname first.
