SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01752.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in OIO for Claims Filed in the Republic of Poland
Effective Dates: 07/16/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201752210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – U.S. and Polish or Polish Claim Only
Prepare a SSA-2960 USA/PL3 to:
•
Request the Polish certified coverage record if one is needed and has not been requested.
Include a copy of the SSA-2960 USA/PL3.
•
Send ZUS or KRUS the information or documentation they have requested.
