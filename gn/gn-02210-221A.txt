A. Policy for how to process death cases
When SSA records a death on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR), the Recovery and
Collection of Overpayment Process (RECOOP) automatically stops billing and produces
a death alert. When we record a death on the Supplemental Security Record (SSR), RECOOP
automatically deletes the debt.
