SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01715.205 - Evidence Required for U.S. Totalization Benefits Under the Canadian Agreement
Effective Dates: 11/07/2003 - Present | TN 6 (10-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201715205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a person files for U.S. Totalization benefits under the agreement with Canada,
the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary to establish a
claim for regular (non-Totalization) retirement, survivors or disability insurance
benefits. In addition, the applicant must submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200 - GN
01702.230 whenever Totalization benefits are claimed from the United States.
