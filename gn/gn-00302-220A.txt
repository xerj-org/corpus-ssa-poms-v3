SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.220 - Range of Years Searched by BVS Custodians
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
The BVS custodian's records in the different States have varying search procedures
for locating requested BCs.
The summaries which follow are designed as aids in determining whether discrepant
DBs shown in the evidence in file have also been searched for a claimant who was born
in a “Yes” State, and who alleges that a request for a BC for the DB alleged on the benefit
application resulted in a “No Record” response. See GN 00302.057 D. for action to take if a discrepant DB is discovered after the initial interview.
The summaries may also be used in any situation where it is important to know for which years a search for a birth record
may be assumed to have been made.
