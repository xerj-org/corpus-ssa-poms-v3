SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01719.210 - Developing Evidence and Establishing Entitlement Factors in Claims for United Kingdom (U.K.) Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/01/1997 - Present | TN 4 (01-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201719210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence Requirements
When a person files for United States (U.S.) Totalization benefits under the agreement
with the U.K., the applicant must submit the same evidence that would be necessary
to establish a claim for regular retirement, survivors, or disability insurance (non-Totalization)
benefits. The applicant must submit the evidence noted in GN 01702.200-GN 01702.230 when claiming Totalization benefits from the United States.
