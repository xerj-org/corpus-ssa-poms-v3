B. Description - International Direct Deposit (IDD) RTN prefix codes
1. Table - RTN prefix codes
The two-digit IDD RTN prefix is different for each country or contractor bank. The
first digit of the prefix is always a “5,” “6,” or “7,” which identifies the payment
method as IDD.
