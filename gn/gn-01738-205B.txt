SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.205 - Preparing Claim Packages for Claims Filed Outside Greece
Effective Dates: 03/13/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. U.S. claim
Complete Form SSA-e2960-USA-GR3. Check item V.A to request a Greek coverage record.
Check item V.B to request medical evidence from the Greek agency’s files if claimant
has filed for U.S. disability benefits.
2. U.S. and Greek claim
Include the following information in claim packages:
•
SSA-e2960-USA-GR3,
REMINDER: Check item V.A to request a Greek coverage record. Check item V.B to request medical
evidence from the Greek agency’s files if claimant has filed for U.S. disability benefits.
•
U.S. earnings record,
•
medical evidence from DIO files if the Greek claim is for disability benefits, and
•
any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit including insurance workbooks
(see GN 01737.212).
3. Greek claim
Include the following information in claim packages:
•
SSA-e2960-USA-GR3,
•
U.S. earnings record,
•
lag wages if proof has been submitted,
•
medical evidence from DIO file if the Greek claim is for disability benefits, and
•
any additional evidence the claimant wants to submit including insurance workbooks
(see GN 01737.212).
