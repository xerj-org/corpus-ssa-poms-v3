SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01085.030 - Paper Retention Policy for the Paperless Program Service Center (PSC)
Effective Dates: 01/03/2025 - Present | TN 3 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201085030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
SSA will destroy original documents and forms that are scanned into the paperless
imaging system once the PSC determines that the images are of high enough quality
to meet processing standards; i.e., the images are clearly readable.
Exclusions from destruction are:
•
Documents/forms contained in the initial claims files;
•
Documents that must be returned to the beneficiary; e.g., marriage certificates;
•
Disability medical documents;
•
Certain Title XVI documents.
