A. Policy for forwarding certified coverage records
The Uruguayan certified coverage record is Form UY-US 2 (Certificate of Insurance
Periods). It is the only acceptable form for the certification of the Uruguayan coverage
record. Forward certified coverage records in any other format to the Division of
Training and Program Support for approval.
