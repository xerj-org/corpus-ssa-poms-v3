A. Process
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the Circuit in the county that granted
the divorce. Marriage records are available from the Recorder of Deeds in the county
that issued the license. The cost of copy varies.
Birth and death records are available from the cities and counties listed in subsection
C below. See Remarks for important information on requesting birth certificates.
