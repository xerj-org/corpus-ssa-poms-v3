SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01704.571 - Obtaining Certified U.S. Earnings Records Under the Canadian Agreement
Effective Dates: 01/19/1999 - Present | TN 2 (09-85)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201704571 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Because of the recency of the Canadian programs, Canada never needs coverage information
prior to 1952. In addition, Canada has agreed to accept initially either a mini-certified
earnings record (MCER) or SSA-794TR whichever is produced based on the SSA-451-A4,
SSA-450 or SSA-5025-NC request. (See GN 01704.990, Exhibit 1.) Therefore, unless otherwise stated, when reference is made to furnishing
a U.S. earnings record, either the MCER or SSA-794TR will suffice. However, if both
are in file, send the SSA-794TR since it contains more information.
