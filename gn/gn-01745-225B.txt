SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01745.225 - Requests for Status of Application for Japanese Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 5 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201745225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Japanese claims
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of the
applicant's claim for Japanese benefits and states that the applicant has not received
notification that DIO forwarded the claim to the Japanese agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Japan
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Japan, advise the applicant to
write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Japan
If PPC shows DIO forwarded the claim to the Japanese agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Japanese agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Japan Pension Service
NOTE: DIO is unable to determine the status of a Japanese benefits claim after a technician
forwards the claim to Japan. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
