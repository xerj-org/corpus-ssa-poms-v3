2. Mailing
If documents must be forwarded separately to another office (to avoid folding), clearly mark the envelope or mailing tube with
the legend: “To be associated with Claim 000-00-0000”. The other office should also be made aware of the two mailings to prevent unnecessary
development requests and to ensure that the two mailings are associated in the receiving
office.
