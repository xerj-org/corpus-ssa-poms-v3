C. PSC/OCO-ODO processing of SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request)
1. PUPS suspension or PUPS/POS suspension or reinstatement exclusion processing
Upon receipt of a Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request), the PSC/OCO-ODO will take
these steps to process the SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) for a PUPS/POS suspension
or reinstatement exclusion:
Steps
Actions on PUPS/POS suspension or reinstatement exclusions
1
Review the FO completed SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request). Specifically, review type
of PUPS exclusion under the EXCLUSION CATEGORY on the SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request).
The FO marks the block next to the “PUPS Automated Processing Exclusion” caption.
The FO also adds a remark in the REMARKS section of the form to explain the type of
PUPS exclusion the FO encountered during processing of a beneficiary’s new prisoner
information.
2
Review the SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) for any additional information that the
FO gathered that could affect your action.
3
Check the PRSNDATA line on the MBR to determine if a beneficiary’s recorded PRSNDATA
prisoner suspension periods correctly match their actual periods of prisoner suspension.
If not, annotate the correct the PRSNDATA information to include all prisoner suspension
periods.
4
Process your action based upon the remark that the FO annotated in the SSA-2790 (Prisoner
Fax Request) REMARKS section, your review of the PUPS record, and MBR. If the SSA-2790
(Prisoner Fax Request) remark indicates:
PUPS suspension exclusion remarks:
Continuous Period of Suspension—PUPS Exclusion. For PSC/OCO-ODO processing information,
see GN 02607.740D.1.b.
PUPS Earlier DOC (Conviction/Confinement) Exclusion—Continuous Period of Suspension.
For PSC/OCO-ODO processing information, see GN 02607.740D.3.b.
PUPS Later DOC (Date of Conviction/Confinement) Exclusion—Date of Confinement/Conviction
MM/DD/YY changed to MM/DD/YY based on new report. For PSC/OCO-ODO processing information,
see GN 02607.740E.3.b.
Go to Step 5 in this chart to continue to process PUPS suspension exclusion alerts.
PUPS/POS reinstatement
exclusion remark
T2 Reinstatement Exclusion Action Sent To PSC For Processing. For PSC/OCO-ODO processing
information for this reinstatement alert, see GN 02607.860A.3. Continue processing this exclusion per the steps in GN 02607.995C.2. in this section.
5
Review the PUPS record to determine if the FO requested a manual incentive payment
on the PUPS suspension exclusion case, if necessary. For information on instances
where you must request a manual incentive payment, see GN 02607.820A.
6
The FO should annotate a remark on the Prisoner Remarks (PRMK) screen if they requested
a manual incentive payment. If there is no indication on the PUPS record that the
FO requested a manual incentive payment and one is payable, forward the request to
the PSC Coordinator. For information on referring a case for a manual incentive payment,
see GN 02607.820B.
7
If a PUPS record requires a manual incentive payment, the PSC/OCO-ODO will annotate
the PUPS PRMK screen with the remark, “Manual incentive payment necessary. Case referred
to PSC coordinator on MM/DD/YYYY for manual incentive payment.” Prepare an email message
that includes the manual incentive payment information and send the email to your
PSC prisoner coordinator. For a list of PSC/OCO-ODO Prisoner Coordinators and contact
information, see GN 02607.990B.
8
If you are suspending the beneficiary’s benefits based on a PUPS suspension exclusion,
send them a notice explaining our decision to stop their monthly benefits. For additional
information of the beneficiary suspension or overpayment notice, see GN 02607.995C.3.b. in this section.
2. PSC/OCO-ODO PUPS/POS reinstatement exclusion processing
Upon receipt of a SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request), the PSC/OCO-ODO will take these
steps to process the SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) for a PUPS/POS reinstatement
exclusion record.
Steps
Actions for processing PUPS/POS reinstatement exclusions
1
Process the beneficiary’s reinstatement using Manual Adjustment, Credit and Award
Processes (MADCAP)/ Manual Adjustment Credit and Award Data Entry System (MACADE).
For information on PUPS/POS processing reinstatement exclusions, see GN 02607.860B.2.
2
Other PUPS/POS reinstatement exclusion processing reminders when reviewing the FO
referred SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) include:
Query Processing Center Action Control System (PCACS) or the Evidence Portal (EP)
to see if the T2R processing action (ACR) is pending, or query the Transaction History
Query (THIS). For information on THIS, see MS QUERIES 003.012. Assume there is a T2R
processing action (ACR) if PSCACS shows an ACR (TOEL2 of PRISON or PRISUS).
Reinstate benefits from LAF S7 on the MBR via MADCAP/
MACADE with the month after the release date on the PUPS record.
Update the PUPS report with TYPE OF ACTION 6 - CHANGE INFORMATION ONLY if *PENDING
is showing on the PUPS record. Make sure the PUPS record contains a RELEASED DATE
entry.
If there are no remarks on the beneficiary’s PUPS PRMK screen, add remarks to show
your review of the beneficiary’s official release documents. Add the date you faxed
the release document into NDRED using the Evidence Portal (EP). End the remark with
your first initial and last name, your SSA office designation code, and the current
date. Close the PUPS record after updating the release information and remarks. For
instructions on processing PUPS/POS reinstatement exclusions, see GN 02607.860B.2.
NOTE: If we incorrectly suspended the beneficiary as a prisoner and you are reinstating
their monthly benefits, review the beneficiary’s PUPS record to determine if we incorrectly
paid the correctional institution an incentive payment. Review the PUPS T2 Prisoner
Status Query (P2QY), to determine if PUPS paid an IP for the Title II prisoner report.
If PUPS paid an incentive payment, we must request the correctional institution to
return the $400 or $200 payment. For information on requesting return of incorrectly
paid incentive payments, see GN 02607.832.
3. PSC/OCO-ODO processing for PUPS T2R suspension exceptions
Upon receipt of a Form SSA-2790 (Prisoner Request Fax), the PSC/OCO-ODO will take
these steps to process the SSA-2790 (Prisoner Fax Request) for a PUPS T2R suspension
exception:
a. PUPS T2R suspension exception processing
The instructions in the chart provides steps for processing PUPS T2R suspension exceptions.
Steps
Actions necessary to process PUPS T2R exceptions
1
Query PCACS or EP to see if the T2R processing action ACR is pending, or query the
THIS. Assume there is an ACR if PCACS or EP shows a prisoner ACR (the ACR shows a
TOEL2 of PRISON or PRISUS).
2
Review the PUPS T2 Prisoner Status Query (P2QY) screen prior to taking a suspension
action to ensure the beneficiary’s due process period expired.
3
Process the suspension via MACADE/MADCAP based on the information shown on the T2R
exception output and on the beneficiary’s PUPS record.
4
If you are placing the beneficiary into suspension (LAF S7), leave the PUPS record
*PENDING. The feedback from the PRSN line on the MBR will close the PUPS record.
5
If you are not placing the beneficiary in continuing prisoner suspension, close the
*PENDING PUPS record. Check to see if you must manually request a manual incentive
payment. For information on requesting manual incentive payments, see GN 02607.820.
6
Suspend the benefits S7 on the MBR via MADCAP/MACADE for the appropriate period of
suspension. Add the RFST of “PRISON” for a prisoner, “MENTAL” for an NGRI or “PREDTR”
for a sexually dangerous person.
7
Send the beneficiary a notice explaining our decision to stop their monthly benefits
For additional information of the beneficiary suspension or overpayment notice, see
GN 02607.995C.3.b. in this section.
b. PSC/OCO-ODO suspension and overpayment notices
Prepare and send a suspension notice or a suspension and overpayment notice to the
beneficiary to explain our stopping their benefits for prisoner suspension. In your
notice, provide information on their prisoner suspension, and provide appeals language.
Include overpayment information if their benefits did not stop timely. For notice
information, see GN 02607.972.
