SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.505 - Representation of Applicants in Totalization Claims
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702505 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures in GN 03900.000 for representation of applicants involving claims for U.S. Totalization benefits.
Neither encourage nor discourage the use of representatives in claims for foreign
benefits. Assume the activities of the representative are legitimate under foreign
law.
