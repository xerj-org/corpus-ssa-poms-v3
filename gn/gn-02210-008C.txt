C. Recovery of title VIII overpayments from monthly title II and title XVI benefits
Under Social Security Act §808(e), we recover title VIII overpayments by decreasing a beneficiary’s monthly title II
benefits or title XVI payments by CPR. For information about CPR recovery of title
VIII overpayments from title II and title XVI benefits, see VB 02005.101.
