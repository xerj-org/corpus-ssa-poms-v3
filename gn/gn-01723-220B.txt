B. Process
The special application forms are not taken in the FO's. After receiving a claim for
French benefits that was filed with SSA, the French agency will mail the appropriate
French forms to the applicant. The French agency will include instructions asking
the claimant to complete the forms and mail them directly to the French agency along
with any additional evidence that may be needed.
