D. Overview of IDD for Sweden
IDD payments sent to Sweden must be in Swedish krona (SEK) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to IBP or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. IBP faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Sweden. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
