D. Applications for Romanian benefits
1. The application process
Along with the Form SSA-2490-BK, an applicant for Romanian benefits must complete
one or more special Romanian application forms. The form(s) required vary depending
on the type of benefit claimed.
FOs do not take the special Romanian application forms. After receiving an application
for Romanian benefits filed with SSA, the Romanian agency mails the appropriate Romanian
application forms directly to the applicant. The Romanian agency instructs the applicant
to complete the forms and mail them directly to the Romanian agency with any required
evidence needed in the application process.
2. Applicant brings Romanian special application forms to the FO
If an applicant brings any Romanian application forms to the FO, the FO should:
•
Accept, photocopy, and certify the photocopied application,
•
Photocopy and certify any additional documents (evidence) the Romanian agency requested
the applicant to include with the Romanian claim,
•
Request an Informational/Certified Earnings Record (ICERS) as described in MS
ICERS 02101.004 ,
•
Return to the applicant any original evidence documents and the certified photocopy
of the application, and
•
Send the original Romanian application and any certified photocopies or additional
claims material to the Central Processing - International Operations (CP/IO), International
Benefit & Processing (IBP) in Baltimore at the following address:
