A. Process
Birth records and death verification are available from all county health departments.
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the Court in the county or city where
the divorce was granted. Marriage records are available from the Clerk of the Court
in the county or city where the license was issued. The cost of obtaining copies of
these documents varies. A health department directory of Virginia county and city
vital records offices is available here.
