C. Using Evidence Submitted in Claim for U.S. Benefits
Evidence used to establish a factor of entitlement for U.S. Social Security benefits
is used by the Czech Republic to establish the same factor for Czech benefits. The
applicant will have to submit additional evidence to document a factor of entitlement
for Czech benefits even if that factor has already been verified for SSA purposes.
