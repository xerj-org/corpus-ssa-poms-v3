D. Child in care
The child in care question is necessary to establish Mother's or Father's benefits.
Do not list children who are students over age 16.
If a child is not living with the claimant, get an SSA-781 (Certificate of Responsibility
for Welfare and Care of Child not in Claimant's Custody) and an SSA-788 (Statement
of Care and Responsibility for Beneficiary) from the person with whom the child resides.
