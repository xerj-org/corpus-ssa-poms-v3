SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.374 - Coding Martinique Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 12/12/2023 - Present | TN 247 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402374 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI for Martinique has a prefix of "70" at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “U” assigned for Martinique.
