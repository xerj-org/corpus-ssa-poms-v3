C. Reporter responsibilities
When entering a Title II/Title XVI IPMOU, the reporting institution agrees to:
•
submit the Social Security Number (SSN) and other definitive identifying information
we need to verify each inmates identity;
•
submit an initial report containing total inmate population and subsequent annual
inmate census reports if SSA requests them; and
•
submit monthly reports of inmate admissions, convictions, and releases; and
•
notify us of changes in its staff and reporting problems that may affect the institution's
monthly inmate reporting cycle.
