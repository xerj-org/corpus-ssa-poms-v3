D. Overview of IDD for Cameroon
IDD payments sent to Cameroon must be in U.S. Dollars (USD) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Cameroon. The FBU
contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
