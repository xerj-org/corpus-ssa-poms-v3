SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00503.120 - Response Received to Advance Notice
Effective Dates: 08/14/2023 - Present | TN 14 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200503120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - reconsideration
Document the receipt of the reconsideration on the Add New Issue screen on eRPS.
Compare the information provided when the protest is filed with the evidence used
in the initial determination.
Request a face-to-face interview with the beneficiary to resolve any problems.
Document the rationale for the decision on the Protest Decision Details screen.
1. Reconsideration filed before effectuation
Record on the Protest Details screen that a “protest” has been filed to prevent effectuation,
per MS 07412.004.
2. Change in initial determination
All notices should be sent by the FO.
a. Capability issue
If the reconsideration involves the need for a payee and you decide that the beneficiary
is capable, initiate direct payment immediately. The system will send the correct
notice to the beneficiary if they had a payee.
If the beneficiary is in direct pay, send a manual reconsideration notice including
the hearing paragraph. Record the non-selection issue on eRPS and process the SSA-11
normally.
b. Selection of payee issues
If the issue is the selection of a specific payee and you decide to select a different
one, process the new SSA-11 in the normal procedure. The eRPS will send the beneficiary
the advance notice of the revised selection. Allow the beneficiary the normal time
to appeal the revised determination. Record the nonselection issue for the first applicant
on eRPS.
For title II cases, the T2R notice system will send the notices to the new payee,
beneficiary and former payee. The FO is responsible for sending the notices to the
new payee, former payee and beneficiary in title XVI change of payee situations. The
title XVI system will send these notices if the beneficiary was in direct pay. However,
the FO must send the SSA-623 when a final accounting is needed.
3. No change in initial determination
If you decide that the original decision is correct, effectuate the payee appointment
and send a manual formal reconsideration notice including the hearing paragraph.
If the issue is the need for a payee, use notice language similar to that in NL 00703.215.
If the issue is selection of the payee, use NL 00703.216.
Suppress the notice on title XVI cases and send the notices to the new payee, former
payee and beneficiary manually.
