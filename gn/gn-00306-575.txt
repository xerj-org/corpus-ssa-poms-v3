GN 00306.575 New York Intestacy Law
NOTE: Inheritance rights conferred in accordance with the provisions below are retroactive
under New York State law. Non-marital children who meet the criteria in GN 00306.575A.2. – GN 00306.575A.7. or GN 00306.575B.2. – GN 00306.575B.7. should be considered to have inheritance rights equivalent to those of children whose
parents are validly married.
