A. Definition of subsequent claim
A subsequent claim is an award of any type of benefit on a particular social security
number (SSN) on which a Primary Insurance Amounts (PIA) was previously established.
Awards after a T, M or Medicare-only (Hospital Insurance (HI)/Supplemental Medical
Insurance (SMI)) claims are also examples of a subsequent claim.
In addition, in some situations the claimant can file a new claim while awaiting a
final determination on a previously filed claim. (i.e., Disability (DIB) claim is
pending an appeal decision at the hearing level when the claimant files a Retirement
(RIB) claim).
