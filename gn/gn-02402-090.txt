GN 02402.090 Direct Deposit Cancellation
Direct deposit cancellations can occur when the beneficiary, recipient or representative
payee:
•
requests the cancellation,
•
indicates that fraud is involved,
•
the account is closed,
•
dies,
•
is having issues with financial institution regarding overdraft/fees, or
•
when cancelled by the financial institution.
NOTE: A change in the claims symbol or entitlement to a new benefit (e.g., BIC B to D
or BIC A to D) does not
cancel direct deposit.
