D. Policy — Adjudication responsibility under the Polish agreement
1. Field office responsibilities for claims under the Polish agreement
Claims Representatives (CRs) in the Field office (FO) are responsible for processing
an initial insured-status denial when the NH has at least 6 QCs but is not insured
based on U.S. coverage alone. (See GN 01702.315 for information about processing Totalization insured-status notices in claims for
U.S. benefits.) This denial will produce a notice advising the applicant that insured
status is not met based on U.S. coverage alone and that the foreign coverage record
is being requested. The FO will route the claim for final adjudication to the following
address:
Social Security Administration
OIO-Totalization
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, MD 21235-7769
2. Office of International Operations Responsibilities for claims under the Polish agreement
The Benefits Earnings Technician (BET) in the Office of International Operations (OIO)
will request the Polish coverage record. When the record is received, the Claims Authorizer
(CA) in OIO will convert the Polish coverage to QCs and finally determine insured
status.
