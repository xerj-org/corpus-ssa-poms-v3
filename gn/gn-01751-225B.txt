SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01751.225 - Requests for Status of Application for Polish Benefits
Effective Dates: 10/18/2022 - Present | TN 3 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201751225 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applicant requests status of a Polish claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of the
applicant's claim for Polish benefits and states that the applicant has not received
notification that DIO forwarded the claim to the Polish agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Poland
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Poland, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO- DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Poland
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Polish agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Polish agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Zaklad Ubezpieczen Spolecznych (Social Insurance Institution)
Kasa Rolniczego Ubezpieczenia
Spolecznego (KRUS) (Agricultural Social Security Fund)
DIO is unable to determine the status of a Polish claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Poland. For more information about status requests for Totalization claims,
see GN 01702.520.
