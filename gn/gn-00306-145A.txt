SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00306.145 - Child Legally Adopted by NH's Surviving Spouse (Benefits for 1/91 or Later)
Effective Dates: 01/04/2017 - Present | TN 26 (12-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200306145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Section 5104 of The Omnibus Budget and Reconciliation Act of 1990 (P.L. 101-508) liberalized
the entitlement requirements for a child who is legally adopted by a NH's surviving
spouse. See GN 00306.146 for prior law.
