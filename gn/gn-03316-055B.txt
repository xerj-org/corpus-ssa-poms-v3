B. To prepare electronic death reports
We assist many States in implementing the Electronic Death Registration (EDR) process.
At the front-end of the EDR process, we verify the SSN submitted by the State BVS.
After the State BVS obtains the SSN verification, the State BVS provides SSA the death
report connected with the verified SSN. When SSA obtains the death report connected
to the verified SSN, we are able to immediately terminate benefits without independently
verifying the accuracy of the report.
NOTE: We provide SSN verifications to State BVSs electronically via agreements between SSA
and the BVSs.
