A. INTRODUCTION
There are two liaison agencies in Portugal that are responsible for implementing the
Portuguese agreement. The Centro Nacional de Pensoes (NCP), located in Lisbon is the
principal agency. There also is a smaller agency in the Azores, Nucleo Coord. de Prest.
Diferidas, which processes claims under the agreement for residents and former residents
of the Azores.
