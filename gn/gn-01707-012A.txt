Effective Dates: 05/28/1997 - Present | TN 4 (07-96)

A. Policy — coverage
1. Employees
Coverage under one of the three major social insurance pension systems is compulsory
for most employees.
2. Self-employed
The Salaried Employees' and Wage Earners' Pension Insurance systems provide compulsory
coverage for a large number of self-employed persons.
