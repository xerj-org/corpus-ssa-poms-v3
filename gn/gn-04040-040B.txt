B. Policy Principle—Relationship to Administrative Finality
The requirements for reopening under the rules of administrative finality need not be met in order to grant
equitable relief, as the statute which rules on equitable relief takes precedence over the rules of
administrative finality, which are in the regulations. Therefore, equitable relief
can be granted regardless of when the error was made or how far back the claimant
should have been or was first entitled to SMI or premium HI.
