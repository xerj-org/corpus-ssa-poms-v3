D. Overview of IDD for New Caledonia
IDD payments sent to New Caledonia must be in CFP Franc (XPF) accounts at local FIs.
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves New Caledonia.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
