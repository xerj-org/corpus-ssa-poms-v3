B. Payment Center (PC) operating procedure
The PC employee must take the following actions to process a waiver of benefits request:
•
Referral—Refer waiver requests received in the (PC) to the servicing Field Office (FO) to
process.
•
Review—Review the SSA-149 for completeness and acceptability.
•
Suspension—If the request for waiver is acceptable, suspend payment to that particular beneficiary.
•
Adjustment—Do not adjust the benefits of other entitled beneficiaries because we do not make payments
to the beneficiary who waived payment.
•
Diary—If the beneficiary is in work suspense, or later reports that they expect to earn
over the allowable amount, establish controls to secure the annual report of earnings.
