A. Recomputation of additional periods of coverage
Italy counts both U.S. and Italian periods of coverage when computing Totalization
benefits. As a result, any additional periods of coverage the Number Holder (NH) earns
in either country after initial entitlement could increase the benefit amount. Under
the agreement, Italy will recompute a Totalization benefit to consider additional
U.S. QCs, or additional Italian coverage and pay any greater recomputed amount.
