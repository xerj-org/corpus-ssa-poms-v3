A. Policy - Compulsory coverage
Sweden's social security system covers virtually all employed and self-employed persons
in the private and public sectors, as well as all residents of Sweden, whether working
or not. In general, all persons residing in Sweden are covered under the basic pension
program. In addition, persons between the ages of 16 and 65 who work in Sweden are
covered under the ATP program for years in which their earnings exceed a base amount.
