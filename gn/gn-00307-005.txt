GN 00307.005 Role of FSPs - Process
FSPs assist in the development of claims and the evaluation of evidence.
Claims-taking FSPs will:
•
examine all evidence submitted through them in light of their knowledge of the recordkeeping
systems and practices in the country;
•
call irregularities to SSA's attention; and
•
resolve any discrepancies.
