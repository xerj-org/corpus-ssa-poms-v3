B. Process - HI entitlement
A TSC code of A1 on the MBR prevents automatic HI enrollment of any entitled wage
earner who does not meet the insured status requirements based only on U.S. coverage
or any totalization beneficiary who is entitled on the record of such a wage earner.
Establishing entitlement to HI for a beneficiary entitled on an earnings record with
a TSC of A1 requires a determination by a claims authorizer except where the beneficiary
is also entitled to a nontotalization benefit. See GN 01701.140for possibilities for
HI entitlement for totalization beneficiaries.
The PEPPER program produces alerts for beneficiaries with a TSC of A1. These alerts
are generated at age 64 years and 6 months and every month thereafter until an action
is taken. The PEPPER program cannot determine whether a totalization beneficiary is
eligible for HI/SMI. Thus, it alerts the case for a manual review. These alerts, identified
as “Totalization Case - Man Act Nec (Code 64)” are initially the responsibility of the EIE in the totalization modules.
