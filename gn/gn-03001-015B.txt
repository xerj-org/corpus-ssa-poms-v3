B. Policy - Final Notice
The beneficiary/claimant (or payee) must receive a final notice for all events that
result in an adverse action after the action is taken.
The notice must provide sufficient information for the beneficiary/claimant to make
an informed appeal decision, such as the:
•
action taken,
•
basis for the action,
•
appeal rights, and
•
waiver provision if the report results in an overpayment.
