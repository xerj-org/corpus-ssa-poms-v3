SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00904.300 - SSI applications for individuals who move to Puerto Rico or the U.S. Virgin islands
Effective Dates: 11/14/2008 - Present | TN 10 (05-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200904300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
1. Requesting office
Use the following instructions to complete Form HA-562-U3.
•
indicate the period(s) involved;
•
specify income, resource, and living arrangement/State supplementation issues and
the types of verification required, and
•
send all necessary forms and instructions for completion with Form SSA-562-U3.
Reminder: The name, area code, and telephone number of the requesting office contact person
should be included for any questions that may arise.
•
notify the Office of Analytics, Review, and Oversight (OARO) to route favorable SSI
ALJ decisions, where the claimant is now residing in Puerto Rico or the Virgin Islands,
to the mainland FO of last jurisdiction.
Note: This will save time in transferring the case from one office to the other and should
result in speedier effectuation of the cases.
2. Assisting office
Send a progress report to the requesting FO if the development will take more than
20 days.
Note: This procedure would be in line with the usual requests for assistance. This would
eliminate unnecessary followups by the requesting office.
3. Followups
Send all followups after thirty days to:
Social Security Administration
Area Director Federal Building
Room 751
Chardon Street Hato Rey, Puerto Rico 00918
Telephone Number: (809) 766-5685 or 5958
