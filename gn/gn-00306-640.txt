GN 00306.640 Texas Intestacy Laws
NOTE: If an alleged child does not qualify under any of the provisions in this section,
and it is not clear that the alleged father is not the child’s father, submit the
case for an Office of the General Counsel (OGC) opinion in accordance with GN 01010.815.
REMINDER: Under Texas law all provisions that confer inheritance rights but do not legitimate
the child, those preceded by an (I) in POMS GN 00306.640A. through GN 00306.640C., operate prospectively from the date of the act conferring those rights.
