GN 00505.024 Countries Which Require Special Payee Procedures - General
In order to assure that representative payees use the funds they receive in the best
interests of the beneficiaries, it has been necessary to modify and expand the representative
payee procedures and policies in certain areas. Except as noted in the following sections,
the procedures to be followed in representative payee cases in the countries listed
below will be the same as those for domestic cases.
