E. Cause of disability
If the person is claiming disability benefits from Portugal, enter in the “Remarks” section of Form SSA-2490-BK whether or not the claimants disability resulted from
a job-related injury. (Portugal needs this information to determine if it should pay
the claim under worker's compensation rather than the disability program.)
