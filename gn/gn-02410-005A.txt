A. Background
The Bankruptcy Reform Act of 1978 (BRA) created a new class of bankruptcy (Chapter
13) for debtors with regular income (including Social Security benefits). One provision
of the BRA allows bankruptcy courts to have debtors' income managed by court appointed
bankruptcy trustees. To effectuate that provision, some bankruptcy courts issue orders
to SSA to pay beneficiary/ debtor's benefits directly to a trustee.
