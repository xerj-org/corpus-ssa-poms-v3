D. When Must A Complaint Be Filed
A complete complaint must be filed no later than 180 days from the date of the last
act of alleged discrimination. Although the time limit for filing a complaint may
be extended by the Deputy Commissioner for Civil Rights and Equal Opportunity for
good cause, members of the public must be advised that they should not delay in filing
a complaint.
