SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01085.030 - Paper Retention Policy for the Paperless Program Service Center (PSC)
Effective Dates: 01/03/2025 - Present | TN 3 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201085030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1.
Set aside exclusions to the destruction process at the front-end of the process. Return
forms and documents to the beneficiary as appropriate. Associate all other forms excluded
from the destruction process with the paper claims file. Destroy the paper claims
file in accordance with current retention schedules.
2.
Destroy paper documents and forms that the PSC processed without imaging (e.g. direct
input work).
3.
Refer to the following chart for imaged documents. Retain documents contained in the
initial claims file, and exclusions shown in GN 01085.030B.
4.
Use the following guidelines for Title II forms and documents that are not listed
in GN 01085.030D:
a.
Computer Output – Destroy once the image is clearly readable.
b.
Responses/Inquiries - Destroy once the image is clearly readable.
c.
Statements - Destroy once the image is clearly readable, UNLESS it is part of the
initial claims file. If the statement is part of the initial claims file, then follow
the procedures in GN 01085.030C.1.
d.
Reporting Events - Destroy once the image is clearly readable.
