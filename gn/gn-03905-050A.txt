Effective Dates: 12/10/2019 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203905050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
When representatives request a change for the direct payment designation, we must
process the amended submission. Once we enter the affiliation information change into
RASR, the system automatically releases notices to appointed representatives and their
claimants. This section contains the notice related to a change in a representative's
direct payment information.
