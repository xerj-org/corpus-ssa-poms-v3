D. Overview of IDD for Republic of Congo
IDD payments sent to Republic of Congo must be in Central African CFA franc (XAF)
accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or
the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that
serves Republic of Congo. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional
information.
