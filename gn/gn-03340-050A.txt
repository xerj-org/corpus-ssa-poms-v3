A. Policy on access to investigatory records
Fraud and other investigatory records are exempt from the subject individual’s access
only when they are part of record maintained within an exempt system of records. 20 C.F.R. §
401.85 provides more information about exempt systems of records, including a list of exempt
system of records notice in section 401.85(b)(2).
