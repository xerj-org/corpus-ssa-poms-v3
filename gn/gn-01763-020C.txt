C. Administrative organization for Icelandic benefits
Tryggingastofnun (The Social Insurance Administration) operates the public pension system of Iceland.
If inquirers request additional information about Icelandic benefits, advise that
the Social Security Administration (SSA) cannot provide official information about
Icelandic benefits. Refer all inquiries about the Icelandic pension system to:
Tryggingastofnun (The Social Insurance
Administration)
Laugavegi 114
105 Reykjavík
Iceland
