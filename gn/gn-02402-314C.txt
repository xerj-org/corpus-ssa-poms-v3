C. Multi-country contract
Citibank is the processor bank for IDD to several countries, including the Bahama
Islands. All Caribbean countries for which Citibank is the processor bank have a prefix
of "70" at the beginning of the Routing Transit Number (RTN) and an assigned letter
in the first position of the Depositor Account Number (DAN). The letter assigned for
Bahama Islands is “D.”
