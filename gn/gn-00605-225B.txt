B. DESCRIPTION OF SSA-6233-BK - Question 8
Question 8 — What is the balance, including any interest earned, in the dedicated
account as of the last month in the report period? If none show zeros.—-[ ][ ],[ ]
[ ][ ]
If the payee has deposited other funds into the dedicated account, count the dedicated
account as a resource. Follow instructions in SI 01130.601C.1, if dedicated account funds have been commingled with other funds.
