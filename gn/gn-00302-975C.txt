C. Difficulty in obtaining records for 1909-1949 period
•
The organization's immigration records begin with 1909. However, records for immigrants
who entered the U.S. prior to 1950 are difficult to access and often illegible.
•
HIAS advises that:
–
For the period 1909-1949 they are able to locate only about 1 record for 50 searches,
and
–
For those who entered the U.S. between 1940 and 1950, only about 1 in 10 of the records
are found.
