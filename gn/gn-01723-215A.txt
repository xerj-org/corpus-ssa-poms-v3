SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01723.215 - Application for Benefits Under the French Agreement
Effective Dates: 07/08/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201723215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An applicant filing for benefits under the agreement with France must complete Form
SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social Security Agreement).
This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only from the United States,
only from France, or from both countries.
