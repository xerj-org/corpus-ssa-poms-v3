SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01701.130 - Crediting U.S. Quarters of Coverage (QCs) During the Year the Totalization Agreement Becomes Effective
Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 12 (09-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201701130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The number holder (NH) will be credited with the appropriate number of QCs in the
year the agreement becomes effective based on the NH's covered earnings for that year,
even if that results in crediting a QC for a calendar quarter after the quarter the
agreement becomes effective.
