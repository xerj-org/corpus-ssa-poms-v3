SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01708.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 01/18/2017 - Present | TN 3 (03-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201708010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
When taking applications under the agreement, the FSP should follow the same general
procedures and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA FOs. (See GN 01702.001 -
GN 01702.525 for FO and FSP development and GN 01707.205 for specific evidence and application requirements for the agreement with Germany.)
