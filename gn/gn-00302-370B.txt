SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.370 - Developing Evidence of Age of Eligible Worker When Uninsured Spouse Applies for HI
Effective Dates: 01/27/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302370 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Rule
It is often necessary to prove only that the worker is age 62 or older. Thus, unless the worker's DB affects the first month of HI coverage for the applicant
or affects insured status, the specific DB need not be determined as long as it is
clear that the worker is at least age 62.
