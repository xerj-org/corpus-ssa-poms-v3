B. Procedure - State Sources
1. FO Code
FO Code 860
You can find the most up-to-date address and vital record information from New Mexico Bureau of Vital Records. In addition, you can find EIN information here .
2. Cost of Copy:
Birth $10.00, Death $5.00, Marriage/divorce varies with County Clerk and/ or District
Court.
3. Type of Payment:
Money order preferred, no cash.
4. Remarks:
For birth records, use EVVE (GN 00302.980). If not successful, the FO should write directly to the State to obtain birth records.
Make birth record requests using SSA-L706.
Requests for death records should be on SSA letterhead and must include name of decedent,
date of birth, and date of death. If exact date of death is unknown, provide a span
of years to be searched.
If SSA is paying for the record (i.e., requirements for SSA purchase in GN 00301.210 are met or verification required by enumeration policy), include a third party draft
payable to “New Mexico Vital Records.”
The parallel FO cannot follow up on any requests.
Can FO verify, certify, or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No
