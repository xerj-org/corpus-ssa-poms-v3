SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02301.065 - Overpayment Owed by Deceased Beneficiary Due Title II Underpayment
Effective Dates: 12/10/2024 - Present | TN 16 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202301065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. The deceased beneficiary has an outstanding overpayment
If an overpaid beneficiary is deceased, we will withhold any underpayment to recover
the outstanding overpayment. We then issue any remaining underpayment to the proper
recipient(s) based on the order of priority listed in GN 02301.030.
For information on Title II overpayments, see GN 02201.001.
