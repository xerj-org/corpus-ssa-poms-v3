B. Applicant requests status of an Australian claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for status of his
or her claim for Australian benefits and states that he or she has not received notification
that DIO forwarded the claim to the Australian agency, check the PPC system. NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Australia
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Australia, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Australia
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Australia, advise the applicant to contact
the Australian agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
•
Department of Human
Services
•
Centrelink
DIO is unable to determine the status of an Australian claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Australia. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
