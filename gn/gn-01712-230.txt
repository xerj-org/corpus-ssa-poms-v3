GN 01712.230 Controlling Requests Sent to the FFU on Form SSA-2960-U2-NE - Procedure
Prepare a diary when information is requested from the FFU.
•
Diary all initial and followup requests for 60 days.
•
Forward the folder to DIO program analyst if no response is received after two followups,
i.e., after 180 days.
