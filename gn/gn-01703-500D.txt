D. Process - pepper alerts
When a totalization beneficiary nears age 65 or the 24th month of disability entitlement,
a PEPPER alert is generated. These alerts are sent to the PETLs in the totalization
modules. The Postentitlement Team Leaders (PETLs) refer the appropriate alerts to
the Exceptions and Inquiry Examiner (EIE.)
