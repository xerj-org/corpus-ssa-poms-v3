B. Definition of Concurrent Claim
For purposes of this subsection, "concurrent claim" means any claim or asserted right
under Title II of the Social Security Act when the claimant also filed a claim for
Title XVI payments, at either the same time or any time in the past, whether the Title
XVI claim was allowed or denied.
