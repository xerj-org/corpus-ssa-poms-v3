Effective Dates: 01/17/2017 - Present | TN 2 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203314085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disclosure policy
LIHEAP is a federally funded program that provides energy assistance, including weatherization,
to low income households. We may disclose certain non-tax return information, including
Social Security Number (SSN) verifications, to State or local agencies for their administration
of LIHEAP. Disclosure is permissible under the health maintenance and income maintenance
routine use cited in a number of the agency’s systems of records, including but not
limited to the following:
•
60-0058—Master Files of SSN Holders and SSN Applications System;
•
60-0089—Claims Folders System;
•
60-0090—Master Beneficiary Record; and
•
60-0103—Supplemental Security Income Record and Special Veterans Benefits System.
For information on the verification of SSNs, see GN 03325.002.
