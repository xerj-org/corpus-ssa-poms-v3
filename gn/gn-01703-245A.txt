SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01703.245 - Adjudicating Claims for U.S. Totalization Benefits After Foreign Coverage Record is Received
Effective Dates: 01/27/2017 - Present | TN 3 (02-95)

A. Introduction
After the foreign coverage record is received, the CS determines if the NH is insured
based on combined U.S. and foreign coverage. See the specific agreement subchapter
for rules on crediting foreign coverage.
