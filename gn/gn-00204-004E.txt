E. Using EC screens to determine I/S
When taking a RIB application, use the EC screens to determine I/S. Information on
I/S is located on the Insured Status (DEI1) and Yearly Earnings Data (DEIE) screens.
For detailed information on requesting an earnings comp preadjudicative request, see
MCSEC 001.004. For additional policy and procedures for using MCS adjudicative earnings
computation screens, see GN 01010.009.
