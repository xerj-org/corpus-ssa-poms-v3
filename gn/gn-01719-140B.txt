B. Exception for non-U.S. citizen dependents and survivors who meet an exception to the
ANP
If a claimant who does not meet another exception to the ANP alleges residence in
the United Kingdom, the field office (FO) should develop for the following:
•
residence status; and
•
refugee or stateless person status, if the claimant alleges such status, or in the
case of an auxiliary or survivor it is alleged that the worker is (or was at the time
of death) a refugee or stateless person; or
•
citizenship of the worker, if a dependent or survivor alleges that the worker is (or
was at the time of death) a U.S. or U.K. citizen.
