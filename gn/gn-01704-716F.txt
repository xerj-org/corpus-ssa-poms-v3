F. Diary periods and status requests
1.
Diary initial requests sent to the Norwegian and Belgian agencies for 6 months.
If no reply has been received when the diary is due, prepare a status request as follows:
a.
Print “Status Request” in red across the top of a new Form SSA-e2960-U2.
b.
Complete part I with the NH's name, SSN and foreign insurance number.
c.
Check other in part IV and enter “Photocopy of original request”—attach photocopy.
d.
Check “D.” in part V and enter date of the original request.
2.
Diary the status request for 90 days. If no response is received when the diary is
due, route the folder to TOAB.
