B. Applicant requests status of an Icelandic claim
If an applicant contacts the Social Security Administration (SSA) for the status of
his or her claim for Icelandic benefits and states that he or she has not received
notification that DIO forwarded the claim to the Icelandic agency, check the PPC system.
NOTE: Field offices are able to check the PPC system through the Evidence Portal (EP) application.
See MS 09701.001 for an overview of the EP application, and to access the EP User
Guide.
1. PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Iceland
If PPC does not show that DIO forwarded the claim to Iceland, advise the applicant
to write directly to DIO at the following address:
Social Security Administration
OEIO - DIO
P. O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
USA
2. PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to Iceland
If PPC shows that DIO forwarded the claim to the Icelandic agency, advise the applicant
to contact the Icelandic agency directly. Applicants can find contact and other information
online at:
Tryggingastofnun (Social Security Administration)
DIO is unable to determine the status of an Icelandic claim after a technician forwards
the claim to Iceland. For more information about status requests for Totalization
claims, see GN 01702.520.
