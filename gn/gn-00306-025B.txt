B. Policy — testimony makes child legitimate by later marriage
In some cases the testimony of the mother and/or her legal husband may tend to show
that the child is not the child of her husband at the time of conception, but tends
to make the child the legitimate child of the biological father by a marriage that
occurred after conception. If such a child cannot be entitled as a stepchild, submit
the case to the Office of the General Counsel (OGC) under GN 01010.800 ff. in the absence of a precedent opinion.
