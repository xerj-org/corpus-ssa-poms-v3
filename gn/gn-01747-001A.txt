A. Introduction
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Denmark:
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Danish benefits,
even though they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
