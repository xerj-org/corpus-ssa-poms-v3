SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 04030.050 - Administrative Finality – Recomputations and Recalculations
Effective Dates: 10/19/2010 - Present | TN 11 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0204030050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
Recomputation:
•
Is a total redoing of the computation, not just the addition of a high year and subtraction
of a low year. A recomputation involves using a year or years of earnings that were
not used in the initial base year period when computing the Primary Insurance Amount
(PIA).
•
Is a new initial determination based on having a new base year or years.
•
Is effective the January following the year of “new” additional earnings.
•
Must result in an increase in the PIA over the PIA of
record. (If the PIA is computed under the 1977 Amendments, the recomputation must result
in an increase of at least $1.00. See RS 00605.501). The PIA increase will be given the maximum retroactivity allowed. See RS 00605.401 for additional information on Recomputations.
NOTE
1: The imposition of the Windfall Elimination Provision (WEP) is an exception to the
$1.00 rule since a recomputation that imposes WEP will always reduce the PIA. Also,
even though the recomputation of a PIA must result in an increase in the PIA, there
may be situations where a PIA will increase but the Monhtly Benefit Amount (MBA) will
not increase because administrative finality applies to the incorrect MBA. The PIA
increase should be processed in these situations, even if the MBA does not change.
NOTE 2: Due to the Social Security Fairness Act (SSFA) of 2023, December 2023 is the last
month that WEP and Government Pension Offset (GPO) will apply. This means that those
rules no longer apply to benefits payable for January 2024 and later.
