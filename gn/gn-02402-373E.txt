E. Description of Latvia IDD bank data
The data to code IDD for Latvia comes from the International Bank Account Number (IBAN)
provided to the beneficiary from their FI. The IBAN for Latvia consists of 21 characters.
However, the bank data reflected on the MBR will reflect 24 characters including the
country code of G for Latvia.
