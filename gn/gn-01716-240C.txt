C. PROCEDURE — RESPONDING TO RRQ REQUESTS
Use the QUE/USA 2 to respond to RRQ requests for information instead of preparing
a new SSA-2960-U3-CQ. To do this:
•
Reverse the heading of the “TO”/“FROM” blocks at the top of the form,
•
Staple the requested material to the QUE/USA 2,
•
Place the material in an envelope addressed to the RRQ. Use a pre-printed label to
address the envelope.
