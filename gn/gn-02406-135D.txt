Effective Dates: 10/29/2008 - Present | TN 129 (02-25)

D. When a person receives the original and courtesy disbursement or replacement check
Ensure the beneficiary, recipient, or representative payee reporting the nonreceipt
understands that only one check is due for the benefit month. If they received both
the original and a courtesy disbursement or replacement check, they must return the
original. If both are cashed, a DCN overpayment exists.
Process returned Title II checks according to instructions in GN 02405.010. Code the lower left-hand corner with the appropriate information (DC for duplicate
checks) and return to the issuing RFC.
NOTE: Instructions for processing returned Title XVI recurring, courtesy disbursement
and replacement checks are located in SM 01315.011, SM 01315.012, and GN 02405.100.
