SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.475 - Coding Azerbaijan Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 12/27/2024 - Present | TN 284 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402475 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Azerbaijan has a prefix of 75 at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “J” assigned for Azerbaijan.
