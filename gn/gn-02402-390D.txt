D. Overview of IDD for Bangladesh
The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit
(FBU) for input. DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Bangladesh.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
