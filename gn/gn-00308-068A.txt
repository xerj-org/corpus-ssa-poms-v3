A. Process
Divorce records are available from the Clerk of the Court in the county where the
divorce took place. The cost of a record varies. Contact information for Clerks of
the Court is available here.
Marriage records are available from the probate judge in the county where the license
was issued. The cost of a record varies. Contact information for probate courts in
Ohio is available here.
Send birth and death records requests to the Health Department at the address shown
unless the records are prior to 1909, then send to the Probate Court. The cost of
the record varies.
For instructions about verifying birth records of U.S.-born individuals age one or
older who apply for an original SSN card or who request a change to the date of birth
on the Numident, please see GN
00308.068B.4.
