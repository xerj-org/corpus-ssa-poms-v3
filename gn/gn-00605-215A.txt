A. Introduction
Representative payees (payee) need certain identifying and financial information for
reference as they read the instructions and complete the questions on the Representative
Payee Report of Benefits and Dedicated Account, SSA-6233-BK. This section covers the
information that should be provided on the report based on your review of the Supplemental
Security Record (SSR). It also provides instructions for handling cases selected for
monitoring but, based on your review, you determine that the past-due benefits did
not meet the criteria for a dedicated account. (See GN 00605.215B.3. IMPORTANT below)
Payees who are exempt from the annual accounting requirement are not require to complete
the "Representative Payee Report of benefits" section of the SSA-6233-BK. The field
office (FO) must instruct the exempted payees to complete only questions 6-8 of the
SSA-6233. See GN 00605.015 for information on exempted payees.
