D. Overview of IDD for Lithuania
IDD payments sent to Lithuania must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Lithuania. The FBU contacts the
beneficiary or FI for additional information.
