C. Preparing material for the claims file
1.
Review each piece of material for completeness and make sure the NH's SSN is shown.
2.
All original documents that are transmitted with the file but which must later be
returned to the claimant should be inserted in an envelope and marked “return document to claimant.”
3.
Punch holes in the left margin of all material listed in GN 01050.150 D. items 1-11, not previously punched.
a.
The hole should be punched to line up with those prepunched on application and other
forms.
b.
Care should be taken to avoid obliterating any information with the holes.
c.
If it is impossible to punch holes without obliterating some information, either insert
the material in an envelope and mark “to be retained in file,” or attach it securely to a regular 8 1/2 by 11 piece of bond paper and punch holes
in the paper.
4.
Remove any staples that would impede the review of the material.
5.
Complete any explanation of special actions or other information on a Report of Contact.
If claim is being forwarded unadjudicated the reason should be explained on a Report
of Contact or Form SSA-3601.
