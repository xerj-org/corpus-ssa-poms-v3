D. Overview of IDD for Malta
IDD payments sent to Malta must be in Euro (EUR) accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Malta. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for additional information.
