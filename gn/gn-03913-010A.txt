A. Representative Registration
Registration information, such as a representative’s name, business address, and affiliations,
is centrally stored in the Registration, Appointment, and Services for Representatives
(RASR) application. Representatives must keep their registration information current.
Failure to do so may result in fee delays, incorrect or missing payments, interrupted
access to our electronic services and written communications, and errors on Forms
IRS 1099-Miscellaneous Income (MISC) or 1099-Nonemployee Compensation (NEC).
