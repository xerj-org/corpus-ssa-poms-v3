B. Requests from external parties to use SSA data for research and statistical purposes
To request SSA data for research purposes, the requester must submit their request
to ODXIA. ODXIA will coordinate the processing of the request amongst agency stakeholders
to determine:
•
the feasibility and resource implications on the agency;
•
if the agency has authority to disclose the data sought;
•
if reimbursement is necessary; and
•
whether or not the requester can or does comply with Federal Information Security
Systems requirements and will agree to our safeguarding and data security requirements,
etc.
Once the appropriate components make a determination on the request, ODXIA will send
a formal response to the requester.
