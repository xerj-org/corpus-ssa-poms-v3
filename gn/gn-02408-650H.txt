SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.650 - Title II Beneficiary Receiving EFT Payments Dies After the End of the Month but Before the Payment Date
Effective Dates: 04/19/2022 - Present | TN 77 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408650 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Examples of underpayments and assets to estates
1. Example 1
Paula Mee was receiving benefits in Cycle 1. The bank deposited their benefits on
June 2, 2006; however, they died on June 1, 2006. The bank returned the monies to
SSA on June 6, 2006 after learning of their death. This is an underpayment since the deposit was a post-death payment and Paula was eligible for May’s benefits
paid in June.
2. Example 2
Jack Mints was receiving their disability benefits in Cycle 2. The bank deposited
their benefits on January 10, 2007. Jack Mints died January 20, 2007. The bank returned
the monies to SSA on January 23, 2007 after learning of their death. This is an asset to the estate since the deposit was a pre-death payment and Jack was eligible for December’s benefits
paid in January.
3. Example 3
Meg Waters, a widow, received their retirement benefits in Cycle 3. The bank deposited
their February benefits on March 19, 2008. Meg Waters died on March 16, 2008. The
bank returned the monies to SSA on March 27, 2008 after learning of Meg Waters death.
This is an underpayment since the deposit was a post-death payment and Meg was eligible for February benefits
paid in March.
4. Example 4
Greg Lakes received their retirement benefits in Cycle 4. The bank deposited their
December benefits on January 28, 2009. Greg Lakes died on January 29, 2009. The bank
returned the monies to SSA on February 5, 2009 after learning of their death. This
is an asset to the estate since the deposit was a pre-death payment and Greg was eligible for December’s benefits
paid in January.
