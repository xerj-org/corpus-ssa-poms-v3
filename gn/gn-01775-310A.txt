A. Background for liaison agency requests a medical examination
If the Romanian liaison agency National House of Public Pensions requests a medical
examination for a U.S. resident, the Social Security Administration (SSA) will schedule
the examination and send the results to the liaison agency. The liaison agency requests
medical examinations using the Form SSA e2960 USA/RO 3 (U.S. Romania Agreement on Social
Security Transmittal/Request/Certification).
