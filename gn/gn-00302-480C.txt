C. Comment
The selection of dates shown by the chart below is entirely arbitrary and is designed
to establish some conformity in DB determinations where no discrepancy exists or where
a discrepancy has been resolved and a nondiscrepant element exists only in the NUMIDENT
or other SSA records
The chart should not be used if:
•
The month and day are material, and
•
Evidence is known to exist that will supply the missing element(s).
