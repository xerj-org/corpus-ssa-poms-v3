C. Filing of Claim
Aside from the issue concerning the person qualified to receive an underpayment, all
other aspects of a claim for reimbursement will be developed in the same manner as
if filed by a living beneficiary. Accordingly, if the beneficiary had not filed a
claim prior to death, a CMS-1490S (Patient's Request for Medical Payment) or CMS-1450
(Medicare Uniform Institutional Provider Bill) depending on the type of service rendered
the beneficiary, is required. The claimant should sign the claimant's own name in
the space provided. If the beneficiary had filed a claim before death, it is not necessary
for a person claiming the underpayment to complete another. Any written request for
the underpayment will suffice.
See Form CMS-1490S Patient's Request For Medical Payment https://www.cms.gov/medicare/cms-forms/cms-forms/downloads/cms1490s-english.pdf See Form CMS-1450 Medicare Uniform Institutional Provider Bill https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/legislation/paperworkreductionactof1995/pra-listing-items/cms-14
