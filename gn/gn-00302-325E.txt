E. Change of position
Since the circumstances under which the incorrect DB's were established were extraordinary
and invariably involved a life-threatening situation, the Secretary of Health and
Human Services released a statement on 9/10/80 which announced a change of position
regarding evidence requirements for proving the age of Holocaust survivors.
Benefits are payable pursuant to this change of position for months beginning no earlier
than 9/80.
