SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01710.315 - Special Applications for Swiss Benefits for Persons Residing Outside Switzerland
Effective Dates: 04/10/2017 - Present | TN 3 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201710315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow this procedure for the proper handing of claims for Swiss benefits.
1. Swiss national
If the claimant is a Swiss national:
•
Advise the claimant to contact a Swiss consular office in the U.S. or the Swiss agency
in Geneva to complete an additional application.
•
Check the first block in Part V of Form SSA-2960-U3-SZ and enter the date.
2. U.S. National, refugee, or Stateless person in U.S.
If the claimant is a U.S national or a refugee or a stateless person residing in the
U.S.:
•
Send the claimant an application Form CH/USA 2.1 (for old age pension), CH/USA 2.2
(for survivors' pension) or CH/USA 2.3 (for disability insurance) along with a return
envelope pre-addressed for P.O. Box 17049.
•
Show the U.S. SSN in the upper right corner of the application form.
•
Follow the instructions in GN 01710.316.
3. Third Country involvement
If the claimant is a refugee or stateless person residing in a third country or a
third country national who cannot derive rights, enter the remark found in the last
bullet in
GN 01710.220D.7. on Form SSA-2960-U3-SZ
4. Third country national with derived rights
If the claimant is a third country national who can derive rights, follow the procedure
above for U.S. national. (See GN 01710.317 for an explanation of derived rights.)
