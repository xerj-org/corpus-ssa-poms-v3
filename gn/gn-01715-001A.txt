A. Introduction
The Social Security agreement between the United States and Canada:
•
eliminates dual Social Security coverage and taxation of the same work, and
•
permits workers (and their family members) to qualify for partial U.S. or Canadian
benefits, even though they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
