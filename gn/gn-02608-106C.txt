SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02608.106 - Exemption Due to Pension Eligibility In November 1982 or June 1983
Effective Dates: 09/12/2025 - Present | TN 62 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202608106 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Proofs for exemption
Obtain the following documentation before applying the exemption:
1. For a pension in November 1982
•
evidence that the spouse met the 1977 entitlement requirements (i.e., the 20-year
duration of marriage for; or the one-half support test for men); and
•
evidence of pension eligibility or entitlement in November 1982 from the employer
or pension paying agency
2. For a pension in June 1983
•
evidence that the spouse met the one-half support requirement; and
•
evidence of pension eligibility or entitlement in June 1983 from the employer or pension
paying agency.
You may use Civil Service Annuity (CSA) numbers as evidence of pension eligibility.
For more information on CSA numbers, see GN 02608.100C.5.
