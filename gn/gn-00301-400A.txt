SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00301.400 - Extract Translations
Effective Dates: 10/27/2022 - Present | TN 84 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200301400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Only authorized SSA translators can prepare extract translations.
1. Prescribed Forms
The prescribed forms are as follows:
•
Form SSA-533 (Translation Request). Translate all required information on the reverse
(translation) side.
•
SSA-4385 (Certification of Contents of Court Records) in accordance with instructions
in GN 00301.115E.
•
The EVID screen, the “RPOC” screen in MCS, or the Report of Contact page in the Consolidated
Claims Experience (CCE). in PE situations. (See GN 00301.286, GN 00301.287, and GN 00301.288)
2. “Translation Stamp”
A “translation stamp” can be used as an alternative method of documenting a translation.
Translate pertinent data from commonly presented proofs (e.g., birth and baptismal
certificates, death and marriage certificates) on reverse side of SSA's retained photocopy
of the foreign- language document. The format may be stamped or handwritten. See the
exhibit in GN 00301.400B.
