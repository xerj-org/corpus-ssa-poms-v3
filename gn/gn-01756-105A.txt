A. Policy for forwarding certified coverage records
The Hungarian certified coverage record is the Form HU-205 (Certificate Concerning
the History of Insurance in Hungary). It is the only acceptable form for the certification
of a Hungarian coverage record. Forward certified coverage records in any other format
to the Office of Earnings and International Operations, Division of Training and Program
Support for approval.
