C. Process - Non-Recurring Payments
Checks for PMA, CPS, OTP, Type 2E underpayments, etc., should be received by the beneficiary
within 7-10 calendar days (not including Sundays and holidays) after the systems run
processing date shown on the PHUS or SSR.
