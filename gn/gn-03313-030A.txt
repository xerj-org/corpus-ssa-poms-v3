A. Disclosures to administer HUD programs
HUD provides subsidized housing assistance programs for low-income individuals who
meet HUD eligibility requirements. HUD subsidized housing programs meet our criteria
for an income maintenance program.
We may disclose certain tax return and non-tax return information to HUD for their
administration of subsidized housing programs. For further instructions about disclosures
of tax return information without consent to HUD, see GN 03320.015.
