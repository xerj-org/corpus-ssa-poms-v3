D. Overview of IDD for Liechtenstein
IDD payments sent to Liechtenstein  must be in Swiss Franc (CHF) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Liechtenstein
. The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
