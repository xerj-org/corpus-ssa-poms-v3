SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01742.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Veterans Administration Regional Office (VARO) in Manila
Effective Dates: 07/10/2001 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201742010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
VARO takes claims from individuals in their service area who wish to file for U.S.
and/or Korean benefits under the Korean agreement and also assists in processing certain
types of claims filed with the NPC.
When taking claims under the agreement, VARO should follow the same general procedures
and obtain the same applications and evidence as SSA field offices.
