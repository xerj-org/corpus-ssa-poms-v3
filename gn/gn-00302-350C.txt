SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.350 - Foreign Record Requested by a U.S. Resident
Effective Dates: 07/27/2012 - Present | TN 26 (11-18)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302350 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - PC
The PC will take the following action when the document is received or the diary matures.
1. Record or Negative Reply Received
When the record or negative reply is received:
a.
compare the interim date of birth and the date on the foreign record,
b.
revise the original date of birth finding, if necessary,
c.
send the claimant written notice that the interim date of birth has either been confirmed
or changed, and
d.
cancel the diary.
2. Diary Matures Before Information is Received
If the diary matures and the requested material has not been received, review the
MCS screens to determine if the material has been received and acted on by the FO.
If the material has not been received by the FO, contact the claimant to see if the
record was sent to him or her.
•
If the record was sent to the claimant, have him or her forward it to SSA.
•
If the record was not sent to the claimant follow up using the instructions in 3 below.
3. Special Assistance Request Form, SSA-2111-U3-FC
Use the SSA-2111-U3-FC only to follow up on a claimant's request for a civil or religious
record of birth from the custodian of the record. Do not use to request initial development.
If necessary, request assistance from the Claims Development Branch in DIO to develop
evidence of age or to resolve a discrepancy, using an SSA-562-U3. Complete the
Form SSA-2111-U3-FC as follows:
a.
Complete all items on the front side of the SSA-2111-U3-FC (if any of the information
is not known, enter “unknown”) (see exhibit in GN 00302.363).
b.
Forward the original and first copy to:
Office of International Operations
Social Security Administration
Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235- 7769.
c.
File the second copy of the form in the claims folder.
d.
Diary for 90 days pending receipt of the response to the SSA- 2111-U3-FC.
e.
If a reply has not been received at the end of the diary period:
•
prepare another SSA-2111-U3-FC,
•
In the heading after “SPECIAL ASSISTANCE REQUEST” annotate: Followup on Request
Dated        , and
•
diary for another 90 days.
