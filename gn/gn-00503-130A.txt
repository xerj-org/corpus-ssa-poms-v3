A. Policy
Any time a payee decision is effectuated, the beneficiary or the person who can act
for the beneficiary must be given formal notice of the action. This notice does not
contain appeal rights if an advance notice was sent to the addressee or if the addressee
does not have appeal rights per GN 00503.110A.2.
EXCEPTION: Formal notices to beneficiaries in foreign countries provide appeal rights because
we do not send an advance notice.
We send courtesy notices to unsuccessful applicants to advise them of the decision.
This notice does not provide any information on who was selected or why, so disclosure
policy is not violated.
