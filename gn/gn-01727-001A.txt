A. Purpose of the U.S. - Portugal Totalization Agreement
The social security Agreement between the United States and Portugal:
1.
Eliminates dual social security coverage and taxation of the same work in the two
countries; and
2.
May permit workers (and their dependents and survivors) to qualify for partial U.S.
or Portuguese benefits, even if they do not meet the normal insured status requirements.
