C. Adjudicating the claim in the United States
1. Field Office (FO) responsibilities
When a Number Holder (NH) does not have enough U.S. coverage to meet fully insured
status, but the NH has at least six U.S. QCs, field office (FO) technicians must take
an initial claim to process the insured status denial. This will automatically generate
a denial notice to inform the applicant that they do not meet the insured status requirement
based on U.S. coverage alone, and that we will request the applicant's Romanian earnings
record. See GN
01702.310 - GN 01702.525 for additional information about processing, developing, and routing claims for foreign
and U.S. Totalization benefits.
The FO should route claims that cannot be electronically routed according to the instructions
in GN 01702.310B.3 to the following address:
Social Security Administration
International Benefit & Processing
P.O. Box 17769 Baltimore
Maryland 21235-7769
If a courier service is used, mail to:
Social Security Administration
International Benefit & Processing
6100 Wabash Avenue Baltimore
Maryland 21235
2. International Benefit & Processing (IBP) responsibilities
IBP requests the Romanian earnings record, converts Romanian periods of coverage on
the Romanian earnings record to U.S. QC's, and adjudicates the claim.
