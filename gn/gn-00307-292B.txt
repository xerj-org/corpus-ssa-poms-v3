SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00307.292 - Use of Form SSA-308 Modified Benefit Formula Questionnaire — Foreign Pension
Effective Dates: 04/24/2025 - Present | TN 152 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200307292 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Obtain form SSA-308 from a claimant who is first eligible for a foreign pension after
1985 and who is entitled or will be entitled to the foreign pension prior to January
2024 based on an application filed with the appropriate foreign agency/ employer.
