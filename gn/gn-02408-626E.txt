SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02408.626 - Processing the Notice of Reclamation (FS-133) and the Request for Debit of the Financial Institution's (FI) Account (FS-135) for Title II Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Payments by the Program Service Centers
Effective Dates: 03/24/2023 - Present | TN 75 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202408626 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Office of the Inspector General (OIG) referrals
OIG generally investigates fraud cases that meet the prosecutive acceptance guidelines
for federal judicial district where the fraud occurred.
When more than $5,000 in incorrect payments exists because they are unavailable to
reclaim after SSA has applied any credits received on an FS-133 or SSA has completed
the subsequent reclamations process found in GN 02408.410:
1.
The technician refers the case to OIG via the Allegation Referral Intake System (ARIS) process prior to contacting the last withdrawer, after applying any credits from
the FS-133 and a balance of more than $5,000 remains.
2.
Diary control the case for 30 days. The technician recontacts OIG directly when no
response is received after 30 days.
3.
Process the FS-133 per GN 02408.626B.2. when OIG does not open a case.
