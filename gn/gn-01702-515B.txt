SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01702.515 - Applicability of Annual Earnings Test/Foreign Work Test
Effective Dates: 03/24/2004 - Present | TN 2 (03-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201702515 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Totalization agreements modify the normal rules that determine whether a person’s
work in an agreement country is covered and may, therefore, affect a determination
as to which retirement test applies. If work which would otherwise be covered is not
covered because the agreement rules provide it is subject only to foreign social security
law, the foreign work test applies.
