SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01050.060 - Priority of Jurisdiction - Two Processing Centers Involved
Effective Dates: 08/14/2023 - Present | TN 13 (08-23)

D. New claims without foreign or disability involvement
New claims without foreign or disability involvement which are cross-referred to previously
adjudicated claims will be forwarded by the DO to the PSC which services the previously
adjudicated claim except when the two claims are both RIB claims (see the Claims Routing
Guide in GN 01050.075).
In most cases, if RIB claims of a married couple are filed or developed simultaneously,
and different processing centers are involved, each will be sent to the processing
center having jurisdiction of that case. If, however, the spouse is entitled to spouse's
benefits, both cases will be sent to the PSC servicing the claim on which the individual
files the spouse's benefits. After PSC adjudicative action, the RIB claim of the individual
who filed the spouse’s benefits will be sent to the PSC servicing their RIB claim
in accordance with GN 01050.075 for payment and filing. Post-entitlement information pertaining to the RIB claim
of the individual who filed the spouse’s benefits should be sent to the PSC servicing
their RIB claim.
