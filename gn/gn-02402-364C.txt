SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.364 - Coding Dominican Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
Effective Dates: 05/05/2021 - Present | TN 255 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402364 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Dominica will have a prefix of “66” at the beginning of the Routing
Transit Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field
has the letter “W” assigned for Dominica.
