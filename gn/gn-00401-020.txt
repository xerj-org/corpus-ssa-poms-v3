GN 00401.020 Facility Closures Due to Disasters
When a disaster situation causes a facility to close for any length of time, notify
other offices and components of the unscheduled closure as quickly as possible.
Post notice of the closure through:
•
InfoCast
•
Enhanced Leads and Appointment System (eLAS)
•
Media
•
Main entrance
