D. Adult individual overpaid as a minor child with a representative payee
If we overpaid a minor child who is now an adult and the individual had a representative
payee at the time of the overpayment, we will find they are not at fault (refer to
GN 02250.016B.7.). We will deem recovery is against equity and good conscience.
NOTE:
The waiver would apply only to the overpayment balance remaining once the individual
attained age 18.
