C. Processing claims inquiries
For module and IBO processing of regular RSDI claims inquiries also apply to totalization
claims inquiries, except when the inquiry concerns a country with which SSA has no
effective agreement. IBO refers such inquiries to the module where the replies are
prepared. Do not send these inquiries to DO's or FSP's for processing. Suggested language
for responding to inquiries of this nature is contained in NL 00711.000 ff.
