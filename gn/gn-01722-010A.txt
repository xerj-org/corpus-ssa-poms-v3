SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01722.010 - How to Develop and Process Applications Filed in the Foreign Service Post (FSP)
Effective Dates: 01/13/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201722010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The FSP in Madrid takes claims from individuals who wish to file for benefits under
the Spanish agreement and also assists in processing certain types of claims filed
with a Spanish agency. When taking applications under the agreement, the FSP follows
the same general procedures and obtains the same applications and evidence as SSA
field offices. (See GN 01702.001 - GN 01702.525 for DO and FSP Development and GN 01721.205 - GN 01721.215 for Evidence and Application Requirements.)
