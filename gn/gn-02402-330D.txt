D. Overview of IDD for New Zealand
IDD payments sent to New Zealand must be in New Zealand Dollar (NZD) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves New Zealand.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
