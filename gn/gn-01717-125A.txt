SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01717.125 - Eligibility for Swedish Benefits Under the Agreement
Effective Dates: 09/09/1993 - Present | TN 1 (09-93)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201717125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy- General
The agreement provides that Sweden will count a worker's U.S. coverage when determining
eligibility for Swedish benefits if the worker:
•
is not insured based on Swedish coverage alone, and
•
has at least one year of ATP coverage or pre-1960 Swedish tax assessment.
1. ATP Pension
U.S. QC's can be used to meet the 3-year ATP coverage requirement regardless of the
claimant's or worker's nationality.
2. Basic Pension
U.S. nationals, refugees, and stateless persons (or their
dependents or survivors) may qualify for basic pensions on the same basis as Swedish
nationals living outside Sweden. Consequently, Swedish basic pensions are payable
if ATP pension eligibility is met, even when U.S. QC's are used to meet the ATP eligibility
requirements.
