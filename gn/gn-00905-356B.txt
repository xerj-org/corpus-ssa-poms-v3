SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00905.356 - Personal Contact Requirement in the Philippines
Effective Dates: 04/17/2018 - Present | TN 10 (01-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200905356 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Since the FBU takes all claims by personal contact, assume that the personal contact
has been made. (The FBU will document the claims file only when this contact has not
been made.)
Exception: Take follow-up action where the FBU has indicated no personal contact was made if
there is third-party involvement and no indication that the FBU noted this involvement
for appropriate action.
•
Third Party Not One Listed in GN 00307.764 and No Fraud Indicated - Check Will Be
at Least $5,000
Process the award under normal procedures.
Advise the FBU of the third-party involvement and request it to document the file
fully about the personal contact before releasing the check.
•
Third Party Listed in GN 00307.764 or Fraud Involved - Initial Check Will be Less
Than $5,000
Request a personal contact and any other indicated development (e.g., validation of
evidence) before approving an award.
