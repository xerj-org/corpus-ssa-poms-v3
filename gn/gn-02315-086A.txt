A. Background for Virgin Islands small estates
The Virgin Island statute relating to small estates provides the procedures that individuals
may use to settle small estates without formal administration. For purposes of receiving
Title II or Black Lung payments, we consider an individual authorized to act pursuant
to these procedures a “legal representative” of the estate.
