SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01738.012 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with the Greek Agency
Effective Dates: 08/29/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201738012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
IKA.
1. Claimant Not Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Obtain Form SSA-2490-F4 (Part 1), the appropriate RSDI applications, and any additional
evidence needed, and forward the claim to DIO with the Form GR/ USA 3 and the Greek
coverage record.
Important: When IKA completes the GR/USA 3, they may indicate they have verified certain eligibility
factors (see GN
01738.235). Verifications done by IKA may be accepted in accordance with GN 01701.320 except for verification of date of birth for claimants born in Greece. Evidence of
date of birth for Greek-born claimants must be developed in accordance with GN 00307.518
- GN 00307.525 even if IKA has verified the date of birth.
2. Claimant Already Entitled to U.S. Benefits
Forward all material to DIO annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits.”
