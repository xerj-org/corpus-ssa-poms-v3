B. Process
1. When the Modernized Claim System (MCS) Version May Be Used
The SSA-2490-BK is available through the modernized claims systems (MCS), but the
MCS version may only be used when the number holder (NH) is filing for:
•
U.S. retirement or disability Totalization benefits only, or
•
U.S. retirement or disability benefits (regular or Totalization) and concurrently
for Belgian benefits.
2. When the MCS Version May Not Be Used
The MCS version of the SSA-2490-BK may not be used to file for:
•
Belgian benefits only, or
•
U.S. or Belgian survivors benefits.
A paper or electronic (e-form) SSA-2490-BK application form must be used in these
cases. Form SSA-2490-BK is also available through the Totalization Benefits Resource
Kit on the Intranet here.
NOTE: It is not necessary to take an SSA-2490-BK in claims for U.S. auxiliary benefits
in life cases or in survivor cases where there was a previously approved award in
a life case since this application would have been completed previously in connection
with the NH's claim.
