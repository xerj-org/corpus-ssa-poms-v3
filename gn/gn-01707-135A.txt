SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.135 - Making Voluntary Contributions to the German System — General
Effective Dates: 05/28/1997 - Present | TN 4 (07-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. General
The agreement permits U.S. nationals who meet certain qualifying conditions to make
voluntary contributions to the German Social Security system. These voluntary contributions
may be used to help establish entitlement to a German benefit or to increase the amount
of an existing benefit.
2. Who can qualify
U.S. nationals who reside outside Germany and who have contributed to the German system
for at least 60 months, or who were eligible to contribute voluntarily immediately
prior to October 19, 1972, may pay German voluntary contributions. Similar rights
are provided for refugees and stateless persons who reside in the U.S.
To be eligible, the person must:
•
be at least age 16,
•
not be compulsorily covered by Germany or receiving retirement benefits from Germany,
and
•
file an application by the end of the year for which the contributions are made.
