A. Introduction
The agreement with Canada includes a main agreement, which applies to Canada's Old
Age Security program and the Canada Pension Plan, and an additional understanding
that extends the agreement to the provincial- level Quebec Pension Plan. The agreement
with Canada, including the understanding with Quebec, became effective August 1, 1984.
A supplementary agreement, which revises several provisions of the original agreement,
became effective October 1, 1997.
