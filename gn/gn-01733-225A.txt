A. Automatic status notice for an application for Luxembourg benefits
When the Division of International Operations (DIO) forwards information about a Luxembourg
claim, certified copies of evidence, and a copy of the worker’s U.S. coverage record
to Luxembourg, the Benefit Earnings Technician (BET) electronically stores the claims
material in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system in DIO before forwarding
the claim to the Luxembourg agency. The system automatically generates a notice that
the BET sends to the applicant.
