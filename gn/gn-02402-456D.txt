D. Overview of IDD for Maldives
IDD payments sent to Maldives must be in Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR) accounts at local
FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits
Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Maldives.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
