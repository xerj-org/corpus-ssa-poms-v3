SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00302.170 - How to Resolve Material DB Discrepancies
Effective Dates: 10/06/1994 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200302170 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Important
Ordinarily you will request the record likely to be highest in probative value (i.e.,
best evidence) according to the guidelines in GN 00302.165. However, if the delay and/or expense to obtain such evidence would be excessive
and, based on the probative value of the evidence already presented, it is not clear that the evidence you are considering requesting is best or would affect the
DB determination if it were obtained, then it need not be requested.
