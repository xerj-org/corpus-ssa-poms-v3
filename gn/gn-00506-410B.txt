Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200506410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
A beneficiary’s record may change from DI to AI or DIB to RIB upon attainment of age
65. Therefore, the payee should stop collecting the higher DAA fee the month the beneficiary
attains age 65 and there’s a change in the factors of entitlement.
FO’s should use the SSI redetermination process and site reviews to determine age
65 attainment. Inform the beneficiary and the payee of this change in fee amounts
using the notice provided in NL 00703.774 Representative Payee — Change in Authorized Fee — Notice to Beneficiary or Person
Standing in Place of Beneficiary .
