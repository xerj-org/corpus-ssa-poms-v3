B. Process for retrieving information and documents from the SSANRC IRG
If the PCACS response contains a “CAVE FILE” reference in the LOCATION field, send
an administrative message (ADMS) to the SSANRC IRG by selecting #16 from the SSA Main
Menu in PCOM; then on the Administrative Applications Menu Screen (ADMM) select function
#15.
Use the following format for the ADMS (see example):
•
List Addressee Routing Indictors: SAXHX
•
To: Cave File (enter location number); IRG BA or CA
•
From: Your name; your position; FO, WSU, or PC and module or component; telephone number;
email; fax number; routing indicator
•
Subject: SSN and NH Name
•
Text: Date and time, briefly summarize the required information and document(s).
For control purposes, maintain a copy of the ADMS electronically. See the following
example of the ADMS:
View In PDF
•
If the requested information is stored electronically, the IRG technician will contact
you via telephone or email.
•
The IRG technician will fax, email, or upload the requested information to the electronic
folder, if unable to provide the necessary information over the telephone.
•
If instructed by the IRG technician, check the electronic folder and evaluate the
material/evidence uploaded to determine if it is adequate to process the pending action.
•
After the IRG responds to the request, and if you still need the paper claims folder,
refer to the folder request guidance on the SSANRC homepage. .
•
Typically, the SSANRC IRG responds within two days. If the SSANRC IRG does not respond
within five full workdays after transmission of the ADMS, contact the IRG Quality
Assurance Technician (QAT) at (888) 281-2999.
