D. Overview of IDD for Martinique
IDD payments sent to Martinique must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO will fax incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Martinique. The FBU contacts
the beneficiary or FI for additional information.
