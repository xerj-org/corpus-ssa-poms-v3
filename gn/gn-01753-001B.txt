B. Purpose of Slovak Republic Totalization instructions
This Program Operations Manual System subchapter GN 01753 provides:
•
a brief overview of the Slovak system;
•
the instructions to take and process claims for both U.S. and Slovak benefits filed
under the agreement; and
•
an explanation of the provisions of the agreement.
NOTE: For additional information on the Slovak social security system, visit us online
at International Programs.
