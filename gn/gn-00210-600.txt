GN 00210.600 Same-Sex Relationships - Lump Sum Death Payment (LSDP) Claims
Because of the Windsor and Obergefell decisions, we are now able to process Lump Sum
Death Payment (LSDP) claims involving same-sex marriages and non-marital legal relationships
(NMLR).
