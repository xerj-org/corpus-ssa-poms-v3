C. Verifying the identity of an appointed representative's designated associate requesting
claimant information by telephone
The caller must provide the required identifying information listed in GN 03380.010B.2.
NOTE: If the caller cannot provide you with the required information, the designated associate
should have the appointed representative call or visit the field office. Alternatively,
the designated associate may submit an SSA-3288, SSA-3288-OP1, or other valid written
consent signed by the beneficiary or claimant.
