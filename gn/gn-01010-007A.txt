A. Introduction to adjudicative responsibilities
The adjudicator is responsible for fully developing and reviewing a claim to ensure
that the claimant meets all factors of eligibility and the entitlement is processed
in accordance with policy. The adjudicator must be impartial while reviewing all the
factors, therefore at any time the adjudicator identifies the claimant as a family
relative, friend, or discovers fraud, follow the instructions below.
