SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01771.210 - Preparing Claims Packages in the Division of International Operations (DIO) for Claims Filed in Slovenia
Effective Dates: 10/22/2024 - Present | TN 1 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201771210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. U.S. and Slovenian claims or Slovenian claim only
Prepare a Form SSA e2960 USA/SI 3 (U.S. - Slovenia Agreement on Social Security
Transmittal/Request/Certification) to:
1.
Request the Slovenian certified coverage record ; and
2.
Send the liaison agency Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of Slovenia any
information or documentation they may have requested.
