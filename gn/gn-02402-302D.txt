D. Overview of IDD for Germany
IDD payments sent to Germany must be in Euro accounts at local FIs. The beneficiary
must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) for input.
DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Germany. The FBU contacts the beneficiary
or FI for additional information.
