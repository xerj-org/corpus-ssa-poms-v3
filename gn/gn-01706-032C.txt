C. Processing Partial Certifications
If the Italian coverage certification is not complete, a special block on the form
IT/USA 3 bis marked “Partial Certification” is checked. Italian coverage shown on a partial certification should be credited
according to the instructions in GN 01706.020 and GN 01706.030.
If insured status cannot be met by using the coverage on a partial certification,
a complete coverage certification must be requested. The totalization claim should
not be denied for lack of insured status if the determination is based on a partial
certification.
