A. Procedure - Title II - Deferred Fee Authorization
When you are processing a title II claim in which you must defer authorizing a fee,
use the following paragraphs in GN 03940.075:
1.
If the representative is eligible for direct payment, use paragraph “A” (ATYR04/Claimant/Auxiliary
Notice – Partial Adjudication-Representative Eligible for Direct Payment) for the
claimant's or auxiliary's notice.
2.
If the representative is not eligible for direct payment, use paragraph “B” (ATYR06/PCNATY002
Claimant/Auxiliary Notice – Partial Adjudication – Non-Attorney Representative (ineligible
for direct payment)) for the claimant's or auxiliary's notice.
