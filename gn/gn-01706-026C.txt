C. No U.S. QCs - Two periods of Italian coverage credited to the same quarter
A coverage certification which shows one period ending and another period beginning
in the same calendar quarter may be correct as recorded. To properly credit Italian
coverage, credit each period separately. Only one Italian QC is credited for the quarter
where the two periods coincide.
