SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01737.110 - Scope of Greek Agreement
Effective Dates: 11/22/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy -Benefit programs included
The provisions of the agreement that permit people to qualify for benefits based on
combined U.S. and Greek coverage credits apply only to retirement, survivors, and
disability benefits under each country's system. The agreement does not apply to U.S.
Medicare benefits or Supplemental Security Income payments.
