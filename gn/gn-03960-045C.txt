C. Procedure - Determination Revised
If the determination or decision about the amount of past-due benefits is revised
on appeal or reopening, do not make a determination on administrative review based
on the original request.
Instead:
•
include the amount of the fee which results from the agreement in the notice of revised
determination or decision about the amount of past-due benefits;
•
notify the claimant, any affected auxiliary beneficiary(ies), the representative,
and the decision maker of the amount of the fee; and
•
advise the parties of the right to request administrative review of the amount of
the fee within 15 days of receiving the notice.
