SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 03960.045 - Recalculation of the Amount of Past-Due Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/05/2005 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - Relationship to Appeals Process and Administrative Finality
When reviewing the amount of the fee under the terms of a properly approved agreement
as described in GN 03960.040C., SSA assumes that the past-due benefits amount on which the fee is based was correctly
calculated, absent cause to believe otherwise. (GN 03960.040C.2.a. explains when a reviewer will assume the calculation was correct.) SSA must consider,
however, whether the requester has questioned or disagreed with the calculation of
the amount of the fee, the amount of past-due benefits, or both. Sometimes the amount
of past-due benefits may be the central or sole issue on administrative review.
When the requester has disagreed with the amount of past-due benefits on which the
fee under the agreement is based, unless D. below applies, SSA must:
•
adhere to the policy in GN 04040.001 or SI 04070.001E., or both in concurrent cases; and
•
consider the request for administrative review as an appeal or as a request to reopen
and revise the determination or decision about the amount of past-due benefits.
