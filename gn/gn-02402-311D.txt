D. Overview of IDD for Anguilla
IDD payments sent to Anguilla must be in Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD) accounts at
local FIs. The beneficiary must provide complete information to DIO or the Federal
Benefits Unit (FBU) for input. DIO faxes incomplete forms to the FBU that serves Anguilla.
The FBU contacts the beneficiary or FI for the additional information.
