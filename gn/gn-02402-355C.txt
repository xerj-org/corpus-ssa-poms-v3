SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02402.355 - Coding Argentina Bank Data on the Master Beneficiary Records (MBR)
Effective Dates: 10/12/2022 - Present | TN 257 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202402355 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multi-country contract
The processing FI in Argentina has prefix “66” at the beginning of the Routing Transit
Number (RTN). The first letter of the Depositor Account Number (DAN) field has the
letter “A” assigned to Argentina.
