SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02315.090 - Wyoming – Small Estates
Effective Dates: 10/09/2014 - Present | TN 14 (10-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202315090 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Contents of Affidavit
The affidavit must state that:
1.
The value of the entire estate, located in Wyoming or otherwise subject to probate
administration in Wyoming, less liens and encumbrances, does not exceed $200,000;
2.
Thirty (30) days have elapsed since the death of the decedent;
3.
No application or petition for the appointment of a personal representative is pending
or has been granted in Wyoming; and
4.
The claiming distributees are entitled to payment; the facts concerning the distributee’s
relationship to the decedent; and there are no other distributees of the decedent
having a right to succeed to the money under probate proceedings.
