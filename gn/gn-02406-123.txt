GN 02406.123 Regional Financial Center (RFC) Disposition of Nonreceipts
After processing nonreceipt reports, the Central Office (CO) receives
•
RFC disposition files,
•
CO disposition files for all nonreceipt claims,
•
photocopy request disposition files for all nonreceipt claims,
•
photocopy requests, and
•
SF-1184 stop payments.
If the RFC is unable to process a report, RFC notifies CO and sends a Department of
Treasury (DT) exception to the transmitting office.
The following explains how to find the codes and their explanations:
•
Title II disposition codes that update the Payment History Update System (PHUS), see
SM 00609.610.
•
Title XVI processing codes that update the Treasury Status Query (UPTQ) screen, see
MSOM BUSSR 003.020 and SM 01315.205.
