D. Procedure – Divorce Records
1. Office to Contact
a. Divorce Prior to 1950
Ministry of Justice and Police
Oslo, Norway
b. Divorce After 1949
Chief Administrative Officer (Fylkesmannen) of the county which issued the decree.
2. Fee
No fee if the request shows the certificate is for Social Security purposes.
