GN 01749.125 Eligibility for Czech Benefits under the Agreement
Under the agreement with the Czech Republic, SSA takes applications for both regular
and Totalization benefits on behalf of the Czech Republic and provides limited assistance
in obtaining evidence needed to support the claim.
