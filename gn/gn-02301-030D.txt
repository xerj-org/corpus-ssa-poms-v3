D. Valid request for payment after we distribute the underpayment
If we receive a valid request for payment after we distribute an underpayment, the
field office (FO) should explore the possibility of an amicable settlement among the
eligible recipients. If settlement is not possible, the FO issues payment to the adverse
claimant for his or her proper share and request refund of amounts erroneously paid
to the other recipient(s). If the recipient(s) we previously paid fails to refund
the money, treat these funds as an overpayment and process accordingly.
