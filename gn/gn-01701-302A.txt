Effective Dates: 03/23/2004 - Present | TN 15 (01-23)

A. Introduction to WEP exceptions based on years of coverage (YOC)
For months payable prior to January 1995, we apply WEP exception for YOCs when WEP
applies in computing Totalization benefits. For more information on WEP exceptions
refer to RS 00605.362.
