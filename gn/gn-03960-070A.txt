Effective Dates: 05/16/2011 - Present | TN 3 (03-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0203960070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Servicing Office and 800 Number Referral Paragraph
SSA must include the address and local telephone number of the servicing FO as well
as 800 number information in all administrative review notices and determinations.
2. Signature Requirement
When a PC has jurisdiction for review, one of the following officials signs the notice
of determination:
•
the Assistant Regional Commissioner for Management and Budget;
•
the Director of the Office of Central Operations; or
•
a designee, acting on his/her behalf.
3. Copies Sent to Other Parties
Because of Privacy Act considerations, SSA must not reveal:
•
the claimant's mailing address when sending a copy of a letter addressed to the claimant,
or the claimant's request for administrative review, to a party other than a representative
or a decision maker; or
•
the auxiliary beneficiary's mailing address when sending a copy of a letter addressed
to the beneficiary, or the beneficiary's request for administrative review, to a party
other than a representative or a decision maker.
NOTE: Representatives are bound by the regulations not to disclose any information we
furnish or disclose about the claim or prospective claim of another person (see 20
CFR 404.1740(d) and 416.1520(d)).
This means the address must be covered before photocopying or completely blocked out
before mailing.
