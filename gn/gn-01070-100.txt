GN 01070.100 Request For Regional Office (RO) Technical Assistance
Contact the Office of the Assistant Regional Commissioner, Management and Operations
Support (ARC, MOS) to obtain technical guidance if an unusual or complicated issue
is not clearly covered by POMS or other procedural guides. (See GN 01010.850 for FO guidelines and GN
01010.810 for PC guidelines for submitting these issues.)
See GN 01070.105 for easy access to telephone and contact information to ARC, MOS components by Region.
