B. Procedure for adjudicating foreign death underpayments
1. Underpayments only (that do not involve awards or disallowances)
Complete any development (if required) and indicate the disposition of the underpayment
on a Form SSA-559, and route it to the proper technician for processing as explained
below.
a. Benefit Authorizers
BAs process underpayments for individuals:
•
in the first three categories of priority; or
•
in the fourth, fifth, or sixth category when the underpayment is one month's benefit
payment, but no more than $750;
b. Claims Specialists
CSs process underpayments for individuals:
•
in the fourth, fifth, or sixth category of priority and the underpayment is more than
$750 or more than one month's benefit payment; or
•
seeking to qualify as a legal representative of the deceased.
2. Underpayments involving awards or disallowances
Do not prepare awards or disallowances in underpayment cases, unless:
•
the underpayment is being paid to the same person for whom you are currently awarding
the lump sum or monthly benefits; or
•
no award has been prepared for the deceased individual; i.e., the claimant died before
adjudication of their application, and the underpayment is currently being paid.
