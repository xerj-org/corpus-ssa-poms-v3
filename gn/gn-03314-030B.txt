B. Procedure for handling requests
The majority of the disclosures we make to State or local agencies under the health
maintenance and income maintenance routine use occur via electronic data exchanges,
as explained in GN 03314.001E.6. However, we also might receive a disclosure request through other methods (letter,
email, fax, telephone, or a field office visit). For more information regarding the
handling of requests from State and local agencies, see GN 03314.001E.
