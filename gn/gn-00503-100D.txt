SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 00503.100 - Advance Notice
Effective Dates: 08/14/2023 - Present | TN 14 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200503100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Process
Almost all representative payee applications are processed via the eRPS. Once a payee
selection has been made in the eRPS, the appropriate advance notice can be triggered
from the eRPS. When a regular advance notice is requested (MS 07412.008), the eRPS
automatically determines who is sent the advance notice and produce the advance notice,
which is mailed from CO. eRPS can only send one advance notice for each title (i.e.,
title II and title XVI). If more than one is required (e.g., you decide both parents
should get advance notice), it must be done manually using DPS. Also in certain situations
described below, eRPS cannot detect that an advance notice is needed; therefore a
manual notice must be prepared.
When a SSA-4164 is requested (option 3 off the RPRR) to satisfy advance notice requirements,
two copies will be generated immediately in the FO.
NOTE: Be sensitive to situations where sending the advance notice to the beneficiary may
seem irrational (e.g., beneficiary is in a coma or in advanced stages of Alzheimer’s
disease); explain to the prospective payee that this is a legal requirement and Congress
enacted this provision to provide a safeguard against a potentially adverse action.
The law does not permit SSA to decide whether a person is capable of fully comprehending
the due process notice. As levels of comprehension are frequently different between
individuals with seemingly similar circumstances, a universal notification policy
provides the greatest protection for all individuals.
