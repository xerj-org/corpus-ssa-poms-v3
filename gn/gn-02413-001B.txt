Effective Dates: 07/12/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202413001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. ACCEPTABLE GIFT
An acceptable gift must be unconditional, unsolicited, and may include:
a.
Money (cash, checks, etc.), or
b.
Monetary bequests, or
c.
Returned or donated benefit checks.
2. UNACCEPTABLE GIFT
An unacceptable gift includes:
a.
Nonmonetary gifts or bequests, or
b.
Conditional gifts or bequests.
3. CONDITIONAL GIFT
A conditional gift is made with specific stipulation(s) for its use other than for
deposit to an SSA trust fund.
