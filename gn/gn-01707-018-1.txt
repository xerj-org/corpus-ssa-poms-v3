SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01707.018 - Refunds of Contributions Under the German System
Effective Dates: 05/16/1991 - Present | TN 3 (04-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201707018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Worker
Workers who meet certain conditions may obtain a refund of previously paid contributions.
Generally, contributions can be refunded if a worker has made some contributions,
but not enough to qualify for a benefit, and is no longer compulsorily covered or
eligible to make voluntary contributions.
