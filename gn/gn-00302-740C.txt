C. WHEN SSA WILL NOT PAY FOR THE CENSUS RECORD
SSA will not pay for the census search when:
•
The claimant needs such evidence to establish his/her DB; for example
–
The claimant has no evidence, or
–
There is a material discrepancy in file and the weight of the evidence indicates that
he/she has not yet reached the required age.
•
The claimant could, but refused to, obtain a public or religious record of age established
before age 5.
