SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01737.215 - Application for Benefits Under the Greek Agreement
Effective Dates: 08/19/2009 - Present | TN 1 (12-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201737215 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
An individual who wishes to file for benefits under the agreement with Greece must
complete Form SSA-2490-BK (Application For Benefits Under A U.S. International Social
Security Agreement). This is true whether the applicant is filing for benefits only
from the United States, only from Greece or from both countries.
NOTE: Applications for Greek benefits may be filed up to 6 months in advance of the entitlement
date.
