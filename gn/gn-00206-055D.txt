D. Canceling conditional WDs
When a claimant bases a conditional WD on an uncertain circumstance, they have 60
days from the date of the WD approval notice to request a cancellation of the conditional
WD. For requirements to submit the cancellation, refer to GN 00206.017 Cancellation of a request for WD.
