GN 01726.310 Dutch Agency Requests for Medical Examinations
Policy
Final arrangements for processing medical examinations on behalf of the Dutch agencies
have not been made. Therefore, any requests for such from the liaison agency, UWV,
or from any of the industrial insurance boards should be routed to DTPS for processing
instructions.
