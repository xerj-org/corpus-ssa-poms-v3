B. Acknowledgment
1.
Normally an acknowledgment will not be necessary.
2.
If a situation should arise when you believe an acknowledgment should be made, include
in the acknowledgment a reference to the servicing office and send a copy to that
office.
3.
If the inquiry is about a specific claim already filed or to be filed, transmit the
inquiry to the resident office without acknowledgment unless as noted in A., the person
wants the matter handled by your office.
