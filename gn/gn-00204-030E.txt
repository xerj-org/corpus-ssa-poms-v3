E. Retroactivity for Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB)
We can allow retroactivity up to 12 months for DIB claimants and their auxiliaries
(i.e. child, young spouse). However, retroactivity cannot be before the NHs date of
disability onset or 5-month waiting period. For more information on retroactivity
for DIB applications, see DI 10105.015.
