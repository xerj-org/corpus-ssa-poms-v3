B. Policy - Taxes included
The provisions of the agreement that eliminate dual Social Security coverage and taxes
apply to:
•
U.S. Social Security taxes (FICA and SECA), including the Medicare portion, and
•
Spanish Social Security taxes that finance benefit programs under Spain's general
and special Social Security systems, including not only retirement, survivors and
disability benefits, but also temporary disability (cash sickness) benefits, unemployment
benefits, family allowances, national health insurance, etc.
