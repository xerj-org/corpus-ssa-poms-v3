Effective Dates: 05/26/2023 - Present | TN 70 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0200605069 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The payee must sign the accounting report. If joint guardians are appointed as payee
(see GN 00502.183B.3.), one signature is acceptable.
Exception: If the payee is an institution or agency, a stamped signature and title is acceptable.
The stamped signature/title usually represents the administrator, business office
manager or supervisor.
