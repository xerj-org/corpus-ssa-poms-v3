A. General instructions for interviewing individuals with claims involving same-sex relationships
Our policy is to provide meaningful access to all of our programs, provide sensitive
service to all individuals, and treat each individual with dignity and respect. When
interviewing individuals with claims involving same-sex relationships, follow the
interviewing guidelines found in GN 00203.000.
Be mindful to do the following:
•
Protect the confidentiality of each individual.
•
Always treat the individual with dignity and respect.
•
Be aware that the individual's relationship is a personal matter. You should inquire
about the relationship only if necessary for claims filing or adjudication purposes.
•
Ask only questions that are necessary to complete the transaction.
•
Use sex-neutral terms to refer to relationships. For example, use spouse instead of
husband or wife. Use parent instead of mother or father.
