GN 01070.226 No Record Of A Prior Folder On The SSN
Instructions for assisting office when there is no record of a prior folder on the SSN
Call the adjudicator in the requesting FO to verify the SSN and ask for cross-reference
numbers as well as any other pertinent information. Research all systems records including
shared evidence for pertinent information.
Determine if further PC search is warranted or if the FO adjudicator should redevelop
the evidence.
