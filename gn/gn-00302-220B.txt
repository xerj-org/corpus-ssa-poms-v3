From 1980-present, records are maintained in individual volumes for each year.
The entire volume containing the alleged year of birth is searched.
If the alleged year of birth is the beginning or ending year of the volume, the nearest
adjacent volume is searched.
EXAMPLE: Before a negative response is given on a request for a birth record for 1919, the
1910-1919 and the 1920-1929 records are searched.

as the supervisor feels appropriate).

Soundex system books which cover 5-year periods are used. The entire index containing
the alleged year of birth is searched.
If the alleged year of birth is the beginning or ending year of a Soundex Book, the
nearest adjacent Soundex Book is searched.
EXAMPLE 1: Before a negative response is given on a request for a 1921 BC, the 1917-1921 and
the 1922-1926 Soundex Books are searched.
EXAMPLE 2: A request for a 1917 BC would require a search of the 1917-1921 and the 1912-1916
Soundex Books before a “No Record” response is given.
BVS also searches only the alleged year of birth in its county index books.
