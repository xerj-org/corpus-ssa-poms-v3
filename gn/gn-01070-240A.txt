A. Policy for use Of FO OPTEL to the PC
The FO should never routinely input to the Debt Management System (DMS) in advance
of an OPTEL call. The issue should be discussed with the PC technician who will determine
whether DMS input is appropriate. If input was made prior to the call, the PC technician
will be alerted to this fact.
