SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 01730.015 - How the Foreign Service Post Processes and Develops Claims Filed with an Austrian Agency
Effective Dates: 12/16/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0201730015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the procedures below which explain how the FSP should process referrals from
the Austrian agencies.
1. Claimant Not Entitled to U.S. Benefits
If the claimant is not entitled to U.S. benefits, obtain form SSA-2490-F4 (Part I)
and the appropriate RSDI applications and evidence and forward the claim to OIO with
the form A/USA 2 and the coverage record, A/USA 4 if available.
2. Claimant Already Entitled to U.S. Benefits
If the claimant is already entitled to U.S. benefits, destroy the forms A/ USA 2 and
A/USA 4 unless they contain a request for information from SSA. In that case forward
the forms to OIO, annotated “already entitled to U.S. benefits”.
