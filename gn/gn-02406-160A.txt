A. Claims Document (FS-3858)
The FS-3858 is Treasury’s claims document cover sheet for the form Claim Against the
United States for the Proceeds of a Government Check (FS-1133) and check photocopies.
Treasury produces this form upon receipt of a nonreceipt or stop payment input. Treasury
posts the form to the Treasury Check Information System (TCIS) for printing, if necessary.
This form contains essential information about the check:
•
Check number,
•
Symbol,
•
Amount,
•
Date,
•
Payee's name, address,
•
Stop code input by SSA, and
•
Treasury's status code response
IMPORTANT: Before forwarding any completed packages to Treasury, make sure the FS-3858 and FS-1133
match the check photocopy by comparing check number, symbol, amount, and date.
