SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - GN 02602.057 - Procedure to Reinstate Medicare Advantage (MA) or Medicare Prescription Drug Plan (Part D) After Correcting an Incorrect Death Report
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 117 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0202602057 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Determine if beneficiary has Medicare Part D or MA
If an individual states their plan, or 1-800-MEDICARE, directed them to SSA to correct
their record, the referral is likely to be Medicare Part D or MA related. To be sure,
ask the individual if they have a Medicare Advantage or Medicare prescription drug
plan.
Check the MBR and MAPS (Medicare Application Processing System) (MSOM INTRANETMAPS
006.002, MAPS Development Worksheet Main (DWMP)) to confirm recent Part D or MA enrollment.
NOTE: Part D coverage will not appear in MAPS if a person:
•
Has Medicare prescription drug coverage (PartD) and is not a low-income subsidy (LIS)
beneficiary, or
•
Has not filed for LIS and is not paying Part D/Medicare Advantage premium by T2 withholding.
IMPORTANT: Not all Medicare beneficiaries receive Title II cash benefits. Examples of Medicare
beneficiaries without Title II cash benefits include:
•
ESRD/CRD (End Stage Renal Disease/Chronic Renal Disease) beneficiaries, see HI 00801.186,
•
MQGE (Medicare Qualified Government Employees), see HI 00801.400,
•
RRB (Railroad Retirement Board) annuitants, see HI 00801.106,
•
EHH (Environmental Health Hazard) beneficiaries, see HI 00803.000
•
Beneficiaries entitled to free Hospital Insurance (HI) who purchase Supplementary
Medical Insurance (SMI) and are in direct bill status because they are not currently
collecting cash benefits see HI 01001.030, and
•
Beneficiaries not entitled to free HI who purchase HI or SMI, see HI 01005.001.
