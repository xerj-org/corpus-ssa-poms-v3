C. Procedure for claims filed in FOs
1. Border FOs
If a resident of Mexico comes to the FO to file a claim, assist them in completing
the necessary applications and related forms. Keep jurisdiction over the claim until
we process the claim to an award or disallowance. Request any additional evidence
or development you need from a source in Mexico directly from the appropriate FBU.
Forward the claim to the Division of International Operations (DIO) when action (including
any District Office Final Authorization (DOFA) action) is complete.
2. Non-Border FOs
If a resident of Mexico visits an FO (other than a border FO) to file a claim, assist
them in completing the necessary applications and related forms. Accept whatever evidence
they have. If the claim is complete (including any DOFA action), forward it to DIO.
If you need additional development, forward the claim to the FBU having jurisdiction
over the claimant's residence. Do not transfer a claim to an FBU simply for a face-to-face
interview as explained in GN 00905.302.
