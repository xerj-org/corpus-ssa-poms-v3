A. Introduction
The computer printed entries reflect information on the MBR/SSR as of the month the
case is selected for accounting. Title II second requests reflect the same data that
was shown on the initial request (i.e., the data on the second request was derived
from the offline annual accounting system control file not the MBR), unless there
has been a payee address change in which case the new address is shown Title XVI (and
concurrent) second requests reflect information on the SSR in the month the AR diary
control is due.
