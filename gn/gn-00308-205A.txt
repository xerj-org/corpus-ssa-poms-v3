A. Procedure
You can find up to date address and cost of copy information at the District of Columbia Department of
Health. In addition, you can find EIN informationhere .
1. FO Code
FO Code 270
2. Type of payment
Birth/Death
Cash, check or money order. For emergencies, call (202) 783-1809 for credit card
purchases.
Marriage
Money order or certified check
Divorce
Money order or certified check
3. Remarks
Can FO verify, certify or obtain free copy of birth/death record? No
•
FOs should request birth and death record verifications by mail or fax directly from
the Vital Records Branch.
Complete and sign the Form SSA-L706 (see GN
00302.070). This form must accompany the employee identification letter (see GN 00308.001B.3) and a copy of the ID of the parent or the person who's applying. Include a legible
copy of the birth certificate, if available. The SSA-L706 form is to be signed by
the SSA employee and by the person(s) whose information is being requested. Requests
for verification should be sent to the following address: DC Vital Records 2201 Shannon
Place SE 1st Floor Washington, DC 20020. Main number is 202-442-9289.
There is a $5.00 fee for verification and a $10.00 fee for an uncertified copy.
•
FOs should request birth, death, marriage, and divorce records by mail directly from
the Division of Vital Records. The request must indicate the number of the check or
money order and the FO's return address. Use SSA letterhead and include the following
information:
a.
Birth/death
•
child's full name
•
child's sex and date of birth
•
certificate number (if known)
•
mother's full maiden and married names
•
father's full name
•
child's place of birth (include the name of hospital)
b.
Marriage
•
Name of wife
•
Name of husband
•
Date of marriage
•
Reason why this record is needed
c.
Divorce
•
Name of husband
•
Name of wife
•
Approximate date of divorce
