A. Who can introduce hearing witnesses
Claimants can introduce witnesses to testify at the hearing. The administrative law
judge (ALJ) may also request people to come to the hearing as witnesses. Witnesses
are not made a party to the hearing and do not receive a copy of the ALJ decision.
