D. Belgium
The Form SSA-e2960-U2-BE is printed in English only. It is identical in format to
the foreign language form the Belgian agencies use to communicate with DIO.
Complete those items which are special to the SSA-e2960-U2-BE as follows:
1. Address of the Belgian agency
Information or requests concerning retirement or survivor claims should be sent to
the Rijksdienst voor Werknemerspensioenen (pensions institute). Information or requests
concerning disability claims should be sent to the Rijksinstituut voor Ziekte-en Invaliditeitsverzekering
(invalidity institute). If both retirement and disability claims have been filed,
send the material to the pension institute. Designate the correct address by crossing
out the address that is not used. This item must be completed in all cases.
2. Part I—Information about the claim
a.
Items A through E are self-explanatory.
b.
Item F—Date Benefit Elected/Date Work Ceased
These blocks are completed when a claim for Belgian retirement benefits has been filed.
The information can be obtained from item 8 on the SSA-799-F4.
c.
Item G—Name and Address of Belgian Sickness Fund
This block is completed when a claim for Belgian disability benefits has been filed.
The information can be obtained from item 9 on the SSA-799-F4.
3. Part III—Certification of data
a. Information about the worker
1.
The alleged citizenship of the worker should be entered in the citizenship block.
It is not necessary to verify citizenship. The Belgian agency will request proof of
citizenship from the claimant if it is required.
2.
Beginning of Incapacity
This block is completed when a claim for Belgian disability benefits has been filed.
The date is obtained from item 9 of the SSA-799-F4. The date should be verified only
if the following conditions are met:
a.
SSA has established a period of disability for the worker; and
b.
SSA's established date of onset agrees with the date alleged for beginning of incapacity.
b. Information about spouse or widow
Self-explanatory.
c. Information about children
Self-explanatory.
4. Part III—U.S. decision
This item is completed only if Belgium specifically requests the information. The
item contains blocks to designate the type of U.S. benefit awarded, the date of entitlement
and initial monthly benefit. The “national” block should be checked if regular RSDI benefits have been awarded and the “Art 10” block should be checked if totalization benefits have been awarded.
