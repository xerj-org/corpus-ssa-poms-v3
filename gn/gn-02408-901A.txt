A. When the Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) processes an IDD reclamation
The appropriate FBUs process reclamations for countries not supported by the National
Payment Center Of Excellence(NPCE). The FBUs use locally developed forms to request
reclamations. For more information concerning reclamations to NPCE see GN 02408.910 and GN 02408.930.
