B. Component roles for disability freeze and onset
The field office and Disability Determination Services roles for establishing disability
freeze and onset are as follows.
1. Field office (FO)
The FO establishes the POD at the earliest possible date to establish a “freeze” that
is most beneficial to the claimant.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The DDS determines the earliest possible EOD based on medical and other factors.
