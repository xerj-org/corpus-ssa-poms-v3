C. Definition of decentralized agency
A decentralized agency is one located in two or more cities or district localities
within the physical confines of the State, excluding those offices which strictly
separate only the fiscal and administrative functions.
Intra-agency or contiguous type operations, or separate offices set up at the request
and direction of the SSA to accommodate special procedures or functions such as face-to-face
hearing offices, do not meet this definition.
