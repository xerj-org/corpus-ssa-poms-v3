A. Hearings and Appeals
The OCALJ is responsible for management oversight of SSA’s national hearing operation
including Federal ALJs, the Chief Judge’s Office, regional offices led by Regional
Chief Judges, and hearing offices located throughout the United States.
In hearing (or satellite) offices ALJs:
•
Conduct hearings; and
•
Make decisions on (or dismiss) requests for hearings.
