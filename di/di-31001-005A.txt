SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31001.005 - Claimant's Consent to Disclosure of his or her Records
Effective Dates: 09/17/2013 - Present | TN 7 (04-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431001005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Entity defined
An entity is any business, firm, or other association, including but not limited to
partnerships, corporations, for-profit organizations, and not-for-profit organizations.
See GN 03305.015 for information on disclosure to legal aid groups and private law firms.
