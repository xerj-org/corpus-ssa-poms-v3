SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.021 - Developing Evidence from Hospitals and Clinics
Effective Dates: 07/28/2014 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure - the Request
Use the instructions below to develop evidence from hospitals and clinics.
1. Claimant Identification Information
Provide the following information:
a.
Name
b.
Address
c.
Date of birth
d.
Hospital number
e.
SSN, if required by vendor.
2. Specific Information Needed
Specify the information needed:
a.
Summaries
b.
Test results, e.g., laboratory, X-ray reports, pathology reports, etc., as appropriate.
c.
Tracings, e.g., EKG, Spirometric, etc.
d.
Progress reports.
e.
Consultative reports.
3. Specific Time Periods
Indicate specific dates for:
a.
In-patient records
b.
Out-patient records
•
General clinics
•
Specialty clinics
