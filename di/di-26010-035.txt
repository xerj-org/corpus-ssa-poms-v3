DI 26010.035 Completing Item 21 (VR Action) on the SSA-831 for Title II and Title XVI Cases
It is no longer necessary to code Item 21 Vocational Rehabilitation (VR ACTION) on
Form SSA-831. The Ticket to Work Program eliminated the need for DDS vocational referral
screening. Ticket to Work procedures tell the DDSs to no longer refer cases for vocational
rehabilitation. (See DI 55001.001, “Ticket to Work and Work Incentives Improvement Act of 1999 (P. L. 106-170) – Overview”).
Public Law 106-170 also eliminated the requirement to refer visually impaired claimants
to the State Blind Agencies.
We are required to continue to refer Title XVI recipients who have not attained age
16 to the appropriate State agency administering the State program under Title V of
the Act.
NOTE: The National Disability Determination Services System (NDDSS) was changed to preclude
the entry of VR referral coding with the exception of “N” (Not referred for Vocational
Rehabilitation), and “C” (Child Claimant Referred to State Agency Responsible for
Administering the Title V Agency Program in the State). Neither of these codes is
shown on Form SSA-831. The NDDSS inserts the code “N” on the database and displays
it on a query response if the VOC field is left blank.
