C. Operating policy
1. Request for review
a.
An Intercomponent Review Draft (IRD) is a DRAFT version of a proposed POMS transmittal
that authoring components send out for agency stakeholders' review and comment.
b.
IRDs will be sent directly from Disability Adjudication (DA) to each reviewing component.
c.
The purpose of the IRD is to ensure that all stakeholders have a forum to submit comments
related to specific workloads or policies. An author can request an electronic IRD
(eIRD) via the PolicyNet Publication System (PPS).
d.
Once requested, an eIRD site is established and the system notifies the author via
email.
2. Due dates
a.
Normally, 21
days for DDS review.
b.
If the author prefers a shorter timeframe or has a need to waive the IRD, the author
must request a waiver from the applicable Disability Adjudication (DA) component.
