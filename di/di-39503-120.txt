DI 39503.120 Regional Office Assistance
Regional office personnel are available to assist agencies with any questions or problems
that may be encountered. Agencies should consult with the regional office on any problem
as early as possible. Regional office personnel may conduct prebudget planning meetings
with DDS officials and State fiscal personnel, as well as offer training and consultation
in budget preparation.
