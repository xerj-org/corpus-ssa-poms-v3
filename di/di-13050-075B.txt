B. Policy
If the individual requires a representative payee, follow existing instructions for
soliciting and selecting a payee (see GN 00502.020). The principles expressed in DI 13050.075 are also applicable to title XVI representative payee issues. Guidelines for considering
an individual's need for a payee and whether direct-payment as an interim measure
is appropriate are provided in GN 00502.020A.
NOTE: If payee development is still pending after 15 days from the date the EXR request
was filed consider direct payment in accordance with GN 00504.105.
