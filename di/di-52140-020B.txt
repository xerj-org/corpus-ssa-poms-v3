B. Information on the WC Resource Page
The website provides links to tools that can help the adjudicator process cases involving
WC. These link resources are not a substitute for POMS instructions on WC/PDB.
The Workers’ Compensation Resource Page includes:
•
links to State WC websites
•
Lists of Carrier Addresses and Contacts
•
WC calculators
•
Guides to using the Interactive Computations Facility and the Online Record Entry
Operation (OREO)
•
Training materials
•
Other internal and external links.
