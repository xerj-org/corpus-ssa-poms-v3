SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.006 - When Not to Purchase a Consultative Examination (CE)
Effective Dates: 06/10/2026 - Present | TN 65 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Case evidence is sufficient and consistent enough to adjudicate the claim
Do not purchase a CE when the existing evidence is sufficient and consistent enough
to adjudicate the claim. For information to evaluate the sufficiency and consistency
of evidence, see DI 24501.016C.
For continuing disability review cases (CDR), a CE is necessary only when the evidence
as a whole, both medical and non-medical, is not sufficient to support a determination.
Do not purchase a CE in a CDR case when the impairment:
•
Continues to meets a listing (even when obsolete) as written at the comparison point
decision (CPD) (DI 28030.020D.6).
•
Meets the requirement for certain Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) or MINE-equivalent
cases, such as amputation of both hands or amputation of the leg at the hip (DI 28040.005 and DI 28040.125B.1).
