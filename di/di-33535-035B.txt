B. McCarthy scales of children's abilities - (generally available since January 1972)
Assesses motor and intellectual development of children 2 1/2 - 8 1/2 years of age.
Yields six separate subscales, including a General Cognitive Index (GCI) which can
be used as an Intelligence Quotient (IQ) score. It is a better technique because it
is more current in terms of normative data.
