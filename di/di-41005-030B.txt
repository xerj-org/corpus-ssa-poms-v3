SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 41005.030 - Redeterminations for Statutory Blindness Cases
Effective Dates: 06/28/2024 - Present | TN 2 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0441005030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Period of disability previously established after the date of onset of statutory blindness
Route the case to an authorizer indicating the first date the beneficiary is both
statutorily blind and fully insured. Ask the authorizer whether the benefit amount
would be increased if the period of disability was established as of the date of onset
of statutory blindness or, if later, the date fully insured status is first met. If
the authorizer indicates that the benefit amount would be increased, prepare a revised
SSA-831-U5 to establish the period of disability as of the date the beneficiary was
both statutorily blind and fully insured. If the authorizer indicates that the benefit
amount would not be increased, proceed as in A. above.
