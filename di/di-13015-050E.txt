E. Procedure – Title II Folder Reading in DDS - OCO/PSC Actions
1.
OCO/PSC obtains a DDSQ to determine folder location.
2.
If still active in DDS, OCO/PSC verifies that a CDR is being performed. If yes, the
direct release alert is discarded.
3.
If no, e.g., DDS is performing a Class Action Review, OCO/PSC informs the DDS contact
of the intervening CDR. OCO/PSC forwards the direct release alert via FAX to the DDS.
