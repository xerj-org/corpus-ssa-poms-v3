SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55060.010 - FO Role in Providing Assistance in the EN Payment Process
Effective Dates: 07/28/2022 - Present | TN 4 (07-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455060010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The FOs have an important role in the EN payment process because the FOs review and
process reports of earnings for beneficiaries/recipients with tickets that come to
SSA from ENs via the TPM. This step is crucial to the operation of the automated EN
payment system. In most cases, if the FO fails to update the earnings information
on the DCF, the EN cannot be paid. The FOs should refer any questions about the TPM
certified evidence to the TPM by either creating an ERNGS issue on the CDRW screen
or telephoning the TPM directly as mentioned in DI 55030.015E.
