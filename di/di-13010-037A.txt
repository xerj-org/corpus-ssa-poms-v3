A. Background on processing fraud cases during the trial work period (TWP)
When a federal court convicts a beneficiary of fraud that involves concealment of
work activity, they are not eligible to receive payments for any TWP months performed
in or after March 2004, and before the date of conviction. For information about allegations
of fraud and the TWP see DI 13010.035.
NOTE: The beneficiary is still entitled to a TWP even though they are not eligible for payment of benefits during the TWP
months covered by the conviction court order.
