A. Blindness (2141.1)
In conformity with Oregon Law, blindness is defined as follows:
1.
The A/R must have a visual defect such that his visual acuity with correcting lenses
does not exceed 20/200 in the better eye: or
2.
The A/R's vision in the better eye is restricted to a field which subtends an angle
of not more than 20 degrees; or
3.
The A/R has an equivalent visual impairment.
