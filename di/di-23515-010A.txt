A. How to complete the SSA-831
Complete the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) for any Title XVI
claim per DI 26510.000 with the following exceptions:
Item Action
15 See DI 23515.005D for onset date instruction
18B Same date as item 15
27 Be alert that change in onset is determined from the alleged onset of disability,
not the filing date, as in regular Title XVI claims.
