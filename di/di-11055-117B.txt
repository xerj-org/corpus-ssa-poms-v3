B. Procedure for routing Title XVI determination information
Great Lakes Payment Service Center/Disability Case Review (GLPSC/DCR) prepares the
Title XVI disability determination.
•
Allowances - GLPSC/DCR, for Certified Electronic folder exclusion cases, sends photocopies of
the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) for any Title XVI or Title
II allowance determination to the field office (FO).
•
Denials - GLPSC/DCR transmits the folders containing any Title XVI or Title II denial determination
to the FO.
