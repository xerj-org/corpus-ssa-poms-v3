SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.315 - Comparison Point Decision (CPD) Residual Functional Capacity (RFC)
Effective Dates: 12/12/2022 - Present | TN 15 (11-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. No CPD RFCs
If:
•
The CPD used a medical/vocational basis but did not include an RFC assessment, or
•
The CPD is assumed to be medical vocational (see DI 28015.005B.2.), then
Assess the individual's prior RFC based on all available evidence concerning the impairment(s)
and particular limitation(s) at the time of the CPD, and assign the maximum RFC consistent
with the CPD evidence, per DI 28015.005.
For additional general information on RFC evaluation, see DI 24510.001, DI 24510.005, and DI 25020.005.
