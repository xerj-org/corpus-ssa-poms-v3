SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40115.020 - Processing Center (PC) Actions for Inappropriate Disability Determination Services (DDS) Determination
Effective Dates: 10/06/2011 - Present | TN 1 (09-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440115020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DDS determination case is not DDS jurisdiction
For a disability determination that is not the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
jurisdiction (e.g., a career railroad case or a case that insured status is not met
at the established onset), the disability examiner (DE) or disability processing specialist
(DPS) may
•
either adopt or reject the DDS determination depending on the facts of the case, and
•
complete the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) then
•
route the case to the field office or claims authorizer/claims technical examiner
(CA/CTE) for final processing.
•
Notify the DDS of the correction per DI 30005.204B.
