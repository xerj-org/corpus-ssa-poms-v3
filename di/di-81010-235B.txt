B. Paper CPD folders
If the CPD folder is paper, the folder can not be linked in EDCS. The CDR is electronic
but the CPD folder is not scanned. Send the paper CPD folder to the DDS. See Transferring
the Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) - DI 81010.240 for more information on routing and transferring. Set the CPD paper folder indicator
(PFI) to “yes” on the transfer screen. It is important that the paper CPD folder contain
a FLAG (See the following eCDR Paper CPD Folder Flag.) so subsequent paper folder
handlers know there is an electronic component to the official folder and that the
paper CPD folder is forwarded with the eCDR through all levels of review and appeals.
The Field Office should locally reproduce the flag for their own use.
