SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34234.007 - Immune Listings from 02/19/02 to 05/23/02
Effective Dates: 06/11/2015 - Present | TN 1 (06-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434234007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

N. One or more of the following infections (other than described in A-M, above), resistant
to treatment or requiring hospitalization or intravenous treatment 3 or more times
in 1 year (or evaluate sequelae under the criteria for the affected body system):
1. Sepsis;
2. Meningitis;
3. Pneumonia;
4. Septic arthritis;
5. Endocarditis; or
6. Radiographically documented sinusitis.
