SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.675 - Exhibit 3 DDS Guide to Establishing a Fee Schedule
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 12 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545675 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Obtain a copy of the Medicare physician fee schedule
CMS website
•
Identify the State Medicare carrier and obtain a copy of the Medicare Provider Program
Fee Schedule and the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule for the current year.
•
NOTE: States with different localities should obtain applicable Medicare Provider Program
Fee Schedules from each carrier for each locality.
•
Obtain annual updates of the Medicare fee schedule to ensure that the DDS fee schedule
is current.
