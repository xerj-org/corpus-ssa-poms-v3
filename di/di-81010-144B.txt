B. ESRD — only case (No CEF exists)
Stand-alone claims for ESRD benefits are exclusions to Electronic Disability Collect
System (EDCS) processing because they are claims for Medicare only, not disability
benefits. Process ESRD-only cases according to current procedures found in HI 00801.300 District Office Final Authorization (DOFA) of ESRD Medicare Awards and Disallowances.
See DI 11052.035B for handling the paper claim and documentation when there is no CEF .
