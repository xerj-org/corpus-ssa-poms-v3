SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11025.030 - Disability Determination Needed to Entitle a Dependent Grandchild
Effective Dates: 03/04/2011 - Present | TN 2 (09-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411025030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. FO action required when a DDS disability decision needed for parent in grandchild
claim
Take the following actions when preparing case for DDS transfer. This case will be
an Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) exclusion.
•
Create a paper modular disability folder (MDF). Use the grandparent’s SSN but show
the parent’s name on the folder tab.
•
Complete paper Forms SSA-3368 (Disability Report-Adult), SSA- 827 (Authorization to
Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration), and SSA-3367 (Disability
Report-Field Office).
•
Prepare an SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) to transfer the case
to DDS. The field office (FO) completes items 1-14 on the SSA-831.
•
In item 7 (type of claim) of the SSA-831, show either P-R (RSI NH) or P-D (DIB NH)
to indicate a parent-child filing on the earnings record of a grandparent.
•
In item 11 (remarks) of the SSA-831, show “Grandchild case, dependency date” followed
by the month and year in which the relationship must be established.
If help is needed in completing these items, see DI 11010.205, Completing the SSA-831.
