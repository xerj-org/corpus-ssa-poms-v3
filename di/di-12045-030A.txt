SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.030 - Same Title Claims – New Claim Filed, Prior Claim at Reconsideration Level
Effective Dates: 07/20/2012 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
This section covers what to do when a claimant files a new claim while a claim is
pending at the reconsideration level under the same title.
NOTE: If a claimant files a new disability application and has a prior disability claim
for the same title and benefit type pending at any level of administrative review, see DI
51501.001 – Procedural Change for Subsequent Disability Applications Effective July 28, 2011.
