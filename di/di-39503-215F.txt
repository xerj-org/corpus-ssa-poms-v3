F. Budget justification
The justification for the specific categories of expense are important in both budget
preparation and subsequent analysis. The detail with which these statements are developed
determines the validity of the estimate of DDS's financial needs. Additionally, it
enables the regional office and each subsequent review level to make more meaningful
and informed judgments in the operating budget.
