C. Procedure
If a decision made at a higher appeals level (e.g., by an Administrative Law Judge)
is found in error for purposes of the error exception and the individual can engage
in SGA, process the cessation first. After processing the cessation, if the time limit
for reopening has not expired, refer the case for possible reopening at the prior
appeal level. For instructions routing reopened cases, see DI 28501.030.
