SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29025.001 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Action When Fully Favorable Reconsidered Determination Is Not Issued
Effective Dates: 08/31/2026 - Present | TN 11 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429025001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Age 18 Redeterminations
The CPD is not relevant in the evaluation of an Age 18 Redetermination. Thus, CPD
evidence should not be identified on the SSA-887. If the determination involves only
an Age 18 Redetermination, identify the current evidence in the case. When you scan
or copy relevant evidence into the folder, as described in DI 23570.020C.2., include this evidence in Section A. on the SSA-887.
