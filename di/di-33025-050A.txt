SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33025.050 - Disability Hearing Unit (DHU) Discovers Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) Issue Prior to or During the Hearing
Effective Dates: 06/08/2023 - Present | TN 25 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433025050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction - DHU responsibilities
This section explains the actions taken when the DHU discovers an SGA issue prior
to or during the hearing in Title II CDR cases; and in initial Title II, Title XVI
or concurrent cases that are escalated to the reconsideration level and are evaluated
together with CDR reconsiderations.
