B. Using the paper MDF in the Processing Center (PC)
In addition to following the MDF instructions in DI 70005.005, follow the PC specific instructions in this subsection.
1. PC Technicians
During the course of case processing and in post-entitlement actions, PC technicians
are to follow the procedures in DI 70005.005B for filing documents in the correct section. Clearly mark documents, indicating the
appropriate MDF section, for filing by clerical staff.
2. PC Clerical staff
PC clerical staff are to file material in the correct section as annotated by the
technician, or as indicated by the nature of the material, as provided in DI 70005.005B.
