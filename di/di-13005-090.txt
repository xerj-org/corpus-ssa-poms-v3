DI 13005.090 Basis for Deciding Disability Issues Subsequent to Suspension or Termination of Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) Benefits
As noted in DI 13005.005, the basis for deciding continuing disability is not the same as the basis for deciding
the issue of disability in an initial claim.
If an SSI recipient is ineligible:
•
for any SSI benefit for 12 consecutive months, or
•
if the recipient is found (under the medical improvement review standard (MIRS)) to
no longer have a disabling impairment, the disability standards applicable to initial
claims in DI 10105.065 apply to any subsequent disability decision (for example, claimant files to re-establish
eligibility).
