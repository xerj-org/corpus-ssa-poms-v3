SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32010.085 - Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Adverse Reopening' Cases
Effective Dates: 10/06/2021 - Present | TN 4 (10-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432010085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The component responsible for the adverse reopening is determined by the level of
the prior CDR determination.
1. Initial CDR determinations
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) is responsible for preparing an adverse
reopening of the initial CDR determinations.
2. Reconsideration CDR determinations
The disability hearings unit (DHU) is responsible for adverse reopening of reconsideration
determinations.
