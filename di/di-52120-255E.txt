E. Time limitations for filing claims
•
Initial claim: An injured worker must file a claim with the WC Commission within 2 years from the
date of the accident or any right to benefits may be lost. Claims for an occupational
disease must be filed within 2 years from the date the doctor tells the employee the
disease is work related, or 5 years from the date the employee was last exposed to
the work condition causing the disease, whichever is sooner. (Certain diseases, such
as coal workers' pneumoconiosis have different limitation periods.)
•
Relapse: If after returning to work the worker is again disabled, they must file a claim for
TP, TT, or PT within 2 years of the date for which they were last paid compensation
under an award. (This is called a "change in condition.") Payment only goes back 90
days from the date of filing with the Commission. However, if the claim is for PP
the worker has 3 years from the last date for which they were entitled to compensation
paid under an award.
