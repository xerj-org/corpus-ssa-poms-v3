A. Blindness — definition of blindness
Any person shall be considered blind whose central visual acuity is 20/200 or less
in the better eye with correcting lens, or whose field defect in which the peripheral
field has contracted to such an extent that the widest diameter of visual field subtends
an angular distance of no greater than 20 degrees.
