DI 13005.065 Subsequent Auxiliary Claims Filed During a Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
When the claim folder(s) is in the field office (FO) or disability determination services
(DDS), immediately forward any subsequent auxiliary or unprocessed auxiliary claim(s)
that is filed on the disabled beneficiary’s record, to the processing center (PC).
Do not leave these claims in the disabled individual’s claim folder. The PC will process
these claims without the disabled individual’s folder. When routing the auxiliary
claim(s) annotate the form SSA-3601 (Claims Routing) with the remark, “Auxiliary Claim-CDR
Pending, Please Expedite.” For electronic FO case procedures, see DI 81010.000.
