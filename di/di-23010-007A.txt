A. In which claim types we apply the failure to follow prescribed treatment (FTFPT) policy
We apply the FTFPT policy in:
•
Claims for new benefits in all Title II and Title XVI claims (see DI 23010.007B in this section);
•
Age-18 redeterminations (see DI 23010.007B in this section;
•
Adverse reopenings (see DI 23010.007C in this section); and
•
Continuing disability reviews (CDRs) (see DI 23010.007D in this section)
