Effective Dates: 07/16/2012 - Present | TN 5 (11-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010141 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Request for the hearing to proceed is received
If the claimant or representative requests that the hearing proceed:
•
Fax a copy of the SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other Person) into the CEF. For
more information on faxing into the CEF see DI 81010.090 - Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes, and
•
Fax a copy of the SSA-795 to the originating HO.
NOTE: This is the only way the HO will know that a hearing must proceed.
