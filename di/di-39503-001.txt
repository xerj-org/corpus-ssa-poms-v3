DI 39503.001 DDS Budget Procedures -- General
Under the Federal-State regulations, the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS)
provides funds necessary to the States for the functions required to make disability
determinations for the Federal Government. Congress provides funding for all DDS operations
in the appropriation to HHS for general salaries and expenses. Accordingly, State
agency financing is locked into the general administrative financing of the Social
Security Administration (SSA). It is subject to the same apportionment, allotment,
and reprogramming pressures as any component of SSA.
