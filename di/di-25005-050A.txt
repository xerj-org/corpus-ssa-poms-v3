A. Policy for making PRW determination
The claimant is the primary source for vocational documentation, and statements by
the claimant regarding past work are generally sufficient for determining the skill
level, exertional demands, and non-exertional demands of such work. For development
and follow-up requirements, see DI 22505.014.
If the claimant refuses or is unable to provide the necessary work history, the evidence
is not available from a third party, and this evidence is material to the disability
determination, deny the claim for insufficient evidence. For information on making
a determination based on the evidence in file, see DI 23007.015.
Explain your determination in the personalized disability notice.
NOTE: For situations adjudicators may apply expedited step 4 processing, see DI 25005.005.
