DI 52120.205 Oregon Workers’ Compensation (WC)
CITATIONS:
AR 95-2(9), Acquiescence Ruling, Hodge v. Shalala, 27F.3.d, 430 (Ninth Circuit 1994)
The Oregon WC system changed significantly due to major legislative reforms enacted
between 1987 and 1995. The following instructions reflect current Oregon WC laws.
For information on the earlier WC system and also for historical WC offset instructions,
see in this section DI 52120.205H.
