SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.007 - Supervisory Hearing Officer (SHO) Case Review — Prior to Disability Hearing
Effective Dates: 05/25/1999 - Present | TN 3 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. SHO screens the case
The SHO screens a certain number of cases, as needed.
The SHO's review:
1.
Takes place after the disability hearing unit (DHU) performs basic receipt function,
but before the before beneficiary (or recipient), appointed representative, and disability
hearing officer (DHO) review the case.
2.
Pinpoints problem areas with case development.
3.
Ensures that all prior actions have been properly performed, including the identification
of adverse effect material (see DI
33010.015), and the need for an interpreter.
4.
Enables the SHO to determine if a fully favorable decision is warranted without a
hearing.
