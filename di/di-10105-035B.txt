SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.035 - Length of Freeze Period
Effective Dates: 05/10/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Ending date
A freeze ends (i.e., the ending date for a period of disability) on the earliest of
the three following dates:
•
The last day of the month before the month the NH dies;
•
The last day of the month preceding the month in which the NH attains full retirement
age (FRA); or
•
The last day of the second month after the month in which disability ceases. (See
DI 10105.035C.).
If cessation of disability or attainment of FRA occurred before 10 /60, the freeze
ended on the last day of the month in which the earlier of these events occurred.
NOTE: In determining when disability ceases, the trial work period (TWP) provision does
not apply to freeze only cases. (See DI 13010.035.)
