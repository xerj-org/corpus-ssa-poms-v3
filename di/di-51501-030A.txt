Effective Dates: 07/14/2025 - Present | TN 15 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0451501030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. New critical or disabling condition
A “new” critical or disabling condition means the onset occurred after the date of the hearing decision on the prior claim. A “critical or disabling condition”
is a condition that meets a listed impairment or that meets the criteria under Terminal
Illness (TERI), Compassionate Allowances (CAL), or Presumptive Disability/Presumptive
Blindness (PD/PB).
