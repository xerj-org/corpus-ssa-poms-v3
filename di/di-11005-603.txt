DI 11005.603 Processing Quick Disability Determinations (QDD) Cases - Field Office (FO) Instructions
CITATIONS:
20 CFR 404.1619 Quick Disability Determination process
20 CFR 416.1019 Quick Disability Determination process
A QDD case is an initial Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) caseidentified
electronically via a predictive model as having a high degree of probability that
the claimant is disabled and that evidence of the claimants allegations is expected
to be easily and quickly obtained and the case can be processed quickly in the disability
determination services (DDS).
