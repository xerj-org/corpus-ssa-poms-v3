F. Policy for determining month of eligibility for Title XVI cases
When the medical determination is favorable, determine the appropriate month of eligibility:
1.
Eligibility for reinstated benefits in Title XVI EXR cases begins with the month following
the month of the EXR request. There is no retroactive eligibility to Title XVI payments
under EXR.
2.
We do not pay Title XVI disability benefits in EXR cases for any month before February
2001, the month after the first possible filing month for EXR.
