A. Disability Determination Services (DDS) authority and responsibility
The DDS administrator shall select an adequate number of vocational specialists (VS)
to meet the States needs. The DDS administrator has the authority and flexibility
to design a quality assurance function best suited to the needs of the agency and
assigns roles accordingly.
