SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11052.030 - Adjudication of Initial End-Stage Renal Disease Medicare Cases
Effective Dates: 01/04/2016 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411052030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Claimant insured on spouse’s or parent’s SSN—no active MBR
Establish an ESRD claim on the claimant’s own SSN in MCS.
•
On the ESRD screen in MCS, enter the SSN of the spouse or parent insured status is
based upon.
•
For a spouse, also enter his or her SSN on the Number Holder Marriage (NMAR) screen.
(EC determines insured status for the spouse or parent only when the SSN appears on
the appropriate screens.)
•
Input an allowance or a denial decision on the DECI screen in MCS and establish any
needed diaries.
•
Overkey the PC JURIS field to PC7 if necessary.
•
Document appropriate notices on NOT3 screen.
•
Prepare an A101 when an EC processing limitation results for these claims.
•
Prepare form SSA 3601 for PC7 marked Initial Claim, Non-MCS, ESRD, Unprocessed. For
required and conditional remarks see DI 11052.020F.
