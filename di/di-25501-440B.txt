B. Component roles regarding establishing the EOD with traumatic impairments
1. Field office (FO)
The FO will identify the date the claimant alleges a traumatic injury, and all relevant
technical entitlement requirements.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The DDS will determine an onset date on the date of the traumatic injury as supported
by the medical evidence for the disability.
