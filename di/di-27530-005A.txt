SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27530.005 - Processing Adverse Reopenings for Paper Cases
Effective Dates: 02/24/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427530005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Title II only
Prepare a revised SSA-831. Do not delete or remove the previous SSA-831.
•
Enter the following remark in item 34, “This revises the previous determination of
(DD/MM/YYYY).”
•
Prepare and release a notice of revised determination in accordance with DI 27540.000 Reopening Notice Instructions.
•
Do not input the revised determination.
•
Route the folder to the field office (FO) with the remark, “Title II adverse revision
– benefits continuing.”
