SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.255 - Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) Cessation Reconsideration
Effective Dates: 02/28/2025 - Present | TN 92 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010255 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. FO responsibilities for eCDR reconsiderations
When an individual wishes to appeal a DDS CDR determination, they file a reconsideration.
The individual contacts the FO to complete the necessary reconsideration forms.
Before processing any cessation appeal, FO technicians should verify the presence
of an initial medical determination (Cessation/Continuance), review the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR) and Supplemental Security Record (SSR) queries to ensure the most recent
medical determination data is posted, and obtain necessary medical cessation appeal
forms. To process the request, follow instructions in DI 12026.020. For information on good cause for late filing see GN 03101.020.
1. Completing eCDR reconsiderations in EDCS
To process the eCDR reconsideration, EDCS users must open EDCS and establish a record.
IMPORTANT: When the CDR is a Title XVI only or concurrent, before initiating the eCDR appeal
in EDCS, the user must use Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claim System (MSSICS)
to update the SSR to document the filing of the medical cessation appeal as well as
the statutory benefit continuation (SBC) election. If Direct SSR update is required,
the FO can manually enter the required fields in EDCS on the CDR Information and Claims
Information pages.
To create an eCDR appeal:
a.
Select CDR Reconsideration from the Select Case Level screen;
b.
Select OK button;
c.
Enter the appeal filing date on the confirm Case Creation screen, if not propagated;
d.
Select Create Case button;
e.
If the case information did not propagate from MSSICCS, key in the CDR information,
the SSA-3367 (Disability Report-Field Office), and the SSA- 3441 (Disability Report-Appeal)
data in EDCS on the Form(s) Selection screen; and
NOTE:
The Copy from Prior button in EDCS will be enabled if information is available to
copy from prior level(s). For example, if the SSA-3368 for Age 18 Redetermination
is completed in EDCS, the EDCS user will have the ability to Copy from Prior (form
3368) when completing the SSA-3441 for an Age 18 Redetermination reconsideration or
hearing level appeal.
f.
Enter appropriate limited data similar to the eCDR Initial CDR including:
•
the Social Security Number (SSN);
•
claim type;
•
name;
•
address;
•
SBC information,
•
attorney representative name and address; and
•
representative payee name and address.
REMINDER: The FO must verify and update the appropriate address, telephone number, and representative
information on the mainframe before transferring the reconsideration in EDCS.
g.
Reminders for the eCDR reconsideration include:
•
Process the CDR appeal in EDCS, if the CDR being appealed was processed in EDCS;
•
If Title XVI only or concurrent eCDR, complete MSSICCS prior to creating case in EDCS;
•
Select and key in SSA-3441 data;
•
Obtain an SSA-789 (Request for Reconsideration-Disability Cessation) and the appropriate
number of SSA-827s (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration)
from the individual, per regional instructions. Also obtain a SSA-3881 (Questionnaire
for Children Claiming SSI Benefits) for SSI childhood disability claims;
•
Fax paper documentation into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), and then dispose
of the form per instructions in DI 81010.135;
•
Add a valid (electronic or hardcopy) SSA-827 to the CEF; and
•
Fax any development regarding SBC (for example, Report of Contact, SSA-792 (Statutory
Benefit Continuation Election Statement), and any other documentation) into section
E of the electronic folder DI 12027.001. Dispose of the development per DI 81010.135.
2. Transferring the eCDR reconsideration
When transferring the eCDR reconsideration, the FO must:
•
Review the case and resolve any edits;
•
Select the Transfer/Updates tab in EDCS;
•
Answer the paper folder indicator (PFI) question (Note: if an electronic CPD is linked
to the eCDR, the PFI will default to No);
•
Ensure Title II SBC fields are completed;
•
Transfer all parts of an eCDR, concurrent or multiple entitlements at the same time;
and
•
Mail all paper folder(s) and paper documents not stored electronically,associated
with the case to the DDS at the time of electronic transfer.
NOTE 1:
The Disability Control File (DCF) CDRI screen automatically records the appeal(s)
on the DCF after EDCS transfer. See MSOM CDR 001.025, for more information on transferring
the eCDR reconsideration.
NOTE 2:
The EDCS automatic updates to the DCF are for appeal cases only. The FO must still
add initial CDR case movement to the DCF manually or through the Workload Action Center
(WAC).
3. Routing the eCDR reconsideration
If a paper CPD folder exists:
•
Place any paper SSA-827s, SSA-3881 if appropriate, and any other relevant documents
submitted by the beneficiary in the paper folder, and
•
Attach an EDCS routing form to the top of the folder and sends the folder to the DDS.
If the CPD folder is electronic, add all forms and documentation to the electronic
file and transfer to the DDS.
4. Processing DDS determinations on eCDR reconsiderations
The DDS or Disability Hearings Unit (DHU) returns both favorable and unfavorable determinations
on all eCDR reconsiderations to the FO.
Upon receipt of a medical cessation determination from the DDS, take the following
appropriate actions.
Title II only cases:
•
Review the MBR to ensure the applicable information received from EDCS and the DCF
updated automatically;
•
Verify SBC termination, if applicable; and
•
Ensure the MBR is updated within 5 days. If the MBR does not update in 5 days, technicians
should proceed with manual action to update the MBR. For potential manual actions,
seeDI 12026.025B .
Title XVI and Concurrent cases:
•
Review both the SSID and MBR to ensure medical cessation appeal determination automatically
posted;
•
Verify the SBC benefits have ended for BOTH titled programs; and
•
Ensure both the MBR and SSID are updated within 5 days. If the MBR and or SSID do
not update in 5 days, technicians should proceed with manual actions to update the
MBR and/or SSID. For potential manual actions, see DI 12026.025B
.
