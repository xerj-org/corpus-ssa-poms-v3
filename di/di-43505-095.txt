DI 43505.095 Appeals Beyond the Reconsideration Level
HALLEX 1-2-5-76 provides hearing office (HO) instructions for obtaining evidence in
foreign disability claims from the Office of International Benefits Office (IBU) in
the Office of International Operations (OIO). See DI 42010.001 for processing instructions on hearing cases, Appeals Council and court decisions.
