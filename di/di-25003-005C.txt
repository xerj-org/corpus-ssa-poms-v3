C. SSA VS training modules
VS training consists of a series of video-on-demand (VOD) training modules. Personnel
may complete the modules in a classroom setting or individually. The DDS administrator
and training staff determine which method is appropriate. The complete VOD training
runs approximately 6 hours. VS training modules and the required certification are
on theVocational Specialist Requirements intranet site and the A-Z Disability Training
Index
