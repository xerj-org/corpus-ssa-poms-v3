DI 40105.001 Initial Disability Claims that are Denied for Non-Medical Reason
An initial disability claim that results in a denial for non-medical reasons is the
adjudicative responsibility of the field office. Specific details about cases that
fall under this category can be found in Title II Technical Denials, and Claims not
Requiring a Disability Determination DI 11010.075.
