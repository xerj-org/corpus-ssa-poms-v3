Effective Dates: 12/14/2011 - Present | TN 2 (09-09)

D. Item 34 — Remarks
•
For Medicare only and concurrent DIB/MQGE claims, enter “MQGE Case”.
•
For situations where a claimant is found to meet the medical definition of disability
after expiration of the DIB DLI, but before expiration of the MQGE DLI, process as
an adverse allowance and enter “Change of Onset”.
See also DI 26510.095, Completing Item 34 (Remarks) on the SSA-831
