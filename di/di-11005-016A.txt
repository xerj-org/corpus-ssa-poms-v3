A. Policy Disability Interview Form SSA-3367 (Disability Report Field Office)
The SSA-3367 is used for title II and title XVI disability or blindness claims. The
field office (FO) must complete an SSA-3367 for each disability claim. The form records
basic information available to the FO interviewer. This includes information used
to determine a potential onset date (POD), obtain information from the earnings record,
determine the prescribed period of disability, include prior claim information (if
any), and observations made by the interviewer. For complete instructions, see DI 11005.045.
