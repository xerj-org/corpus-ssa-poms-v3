DI 52130.020 Office of Personnel Management (OPM) Data Exchange Alerts
SSA’s Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) is matched periodically against OPM records.
Alerts are sent to the processing center (PC) when the MBR contradicts the OPM data
or the system cannot determine if the MBR information is correct.
NOTE: The Modernized Claims System Earnings Computation (MCS EC) system also interfaces
with OPM. See DI 52155.040 - Processing Initial Claims with Federal PDB for the initial claims interface.
