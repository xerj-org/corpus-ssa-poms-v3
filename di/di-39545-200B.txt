Effective Dates: 08/22/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure – obtain CE provider certification statement
DDSs should:
•
Obtain a signed statement (see exhibit, DI 39569.400) from the CE provider certifying that all support staff used to help perform CEs
meet State licensing or certification requirements and have not been sanctioned.
•
Retain the signed statement in the CE provider’s file.
CE providers will provide the names of all support personnel, if requested by the
DDS.
