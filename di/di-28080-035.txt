DI 28080.035 Title XVI and Concurrent Title II/Title XVI Due Process Notice Content
Cessation determination notices in Title XVI and concurrent Title II/Title XVI cases
must contain personalized information explaining the basis for the adverse action.
(Refer to DI 28080.010B.2., items c. through h., for assistance in preparing the personalized notice.)
See NL 00708.000 for instructions for preparation of the cessation determination notices, including
appropriate cessation determination paragraphs.
