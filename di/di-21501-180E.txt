E. Presumption of permanently and totally disabled: preinvestigation grant (OFFICIAL
REGULATIONS, article 11, regulation 3, subdivision c) (122).
An applicant for aid to the disabled shall be presumed to be permanently and totally
disabled for purposes of a preinvestigation grant (Refer to Bulletin No. 68 series)
when there is medical information to indicate that he suffers from a disability or
impairment believed to be permanent in nature and which, at the time of such presumption,
appears to be total in effect within the meaning of the definition.
