DI 13010.180 Completing the Explanation of Determination SSA-4268
Include a summary of the evidence supporting the determination that the beneficiary
was or was not engaged in substantial gainful activity (SGA) in the SSA-4268-U4. Also
list TWP service months, if applicable. If eWork is used to prepare the determination,
the SSA-4268 information will automatically be generated.
