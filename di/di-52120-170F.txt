F. Uninsured Employers’ Fund
For injuries occurring on or after 06/20/2003, a New Mexico law created a special
workers’ compensation fund, Uninsured Employers’ Fund, to pay injured workers whose
employers failed to insure them for workers’ compensation but should have. Such payments
are subject to WC offset.
