A. Reopening final determinations
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) may only reopen final determinations.
A determination is final as of the date of the determination notice. For more information
about reopening and revising a determination, see DI 27501.001.
NOTE: If the DDS did not send a determination notice, prepare a revised determination
following the usual CDR determination procedures.
