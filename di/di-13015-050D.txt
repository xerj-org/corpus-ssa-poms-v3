SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13015.050 - OCO/PSC Retrieving The Title II and Title II Portion of the Concurrent TII/TXVI CDR Folder/Medical Evidence
Effective Dates: 12/09/2013 - Present | TN 23 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413015050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - TII Folder Now Reading in FO
1.
OCO/ PSC will forward the direct release alert to the FO of jurisdiction to search
for the Title II folder.
2.
FO will locate the folder in accordance with DI 13015.055. FO follows DI 13005.030 if folder is found, and DI 13015.080 if folder is not found.
