SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11050.055 - Transferring Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases to Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR)
Effective Dates: 10/01/2026 - Present | TN 7 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411050055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Concurrent Title II/Title XVI Claims with RR Involvement
If the non-disability requirements are met on both claims:
•
Transfer claims in EDCS to V32 and mail paper associated with the case to DCR under
cover of the EDCS Routing Form per DI 81010.085, Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS).
•
Effectuate the presumptive disability decision if the criteria are met.
NOTE: If the FO subsequently determines that the claimant is not eligible for SSI, the
FO closes the Title XVI portion of the case on EDCS, which automatically notifies
DCR.
If the SSI non-disability requirements are not met:
•
Prepare a Report of Contact (RC) for the SSI file showing Title II/Career RR claim
forwarded to DCR on (date), and send only the Title II claim to DCR. Be sure to include
all evidence pertinent to the Title II claim;
•
Enter SSI denied by FO on non-disability issue in the SSA-3367, Remarks section to
alert DCR as to the disposition of the SSI claim; and
•
Retain any SSI claims related materials in the FO.
