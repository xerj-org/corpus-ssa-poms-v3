A. Background
Social Security Administration (SSA) is required to establish by regulations, standards
of performance and administrative requirements and procedures necessary to ensure
effective and uniform administration of the disability programs.
The performance standards are used to:
• improve the disability determination process;
• ensure effective, timely and accurate service to the public.
