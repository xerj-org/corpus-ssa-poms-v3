DI 39545.400 Ensuring Quality and Integrity of Consultative Examination (CE) Reports
CITATIONS:
20 CFR 404.1519p, 20 CFR 404–1519q
A quality CE report contains all of the information relevant to the examination and
to the tests that were authorized, and includes accurate information.
