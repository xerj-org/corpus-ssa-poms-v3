H. LS case situations requiring special ICF considerations
There is a continually evolving area of identified system limitations and program
problems. These cases require special consideration when processing. For a list of
program limitations and case specific processing instructions, see Interactive Computations Facility (ICF)
Resource Page for WC/PDB Offset Computations .
1. Multiple injury or disability cases
Prorate LS awards for different injuries or disabilities separately. The total amount
of WC for offset purpose can exceed the State maximum in this situation.
a. Manual computations
For offset purposes, add together monthly amounts for any concurrent period. See DI 52170.030 - Manual Proration of a Lump Sum Award.
b. ICF
Enter any concurrent injury or disability into ICF as separate claims. The system
has the capability of processing a maximum of four separate WC/PDB claims at one time.
