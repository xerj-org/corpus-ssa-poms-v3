B. Disability Reports Available for Completion in EDCS
1. Initial Claims/Disability Reports
The following forms are available for initial claims processing:
•
SSA-3367 Disability Report – Field Office;
•
SSA-3368 Disability Report – Adult;
•
SSA-3820 Disability Report – Child; and
•
SSA-3369 Work History Report (if required per Completing the SSA-3369 - DI 11005.025).
For details on completing EDCS forms for initial claims, see Completing EDCS Forms
for Initial Claims - DI 81010.025. For general information regarding paper disability forms see Forms Used in Disability
Claims - DI 11005.016. Data Propagation - DI 81010.035, provides instructions regarding data propagated from an IDR.
2. Appeals (Reconsiderations and Hearings)
The following forms are available for appeals processing:
•
SSA-3367 Disability Report – Field Office (for Reconsideration)
•
SSA-3441 Disability Report – Appeal (for Reconsideration, and Hearing requests)
For details on processing Appeals in EDCS, see Processing Appeals in EDCS - DI 81010.150.
