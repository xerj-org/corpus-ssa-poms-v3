SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.026 - Completing the SSA-3373-BK (Function Report – Adult)
Effective Dates: 06/20/2007 - Present | TN 11 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005026 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Use the SSA-3373 to collect information about a claimant's ability to function and
to perform the activities of daily living.The information detailed on the SSA-3373
may be obtained by telephone and recorded on an SSA-5002 in lieu of using the SSA-3373.
Supplemental information may be added to the SSA-3373.
