D. Preparing the SSA-831 withdrawal approved
When the request for withdrawal is approved prepare an SSA-831, complete the following
items:
•
In item 22: Enter “T1-615”;
•
In item 34: Indicate “DIB (Freeze) (DWB) (CDB) Application Withdrawn,” as appropriate,
and
•
Item 37 and 38: Enter the examiner's signature, section number, SSA code, and the
date.
After completing the SSA-831
•
Scan a copy into the “Front Yellow” section of the electronic folder and another into
the paperless processing center (PPC).
•
Move the prior SSA-831 into the “Green Section” of the electronic folder and prepare
an SSA-559 to the CS with instructions to complete the withdrawal per the directions
in GN 00206.120B.
