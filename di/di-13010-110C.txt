C. Policy - Return to work less than 12 months after onset and information received after
the date of final determination
1. SGA began before date of final determination
When SGA began before the date of the final determination but the information was
received after this date, reopen the prior determination and revise to a denial. If
the folder is not on hand, recall it from the office retaining it (e.g., WBDOC, OCO
or PSC-DCR).
When SGA began before the date of the final determination but stopped, and the unsuccessful
work attempt (UWA) criteria are not met (see DI 11010.145), forward the case to the DDS to consider later onset dates.
2. SGA began after date of final determination
When SGA began after the date of the final determination, handle under the 1619 (see
SI 02302.030) provisions (see DI 13005.016).
3. Work is not SGA
Follow the applicable instructions in DI 13010.020.
