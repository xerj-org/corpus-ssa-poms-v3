F. Verifying WC
1. Carriers
Carriers are usually the best source of WC payment information.
2. State Contact

Early State records may not be available. Some records were lost due to flooding in
the early 1990’s in Hartford.
When sending a request for WC information, make sure to provide the beneficiary’s
date of birth (DOB). If you are using Form SSA-1709, provide the DOB in the Remarks
section (field #12). The Connecticut Workers’ Compensation Commission is now using
DOB information (as opposed to Social Security numbers) to identify claimants.
