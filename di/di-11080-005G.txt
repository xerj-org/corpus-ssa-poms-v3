G. Inquiries about COBRA
SSA is only responsible for facilitating the disability decision.
The DOL administers the basic COBRA provisions. Direct routine inquiries to the local
DOL office.
Information is also available online at: http://www.dol.gov/dol/topic/health-plans/cobra.htm.
