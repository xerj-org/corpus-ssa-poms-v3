C. Work Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs)
POD participants were exempt from work CDRs while participating in the POD. Instead,
SSA evaluated earnings outside of eWork and applied offset using the POD Automated
System (PAS). eWork generates the following warning message when trying to initiate
a work CDR for a POD participant during the POD participation period:
“POD Participant. The beneficiary is currently in the POD program. Access to eWork
– Initiate/Update Work Review is restricted. Refer beneficiary to the POD Call Center 888-771-9188.”
