C. Premium arrearage of 6 months or more involved - claimant elects SMI as of month of
filing or as of current operating month
Your supplementary medical insurance (SMI) entitlement begins later than your hospital
insurance because of 1. or 2. below:
Enter whichever applies:
1.
You requested we begin your SMI the month you filed your application for Medicare
based on chronic renal disease.
2.
You requested we begin your SMI the month your enrollment was processed.
