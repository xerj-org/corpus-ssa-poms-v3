Effective Dates: 03/04/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411025020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Processing LSDP Allowance or Denial
1. Disability is denied, deny LSDP
To process the denial, take these actions:
•
DWB- In MCS on the DECD screen in the section titled: IS SPOUSE __, enter "3" to indicate
not eligible;
•
If the denial code is not automatically generated, use disallowance code 47 for DWB,
or 51 for CDB;
•
Process the case through EC.
2. Disability is established, LSDP can be paid
To process the allowance, take these actions:
•
DWB- In MCS on the DECD screen in the section titled: IS SPOUSE __, enter "2" to indicate
eligible or entitled to survivor benefits for the month of death is the basis for
allowance of the lump sum.
•
CDB- There is no special screen to indicate that the child is receiving the LSDP because
they meet all the eligibility factors except for filing. Document the file that we
established disability and that they meet all factors of entitlement for CDB except
for actually filing a CDB claim.
