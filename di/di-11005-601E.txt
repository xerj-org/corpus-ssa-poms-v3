E. Procedure -- Special handling of TERI cases in the PC
1. Title II--TERI allowances, EDCS exclusions
For paper “E101” awards and other manual awards, process as follows:
a.
Separate the TERI case from regular mail when received in the mailroom.
b.
Hand-carry TERI cases to the Inquiry and Expediting (I&E) Staff for payment processing.
c.
Control and monitor these cases through completion.
d.
The manager ensures that these cases are properly read into the system with a special
event indicator.
e.
The technical assistant performs a consistency check on the Form SSA-101-U3 (Determination
of Award) per GN 01010.285 and GN 01010.220 prior to issuing the case for review by the specialist.
2. Title XVI--TERI allowances
When a favorable determination is made at any level of appeal, follow the procedures
in SI 00603.011 and SI 00603.015.
