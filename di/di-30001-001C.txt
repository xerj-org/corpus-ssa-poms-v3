C. Meet regulatory requirements
1. Regulation
The Code of Federal Regulations requires each state to have a quality assurance function.
However, each state decides how to structure their quality assurance function and
the scope of activities it performs.
2. Social Security Administration (SSAs) recommendations
SSA recommends that DDSs implement a QA system involving the full range of QA activities
described in this subchapter.
3. Limitations on DDS QA reviews
State DDS quality assurance unit (QAU) reviews must be completed prior to the Federal
QA and pre-effectuation review (PER) sample selection. QAU reviews must not be designed
as a means of reviewing cases selected for QA review by SSA.
