SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26515.001 - Documenting the Rationale in Adult Initial Claims
Effective Dates: 03/24/2017 - Present | TN 7 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426515001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy overview
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) are required to fully document their analysis
of case facts and material issues in a clear and cohesive rationale.
Rationale requirements include:
•
Thorough, evidence-based documentation;
•
Compliance with applicable agency policies and standards; and
•
Support for the sequential evaluation process findings that lead to the conclusion
of "disabled" or "not disabled."
The DDS will use the Disability Determination Explanation (DDE) to organize this information
so that the analysis is clear to subsequent reviewers.
