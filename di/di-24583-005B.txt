B. How do we use the PRT?
We use the PRT by following these steps:
•
First, determine whether the person has a medically determinable mental impairment(s)
(see DI 24583.005C);
•
Second, rate the degree of functional limitation resulting from the mental impairment(s)
(see DI 24583.005D);
•
Third, determine the severity of the mental impairment(s) (see DI 24583.005E);
•
Fourth, determine whether the mental impairment(s) meets or medically equals a listed
impairment (see DI 24583.005F); and
•
Fifth, document the application of the PRT (see DI 24583.005G).
