SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.143 - Processing Medical Reactivations
Effective Dates: 01/20/2010 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010143 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to Do a Medical Reactivation
Medical Reactivation may be used when there are no open claims pending in Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS)/eView and:
•
Trailer mail is received that affects the final decision;
•
The Field Office (FO) detects an error on the SSA-831 such as an incorrect regulation
basis code, onset date, etc. and verifies with the Disability Determination Service
(DDS), Disability Case Review (DCR) or Flexible Disability Unit (FDU) that the claim
should be reopened;
•
The FO revises pertinent factors in the case such as the insured status dates or provides
a statutory blind date last insured, etc. and verifies with the DDS, DCR or FDU that
the claim should be reopened;
•
The DDS, DCR or FDU makes a favorable decision with evidence that is readily available,
but needs to develop for an earlier onset.
