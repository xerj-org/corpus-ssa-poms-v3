B. Procedure - verification of cessation reason
Review the most recent SSA-833-U3 in file to be certain that benefits were terminated
because of SGA. (See DI 28084.015.)
1. Non-work cessation
If the cessation was based on medical reasons, i.e., current severity, medical improvement
or an exception to medical improvement OTHER than SGA:
a.
Prepare an SSA-831-U3 per DI 40510.175 and forward the folder to the appropriate technician to complete an SSA-2795-U2 to
post a denial to the RDD field of the MBR.
b.
Forward the folder to the typist for preparation and release of a disallowance notice
to the individual per Exhibit 1, DI 40510.185.
c.
Instruct the typist to retain a copy of the notice in the claim folder for documentation.
2. Cessation based on SGA
If the cessation is based on SGA, proceed to C. below.
