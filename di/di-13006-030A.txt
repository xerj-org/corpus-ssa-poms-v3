SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13006.030 - Field Office (FO) Procedures after the Disability Determination Services (DDS) Makes A Determination on an Age-18 Redetermination Case
Effective Dates: 11/16/2021 - Present | TN 1 (11-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413006030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Fully favorable determinations
The DDS will process fully favorable determinations in the same manner as CDR continuances.
The DDS will only return folders to the FO if there are any unresolved non-disability
issues. If the case involves paper folder(s), the DDS will forward the folder to the
National Records Center (SSANRC) following usual procedures.
