SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39527.015 - Privately Owned Space
Effective Dates: 07/08/2026 - Present | TN 40 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Comparable rental rate
a.
Determine
the rental rate of comparable privately owned space.
b.
Consider only rates that are based on like elements of cost.
EXAMPLE:Rental charges which include service and maintenance charges must not be compared
with rates that do not include such expenses.
2. Inflation
Consider inflationary trends
in utility and tax costs.
3. Documentation
a.
Provide DA with the basis and documentation
for establishing the rental rate.
b.
Retain a copy
of (a.) above on file in the DDS.
4. Cancellation clause
In executing or renewing leases, the DDS must:
a.
Include
a reasonable right of cancellation
(less than 90 days), if possible; and
b.
Secure
a cancellation clause
in all leases exceeding 1 year; or
c.
If unsuccessful in securing a cancellation clause,secure
a lease not to exceed 1 year, with an annual renewal option for an extended period
such as 3 to 5 years.
d.
If (b.) or (c.) is not possible, consult
DA
at least 30 days in advance of the date proposed for the signing of the lease.
