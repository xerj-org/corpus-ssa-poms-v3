I. Carotid phonoangiography (CPA) (generally available since November 1980)
Caroid Phonoangiography (CPA) is used to record and evaluate alterations in the sound
emanating from the carotid artery. It is a technical extension of auscultation and
is used to assess the possibility of carotid obstruction. Although it can be used
alone, its primary value is as an adjunct to hemodynamically based studies such as
oculoplethysmography (OPG). It is an improved technique because it provides noninvasive
evidence for the diagnosis of narrowing of the carotid artery.
