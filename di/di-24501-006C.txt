C. How to complete the SSA-416 and MA-416
The MC/PC completes the SSA-416 and the MA completes the MA-416 for electronic and
paper claims in DCPS. When processing electronic and paper claims, access the SSA-416
and MA-416 in the “Evaluation
and
Assessments” section on the “Impairment
Severity/Sign” page in DCPS. For more information about medical evaluations in electronic cases,
see DI 81020.110.
NOTE: The disability examiner (DE) may assist in completing the SSA-416 and MA-416, but
only the MC, PC, or MA may sign the form. The DE cannot edit a medical assessment
that the MC, PC, or MA has signed. For more information on editing a signed medical
assessment, see DI 81020.110B.2.
The following chart provides instructions on how to complete the SSA-416 and MA-416:
Form Section
Action
1. Evaluation Period
Select the evaluation period covered in the medical evaluation from the dropdown list:
•
Current evaluation
•
Date last insured (DLI)
•
12 months after onset
•
Other period(s)
For more information about medical evaluation, see DI 24501.002.
2. Subject
Identify which type of medical finding you are documenting (e.g., meets or medically
equals listing). There may be more than one subject for each SSA-416 and MA-416.
3. Evaluation
Document the medical evaluation in this section. As appropriate, address the following:
•
Onset (see DI 25501.200 through DI 25501.470),
•
Duration (see DI 25505.025 through DI 25505.040),
•
Drug addiction and alcoholism (DAA) materiality analysis (see DI 90070.050),
•
Symptom evaluation (see DI 24501.021), and
•
Closed period (see DI 25510.001 through DI 25510.015).
4. MC/PC Statement and Signature (SSA-416 only)
•
Check the box labeled “These findings complete the medical portion of the disability
determination” when the evaluation is complete.
•
Do not check the box when the medical evaluation is preliminary, partial, or advisory.
•
Click the “Sign and
Save 416
Evaluation” button. This will populate the signer's medical specialty code per DI 24501.004, as well as the date signed.
5. MA Signature (MA-416 only)
Click the “Sign and
Save MA-416
Evaluation” button. This will populate the signer's medical specialty code per DI 24501.004, as well as the date signed.
