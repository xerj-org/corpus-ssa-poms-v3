DI 11010.620 Request for Additional Field Office (FO) Development before Determination by the Disability
Determination Services (DDS) or Processing Center Disability Case Review (PC-DCR)
The DDS or federal disability component may refer a case back to the FO for additional
development before making a determination on a disability claim or continuing disability
issue.
