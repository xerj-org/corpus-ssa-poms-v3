SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 71001.070 - Border Field Office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) Process for Reconsideration Requests Canadian Residents File in the United States
Effective Dates: 03/22/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0471001070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Border FO reconsideration processing procedures
The Disability Prototype process does not apply to Canadian claims. Do not use the prototype procedures for Canadian reconsideration claims processed
in the following prototype states: Alaska, New York, and Michigan. All prototype states
will make both the initial and reconsideration determination on reconsideration claims
filed by residents of Canada.
1. Medical denials
The border field office (FO) takes reconsideration requests on initial disability
determinations residents of Canada file in the United States. If the reconsideration
request is on a medical determination, forward the reconsideration request to the
DDS.
2. Non-medical denials
a. Affirmation
If the FO affirms the initial determination, forward the case to the Office of International
Operations after adjudication.
b. Reversal
If the FO reverses the initial determination, forward the case to the DDS for an initial
medical determination, according to the instructions in DI 81010.145.
For additional information on processing reconsideration requests for non-medical
or technical denials, see DI 12005.010 Processing a Reconsideration Request for an Initial Non-Medical or Technical Denial.
