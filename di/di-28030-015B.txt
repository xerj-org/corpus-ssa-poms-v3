B. DDS requests assistance from the FO or another DDS
When the DDS has difficulty obtaining needed information from an individual or representative,
request assistance from another component (the FO or another DDS). The DDS may need
to request assistance from other components in the following situations:
•
The individual has not responded (after appropriate follow-up by mail or telephone)
to requests for information.
•
The individual's response is inadequate to resolve the raised issues.
•
The DDS needs to secure specific information, and believes the other component is
in a better position to obtain it.
•
To obtain medical evidence from certain sources.
•
To arrange a consultative examination (CE) across state lines.
The DDS initiates an electronic assistance request (AR) using their case processing
system. The initiating DDS may also make request by telephone, and confirm by mail
using a Form SSA-883-U3 (Request for Evidence or Assistance (Disability Case)). Requests for assistance should
specify exactly what information the DDS needs for adjudication.
The initiating DDS receives an alert when the receiving office completes the AR and
adds the reply to the electronic folder (EF). The receiving component may also respond
by telephone, email, or mail based on the nature of the request.
