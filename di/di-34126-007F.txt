SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34126.007 - Genitourinary Listings from 09/06/05 to 12/17/07
Effective Dates: 12/09/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434126007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. What does the term “persistent” mean in these listings? Persistent means that the
longitudinal clinical record shows that, with few exceptions, the required finding(s)
has been at, or is expected to be at, the level specified in the listing for a continuous
period of at least 12 months.
