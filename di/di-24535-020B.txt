SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24535.020 - Evaluating Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Claims for Beneficiaries with Cochlear Implants
Effective Dates: 02/17/2011 - Present | TN 7 (08-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424535020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. How we evaluate the hearing loss of beneficiaries with cochlear implants
When evaluating the hearing loss of beneficiaries with cochlear implants the approach
on CDR depends
•
on the date of the comparison point decision (CPD), and
•
when the beneficiary received the cochlear implant.
1. CPD before August 2, 2010, beneficiary had a cochlear implant(s) at the time of CPD,
and the CPD was based on the results of pre-implant hearing testing
Use the results of the pre-implant hearing testing and find that no medical improvement
(MI) occurred since CPD.  Continue benefits on that basis if no exception to medical
improvement (MI) applies.
2. All other cases of hearing loss for beneficiaries who currently have a cochlear implant(s)
Apply the appropriate medical improvement review standard for adults or SSI children
to determine whether the person's hearing impairment has improved since the CPD, regardless
of whether we made the CPD before August 2, 2010, or on or after August 2, 2010.
NOTE: In all cases, do not apply the Group I exception to medical improvement for advances in medical technology
in any adult CDR. (See, Group I Exception-Advances in Medical or Vocational Therapy
or Technology DI 28020.100)
