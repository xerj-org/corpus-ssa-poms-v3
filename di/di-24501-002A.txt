A. Policy for medical evaluation
The medical evaluation is the agency explanation of the medical determination.
Every disability determination must contain a medical evaluation unless the case contains
no medical evidence.
The medical evaluation must address the alleged and discovered impairments for the
full assessment period on one or more medical assessment forms.
