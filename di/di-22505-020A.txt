SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.020 - Developing Evidence through a Field Office (FO) or Another Disability Determination Services (DDS)
Effective Dates: 05/13/2011 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The DDS may contact an FO or another DDS for assistance when experiencing difficulty
in contacting the claimant or other sources for any needed additional evidence. However,
a request for FO assistance in contacting the claimant should be rare.
