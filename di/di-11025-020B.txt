B. Obtaining a Disability Determination Services (DDS) decision if all non-disability
requirements are met
The following additional instructions relate to these cases:
•
At present, these cases are Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) exclusions.
Complete paper Forms SSA-3368 (Disability Report-Adult), SSA-827 (Authorization to
Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration), and SSA-3367 (Disability
Report-Field Office).
•
Prepare a modular disability folder (MDF). Show the NH’s SSN and the DWB or CDB’s
name.
•
Transmit the paper folder to DDS using an SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal).
Complete items 1-14 of the SSA-831. In item 11 remarks show “For LSDP only. NH’s date
of death (date)”.
If you need help in completing these items see DI 11010.205, Completing the SSA-831.
