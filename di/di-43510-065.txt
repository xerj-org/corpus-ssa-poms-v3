DI 43510.065 Nonmedical Development
The foreign disability examiner may speak directly to an adjudicator in the claims
development branch of INTPSC if there is a question about proof for entitlement purposes
or initial folder development. Issues pertaining to insured status, coverage, earnings,
etc., should be routed by an SSA-559 to the claims adjudicator or claims authorizer
in the MOD, depending on which technician has DO jurisdiction.
