B. Operating Procedures
1. Budgeting
a.
Include information about contracting out DDS functions in both the quarterly spending plan and the annual budget submission as a separate
line item.
b.
Include in the Annual Plan submission an estimate of workpower that has been contracted out
and a description of how this workpower was calculated. (This estimate is for work
customarily performed by the DDS, e.g., for transcribing services as opposed to such
work as equipment and building maintenance, guard services, etc.)
c.
Do not include indirect costs with direct cost budget items. See 2 CFR 200 for guidelines pertaining
to the calculation, allocation, and submission of indirect cost proposals.
2. Fiscal
Include contracting out costs on the SSA-4513 (State Agency Report of Obligations for SSA
Disability Programs) under “All other
Non-Personnel
Costs” and, on the Monthly Obligations Report under the “Other Costs” category.
