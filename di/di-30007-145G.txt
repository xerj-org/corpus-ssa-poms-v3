G. Suspending the panel discussion to obtain policy guidance
Prior to the vote, if the panel members are unable to agree on a policy-supported
outcome, any panel member may request additional policy guidance.
Prior to the vote, if the panel members agree on a policy-supported outcome, the involved
DDS or DQR is not clear on how policy should be applied, and the panel members can
explain their application of the policy, no further additional policy guidance will
be requested.
Prior to the vote, if the panel members agree on a policy-supported outcome, the involved
DDS or DQR is not clear on how policy should be applied, and the panel members cannot
explain their application of the policy, DDS or DQR may request additional policy
guidance.
NOTE: Any DDS or DQR may seek policy clarification from the division in DP responsible for
the policy by sending policy questions to ^ODP
Controls.
