A. Policy Principle
Avoiding Restrictions
Subject to appropriate Federal funding, the State will facilitate the processing of
disability claims by avoiding personnel freezes, restrictions against overtime work,
or curtailment of facilities or activities.
NOTE: There may be occasions when circumstances require SSA to restrict the number of DDS
workyears
to a level below the numbers previously established in the budget process.
