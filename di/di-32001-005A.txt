SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32001.005 - Definitions of Terms Affecting Routing
Effective Dates: 12/13/2010 - Present | TN 3 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432001005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Claims with a common issue
Two or more “related claims” (see DI 32001.005J in this section) that have any overlapping period of time (common period of claimed
disability), and the same deciding issue, whether medical or substantial gainful activity
(SGA).
NOTE: It is not necessary that the onset dates or time periods are identical (see DI 12045.005).
