SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28035.030 - Comparison Point Decision (CPD) Folder Received in the Disability Determination Services (DDS) After the Review Process Begins
Effective Dates: 11/15/2021 - Present | TN 3 (11-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428035030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Determination not completed
If the DDS has not completed a determination, the adjudicator will verify the CPD
is in the folder, and consider the material in the CPD folder before making the determination.
The adjudicator will associate the found CPD folder with the current CDR and forward
it as appropriate after making the determination.
