Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for when to purchase a CE
Generally, do not request a CE before you make every reasonable effort to obtain needed
evidence from the claimant’s medical sources. You may order a CE:
•
when case evidence is insufficient to evaluate the claim, or
•
to obtain evidence needed to resolve a conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity in the
evidence.
If the case is a continuing disability review, also see DI 28030.020D.
