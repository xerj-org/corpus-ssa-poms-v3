SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55075.025 - Disputes Involving Payment Issues
Effective Dates: 02/18/2022 - Present | TN 1 (02-22)

A. Policy – disputes about a milestone or outcome payment
1. EN disputes
If an EN disputes a milestone or outcome payment decision, SSA will handle the dispute
according to the procedures in the EN's Ticket Program Agreement (TPA).
2. State VR agency disputes
SSA does not have contracts with State VR agencies. If a State VR agency serving a
beneficiary/recipient as an EN disputes a milestone or outcome payment decision:
•
The State VR agency must send a written request for reconsideration to the TPM within
60 days of receiving the written payment decision notice from SSA.
•
The TPM must review the issue in dispute, make a recommendation, and forward it along
with the reconsideration request to SSA.
•
SSA will review the issue in dispute and the TPM's recommendation and issue a written
decision.
NOTE: Staff in ORDES will issue the SSA written decision. Payment disputes will not be
referred to the Dispute Resolution Board.
3. Payment disputes involving an SSA determination about an individual's right to benefits
Determinations that SSA makes about an individual's right to benefits may:
•
affect an EN's (including a State VR agency's) eligibility for a milestone or outcome
payment;
•
cause a milestone or outcome payment already made to be incorrect, resulting in an
overpayment; or
•
cause a denial of a request for a milestone or outcome payment to be incorrect, resulting
in an underpayment.
Although an EN (including a State VR agency) cannot appeal a determination that SSA
makes about an individual's right to benefits, the EN (or State VR agency) may furnish
any evidence it has that relates to the issues to be decided. Such issues could involve
determinations that benefits should be suspended, terminated, continued, denied, or
stopped or started on a different date than alleged.
