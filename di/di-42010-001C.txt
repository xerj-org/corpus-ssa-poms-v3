C. Congressional Interest in Favorable ALJ Decisions
When effectuating a favorable ALJ decision, you may find in file a congressional inquiry
which has been closed out by the ALJ or the OHO Central Office notifying the congressman
or Senator of the ALJ's favorable action. If this has occurred, act upon the congressional
interest in accordance with current local operating instructions when releasing the
approved determination.
