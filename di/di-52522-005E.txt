E. Title XVI claim with a title II disability denial determination or decision for the
same period or later
We cannot adopt the prior title II denial determination or decision under the Clark
settlement. Evaluate all available evidence, including the evidence used in making
the prior determination or decision, and any additional evidence received to make
an independent determination on the title XVI claim. A favorable Clark determination
is possible only if there is a reason to reopen the title II determination or decision.
