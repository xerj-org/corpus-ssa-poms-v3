D. Completing the Spanish notices
1.
Write the beneficiary's (or recipient's) name, address and Social Security number
in English on the English and Spanish versions of the notice.
2.
Write the date in Spanish on the Spanish version of the notice (i.e., (day) de (month
de 20XX ).)
3.
The Spanish translation for each month is:
January (enero)
February (febrero)
March (marzo)
April (abril)
May (mayo)
June (junio)
July (julio)
August (agosto)
September (septiembre)
October (octubre)
November (noviembre)
December (diciembre)
