A. Policy
Disability
Adjudication
(DA)
concurrence
The DDS must obtain DA concurrence for any proposed leasebecause of the substantial
liability to the Trust Fund for space acquired for the disability program.
DA must review and approve any State plans for physical moves to new space where decentralized
offices are involved.
