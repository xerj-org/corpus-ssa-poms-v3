E. Disabled - Impairment(s) is/are Confinement-Related (Continuance for Freeze Purposes
Only)
The determination of continuing disability is justified only when consideration is
given to (an) impairment(s) associated with the individual's confinement for felony
conviction. FREEZE ONLY - The individual is not under a disability for cash benefit
purposes during any month confined.
