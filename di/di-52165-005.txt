DI 52165.005 Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Offset Data on the Master
Beneficiary Record (MBR)
The WC/PDB data on the MBR is used for taxation purposes and to determine if future
action is necessary. If the MBR data is not correct, the case may not be identified
for action or may be incorrectly identified.
