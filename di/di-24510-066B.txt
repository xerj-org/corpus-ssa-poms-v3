B. Adoption of initial level RFC
After a complete, independent, and thorough review of all the evidence in file at
the reconsideration level, the MC or PC may adopt the physical RFC or MRFC prepared
with the initial determination. For more information on when the evidence supports
adopting initial level medical assessments, see DI 27001.015B.2.
If the MC or PC adopts the initial level physical RFC or MRFC, include the following
statement on the adopted medical assessment:
“I have reviewed all the evidence in file.
I adopt as written
the
[medical
assessment]
(e.g., physical RFC or MRFC) of [date]
by [MC or PC
name].”
The MC or PC must sign and date the medical assessment based on the type of claim
or disposition selected using an approved electronic signature.
For information on:
•
Adopting an initial level Psychiatric Review Technique (PRT), see DI 24583.005G.1
•
Adopting an initial level medical assessment, see DI 27001.015B.2.
•
Adopting electronic medical assessments, see DI 81020.025D.
•
Adjudicating disabled child claims at the reconsideration level, see DI 25230.001B.6.
NOTE: Adopting the physical RFC or MRFC assessment is not the same as affirming the initial
determination because the medical assessment is only part of the determination. For
more information on affirming (or reversing) the initial determination, see DI 27001.015C.
