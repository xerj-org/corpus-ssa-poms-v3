A. Federal PDB offset - covered service exclusion
The decision whether to offset depends on the date of onset of disability and the
amount of covered service. Covered service is employment on which Federal Insurance
Contribution Act (FICA) taxes were paid either through payroll deduction or other
arrangements. Medicare Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) is not covered employment
for this purpose.
