D. Public disability benefits (PDB)
1. Payments
Verify any PDB payment using any applicable procedures used for WC benefits.
NOTE: WC benefits are usually paid by the insurance carrier and not by the employer. A
carrier can also pay a PDB. Make sure that the PDB is paid pursuant to a Federal,
State, or local law or plan and is not a private disability insurance benefit. (See
DI 52125.001 for PDB information).
2. PDB causes offset
Verify the amount, dates paid, etc. of any PDB that causes offset.
3. Covered service exclusion met
Verify the total service used to establish the PDB and the amount of covered service
according to the rules in DI 52125.001.
4. Possible reverse offset
Verify whether the PDB is offsettable or is excluded from offset due to reverse jurisdiction per
DI 52105.001 - Public Disability Benefit (PDB) Rules and Definitions.
5. Civil Service Retirement System (CSRS) disability
Follow DI 52130.005 – Civil Service Retirement System (CSRS) Disability Benefits.
6. Federal Employees' Retirement System (FERS) disability
Follow DI 52130.010 – Federal Employee Retirement System (FERS) Disability Benefits.
