C. Processing a cessation
1. SSA-833-U5 determination
This will be prepared in the usual manner per DI 13010.170 and DI 41005.035 and the instructions below.
a. Individual entitled to a TWP
In item 9 of the SSA-833-U5, check “disability.” Complete blocks 9B though 9H as appropriate (see DI 41005.001J.3. for completion of block 9C if an entry is needed there.) Also check block 9J and
9J(1). See DI 41005.035, Exhibit 2 for an example of the completed form.
b. Individual not entitled to a TWP
In item 9 of the SSA-833-U5, check “disability.” Complete blocks 9J, 9J(1) and 9J(1)(b). Enter the first month and year of SGA in
block 9J(1)(b). Complete item 9C as shown in DI 41005.001J.3. and show the second month (plus year) after the month shown in block 9J(1)(b). See
DI 41005.035, Exhibit 13 for an example of the completed Form SSA-833-U5.
2. Notice guidelines
a.
If EPE is involved, use the exhibits in DI 13095.140.
b.
If the EPE is involved but ended and benefits will terminate at the time the SSA-833-U5
is prepared, include the following sentence in the notice: "Your disability freeze
will continue until you reach full retirement age or you no longer meet the definition
of blindness contained in the law."
c.
If the EPE is not involved use T21 and include the following sentence: "Your disability
freeze will continue until you reach full retirement age or you no longer meet the
definition of blindness contained in the law."
d.
Prepare a personalized explanation of the decision on an SSA-4268-U4 (see NL 00601.010B.2.) and ask the typist to attach it to the notice to the beneficiary.
3. Rationale
The technical rationale should be prepared on a separate SSA-4268-U4. See DI 41005.005.
