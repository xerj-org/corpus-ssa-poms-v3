C. Form SSA-1099 or SSA-1042S Annual Social Security Benefit Statement
The Social Security Administration (SSA) must provide an annual Social Security Benefit
Statement Form SSA-1099 for each beneficiary that shows the amount of benefits we
paid in the prior year. We send nonresident aliens Form SSA-1042S rather than Form
SSA-1099. Social Security benefits first became subject to the federal income tax
in 1985 for tax year 1984. For more information about Taxation of Benefits, see GN 05001.005.
