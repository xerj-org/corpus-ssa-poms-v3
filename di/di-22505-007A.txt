SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.007 - Developing Evidence from Medical Sources
Effective Dates: 09/17/2020 - Present | TN 49 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Request medical evidence
We request medical evidence from each medical source that the claimant identifies
as having evaluated, examined, or treated him or her. For the definition of medical
source, see DI 22505.001A.4.
For general instructions on requesting medical evidence, see DI 22505.006.
For a suggested model letter to use when requesting medical evidence of record in
adult claims, see NL 00705.770.
