E. How to apply the age categories
Use the age category (or categories) applicable to the claimant during the entire
period that you are deciding whether he or she can do other work.
REMINDER: Do not apply the age categories mechanically in a borderline age situation. For borderline
age policy and procedures, see DI 25015.006.
