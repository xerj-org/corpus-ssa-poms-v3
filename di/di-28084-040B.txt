B. Procedures for completing Item 23
Enter diary type, month, and year of the diary and diary reason in Item 23. A diary
entry is required for all continuance determinations. For instructions on completion
of diaries, see DI 28001.020.
NOTE: The National Disability Determination Service System (NDDSS) require that a day entry
of “01” also be entered for diary dates. For information on disability cessation diaries,
see SM 01301.235.
