SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24501.030 - Rounding a Claimant’s Height When Assessing Impairment Severity
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 24 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424501030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to round height
When assessing impairment severity for claimants with obesity, growth, or digestive
disorders, round the claimant’s height measurement as indicated in DI 24501.030B.
IMPORTANT: Do not round height for respiratory disorders. The respiratory disorders listings’
tables provide ranges of height in the Listing of Impairments in DI 34001.014 and DI 34005.103.
