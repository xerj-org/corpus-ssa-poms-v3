DI 55050.015 Handling Complaints about the Ticket Program Manager (TPM)
Procedure
Field components should takes statements from individuals with complaints about SSA's
TPM along with any documentation they wish to submit. Then, field components should
send this material to the Regional Center for Disability Programs for subsequent forwarding
to ORDES (see DI 55075.030).
