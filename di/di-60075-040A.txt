A. Eligibility for the $1 for $2 offset
To be eligible for the $1 for $2 offset, a POD participant must:
•
have been within the POD participation period (For a definition of POD participation
period, please see DI 60075.035.);
•
earned above the POD monthly threshold. The POD monthly threshold was the greater
of the trial work period (TWP) level or the total approved amount of the beneficiary’s
monthly impairment related work expenses (IRWE); and
•
have been entitled to Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) benefits.
