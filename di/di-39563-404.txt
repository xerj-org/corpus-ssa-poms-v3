DI 39563.404 Workload Management Priorities
DDS administrators should establish and maintain internal workload management priorities
consistent with program processing goals.
SSA, through analyses of the DDS performance and through knowledge of national and
other trends, may identify priorities based on current needs.
The DDS and SSA should reconcile any diverging priorities of workload management as
soon as possible.
