B. Policy - Notification of Overpayment
When a determination is made that an individual is overpaid, they will be informed
of the overpayment in accordance with the notice procedures in GN 02201.009.
While a decision to pay, not pay, or adjust a provisional benefit carries no appeal
rights, an overpayment involving provisional payments does carry normal rights to
file a request for waiver of recovery and to appeal the overpayment decision.
