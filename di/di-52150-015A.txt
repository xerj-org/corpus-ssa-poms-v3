SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.015 - Determining the Applicable Limit
Effective Dates: 09/25/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Factors to determine the applicable limit
The applicable limit is the higher of:
1.
The total family benefit (TFB) per DI 52150.005; or
2.
80 percent of the average current earnings (ACE). See DI 52150.010.
Subtract the monthly WC/PDB amount from the applicable limit to determine if the disability
insurance benefit (DIB) is offset (i.e., reduced).
NOTE: Redetermine the applicable limit if there is a change in the TFB or ACE.
