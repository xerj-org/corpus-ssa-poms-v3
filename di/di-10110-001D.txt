D. Found disabled before the end of the prescribed period
To qualify for disability benefits, a widow(er) (including certain surviving divorced
spouses) must be found disabled before the end of a certain prescribed period as defined
in the law. The widow(er) may allege a disability onset date that is earlier than
the prescribed period however, DDS will determine if disability can be established
before the prescribed period end date.
To determine the prescribed period, follow instructions in DI 11005.050, Prescribed Period and Controlling Date.
