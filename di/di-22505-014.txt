DI 22505.014 Requesting Evidence or Action from the Claimant or Third Party
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) may need to request that the claimant
provide evidence or take action on their claim. Generally, DDS makes the initial request
by letter. However, DDS may make the initial request by telephone. In some cases,
the claimant may not comply with the initial request. This section explains what DDS
must do to make a reasonable effort to get the claimant to comply with that initial
request (whether by letter or telephone) before discontinuing efforts to obtain evidence
that requires the claimants cooperation.
REMEMBER: Our goal is to ensure we make a reasonable effort to secure all relevant evidence
to make a determination, regardless of whether the claimant cooperates.
