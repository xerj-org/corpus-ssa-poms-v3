DI 26010.020 Completing Items 16A (Primary Diagnosis) and 16B (Secondary Diagnosis) on the SSA-831
for Title II and Title XVI Blindness Cases
In all claims in which statutory blindness is established, you must enter “Statutory
Blindness” and diagnosis code 3694 in Item 16A (Primary Diagnosis) or Item 16B (Secondary
Diagnosis).
