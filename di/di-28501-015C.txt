SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28501.015 - Reopening of Prior Favorable Initial Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 03/22/2023 - Present | TN 6 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428501015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Administrative finality does not allow reopening
When the rules of administrative finality do not allow reopening, the disability examiner
must initiate a CDR claim and prepare a determination of continuance or cessation
using the usual CDR procedures.
