DI 40505.305 Pre-effectuation Review (PER)
State Disability Determination Services (DDS) Title II continuances are subject to
selection for PER in the processing center (PC) unless we select them for quality
assurance review.
Cases we select for PER require disability examiner review. For types of cases we
review, see QR 04440.005
- Types of Federal Quality Reviews (Types of Federal Quality Reviews (QR).
