B. When not to reconstruct a prior folder
The DDS will not pursue folder reconstruction when a continuance determination can
be made based on current evidence (i.e., individual currently meets or medically equals
a listing or can be a current medical-vocational continuance case).
NOTE: If the CDR is an Age-18 Redetermination, it is evaluated under the adult rules as
a "new initial claim." Therefore, the DDS does not need to reconstruct the CPD folder.
However, if the CPD folder is readily available, the field office (FO) will send it
to the DDS.
