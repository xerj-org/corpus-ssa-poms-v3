SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31001.005 - Claimant's Consent to Disclosure of his or her Records
Effective Dates: 09/17/2013 - Present | TN 7 (04-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431001005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Claimant's consent to disclosure does not authorize the individual or entity to act
on the claimant's behalf
A claimant’s consent for SSA to disclose his or her records to an individual or entity
does not authorize that individual or entity to act on the claimant’s behalf as his or her
appointed representative. See GN 03910.020 for information on qualifications for and recognition of representatives and GN 03910.025 for information on authority of representatives.
NOTE: If a claimant wants more than general assistance on his or her pending claim from
an individual, the claimant must appoint that individual as his or her representative
as explained in GN 03910.040. However, if the claimant attempts to appoint an entity as his or her representative,
SSA will not recognize the entity as the claimant's appointed representative and will
follow the instructions in GN 03910.040B.6.
