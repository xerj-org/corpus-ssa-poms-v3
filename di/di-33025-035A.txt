A. Background on fraud or similar fault (FSF) violations
The disability hearing unit (DHU) may receive a case that is under investigation for
FSF, or a case with a completed investigation. Rarely, will the DHU be the first to
identify a potential FSF issue. The DHU may make an FSF referral; see the FSF referral
policy and procedures in DI 23025.015 Disability Determination Services (DDS) – Referring
Potential Fraud or Similar Fault (FSF) Cases for Possible Investigation.
