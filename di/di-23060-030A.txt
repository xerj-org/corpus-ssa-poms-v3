A. Evaluating evidence furnished by excluded medical sources of evidence
This section applies to initial and reconsideration determinations and continuing
disability reviews (CDR) for disability claims under titles II and XVI of the Social
Security Act (Act).
Do not consider evidence furnished by excluded medical sources of evidence unless
a good cause exception(s) applies. See DI 23060.010B.1 for the definition of excluded medical sources of evidence.
To document the evaluation of evidence furnished by an excluded medical source of
evidence, follow the instructions in section DI
23060.030D.
REMEMBER: Evaluating evidence furnished by an excluded medical source of evidence does not affect
the Social Security Administration’s (SSA) fraud or similar fault (FSF) policy. See
DI 23025.000 for instructions on FSF.
