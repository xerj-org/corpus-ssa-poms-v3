B. Identifying TERI cases
TERI cases are identified either directly by the claimant’s allegation or indirectly
by the TSC, FO, or DDS staff by the use of TERI descriptors. All claims representatives
and disability examiners must be alert to identify possible TERI cases, see DI 11005.601C. in this section.
Once designated as a TERI case, that designation remains on the case until all administrative
appeals are exhausted. TERI case processing is expedited at each step in the process.
