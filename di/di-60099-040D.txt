D. Full offset
When the monthly total available for offset exceeds the monthly benefit amount, the
beneficiary is in full offset. The MBR displays a LAF code of SQ and the RFD displays
a code of Q and a RFST of DMPRDD indicating demo project “DD” BOND offset case.
