Effective Dates: 03/24/2015 - Present | TN 11 (11-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425501450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Component roles for inferring onset
1. Field office (FO)
The FO does not have a role in inferring onset.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
If additional information is not available, but the evidence in the file supports
an allowance determination, the adjudicator will consider inferring onset.
