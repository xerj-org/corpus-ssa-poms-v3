A. Introduction
To assure a comprehensive assessment of mental RFC, the SSA-4734-F4-SUP requires the
medical or psychological consultant (MC/PC) first to record preliminary conclusions about the effect of the impairment(s) on each of four general areas of mental function (described in B.1-4 below), then to prepare a narrative statement of mental RFC.
