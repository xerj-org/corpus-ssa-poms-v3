SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24510.066 - Streamlining Residual Functional Capacity Assessment Form Completion
Effective Dates: 01/15/2020 - Present | TN 71 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424510066 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Partial completion of Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) form
The medical consultant (MC) or psychological consultant (PC) is responsible for assessing
the RFC when an RFC finding is required. Disability examiners (DE) may assist the
MC or PC in completing the RFC form. The RFC assessment must describe the most the
individual is able to do despite impairment-related limitations.
The adjudicative team is not required to complete the entire RFC form in fully favorable
determinations. When the physical RFC or mental residual functional capacity (MRFC)
assessment is completed to the point that the determination will be fully favorable,
the MC or PC may sign the medical assessment without completing the entire form.
However, the adjudicative team must provide at a minimum, a statement explaining the
physical RFC or MRFC. In the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS), add the summary
statement in the explanation box under the specific limitation or in the additional
explanation box at the end of the form. The summary statement must describe all the
individual's limitations, including any exertional, non-exertional, and environmental
limitations.
