DI 39542.030 DA Review and Clearinghouse - Background
Disability Adjudication (DA) review will address:
•
the concept of contracting out a particular DDS function;
•
the basic plan for getting the work accomplished;
•
whether or not the proposed contract is consistent with Federal procurement guidelines.
NOTE: The review will not ordinarily address the State's contracting process.
SSA will maintain a clearinghouse library containing:
•
reports of State contracts; and
•
contract proposals which have been disapproved.
NOTE: This clearinghouse library will service as a resource to:
•
help States in proposing similar contracts; and
•
facilitate DA review and approval of State contracts.
