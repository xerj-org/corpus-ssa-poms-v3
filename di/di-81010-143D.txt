D. How to Cancel a Medical Reactivation
If a claim is medically reactivated by mistake, users have the ability to cancel the
medical reactivation.
FO users must cancel the medical reactivation using EDCS by going to the Claims Actions
tab and choosing Cancel Reactivation. The FO cannot cancel a Medical Reactivation
through eView.
DDS, DCR or FDU users receipt the claim and close it as a No Decision and select the
reason Mistakenly Reactivated in their case processing system.
