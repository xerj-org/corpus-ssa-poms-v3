E. Policy - Workers’ Compensation (WC) Payments
•
If the claimant is receiving SBC and WC payments at the same time, then the current
rules regarding WC offset apply.
•
When electing SBC, if WC offset applies to benefits at the time of cessation, continue
applying WC offset and make adjustments as if the individual were receiving regular
disability (DIB) payments.
•
If WC is first received during the SBC period, offset will begin at that time as if
the individual were receiving regular disability (DIB) payments.
See Offset Provisions, DI 52101.001 regarding workers' compensation offset.
