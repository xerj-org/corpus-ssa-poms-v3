A. Operating policy – suggested guides
DDS and SSA staffs should work together in assuring meaningful and continuing DDS
self-appraisal. Published processing and accuracy standards should be translated into
internal operating goals and performance standards.
The DDS employee is then aware of the expected performance level.
DDS management and QA personnel should gain proficiency in such areas as:
•
operational analysis;
•
sample selection;
•
workflow charting and analysis;
•
report writing;
•
designing and conducting special studies.
