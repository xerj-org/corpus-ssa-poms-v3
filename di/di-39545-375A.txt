Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 12 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545375 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
All complaints will be investigated and treated seriously. DDS are required to investigate
all claimant complaints and take appropriate action per their State procedures, and
notify Disability Adjudication (DA) as appropriate.
References:
PM 00233.005 Consultative Examination (CE) Oversight Procedures and
PM 00233.900 Guide for Evaluating Disability Determination Service (DDS) Management
of the Consultative Examination (CE) Process
