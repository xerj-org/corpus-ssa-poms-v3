SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 44001.105 - Jurisdiction of Dual Eligibility Claims
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 10 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0444001105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Inappropriate DDS determinations on dual eligibility claims
If a career RR or dependent RR survivor disability annuitant claim has been erroneously
transferred to the DDS, the FO will immediately phone the DDS and ask them to transfer
jurisdiction of the case to DCR, location code V32, according to the instructions
in this section.
If DCR is the first to discover the erroneous transfer, it will contact the DDS to
request the transfer.
1. Case pending in DDS before final determination - No consultative examination (CE)
scheduled
Contact the DDS and ask them to transfer jurisdiction to DCR, location code V32. Ask
the DDS to forward any additional evidence they receive on the case to DCR.
NOTE:
In a fully electronic case, the DDS should scan any additional evidence into the electronic
file (EF).
2. Case pending in DDS before final determination - CE appointment scheduled
Contact the DDS:
•
Advise DDS that the claim is the jurisdiction of DCR and that DCR will make the disability
determination on the case once the DDS development is complete.
•
Ask DDS to continue processing the claim until the CE has taken place and they have
received the CE report.
•
Ask DDS to transfer jurisdiction of the claim, including any additional evidence,
to DCR, location V32, upon receipt of the CE report.
3. Final determination completed - DDS case closure to FO jurisdiction - Notice released
If the notice on the medical determination has already been released (including the
release of the SSA-L1157, explaining a partially favorable onset determination), the
DDS determination becomes the official determination. There is no reason to return
the case to DCR for review unless there is an obvious error on the face of the determination.
The FO should always notify DCR of the DDS determination for coordination with the
RRB.
If a request for reconsideration is subsequently filed, the FO should transfer the
case to DCR for processing of the reconsideration determination. Refer to DI 44001.105B in this section for more information.
4. Determination completed - DDS case closure to FO jurisdiction - Notice not released
If the notice of the medical determination has not been released, the FO notifies
DCR of the error. A DCR DPS and a medical consult/psychological consultant (MC/PC)
review the case to determine if the DDS determination may be adopted. This may require the FO to medically reactivate the case.
NOTE:
The FO must contact DCR before sending it a medical reactivation due to erroneous
processing, per DI 81020.117. The DCR must agree with the FO decision to medically reactivate the claim.
DCR action upon case review:
a. DPS adopts DDS determination
•
Annotate the DDS SSA-831(s) in the electronic folder with a NOTE: DDS determination adopted by DCR;
•
Follow current procedures for a "No Decision" (ND) determination in the Disability
Case Processing System (For more information refer to DI 81020.127); and
•
Transfer jurisdiction to the appropriate FO or PC for effectuation of the DDS determination.
NOTE:
If the case is closed and not medically reactivated, print the existing 831, make
annotations, and fax back into the CEF.
b. DPS does not adopt DDS determination
•
Medically reactivate the case;
•
Prepare a new SSA-831 determination and rationale;
•
Annotate the erroneous DDS SSA-831 with a NOTE: Do not process. See SSA-831 dated _________.; and
•
Process the determination and transfer the closed case to the FO for final processing.
NOTE:
For paper case folders, continue to follow the procedures in Inappropriate DDS Determination
DI 40115.020B.
