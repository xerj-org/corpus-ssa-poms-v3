DI 52120.185 North Dakota Workers' Compensation (WC)
The North Dakota Workforce Safety & Insurance Organization (WSI) handles WC cases.
WSI is the sole provider and administrator of the WC system in North Dakota. All employers,
except those authorized to self-insure, must purchase WC insurance from the State
fund, rather than from private insurance companies.
