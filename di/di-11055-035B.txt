SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11055.035 - Determining Whether the SSI Claim Needs a Field Office (FO) or Disability Determination Services (DDS) Determination
Effective Dates: 06/01/2010 - Present | TN 81 (11-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure for DDS development and medical determination
When the claimant meets the non-medical eligibility criteria, transfer the disability
case to the DDS the same day with a complete disability application and medical materials
(e.g., medical release forms, disability reports, and work activity forms).
Accept and include in the official disability folder any evidence or information that
the claimant possesses relating to alleged disabling condition. Stress to the claimant
that submitting this evidence does not relieve the responsibility to assist SSA or
the DDS to develop additional evidence.
