DI 43520.055 Routing Of Completed SSA-831-U5
Upon completion of the SSA-831-U5 determination, the disability examiner will file
the folder copy of the SSA-831-U5 on the left side of the folder. The green, pink
and blue copies will be dispatched to the appropriate baskets in each section in the
Claims Development or Reconsideration Branch. The folder will be sent to a claims
adjudicator in CDB or the claims authorizer in the MOD (depending on which technician
has DO jurisdiction) for the necessary action on the allowance or denial.
