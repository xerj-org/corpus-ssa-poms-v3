A. Title II evidence of statutory blindness
For Title II claims:
1.
We need evidence from an acceptable medical source to establish a medically determinable
visual impairment.
2.
Acceptable medical sources for visual disorders include licensed:
•
Physicians (medical or osteopathic doctors); and
•
Licensed optometrists for impairments of visual disorders, or measurement of visual
acuity and visual fields only, depending on the scope of practice in the State in
which the optometrist practices.
For more information about who are acceptable medical sources to establish whether
an individual has a medically determinable impairment(s), see DI 22505.003.
3.
For additional information about evidence needed to evaluate visual disorders and
statutory blindness under Title II, see “How do we evaluate visual disorders?” in
DI 34001.012A.
