DI 81010.210 Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) Exclusions
Most Continuing Disability Review (CDR) cases can be processed electronically; however,
there are CDR cases still processed as Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
exclusions. See DI 81010.030 for more information on EDCS exclusions.
