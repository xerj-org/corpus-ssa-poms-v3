A. Introduction
This section provides guidance regarding the processing of appeals on Special Disability
Workload (SDW) cases. All SDW cases must be mapped by an SDW Cadre member. The map
is a document which serves as an important source of information, including critical
factors of entitlement. If a case was not mapped at the initial level, it must be
mapped at a later point based on the instructions below.
This section contains instructions on SDW appeal workflow for FOs/Cadres, DDS offices,
OHO, Office of Appellate Operations (OAO) and PSCs.
