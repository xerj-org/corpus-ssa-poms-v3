A. Blindness (3412)
A person is considered blind if his visual acuity with best correcting lens is 3/60
or 10/200 or less in the better eye. He must undergo an examination for visual acuity
as required by the Department. He has the choice of examination by a physician skilled
in the disease of the eye or by an optometrist.
