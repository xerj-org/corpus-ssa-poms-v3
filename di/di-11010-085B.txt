B. Procedure Disability Insurance Benefits/Disabled Widow(er)s Benefits (DIB/DWB)
If a claimant files a DIB claim as well as a DWB claim, transmit the claims together.
If one of the claims does not require a DDS determination, prepare an SSA-5002 (Report
of Contact) and place it in, or upload to, the official DIB folder. See Preparation
of Folder for Transmittal of Disabled Widow or Widower (DWB) Claims - GN 01050.175, Unsuccessful Work Attempt (UWA) - DI 11010.145 and General Overview of the MDF - DI 70005.005. For claims entered into EDCS see Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect
System (EDCS) - DI 81010.085.
