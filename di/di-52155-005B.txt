B. MCS systems generated diaries
MCS will establish a Diary Code 042 based on entries on the WPCL screen. Diary code
042 is generated when:
•
INTEND-TO-FILE equals Y or
•
WC/PDB CLAIM PENDING equals Y or
•
CLAIM DENIED equals Y and APPEAL PENDING equals Y or
•
PERIODIC PAYMENTS AWARDED equals Y and “APPEAL PENDING equals Y or
•
LUMP SUM AWARDED equals Y and APPEAL PENDING equals Y
