A. Introduction to MRFCs in CDRs
This section discusses the need for an SSA-4734-F4-Sup (MRFC) assessment form in CDRs.
The discussion focuses on steps in the Title II and adult Title XVI CDR sequential
evaluation process. For more information on this process, see DI 28005.015.
