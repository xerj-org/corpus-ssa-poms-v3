1. Filing Instructions - Form SSA-4514
Form SSA-4514 is required quarterly from each agency.
Submit copies as follows:
•
the original will be sent to Disability Adjudication (DA)
•
the original will be sent to the SSA Central Office no later than the 25th day of
the month after the close of the quarter.
•
For example, for the quarter that ends 9/30/2026, reports will be due by 10/25/2026.
