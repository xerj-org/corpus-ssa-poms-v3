SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.112 - Adult Consultative Examination (CE) Report Content Guidelines for Mental Disorders
Effective Dates: 07/16/2025 - Present | TN 55 (07-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510112 (fetched 2026-10-04)

J. Provide a medical opinion
1. The CE provider will specify the nature and extent of the condition(s) or disorder(s);
2. The CE provider will discuss any apparent discrepancies in the medical history
or in the examination findings;
3. The CE provider will specify any limitations in functioning that result for the
condition(s) or disorder(s), including the claimant’s ability to:
a. Understand, carry out, and remember instructions (both complex and one-two step);
b. Sustain concentration and persist in work-related activity at a reasonable pace;
c. Maintain effective social interaction on a consistent and independent basis, with
supervisors, co-workers, and the public; and
d. Deal with normal pressures in a competitive work setting.
