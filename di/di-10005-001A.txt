A. FO responsibilities to the claimant
The claimant must file a valid application and furnish certain evidence to support
a claim for disability, but it is the role of the FO, specifically, the claims representative
(CR), to ensure complete and accurate claims development. The FO informs and assists
the claimant through the disability claim process and makes certain the claimant is
aware of his or her rights and responsibilities.
