SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 45010.045 - Equitable Relief in Postentitlement ESRD Cases
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0445010045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Completion of SSA-892-U3
Completion of the SSA-892-U3 for ESRD terminations involving equitable relief should
follow the instructions in DI 45010.030 with the following exceptions:
1.
Complete item 31. The R-HI terminating date will be the first day of the month after
entitlement ends (e.g., 03/01/86); however, the R-SMI terminating date will be first
day of the current operating month (e.g., 05/ 01/86).
2.
Complete item 32.
3.
Complete item 39 by showing “ceased” and the actual date ESRD ceased (e.g., ceased 02/28/86).
