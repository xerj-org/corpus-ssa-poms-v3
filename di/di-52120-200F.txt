F. Assessment Paid by Employee
For injuries occurring on or after 7/1/2019, the Oklahoma Tax Commission collects
a temporary assessment from the injured employee's award or settlement for PP or PT
disability compensation. The employer or insurance carrier will withhold 3% of the
total award or settlement due the employee and pay that amount to the Oklahoma Tax
Commission. This assessment incurred by the employee in connection with their WC claim
is an excludable expense. See DI 52150.050 for more information on excludable expenses.
