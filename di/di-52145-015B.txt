B. Returning WC/PDB proofs
FO and PC action: When the proofs are successfully added to the EF or NDRed, return
claimant submitted evidence per GN 00301.275. Also see GN 00301.322 for policy instructions related to the retention of paper material. Documents from
sources other than the claimant can be destroyed unless return was requested by the
individual who submitted the documents.
