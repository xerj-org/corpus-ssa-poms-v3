B. Making a determination for a work CDR
The FO determines whether the person receiving disability benefits qualifies or is
already participating in a trial work period (TWP), an extended period of eligibility
(EPE), Section 1619, or other work incentive programs (for example, the Ticket to
Work program). The FO must make any work-related determination prior to sending a
case to the disability determination services (DDS) for a medical review. If the FO
determines the need for a full medical CDR, the FO will initiate the CDR process by
establishing the review on the Disability Control File (DCF), if it is not already
established.
For cases involving a work-related determination, see:
•
DI 13010.000, Work Activity
•
DI 55025.000, Ticket Use Status; and
•
DI 13010.610, The Disability Review Establishment (DRES) Screen.
