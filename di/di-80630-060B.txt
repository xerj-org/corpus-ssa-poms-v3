B. Non-Traditional workload
DCRs assist ODAR and the DDS in special initiatives such as Decision Writing and assisting
DDSs with processing Initials, Reconsiderations and Continuing Disability Reviews
(CDRs). DCRs will follow current operating procedures to process these workloads with
paper folders.
The DCR is able to access all EF functions through the DDS case processing system.
See the DDS Guide in DI 81020.000 for detailed information.
