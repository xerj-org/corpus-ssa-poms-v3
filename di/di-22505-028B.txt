B. Procedure - SSA-827, Authorization to Release Information to the Social Security Administration
1. Specific Authorization Required
a.
The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Individuals with Disabilities
Education Act (IDEA), and the regulations of the Department of Education (DOEd) require
specific authorization from an individual’s parent, caregiver, or guardian before
a school can disclose information about the individual.
b.
To satisfy this requirement, the DOEd helped SSA develop language specifically tailored
to the education community. This language has been incorporated into the SSA-827 in
the opening sections of the form that ask what records are to be released and from
whom they are to be obtained.
2. Enter Claimant Identification Information
a.
Name
b.
SSN
c.
Birth date (mm/dd/yy)
3. Signature Requirements
a.
Individual authorizing disclosure
b.
Witness to signature of person signing the form
