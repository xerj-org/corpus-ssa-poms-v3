DI 11055.230 Field Office (FO) Responsibilities in Presumptive Disability (PD) and Presumptive
Blindness (PB) Cases
CITATIONS:
Section 1631 of the Social SecurityAct
20 C.F.R §§ 416.931 - 416.934
The presumptive disability (PD) presumptive blindness (PB) provisions only apply to
Supplemental Security Income (SSI). The purpose of the PD/PB provision is to expedite
monthly benefits to claimants that we presume are disabled or blind and are otherwise
eligible while the more extensive development is completed.
