B. War Claims Act
Payments under the War Claims Act are WC. The Foreign Claims Settlement Commission,
a division of the Department of Justice, administers the War Claims Act. It covers
for example, civilian American citizens captured and held in Southeast Asia or their
eligible survivors, and members of the Armed Forces of the United States held as prisoners
of war during the Vietnam conflict or their eligible survivors. Information is available
on the DOJ website: http://www.justice.gov/jmd/war-crimes-act-1996-pl-104-192
