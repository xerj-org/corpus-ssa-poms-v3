SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32005.085 - Disability Determination Services Reconsideration Case Routing — Concurrent Title II or Concurrent Title II-Title XVI – Paper Case Only
Effective Dates: 10/17/2022 - Present | TN 2 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432005085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Concurrent Title II — two folders
Route these cases as initial Title II concurrent cases based on the age of the number
holder (NH), except for “special handling” cases shown in DI 32005.065 and DI 32005.070. Route the following cases as indicated:
•
DIB, CDB, and DWB affirmation special handling
cases to Social Security Administration, Office of Central Operations, 6401 Security Blvd.,
Baltimore, MD 21235-6401. Explain the special handling situation in the “Remarks”
section of the SSA-408.
•
DWB Medicare Only Reversals to the appropriate PSC. (For PSC jurisdiction chart, see DI 32005.015.)
•
DWB Medicare Only Affirmation Special Handling cases to Social Security Administration, Office of Central Operations, ATTN: DDSO
Recon Disability Examiners, 6401 Security Blvd., Baltimore, MD 21235-6401. Explain
the special situation in the “Remarks” section.
