Effective Dates: 04/22/2026 - Present | TN 32 (04-26)

B. Policy
When a reviewer is processing a title II only SGA cessation and the folder reveals
that a title XVI application has been filed, the reviewer will obtain an SSI Online
Query printout to determine the status of the title XVI claim.
