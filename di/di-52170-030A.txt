SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52170.030 - Manual Proration of Lump Sum Awards
Effective Dates: 12/17/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452170030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Potential problem cases
The following types of cases may require manual proration of the lump sum (LS) award:
•
Dual entitlement cases;
•
Combined family maximum (CMAX) cases;
•
Pre-1978 cases;
•
Cases that involve more than four WC claims; or
•
Cases the ICF program cannot process correctly. For WC/PDB ICF processing and limitations,
see DI 52165.020.
