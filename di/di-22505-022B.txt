B. Policy for telephone contacts with VHA medical sources
In the rare case where the claimant is a current VHA medical facility patient and
a program medical or psychological consultant determines that a complex medical issue could be resolved by discussion with a medical source, the program medical or psychological
consultant may attempt to contact the medical source at the VHA medical facility by
telephone for this purpose. See the procedure for documenting medical evidence obtained
by telephone in DI 22505.030B.
