SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.005 - Diaries in Multiple Entitlement Situations
Effective Dates: 05/09/2002 - Present | TN 13 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Generally, use the same medical diary for multiple entitlement. (See exception in
DI 26525.005B.1. for title XVI statutory blindness cases.)
NOTE: A later claim does not in itself raise a CDR issue on the existing entitlement, even
when there is an expired diary (i.e., collateral estoppel). See DI 27515.000 for adoption policy.
