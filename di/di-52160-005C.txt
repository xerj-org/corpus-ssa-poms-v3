C. How to compute the ACE in EXR cases
Regardless of whether WC/PDB payments continued through the T8 period, compute the
ACE as follows:
1.
Calculate the ACE based on the onset of the disability period that is being reinstated.
2.
Consider recomputing a previous High-5 or average monthly wage (AMW) ACE if additional
earnings could yield a higher ACE. See DI 52150.010 – Average Current Earnings (ACE).
