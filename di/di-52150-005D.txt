D. Changing the TFB in the first-considered date of offset
The TFB may change if any of the following occur and are retroactively effective with,
or prior to, the first-considered date of offset:
1.
A PIA increase is effective;
2.
A change in family composition is processed; or
3.
A combined family maximum (CFM) applies.
