A. FO Responsibilities in Developing Medical Evidence for Railroad (RR) Cases
1. Career RR Employee Files a Disability Application
Accept any medical evidence offered by the applicant, and obtain the required number
of signed and dated SSA-827s (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social
Security Administration (SSA)).
2. Career RR Employee Alleges a Disability Application Filed with the RRB
When the claimant states there is pertinent medical evidence in RRB, review the MCS
EC and/or ICERS to determine if medical evidence is available.
In DIB-freeze cases when the RR earnings are shown in EC or ICERS, the RRB indicates
that medical evidence is available when the records include a RRB claim number, and
shows that the NH has at least 120 months of RR service after 1936 (or at least 60
months of RR service after December 31, 1995) and is considered a career RR worker.
