SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.300 - Routing Concurrent Disability Insurance Benefits Retirement and Survivor Insurance (DIB-RSI) Claims
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - PC and DDS or DCR determination required
1. DIB- freeze claimant also files for Wife's, Mother's, or unreduced RIB
Use the following procedure:
•
FO prepares an award or disallowance of the claim, as appropriate, when the FO finds
that a DIB claimant is eligible or ineligible for spouse's or mother's/father's benefits.
•
Forward the completed claim with any pertinent material on the same SSN to the PSC
or Office of Disability Operations (Determining Processing Center Jurisdiction GN 01050.055).
•
Forward the DIB file to the DDS per DI 11010.300B (in this section) or to the DCR as per DI 11010.300C (in this section).
•
For claims entered into Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS), see Creating
Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases DI 81010.020 route the claim to the appropriate PC/DCR. Transfer any claims entered into EDCS
per Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.085.
•
Document the official folder by completing a Report of Contact (RC). For claims entered
into EDCS, see eForms DI 81010.115.
•
Transfer the RIB file to the PSC via MCS when a disabled claimant, who is at least
full retirement age (FRA), but not more than 12 months past FRA, applies for a period
of disability and RIB. Annotate the cover sheet to say "RIB-Freeze.
2. DIB claimant not eligible for Widows or Parent's insurance benefits
Use the following procedure:
•
Forward the completed RSI file to the PSC. (If claim was proxy signature there may
not be any material to send to the PC.)
•
Forward the DIB file. For claims entered into EDCS see Transferring Cases in Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.085.
•
Complete a RC to document that the claimant is not eligible for Widows or Parent's
Insurance Benefits For claims that have been entered into EDCS see eForms DI 81010.115.
•
Send the documentation with the claim to the DDS or the DCR, as appropriate, per DI 11010.300B and DI 11010.300C. (in this section). See the routing guide in Claims Routing Guide GN 01050.075.
