SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28040.110 - Attempts to Contact are Unsuccessful or Unproductive in Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) or MINE-Equivalent Cases
Effective Dates: 03/10/2022 - Present | TN 13 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428040110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. WU in a MINE or MINE-equivalent case
WU applies when there is a question that needs to be resolved about whether the beneficiary
or recipient continues to be disabled and the DDS is unable to locate the beneficiary
or recipient. Please note, WU cases (both Title II and Title XVI) are not considered
medical cessation cases. For additional information about whereabouts unknown scenarios
in CDR cases, see DI 28075.007.
MINE and MINE-equivalent cases require limited medical documentation. If the beneficiary
or recipient cannot be located, the DDS must stop development and return the case
to the FO as a "no determination (ND)," even if sufficient documentation exists to
continue the case. Inform the FO whether there is sufficient medical documentation
to prepare a determination, and whether a call-in letter, or other letter, to the
beneficiary or recipient, representative payee, or representative was returned as
undeliverable.
If the FO is unsuccessful in locating the beneficiary or recipient, the FO will prepare
a WU cessation determination for Title II claims, or suspend payment of benefits in
Title XVI cases in accordance with DI 13015.011 through DI 13015.025.
