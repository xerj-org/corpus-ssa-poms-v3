G. Luria-Nebraska neuropsychological battery - (generally available since January 1981)
A comprehensive neuropsychological test battery designed to assess the functioning
of all major lobes of the brain. It is a better technique because it provides a low
cost, portable, relatively brief alternative to the Halstead-Reitan Neuropsychological
Battery.
