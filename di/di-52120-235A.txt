A. Tennessee WC payment provisions
Payments are calculated as 2/3 of the workers average weekly wage. The payments are
subject to minimum and maximum rates. For minimum and maximum rates visit: Tennessee Department of Labor & Workforce Development - Compensation
Rates.
1. Tennessee WC Lump Sum Payments (LS)
Tennessee law allows for amendments to WC awards at any time with approval of the
court. Amended WC awards are valid in Tennessee. If the individual is awarded a lump
sum and it includes an amendment refer to DI 52150.065 Subsequent Addenda to Lump-Sum Settlements.
