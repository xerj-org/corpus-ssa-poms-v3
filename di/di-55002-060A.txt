SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55002.060 - Add or Terminate Ticket Process
Effective Dates: 10/29/2002 - Present | TN 1 (10-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455002060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
When a ticket terminates for certain events in DI 55002.055A:
•
the beneficiary/recipient will receive a systems-generated ticket termination notice
(see DI 55099.070 and DI 55099.071);
•
any representative payee, legal guardian, or authorized representative will receive
a notice with the exact text of the notice being sent to the beneficiary/recipient
(see DI 55099.075 and DI 55099.076); and
•
the Ticket to Work and the General Ticket Queries (see DI 55002.025) will reflect the individual's terminated ticket status.
