DI 26535.005 Types of Notices
This section provides the procedures and instructions for the preparation of Personalized
Disability Notices (PDNs). The claimant, beneficiary, and/or representative has the
legal and statutory right to be notified in writing of any determination made on his
or her claim, the reason(s) for the determination, and his or her appeal rights if
he or she disagrees with the determination. The Disability Determination Services
(DDS) is responsible for preparing a notice to the claimant when a totally or partially
unfavorable disability determination is made.
