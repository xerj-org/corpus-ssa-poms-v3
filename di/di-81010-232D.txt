D. DDS determinations for Age 18 eCDRs
Upon receipt of the DDS determination for the Age 18 Redetermination on the EDCS Actions
Page, follow instructions in Field Office (FO) Procedures After the Disability Determination
Services (DDS) Makes A Determination on an Age-18 Redetermination Case - DI 13006.030. This section provides further CR instructions for ensuring the proper inputs are
complete for the cessation or continuance determination.
The CR must remember to query the SSID (supplemental security income display) to verify
DDS determinations are updated.

SSID can be found in Disability Data—DISB - SM 01601.595.
