B. Determination may be adopted
•
Enter in item 34 of the SSA-831 “Improper DDS Jurisdiction - Determination Adopted.”
•
Code the SSA-831 in accordance with DI 27515.001H.
•
Delete the DDS code in item 2.
•
Notify the DDS of the correction per DI 30005.204B.
