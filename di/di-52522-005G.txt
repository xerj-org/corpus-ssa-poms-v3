G. FO requests DDS to vacate subsequent medical denial determination
If the recipient received title XVI disability benefits and the case terminated because
of N25 parole or probation violation suspension, and then we denied a subsequent disability
claim, the FO should request the DDS to vacate the subsequent denial determination.
A new disability determination is not required in this situation because the FO can
reinstate title XVI disability benefits on the previously terminated case.
Vacate the subsequent claim as follows:
•
For paper cases, follow the reopening process.
•
For electronic cases, receipt in the subsequent claim as a reactivation.
•
Document the claim with a SSA-5002 stating: “Clark court order requires the DDS to
vacate the subsequent claim determination made on ___________. Accordingly, the DDS
vacates this disability determination.”
•
Annotate all documents with the remark “Determination vacated.” in the Notes column
in eView.
•
Close the case with No Determination (ND) decision code, selecting the ND reason “FODR
– FO Determination Requested.”
•
Transfer the claim to the FO.
