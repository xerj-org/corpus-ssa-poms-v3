P. What are motor neuron disorders, and how do we evaluate them under 111.22? Motor neuron disorders are progressive neurological disorders that destroy the cells
that control voluntary muscle activity, such as walking, breathing, swallowing, and
speaking. The most common motor neuron disorders in children are progressive bulbar
palsy and spinal muscular dystrophy syndromes. We evaluate the effects of these disorders
on motor functioning or bulbar and neuromuscular functioning.
