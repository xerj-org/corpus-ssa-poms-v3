B. Imposing and removing offset
Determine the employment category.
1. Private employees
•
Impose offset.
•
Verify the ending date.
•
Remove offset as of the ending date.
2. State and local government employees
•
Offset if the TDI benefit paid to public employees is based on less than 85 percent
employment covered for Social Security purposes.
•
Do not offset if the TDI benefit is based on State or local service all, or substantially
all (85 percent or more), of which is covered for FICA purposes.
