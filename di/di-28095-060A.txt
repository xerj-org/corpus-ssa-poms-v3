SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28095.060 - Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Notices for Continuances
Effective Dates: 08/29/2025 - Present | TN 16 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428095060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Title II CDR notice
The disability determination service (DDS) is responsible for preparing the Title
II continuance notices in a determination of continuance based solely on medical reasons.
The DDS will prepare and (if not subject to a Quality Assurance (QA) review) release
the preprinted form SSA-L1013 (Social Security Notice of Continued Disability), see DI 13095.155. The examiner will specify in Item 29 of the form SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
– Title II) that:
•
The office is to prepare the notice and “L1013” followed by the block number or numbers
to be checked followed by the appropriate fill-in for the block in parentheses (for
example, L1013-1 (4/86), 2(6)).
•
The block or blocks, that you should check for each specific case is shown in the
chart in DI 28095.100 Stock Letters for Title II Only, Title XVI Only and Concurrent Title II and Title
XVI Claims Continuances. Check Block 6 in most situations.
NOTE: If there is an investigation to determine continuing entitlement to benefits under
the extended period of eligibility provisions, for notice instructions see DI 28055.035 through DI 28055.060.
1. Benefits suspended because of a felony conviction
If the benefits are currently in suspense because of a felony conviction, add the
following paragraph to the bottom of the form SSA-L1013 (Social Security Notice of
Continued Disability):
“Your benefits must remain in suspense since our records show that you are confined
because of an offense that constitutes a felony”.
2. The case is subject to a QA review
If the case is subject to a QA review:
•
Do not date the notice,
•
Fold it under the flap of the mailing envelope, and
•
Place it in the file for release by the Office of Quality Review (OQR).
For cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) involving a manual notice,
see QR
04440.150 Processing Correct or Corrected Disability Cases, DI 81020.130 Electronic Case Closure and NL 00603.100 How to Prepare the Notice.
