B. Report content specific to pediatric hematological disorders
Use the specific requirements below to complete the CE report for a hematological
disorder.
1. Current medical history
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
Fatigue;
b.
Anorexia;
c.
Pain crises;
d.
Headaches;
e.
Weight loss or gain;
f.
Edema;
g.
Jaundice;
h.
Ecchymoses bleeding;
i.
Frequency of hospitalizations;
j.
Hemoglobin values;
k.
Infections; and
l.
Frequency of red blood cell (RBC) or other blood component transfusions or infusions.
2. Physical examination
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
Any apparent abnormalities such as enlarged lymph nodes, edema, fatigue level, abnormal
bruising, pallor, scleral icterus, and jaundice;
b.
Evidence of bleeding – ecchymoses, petechiae, and hemarthrosis;
c.
Liver and spleen size; and
d.
Abnormal neurological findings.
