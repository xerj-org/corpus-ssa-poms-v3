Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413010026 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Routing title II work CDR documentation
For direct input exclusion cases:
•
Prepare and send the final notice advising the beneficiary that we will send them
information about any overpayment issues in a separate notice. Fax the DIB CESS Exclusion
Fax cover sheet document using the paperless barcode fax for “eWork DECEFFECT – Effectuation
of eWork decision,” the SSA-833-U5, the SSA-4268-C4 (Explanation of Determination),
and the earnings and event report to the PC of jurisdiction.
•
Write what the exclusion is, and provide clear instructions on what you need the PC
to do (e.g., notice language, MBR coding, etc.).
•
Fax the documents into eView or Evidence Portal (EP).
The claims folder should contain:
•
copies of any determinations: SSA-899-U2 (Continuing Disability Review), SSA-833-U5,
and SSA-4268-C4;
•
copies of notices sent;
•
impairment-related work expenses (IRWE) documentation or subsidy or special condition
computations;
•
any other information, such as relevant reports of contact, necessary to expedite
the reinstatement or suspension of benefits;
•
SSA-820, SSA-821, and SSA-823 (Report of SGA Determination-For SSA Use Only); and
•
any evidence that supports the determination.
