A. When to apply prior entitlement months to the Medicare waiting period
This section provides field office (FO) processing instructions when a claimant has
a prior period of disability entitlement. Prior months of disability benefit entitlement
may count towards the 24-month Medicare waiting period for the current entitlement.
For details about counting months in reentitlement cases, see HI 00801.152.
