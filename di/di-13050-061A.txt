A. Overview for a favorable medical decision
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) makes a favorable determination regarding
entitlement, documents the determination on form SSA-832 and returns the case allowance to the FO via the Electronic Disability Collect System
(EDCS) Action Items list. The FO technician must then process the award.
