DI 44001.130 Notices Dual Eligibility Railroad Cases
When Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR) makes a disability determination, the
disability processing specialist (DPS) completes the disability determination forms
SSA-831/832/833, personalized disability notices (PDNs), and any supplemental rationales
using the Disability Case Processing System. When the case is closed, the decisional
notice and rationale are uploaded to the certified electronic folder (CEF) and stored
in the appropriate section.
The DPS follows State Disability Determination Services (DDS) guidelines for preparing
notices when a totally or partially unfavorable disability determination is made,
including preparation of the PDN and any supplemental rationales. Most fully favorable
allowance determination notices are systems-generated; however, in certain situations,
the DCR provides the field office (FO) with special instructions for notices released
by the FO. For more information, refer to DI 26535.005.
