B. Viewing Documents in Current and Linked Folders
eView simultaneously displays all documents in both the:
•
Current eCDR CEF; and
•
Any linked CPD CEF(s).
All documents in the CPD CEF(s) indicate “CPD” as the document level of the eCDR CEF,
whether they are from an initial, reconsideration, or hearing level CEF.
