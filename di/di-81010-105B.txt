B. Manually Clearing EDCS Cases
EDCS Cases can only be Manually Cleared by a member of FO staff with a management
profile. Manually Clearing a claim is a disposition which stops all further electronic
processing of the claim. A Manually Cleared claim does not allow the user to reestablish
a record for the same filing and prevents linking of the claim as a comparison point
decision (CPD) folder for any future CDRs unless the folder contains one case that
has a medical allowance or continuance. For further guidance, refer to EDCS - Delete,
Clear, Determine Flowchart in the PDF below:

NOTE: Manually clearing cases in EDCS does not clear claims from MCS/MSSICS.
1. Before Inputting a Manual Clearance
a. Case is Pending at the Disability Determination Services (DDS) in EDCS
If the case was pending at the DDS:
•
Obtain a Disability Determination Services Query (DDSQ); and
•
Verify that the case has been closed and is pending at your FO.
b. Case is Pending at the Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR) in EDCS
If the case was pending at the ODAR, verify that:
•
The case has been closed on case processing and Management System (CPMS); and
•
Your FO has jurisdiction of the case.
c. Case Closure Documents
Check eView and verify that the proper closure documents (i.e., SSA-831, PDN, and
DDS worksheet) from the DDS or ODAR are imaged in the Certified Electronic Folder
(CEF). If not, management personnel must request DDS or ODAR add these documents to
the CEF prior to inputting the Manual Clearance. It is imperative for proper processing
that the closure documents are in the CEF before the Manual Clearance is input.
NOTE: If the case is not a CEF, ensure the paper copies of the closure documents are also
contained in the paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF).
2. Manually Clearing the Case
To manually clear the case:
•
Select the Manual Clearance button on the Disability Case Selection page;
•
Select one of the following reasons for manual clearance from the drop down box:
–
“FO did not transfer the EDCS report to DDS”
The FO failed to timely transfer the EDCS report to the DDS and the National Disability
Determinations Service System (NDDSS) shows the case was receipted.
–
“FO did not transfer the EDCS report to ODAR”
The FO failed to timely transfer the EDCS report to ODAR and ODAR has posted their
decision to the Case Processing and Management System (CPMS) and the CPMS status shows
the case is closed.
–
“DDS decision, transaction failure, EDCS segment still open”
DDS inputs the disability decision and closes the case on NDDSS but EDCS still shows
the case is open.
–
“ODAR decision, transaction failure, EDCS segment still open”
ODAR inputs the disability decision and closes the case on CPMS but EDCS still shows
the case is open.
–
“Applicant wants to withdraw a claim”
–
"MPC created in error"
The FO disconnects two cases of a Multiple Pending Case (MPC) when both are pending
at the FO.
–
“Other”
If none of the listed reasons apply, select “Other” and enter the rationale for manual
clearance in the dialogue box (up to 50 characters).
•
Select the "OK” button.
Once manually cleared:
•
The case closes; and
•
“Manually Cleared” appears under claim status.
NOTE: The jurisdiction does change for a manually cleared claim.
