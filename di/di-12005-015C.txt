SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12005.015 - Late Filing of a Request for Reconsideration
Effective Dates: 10/02/2009 - Present | TN 10 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412005015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. If good cause is not established
If the FO determines that the claimant did not have good cause for late filing:
•
Dismiss the request for reconsideration.
•
Send to the claimant the Recon Request - No Good Cause for Late Filing notice, under
Initial Claims, in the Document Processing System (DPS) .
NOTE: The notice does not contain appeal language. The good cause determination and dismissal actions are not subject to administrative
or judicial review because they are not initial determinations.
•
Send a copy of the notice to the Online Retrieval System (ORS).
•
Treat the request for reconsideration as intent to file a new application and close-out
the protective writing following DI 12005.015D or DI 12005.015E.
