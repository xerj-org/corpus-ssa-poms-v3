A. Policy for EXR medical denials
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) can deny an EXR request because the disability
is not the same as or related to the prior disability, or because the claimant is
not disabled when evaluated under the medical improvement review standard (MIRS).
An EXR denial is an initial determination that carries appeal rights as discussed
in DI 13050.085C. However, the claimant cannot appeal the termination of provisional payments due to
the determination.
