B. Special freeze provisions for blindness
A DIB claimant with statutory blindness is entitled to a disability freeze based on
medical findings alone regardless of his or her ability to perform substantial gainful
activity (SGA). SGA limits are higher for blind DIB claimants than for nonblind DIB
claimants.
A blind claimant working:
•
at the blind SGA level is performing SGA. He or she is entitled to a disability freeze
but not cash benefits
•
below the blind SGA level is not performing SGA. He or she is entitled to both a disability
freeze and cash benefits.
