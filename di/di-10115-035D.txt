D. Policy - Medicare
A child reentitled based on a disability may also be reentitled to hospital insurance
benefits and eligible for supplementary medical insurance once the requirements of
the law are met (see HI 00801.146). See HI 00801.152 for instructions on counting prior months of disability entitlement towards the 24-month
Medicare waiting period.
