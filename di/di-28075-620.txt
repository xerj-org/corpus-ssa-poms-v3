DI 28075.620 1619 Determinations - Preparation of the Forms SSA-832 and SSA-833
To prepare the Form SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal - Title XVI), see the instructions in DI 28084.001 through DI 28084.065; to prepare the Form SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal - Title II), see the instructions in DI 28084.001 through DI 28084.065.
For 1619 cases, the following specific instructions apply:
•
In concurrent cases for Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB), Disabled Widow(er) Benefits
(DWB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), and 1619 cases, SSA requires a determination
on both titles; and
•
If the evidence supports a cessation, SSA will cease benefits on both titles. This
situation applies even if the individual is in a trial work period (TWP) under Title
II.
