SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34134.003 - Immune Listings from 07/02/93 – 02/18/02
Effective Dates: 09/23/2002 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434134003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

M. One or more of the following infections (other than described in A-L, above), resistant
to treatment or requiring hospitalization or intravenous treatment 3 or more times
in 1 year (or evaluate sequelae under the criteria for the affected body system).
1. Sepsis; or
2. Meningitis; or
3. Pneumonia; or
4. Septic arthritis; or
5. Endocarditis; or
6. Radiographically documented sinusitis.
