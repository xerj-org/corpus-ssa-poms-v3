H. Work issues in foreign claims
The Office of International Operations (OIO) has jurisdiction and responsibility for
work issues in foreign claims. If the DDS identifies a work issue on a case after
the claim has been transmitted to international program service center (INTPSC), send
the work report to INTPSC for processing.
