A. Correspondence in claims processing
Instructions in this section apply to claimant correspondence and notices sent by
the Disability Determination Services (DDS) for initial level claims at the initial
and reconsideration level. We communicate with claimants, and other individuals who
may have a duty or responsibility to act on behalf of or help the claimant or beneficiary
in the disability claims process.
