G. Policy - Suspension/Terminating Events
Provisional payments will be suspended or terminated following normal benefit policies.
Such suspension or termination events include prisoner suspense, institutionalization,
alien suspense, etc. (See SI 02301.001 through SI 02301.330.) Do not resume provisional payments when a suspension event continues beyond the
provisional payments ending month.
