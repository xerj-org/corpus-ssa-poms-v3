Effective Dates: 01/12/2016 - Present | TN 5 (05-23)

B. Documentation for not reconstructing prior folder
When the DDS determines not to reconstruct a prior lost or destroyed paper comparison
point decision (CPD) folder, the DDS must document the reason on Form SSA-5002 or
similar document. See Report of Contact Form SSA-5002
