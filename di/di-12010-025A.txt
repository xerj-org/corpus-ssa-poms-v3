SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.025 - Action Following A Favorable Determination by the Disability Determination Service (DDS) in Informal Remand Cases
Effective Dates: 07/16/2010 - Present | TN 3 (07-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy following a favorable DDS determination
Following a favorable disability determination in informal remand cases, the DDS prepares
and sends a notice of revised determination to the claimant. The claim folder(s) is
then sent to the field office (FO) for action.
See also:
DI 81010.141 – Processing Electronic Informal Remands.
