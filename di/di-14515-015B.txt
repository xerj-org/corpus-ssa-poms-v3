B. Procedure - Title II Auxiliaries in Separate Household
Send an SSA-L951-U2 notice to auxiliaries in a separate household with the following
and AURORA paragraph REF196.
AURORA paragraph 415:
•
This notice concerns your continuing eligibility to benefits. We cannot continue to
pay you benefits because the person on whose Social Security record you received benefits
is no longer entitled to continued payments for participating in a vocational rehabilitation,
employment, training, or educational program. Therefore, the last month you will receive
benefits is (mm/yy).
