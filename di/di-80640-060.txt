DI 80640.060 Security
Access to the EF will be determined by the position type associated with the PIN of
the individual logged into the workstation. Employees will have access only to those
EF functions needed to perform their jobs. Access will be restricted to the minimum
amount necessary for your position type.
If you have difficulty accessing the application, contact your designated Security
Officer for assistance.
