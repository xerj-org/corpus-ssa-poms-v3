SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55050.005 - Handling Specific Ticket Eligibility Questions
Effective Dates: 05/02/2022 - Present | TN 2 (05-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455050005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Answer specific ticket eligibility questions
Field components should answer specific ticket eligibility questions that a beneficiary/recipient
may have. Should the question involve a complex ticket eligibility issue, refer the
beneficiary/recipient to the servicing FO or the Ticket Program Manager (TPM) for
assistance (see DI 55002.025).
