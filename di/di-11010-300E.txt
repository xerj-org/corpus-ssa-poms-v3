E. Procedure - DIB-RIB claimant lacks DIB insured status - either eligible or ineligible
for RIB
Use the following procedure:
•
If the claimant lacks DIB insured status at alleged onset date (AOD) or after the
period adjudicated by the last prior determination or decision of denial, and is either
eligible or ineligible for RSI benefits, deny the DIB claim in accordance with Title
II Technical Denials, and Claims not Requiring a Disability Determination DI 11010.075. For technical denials other than SGA denials, it is not necessary to access EDCS
if it is known that the claim is denied before the EDCS case is created. See Processing
Field Office (FO) Determinations DI 81010.140.
•
If the claimant indicates he/she does not intend to appeal the DIB claim and, if the
official folder is paper send it to the PC with the RIB.
•
If the claimant files an appeal on the disability claim, forward the RIB claim to
the PSC.
•
If the claimant has not expressed his/her intention with regard to the DIB claim,
where the official folder is paper, retain it in the FO.
•
If the claimant is deceased, do not process a DIB denial. Forward both claims to the
PSC.
