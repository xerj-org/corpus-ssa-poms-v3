F. Aurora WC/PDB notices
The PCs use Aurora to create notices. Aurora is a notice processing system that automatically
posts the notices to the Online Retrieval System (ORS) for viewing. See NL 00702.001 through NL 00702.240 for Aurora functioning and content. PCs can use standard WC/PDB notices available
through Aurora to develop proof.
