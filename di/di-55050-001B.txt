SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55050.001 - Handling General Questions About SSA's Ticket To Work Program
Effective Dates: 05/02/2022 - Present | TN 2 (05-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455050001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples of general ticket questions
1. Is the ticket program available in every state?
Yes.
2. Will my disability benefits stop if I don't use my ticket?
No, your benefits will not stop. The Ticket to Work program is strictly voluntary.
You have the option of deciding whether and when you want to use your ticket (see
DI 55002.010A.).
3. Can someone who gets retirement benefits get a ticket?
No. To get a ticket you must getting a benefit based on disability or blindness (see
DI 55002.005B.2.).
4. Will I get a new ticket if SSA approves my new claim for disability benefits?
Yes, provided you are eligible for a ticket when your new claim is approved (see DI 55002.010B.1.).
5. Will I get a new ticket if SSA approves my request for reinstated (EXR) benefits?
Yes, provided you are eligible for a ticket when your entitlement or eligibility is
reinstated (see DI 55002.010B.3.).
6. What criteria do I have to meet to be eligible for a ticket?
Refer to DI 55002.005 to answer this question.
