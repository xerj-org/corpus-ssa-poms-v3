B. Title XVI disability definitions
1. Individuals age 18 or older
A disabled individual, age 18 or older, has a medically determinable physical or mental
impairment that results in an inability to engage in any substantial gainful activity
(SGA) and
•
is expected to result in death; or
•
has lasted, or is expected to last, for a continuous period of not less than 12 months.
Reference:
DI 10501.001, Meaning of SGA and Scope of Subchapter
2. Individuals under age 18
A disabled individual, under age 18, has a medically determinable physical or mental
impairment or combination of impairments that causes marked and severe functional
limitation(s) and either
•
is expected to result in death; or
•
has lasted, or is expected to last, for a continuous period of not less than 12 months.
References:
•
SI 00501.010, Individual is a Child - SSI
•
SI 00501.020, Student - SSI
