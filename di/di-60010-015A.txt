A. YTED Waiver Policy
We are waiving the standard SSI earned income counting rules for participants’ earnings.
The following alternate program rules will apply to the YTED participants who are
SSI recipients:
•
The first waiver applies to earnings that directly affect participants’ SSI payments.
All earned income by participants is excluded from SSI income counting.
•
The second waiver applies to any deeming that results from participants’ earnings.
For example, a participant’s earnings will not count against the SSI benefit of their
child who receives SSI or their spouse who receives SSI. However, if a participant
is under the age of 18, any income (earned or unearned) from their non-participant
deemor parent(s) will still be subject to deeming.  Additionally, if a minor participant
is married, any income from their non-participant spouse will still be subject to
deeming.
