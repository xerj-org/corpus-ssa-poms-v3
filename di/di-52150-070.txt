DI 52150.070 How Changes in Family Composition Affect Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefits
(WC/PDB) Offset
The original offset computation may not involve the family maximum. However, the addition
or deletion of a beneficiary may cause the total family benefits (TFB) to become the
applicable limit for offset or cease to be the applicable limit for offset.
