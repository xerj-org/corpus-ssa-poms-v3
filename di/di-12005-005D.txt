D. Incomplete appeal requests
Occasionally, a claimant requests reconsideration but refuses or fails to complete
the necessary forms or to provide additional evidence to support the request.
When this occurs, take the following actions:
•
Develop and document the claim folder(s) per GN 03102.300B.1.
•
Send a letter per “Additional Evidence Not Received” GN 03102.300B.2.
•
If the claimant does not submit the SSA-3441 or other forms, such as the SSA-827,
etc., and the case can be processed in EDCS, follow Processing Claims Appeals of Medical
Decisions in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.150.
•
If an SSA-827 is not received and the appeal can be processed in EDCS, then document
the attempts made to obtain the 827 on the “Transfer” screen before sending the case
to the DDS or OHO for review."
