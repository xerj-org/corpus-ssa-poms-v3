DI 28065.035 Additional Requirements for Completing Form SSA-833-U5 in Felony/Prisoner Cases
For the most part, completion of Form SSA-833-U5 Cessation or Continuance of Disability
or Blindness Determination and Transmittal - Title II is the same for felony/prisoner
cases (see DI 28065.001 CDR Considerations for Individuals Convicted of a Felony) as for any medical CDR
determination. However, certain items require special entries as described below.
