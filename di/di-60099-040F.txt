F. Auxiliary payments
Any auxiliaries receiving benefits based on a BOND beneficiary’s record continue to
receive full benefits until the BOND beneficiary goes into full offset. As long as
the BOND beneficiary is eligible for a partial offset amount, the auxiliaries continue
to receive full monthly benefits. If the BOND beneficiary does go into full BOND offset
(LAF SQ.), any auxiliary benefits will also stop due to the BOND beneficiary’s work
activity (LAF SQ).
