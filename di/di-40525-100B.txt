B. Process – PC actions based on PCACS and Disability Control File (DCF) reads
1.
Alerts for folders located in operational sites and components are printed in the
jurisdictional PC's.
2.
These alerts can include Module/DRS locations and any auxiliary files locations.
3.
OCO and the PSC's are responsible for retrieving Title II folders and the Title II
portion of concurrent TII/TXVI folder(s) for these CDR direct release alerts and mailing
them to the FOs.
4.
OCO/PSC is responsible for initiating special search activities and preparing a duplicate
folder if the folder shown on PCACS is not in file (NIF) in the Megasite/CAVES, cannot
be located or has been destroyed.
5.
The OCO/PSC sends any folder belonging to the NH located during the search even if
it is not the appropriate folder, i.e., does not contain the needed medical evidence.
EXCEPTION: If ODO/PSC determines the Title II folder is in a FO, they will forward the alert
to the servicing FO to retrieve the Title II folder and conduct the CDR interview.
6.
DO NOT consider a folder "missing" until the Megasite/Caves/PSC complete the Initial
Pull Period (not to exceed 90 days) and it yields "NO FOLDER".
7.
AT THIS POINT, OCO/PSC changes PCACS to read "AUXRH QUES QUES", or "AUXRH TEMP QUES"
or "CAVES QUES QUES or PC1-6 QUES QUES" for the direct release/mailer alerts.
8.
OCO/PSC updates the FOLDER RECONSTRUCTION field on the DCF to read "Q". See CHART
below.
9.
OCO/PSC is still searching (can take up to 120 days from the "AUXRH QUES QUES", "AUXRH
TEMP QUES" or "CAVES QUES QUES or PC1-6 QUES QUES" entry date) for the medical CDR
folder in other locations.
10.
OCO/PSC still unable to locate the folder, updates the FOLDER RECONSTRUCTION field
on the DCF to read "L".
11.
Prepares a duplicate folder, updates the DCF, and forwards the duplicate folder to
the FO. The FO will allow 15 working days for receipt of the duplicate folder before
contacting OCO/PSC-DRS for status. See DI 40525.100C.
