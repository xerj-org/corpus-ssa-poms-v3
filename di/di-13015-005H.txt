Effective Dates: 10/21/2024 - Present | TN 26 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413015005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Beneficiary or recipient moves to another FO jurisdiction while benefits are suspended
If the beneficiary or recipient moves to another FO jurisdiction while in suspense
(and continues to not respond to the FO's request), transfer the case on the DCF to
the new FO. This transfer will not reinstate benefits.
