DI 11055.150 Preparing the Official Disability Folder for Title XVI and Concurrent Title II/XVI
Claims
If all the claims in a case are in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
and there are no EDCS exclusions or limitations, the case is a Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF) and the official folder is electronic. If one claim in a case is an EDCS
exclusion or limitation, the official folder is a paper Modular Disability Folder
(MDF).
