DI 70005.005 Overview of the Paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF)
Some disability claims are excluded from the Electronic Disability Collect System
(EDCS) (the method by which they are entered into the CEF), and cannot be processed
completely electronically. For a discussion of what constitutes a CEF Exclusion, see
DI 81010.030. In other instances, a processing limitation occurs that removes a case from electronic
processing. The field office (FO) must establish a paper folder if any EDCS exclusions
or limitations are present for any claim associated with a case. The following subsections
discuss processing paper cases via the Modular Disability Folder (MDF).
