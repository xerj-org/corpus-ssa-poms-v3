DI 32010.020 Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Continuance Determination Cases with Additional
Action Required'
Procedures for routing continuance cases that require additional action
There are times when additional action is necessary on a case after the Disability
Determination Services (DDS) makes an initial CDR determination. For example, the
most common reasons for a CDR continuance case to require additional actions include:
•
Payee issues
•
Work Activity Involved (Title II cases)
•
Payments in suspense
•
Change of disability status from 'Blind' to 'Disabled'
•
Change of disability status from 'Disabled' to 'Blind'
•
Field Office (FO) requests folder return
For cases requiring additional action(s), return the entire case to the originating
FO. Add a remark in eView for electronic cases or an SSA-408 (Route Slip) for paper folders. Notate what additional action(s) is necessary or
provide a reason for the return.
