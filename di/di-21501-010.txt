DI 21501.010 Supplemental Manual Instructions of State Welfare Agencies
Reference is made to manual instructions which are officially published by a State
welfare agency for the purpose of providing direction, coordination, and control of
its program. Where such a manual contains guidelines which interpret provisions in
a State plan defining disability and blindness which reasonably need interpreting
and which do not conflict with the approved State plan, they may be treated as part
of the State plan definition. Where the DDS obtains any such written supplemental
manual instructions which provide clarification of State plan requirements, a copy
of such instructions should be forwarded to the DCR. If these supplemental instructions
are used for the adjudication of a conversion case, the SSA-834 rationale of the decision
should identify or describe the relevant supplemental manual instructions and explain
the import of these instructions upon the decision. Should any questions arise refer
them to the DCR.
