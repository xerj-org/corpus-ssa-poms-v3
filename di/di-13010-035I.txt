I. Entitlement to an EPE after completing the TWP -
After the TWP is complete, the EPE provisions apply. Effective 01/1992, the EPE begins
the month immediately following the ninth TWP service month completed within 60 (consecutive)
months. If a beneficiary is not entitled to a TWP, they cannot be entitled to an EPE.
For more information on EPE, see DI 13010.210, Extended Period of Eligibility.
