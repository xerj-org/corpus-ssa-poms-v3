B. Procedure for expediting cases under the emergency clause
OCO, DCR, or DQB make disability determinations for cases temporarily transferred
by the State agency when SSA and DDS have an agreement.
1. DDS action
Transfer jurisdiction of those cases to OCO, DCR, or DQB. (OCO, DCR, or DQB retain
jurisdiction until transfer of jurisdiction back to DDS.)
2. Field office (FO) actions
•
Receive notification from the Regional Office when a State has such a temporary agreement
with SSA.
•
Transfer disability claims directly to OCO, DCR, or DQB.
•
Enter "Emergency Clause case: (Name of DDS)" in the Remarks section of the transmittal
form.
•
For MSSICS or MCS claims, use Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) to transmit
the case For MSSICS or MCS claims,
•
For non-MSSICS or non-MCS claims, use the SSA-831, Disability Determination and Transmittal
form.
NOTE: This rule also applies to CDR actions and other cases specified by the agreement.
Reference:
DI 11010.290, Transferring Emergency Clause Cases by Disability Determination (DDS) to Office
of Disability Operations (ODO), Flexible Disability Unit (FDU), or Program Service
Center/Disability Case Review (PSC/DCR) for claims having Title II involvement.
