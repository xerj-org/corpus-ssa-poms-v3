SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33015.030 - Holding the Record Open for Submission of Documentary Evidence Post-Hearing
Effective Dates: 04/17/1990 - Present | TN 12 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433015030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Request to submit evidence post-hearing
At the beneficiary's (or recipient's) request, the disability hearing officer (DHO)
may allow up to 15 days after the hearing for receipt of evidence which was not available
at the hearing if:
•
The DHO determines the evidence has a direct bearing on the outcome of the hearing;
and
•
The evidence could not have been obtained before the hearing.
NOTE: The DHO applies the two factors in a flexible manner.
