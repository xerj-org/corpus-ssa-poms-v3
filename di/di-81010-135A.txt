SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.135 - Storing Non-Medical Evidence in the Electronic Folder (EF)
Effective Dates: 10/17/2023 - Present | TN 63 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence for pending claims or appeals
When a copy of a document must be stored in the file for pending claims or appeals:
•
Fax the documents into the EF per Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder
(CEF) Using Barcodes - DI 81010.090.
•
Shred the document after successful faxing, unless other procedures require the return
of the evidence to the claimant.
