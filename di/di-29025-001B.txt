SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29025.001 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Action When Fully Favorable Reconsidered Determination Is Not Issued
Effective Dates: 08/31/2026 - Present | TN 11 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429025001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. SSA-887 Summary of Evidence
1. Section A.
Complete Section A. as follows:
•
List all medical reports obtained since the Comparison Point Decision (CPD), including
consultative examinations (CEs), in chronological order (newest to oldest based on
the date of the evidence or report);
•
List any vocational reports obtained since CPD; and
•
List all medical assessment(s) required for the case (e.g., RFC, MRFC, PRT).
2. Section B.
Complete Section B. as follows:
•
List all medical reports used at CPD, including CEs, in chronological order (newest
to oldest based on the date of the evidence or report);
•
List any vocational reports used at CPD; and
•
List all medical assessment(s) prepared at the CPD (e.g., RFC, MRFC, PRT).
