SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11055.035 - Determining Whether the SSI Claim Needs a Field Office (FO) or Disability Determination Services (DDS) Determination
Effective Dates: 06/01/2010 - Present | TN 81 (11-22)

C. Concurrent claims
A concurrent claim exists when a claimant files claims under both the Title XVI and
Title II programs. For example:
•
A claimant files concurrently for disability under both Title XVI and Title II, and
both claims require a disability determination; the FO transmits the case to DDS.
•
A claimant files a Title XVI disability claim and the claimant has a Title II disability
claim already pending at DDS, the FO transmits the Title XVI portion of the case to
the DDS. The FO must notify DDS that the claimant now needs concurrent disability
determinations.
•
A claimant files a Title XVI disability claim and a Title II non-disability claim
(e.g., retirement or survivor’s claim); transmit only the Title XVI case to DDS. The
FO processes a determination for the Title II non-disability claim.
