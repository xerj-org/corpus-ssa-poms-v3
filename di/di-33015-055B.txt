B. Procedure to adopt prior determination
Follow the guidance in DI
27515.000 to determine whether the prior final determination or decision is to be adopted.
Assess whether the prior determination should be reopened and revised if new and material
evidence is presented or there are other reasons for reopening. See DI 27501.000 and DI
33020.025 for additional information on administrative finality and reopenings.
