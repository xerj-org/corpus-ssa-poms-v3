DI 28005.007 Substitution of Judgment (SOJ) During a Medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
During a CDR, the current adjudicator may arrive at a different determination than
the adjudicator (including potentially an administrative law judge or the Appeals
Council) who made the determination at the comparison point decision (CPD). This may
occur even when the previous determination is fully documented and rationalized.
