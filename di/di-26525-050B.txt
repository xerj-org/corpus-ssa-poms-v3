SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.050 - The Vocational Diary
Effective Dates: 05/12/2009 - Present | TN 13 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Complete the diary block of Forms SSA-831-C3/U3 (item 17) and SSA-832-C3/U3 SSA-833-C3/U3
(item 23) as follows:
1. Diary Type
Enter “MRN”.
2. Diary Date
Enter two-digit month, day, and year for the month after the month the vocational
therapy is expected to end. Use “01” for the day.
NOTE: The diary date can contain four to six characters depending on the Legacy system
used by a particular State DDS e.g. 08/05; 08/01/05; 08/2005.
3. Diary Reason
Enter “U”.
