K. Appealing an FTC cessation determination
The individual has 60 days to appeal a cessation determination. The usual CDR appeal
procedures apply. For Standards and Development Guidelines for the Reconsideration
CDR Process, see DI 29005.001. If applicable, the DDS hearing officer will revise the cessation date. To determine
the cessation month, see DI 28005.205C.1.
When the appeal period has expired, the DDS may only consider reopening if the rules
of administrative finality allow (See Conditions for Reopening a Final Determination
or Decision, DI 27505.001).
