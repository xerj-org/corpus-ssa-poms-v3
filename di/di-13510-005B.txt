B. Role of ODO, PSC-DRS
Certification of SGA for the VR continuous period may be made in conjunction with
a CDR, if a CDR is required in accordance with current CDR instructions. If a CDR
is not necessary (a CDR was already completed), verify the VR continuous period of
SGA from the information in file, if possible.
ODO or PSC-DRS will do the following:
1.
Obtain the folder, if available.
2.
Review the folder, and if the information is sufficient, verify and certify the VR
continous period of SGA by completing the SSA-289-U3 in accordance with DI 13510.010. If a CDR is necessary, ODO or PSC-DRS will route the folder along with the VR certification
form via a SSA-5526-U3 to the servicing DO. Item 6.g. of the SSA-5526-U3 will request
a contact with the individual or employer to certify the VR continuous period of SGA.
3.
If the information in the folder is not sufficient to certify the VR continuous period
of SGA and it is determined that a CDR is not necessary, a form SSA-5526-U3 without
the folder, will be sent to the DO for clarification. Item 6.g., of the SSA-5526-U3
will indicate the actual months of SGA (e.g., 9 /81-10/81) as recorded in the claims
folder. The name of the employer will be indicated, if known. The DO will contact
the individual or the employer to certify the VR continuous period of SGA.
