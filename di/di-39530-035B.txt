SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39530.035 - Loss or Destruction of DDS Equipment
Effective Dates: 09/04/2026 - Present | TN 3 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439530035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedures
1. Notification
The DDS must advise Disability Adjudication (DA) regarding any loss or destruction
of equipment. The notification should include a description of the equipment and impact
to DDS operations.
2. Supporting documentation
The DDS must send DA supporting documentation which:
•
Describes the loss
•
Describes the actions taken by the State to resolve
•
Outlines final resolution
•
Includes signature of the State official empowered to make such a disposition.
NOTE: This statement will serve as supporting documentation until an audit has been conducted
and resolved.
