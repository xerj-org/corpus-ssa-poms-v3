A. When the error on the face of the record exception applies
Disability Determination Services (DDS) should find a prior determination or decision
in error if the evidence shows an error on the face of the record that led to an incorrect
decision. This category includes misreading evidence or misapplying an adjudicative
standard (such as the Listing of Impairments or the vocational rules).
