SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.015 - Informal Remand of Medical/Vocational Determinations
Effective Dates: 04/11/2018 - Present | TN 3 (07-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Claim folder(s) not in FO
1. Action upon receipt
Determine if an informal remand is indicated. If the criteria are met, notify the
HO by sending a photocopy of the HA 501-U5, annotated as in DI 12010.015C.1.a.
2. Claim folder(s) in the DDS
If the claim folder(s) is in the DDS, do not request the claim folder(s). Take the
following actions:
a.
Annotate the HA 501-U5 that the DDS has been requested to forward the file to the
HO if a revised favorable determination is not made.
b.
Do not annotate the claimant's copy of the HA 501-U5.
c.
Send the claim folder(s) copy of the HA 501-U5 with any materials submitted by the
claimant, to the DDS.
d.
Annotate the route slip “Informal Remand-Claim Folder(s) in Your Office.”
e.
Advise the DDS to notify the FO when the file is forwarded to the HO.
f.
Distribute all other copies of the HA 501-U5 as usual.
g.
Follow-up on the status of the claim folder(s), if necessary, 15 business days from
the date the claim folder(s) is sent.
