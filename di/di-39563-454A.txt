SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39563.454 - Medical/Vocational Development Controls
Effective Dates: 06/07/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439563454 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Good management control of the agency's medical-vocational development criteria means
that procedures should be clearly written, current, easily available and uniformly
applied. Periodically, management should check to see if medical or vocational development
procedures are well written, effective and are being followed. Where a procedure is
not working or not followed, management should review the situation and take appropriate
action.
