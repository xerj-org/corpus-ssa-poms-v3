E. Initial BOND responsibilities for FO, PSCs, and N8NN
Field office (FO), payment centers (PSCs) and 800 Number Network (N8NN) sites did
not have any role in selecting project participants or in implementing BOND. Abt recruited
and enrolled participants randomly. Enrollment occurred between January 2011 and September
2012. If a caller contacts the FO or the N8NN sites with questions pertaining to the
legitimacy of BOND, follow the instructions in DI 60099.030.
