D. Procedure for WU at the DHU
1. WU occurs at the DHU level
When a WU issue arises at the DHU level, the disability hearing officer (DHO) is required
to follow instructions in DI 33015.033.
2. WU occurs at the PH level and exists at time of receipt in the DHU
When a WU issue arises at the PH level and is not resolved before the case is sent
to the DHU, the DHO is not required to duplicate attempts of the following actions:
a.
Development not requiring the beneficiary's (or recipient's) cooperation if the case
file documents that development was completed at the PH level.
b.
Requesting FO assistance to locate the beneficiary (or recipient) if the case file
documents the request was completed at the PH level.
NOTE:
The DHO must follow all other instructions in DI 33015.033, even if completed at the PH level.
