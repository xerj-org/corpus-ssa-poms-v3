B. Policy when a claimant cannot be contacted or Whereabouts Unknown (WU)
1. Unable to contact the claimant
When you cannot contact the claimant despite all reasonable efforts, including a personal
contact at the claimant’s residence, record the efforts made, evaluating the claim
based on evidence in file.
2. Claimant's WU
If you cannot locate the claimant at the last-known address, make reasonable efforts
to locate him or her, including contacts with the post office, neighbors, physician,
and others.
Record your efforts to locate the claimant and evaluate the claim based on evidence
in file.
