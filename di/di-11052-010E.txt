SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11052.010 - Processing End-Stage Renal Disease Medicare Claims with Railroad Retirement Board Involvement
Effective Dates: 08/13/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411052010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Process award when the LAF code on the MBR is “E” (RRB has jurisdiction for payment
of Title II benefits)
•
Photocopy the memorandum in Notice to RRB HI 00801.325: ESRD Medicare Award-LAF Code on MBR is “E”-Exhibit. Complete the requested items.
Send original memorandum to RRB and place a copy in claims folder as documentation.
•
Annotate the appropriate notice on RPOC per DI 45001.348, Notice – R-HI/R-SMI Award when RRB Jurisdiction Involved – LAF Code “E” Cases.
•
Route the SSA-3601 to the Office of Disability Operations (ODO).
