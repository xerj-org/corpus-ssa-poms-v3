E. Disposition of the SSA-2454
If the SSA-2454 cannot be stored or recorded in the Modernized Claims System (MCS),
Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS), or the ICF, fax the form into the Non-Disability
Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRED) or the electronic disability (eDIB) folder,
as appropriate.
NOTE: If processing center (PC) action is required, the FO will also fax the form to the PC’s paperless fax number.
