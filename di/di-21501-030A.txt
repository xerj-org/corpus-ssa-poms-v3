A. Blindness
3-510.1 Qualifications for Eligibility. The following visual limitations are established
by law:
1.
Has no vision or acuity.
2.
Has central visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the better eye with the best correction
by single magnification.
3.
If there is a field defect in which the peripheral field has been contracted to such
an extent that the widest diameter of visual field subtends an angular distance no
greater than 20 degrees.
