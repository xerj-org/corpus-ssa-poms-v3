SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13005.055 - Processing Possible Medical Recovery Issues Raised in the Field Office (FO)
Effective Dates: 04/28/2026 - Present | TN 38 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413005055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The disability determination services (DDS) is responsible for the issue of medical
recovery. The FO should be alert to situations that indicate the occurrence of medical
recovery. This section provides instructions for addressing the issues of possible
medical recovery when the issues rise at the FO level. Situations in which medical
recovery issues arise are when:
•
There is a report of medical recovery regardless of the diary type, and
•
Work activity by individuals with a medical improvement diary (MIE) diary scheduled
in Title II cases.
NOTE:
For medical continuing disability review (CDR) processing instructions for beneficiaries
in the Ticket to Work program, see DI 55025.010.
