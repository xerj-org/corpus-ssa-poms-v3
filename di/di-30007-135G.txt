G. Expedited RPC procedure
The expedited RPC procedure:
1.
DDS can request a case be expedited or discussed in panel. However, if DP/RPC reviews
the case evidence and determines the expedited process may apply, the reviewer discusses
the case with the RPC technical expert.
2.
The RPC technical expert reviews the case and the recommendation and determines whether
the expedited process is appropriate.
3.
If RPC determines a case can be expedited instead of scheduling for a panel discussion,
the RPC technical expert will contact the DDS to discuss this option and get approval.
EXAMPLE: DDS requests panel discussion and review of case reveals that DDS followed
all policy and their determination is correct. The deficiency can be rescinded with
no policy issue to discuss.
4.
Upon receiving approval to expedite, the reviewer prepares an RPC resolution and puts
the resolution into the RPC tool. The resolution will include a notice under both
the DP summary and the DP narrative stating Expedited RPC No RPC Panel Discussion
Held.
5.
The reviewer will code the case as an expedited RPC in the Categories of Issue on
the RPC tools DP Data Screen.
6.
The reviewer will submit the case to the RPC technical expert for closure in the RPC
tool.
