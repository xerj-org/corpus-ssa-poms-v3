A. Introduction
There is no special coding, tracking, or notice required by the disability determination
services (DDS) for processing Clark cases. The DDS follows existing case development
and adjudicative procedures and disability policy.
NOTE: The DDS may have to adjudicate periods different from typical title XVI claims in
which the DDS generally evaluates the period from the application date forward. Before
transmitting the case to the DDS, the field office (FO) should identify the case characteristics
on the Clark Court Case flag.
