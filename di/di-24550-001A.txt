SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24550.001 - Weight-for-Length Tables (Birth to the Attainment of Age 2)
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 2 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424550001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Listing 105.08 uses Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) growth charts
to determine the severity of a child's malnutrition. The criterion in listing 105.08B1
is met when the child's weight-for-length measurements are less than the third percentile
on the most recent CDC growth chart.
The tables in subsections DI 24550.001C and DI 24550.001D provide the third percentile data for weight-for-length for males and females from
birth to the attainment of age 2.
