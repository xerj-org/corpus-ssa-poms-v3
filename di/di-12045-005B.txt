B. Definition – Common Issue
A common issue exists when two or more claims, in any combination, share any overlapping
period of time, all have the same deciding issue, whether medical or substantial gainful
activity (SGA).
NOTE: It is not necessary that onset dates coincide, only that a common period of disability
be involved.
