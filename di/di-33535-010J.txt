J. 2 D echocardiography (generally available since October 1984)
This test, like the M mode echocardiography (item E above), records ultrasound waves
reflected by heart structures. It allows visualization over time of the cardiac structures
and the great vessels (aorta and pulmonary artery). It displays the anatomic relationships
of these structures as well as their movement during the cardiac cycle. It is better
because it is noninvasive and, as such, may provide evidence which would otherwise
be unavailable for detection of cardiac dysfunction.
