SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25205.030 - Teacher Questionnaire and Request for Administrative Information
Effective Dates: 08/01/2023 - Present | TN 11 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425205030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure – Using Information from the TQ and RAI
1. Use Details about Functioning To Help Create a Profile
Examine the teacher’s responses on the TQ about the individual’s functioning in school-related activities, along with information
from the RAI, to help create a profile of the person’s limitations. Are the limitations impairment-related?
That is, is it reasonable to conclude that the limitations result from the individual’s
medically determinable impairment(s)
2. Consider Any Factors Related to Person’s Functioning
Incorporate into the individual’s profile all information from the TQ and RAI about
factors that are relevant to the person’s functioning. See DI 25210.005ff. For example,
a child may have a placement in a self-contained classroom with a low teacher/student
ratio and adult aides that make possible a lot of one-to-one instruction and unusually
close supervision. What does the child’s need for such structure and support say about
the severity of the child's impairment?
3. Determine Importance of Information from Other Sources
Consider information provided by the TQ and RAI in light of all the other relevant
evidence, and determine how information from other sources, such as a family member
or therapist, relates to the information obtained in the TQ/RAI: Does that information
corroborate the TQ/RAI responses? Does it supplement the TQ/RAI information? Does
it contradict the TQ/RAI?
4. Resolve Any Material Discrepancies in the Evidence
If a teacher’s responses on the TQ are not consistent with the evidence from other
sources, and the discrepancy in information is material to a determination, contact
the teacher (and, if necessary, the other sources) to clarify and resolve the discrepancy.
(See DI 24515.001B. concerning procedures for resolving inconsistencies.)
5. Do Not Equate TQ Ratings with Other Rating Scales
Do not equate a teacher’s ratings on the TQ with the ratings on the SSA-538, or with
rating scales in the adult rules (e.g., the Residual Functional Capacity guidelines);
there is no formula for such an equation. See DI 25205.030B.7. for more discussion of the use of the TQ in assessing the severity of an individual’s
limitations in functioning.
6. Integrate TQ Responses with Other Evidence To Make Determination
Use the TQ responses to inform a determination at any step of the title XVI childhood
or title XVI adult or title II sequential evaluation processes. Keeping in mind that
any particular activity listed in the TQ generally requires the integration of several
abilities (i.e., domains, areas of functioning), and that a teacher’s responses on
the TQ are only one piece of evidence, determine the individual’s level of limitation
(in each domain or area of functioning) based on the entire profile of functioning
presented across all the evidence.
