F. Procedure - DIB-RSI claimant ineligible for RSI but possibly eligible for DIB
If the claims are on the same earnings record and the DIB is not a DDS jurisdiction
case, keep the claims together throughout the processing when the official folder
is paper. If the claims are on different earnings records, annotate the cover sheet
accordingly on the RSI claim and forward to the PC.
