SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10515.001 - Evaluation of Work of Blind Employees (Title II)
Effective Dates: 03/25/2003 - Present | TN 8 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410515001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Work Activity Before 1978
For work activity performed before 1978 by blind employees under title II, the principles
of evaluation and the earnings considered sufficient to show an ability to do SGA
are the same for blind employees as for other employees. (See DI 10505.001 - DI 10505.020)
