SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.030 - Field Office (FO) Reversal of Nonmedical Denial When Request for Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Hearing Filed
Effective Dates: 01/08/2025 - Present | TN 12 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for FO reversal of nonmedical determination
The FO reviews the request for hearing and any new evidence to determine whether the
reconsidered nonmedical determination was correct, if the determination was made by
the FO. If warranted, the FO may reverse an adverse determination to a wholly or partially
favorable nonmedical determination.
