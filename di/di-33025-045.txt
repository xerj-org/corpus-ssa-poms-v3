DI 33025.045 Potential Suicidal or Homicidal Behavior
For cases involving threats of suicidal or homicidal behavior, follow the procedures
outlined in POMS DI 23030.000 Suicide/Homicide, substituting disability hearing unit (DHU) for disability determination
services (DDS).
