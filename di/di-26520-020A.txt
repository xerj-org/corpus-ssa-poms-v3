A. Procedure- screening criteria
Guidelines have been developed to screen disability applicants for referral to vocational
rehabilitation (VR) services. The screening criteria in DI 26520.025 provide guides for increasing quality referrals to VR agencies and for insuring that
all disability applicants with rehabilitation potential are given the opportunity
to receive services.
