DI 45005.001 Processing Medicare End-Stage Renal Disease Reconsideration Cases
Requests for reconsideration of entitlement issues will all be handled by reconsideration
examiners in the appropriate ODO Modular Processing Group. The basic interpretations
of ESRD policies and provisions are provided in HI 00801.186 through HI 00801.325 and HI 00820.030. Adjudicative and processing instructions for ODO earnings reviewers are shown in
the chapters DI 45001.001 through DI 45001.210 and DI
45010.001 through DI 45010.050. Except as shown in A. below, the reconsideration examiner is responsible for all
aspects of the determination, although advice and/or nonmedical decisions and forms
preparation may be obtained as required from authorizers, earnings reviewers, etc.
