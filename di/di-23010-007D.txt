SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.007 - When to Apply Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Policy
Effective Dates: 04/24/2024 - Present | TN 6 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. CDR
During the CDR evaluation process, although you may develop evidence for FTFPT during
normal development procedures, you only determine whether an individual failed to
follow prescribed treatment without good cause once you determine that the individual
otherwise remains under a disability or remains blind and either:
•
The individual’s own medical source(s) prescribed a new treatment for the disabling
impairment(s) since the last favorable determination or decision and the individual
did not follow that prescribed treatment; or
•
We find that the individual would continue to be entitled to disability or blindness
benefits based upon an impairment first alleged during the CDR, and there is evidence
that the individual has not followed their own medical source’s prescribed treatment
for that new impairment.
For all cases in which FTFPT may be applicable, follow the development procedures
in DI 23010.013 Development Procedures for a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination.
If you determine an individual does not have good cause for failing to follow prescribed
treatment from their own medical source and that the individual would not be disabled
or blind if they followed the prescribed treatment, you must issue a predetermination
notice before ceasing benefits, as described in DI 23010.040 Adjudication and Notice Procedures for a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination.
NOTE:
In claims that may involve both drug addiction and alcoholism (DAA) and FTFPT, make
the DAA determination first. If DAA is material, do not allow the claim and do not
make an FTFPT determination. If DAA is not material, then make an FTFPT determination.
See DI 90070.000 Drug Addiction and Alcoholism Case Processing Under Public Law 104-121.
