DI 52135.080 Illinois Public Disability Benefits (PDB)
This section provides information for only the Illinois State Employees’ Retirement
System (SERS) and the Illinois Municipal Retirement Fund (IMRF). When encountering
any other Illinois PDB, follow instructions for offset in DI 52125.001.
