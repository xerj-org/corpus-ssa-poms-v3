C. If a case fails to close
Upon adjudication, if an eCDR CEF fails to close on the DDS system, case development
and determination documents that were electronically generated or created will not
upload to the CEF and jurisdiction of the CEF will not be transferred to the appropriate component.
At closure, DDS ensures that:
•
All documents are uploaded to the CEF;
•
The case is closed on the DDS case processing system; and
•
Jurisdiction changes to the appropriate office.
