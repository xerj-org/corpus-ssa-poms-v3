SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31001.005 - Claimant's Consent to Disclosure of his or her Records
Effective Dates: 09/17/2013 - Present | TN 7 (04-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431001005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Further disclosure by an appointed representative or entity is generally not permitted
An appointed representative (including his or her designated associate) or entity
should not disclose the claimant's information further without prior written consent
from the claimant. See GN 03910.025B.2 for information on disclosure of records.
