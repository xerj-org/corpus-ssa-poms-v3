SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25501.350 - Establishing an Established Onset Date (EOD) for Disabled Widow(er) (DWB) Cases (Not Medicare)
Effective Dates: 11/19/2012 - Present | TN 51 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425501350 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Component roles and responsibilities for establishing the EOD for disabled widow(er)
claims
1. Field office (FO)
•
Establish a prescribed period,
•
Establish a controlling date, and
•
Assign the potential onset date as of the controlling date (no earlier).
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
Develop medical evidence focused on establishing the EOD as of the controlling date.
