B. Procedure for processing fully favorable determination
Take the following actions when processing a fully favorable determination:
1.
Effectuate payment.
2.
Hold claim folder(s) for 45 days pending claimant's request to proceed with the hearing.
3.
If the claimant requests a hearing, obtain a signed SSA 795 (Statement of Claimant
or Other Person) that indicates that the claimant wants to proceed with hearing. For
paper cases, send the claim folder(s) and SSA 795 to the appropriate hearing office.
For electronic cases follow instructions in DI 81010.141.
4.
After 45 days, if a hearing is not requested, route the claim folder(s) to the appropriate
component for filing.
