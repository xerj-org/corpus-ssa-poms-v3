SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.210 - Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) Exclusions
Effective Dates: 06/08/2009 - Present | TN 90 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Beneficiaries without Social Security Numbers (SSN)
Some auxiliary and survivor beneficiaries may not have their own Social Security Numbers
(SSNs). These individuals never filed for SSNs and are receiving Child Disability
Benefits (CDB) or Disabled Widows Benefits (DWB) on other wage earners’ SSNs. Process
cases for beneficiaries without their own SSNs using paper CDR procedures.
