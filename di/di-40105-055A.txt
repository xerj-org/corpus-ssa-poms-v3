SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40105.055 - Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB) Insured Status Not Met – Office of Disability Operations (ODO) Only
Effective Dates: 01/18/2011 - Present | TN 1 (01-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440105055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Claimant does not meet insured status requirements
When the claimant does not meet the insured status requirements at or after the alleged
onset date (AOD), in the case of traumatic onset, or more than 1 year before the AOD,
in the case of non-traumatic impairments with gradual onset, a disallowance for lack
of insured status is proper.
Review the information in file to determine whether an earlier onset is possible at
a point when the claimant meets the earnings requirements.
Assume that the field office (FO) gave the claimant the explanation and opportunity
to submit additional evidence unless the file contains an unresolved earnings discrepancy
or coverage issue.
