B. Preparing the SSA-833 in extended period of eligibility (EPE) reinstatement cases
(whether allowed or denied)
The PCs are responsible for preparing the SSA-833 in impairment severity cessations
when the beneficiary performs SGA during the extended period of eligibility (EPE)
and appeal of impairment severity cessation is favorable. Most likely these cases
will be ALJ, AC, or court reversals forwarded to the PC for payment effectuation.
Prepare the SGA SSA-833 when the cases described above are received, in accordance
with DI 13010.170B.
Do not prepare the SGA SSA-833 if the cessation is affirmed.
•
Reinstatement allowed
Prepare the SSA-833 as indicated in DI 13010.170B.3.a.
•
Reinstatement denied
Prepare the SSA-833 as indicated in DI 13010.170B.3.b.
