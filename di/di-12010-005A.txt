A. Policy for developing a case for an ALJ hearing
The field office (FO) does not undertake any disability or work development after
receiving a request for hearing unless one of the following applies:
•
The ALJ requests additional development;
•
The claimant submits additional information or evidence; or
•
When the case has been informally remanded and the DDS requests additional development.
See GN 03103.130 for more information on developing hearing cases.
NOTE: In Title XVI childhood cases, an SSA-3881 (Questionnaire for Children Claiming SSI
Benefits) is mandatory and should be obtained along with the SSA-3441-BK (Disability
Report-Appeal), see DI 11005.016B.11.
