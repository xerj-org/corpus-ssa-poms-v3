DI 11005.003 Field Office (FO) Instructions for Identifying Claims, Applicable Terms, and Scheduling
Appointments for Military Casualty (MC)/Wounded Warrior (WW) Cases
This section provides field office (FO) instructions for identifying disability claims
for military service members who incurred an injury, illness, or wound (regardless
of how or where it occurred) while on active duty on or after October 1, 2001.
MC/WW cases are identified as priority cases under DI 23020.001 and are given priority processing through all levels of case development and adjudication.
We flag the disability folder for these cases as MC/WW to alert the components to
the special case processing and development requirements. We also provide expedited
appointment scheduling.
NOTE: We process survivor claims associated with military service related deaths under
the disaster/emergency instructions in GN 00207.055 Expedited Claims Procedures for
Military Service Casualties-Survivors Applications.
