D. Terminating Part B-ID when entitlement to Medicare exists
If the record has an active Part B-ID enrollment and an action to entitle the person
to Medicare occurs, terminate Part B-ID effective the same month the new Supplemental
Medical Insurance (SMI) coverage begins. See SM 00850.800.
