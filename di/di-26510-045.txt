DI 26510.045 Completing Item 22 (Regulation Basis Code) on the SSA-831
The Field Office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) complete entries
in Item 22. The Regulation Basis Code (RBC) reflects the nature of the allowance or
denial at the time of adjudication. Enter the RBC applicable to a new determination
returned by Central Office or the Office of Quality Review (OQR.)
