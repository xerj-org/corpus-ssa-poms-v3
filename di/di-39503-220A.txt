A. Count (DI 39503.810 C.1.)
A change in the count factor simply depicts the amount of workpower needed because of a change in the number of
case dispositions from one year to the next. This may be reflected as either a plus
(+) factor or a minus (-) factor, depending on whether the succeeding year's workload
is greater or less than the year before. The disposition difference (whether plus
or minus) is divided by the prior year production per year for that agency to convert
the factor into a workpower figure. That workpower figure is then placed on the analysis
work sheet under item 3A.
