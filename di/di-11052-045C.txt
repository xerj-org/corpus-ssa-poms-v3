Effective Dates: 02/26/2018 - Present | TN 4 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411052045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. ESRD appeals
•
Route Reconsideration requests to PC7. PC7 completes another SSA-892 (End-Stage Renal
Disease (ESRD) Medicare Determination) with their determination of award or denial.
Item 19 of the SSA-892 indicates the level of RECON.
•
Route all non-medical Medicare hearing requests to the Department of Health and Human
Services’ Office of Medicare Hearings and Appeals (OMHA). For Medicare Qualified Government
Employee (MQGE) and medical ESRD appeals cases, route the hearing requests to the
Office of Hearings Operation (OHO) for an ALJ decision. Associate the folder with
the request and forward all pertinent material to the appropriate OMHA or OHO hearing
office.
•
For MQGE cases for which SSA retains jurisdiction, route requests for review of a
hearing decision to the Appeals Council.
•
For all cases forwarded to OMHA, see GN 03108.210 for routing instructions.
