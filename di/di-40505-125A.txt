A. Introductions regarding additional evidence or information
Additional information may raise a continuing disability review (CDR) issue when:
1.
We receive evidence in connection with a new or existing claim or case;
2.
Reports of work or increased earnings or responsibilities may indicate medical improvement
(MI) or new vocational skills; or
3.
The Office of Disability Operations (ODO) receives information during a Title XVI
redetermination.
