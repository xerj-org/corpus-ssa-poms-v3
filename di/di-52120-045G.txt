G. Forms
The following forms may contain useful payment information.
1.
Agreement Form – If the injured individual and the employer reach an agreement in regard to compensation,
this memorandum of that agreement signed by both parties is filed with the State Dept
of Labor. It becomes binding when it is approved by the state.
2.
Receipt of Compensation Paid – When the WC agreed upon has been paid in full, this
final receipt form is filed with the State Dept. of Labor. A final receipt signed
by the injured individual will be accepted by the WC Board as sufficient evidence
that the disability has ceased.
3.
Petition for Commutation – Requests for commutation of compensation are favorably considered by the WC Board
where such communication will be in the best interests of the injured individual.
The Board may lay down guidelines and impose conditions as it deems advisable for
the disbursement of all funds commuted.
