B. Procedure – non-medical completion
This process is contingent upon the FO completion of non-medical development per Guidelines
for Deferral of Non-Medical Development -
DI 11010.025 - DI 11010.075 and signaling the system that the claim is ready for final processing when the DDS/DQB
enters the medical decision. This is accomplished by FO completion of the appropriate
screens (DECI, MREQ, MCR1, DAPP) in the Modernized Claims Systems (MCS) and EC paths.
See Earnings Computation Screens - Overview - MSOM MCSEC 001.001.
