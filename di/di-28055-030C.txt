Effective Dates: 11/27/1990 - Present |

C. Procedure - Completion of Title II Only Cessation
1. Item 9 - Determination Findings
a.
Check the “Impairment Severity” block.
b.
Check block B.; fill in the month, first day of the month, and year in which impairment
severity ceased.
c.
Do not complete block C.
d.
Do not complete blocks D, E, F, G, or H. (These blocks are completed by the FO and
ODIO/PSC-DRS.)
2. Item 20 - Why Review Was Made
Enter: 08.
3. Item 24 - Remarks
a.
Enter: “Supplemental Determination-Impairment Not  Disabling-Disability Previously Ceased”.
b.
Enter other remarks as appropriate.
