B. Description of mailer form SSA-455 Disability Update Report
Form SSA-455 (Disability Update Report) is a self-help mailer form designed to solicit
key information from a disabled beneficiary about his or her medical conditions and
recent treatment for it. “Recent” usually means within the last two years. The mailer
form also asks for information about recent education or training, and recent attempts
to return to work.
