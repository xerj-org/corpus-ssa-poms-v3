Effective Dates: 03/15/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424503020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. How we evaluate evidence from nonmedical sources
Do not consider evidence from nonmedical sources when establishing a medically determinable
impairment (MDI). However, once an MDI is established, consider evidence from nonmedical
sources for all other findings in a claim, as appropriate.
