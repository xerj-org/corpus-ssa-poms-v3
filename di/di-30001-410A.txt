A. Field Office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) Responsibilities
The FO and DDS are responsible for accurately completing the appropriate blocks on
the SSA-831.
1. Items 1 - 14
The FO is responsible for the completion of items 1-14 of the SSA-831-U3.
2. Items 15 - 34 and item 36
The DDS completes items 15-34 and item 36 of the SSA-831-U3 to record the disability
determination.
