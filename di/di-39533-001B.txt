B. Policy principles
1. Written guidelines
a.
SSA will provide the Disability Determination Services (DDSs) with written guidelines necessary for
carrying out their responsibilities in performing the disability determination function.
b.
The DDS will comply with the written guidelines in Part 04 of the Program Operations
Manual System (POMS) AO 40305.029.
c.
POMS includes certain policies, procedures, operating instructions, and guides designated
as advisory or discretionary.
2. Opportunity to participate
a.
The DDSs representative group will be given an opportunity to participate in formulating
disability program policies that have an effect on their role in carrying out the
disability determination function.
b.
The DDSs will be given the opportunity to comment before changes are made in written guidelines unless delay in issuing a change may
impair service to the public.
