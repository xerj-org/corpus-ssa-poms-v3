SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.010 - Effect of EPE on Period of Disability
Effective Dates: 11/27/1990 - Present | TN 20 (09-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples
1. Work-Related Endings
a.
Disability ceased 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are made for the month of cessation and
the 2 succeeding months, then placed in suspense status effective 4/89. The disabled
person performs SGA throughout the reinstatement period. The period of disability
ends 3/89,i.e., the second month after the month disability ceased due to SGA.
b.
Disability ceased 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are made for the month of cessation and
the 2 succeeding months, then placed in suspense status effective 4/89. Payments are
reinstated 7/89 due to below SGA earnings, suspended 9/89 due to a return to SGA,
reinstated 12/89 due to below SGA earnings, and suspended again in 3/90 due to SGA
level work. SGA continued. The period of disability ends 2/90, i.e., the last month
of payment after the SGA cessation.
2. Medical-Related Endings
a.
Disability ceased 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are made for the month of cessation and
the 2 succeeding months, then placed in suspense status effective 4/89. Payments are
reinstated 7/89 due to below SGA earnings. The DDS finds that a disabling impairment
no longer exists as of 10/89. The period of disability ends 12/89,i.e., the second
month after the month the impairment is not disabling.
b.
Disability ceased 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are made for the month of cessation and
the 2 succeeding months, then placed in suspense status effective 4/89. Payments are
reinstated 7/89 due to below SGA earnings and suspended 9/89 due to a return to SGA.
SGA continued. The DDS finds that a disabling impairment no longer exists as of 10/89.
The period of disability ends 8/89, i.e., the last month of payment based on SGA.
