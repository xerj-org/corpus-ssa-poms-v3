B. Disability determination at the ALJ level or above
Processing Center Claims Authorizers (CAs) adjudicate and process Administrative Law
Judge (ALJ) level or above claims. A CA will attempt to process the claim through
MCS but if that attempt fails, the CA sends the record to a Benefit Authorizer (BA)
for MACADE processing.
