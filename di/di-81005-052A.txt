A. General – View and copy documents from prior electronic folders
Users may need to review or copy documents from prior filings for current disability
filings or continuing disability reviews (CDRs). Users have the capability to view
documents from prior EFs with the same Social Security Number (SSN) and select those
documents to copy into the current EF without leaving the current EF. The prior EF(s)
does not have to be a certified electronic folder (CEF).
NOTE:
Users cannot copy Multimedia files.
