D. Documenting development of evidence by telephone
When developing or obtaining evidence about functioning from a medical or non-medical
source by telephone, verify the source's identity prior to discussing the claim (see
DI 22511.007B.3. in this section) document the date, the content of your conversation, and with whom
you spoke on an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) or a Case Note in the Disability Case
Processing System (DCPS), and "write to" the case development worksheet (CDW) or the
Disability Determination Explanation (DDE), as applicable.
For information on documenting evidence from a medical source obtained by telephone
contact, see DI 22505.030.
For information on the required elements of the DDE, see DI 26515.001.
