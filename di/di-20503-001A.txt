A. Policy on material in disability folders
All disability folders (e.g., electronic, modular, and two-part brown) must contain
material that documents the development steps and is:
•
Essential to the disability determination; and
•
Required for continuing disability reviews (CDRs).
NOTE: The claimant’s file is a legal document and must reflect an objective assessment.
Avoid subjective comments that may be interpreted as bias.
