DI 28001.060 Cases Involving Individuals Convicted of a Felony (Prisoner Cases)
Public Law (P.L.) 96-473 and P.L. 98-21 place restrictions on certain Title II individuals convicted of a felony. We may
apply policy in DI 23501.001 if:
•
a continuing disability review (CDR) case has been identified as a prisoner case;
•
during a CDR it is discovered for the first time that an individual is confined; or
•
exclusions in the prisoner provisions may be applicable.
For additional information on evaluating a prisoner/felon CDR case, see DI 28065.010.
