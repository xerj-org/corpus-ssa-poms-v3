B. Entitlement prior to 1961
Where the NH became entitled (i.e., had filed and met eligibility requirements) to
a DIB before 1961, the monthly DIB was equal to the PIA computed under the rules that
would apply if the individual had attained retirement age and filed application for
an RIB in the first month of the waiting period. If the NH was entitled to a DIB without
a waiting period, the DIB was equal to the PIA computed as though he/she had attained
retirement age and filed for an RIB in the first month of entitlement to a DIB.
