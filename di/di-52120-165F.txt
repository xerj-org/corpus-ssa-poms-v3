F. Time limitations for filing claims
There is a 2 year statute of limitations that applies to workers' compensation cases. A formal claim petition must be filed within 2 years of the date of injury or the date of
last payment of WC, whichever is later. In cases of occupational illness, such as
asbestosis or hearing loss, the claim petition must be filed within 2 years from the
date the worker first became aware of the condition and its relationship to employment.
