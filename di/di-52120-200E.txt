E. Oklahoma WC Court of Existing Claims and Oklahoma WC Commission
1.
Oklahoma WC Court of Existing Claims
For claims involving injuries and death based on accidents that occurred prior to
2/1/2014, the Oklahoma WC Court of Existing Claims determines claims for compensation,
the liability of employers and insurers, and rights asserted under the prior WC law
found in Title 85 of the Oklahoma Statutes (now repealed).
2.
Oklahoma WC Commission
a.
For claims involving injuries and death based on accidents that occurred on or after
2/1/2014, the Oklahoma WC Commission administers the AWCA.
b.
The Commission’s responsibility is to determine claims for compensation, the liability
of employers and insurers, and any rights asserted under the AWCA. An administrative
law judge will issue the judgment/award on WC claims. Any appeal of an administrative
law judge’s decision goes to the Oklahoma WC Commission.
c.
Information on the Oklahoma WC
Commission is available.
