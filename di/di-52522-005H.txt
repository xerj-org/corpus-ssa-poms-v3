H. Age 18 title XVI adult disability redetermination
If a child recipient’s benefits terminated because of a parole or probation violation
(PPV) warrant, the FO will reinstate payments from the N25 PPV suspension month and
continuing until the DDS determines the recipient is disabled as an adult or until
the DDS medically denies the case. If the DDS medically denies the case, the DDS should
follow the processing instructions for development and evaluation of childhood and
age 18 disability redeterminations in DI 23570.020.
