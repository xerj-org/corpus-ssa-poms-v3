Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452120085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Types of WC payments
1.
Temporary Total (TT) - maximum period of 500 weeks
2.
Temporary Partial (TP) - maximum period of 300 weeks
3.
Permanent Total (PT) - maximum period of 500 weeks
4.
Permanent Partial Impairment (PPI) – (code as PP) based on a one time medical assessment, so payable one time. PPI can
be awarded independent of TT.
There is no maximum payable period. It may be paid in a lump sum upon agreement of
the parties and approval of the WC Board. However, PPI is usually paid weekly. See
DI 52120.085C in this section.
All disability payments are paid weekly, unless the WC Board orders payment in biweekly
or monthly installments. Back payments can be brought up to date by payment in a lump
sum.
NOTE: Tort payments are not WC and do not cause offset. Tort payments are awarded based
on civil lawsuits. For list of payments not considered WC, see DI 52105.015.
