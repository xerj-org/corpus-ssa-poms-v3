Effective Dates: 03/03/2022 - Present | TN 3 (03-22)

D. Additional roles of the TPM.
TPM assists with:
•
program evaluation,
•
financial reporting and accounting,
•
special studies,
•
conference planning (e.g., for ENs and prospective ENs), and
•
training activities.
