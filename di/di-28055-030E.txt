SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.030 - Preparation of Form SSA-833-U3 Impairment Severity Determination
Effective Dates: 11/27/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure - Rationale Requirements
Follow the same requirements applicable to other continuing disability determinations
except for the nonmedical issues and conclusion sections discussed in 1. and 2. below. See
DI 28090.000 for when a rationale is required.
1. Nonmedical Issues
a.
Discuss entitlement to a TWP and state the month/year the TWP was completed. (This
information is either in the rationale for the SGA cessation determination or the
notice of SGA cessation.)
b.
Incorporate the SGA determination, noting the date disability ceased due to SGA.
c.
State that the present CDR is the result of a review of the individual's continued
entitlement to the EPE provisions of the Social Security Act.
2. Conclusion
In the EPE medical determination, address the issue of continued impairment severity,
not the issue of continuation or cessation of disability.
•
Indicate that impairment severity continues/has ceased for purposes of the EPE provisions
of the Social Security Act.
•
Do not indicate a month of benefit termination in impairment severity cessation determinations.
