C. Substance (DI 39503.810 C.3.)
A change in the substance factor is generally a plus in terms of workpower because it usually is a change in
the nature of the workload. Here again, to calculate the magnitude of the workpower
requirement, the agency must estimate the added time (in minutes) per case and the
number of cases requiring such added activity.
