C. Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) eligibility paragraph
If the file does not reflect whether the claimant filed a claim with RRB, add the
following paragraph to the allowance notice when the individual has at least 60 months
of creditable RR service earned after 12/31/95 (or 120 months earned at any time):
We note that your railroad earnings record indicates that you have at least 60 months
of
creditable RR service earned after 12/31/95 (or at least 120 months earned at any
time) which makes
you eligible to apply for disability benefits under the disability provisions of the
Railroad
Retirement Act. If you have not already done so, you may wish to contact the Railroad
Retirement
Board regarding the filing of an application for disability benefits.
