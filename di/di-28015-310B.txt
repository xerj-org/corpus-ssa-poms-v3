SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.310 - Consider Age and Time on the Rolls When Assessing Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) in Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs)
Effective Dates: 02/18/2025 - Present | TN 14 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Medical literature shows:
a.
There is a gradual decrease in organ function with age;
b.
Major losses and deficits become irreversible over time;
c.
Maximum exercise performance diminishes with age;
d.
Other changes related to sustained periods of inactivity and the aging process include:
•
muscle atrophy,
•
degenerative joint changes,
•
decreased range of motion, and
•
changes in the cardiac and respiratory systems, which limit exertional range.
e.
A wide variation exists in the degree and time of life when major losses (e.g., muscle
atrophy) occur. Evaluate each beneficiary individually, giving due consideration to the effects of aging.
