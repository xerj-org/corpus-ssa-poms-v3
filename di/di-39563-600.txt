DI 39563.600 Workload Management Procedures
Budget procedures for workload management are included in the subchapter on DDS Budget
Procedures, DI 39503.000. The DCRDirector in the regional office, the Disability Program Administrators and
the DCR staff budget specialist are available to assist the DDS with workload management
budget considerations.
