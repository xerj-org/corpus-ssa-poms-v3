C. Who can use the i3820
IUs can establish an i3820 partial for Title XVI child disability benefits. The child
and the IU must meet certain requirements. The child must
•
Have a Social Security number;
•
Be under age18 (an i3820 can be established in the month a child attains age 18, if
started before the day of attainment);
•
Not have been denied disability benefits within the previous 60 days; and
•
Reside in one of the 50 states, the District of Columbia, or the Northern Mariana
Islands.
The IU must:
•
Not be a child completing the i3820 for themselves; and
•
Reside in one of the 50 states, the District of Columbia, or the Northern Mariana
Islands.
