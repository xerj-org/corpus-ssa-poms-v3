C. Events that do not change the TFB
The following events are protected increases and do not change the TFB:
1.
Cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) increase.
2.
Primary insurance amount (PIA) increase due to a recomputation effective after the
first-considered date of offset.
3.
Combined family maximum that is first used in a month after the first-considered date
of offset.
4.
Resumption of WC/PDB payments based on the same injury after a break in payment.
For information regarding protected increases and breaks in WC/PDB payment, see DI 52150.055.
