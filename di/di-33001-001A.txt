A. Introduction to the disability hearing process
1. Disability hearings and reopenings
This chapter addresses disability hearings at the reconsideration level of appeal.
These procedures generally refer to reconsiderations of medical cessations, but they
also include adverse reopening determinations.
2. Explanation of terms
When using these procedures, remember:
•
the terms “Disability Hearing Officer (DHO) reconsidered determination” and the “DHO
hearing decision” are synonymous;
•
the term “Disability Hearing Unit (DHU)” means the separate adjudicatory Disability
Determination Services (DDS) unit established for the purpose of processing disability
hearing cases; and
•
the terms “supervisory hearing officer (SHO)” and “DHO” refer to the DDS counterpart
for these functions.
3. Availability of disability hearings
Disability hearings are available at the reconsideration level of appeal in:
•
CDR medical cessation cases;
•
CDR reopenings that change only the basis for the cessation;
•
Adverse reopenings at the initial level (cases involving favorable initial CDR determinations which were reopened and
revised to unfavorable or only partially favorable determinations) based on medical
factors;
•
Adverse reopenings of cases involving favorable reconsideration CDR determinations which were reopened and which we propose to adversely revise based
on medical factors.
Note:
For additional information about the claimant’s right to a disability hearing at the
medical CDR reconsideration level, refer to DI 29001.001. For DHO reopening policy, refer to DI 33020.025.
