D. Updating Prior Paper Folder Indicators (PPFIs)
To update PFIs, use the DDS case processing system and follow local procedures.
Update PFIs when:
•
Adding an EDCS Exclusion to an existing case after DDS case receipt.
NOTE: DDS updates the PFI upon receipt of the paper folder;
•
Reopening a prior filing per Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions - DI 81010.030 and Processing Reopenings - DI 81020.115.
NOTE: DDS updates the PFI prior to closing the electronic case.
•
Receiving a prior paper folder after DDS case receipt. NOTE: The component with jurisdiction of the electronic folder must update the PPFI as
soon as they receive the prior paper folder.
