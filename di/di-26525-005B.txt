SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.005 - Diaries in Multiple Entitlement Situations
Effective Dates: 05/09/2002 - Present | TN 13 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Simultaneous or Concurrent Claims
a. Medical Diary
Set the same medical diary in simultaneous and concurrent claims.
b. Exceptions
Set a MIE diary in title XVI blindness cases to mature up to age 64 1/2 when the individual
is expected to no longer meet the title XVI statutory blindness requirement.
2. Existing Entitlement and New Claim
Follow adoption instructions in DI 27515.000 for existing entitlement and new claims.
When a medical improvement issue is currently raised, immediately initiate a CDR on
the existing entitlement. (See DI 28001.015)
When a medical improvement issue is not raised, set whatever diary is appropriate
on the new claim following the guidelines in DI 26525.00ff.
