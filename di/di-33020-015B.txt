B. Procedure for consideration to reopen
Review any evidence received at the time of the notification of the individual’s death.
If the DHU does not have a copy of the death certificate, have the FO secure a copy
of it, prior to requesting DDS development.
For additional information and consideration of DDS actions when DDS receives notice
of death, follow DI 23510.010.
