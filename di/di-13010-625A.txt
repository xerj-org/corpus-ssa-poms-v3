A. Procedure – When to use the IPCA/IPCB Screen
The IPCA is restricted to OCO/PSC inputs. Use the IPCA screen to update the DCF with
status on an action and the IPCB to indicate the office responsible for the next action.
The only IPCA event codes applicable for T2 WORK and EXR cases are 01, 08, 09, 10,
11, 12 and 14.
