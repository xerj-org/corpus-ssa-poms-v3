D. Process - Appeals
All SGA determinations involving IRWE are subject to reconsideration, hearing and
appeal, according to usual procedures. All SSI determinations involving reduction
or cessation of payment because of IRWE require advance notice and the opportunity
for continuation of payment pending appeal. (See NL 00803.015 for notices.)
