B. What we mean by “within a few
days to a few
months”
We do not have a more precise programmatic definition for the phrase “within a few
days to a few
months.” We define the term “a
few” using its ordinary meaning, a small number. Consider a few days to a few months to
mean a period not to exceed six months.
