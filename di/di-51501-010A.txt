SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 51501.010 - Field Office Actions on Subsequent Disability Applications
Effective Dates: 04/14/2015 - Present | TN 10 (04-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0451501010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Subsequent disability application pending in the field office (FO) and prior disability
claim pending appeal
If the FO accepted a new disability application filed on or after July 28, 2011, and
discovers there is a pending appeal for the same title and benefit type, you must take the following actions:
1.
Verify that the protective filing date or the date of the new application is July
28, 2011 or later.
2.
Send the claimant the Subsequent Application for Disability Benefits notice in the
Document Processing System (DPS) explaining the choice to continue the pending appeal
or withdraw the pending appeal and continue the new application.
REMINDER: Do not continue to process the new application.
3.
Document the Modernized Claims System (MCS) or the Consolidated Claims Experience
(CCE) development worksheet page on the new application:
•
Create an issue: “SUB APP;”
•
Set a “tickle” 10 days (plus 5 for mailing time) from the date the notice is sent;
and
•
Document in Remarks: “SUB APP NOTICE.”
4.
If the claimant does not respond to the Subsequent Disability Application notice within
15 days of the date of the notice, dispose of the new disability application and,
if applicable, close the new Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) segment (see
Disposition of Subsequent Disability Applications, DI 51501.025).
1. Claimant chooses to continue the pending appeal
If the claimant chooses to continue the pending appeal, take the following actions:
•
Document the claimant’s choice on the Report of Contact screen in MCS or the CCE;
and
•
Forward any additional evidence to the office handling the pending appeal and dispose
of the new application (see DI 51501.025).
2. Claimant chooses to withdraw the pending appeal and continue the new application
Follow the instructions in Claimant Requests to Withdraw Pending Appeal, DI 51501.020.
NOTE: If you receive a paper application, send the notice and return the paper application
to the claimant per DI 51501.005A.2.
