E. Notices
Do not send a notice of referral or a copy of the protest memorandum to the hearing
office, the claimant, and/ or the representative. The AC staff will notify the claimant
and the representative directly if it decides to take action in the case based on
the own motion or reopening. In payment cases, follow procedures in GN 03103.250C without mentioning the referral.
