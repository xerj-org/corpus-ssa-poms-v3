Effective Dates: 03/10/2026 - Present | TN 7 (03-26)

B. Computation rules
When manually computing WC/PDB offset, consider the computation rules for:
•
Rounding in DI 52170.025.
•
Estimating Earnings for Years Prior to 1978 in DI 52170.035.
•
Applying the pre-1981 notice provision in DI 52170.040.
•
Computing offset for phase-out students in DI 52170.045.
•
Estimating gift quarters (1951-1977) in DI 52170.050.
•
Using calendars (1964-2028) to prorate lump sum settlements in DI 52170.055.
NOTE: See factors in computing WC/PDB offset in DI 52150.001.
