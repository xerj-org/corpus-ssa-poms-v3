A. Operating Policy
Payment Responsibility
If the State DDS receives a request to make disability determinations on claims not
related to Title II and/or Title XVI benefits, the requesting agency bears the responsibility for the
funding and,
if necessary the
separate, staffing relating to the non-SSA program work.
