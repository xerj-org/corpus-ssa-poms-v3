D. What evidence do we need?
1. We need medical evidence that specifies the type, extent, and site of the primary,
recurrent, or metastatic lesion. In the rare situation in which the primary site cannot
be identified, we will use evidence documenting the site(s) of metastasis to evaluate
the impairment under 13.27 in part A.
2. For operative procedures, including a biopsy or a needle aspiration, we generally
need a copy of both the:
a. Operative note.
b. Pathology report.
3. When we cannot get these documents, we will accept the summary of hospitalization(s)
or other medical reports. This evidence should include details of the findings at
surgery and, whenever appropriate, the pathological findings.
4. In some situations we may also need evidence about recurrence, persistence, or
progression of the malignancy, the response to therapy, and any significant residuals.
(See 113.00G.)
