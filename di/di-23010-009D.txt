SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.009 - Necessary Conditions to Make a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination
Effective Dates: 01/03/2019 - Present | TN 5 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Condition 3: Individual did not follow the prescribed treatment
This condition is met if you have any evidence that the individual did not follow
the prescribed treatment during the time(s) which the individual may be entitled to
disability or blindness benefits.
EXAMPLE: A medical source’s treatment note includes a statement that the patient has not
been compliant with a prescribed medication regimen.
