SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.040 - Payment for Medical Evidence of Record (MER)
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Payment
Pay for evidence per DI 22505.040B. For detailed guidance on DDS payment for MER, see DI 39545.000 Purchased Medical Services.
2. Payment Appeal
a.
Review the facts and either confirm or revise the initial payment decision.
b.
Maintain a record of sources whose payment requests have been denied.
c.
Bring problems that cannot be resolved at the DDS to the attention of the Assistant Regional
Commissioner, Management and Operations Support.
