B. Unfavorable Onset
When a claimant who resides in one of the four States (California, Iowa, Massachusetts,
or Nevada) that pays a blindness supplement files for disability and blindness and
the blindness onset is unfavorable, prepare a personalized notice addressing the visual
impairment and release it with an SSA-L1157-U2 (SI)
