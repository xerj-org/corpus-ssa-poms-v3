B. Implied request for reconsideration
The FO may receive documentation other than an SSA-561 (Request for Reconsideration) from a claimant requesting an appeal of the initial
medical determination. If the FO receives a written request for an appeal, treat the
document as an Implied Request for Reconsideration. The FO faxes the Implied Request
into the CEF.
The DDS must review the Implied Request and use it in place of the SSA-561.
NOTE: The DDS must review the CEF for this document and use it in lieu of the SSA-561 when
completing the medical evaluation.
