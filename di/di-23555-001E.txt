E. CDB determination needed for payment of LSDP
When a case is received because CDB determination is required to determine entitlement
to payment of a LSDP it will be evaluated, and onset established, under the same criteria
used for a regular CDB case. For complete CDB instructions, see DI 10115.000 Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB).
