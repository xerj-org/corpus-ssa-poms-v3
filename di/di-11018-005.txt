DI 11018.005 Failure to Cooperate-Insufficient Evidence Determination in an Initial Disability
Claim
Citations: 20 CFR 404.1516; 416.916
This section provides field office instructions when, during the development of an
initial disability claim, you have not received a response from the claimant to your
requests for evidence or action.
NOTE: When the claimant's whereabouts are unknown or the claimant does not wish to pursue
their claim, refer to Title II claims DI 11010.045
and for Title XVI claims DI 11055.085 .
