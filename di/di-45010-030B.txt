Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0445010030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Possible CDR action
When the termination Medicare (ESRD) beneficiary is also in any DI or SSI disability
(payment, suspense, or freeze) status, refer the case by Form SSA-567 to a disability
(CDR) examiner after the terminating actions have been completed unless there is clear
indication that CDR is in progress or was completed. Indicate on the SSA-567 “Possible medical improvement on related disability claim.”
