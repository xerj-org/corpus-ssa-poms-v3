DI 43525.005 Initial Claim - Work Issue Discovered by Disability Examiner
If a work issue is discovered in the course of the foreign disability examiner's (FDE)
review or development of the claim, the examiner will forward the file to the CDB
SGA specialist or the domestic DO (if applicable) for development, resolution of SGA
issues, and an SGA determination.
