C. Prescribed period ends prior to the alleged onset date (AOD)
If the DWB claimant’s AOD is after the prescribed period and, you cannot establish
a potential onset date (POD) prior to the end of the prescribed period, curtail POD
development and deny the claim.
For information on curtailing POD development, see Curtailing Potential Onset Date
Development DI 11005.065.
For information on determining the POD refer to the chart in DI 25501.220 (POD).
REMINDER: Do not rigidly compare the AOD to the date the prescribed period ends. You must determine
if an earlier POD exists prior to denying the claim.
