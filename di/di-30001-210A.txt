SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 30001.210 - Processing Deficiencies Identified by the Quality Assurance Unit (QAU)
Effective Dates: 10/23/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0430001210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. QAU case returns
QAU should return all deficiencies requiring correction to the responsible components.
Supervisors ensure that the appropriate corrective action is taken on QAU case returns,
and inform the QAU of the final disposition of returned cases.
