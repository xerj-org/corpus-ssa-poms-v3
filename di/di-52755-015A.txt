A. Policy
The prior file or a copy of the Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) or Appeals Council
(AC) decision must be requested any time the Chavez Acquiescence Ruling applies in adjudicating a subsequent claim. In the 9th Circuit
(AK, AZ, CA, GU, HI, ID, MT, NV, MP, OR, WA), the Chavez AR applies when there was
a prior final ALJ or AC denial, or a partially favorable decision on the claimant’s
previous claim under the same title of the Act. The Chavez AR does not cover fully
favorable, withdrawal, abandonment, and other dismissal decisions. There is no limit
as to how much time may have elapsed since the last ALJ/AC decision. For additional
information, see DI 52755.005.
