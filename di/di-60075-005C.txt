C. Eligibility for the POD
Based on the requirements outlined in DI 60075.010, the Office of Research, Demonstration, and Employment Support (ORDES) provided a
list of 300,000 SSDI-only and concurrent beneficiaries to Mathematica Policy Research
(MPR), the contractor tasked with recruitment for and evaluation of the POD. MPR will
use this list to contact beneficiaries about volunteering for the POD.
