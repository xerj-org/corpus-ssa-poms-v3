DI 41005.005 General Completion of the SSA-4268-U4 and Rationale for Statutory Blindness Cases
Discussed in this Subchapter
The SSA-4268-U4 should contain a summary of the evidence supporting the conclusion
that the beneficiary was or was not engaged in SGA. If the TWP applied and there were
TWP service months, they should be enumerated.
If comparability of work activity for DIB, CDB, and DWB individuals age 55 and over
is at issue, the SSA-4268-U4 should contain a summary of the evidence supporting the
conclusion.
