C. Procedure - pending claim in DDS or ODO
When the FO learns of the claimant's death while the disability claim is in the DDS
or DCR or ODO and the FO has the death certificate, for claims entered into EDCS,
barcode and fax the death certificate to Section F (back yellow) of the electronic
folder. See Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes
- DI 81010.090 for barcode and fax instructions. When the official folder is paper, forward a copy
of the death certificate or an SSA-553 to the appropriate office for association and
necessary action.
If the FO does not have the death certificate, do not make any other efforts to obtain
it unless requested by the DDS, DCR or ODO. This avoids duplicate or unnecessary development.
(See Jurisdiction and Routing of Cases after Return from Disability Determination
Services (DDS) or Disability Quality Branch (DQB) - DI 11010.355 for routing instructions.)
