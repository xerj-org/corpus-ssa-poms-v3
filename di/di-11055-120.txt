DI 11055.120 Preparing to Transmit Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusion Cases to the Disability
Determination Services (DDS)
When the field office (FO) establishes all claims for a case in the Electronic Disability
Collect System (EDCS), the official folder is a Certified Electronic Folder (CEF).
If one claim in a case is an exclusion to EDCS, the official folder is paper.
The following section contains FO procedure for preparing to transmit disability claims
that are Modernized Supplementary Security Income Control System (MSSICS) exclusions
or limitations (DI 81010.030).
Use of the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) for claims that are
CEF exclusions
For claims that cannot be processed in EDCS, use the SSA-831 to transmit the claim
to the DDS, complete Sections 1-14 on the SSA-831, see DI 11010.205, Completing the SSA-831. Place in the front section of the Modular Disability Folder
(MDF).
