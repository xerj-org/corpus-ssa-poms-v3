A. Statute
Section 221(f) of the Social Security Act as amended provides “all money paid to a State under this section shall be used solely for the purpose
for which it is paid; and any money so paid which is not used for such purposes shall
be returned to the Treasury of the United States for deposit in the Trust Fund.”
