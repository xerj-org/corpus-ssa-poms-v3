B. Procedure - Completion of Title II Only Continuance
1. Item 9 - Determination Findings
a.
Check the “Impairment Severity” block.
b.
Check block A.
c.
Do not complete blocks D, E, F, G, or H. (These blocks are completed by the FO and
ODIO/PSC-DRS.)
2. Item 20 - Why Review Was Made (WRM)
Enter: 08.
3. Item 24 - Remarks
a.
Enter: “Supplemental Determination-Impairment Disabling-Disability Previously Ceased”.
b.
Enter other remarks as appropriate.
