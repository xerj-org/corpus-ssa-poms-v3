D. Length of freeze for statutorily blind NH
An individual who meets the statutory definition of blindness meets the disability
requirement for purposes of the freeze regardless of ability to engage in substantial
gainful activity (SGA). Such blindness alone does not necessarily constitute disability
for benefit payment purposes. To be eligible for Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB),
blind persons under age 55, like those with other impairments, must be unable to engage
in any SGA. If the statutory blind NH engages in SGA, DIB will terminate, and the
freeze will continue if the blindness continues to meet the statutory definition of
blindness. (See DI 10105.065.)
