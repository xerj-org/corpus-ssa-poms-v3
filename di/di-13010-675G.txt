G. Edits for fraud conditions
Edit conditions are also present for input of fraud information to suspend benefits
during the TWP, see DI 13010.035. In addition to the edit conditions listed above for TWP months, use of the “F” VRFD
indicator must meet the following conditions:
•
All months must be in or after 03/2004 and before the date in the FRAUD DECISION DATE
field
•
An entry must be present in the FRAUD DECISION DATE field for each TWP fraud month.
•
The “F” indicator in the VRFD field must be for a TWP month.
•
The “F” indicator must be input with a WORK DET code of one through nine.
