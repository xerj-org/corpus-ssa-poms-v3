A. Oral (bench) decisions
The administrative law judge (ALJ) oral (bench) decisions are abbreviated fully favorable
decisions that are entered into the record of the hearing proceedings. The oral (bench)
decision provides an alternative procedure for the ALJ to use when issuing the written
decision.
