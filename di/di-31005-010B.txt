SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31005.010 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Procedures for Handling Inquiries from Public Officials Other than United States (U.S) Congress Members
Effective Dates: 04/15/2013 - Present | TN 2 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431005010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Responding to requests for general claim information
Non-congressional inquiries about disability claims processing and related issues
are generally answered by the office to which the inquiry is addressed. DDS personnel
may use the telephone whenever possible to acknowledge receipt of an inquiry, provide
an interim status update, or to provide a final reply to Congressional offices accepting
or requesting this form of communication.
