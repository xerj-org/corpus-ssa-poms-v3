B. Process
1. Process requirements
a.
Licensed providers in appropriate specialties will conduct the examinations.
b.
The same providers who conducts the examination will prepare and sign the CE report.
NOTE: CE provider may include license number and/or expiration date on the report.
2. Facilities
The medical provider will correct any deficiencies found as a result of an onsite
inspection.
NOTE: The source may submit photos of the corrections made at the facility to the DDS
as proof of compliance.
