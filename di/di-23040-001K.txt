K. Procedures to submit a complaint on a State-contracted provided interpreter
To submit a complaint about a State-contracted interpreter not performing completely
or appropriately, follow local procedures. Be sure to include the following information
in all complaints:
•
Date of incident,
•
Time of incident,
•
Interpreter identifying information,
•
Language requested, and
•
Details regarding the nature of the complaint.
