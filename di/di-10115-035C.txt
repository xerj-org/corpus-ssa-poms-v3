C. Policy- WE entitlement terminated
A child's prior entitlement may have been based on the WE's entitlement to a Disability
Benefit (DIB) which has terminated because of cessation of the disability. If the
WE has since died, or again becomes entitled to a DIB or Retirement Benefit (RIB),
the initial entitlement provisions in RS 00203.001 apply instead of the reentitlement provisions in this section.
