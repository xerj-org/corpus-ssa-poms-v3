SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.450 - Independent Consultative Examination (CE) Report Review System
Effective Dates: 04/20/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
As a general rule, DDSs should review:
•
a minimum of the first five CE reports from all new CE providers;
•
a sample of reports from all providers on an ongoing basis (this can be covered by
the Quality Analysis (QA) sample);
•
reports from CE providers that are known through experience to have a high likelihood
of being deficient.
