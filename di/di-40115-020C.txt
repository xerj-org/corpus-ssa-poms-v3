SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40115.020 - Processing Center (PC) Actions for Inappropriate Disability Determination Services (DDS) Determination
Effective Dates: 10/06/2011 - Present | TN 1 (09-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440115020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Determination may not be adopted
•
Prepare a new determination and rationale;
•
Scan or fax the new SSA-831 and rationale into the appropriate sections of the certified
electronic folder (CEF);
•
Place all copies of the DDS SSA-831 in the “Green Section” of the CEF. If a paper
folder exists, destroy any copies of the DDS SSA-831 and write “DO NOT PROCESS. SEE
ODO OR PC DETERMINATION DATED” on the original;
•
Sign and date the notation on the original SSA-831 and scan it into the CEF. For paper
folders, prong-file the SSA-831 on the right side of the folder; and
•
Notify the DDS of the correction per DI 30005.204B.
