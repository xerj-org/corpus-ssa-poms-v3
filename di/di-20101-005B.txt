B. SSA jurisdiction for reopening a prior determination
If the prior determination was prepared by SSA or was a work-issue case only, the
jurisdictional guidelines are the same as for initial claims in the following areas:
•
Foreign claim – see DI 20101.030B.
•
Claimants in U.S. Possessions – see DI 20101.030C.
•
Career Railroad or RR annuitant Case – see DI
20101.040A.
•
Changed Identity Claims – see DI 20101.040B.
•
Emergency Clause – see DI 20101.040C.
•
Substantial Gainful Activity Case – see DI 24001.005 – Initial and Subsequent Work Issue Cases.
