C. Waiving the Spanish notice
A beneficiary (or recipient) who falls within one of the criteria in subsection A
above will not receive a Spanish notice if:
•
The person signs an SSA-381-SP Waiver of Right to Receive Notices in Spanish, waiving
the right to receive translated notices, and
•
The claims folder contains documentation to show that the person does not need translated
notices, e.g., English-speaking and English-educated beneficiary (or recipient).
