DI 43505.015 Claimant Moves from Foreign Country to U.S.A. Before his/her Claim is Adjudicated
When the claimant moves from a foreign country to the U.S.A. before the claim is adjudicated,
forward the claim to the DDS of the State in which the claimant is currently residing.
If jurisdiction of a title II claim is transferred to the U.S.A. DDS, notify the U.S.A.
DO.
