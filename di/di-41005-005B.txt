B. Cessations
The rationale for cessations should show that the due process requirements are met
(see DI 13010.185 ff.) and that the beneficiary was or was not entitled to a TWP; that the work activity
was determined to be SGA; that the month of cessation was determined to be (state
month and year).
In “Failure To Cooperate” cases, the efforts to gain the beneficiary's cooperation should be cited, giving
pertinent dates.
