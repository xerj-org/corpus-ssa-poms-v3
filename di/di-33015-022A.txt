SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33015.022 - Disability Hearing Officer (DHO) Who Held Hearing Becomes Unavailable to Write Determination
Effective Dates: 09/18/1992 - Present | TN 12 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433015022 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy regarding situations when the DHO becomes unavailable
When the DHO who held the hearing becomes unavailable (e.g., due to illness, injury,
or resignation) to write the determination, another disability hearing must be offered
before an unfavorable determination is issued.
