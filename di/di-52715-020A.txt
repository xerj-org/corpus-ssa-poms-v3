A. Policy
The prior file must be obtained anytime that the Albright Acquiescence Ruling (AR) applies in adjudicating a subsequent claim. If the prior
file is not available, a copy of the administrative law judge (ALJ) or Appeals Council
(AC) decision should be secured. See DI 52715.020B.1. Normal case development procedures apply.
