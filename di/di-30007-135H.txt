H. Disability Compliance (DC) actions
DC is not required to take any action on an initial RPC request submitted within the
appropriate time frame (see DI 30007.125B.). However, DC has access to the RPC database for use in their ongoing oversight,
liaison, training, and leadership responsibilities.
