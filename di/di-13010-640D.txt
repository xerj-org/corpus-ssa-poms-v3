D. Procedure – Reading and Completing the MMDS
The MMDS contains the disability related decisional and diary data. The information
displayed will depend on the method of accessing the MMDS and will be propagated based
upon determination data on file. If accessing via SELECT 15 from the MCDR screen,
all data will be displayed. Incomplete or incorrect data can be entered by correcting
the incorrect data or filling-in missing data. Corrective data should be taken from
the SSA-832/SSA-833 in file if it has not propagated correctly. When accessing the
MMDS screen through the IPCA screen the first column will be left blank.
