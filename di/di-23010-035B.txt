SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.035 - Required Written Analysis for a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination
Effective Dates: 04/23/2024 - Present | TN 4 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Location of the analysis
Include the FTFPT determination:
•
In eCAT, in the Findings of Facts and Analysis of Evidence (FOFAE) section of eCAT
in the Disability Determination Explanation (DDE);
•
In the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS), in the appropriate section; or
•
In a paper claim, in the written rationale.
In claims adjudicated using DCPS, include the FTFPT determination where indicated
in the claims path.
