B. Second followup
1. FSP
When there is no response to the SSA-423-U6 or SSA-423A, send a teletype to the FSP.
Ask the FSP for status of the prior request and expected date of completion of requested
development. Diary for 30 days. Send folder to the RIF holding file.
2. DO, DDS
If there has been no response to the initial request and first followup, telephone
the DO or DDS for status of the development and expected date of completion.
3. VARO
Send a teletype to VARO requesting status of development and expected date of completion.
Send the folder to the RIF holding file for 30 days.
