D. Auxiliary claims
When an ALJ issues a favorable hearing decision, and auxiliary claims exist on the
same earnings record, we process the primary and auxiliary claims together if possible.
If development is incomplete for one or more auxiliary claims, process the primary
claim to payment and initiate development of auxiliary claims. See GN 01010.140 for instructions on delayed claims.
