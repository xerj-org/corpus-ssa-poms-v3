C. Diary cases with impairment related work expenses (IRWE) or a likely change in status
Procedure for diarying IRWEs:
1.
Establish a one-year follow-up diary (or earlier, if necessary) in eWork or on the
DCF CDRW screen when
•
processing a CDR action that involves IRWE, or
•
there is a reason to believe that the level of the beneficiarys work activity is likely
to change within a year.
2.
Take manual action to establish the diary date. Choose a date you determine work activity
may change the SGA determination. In IRWE cases, this date should not extend beyond
12 months.
