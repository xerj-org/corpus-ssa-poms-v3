B. Obtaining additional information from the claimant
Do not tell the claimant that we selected their case for CAL or QDD unless they specifically
ask the question.
If necessary, contact the claimant or third party upon receipt of the case to:
1.
verify allegations and claimant or third party contact information;
2.
identify current medical source(s), obtain medical source contact information, and
obtain dates of any pending medical appointments;
3.
request assistance in obtaining medical evidence from medical sources, including a
request for fax contact information; and
4.
obtain information about Activities of Daily Living (ADLs).
