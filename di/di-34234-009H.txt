Effective Dates: 06/11/2015 - Present | TN 1 (06-15)

H. Neurological manifestations of HIV infection (e.g., HIV encephalopathy, peripheral
neuropathy), as described under the criteria in 111.00ff, or resulting in one or more
of the following:
1. Loss of previously acquired, or marked delay in achieving, developmental milestones
or intellectual ability (including the sudden acquisition of a new learning disability);
2. Impaired brain growth (acquired microcephaly or brain atrophy - see 114.00D5);
or
3. Progressive motor dysfunction affecting gait and station or fine and gross motor
skills.
