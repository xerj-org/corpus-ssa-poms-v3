A. Background
On 7/2/03, SSA published SSR 03-1p, “Titles II and XVI: Development and Evaluation
of Disability Claims Involving Postpolio Sequelae.” This Ruling is published in its
entirety in DI 24580.010 and replaces the previous text, “Evaluation of Late Effects of Poliomyelitis.”
