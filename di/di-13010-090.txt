DI 13010.090 Processing a Trial Work Period (TWP) Determination
This section provides TWP processing instructions for the field offices (FOs) and
processing centers (PCs). For information on the definition and entitlement policies
for the TWP, see DI 13010.035, The Trial Work Period (TWP). For more information on how fraud affects the TWP,
see DI 13010.037.
