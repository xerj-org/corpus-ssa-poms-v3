DI 11020.012 When an Application for Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB) is Not Needed
An application for CDB benefits is not needed if the child
•
is currently entitled to child benefits on the account, see GN
00201.005B); or
•
was previously entitled to child benefits on the account, see Forms Used in CDB Claims
DI
11020.025B.1.b.
For these cases, complete the SSA-3368-BK and other required forms per DI 11020.025B.
For Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) processing, see DI 81010.020.
