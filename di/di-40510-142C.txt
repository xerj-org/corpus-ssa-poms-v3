SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40510.142 - Locating Folders
Effective Dates: 06/28/2024 - Present | TN 30 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510142 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - lost folders
1. Search efforts
If the folder is lost:
a.
Obtain a readout and attempt to obtain the necessary folder(s) based on the readout
before screening in.
b.
If the folder cannot be located based on the readout, before continuing with a full-scale
lost folder search:
•
determine if the individual filed in the proper enrollment period per DI 40510.145B, and
•
verify cessation reason per D. 2. below.
c.
If the individual filed in the proper enrollment period and was ceased for SGA:
•
prepare an interim award on an SSA-831-U3 per DI 40510.175.
•
send an interim award notice per Exhibit 3 DI 40510.185.
d.
Continue with the full-scale lost folder search per DI 40525.020B.
2. Folder located
If the folder is located:
a.
Review the original folder to verify eligibility per DI 40510.145.
b.
If not eligible, terminate premium Medicare effective with the end of the month after
the month in which the individual is notified they are not eligible.
3. Folder not located
If the folder is not located:
a.
Do not send to FO for reconstruction.
b.
Obtain current medical evidence of record per DI 40510.165 B.2.
c.
When the medical evidence is returned, follow DI 40510.155 B.1.a.
d.
If the individual still cannot be screened in:
•
Follow the lost folder reconstruction procedures in DI 40525.075C through DI 40525.075F.
•
Initiate a CDR per DI 40510.165.
e.
Determine the comparison point decision (CPD), if possible and begin reconstruction
of the prior evidence.
f.
Since current medical evidence has already been obtained, instruct the FO to:
•
conduct a personal contact interview with the individual and to obtain information
and documents regarding prior medical decisions; and
•
obtain medical treatment sources for at least the 12 months preceding the CPD.
