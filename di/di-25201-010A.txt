SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25201.010 - If You Attain Age 18 After You File Your Disability Application But Before We Make A Determination or Decision
Effective Dates: 07/31/2023 - Present | TN 5 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425201010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy (section 416.924(f))
For the period during which you are under age 18, we will evaluate whether you are
disabled using the rules in this chapter. For the period starting with the day you
attain age 18, we will evaluate whether you are disabled using the disability rules
we use for adults who file new claims, in DI 22001.001.
