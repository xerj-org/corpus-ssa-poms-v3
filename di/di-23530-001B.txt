SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23530.001 - Prerelease Case Processing
Effective Dates: 06/29/2026 - Present | TN 3 (06-26)

B. FO processing of prerelease cases
For complete FO processing instructions, see Processing Prerelease Cases SI 00520.920.
1. FO actions upon receipt of referral
When the FO receives a referral, or identifies a case that meets the prerelease criteria,
the FO:
•
Arranges completion of application and other necessary forms, including a signed release
from the individual allowing the FO to discuss the case with the institutions staff.
Use form SSA-3288 for this purpose.
•
Develops for capability and representative payee when required.
•
Obtains medical evidence if not submitted with referral form.
•
Transfers case to the DDS for a medical disability determination.
•
Initiates nondisability development.
•
Does not use deferred development procedures.
•
Adds institution contact information to the 3368 contact information page in the electronic
disability collect system (EDCS).
•
Provides the anticipated release date in Item 5 of the SSA-3367.
NOTE: Concurrent title II claims may need to be evaluated under the prisoner provisions.
For instructions on identifying Prisoner Cases, see Disability Determination Services
(DDS) Identifying Title II Prisoner Cases DI 23501.005.
2. FO actions upon notification of a favorable medical disability determination
When the FO receives notice of a favorable medical disability determination take the
following actions:
•
Notify the institution of a favorable disability determination.
•
When an individual is not released from the institution within a specified time period
(preferably 30 days) but release within the life of the application is likely, the FO holds the claim (placing the SSI record in payment status H10 living arrangement
change in progress) until release.
•
If release is not likely within the life of the application, FO takes final action to deny the case on a technical
basis.
NOTE: For complete processing instructions, see SI 00520.920 Processing Prerelease Cases. For information regarding the life of an application,
see GN 00201.005D.
3. New application or appeal filed after technical denial of favorable DDS disability
determination
There are some situations in which the DDS makes a determination establishing disability
or blindness, but the FO must deny the case for non-medical reasons (i.e., not released
from an institution within 30 days of notification of decision (prerelease), or other
technical denial). If the claimant files an application later than 12 month after
the date of the favorable medical determination and meets all non-medical factors,
the DDS must make a new determination.
NOTE: For instructions on when no new initial medical determination is required for a claimant
who files another SSI claim within 12 months of the date of the prior favorable medical
determination and meets all SSI eligibility requirements, see DI 11055.077.
