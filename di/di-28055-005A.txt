SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.005 - How the EPE Works
Effective Dates: 04/11/2011 - Present | TN 20 (09-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. When It Begins
The EPE begins the month immediately following the 9th TWP month (but no earlier than
December 1980). If the TWP was completed before November 1980 and cessation was not
appropriate, the EPE begins December 1980.
NOTE: A person need not be working in the first month after the TWP for the EPE to begin.
2. When Benefits Can Be Reinstated
Benefits can be reinstated through the earlier of:
a.
the month before the first month in which the impairment is not disabling, or
b.
the last day of the 36th (or 15th) month following the TWP.
3. When It Ends
The EPE ends the earlier of:
a.
the second month after the month in which the impairment is no longer disabling, or
b.
the later of:
•
the month before the first month of SGA earnings after the 36th month (or 15th month)
following the TWP, or
•
the second month after the month disability ceased due to SGA.
NOTE: If an individual with a disabling impairment never engages in SGA and all other entitlement
factors are met, payment of benefits will continue indefinitely. Nondisability events
such as death and HA/DWB attainment of full retirement age will end the EPE.
4. The BTM
The BTM is first month no cash benefits are payable after the EPE. It is the earlier of:
a.
the third month after the month in which the impairment is no longer disabling, or
b.
the later of:
•
the first month of SGA earnings after the 36-month (or 15-month) following the TWP,
or
•
the third month after the month disability ceased due to SGA.
NOTE: Absent medical improvement, a person whose benefits ceased due to SGA can be reinstated
in the 37th (or 16th) month if earnings drop below SGA in that month since benefits
could only terminate with the first month of SGA that occurs after the 36th (or 15th) month.
