SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 43515.001 - Translation of Foreign Documents
Effective Dates: 09/10/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0443515001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Preparation
a.
Remove document for translation from case file.
b.
Write wage earner's Social Security number at top of each page.
c.
Complete SSA-533-FC (Translation Request) for each document to be translated. Instructions
for completion are found in DI 43520.010. (See GN 01704.600 for DO/PC procedures.)
d.
Staple document(s) to be translated to SSA-533-FC.
e.
Diary according to internal Division of Reconsideration Disability Determination (DRDD)
procedure.
2. Dispatch
a.
Release folder to Retention in File (RIF) bin.
b.
Place SSA-533-FC and material to be translated in Central Translation Section (CTS) bin
located in each section.
3. Followup
Followup according to internal DRDD procedure.
