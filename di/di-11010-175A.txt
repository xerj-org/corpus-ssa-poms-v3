SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.175 - Work Comparability Provisions for Title II Disabled Individuals Age 55 and Older
Effective Dates: 04/20/2026 - Present | TN 89 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010175 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy disabled individuals age 55 and older
A blind worker age 55 or older, may be found disabled if he or she is unable to engage
in Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) requiring skills or abilities comparable to
those of any gainful activity in which he or she was previously engaged with some
regularity, and over a substantial period of time. The comparability provisions for
blind individuals age 55 and older apply to blind workers (the wage earner on whose
account payments are being made), blind disabled adult children, and blind disabled
widow(er)s.
