B. Definitions of services
1.
Medical Provider means any provider of medical services, including:
•
individual practitioners, such as physicians and psychologists,
•
health care facilities such as group practices, labs, clinics and hospitals, and
•
volume providers.
2.
Volume Provider means a provider of medical services that specializes in performing
CEs for state agencies and other insurers. Usually, but not necessarily, it includes
a provider with estimated annual billings of $150,000 or more.
3.
Medical Provider Contract means a formal, written contract between the State and a
provider of medical services to perform consultative examinations using physicians
and other health care practitioners.
