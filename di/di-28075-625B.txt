B. Cessations
1. Title XVI determinations
Prepare and release, subject to Federal review selection, a Title XVI cessation notice
on SSA-L8052-U2/C2 (Supplemental Security Income Notice of Disapproved Claim) following
the instructions in DI 28075.630. If the adjudicator uses versions dated after March 1982, add the third line heading
“Notice of Disability Cessation.” Follow normal cessation notice instructions when
preparing the personalized rationale in DI 28095.040.
NOTE: In the personalized rationale, when providing the individual’s allegations, eliminate
the reference to whether or not they are engaging in SGA.
2. Title II (Concurrent Title II and Title XVI cases)
Prepare and release, subject to Federal review selection, the Title II cessation notice
following the normal procedures in DI 28095.105.
