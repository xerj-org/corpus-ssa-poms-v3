SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11055.242 - Using the Certification of Low Birth Weight for SSI Eligibility (SSA-3830) Form in Making Presumptive Disability Determinations
Effective Dates: 05/08/2026 - Present | TN 96 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055242 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. FOs and DDSs may not substitute their own forms for the certification form
This national form (SSA-3830) replaces all locally-produced forms used to obtain information
from hospitals in LBW cases. FOs or DDSs may not delete, modify, reword, reorganize,
add to, or change in any way the questions presented in this form.
