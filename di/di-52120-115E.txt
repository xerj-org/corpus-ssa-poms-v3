E. Time limitations for filing claims
•
Injury: If the injured individual fails to file a claim within 2 years after the date of
injury, the claim is completely barred.
•
Occupational Disease: If the individual is disabled as a result of an occupational disease, they must file
a claim within 2 years (in the case of pulmonary dust disease within 3 years), counting
from either the date of disablement or when they first had knowledge that the disability
was caused by the employment.
