Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Trial Pending
When a potential prisoner case is identified, but the trial is pending, a determination
or decision will be made in the customary manner considering all impairments. The
DO will diary the case, and if the claimant is convicted at the trial, the claim will
be returned to the DDS, ALJ, or AC, as appropriate, to determine if a felony related
impairment is involved and to consider whether reopening and reversal is necessary.
