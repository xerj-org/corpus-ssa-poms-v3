A. General guidelines for CE report content for pediatric special senses and speech disorders
The CE report content guidelines in this section are in addition to the general pediatric
CE report content guidelines in DI 22510.035. For pediatric CE report content guidelines for speech disorders and language disorders,
see DI 22510.055 and DI 22510.060. For additional CE policy in a child’s case, also see DI 25205.015.
