E. Time limitations for filing claims
There is a 2 year time limit for filing a WC claim. If the worker does not file a
petition within 2 years of the date of injury, they may lose the right to claim benefits
in the future. However, if the employer made payments ‘without prejudice’ (without
admitting liability), then the worker has 6 years from the date of the most recent
payment to file.
