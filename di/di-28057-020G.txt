G. Procedure for paper case clearance
Most EXR cases are electronic, however, for any paper cases, follow these procedures
to clear the case:
1.
If the determination is to deny the EXR request, add a remarks to route the slip,
"Terminate provisional payments"; If the determination is favorable, fax the EXR determination
(SSA-832/833) to the FO, so the FO can initiate necessary non-medical development,
see DI 11010.340.
2.
Forward the folder to the FO under cover of a route slip and note any appropriate
action.
