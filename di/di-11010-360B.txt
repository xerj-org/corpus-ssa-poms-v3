B. Procedure - benefit payments not affected
If a case is in the DDS and the subsequent material (e.g., a medical report) does
not have an immediate effect on current benefit payments, the FO sends the material
directly to the DDS. Do not notify the PC making the benefit payment. If the case
is in the PC, send the material to that office or fax into the CEF.
