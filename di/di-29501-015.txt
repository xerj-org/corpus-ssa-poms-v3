DI 29501.015 Administrative Law Judge Requests Completion of Medical Opinion Forms
After reviewing the medical evidence, an administrative law judge (ALJ) may request
that a medical source provide a medical opinion on form:
•
HA-1151 (Medical Source Statement of Ability to Do Work Related Activities (Physical))
or
•
HA-1152 (Medical Source Statement of Ability to Do Work-Related Activities (Mental))
ALJ requests for medical opinions are made through the disability determination services
(DDS) and may be in conjunction with any other request to a medical source or for
a consultative examination (CE). If an ALJ requests the medical opinion separately,
follow the development procedures for ALJ-requested medical evidence from a medical
source.
