C. Title II benefits terminated due to FTC, family maximum payable
NOTE: Do not take this action while in FTC suspension, take this action only after the
beneficiary's FTC termination processes and the appeal period expires.
If title II benefits terminate due to the disabled auxiliary's (BIC= C or W) failure
to submit the requested information and other auxiliaries are entitled on the record,
redistribute the disabled auxiliary's benefits to the other auxiliaries based on the
family maximum provisions. The redistribution of the terminated disabled auxiliary's
benefits will be from the suspense start date to the time of termination. The PSC
is also responsible for releasing the appropriate notices.
