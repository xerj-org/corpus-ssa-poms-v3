DI 43530.030 Events Which May Affect Continuing Eligibility, General--FAM 241.8-1
A.
SSA furnishes all beneficiaries outside the United States with Booklet SSA-10137,
“Your social security check...while you're outside the United States.”The booklet SSA-10137, which is printed in English, as well as bilingually (English
and...) in Chinese, Danish, French, German, Greek, Italian, Japanese, Norwegian, Polish,
Portuguese, Serbo-Croatian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish and Tagalog, describes the occurrences
which affect the eligibility of a person to receive benefits.
B.
All posts should maintain a supply of the SSA-10137 booklet and freely distribute
it to inquirers.
