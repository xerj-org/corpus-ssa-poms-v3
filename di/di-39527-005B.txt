Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. General
Subject to appropriate Federal funding, the State will provide adequate space  to facilitate making accurate and prompt disability determinations.
2. State laws and regulations
The DDS must adhere to State laws and regulations  governing leasing of space by State agencies.
3. Proper charges
SSA will fund costs for:
a.
Office space such as:
•
rent, service, and maintenance costs in privately owned
buildings, or
•
charges based on the approval of the Division of Cost Allocation (DCA) in the Regional
Administrative Support Center in publicly owned buildings.
b.
Repairs and alterations to either privately or publicly owned buildings.
See Repairs and Alterations, DI 39527.025.
Payment will be made only for periods of occupancy unless advance approval is received
from the regional office (RO).
