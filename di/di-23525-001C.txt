C. State plans
State plans define:
•
disability (in terms of permanence and totality), and
•
blindness (in terms of ophthalmic measurements).
In addition, State plans contain criteria that States may use
•
to interpret what is meant by “permanently and totally disabled” and
•
to assess the degree of visual loss that constitutes “blindness.”
The definitions of disability and blindness approved under Title X, XIV, or XVI, in
these State plans, were used to convert recipients to the Federal Supplemental Security
Income (SSI) program.
The definitions and criteria set forth in the State plans will be used to evaluate
whether recipients remain eligible for SSI benefits if they are selected for continuing
disability reviews (CDRs).
