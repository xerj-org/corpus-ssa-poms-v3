B. Cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs)
See DI
52120.050H - Exhibits for a COLA chart.
•
New Law: The District of Columbia gives a ‘supplemental allowance’ on its WC to individuals
who are totally and continuously disabled. These increases are effective January 1st.
•
Old Law: COLA percentages are the same as LSHW COLAs, and are effective October 1st.
