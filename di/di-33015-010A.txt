A. Disability Hearing Unit (DHU) responsibilities of personnel protection
•
The DHU personnel are to be aware of plans for employee protection (e.g., fire, bomb
threats, and hostile acts) already established at the place of hearing.
•
The DHU staff are to be alert to possible serious disturbances that may occur before,
during, and after a hearing.
•
The DHU staff must alert local management staff and the guard of any potential issues
with hearing participants.
