D. Completing the disability determination and transmittal form SSA-831
A small number of EDCS exclusion cases may use an SSA-831 instead of the EDCS routing
form. The controlling date for these cases is located in Item 11 (Remarks). Complete
remaining items according to “Completion of Form SSA-831 – Title II, Title XVI and
Concurrent Claims” per DI 26510.000.
