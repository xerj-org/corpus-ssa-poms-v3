D. Reminders for choosing an impairment code for special senses and speech impairments
•
Use 3694 only if the visual impairment meets listing 2.02, 102.02, 2.03A, or 102.03A.
•
Use 3694 (Statutory Blindness) as primary diagnosis when the established disability onset
date (Item 15 of the SSA-831) is the same as the date statutory blindness begins.
•
Use 3694 (Statutory Blindness) as the secondary diagnosis when the established disability
onset date (Item 15 of the SSA-831) is based on another impairment and that impairment's
onset date is before the onset date for statutory blindness.
