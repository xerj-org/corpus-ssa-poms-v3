DI 34105.001 Summary of Changes in the Listing of Impairments (Updated to Include Final Revisions
of 8/68)
The Listing of Impairments, published as an appendix to the new regulations, Subpart
P, reflects changes made since issuance of Transmittal Letter Number 6, dated 5/10/68.
To aid in ready identification of these changes, as they relate to the entire revision
issued earlier, this summary of changes has been revised. Those items in the summary
marked in the margin by *(asterisk) identify broadly those changes made since 5/10/68.
The significant changes in the Listing of Impairments identified by this revised summary
should be read and studied.
