SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23501.015 - Completing the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) for Prisoner Cases
Effective Dates: 03/12/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423501015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Item 29
The DDS prepares and releases all denial notices. If a system-generated notice cannot
be used, the DDS examiner designates “DDS D/L” and the appropriate notice or rider
in item 29 of the SSA-831. (For a list of prisoner case notices and riders see DI 23501.025.)
