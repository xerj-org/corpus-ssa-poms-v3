SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.070 - Administrative Law Judge Issues Closed Period Decision Where Disability Ceased More Than 14 Months before the Month in Which the Application Was Filed
Effective Dates: 05/04/2009 - Present | TN 16 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Overview
When an administrative law judge (ALJ) issues a closed period of disability award,
but in their decision, they find that the claimant's disability ceased more than 14
months before the month in which the application was filed, a freeze or denial must
be established. In their decision, the ALJ must decide whether the claimant's failure
to file a timely application was due to a physical or mental incapacity, and whether
a freeze is warranted with no cash payments. See DI 25510.010B2.
