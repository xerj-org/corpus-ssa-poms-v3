Allowance notice language
You asked us to see if you were   (1)  under our rules so that we could make your Supplemental Security Income (SSI) case
active again.
We are writing to tell you that you are   (1)   under our rules. Therefore, we are making your SSI case active again. We may review
your case from time-to-time to be sure you are still   (1)   under our rules.

For 1634 States (states for which SSA makes Medicaid Determinations)
You may still get Medicaid under special SSI rules even though your SSI checks have
stopped. An agency of your state will advise you about the Medicaid program.
If you have any questions about your eligibility for Medicaid or if you need immediate
medical assistance, you should get in touch with [choices under paragraph 1144(l)].
Choice 2 -
For SSI States (States that make their own Medicaid determinations based on SSI criteria)
You may be able to get Medicaid under special SSI rules even though your SSI checks
have stopped. However, you must apply for Medicaid with [choices under paragraph 1151(6)].
Take this letter with you when you apply. The people in your state will decide if
you can get Medicaid.
Choice 3
For 209(b) States (States that make their own Medicaid determinations based on more
restrictive criteria)
You may be able to get Medicaid under special SSI rules even though your SSI checks
have stopped. However, you must apply for Medicaid with [choices under paragraph 1151(6)].
Take this letter with you when you apply.
The people in your state will see when you last had Medicaid coverage. If you had
Medicaid coverage when you stopped getting SSI, then your Medicaid may continue, even
though your SSI checks have stopped.
