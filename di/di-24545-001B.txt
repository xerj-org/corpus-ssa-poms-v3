Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424545001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Operating Policy
1. STABILITY POST MI
A period of recovery is needed post MI before stability is achieved.
2. EVALUATION POST MI
Evaluation of the clinical findings should ordinarily be no earlier than 3 months post MI in order to be able to adequately assess severity and duration.
3. EXCEPTION TO 3-MONTH GUIDELINE
An earlier decision is appropriate if the evidence is persuasive.
