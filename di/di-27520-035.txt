DI 27520.035 Model Language for Fully Favorable Title XVI Applicants (Informal Remands)
“As a result of our additional review, we find that your Supplemental Security Income
(SSI) claim is fully favorable because your disability prevents you from engaging
in substantial gainful work. Therefore, it is not necessary to have your case decided
at the hearing level. You will receive another notice about the amount of your payments.
If you agree with our revised determination, you need take no further action, and
your hearing request will be dismissed. If you disagree with our revised determination,
you may request that the Office of Disability Adjudication and Review proceed with
your pending request for hearing. Your request should be made in writing and filed
within 30 days from the mailing date of this notice. Your request may be filed with
any Social Security Field Office. The Administrative Law Judge will dismiss your hearing
request 45 days from the date of this fully favorable notice unless you or your representative
requests that the hearing proceed.”
