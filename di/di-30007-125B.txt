B. RPC case submission time frame
The DDS should submit a case for RPC as soon as possible, but no later than 21 calendar
days from the date of receipt of a deficiency. The DDS is not required to perform
any documentation actions directed by the DQR prior to submitting the case for RPC.
If more than 21 calendar days have elapsed since the DQR return, the DDS must obtain
permission from Disability Analysis Branch (DAB) prior to submitting the case for

A case the DQR closes without returning it to the DDS is NOT a case the DDS has already
closed.
