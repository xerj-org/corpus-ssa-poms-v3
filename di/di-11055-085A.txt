Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
In initial claim situations when the claimant's whereabouts are unknown or the claimant
does not wish to pursue the Supplemental Security Income (SSI) claim, the field office
(FO) must close out the claim appropriately. An SSI denial is usually preferable to
a withdrawal.
For claims associated with a Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), see Processing FO
Determinations DI 81010.140.
