B. Regular cases
DDS administrators should set production standards for experienced examiners and for
those at various levels of training. Generally, a case requiring little or no additional
development should be processed promptly by the DDS. Cases needing medical development
should be processed in a timeframe consistent with the threshold levels for both titles,
but as early and accurately as possible.
