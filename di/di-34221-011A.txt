SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34221.011 - Musculoskeletal Listings from 06/16/08 to 09/28/16
Effective Dates: 09/28/2016 - Present | TN 2 (09-16)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434221011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disorders of the musculoskeletal system may result from hereditary, congenital, or
acquired pathologic processes
Impairments may result from infections, inflammatory, or degenerative processes, traumatic
or developmental events, or neoplastic, vascular, or toxic/metabolic diseases.
