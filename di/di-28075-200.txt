DI 28075.200 Special Instructions for Processing Overdue Medical Cessation and Uneffectuated Medical
Cessation Cases
This section provides Disability Determination Services (DDS) instructions for processing
overdue medical cessation (OMC) cases and uneffectuated medical cessation (UMC) cases.
The policies and processing procedures apply to all cases (Title II only, Title XVI
only, concurrent Title II and Title XVI, and dual Title II entitlement).
