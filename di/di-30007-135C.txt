SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 30007.135 - Actions Prior to the Request for Program Consultation (RPC) Panel Discussion
Effective Dates: 08/24/2026 - Present | TN 54 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0430007135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Disability Policy (DP), Disability Adjudication (DA), and Quality Review (QR) RPC
panel member actions prior to the RPC panel discussion
1. All three panel members (DP, DA, and QR):
•
Review the DDS's RPC submission and the DQR's SSA-1774 (Request for Corrective Action)
for the involved policy issues and policy references.
•
Review the entire case record and prepare to resolve the policy issues in question.
2. The DP panel member:
•
Completes the DDS and DQR data screens in the RPC tool.
•
Reviews the RPC tool database for cases with similar issues.
•
Determines the need for additional medical or policy component expertise. If the panel
member finds additional expertise is necessary, the panel member will not schedule
the case for panel discussion. The panel member will provide a synopsis of the case
issues to the RPC Technical Expert (TE) and request the appropriate policy guidance.
The TE will forward the request to the consulting component. Upon the policy component
response, the panel member may expedite the case or schedule it for discussion.
•
Expedites the claim when appropriate. If the DP panel member finds the case record
supports using the expedited process, the DP panel member applies the procedures for
DI 30007.135F. and DI 30007.135G. in this section.
•
Schedules the RPC panel discussion using the RPC tool, allowing two business days
notice to all interested parties, if the case record does not support use of the expedited
process or DDS requests panel discussion. When the RPC panel discussion is scheduled,
the RPC tool automatically sends an email to the involved DDS, DQR, and DC, notifying
them of the date and time of the RPC panel discussion.
NOTE: RPC makes every attempt to schedule RPC panel discussions in a timely manner. If
RPC has not resolved a submission within 21 calendar days of the DDSs RPC request,
the DDS may request status using the ^LP DP RPC Controls mailbox. In the email subject line, enter RPC Status Request and the RPC case number.
