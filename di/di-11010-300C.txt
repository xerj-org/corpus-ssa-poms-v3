SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.300 - Routing Concurrent Disability Insurance Benefits Retirement and Survivor Insurance (DIB-RSI) Claims
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure non-DDS cases requiring PC/DCR determination
If a claim is an exception to DDS jurisdiction, it is normally the Disability Case
Review (DCR) jurisdiction in a DIB/Retirement Insurance Benefit (RIB), widows, or
parent's insurance benefits case because the primary number holder (NH) is age 54
or older. To determine if the case is other than DCR jurisdiction, see Cases to be
Processed By DCR - SSI Emergency Clause Case DI 11055.110, and send the claim to the component that has jurisdiction. For claims entered into
EDCS see Creating Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases DI 81010.020. Route the claim to the appropriate PC/DCR. Transfer any claims entered into EDCS.
See Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.085.
1. Claimant insured for DIB; eligible for RIB, Widows, or Parent's insurance benefits
When development is complete, assemble the official folder according to General Overview
of the MDF DI 70005.005 (paper folder) Transfer the MCS disability claim to the appropriate PSC/DCR.
When the RIB, aged widow(er), or parent's claim is completely developed:
•
Adjudicate the Retirement and Survivors Insurance (RSI) claim;
•
Assemble the RSI claim material; and
•
If paper documents exist, forward the entire RSI file to the PSC with the electronic
cover sheet prepared by MCS. If there is no action by the PC send the paper folder/material
to National Record Center (NRC).
When the DIB-RSI claims are ready for transfer to the PSC/DCR at the same time:
•
Adjudicate the RSI claim completely;
•
Wait one day and then transfer the remaining MCS DIB claim to the appropriate PSC/DCR;
•
Assemble the official DIB folder/file under regular procedures.
•
If there is an electronic folder and a prior paper folder, route the electronic folder
using the electronic procedure, see Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect
System (EDCS) DI 81010.085, and forward the paper folder to the correct PSC/DCR using a SSA-408 (Route Slip);
•
For official paper folders where signature proxy does not apply, staple the RSI claim
on the front cover of the DIB folder and annotate the MCS cover sheet of the need
for a disability determination on the attached non-DDS jurisdiction claim.
•
When transmitting a prior RSI claims folder, if signature proxy does not apply, place
the current RSI claim, in the prior paper RSI folder, staple the back cover of the
DIB folder to the front cover of the RSI folder and forward both to the PC/DCR.
•
When signature proxy does not apply place a copy of the MCS cover sheet on the front
of the DIB file and annotate the cover sheet to state that a non-DDS jurisdiction
decision is needed on the disability claim.
2. Claimant insured for DIB - not eligible for RIB
When a claimant is insured for DIB but not eligible for RIB:
•
When the RIB claim is completely developed, adjudicate the RIB denial.
•
When the DIB development is complete, forward the DIB file to the PSC/DCR.
•
When the official DIB folder is paper and DIB-RIB files are ready for transmittal
at the same time, staple the RIB claim with the MCS cover sheet, to the front cover
of the entire DIB folder and forward both to the PC/DCR. Annotate the cover sheet
stating the need for a Non-DDS decision.
•
For claims entered into EDCS see Creating Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
Cases DI 81010.020 and route the claim to the appropriate PC/DCR.
•
Transfer any claims entered into EDCS, see Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability
Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.085.
