DI 81005.045 Case Data Tab
The Case Data tab displays disability report information from Electronic Disability
Collect System (EDCS) in two basic formats:
•
Case Data view, and
•
Forms view.
To access information in these views, use the navigation tree on the left side of
the display.
NOTE:
A Record of Change (ROC) folder appears only if ROC data exists on a case.
For details on ROCs, see DI 81005.045C.
