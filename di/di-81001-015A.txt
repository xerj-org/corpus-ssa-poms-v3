SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81001.015 - Determining if a Folder is a Certified Electronic Folder (CEF)
Effective Dates: 03/15/2019 - Present | TN 2 (03-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481001015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Certified or non-certified electronic folder
The CEF is the official electronic folder. There are no paper folders associated with
the case. There are also electronic folder (EF) cases, which are electronic cases,
but the official folder is paper. It is important to distinguish between the two:
•
The CEF contains all documentation necessary to the case.
•
An EF may contain some, but not all, necessary documentation. An EF has a paper folder
associated with it.
