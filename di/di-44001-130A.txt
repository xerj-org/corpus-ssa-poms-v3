* Use the RRB T2 20/40 DENIAL notice for dual eligibility medical denials when the
number holder (NH) does not meet the insured status requirements for cash disability
insurance benefits (DIB). Do not use the RRB T2 20/40 DENIAL notice for joint freeze
20/40 cases, since joint freeze cases are paper cases and are not processed electronically.
Refer to DI 44005.000 for joint freeze cases.
**Use the RRB FREEZE ALLOWANCE notice for dual eligibility disability allowances where
the NH does not meet the insured status requirements for cash DIB benefits but has
been awarded a period of disability freeze.
