B. Policy for comparison point decisions (CPD)
Use the most recent favorable medical decision as the CPD when making EXR medical
determinations. An intervening medical determination that the claimant had medically
improved does not prevent a claimant from requesting EXR, as long as their previous
entitlement terminated due to substantial gainful activity (SGA) for Title II or earned/unearned
income for Title XVI. The disability determination services (DDS) must determine that
the claimant is disabled under the MIRS in the month of the EXR request in order for
them to qualify for EXR.
Likewise, a claimant can request EXR even if a previous EXR request was a denial.
In cases where a previous EXR medical denial exists, the DDS should use the most recent
favorable medical determination as the CPD for making the current EXR medical determination.
