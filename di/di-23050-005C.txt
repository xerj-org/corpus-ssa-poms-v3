C. DDS case processing
When the DDS receives an ESRD Medicare only case requesting a prior to age 22 determination,
the DDS takes the following actions:
•
Receipt the paper case in as a CDB - this applies to cases at the initial and reconsideration
levels.
•
Develop for onset of a mental or physical disability prior to age 22. The disability
does not have to be based on ESRD specifically. The FO will prepare and release the
notice.
•
For unfavorable and partially favorable disability determinations, the DDS will prepare
a personalized disability explanation (PDE) for inclusion with the FO notice. For
additional information on preparing a PDE, see DI 26530.001.
•
Transfer the case to the FO to finalize and issue the determination.
