DI 52145.015 Retention of Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Proofs
WC/PDB proofs often involve complex, State-specific awards, forms, or settlements
that require some degree of interpretation and analysis. Therefore, retain all WC/PDB
proofs permanently. Do not annotate WC/PDB proofs on the Shared Processes Evidence
(EVID) Screen.
