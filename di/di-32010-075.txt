DI 32010.075 Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Section 301 Reconsideration of Initial Cessation
Determination' Cases
Procedure for handling folder transfer
When an initial cessation determination involves reverting a 'Section 301 Reconsideration
of Initial Cessation Determination for a Medical CDR or Age 18 Redetermination' to
'continuance,' annotate the following remarks on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR)
special message field, the Supplemental Security Record (SSR) Remarks, or both: 'Potential
301 case sent to DDS on mm/dd/yr.'
