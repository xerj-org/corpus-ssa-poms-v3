E. No waiting period and no retroactivity for Title XVI
Unlike Title II, there is no 5-month waiting period, nor is there any retroactivity
of benefits for Title XVI.

•
DI 10105.015, Retroactivity of Disability Application about Title II retroactivity of benefits
•

Title II 5-month waiting period requirement
