C. Established period of disability not based on statutory blindness
If the established period of disability was not based on statutory blindness, it is
possible that the beneficiary may have since become statutorily blind. Since this
date would be later than the beginning date of the established period of disability,
the beneficiary is not entitled to a redetermination. In this situation, proceed as
in A. above.
