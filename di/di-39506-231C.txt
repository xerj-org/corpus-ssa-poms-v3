C. Procedure - Report Columns
1. Column A (on duty hours—excluding overtime)
For each staffing category listed under lines 1-3 (full-time, part-time, and temporary),
enter the hours worked during the reporting period by all personnel engaged in the
SSA disability program--excluding overtime. The entries in this column should not include hours for holidays observed by the State agency, sick, annual, or other paid
or unpaid leave.
2. Column B (holiday and leave hours)
For each staffing category listed under lines 1-3 (full-time, part-time, and temporary),
enter the hours for holidays observed by the State agency and for sick, annual, or
other paid leave (e.g., lump sum leave, military leave, etc.). The entries in this
column should include the proportionate share of holidays and leave time of the regular
staff of the agency who worked part-time on the SSA disability program.
3. Column C (total hours--exluding overtime)
This column should reflect the sum of column A (On Duty Hours—excluding overtime)
and column B (Holiday and Leave Hours).
4. Column D (overtime hours)
For each staffing category listed under lines 1-3 (full-time, part-time, and temporary),
enter the overtime hours worked during the report period by all personnel engaged
in the SSA disability program.
5. Column E (total hours)
This column should reflect the total hours to include overtime (sum of columns C and
D).
6. Column F (equivalent full-time positions)
The FTE calculation is based on the workyear (WY)--a standardized unit for measurement
of government personnel effort and costs. A workyear is the equivalent of 2,080 work
hours. The overtime workyear is equivalent to 1,755 hours because these hours do not
include holidays, sick, annual, or other paid leave.
This column should represent the quarterly FTE calculation for each staffing category
listed under lines 1-3 (full-time, part-time, and temporary). The quarterly FTE calculation
is the sum of column C (divided by 520 hours) plus column D (divided by 438.75 hours).
For example, the calculation for each line would be as follows:
(column C/520 hrs) + (column D/438.75 hrs) = FTE
Computations should be carried to two decimal places.
