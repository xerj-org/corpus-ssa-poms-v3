B. Step 1 – Identify the individual’s skills from their PRW
Ensure the case evidence provides sufficient work history information to identify
the skills gained from PRW as the claimant performed their job. For information on
developing additional vocational evidence, see DI 22515.010C.
For information about what we consider a skill, see DI 25015.017B.1.
