SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10505.015 - Averaging Countable Earnings
Effective Dates: 05/22/2020 - Present | TN 27 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410505015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Section § 404.1574(b)(2) of agency regulations requires us to determine countable earnings from work activity
as substantial gainful activity (SGA) if they average more than the SGA threshold
amounts. For the tables of SGA earnings, see DI 10501.015. Do not average earnings simply because it is or is not advantageous to the beneficiary.
Section § 404.1574a states we will average earnings if:
•
an employee or self-employed person’s work was continuous; without significant change
in work patterns or earnings;
•
there has not been a change in the SGA level; and
•
monthly earnings fluctuate from above to below the SGA threshold.
NOTE: Using eWork’s quick distribution tool is not the same as averaging countable
earnings to determine SGA. Do not use it to replace earnings development policies
found in DI 10505.005. Do not use the quick distribution tool when you have evidence of weekly, bi-weekly
or monthly earnings.
