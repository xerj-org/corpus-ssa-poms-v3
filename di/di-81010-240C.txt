Effective Dates: 03/07/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Associate the Transfer/Routing Sheet
The EDCS route sheet is automatically generated at the point of transfer. If the CPD
folder is a (n):
1. Electronic CPD
Attach the EDCS routing form to the paper forms (SSA-454, SSA-827s, SSA-821, etc.)
and mail to the DDS. The DDS will image the 454 into the electronic folder.
2. Paper CPD
Insert the appropriate Disability Report Form and SSA-827s into Section E Disability
Related Development (Blue) of the paper MDF. Additionally,
•
Ensure the question “Are you sending a paper folder that contains medical evidence for the last favorable
disability determination?” is answered correctly by selecting the “yes” button;
•
Complete the top portion of the eCDR Paper CPD Folder Flag and attach it to the paper
folder;
•
Place the EDCS routing form on top of the CPD folder flag; and
•
Mail the paper CPD folder to the DDS/ODAR.
NOTE: If using CDR WWW to control the CDR, DO NOT select and use the route sheet produced
by that program; use the EDCS route sheet.
