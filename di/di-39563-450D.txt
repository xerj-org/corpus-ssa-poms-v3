D. Supervisory training
The need for training of new and experienced supervisory staff in management techniques
and all aspects of workload and personnel management is paramount. DDS's should ensure
that supervisors are trained and that continuing education in this area is provided.
For further information regarding DDS training, see DI 39521.000 ff.
