SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23005.001 - Processing Whereabouts Unknown Disability Cases in the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
Effective Dates: 09/11/2024 - Present | TN 14 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423005001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When WU arises in the field office (FO)
The FO must review the claim to determine whether a medical determination is necessary.
Before transferring a WU claim to the DDS, the FO must resolve any substantial gainful
activity (SGA) issues (DI 10505.000) and provide an accurate potential onset date (POD) (DI 25501.220).
For additional information about FO responsibilities when a WU situation arises, see
DI 11055.085.
