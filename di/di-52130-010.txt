DI 52130.010 Federal Employees' Retirement System (FERS) Disability Benefits
FERS disability benefits are paid by the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) to disabled
federal employees covered under the FERS. FERS began 01/01/1987 and all FERS employment
is covered under Social Security. FERS disability benefits are paid monthly.
