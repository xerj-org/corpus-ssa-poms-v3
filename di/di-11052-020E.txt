E. Diaries
The field office (FO) must prepare a diary whenever an ESRD claimant is on dialysis
throughout the qualifying period but discontinues treatments and does not resume dialysis
or receive a kidney transplant and cessation occurred less than 10 months before adjudication,
prepare a diary for notification of termination. Prepare a diary to mature in PC7
for the first day of the 10th month from the month of cessation. Use diary code 41.
