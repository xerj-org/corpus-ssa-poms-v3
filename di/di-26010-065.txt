DI 26010.065 Item 34 (Remarks) on the SSA-831
Explain briefly any special circumstance or situation in previous items on the SSA-831
(for example, “Item 18, FO revised systems input required –statutorily blind, effective
(date) (MM/DD/YY)” or “Item 27, Onset of statutory blindness established (date) (MM/DD/YY),”
as appropriate.
In a Title XVI claim, if Item 34 shows, “Presumptive Blindness Decision,” delete the
entry.
For a detailed list of special situations that may be annotated in the “Remarks” section
of the SSA-831, see DI
26510.095 Completion of Item 34 (Remarks).
