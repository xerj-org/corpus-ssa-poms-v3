B. Authority and scope for technical review
The Quality Assurance Unit (QAU) specialists perform both a substantive and technical
review of all sample cases. Supervisors and disability examiners (DEs) are responsible
for ensuring substantive and technical accuracy for all cases adjudicated, even those
not chosen as part of the QA sample. The disability case processing system also provides
checks and edits to prevent technical errors on cases.
