A. Policy – termination of CDB entitlement
NOTE: Terminating events are described in detail in RS 00203.035.
Those events and months that specifically relate to CDB entitlement termination are:
•
The first month for which the wage earner (WE) is no longer entitled to a Disability
Benefit (DIB), unless the WE's entitlement ended because of his/her death or entitlement
to Retirement (RIB).
•
The entitlement of a CDB also ends with the second month following the month in which
he/she ceases to be under a disability, unless in such month he/she is under age 19
and is a full-time student.
