A. Introduction to non-essential material
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) should identify non-essential material
and document it on a Disability Development Worksheet or on an SSA-5002 Report of Contact. Attach the non-essential material to a Non-essential Material flag. See DI 20503.010 Exhibit – Sample Non-essential Material Flag.
