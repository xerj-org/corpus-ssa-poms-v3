Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455030045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. How to get to the VERN screen
To get to the VERN screen:
•
select 32, “Continuing Disability Review File,” from SSA's main menu (MAIN) screen;
•
select 13, “Verify/Update Earnings,” from the CDR selection menu (MCDR) screen; and
•
enter the beneficiary/recipient's SSN and the “earnings beginning” date.
