A. Role of DDS adjudicative teams
The DDS adjudicative teams evaluate medical improvement review standard (MIRS) issues
when conducting a CDR. These teams consist of a medical or psychological consultant
a disability examiner, trained in the disability evaluation process. In deciding MIRS
issues, the team members have the same responsibilities as when making initial determinations.
