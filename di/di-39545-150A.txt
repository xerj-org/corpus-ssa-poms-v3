Effective Dates: 06/15/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The DDS is responsible for determining claimants’ disabilities and ensuring that adequate
evidence is available to support its determination. When existing medical evidence
is insufficient, not available or cannot be obtained, DDSs are authorized to purchase
CEs.
