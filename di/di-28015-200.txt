DI 28015.200 Title II Beneficiary Now Over Age 18, but Comparison Point Decision (CPD) Used Part
B Listing
Apply the regular “adult” evaluation process described in DI 28005.005.
For Title XVI recipients originally allowed as children and who attained age 18 before
08/22/96, the continuing disability review (CDR) evaluation process is applied.
For Title XVI recipients originally allowed as children who attained age 18 on 08/22/96
or later, see Public Law 104-193 in DI 23570.006.
