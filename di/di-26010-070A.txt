SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26010.070 - Title II — Under-Age–55 Disabled Widow(er)’s Benefits (DWB) and Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB) Cases Involving Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) in Blindness Cases
Effective Dates: 09/22/2014 - Present | TN 2 (10-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426010070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Claimant is not statutorily blind
Complete only the following items (in addition to the identifying items that the field
office (FO) routinely completes):
1.
Item 16A (Primary Diagnosis)—show “Not Statutorily Blind.”
2.
Items 30, 31, 32, 33, 32A, and 32B—signature blocks.
3.
Item 34 (Remarks)—show “SGA-Statutorily Blind decision only.”
NOTE: The FO incorporates the Disability Determination Services (DDS) determination in
a denial or cessation based on the non-blind SGA level.
