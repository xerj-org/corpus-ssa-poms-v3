DI 11020.001 Making a Childhood Disability Determination to Entitle Someone Else to a Different
Benefit
Under P.L. 97-35 (the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1981), a disability decision
may be needed solely to:
•
Entitle a spouse, mother, or father caring for a child age 16-18 entitled to child's
benefits; or
•
Pay the lump-sum death payment (LSDP) to a disabled widow(er) or a childhood disability
beneficiary (CDB) when the spouse was not living with the deceased worker at the time
of death.
In these situations, if the child-in-care provision is an issue, a disability determination
for a child as young as age 15 years 6 months may be needed.
For details about Title II situations requiring a disability determination for a child
to entitle another person for a different type of benefit, see DI 11025.001 Introduction to Disability Determinations for Entitlement to a Non-Disability Payment.
For the general requirements for CDB entitlement, see DI 10115.001.
