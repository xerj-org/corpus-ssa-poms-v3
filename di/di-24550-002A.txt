SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24550.002 - Body-Mass-Index-for-Age Tables (Age 2 to the Attainment of Age 18)
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 2 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424550002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Listing 105.08 uses Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) growth charts
to determine the severity of a child's malnutrition. The severity criterion in listing
105.08B2 considers the child's body mass index (BMI) and is met when the child's BMI-for-age
is less than the third percentile on the most recent CDC growth chart.
The tables in subsections DI 24550.002C and DI 24550.002D provide the third percentile data for BMI-for-age for males and females from age
2 to the attainment of age 18.
