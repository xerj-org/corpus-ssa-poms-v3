A. Disability Case Review and the Electronic Folder (EF)
Until the next phase of the eDib process which allows components to take action on
information in the EF is implemented in the PSCs, DCR components will continue to
process disability cases using the paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF). Currently,
the paper MDF is the official folder for all disability cases. All components involved
in disability case processing should use eView to access and print disability reports
stored in the EF.
