SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.120 - Sources and Types of Purchased Medical Services
Effective Dates: 06/05/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions – types of purchased medical services
1. Consultative Examinations (CEs)
See DI 39545.075C.1. (e.g., psychiatric and psychological examinations, x-rays, and laboratory tests
(including specialized tests such as pulmonary function studies, electrocardiograms
and stress tests)).
2. Medical Evidence of Record (MER)
See DI 39545.075C.4. (e.g., abstracts, copies, narratives or teledictation of existing medical records
and statements used to document disability claims).
