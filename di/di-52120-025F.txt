F. Lump sum settlements
1.
A compensation order or award of an administrative law judge or a single commissioner
becomes final unless a party to the dispute, within thirty (30) days from the receipt
by the worker of the order or award, petitions in writing for a review by the full
commission of the order or award.
2.
The Arkansas Workers’ Compensation Commission (AWCC), through its three Commissioners
and a staff of Administrative Law Judges, adjudicate disputed workers' compensation
cases with binding decisions that can be appealed to the Arkansas Court of Appeals
and the Arkansas Supreme Court.
3.
In computing offset, exclude payments made directly to a spouse. This is similar to
Massachusetts' payments to a spouse for release of inchoate rights to death benefits.
