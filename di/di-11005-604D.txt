D. Processing initial claims involving CAL
When transferring a case in EDCS to the DDS, the selection software determines whether
the case is identified for processing as a CAL case. If a CAL condition is identified,
the FO receives one of the following on-screen message boxes in EDCS:
•
"This case has been marked as a Compassionate Allowance case. Full non-medical development
is required." or,
•
"This case has been marked as a Quick Disability Determination and Compassionate Allowance
case. Full non-medical development required."
Process CAL cases in the same manner as a Quick Disability Determinations (QDD) case
in DI 11005.603C - DI 11005.603F.
If you identify a potential CAL case during your interview or review of an online
application, follow these steps:
1.
If the claimant alleges cancer, obtain the type and stage (if known) and record this
information on the SSA-3368 or SSA-3820 in EDCS.
2.
For Title XVI cases, revisit the possibility of Presumptive Disability/Blindness (PD/PB)
payments if one of the PD/PB conditions is met. For PD/PB instructions see DI 11055.230.
3.
Annotate CAL as an issue on the MCS DW01 or CCE Development Worksheet page with a
tickle date of 20-days and initiate non-medical development.
