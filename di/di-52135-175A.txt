A. Policy – When receipt of New York TD causes offset
The State reduces TD benefits due to receipt of Social Security disability insurance
benefits (DIB) if both benefits are for the same impairment or disability. This is
reverse offset.
Assume the same impairment or disability is the basis for both benefits absent evidence
to the contrary.
