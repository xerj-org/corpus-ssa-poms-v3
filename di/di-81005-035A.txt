A. The Primary Title Bar
The Primary Title Bar consists of:
•
Claimant’s name and Social Security Number (SSN);
•
Claim Type(s);
•
Alleged Onset Date (AOD);
•
Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Folder Number;
•
Edit (Lock Case) button; and
•
Links to Folder Search, Print Forms, EDCS, and Help.
