DI 11050.065 Additional Remarks for the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) for
Railroad (RR) Cases
Include the following additional remarks when transmitting the case to Branch 2A of
Disability Case Review via the SSA 831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal):
•
Medical
evidence to be secured from RRB
When the claimant states that there is pertinent medical evidence at the Railroad
Retirement Board (RRB) or EC or ICERS indicates that medical evidence is available.
•
Career
RR case
If EC or ICERS shows 120 months RR service (or 60 months RR service after December
31, 1995).
•
Dependent
RR Annuitant
If a claim for SSI, DIB, DWB, or CDB is filed by an individual who is entitled to
a survivor's RR annuity as a disabled dependent of a career RR worker.
