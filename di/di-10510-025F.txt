F. Coordination of coverage and SGA development in farm cases
The information given on the SSA-7156 (Farm Self-Employment Questionnaire) and SSA-7157-F4
(Farm Arrangement Questionnaire), obtained in the development of earnings and coverage,
is pertinent to an SGA determination. This form also collects a thorough explanation
on the experience, education, and training of the claimant or beneficiary. For more
information on the SSA-7156, see RS 01804.200.
