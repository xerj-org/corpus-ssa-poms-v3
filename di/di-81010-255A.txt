A. Background for CDR reconsideration appeal
The field office (FO) serves as the starting point for CDRs. Most CDRs are processed
electronically through all adjudicative levels. The eCDR may have either a paper or
electronic Comparison Point Decision (CPD) folder. The Electronic Disability Collect
System (EDCS) provides the capability to link eCPD folders to the current eCDR. Paper
CPD folders follow the eCDR to each adjudicative component (Disability Determination
Services [DDS], Office of Hearing Appeal Operations [OHO], etc.)
For further information regarding the creation of the Initial eCDR, see eCDR Short
Term Process Overview - DI 81010.200.
