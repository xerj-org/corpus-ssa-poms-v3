A. Introduction - referrals
Use Form SSA-3661-U2 to transmit all under age 16 title XVI disability and blind recipient
referrals and additional material to designated State agencies. Do not check the VR
referral box on the SSA-832-U5 unless a separate referral is also being made to vocational
rehabilitation and VR procedures call for it.
