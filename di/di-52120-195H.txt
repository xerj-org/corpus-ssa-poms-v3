H. Verifying WC
Ohio WC can be verified online, by fax, or by phone. To help interpret the Ohio State
WC printout see, Guide to Ohio Bureau of Workers' Compensation printout
1. Online access
On-line access is available and should be the first option to verify Ohio WC records.
Once you are certain that WC evidence is not already in file, check the Workers' Compensation
(WC) Resource Page in DI 52140.020 since OH is a state that provides SSA with access to its online records. The value
of online records can vary State to State and some development may still be required.
However, the sites available usually provide contact information for the employer,
WC carrier or the WC attorney, which can jump-start the development process. In addition,
included in the WC resource intranet site is a list of individuals in the PC with
online access to those individual State records.
2. Fax
Based on Senate Bill 7, effective 06/30/06, the OH WC law now requires a written consent
for release of any WC information from BWC files. If contacting the Ohio BWC directly,
a signed consent form must accompany the request. The request (an SSA-1709), along
with the necessary consent, can be faxed directly to 866-336-8352 for processing.
3. Phone
Call toll free to 1-800-644-6292. The representative can usually provide a printout
of the WC information, but can also provide the name and phone number of the caseworker.
As indicated in the previous section, you will need a written consent. If you have
a more involved question, it may be necessary to ask for assistance from the caseworker
who can review the actual file.
