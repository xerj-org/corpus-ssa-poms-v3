B. Assisting the VR Agency in Locating Claimants
The State VR Agency will notify the DO when a claimant cannot be located. The DO will
handle the request as follows:
1. INDIVIDUAL IS RECEIVING BENEFITS
Verify the address. The DO may query the MBR or SSR for the current address. Notify
the State agency if a new address is found or if the address which the VR agency has
is correct.
2. INDIVIDUAL IS NOT RECEIVING BENEFITS
If DO records show information regarding the individual's address, advise the VR agency
by phone or in writing. If DO records do not contain such information, the VR agency
should be advised.
