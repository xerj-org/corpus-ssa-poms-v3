DI 14515.015 Notices for Termination of Section 301 Payments
ODO will prepare advance and final notices for termination of Section 301 payments.
Advance notice must be provided before payments can be stopped (GN 03001.015). Use the model advance notice in NL 00703.122 for title II cases. Prepare a manual SSA-L8155 (SSI Notice of Planned Action) for
title XVI cases (NL 00803.015).
