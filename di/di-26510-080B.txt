B. Completing Item 28 in DIB claims
1.
Check Block A “Period of Disability” in DIB claims.
2.
Do not check Block B “Disability Period” in DIB claims.
3.
Block C “Estab. Beg.”
•
Check this block when the claimant is found disabled on or before the date the earnings
requirement is last met in DIB claims.
•
Make no further entries in block C in DIB claims.
