C. Informal remands (IR)
Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR) may remand a paper case to the DDS. For more information on IRs, see DI 27520.005.
The disability determination services (DDS) cannot reopen a disability hearing officer’s (DHO) determination. The DDS must return the
paper case to the DHU for reopening consideration. For additional information regarding
the reopening of a determination or a decision, see DI 27501.005.
