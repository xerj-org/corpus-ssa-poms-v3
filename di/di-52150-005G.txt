G. How to determine the TFB when a combined family maximum (CFM) is involved
Determining the TFB when CFM is involved depends on when the CFM is effective.
1.
If the CFM is effective in the first month offset is considered or earlier, use the benefit amounts determined using the CFM
in the TFB.
NOTE: A simultaneously entitled child cannot request payment on the other record in order
to avoid offset. For information regarding simultaneous entitlement and CFM, see RS 00615.776.
2.
If the CFM is effective after the first month offset is considered, the resulting increase in benefits is protected and the TFB
is not recomputed.
