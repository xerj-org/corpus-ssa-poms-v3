D. Procedure - when screening performed by ODO examiners
Any applicant whose disability determination, based in part or in whole on medical
factors, is prepared by ODO or the regional office (e.g., railroad annuitant case
or emergency clause case) will be considered for VR referral by the ODO or regional
office examiner who prepared the determination. The examiner will refer the applicant
to the appropriate State VR agency unless one of the screen-out criteria in DI 28087.020 is met or the applicant was previously referred by a DDS or ODO.
