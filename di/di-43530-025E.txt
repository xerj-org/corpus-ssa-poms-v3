E. Payment procedures
Payments in connection with disability cases for which expenditures have been authorized
by SSA shall be made in accordance with 4 FAM 335. Requests for services sent to the
post will show the appropriation number, allotment and organizational codes, and a
control number for use by the post in preparing vouchers. Copies of paid vouchers
should be mailed to: SSA, Division of Finance, OFR, OMBP, P.O. Box 47, Baltimore,
Maryland 21203.
