A. Policy - Commercial product alternative signature(s) (CPAS)
Individuals may use a commercial software product (“commercial product”) to affix
their signatures to the forms or writings listed in this section. In this section,
these signatures are referred to as CPAS.
NOTE: Individuals may also use other signature and submission methods permitted by current
agency policy.
Accept the CPAS on any of the forms and writings listed in this section if it meets
the criteria described in this section.
