C. Completing items 15- 24 of SSA-831-U3
The process for completing items 15 – 24 on the SSA-831-U3 at the reconsideration
level is generally the same for initial claims. For instructions, see DI 26510.000 Completion of Form SSA-831-C3/U3 – Title II, Title XVI and Concurrent Claims – Table
of Contents.
