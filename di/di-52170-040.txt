DI 52170.040 Notice Provision for Pre-1981 Amendment Cases
This section only applies to cases with a date of disability onset (DDO) before March
1, 1981 or disability insurance benefit (DIB) month of entitlement (MOET) before September 1,
1981.
NOTE: Only workers’ compensation (WC) including Part C Black Lung Benefits (BLB) can cause
offset in these cases. PDB offset does not apply to notice provision cases.
