DI 11050.015 Jurisdiction of Disability (DIB) and Freeze Applications for Career Railroad (RR)
Employee and Surviving Dependent RR Annuitant
Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR), within Risk and Quality, has jurisdiction
over DIB claims filed by claimants who have 120 or more RR service months (or 60 or
more months after December 31, 1995) or who have filed or are entitled to a survivor
annuity with the Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) on a different account number.
Refer to Transferring Electronic Cases to Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR),
DI 11050.055.
DCR returns all cases to the field office (FO) for processing after making the decision.
DCR coordinates its determination with the disability determination prepared by the
RRB on the same claimant.
