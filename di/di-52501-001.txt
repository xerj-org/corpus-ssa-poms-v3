DI 52501.001 Background on Court Cases
This chapter contains the background information, and processing instructions which
will enable State agencies to adequately handle issues and case processing related
to court orders.
The chapter is divided into segments, each which includes cases with similar issues.
The segments are:
A.
Not Severe Cases,
B.
Medical Improvement Cases, and
C.
Other Court Cases.
Each of the above segments includes subchapters devoted to a single court case which
is related to others within the segment. Complete processing instructions for an individual
case can be found within the subchapter. This enables the user to answer inquiries
and process cases affected by the court order with minimal need to cross-refer to
other sections of POMS.
