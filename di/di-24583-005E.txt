E. How do we determine the severity of the mental impairment(s)?
After rating the degree of functional limitation resulting from the impairment(s),
we determine the severity of the mental impairment(s). If the degree of limitation
in the areas of mental functioning are “none” or “mild,” we generally conclude that
the impairment(s) is not severe, unless the evidence otherwise indicates that there
is more than a minimal limitation in the ability to do basic work activities. For
example, in some cases with multiple “mild” ratings, we may still find there is a
severe impairment. For more information about the rating scale, see DI 34001.032 (12.00F of the adult mental disorders listings).
