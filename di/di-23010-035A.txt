SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.035 - Required Written Analysis for a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination
Effective Dates: 04/23/2024 - Present | TN 4 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Required content
The Failure To Follow Prescribed Treatment (FTFPT) determination must include all
of the following:
•
The identity of the prescribing medical source.
•
The identity of the impairment(s) for which the treatment was prescribed.
•
A description of the prescribed treatment.
•
A description of the evidence that demonstrates the individual did not follow the
prescribed treatment.
•
An explanation of your assessment(s) about why we do or do not expect the prescribed
treatment to result in the individual no longer being disabled and/or why the individual
does or does not have good cause for not following the prescribed treatment.
•
An explanation on why you did not need to make both assessments described in the fifth
bullet above.
