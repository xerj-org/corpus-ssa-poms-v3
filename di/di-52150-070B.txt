SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.070 - How Changes in Family Composition Affect Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Offset
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Benefit computation
Compute new rates using:
•
The same average current earnings (ACE) applicable in the first possible month of
offset; and
•
The TFB determined for the new number of beneficiaries that would have been subject
to offset in the first possible month of offset.
