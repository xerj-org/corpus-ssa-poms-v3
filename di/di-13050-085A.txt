A. Background for EXR determinations
A request for EXR involves two types of determinations; a provisional benefit determination
and an EXR determination. Provisional benefits determinations are not subject to the
administrative and judicial reviews (appeals), see DI 13050.025G. EXR technical and medical determinations are initial determinations subject to appeals,
see GN 03101.070 and SI 04010.010.
