SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40105.050 - Administrative Res Judicata Processing Center (PC) Instructions
Effective Dates: 06/16/2025 - Present | TN 4 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440105050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Administrative Res Judicata does not apply when the administrative decision is subject
to administrative revision.
Some examples are:
1.
In a DIB claim, there has been additional work activity, which could extend the date
the earnings requirement was last met.
2.
In a DWB or CDB reentitlement, there is evidence that could extend the date the prescribed
period or reentitlement period ended.
3.
There has been a change in policy that must be considered for the period at issue.
For more information, see DI 27615.010.
These claims are usually FO jurisdiction; however, they occasionally come into the
Office of Disability Operations, Processing Center-Disability Case Review, and Office
of International Operations (ODO/PC-DPB/OIO).
