SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52170.025 - Rounding Rules for Offset Computations
Effective Dates: 05/18/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452170025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Rounding benefit amounts payable after offset
1. Before June 1982
Round up to the next higher multiple of $.10 for monthly benefit amounts payable after
offset.
2. June 1982 or later
Round down to the next lower multiple of $.10 for monthly benefit amounts payable
after offset.
