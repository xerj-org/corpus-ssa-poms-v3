A. How employers obtain WC coverage
Employers obtain WC coverage in one of the following ways:
1.
Employers can self-insure, subject to gaining the necessary approval from the State.
Typically larger-scale employers or those who participate in a group can self-insure.
2.
Employers may purchase insurance from carriers, either private firms or competitive
State-operated insurance companies (where they exist).
NOTE: In some States, employers can purchase insurance only from a State-operated insurance
company.
