DI 39525.015 Reporting for Applicant Travel--Operating Procedure -- DDS
Include applicant travel costs under “All Other Nonpersonnel Costs” on such reports as:
•
the SSA-4513 (State Agency Report of Obligations for SSA Disability Programs), and
•
the Monthly Obligations Report (MOR)
