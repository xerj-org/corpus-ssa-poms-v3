H. Documenting the FTC determination
Follow the FTC development procedures Failure to Cooperate in DI 23007.000 and document all efforts made to resolve the issues on an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact),
or on a Case Note in the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS) and "write to" the
case development worksheet (CDW). Include the following:
•
Efforts to resolve the issue(s), and the dates,
•
Results of the efforts, and
•
Justification for a cessation.
The DDS makes the FTC determination. For all FTC situations, the cessation date cannot
be earlier than the first month in which the individual failed to do what we asked.
