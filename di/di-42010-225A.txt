A. There is no interim benefits start order
The notice to a claimant about the Appeals Councils review of a favorable ALJ decision
tells the claimant that we will pay interim benefits effective with the month before
the month in which the 110th day falls. The claimant may contact SSA as the 110th day approaches to inquire about
interim benefits. If the processing center (PC) had not received the start order for
Title II interim benefits, request a start order to initiate interim benefits by sending
an email to ^DA Compliance 8001.
