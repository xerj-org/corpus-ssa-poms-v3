SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25201.020 - Role of Qualified Pediatrician and Other Specialist in the Evaluation
Effective Dates: 06/17/2025 - Present | TN 8 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425201020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
We must make every reasonable effort to ensure that a qualified physician reviews
the medical portion of a case involving a physical impairment and that a qualified
psychiatrist or psychologist reviews the medical portion of a case involving a mental
impairment.
For claims of children under age 18 filing for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
benefits based on disability, we must make reasonable efforts to ensure that a qualified
pediatrician or other individual who specializes in a field of medicine appropriate
to the child's disability evaluates the medical portion of the case.
NOTE: For additional guidance on medical and psychological consultants' adjudicative
roles and qualification requirements, see DI 24501.001.
