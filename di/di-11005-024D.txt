D. Protective filing and the i3368
An i3368 initiated before January 25, 2014, may potentially be a protective writing
to protect a filing date for disability benefits. However, an i3368 initiated after
January 24, 2014, cannot serve as a protective writing.
NOTE: The electronic submission of the iClaim may constitute a protective writing and establish
a protective filing date if a claimant meets all requirements for establishing a protective
filing. See details in GN 00204.010 and GN 00204.055.
