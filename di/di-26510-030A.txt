SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.030 - Completing Item 19 (Claimant Not Disabled) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB)
1. Block A – (Through date of current determination)
Check block A when:
•
A DIB or Freeze case in which the insured status expires after the date of adjudication
and disability/blindness is not established through the date of the current determination;
Or
•
A Closed Period case in which the period of disability ends earlier than the 14th month before the month of filing.
NOTE: The Disability Examiner (DE) will also enter the determination for the closed period
in the rationale of the DIB denial on the SSA-831 or on an SSA-4268-U5. The claimant
must receive a personalized notice. The notice should include personalized language
regarding why extension of the 14-month period for filing for a period of disability
cannot be granted.
2. Block B – Through (insert appropriate date)
•
Check this block in DIB or Freeze cases where the date that insured status is last
met before the date of the current determination, and the individual is found not
disabled on or before the date last insured.
•
Enter the date the earnings requirement is last met.
NOTE: When the field office (FO) receives the earnings record and finds that the Quarters
of Coverage requirement is last met in the past, the FO will contact the Disability
Determination Services (DDS) and furnish the date last insured.
