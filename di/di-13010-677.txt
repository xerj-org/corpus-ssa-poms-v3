DI 13010.677 The Entitlement Selection (ESEL) Screen
Introduction
The ESEL screen is a entitlement selection screen that you receive prior to viewing
or updating the Verify/Update Earnings (VERN) screen, or the Query Earnings (QERN)
screen. You have the opportunity to view and select the period of disability that
you want to work with.
