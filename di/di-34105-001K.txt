Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434105001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

K. Neurological
The former, non-specific criterion to “evaluate on the basis of resultant neurological involvement” has been replaced by specific signs. Listing 11.05 has been added for the evaluation
of primary brain tumors.
