SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 43501.001 - Processing Responsibilities for Foreign Claims
Effective Dates: 09/23/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0443501001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - responsibilities
Office of Disability and International Operations (ODIO), Office of International
Operations (OIO), is responsible for:
•
Claims filed by or on behalf of individuals who use a foreign address (see DI 43505.001);
•
Taking claims and making disability determinations when the primary individual is
using a foreign address;
•
Assisting Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR) when necessary;
•
Claims filed under the international social security agreements (totalization).
