SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.300 - General Approach to Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) Comparison in Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs)
Effective Dates: 11/25/2025 - Present | TN 15 (11-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. CPD RFC
Compare the MIRS RFC with the CPD RFC to determine if MI is related to the ability
to work. If the MIRS RFC has not increased from the CPD RFC, find MI not related to
the ability to work, and continue benefits if no exception applies. If the MIRS RFC
increased from the CPD RFC, find that MI is related to the ability to work and consider
the remaining steps in CDR sequential evaluation process.
