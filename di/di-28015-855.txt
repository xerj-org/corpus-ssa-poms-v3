DI 28015.855 Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) Form SSA-4734-BK Documentation in Continuing Disability
Reviews (CDRs)
Except as noted below, follow the usual procedures for completing RFC forms, including
Form SSA-4734-F4- SUP in cases involving a mental impairment(s), see DI 24510.001. Enter a remark on each form to indicate the type of assessment reflected.
