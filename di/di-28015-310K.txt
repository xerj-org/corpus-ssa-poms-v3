SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.310 - Consider Age and Time on the Rolls When Assessing Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) in Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs)
Effective Dates: 02/18/2025 - Present | TN 14 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

K. Documenting age and time on the rolls in RFC assessment
Discuss the impact of age and time on the rolls in the RFC assessment when the beneficiary
is age 50 or over and received disability benefits for seven years or more. If the
adjudicative team finds these factors do not affect RFC, make a note in the assessment
to assure you have not overlooked these factors.
