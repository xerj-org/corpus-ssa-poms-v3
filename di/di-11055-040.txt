DI 11055.040 Determining the Proper Disability Determination Services (DDS)
In general, the State where the claimant physically resides, or was last known to
reside, determines to which DDS the field office (FO) sends the claim for the medical
determination.
NOTE: The FO determines the claimant’s permanent residence during the application process.
