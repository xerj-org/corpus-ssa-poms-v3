D. Disposition of Paper SSA-827s in QR Cases
If an SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration
[SSA]) is available, be sure to store the image of the SSA-827 in the CEF when selecting
the case for Federal QR. Destroy extra paper copies of the SSA-827 once the image
is saved to the CEF.
