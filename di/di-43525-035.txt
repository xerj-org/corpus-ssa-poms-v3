DI 43525.035 Failure to Cooperate-CDR
NOTE: See DI 43510.080
When continuing disability review is necessary and the individual does not furnish
information about work activity or medical treatment, or otherwise fails to cooperate
with efforts of the foreign service posts (FSP), assume that the individual is unable
rather than unwilling to cooperate. (The failure to cooperate may be a result of the
impairment.) If the disabled beneficiary fails to comply with a written request for
information, the FSP should follow the same procedures as the DO. (See DI 13005.000 for DO instructions.) The FSP must fully document all efforts to have the individual
cooperate because benefits may be terminated due to lack of cooperation.
