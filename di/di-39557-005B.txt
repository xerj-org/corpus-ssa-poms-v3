Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439557005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definition
Performance support may include, but is not limited to, any or all of the following:
1.
A review of cases processed by the DDS emphasizing adherence to written guidelines.
2.
A request that necessary administrative measures be implemented.
3.
Federal personnel to perform onsite and virtual reviews, conduct training, or perform
other functions needed to improve performance.
