C. How work deductions affect the total family benefit (TFB)
The TFB is the total of the benefits payable to HA and all entitled auxiliaries before
work deductions. Changes in auxiliary rates due to work deductions do not affect the
TFB. See DI 52150.005 - Total Family Benefits (TFB).
