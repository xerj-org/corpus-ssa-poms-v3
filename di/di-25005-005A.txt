A. Vocational assessment issues
The instructions in this section apply at the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
and to all adjudicators at each level of the administrative review process.
The instructions in this section apply to adult disability and disabled adult child
claims under Title II, adult disability claims and age 18 redeterminations under Title
XVI, and to determinations of whether a current adult beneficiary’s disability continues.
Consider borderline age prior to expediting the vocational assessment at step 5 of
the sequential evaluation process. For more information on borderline age, see DI 25015.006.
The instructions in this section do not change our rules for determining whether a
claimant is disabled.
