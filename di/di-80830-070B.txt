B. Receipt and Routing – Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Reversal/or Later Appeals Council
(AC) Favorable Decision
ALJ reversals and favorable AC decisions will be routed electronically to the servicing
PC via CPMS.
•
In the certified electronic process, an electronic “stub record” will be produced
in the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) system with the appropriate TOEL combination
(APPEAL/HEARING CPMS REVERSL for PC 1-6 and 8; APPEAL/DHEAR CPMS REVERSL for PC7).
•
A second ACR will also be automatically produced in the site’s PPC for the corresponding
attorney fee agreement action if applicable. The assigned TOEL will be ATFEE/FEEPAY.
•
The two ACRs should move together until the claim is completely processed.
•
Once the claim is processed to payment, the “stub record” will be moved to Files In
(FIN), and the ATFEE ACR will continue on its way until all attorney fee issues are
resolved.
