A. SSA's use of impairment codes on disability determination forms
SSA uses a standardized set of four-digit impairment codes on the SSA-831 (Disability
Determination and Transmittal), SSA-832 (Cessation Or Continuance of Disability or
Blindness Determination and Transmittal - Title XVI), and SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance
of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal - Title II). These codes
are loosely based on ICD-9 coding. Generally, the highest level code that is appropriate
is used. When that code is only three digits, a zero is added as the fourth digit.
Each code identifies the underlying cause of the person's disability, and the disabling
manifestations. The codes most relevant to the determination must be used.
