C. Procedure for systems input in cessation cases
1. Title II cases
Input a “Y” in the 301 field on the D833 screen of the NDDSS (see SM 06001.505B.11.).
Enter remarks in field 50 as follows: “Potential 301 case – handle per DI 14510.003C.” Route paper folders to the servicing FO. For closing and routing instructions, see
DI 81020.250.
2. Title XVI cases
Input a “Y” in the 301 field on the D832 screen of the National Disability Determination
Service System (NDDSS) (SM 06001.505B.11.). Enter remarks in field 50 as follows:
“Potential 301 case – handle per DI 14510.003C.” Input an N7 diary (disability cessation pending) or N8 diary (blindness cessation
pending) with a diary date of no less than 60 days. Route paper folders to the servicing
FO. For closing and routing instructions, see DI 81020.250.
