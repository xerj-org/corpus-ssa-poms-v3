E. Recalling claims transferred to DDS
Very rarely is it necessary to recall claims transferred to DDS. For example, if a
claim was routed to the wrong DDS, it may have to be recalled.
To recall a transferred claim:
•
Contact the office with jurisdiction using local procedures; and
•
Ask them to return jurisdiction.
For information regarding jurisdiction of cases, see Sending Initial Disability Insurance
Benefits (DIB), Freeze Claims to the Disability Determination Services (DDS) - DI 11010.255.
