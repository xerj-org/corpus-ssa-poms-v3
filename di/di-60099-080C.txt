C. MACADE ENB coding
For cases that the BSAS cannot process, the Office of Central Operations (OCO) centralized
unit codes the enclosure notice block (ENB) field on the history (HST) screen with
the BOND paragraphs. The manual adjustment credit and award data entry (MACADE) system
generates the following notices:
•
The initial and revised BOND offset notice;
•
The auxiliary suspense and reinstatement notice;
•
The BOND BRI adjustment notice
•
The beneficiary and MEF end of year reconciliation (EOYR) notice; and
•
The EOYR appeal notice.
NOTE: The ENB field begins with “G” for a complete BOND notice or “F” for an incomplete
BOND notice.
