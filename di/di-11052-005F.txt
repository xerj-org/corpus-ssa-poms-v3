F. Consider retroactivity of Medicare enrollment
ESRD cases are eligible for up to 12 months retroactivity. This may result in possible
premium arrearage. Consider this retroactive amount when determining entitlement dates
for R-HI and R-SMI. DI 11052.020B.1. discusses how to handle premium arrearage.
