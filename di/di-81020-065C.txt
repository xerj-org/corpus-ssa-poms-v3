C. Adding Medical Tracings and Color Images to the CEF
Images of medical evidence such as EKG tracings, visual field graphs and Pulmonary
Function Study (PFS) tracings, must allow the user to accurately evaluate the evidence
based on the Medical Listing specifications. To improve readability, scan in color:
•
Medical tracings (e.g., electrocardiogram, PFS); and
•
Documents containing color that is of adjudicative significance.
