D. Wechsler intelligence scale for children-revised (WISC-R) - (generally available since
January 1974)
Assesses a wide range of intellectual abilities in children ages 5 through 16. It
yields a Verbal, Performance, and Full Scale IQ. This is an improved technique because
it is more current in terms of normative data.
