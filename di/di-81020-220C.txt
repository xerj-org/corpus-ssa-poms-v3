C. eCDR Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) Development
In certain limited cases, which have a MINE diary, the DDS may process a continuance
based on the CPD evidence in file and contact with the individual. In these cases,
scan the relevant previous medical information from the CPD folder into the CEF. For
a list of the specific impairments in this category, see Medical Source Not Available
- DI
28040.125B.2.a.
