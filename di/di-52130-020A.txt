A. OPM/Public Disability Benefit (PDB) data exchange alerts – Due process
OPM/PDB data alerts, especially alert codes A and B, can result in PDB overpayments
and/or reductions to ongoing benefits. Therefore, it is important to remember that
OPM alerts are third party reports. Give the individual due process per GN 03001.025 prior to taking any reduction or overpayment action due to OPM/PDB alerts. For due
process notices, see NL 00703.511-NL 00703.513.
