D. Procedure for FO involvement in the mailer completion process
FO personnel may assist in the completion of this form if the beneficiary requests
help or the PC requests assistance.
The FO forwards signed SSA-455-OCR-SMs to the WBDOC for processing. The mailing address
for the WBDOC is provided at the top of the front side of Form SSA-455-OCR-SM. Note
that Title II and Title XVI mailers have different Post Office Box numbers.
The FO forwards hard paper SSA-455s to the servicing PC for processing.
