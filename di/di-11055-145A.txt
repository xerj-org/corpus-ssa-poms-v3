Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Routing the SSA-831 for a Certified Electronic Folder (CEF)
The field office (FO) does not mail/fax a copy of the SSA-831 (Disability Determination
and Transmittal) to WBDOC if the official folder is a CEF. All data needed for WBDOC
to key is available electronically.
