B. Component roles for establishing an interim EOD
1. Field office (FO)
When DDS establishes an interim EOD, the FO effectuates the claim and returns jurisdiction
of the claim to DDS. The FO also forwards any modular disability folders in their
possession, associated with the claim, to the DDS.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The DDS establishes an interim EOD when the claimant meets the criteria in DI 25501.270A.2. in this section. When the FO returns jurisdiction of the claim to the DDS, the adjudicator
undertakes additional development to determine a final EOD.
