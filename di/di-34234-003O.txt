SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34234.003 - Immune Listings from 07/02/93 to 04/13/97
Effective Dates: 06/11/2015 - Present | TN 1 (06-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434234003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

O. Any other manifestation(s) of HIV infection (including any listed in 114.08A-N,
but without the requisite findings, e.g., oral candidiasis not meeting the criteria
in 114.08F, diarrhea not meeting the criteria in 114.08J, or any other manifestation(s),
e.g., oral hairy leukoplakia, hepatomegaly), resulting in one of the following:
1. For children from birth to attainment of age 1 at least one of the criteria in
paragraphs A-E of 112.12;
2. For children age 1 to attainment of age 3 at least one of the appropriate age-group
criteria in paragraph B1 of 112.02; or
3. For children age 3 to attainment of age 18, at least two of the appropriate age-group
criteria in paragraph B2 of 112.02.
