A. Part B Black Lung Benefits (BLB)
Part B BLB is not workers’ compensation (WC) for offset purposes. However, a Part
B benefit could be offset as a public disability benefit (PDB). See details in DI 52125.020 - Determining Public Disability Benefit (PDB) for Part B Black Lung.
