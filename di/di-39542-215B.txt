B. Policy- contract considerations
The Contract will:
•
Include provisions that define a sound and complete procurement contract.
•
Identify the parties covered in the contract as well as the claimant population to be examined.
•
Specify the scope of work to be performed.
•
Specify procedures and criteria for terminating the contract, including a requirement that the contractor promptly
return all information gathered in the process of the contract.
•
Provide that the contractor maintains an appropriate record system for the services provided.
•
Provide that the contractor safeguards information provided by the DDS and the claimants.
•
Specify what functions may be subcontracted and the responsibilities of the subcontractor.
