DI 40101.035 Processing Center (PC) Project Agreements with the Disability Determination Services
(DDS) to Help Expedite Processing of Disability Claims/Workload Transfers
If the State has a Project Agreement with the Regional Office (RO) or Processing Centers
Disability Case Review (PC-DCR), the jurisdiction of cases that would belong to the
Disability Determination Services (DDS), are transferred from the DDS to the PC-DCR
named in the agreement. These agreements will specify the:
•
effective date,
•
period of time, and
•
case type being transmitted.
The RO notifies Field Offices (FO's) whenever a State has a Project Agreement that
requires the FO to route disability cases directly to PC-DPB.
For cases transferred within the Project Agreement to PC-DCR, jurisdiction ends when
a determination is made or the jurisdiction is transferred to the FO or DDS as a no
determination/transfer (ND/TR) case.
