DI 11005.006 Field Office (FO) Instructions for Claims Development and Processing for Military
Casualty (MC)/Wounded Warrior (WW) Cases
MC/WW cases are identified as priority cases under DI 23020.001 and are given priority processing through all levels of case development and adjudication.
These cases are tracked by the FO to ensure that the Disability Determination Services
(DDS) receives the case timely. Favorable DDS determinations will result in expedited
payment action.
