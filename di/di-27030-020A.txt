A. Affirmation of initial denial
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) prepares and releases a personalized notice
for affirmations of initial denials. The notice consists of a preprinted form notice
SSA-L1130-U2- (Supplemental Security Income Notice of Reconsideration – Disability)
and a personalized explanation. (See DI 27025.020 for preparation of the personalized explanation.) In concurrent Title II/Title XVI
claims, include paragraph 841 in the Title XVI notice when the SSA-L1130-U2 is not
preprinted. For additional instructions on paragraphs 841 and 842 see, NL 00708.100 – Paragraphs Used in Notices.
