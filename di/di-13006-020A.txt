SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13006.020 - Field Office (FO) Actions to Screen and Process an Alert for an Age-18 Redetermination Case
Effective Dates: 08/18/2022 - Present | TN 2 (08-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413006020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Screening out a Title XVI age-18 redetermination
Screen out a Title XVI age-18 redetermination if:
1.
The claim is pending a request to withdraw or has been withdrawn.
2.
There was a prior cessation and termination determination, but no action occurred
to terminate the case, see DI 13015.265.
3.
The recipient was not in PSY C__, E__, or M__ on the Supplemental Security Record
(SSR) in the month before the month the person attained age 18. Do not screen out
a Section 1619 case, which may be in payment status N01, see DI 13005.016. For additional instructions on processing cases in suspense payment status, see
DI 13005.020E; or
4.
Review of the claim folder (paper or electronic) shows that a disability determination
using the adult sequential evaluation of disability for initial claims was done after
the recipient attained age 18, see DI 13006.010A4.
