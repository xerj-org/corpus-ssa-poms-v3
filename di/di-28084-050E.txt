E. Item 28 date
If the MC or PC only signed a medical evaluation document(s), the DE will enter the
date the MC or PC signed that document(s). Otherwise, the MC or PC must enter the
date the SSA-832 or SSA-833 is signed. For MC or PC signature requirements, see DI 26510.090.
