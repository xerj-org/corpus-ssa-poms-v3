A. Policy - Who Has Authority to Sign
The head of the State Agency designated to act on behalf of the State or the organization
head in the direct line of authority above the designated State Agency is authorized
to sign the State Agency Report of Obligations for SSA Disability Programs, the SSA-4513,
report of actual expenditures. Where the designated State agency is a board, the executive
officer of that board may be considered the head of the State agency.
