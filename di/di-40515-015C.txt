C. Processing Center (PC) responsibilities in due process
•
Reviewers in ODIO-PSC/DCR and the Office of Quality Review (OQR) must ensure that
the DDS/FO follows appropriate guidelines for affording due process and that the file
or record is properly documented.
•
Reviewers must afford due process before benefits are stopped and must properly document
the file or record in those cases that are ceased or reversed to denial in ODIO-PSC/DCR
or the OQR.
