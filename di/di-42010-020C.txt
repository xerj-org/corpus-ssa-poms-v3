SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.020 - Completing Form SSA-831 in Favorable Administrative Law Judge Decisions (Initial Awards)
Effective Dates: 09/19/2024 - Present | TN 23 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Multiple entitlement scenarios
An ALJ may address an individuals entitlement to multiple classes of title II disability
benefits (e.g., DIB and CDB) in the same decision. When this occurs, prepare separate
Forms SSA-831 for each earnings record under which the individual claimed benefits.
Fax or upload the completed forms to the electronic folder and include printed copies
in the paper folder or folders as applicable.
When a primary and secondary benefit are involved (e.g., primary claimant (HA), and
child(C)), always process the primary award first.
NOTE:
Always include the cross-reference SSN and BIC in the Note field in eView or EP, when
adding the SSA-831 to the electronic folder.
1. DIB claim allowed; secondary claim denied
Prepare an allowance SSA-831 for the DIB award and a disallowance SSA-831 for the
secondary claim. Scan each form into the separate electronic folders for the respective
claims with notes indicating the cross-reference SSN and BIC and the disposition of
the other claim (favorable or unfavorable).
2. CDB claims on multiple records
We will not again decide the issue of disability if, in our prior final determination
or decision (and any subsequent continuance determination or decision), we have already
decided the individual is disabled for the time period covered by the new application
and the collateral estoppel criteria in DI 11011.001A are met. For example, adopting a favorable ALJ decision on one title II CDB claim
under the principle of collateral estoppel when the disabled child files another CDB
claim on a different SSN and meets all other factors of entitlement. See DI 11011.001 and DI 27515.001 for application of collateral estoppel.
For collateral estoppel in Social Security disability claims, processing instructions
in DI 11011.001E are generally applicable for Claims Specialists (CS) and Claims Technical Experts
(CTE), including those at the PC. Processing instructions in DI 27515.001D are generally applicable for Disability Examiners (DE) and Disability Processing
Specialists (DPS) at the Disability Determination Services (DDS) or Disability Case
Review (DCR).
NOTE:Certain administrative determinations in subsequent Social Security disability claims
do not require completion of a new Form SSA-831, e.g., application of collateral estoppel
when fully favorable for a new initial claim or reconsideration, and application of
res judicata when the conditions in DI 27516.001B are met for a new initial claim or reconsideration.
