A. Policy – overview of work incentive policies
The Ticket to Work Program is part of a comprehensive work incentive package. Every
SSA employee with public contact responsibilities must be prepared to offer accurate
information to individuals so that they can make sound decisions about work activity.
The work incentives other than the Ticket to Work Program are described in this section.
When conducting an interview, be alert to opportunities to encourage and inform SSA
beneficiaries who are working, or who are considering working, of all employment support
policies.
