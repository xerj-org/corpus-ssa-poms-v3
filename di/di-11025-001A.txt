SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11025.001 - Introduction to Disability Determinations for Entitlement to a Non-Disability Payment
Effective Dates: 03/04/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411025001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disabled minor child determination needed to continue entitlement to a parent
While a child’s benefits can continue to age 18, the parent’s benefit normally ends
when the youngest child attains age 16. If a child is disabled and in the parent’s
care, then the parent’s benefit can continue after the child attains age 16. SSA must
obtain a disability determination to establish that the child under age 18 is disabled.
