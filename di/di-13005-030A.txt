A. Introduction to personal contact
SSA recommends, but does not require personal contact in each CDR case. Providing
personal contact helps to ensure that individual have a full opportunity to present
their case. The FO should make personal contact with:
•
the individual; if there is no mental impairment;
•
the individual only or both the individual and the representative payee, if there
is one, when the individual has a physical impairment only; or
•
the representative payee only if there is one, when the individual has a mental impairment
that is legally incompetent.
One of the purposes of the personal contact is to alleviate the individual or representative
payees apprehension about the CDR by providing a thorough explanation of the CDR process,
including the individuals role in the process.
