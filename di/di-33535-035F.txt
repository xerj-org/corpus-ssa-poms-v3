F. Peabody picture vocabulary test-revised) (PPVT-R) - (generally available since January
1981)
An individually administered measure of hearing vocabulary for those aged 2 1 /2 through
adulthood. It provides a quick estimate of verbal ability and scholastic aptitude.
Because this revised test is more current in terms of normative data, it is a better
technique.
