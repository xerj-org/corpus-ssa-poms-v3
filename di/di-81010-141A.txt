SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.141 - Processing Electronic Informal Remands
Effective Dates: 07/16/2012 - Present | TN 5 (11-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010141 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Overview
Informal Remand (IR) cases from the Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR),
Hearing Offices (HO) are no longer Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) exclusions. However,
Field Office (FO), Disability Determination Services (DDS), and Office of Quality
Performance (OQP) users will not have access to this feature until the relevant state
DDS legacy system is upgraded to release 13.0. Users will be notified on a state-by-state
basis when this feature is activated. Once activated, EDCS will add new alerts to
the EDCS Actions Page. DDSs that have the necessary upgrade to their case processing
software can process HO IRs electronically.
NOTE: FO IRs remain CEF exclusions. Follow current procedures for processing paper FO
IRs in DI 27520.005B. - DDS Action upon Receipt of Informal Remand Cases).
