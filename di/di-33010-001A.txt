SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.001 - General Review of Claims Folder and Preparation of In-House Folder in the Disability Hearing Unit (DHU)
Effective Dates: 08/25/2011 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Pre-hearing (PH) transfers the case to the DHU
When the Disability Determination Services (DDS) is unable to make a favorable determination
after the PH review, the DDS closes the case as a No Determination (ND) with a reason
of "PH Transfer to Disability Hearing (DH)." An electronic folder interface (EFI)
download changes the claim to "DH" level.
