DI 23020.055 Disability Determination Services (DDS) Claims Instructions for Identifying, Developing,
and Processing Veteran’s Affairs 100 Percent Permanent and Total (VAPT) Disability
Cases
This section provides instructions for processing cases involving any veteran of a
military service who has a VAPT disability compensation rating and is alleging a physical
or mental impairment, regardless of how the impairment occurred. VAPT cases are identified
as priority cases under DI 23020.001 and are given priority processing through all levels of case development and adjudication.
Receipt and assignment of VAPT cases are expedited at the DDS.
