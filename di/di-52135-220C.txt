C. Dependency allowance
The State pays a dependency allowance for dependent children less than 18 years of
age. Handicapped children over 18 may also qualify for the allowance. This dependency
allowance is limited to five dependents and is equal to the greater of $10 or 7 percent
of the worker's benefit rate. Dependency allowances are determined at the start of
the benefit year and remain the same for the entire period.
To determine if the dependent allowance is offsettable follow DI 52150.035A.
