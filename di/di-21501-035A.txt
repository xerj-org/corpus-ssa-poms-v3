A. Blindness
An individual is considered blind if he has central vision acuity of 20/200 or less
in the better eye with correcting glasses or a field defect in which the peripheral
field has contracted to such an extent that the widest diameter of visual field subtended
an angular distance of no greater than 20 degrees.
