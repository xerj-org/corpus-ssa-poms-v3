SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.010 - Assembly and Routing of Folders for Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Hearing
Effective Dates: 07/16/2010 - Present | TN 3 (07-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Folder assembly
File the request for hearing and related materials in the proper folder order. Refer
to DI 81005.050, DI 81010.090, DI 81010.125, DI 81010.135, DI 81010.150, and DI 81020.035, for electronic folders. Refer to DI 70005.001 - DI 70005.005 and DI 70010.001 for instructions on the modular disability folder procedures.
NOTE: An appeal taken on a claim filed in a two-part folder will be kept in the two-part
folder.
