E. DDS identifies dire need case during development
Take the following steps when dire need is identified after transfer from the FO:
•
Flag the case as dire need in eView and DCPS, see DI 81020.085. If the case is an EDCS exclusion case, attach a dire need flag to the front of the
paper folder.
View In PDF
•
Notify the FO by telephone, AR, or other electronic means to initiate simultaneous
development.
