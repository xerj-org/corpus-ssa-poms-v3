SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.015 - Claims Under Different Titles – Common Issue at the ALJ Hearing Level
Effective Dates: 11/04/2002 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If a claim at the ALJ hearing level shares a common issue with a new claim under the
other title or with a claim pending appeal, the claims are generally combined. Claims
are not combined if either the claimant objects or the ALJ does not agree that the claims
should be joined.
