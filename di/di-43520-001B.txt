SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 43520.001 - SSA-423 Request for Services and Reply (Foreign Disability Case)
Effective Dates: 08/09/2023 - Present | TN 2 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0443520001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Completion of the SSA-423
IDU uses an electronic assistance request to complete and submit the SSA-423. The information populates from Disability Case Processing System (DCPS). However,
individual case facts will require IDU to select or complete certain fields, as described
in DI 43520.001B.1 through DI 43520.001B.8 in this section.
For more information on electronic assistance requests (AR), see DI 81020.100.
1. Social Security Administration (SSA) appropriation Number
Enter the SSA appropriation number applicable to the fiscal year during which the
expense actually incurred. The SSA appropriation number for the fiscal year, October
1, 2022, through September 30, 2023, is 283 8704. Each succeeding year the third digit
of the SSA appropriation number should be changed to correspond to the final digit
of the current fiscal year, (e.g., for fiscal year 2024, the appropriation number
will be 284 8704).
2. Allotment and organizational code
Enter the allotment code 4005113, followed by the object class code for Contracts
for Foreign Claims Services 2533 (4005113-2533). These numbers do not change with
the fiscal year.
3. Consular code
Enter the consular code associated with country and FBU.
4. Type of request
Select the type of development being requested from FBU as follows:
•
Medical and nonmedical records
•
Consultative Examinations
•
Other
IDU may select more than one type of development on the SSA-423. DCPS will include the barcode specific to the type of evidence requested on the
Reply. If there are multiple types of development, DCPS will default to the barcode
for MER (001). IDU (or the original requesting office) will ensure the evidence received
is separated accordingly and included in the correct section of the certified electronic
folder (CEF).
For more information on:
•
Organization of documents in the CEF, see DI 81001.005E.
•
Documents types, see DI 81020.040.
•
Moving documents in the CEF, see DI 81020.065D.
5. Allegations
Include the claimant's allegations. The allegations populate from DCPS. IDU should
review the allegations for completeness and correct any errors or misspellings.
6. Records requested
Include:
•
The name of each medical and nonmedical source(s) needed.
•
The type of evidence needed, and dates requested or a statement, "Please provide all
evidence from MM/DD/YYYY to MM/DD/YYYY."
•
The request for a medical opinion, if applicable.
•
The name of any questionnaire(s) (e.g., Physical Report, Cardiac Questionnaire, Speech
and Language Questionnaire, SSA-5665 (Teacher Questionnaire), SSA-5666 (Request for Administrative Information), etc.) for the medical and nonmedical source(s)
to complete, if applicable.
•
The name of any other enclosure(s) (e.g., SSA-827 (Authorization To Disclose Information To The Social Security Administration), etc.).
For more information on development of CEs, see DI 43510.020.
7. Examination requested
Include:
•
The type of examination and testing needed, as applicable.
•
The request for a medical opinion, if applicable.
•
The name of any questionnaire(s) (e.g., Range of Motion Chart, Muscle Evaluation,
etc.) for the CE provider(s) to complete, if applicable.
•
The name of any other enclosure(s) (e.g., CE guidelines, SSA-827, etc.).
NOTE: FBU will review the SSA-3368 (Disability Report - Adult), SSA-3820 (Disability Report - Child), SSA-3441 (Disability Report - Appeal), or SSA-454 (Continuing Disability Review Report) in the CEF to obtain the address for each source
listed on the SSA-423.
For more information on development of medical evidence, see DI 43510.015.
8. Other
Include an explanation of other development needed, including, but not limited to:
•
Questionnaire(s) (e.g., SSA-3369 (Work History Report), SSA-3373 (Function Report - Adult), SSA-3380 (Function Report - Adult - Third Party), SSA-3385 (Report of Adult Functioning - Employer), etc.) for the claimant, parent of a minor
child, legal guardian, third party, or employer to complete.
•
Assistance to the claimant.
•
Verification of foreign medical source's qualifications.
•
Field investigation(s).
9. Enclosures
FBU will obtain any questionnaires that are available in inForm. Otherwise, IDU will select enclosures for any questionnaire(s) or other documents
not available in inForm. DCPS will upload these questionnaires and documents to CEF with the SSA-423.
a. Consultative examinations
Examples of enclosures (not available in inForm) for CE development may include:
•
Copies of medical and nonmedical evidence for background.
•
Questionnaires for completion by the CE provider(s).
•
CE guidelines for the type of examination or testing.
•
SSA-827 signed by or on behalf of the claimant.
b. Medical and nonmedical records
Examples of enclosures (not available in inForm) for medical and nonmedical records development may include:
•
Questionnaires for completion by the medical or nonmedical source(s).
•
SSA-827 signed by or on behalf of the claimant.
c. Other
Examples of enclosures (not available in inForm) for other development may include:
•
Questionnaires for completion by the claimant, parent of a minor child, legal guardian,
third party, or employer.
•
SSA-827 signed by or on behalf of the claimant, if applicable.
