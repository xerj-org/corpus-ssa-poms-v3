D. Eligibility for BOND
The Office of Research, Demonstration, and Employment Support (ORDES) provided a list
of one million beneficiaries to Abt Associates (Abt), the contractor tasked with designing
and implementing BOND based on the requirements outlined in DI 60099.010.
