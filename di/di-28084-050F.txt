F. Items 30 and 30A - physician or medical specialist name and specialty code
1. When to complete Items 30 and 30A
The DE (or the MC/PC, if signing the SSA-832 or SSA-833) must follow these procedures
to complete Items 30 and 30A. For the MC or PC signature requirements, see DI 26510.090.
2. Procedure for completing Items 30 and 30A
a. Item 30
Type or print the MCs or PCs name.
b. Item 30A
Enter the MCs or PCs medical specialty code from the list below. If a MC has multiple
specialties (internist and rheumatologist), enter the code most closely related to
the case:
