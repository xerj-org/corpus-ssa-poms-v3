Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510180 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If an individual is denied Medicare coverage and subsequently files a request for
reconsideration or hearing, the FO obtains an SSA-561-U2 or HA-501-U5 and any additional
evidence/material the claimant offers and sends it to ODIO/ PSC for processing.
