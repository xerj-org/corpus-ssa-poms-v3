SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10520.001 - Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE)
Effective Dates: 03/25/2004 - Present | TN 17 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410520001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. IRWE Paid Before December 1, 1980
Impairment-related work expenses (IRWE) paid before December 1, 1980, are deductible
in determining countable earnings only to the extent that such expenses exceeded what
would have been work-related expenses if the person did not have a disability (e.g.,
the amount by which the use of special transportation exceeded what the person, if
not disabled, would have had to pay for regular transportation to and from work).
Expenses paid before December 1, 1980, for items or services which the person required
whether or not
they
worked would not be deductible.
Example 1:
A person with a disability could get to work only by cab which costs $7.00 a day for
the round trip. The difference of $5.20 a day between the cost of normal transportation
(bus at $1.80 a day) and the special transportation of $7.00 could be deducted from
their wages to obtain countable earnings.
Example 2:
A person with a disability who managed a small book store required expensive medication
to keep a serious heart condition under control. Since this medication was required
whether or not they worked, its cost was not deductible in calculating their countable
earnings.
