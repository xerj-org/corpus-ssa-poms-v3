A. Policy for Duplicate, Non-Duplicate and Composite Evidence
We determine whether the submitted material is duplicate evidence, non-duplicate evidence,
or composite evidence comparing it to the evidence in the record. Duplicate evidence
is non-essential material for purposes of disability determination or continuing disability
reviews. However, non-duplicate evidence may be essential or non-essential, depending
on various factors.
NOTE: Disability Determination Services (DDS) staff can refer to DI 20503.001 Documenting the Disability Folder for the definitions and procedures on how to handle
essential and nonessential material.
