A. Introduction to statutory benefit continuation during medical cessation appeal
Congress enacted a benefit continuation statute to help prevent financial hardship
to beneficiaries who appeal a medical cessation determination.
Operationally, the field office (FO) is the focal point and is responsible for processing
statutory benefit continuation cases after the adverse medical determination has been
made.
