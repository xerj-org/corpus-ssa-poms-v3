DI 25220.010 Meets or Medical Equals
Step 3 of the sequential evaluation process for children under Title XVI requires
a determination whether the child's impairment(s) meets or medically or functionally
equals the listings. This section provides the policy for evaluating whether the child's
impairment(s) meets or is medically equivalent in severity to a listing,
For information on functional equivalence, see DI
25225.001.
