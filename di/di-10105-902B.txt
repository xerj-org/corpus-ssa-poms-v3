SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.902 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary or Survivor Applications Filed During the Benefit Continuation Period (BCP) - Disability Benefit Equals or Exceeds Auxiliary or Survivor Benefit
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105902 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Field Office (FO) role
The FO will:
1.
Develop the evidence required as in any other auxiliary or survivor claim.
2.
Prepare an A101 Automated Determination of Award :
•
Enter the amount of the disability benefit in the smaller actuarially reduced monthly
benefit amount (“SAMBA”) on the MBEN screen and 0.00 in the benefit amount.
•
Enter the claimant’s own Social Security number (SSN) in the “Cross Reference SSN”
entry on the BENE screen, with the applicable status code “A”, or, in the case of
a DWB or CDB the SSN on which the disability benefit is being paid and the applicable
stat code.
•
Annotate the Remarks portion of the BCRN screen, “Do Not Process” and “BCP claim-Do
not process Auxiliary (Survivor) Claims Unless Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB)
Cessation is Affirmed.” Forward the A101 to the processing center (PC)
3.
Adjudicate the A101. Modernized Claims System (MCS) will establish a control and transfer
ownership to the PC.
