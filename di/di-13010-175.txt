DI 13010.175 Completing the SSA-899-U2 (Continuing Disability Review)
Use the SSA-899-U2 form to document favorable work-related determinations that affect
a beneficiary’s continuing entitlement. For an exhibit of the SSA-899-U2 form, see
OS 15020.205. eWork prepares this form for you. The instructions in this section are
for manual preparation of the form.
