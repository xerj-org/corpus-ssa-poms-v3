SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13006.030 - Field Office (FO) Procedures after the Disability Determination Services (DDS) Makes A Determination on an Age-18 Redetermination Case
Effective Dates: 11/16/2021 - Present | TN 1 (11-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413006030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Unfavorable determinations and cessation dates
Disability ceases as of the date of the age-18 redetermination decision. Eligibility
ends the last day of the second month following the month of cessation. During the
period between the month of cessation and the last month of payment, the disabled
individual must otherwise be eligible for benefits. Non-disability actions (for example,
income and resource changes, etc.) are still appropriate.
NOTE: If the case involves a DDS failure to cooperate (FTC) or whereabouts unknown (WU)
determination, see DI 28075.005 and DI 28075.007.
