SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.065 - Medical Review Diary Followups
Effective Dates: 06/30/2026 - Present | TN 25 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. First followup alert
The first follow-up alert will be generated on all medical review diaries 180 days
after the initial selection of the case for review, unless:
•
a cessation is effectuated (title II), or
•
a cessation diary is established (title XVI).
2. Subsequent follow-up alerts
Subsequent alerts will be generated at 90-day intervals and routed to the DDS location
to alert the component that:
•
the CDR is past due, and
•
all actions should be expedited.
3. Monitoring of alerts
Central Processing will monitor the alerts until all actions have been completed.
Central Processing will:
•
contact the DDS by phone or memorandum requesting the status of past due CDRs.
•
send a listing of the third and sixth follow-ups to Disability Adjudication (DA) to
alert it to CDRs, which are backlogged in DA.
