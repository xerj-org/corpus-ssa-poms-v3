E. SSA-3820 (Disability Report - Child)
This report is used for initial Title XVI disability or blindness claims for children
under age 18 (See DI 11005.030 for additional instructions. The form incorporates the information collected on the
SSA-3881 (Medical and Job Worksheet). Therefore, the SSA-3881 is no longer used in
initial claims; however, the SSA-3881 is still used for appeals.)
