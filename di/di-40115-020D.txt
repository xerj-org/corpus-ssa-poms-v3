D. Onset established prior to date first insured status is first met
When the DDS establishes an onset date prior to the insured status first met date
•
Enter the correct EOD in item 15A of the SSA-831 to show the first date that both
insured status and medical disability are met;
•
On the SSA-831 the PC will enter remarks in Item 34: “Item 15A is based on the date
first insured. Impairment was of a disabling severity, for purposes of the duration
requirement, as of [date]”.
