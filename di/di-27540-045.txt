DI 27540.045 Reopenings, Revisions, and Other Related Special Situations Including Informal Remands
The specific due process notice requirements for felony-related impairments, erroneous
notice/payment cases, conflicting determinations in disability/blindness common-issue
claims, and fully favorable Title XVI informal remand situations are detailed in DI 27520.001 through DI 27520.035.
