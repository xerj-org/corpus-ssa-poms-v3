B. Procedure – How to get to the QCDR Screen
To get to the QCDR screen.
•
Select 32, DISABILITY CONTROL FILE, from SSA's main menu (MAIN) screen.
•
Select 1 on the MCDR screen with the beneficiary's own SSN or the entitlement SSN
and BIC.
•
Select (type of CDR action 1 through 6) the appropriate action to be queried on the
ECDR.
