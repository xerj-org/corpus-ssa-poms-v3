D. Procedure – Completing the MCDR Screen
Use the MCDR screen to determine the action to be taken and the beneficiary on whom
to take the action. Complete the SELECT filed with the appropriate selection from
1 to 15, depending on the action being taken. Enter the beneficiary's own SSN or the
claim number and BIC for the beneficiary. If accessing the Query or Verify Earnings
screens enter the Earnings Beginning date.
