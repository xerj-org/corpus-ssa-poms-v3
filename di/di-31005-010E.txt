SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31005.010 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Procedures for Handling Inquiries from Public Officials Other than United States (U.S) Congress Members
Effective Dates: 04/15/2013 - Present | TN 2 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431005010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Responding to an inquiry when the claim is in another office
Inform the inquirer the DDS forwarded the claim to another office and take the following
action(s):
•
forward the inquiry to the office with jurisdiction of the claim for a response;
•
advise the inquirer to contact the servicing FO if he or she has further questions
about the claim;
•
for electronic claims, add an alert to the electronic folder according to the instructions
in DI 81020.080;
•
for paper claims, complete an SSA-408 (Route Slip) and annotate with the comment:
Inquiry Action Needed.; and
•
forward a copy of the reply and the inquiry to the office in possession of the claim.
