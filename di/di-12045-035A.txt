SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.035 - Same Title Claims – New Claim or Appeal Filed, Prior Claim Pending at the Hearing Level
Effective Dates: 04/05/2012 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Claimant files a new claim or appeal while a prior claim under the same title is pending
before the Administrative Law Judge (ALJ).
NOTE: If a claimant files a new disability application and has a prior disability claim
for the same title and benefit type pending at any level of administrative review, see DI
51501.001 – Procedural Change for Subsequent Disability Applications Effective July 28, 2011.
