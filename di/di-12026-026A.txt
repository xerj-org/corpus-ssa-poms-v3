A. Overview of the SSA-769-U4
The individual has the right to request a change in the time or place of a scheduled
disability hearing; however, the disability hearing unit (DHU) decides whether to
reschedule the hearing. The SSA-769-U4 allows the individual or an appointed representative to request a change in the time
or place of a scheduled disability hearing or both.
