G. FERS Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA)
If the FERS is offsettable, the FERS COLAs are also offsettable. FERS COLAs are separate
from and generally lower than CSRS COLAs.
For FERS disability recipients under age 62, no COLA is paid for the first twelve
months. After the first twelve months, OPM applies the CSRS COLA to any portion that
is due to CSRS employment and the FERS COLA to the remainder. Therefore, merely applying
the standard FERS COLA to the previous year’s FERS benefit will not yield the correct Federal PDB amount. Verify the FERS amounts by contacting the individual or OPM directly whenever possible.
