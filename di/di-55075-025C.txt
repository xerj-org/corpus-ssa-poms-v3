C. Procedure – handling inquiries about payment disputes
When a question arises about an EN or a cost reimbursement payment decision, field
components should follow the procedures in DI 55060.001C.2. and DI 55060.001C.3.
NOTE: Field components have no direct role in the dispute resolution process involving
a payment issue. However, FOs and PCs staff may get involved indirectly if a payment
dispute involves a question about a beneficiary's/recipient's right to benefits.
