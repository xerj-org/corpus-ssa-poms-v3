J. Can we establish the existence of a disabling impairment prior to the date of
the evidence that shows the cancer satisfies the criteria of a listing? Yes. We will consider factors such as:
1. The type of cancer and its location.
2. The extent of involvement when the cancer was first demonstrated.
3. Your symptoms.
