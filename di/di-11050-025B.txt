B. Filing a New Initial Non-Disability Claim - Freeze Previously Completed
If EC or ICERS shows that a joint RRB-SSA freeze has been established, the FO:
•
Takes the initial application,
•
Secures all proofs, and
•
Sends the completed RIB, auxiliary, or survivor claim to the servicing PSC via A101
for the necessary award. If the claim is a denial, process through EC or MACADE.
