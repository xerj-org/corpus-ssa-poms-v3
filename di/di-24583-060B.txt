SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24583.060 - Additional Guidelines for Using Psychological Tests to Evaluate Mental Disorders in Children
Effective Dates: 05/26/2023 - Present | TN 5 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424583060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When do these additional guidelines for children apply?
Follow these additional guidelines for children when evaluating standardized tests
for a child who has not attained age 18. Use the person’s chronological age when determining
whether these guidelines apply.
