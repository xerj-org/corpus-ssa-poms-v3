SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.902 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary or Survivor Applications Filed During the Benefit Continuation Period (BCP) - Disability Benefit Equals or Exceeds Auxiliary or Survivor Benefit
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105902 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Filing considerations
A disabled number holder (NH), Disabled Widow’s Benefit (DWB) or Childhood Disability
Benefit (CDB) at any age may wish to file for an auxiliary or survivor benefit while
receiving benefit continuation payments. As in RIB, the Month of Election (MOEL) considerations
are similar. Explain to the applicant that while he/she is entitled to the benefit
applied for, he/she cannot receive it while receiving benefit continuation payments.
