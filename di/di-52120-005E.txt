E. Verifying WC:
If the individual cannot supply the necessary information there are other methods
of verification.
1. The Carrier
Contact with the WC carrier is the preferred method of verification if the carrier
is known.
2. Alabama Department of Industrial Relations
•
Available information may be limited.
•
All requests for information from this agency should be made through the servicing
Field Office (program centers would use a PCACS Request for Assistance, not direct
contact). This is because this agency charges a fee for each inquiry. Alabama Field
Offices already have procedures in place to pay for the evidence when required.
