F. Appeals of COBRA only denials
If a claimant wants to appeal a dis-allowance, take the request for reconsideration
and obtain the required appeal documentation (SSA-561, SSA-3441 etc.). Forward the
documentation to the appropriate DDS or ODO DCR.
However, if the deadline for notification to the insurance carrier closed or has passed,
the individual may have difficulty persuading the carrier to allow the additional
coverage. Advise the individual to contact the Department of Labor (DOL) for assistance.
NOTE: If you receive a request for a hearing on a COBRA denial, follow existing procedures
to route the appeal request to the Office of Hearings Operation (OHO). Advise the
individual to contact the DOL for assistance, to avoid missing the deadline for notifying
the insurance carrier.
