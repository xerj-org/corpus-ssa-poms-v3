C. Viewing EDCS case technical reactivation information
1. EDCS
Effective with the reactivation of FO determined cases, the Claims Action screen in
EDCS displays the:
•
Reactivated claim(s) in “Pending” status;
•
Previous FO Determination under the “Determination/Disposition/Decision” column;
•
Claim(s) type and claim status as the case is processed;
•
Most recent determination when claim(s) has multiple FO determinations.
2. Use eView to see previous FO determinations
To view previous FO determinations, access the Case Data Tab in eView. The previous
FO determination is located under Record of Change.
