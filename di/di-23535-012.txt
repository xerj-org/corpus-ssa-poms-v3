DI 23535.012 Disability Determination Services (DDS) Responsibilities in Presumptive Disability
(PD) Cases Involving an Allegation of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection
or Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS)
For details about field office (FO) responsibilities in presumptive disability (PD)
cases involving an allegation of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) or acquired immunodeficiency
