C. What to Scan
Ensure that all disability documents received from external sources are scanned and
indexed to the CEF except for those noted in Onsite Scanning Procedures what not to
scan - DI 81020.050B in this section.
Follow local operating procedures to determine when documents are locally scanned
by DDS or sent to the contract scanner.
