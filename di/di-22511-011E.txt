E. Procedure -- Mail Requests to the Claimant or Collateral Source
1. Request to Claimants
When requesting information from the claimant by letter, follow the policy and procedures
in DI 23007.005 and DI 22505.014.
2. Request to Collateral Sources
When requesting information from collateral sources by letter, make a reasonable effort
to obtain the requested information. When the collateral source does not respond within
10 calendar days from the date of the initial, written request, follow up once by
telephone or letter. Give the collateral source 10 additional calendar days to respond.
The file must be documented to show that a reasonable effort has been made to obtain
the requested evidence.
