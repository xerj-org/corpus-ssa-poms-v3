DI 28087.050 Individual Moves Out of State
PROCEDURE
An individual may move out of the State of jurisdiction before the case is released
by the DDS. In this event, if referral is considered appropriate, send copies of the
forms and material referred to in DI 28087.030 to the DDS in the State in which the individual now lives. Enter under “Remarks”
on the SSA-1407-U4 or SSA-3312 “Claimant moved to your State--please consider referral
to VR agency.”
NOTE: DDS address may be found in the Service Area Directory. DDS addresses may also be found in DI 33500.000 ff.
