C. Federal Mine Safety and Health Act (FMSHA)
The OWCP Division of Coal Mine Workers' Compensation (DCMWC) administers the Black
Lung Benefits Program under FMSHA. DOL pays Part B Black Lung benefits and oversees
the Part C Black Lung program. See DI 52115.015 Federal Mine Safety and Health Act.
