SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.035 - The MIP Diary
Effective Dates: 05/11/2009 - Present | TN 13 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Set the MIP (3-year) diary when the current determination is made for individuals:
•
who may improve and be able to perform work if an adult or no longer result in marked
and severe functional limitations if a child, but
•
for whom, based on the individual case facts, cessation under the MIRS is not probable
within 3 years of adjudication.
