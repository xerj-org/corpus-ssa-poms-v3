C. Attorney fees
1.
Attorney fees are determined on individual case basis and are usually a lien upon
any workers’ compensation benefits due the claimant.
2.
Fees must be approved by the Disability Compensation Division, Department of Labor
and Industrial Relations, or by the appellate Board or court deciding an appeal.
