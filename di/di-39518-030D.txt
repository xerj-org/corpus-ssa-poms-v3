D. Operating Policy - Public Liability
1. Not Chargeable to SSA
The cost of awards, judgements, or settlements
arising from injury to person or property, is not chargeable to SSA, except as provided
for in DI 39518.025ff.
2. Chargeable to SSA
a. Protection for Injury to Person or Property
An appropriate share
of the cost to the State agency of
protecting the State agency against financial responsibility for injury to person or property is properly charged to the SSA disability program.
EXAMPLE: Such expenses are a proper charge when they are in the form of:
•
premiums for public liability, or
•
property damage insurance.
b. Motor Vehicles
A share
of the public liability and property damage insurance costs can be charged if:
•
the motor vehicle is used in the proper discharge of State agency official business, and
•
the cost is proportionate
to the share of all motor vehicle travel of personnel devoted to activities concerned
with making disability determinations.
