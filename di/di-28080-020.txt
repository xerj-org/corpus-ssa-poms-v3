DI 28080.020 Provide a Predetermination Notice to Auxiliaries for Title II and Concurrent Title
II/Title XVI Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Disability Determination Services (DDS) has jurisdiction for providing advance notice
to auxiliaries at the time of the CDR. The processing center (PC) has jurisdiction
for subsequent or unprocessed auxiliary claims. For instructions, see DI 13005.065.
