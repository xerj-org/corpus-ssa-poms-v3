F. The scannable mailer form
We use an optically scannable variation of the mailer form, the SSA-455-OCR-SM, most frequently. Mailer recipients return the scannable mailer forms to the Wilkes-Barre Direct Operations Center (WBDOC). (For the WBDOC process, see DI 13004.005E. (in this section)). Since October 1995, we use the scannable mailer form SSA-455-OCR-SM most of the
time, although the processing centers (PCs) or field offices (FOs) may use the standard
“hard paper” form.
