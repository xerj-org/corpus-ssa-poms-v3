C. Operating Policy - Professional and Clerical Staff
1. General state responsibility
a.
The State is responsible for establishing positions, recruitment, selection and tenure,
compensation, and staff development policies.
b.
The disability staff will consist of the necessary medical, professional, technical,
stenographic, and clerical personnel, including a full-time disability supervisor
and intermediate supervisor, where necessary.
2. Annual Workyear Plan
The Disability Determination Service (DDS) must submit an annual workyear plan to
Disability Adjudication (DA) and receive SSA approval.
NOTE: If a State is budgeted below the national production per workyear target, central
office (CO)
concurrence is required for any hiring exceptions.
3. Clerical Operations
The DDS must:
a.
Make certain that sufficient workforce is available
for all clerical operations;
b.
Study clerical productivity
to determine workpower needs; and
c.
In contracts for clerical functions, require
that an outside vendor provide expeditious delivery
of completed work.
