[BOND Caption 1] Benefit Offset National Demonstration (BOND)
[WDS017]
The BOND project offers qualified individuals a more generous treatment of earnings
than under the current rules. A qualified individual is provided an opportunity to
work and earn over $*F1 (BOND non-blind yearly amount) or $*F2 (BOND blind yearly
amount) ,and benefit from having only $1 of benefits withheld for every $2 earned
over this amount. Under current Social Security rules, the same qualified individual
working and earning over $*F3 (BOND non-blind yearly amount) or $*F4 (BOND blind yearly
amount), generally has his or her benefits stopped.
