D. Clerical Error in the Computation or Re-computation
This refers to any clerical error (on the part of either SSA or DDS) that resulted
in an incorrect payment, including clerical errors in a determination or decision
which led to an incorrect payment.
For further discussion of reopening on this basis under title II and title XVI, respectively,
see Reopenings - Clerical Error (GN 04010.010), and Title XVI Administrative Finality General Reopening Policies--Clerical Error
(SI 04070.010F.5.b.).
NOTE: A title II wholly or partially unfavorable determination or decision may be reopened
at any time to correct a clerical error, if the net result is more favorable to the
claimant. See (DI 27505.001A.4.b.).
