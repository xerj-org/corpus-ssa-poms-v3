C. Procedures for completing Forms SSA-832 or SSA-833
For Title XVI cases, obtain a Supplemental Security Record (SSR) query with segments
disability information (DISB), conversion data (CONV), and mailing or payment address
(ADDR) to determine whether the case is concurrent (Title II and Title XVI) or a non-rollback
conversion. For Title II cases, obtain a Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) query to
determine other entitlement to benefits, disabled individual's current address, etc.
To complete the form, enter the following:
•
an "X" on the form (SSA-832 or SSA-833) for items that require completion of a block;
and
•
an "X” in the block on the left side of the line where a date entry is required.
