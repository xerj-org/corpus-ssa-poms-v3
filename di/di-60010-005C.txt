SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 60010.005 - Youth Transition Exploration Demonstration (YTED) Overview
Effective Dates: 02/06/2026 - Present | TN 6 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0460010005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Field Office (FO) Representatives
YTED requires the support of SSA Field Offices (FOs). SSA technicians should:
•
Assist with filing applications for SSI
•
Implement YTED policy waivers
•
Input work start and stop dates
•
Enter wages with exclusion (see DI
60010.015B)
•
Answer questions related to the YTED policy waivers
•
Provide general program information (see DI 60010.010)
