C. How we evaluate objective medical evidence
We require objective medical evidence from an AMS to establish the existence of a
medically determinable impairment (MDI) at step 2 of the sequential evaluation process.
Once an MDI is established, we also consider objective medical evidence for all other
findings in a claim, as appropriate.
For information on establishing an MDI, see DI 22505.003 Evidence from an Acceptable Medical Source (AMS).
