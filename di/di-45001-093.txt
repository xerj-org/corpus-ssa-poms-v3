DI 45001.093 R-HI/R-SMI Coverage Continuance—Dialysis Resumed After Transplant
We have reviewed current medical evidence concerning your kidney condition. The evidence
shows that you continue to meet the eligibility requirements for Medicare coverage
based on kidney disease. If you receive another kidney transplant, or there is any
significant change in your condition or treatment, please notify any social security
office promptly.
If you have any questions about your Medicare coverage, contact any social security
office. If you visit an office, please take this notice with you.
