SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11020.015 - Technical Requirements and Onset Development for Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB)
Effective Dates: 07/29/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411020015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Initial Entitlement Cases
In most Title II childhood disability cases, a precise onset date is not necessary
as long as disability is found to have begun prior to the attainment of age 22. A
precise onset is necessary to determine the first month of entitlement when all the
following conditions are met:
•
The application is filed before attainment of age 23;
•
Disability began within 12 months of the date the application is filed for CDB-D claims
and within 6 months of the date the application is filed for CDB-R claims; and
•
Disability is established after attainment of age 18.
