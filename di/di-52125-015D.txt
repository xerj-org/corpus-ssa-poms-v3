D. When coverage determination cannot be made
If it is still unclear, after full development, whether the disability is “based on”
covered State or local employment, take the following actions:
1.
Submit the case to the processing center (PC) and include all development.
2.
Obtain a repayment statement. If the claimant refuses to sign a statement, document
the file. See instructions in DI 52140.001 - Field Office (FO) Responsibilities for Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability
Benefit (WC/PDB) Offset.
3.
Do not impose offset unless some other offsettable workers' compensation/public disability
benefit (WC/PDB) payment is also being received.
NOTE:
: If the file is not available to the PC for review, request the information from
the appropriate Information Retrieval Group (IRG) unit. For information about specific
State PDBs, see DI 52135.001 - State Specific Public Disability Benefit (PDB) Procedures.
