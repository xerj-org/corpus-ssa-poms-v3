DI 43530.001 General
Awareness of the following sections of the Foreign Affairs Manual (FAM) will help
the disability examiner work within the framework of the Department of State. Note
that these references are reproduced verbatim from the most current version of the
FAM and may not reflect subsequent policy or procedural changes.
