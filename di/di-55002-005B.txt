SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55002.005 - Who Is Eligible For a Ticket?
Effective Dates: 04/13/2023 - Present | TN 4 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455002005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy – two general ticket eligibility requirements
1. Age 18 through 64
Only an individual who is age 18 or older and has not attained age 65 is eligible
for a ticket.
2. Entitled to or eligible for a disability/blindness-based benefit
Also, the individual must be either:
•
a title II disability beneficiary (i.e., a disabled worker, a disabled surviving spouse,
a disabled surviving divorced spouse, or a childhood disability beneficiary); or
•
a title XVI recipient eligible for SSI benefits based on disability or blindness.
