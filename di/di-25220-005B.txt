SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25220.005 - Determining If a Child Has a Severe Impairment(s)
Effective Dates: 01/31/2001 - Present | TN 3 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425220005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Consider the effect(s) of the impairment(s) in conjunction with the factors described
in DI 25210.001 ff., on the child's functioning to decide whether the impairment(s) is severe per
the policy in DI 25220.005A.
If the child does not have a severe impairment(s), find that the child is not disabled.
Explain your determination in Section I of the SSA-538 (see DI 25230.001 ff.).
If the child has a severe impairment(s), consider whether it meets or medically equals
the severity of a listed impairment per DI 25220.010 ff.
