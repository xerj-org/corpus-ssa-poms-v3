SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27520.020 - Prior Favorable or Unfavorable Title II Determination or Decision - Felony Related Impairment
Effective Dates: 07/28/2008 - Present | TN 12 (07-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427520020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Denied Determination or Decision
Reopen at any time to consider any excluded impairment because the claimant's felony
conviction has been overturned. For more information, see “Reopening and Revision
after Conviction of a Felony or Reversal of Felony Conviction” DI 23501.075.
