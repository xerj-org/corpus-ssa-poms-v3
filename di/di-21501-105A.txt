A. Blindness requirements (2050)
To qualify for AABD or MA only due to blindness, a client must have:
1.
A central visual acuity of 20/200, or less in the better eye with corrective glasses,
or
2.
A field defect in which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent that
the widest diameter of visual field subtends an angular distance of no greater than
20 degrees.
