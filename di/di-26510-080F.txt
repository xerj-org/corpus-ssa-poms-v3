F. Completing Item 28 in CDB claims
1.
Do not check Block A in CDB claims.
2.
Check Block B for CDB “closed period” allowances only
3.
Check Block C for CDB claimants found disabled. On the line following Block C, enter
the date shown in Item 15A for CDB claims.
4.
Check Block E in CDB closed period claims, and on the line following Block E, enter
the last day of the second month after the month the disability ceased (the date shown
in Item 15B).
