SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 71005.010 - Border Field Office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) Process for Reconsideration Requests Mexican Residents File in the United States
Effective Dates: 02/22/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0471005010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Border FO reconsideration claim procedures
The border FO accepts reconsideration requests on initial disability determinations
Mexican residents file in the United States and completes the following steps for
medical or non-medical denials.
1. Medical denials
Forward the request directly to the DDS according to normal procedures found in DI 27000.000.
2. Non-medical denials
a. FO affirms initial determination
If the FO affirms the initial determination, transfer the claim to the Office of International
Operations after adjudication.
b. RO reverses initial determination
If the FO reverses the initial determination, transfer the claim to the DDS for an
initial medical determination, according to the instructions in DI 81010.145.
For additional guidance on processing reconsideration requests for non-medical or
technical denials, see DI 12005.010 - Processing a Reconsideration Request for an Initial Non-Medical or Technical Denial.
