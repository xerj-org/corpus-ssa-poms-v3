DI 52135.220 Rhode Island Public Disability Benefits (PDB)
Rhode Island was the first state to establish a Temporary Disability Income (TDI)
program. It did so in 1942. TDI provides income support to workers who are out of
work because of a non-work related illness or injury. State law provides a TDI benefit
for private and certain public employees for 30 weeks.
