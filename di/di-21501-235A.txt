A. Blindness — examination for degree of vision (2215.1)
In terms of ophthalmic measurement, central visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the
better eye with corrected lenses is considered economic blindness. “A field defect in which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent that
the widest diameter of visual field subtends an angle of no greater than 20 degrees,” may be considered equally disabling as may certain ocular conditions which do not
necessarily involve visual acuity or peripheral field loss but which constitutes severe
visual handicaps.
