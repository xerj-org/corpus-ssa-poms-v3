F. Impact of other work incentives on the TWP
Do not apply any work incentive policy rules when determining the existence of service
months during the TWP. Do not apply: impairment-related work expenses (IRWE), subsidy,
special conditions, unsuccessful work attempts (UWA), un-incurred business expenses,
unpaid help, or any other work incentive to determine countable income for the TWP.
NOTE: Do not apply averaging when calculating TWP months. If you are unable to verify a
monthly breakdown of earnings (see DI 10505.005F).
