C. Component instructions for establishing the EOD for DWB Medicare claims
1. Field office
Complete the EDCS-3367 “Onset” page and EDCS “Miscellaneous, DWB and prior filing”
page following the instructions found in DI 81010.025.
NOTE: If the claimant remarried, use the Capability/Remarks page in the EDCS-3367 to annotate
the date the claimant remarried.
For EDCS exclusion claims:
•
Calculate the prescribed period and controlling date using the instructions outlined
in DI 11005.050.
•
Annotate the prescribed period and controlling date in block 6 of the Form SSA-3367.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
Develop medical evidence focused on establishing the EOD as of the controlling date,
or the AOD if later than the controlling date but within the prescribed period. If
the EOD is not fully favorable, prepare a personalized disability explanation (PDE)
explaining why we established the partially favorable EOD. Send the PDE with an SSA-L1157–DI.
(See DI 25501.280 Notifying Claimants of Partially Favorable Allowances.)
