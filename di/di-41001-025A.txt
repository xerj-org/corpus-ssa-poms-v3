SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 41001.025 - TWP and EPE Provisions of the Law as They Apply to Statutorily Blind Individuals
Effective Dates: 07/11/2012 - Present | TN 2 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0441001025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Statutorily blind individuals except DIB, CDB, and DWB persons age 55 and older
The trial work period (TWP) and extended period of eligibility (EPE) provisions of
the law that apply to nonblind individuals also apply to these persons. Use the monetary
guidelines in DI 13010.060 and the evaluation guides in DI 41001.010 and DI 41001.015.
