L. Electrophysiologic testing (generally available since October 1984)
Electrophysiologic testing requires cardiac catheterization and the placement of electrode
catheters within selected portions of the heart. The application of an electrical
current is used to assess the propensity of the patient to develop selected cardiac
arrhythmias and of the value of various drugs in preventing these arrhythmias. It
is a better technique because it may document severe, nonresponsive ventricular arrhythmias.
