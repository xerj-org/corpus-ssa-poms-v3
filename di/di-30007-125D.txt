D. Cases accepted for RPC
The RPC process includes review of initial, reconsideration, and continuing disability
review determinations in which the DQR cited a Group I or Group II deficiency but
the DDS does not believe the cited deficiency is supported by policy.
For a discussion of Group I deficiencies see QR 04440.202 and QR 04440.230. For a discussion of Group II deficiencies see QR 04440.204.
NOTE:
The RPC process only applies to certified electronic folders (CEF).
