DI 52130.015 Military Disability Benefits
A Federal public disability benefit (PDB) includes a military disability benefit or
a military retirement pension based on disability. It does not include benefits paid
by the Department of Veterans Affairs under Title 38 U.S.C., which are specifically
excluded from offset by law. Disabled military personnel may receive military disability
benefits from the branch of service in which they served.
