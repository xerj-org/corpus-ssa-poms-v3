DI 81010.060 Accessing a Pending Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Case
A pending EDCS case can be accessed at any time to:
•
Continue keying data; or
•
Update data.
To access a pending claim, choose one of the following searches on the EDCS Case Selection
page:
•
Name search; or
•
SSN search.
EDCS returns a list of all cases and displays:
•
The Claimant’s name;
•
A Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Indicator to inform users if the case is a CEF
by displaying a “Y” or an “N”;
•
The current status of each claim;
•
Office code for the office with jurisdiction of the case;
•
Office type; and
•
Status Date.
