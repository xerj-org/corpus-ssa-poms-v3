Effective Dates: 10/20/2015 - Present | TN 16 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428075625 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Continuance notices
1. Title XVI determination
Prepare and release, subject to Federal review selection, a Title XVI continuation
notice on SSA-L1014 (Supplemental Security Income Disability Information) (see NL 00701.430). The chart in DI 28095.100 shows the block(s) you need to check for each specific case situation. In most situations
block 6 is checked.
2. Title II (Concurrent Title II and Title XVI cases)
Unless the FO processed an SGA cessation, prepare and release, subject to Federal
review selection, SSA-L1013 (Social Security Notice of Continued Disability - Title
II).
