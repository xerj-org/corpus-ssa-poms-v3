C. Medical history
1. Longitudinal medical history
As appropriate, the CE provider will:
a.
Cite the specific medical records and any other documents reviewed during the course
of the evaluation; and
b.
Identify the person(s) providing the oral medical history and an assessment of the
validity and reliability of such information.
2. Current medical history
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
The primary impairment(s) alleged for which the disability claim is being filed;
b.
Onset, frequency, and duration of the impairment(s);
c.
Current treatment plan including medicines and dosages, role of child and caregiver
in administration of treatment plan, and need for periodic adjustments in medication
regimen;
d.
Special therapy, equipment or devices;
e.
Response to treatment and overall extent of control of the impairment(s); and
f.
Source(s) of medical care including specialist(s) or specialty clinic(s).
3. Past medical history
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
Prenatal, delivery, and neonatal course; and
b.
Other significant events including past illnesses, injuries, operations, hospitalizations,
and urgent care encounters. When possible, provide the dates of events and names of
facilities that provided treatment.
4. Information about the child's development
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a. For children who have not attained age 6, developmental milestones – the results
of a formalized developmental screening test is one method to meet this requirement;
b. Early infant or preschool intervention services;
c. For children age 6 and older, usual daily activities; and
d. Current grade, type of class, limitations of activities, and need for special assistance
or extra care.
5. Provide a list of current medications
The CE provider will list the name, dose, and frequency of medication(s), including
both beneficial and adverse effects, and plans for continued drug administration,
schedule, and extent of any therapy.
