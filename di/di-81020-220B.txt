B. Evaluating an Age 18 Redetermination
Use the information on the SSA-3368-BK to request and develop medical evidence and
to establish the date of onset. The Medical Improvement Review Standard (MIRS) does
not apply; instead, the Age 18 Redetermination uses the disability evaluation process
for initial Title XVI adult claims.
