B. Unresolved earnings discrepancy or coverage issues
When there is an unresolved earnings discrepancy or coverage issue
•
Develop gaps in work only when it is material to establishing an earlier onset date;
•
Do not develop for a specific explanation by the claimant if information in file indicates
a reasonable basis for the lack of posted earnings during such periods;
•
Explain gaps in the earnings record if a year or more of continuous, substantial postings
follow them.
