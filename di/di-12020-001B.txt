B. Field Office (FO) procedure when a claimant requests AC review
When a claimant requests AC review:
1.
Explain to the claimant the AC review process and the claimant’s rights and responsibilities.
2.
Obtain a written request for review (assisting the claimant or the claimant’s authorized
representative in completing the HA-520, “Request for Review of Hearing Decision/Order”).
The claimant or the claimant’s authorized representative may submit a letter or other
written document, as described in GN 03104.100. If the written request is other than the HA-520, attach that “writing” to the claims
folder copy of the form. For instructions on preparing and distributing Form HA-520,
see GN 03104.200B.
3.
Input the request for AC review into the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
if the claimant’s file is a Certified Electronic Folder (CEF). For electronic cases,
you must fax the white copy of the HA-520 into the CEF. If you cannot establish an
AC case in EDCS, you must fax or email the white copy of the HA-520 to the Office
of Appellate Operations (OAO). When faxing the HA-520 to OAO, use the following desktop
fax number: (833) 509-0814. When forwarding the HA-520 via email, send the email to
^DCDA OAO. For detailed instructions on how to process in EDCS, see DI 81010.150A.3.
4.
Follow the instructions in GN 03104.200C.4, if the request for AC review is not timely filed.
