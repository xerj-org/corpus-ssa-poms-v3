B. Responsibilities for completing disability reports
FO interviewers or reviewers must ensure that information documented on the disability
report (and other forms that are designated as the FO's responsibility in DI 11005.018) is as complete and accurate as possible. For more information on the responsibility
to ensure all items and questions have been completed to the extent possible, see
DI 11005.040.
FO interviewers and reviewers also resolve discrepancies in information contained
in the file before the claim transfers to the DDS (e.g., reports of different dates
last worked on the EDCS 3368 and SSA-3369).
NOTE: In general, the disability report and SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose Information
to the Social Security Administration) are required to transfer an initial claim to
the DDS. For instructions on when to transfer a claim in EDCS, see DI 81010.085.
