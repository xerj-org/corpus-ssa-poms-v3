SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.330 - Developing Cause of Death on a Pending Disability Claim
Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present | TN 89 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010330 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - pending claim in the field office (FO)
If a claimant dies while the claim is still pending in the FO, determine the precise
cause of death as follows:
1.
Obtain a copy of the death certificate, or certify the cause of death from the death
certificate using an SSA-553 (Special Determination) to document the information;
or
2.
Determine the cause of death from the local registrar, attending physician, coroner,
hospital records, or the Office of Vital Statistics. Obtain the evidence, in addition
to the usual development, before sending the file to the DDS or DCR ODO for a medical
determination. For claims entered into EDCS, barcode and fax the Death Certificate
to Section F (back yellow) of the electronic folder. See Faxing Documents into the
Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes - DI 81010.090 for barcode and fax instructions.
