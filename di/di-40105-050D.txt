D. New determinations
If res judicata does not apply to a subsequent filing, a new independent determination
is needed. A new independent determination may be made without reopening the prior
determination. Consider reopening if it is possible under the rules of administrative
finality. For processing instructions and reopening considerations for subsequent
filings in which res judicata does not apply, see DI 27516.005C. These cases will require a new medical determination and Medical or Psychological
Consultant signature.
If the PC is not able to make a new medical determination, forward the case to the
servicing FO for further processing. The FO will complete processing in the Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS) (or establish a paper folder for EDCS exclusions)
to transfer the case to the DDS for a new medical determination.
