SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52165.015 - Deleting Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Data through Interactive Computations Facility (ICF)
Effective Dates: 09/25/2008 - Present | TN 2 (02-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452165015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to delete WC/PDB data
•
A pending WC/PDB claim is denied and there is no appeal filed.
•
All WC/PDB payments are repaid due to a third party settlement.
•
WC/PDB data is added erroneously to the MBR when WC/PDB was never an issue.
