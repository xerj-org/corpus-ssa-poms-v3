SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.060 - Special Documentation Requirements - Medical Sources of National Significance
Effective Dates: 02/14/2017 - Present | TN 14 (12-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - general
1. Providing information for DDS use
Include the specific information on the applicable disability report form.
2. FO authorized to send request
If the FO is authorized to prepare and release a request to the medical institution:
a.
Always send a recently completed, signed, and dated SSA-827.
b.
Ensure that the specified information is on this SSA-827.
3. Advising RO of additional special sources
a.
Inform the Regional Office Center for Disability Programs of other medical sources
that appear to fall into this category.
b.
Estimate or establish by inquiry the number of SSA requests received by this particular
source in a given period.
