DI 55075.030 Handling Complaints About the TPM
Procedure
Field components may receive complaints about the TPM. If this occurs:
•
Identify the individual making the complaint (e.g., a beneficiary, EN, State VR agency,
other service provider, State P&A specialist);
•
Take a signed statement from the individual on Form SSA-795; and
•
Forward the Form SSA-795 and any accompanying documentation the individual wishes
to submit, to the Regional Center for Disability Programs for subsequent forwarding
to ORDES.
NOTE: If the individual submits a letter or other statement complaining about the TPM,
forward it to the Regional Center for Disability Programs.
