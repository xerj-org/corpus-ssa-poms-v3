F. How do we evaluate impairments that do not meet one of the cancer listings?
1. These listings are only examples of cancers that we consider severe enough to result
in marked and severe functional limitations. If your severe impairment(s) does not
meet the criteria of any of these listings, we must also consider whether you have
an impairment(s) that meets the criteria of a listing in another body system.
2. If you have a severe medically determinable impairment(s) that does not meet a
listing, we will determine whether your impairment(s) medically equals a listing.
(See §§404.1526 and 416.926 of this chapter.) If your impairment(s) does not meet
or medically equal a listing, we will also consider whether you have an impairment(s)
that functionally equals the listings. (See §416.926a of this chapter.) We use the
rules in §416.994a of this chapter when we decide whether you continue to be disabled.
