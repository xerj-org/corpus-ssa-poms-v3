C. Entering the diary for Medical Improvement Expected (MIE)
1. Diary type
Enter "MRN."
2. Diary date
•
Establish an appropriate diary date 12 to 35 months in the future.
•
Enter a four-digit code for month, and year (MM/YY), counting the month of adjudication
(e.g., if the month of adjudication is 08/07, and the diary is set for 24 months,
set the diary at 07/09).
3. Diary reason
Enter an appropriate MIE diary reason code. For diary reason entries, see “MIE Criteria,
DI 26525.025B.

•
