I. Third party settlements involving FECA payments
Disabled federal employees sometimes sue and recover monetary damages from third parties
who cause or contribute to an on-the-job injury or illness. Third-party settlements
are not offsettable. However, Federal law requires repayment of FECA WC. For complete
instructions on third-party settlements, see DI 52105.010.
