A. Blindness — requirement
A person must be blind or near blind in ophthalmological measurements to be eligible
for Aid to the Blind. Ophthalmological measurements are defined as central visual
acuity of 20/200 or less in the better eye with glasses or a visual field defect in
which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent that the widest diameter
or visual field subtends at an angular distance of no greater than 20 degrees.
