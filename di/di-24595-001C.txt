SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24595.001 - Expediting Claims Involving Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection
Effective Dates: 09/08/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424595001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. When the claimant alleges HIV infection but has no medical source
Order a consultative examination (CE) immediately (see DI 22510.005) if:
•
the disability determination services (DDS) receives cases flagged with an electronic
or paper Form SSA-441 indicating “No Medical Source/CE needed”; and
•
there is no medical source.
