A. Linking the CPD Folder
To associate the correct CPD folder(s) with the eCDR, use the Folder Linking option
located under the Case Documents tab in eView. The office with jurisdiction of the
case can unlink the incorrect folder(s), and link the correct folder(s). See “Folder
Unlinking”.
You may link multiple prior Certified Electronic Folders (CEFs) to an eCDR case, if
appropriate. Electronic linking allows you to place Annotations, Bookmarks and Categories
(ABCs) on all documents in the eCDR CEF, including electronically-linked CPD documents.
All ABCs are removed from the prior CEF if it is unlinked from the eCDR.
NOTE: Older CEF documents may be electronically archived and, if selected for linking,
may take several minutes to retrieve. Electronic documents in the CEF are archived
after 3 years, but are still available in eView. It may take several minutes to retrieve
archived documents each time they are retrieved.
