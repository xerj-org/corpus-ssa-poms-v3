SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.085 - Completing Item 29 (Letter/Paragraph Number) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 06/06/2012 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. How/When to complete Item 29
Item 29 of the SSA-831, Disability Determination and Transmittal will be left blank.
Disability Determination Services notices are computer generated based upon systems
input.
If the system is unable to generate the proper disability determination notice and
a manual notice must be prepared, Item 29 of the SSA-831 must contain special notice
and/or coding instructions.
