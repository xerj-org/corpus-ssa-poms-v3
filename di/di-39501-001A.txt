A. Introduction
The Commissioner of the Social Security Administration (SSA) provides funds to the
States for the functions necessary to make disability determinations for the Federal
Government.
This subchapter addresses the funding process and the Federal/State relationship between
the Disability Determination Services (DDSs) and the Social Security Administration.
