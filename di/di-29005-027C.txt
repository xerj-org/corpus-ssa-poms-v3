SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29005.027 - Whereabouts Unknown (WU) Occurs at the Pre-Hearing (PH) Level
Effective Dates: 06/12/2023 - Present | TN 7 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429005027 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure for WU at the PH level
1. Required development for WU at the PH
If the evidence in the claim(s) folder is sufficient to support a fully favorable
determination, do not send the case to the disability hearing unit (DHU). The PH examiner
will follow instructions in DI 29005.005C.
If the evidence in the claim(s) folder is not sufficient to support a fully favorable
determination, the PH examiner must complete the following:
a.
Complete all development not requiring the beneficiary's (or recipient's) cooperation;
b.
Attempt at least one phone contact with the beneficiary (or recipient) and all other
authorized persons, e.g., representative payee, authorized third party, and appointed
representative;
c.
Either attempt to locate the beneficiary (or recipient) or request FO assistance with
locating the claimant. See DI 23005.001 for additional instructions.; AND
d.
Document all actions to locate the beneficiary (or recipient) on an SSA-5002 Report
of Contact or in the claim(s) folder.
NOTE:
Resume normal case processing if the beneficiary (or recipient) is located at any
point of development.
2. Completing the forms if WU development is unsuccessful
If the evidence in the claim(s) folder is not sufficient to support a fully favorable
determination, the PH examiner will follow instructions in DI 29025.001.
NOTE:
Do not return the case to the FO as a ND. Forward all adverse PH determinations to
the DHU for a disability hearing.
