A. Background
20 CFR 404.1626 and 416.1026, effective June 1, 1981, provide that the Commissioner
of the Social Security Administration will give the State funds...for necessary costs
in making disability determinations.... The regulations provide that SSA will notify
the State of the amount of funding that will be made available to it as well as what
anticipated costs are being approved. The State may not incur or make expenditures
for items of cost not approved by SSA or in excess of the amount SSA makes available
to the State. The State will submit a report of its actual expenditures. Any money
the State spends for purposes not within the scope of the regulations must be repaid
to the Treasury of the United States.
