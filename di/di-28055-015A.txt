SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.015 - Disabling Impairment Requirement and When to Address the Issue
Effective Dates: 06/21/2024 - Present | TN 4 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
Disabling impairment - an impairment or combination of impairments which, of itself, is so severe that
it meets or equals the Listing of Impairments or which, when considered with the person's
vocational factors, would result in a finding that the person continues to be disabled
under the medical improvement review standard (DI 28005.001.) were it not for their earnings.
