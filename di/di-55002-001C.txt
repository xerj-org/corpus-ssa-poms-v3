C. Process – role of field components in ticket eligibility issues
1. Assist beneficiaries/recipients with ticket-eligibility questions
Field components assist beneficiaries/recipients with questions about when ticket-eligibility
letters will be mailed (see DI 55002.015 and DI 55002.016), why the system did not select their records for a ticket (see DI 55002.035), and why their tickets terminated.
2. Input corrections and updates to the MBR, SSR, and DCF
When appropriate, Field Office (FO) and Program Center (PC) staff input data corrections
and updates to the MBR, SSR, and DCF (see DI 55002.045), to enable beneficiaries/recipients to get a ticket, move into “assignable” status,
or to be moved from ticket-terminated status.
