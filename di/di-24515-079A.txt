A. Definition of genetic tests
Genetic tests use laboratory methods to detect genetic variations associated with
a disease, condition, or genetic disorder. For SSA’s purposes, we will consider tests
that analyze chromosomes, deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), or ribonucleic acid (RNA) for
identifying congenital genetic variations to be genetic tests. You may see genetic
test results in medical evidence when making a disability determination or decision.
