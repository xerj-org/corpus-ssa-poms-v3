A. Procedure – When to use the DRES Screen
Use the DRES screen to establish an action in the DCF if the same action is not already
pending. In essence, the DRES establishes the primary issue (such as T2 Work CDR,
EXR case, Medical CDR, etc). Do not attempt to establish a duplicate pending action
(such as two EXR requests, or two T2WORK reviews). The DCF can control more than one
pending action provided the actions are in different categories (e.g. a medical CDR
and a work CDR).
