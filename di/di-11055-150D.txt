D. Associating prior paper folders with the current claim
If a medically denied individual files a subsequent application and the prior paper
folder is associated with the current claim, cross out any previous date(s) of filing
indicated on the front cover of the MDF or brown claims folder containing the medical
evidence and SSA-831. No current date entry is necessary.
