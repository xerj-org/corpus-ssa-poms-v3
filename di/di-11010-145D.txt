D. Policy for an event that must follow a UWA
After the first significant break in a claimant’s or beneficiary’s continuous work
activity, regard the following period of work as continuous until the impairment or
the removal of special conditions related to the impairment essential to the further
performance of work, causes the work to discontinue, or reduce to the non-SGA level.
Each continuous period, separated by significant breaks, may be a UWA as the beneficiary
or claimant met the duration and conditions of work criteria.
