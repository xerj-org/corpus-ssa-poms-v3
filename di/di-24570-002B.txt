SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24570.002 - Body Mass Index (BMI) Tables for Establishing Obesity in Children from Age 2 to the Attainment of Age 18
Effective Dates: 10/19/2023 - Present | TN 7 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424570002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1.
For each set of weight and height measurements, calculate the BMI. Use the formula
for calculating BMI in 105.00F2c (see Digestive Disorders -- Child DI 34005.105F). Or use the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Child and Teen BMI
Calculator at:
https://www.cdc.gov/healthyweight/bmi/calculator.html.
2.
Use the appropriate chart in subsection DI 24570.002C. or DI 24570.002D. below and find the child's age (in years and months) in Column 1. If the BMI is equal
to or greater than the value shown in Column 2, the BMI-for-age measurement is above
the 95th percentile.
3.
Repeat step 1 for each additional weight and height measurement in file.
4.
We generally consider the child to have a medically determinable impairment of obesity
as long as there is a consistent pattern of BMI-for-age measurements and other medical
findings.
