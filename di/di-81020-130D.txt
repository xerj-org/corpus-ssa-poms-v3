D. Dating the Personalized Disability Notices (PDNs)
The DDS case processing system electronically dates the PDNs when the PDNs print upon
case closure. The date appears on the paper and electronic versions of the PDN. However,
due to system limitations, there are times when no date appears on the paper or electronic
image versions of the PDN. To determine the correct date of the PDN when no date appears:
•
Open the case in eView;
•
Locate the PDN under Section B – Jurisdictional Documents and Notices (Red) in the
Case Documents tab; and
•
Use the date in the “EF Received” column as the date of the PDN.
NOTE: When a case is selected for sampling by the Office of Quality Review (OQR), the PDN
is not dated until OQR completes the review. After OQR’s review is completed, the
notice release date is displayed in eView in the “Notice Release Date” field found
under the “Case Review” link on the Case Data screen.
