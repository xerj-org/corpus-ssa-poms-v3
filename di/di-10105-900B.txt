Effective Dates: 06/27/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105900 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Filing for other entitlements
The individual receiving continued benefits may be eligible to receive non-disability
based benefits (e.g., retirement, widow’s, child’s benefits, etc.).
Advantages to filing a non-disability based claim during the benefit continuation
period are:
•
Protection of the filing date, and
•
Establishment of immediate entitlement to benefits when the reconsideration or hearing
decision of cessation becomes a final determination.
