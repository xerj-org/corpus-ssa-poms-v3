C. Title XVI and extended period of eligibility (EPE)
In Title XVI and EPE impairment severity determinations, the adjudicator will prepare
a continuance of disability determination if there are no Group II medical improvement
exceptions. The performance of SGA is not a basis for an unfavorable Title XVI continuing
disability review (CDR) determination. For additional information on medical improvement
exceptions, see DI 28005.015, DI 28005.030, and DI 28020.900.
