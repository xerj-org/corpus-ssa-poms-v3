I. Auxiliary due process notice
Provide due process to auxiliaries even if they are in suspense. If auxiliaries are
already in nonpayment status,
•
modify the due process language to exclude reference to “receipt of benefits” and
“benefits being paid.”
•
Prepare and release notices (including a file copy) at the same time you provide due
process to the primary beneficiary.
•
Do not send separate notices to auxiliaries living in the same household as the primary
beneficiary.
Advise all auxiliaries not residing in the same household of the proposed adverse
action prior to the cessation and or termination of benefits.
