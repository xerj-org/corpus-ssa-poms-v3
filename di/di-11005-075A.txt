SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.075 - Difficult Interviewing Situations
Effective Dates: 09/19/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Some interviews will involve technical difficulty, personality conflicts, extreme
claimant dissatisfaction, or possible conflict of interest. Even if the claimant is
responsible for a tense or unpleasant interviewing situation, good public relations
dictates that every effort be made to conclude the matter with tact, diplomacy, and
discretion.
