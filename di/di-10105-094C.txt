C. Potential prisoner case for a pending trial
1. Trial pending
When you identify a potential prisoner case, but the trial is pending, the adjudicating
component; i.e., the DDS, administrative law judge (ALJ), or Appeals Council (AC),
will make a determination or decision in the usual manner and will consider all impairments.
2. Claimant convicted of a felony at the trial
Return the claim to the DDS, ALJ, or AC, as appropriate. The adjudicating component
will determine if a felony- or confinement-related impairment is involved, and will
consider whether reopening and reversal is necessary. In addition, annotate the MCS
development worksheet (DW01) with the issue, “TRLPND” (to indicate the trial in pending).
