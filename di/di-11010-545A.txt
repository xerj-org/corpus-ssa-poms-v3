SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.545 - Payment for Medical Evidence of Record (MER)
Effective Dates: 02/14/2017 - Present | TN 41 (01-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010545 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
In title II disability cases, the DDS will pay a reasonable fee, in accordance with
its established payment rates, for MER requested by us (e.g., SSA or the DDS) from
a non-Federal source if a fee is charged. The DDS will not pay for the examinations
or tests upon which the MER is based. The DDS also has the authority to pay for unsolicited
MER when, in its judgment, the evidence is needed to adjudicate a claim.
In DDS jurisdiction claims involving special medical evidence arrangement situations,
where the FO asks a claimant to take a request for a medical report directly to a
non-Federal source, tell the claimant to inform the source that we will pay a reasonable
fee for the existing record if a fee is charged. Where medical evidence is developed
with a non-Federal special arrangement source, inform the source that SSA will pay
a reasonable fee for the medical evidence of record when a fee is charged. Tell the
source that if a fee is charged, he or she should submit an invoice that shows the
claimant's name and SSN with the medical report. If you receive the evidence and/or
invoice, forward both the evidence and invoice to the DDS for disposition.
