SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39530.005 - Purchase of DDS Equipment
Effective Dates: 09/04/2026 - Present | TN 3 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439530005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Disability Adjudication (DA) Approval
The DDS must request and receive approval from DA before purchasing equipment with
a unit cost of $5,000 or more. The $5,000 includes applicable taxes, delivery and
installation charges. In those instances where the acquisition cost of equipment in
State law, regulation, or practice is lower than the foregoing, the State definition
is applicable.
This policy does not apply to IT equipment. IT equipment is different from general
DDS equipment.
