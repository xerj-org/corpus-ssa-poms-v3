DI 11020.055 Routing Paper Childhood Disability Benefit (CDB) Cases to a Disability Determination
Services (DDS) or Program Center (PC)
If the claim is an exclusion to the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS), create
a paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF). For more information regarding EDCS exclusions,
see DI 81010.030.
