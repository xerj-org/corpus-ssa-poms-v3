Effective Dates: 07/13/2023 - Present | TN 16 (07-23)

B. Procedure for vacating a medical cessation determination on an electronic CDR (eCDR)
If a CDR is currently pending at the DDS, and the case meets screen out criteria,
close the case in the National Disability Determination Services System (NDDSS) as
a “No Determination -- No CDR Issue” as follows:
1.
Receipt the CDR as a reactivation.
2.
Document the certified electronic folder (CEF) by adding an SSA-5002, (Report of Contact)
that contains the following language:
“Inappropriate medical CDR cessation determination was initiated or processed. The
disabled person had CDR protection based on their _________ (example: Ticket to Work)
status. Do not use any documentation contained in this file related to this CDR. Do
not consider any medical evidence in file obtained for this CDR for future CDR or
claim purposes.”
3.
In the “Notes” column in eView, annotate any documents created, received, or used
for the eCDR with the remark “Do not use – CDR vacated.”
4.
Prepare and send the notice of the vacated medical cessation determination to the
disabled person (see exhibit in DI 28075.310).
5.
Close the case in the National Disability Determination Services System (NDDSS) as
a “No Determination -- No CDR Issue.”
6.
Transfer jurisdiction of the eCDR CEF and paper CPD folder, if applicable, to the
FO.
