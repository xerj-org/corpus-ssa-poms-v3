C. Case at the hearing or appeals level
When a case is at the hearing or Appeals Council level, refer any inquiries to the
Office of Hearing Operations (OHO) or the Appeals Council (AC), as appropriate. OHO
and AC employees can enter appointment information into RASR. Follow the same process
as described in DI 31001.001B.
