D. Adding a Claim to a Case
Use the UAT utility to add a claim to a case if at least one claim on the case has
been transferred to the jurisdiction of the DDS or OHO.
To add a claim in EDCS:
•
Open the case in EDCS;
•
Select Transfer/Updates on the toolbar at the top of the EDCS screen path;
•
Select the Transfer Claims tab;,
•
Select the claim pending at the field office; and
•
Click the OK button to send the claim to DDS or OHO.
REMINDERS for adding a claim:
•
Never route an added claim to the Office of Quality Review (OQR), even if a claim
is pending at OQR. Send the added claim to DDS, who must first make a decision and
then route the claim to OQR.
•
If the added claim is an EDCS exclusion, see Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions
- DI 81010.030 or instructions on converting the case to a paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF).
