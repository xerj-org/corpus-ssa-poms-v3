C. PAS processing logic
1. Monthly offset amount is greater than MBA
If the monthly offset amount was greater than the MBA, PAS:
•
suspended benefits to the POD participant and all auxiliaries receiving benefits on
the record, and
•
issued automated notices to the POD beneficiary and auxiliaries explaining the reason
for suspension.
2. Monthly offset amount is less than the MBA
If the monthly offset amount is less than the MBA, PAS:
•
reduced benefits under the POD rules, and
•
issued an automated notice to the POD beneficiaries.
If the month of offset was in the past, MADCAP:
•
posted an overpayment to the beneficiary's record, and
•
generated an overpayment notice for any prior months.
