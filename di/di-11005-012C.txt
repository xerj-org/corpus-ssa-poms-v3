SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.012 - Field Office (FO) Instructions for Claims Development and Processing for Veterans Affairs 100 Percent Permanent and Total (VAPT) Disability Compensation Rating Cases
Effective Dates: 11/29/2023 - Present | TN 116 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Claim documentation
1. Development worksheet (DW01) screen in MCS or CCE Development Worksheet page
•
Establish a UNIT code of “___P&T” on the MCS DW01 screen or CCE Development Worksheet
page for claimants who meet the VAPT definition in DI 11005.007.
•
Use the first three characters of the unit code per local instructions (e.g., unit
number, initials).
•
Establish an ISSUE of “VAPT Case” to track the case.
2. VAPT flag
a. Certified electronic folder
If the VAPT flag does not automatically propagate to the claim in EDCS, follow instructions
in DI 81010.080B to create a “Disabled Veteran rated 100% permanent and total” flag in EDCS. The flag
will alert the DDS to expedite processing of the claim.
b. Paper modular disability folder (i.e., EDCS exclusion)
Attach a VAPT flag to the front of the folder before sending to the DDS. (See an exhibit
of the VAPT flag in DI 11005.012G. in this section.)
