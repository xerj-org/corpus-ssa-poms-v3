A. Policy - Allowance Before 1/6/86
Set a MINE diary at CDR for cases initially allowed before January 6, 1986, under
Listing 5.05A/B.
NOTE: Since language in the previous Listing 5.05A/B did not limit the presumption of disability
to 3 years, a finding of medical improvement related to the ability to work is not
possible under the MIRS.
