SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23540.010 - Completing Form SSA-831 for Medicare Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) Cases
Effective Dates: 12/14/2011 - Present | TN 2 (09-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423540010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Item 15 – claimant disabled
1. Disabled Insurance Beneficiary (DIB)
Enter the actual onset date when the claimant is determined to be under a disability
on or after January 1, 1983. Enter "01/01/83" when the claimant is determined to be
under a disability prior to January 1, 1983.
2. Childhood Disability Beneficiary (CDB)
Same as for non-MQGE CDB, i.e., onset need be no earlier than attainment of age 18
years old, and can be no later than attainment of age 22 years old.
See Also:
•
DI 25501.330 - Establishing an Established Onset Date (EOD) for Childhood Disability Benefits
(CDB) Claims
3. Disabled Widow(er) Beneficiary (DWB)
Enter the actual onset date when the claimant is determined to be under a disability
on or after January 1, 1983.
