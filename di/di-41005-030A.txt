SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 41005.030 - Redeterminations for Statutory Blindness Cases
Effective Dates: 06/28/2024 - Present | TN 2 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0441005030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Period of disability previously established as of the date of onset of statutory blindness
If the period of disability was previously established as of the date of onset of
statutory blindness, the beneficiary is not entitled to a redetermination. Prepare
an SSA-831-U5. Complete only Items 3-5, 7, 9, 12, 27, 29, 37 and 38. On an SSA-4268-U4,
record the fact that the beneficiary is not entitled to a redetermination. Notify
the beneficiary by Model Letter W (see DI 41005.035, Exhibit 15). Destroy all copies of the SSA-831-U5 except the Folder Copy and DO
Copy.
