B. SSA-827 Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration
(SSA)
Print and secure the appropriate signatures on SSA-827s per Completing the Authorization
Form SSA-827 - DI 11005.055 for instructions if necessary. EDCS defaults to print one SSA-827. You can over key
the default number if more forms are needed.
