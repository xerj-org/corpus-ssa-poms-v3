J. Procedures to submit a complaint on a TIS interpreter
To submit a complaint about a TIS interpreter not performing completely or appropriately,
follow local procedures and provide the following information:
•
Date of incident,
•
Time of incident,
•
DDS Office code – DDS office that has the complaint against Language Select (TIS),
•
Interpreter identification number,
•
Language requested,
•
Details regarding nature of the complaint, and
•
DE or other DDS staff should call Language Select at 1-877-526-8438 or email to report the complaint and provide Language Select with detailed information.
