SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55075.015 - Disputes Between ENs and the TPM that Do Not Involve EN Payment Issues
Effective Dates: 02/18/2022 - Present | TN 1 (02-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455075015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy – 2-step dispute resolution process
There is a 2-step dispute resolution process available to ENs and the TPM for issues
that do not involve an EN's request for a milestone or outcome payment.
1. Step 1 – TPM's internal grievance procedures
The first step of this dispute resolution process involves an internal grievance procedure
that the TPM is required to have. If the use of this procedure does not result in
a mutually agreeable solution, the TPM must refer the dispute to SSA for a decision.
2. Step 2 –SSA review and decision
The decision that SSA makes at the second step of this dispute resolution process
is final.
NOTE: The TPM will refer the request for SSA review to staff in ORDES, where the final
SSA decision will be made by the Dispute Resolution Board.
