A. Blindness (2-771)
To meet the condition of blindness for ANB, the client shall have no vision, or vision
which is insufficient for use in an occupation or activity for which sight is essential,
usually vision of 20/200 or less in the better eye with correcting glasses, or a disqualifying
defect in the visual field.
