SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 43510.080 - Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Development
Effective Dates: 05/09/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0443510080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Primary beneficiary resides in U.S.
When a CDR is necessary for a U.S. resident forward the folder to the appropriate
DO via SSA-5526-U3. Request the DO to obtain an SSA-454-BK and conduct a face-to-face
interview. The DO will also obtain the required SSA-827's at the same time and forward
the folder to the DDS for medical development and a determination. Upon completion
of its action, the DDS will return continuances to INTPSC and cessations to the DO
for the appeals period.
