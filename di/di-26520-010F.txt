F. Policy - referral of 'parents' and certain other non-beneficiaries to vocational rehabilitation
(vr)
The Social Security Act does not require that “parents” or widows determined disabled
for Medicare purposes only be referred to VR. (State VR agencies can not be reimbursed
by SSA when these individuals receive VR services.) If the DDS believes that a referral
is appropriate, the State VR agency should be alerted to the fact that the individual
is not a disability/blindness beneficiary.
