SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33015.033 - Whereabouts Unknown (WU) Occurs at the Disability Hearing Unit (DHU) Level
Effective Dates: 06/08/2023 - Present | TN 22 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433015033 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedures for WU at the DHU level
1. Required development for WU at the DHU
If the evidence in file is not sufficient to support a fully favorable determination,
the DHO must complete the following:
a.
Complete all development not requiring the beneficiary's (or recipient's) cooperation;
b.
Attempt phone contact with the beneficiary (or recipient) and all other authorized persons, e.g., representative payee, third party, and appointed
representative. Document all attempts on an SSA-5002 Report of Contact;
c.
Either attempt to locate the beneficiary (or recipient) or request FO assistance with
locating the claimant. See DI 23005.001 for additional instructions Document all attempts on an SSA-5002 Report of Contact;
d.
Consider special handling considerations and follow procedures as appropriate. See
DI 33015.021B.2.; and
e.
If the beneficiary (or recipient) does not appear at the scheduled hearing, wait ten
days from the date of the scheduled hearing prior to completing a determination, unless
the beneficiary or recipient waived the right to appear.
NOTE: Follow procedures for rescheduling the hearing if the beneficiary or recipient is
located at any point of development. See DI 33010.005.
NOTE: If WU occurs at the PH level and exists at time of receipt in the DHU, follow instructions
in DI 29005.027D.2.
2. Completing the forms
a. Writing the DHO's determination
Make a determination based on the evidence in the file. Complete as described in DI 33015.020 with the following considerations:
1.
Focus independently on the relevant items on the “Disability Hearing Officer's Report
of Disability Hearing.”
2.
Evaluate any new documentary evidence submitted.
3.
Make a determination based on all of the evidence in the file and address WU as applicable.
NOTE: Do not return the claim to the FO as a No Determination.
b. Completing the Forms SSA-832 Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal – Title XVI or SSA-833 Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal – Title II
Complete as described in DI 33020.005 and take the following actions:
1.
Enter the appropriate cessation code based on the evidence in the file. Generally,
this will be the same code as the prior determination.
2.
Enter "Whereabouts unknown" in the remarks section.
c. Completing the notice
1.
Mail the notice to the beneficiary (or recipient) at their last known address.
2.
See DI 33015.025 for complete notice instructions.
