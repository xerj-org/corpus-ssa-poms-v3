DI 39536.600 IT Budgeting Procedures -- General
Cost of IT is a line item in the DDS annual budget and quarterly spending plan submissions.
(See DI 39503.000 and DI 39506.000.) Requests for new IT equipment and services must be supported by the appropriate
documentation as given in DI 39536.210 and receive the appropriate Central Office reviews as given in DI 39536.235.
All requests for funding involving IT equipment and services, whether involving new
or ongoing functions, must be made in accordance with established budget processes.
Salaries and wages of DDS employees involved in the development of IT Systems should
be included in budgetary/spending plan projections as a “Personal
Services” cost. Salaries and wages of State programmers or other State related personnel who
are not DDS employees must be budgeted for under “Indirect Costs”. Costs of private vendor services involved with the development of IT systems should
be included as a part of the acquisition cost of the system, and included in the breakout
line item “IT
new”. IT maintenance changes should be included in the “miscellaneous” cost category.
Budget projections for IT should be made as soon as the DDS has reasonable expectation
of undertaking these IT developments; the DDS should not delay budget projections
until the full documentation required is completed.
