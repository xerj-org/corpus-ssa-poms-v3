Effective Dates: 09/25/2008 - Present |

C. Verification of change
Verify any allegation of a WC/PDB rate change. If the NH alleges WC/PDB payment ended,
see instructions in DI 52150.025 - Workers’ Compensation/ Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Offset Ending Date.
For verification and documentation instructions, see DI 52145.001 - Obtaining Verification/Proof of Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit
(WC/PDB) Information.
