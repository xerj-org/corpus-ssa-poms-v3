SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24005.001 - Unsuccessful Work Attempts (UWA) for Initial Claims and Reconsiderations
Effective Dates: 06/27/2024 - Present | TN 2 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424005001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of UWA
A UWA is an effort to do work in employment or self-employment that discontinues or
reduces to the non-Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) level after a short time (no
more than 6 months) because of the claimant’s or beneficiary’s impairment or the removal
of special conditions related to the impairment that are essential to continued further
performance of work.
