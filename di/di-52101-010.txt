DI 52101.010 Social Security Rulings (SSRs) Involving Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit
(WC/PDB) Offset
Social Security Rulings (SSRs) are case decisions which establish precedents relating
to all SSA benefit programs. Rulings may be based on case decisions made at all administrative
levels of adjudication.
Although the rulings do not have the force and effect of the law or regulations, they
are binding on all SSA components.
A district court decision binds SSA to that particular case only. SSA adjudicators
must follow SSA regulations and policy for all other cases. The only exceptions are
Circuit Court decisions for which SSA issues an Acquiescence Ruling and Supreme Court
decisions.
