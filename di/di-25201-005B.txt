B. Policy - step 1 - if you are working (section 416.924(b))
If you are working and the work you are doing is substantial gainful activity, we
will find that you are not disabled regardless of your medical condition or age, education,
or work experience. (For our rules on how we decide whether you are engaging in substantial
gainful activity, see DI 22001.001D.1. and DI 24001.000.)
