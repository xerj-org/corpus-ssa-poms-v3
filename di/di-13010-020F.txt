F. Work review pending
If the Development Summary block shows that a work review is pending, establish the
work report. eWork automatically assigns the work report to the office with jurisdiction
of the pending work review.
IMPORTANT: If the beneficiary indicates that they have moved, or wants your office to handle
the work review, contact the office with jurisdiction and ask them to transfer the
work review to you. After you transfer jurisdiction in eWork, the system automatically
transfers the work review in the disability control file (DCF).
