D. Reminders for choosing an impairment code for cardiovascular disorders
•
Do not use 3750 for fully favorable determinations more than one year after the date of the heart
transplant. Use the impairment code for the residual impairment. For example, use
4140 when the residual impairment is cardiac allograft vasculopathy.
•
Do not use 7850 for determinations made after the attainment of age 18.
