SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23535.012 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Responsibilities in Presumptive Disability (PD) Cases Involving an Allegation of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection or Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS)
Effective Dates: 08/01/2013 - Present | TN 32 (03-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423535012 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure -- Medical File Received in DDS without the SSA-4814-F5 or SSA-4815-F6 Check-Block
Form
If the medical file is received in the DDS without the check-block form, the DDS:
•
Verifies that a PD finding has been made by the field office (FO); or
•
Determines if a PD finding is appropriate if a PD finding was not made by the FO. (Assess using DDS procedures as detailed in DI 23535.010.)
ALERT: It is not necessary to await receipt of the form from the FO before making a PD finding.
The DDS is not restricted to the categories on Forms SSA 4814-F5 or SSA-4815-F6, but
may make a PD finding at any time that the evidence is sufficient to establish a high
degree of probability that the claimant alleging HIV infection or AIDS will be found
disabled.
