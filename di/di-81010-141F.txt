F. Unable to locate the claimant
When the FO receives a fully favorable IR determination and cannot determine the whereabouts of the claimant:
•
Follow existing procedures in GN 01010.300 – Handling Unknown Address Claims.
•
Document the CEF with the findings using eForms SSA-5002.
•
Fax a copy of the SSA-5002 to the originating HO.
