B. Incorporating evidence
Incorporate the sources of evidence used in the prior determination by using the following
introductory paragraph “The statement of evidence in the prior determination of (MM/DD/YYYY),
except as modified herein, is hereby incorporated by reference, but not the inferences,
the findings, or the conclusions based thereon.”
