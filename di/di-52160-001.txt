DI 52160.001 Expedited Reinstatement (EXR) Provisional Payments with Workers’ Compensation/Public
Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
CITATIONS:
Soc. Sec. Act, Section 223(i)
, - Reinstatement of Entitlement
Certain previously entitled disabled workers may request reinstatement of disability
benefits when their medical condition no longer permits them to perform substantial
gainful activity (SGA) within 60 months of their prior termination. The EXR provision:
•
Allows a disabled worker to receive up to 6 months of provisional payments while SSA
conducts a medical review to determine whether the work can be reinstated to benefits.
•
Establishes a new period of disability with a new onset date, but the disability insurance
benefit (DIB) is computed as if the termination never occurred.
For detailed EXR information, see DI 13050.001 – Expedited Reinstatement.
