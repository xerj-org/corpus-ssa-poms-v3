J. Item 5 - date disability began
1. SSA-831-U3 available
Enter the established onset date from Item 15A or Item 18B of the latest approved
SSA-831-U3 in file or the date in the Date of Disability Onset (DDO) field of the
Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB) section of the Abbreviated MBR Query (AACT) or
Supplemental Security Income Data (SSID).
2. SSA-831-U3 not available
Query SSA's system to obtain the date disability began, and then enter the date in
the DDO field of the DIB section of the AACT or SSID.
3. Title II or Title XVI case statutory blindness ends and disability begins
Enter the latest approved onset date from the SSA-831-U3 in cases where the disabled
individual is no longer statutorily blind, but is still disabled.
4. Conversion cases
Enter "before 01/01/74" if disability continued or ceased under Title XVI or the State
plan in conversion cases.
