SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81020.005 - Electronic Folder Interface (EFI) Download
Effective Dates: 01/23/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481020005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Download Not Available
If there is no EFI Download available when the DDS receives the EDCS Routing Form,
review eView to determine where the case is pending (see Status/History Tab — DI 81005.055).
1. Claim Pending in the Field Office
Contact that office and request the case be transferred to the DDS. Do not receipt any claims in the case until the EFI download for all claims are received.
2. Claim Pending in Another Office (e.g., another state DDS)
Determine which office should have jurisdiction of the claim.
a. Your Office Should Have Jurisdiction
Contact the office with jurisdiction and request the claim(s) to be electronically
transferred to your DDS. Do not receipt any claims in the case until the EFI download for all claims are received.
b. Other Office Should Have Jurisdiction
Mail the EDCS Routing Form and any attachments, including any remaining paper SSA-827s
(Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration [SSA]),
to the other office. Post a message in eView indicating that the material was incorrectly
routed to your office and your DDS is mailing it to the other office.
3. Claim Pending in Your DDS
Ask the DDS systems administrator to investigate, and if necessary, to open a priority
one Change Asset and Problem Reporting System (CAPRS) ticket. Do not receipt any claims in the case until the EFI download for all claims are received.
