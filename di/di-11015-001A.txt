A. Requirements for entitlement to DWB
To become entitled under this provision, an applicant must meet the requirements in
Requirements for Disabled Widow(er)’s Benefits (DWB) DI 10110.001.
IMPORTANT: See NOTE: in DI 11015.020C.2. regarding a circumstance that does not require disability to exist throughout the
month.
