C. Subsequent Claims
When a subsequent claim is filed, update the medical information by completing SSA-3368-BK
and SSA-3367. If work is involved, complete an SSA-821/SSA-820 before transferring
the claim to the DDS. If the subsequent claim is being entered into EDCS and the prior
claim is a closed electronic folder, some data may be propagated from the prior claim
(see DI 81010.035C).
NOTE: Complete the SSA-3369-BK in special situations as explained in DI 11005.025.
