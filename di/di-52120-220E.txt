E. Time Limits for filing
If the carrier/employer paid some WC without accepting liability, the worker has 2
years from the date of injury to file a petition with the WC Court to see if the carrier
is liable for additional payments.
If the worker has agreed to the cessation of their weekly WC and signed a Suspension Agreement,
they then have up to 10 years to reopen the claim.
