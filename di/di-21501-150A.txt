A. Blindness
In terms of ophthalmic measure, central visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the better
eye with correcting glasses is generally considered as economic blindness. A field
defect in which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent that the widest
diameter of visual field subtends at an angular distance of no greater than 20 degrees
is considered equally disabling.
