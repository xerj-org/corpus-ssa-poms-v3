Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428501020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Revision of a prior favorable continuance under administrative finality
If a prior favorable continuance is incorrect, the Disability Determination Services
(DDS) must complete any outstanding development of the case. If the evidence shows
that the prior determination needs revision and the rules of administrative finality
allow, the DDS should take the following actions:
•
follow the rules of due process in DI 27525.000, and
•
prepare a revised determination using the evidence received from the outstanding development.
