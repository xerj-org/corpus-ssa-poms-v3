DI 23535.015 Expediting the Formal Determination after a Presumptive Disability/Presumptive Blindness
(PD/PB) Finding
Expedite the formal disability or blindness determination after a PD/PB finding has
been made to avoid an interruption in payments. The intended purpose of PD/PB payments
is largely offset if there is a later interruption in payments. Such an interruption
occurs if the formal determination is not completed prior to the end of the 6-month
period during which payment can be made on the basis of PD/PB.
