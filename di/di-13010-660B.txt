B. Procedure – How to get to the QMID Screen
To get to the QMID screen.
•
Select 32, DISABILITY CONTROL FILE, from SSA's main menu (MAIN) screen.
•
Select 1 on the MCDR screen with the beneficiary's own SSN or the entitlement SSN
and BIC.
•
Select type of CDR action 1,2,3,4,or 5 on the ECDR.
•
Push ENTER after the last event is displayed on the QCDR screen.
