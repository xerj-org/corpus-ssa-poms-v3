B. Report content specific to pediatric genitourinary disorders
The CE provider will use the specific requirements below to complete the CE report
for a genitourinary disorder.
1. Current medical history
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
Malaise;
b.
Fatigue;
c.
Poor appetite;
d.
Edema;
e.
Headaches;
f.
Bone pain;
g.
Urinary frequency, dysuria, hematuria, and/or incontinence;
h.
Renal ultrasound results;
i.
Type and frequency of urological procedures; and
j.
Acute weight gain or loss.
2. Physical examination
The CE provider will describe and discuss, as appropriate:
a.
Pallor;
b.
Fundoscopic and any retinal change;
c.
Edema – type and location;
d.
Anasarca;
e.
Congenital abnormalities; and
f.
Neurological findings – irritability and hypotonia.
