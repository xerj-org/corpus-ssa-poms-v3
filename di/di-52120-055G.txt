G. Verifying WC
Effective July 2017, the State of Florida discontinued
public access to its Workers Compensation Claims Database.
A limited number of SSA personnel in the Atlanta Region have secure access to the
database for the purpose of verifying WC claims information.
Once you are certain the WC evidence is not already in file, check the Workers' Compensation
(WC) Resource Page in DI 52140.020 since FL is a state that provides SSA with access to its online records. The value
of online records can vary from state to state and some development may still be required.
However, the sites available usually provide contact information for the employer,
WC carrier or the WC attorney, which can jump-start the development process. Also
included in the WC resource intranet site is a list of individuals in the PC with
online access to those individual state records.
After attempting to develop proof of WC directly from the NH or their attorney per DI 52145.001F, field offices can verify FL WC by sending an e562 (Request for Assistance) to the FO servicing the Florida zip code for the NH's employer
at the time of the FL WC injury/claim. This information is found in the Modernized
Claims System (MCS) on the WPAD screen, or in information or documentation provided
by the NH or their attorney.
Processing Centers only may contact the Southeastern Program Service Center (SEPSC) to verify FL WC by sending
an email to the mailbox to SEPSC's Operations Support Branch (^CentProc Portfolio
2 SEPSC OpsSuptDiv).
Detailed Florida WC claim documentation is available for access without a password
at the website for Florida's Office of the Judges of Compensation Claims. Type all of the available
information on the case search
page.
