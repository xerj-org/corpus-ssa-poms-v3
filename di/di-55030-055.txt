DI 55030.055 Handling Fraudulent Earnings Reports
Fraudulent earnings reports are a major concern. If in the course of developing a
program manager (PM) earnings document, you suspect that an employment network (EN)
or State vocational rehabilitation agency serving as an EN has falsified a document
or that the PM falsified a certification report, refer the issue to your regional
center for disability programs immediately.
