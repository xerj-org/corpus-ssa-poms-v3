A. Affirmation of an initial denial
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) must prepare and release a personalized
notice for affirmations of initial denials. The personalized notice consists of preprinted
form notice (SSA-L928 Denial Notice – Recon for Benefits) and a personalized explanation.
(See DI 27025.020), for instructions on preparing a personalized explanation.) Include paragraph 842
(Title XVI disclaimer) on concurrent Title II/Title XVI claims when the SSA-L928 is
not preprinted. For additional instructions on paragraph 842 see, NL 00708.100 - Numbered Paragraphs .
