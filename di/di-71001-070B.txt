B. DDS reconsideration processing procedures
The DDS processes reconsideration requests filed by Canadian residents in the U.S.
following normal procedures. For further instructions, see DI 27001.001 The Reconsideration Process and DI 81020.025 Processing Electronic Reconsideration Cases.
