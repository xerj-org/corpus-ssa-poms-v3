SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32010.085 - Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Adverse Reopening' Cases
Effective Dates: 10/06/2021 - Present | TN 4 (10-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432010085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Destination of certified electronic (CEF) folder
Route folder to the field office (FO) for appeals period.
2. Destination of paper folder
Create an SSA-408
(Route Slip) entry, indicating the type of case - 'CDR Adverse Reopening Indicated.'
Route to the appropriate component.
