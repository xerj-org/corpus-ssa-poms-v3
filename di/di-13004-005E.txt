E. Mailer form processing is not a CDR
The action to defer a CDR or refer a mailer case for a CDR is not a CDR and is not
an initial determination subject to administrative review, nor is a new comparison
point decision (CPD) established. For an explanation of CPD, see DI 13005.022C.1.
