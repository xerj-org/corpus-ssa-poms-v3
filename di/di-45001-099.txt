DI 45001.099 Final Notice of Medicare Termination Due to ESRD Cessation (Transplant)
The law provides that Medicare coverage, based on kidney disease, will end with the
last day of the 36th month after the month a kidney transplant is received unless,
before that point, a new transplant is received or regular dialysis treatment is started.
Since you received a kidney transplant in   (month/year)   , your Medicare coverage ends with the last day of   (month/year)   .
If you believe that this determination is not correct, you may request that your case
be reexamined. If you want this reconsideration, you must request it not later than
60 days from the date you receive this notice. You may make your request through any
social security office.
Please read the enclosed leaflet for a full explanation of your right to question
the determination made on your claim. If you again begin a course of dialysis or if
you receive a kidney transplant, please contact any social security office.
