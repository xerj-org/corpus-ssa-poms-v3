D. Procedure – obtaining a “Ticket Status Range Query”
1. Access the TKSR
The TKSR screen is a continuation screen from the TKQY screen (see DI 55002.025C) and will appear after the TKQY if there is history on the DCF for the SSN transmitted.
MSOM Reference
MSOM CDR 004.009 provides information on how to read the fields of the TKSR screen.
