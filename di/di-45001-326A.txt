SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 45001.326 - Processing ESRD Claims Involving Insured Status Based on Government Employment
Effective Dates: 03/03/2025 - Present | TN 24 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0445001326 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Government employment may be used in determining insured status for Medicare entitlement
based on ESRD. See HI 00801.400 for instructions in the use of these wage credits. See DI 11052.015 for special instructions regarding government employment and ESRD cases.
