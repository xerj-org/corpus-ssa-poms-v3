Effective Dates: 09/25/2008 - Present |

E. Computing offset
Compute offset as if the new WC/PDB monthly rate were payable in the first possible
month of offset.
NOTE: Where an overpayment to the carrier occurs because of a change in the WC rate, see
DI 52150.035A.4. for information on the correct rate at which to offset.
Use the number of auxiliaries who are entitled in the month of the change as if they
had been entitled in the first possible month of offset.
Add any protected DIB increases that have accrued since the first possible month of
offset up through the month of the rate change.
