SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.024 - Developing Evidence from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM)
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505024 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
1. General
OPM will furnish medical evidence on individuals who have filed for civil service
disability retirement.
2. Record Content
These records may include:
•
A letter from the treating source(s)
•
A Certificate of Medical Examination (with detailed clinical signs and laboratory
findings)
•
A report from the employing agency describing job duties and the employee's ability
to perform those duties.
