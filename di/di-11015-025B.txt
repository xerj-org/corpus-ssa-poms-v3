Effective Dates: 05/04/2011 - Present | TN 35 (08-20)

B. Processing a denial
Always attempt to process the claim via MCS EC first.
If you cannot adjudicate the denial via MCS EC, process via Manual Adjustment, Credit,
and Award Direct Data Entry (MACADE). For complete MACADE processing instructions,
see SM 00380.000 Disallowances, Abatements, and Withdrawals.
