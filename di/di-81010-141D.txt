D. Non-medical technical issues discovered after fully favorable determination
When the FO receives a fully favorable determination and realizes that non-medical
technical issues exist:
•
Document the CEF using eForms SSA-5002 with detailed information about a possible
technical denial and advise the HO not to dismiss the hearing, and
•
Fax a copy of the SSA-5002 to the originating HO. This is the only way the HO will
know they must follow existing procedures to process the hearing.
NOTE: The FO does not receive partially favorable determinations. The DDS only sends the
case to the FO if all claims result in a fully favorable determination. Otherwise, the DDS returns the
case to the HO, and the HO follows existing procedures to process the hearing.
