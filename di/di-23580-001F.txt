F. Procedure – Reconsiderations
If the claimant files a reconsideration on a partially favorable or unfavorable determination,
follow normal reconsideration procedures. See DI 23580.008 Exhibit 6 for Reconsideration Notice – Partially Favorable Determination and DI 23580.009 Exhibit 7 for Reconsideration Notice – Unfavorable Determination.
NOTE: Although post-entitlement ALS cases are receipted as continuing disability reviews
(CDRs) and an SSA-833 is completed, the claimant is not entitled to Disability Hearings
Office (DHO) appeal rights, (i.e., no face-to-face hearing is required) because no
cessation or adverse reopening is involved.
