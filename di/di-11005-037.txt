DI 11005.037 Applicant Forms Needed in Concurrent Claims
Policy
When an applicant files concurrent claims, only one disability report needs to be
completed. In the situation in which adult concurrent claims are filed, the SSA-3368
should be completed. In the situation in which a title XVI disability or blindness
claim is filed for a child (i.e., an individual who is under age 18) concurrently
with a title II disability claim (which would normally require completion of adult
forms), the FO is responsible for obtaining an SSA-3820 and an SSA-3379.
