DI 81010.005 Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Overview
EDCS is a web-based application that is used by the Field Office (FO) to record disability
information for a claim. The information collected in EDCS mirrors the information
collected on the paper disability reports (e.g., SSA – 3368) and Internet Disability
Reports (IDRs). Employees in other components (i.e. Disability Determination Services
(DDS), Office of Quality Review (OQR), Office of Disability Adjudication and Review
(ODAR), and Processing Centers (PC) use eView to view the information entered into
EDCS by the FO.
