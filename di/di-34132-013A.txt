A. How are the listings for mental disorders arranged, and what do they require?
1. The listings for mental disorders are arranged in 11 categories: neurocognitive
disorders (12.02); schizophrenia spectrum and other psychotic disorders (12.03); depressive,
bipolar and related disorders (12.04); intellectual disorder (12.05); anxiety and
obsessive-compulsive disorders (12.06); somatic symptom and related disorders (12.07);
personality and impulse-control disorders (12.08); autism spectrum disorder (12.10);
neurodevelopmental disorders (12.11); eating disorders (12.13); and trauma- and stressor-related
disorders (12.15).
