E. Claimant moved out of the State
If the claimant moves out of the State of jurisdiction, see DI 11010.255.
If the FO learns of the move first, notify DDS using the Update After Transfer Utility
(UAT) in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) per DI 81010.095. To transfer EDCS, see DI 81010.085.
