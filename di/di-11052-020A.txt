SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11052.020 - Adjudication Considerations for Initial End-Stage Renal Disease Case Processing
Effective Dates: 05/31/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411052020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Unable to process claim via Earnings Computation (EC) or A101
Whenever you do not process the claim via EC or A101, you must manually clear (MANCLR)
the claim to close and lock the segment you have established in Modernized Claims
System (MCS).
