H. Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR)
The administrative law judges responsibility includes:
1.
Schedule and hold hearings (unless the beneficiary waived the right to appear).
2.
Prepare decisions and medical determination forms based on evidence in file and findings
at the hearing (if held).
3.
Issue decision notices.
