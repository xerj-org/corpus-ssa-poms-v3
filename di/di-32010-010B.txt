B. Routing 'no determination' eCDR cases
The following chart displays the destination and necessary remarks for the Disability
Determination Services (DDS) routing of 'no determination' eCDR cases:
Situation
Destination
Remarks
Individual no longer lives in the State
Send to the DDS of the individual's current State of residence (see DI 81020.250B for eCDR routing instructions).
Indicate Type of Case:
(Title II, Title XVI or concurrent) 'CDR Required.'
Title II: Whereabouts Unknown
Send to the originating Field Office (FO) of individual's last known address (see
DI 14510.003B).
'Title II - Whereabouts Unknown. Possible cessation determination needed.'
Title XVI: Whereabouts Unknown
Send to the originating or FO of the individual's last known address (see DI 14510.003B).
'Title XVI - Whereabouts Unknown. Possible cessation determination needed.'
Title XVI: Re-entitlement Case: - Payments not Reinstated. No CDR Issue
Send to the originating (sending) FO.
'Title XVI - No CDR Necessary. Not in Payment Status. No Payment Reinstatement Necessary.'
Folder misrouted to the DDS
Forward to appropriate FO or DDS based on the individual's state of residence. If
appropriate office cannot be determined, return to originating FO.
'No Determination. Folder misrouted.'
No CDR Issue
