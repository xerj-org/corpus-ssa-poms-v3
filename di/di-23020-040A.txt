SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23020.040 - Delayed Cases
Effective Dates: 02/20/2024 - Present | TN 14 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423020040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
Delayed cases are those which are at a work station for a time considered to be excessive
when measured against normal case processing experience. In order to effectively identify
such cases, a flagging procedure is followed at the various work stations, i.e., the
Field Office (FO), Disability Quality Branch (DQB), the Program Service Center (PSC),
and the Disability Determination Services (DDS). A flag attached at one station will
remain on the case through all subsequent operations including final handling in the
Office of Disability Operations (ODO) or the PSC.
