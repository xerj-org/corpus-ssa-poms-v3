DI 81010.070 Printing Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Forms
After opening the EDCS case, select the “Print Forms” link at the top of the screen
to print:
•
Disability Reports;
•
Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration (SSA-827);
•
Disability Determination and Transmittal (SSA-831);
•
SSA-3369 Work History Report; and
•
EDCS Routing Form.
