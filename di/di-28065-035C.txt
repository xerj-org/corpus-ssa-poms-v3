SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.035 - Additional Requirements for Completing Form SSA-833-U5 in Felony/Prisoner Cases
Effective Dates: 05/25/2023 - Present | TN 1 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Item 24 - Remarks
When there is insufficient evidence to make a determination about whether disability
continues because of confinement to a penal institution, continue the period of disability
and enter: "Prisoner Case - Resolution of CD issue required upon reinstatement of
benefits."
