DI 40101.005 Processing Center (PC) Processing Title II Disability and MQGE Claims
The following instructions include the responsibilities of the Processing Centers
(PCs) when they are processing initial and subsequent Title II Disability and Medicare
Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) claims. The term PC includes the following
SSA components:
•
the Office of Disability Operations (ODO),
•
the Office of International Operations (OIO), and
•
six other Program Service Centers (PSCs).
