SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.017 - Notifying Consultative Examination (CE) Source of Scheduled Appointment
Effective Dates: 01/06/2020 - Present | TN 19 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510017 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Notifying the CE source of the appointment in writing
Notify the CE source of the appointment in writing and include the following:
1.
Date and time of the CE appointment,
2.
Required type of CE, required tests, and specific issues for the CE source to address,
3.
Authorizing documents (e.g., an authorization for diagnostic studies or claim for
payment),
4.
Form(s) to complete, such as a request for the CE medical opinion of what the claimant
can still do despite his or her impairment(s),
5.
A duplicate, fax, or other agency-approved electronic transmission of an originally
signed and dated SSA Authorization To Disclose Information To The Social Security
Administration unless the source prefers to use its own authorization forms, see DI 11005.055, and
6.
Background information on the claimant as described in DI 22510.017B in this section.
For a rescheduled CE appointment, notify the CE source of the change and include the
information listed above in 22510.017A.
