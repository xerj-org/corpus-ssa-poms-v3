B. Manual case control
In the absence of an automated case control system, DDS's should establish centrally
maintained control cards containing identification data, receipt and disposition data
and other valuable information about the case. At any point while the case is in the
DDS, or after it has been processed and dispatched, the control card should give the
reader a complete history of the case.
