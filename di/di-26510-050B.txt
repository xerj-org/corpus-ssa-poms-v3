SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.050 - Completing Item 23 (Medical List Number) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 02/03/2012 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Title XVI
1. Adult (individual age 18 or older)
When Item 15 or 18B is checked, complete Item 23 as in DIB cases.
2. Child (individual under age 18)
a.
When the impairment(s) meets or medically equals a listing, complete Item 23 as in
DIB cases.
b.
When the impairment(s) functionally equals the listings, make no entry in Item 23.
