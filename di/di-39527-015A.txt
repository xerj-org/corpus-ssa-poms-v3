SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39527.015 - Privately Owned Space
Effective Dates: 07/08/2026 - Present | TN 40 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Comparable space
a.
Rental charges to SSA must not exceed
the rental rate of comparable privately owned
space in the same or similar localities.
b.
The rental rate:
•
Need not be a fixed rate
in any particular locality;
•
May vary
within, as well as between, localities; and
•
Must consider inflationary trends
in utility and tax costs.
2. Cancellation or conditional clause
A cancellation or conditional clause must be in the rental agreement
unless Disability Adjudication (DA) has approved a lease without a cancellation clause.
3. Billing
a. Cost allocation plan
If the lease is controlled by a central State organization, the cost is included in
the
cost allocation plan.
b. Direct bill
If the Private Owned building is entirely rented out to a state, the central State
organization can still direct bill the DDS for the leased space based on square footage.
If a service can be identified to the space held by the DDS, it can be directly billed.
