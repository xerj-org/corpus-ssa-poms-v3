C. Viewing WC/PDB proofs
All FOs, PCs, Disability Determination Services (DDSs) and the Office of Quality Performance
(OQP) can view documents stored in NDRed by using the Evidence Portal (EP).
PC users can view material faxed into NDRed in EP. The EP icon is located on the Paperless
toolbar. See MS 09701.000 for procedure on using EP.
