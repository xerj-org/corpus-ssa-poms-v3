C. Insured status for R-HI and R-SMI
R-HI and R-SMI cannot begin before the first month in which a claimant meets either
fully or currently insured status requirements. If an individual is fully or currently
insured for monthly benefits, he or she is insured for ESRD. In addition, we can use
actual or deemed Medicare Qualified Government Employee (MQGE) quarters of coverage
(QC), railroad earnings, military service credits, and HI Freeze QCs for insured status,
if necessary.
If the claimant is not insured on his or her own earnings record, we can use the earnings
record of a spouse or parent (if child is dependent on the parent) as the insured
person.
