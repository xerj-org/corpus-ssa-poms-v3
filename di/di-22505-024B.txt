SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.024 - Developing Evidence from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM)
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505024 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow the instructions below to request the evidence.
1. General
a.
Send the request to:
Office of Personnel Management
Employee Service and Records Center
Claims Correspondence Section
Boyers, Pennsylvania 16017
b.
Include a signed SSA-827.
c.
Include the claimant's:
•
Name
•
Current address
•
Date of birth
•
Civil service claim number, or, when not immediately available, last government employer,
location, dates of employment, and date of retirement.
2. Followup
If a response is not received within 45 calendar days from the date of the initial
request, send a followup request.
3. Reference
See DI 22505.035 for additional followup procedures.
