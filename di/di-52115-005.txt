DI 52115.005 Longshore & Harbor Workers' Compensation Act (LHWCA)
The LHWCA provides compensation and medical care to employees disabled from injuries
that occur on the navigable waters of the United States or in adjoining areas used
in loading, unloading, repairing or building a vessel. Survivor benefits also are
provided if the work-related injury causes the employee's death.
