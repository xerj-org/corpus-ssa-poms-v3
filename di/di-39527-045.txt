DI 39527.045 DDS Layout of Office Space -- Policy
Consider the following items in an office layout to ensure an efficient operating arrangement:
•
adequate safety and security factors,
•
principle workflow that is efficiently organized,
•
office operations that are physically separated to allow for efficient procedures
and controls over the work operations within each segment,
•
individuals having frequent contact placed near each other,
•
intra-office movement space not more than 15 percent of the basic DDS space requirements,
•
files that are readily accessible to principal users,
•
adequate privacy for staff, and
•
adequate lighting, noise and temperature controls.
