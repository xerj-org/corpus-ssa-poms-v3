B. Federal and State disability hearings
We conduct hearings at both State and Federal levels. If you need to modify these
procedures to conform to State business processes, you cannot modify them in any manner
that would conflict with the Federal regulations governing disability hearings.
A Federal DHO may conduct hearings where SSA made the determination under appeal,
such as railroad and foreign claims. The Associate Commissioner for the Office of
Disability Determinations, or delegate, appoints Federal DHOs. There may be circumstances
when a Federal DHO assists State DHUs.
