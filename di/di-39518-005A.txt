A. Policy Principle
1. Qualified Personnel
The State will provide sufficient qualified personnel
and medical consultant services to insure disability determinations are made accurately
and promptly.
2. Selection, Tenure, and Compensation
The State will, except as may be inconsistent with 20 CFR 404.1621(a) and 416.1021(a),
adhere
to applicable State approved personnel standards
in the selection, tenure, and compensation of any individual employed in the disability
program.
