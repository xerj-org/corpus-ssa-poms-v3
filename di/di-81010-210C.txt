C. Statutorily Blind Determination Cases
During a work CDR, a beneficiary may allege having a visual impairment. These statutorily
blind determination cases are EDCS exclusions. Though these cases are processed as
CDRs, the DDS must make an initial statutorily blind determination. Software changes
are needed to process these cases in EDCS. For paper processing procedures, see DI 13010.135.
