SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.030 - Completing Item 19 (Claimant Not Disabled) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Title XVI
Check the box in block A in all cases when a disability is not established. Also check
this block when disability ceased prior to the month of filing or prior to 1/74. If
the FO or DDS had entered a date in Item 15 for a presumptive disability, and the
SSA-831 is paper, draw a line through the checkbox and the check in item 15.
