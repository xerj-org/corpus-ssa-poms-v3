Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)

F. Parent claim
Check the box in block B in a parent's disability determination when the evidence
establishes that the parent was not disabled at the appropriate specified point. Enter
the date here. (For complete instructions on Parent claims, see Parent Claim to Entitle
Dependent Grandchild, DI 23505.005.)
