B. SSA jurisdiction for CDR
Jurisdiction guidelines for CDRs in the following areas are the same as the guidelines
for initial claims:
•
Foreign claims – see DI 20101.030B.
•
Claimants in U.S. possessions – see DI 20101.030C.
•
Career railroad or RR annuitant cases – see DI 20101.040A.
•
Changed-identity claims – see DI 20101.040B.
•
Emergency clause cases – see DI 20101.040C.
•
SGA cases – see Initial and Subsequent Work Issue Cases DI 24001.005.
