SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 31001.010 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Responsibilities When an Appointed Representative is Involved
Effective Dates: 07/26/2022 - Present | TN 26 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0431001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Events that end a representative's appointment
For actions and events that end the appointment of a representative and his or her
authority, see GN
03910.060A.
NOTE: If a claimant files a new disability claim, they and their representative must complete
a new written notice of appointment (i.e., Form SSA-1696 (Claimant's Appointment of
a Representative)) and submit it to the Field Office (FO).
