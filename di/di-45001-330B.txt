B. Other equitable relief situations in initial ESRD cases
Situations may occur which warrant individualized measures to grant equitable relief
at the initial level; for instance, the erroneous sending of a notice to the individual
that he/she has coverage which may in fact not have been established and to which
he/she is not entitled (such as later evidence of dialysis ending during the qualifying
period). In order to determine whether granting equitable relief is appropriate in
the particular case, review the guidelines in HI 00805.170. Be especially aware of the provisions of HI 00805.170A. and HI 00805.170B. Document the reasons for relief on Form SSA-5002.
