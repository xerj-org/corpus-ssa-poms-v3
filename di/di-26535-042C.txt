C. Who must receive correspondence and notices
In some instances, other individuals or entities who have a duty or responsibility
to act on behalf of or to assist a claimant or beneficiary must receive correspondence
or notices that the claimant also receives to fulfill the duty they have to the claimant.
1. Correspondence and notices for adult claimants
An adult claimant, including individuals who have attained age 18 in an age-18 redetermination
case, is the primary recipient of correspondence and notices.

2. Correspondence and notices for child claimants and adult claimants with a legal guardian
For claimants under age 18, the primary recipient of correspondence and notices is
the parent or legal guardian. For adult claimants with a legal guardian, the primary
recipient of correspondence and notices is the legal guardian.
