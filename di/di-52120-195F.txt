F. Attorney fees
The Ohio WC law allows Attorney fee payments that are a reasonable amount after review
of the application. Attorney fees paid in a lump sum agreement are not permitted to
cause a reduction in the individual's existing weekly compensation rate of more than
twenty percent (20 percent).
Attorneys typically take 1/3 from a lump sum settlement and 1/3 from an accrued period
award. When the attorney is listed on the printout as the payee, this is evidence
that there are attorney fee expenses. The amount of the fee should be verified with
the attorney.
