D. Waiting period
Under the comparability provisions, if a worker age 55 or older, is determined to
be statutorily blind and engaging in non-comparable SGA, the period of time beginning
with the first month of disability may be counted toward the waiting period while
the individual is engaging in SGA. Therefore, if SGA continues for 5 full months or
more, disability benefits may begin with the first month of non-SGA. This contrasts
with the requirement for blind workers in regular freeze status only, who must serve
a waiting period after the cessation of SGA in order to be entitled to disability
benefits.
