A. Procedure – Retirement Survivor Insurance (RSI) claimant not insured for Disability
Insurance Benefits (DIB)
The Field Office (FO) forwards Retirement Insurance Benefits (RIB) cases to the Program
Service Center (PSC) when there is a concurrent filing of a retirement claim with
a disability claim, or when there is an allegation of disability under the following
conditions:
•
The claimant does not meet the insured status requirements for Disability Insurance
Benefits (DIB); or
•
An earlier onset is not possible; and
•
There is no prior period of disability ending within 5 years before the month the
current disability began.
•
Use the Document Processing System (DPS) to prepare the notice. See General Notice
Policy - Title II and Title XVI - NL
00603.001 for FO notice instructions.
