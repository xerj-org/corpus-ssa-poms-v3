B. Definition
The term “caregiver,” as used in this section, means a person who has a
close, familial-type relationship with a child . A child's parent is usually a caregiver, but other individuals may also be caregivers.
For example, grandparents, siblings, other relatives, or non-relatives with whom the
child lives or spends a lot of time. There may be more than one caregiver. The applicant
is usually a caregiver.
