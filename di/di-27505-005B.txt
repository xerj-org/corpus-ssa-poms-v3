SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27505.005 - Late Completion of Timely Investigation: Diligent Pursuit
Effective Dates: 06/05/2008 - Present | TN 2 (06-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427505005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When it Applies
Diligent pursuit only applies to reopenings with defined time limits. Diligent pursuit
would, therefore, not apply to reopenings that fall under the “at any time” rule referenced in Conditions
for Reopening a Final Determination or Decision (DI 27505.001A.4.).
