D. Field Office (FO) processing
The FO forwards MQGE initial and reconsideration DIB, CDB, or DWB claims to the DDS.
The DDS can identify MQGE cases by the:
•
MQGE claim type in eView.
•
MQGE claim type indicated on the SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal)
in paper MQGE claims.
•
“MQGE claim for Medicare entitlement only” remark annotated on item 5 of the paper
SSA-3367 (Disability Report-Field Office) or the explanation section of the Potential
Onset Reason screen on the 3367 in eView.
