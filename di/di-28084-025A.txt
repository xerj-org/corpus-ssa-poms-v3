A. Introduction to completing Items 14 to 17
The disability determination services (DDS) will follow these instructions to complete
Items 14 through 17 when processing a continuing disability review (CDR) on the Forms
SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
–Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal – Title II).
