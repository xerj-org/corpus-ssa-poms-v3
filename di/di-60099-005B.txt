B. Title II program current rules
Under current Title II program rules, disability beneficiaries who complete their
trial work period (TWP) and subsequently engage in substantial gainful activity (SGA)
have their benefits suspended after a three-month grace period.
1. BOND rules
Beneficiaries in BOND, however, do not lose their benefits at this point. Their benefits
are reduced $1 for every $2 earned above the BOND yearly amount. In 2021, the yearly
amount is $15,720 for a beneficiary who is not blind or $26,280 for a blind beneficiary.
