SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27525.010 - Providing Due Process in Adverse Reopenings Involving Title XVI and Concurrent Title II and Title XVI Cases
Effective Dates: 12/06/2011 - Present | TN 1 (12-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427525010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Provide due process decision notices with benefit continuation language for title
XVI and common issue concurrent title II and title XVI reopenings that are adverse
to the claimant. The Disability Determination Services (DDS) does not provide a due
process pre-determination notice for concurrent cases.
