A. General guidelines for adult special senses and speech disorders CE report content
The CE report guidelines for adult special senses and speech disorders in this section
are in addition to the general CE report content guidelines in DI 22510.100, and the adult CE report content guidelines for speech and language impairments in
DI 22510.115.
