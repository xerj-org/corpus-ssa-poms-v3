A. Definition of disability
Since the Social Security Amendments of 1965, the disability impairment(s) preventing
an individual from engaging in any substantial gainful activity (SGA) must be expected
to result in death, or must have lasted or be expected to last for at least 12 continuous
months from the date of onset.
Consider an impairment likely to result in death if:
•
Based on established medical knowledge, and under the particular circumstances in
the case, it is found to be terminal; or
•
It actually results in death.
