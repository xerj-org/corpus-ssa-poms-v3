SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55002.060 - Add or Terminate Ticket Process
Effective Dates: 10/29/2002 - Present | TN 1 (10-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455002060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - Program Service Center (PSC) or Field Office’s (FO'S) may have to take
action to terminate or add a ticket in limited situations and only until current systems
limitations are updated
The system will not have the capability to add or terminate a Ticket due to the following
events until a future systems update is done. In the meantime, the FO or the PSC must
use the “Add/Terminate Ticket” screen to take the appropriate action:
1. FO termination of a ticket:
An individual's ticket will be terminated in the FO in the following situations (see
DI 55002.055A.2):
•
in the month SSA makes a final determination/decision not to reinstate entitlement
to or eligibility for benefits for the individual under the EXR provisions (Reason
8: EXR DENIAL);
NOTE: This is not the determination that SSA makes about provisional (temporary) cash benefits. It
is the EXR entitlement/eligibility determination requiring a favorable medical decision.
•
in the month in which the individual is eligible to receive another ticket because
SSA reinstates entitlement to or eligibility for title II/XVI benefits under the EXR
provisions (Reason 7: EXR REINSTATEMENT) (see DI 55002.010B.3.).
NOTE: Reinstatement of entitlement/eligibility requires a favorable medical decision. It
does not mean that SSA has decided to pay provisional (temporary) cash benefits.
2. FO issuance of a new ticket:
A new ticket will be issued to an individual in the following situations:
•
in the FO in the month in which the individual is eligible to receive another ticket
because SSA reinstates entitlement to or eligibility for title II/XVI benefits under
the EXR provisions (see DI 55002.010B.3.);
•
The system will not issue a ticket to an individual over 64 (see DI 55002.005B.1.). A new ticket will be issued to an eligible individual age 64 to age 65 by an authorized
user in the FO at the beneficiary's request.
NOTE: Reinstatement of entitlement/eligibility requires a favorable medical decision. It
does not mean that SSA has decided to pay provisional (temporary) cash benefits.
