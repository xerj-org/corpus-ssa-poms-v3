SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.190 - Initial or Subsequent Disability Claims - Claimant Engaging in Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA)
Effective Dates: 08/08/2008 - Present | TN 89 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010190 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
These instructions apply when the claimant is engaging in SGA or was engaging in SGA
through the date Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB) insured status was last met, and
a period of disability cannot be established nor is a visual impairment alleged Claims
Involving Work Activity - DI 11055.045. See Work Reports -DI 13010.020 - DI 13010.025 for Continuing Disability Review (CDR) cases involving work activity and Return to
Work Within a Year of Onset (Title II) DI 13010.105 for special considerations regarding a claimant's return to work within 12 months
of onset.)
For SGA denials for electronic claims entered in Electronic Disability Collect System
(EDCS), see Processing Field Office (FO) Determinations - DI 81010.140D. For claims not entered into EDCS, complete the SSA-831 (Disability Determination
and Transmittal) per Completing the SSA-831 - DI 11010.205.
