F. Report of direct expenditures
Form SSA-423 includes data on direct expenditures for medical development. When medical
evidence is obtained at SSA's expense, the bottom portion of Form SSA-423 should be
completed by checking the appropriate boxes and indicating the amount spent in U.S.
dollars. If the actual amount is not known at the time the case is otherwise completed,
an estimate should be used, since the figures are for administrative and program analysis
use only, not for audit purposes. It is not necessary to submit corrections of estimates.
In supplemental request cases, only additional expenditures need be reported. A copy
of the completed Form SSA-423 should be returned with the medical report.
