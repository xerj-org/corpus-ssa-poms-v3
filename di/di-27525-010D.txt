D. When to apply title II due process in concurrent claims
Title II due process provisions apply when processing a title XVI determination of
continuance concurrently with a title II adverse reopening determination. For example,
this situation occurs when we reverse the title II claim to a denial because the recipient
did not meet insured status requirements.
