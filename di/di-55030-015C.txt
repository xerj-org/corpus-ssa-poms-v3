Effective Dates: 02/26/2014 - Present | TN 4 (04-23)

C. STEP 3 – Review the alleged and gross earnings posted to the DCF
Review the alleged and secondary evidence of gross earnings posted to the DCF for
the months relating to a pending ticket worksheet issue(s) for a particular Ticketholder.
To do this, access the query earning (QERN) screen. (See DI 55030.035.)
