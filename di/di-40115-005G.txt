G. Processing amended determinations in the PC
If an amended determination is necessary and the determination is fully favorable
or less than fully favorable, the review technician must:
•
take jurisdiction of the case,
•
prepare a new SSA-831, and
•
provide a rationale in accordance with DI 26515.001.
In situations where the determination is less than fully favorable, the review technician
returns the case to the adjudicating component for preparation of the amended award.
