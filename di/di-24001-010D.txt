Effective Dates: 03/20/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Continuing Disability
Disability ceases when the severity of the physical or mental impairment does not
prevent the individual from engaging in substantial gainful activity (SGA) and medical
improvement or an exception occurs or, subject to the trial work period (TWP) provisions,
the individual demonstrates, by working, the ability to engage in SGA (except in certain
title II cases based on statutory blindness or title XVI cases).
