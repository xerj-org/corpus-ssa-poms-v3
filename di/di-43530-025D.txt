D. Charges for services
Expenses in connection with the performance of services in disability cases will be
authorized either when the original request is made, or later as circumstances dictate.
In many instances, payment for expenses will be the claimant's responsibility, in
which event SSA will so specify in its letter of authorization. SSA will not specify
the exact amount to be spent because costs vary throughout the world. All charges
must, of course, be reasonable and customary and not in excess of prevailing costs
for such services in the locality concerned. Costs of travel for claimants, local
attendants, or physicians shall not exceed those which the persons concerned would
incur were they traveling at their own expense.
