SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.040 - Adjudication and Notice Procedures for a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination
Effective Dates: 04/23/2024 - Present | TN 4 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Claims for new Title II and/or Title XVI disability benefits
A claimant is not entitled to benefits for any month after the month in which they
fail, without good cause, to follow prescribed treatment that would be expected to
restore the claimant's ability to work or result in the claimant being no longer disabled.
1. If treatment is prescribed, Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment (FTFPT) is established
within 12 months of onset, and no good cause is established
Deny the claim on duration.
2. If treatment is prescribed, FTFPT is established more than 12 months after onset of
disability, and no good cause established
Allow the claim for a closed period. Find that disability ended the month in which
the claimant failed to follow the prescribed treatment without good cause.
