Effective Dates: 04/01/2014 - Present | TN 7 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452140005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Verification of alleged WC/PDB
The information obtained on the SSA-546 or electronically through MCS is not considered
proof; therefore, all alleged WC/PDB information must be verified.
1.
Verify any allegation of WC/PDB payment rates, dates, periodic payments, lump sum
(LS) settlements, expenses and all changes to current WC/PDB rates, in accordance
with DI 52145.001 and DI 52145.005.
2.
For deferral policy, follow DI 11010.030.
3.
If reverse offset is alleged or known, follow policy in DI 52105.001.
