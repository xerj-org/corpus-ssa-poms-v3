G. Retirement insurance benefit (RIB) considerations
Payment of permanent partial (PP) other than impairment award, permanent total (PT)
and rehabilitation ends when a worker receives SSA RIB or is eligible to receive SSA
RIB or retirement benefits from a system that is an alternative to SSA RIB. The insurer
remains liable for temporary total (TT), impairment award (IA), and medical benefits.
