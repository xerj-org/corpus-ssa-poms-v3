G. Work issues in continuing disability reviews (CDR)
The FO has jurisdiction of all Title II work issues, including the determination of
a UWA in CDR cases. The FO develops and documents all work activity before forwarding
the case to the DDS if the beneficiary is working, regardless of whether or not they
are engaging in SGA see DI 24001.005..
