SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26520.010 - Initial Referrals and Re-referrals to the Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) Agency and Initial Referrals to Other Agencies
Effective Dates: 04/08/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426520010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

J. Policy - applicant is known to vocational rehabilitation (vr) (re-referral)
If the applicant is already a client of the State VR agency or agency for the blind
but had not been referred by the DDS referral should be made along with copies of
pertinent material (see DI 26520.030) from the applicant's file.
