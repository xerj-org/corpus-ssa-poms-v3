C. Viewing a Flag
DDS users can view CEF flags in eView on the Secondary Title Bar (see DI 81005.035B.). The “Flags” page displays the flag type, date added, the office that added the
flag, and any information keyed when the flag was added. To view the flag details,
select the description link. The Secondary Title Bar displays a maximum of two flags.
If more than two flags exist on a case, the word “More” displays after the title of
the second flag. To view the additional flags, select “More.”
Flags considered to be priority are displayed first, they are:
Special –S
Special-H
Special-F
Special-A
TERI Case
Military Casualty (MC)/Wounded Warrior (WW) Case
HIT
HALE Case
All other flags display in reverse alphabetical order.
