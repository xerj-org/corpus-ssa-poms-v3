SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.020 - Disability Determinations - Individual Convicted and Confined for a Felony Committed After October 19, 1980
Effective Dates: 04/09/1991 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
When a claimant has been convicted of a felony and confined, all of the medical evidence,
including that for any impairment which arose or was aggravated during confinement
for the conviction of the felony (as well as any felony-related impairments) must
be evaluated and a determination or decision prepared.
