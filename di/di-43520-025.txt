DI 43520.025 SSA-831-U5 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) - General
The SSA-831-U5 is used to transmit disability claims to the foreign disability examiners
(FDE) who act as the DDS for claims in which the disabled individual resides in a
foreign country. The FDE also uses the SSA-831-U5 to record the disability determination.
The district office, CA in the MOD, or CDA in the Claims Development Branch (depending
upon DO jurisdiction) is responsible for completing the identifying information in
items 1-14. The CDA SGA specialist will prepare the entire SSA-831-U5 for SGA denials
as outlined in DI 43520.040.
(See DI 43520.070, Exhibit F., for a sample SSA-831-U5).
