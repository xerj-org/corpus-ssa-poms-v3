SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28010.155 - Using the SSA Chronic Liver Disease (SSA CLD) and SSA Chronic Liver Disease – Pediatric (SSA CLD-P) Scores in Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 05/14/2025 - Present | TN 18 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428010155 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Individuals who have undergone liver transplant since CPD
If the individual has undergone a liver transplant since the CPD, use post-transplant
laboratory values to calculate the SSA CLD or SSA CLD-P scores.
