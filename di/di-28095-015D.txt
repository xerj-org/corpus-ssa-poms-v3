D. Authorized disclosure for a “Z” suffix
A beneficiary or recipient sometimes authorizes entitlement information for disclosure.
You can recognize the authorization by its title and the beneficiary or recipient’s
signature. It usually authorizes disclosure to an employer, labor union, welfare organization
or mental institution. In these cases, place a “Z” in Item 29 of the SSA-832 or SSA-833
(Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title
II or Title XVI), for example, 443-Z.
