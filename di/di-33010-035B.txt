SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.035 - Beneficiary (or Recipient) Change of Address
Effective Dates: 04/17/1990 - Present | TN 3 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. DHU actions upon change of address notification
1. DHU told of change by source other than FO, DDS, or DHU
a.
Notify the FO of the change.
b.
Notify the DDS of the change if the claims folder is in the DDS.
c.
Document confirmation of the change in the claims folder.
d.
Determine the service office for the beneficiary's (or recipient's) new address. See
GN
00904.010 for detailed instructions.
2. Address change does not change DHU
When the change of address does not change the DHU:
a.
Amend the DHU folder and the hearing schedule to reflect the current address,
b.
Resend the scheduling notice to the new address if appropriate,
c.
Use the new address in all future correspondence, and
d.
Upon completion of the reconsidered determination, send the claims folder to the FO
servicing the beneficiary's (or recipient's) new address.
3. Address change causes change in DHU
When the change of address causes a change in the DHU:
a.
Postpone any scheduled hearing and send a notice of postponement to the beneficiary
(or recipient) and representative, which explains the transfer of the case to the
new DHU,
b.
Document the claims folder. Maintain a record of the transfer in the DHU in-house
folder (for paper claims folders), and
c.
Transfer the case to the new DHU. Forward any relevant material along with the DHU
folder to the new DHU via an SSA-408 Route Slip showing the reason for the transfer
for paper cases. See DI
33010.040 for additional information on transferring a case to a different DHU.)
