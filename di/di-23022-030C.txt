SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23022.030 - Identifying and Receipting Compassionate Allowances (CAL) and Quick Disability Determinations (QDD) Cases

C. CAL condition identified after receipt of claim
The DDS, the Office of Quality Review (OQR), and the Office of Hearings Operations
(OHO) can manually add cases to CAL processing. If the adjudicator identifies an alleged
or existing condition or illness that would allow CAL processing, refer to the processing
instructions in DI 23022.055.
