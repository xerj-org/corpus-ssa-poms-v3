SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42001.015 - Material Which May Result in a Reopening
Effective Dates: 04/20/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442001015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Report of death during waiting period and after award processing
Notice of a claimant's death during the waiting period may raise a question of whether
the prior determination should be changed. The DE should examine the death certificate
to determine the cause of death. If death was traumatic (i.e., auto accident, etc.)
and not related to the impairment on which an allowance was made, the determination
should be reopened to a denial. (See DI 40105.000 for processing instructions.)
