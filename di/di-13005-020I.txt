Effective Dates: 01/25/2024 - Present | TN 30 (03-25)

I. Deferring a CDR
1. When to defer a CDR
The FO may defer a CDR in the following situations:
a.
Short-term
deferral:Reasons unrelated to the disability (for example, weather, travel, hospitalization
(short duration)); or
b.
Long-term
deferral:Life-threatening situations (only upon Regional Office approval), see DI 13005.070.
NOTE: The FO may receive a deferred CDR from the DDS as a ND and the FO must follow the
procedures in DI 13005.020I.2 in this section. For additional guidance on how to identify a deferral from the DDS,
see DI 28003.010.
2. How to handle a deferred case
The FO must handle deferred cases as follows:
a.
Short-term deferral: If FO expects to complete development within 2 months, retain the folder. When the
FO development is complete, the FO will use the CDRW screen to establish controls
for resuming, see MS 00501.013.
b.
Long-term deferral: Document the folder with the reason for the deferral and a specific diary date not
exceeding 6 months in the disability control file (DCF). Use the CDRW screen to establish
controls for requesting the folder and resume the CDR when the diary is mature, see
MS 00501.013. On the CDRW screen add the following in the Remarks section:
•
"Deferral for reasons unrelated to the disability on (date); not to exceed 6
months. This date is not the CPD, due to deferral of the determination."; or
•
"Deferral for a life-threatening situation with regional office approval received
on (date); not to exceed 6 months. This date is not the CPD, due to deferral of the determination."
Upon completion of the CDR, the FO must return the case to the PSC or WBFSO, according
to DI 13005.020H in this section.
NOTE: The FO must manually track these cases within the DCF using the CDRW. To ensure accurate
case management do not process a STOP CDR for these cases and hold the case until
the diary matures for a medical determination.
3. Requesting guidance for deferral of a CDR
Contact
your Regional Office
(RO)for guidance if it appears that the CDR is entirely inappropriate or it should be
deferred but is not covered by any of the instructions in DI 13005.020D or DI 13005.020E in this section. These situations should be rare.
