D. Special instructions
When completing the SSA-832 for rollback and non-rollback cases see DI 28075.015, and for 1619 cases see DI 28075.620.
When completing the SSA-833 for extended period of eligibility (EPE) cases see DI 28055.030 and for prisoner and felon cases, see DI 28065.035.
When completing the SSA-833 for a determination of blindness when blindness was not
previously established, see DI 13010.135I.
