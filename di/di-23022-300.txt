DI 23022.300 Small-Cell Cancer of the Large Intestine
COMPASSIONATE ALLOWANCES INFORMATION
SMALL-CELL
CANCER OF THE LARGE INTESTINE
ALTERNATE NAMES
Cancer of the Colon; Colorectal Small Cell Cancer; Small Cell Carcinoma of the Large
Intestine
DESCRIPTION
Small-cell
cancer of the large intestine is an aggressive (fast-growing) cancer that forms in tissues of the intestine and
can spread to other parts of the body. The cancer cells look small and oval-shaped
when looked at under a microscope. Small-cell cancer of the large intestine represents
0.2-.08% of all colorectal malignancies.
DIAGNOSTIC TESTING, PHYSICAL FINDINGS, AND ICD-9-CM/ICD-10-CM CODING
Diagnostic testing: The diagnosis of small-cell cancer of the large intestine is confirmed by pathology
and the use of immunohistochemistry. Specific staining for CD56, pancytokeratin, LMWK,
CK 7, and 19 are usually positive in small-cell malignancies.
Early diagnosis depends on:
•
Routine screening;
•
Occult blood study;
•
Sigmoidoscopy;
•
Colonoscopy;
•
Computed tomography (CT) scan; and
•
Endoscopy.
Physical findings: General symptoms may include:
•
Change in bowel habits,
•
Abdominal pain; and
•
Weight loss.
ICD-9: 209.0
ICD-10: C7A.019
PROGRESSION
Survival rates are poor, usually less than two years.
TREATMENT
Treatment for small-cell cancer of the large intestine is the use of combined chemotherapy
and radiotherapy as is used in small-cell cancer of the lung.
SUGGESTED PROGRAMMATIC
ASSESSMENT*
Suggested MER for Evaluation: The diagnosis of small-cell cancer of the large intestine is a pathologic diagnosis
which would be confirmed by a pathology report of colonoscopy specimen.
