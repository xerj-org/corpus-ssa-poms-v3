E. Cost-of-living adjustments (COLA)
The DWRF guaranteed amount increases yearly and was created to help permanently and
totally disabled individuals keep pace with the cost of living. If an individual is
below the DWRF guaranteed level, they will receive increases yearly based upon the
DWRF increase.
