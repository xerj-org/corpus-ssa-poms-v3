SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.160 - Receiving a Work Report after a Claim is Transferred to Disability Determination Services (DDS), Office of Disability Operations (ODO), or Processing Center (PC)
Effective Dates: 08/08/2008 - Present | TN 82 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure -- Title II claim forwarded to ODO or PC
If the Title II claim has been adjudicated, follow procedures in The Trial Work Period
- DI 13010.035 through DI 13010.060 for determination of a possible trial work period. If work activity represents SGA
and occurs within one year of onset, follow procedures in Return to Work Within a
Year of Onset (Title II) - DI 13010.105 and DI 13010.110 to determine if a reopening to denial is necessary.
