C. Procedures
In determining when to schedule a CDR, ask, in the following sequence:
•
Is cessation under the MIRS probable within 3 years of adjudication, taking into account the case facts and the MIE guidelines,
or will a vocational therapy diary apply? If so, set the MIE or vocational diary,
as appropriate.
•
If not, is a continuance under the MIRS probable, taking into account the case facts and the MINE guidelines? If so, set a MINE diary.
•
If a MIE, Vocational, or MINE diary is not appropriate, schedule the 3-year medical
improvement possible diary.
