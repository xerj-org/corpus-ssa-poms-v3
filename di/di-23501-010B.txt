Effective Dates: 01/26/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423501010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Not disabled when all impairments considered
When, after considering all impairments, the individual is not disabled, prepare a
formal denial determination. Include the following statement in the rationale: “Impairment(s)
not disabling; no effort has been made to isolate any felony-related impairment(s).”
