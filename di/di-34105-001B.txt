B. Special Senses
Emphasis has been given to the fact that the criterion for impairment of central visual
acuity is based on measurement of distant vision alone. The criteria for evaluating
monocular and binocular aphakia have been clarified and a single table for determining
central visual efficiency in phakic and aphakic cases is provided.
*In Listing 2.00 ff. all references to correction of central vision by “regular ophthalmic lenses” were deleted. However, 2.00B specifies that correction obtained by special visual
aids (e.g., contact lenses) will be considered only if the individual has the ability
to wear such aids.
