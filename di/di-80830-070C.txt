C. Examiner/Authorizer Actions
The technician will examine the case information in CPMS, eView and other electronic
resources available and determine whether the case can be effectuated as written by
the ALJ or AC.
If the case can be effectuated, the technician will prepare the SSA-831 and, in some
sites, any necessary instructions for module processing.
These instructions should be added to the PPC ACR “stub record”, the technician should
make sure that the ACR is coded for long-term retention, and in some sites, route
it to the module technician for processing.
In addition to the steps above, the technician will fax the completed SSA-831 for
inclusion in the EF, using the procedures in DI 80830.100.
