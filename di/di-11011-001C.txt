C. When to apply collateral estoppel
When an individual receiving Social Security benefits or Supplemental Security Income
(SSI) based on disability files a subsequent disability claim under a different title
or for a different benefit under the same title, the FO must adopt the prior favorable
disability determination or decision if the criteria in DI 11011.001A in this section are met.
EXCEPTION: The FO may not
apply collateral estoppel to establish that disability began on the new claim before
the EOD in the prior claim. For more information, see DI 11011.001F in this section.
