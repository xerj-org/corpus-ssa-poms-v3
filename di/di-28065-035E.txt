SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.035 - Additional Requirements for Completing Form SSA-833-U5 in Felony/Prisoner Cases
Effective Dates: 05/25/2023 - Present | TN 1 (05-23)

E. Item 34 - List Number
In addition to any other applicable list numbers, enter one of the following as applicable:
1.
608 - Cessation in which a felony-related impairment is excluded from consideration.
2.
610 - Benefits suspended due to confinement
3.
623 - Prisoner Case. Freeze Only established.
