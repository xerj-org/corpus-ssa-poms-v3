A. Disability interview forms
The disability report forms (e.g., SSA-3368 (Disability Report Adult)) provide the
Disability Determination Services (DDS) with information needed to make a disability
determination, such as a detailed explanation of the claimant's medical information,
functional abilities, and any work history. In the disability interview process, a
Field Office (FO) interviewer may assist the claimant to complete these forms before
case transfer to the DDS.
NOTE:When the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) establishes an EDCS 3368 for
a case with the "Homeless" flag, the system displays the message “Complete forms SSA-3373
(Function Report – Adult) and SSA-3369 (Work History Report) prior to transfer for
homeless cases. Refer to DI 11005.026 and DI 11005.025." At case creation, EDCS automatically adds the "Homeless" flag when the claimant's
address includes certain keywords, e.g.:
•
General Delivery
•
Homeless
•
No Address
•
None
•
Shelter
•
Transient
•
Unknown
For more information on how to identify a potential homeless case, see DI 11005.004.
