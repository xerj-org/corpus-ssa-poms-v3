A. Background for eDib and the EF
eDib is an electronic business process that enables all disability components to process
claims electronically. When processing a disability case in eDib, the EF stores most
evidence and determinations related to the disability determination. There are other
SSA systems, such as the Non-disability Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRED),
that store non-disability related materials for a case.
