A. Field office (FO) procedure for work-issue-only reconsideration requests
When a Title II claimant requests reconsideration of a cessation and work is the only
issue, develop the work issue per DI 13010.145.
NOTE: If the initial determination was made outside of eWork, the reconsideration is an
eWork exclusion. For instructions on processing eWork exclusion cases, see DI 13010.145D.
