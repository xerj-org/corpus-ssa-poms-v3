C. Concurrent cases
The title XVI due process requirements apply to both claims in a concurrent case when
making the same determination on reopening both claims. If a determination under only
one title is reopened (e.g., if the claimant's date last insured affects the title
II claim), the reopening action is subject to the procedures under that title.
•
For title II claims, follow the instructions in DI
27530.005A in this section.
•
For title XVI claims, follow the instructions in DI
27530.005B in this section.
•
Route the folder to the FO with the remark, “Title II and title XVI adverse revision
– benefits continuing.”
