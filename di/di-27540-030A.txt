SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27540.030 - Title XVI-Only – Goldberg/Kelly Payment Continuation Applies
Effective Dates: 09/22/2009 - Present | TN 3 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427540030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Goldberg/Kelly automatic payment continuation governs the following types of reopenings
and revisions:
•
failure to follow prescribed treatment; and
•
fraud or similar fault.
A predetermination notice is not required when Goldberg/Kelly automatic payment continuation
applies.
For information on the cases where the Disability Determination Services (DDS) sends
a predetermination due process notice, see Policy – Due Process for Adverse Reopenings,
DI 27525.001C.

Also:

DI 28080.025 Providing Due Process Notification for Title XVI-Only Cases

DI 28075.005 Failure to Cooperate (FTC) During a Medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
•
DI 28075.007 Whereabouts Unknown (WU) During a Medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
NOTE: Goldberg/Kelly procedures do not apply to Title XVI whereabouts unknown cases because
WU (S06) is considered an administrative suspension and does not result in a loss
of eligibility.
