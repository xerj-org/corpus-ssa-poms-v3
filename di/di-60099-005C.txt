C. Purpose of BOND
The purpose of BOND is to test whether gradually reducing benefits by $1 for each
$2 of earnings made over the BOND yearly amount leads to increased earnings and employment
for Social Security disability insurance (SSDI) beneficiaries. In addition, we offer
enhanced benefits counseling to certain BOND participants in a specific treatment
group. The BOND evaluation estimates the impact of reducing benefits and the effects
that counseling on work activity and earnings have on BOND participants.
