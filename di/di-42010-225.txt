DI 42010.225 Followups and Controls on Continuing Title II Interim Benefits
The Social Security Administration (SSA) must pay monthly Federal benefits prospectively
if the Commissioner does not make a final decision within 110 calendar days after
the date of the administrative law judge (ALJ) decision. These interim benefits start
the month before the month in which the 110th day falls and end the month before the
month the Commissioner issues a final decision.
