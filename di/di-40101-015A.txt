A. Foreign claims process
A Foreign claim is any one of the following disability claims:
•
Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB),
•
Disabled Widow(er)’s Benefits (DWB),
•
Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), or
•
Disabled Minor Child (DMC).
Claims filed by or on behalf of an individual temporarily in the U.S. who is physically
residing outside the:
•
50 States,
•
District of Columbia,
•
Virgin Islands,
•
Guam,
•
American Samoa, or
•
Puerto Rico.
The domestic field office (FO) loads the claim into Modernized Claims System (MCS)
and Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) per DI 43501.000-DI 43535.000 and process as follows:
•
Technical Denial: Process denial indicating PC8 on MCS DECI Screen; or
•
Insured: Load into MCS and EDCS and transfer both to FO E15 (which is the field office
for OIO PC8).
EXCEPTION: The U.S. northern border States FOs files and process Canadian claims. If the claim
involves totalization, the FO also develops and combines the Canadian earnings.
