A. PC responsibilities
All of the PCs have responsibility for processing and maintaining some disability
claims. However, ODO processes the majority of domestic disability and MQGE claims
and all the End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) claims. The OIO has responsibility for
processing disability, retirement, and survivor claims for any record where a claimant
resides outside of the United States.
