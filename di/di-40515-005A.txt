A. Introduction to due process requirements for Title II
The primary issue about ensuring due process is determining the point at which the
individual has had the opportunity to be sufficiently informed about the decision
in his or her case and to provide input about that decision prior to our effectuating
the adverse action. Due process requires that before SSA takes an adverse action that affects continued receipt of Title II benefits, we
provide the beneficiary:
•
notice of the proposed action and the reason for it;
•
a written summary of the evidence supporting the proposed action; and
•
an opportunity to rebut the evidence.
