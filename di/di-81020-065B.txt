B. DDS Case Processing System Document Controls
The DDS case processing system uses the DMA barcode to track evidence requests and
to ensure imaged documents are:
•
Stored in the correct CEF;
•
Filed in the appropriate section of the CEF; and
•
Labeled accurately.
For more information, see Document Management Architecture (DMA) Barcodes and Document
Indexing - DI 81020.035.
