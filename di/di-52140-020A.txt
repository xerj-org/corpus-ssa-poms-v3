A. Accessing the WC Resource Page
The Workers' Comp Resource
Page is a resource created by the eight processing centers (PCs). The Northeastern PC
maintains the website. The website provides links to various WC resources.
Established links to the website are in the Claims Authorizer, Title 2 Claims Representative,
and Technical Expert Resource kits.
