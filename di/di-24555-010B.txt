B. Requirements for using the CMS-2728-U3 to meet listing 6.03 or 106.03
An adjudicator may use the CMS-2728-U3 to make a fully favorable determination based
on meeting listing 6.03 or 106.03, if the medical source completes certain items on
the form. Items 1-12 should be completed in all cases, but non-completion of these
items does not preclude using the form to make a determination.
To meet listing 6.03 or 106.03 using the CMS-2728-U3, the following four items must
be complete:
•
Item 13 (Primary Cause of Renal Failure): However, if certain diagnosis codes are
present in Item 13 (see DI 24555.010C in this section), we cannot make a fully favorable determination without additional
evidence that shows that the renal disease is chronic and will meet the duration requirement,
•
Item 17 (Laboratory Values Within 45 Days Prior to the Most Recent ESRD Episode),
•
Item 30 (Date Regular Chronic Dialysis Began) or Item 31 (Date Patient Started Chronic
Dialysis at Current Facility), and
•
Item 58 (Attending Physician's Signature of Attestation) – A physician must sign the
form. Acceptable signatures include handwritten and electronic signatures. A stamped
signature does not satisfy this requirement.
In addition, if the form is the only evidence used for an allowance, a physician must
have completed the CMS-2728-U3 no more than 12 months prior to the date of filing. If
the physician signed the form more than 12 months before the date of filing, call
the dialysis center to confirm that the person is still receiving dialysis.
