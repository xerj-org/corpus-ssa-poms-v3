SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.325 - Medical Development Requests from Foreign Countries under International Agreements (Totalization)
Effective Dates: 04/20/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545325 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
SSA's international agreement requires parties to the agreement to assist each other
in obtaining consultative examinations (CEs) and medical evidence of record (MER)
when there is a claim under one country's system. These assistance requests differ
from regular totalization claims because:
•
no disability determination is required, and
•
the cost of the CE or MER is subject to reimbursement from the requesting foreign
country.
