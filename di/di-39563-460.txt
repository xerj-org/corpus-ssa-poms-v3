DI 39563.460 Medical Consultant Staff Controls
DDS management should ensure that the following concepts and procedures are formalized,
functional and controlled:
A.
Medical consultant staff time
B.
Specialty coverage
C.
Recruitment and salary
D.
Interaction with examiners
E.
Physician-to-physician telephone calls
F.
Relationship to the C/E process
G.
Case review techniques
H.
Priority arrangements
I.
Medical community relations
J.
Training of DDS professional staff
