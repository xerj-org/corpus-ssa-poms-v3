SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13050.015 - Protective Filing for Expedited Reinstatement (EXR)
Effective Dates: 07/16/2026 - Present | TN 35 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413050015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Protective filing policy
We may use an oral inquiry or written statement that indicates an individual's intent
to file for EXR as the filing date for the reinstatement request for Title II disability
benefits (including Medicare Part B) and Title XVI disability benefits. The rules
for a protective filing apply to an EXR request see GN
00204.010.
EXR protective filings are closed out with a 60-day notice, versus the 6-month notice
for Title II initial claims. Protective filing does not apply to the start month for
provisional benefits. For determining when provisional benefits begin see DI 13050.025 (Title II) and DI 13050.030 (Title XVI).
EXR protective filing date protects the filing date of a new application if the EXR
request is denied. A protective filing established for the wage earner also protects
the filing date of Title II auxiliaries who are listed in the wage earner's request
for reinstatement when we obtained intent to file from the wage earner, as stated
in GN
00204.010A.
EXR request for one title can be used as a protective filing for the other title.
For Title XVI cases, the date an individual contacts SSA also protects the filing
date for an eligible spouse, for more information refer to GN 00204.0140A.
