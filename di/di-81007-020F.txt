F. Editing IDRs
Except for the claimant’s name, birthdate, and SSN, IUs can add, delete, or modify
information in an IDR partial as often as they feel necessary. However, after submission,
the system locks the IDR and does not allow access.
If an IU wants to add, modify, or delete information after IDR submission to SSA,
the IU can:
•
Record the new or modified information in writing and submit it to SSA; or
•
Call SSA to report any changes.
NOTE: After propagating an IDR into EDCS, the FO must review it for completeness. To prepare
the IRD for transfer to the disability determination services (DDS) or hearing office
(HO), EDCS users can modify, update, and supplement the information in the disability
report as necessary.
