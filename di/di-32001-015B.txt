Effective Dates: 04/03/2026 - Present | TN 4 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432001015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. DDS responsibility for disability determination input:
1.
The DDS generally enters the disability decisional data for:
a.
Title II initial and reconsideration allowance and denial claims including statutory
blindness determinations.
b.
Title XVI initial and reconsideration affirmations allowance and denial claims including
statutory blindness determinations.
NOTE:If statutory blindness was previously determined and benefits ceased due to SGA,
a new claim for cash benefits should be sent to the PC rather than the DDS. See DIB
Attainment case processing in DI 13010.135G and DI 41001.001C.In the event a DIB attainment case is misrouted to DDS, the case should be returned
to the FO to forward to PC.
2.
The DDS does not enter the disability decisional data for:
a.
Any determination if:
•
Selected for OQR review;
•
Processed as a no determination case;
•
An adverse reopening;
•
The claimant is deceased; or
•
A type of claim change other than from disabled to blind is required and the claim
is being routed directly to the FO per the instructions in SM 05905.308.
b.
Title II determination if it is a:
•
Prisoner case (see DI 23501.000);
•
Trial Work Period case (see DI 13010.035);
•
Medicare Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) case (see DI 23540.000);
•
Totalization case (see DI 23550.000);
•
Continuing disability review (CDR) 301 case (see DI 28060.000); or
•
Extended period of eligibility (EPE) case (see DI 28055.000).
c.
Title XVI determination if it is a:
•
Systems Limitation case;
•
Reconsideration reversal; or
•
Favorable reopening.
