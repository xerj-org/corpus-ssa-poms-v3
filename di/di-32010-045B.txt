Effective Dates: 10/06/2021 - Present | TN 4 (10-21)

B. Routing CDR cases with potential Section 301 involvement
Return all Section 301 (individual enrolled in a Vocational Rehabilitation or similar
program) and potential Section 301 cases to the originating field office. See DI 14510.010 for detailed continuation and cessation instructions.
