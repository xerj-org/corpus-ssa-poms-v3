B. Deciding if a comparison point decision (CPD) listing is now met or equaled
In deciding whether a CPD listing is currently met or equaled, consider all subsections,
not just the particular one used in the CPD. Consider the prior listing section in
light of the relevant prior introductory material under that body system heading (e.g.,
read Listing 5.05 in light of 5.00).
If the CPD listing designated a time period for which an impairment is considered
disabling, see DI 28010.029.
