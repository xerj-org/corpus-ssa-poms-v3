DI 55002.040 What to Do When Unable to Determine Why A Beneficiary/Recipient is Not Ticket-Eligible
Procedure
FOs and PCs should contact the Regional Center for Disability Programs when:
•
a beneficiary/recipient requests a ticket; and
•
the DIBT or TKQY query shows the beneficiary/recipient was not selected to receive
a ticket (see DI 55002.025); and
•
the FO/PC is unable to determine why, after reviewing the MBR/SSR (see DI 55002.035B. and DI 55002.035C.).
