SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27010.015 - Deficiency Return on Effectuated Reconsideration
Effective Dates: 09/16/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427010015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. If claimant contact necessary
Do not mention the possibility of revising the determination or possible overpayments.
Tell the claimant:
•
His or her case has been selected for review by the Social Security Administration;
•
What additional information is needed; and
•
He or she will be notified of the final result of the review.
