B. Start and end date of offset
1. Offset start date
The start date of offset is the first month that a beneficiary utilizes the $1 for
$2 offset. The work indication code (WIC) “Z” on the MBR HISTORY field represents
this.
Participation has ended for all POD participants. POD offset within the participation
period may not be adjusted. POD offset may not be applied to benefits outside the
participation period.
2. Offset end date
The offset end date is equal to the end date of participation in the POD.
