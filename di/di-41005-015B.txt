B. Processing a continuance
1. SSA-833-U5 determination
The instructions in DI 41005.001 apply. Also see the next paragraph. DI 41005.035, Exhibit 11 contains an example of the completed form SSA-833-U5.
In item 9 of the SSA-833-U5, check “disability” and blocks 9A, 9J and 9J(1). Check and complete item 9D if appropriate (see DI 41005.001J.4.).
2. Notice
See NL 00701.425 for the proper completion of the SSA-L1013 notice.
3. Rationale
The technical rationale should be prepared on an SSA-4268-U4. See DI 41005.005.
