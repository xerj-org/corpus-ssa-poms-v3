D. Responding to an inquiry about Social Security Administration (SSA) programs
If an inquiry contains general questions or criticisms about SSA programs not related
to a specific claim, acknowledge the inquiry and take the following actions:
•
Electronic claims - complete an electronic assistance request according to the instructions
in DI 81020.100; or
•
Paper claims - send the inquiry and a copy of the acknowledgement to the regional
office , using a Form SSA-408; and
•
Include the remark: “Please respond to the inquiry.”
