E. Wechsler adult intelligence scale-revised (WAIS-R) - (generally available since January
1981)
Copyrighted and available for general use in 1981, it is an updated and renormed edition
of the WAIS and, as such, is a better test. It assesses a wide range of intellectual
abilities in those aged 16 through 74 and yields a Verbal, Performance, and Full Scale
IQ.
