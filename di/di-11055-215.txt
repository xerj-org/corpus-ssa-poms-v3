DI 11055.215 Resolving Representative Payee Issues
The field office (FO) has the responsibility for resolving representative payee issues,
per GN 00501.015. For additional guidance in determining capability, see GN 00502.020.
Be alert to the possibility of having the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
develop for capability.
