DI 23501.020 Prisoner Case Notices
Specific notices have been prepared to meet the requirements of prisoner cases. They
are used only when the exclusion of felony-related or confinement-related impairments
under section 223(d)(6) of the Social Security Act affects the outcome of the disability
determination.
The various model letters and rider paragraphs explain the general provisions of the
law and are used by themselves or with existing notices as shown in Prisoner Notice
Finder Chart - DI 23501.025. The notices are intended to supplement but not replace any requirements for personalized
notices.
NOTE: A personalized disability notice (PDN) is needed with model letter S, U, and V.
