DI 52120.100 Kentucky Workers’ Compensation (WC)
Kentucky WC awards rarely pay out as outlined in the agreement. Therefore, it is almost
always necessary to verify the actual payments paid. An award may stipulate weekly
payments then be issued in whole or in part as a Lump Sum. Also, see in this section
DI 52120.100F concerning crediting of attorney fees.
