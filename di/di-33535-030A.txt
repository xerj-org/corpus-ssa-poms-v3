A. Electronystagmogram (ENG) (generally available since January 1972)
Electronystagmography is an accepted, reliable, and accurate method for determining
the status of a patient's vestibular functions. The test is based on recording eye
movements based on the cornea-retinal potentials. The printed record of the nystagmus
makes it available for comparison with subsequent tracings. It is a better technique
for diagnosing true vertigo or labyrinthine disease.
