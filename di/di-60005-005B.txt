Effective Dates: 04/19/2023 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0460005005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Summary of GIFTT Waivers
The following GIFTT waivers apply to GIFTT participants and their spouses and children
in the same household:
•
Exclude the GI payments from income (see DI 60005.015).
•
Exclude the GI payments from resources for up to four years, during the 12 months
of receipt of GI plus a period of three years after receipt of the final GI payment
(see DI 60005.020).
