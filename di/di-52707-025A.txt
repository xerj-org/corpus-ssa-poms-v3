A. Procedures
Comply with requests from field offices (FOs) and disability determination services
(DDSs) for prior folders as expeditiously as possible.
Follow usual procedures when effectuating subsequent claims decided by the DDS, an
Administrative Law Judge, or the Appeals Council.
