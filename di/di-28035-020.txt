DI 28035.020 Disability Determination Services (DDS) Reconstruction of Prior Folder
CITATIONS:
Regulations - 20 CFR 404.1594(c)(3)(v), 416.994(b)(2)(iv)(E), and 416.994a(d)
IMPORTANT: These instructions incorporate policy and procedure for both electronic continuing
disability review (eCDR) and eCDR exclusion (paper) cases when there is a lost or
destroyed folder involved. If the comparison point decision (CPD) folder is a certified
electronic folder (CEF), these instructions do not apply.
