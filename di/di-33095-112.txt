Preparation of Form SSA-L1678.
Refer to Form SSA-831-U5 or Form SSA-832-U5 for completing the name, address, and
claim number. This letter needs one of the following fill-ins which will complete
the opening paragraph:
Fill-ins:
Explanation
We find that your disability began on (month/day/year).
Reopening to Allowance
We find that your disability did not begin until (month/day/year).
Reopening to Later Onset
We find that your disability did not end. Therefore, your payments will continue.
Reversal to Continuance
We find that your disability ended in (month/year). But you were disabled again in
(month/year). This means that your checks will start again as of (month/year).
