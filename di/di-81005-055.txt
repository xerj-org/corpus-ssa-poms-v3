DI 81005.055 Status/History Tab
The Status/History tab in eView displays a summary for each adjudicative level for
an eDib case in separate sections. The case status summary displays the following
information:
•
Claim type;
•
Level;
•
Date filed;
•
Current status;
•
Office;
•
Decision date;
•
Decision;
•
Regulation basis; and
•
Deciding office.
