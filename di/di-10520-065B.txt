B. Procedure for Review of IRWE Cases
When a diary matures on a case in which IRWE were material to a determination of either
SGA or SSI countable earned income, review the case according to the following procedures.
1. Development
Contact the beneficiary to:
•
ascertain whether there have been any changes in the amount of earnings or work expenses;
and,
•
assure that the expenses upon which the determination of SGA or SSI countable earned
income was based have actually been paid.
2. Verify Payment
a.
Obtain proper verification from the beneficiary (as discussed in DI 10520.025) that demonstrates that payment was, in fact, made in the months for which the determination
of SGA or SSI countable earned income was made.
b.
It is not necessary to verify retrospectively the person's need for and use of the
impairment-related item or service, unless evidence comes to the FO's attention which
indicates the item or service was not used, or suggests the item or service was not
needed, during the diaried period.
3. IRWE Material To Determination
Where IRWE were material to a determination of either SGA or SSI countable earned
income, and either there has been a change in the amount of earnings or of IRWE, or
development shows that the person did not pay for the IRWE for the entire period as
projected in the original determination, reprocess the work activity.
a.
Where the person continues to be eligible for benefits, inform them of the results
of the FO determination, remind them of the responsibility to report any future changes
in earnings or IRWE, and diary the case for another 12-month period (or less, if appropriate).
b.
Where the person is found to be engaging in SGA, see DI 13010.160 concerning cessation or suspension.
