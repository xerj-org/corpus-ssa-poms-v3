B. ICF #28 (WC/PDB)
If you are not experienced using this program, refer to DI 52170.001 - Manual Computations for Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
Offset to manually calculate the offset.
ICF #28 is less user-friendly than the newer offset computation functions. ICF #28
may be used to calculate the average current earnings (ACE) and the amount of offset
and a triennial redetermination. However, ICF #28 will not correctly compute a rate change after a redetermination. For ICF #28 program limitations,
click here.
