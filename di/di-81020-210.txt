DI 81020.210 Scanning the SSA-454-BK (Continuing Disability Review Report) into the CEF
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) is responsible for scanning the SSA-454-BK
into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) and has the option of local scanning or
contractor scanning. To scan the SSA-454 into the CEF:
1.
Print a barcode coversheet for Form SSA-454-BK;
2.
Scan and upload the SSA-454-BK to the CEF; or
3.
Send the paper document to the scanning contractor for imaging and retention;
4.
Retain the paper document according to paper retention guidelines in Document Retention
and Destruction - DI 81020.055.
NOTE: The Field Office (FO) is responsible for keying the SSA-3368-BK, Disability Report
– Adult, for Age 18 Redeterminations, into the Electronic Disability Collect System
(EDCS). If the DDS receives a paper SSA-3368 the DDS scans the SSA-3368 into the CEF
following the procedures for scanning the SSA-454-BK.
