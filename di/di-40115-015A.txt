SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40115.015 - Processing Center (PC) Action when Disability Determination Services (DDS) Establishes an Onset in Period Covered by a Prior Determination
Effective Dates: 03/07/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440115015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DDS determination when reopening is possible
When the Disability Determination Services (DDS) establishes an onset in a period
covered by a previously denied claim, refer the case:
•
to the reconsideration disability examiner (REDE) in the Office of Disability Operations,
and
•
to the disability processing specialist (DPS) in the Processing Center.
When the rules of administrative finality warrant reopening or revision, the REDE/DPS
can adopt the DDS determination and establish an onset that invades a prior period
of adjudication.
