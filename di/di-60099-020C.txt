C. April 2011 through September 2012
Abt actively recruited and enrolled volunteers for stage 2 by sending 20,000 letters
each month until we reached 12,600 volunteers. The BOND work CDR unit codes the DCF
indicating that the beneficiary is a BOND participant when a beneficiary volunteers
to participate in the demonstration project and is assigned randomly to a $1 for $2
treatment group. We do not code the DCF for those randomly selected to the control
group. For a definition of a BOND participant, see DI 60099.015A.
