D. Work experience categories
When evaluating whether a claimant can do other work, use the highest work experience
category reflective of the claimant’s PRW. If the claimant has no PRW, use the “no
work
experience” category.
1.
No work experience,
2.
Unskilled work experience,
3.
Semiskilled or skilled work experience, with no transferable skills, or
4.
Semiskilled or skilled work experience with transferable skills.
NOTE: If the claimant has semiskilled or skilled work experience, transferability of skills
may or may not be material to the determination. For information about when a TSA
is required, see DI 25015.017A.
