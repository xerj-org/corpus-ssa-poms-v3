DI 52165.025 Old Interactive Computations Facility (ICF) Function 9 (WC Lump Sum Proration) and
Function 28 (WC/PDB)
ICF functions #31, #32, and #34 replaced ICF #9 and #28. In some cases, ICF #9 and
#28 may be used to perform WC/PDB calculations. Use both programs with caution. There
are limitations that will compute incorrect calculations. Before using #9 and #28,
review the limitations on the Interactive Computations Facility (ICF) Resource Page
for WC/PDB Offset Computations – Click here.
NOTE: The last systems update to ICF #9 and #28 was prior to the 09/2001 release of ICF
functions #31, #32, and #34.
