DI 23510.020 Death after Filing – Title II (Chart)
Use this chart to determine required disability determination services (DDS) actions
regarding Form SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal), notification, and
any special routing instructions. For additional instructions related to these procedures,
see DI 23510.001 and DI 23510.010.
