C. OQR Quality Review Office Selection
The OQR review office is no longer limited to the regional OQR office when the current
case is a CEF and no prior paper folders are attached. It is very important that the
DDS verifies the current Paper Folder Indicator (PFI) and the prior PFI are accurate
before they close a case so the appropriate OQR review office is selected. For more
information about PFIs, see Paper Folder Indicators (PFI) - DI 81020.010.
1. CEF – Prior PFI is Blank
•
The OQR review office is not limited to the regional OQR office. In some situations,
OQR transfers these CEF cases from one office to another. When this occurs, NDDSS
may not display the final OQR review office code.
•
After OQR transfers the case, eView displays the current office with jurisdiction.
NOTE: When returning a QR case to the DDS, the Electronic Folder Interface (EFI) download
contains the correct OQR review office code. If the DDS needs to return the case to
the OQR review office, they must return it to the OQR office that is in the EFI download.

3. Prior Paper File Received in DDS While OQR is Reviewing Case
If the DDS receives a prior paper folder and OQR currently has jurisdiction of the
CEF, the DDS:
•
Adds a message to the CEF indicating that a prior paper folder was received during
the OQR Review process and is being mailed to OQR. The OQR office is responsible for
updating the Prior PFI; and
•
Mails the paper folder to the OQR reviewing component that has jurisdiction of the
CEF. For Quick Disability Determination cases, use overnight mail.
