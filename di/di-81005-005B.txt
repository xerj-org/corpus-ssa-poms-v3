B. Access eView
To access eView:
•
Use the eView desktop icon; or
•
Go to the eView website; or
•
Use interfaces from case processing systems that link directly to the eView application
(for example, Disability Determination Services [DDS] Legacy systems, Disability Case
Adjudication and Review System [DICARS], Case Processing and Management System [CPMS]);
or
•
Link to eView through Evidence Portal (EP) EDCS, and WAC.
