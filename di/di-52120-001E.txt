E. Cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs)
Cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs) information provided under this heading for each
State entry includes true COLAs, as well as increases in the State WC rate based on
other factors, i.e., a State legislative action, or an increase in the State average
wage. Consider the information provided under this heading to determine when you may
need to develop for an expected adjustment in the WC payment rate. Prepare a diary
to develop for the next expected adjustment.
