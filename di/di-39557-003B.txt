SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39557.003 - DDS Quality Performance Standards
Effective Dates: 07/31/2024 - Present | TN 1 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439557003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Quality Performance Standards:
1. Decisional Accuracy - 97 percent for combined title II and title XVI initial cases with a corresponding
decision accuracy rate of 99 percent.
2. Performance Accuracy - 90.6 percent for combined title II and title XVI initial (Allowances and Denials)
cases.
NOTE: These performance standards apply to all sites including Federal adjudicating sites.
