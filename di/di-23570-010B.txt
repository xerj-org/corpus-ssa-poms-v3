B. Notification for an age-18 redetermination
The FO must provide a notice initiating the medical redetermination in cases identified
as they attain age 18. The agency requires a special notice for those cases identified
by the DDS during a CDR as requiring a medical redetermination or in situations in
which a CDR was erroneously developed within 12 months after the age-18 medical redetermination
was made (and the medical diary has not matured or will not mature in the next 3 months).
The DDS must send the special notice prior to the issuance of a medical determination
and must inform both the individual and representative payee that a medical determination
will be processed using the initial adult standard. For an example of the medical
redetermination notice, see DI 23570.100.
NOTE: The DDS must send a separate notice to the disabled individual and the individual's
representative payee.
