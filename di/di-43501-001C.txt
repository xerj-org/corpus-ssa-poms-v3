C. Policy - performs ODO/PSC function
FDE's, with assistance of claims adjudicators and earnings review examiners, also
perform various ODO/PSC functions. These functions are:
•
Screen and route cases alerted for a medical CDR.
•
Develop, adjudicate and review substantial gainful activity determinations for initial
and continuing cases.
•
Screen, process and review annual CDR enforcements.
•
Implement Administrative Law Judge, Appeals Council and Court decisions and remands
for all PC8 jurisdiction cases.
