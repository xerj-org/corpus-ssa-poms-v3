Effective Dates: 01/26/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452165001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to use ICF
Use ICF when:
•
You need to make an input to add to, change, or delete the existing WC/PDB information
on the MBR; or
•
You are processing an A101 or EF101, WC/PDB involved, and the MCS EC offset computation
cannot be used.
