A. List of services
The following medical services may be contracted:
a.
Consultative Examinations
b.
Radiological Examinations
c.
Laboratory Testing
d.
Diagnostic Procedures
e.
Psychological Testing
f.
Psychiatric Evaluation
NOTE: See DI 39542.005 for Federal requirements of all contractors involving DDS functions.
