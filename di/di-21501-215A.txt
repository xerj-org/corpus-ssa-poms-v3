A. Blindness—Definition of Blindness
To be eligible for AABD because of blindness a person's vision must meet the following
definition: In terms of ophthalmic measurement, central visual acuity of 20/200 or
less in the better eye with correcting glasses, or a field defect in which the peripheral
field has contracted to such an extent that the widest diameter of visual fields subtends
an angular distance of no greater than 20 degrees.
