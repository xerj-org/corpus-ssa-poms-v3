C. Attorney fees under the AWCA
When evaluating attorney fees, consider the following:
1.
An attorney representing an injured employee may recover attorney fees subject to
limitations set forth in the law.
2.
Attorney fees paid or incurred by the employee in connection with the employee’s WC
claim are “excludable expenses.” See DI 52150.050 for more information on legal fees as excludable expenses.
