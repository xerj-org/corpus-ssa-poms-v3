C. Title XVI Claims -- MSSICS Processing
Do not use the language in Procedure When a Nondisability Denial Is Reversed SI 00603.011B.2., that says For a reopening that can be processed on MSSICS, choose DENIED CLAIM REOPENING
from the SSI Menu (MSSI) and complete the full path. Use the following instructions.
When a claim has been denied by the FO for failure to cooperate (N18) or another non-medical
reason (N01, N04, etc.), and the denial is reversed based on an actual or implied
reconsideration request, document the file with the special determination and the
interim notice described in Processing a Reconsideration Request of an Initial Denial
Based on Non-medical Issues - DI 12005.010.
NOTE: When a claimant, denied for failure to cooperate, later submits the requested development,
e.g., signed SSA-827s, within the appeal period, treat it as an implied request for
reconsideration.
If a DDS medical decision is required, you will have to update the existing denied
claim with the appropriate appeal information, and establish a new MSSICS claim.
1. DAY 1
Open an appeal event to record the appeal information, and complete these screens
with the following information:
•
ACAD - Enter an appeal level of RECONSIDERATION.
•
AISS Choose the appropriate appeal issue. For an N18 denial, enter an appeal issue of
OTHER, and type in N18 next to EXPLAIN.
•
AJAS Leave the APPEAL STATUS as 1' PENDING IN APPEAL CONTROLLING OFFICE.
•
Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) Development Worksheet
- Enter a receipt date for the RECON REQ issue.
•
AJAS - After receipting in the RECON REQ issue on the CCE Development Worksheet, complete
the APPEAL STATUS with '5' - DECISION EFFECTUATED.
•
ADIS - Enter DECISION TYPE as '1' - FULLY FAVORABLE, and enter the date of the interim
notice in FULLY OR PARTIALLY FAVORABLE EFFECTUATION NOTICE DATE.
•
Build the SSR. The establishment of the APPE segment on the denied SSR will provide work credit
for processing the SSI reconsideration, and the presence of the EFT date will prevent
the appeal from pending on the WMI Appeals List.
2. DAY 2
Input a MANUAL CLEARANCE (2/12) to the denied record.
REMINDER: Day two will be Tuesday after a weekend, or the second workday after a holiday.)
Establish a new claim in MSSICS using the same application date. You may propagate
the information from the MSSICS pending file that you just MANCLRd. Be sure to annotate
REOPENED PRIOR CLAIM on CMSC to exclude the record from statistics. When you build
the SSR, MSSICS will terminate the denied record and establish a new SSR in PSY:H80,
unless you do full development and adjudicate the claim to pay.
By following the steps shown above, you will establish a new claim that will be controlled
on the SSI WMI I/C PENDING list, and in the event the claim is allowed, most notices
will be automated by the system. Your office will also receive work credit for an
SSI RECONSIDERATION when you build the APPE segment to the prior SSR.
