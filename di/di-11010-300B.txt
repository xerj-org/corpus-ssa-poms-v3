SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.300 - Routing Concurrent Disability Insurance Benefits Retirement and Survivor Insurance (DIB-RSI) Claims
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)

B. Procedure routing the DIB claim to Disability Determination Services (DDS) for a Disability
determination
Use the following procedure:
•
Forward the DIB claim to the appropriate DDS unless it is excluded from electronic
processing. See Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions and Limitations DI 81010.030. For DIB claims entered into Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) see Creating
Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases DI 81010.020 and Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) DI 81010.085.
•
Do not delay sending the DIB claim to the DDS for the RSI claim development.
•
Document the official folder with any material pertinent to the DIB claim.
•
Complete development of the RSI claim, adjudicate, and route to the Processing Center
(PC) if there is an action to be taken. Due to signature proxy, frequently there is
no material to be routed to PC for the RSI claim.
•
Use the electronic cover sheet produced by MCS if there are paper materials to be
forwarded to PC for retention.
