SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.100 - Key and Volume Providers
Effective Dates: 09/15/2025 - Present | TN 8 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Key provider
A CE provider who meets at least one of the following conditions:
•
an estimated annual (fiscal year) billing to the Social Security disability programs
of at least $150,000; or
•
practice of medicine, osteopathy or psychology is primarily directed towards evaluation
examinations rather than the treatment of patients; or
•
does not meet the criteria in bullets 1 and 2 of this list, but is one of the top
five CE providers in the State by dollar volume, as evidenced by prior year data.
2. Volume provider
Usually, but not necessarily, includes a CE provider with estimated annual CE billings
of $150,000 or more.
