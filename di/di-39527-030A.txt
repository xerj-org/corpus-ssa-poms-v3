A. Policy for DDS budget request
1. Cost of DDS office space
The DDS must include in its budget request thecost
of DDS office space for its operations and specify any additional costs because of additional space or new space which will occur in the budget year.
2. Allowable cost for office maintenance
Office maintenance is an allowable cost and should be included in the budget.
