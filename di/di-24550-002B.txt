B. Procedure for using BMI tables
1.
For each set of weight and height measurements, calculate the BMI. Use the formula
for calculating BMI in 105.00F2c (see Digestive Disorders -- Child DI 34005.105). Or use the
CDC Child and Teen BMI Calculator at:
https://www.cdc.gov/healthyweight/bmi/calculator.html.
2.
Use the appropriate chart in subsection DI 24550.002C or DI 24550.002D and find the child's age (in years and months) in Column 1. If the BMI is equal to
or less than the value shown in Column 2, the BMI-for-age measurement is below the
third percentile.
3.
Repeat step 1 for each additional weight and height measurement in file.
4.
If there are at least three BMI-for-age measurements that are below the third percentile
in a consecutive 12-month period, the B2 criterion for listing 105.08 is met.
