D. SSA jurisdiction for disability claims from Northern Mariana Islands
Title XVI payments were extended to the Northern Mariana Islands effective January
9, 1978. Title II “coverage” of wages, etc., does not currently exist, however, there
may be instances where claimants have acquired status elsewhere or may be entitled
as a dependent of a number holder, e.g., disabled adult child. The Federal Disability
Examiner stationed in the Agana, Guam, field office has jurisdiction for preparing
the determination in Title II, Title XVI-only, and concurrent Title II-Title XVI disability
claims.
