A. Introduction on completing Item 20
Follow these instructions to complete Item 20 when processing a continuing disability
review (CDR) on the Forms SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal –Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of
Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal – Title II).
