A. Applying offset to CSRS disability benefits
Apply offset unless one of the exclusions in DI 52130.001 – Types of Federal Public Disability Benefit (PDB) Payments is met. Offset the gross
amount of CSRS disability before any deductions. Life or health insurance premiums
and other payroll type deductions are not excludable expenses.
