C. Cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs)
Annual cost of living adjustments given for Permanent Total (PT), which are under
reverse offset, after 104 weeks of benefits have been paid and are computed each July
1. Increases for the dates of injury prior to 03/26/2003 limited to 3 percent.
