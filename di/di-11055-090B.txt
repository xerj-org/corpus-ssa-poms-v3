B. Procedure for processing a request for withdrawal
Take the following actions:
•
If the claim is at the Disability Determination Services (DDS) and the FO processed a request for withdrawal, notify the DDS, advising the DDS to
stop development of the medical evidence, and to return jurisdiction of the case to
the FO.
•
If the claim development results in a denial before the withdrawal is processed, notify the applicant and process the claim as though there was no request
for withdrawal filed.
•
If the claimant requests a timely cancellation of an approved withdrawal request in
order to pursue the claim, process the claim as though no withdrawal had taken place.
