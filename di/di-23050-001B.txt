B. DDS role in applying prior entitlement months to the current Medicare waiting period
If the current onset is more than 5 years (7 years for DWB or CDB) after the prior termination, then the FO will
request DDS assistance with determining the Medicare waiting period information.
If the current onset is more than 5 years (7 years for DWB or CDB) after the prior termination, DDS will consider
whether:
•
the current impairment is the same as or directly related to the prior impairment;
and
•
the prior period of disability benefits entitlement ended 03/01/88, or later.
