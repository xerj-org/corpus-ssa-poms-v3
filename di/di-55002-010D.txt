D. Policy – requirements for assigning or reassigning a ticket
Individuals with tickets cannot automatically assign or reassign them to an EN or
State VR agency. Certain requirements must be satisfied and these are discussed in
DI 55025.001B.1., DI 55025.001B.2. and DI 55025.001B.5.
