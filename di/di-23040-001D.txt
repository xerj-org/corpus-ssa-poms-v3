SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23040.001 - DDS: Interpreters for Individuals with Limited English Proficiency (LEP) or Individuals Requiring Language Assistance
Effective Dates: 08/01/2023 - Present | TN 9 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423040001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Qualified Interpreter Sources
In priority order, the DDS will accept the following list of qualified interpreters
if they satisfy the requirements outlined in DI 23040.001B.3:
1.
DDS bilingual employees including bilingual CE providers, and
•
Employees of the Veterans Administration Regional Office in Manila,
•
U.S. Military Consular Officers,
•
Officials of foreign government liaison agencies, and
•
Designated employees of the Federal Benefits Unit of a U.S. Foreign Service Post,
who are fluent in the language that needs interpretation;
2.
Other bilingual State employees;
3.
SSA bilingual field office (FO) employees, see DI 23040.001E.2;
4.
Non-DDS interpreters in the community who have an agreement with the DDS to provide
interpretations and who do not charge a fee for interpreter services, for example:
•
members of church groups,
•
university employees or students,
•
employees of other Federal, State or local agencies,
•
ethnic social service agencies, or
•
members of advocacy groups.
5.
Employees of vendors contracted by the DDS to provide interpreter services. This category
also includes all interpreters working for the nationwide SSA TIS; and
6.
Non-DDS interpreters in the community who charge a fee for interpreter services; for
example:
•
members of church groups,
•
university employees or students,
•
employees of other Federal, State or local agencies, ethnic social service agencies,
or
•
members of advocacy groups
