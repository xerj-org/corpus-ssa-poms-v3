C. Processing the subsequent claim or reconsideration as a Non-CEF
If the subsequent claim or reconsideration is a CEF exclusion (see Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF) Exclusions - DI 81010.030),
•
Create a paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF) for the claim per Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF) Exclusions - DI 81010.030C; and
•
Follow procedures in New Claims or Appeal Filed While a Prior Claim or Appeal is Pending
before the Appeals Council (AC) – Title II or Title XVI - DI 12045.027.
