DI 52120.220 Rhode Island Workers’ Compensation (WC)
The Rhode Island Division of Workers’ Compensation is responsible for the administration
of their workers’ compensation law. WC insurance may be provided through a competitive
State fund, private insurance carriers, or employers may self-insure. Employees are
also permitted to assert their common law rights to opt out of the worker's compensation
system.
