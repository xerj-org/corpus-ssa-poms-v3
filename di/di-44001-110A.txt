A. Field office (FO) responsibilities
The FO processes railroad (RR) related claims and transfers specific RR claims to
Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR), location code V32, instead of the State
disability determination services (DDS). DCR will contact the local DDS for assistance
in obtaining medical and non-medical information.
DCR adjudicates the case and forwards it to the FO for final processing.
Complete FO instructions are located in DI 11050.000.
