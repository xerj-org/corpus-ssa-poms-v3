SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24503.015 - Evaluating Other Medical Evidence
Effective Dates: 03/24/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424503015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of other medical evidence
Other medical evidence is all other evidence from a medical source that is not objective
medical evidence or medical opinion. This includes judgments about the nature and
severity of the claimant’s impairments, medical history, clinical findings, diagnosis,
treatment prescribed with response, or prognosis. For claims filed before March 27,
2017, other medical evidence does not include the nature and severity of an individual’s
impairments, diagnosis, or prognosis. For more information, see DI 24503.005B.3. For more information about how to determine the filing date of a claim, see DI 24503.050 Determining the Filing Date for Evaluating Evidence.
