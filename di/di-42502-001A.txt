A. Introduction
As a result of class action court decisions, SSA is at times required to readjudicate
its past determinations. The special review criteria required by a court's order are not generally well known in areas outside of the State(s) affected by that order.
This becomes a problem if a claimant has moved out of the area affected by the court's order since DDS/FO personnel in the new State
may not be familiar with the pertinent review criteria.
