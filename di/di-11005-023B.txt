B. When to obtain the SSA-3368-BK
Obtain the SSA-3368-BK for:
•
Title II disability or blindness claims for Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB), Disabled
Widow(er)’s Benefits (DWB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), and Disabled Minor Child (DMC) claims.
•
Title XVI disability or blindness claims for an adult, that is, an individual who
is age 18 or over.
NOTE: Use the claimant’s own words whenever possible to document their information.
