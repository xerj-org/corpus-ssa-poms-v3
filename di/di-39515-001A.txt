SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39515.001 - Guidelines for Providing a QA Function
Effective Dates: 08/12/2025 - Present | TN 1 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439515001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy principle
The State will provide quality assurance (QA) function to ensure that disability determinations
are made accurately and promptly.
NOTE: If a State agency fails to meet minimum standards of performance, SSA may impose specific
requirements in the QA area as part of SSA's plan of delivering technical and management
assistance to improve their performance.
