SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27010.015 - Deficiency Return on Effectuated Reconsideration
Effective Dates: 09/16/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427010015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. ALJ hearing requested
Process case as promptly as possible as an informal remand according to the instructions
in DI 81020.119—Processing Electronic Informal Remands for certified electronic folders (CEF) or
DI 27520.001—Informal Remand (IR) Cases to the Disability Determination Services (DDS) for modular
disability folders (MDF) and:
•
Notify the reviewing component by annotating a copy of the SSA-1774 (deficiency transmittal)
to show that an ALJ hearing request was filed.
•
Route the case according to the instructions in DI
27520.005—Disability Determination Services (DDS) Action upon Receipt of Informal Remand (IR)
Cases.
