C. Attorney fees
Attorney fees are decided on a case-by-case basis. When the worker’s date injury was
prior to 1993 the employer usually paid the attorney fee, but workers injured 01/01/1993
or later are generally responsible for the fee. Note that only fees paid by the workers
are excludable WC expenses.
