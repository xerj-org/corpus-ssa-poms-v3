B. Procedure - reconsideration
1. Non-medical issue
For reconsideration of a non-medical issue:
a.
Associate the SSA-561-U2 and any related material with the folder.
b.
Forward to a reconsideration DE for processing.
2. Medical issue
For reconsideration of a medical issue:
a.
Associate the SSA-561-U2 and any related material with the folder.
b.
Forward to the appropriate DDS for processing.
