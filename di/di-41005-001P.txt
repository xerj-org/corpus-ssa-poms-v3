P. Item 16 - occupational years
This block is completed in only work issue cases.
Show the number of years that the claimant worked in the customary occupation, if
determined, or, if not determined, in the longest full-time occupation. When such
information is not reflected on the SSA-3369-BK, it may appear elsewhere in the file.
Where there are broken periods of employment in that occupation, determine the total
number of years the individual was employed in the shown occupation. If the number
of occupational years is unknown, enter “unk.”
