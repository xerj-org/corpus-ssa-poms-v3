J. What is respiratory failure and how do we evaluate
it? Respiratory failure is the inability of the lungs to perform their basic function
of gas exchange. We evaluate respiratory failure under 103.04E if you have CF-related
respiratory failure, or under 103.14 if you have respiratory failure due to any other
chronic respiratory disorder. Continuous positive airway pressure does not satisfy the criterion
in 103.04E or 103.14, and cannot be substituted as an equivalent finding, for invasive
mechanical ventilation or noninvasive ventilation with BiPAP.
