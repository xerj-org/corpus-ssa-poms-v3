DI 45001.070 State Buy-In Agreement
If the FO indicates the R-SMI premiums will be covered under a state buy-in agreement,
process in accordance with DI 45001.060A.3. If the FO does not mention state buy-in and SSI is involved, develop state buy-in
coverage postadjudicatively using reference HI 00815.030.
