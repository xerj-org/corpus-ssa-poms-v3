B. Procedure for combined determination rationales
•
Prepare a combined initial and reconsideration determination on the related claim
with the heading: Combined Initial and Reconsideration Determination.
•
Prepare a reconsideration determination on the pending claim.
•
Indicate in the discussion of technical issues the reason for the combined determination.
The other elements of the rationale as outlined in DI 27020.005 remain unchanged.
