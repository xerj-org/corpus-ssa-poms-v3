SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.035 - Completing Item 20 (Vocational Background) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 09/06/2012 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB)
NOTE: Completion of item 20 is not required.
1. Occupational Years
Show in the "OCC YRS" block the number of years that the claimant worked in the customary
occupation, if determined, or, if not determined, in the longest full-time occupation.
NOTE: When such information is not reflected on the SSA-3369-BK it may appear elsewhere
in the file.
Where there are broken periods of employment in that occupation, determine the total
number of years the individual was employed in the shown occupation. If the number
of occupational years is unknown, enter "unk" in the OCC YRS block.
2. Educational Years
In the “ED YRS” block, enter the highest grade of school or college completed by the claimant in
the following manner:
•
The claimant may have attended classes either full-time or part-time and in the daytime
or nighttime. “12th grade” will indicate the claimant completed high school, “13th
grade” will indicate one year of college, etc.
•
If the claimant also attended a specialized school, such as nursing school, accounting
school, ungraded vocational school, etc., also enter “See 34.” Then in Item 34, “Remarks,”
enter the type of school and years completed.
•
If the number of years of education is unknown, enter “unk” in the “ED YRS” block.
