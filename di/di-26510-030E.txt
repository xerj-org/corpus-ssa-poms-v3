SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.030 - Completing Item 19 (Claimant Not Disabled) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. CDB Re-entitlement
1. Block A
Check this block in CDB re-entitlement claims when the individual's impairment is
not disabling and the re-entitlement period has not expired.
NOTE: The FO will identify subsequent childhood disability benefit claims by entering “Re-entitlement
CDB Claims” in item 11. It will also show the beginning and ending dates of the re-entitlement
period.
2. Block B
Check this block in a CDB re-entitlement claim when the evidence establishes that
the individual was not disabled before the end of the re-entitlement period and the
re-entitlement period has expired. Enter the last day of the re-entitlement period.
