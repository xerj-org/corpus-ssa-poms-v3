SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.020 - Completing Form SSA-831 in Favorable Administrative Law Judge Decisions (Initial Awards)
Effective Dates: 09/19/2024 - Present | TN 23 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Form SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal)
Document the disability determination or decision on a Form SSA-831. When we receive
a fully or partially favorable title II hearing decision, we complete the Form SSA-831
using information in the decision and in our electronic systems, which include eView,
Evidence Portal (EP), Modernized Claims System (MCS), Case Processing and Management
System (CPMS), and Hearings and Appeals Case Processing System (HACPS).
Process and certify favorable hearing decisions upon receipt. If an error in the decision
prevents effectuation, follow the instructions in DI 42010.065 for referring a protest to the Office of Appellate Operations (OAO). If the decision
contains only typographical or clerical errors that do not rise to the threshold of
a protest, follow the instructions in DI 42010.075.
