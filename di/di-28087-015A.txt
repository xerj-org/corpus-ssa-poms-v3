Effective Dates: 05/28/2002 - Present | TN 1 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428087015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Guidelines are in place to screen disability applicants for referral to VR services.
The screening criteria in DI 28087.020 provide guides for increasing quality referrals to State VR agencies and for insuring
that all disability applicants with rehabilitation potential are given the opportunity
to receive services.
