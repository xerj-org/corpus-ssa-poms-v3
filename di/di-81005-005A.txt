A. eView Background
eView is a web-based application that enables users involved in case processing to
view, print, copy to compact disc (CD), and take specified action on the disability
information contained in the Electronic Folder (EF).
A Field Office (FO) establishes a case in eView once a user creates and transfers
or makes a determination on the case in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS).
See DI 81010.020 for information on creating an EDCS case.
eView is comprised of electronic folders. Each folder contains one or more cases.
Each case in an EF contains the following:
•
One or more disability claims (Title II Disability, Disabled Widow(er), Title XVI
Disabled Child, etc.) filed by an individual;
•
Each adjudicative level (initial, reconsideration, hearing, etc.) of a claim, when
appropriate; and
•
Documentation and data for each adjudicative level, including, but not limited to,
applications, development requests and results, worksheets, medical and non-medical
documents, determinations and decisions.
