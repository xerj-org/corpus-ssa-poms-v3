SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12026.025 - Field Office (FO) Processing Guidelines for Requesting a Reconsideration in a Medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Determination
Effective Dates: 08/12/2026 - Present | TN 19 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412026025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Reviewing the CDR reconsideration appeal forms
The FO must ensure that the appropriate appeal forms are complete and filed timely
(or establish 'good cause' for extending the time limit to file an appeal). All forms
must be stored in the electronic or paper folder, as applicable.
1. SSA-789 (Request for Reconsideration Disability Cessation Right to Appear)
When processing the form SSA-789, the FO must:
a.
Check that the form is complete with the following information.
1.
The individual indicated whether they wish to appear at the disability hearing;
2.
The individual completed the block for 'interpreter' and 'language' needs, if applicable;
3.
The individual or representative payee does not need to sign the form; and
4.
The FO must date the SSA-789 at the top of the form upon receipt.
NOTE:
A completed Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System (MSSICS) appeals
output can act in place of the SSA-789 in Title XVI and concurrent cases if the individual also provides a clear written indication of dissatisfaction with the initial CDR determination.
b.
If the SSA-789 is incomplete, take the following actions.
1.
Call the individual for the necessary information.
2.
If the individual has no phone or the attempt is not successful, send a call-in/come-in
letter and set a 15-day diary.
3.
The FO must document attempts to contact the individual on an SSA-5002 (Report of
Contact). The FO must place a copy of the SSA-5002 in the folder.
4.
If the individual does not respond within the 15 days regarding whether they want
a hearing or need an interpreter, forward the case to the disability determination
services (DDS) for resolution.
5.
Provide the individual with a copy of the SSA-789 if applicable.
6.
Provide the individual with the English or Spanish version of the form SSA-888 (Reconsideration/Disability
Hearing Procedures). Versions of these documents are located in DI 33095.025 or DI 33095.030.
7.
Provide the individual with the local and toll free telephone numbers for the FO and
DDS in their service area. Explain to the individual that we prefer they call the
DDS with substantive questions or if they need to report a change related to the disability
hearing.
NOTE:
The Social Security Administration (SSA) does not expect individuals to make long
distance phone calls. Individuals may call either the FO or DDS using the local or
toll free number.
2. SSA-792 (Statutory Benefit Continuation Election Statement)
The FO must provide the individual with a copy of their statutory benefit continuation
(SBC) election statement. For information about the SBC election statement, see DI 12027.010.
3. SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration)
The FO must ensure that form SSA-827 has the appropriate signature and date, as in
DI 11005.056.
4. SSA-3441-BK (Disability Report Appeal)
The FO must verify that form SSA-3441-BK is complete. If the form is incomplete, the
FO must take the following actions:
a.
Contact the individual if the SSA-3441-BK received was not fully completed or any
information appears to be incorrect.
b.
Note if there are any auditory observations made during the telephone conversation
with the individual.
c.
Document additional information that you received from the individual in the Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS), the form-3367 (Disability Report - Field Office)
or on an electronic or paper form SSA-5002 (Report of Contact).
1.
For Title II cases, see MS MCS 008.007 (Report of Contact [RPOC]); and
2.
For Title XVI cases, when documenting the report of contact in:
a.
Report of Contact page.
b.
Non-MSSICS, prepare a paper form SSA-5002 to document the additional information and add this form to the electronic or paper
folder.
5. SSA-3881-BK (Questionnaire for Children Claiming SSI Benefits)
In Title XVI childhood disability cases, the FO must verify that form SSA-3881-BK
is complete. If the form is incomplete, take the same actions described in DI 12026.025A.4 in this section.
