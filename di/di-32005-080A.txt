SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32005.080 - Disability Determination Services Reconsideration Title XVI-Only Reversal Routing – Paper Case Only
Effective Dates: 09/02/2010 - Present | TN 1 (09-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432005080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Appointed representative or representative payee
In all title XVI reconsideration reversals, where the claimant has an appointed representative
or needs a representative payee, use the SSA-408 (Route Slip) to advise the Field
Office (FO) to prepare and release a copy of the award notice to the representative.
When the claimant has an appointed representative, instruct the FO to add the following
to the original and all copies of the award notice: “A copy of this notice was sent
to (name of appointed representative) on (date notice was released).”
