B. Favorable revised determination not warranted
If the DDS cannot make a fully favorable revised disability determination in an IR
from the FO:
•
Route the folder to the Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) hearing office specified
on the Form HA-501 (Request for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) within 7 calendar
days from the receipt of the folder in the DDS; and
•
Indicate on the SSA-408 (Route Slip) that the DDS could not take favorable action
during the pre-hearing review.
