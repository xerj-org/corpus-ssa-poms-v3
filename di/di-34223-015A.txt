SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34223.015 - Respiratory Listings from 10/07/16 to 10/05/23
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 6 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434223015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Which disorders do we evaluate in this body system?
1. We evaluate respiratory disorders that result in obstruction (difficulty moving
air out of the lungs) or restriction (difficulty moving air into the lungs), or that
interfere with diffusion (gas exchange) across cell membranes in the lungs. Examples
of such disorders and the listings we use to evaluate them include chronic obstructive
pulmonary disease (103.02), chronic lung disease of infancy (also known as bronchopulmonary
dysplasia, 103.02C or 103.02E), pulmonary fibrosis (103.02), asthma (103.02 or 103.03),
and cystic fibrosis (103.04). We also use listings in this body system to evaluate
respiratory failure resulting from an underlying chronic respiratory disorder (103.04E
or 103.14) and lung transplantation (103.11).
2. We evaluate cancers affecting the respiratory system under the listings in 113.00.
We evaluate the pulmonary effects of neuromuscular and autoimmune disorders under
these listings or under the listings in 111.00 or 114.00, respectively.
