B. FO Responsibilities in developing work activity
In all cases with work issues, the FO completes form SSA-821-BK (Work Activity Report
- Employee) or form SSA-820-BK (Work Activity Report - Self Employment) to develop
the earnings and work activity, and all applicable work incentives to determine SGA
per DI 10505.035 or DI 10510.025. The FO completes Form SSA-823 (Report of SGA Determination - For SSA Use Only) to
document the SGA determination prior to sending the case to DDS.
