D. Reminders for choosing an impairment code for genitourinary disorders
When you are choosing an impairment code for genitourinary disorders, keep the following
in mind:
•
Do not use 9960 for favorable determinations more than one year after the date of the transplant.
Use the code for the residual impairment.
•
Do not use 7880 for determinations made after the attainment of age 18.
