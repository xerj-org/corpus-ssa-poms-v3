A. Introduction to personal contact during a CDR with a subsequent claim
The field office (FO) is responsible for explaining the possible impact of a subsequent
disability claim or a CDR on current benefits. For example, the filing of a disabled
widow(er) beneficiary (DWB) claim by a disabled individual should not raise a continuing
disability issue on the disability claim. For FO procedures involving potential collateral
estoppel claims, see DI 11011.001.
