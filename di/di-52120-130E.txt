E. Attorney fees
Attorneys cannot receive any more than 20 percent of the first $130,000 awarded. This
means the maximum attorney fee paid will be $26,000.
NOTE: Treatment of subdivision 7 fee reimbursements under Minn. Stat.176.081, subd.7, should
be deducted from the excludable legal expenses, because they serve as reimbursement
to the employee for attorney fees. See POMS PR 02505.026 Minnesota PR 09-134 for further information.
