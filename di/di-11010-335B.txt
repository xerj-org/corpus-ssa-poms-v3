SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.335 - Developing Survivor Claims when the Processing Center (PC) has Jurisdiction of the Official Folder
Effective Dates: 11/12/2008 - Present | TN 89 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010335 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure official folder in PSC
Use the following procedure:
•
If the claim is pending in a PSC when the claimant dies, the FO notifies the PSC and
forwards any necessary survivor development.
•
The PSC converts entitled auxiliary(ies) to survivor benefits, or processes the auxiliary(ies)
claims.
•
The DCR initiates action to reevaluate the medical determination if the cause of or
circumstances surrounding the death of the claimant requires reevaluation of the medical
denial determination.
