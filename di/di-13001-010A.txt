SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13001.010 - Component Responsibilities for Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 08/09/2023 - Present | TN 3 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Field Office (FO)
The FO responsibilities include:
1.
Conduct CDR personal contacts.
2.
Accept voluntary reports of medical recovery.
3.
Determine payment status under Title II and Title XVI.
4.
Develop work activity.
5.
Alert the Disability Determination Services (DDS) of potential Section 301 involvement
(DI 14505.001).
6.
Provide advance due process or notice of intended actions.
7.
Prepare and release Title XVI notices.
8.
Suspend and terminate benefits or payments.
9.
Maintain and update the Disability Control File (DCF).
10.
Identify situations that require a CDR, because of section 1619 eligibility and, in
some cases, issue CDR informational notices (see SI 02302.310).
11.
Coordinate CDRs for 1619 eligible recipients with pending redeterminations.
12.
Issue initial and reconsideration substantial gainful activity (SGA) determinations,
in Title II cases.
