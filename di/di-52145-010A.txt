A. When to use form SSA-1709 (Request for Workers Compensation/Public Disability Benefit
Information)
1.
Use the SSA-1709 to verify WC/PDB payments from the agency or carrier responsible
for the WC/PDB payment when the number holder (NH) is unable to provide the necessary
verification.
NOTE: It is permissible to obtain multiple SSA-1709s to be used for future development.
2.
Some states and other entities require authorization to release information to SSA.
3.
For PDF and eForm versions of the SSA-1709, see OS 15020.295.
