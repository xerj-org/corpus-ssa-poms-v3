DI 27540.025 Title II-Only Predetermination Notice Previously Mailed – Final Decision Notice Instructions
The following types of reopenings and revisions should have had a due process predetermination
notice:
•
failure to follow prescribed treatment;
•
change from an earlier onset to a later onset; or
•
fraud or similar fault.
For preparing and releasing the final determination notice, follow the notice procedures
in DI 27540.010. The italicized instructions in the notices in NL 00705.201 through NL 00705.266 indicate when and when not to use Statutory Benefit Continuation (SBC) language.
