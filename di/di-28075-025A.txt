A. Policy for MQGE cases
1. Eligibility criteria
To be eligible for Medicare coverage based on disability, an individual must meet
the definition of disability in the Social Security law.
2. Continuing disability reviews (CDR)
The MQGE individuals are subject to all conditions and reviews that apply to regular
Title II disabled individuals (e.g., extended period of eligibility (EPE), medical
and trial work period (TWP) diaries).
