DI 23020.050 Disability Determination Services (DDS) Instructions for Identifying, Developing,
and Processing Military Casualty/Wounded Warrior (MC/WW) Cases
MC/WW cases are identified as priority cases under DI 23020.001 and are given priority processing through all levels of case development and adjudication.
Receipt and assignment of MC/WW cases are expedited at the DDS.
