DI 27536.015 Optional Use of a Summary Decision Rationale for Reopening
This section addresses the summary decision rationale for reopenings. A summary decision
rationale may still be prepared when required by Disability Determination Services
(DDS) management (e.g., training purposes). Use an SSA-4268 Explanation of Determination
to prepare the reopening rationale. For simplified rationale instructions, see DI 27536.001.
