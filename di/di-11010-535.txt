DI 11010.535 Confidentiality of Medical Information
Policy
The basic principles governing disclosure of information are set forth in GN 03301.000. See GN 03305.001 for the guides, procedures, and special rules that apply to the
handling of requests for medical information from claimants, their representatives,
medical sources, and Federal and State agencies. Handle any request by an individual
for access to all medical records, whether or not they are considered sensitive, in
accordance with GN 03340.035.
