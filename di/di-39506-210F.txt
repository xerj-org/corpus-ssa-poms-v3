F. Submitting Quarterly Reports
Form SSA-4513 is required quarterly from each agency. Submit copies as follows:
•
the original will be sent to DA
•
the original will be sent to the SSA Central Office no later than the 25th day of
the month after the close of the quarter.
•
For example, for the quarter that ends 9/30/2025, reports will be due by 10/25/2025.
