B. RADS received at the pre-hearing (PH) level
When the DDS receives the additional evidence, the DDS will review the information
and perform the following actions:
•
Review the case.
•
Contact the component requesting the development if you wish to discuss the request.
•
Make a fully favorable determination if the evidence warrants such a determination. For additional information regarding when
a fully favorable determination can be made, see DI 29005.005C.
•
For electronic cases, transmit the fully favorable determination to the disability
hearing unit (DHU). If the additional development resulted from a quality review,
transmit the fully favorable determination to the OQR, and notify the DHU of the transmission.
•
For paper cases, send a copy of the fully favorable determination to the DHU. If the
additional development resulted from a quality review, send a copy of the fully favorable
determination to the OQR.
•
If the evidence does not support a fully favorable determination, return the case
to the DHU.
