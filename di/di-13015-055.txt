DI 13015.055 FO Retrieving The Title II and Title II Portion of the Concurrent TII/TXVI CDR Folder/Medical
Evidence
Procedure
DO NOT request the Title II medical folder prior to or during the initial pull period (see
DI 13015.050B. and DI 13015.050C.).
See DI 13015.055 ff. and the chart in DI 13015.100C. for a quick overview of the FO actions in a Title II CDR folder, for example, INTRN
FOUT TTRN location.
