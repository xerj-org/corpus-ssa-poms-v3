E. Destroying records - Policy
1. Authority
Federal agencies are required to follow regulations issued by the Archivist of the
United States governing the methods of destroying records (44 U.S.C. 3302). Use Only
the methods described in this section.
2. Requirements for the destruction of sensitive records
Sensitive paper documents (e.g., medical evidence) ready for disposal must be destroyed
in such a way (i.e., burning, pulping, shredding, macerating, or other suitable similar
means) that the material cannot be reassembled and used in an inappropriate manner
in violation of law and regulation.
Electronic records (e.g., CDs) must be destroyed in a manner that prevents reconstruction
and use of the record.
NOTE:
Non-sensitive paper records ready for disposal must normally be sold as wastepaper.
3. Methods of destruction
For procedures regarding destruction of data and paper records the DDS should refer
to:
