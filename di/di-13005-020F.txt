F. Routing career Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) cases
The FO must route career RRB cases to the Great Lakes Program Service Center (GLPSC)/Disability
Case Review (DCR) for processing and coordination with the Railroad Board, see DI 44001.101 and DI 44001.140.
NOTE:The GLPSC-DCR does not have jurisdiction of medical CDRs for the spouse or child of
a living career RR worker who has filed on their own Social Security Number (SSN).
Route these CDRs to the jurisdictional DDS in the claimants state of residence per
DI 11050.010.
