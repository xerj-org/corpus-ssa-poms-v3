B. Continue to certify VR continuous period of SGA
Continue to follow the instructions in DI 13510.010 on determining and certifying a VR continuous period of SGA for title II and title
XVI beneficiaries when a Form SSA-289-U3 (Request for Certification of SGA for Vocational
Rehabilitation Reimbursement) is received.
