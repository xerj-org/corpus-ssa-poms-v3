A. Operating policy
1. Performance monitoring
DDSs:
•
maintain a continuous performance monitoring system to carry out its responsibilities
for making timely and accurate disability determinations.
•
use all management information data, QA review results, and findings from internal
organization and workflow analysis.
•
work closely with its parent agency and the SSA regional staff to ensure timely and
appropriate actions are taken to maintain satisfactory performance and meet disability
program goals and objectives.
•
reach out to SSA Headquarters (HQ) components for additional support if needed.
2. Performance management program
a.
Each DDS should develop and implement a comprehensive performance a management program.
b.
The program design should:
•
enable DDSs to achieve and sustain a level of performance consistent with SSA disability
program objectives and operating goals; and
•
exhibit flexibility to accommodate changes resulting from legislations, court actions,
significant workload fluctuations, and operational demands.
