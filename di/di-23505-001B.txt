B. Applying sequential evaluation to a Title II DMC case
To determine if a child is under a disability for child-in-care provisions, use the
sequential evaluation process applicable to Title II Adult and CDB claims. See “Sequential
Evaluation” per DI 22001.000.
At step 3 of the disability evaluation process (Does impairment(s) meet or equal a listing?),
consider Part “B” of the listings first. If the child’s alleged impairment(s) does
not meet or equal a Part “B” listing, consider the Part “A” listings.
NOTE: The “functional equivalence” policy applicable to Title XVI child cases does not
apply to DMC cases, or any other Title II cases. For details, see DI 25225.001.
