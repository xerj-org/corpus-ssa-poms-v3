SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.045 - Chart of States’ Maximum Workers’ Compensation (WC) Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/22/2026 - Present | TN 53 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples of how to use the charts
1. Example 1
•
You are adjudicating a claim on 10/10/2005.
•
The State of Washington awarded WC benefits to this NH with a date of injury of 03/15/2001.
•
SSA date of entitlement to disability (DOED) is 06/01/2003.
•
Offset effective date is 06/01/2003.
•
The NH cannot or is unwilling to provide proof of their WC rate.
Take the following actions to obtain the weekly maximum for your computation and impose
the state maximum:
•
Click on WA for State of Washington charts.
•
Look for the rate that would be effective for 03/15/2001. That rate is $821.83 (after
07/01/00 but before 07/01/01).
•
Use $821.83 as the ongoing weekly rate for your computation unless the NH has provided
a different alleged rate.
•
Impose the state maximum rate effective with the current operating month (COM).
2. Example 2
•
You are adjudicating a claim on 10/10/2005.
•
The State of Maine awarded a WC lump sum settlement to this NH with a date of injury
of 03/31/2001.
•
SSA DOED is 06/01/2003.
•
Offset effective date is 06/01/2003.
•
The award does not provide a specified period or start date, or a weekly proration
rate, and there were no prior periodic payments from which to obtain a rate.
Take the following actions to obtain the weekly maximum for your computation and impose
state maximum:
•
Click on ME for the State of Maine charts.
•
Look for the rate that would be effective for 03/31/2001. That rate is $458.83 (after
7/01/00 but before 07/01/01).
•
Impose the state maximum rate effective 03/31/2001, the date of injury or illness
for WC (see DI 52150.060D.2 and DI 52150.060D.3).
