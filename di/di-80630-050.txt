DI 80630.050 Case Transfers to PC
During the implementation period, all cases processed electronically will be received
in the PC as paper folders.
The transferring electronic component will print out any pertinent information that
is in their legacy system, CPMS, DICARS, VERSA, etc. That information can not be transferred
electronically to the PC at this time. They will combine that printed material and
the material in the EF, including SSA-827’s, and mail the material to the receiving
PC.
The route slip should alert the PC that material exists in an electronic form in the
EF.
The receiving PC should call up the electronic data via eView and print out any miscellaneous
material not already in the paper folder which may be pertinent to the disability
application.
