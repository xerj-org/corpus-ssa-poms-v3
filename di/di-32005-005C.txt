C. Triple claims for the same individual
Maintain triple claims in two folders, e.g., Supplemental Security Income (SSI), DIB,
and DWB filed on same individual. Triple claims follow the same routing procedures
as the concurrent claims with two folders, see DI 32005.005A. in this section.
•
If the case is selected as a DQB quality assurance review sample case, send both folders
to the Disability Quality Branch (DQB).
•
If the title II claim is a denial and normal routing shows the file should go to the
Office of Disability Operations (ODO) as a review case, route the folder to the FO
instead. (ODO-CO sampling does not apply to triple claims).
