A. General
Narcolepsy is a chronic neurological disorder characterized by recurrent periods of
an irresistible urge to sleep accompanied by three accessory events:
1.
Cataplexy—attacks of loss of muscle tone, sometimes with actual collapse, during which
the individual always remains conscious.
2.
Hypnagogic hallucinations—hallucinations which occur between sleep and wakening.
3.
Sleep paralysis—a transient sensation of being unable to move while drifting into
sleep or upon awakening. In addition, some persons have periods of automatic behavior
and most have disturbed nocturnal sleep.
