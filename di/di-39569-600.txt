DI 39569.600 Violations and Fraud in the Social Security Administration Programs and Operations
The Social Security Administration (SSA) must protect its programs against fraud,
waste, and abuse. The Office of the Inspector General (OIG) investigates:
•
allegations of fraud, waste, and abuse within SSA programs and operations; and
•
suspected violations involving disability cases processed by federal employees, Disability
Determination Services (DDS) employees, and contractors.
