SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32010.075 - Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Section 301 Reconsideration of Initial Cessation Determination' Cases
Effective Dates: 06/09/2022 - Present | TN 5 (06-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432010075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. Electronic CDR (eCDR)
Add a 'Potential Section 301 Case' flag in the Electronic Disability Collect System
(EDCS) to identify that the beneficiary is participating in a Vocational Rehabilitation
(VR) or a similar program.
NOTE: Children under the age of 18 who are participating in the programs described
in DI 14505.010B.2. through DI 14505.010B.6. are eligible for Section 301 benefits. If a beneficiary indicates they are participating
in an appropriate program including Promoting Readiness of Minors in SSI (PROMISE),
add the Section 301 flag to the case.
