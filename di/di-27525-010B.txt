SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27525.010 - Providing Due Process in Adverse Reopenings Involving Title XVI and Concurrent Title II and Title XVI Cases
Effective Dates: 12/06/2011 - Present | TN 1 (12-11)

B. Title XVI and concurrent adverse reopenings
Statutory benefit continuation or Goldberg/Kelly provisions continue benefits for title XVI and concurrent reopenings.
For reopenings governed by Goldberg/Kelly, see DI 27540.030.
Benefit continuation as provided under the Act (as amended by P.L. 98-460) requires
the claimant to request benefit continuation. For reopenings governed by P.L. 98-460,
see DI 27540.015.
