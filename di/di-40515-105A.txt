A. What the Processing Centers (PCs) do in Statutory Benefit Continuation (SBC) cases
In SBC cases, the PCs are responsible for processing:
•
Disability cessation output based on field office (FO) direct input, see SM 00614.000;
•
FO cessation/adverse reopening faxes via the MADCAP system; and
•
Critical payment system (CPS) output based on FO CPS reinstatements, see SM 00635.720.
