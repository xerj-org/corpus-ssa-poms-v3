DI 40515.025 Allegations of Improper or No Due Process
•
Review the folder/electronic file/record and other repositories of notice documentation,
including the Online Retrieval System (ORS), when there is an allegation of improper
or no due process.
•
When the folder/electronic file/record confirms that benefits were suspended or terminated
without proper due process:
a.
Reinstate benefits from the date of the original suspension or termination action;
b.
Continue benefits until due process is provided; and
c.
Return the folder either to the Disability Determination Services for remedial due
process notice action or to the field office after reinstatement.
