Effective Dates: 07/02/2026 - Present | TN 10 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Requesting the subpoena from DA
1. When Form SSA-1272 is completed
Retain the DHU control copy of Form SSA-1272 Subpoena- Disability Hearing.
Request the subpoena via memorandum with the SSA-1272 attached.
NOTE: If a hearing has been scheduled, you may need to make the request by phone or the
hearing may need to be rescheduled to allow time for the subpoena to be issued. Place
the claims folder copy of the SSA-1272 returned by DA in the claims folder.
2. Request involves persons who worked on case
a.
Be sure the requirements in A.2. above have been met.
b.
Decide if information can be obtained by written questions. If it can, request that
the beneficiary (or recipient) or representative submit written questions.
c.
Remove questions that are not relevant (e.g., asking whether the beneficiary (or recipient)
is disabled under the Social Security Act).
d.
Submit the questions to the DDS with a cover memorandum explaining the situation.
Attach copies of the documents in question to refresh the memory of the individual
to whom the questions are posed.
e.
If written questions are not sufficient to prove the facts in question:
•
send a written memorandum to the DDS or DA requesting the presence of the involved
individual,
•
make all efforts to accommodate the schedule of the individual, and
•
give the document in question appropriate weight in consideration of all the evidence
of record if the individual refuses to appear. Also, consider whether a CE is warranted.
3. Subpoena denied
a.
The person denying the subpoena request (i.e., the DHO, SHO, or DA), will explain
the rationale for the denial.
b.
The DHO will state that a subpoena was requested and denied, and will include the
rationale for the denial in their determination.
4. Subpoena issued-individual fails to comply
If a subpoena was issued and the individual fails to comply with it:
a.
Refer the matter to DA, and
b.
Send a copy of the subpoena and a cover memorandum explaining the situation to DA.
