DI 43530.015 Inquiries About Benefits, Questionnaires--FAM 241.3-2
All posts (other than those which stock SSA application forms) will maintain supplies
of the following social security forms:
Form
Title/Purpose
SSA-784F
Retirement or Disability Insurance Benefit Questionnaire, for use where the worker
on whose social security account benefits are claimed is alive; and
SSA-485
Questionnaire Regarding Survivors Insurance Benefits, for use where the insured worker
is deceased.
