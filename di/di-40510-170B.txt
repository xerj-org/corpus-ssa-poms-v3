SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40510.170 - Processing Cases Returned From DDS After CDR
Effective Dates: 07/27/1992 - Present | TN 14 (08-92)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510170 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Allowance
If the case is an allowance:
•
Route folder to the appropriate technician to update the medical reexamination diary
data.
•
Route the folder to the typist for preparation and release of the final award notice
per Exhibit 4, DI 40510.185.
2. Disallowance
If the case is a disallowance:
•
Route the folder to the appropriate technician to terminate coverage first, with subsequent
routing to the typist for notice action.
•
Request the typist on an SSA-573 to prepare and release a coverage termination notice,
Exhibit 5, DI 40510.185. Retain a copy of the notice in the claim folder.
•
Hold the case for the appeals period.
