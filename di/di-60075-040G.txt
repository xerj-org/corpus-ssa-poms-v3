G. Auxiliary payments
Any auxiliaries receiving benefits based on a POD participant’s record continue to
receive full benefits until the POD participant was put into full offset. As long
as the POD participant was eligible for a partial benefit amount, the auxiliaries
continued to receive full monthly benefits. If the POD participant was in full POD
offset (LAF SQ.), any auxiliary benefits also stopped due to the POD participant’s
work activity (LAF SQ).
