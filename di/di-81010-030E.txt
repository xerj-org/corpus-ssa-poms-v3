E. Converting existing CEF to paper
There are certain scenarios that require the CEF to be converted to paper case processing.
When converting a CEF to a paper case, the FO will:
1.
Open the corresponding case in eView.
2.
Lock the case for editing in eView.
3.
Select the Status/History Tab and check the box a paper folder has been created for
this filing.
4.
Add a message (with no expiration date) explaining the reason for converting the case
to paper and the date the case was converted to paper.
5.
Unlock the case in eView.
6.
Print sections A, B, and D of the case using single sided printing.
7.
Discard the barcode cover sheets.
8.
File the printed documents in the corresponding sections (A, B, and D) of the paper
MDF.
