C. Possible future resumption of WC
Some agreements between the number holder (NH) and the employer and/or WC carrier
after a third party settlement call for the WC payments to resume at a certain date
(usually a number of years in the future). Review the documents carefully for any
possible future WC payments and set a diary to re-contact the NH one month before
the WC is set to resume. Development will be needed when the diary matures – do not
merely assume that payments have resumed or will automatically start up again.
NOTE: The PC jurisdiction may change by the time the diary matures. Be sure the diary will
mature in the correct PC.
