C. PSC allowance and denial process
The PSC processing of actions relating to EXR provisional benefits, Medicare, and
reinstatement allowance and denial processing are in SM 00856.100 to SM 00856.400
and HI 00801.164 to HI 00801.167. EXR notices are available in DPS and AURORA.
