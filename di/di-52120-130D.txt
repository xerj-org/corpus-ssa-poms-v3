D. Cost-of-living Adjustments (COLA)
Minnesota provides a COLA increase on WC payments. The COLA increase may have an affect
on offset.
•
For Date of Injuries (DOIs) 10/01/95 and later
The first COLA is paid on the 4th anniversary of the DOI. Future COLAs are then paid every year on the anniversary
date of the injury.
•
For DOIs 10/01/92-09/30/95
COLAs were paid on the anniversary date of the injury and the first COLA was delayed
until the 2nd anniversary.
•
For DOIs 10/01/75-09/30/92
COLAs were paid every year effective on October 1st.
•
For DOIs prior to 10/01/75
There were no COLA increases.
