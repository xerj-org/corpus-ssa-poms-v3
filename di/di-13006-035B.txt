SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13006.035 - Field Office (FO) Procedures When Appealing an Age-18 Redetermination
Effective Dates: 10/13/2021 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413006035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. SBC
Always offer SBC and obtain a signed Form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other
Person) regarding the election to continue or refuse benefits if the disabled individual
or representative payee files an appeal within 10 days of receipt of the notice. Always
discuss good cause if the disabled individual or representative payee files after
the 10 day period, even if the disabled individual or representative payee does not
inquire or declines the election of the SBC, see DI 12027.010.
NOTE: Never attempt to dissuade a disabled individual or representative payee from filing
an appeal or electing SBC.
