SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28057.020 - Effectuating Expedited Reinstatement (EXR) Determinations
Effective Dates: 11/24/2015 - Present | TN 8 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428057020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
The disability determination services (DDS) make one of the following determinations
on a request for EXR:
1.
Unfavorable determination for not "same as or related"; or
2.
Unfavorable determination due to medical improvement (MIRS); or
3.
Unfavorable determination due to failure to cooperate; or
4.
Favorable determination under MIRS.
NOTE: For paper cases, DDS sends the folder to the field office (FO) upon determination.
