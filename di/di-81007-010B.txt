B. Claims leads and referring IUs to IDRs
Agents at SSA’s 800 phone number and field office (FO) representatives must follow
existing procedures to establish claims leads whenever they refer an individual to
the iClaim/i3368 or i3820 websites. They must take action appropriate for appeals
when they refer an individual to the iAppeals/i3441. TSCOG - TC 31507.020. - Handling
iClaim or i3368 Inquiries - provides instruction for handling calls from IUs. TC 31507.035.
– Handling Calls from an Individual Who Intends to Complete the Adult Disability Report
(i3368). - has instructions regarding how to handle calls about the i3368. TC 31510.030.
– Handling Calls from an Individual Who Intends to Complete an Internet Child Disability
Report (i3820). - has instructions regarding how to handle calls about the i3820.
