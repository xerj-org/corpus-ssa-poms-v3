A. Background
Most of the following information on brachial plexus injuries (BPI) was originally
prepared in collaboration with the United Brachial Plexus Network (UBPN), and presented
in a Factsheet that was distributed in an administrative message. This information
pertains primarily to infant birth injuries, however, much of it is also relevant
to adults and older children who sustain BPI injuries. For more detailed information
about BPI, and the latest developments in this specialized field of medicine, visit
the UBPN website at www.ubpn.org and the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke’s BPI page at http://www.ninds.nih.gov/disorders/brachial_plexus/brachial_plexus.htm.
