A. DDS procedures for responding to field office (FO) requests for capability assistance
If the FO is unable to make a capability determination and requests DDS assistance,
the FO will notify the DDS by:
•
completing item 12 of the SSA-3367 (Disability Report – Field Office) capability remarks
section in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS);
•
completing the SSA-3368-BK (Disability Report – Adult);
•
completing the SSA-3820 (Disability Report – Child)
NOTE: If the FO changes the DDS capability opinion, the FO will annotate the folder by
completing item 34 of the SSA-831 or a SSA-553 (which is stored in section A of the
electronic folder).
