B. Medical Denial
1. Processing Earnings Computation (EC) Exclusions
If the new claim is a denial and cannot be processed through EC, prepare a Manual
Adjustment Credit and Award Data Entry (MACADE).
SSA-1418 inputs are required for District Office Workload Report (DOWR) credit.
2. Updating the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) with denial data
If disability is not established for a claimant already on the rolls, the FO must
make an appropriate systems input to establish or update the MBR (see MSOM COMMON
005.017 Develop for Child Disability Determination).
If systems input, such as Modernized Claims Systems (MCS) or Postentitlement Online
System (POS) is not possible,the FO should use MACADE to update the MBR.
