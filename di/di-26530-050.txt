Footnotes:
[1]For information on the regulation basis codes, see DI 26510.045B.
[2]The third element of the PDE.
[3]The last element of the PDE.
[4]N44 is used for all DC/BC denials at step 2 of the sequential evaluation process (i.e.,
not severe), including those with an allegation of visual impairment.
[5]N43 is used for all DC/BC denials at step 3 of the sequential evaluation process (i.e.,
does not meet, medically equal, or functionally equal the listings), including those
with an allegation of visual impairment.
[6]N39 is used for both failure to follow prescribed treatment (FTFPT) and drug addiction
and/or alcoholism (DAA) material denials. See DI 26510.045B.
[7]Use 330(D) for FTFPT or 330(E) for DAA material.
