C. Non-countable income
The POD determined offset using a participant’s monthly gross earnings when paid.
The POD did not deduct paid time off (PTO) from the gross total.
Non-countable income (NCI) was only involved when a beneficiary entered the POD in
a partial year. The POD counted the earnings paid prior to random assignment from
the beneficiary and documented it as NCI in the POD Automated System (PAS). The PAS
subtracted the NCI total from the yearly Master Earnings File (MEF) total when calculating
the End of Year Reconciliation (EOYR) for the initial POD offset year.
