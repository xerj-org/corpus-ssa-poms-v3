B. Description of the EDCS Routing Form
The EDCS routing form contains:
•
the office address information used to route the case from one office to another;
•
case identifying information including claimant name, social security number (SSN),
claim type, and claim level; and
•
EDCS folder number (sometimes used to search for the case in eView).
NOTE: The EDCS folder number is not an SSN.
