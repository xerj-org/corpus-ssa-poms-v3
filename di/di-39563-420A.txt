SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39563.420 - Case Controls
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439563420 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
An effective management case control system should allow the DDS to:
1.
locate promptly all pending cases;
2.
readily determine what action was taken on cases processed by the agency;
3.
provide timely and accurate data necessary for required reports;
4.
furnish pertinent data to facilitate the evaluation of the unit's accomplishments
as a basis for identifying possible areas requiring corrective action; and
5.
identify aged cases so that priority actions can be taken to complete the processing
of the case.
