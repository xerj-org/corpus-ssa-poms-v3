E. Processing work CDRs for blind beneficiaries
Use eWork to develop and control work CDRs for blind beneficiaries. However do not
use eWork to process a work determination if the beneficiary is age 55 and older,
or not entitled to a TWP based on one of the exceptions in DI 13010.135D in this section. The Office of Central Operations and Program Service Centers maintains
jurisdiction for all work CDRs for blind beneficiaries age 55 and older, and all determinations
that cannot process through eWork.
