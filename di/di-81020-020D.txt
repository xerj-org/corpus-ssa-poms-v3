D. Appointed representative data
If the DDS receives a Title XVI-only case with an appointed representative and the
appointed representative data is not included in the Electronic Folder Interface (EFI)
download:
•
Check EDCS for a Representative Involved flag and an Appointment of Representative
Form SSA-1696; and
•
Key the appointed representative data (name, address, telephone number, fax number)
into the case processing system to ensure that copies of letters and notices are sent
to the representative.
REMINDER: The FO receives an alert that appears on the EDCS Action Page. The action item appears
as, Request FO to add Client Contact.
NOTE: EDCS/eView/EFI no longer distinguishes between attorney and non-attorney representatives.
An appointed representative is always referred to as Appointed Representative.
