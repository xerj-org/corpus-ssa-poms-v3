Effective Dates: 06/05/2020 - Present | TN 2 (06-20)

D. Disposition of the SSA-2455
If the SSA-2455 cannot be stored or recorded in the Modernized Claims System (MCS),
Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS), or the ICF, fax the form into the Non-Disability
Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRED) or the electronic disability (eDIB) folder,
as appropriate.
NOTE:
If processing center (PC) action is required, the FO will also fax the form to the PC’s paperless fax number.
