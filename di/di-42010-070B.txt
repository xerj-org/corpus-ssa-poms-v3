SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.070 - Administrative Law Judge Issues Closed Period Decision Where Disability Ceased More Than 14 Months before the Month in Which the Application Was Filed
Effective Dates: 05/04/2009 - Present | TN 16 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
When an ALJ decision is impossible to effectuate for payment due to the claimant's
disability ceasing more than 14 months before the month in which the application was
filed as explained in DI 42010.070A, the technician refers the matter to the Office of Appellate Operations (OAO). See
DI 42010.080 for protest memorandum guidelines and procedure.
