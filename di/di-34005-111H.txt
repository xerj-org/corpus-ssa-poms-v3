H. What are benign brain tumors, and how do we evaluate them under 111.05? Benign brain tumors are noncancerous (nonmalignant) abnormal growths of tissue in
or on the brain that invade healthy brain tissue or apply pressure on the brain or
cranial nerves. We evaluate their effects on your functioning as discussed in 111.00D.
We evaluate malignant brain tumors under the cancer body system in 113.00. If you
have a vision impairment resulting from your benign brain tumor, we may evaluate that
impairment under the special senses body system, 102.00.
