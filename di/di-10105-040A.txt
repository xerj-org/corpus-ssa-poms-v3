SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.040 - Effect of Retirement Insurance Benefit (RIB) Entitlement on Establishment of Freeze
Effective Dates: 06/27/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy-effective date 9/65
An individual's entitlement to any other benefit will not preclude establishing a
period of disability beginning at a later date. A freeze cannot begin after attainment
of full retirement age (FRA) although an application may be filed after FRA (See GN 00204.000 and DI 10105.080).
