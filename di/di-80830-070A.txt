SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 80830.070 - Special Situations – Office of Disability and Adjudication Review (ODAR) Reversals
Effective Dates: 05/11/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0480830070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Hearing Offices (HO) now use the Case Processing and Management System (CPMS) to track
and route their cases electronically. CPMS, in conjunction with the electronic folder
(EF), will contain enough information for Processing Center (PC) technicians and examiners
to effectuate the Administrative Law Judge’s (ALJ's) decision.
