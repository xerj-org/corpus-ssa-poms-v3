SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40525.005 - Processing S-0 Alerts
Effective Dates: 01/14/2010 - Present | TN 1 (10-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440525005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. When alert is generated
Each month, the Regular Transcript Attainment and Selection Pass system generates
an S-0 alert (see SM 00619.050).
2. Who is identified
The alert identifies disabled individuals who have been in suspense status for at
least 6 months.
3. Contents of alert
•
Social Security Number,
•
Age of suspense action, and
•
Month account was selected by the system for review.
4. Who processes alerts
Disability examiners (DE) in the PCs process the alerts.
NOTE: S-0 alert code will remain on the MBR until final action is effectuated.
