DI 28001.055 Cases Involving Blindness, Title II
Disability Determination Services (DDS) may receive Title II continuing disability
review (CDR) blindness cases only if the issue to be resolved is whether the individual
is blind or whether blindness, previously established, continues.
The field office (FO) or Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO) will
ordinarily resolve issues involving work activity by blind persons. Comparability
of work issues will be resolved, in CDR blind cases, by OEIO- Office of Disability
Operations (ODO)/Disability Review Section (DRS).
