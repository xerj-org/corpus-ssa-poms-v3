Effective Dates: 10/23/2009 - Present |

C. Studies and feedback
Present findings and conclusions from special studies to management as soon as possible
after the study is completed so that recommendations can be addressed promptly.
Management should provide QAU with feedback concerning implementation of study recommendations.
