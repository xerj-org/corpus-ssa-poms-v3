Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055090 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When the claimant requests withdrawal of his or her application and the Field Office
(FO) approves the request, discontinue development and process the claim. In this
situation, an SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal) is not required.
