C. Field office (FO)
It is possible for IUs to enter false or erroneous information into iClaim, iAppeals,
and IDRs that satisfies screening criteria. Since it is possible that IUs can pass
through the identity checks and other screening criteria within those systems by accident
or design, FO employees must:
•
Review each iClaim, iAppeals request, and IDR carefully;
•
Query SSA systems (for example, Appointment Referral Calendar screens, MBR, SSR, MCS,
MSSICS, etc.); and,
•
Contact the claimant by phone or mail to resolve discrepancies.
