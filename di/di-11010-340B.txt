B. Procedure - DDS notification to FO - Title II
The DDS may modify their notification procedures for allowances to include telephone/teletype
contacts, routing a copy of SSA-831 (Disability Determination Form and Transmittal)
to specific FO personnel, flags, etc. The regional office works with individual DDSs
and FOs to arrange notification procedures in those offices in which such a procedure
would be feasible.
