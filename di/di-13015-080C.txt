SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13015.080 - Unable to Locate Medical Folder after Initial FO Search - Detective Work
Effective Dates: 02/06/2024 - Present | TN 19 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413015080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - Documentation
Document on the SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) all efforts to obtain the folder(s) for follow-up purposes and
to inform the next person/component who handles the case in order to reduce duplication
of efforts. For an example of how draft a detailed SSA-5002 (Report of Contact), see
DI 13015.250.
Keep all pertinent queries in file. Circle/highlight pertinent information, e.g.,
on the NUMI, circle/highlight any name change. If unable to locate the folder(s),
proceed to DI 13015.070.
