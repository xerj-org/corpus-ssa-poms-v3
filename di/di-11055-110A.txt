SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11055.110 - Emergency Clause that Allows Transferring Jurisdiction of the Medical Determination to the Office of Central Operations (OCO), Program Service Center- Disability Case Review (PSC-DCR), or Disability Quality Branch (DQB)
Effective Dates: 04/21/2026 - Present | TN 95 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
In an attempt to expedite disability determinations, an emergency clause exists allowing
SSA and the State to enter into an agreement allowing OCO, DCR, or DQB to help process
disability determinations when the Disability Determination Services (DDS) has large
number of disability claims pending. The agreement specifies the effective period
and the class or classes of cases the DDS may transfer.
