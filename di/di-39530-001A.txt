SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39530.001 - DDS Equipment -- General
Effective Dates: 05/03/2023 - Present | TN 1 (05-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439530001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Funding
Subject to appropriate Federal funding, the State will provide adequate equipment
to facilitate accurate and prompt disability determinations.
2. Title/Ownership
Title to equipment rests with the State but ownership is transferred to SSA on:
•
the date the State is notified that SSA will assume the disability determination function;
or
•
the date SSA is notified that the State wishes to terminate the relationship.
3. Obtaining equipment
The disability determination services (DDS) may obtain equipment through:
•
Purchase
•
Rental
•
Lease/Purchase agreement.
4. Information Technology equipment
Information technology (IT) equipment is to be considered separately from other equipment.
Standards and requirements applicable to IT equipment are found in specific chapters
in DI 39536.000. All other references in DI 39536 to "equipment" are irrelevant to IT equipment.
