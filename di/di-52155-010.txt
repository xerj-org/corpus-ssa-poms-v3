DI 52155.010 Modernized Claims System (MCS) Earnings Computation (EC) and Workers' Compensation/Public
Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
After you complete the MCS claims path, always request a preadjudicative MCS EC request.
If a disability claim includes WC/PDB common screens, review those EC screens.
Always request the Case Display and the Claim Display screens from the Earnings Comp
Determination menu page (MCR1).
