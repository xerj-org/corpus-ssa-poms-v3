F. Valvular heart disease
Valvular heart disease requires documentation by appropriate imaging techniques, including
Doppler echocardiogram studies or cardiac catheterization if catheterization results
are available from a treating source or other medical source. Listing-level impairment
is usually associated with critical aortic stenosis in a newborn child, persistent
heart failure, arrhythmias, or valve replacement and ongoing anticoagulant therapy.
The usual time after valvular surgery for adequate assessment of the results of treatment
is considered to be 3 months.
