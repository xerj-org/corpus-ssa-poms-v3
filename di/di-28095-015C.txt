C. Attorney or other authorized representative for an "A” suffix
In some cases, a beneficiary or recipient authorizes another person to act on their
behalf in pursuing their case and asserted rights before SSA. It is important for
both the disabled individual and their representative to receive a copy of the determination
notice. To notify both parties of the determination, enter suffix “A” in Item 29 of
the SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and
Transmittal-Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal-Title II). For accuracy of information, verify the name
and address of the representative in Item 24 (Remarks) of the initial or reconsideration
SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title
XVI or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal-Title II).
NOTE: A representative payee is the person designated to handle benefits on behalf of an
incapable beneficiary. The duties and responsibilities are to receive, disburse, conserve,
and account for benefits belonging to a beneficiary. A representative payee may also
act as a legal representative and seek a fee.
