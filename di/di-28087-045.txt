DI 28087.045 Advising SSA of VR Screening Results
Regardless of whether screening is performed by DDS examiners or a special VR screening
unit, it is essential that the screening decision be recorded on the disability determination
form (SSA-832-U5 or SSA-833-U5).
NOTE: Referrals must also be reported on the State Agency Operations Report (SAOR).
