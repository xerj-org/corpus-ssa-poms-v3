F. Procedure when ODO requests assistance from the FO
ODO may request assistance from the FO by email to:
•
Obtain information about the service provider, or
•
Determine the status of a beneficiary’s participation in a VR or similar program,
or
•
Determine whether such program ended,
If ODO requests FO assistance, obtain an updated SSA-4290-F5 (Development of Participation in Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) or Similar Program)
and complete parts I and II. Return the SSA-4290-F5 to ODO using the EP's 4290 Barcode.
Select the "Yes" on the Document Information screen, to make the transmission actionable
when PC involvement is required.
