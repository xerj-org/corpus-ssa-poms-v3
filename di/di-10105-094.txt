DI 10105.094 Field Offices Identifying and Documenting Prisoner Status for Title II Disability
Benefits
NOTE: Prisoner case restrictions do not apply to title XVI claims. For title II and title
XVI concurrent case processing instructions, see GN 02607.780.
We must determine prisoner status when we adjudicate a title II disability claim.
To prevent erroneous payments, it is essential that you work with the adjudicating
component, e.g., the Disability Determination Services (DDS), to identify and document
title II cases where a felony or confinement-related impairment may exist.
