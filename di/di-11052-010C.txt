C. Process award when a Medicare record not established and LAF not E
If no Medicare record is established and the LAF code on the MBR is not “E”, process
an award per the instructions DI 11052.010D.1. in this section. (We establish the claimant’s ESRD under his or her own SSN with
a Beneficiary Identification Code (BIC) of “T”.)
