G. DC WC forms
The District of Columbia does not have standardized WC forms that have WC information
useful to SSA. However, within 16 days after final payment of WC has been made, the
employer must notify the Mayor:
•
the date of the first WC payment,
•
that final payment has been made,
•
the total amount of compensation paid, and
•
the date to which compensation has been paid.
