C. Procedure for Title II — substantial gainful activity alleged in DIB case
Enter regulation basis code “N1” or “N2” when the claimant:
•
Is engaging in SGA, as determined by the field office;
•
Alleges a visual impairment; and
•
Has an impairment that does not meet the definition of statutory blindness.
