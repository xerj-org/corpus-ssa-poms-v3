SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.100 - Permanent Disregard of Impairments Incurred or Conditions Aggravated in the Commission of a Felony
Effective Dates: 08/04/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Any physical or mental impairment(s) arising or aggravated (but only to the extent
of the aggravation) in connection with the commission of a felony is to be permanently
disregarded for all disability program purposes. (These restrictions do not apply
to title XVI cases.)
