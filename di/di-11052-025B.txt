B. Denial notices
For disallowances processed through Earnings Computation (EC) using code 109, you
can use dictated paragraph HIBD01. Include the information from DI 45001.076 ESRD Medicare Disallowance as appropriate on the NOT3 screen.
If the denial notice cannot be requested through MADCAP, the technician will release
a denial notice after the denial has posted to the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR).
