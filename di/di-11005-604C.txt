C. Screening for CAL at the initial interview or when reviewing an iClaim
All adjudicators should be familiar with the current CAL criteria, found in the list
of CAL conditions in DI 23022.080. If you identify a potential CAL condition during a disability interview or while
reviewing an iClaim, promptly begin full development of non-medical evidence and complete
the full Title XVI claims path for SSI cases.
NOTE: The SSA-3820 in EDCS has a "Calculate CAL" button that gives the technician
immediate feedback on whether the case meets CAL criteria. The CAL list is updated
frequently with new conditions. Please review the list regularly to ensure you have
the most current information.
