SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39536.001 - Information Technology Systems - Background
Effective Dates: 09/10/2026 - Present | TN 2 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439536001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
SSA's policy is to provide the State DDS with the information technology (IT) capabilities
necessary to accomplish their disability determination work in a cost effective manner.
IT is essential and necessary for DDS to operate efficiently.
While SSA recognizes the need for IT by DDS, expenditures must be fully documented
and justified.
IT must be well managed or the anticipated benefits may not be realized.
