A. Documenting YTED Participation
Every month, the Income Security Policy (ISP) gathers a list of participants who enrolled
in or withdrew from YTED.  This list is sent to the Field Operations Performance (FOP)
Division contact (listed in DI 60010.005E). For SSI recipients who enroll in YTED, the FOP contact sends the monthly project
participation information to the appropriate Field Offices (FO) of jurisdiction. The
FO technician inputs the data into the Disability Control File (DCF) and Supplemental
Security Record (SSR) in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE).
The FOP contact will provide the FO with monthly project participation information
and will maintain an updated list of participants. If a YTED participant does not
appear on the list, FO technicians should immediately alert the FOP contact to confirm
the participant's status. Once participation is confirmed, the FOP contact will provide
the FO with the project participation information to input into the DCF and SSR Special
Message (listed in DI
60010.010B)
NOTE:
YTED waivers apply only to participants in the treatment group. The list of participants
sent by the FOP contact to the FO includes only those in the treatment group. As a
result, the FO does not need to distinguish between treatment and control group participants.
The YTED waivers are effective beginning the first month of project participation.
Any wages earned during this initial month will be excluded in accordance with the
waiver guidelines.
YTED participation does not stop when a participant moves out of an area served by
the YTED program. The participant who moves out of the area should inform the YTED
program and the new servicing field office. If a claimant alleges participation in
YTED but resides outside of the Greater Philadelphia area, the FO should contact the
FOP Division to verify participation in the YTED.
