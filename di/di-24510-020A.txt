SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24510.020 - Projecting the RFC
Effective Dates: 06/30/2015 - Present | TN 42 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424510020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principle
When criteria in DI 24510.001 A.2. are met, an RFC must be projected 12 months from the onset date when:
•
the case is adjudicated within 12 months of onset,
•
the severity of the impairment is disabling at the time of adjudication, and
•
the impairment(s) will not meet or equal a listed impairment, but will continue to be severe 12 months from onset.
NOTE: Do not project severity if a claimant's impairment, though severe, does not currently
prevent substantial gainful activity (SGA). In this case, denial would be appropriate
based on assessment of function and ability to do either past or other work.
