DI 26010.010 Completing Item 8 (Type of Claim (Title XVI)) on the SSA-831 for Blindness Cases
On a Title XVI claim, the field office (FO) always checks “DI,” “DS,” or “DC,” even
if a visual impairment is alleged.
If the claimant is statutorily blind, change the claim type to “BI,” “BS,” or “BC,”
as appropriate.
