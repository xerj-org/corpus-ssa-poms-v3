SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 43510.070 - Diary Control and Followups
Effective Dates: 05/09/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0443510070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. First followup
1. FSP
If the evidence or status of the development has not been received within the 120
day diary period, an SSA-423A will be used to followup the SSA-423-U6. Send a copy
of the original SSA-423-U6 with the SSA-423A. Diary for 60 days.
2. DO, VARO, DDS
If the evidence or a status report has not been received within the 90 day diary period,
send another SSA-5526-U3 or SSA-847-U3 and annotate it “Second Request.” Diary for 60 days.
