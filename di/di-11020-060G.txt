SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11020.060 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Jurisdiction and Routing
Effective Dates: 04/20/2026 - Present | TN 7 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411020060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Disabled child over age 18 and other individuals are eligible for benefits
Separate the award for benefits for other individuals, the application, and supporting
material, and send this material to the appropriate PC (see GN 01020.250). Send the disability material for the child applying for CDB to the appropriate
DDS. Annotate both sets of claims material regarding the whereabouts of the other
material.
