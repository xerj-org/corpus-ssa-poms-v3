D. Policy - treatment and intervention, in general
With treatment or intervention, you may not only have your symptoms or signs reduced,
but may also maintain, return to, or achieve a level of functioning that is not disabling.
Treatment or intervention may prevent, eliminate, or reduce functional limitations.
