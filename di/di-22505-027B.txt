SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.027 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Adjudicative Team Member Furnishes Evidence
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505027 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy - DDS Staff
1. MC/PC
A DDS MC/PC who gives evidence in any way to the MC/PC participating in the determination should introduce this evidence
into the file in writing.
2. Examiner
If examiners preparing determinations consider information known personally to them, they should:
a.
Identify and describe such information in a separate report for the file.
b.
Have the case reassigned to another examiner.
