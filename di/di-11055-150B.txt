B. Using the MDF for CEF exclusions
Assemble initial disability claims that are CEF exclusions in an MDF. Once a claim
is filed in an MDF, it generally remains in that folder throughout its life (i.e.,
through appeals, post-entitlement actions, and storage).
For MDF policy and procedures, see DI 70005.005.
