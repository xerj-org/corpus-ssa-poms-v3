A. Policy when a claimant is no longer engaged in SGA
A previously entitled claimant has the option of filing a new claim or requesting
expedited reinstatement (EXR) in the following situation:
•
the claimant files a request for an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) hearing;
•
the field office (FO) determines that the reconsideration SGA determination was appropriate;
and
•
the claimant is no longer engaging in SGA.
For an overview of EXR, see DI 13050.001.
