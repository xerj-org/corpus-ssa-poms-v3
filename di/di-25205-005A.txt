SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25205.005 - Evidence of a Medically Determinable Impairment
Effective Dates: 04/04/2017 - Present | TN 9 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425205005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A medically determinable physical or mental impairment must result from anatomical,
physiological, or psychological abnormalities which can be shown by medically acceptable
clinical and laboratory diagnostic techniques. The impairment must be established
by objective medical evidence (signs, laboratory findings, or both) from an acceptable
medical source, not on an individual’s statement of symptoms (see DI 24501.020). See DI 22505.003A.2.
