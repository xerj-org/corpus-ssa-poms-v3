SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10515.015 - Work Comparability Provisions for Blind Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), and Disabled Widow(er) Benefits (DWB) Claimants, Age 55 and Older
Effective Dates: 06/21/2012 - Present | TN 9 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410515015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Waiting period
Under the comparability provisions, if a worker or DWB claimant age 55 or older is determined to be statutorily blind
and engaging in noncomparable SGA, the period of time beginning with first month of
disability may be counted toward the waiting period while the individual is engaging in SGA. Therefore, if SGA continues for 5 full months or
more, disability benefits may begin as soon as the individual ceases SGA. This contrasts
with the requirement for blind workers in regular “freeze” status only (CDB and DWB claimants are not eligible for “freeze” ) who must serve a waiting period after the cessation of SGA in order to be entitled
to disability benefits.
