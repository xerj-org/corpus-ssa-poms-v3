SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52155.020 - Disability Claims with a Prior Period of Disability with Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452155020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. WC/PDB benefits paid in the prior period of DIB have ended
If periodic payments and/or lump sum (LS) proration end prior to new date of entitlement
(DOE), WC/PDB offset (based on the prior WC/PDB claim) does not apply in the new period
of DIB.
