D. Articulation requirements for objective medical evidence
Provide written analysis of how you considered objective medical evidence when establishing
an MDI. When objective medical evidence is material to other analyses or conclusions
in a claim, articulate that in the determination. For example, a discussion of objective
medical evidence may be material to an analysis of symptoms. For more information,
see DI 24501.021 Evaluating Symptoms.
