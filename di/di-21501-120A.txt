Effective Dates: 05/12/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0421501120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Blindness
Central visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the better eye with correcting glasses.
Or, if central visual acuity of more than 20/200 in the better eye with proper correction,
a field of vision defect in which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent
that the widest diameter of visual field subtends at an angular distance no greater
than 20 degrees.
