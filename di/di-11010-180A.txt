SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.180 - Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE) Paid 12/01/80, and Later
Effective Dates: 08/08/2008 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010180 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. IRWE definition
An IRWE is an expense for an item or service which is directly related to enabling
an individual with a disability to work and which is necessarily incurred by that
individual because of a physical or mental impairment. Such an expense may involve
payment for the purchase, installation, maintenance and repair of an impairment-related
item or payment for an impairment-related service.
