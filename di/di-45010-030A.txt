SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 45010.030 - ESRD Medicare Termination Action—Completion of SSA-892-U3
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0445010030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. How to prepare SSA-892-U3
If termination of entitlement is appropriate, prepare an SSA-892-U3 as follows:
1.
Complete items 1-14, and 16.
2.
Check item 19, block C. If appropriate, also complete block D per DI 45001.025.
3.
Complete item 25, block A or item 26, block B according to whether entitlement has
ended based on last date of dialysis or date of successful transplant.
4.
Complete item 31, block A and/or B, as appropriate. If entitlement terminated due
to a successful transplant, always check item 26 B.1.
5.
Complete item 32, as appropriate. See DI 45010.045.
6.
Complete item 39 as appropriate in accordance with POMS procedure. In addition, write
“death” or “ceased,” then show the date of death or enter the appropriate of either the last day of the
36th month after the month of transplant or the last day of the 12 month after the
month a course of dialysis ended.
NOTE:
If equitable relief is involved, see DI 45010.045C.
7.
Complete items 37, 40, and 41.
8.
Distribute copies of the SSA-892-U3 in the following manner: Prong file the original
(folder) copy on the left side and release the DO and data copies without the folder.
NOTE:
When the termination date is either impending or well in the future, prepare an SSA-1598
(per DI 45010.010) except in closed period or equitable relief cases, even if the termination date
is to be changed from one established termination date for automatic and timely termination
action. If no immediate termination action is required and the ESRD claimant is in
DI/SSI disability status, forward the reporting event to a disability (CDR) examiner
as soon as possible via Form SSA-567, as shown immediately below.
