SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.040 - Transferring Case to a Different Disability Hearing Unit (DHU)
Effective Dates: 07/01/2026 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to transfer the case
1. Reasons for a transfer
a.
A beneficiary (or recipient) requests transfer of the case to a different DHU and
there is good cause for doing so,
b.
A Federal employee made the determination being appealed; these cases must be transferred
to a Federal DHU,
c.
The disability hearing officer (DHO) has been disqualified. See DI
33015.045, or
d.
The DHU requests transfer.
2. Factors to consider when determining whether good cause exists to change the place
of the hearing
a.
Reasons for requesting the change in place,
b.
The facts supporting the request, and
c.
The impact of the proposed change on the efficient administration of the hearing process.
3. Who may grant transfer
Transfers within the State of the beneficiary's (or recipient's) residence may be
granted by the supervisory hearing officer (SHO), or the DHO, if so delegated.
Transfers outside the jurisdiction of the State of the beneficiary's (or recipient's)
residence may only be granted by Disability Adjudication (DA) after it has assessed the reasons for the
transfer.
