H. Verifying WC payment amounts
Because many beneficiaries getting PR WC speak only Spanish, check the language indicator on the MBR before contacting a beneficiary by phone or
mail. DIRCON (unless in Spanish) should not be used if the language indicator is S. In
this case, contact the FO to obtain the SIF information (if on-line printouts do not
provide the necessary information).
