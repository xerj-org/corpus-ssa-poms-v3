SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.525 - Assisting Claimants who are Unable to Provide Medical Evidence
Effective Dates: 12/22/2014 - Present | TN 55 (07-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010525 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Claimant cannot obtain medical evidence in foreign claims
If the file contains the name of a third-party contact who may help in securing the
necessary evidence, the Office of Earnings and International Operations may contact
the third party:
•
directly,
•
through the resident FO, or
•
through the Foreign Service Post.
If the claimant is unable to pay for medical evidence, the FO should inform the claimant
that the Social Security Administration will pay necessary expenses due to the unusual
circumstances of the case.
