SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.050 - Death of Claimant - Appeals Council (AC) Level
Effective Dates: 05/23/2006 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Notification
Notify the AC of the claimant's death.
2. Assistance
Provide information and assistance to survivors or other eligible parties as appropriate.
NOTE: Often it is the AC that initiates the action in this situation. Office of Disability
Adjudication and Review (ODAR) operating procedures, based on Sections 404.971(b)
and 416.1471(p) of the regulations, require the AC to determine whether there are
persons who could be parties in a case when the claimant dies.
