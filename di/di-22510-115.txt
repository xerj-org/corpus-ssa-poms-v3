DI 22510.115 Adult Consultative Examination (CE) Report Content Guidelines for Speech and Language
Impairments
Use the following guidelines to provide the minimum content in a CE report for an
adult with a speech or language impairment. Each Disability Determination Service
(DDS) will notify medical sources of any additional requirements.
