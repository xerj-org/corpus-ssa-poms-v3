SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10135.005 - Deemed Entitlement to DWB Following Actual Entitlement to DWB
Effective Dates: 01/10/2006 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410135005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - Disabled Reduced RIB Beneficiaries
When an individual’s entitlement to DWB terminates upon entitlement to RIB before
attainment of age 65, the individual is deemed, for purposes of HI entitlement only,
to be entitled to DWB until the month of attainment of age 65 or until an occurrence
of a DWB terminating event.
EXAMPLE:
Mrs. Baker became entitled to HI effective 7/04 based on DWB entitlement which began
7/02. Her DWB was terminated because of entitlement to RIB effective 12/04, the month
she attained age 62. Her entitlement to HI is not affected because DWB entitlement
is deemed to continue despite termination upon entitlement to a higher RIB.
