DI 80630.030 Forwarding Evidence
•
Forward any medical evidence to the appropriate DDS if the claim in pending in the
DDS.
•
Forward any medical evidence to the appropriate ODAR office if the claim is pending
in ODAR.
•
If the claim has been adjudicated, handle trailer material per your existing instructions.
•
Forward any non-medical evidence, based on existing procedures in your PC, for placement
in the MDF (since the paper folder is currently the official folder).
