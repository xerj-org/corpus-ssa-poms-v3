G. End of Promoting Opportunity Demonstration
All POD participants have exited the Promoting Opportunity Demonstration and have
returned to regular work rules as of June 30, 2021. All earnings for POD participants
that were earned after June 30, 2021 should be evaluated under current law. All POD
offset calculated during the POD participation period is final and should not be adjusted.
