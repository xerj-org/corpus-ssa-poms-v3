SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.902 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary or Survivor Applications Filed During the Benefit Continuation Period (BCP) - Disability Benefit Equals or Exceeds Auxiliary or Survivor Benefit
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105902 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Applicant not entitled to auxiliary or survivor benefit
If the applicant is not entitled to the auxiliary or survivor benefit (in a DIB continuance
situation) as the DIB equals or exceeds the original rate on which the auxiliary or
survivor claim is filed, the claim should be denied and the NH informed of this determination.
Always include the blanket denial paragraph in your notification.
