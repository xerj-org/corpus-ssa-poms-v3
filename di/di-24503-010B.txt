B. Sources of objective medical evidence
All medical sources, not just acceptable medical sources (AMSs), can submit evidence
we categorize as objective medical evidence. For a definition of medical source, see
DI 22505.001A.4. For a definition of AMS, see DI 22505.003 Evidence from an Acceptable Medical source (AMS).
