G. Case fails to close
If a CEF fails to close on the DDS case processing system upon adjudication, case
development and determination documents that were generated or created in an electronic
format do not upload to the CEF and jurisdiction of the CEF does not transfer to the
appropriate downstream component.
It is the responsibility of the DDS to confirm that:
•
All cases close on their case processing system;
•
Documents upload to the CEF; and
•
Jurisdiction changes to the appropriate component.
