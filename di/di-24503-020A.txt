SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24503.020 - Evaluating Evidence from Nonmedical Sources
Effective Dates: 03/15/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424503020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of evidence from a nonmedical source
Evidence from a nonmedical source includes information or statement(s) from a nonmedical
source (including the claimant) about any issue in a claim. We may receive evidence
from a nonmedical source either directly from a nonmedical source or indirectly, such
as from forms and our administrative records.
