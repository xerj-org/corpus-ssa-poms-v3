SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39518.065 - Administrative Provisions for Assuring that Mental Impairment Cases are Evaluated by Qualified Professionals
Effective Dates: 06/07/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439518065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Operating procedures
1. Staff levels
Determine
whether the current staff is sufficient to provide specialist reviews.
2. Less than full time
Develop a plan to overcome less than full and continuous coverage. Include:
a.
The delays, caseload and/or anticipated number of days, specialist case reviews are pending;
b.
Planned recruitment
initiatives and contacts to be made.
EXAMPLE: The following actions can be taken:
•
Increase existing staff hours;
•
Contact other State agencies for qualified staff willing to participate or for other recruitment
leads;
•
Contact medical schools/universities;
•
Contact State/national specialist societies and associations; and
•
Advertise in professional publications.
3. Fee schedule
Develop a strategy
to update inadequate fee/payment rates if revisions are needed to obtain qualified
specialists.
4. Reports
a.
Report to the RO
within 45 days after specialist shortage is determined.
b.
Submit subsequent reports
as specialty staff changes occur.
c.
Include in the report:
•
the status of recruitment efforts,
•
shortages still existing,
•
impediments to recruitment,
•
status of efforts to alleviate impediments, and
•
the level/degree of State (parent agency) cooperation.
