SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 20101.025 - Subsequent Claim Filed or Reconsideration on a Subsequent Claim is Requested While Hearing, Appeals Council (AC) Review, or Court Action Pending on Prior Claim
Effective Dates: 11/08/2013 - Present | TN 4 (12-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0420101025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy for handling a prior claim at the ALJ level and a new initial/reconsideration
claim filed under the same title as the prior claim
If the new claim is filed under the same title as the prior claim, the ALJ generally
considers the subsequent claim with the prior claim. The DDS returns the subsequent
claim to the field office (FO) to be routed to the ALJ.
If the ALJ affirms a cessation of disability on a prior claim and a medical determination
is necessary on a subsequent claim, send the subsequent claim to the DDS for a determination
covering the period following the date of cessation.
