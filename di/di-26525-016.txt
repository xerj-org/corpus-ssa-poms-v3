For all other impairments related to HIV infection, assign a medical improvement possible
(MIP) diary unless additional factors exist that would influence the diary assignment.

before 1/17/2017 that had a primary impairment code of 0430 Symptomatic HIV and met
or medically
equaled listing 14.08.
