D. DQR wishes to rescind a deficiency
1. Submitting a rescission request to RPC
When a DQR chooses to rescind a deficiency prior to the RPC panel discussion, the
DQR uses the RPC tool to notify RPC they wish to rescind the deficiency. The RPC tool
automatically informs the DDS by e-mail when RPC accepts the DQR rescission request.
2. RPC accepts or rejects the rescission request
•
If the DQR submits the request prior to RPC panel discussion and the DQR does not
make the rescission contingent upon a DDS action, RPC generally accepts the DQR rescission
request.
•
If the DQR makes the rescission contingent on a DDS action, the technical expert will
contact a DDS RPC liaison to see if the component will agree to DQR terms.
•
If a proposed rescission would clearly result in an incorrect determination, RPC will
reject the rescission. When RPC rejects a rescission request, RPC notifies the DQR
and the DDS, and schedules the case for RPC panel discussion following the regular
RPC process.
