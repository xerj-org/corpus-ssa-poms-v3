A. Procedure – When to use the CDRI Screen
The CDRI provides information propagated from the MBR, SSR, and RPS systems depending
upon the individual's benefit status and whether a representative payee is present.
In addition, use this screen to record appellate actions pending and whether continuing
benefits have been elected, if applicable.
