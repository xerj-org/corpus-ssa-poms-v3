B. Effectuation of AC actions
To effectuate an AC decision for an initial award, prepare an SSA-831-U5 according
to the instructions in DI 42010.020, changing the appeal level indicators to indicate an AC decision where appropriate.
To effectuate an AC reversal of a termination to a continuance, prepare an SSA-833
following instructions in DI 42010.021.
When the AC remands a case to the ALJ, the ALJ will either affirm, modify, or reverse
the prior decision. Process ALJ remand decisions according to the instructions in
DI 42010.020 and DI 42010.021, as appropriate.
If an AC decision contains an error of fact or law that prevents effectuation, prepare
and send a protest memorandum to OAO following the guidelines in DI 42010.080.
