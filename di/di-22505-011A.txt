SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.011 - Development Issue - Title II Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB) Case
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. Claimant Not Disabled Before Age 22
When it is determined that the claimant was not disabled in or before the month of
attainment of age 22, discontinue development of evidence and prepare the determination.
2. Claimant Under 17 1/2 Years Of Age
If the claimant is under age 17 1/2:
a.
Transmit the case to the field office via form SSA-408.
b.
Annotate the case “Unadjudicated CDB case. Claimant under age 17 1/2.”
