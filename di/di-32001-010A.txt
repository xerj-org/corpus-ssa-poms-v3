A. Incoming claims
The disability determination services (DDS) receives disability claims folders for
processing from the following components.
1.
Field offices (FOs) located in various cities throughout the United States
2.
Office of Disability Operations - Central Office (ODO-CO) located in Baltimore, Maryland
(also known on case control systems as PC-7)
3.
Office of International Operations (OIO/PC-8/INTPSC)
4.
The Office of Quality Performance in Baltimore or any regional offices:
•
Region I - Boston
•
Region II - New York
•
Region III - Philadelphia
•
Region IV - Atlanta
•
Region V - Chicago
•
Region VI - Dallas
•
Region VII - Kansas City,

Region VIII - Denver
•
Region IX - San Francisco
•
Region X - Seattle

The Disability Case Review (DCR) of any Retirement and Survivor's Insurance Program
