C. SSA-2454 versus SSA-2455
Use Form SSA-2455 for WC rate change computations and family composition changes as
long as a triennial redetermination of the ACE does NOT yield an increase in benefits payable to any beneficiary on the account. If a triennial
redetermination of the ACE DOES yield an increase for any beneficiary on the account; any subsequent WC rate change
must be computed using Form SSA-2454. Any subsequent family composition change resulting
in a change in the maximum must be computed using the Form SSA-2454. .
