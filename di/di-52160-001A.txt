A. Provisional payments
Provisional benefits are paid based on the monthly benefit payment at the time the
previous entitlement terminated. However, provisional payments are increased by any
intervening cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs) and applicable primary insurance amount
(PIA) recomputations. If the prior monthly benefit amount was reduced due to offset,
the provisional benefit amount is based on that prior reduced benefit payable.
See DI 13050.025 - Provisional Benefits - Title II.
