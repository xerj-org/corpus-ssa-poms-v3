B. Statutory blindness duration requirement
The 12-month duration requirement to find disability does not apply to the following
types of claims:
•
Title II statutory blindness, age 55 or older, or
•
Statutory blindness under Title XVI.
NOTE: A Title II stat blind claimant can be entitled to a period of disability (not cash
benefits) if their blindness or visual impairment condition has lasted for at least
5 full calendar months; see DI 10105.001.
