B. Processing technical reactivations
1. Reactivating the case
To reactivate an FO Determination or No Determination (technical denial) claim in
EDCS:
•
Verify that the claim meets the criteria in Overview – DI 81010.110A. of this section;
•
Select the “Reactivate Claim(s)” button on the Disability Case Selection screen;
•
Complete the Reactive Claim(s) screen;
•
Select the appropriate claim type; and
•
Select the “OK” button.
NOTE: After selecting the “OK” button, claim status displays “Reactivated”. If the “Cancel”
button is selected, the reactivation is terminated and the user returns to the page
where the reactivation was initiated.
2. Updating EDCS after technical reactivation
After reactivating the EDCS claim:
•
All actions needed to process an initial claim can be taken on the reactivated claim,
including repropagation and the Update-After-Transfer (UAT) Utility.
•
All disability reports and data that existed in the case at the time of the FO closure
are available and can be edited as needed.
•
Update all reports and add any new ones as necessary (e.g., an Adult Disability Report
(SSA-3368) if one was not completed already) to prepare the case for transfer to the
DDS.
•
All of the functionality for that case (e.g., the ability to update the data, editing,
transfer capability, etc.) is exactly the same as an initial non-reactivated case
that is pending in the FO. An FO determination can be made on the reactivated case.
3. Transferring the case to the DDS
After all updates are complete, follow normal transfer procedures to transfer the
case to the DDS per Transferring Cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
- DI 81010.085.
4. Inputting another FO determination or no determination
If it is later decided that the reactivated claim requires an FO Determination or
No Determination, close the claim by adding another FO Determination or No Determination
via the Claims Action page. For more information on FO determinations, see Processing
Field Office (FO) Determinations - DI 81010.140.
