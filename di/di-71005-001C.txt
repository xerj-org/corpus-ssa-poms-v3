C. Border FO adjudication process after receiving a DDS determination
After receiving the DDS determination, complete the following steps:
1. Title II allowance
Trigger payment in MCS and transfer the claim to OIO. For paper folders, complete
an SSA-3601 (Claims Routing) and route the folder to OIO at the following address:
OIO
P.O. Box 17769
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-7769
System Destination Code: PC8
2. Title II denial
Hold the title II claim in the border FO (electronic or paper folder) until the claimant
files an appeal, or the 6-month retention period expires.
