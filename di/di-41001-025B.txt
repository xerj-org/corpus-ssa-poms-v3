B. Statutorily blind DIB, CDB, and DWB individuals age 55 and older
If the statutorily blind claimant age 55 or older became entitled to disability benefits
while engageing in noncomparable SGA, they are entitled to a TWP only if:
1.
They later return to SGA that requires skills or abilities comparable to those required
in the work they regularly did before they became blind or became 55 years old, whichever
is later; or
2.
Their last previous work ended because of an impairment and the current work requires
a significant vocational adjustment.
