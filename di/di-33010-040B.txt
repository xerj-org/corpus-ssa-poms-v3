SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.040 - Transferring Case to a Different Disability Hearing Unit (DHU)
Effective Dates: 07/01/2026 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Actions required when case is transferred
1. When RO approval is required
a.
Contact DA if assistance is needed as to where a case should be transferred, or the
transfer is to a DHU outside the jurisdiction of the State of the beneficiary's (or
recipient's) residence.
b.
Advise DA of any problems you have with the case being transferred. This is important
because DA must assure the concurrence of all parties involved before granting the
transfer.
NOTE: If necessary, DA will have a Federal DHO conduct the hearing at a site convenient
to the beneficiary (or recipient) and the DHO.
2. DHU action after approval
After the request for transfer has been granted:
a.
Adjust the hearing schedule,
b.
Prepare an SSA-408 Route Slip for paper cases or SSA-5002 Report of Contact for electronic
cases and annotate with the reason for the transfer,
c.
Forward the DHU folder with the claims folder (if available) to the receiving DHU,
d.
Update the case control system if the claims folder is being forwarded, and
e.
Advise the Disability Determination Service (DDS) of the DHU now having jurisdiction
if the claims folder is in the DDS.
