DI 52105.010 Third Party Settlements
A third party is a person or entity other than the employer who may have caused or
contributed to the work related injury or illness. Individuals may sue the third party
and recover monetary damages. The proceeds from the third party settlement are not
offsettable. Workers’ compensation (WC) received prior to any third party settlement
is offsettable and normally stops due to the third party action. The WC carrier usually
attaches a lien to the third party settlement to recover some or all of the WC. Offset
is then removed in proportion to the amount of WC repaid.
