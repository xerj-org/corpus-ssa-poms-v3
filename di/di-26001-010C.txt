C. Freeze exceptions for childhood disability benefits (CDB) and disabled widow(er)’s
benefits (DWB)
Blind CDB and DWB claimants are not entitled to a disability freeze.
If a blind CDB or DWB claimant is working at the blind SGA level, we must deny or
terminate a period of disability and cash benefits within the trial work period provisions.
For work comparability provisions that apply to CDB and DWB claimants who are age
55 or older and statutorily blind, see DI 26005.001E.
