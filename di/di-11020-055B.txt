B. Preparing and requesting paper folders for CDB claims
For instructions on folder assembly, see
•
DI 70005.005 Overview of the Paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF); and
•
DI 70010.001 Paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF) - Field Office (FO) Procedures.
If the current claim is a duplicate claim, do not prepare a new folder. Use a route
slip when transmitting the material to the Processing Center (PC).
For FO procedures on potential collateral estoppel claims that may not require a new
disability determination, see DI 11011.001C.
