G. “Assistance Request History” Screen
The CEF maintains a list of all AR that were performed throughout the life of the
case. This list is found within the “Status/History” tab of eView by selecting the
“Assistance Request History” screen. The list includes:
•
The date of the AR:
•
The issues involved; and
•
The jurisdiction of the AR.
