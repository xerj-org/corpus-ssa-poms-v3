A. Policy on Title XVI due process requirements
1. Due process and notice of planned action
a.
Due process requires that you send the beneficiary a notice of planned action before benefits are adversely affected.
b.
Benefits are continued if:
•
An appeal is requested within 10 days of receipt of the notice of planned action for
statutory benefit continuation (SBC) or within 60 days for Goldberg-Kelly (G/K) payment
continuation; and
•
The beneficiary does not waive, in writing their right to continuation of benefits. See SI 02301.300 for additional guidance in Title XVI due process cases.
EXCEPTION: Due process notification is not required when the beneficiary's death has been confirmed.
2. Statutory benefit continuation option applies
The determination notice provides due process when the SBC option applies. The policy
for Title XVI cases is the same as that for Title II cases. See DI 40515.005B.2.
3. Title II/Title XVI concurrent cases
•
Medical
Process the Title II case under Title II procedures, and the Title XVI case under
Title XVI procedures. The determination notice provides due process for Title II and
Title XVI when the SBC option applies.
•
Work and earnings
The Title II case receives Title II due process per DI 13010.185F, and the Title XVI case receives G/K due process per SI 02301.300. This applies whether you process the adverse actions at different times or at the
same time.
