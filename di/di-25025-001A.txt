A. History and purpose of the medical-vocational guidelines
In 1979, we published the medical-vocational guidelines in Appendix 2 to Subpart P
of the regulations. The purpose of the medical-vocational guidelines is to increase
the consistency of disability determinations and decisions at step 5 of sequential
evaluation.
