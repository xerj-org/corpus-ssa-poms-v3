SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40525.075 - Step Three - Preparing Duplicate Folder for CDR Lost Folder/Medical Evidence
Effective Dates: 11/12/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440525075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
OCO and the PSCs are important links in upholding the integrity of SSA"s disability
decisions/determinations and continuing payments of disability benefits. Finding or
replacing missing/misplaced medical folders is extremely costly, as is erroneous continuation
of medical disability benefits to an individual who is no longer disabled, but continues
in pay because the CPD medical folder cannot be found.
