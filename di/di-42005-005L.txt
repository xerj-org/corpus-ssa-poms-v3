L. Cessation affirmed on reconsideration—new period of disability established
The Reconsideration Staff or module may affirm a cessation of disability, while finding
that the claimant became disabled again subsequent to the cessation date. If the claimant
will lose benefits because his or her new application (or protective writing) is not
retroactive to the first month of potential entitlement under the new period of disability,
see DI 42010.040.
