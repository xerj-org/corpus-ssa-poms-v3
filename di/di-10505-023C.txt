C. Comparison based on contact with other sources
Other steps may be indicated if reasonable doubt remains as to what represents an
equivalent civilian job or as to how the value of the service member's work activities
compares with the pay for similar civilian work. Contact may be made with employers,
the U.S. Employment Service, or other informed sources of employment and vocational
data, including a vocational specialist. These contacts are expected to be for advice
and information gathering only, to aid judgments in the case. In some instances the
military occupation manuals will not list, in conjunction with a military job, any
related civilian job, or they may list only one civilian job which happens not to
exist in the community in which the comparison is conducted. The title of the disabled
service member's military service occupation and/or listed civilian job may, however,
lead the vocational specialist to identify closely related civilian occupations discussed
in the DOT with which the adjudicator may satisfactorily compare the military service
occupation.
