C. Procedure - SSA-5665-BK, Teacher Questionnaire
1. SSA-5665-BK Is Required Form
a.
The SSA-5665-BK, Teacher Questionnaire (TQ), is the only form approved by SSA and
the Office of Management and Budget for requesting information from teachers about
an individual’s functioning.
b.
b. The TQ elicits teacher observations and assessments of an individual’s functioning
in a broad range of activities compared to the functioning of same-aged individuals
who do not have impairments.
