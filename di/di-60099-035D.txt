SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 60099.035 - Benefit Offset National Demonstration (BOND) Participation Period

D. Disability entitlement terminated during the participation period
1. Termination due to substantial gainful activity (SGA)
If a BOND participant’s entitlement to disability benefits terminated due to SGA in
the month of random assignment to the BOND project or during the participation period,
reopen the termination decision and apply the $1 for $2 offset rules. Normal SGA rules
apply to the month of random assignment.
2. Termination not due to SGA
If a BOND participant’s entitlement to disability benefits terminated for a non-SGA
reason (other than those listed in DI 60099.035E in this section.) after the month of random assignment and the participant completed
the TWP, the beneficiary’s participation period continues despite not being entitled
to benefits.
