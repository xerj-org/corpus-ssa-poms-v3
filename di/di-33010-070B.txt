B. Procedure for suspension or disqualification of appointed representatives
1. Person suspended or disqualified attempts to act as an appointed representative
•
Follow GN
03970.060 when a suspended or disqualified individual attempts to act, directly or indirectly,
as an appointed representative.
2. Conflict of interest involved
•
Process per GN
03980.000 if a conflict of interest is involved.
•
Contact Disability Adjudication (DA) if unsure as to whether an individual served
as a senior level employee.
