A. Identification
1. The CE provider will include the claimant's name, date of birth and/or claim number
and;
2. The CE provider will indicate that the claimant provided proof of identity by showing
a valid and current government photographic identification (for example, U.S. State-issued
driver’s license, U.S. State-issued non-driver identity card, U.S. passport, U.S.
military ID, student or school ID, etc.); or provide a physical description of the
claimant to verify that the person being examined is the claimant, except if the claimant’s
medical source with a treating relationship is the CE provider.
