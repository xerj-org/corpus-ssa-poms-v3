C. Procedure - when screening performed by state agency personnel
In those agencies where the DDS is not a part of the State VR agency, the VR agency
may elect to perform the screening and referral functions. In the latter situation,
the DDS may be asked to participate in the development of the screening criteria,
and should obtain for its records, copies of the criteria that are adopted.
