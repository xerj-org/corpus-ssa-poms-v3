D. Adopting medical assessments
If applicable, the medical consultant (MC) or psychological consultant (PC) may adopt
any of the initial level medical assessment(s) when evaluating reconsideration cases.
To determine if the initial level medical assessment(s) may be adopted, see:
•
DI 24510.066B, for information on adopting the prior SSA-4734-BK (Physical Residual Functional Capacity Assessment), or SSA-4734-F4-SUP (Mental Residual Functional Capacity Assessment);
•
DI 24583.005.G.1., for information on adopting the prior SSA-2506-BK (Psychiatric Review Technique); and
•
DI 25230.001B.6. for information on adopting the prior SSA-538.
To adopt the prior initial-level medical assessment(s):
•
Prepare an SSA-416 (Medical Evaluation)
The MC or PC may complete an SSA-416and include the reconsideration adoption statement:
“I have reviewed all the evidence in file, and the medical assessment of (date) is
affirmed as written.” The reviewing MC or PC must sign and date the SSA-416 using
the approved electronic signature function. The SSA-416 must be applied to the Disability
Determination Explanation (DDE), that is uploaded to the CEF prior to the case closure.
EXCEPTION: The SSA-416 cannot be used to affirm an SSA-538, a new SSA-538 must be prepared. For more information
on affirming the initial level SSA-538 at the reconsideration level, see DI 25230.001B.64.
•
Prepare a new SSA-416, SSA-4734, SSA-4734-F4-SUP, SSA-2506, or SSA-538 and include
the reconsideration adoption statement, “I have reviewed all the evidence in file,
and the [medical assessment] of [date] is affirmed as written.” The MC or PC must
sign and date the medical assessment using the approved electronic signature function.
The medical assessment must be applied to the DDE, which is uploaded to the CEF prior
to the case closure.
