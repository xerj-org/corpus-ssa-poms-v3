A. Background for CDR hearing appeal
The Field Office (FO) serves as the starting point for CDRs and CDR appeals. Most
CDRs are processed electronically through all adjudicative levels. The eCDR may have
either a paper or electronic Comparison Point Decision (CPD) folder. The Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS) provides the capability to link eCPD folders to the
current eCDR. Paper CPD folders follow the eCDR to each adjudicative component (Disability
Determination Services [DDS], Office of Hearing Operations [OHO], etc.)
