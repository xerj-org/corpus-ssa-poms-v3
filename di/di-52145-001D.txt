SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52145.001 - Verification of Proof of Workers Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB)
Effective Dates: 01/03/2024 - Present | TN 8 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452145001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Acceptable evidence
Use the following evidence to prove WC/PDB rates:
1.
A signed and dated copy of the court order awarding WC/PDB payments.
2.
A notice or mailer insert enclosed with the WC/PDB check that explains payments.
3.
A WC/PDB check or check stub showing the exact periodic rate and the effective date
of change.
4.
A WC/PDB check or stub that verifies a current ongoing rate if payment began before
entitlement to DIB and continues at the same rate. CAUTION: Only accept the check or stub if it shows the gross amount.
5.
Obtain a signed and approved LS settlement copy that shows:
a.
the exact date entitlement to periodic payments ended;
b.
the gross amount of the LS;
c.
the rate at which to prorate; and
d.
the medical, legal, or related expenses.

6.
Accept a computer printout or copies of WC/PDB documents submitted by the WC payer
in lieu of completing the SSA-1709 if they contain all the same information normally
found on the SSA-1709.
7.
A completed SSA-1709, signed by the NH and received from the agency or the WC carrier,
see DI 52145.010.
