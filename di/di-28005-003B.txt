B. When to perform an age-18 disability redetermination
An age-18 disability redetermination is required for Title XVI recipients receiving
disabled child (DC) benefits who are attaining age 18 on August 22, 1996 or later.
See DI 23570.006 and DI 23570.010. If an age-18 disability redetermination has already been done, conduct a CDR following
the sequential evaluation process in DI 28005.015.
A CDR is completed for Title XVI adults who were originally allowed as children who
attained age 18 before August 22, 1996.
