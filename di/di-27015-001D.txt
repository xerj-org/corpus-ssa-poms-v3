D. Completing items 25 and 25A of SSA-831-U3 — (Revised Determination)
1. SSA-831-U3 block 25
Select block 25 in all revised reopening cases except escalated determinations.
2. SSA-831-U3 block 25A
Select the claim level. B, for “Recon,” on all reconsideration reopenings except when
the reopening is for an escalated determination. Select C, “Recon Disability Hearing
Unit,” if the case is a Disability Hearing Unit jurisdiction case per DI 29001.001B.
