Effective Dates: 06/20/2024 - Present | TN 85 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413010175 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to complete the SSA-899-U2
Use the form in the following situations:
•
The beneficiary did not complete their trial work period (TWP);
•
We conducted a medical folder review for the extended period of eligibility (EPE);
or
•
When a determination of Extended Medicare eligibility exists.
