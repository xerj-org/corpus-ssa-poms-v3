SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32005.085 - Disability Determination Services Reconsideration Case Routing — Concurrent Title II or Concurrent Title II-Title XVI – Paper Case Only
Effective Dates: 10/17/2022 - Present | TN 2 (10-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432005085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Concurrent Title II -Title XVI — one folder (or stapled together)
Route all cases as initial concurrent cases, except for Title II special handling
cases shown in DI 32005.065 and DI 32005.070. In special handling cases, split the folders following the guidelines in DI 32005.005. Route Title II special handling cases to Social Security Administration, Office
of Central Operations, ATTN: DDSO Recon Disability Examiners, 6401 Security Blvd.,
Baltimore, MD 21235-6401. Explain special situation in “Remarks” section. Route Title
XVI folders to the FO.
