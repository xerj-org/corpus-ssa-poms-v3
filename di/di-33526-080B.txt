B. Reminders for choosing an impairment code in special or other situations
•
Use 20 to identify the Body System in Item 16A of the SSA-831 or Item 21 of the SSA-832
or SSA-833.
•
Do not use 2480 as the primary diagnosis in Item 16A of the SSA-831 or Item 21 of the SSA-832 or
SSA-833 if the decision is an allowance or a continuance.
•
Do not use 0000 as the primary diagnosis for continuing disability reviews (CDRs) in Item 21 of the
SSA-832 or SSA-833.
