A. Introduction
The Office of Media and Logistics Management (OMLM) manages the Electronic Automated
Distribution Management System (eADMS). The eADMS delivers selected SSA publications
(e.g., POMS, MSOM, CFR, Federal Register, Commissioner Bulletins, etc.) to offices
on predetermined dates.
