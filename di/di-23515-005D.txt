D. Onset development for sponsor-to-non-citizen deeming claims
1. Onset after U.S. admission for permanent residence
An exact onset date is required when onset is established after admission into the U.S. for permanent residence. Use the medical onset date, rather
than the filing date, as the established onset date.
2. Onset before U.S. admission for permanent residence
When the onset is established before admission into the U.S. for permanent residence, and the medical evidence does not
pinpoint a precise date, use any date prior to the date of admission.
