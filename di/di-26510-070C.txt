SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.070 - Completing Item 26 (List Number) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 08/19/2010 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Coding order
Enter codes in Item 26 in any order, except when procedures indicate otherwise (e.g.,
OQA list codes which identify cases for automatic sample selection in special review
situations, must be entered in Block 26C, even when there are no other codes entered
in Blocks 26A and 26B).
