A. Abt call center staff
Convergys Government Solutions, LLC, staffs the Abt call center, located in Denver,
Colorado, to handle BOND calls. The call center:
•
responds to inquiries from BOND participants and the public;
•
responds to e-mails sent to the BOND e-mail address, located on the public website;
•
makes referrals to Work Incentive Counselors/Enhanced Work Incentive Counselors (WIC/EWIC)
staff or BOND central operations staff; and
•
makes stage 1 and stage 2 outreach calls.
