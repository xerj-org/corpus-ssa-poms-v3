SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24510.010 - Medical Opinions About What Claimants Can Still Do
Effective Dates: 03/31/2017 - Present | TN 59 (09-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424510010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Limitations
The medical opinion does not:
a.
reflect the findings of other sources.
b.
establish conclusive judgments about levels of work an individual can perform.
2. Consideration
The medical opinion must always be carefully considered and addressed. (See DI 24503.000.)
