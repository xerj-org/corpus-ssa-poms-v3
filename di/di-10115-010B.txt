B. Policy - Disability Determination for Disabled Minor Child to Enable Continuation
of Child Benefits at Age 18
When a disability determination is made to continue a child's entitlement upon attaining
age 18 as a CDB, the disability determination must be posted to the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR).When this has been completed the Entitlement Eligibility function of
the Title II Redesign Release 3 will evaluate data for benefit entitlement status
and identify benefit conversion conditions.
