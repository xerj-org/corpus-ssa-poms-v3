C. Evaluating work activity for blind beneficiaries
We use different substantial gainful activity (SGA) earnings guidelines to evaluate
the work activity performed in 1978 or later by blind Title II beneficiaries. For
a table of SGA earnings guidelines, see DI 10501.015.
For blind beneficiaries who are self-employed, use only the countable income test
to determine if the self-employment represents SGA. For complete information on evaluating
work activity for SGA and the countable income test see DI 10515.001through DI 10515.015.
