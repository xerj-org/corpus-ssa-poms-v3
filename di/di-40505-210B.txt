B. Description of a case with Title XVI involvement
A case with Title XVI involvement will generally consist of a Title II case folder
with “S” or “SSI Conversion Case” cited in file. Other indications of possible Title
XVI involvement:
•
In non-conversion cases, an SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal), and/or SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
Title XVI), showing Title XVI involvement;
•
Other payment forms or documents indicating Title XVI involvement.
