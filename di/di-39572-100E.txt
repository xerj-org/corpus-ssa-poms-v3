SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39572.100 - Decentralizing DDS Operations
Effective Dates: 07/08/2026 - Present | TN 3 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439572100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Related policy
1. Budgeting request
a.
DDS will include
the cost of any proposed DDS decentralization in their budget.
b.
DDS will not include
funding for decentralization in their quarterly spending plans until Federal approval
has been obtained. When approved, the cost will be entered in the narrative section.
2. Keeping offices informed
a.
The DDS will keep SSA informed of progress of the decentralization and of any significant
events or problems.
b.
Disability Adjudication (DA) staff will keep the DDS informed of any systems requirements
or changes for the decentralized operation.
