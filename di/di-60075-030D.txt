D. Reopening or reconsideration of a determination prior to the POD start date
FOs and PCs can reopen or reconsider a determination in eWork made prior to the start
date of POD participation (found on the DCF). The reopening or reconsideration is
for a closed period and will only evaluate earnings up to the end date of the last
review period. Earnings from the month after the prior review period end date to the
date of current effectuation are not evaluated.
eWork may allow a technician to input earnings during the POD participation period
when performing a reconsideration or reopening, however the earnings are not used
in the determination. The “Earnings and Events” screen in eWork will show all earnings
input, but will not record a TWP/EPE or IRP code as a Decision Event Code for earnings posted during the POD participation period.
NOTE: Once a beneficiary completes POD participation, all reopening or reconsiderations
of pre-POD determinations are restricted.
