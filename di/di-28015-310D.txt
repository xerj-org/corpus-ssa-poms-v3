SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.310 - Consider Age and Time on the Rolls When Assessing Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) in Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs)
Effective Dates: 02/18/2025 - Present | TN 14 (02-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Effect of age
When the beneficiary is age 50 or over and received disability benefits for a considerable
period of time, consider the factors in this section along with their age in assessing
RFC.
Generally, use a period of at least seven years as “a considerable period of time.”
