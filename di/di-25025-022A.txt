SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25025.022 - Using a Medical-Vocational Rule as a Framework When the Issue of Transferable Skills is Not Material to the Determination
Effective Dates: 02/13/2015 - Present | TN 11 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425025022 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. How to determine if transferability of skills is not material to the determination
If the claimant has past relevant skilled or semiskilled work and the decision rules
for skills transferable and skills not transferable both direct a finding of not disabled,
the issue is not material to the determination.
