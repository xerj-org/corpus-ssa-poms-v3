SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.150 - Processing Return to Work Reports
Effective Dates: 03/14/2025 - Present | TN 83 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Work report is received in field office (FO)
When a return-to-work report is received in the FO, and the claim is pending at the
initial or reconsideration level, undertake other development as necessary (e.g.,
complete a SSA-821 (Work Activity Report) and other appropriate forms). Develop the
work activity and make an Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) determination. See Meaning
of SGA and Scope of Sub-chapter - DI 10501.001 - DI 10501.025. For cases pending at the hearing level, the FO does not have jurisdiction of the
work issue. The development of the work issue must be done by the Office of Hearings
Operations (OHO). For post entitlement situations see Work Reports - DI 13010.020 - DI 13010.025 and DI 13010.105 - DI 13010.110.
