A. Introduction to completing Items 8 to 10
Follow the instructions to complete Items 8 through 10 when processing continuing
disability review (CDR) on the Forms SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability
or Blindness Determination and Transmittal Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance
of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal Title II).
