DI 39545.675 Exhibit 3 DDS Guide to Establishing a Fee Schedule
The following guide is for DDS that elect to use the Medicare fee schedule to establish
the DDS fee schedule. It is not intended for DDS that currently use a fee schedule
based on fees paid by other agencies in the State.
