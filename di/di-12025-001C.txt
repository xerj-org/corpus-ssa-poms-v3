C. FO procedure when a claimant files a late request for reconsideration
When a claimant files their request for reconsideration late (i.e., more than 60 days
after receipt of the initial determination) take the following actions:
•
Determine whether there is good cause for the late filing. For instructions regarding
a good cause determination for late filing of a request for reconsideration, refer
to GN 03101.020.
•
Prepare and send a notice, if you do not find good cause, following the instructions in DI 12005.015C.
