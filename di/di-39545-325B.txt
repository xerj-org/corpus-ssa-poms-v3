SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.325 - Medical Development Requests from Foreign Countries under International Agreements (Totalization)
Effective Dates: 04/20/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545325 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The Office of International Operations (OIO) will send a development request (Form
SSA-847-U3) to the DDS. The DDS will return the requested evidence to OIO and include
a statement of costs paid by the DDS broken down into:
•
CE costs,
•
MER costs,
•
applicant travel expenses,
•
separately billed x-rays or laboratory tests.
If the provider submits the evidence electronically it should be printed and mailed
to OIO.
NOTE: SSA will closely monitor these medical assistance requests through the DDSs. If there
is an indication that a DDS needs additional funds to cover these expenditures, supplemental
funding will be considered.
