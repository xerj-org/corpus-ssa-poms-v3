SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40510.160 - Establishing Future Diary
Effective Dates: 07/27/1992 - Present | TN 14 (08-92)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. New MFR/CDR done at time of enrollment
Establish a future diary date based on the date of the current favorable medical determination,
e.g. three years for a MIP impairment etc. (Refer to DI 26525.001 - DI 26525.045).
2. MFR/CDR completed within past two years
Establish a future diary date based on the MFR/CDR unless a future diary date is already
on the MBR.
