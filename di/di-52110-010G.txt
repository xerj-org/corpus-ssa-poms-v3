SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52110.010 - Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Payments Made to Employer
Effective Dates: 06/20/2023 - Present | TN 1 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452110010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Action when WC/PDB no longer paid to employer
Take the following actions when WC/PDB is no longer paid to the employer:
1.
Develop for any changes to the WC/PDB.
2.
Verify that the employer no longer receives any of the WC/PDB.
3.
Impose offset via ICF beginning with the first month the NH is allowed to keep any
of the WC/PDB, unless the NH became disabled prior to 03/01/1981 or the month of entitlement is earlier than 09/1981. In that case, follow the procedure
in DI 52170.040 - Notice Provisions – Pre 1981 Amendment Cases to determine the correct month to
begin offset.
