B. Item 14 - vocational rule
1. Procedure for vocational rule
Enter an "X" in this block when a case meets an appendix 2 vocational rule. Enter
the five-digit number when identifying the rule, e.g., “202.03” that is met in the
“Cite Rule” box.
2. References
For medical vocational guidelines, see DI 25025.000.
For exceptions to medical improvement, see DI 28020.000.
