SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40505.020 - Procedures for Field Office (FO) or Disability Determination Services (DDS) Deferral or Discontinuance
Effective Dates: 07/23/2025 - Present | TN 5 (07-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440505020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure for deferral or discontinuance
If the FO or DDS returns a CDR to the PC, the PC must:
•
Verify the reason for return of the discontinuance (Do not review a decision just
to defer.);
•
Set a new diary as appropriate (but no more than 6 months into the future) for a deferral;
or
•
Return the case to the FO or DDS if the basis for discontinuing the CDR is incorrect.
Explain the reason for the return on an SSA-5002, Report of Contact.
