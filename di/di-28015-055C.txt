C. What section to use if a CPD equals decision does not cite a listing
If a CPD equals decision does not cite a specific listing section (e.g., CPD says
“equals 11.01,” which is merely a heading, rather than specifying a section -- e.g.,
11.02, 11.03), use the section applicable to the primary impairment. If undecided
between two (or more) listing sections, use one that the beneficiary currently meets
or equals.
