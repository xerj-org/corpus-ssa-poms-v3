B. Procedure – FO responsibilities for changed-identity claims
If the FO receives a “changed-identity” claim, the FO should:
1.
Place the claim in double envelopes.
2.
Annotate (in red) the outside of the envelope to read:
"CONFIDENTIAL -- DO NOT OPEN IN MAILROOM"
3.
Mail the envelope to the appropriate address:
Social Security Administration
P.O. Box 359
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-0359
