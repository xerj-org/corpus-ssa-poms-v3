SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13005.115 - Field Office (FO) Procedures When the Disability Determination Services (DDS) Finds Undocumented Work Activity and a Continuance is Not Possible in Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) or MINE-Equivalent Cases
Effective Dates: 01/17/2017 - Present | TN 11 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413005115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure for (MINE) and MINE-equivalent cases with undocumented work activity
For these cases, the DDS will send the medical folder to the FO and the FO will follow
normal procedures to develop for work activity according to DI 13010.025. If the FO determines that benefits should continue despite work activity (for example,
there have been less than nine worked trial work service months) establishment Section
1619 eligibility, or process extended period of eligibility (EPE), see DI 13010.025 and return the folder to the DDS for a medical review.
