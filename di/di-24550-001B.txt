B. Procedure for using weight-for-length tables
1.
Use the appropriate chart in subsection DI 24550.001C or DI 24550.001D and find the reported length in Column 1. If the child's weight associated with that
length is equal to or less than the value shown in Column 2, the weight-for-length
measurement is below the third percentile.
2.
Repeat step 1 for each additional weight and length measurement in file.
3.
If there are at least three weight-for-length measurements that are below the third
percentile in a consecutive 12-month period, the B1 criterion for listing 105.08 is
met.
