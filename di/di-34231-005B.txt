SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34231.005 - Neurological Listings from 12/12/90 to 05/23/02
Effective Dates: 04/15/2005 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434231005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Minor motor seizures. Classical petit mal seizures must be documented by characteristic EEG pattern, plus
information as to age at onset and frequency of clinical seizures. Myoclonic seizures,
whether of the typical infantile or Lennox-gastaut variety after infancy, must also
be documented by the characteristic EEG pattern plus information as to age at onset
and frequency of seizures.
