DI 52120.255 Virginia Workers' Compensation (WC)
Workers'
compensation is monitored by the Virginia WC Board. WC insurance is provided through
private
carriers or employers may self-insure.
Virginia's
Section 50-Rule 3 provides that all agreements as to payment of WC must be put in
writing by the
employer and promptly filed with the WC Commission. If the claim is denied the employer
must notify
the worker and the commission in writing.
