D. Steps when the evidence supports a less than fully favorable determination
Follow the guidelines in DI 29025.001 and prepare the case for the disability hearing.
NOTE: We consider a case less than fully favorable when any period under assessment is
unfavorable. That is, either the childhood MIRS or the adult rules assessment results
in an unfavorable determination.
