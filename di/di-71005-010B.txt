B. Procedures for DDS reconsideration claims
Process reconsideration claims filed by residents of Mexico following normal adjudication
procedures found in DI 27000.000. Where applicable, the procedures described in DI 71005.005 may also apply at the reconsideration level.
NOTE: Unit 22 of the Texas DDS handles all reconsideration claims filed in Texas by Mexican
residents.
