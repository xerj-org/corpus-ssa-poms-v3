DI 28060.001 General - VR or Similar Program Participation
Sections 225(b) and 1631(a)(6) of the Social Security Act provide for continued payment
of disability or blindness benefits to individuals whose disability or blindness ends
for medical reasons while they are participating in the Ticket to Work and Self-Sufficiency
program or another program of VR services, employment services, or other support services
approved by us, if we determine that completion or continuation of the program will
increase the likelihood of the individual's permanent removal from the disability
or blindness benefit rolls. This provision is often referred to as “section 301” because
the initial legislative authority for continued payment of benefits to individuals
in a VR program was provided in section 301 of the Social Security Disability Amendments
of 1980.
The title XVI provision for section 301 payments also applies to an individual age
18 or older whose disability is determined to have ended as a result of a title XVI
age-18 redetermination.
See DI 14510.010 for DDS processing instructions for cases involving this provision.
