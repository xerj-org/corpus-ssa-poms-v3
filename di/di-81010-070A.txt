A. Disability Reports
Disability reports print one case level at a time from the “Print Forms” screen.
The following Disability Reports are available from the “Print Forms” page in EDCS:
•
SSA- 3368 Disability Report – Adult
•
SSA-3820 Disability Report – Child
•
SSA-3441 Disability Report – Appeal
•
SSA-3367 Disability Report – Field Office
