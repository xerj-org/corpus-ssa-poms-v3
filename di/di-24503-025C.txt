C. Sources of medical opinions and prior administrative medical findings
All medical sources, not just acceptable medical sources (AMSs), can make evidence
we categorize as medical opinions. Only medical consultants (MC) and psychological
consultants (PC) can make evidence we categorize as prior administrative medical findings.
