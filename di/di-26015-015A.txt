Effective Dates: 02/23/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426015015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Closed Period
When a claimant who resides in one of the four States (California, Iowa, Nevada or
Massachusetts) that pays a blindness supplement is found disabled but a closed period
is established on the visual impairment, prepare a personalized disability explanation
addressing the visual impairment issue and release it with an SSA-L1157-U2 (SI). This
is not a true multicategory case.
