D. Documenting the medical evaluation
Complete the medical evaluation on SSA-approved medical assessment form(s).
The DDS DE, including medical consultants (MC) and psychological consultants (PC),
complete medical assessment forms in the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS).
For guidance on completing medical assessment forms see the current "Claims Analysis"
functionality guide.
1. SSA-approved medical assessment forms
•
SSA-2506-BK (Psychiatric Review Technique)
•
SSA-4734-BK (Physical Residual Functional Capacity Assessment)
•
SSA-4734-F4-SUP (Mental Residual Functional Capacity Assessment)
•
SSA-538 (Childhood Disability Evaluation Form)
•
SSA-416 (Medical Evaluation)
2. Signature Requirements
The proper individual with an acceptable physical signature or an SSA-approved electronic
signature must sign each medical assessment form. Only an MC/PC may sign medical assessment
forms.

3. Medical assessment form(s) retention
Never remove any medical assessment form(s) from previous levels of adjudication.
a. Retain in file
Retain any medical assessment(s) in the file that:
•
Completes the medical portion of the claim, or
•
Supports the determination.
b. Discard at case closure
Move other medical assessments that do not reflect the final determination (i.e.,
partial or preliminary forms) to the temporary (green) section of the certified electronic
(CEF) or paper folder.
The system automatically discards all material from the green section after all quality
reviews are complete.
The office taking the last action for a paper folder will discard all material marked
as nonessential.
c. Correcting forms
Cases involving federal quality assurance review may require the creation of corrected
medical assessment forms.
For details on correcting forms, see QR 04440.232.
