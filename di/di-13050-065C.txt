C. Policy for EXR technical denials
FO technicians must screen cases for EXR eligibility per DI 13050.001, DI 13050.045A and DI 13050.050A, and discuss filing a new claim versus an EXR with the claimant per DI 13050.020. If the claimant does not meet the technical requirements for EXR but the claimant
insists on filing, technically deny the request. Send an EXR denial notice to the
claimant and any appropriate third party. Title II EXR denial notices are available
in DPS. Include Medicare information as appropriate. For instructions on inserting
the appropriate Medicare language, see DI 13050.070. If the claimant chooses to file a new initial claim for disability benefits, you
may use the request for EXR as a protective filing. If you determine that the claimant
is not eligible for EXR after the claimant files the request (for example, if the
claimant engaged in SGA in both the month of the EXR request and the following month),
technically deny the request.
