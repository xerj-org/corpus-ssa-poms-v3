B. When are interim benefits payable after a protested ALJ case
The OAO will notify OCO/PSC to start interim benefits (IB) payments. If OAO does not
issue a start order after 110 days have passed, see DI 42010.225A.
See DI 42010.215C.1 for an explanation on how to initiate Critical Payment System payments for Interim
Benefits.
