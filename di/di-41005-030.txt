DI 41005.030 Redeterminations for Statutory Blindness Cases
RS 00301.160, last paragraph, describes the circumstances under which a statutorily blind WE entitled
to DIB for months before 1/73 can have their period of disability redetermined based
on the fully insured status requirement based on a new application. DO's will route
such applications to ODO/PSC-DRS/INTPSC by a route slip with the remark “For redetermination—RS
00301.160.”
