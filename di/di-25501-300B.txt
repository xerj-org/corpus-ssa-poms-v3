B. Component roles for establishing the EOD for DIB claims
1. Field office (FO)
The FO provides the DDS with the claimant’s AOD, POD, DFI, and the DLI. If the DLI
is in the past and claimant alleges vision loss, the FO provides a blindness DLI.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The DDS establishes the earliest possible EOD supported by the medical evidence of
record and the policy stated in DI 25501.300A in this section. The EOD cannot be earlier than the DFI or later than the DLI.
