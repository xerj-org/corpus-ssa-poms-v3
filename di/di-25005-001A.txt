SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25005.001 - Determination of Capacity for Past Relevant Work (PRW) —Basics of Step 4 of the Sequential Evaluation Process
Effective Dates: 07/08/2024 - Present | TN 20 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425005001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Burden of proof at step 4
At step 4 :
•
If the disability determination proceeds to step 4, the claimant must provide evidence
that their medically determinable impairment(s) prevent them from performing past
relevant work (PRW).
•
If the claimant has PRW that is within their residual functional capacity (RFC) as
the claimant did the work or as generally performed in the national economy, or the
claimant does not provide relevant evidence of PRW, stop evaluation of disability
and deny the claim. For situations in which adjudicators may apply expedited step
4 processing, see POMS DI 25005.005.
•
If the evidence supports a finding that the claimant cannot do PRW, the disability
determination proceeds to step 5.
