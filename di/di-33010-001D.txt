SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.001 - General Review of Claims Folder and Preparation of In-House Folder in the Disability Hearing Unit (DHU)
Effective Dates: 08/25/2011 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Folder documents
1. Documents and review
a.
Review the folder for any "alerts" or identifying remarks. These may be found on an
SSA-5002 Report of Contact (or SSA-408 Route Slip for paper folders), or in the alerts
and message tab of eView.
b.
Review the folder for waivers or situations, such as confined individuals, that affect
scheduling and take appropriate action as needed.
c.
Review the SSA-1696 Appointment of Representative, or other written notice of appointment,
if applicable. See details in DI
33010.001E.
d.
Review the SSA-887 Summary of Evidence, if prepared by the pre-hearing unit, to assure
all medical and vocational reports listed are in the folder. See DI
29025.001B for details on the preparation of the SSA-887 Summary of Evidence.
e.
Review the SSA-789 Request for Reconsideration-Disability Cessation Right to Appear
or MSSICS generated printout to determine hearing preference.
f.
Ensure field office (FO) work development is complete in extended period of eligibility
(EPE) cases. An SSA-408 Route Slip should have annotation "EPE Case". An SSA-823 Report
of SGA Determination will be in file for cases without medical improvement expected
(MIE) diaries.
g.
If foreign documents need to be translated, see instructions in DI
33025.015B.2.
h.
Refer any potentially harmful documents to the supervisory hearing officer (SHO).
See DI
33010.015 for detailed instructions on adverse effect material.
i.
Review the SSA-416 Medical Evaluation, SSA-4734-BK Physical Residual Functional Capacity,
and SSA-4734-F4-SUP Mental Residual Functional Capacity assessment forms when required.
See DI 24501.006, DI 24510.005, DI
24510.060, and DI 28010.145.
j.
The SSA-2506-BK Psychiatric Review Technique, signed by a medical consultant, is included
in all cases involving a current evaluation of a mental impairment. See DI
24583.005 and DI
28010.140.
k.
The SSA-538 Childhood Disability Evaluation Form or Childhood Disability Evaluation
Form (CDE) is included in all cases involving childhood disability claims. See DI
25230.001.
NOTE: "ADVISORY" [in bold, upper case letters] will be noted on the following forms in the appropriate
sections or in the Notes section of eView as outlined in DI
29025.001A.2:
•
SSA-4734-BK Physical Residual Functional Capacity,
•
SSA-416 Medical Evaluation,
•
SSA-4734-F4-SUP Mental Residual Functional Capacity,
•
SSA-2506-BK Psychiatric Review Technique,
•
SSA-538 Childhood Disability Evaluation Form, or
•
Disability determination explanation (DDE).
