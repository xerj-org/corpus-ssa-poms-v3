DI 11010.370 Field Offices (FO) Retaining and Shipping Paper Modular Disability Folders (MDF)
This section provides field office (FO) instructions for retaining and shipping paper
modular disability folders (MDF).
NOTE: If a case is a certified electronic folder (CEF), fax the material into the electronic
folder.
