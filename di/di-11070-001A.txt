A. Background
On August 22, 1996, the President signed P.L. 104-193, (the Personal Responsibility
and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996), which includes significant changes
affecting the title XVI childhood disability evaluation and determination process.
The new law requires more serious limitations for a finding of disability than was
required under the old law.
Subsequently, on August 5, 1997, P.L. 105-33 was passed. This legislation modified
the earlier legislation by extending the time frames for conducting certain disability
redeterminations required under the earlier law. The legislation affects:
•
All child disability applicants who file applications on or after August 22, 1996;
•
Most childhood applicants whose claims for benefits are finally adjudicated on or
after August 22, 1996; and
•
Many current beneficiaries.
This subchapter explains the provisions of the law and provides field office (FO)
instructions for processing childhood and age 18 disability redetermination cases.
