SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10515.015 - Work Comparability Provisions for Blind Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), and Disabled Widow(er) Benefits (DWB) Claimants, Age 55 and Older
Effective Dates: 06/21/2012 - Present | TN 9 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410515015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applications
A blind DIB, CDB, or DWB claimant entitled to disability benefit status under the
special work comparability provisions whose benefits are suspended due to SGA need not file a new application when they
cease SGA in order to receive disability benefits. (This contrasts with the requirement
for blind workers
in regular “freeze” status only — CDB and DWB claimants are not eligible for “freeze” — who have been denied for cash benefit purposes because of SGA, and who must, therefore,
file a new application when SGA ceases in order to receive disability benefits.)
