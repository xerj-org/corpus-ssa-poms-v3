SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11052.015 - Processing End-Stage Renal Disease Medicare Claims Involving Insured Status Based on Medicare Qualified Government Employment
Effective Dates: 05/31/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411052015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. ESRD case processing
If the claimant meets insured status without considering GEQCs, process the claim
under regular ESRD procedures. If we use government employment to determine if insured
status is met, process the claim as in an initial claim with the following additional
considerations:
•
Complete Form SSA-892, End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) Medicare Determination as outlined
in HI 00801.308. If the claimant is the spouse or dependent child of the insured government employee,
indicate the government employee's Social Security number (SSN) in item 4 and his
or her name in item 6. We establish Medicare entitlement on the government employee's
SSN followed by the appropriate government Medicare Beneficiary Identification Codes
(BIC). Show “GEQC” in item 39 (this entry alerts the adjudicators to the SSN entry
in item 4). See SM 00550.010, Beneficiary Identification Codes (BIC) and Payment Identification
Codes (PIC).
EXAMPLE: If a child is filing for ESRD Medicare using the father’s MQGE insured status, we
do not establish the child as a BIC T on his or her own SSN. Instead, we establish
the child as a BIC TC1 on the father’s SSN. The field office (FO) will still take
the ESRD claim on the claimant’s SSN and prepare an A101.
•
When adjudicating ESRD Medicare awards using GEQCs for insured status, use the government
worker’s SSN and process these cases via Earnings Computation (EC). On the MREQ screen
(first screen of the earnings computation request), indicate “Y” to process as MQGE.
Preparation of an A101 is necessary only when you base insured status on a spouse’s
or parent’s GEQCs.
•
If the claimant receives a pension from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM),
complete the Health Insurance-Supp Medical Insurance (HICL) screen in the clearance
screen path in MCS to establish SMI premium deduction from the civil service pension.
If OPM withholds premiums from the spouse’s civil service annuity, show a STAT of
“S”; otherwise leave this field blank.
