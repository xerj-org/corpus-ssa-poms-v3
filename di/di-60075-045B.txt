B. PAS automated cases
PAS automatically processed the following cases:
•
Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) benefit only (BIC = HA);
•
SSDI benefit and auxiliary suspension. (NOTE: Auxiliaries were paid full benefits until the SSDI beneficiary went into full benefit
offset with a ledger account file (LAF) code of SQ);
•
Expedited reinstatement (EXR) cases after the beneficiary returned to payment status;
and
•
Concurrent entitlement (DIB and SSI). The offset will applied only to the SSDI portion.
