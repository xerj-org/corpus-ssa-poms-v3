SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.030 - Same Title Claims – New Claim Filed, Prior Claim at Reconsideration Level
Effective Dates: 07/20/2012 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy
1. Field Office (FO) Jurisdiction
If a prior claim is being reconsidered, the FO continues to process any new claims
that are the jurisdiction of the FO.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS) Jurisdiction
After any necessary action, the FO forwards new applications that are DDS jurisdiction
to the DDS for determinations and required notice(s). The DDS will combine the claims
and issue one determination or prepare two separate determinations and issue appropriate
notices.
