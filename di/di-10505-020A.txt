A. Policy - earnings which ordinarily demonstrate SGA
An employee will ordinarily be considered engaged in SGA if their “countable earnings”
average more than the amount shown in the Earnings Guidelines (DI 10501.015). When evaluating work in special employment situations other than military service,
see DI 10505.025.
NOTE: Military service members can have severe impairments and be performing limited, or
no, services as part of a limited duty or a work therapy program. Do not assume their
salary represents the value of the services they are doing. These services must be
evaluated based upon the policies and procedures provided in DI 10505.023.
