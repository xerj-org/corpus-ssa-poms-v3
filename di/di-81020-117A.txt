A. Definition of Medical Reactivations
Medical Reactivation changes a claim that is in closed, transferred, or manually cleared
status in the electronic folder to pending or "active" again. Medical Reactivation
is only permitted on a claim with a medical determination in the most recently closed
Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) case. A case is defined as all of the claims filed/adjudicated
at the same time.
