A. Procedure for routing forms
1. Field Office (FO) cases
Use an SSA-5526-U3, Request for Assistance – Disability. ODO may use this form to provide information
for the FO.
2. The FO will route cases to the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The FO will use an SSA-847-U3, SSA Request for Case Action, to send the case to DDS. The Processing Center (PC)
does not route cases directly to DDS.
