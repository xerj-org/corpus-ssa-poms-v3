DI 80630.040 Security
Access to the EF will be determined by the position type associated with the PIN of
the individual logged into the workstation.  Employees will only have authorized access
to those EF functions needed to perform their jobs.  Access will be restricted to
the minimum amount necessary for your position type.
If difficulty is encountered accessing the application, contact your designated Security
Officer for assistance.
