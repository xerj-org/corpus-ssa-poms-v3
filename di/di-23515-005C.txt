C. Determining onset of blindness or disability for non-citizens
To determine if the medical onset of blindness or disability occurred before or after
entry into the U.S.:
•
Request medical evidence of record (MER) from foreign sources only when the source(s)
treated the claimant during the 12-month period preceding the application filing date,
and a fully favorable determination cannot be made based on evidence from domestic
sources (including the medical records in the files of the Department of Homeland
Security (DHS).
•
Send MER requests from foreign sources directly to the source or obtain assistance
from the International Disability Unit (IDU).
