C. Creating an SSA-5002 in EDCS
Add information to the CEF by creating an SSA-5002 in EDCS. Users can use this method
to add new or changed generic information. For example, this method can be used to
update information on a disability report, such as new medication or a new provider
source.
To update information via an SSA-5002 in EDCS:
•
Open the case in EDCS;
•
Select Transfer/Updates on the toolbar at the top of the EDCS screen path;
•
Select the Other Updates tab, and enter the new or changed information in the space
provided;
•
Review all changes on the Other Updates tab; and
•
Click the OK button to send update to DDS or OHO.
