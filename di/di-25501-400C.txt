C. Component instructions for establishing the EOD involving a UWA period
1. FO instructions
The FO will:
•
follow the instructions in DI 11010.145 when there is a possibility of a UWA.
•
document work on form SSA-820-BK or SSA-821-BK.
•
record the UWA information and make recommendations on form SSA-823.
2. DDS instructions
The DDS will:
•
Determine the duration of the potential UWA.
NOTE: If the claimant or beneficiary’s work effort does not exceed 6 months, and if the
alleged reason work ended or reduced has a reasonable relationship to the impairment,
then we do not require verification of the reason work ended or reduced. The DDS should
request assistance from the FO to obtain any additional information necessary to make
a UWA determination.
•
Document the file with the UWA determination, either in the disability determination
explanation (DDE) or on a Form SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) that explains your UWA
determination and the facts used to support your determination.
