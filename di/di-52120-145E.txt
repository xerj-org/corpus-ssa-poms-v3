E. Reverse offset factored into WC settlement award
TT and PT settlement awards are processed through the State WC office for approval
and therefore always reflect consideration of the State reverse offset provisions
and DIB entitlement by SSA.
1.
If the type of payment is TT or PT, then reverse offset provisions apply whether or
not reverse offset is mentioned in the award.
2.
If the award specifies a type of payment other than TT or PT, then reverse offset
provisions do not apply, even if that award also mentions reverse offset.
3.
If the award does not specify the type of payment, further development is needed.
