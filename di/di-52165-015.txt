DI 52165.015 Deleting Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Data through Interactive
Computations Facility (ICF)
Use ICF function #32 to delete old and immaterial or erroneous WC/PDB data that appears
on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR).
