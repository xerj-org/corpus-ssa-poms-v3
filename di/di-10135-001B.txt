SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10135.001 - Deemed Entitlement to Disabled Widow(er)’s Benefits (DWB) for Medicare Part A Hospital Insurance (HI) Coverage
Effective Dates: 02/26/2015 - Present | TN 2 (05-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410135001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disabled widow(er)s who may be deemed entitled to DWB for Medicare
A disabled widow(er) may be deemed entitled to DWB for Medicare purposes if he or
she is:
•
not eligible for DWB prior to attainment of age 60; or
•
entitled to retirement insurance benefits (RIB) prior to age 65; or
•
entitled to mother’s or father’s benefits based on having a child in his or her care
and is at least age 50.
Note For the widow to qualify for DWB-Medicare benefits the EOD must be established before
the end of the prescribed period (See DI 25501.360A.4 for definition of prescribed period.)
