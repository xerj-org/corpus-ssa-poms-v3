D. DDS procedures for developing prior medical evidence
1. Residual file material
The DDS no longer creates or maintains paper residuals. All prior medical evidence
should be available electronically.
2. Title XVI conversion:
Attempt to locate the prior State records because two CPDs may apply. The actions
taken vary on a State-by-State and case-by-case basis. For additional information,
see DI 28010.020.
