A. Mississippi WC payment provisions
All payments are limited to 450 weeks and subject to a State maximum.
Once you are certain the WC evidence is not already in file, check the WC Resource
Page in DI 52140.020 to see if that state is one that provides SSA with access to its online records.
The value of online records can vary from State to State and some development may
still be required. However, the sites available usually provide contact information
for the employer, WC carrier or the WC attorney, which can jump-start the development
process. Also included in the WC resource intranet site is a list of limited individuals
in the PC with online access to those individual State records.
