SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.902 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary or Survivor Applications Filed During the Benefit Continuation Period (BCP) - Disability Benefit Equals or Exceeds Auxiliary or Survivor Benefit
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105902 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Applicant remains technically entitled to auxiliary or survivor benefit
If the NH, DWB, or CDB remains technically entitled to the auxiliary or survivor benefit
in continuance situations (when the disability benefit equals or exceeds the auxiliary
benefit amount but does not exceed the original rate), follow DI 10105.901C.2. for award processing. Use language in the notice that is appropriate to the situation.
As described in DI 10105.902C.2., it may be appropriate to recontact the claimant to withdraw the application if
the auxiliary or survivor entitlement appears disadvantageous.
