Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452120145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Types of WC Payments
1.
Temporary Total (TT) – reverse offset applies (see DI 52120.145B below)
2.
Permanent Total (PT) – reverse offset applies (see DI 52120.145B below)
3.
Temporary Partial (TP) - limited to a total of 26 weeks, but the period can be extended by the insurer
4.
Permanent Partial (PP)
a.
Impairment Awards (IA) – (code as PP)
b.
Wage Supplement (WS) – (code as PP); paid for dates of injury from 07/01/1987 through 06/30/1991. Maximum
period was 500 weeks
5.
Total Rehabilitation (TR) – (type of VR); maximum period is 104 weeks. A written, signed rehabilitation plan
must be in place before these benefits can be paid.
