SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 45001.324 - Processing ESRD Medicare Claims With Railroad Retirement Board Involvement
Effective Dates: 01/07/2016 - Present | TN 24 (03-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0445001324 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Award processing—LAF code on MBR is “E” (RRB has jurisdiction for payment of Title
II benefits)
1.
Complete Form SSA-892-U3.
2.
Photocopy the memorandum in HI 00801.325. Complete the requested items. Make one copy of the completed memorandum. Send original
memorandum to RRB:
Railroad Retirement Board
Medicare Program
844 North Rush St
Chicago, IL 60611–2092
Place a copy in the claims folder as documentation.
NOTE: All RRB letters and forms SSA-892s and SSA-3103s need to go to RRB at the address
above.
3.
Prepare Form SSA-5002 requesting the letter in DI 45001.348.
4.
Check block 4.f. of SSA-3601 and indicate in remarks “See SSA-5002 in file for notice
information.”
5.
Route SSA-3601 to ODO.
