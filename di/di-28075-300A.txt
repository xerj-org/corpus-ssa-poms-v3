A. Introduction to vacating a cessation determination
There are times when the Disability Determination Services (DDS) makes a Continuing
Disability Review (CDR) medical cessation determination, but the FO should never have
initiated the CDR. For example, the DDS completes a CDR on a case that should have
been protected from a medical review because a Ticket to Work was assigned and in
use, or the State Vocational Rehabilitation (SVR) program was in use before the CDR
was initiated. When this situation occurs, DDS must vacate the cessation determination
to restore the case to its prior status.
