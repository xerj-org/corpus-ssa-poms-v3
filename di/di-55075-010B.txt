B. Process – dispute resolution process used by State VR agencies
Under the dispute resolution process of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended,
beneficiaries/recipients and State VR agencies can resolves their disputes by formal
mediation, an impartial hearing, or civil action. Also, beneficiaries/recipients can
receive State P&A services under the Client Assistance Program (CAP) and State VR
agencies must advise beneficiaries/recipients of the availability of these services.
