H. Title XVI cases
The final section of the VERN screen displays Title XVI earnings and work incentive
information. It includes the same fields as the Title II section. The verified earnings
and applicable SSI work incentive information input to the SSR automatically propagates
to the DCF. The information is display only. If the information is not correct, correct
it via an EN field input to the SSR (see SM 01305.101B). Document the reasons for
differences in Title II and Title XVI countable earnings on the CDR REMARKS INPUT
SCREEN (IRMK) (see MSOM CDR 001.009 and DI
13010.630) if the reason is not apparent. Documentation is not necessary if the sole reason
is due to the difference is the result of paid vs. earned.
