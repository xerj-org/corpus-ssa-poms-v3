DI 25501.450 Inferring Onset for the Established Onset Date (EOD)
This section explains our policy regarding inferring onset dates.
REMINDER:
•
The onset date is the first day the claimant meets the definition of disability or
statutory blindness as defined in the Act and regulations.
•
The established onset date (EOD) is the date that a claimant meets the definition
of disability and all the relevant entitlement or eligibility factors.
