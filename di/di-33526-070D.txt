D. Impairment codes for secondary diagnosis associated with symptomatic HIV infection
When the impairment code for symptomatic HIV (0430) is recorded in the Primary Diagnosis
block of the SSA-831 (Item 16A), SSA-832 (Item 21), or SSA-833 (Item 21), use the
appropriate code from the table below to identify the manifestation of HIV in the
Secondary Diagnosis block (Item 16B of the SSA-831, or Item 22 of the SSA-832 or SSA-833).
Do not use the codes in this table in the Primary Diagnosis block of the SSA-831,
SSA-832, or SSA-833.
