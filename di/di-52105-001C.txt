C. Scope of recognized reverse offset plans
Reverse offset applies only to the WC/PDB paid under that State or entity’s plan or
system. It does not apply to other sources of WC or other types of WC/PDB that a number
holder (NH) might receive.
EXAMPLE: The State of Colorado has a reverse jurisdiction plan, but a Colorado resident who
receives Federal WC under the Federal Employees Compensation Act (FECA) would not
be receiving payments under Colorado’s reverse offset plan. Offset would still apply
to the DIB benefits due to receipt of Federal WC payments.
