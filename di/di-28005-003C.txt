C. How to evaluate age-18 disability redeterminations
The medical improvement review standard (MIRS) does not apply to an age-18 disability
redetermination. Age-18 disability redeterminations follow the sequential evaluation
process for Title II and adult Title XVI initial claims as outlined in DI 22001.001; however, the substantial gainful activity (SGA) step of the sequential evaluation
process does not apply. For additional information about the development and evaluation
of age-18 disability redeterminations, see DI 23570.020.
