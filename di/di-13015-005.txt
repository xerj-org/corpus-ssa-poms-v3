DI 13015.005 Failure to Cooperate-Insufficient Evidence Decision (FTC) Suspension Procedures for
Continuing Disability Reviews (CDR)- Field Office (FO)
This subchapter provides FO procedures for processing FTC suspension actions when
the beneficiary or recipient does not respond to our request for necessary information
in a CDR.
