SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.025 - Developing Evidence from Medical Sources of National Significance
Effective Dates: 08/18/2022 - Present | TN 38 (08-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure - General
Follow these instructions to develop evidence from medical sources of national significance.
1. SSA-827
Always send a completed, signed, and dated SSA-827.
2. Cover Letter
Also send the standard hospital cover letter, except where the instructions require
that an SSA-883-U3 be sent. (A computer-generated request for assistance letter may
be used in lieu of an SSA-883-U3.)
3. Follow up
Follow up with the appropriate contact person if evidence is not received within 30
calendar days from the date of the request.
