A. Identifying MC/WW claims
MC/WW is a claim involving any current or former member of a military service who:
•
sustained an illness, injury, or wound while on active duty status on or after October
1, 2001; and
•
alleges a physical or mental impairment, regardless of how the impairment occurred,
or where it occurred (i.e., United States or on foreign soil).
