B. Who is entitled to Title II disability IB
We pay IB to disabled wage earners, disabled widow(ers), and childhood disability
beneficiaries.
IB are payable only to the claimant filing for disability benefits. Defer payment
of auxiliary benefits until receipt of a final decision.
