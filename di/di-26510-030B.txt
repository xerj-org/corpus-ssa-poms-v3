SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.030 - Completing Item 19 (Claimant Not Disabled) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disabled Widow(er) Benefits (DWB)
1. Block A – Through date of current determination
Check this block in DWB cases where the prescribed period requirement expires after
the date of adjudication and disability is not established through the date of the
current determination.
NOTE: The prescribed period entry (date last met) can be found in item 6 of the SSA-3367.
2. Block B – Through (insert appropriate date)
•
Check this block in DWB cases where the individual is found not disabled on or before
the date that the prescribed period requirement is last met and such date is before
the date of the current determination.
•
Enter the ending date of the prescribed period.
