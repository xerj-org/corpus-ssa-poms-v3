DI 39554.001 DDS Audits and Administrative Reviews--General
This subchapter establishes standards and guidelines for the disability determination
services (DDS) relating to audits. These standards and guidelines are necessitated
by 20 CFR Parts 404 and 416 governing the Federal/State relationship as required by Public Law 96-265. 20 CFR
404.1627 and 416.1027, as supplemented by 20 CFR 200, which contain the basic information regarding DDS
Audits. Audits will be conducted as soon as possible after the close of each fiscal
year and establish that there has been compliance with pertinent Federal statutes
and regulations.
