SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.025 - MIE Criteria
Effective Dates: 07/27/2015 - Present | TN 15 (12-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure - general
1. Initial Claims
Set a MIE diary in initial allowances to mature at a time when the MIRS criteria will probably be met.
a. Title II Cases and Title XVI Adult Cases
For all title II cases and title XVI adult cases, this means that all the following
conditions will probably be met:
•
The individual's impairment(s) will have medically improved, and
•
The medical improvement will be related to the ability to work, and
•
The individual will have the ability to work.
b. Title XVI Child cases
For title XVI child cases, this means that all the following conditions will probably
be met:
•
The child's impairment(s) will have medically improved, and
—
The impairment(s) will no longer meet or equal the comparison point decision listing,
and
—
The impairment(s) will no longer cause marked and severe functional limitations.
c. To Make Judgment
To make this judgment:
•
evaluate the case facts under the criteria in DI 26525.025B.
•
take into account other impairments or any adverse vocational factors (at the time
of diary maturity) which would preclude cessation.
d. Set MIE Diary
When warranted per DI 26525.025A.1., set the MIE diary for the most appropriate reason in DI 26525.025B.
NOTE: See DI 26525.030 for examples of impairments, which may warrant a MIE diary when a criterion in DI 26525.025B. is met.
2. CDRs
Set a MIE diary after a CDR only when MIRS criteria will almost certainly be met (e.g., the 3-year remission rule will be met in a case which meets or medically equals
the cancer listings (13.00 or 113.00)).
3. Exclusions
a. General
Generally exclude from MIE diary cases which will mature at age 54 1/2 or later. Set
a MINE diary in these cases.
b. Exceptions
Set the MIE diary to mature up to age 59 1/2 for individuals whose case facts indicate
that remission or recovery is almost certain (e.g., the individual is currently in
remission; recent or scheduled surgery will resolve the impairment; current significant,
sustained, and progressive improvement will lead to full recovery). Examples include
certain cases involving:
•
fractures, sprains, or dislocations
•
cancers (see DI 26525.010), lymphomas, or leukemias
•
title XVI statutory blindness when the individual is expected to no longer be statutorily
blind (set the MIE diary to mature up to age 64 1/2 in these cases). CDRs are necessary
in these cases to determine if these individuals continue to be eligible for special
blindness benefits.
