SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28057.045 - Expedited Reinstatement and Initial Claim Filed for the Same Benefit
Effective Dates: 12/01/2011 - Present | TN 8 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428057045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Most individuals who meet the requirements for EXR also meet the requirements to file
a new initial claim. The field office (FO) technician discuss the filing considerations
in DI 13050.020. The individual must decide which claim they wish to pursue. While an individual
may insist on filing both a new initial claim and a request for EXR at the same time,
we can only process one claim allowance.
