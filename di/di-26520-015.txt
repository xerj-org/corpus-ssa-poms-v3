DI 26520.015 Advising the Designated State Agency About Title XVI Childhood Cessations
POLICY
When the Disability Determination Services (DDS) determines that a child under age
16 is no longer blind or disabled for title XVI purposes, forward a copy of Form SSA-832-U5,
Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal—Title
XVI, to the State agency designated to receive referrals of allowed title XVI children
(see DI 26520.010).
