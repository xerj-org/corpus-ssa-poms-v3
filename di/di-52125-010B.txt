SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52125.010 - Determining When a Public Disability Benefit (PDB) is Based on Covered Service
Effective Dates: 06/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452125010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Alleged amount of covered service precludes offset
If the alleged amount of covered service precludes offset:
1.
Verify covered service.
NOTE: Do not accept the NH’s allegation.
2.
Do not offset if verification shows the covered exclusion is met.
3.
Verify the amount of the PDB and impose offset if the exclusion is not met.
