C. Adjudicator responsibilities related to the filing date of a claim
1. Policy responsibility
An adjudicator must use the correct set of rules related to the filing date of a claim
to evaluate evidence before issuing a determination or decision.
•
Adjudicate a claim(s) with a filing date before March 27, 2017, using the prior rules.
•
Adjudicate a claim(s) with a filing date on or after March 27, 2017, using the current
rules.
Follow the guidance in DI 24503.050D in this section to determine which set of rules to follow.
2. Procedural responsibility for electronic claims
An adjudicator must ensure that the eView MedEv indicator shown displays the correct
value based on the filing date of the claim before issuing the determination or decision.
If an adjudicator believes that the indicators value may be incorrect, contact the
FO to resolve any issues, as appropriate.
If the indicator displays an incorrect value, the adjudicator must manually correct
the indicators value in eView by clicking on the eView MedEv indicator hyperlink to
change the value displayed.
