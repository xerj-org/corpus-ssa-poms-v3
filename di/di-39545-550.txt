DI 39545.550 DDS Consultative Examination (CE) Oversight Reporting
The DDS Annual CE Oversight Report is designed to capture substantive data and facilitate
meaningful national and regional analyses. This is an annual (fiscal year) report
covering the preceding 12-month period.
Procedure
•
To gather and report CE oversight information use Exhibit 2 (DDS Annual CE Oversight
Report)DI 39545.575.
•
Submit the Annual CE Oversight Report to Disability Adjudication (DA) within 45 days
following the end of the fiscal year.
