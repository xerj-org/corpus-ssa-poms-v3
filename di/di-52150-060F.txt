SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.060 - Prorating a Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Lump Sum Settlement
Effective Dates: 08/01/2023 - Present | TN 36 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Systems proration of the LS
1. MCS earnings computation (EC)
Follow instructions in DI 52155.010 to process WC/PDB LS actions involving an MCS claim.
2. ICF
Prorate the LS through the ICF unless a program limitation applies. Follow instructions
in DI 52165.001 - Interactive Computations Facility (ICF) for Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability
Benefit (WC/PDB) Computations.
NOTE: Future proration end dates computed via MCS or ICF WC/PDB are propagated to the MBR.
When the future date matures, RETAP selections and alerts enable the T2 process to
adjust the WC/PDB automatically or alert the PC to complete the adjustment action.
