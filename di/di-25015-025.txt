DI 25015.025 Evaluation of Disability and Blindness in Initial Claims for Individuals Aged 65 or
Older — SSR 03-3p
NOTE:
On February 25, 2020, SSA published final rules “Removing Inability to Communicate
in English as an Education Category” (85 FR 10586). These final rules removed the
education category of “inability to communicate in English” from our regulations.
Therefore, the individuals aged 65 or older referenced in the third paragraph under
Applying the Criteria in Appendix 2 to Subpart
P of 20 CFR Part 404 in section titled “Step 5—Can the Individual Do Other Work?” cover individuals who are illiterate (i.e., those unable to read and write in any language), and do not include those who are unable to communicate in English.
NOTE:
On June 22, 2024, SSRs 82-61, 82-62, 82-63, and 86-8 were rescinded.
