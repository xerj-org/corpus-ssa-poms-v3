Effective Dates: 02/18/2022 - Present | TN 1 (02-22)

C. Procedure – handling questions and inquiries
Field components have no direct role in disputes between beneficiaries/recipients
and ENs that are not State VR agencies.
1. Beneficiary/Recipient complaints about an EN
Refer beneficiaries/recipients with complaints about an EN to the TPM or suggest they
contact their State's P&A system.
TPM toll free numbers are:
•
1-866-968-7842 (1-866-YOUR TICKET); and
•
TTY/TDD 1-866-833-2967 (1-866-TDD 2WORK).
2. EN complaints about a beneficiary/recipient
Refer ENs with complaints about a beneficiary/recipient that they are serving to TPM.
3. State P&A inquiries
Refer State P&A employees who are representing beneficiaries/recipients in a dispute
with an EN to TPM.
EXCEPTION: Answer any general, program-related questions the P&A employee may have.
