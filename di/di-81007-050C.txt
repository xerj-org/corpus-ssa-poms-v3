C. Who can use iAppeals and the i3441
Any claimant, or third party acting on behalf of a claimant, can file an iAppeals
request if the claimant has received an unfavorable medical determination on:
•
An initial Title II or Title XVI claim processed in the Electronic Disability Collect
System (EDCS); or
•
A reconsideration processed in EDCS.
NOTE: IUs cannot request Appeals Council review via the Internet. The system will block
such attempts and tell the IU to contact SSA.
