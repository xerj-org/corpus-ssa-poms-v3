SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.125 - Developing Cause of Death - Claimant Dies within 1 Year of Denial
Effective Dates: 07/14/2022 - Present | TN 73 (07-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010125 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Overview
When a disabled claimant dies from a non-traumatic impairment within 1 year of the
date of denial or cessation, and a reconsideration was not requested by the claimant
before his/her death, or one has not been filed subsequently by a survivor, the case
is reexamined and possibly reopened. No SSA-561 (Request for Reconsideration) is required.
If death resulted from a subsequent traumatic event, no reexamination is made under
this procedure.
