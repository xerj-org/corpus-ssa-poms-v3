SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52125.010 - Determining When a Public Disability Benefit (PDB) is Based on Covered Service
Effective Dates: 06/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452125010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Alleged amount of covered service does not preclude offset
If the alleged amount of covered service does not preclude offset:
1.
Accept the NH’s allegation.
2.
Verify the amount of the PDB and impose offset.
3.
Verify post-adjudicatively; and
4.
Remove offset if verification shows exclusion met.
