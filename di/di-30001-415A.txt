Effective Dates: 10/23/2009 - Present |

A. Field Office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) Responsibilities
The FO and DDS are responsible for accurately completing the appropriate blocks on
the SSA-832-U3 and SSA-833-U3.
1. Forms completion
The DDS completes all items except items 31-33 on both forms.
2. Concurrent cases
In concurrent Title II/XVI cases, separate SSA-832-U3 and SSA-833-U3 determinations
must be prepared.
