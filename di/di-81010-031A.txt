Effective Dates: 04/28/2025 - Present | TN 85 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010031 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Multiple pending cases (MPC)
MPCs exist when both an initial claim and a continuing disability review (CDR) or
Expedited Reinstatement (EXR) Classification case are pending for the same individual
at the same time. An example of an MPC includes a Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
CDR with an initial claim for Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB) or a Disability
Insurance Benefit (DIB) CDR with an initial claim for SSI.
EDCS can process initial, reconsideration, and hearings level MPCs at the same adjudicative
level as well as MPC scenarios listed in subsection D.  For MPCs that cannot be processed
in EDCS, refer to DI 81010.030D.
