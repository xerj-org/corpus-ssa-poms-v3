A. Process – Alert Intervals
1. Reconsideration Level
•
Initial alerts occur when the reconsideration has been pending 4 months from the date
of request.
•
Subsequent reconsideration alerts occur at 3-month intervals until the appeal is resolved.
2. ALJ Hearing Level
•
Initial alerts occur when the hearing has been pending 6 months from the date of request.
•
Subsequent hearing alerts occur at 3-month intervals until the appeal is resolved.
