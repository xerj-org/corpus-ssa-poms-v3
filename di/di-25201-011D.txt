SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25201.011 - Making a Determination for Youth in Foster Care When the Application Effective Date Is On or After the Attainment of Age 18
Effective Dates: 01/12/2010 - Present | TN 6 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425201011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure when the claimant meets non-medical eligibility factors before attainment
of age 18
If the claimant meets non-medical eligibility factors for any month prior to the attainment
of age 18, follow normal procedures in DI 25201.005 and DI 25201.010.
