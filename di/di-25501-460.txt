DI 25501.460 Determining Onset for Non-traumatic Static and Progressive Impairments
This section explains our policy for determining an onset date in cases with non-traumatic
static and progressive impairments.
REMINDERS:
•
The onset date is the first day the claimant meets the definition of disability or
statutory blindness as defined in the Act and regulations.
•
The established onset date (EOD) is the date that a claimant’s physical or mental
impairment(s) meets the definition of disability and all the relevant entitlement
or eligibility factors.
