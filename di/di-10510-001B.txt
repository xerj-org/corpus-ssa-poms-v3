B. Policy — Unsuccessful work attempt not a measure of SGA
Work in self-employment does not demonstrate the ability to engage in SGA if, after
a short time (that is, no more than 6 months), the work was involuntarily discontinued
or reduced below the level of SGA for reasons relating to the individual's impairment.
Such an effort would be identified as an unsuccessful work attempt (UWA). (See DI 11010.145.)
