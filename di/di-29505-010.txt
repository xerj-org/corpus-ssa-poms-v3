DI 29505.010 Development Requests for Residents of Other Localities in Mexico and for Residents
of Some Canadian Provinces
Designated disability determination services (DDS) in northern and southern Border
States perform the disability development and make the final disability determination
for eligible Canadian and Mexican residents. For more information about Canadian and
Mexican claims in these states, see DI 71000.000 – Processing Disability claims Filed in the United States by Canadian and Mexican
Residents.
