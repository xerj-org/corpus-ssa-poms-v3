There are separate mechanisms for resolving disputes that may arise under the Ticket
to Work program. These mechanisms provide the parties to a dispute with reasonable
opportunity for a full and fair review of the matter in dispute. The chart below lists
various issues or complaints that may arise, the parties to these disputes, and a
reference to the section of this subchapter that discusses the type of issue or complaint.
