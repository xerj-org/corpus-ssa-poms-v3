B. UniForms generated by the field office (FO)
When an FO adds a UniForm to the CEF after transferring the case to the DDS, there
is an alert to notify the DDS of an update to the CEF. UniForms completed by the FO
are stored in the appropriate section of the CEF.
NOTE: This subsection does not apply to documentation in electronic tools, such as DCPS,
which is not currently used in the FO.
