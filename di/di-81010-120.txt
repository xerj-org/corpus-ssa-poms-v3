DI 81010.120 Using Form Attestation for Work Activity Reports
Use signature proxy for the following forms for Title TII and Title XVI claims:
•
SSA-820 (Work Activity Report for Self Employed Person); and
•
SSA-821 (Work Activity Report – Employee).
NOTE: If creating the SSA-820/SSA-821 for a CEF exclusion case with an MDF as the official folder, print and insert the report and attestation cover notice (when applicable)
into the Modular Disability Folder (MDF) or mail them to the office with jurisdiction
of the MDF. For further instructions involving CEF exclusions, refer to Certified
Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions - DI 81010.030.
For additional information on signature proxy and attestation, see Alternative Signature
Methods - GN 00201.015.
