SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 14510.010 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Procedures for Cases Involving Participation in Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) or Similar Program
Effective Dates: 02/29/2024 - Present | TN 12 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0414510010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure to make a medical determination
1. Continuance of disability
If DDS determines a continuance of disability or blindness (or a beneficiary in an
Age 18 Redetermination is found disabled under the adult standard for initial claims),
make all systems inputs and release the case according to existing procedures.
For CEF cases, process per DI 81020.200 through DI 81020.255.
For routing CEF cases, process per DI 81020.250.
2. Cessation of medical determination
Process cessation cases per DI 81020.200 through DI 81020.255.
CEF cases with a “Potential Section 301 Case” flag generate an alert to the FO via
the EDCS Actions Page “CDR Cessation Added-Potential 301 Benefit Continuation.” For
paper cases, the DDS alerts the FO of the cessation with the remark on the SSA-408,
“Potential 301 benefits – handle per DI 14510.003C.”
NOTE: Continuation of benefits under Section 301 does not apply when medical cessation
is due to failure to cooperate (FTC) or whereabouts unknown (WU).
a. Cessation notice
Prepare and release the cessation notice according to existing procedures. Include
the following paragraph in the cessation notice:
“You may be eligible for continued payments even though your disability has ended
if you are currently participating in an appropriate program of vocational rehabilitation,
employment, training, educational, or other support services. We are sending your
case to another office for a special decision about continued payments based on the
information we have about your participation in such a program. You will be notified
of that decision in another letter.”
Place a copy of the notice in the paper or electronic folder.
b. Title II cases
Complete the SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal)
and enter the cessation date in item 9B. Do not enter the disability termination date.
Check “301” case in item 9I. Enter “301 Case” in Remarks.
c. Title XVI cases
Complete the SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuation of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal)
and enter the cessation date in item 9B. Do not enter the disability termination date.
Check “301” case in item 9I. Enter “301 Case” in Remarks.
NOTE: For paper cases, fax a copy of the SSA-832 to the servicing FO with a cover sheet
annotated “301 Case – Input VP diary per DI 14510.003D.2." (Refer to Detailed Office/Organization Resource System (DOORS) to obtain the FO
fax number.)
