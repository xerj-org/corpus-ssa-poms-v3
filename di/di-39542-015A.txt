SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39542.015 - Contract Proposals Not Requiring DA Approval
Effective Dates: 09/16/2026 - Present | TN 33 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439542015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating policy - new contracts
The State is not required to obtain Disability Adjudication (DA) approval (unless the State is receiving
TMA) of plans to contract for personnel management or administrative functions which
do not involve disclosure of SSA claimant information to the private sector. These
include contracts for:
•
in-house medical consultants;
•
building services
and equipment maintenance;
•
services provided by other agencies of the State;
•
functions, services, or purposes
that do not concern SSA in terms of charges to the SSA/DDS budget, e.g., for non-SSA
work such as processing claims for a State teachers' disability program or Medicaid.
