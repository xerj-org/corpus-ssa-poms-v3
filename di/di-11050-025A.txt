A. Filing a New Initial Disability Claim - Freeze Previously Completed
When the NH files a claim for DIB based on work performed since the prior determination,
or now meets the DIB insured status requirement; and EC or ICERS shows that a joint
RRB-SSA freeze decision has been made,
•
Secure application(s) and complete EDCS,
•
Document prior claim information in EDCS, and
•
Transfer the claim to Branch 2A of Disability Case Review, V32 for processing.
