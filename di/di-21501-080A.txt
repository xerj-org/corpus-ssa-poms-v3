A. Blindness — definition of blindness (3231)
A person shall be considered blind whose vision with corrective lenses is 20/ 200
or less in the better eye, or whose visual acuity is greater than 20/200 but with
a limitation in the field of vision such that the widest diameter of the remaining
visual field subtends to an angle no greater than 20 degrees, or who has other eye conditions which render vision equally defective, as determined by the Supervising Ophthalmologist.
