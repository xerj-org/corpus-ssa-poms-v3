SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29025.001 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Action When Fully Favorable Reconsidered Determination Is Not Issued
Effective Dates: 08/31/2026 - Present | TN 11 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429025001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Case preparation disability hearing
Less than fully favorable disability cessation appeals are not adjudicated at the
pre-hearing (PH) level. The PH level completes assessment forms and sends all less
than fully favorable determinations to the disability hearing unit (DHU).
The DDS will take the following actions to prepare a case for the disability hearing:
1.
Ensure proper documentation of the folder. For more information about documenting
the disability folder, see DI 20503.001.
2.
Enter "Advisory" on the medical assessment form(s) completed by the medical/psychological
consultant (MC/PC) at the PH level:
•
SSA-4734-BK Physical Residual Functional Capacity Assessment,
•
SSA-4734-F4-SUP Mental Residual Functional Capacity Assessment,
•
SSA-2506-BK Psychiatric Review Technique Form,
•
SSA-538-F6 Childhood Disability Evaluation Form,
•
SSA-416-UF Medical Evaluation.
NOTE 1: If the case is sent to the DHU, the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS) marks
all applied medical assessments as "Advisory" on the Disability Determination Explanation
(DDE). If the assessment form has been imaged into the certified electronic folder
(e.g., paper cases) and cannot be changed, the user will access eView and annotate
"Advisory" in the "Notes" field next to the assessment form. To access the "Notes"
field, the user will click on Details and type "Advisory" in the note field.
NOTE 2: If the MC/PC is adopting the initial level medical assessment(s), ensure all the criteria
in DI 27001.015B.2. are met.
For more information about processing a reconsideration appeal of an electronic continuing
disability review (eCDR) cessation, see DI 81020.255B.1.b.
3.
Prepare an SSA-887 (Summary of Evidence). See DI 29025.001B. for more information about completing the SSA-887, and DI 29025.010 for an exhibit of the form.
NOTE: The DDS Administrator has the discretion to determine whether the DDS or the DHU
completes the SSA-887 (Summary of Evidence). If a Federal DHU is responsible for issuing
the reconsidered determination, the DDS Administrator and Disability Adjudication
(DA) must agree whether the Federal DHU or the DDS will complete the SSA-887.
