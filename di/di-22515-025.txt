DI 22515.025 Use of Form SSA-3368-BK (Disability Report – Adult)
The SSA-3368-BK (Disability Report – Adult) collects information to develop technical
aspects of a disability claim that can be used to establish the correct onset date,
identify unsuccessful work attempts (UWA), and determine whether work is considered
substantial gainful activity (SGA).
The SSA-3368-BK also collects information to develop medical and other evidence that
can be used to assess the claimant’s alleged impairments in conjunction with non-medical
factors, such as education and work history information.
