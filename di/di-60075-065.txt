DI 60075.065 Appeal Process for Promoting Opportunity Demonstration (POD) Overpayments
When a POD participant reports monthly earnings higher than the offset threshold amount,
PAS applied a $1 for $2 benefit offset, following the procedure set out in DI 60075.040. An overpayment occurred when a POD participant submitted a late earnings report
or when PAS applied additional benefit offset based on the annual earnings posted
at the end of the year. The master beneficiary record (MBR) displays POD overpayments
by the type of event (TOE) code of 80 with an explanation of BOND/POD DEMO. Since
POD overpayments have the same type-of-event (TOE) code (80) as Benefit Offset National
Demonstration (BOND) overpayments. To distinguish between a POD overpayment and a
BOND overpayment:
•
View the Special Message field on the MBR - Special Message will explain whether the
beneficiary is in either the POD or the BOND;
•
View the HISTDEMO on the MBR for a DPI of DP; and
•
View DCF DEMO screen for DPI of DP and Start and Stop dates of participation.
A POD participant could request reconsideration or waiver of the overpayment.
