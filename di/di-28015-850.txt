DI 28015.850 Types of Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) Assessment(s) Needed
Medical improvement review standard (MIRS) continuing disability reviews (CDRs) sometimes
require more than one RFC assessment.
•
One that considers only the comparison point decision (CPD) impairment(s) to assess
“MI-related to the ability to work,” (MIRS RFC); and
•
Another that considers all current impairments to assess current disability, and to
document the file for future CDRs if disability continues.
