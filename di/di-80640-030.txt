DI 80640.030 eView
eView is an application that enables all users involved in case processing to view and
print the disability information contained in the Electronic Folder. See DI 81005.045 for details about viewing disability reports stored in the EF from the Case Data
Tab in eView.
Training materials for eView are available on the Office of Learning’s web site.
