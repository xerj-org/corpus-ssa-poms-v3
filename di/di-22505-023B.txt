B. Requesting MER from military health sources
The guidelines for requesting MER from military health sources supplement general
instructions for requesting MER in DI 22505.000. Use these guidelines to develop initial claims, reconsideration claims, and continuing
disability reviews:
If…
Follow…
The claim file is electronic, you need evidence from the DoD, and one of the following:
1.
There is no evidence from the DoD in the medical records (yellow) section of the electronic
folder (EF),
2.
There is a health information technology (HIT) response document in the blue section
of the EF indicating the MEGAHIT evidence transfer did not occur successfully, or
3.
You need new or updated evidence from the DoD that is not already in the EF
DoD User Trigger request procedures in DI 22505.023C in this section.
The claim file is electronic, you need evidence from the DoD, and both of the following:
1.
You already used the User Trigger request to receive evidence from the DoD but did
not receive the needed evidence per procedures in DI 22505.023C (in this section), and
2.
Your office uses the electronic Outbound Request (eOR) feature of electronic records
express (ERE)
DoD central location request procedure in DI 22505.023D in this section.
Any of the following applies:
1.
The claim file is paper,
2.
You need military health records from the VA or NPRC, or
3.

a.

DoD central location request procedures in DI 22505.023D in this section
