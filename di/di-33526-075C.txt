C. Reminders for choosing an impairment code for LBW or FTT
•
Do not use 7649 or 7650 for a continuing disability review (CDR) continuance. [LBW is no longer considered
a medically determinable impairment (MDI) after the infant attains age one.]
•
Do not use 7830 if the child has attained age three before the date of the determination. Use an
impairment code in the body system that is primarily affected.
