B. FO improperly transfers new disability claim to the Disability Determination Services
(DDS)
If you improperly transfer a new disability claim to the DDS, alert the DDS. Use the
Update-After-Transfer (UAT) function to create an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) in
EDCS.
Then, the DDS will:
•
Follow the procedures in DI 51501.030.
•
If the DDS finds no evidence indicating a new critical or disabling condition, the
DDS will transfer jurisdiction back to the FO.
When the DDS transfers the case back to the FO, you will receive a “Transfer” (TR)
on the EDCS Actions Page with the remark: “Subsequent Claim.” Follow the instructions
in DI 51501.010A in this section.
