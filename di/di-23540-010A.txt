SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23540.010 - Completing Form SSA-831 for Medicare Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) Cases
Effective Dates: 12/14/2011 - Present | TN 2 (09-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423540010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Item 11 - remarks
This item should show the applicable remarks made by the Field Office (FO) if the
claim is in the Modernized Claims System (MCS). Item 11 of all MQGE claims should
show “MQGE,” as well as “insured status under transitional provisions of P.L. 97-248,”
and “MQGE claim for Medicare entitlement only” if identified by the FO.
