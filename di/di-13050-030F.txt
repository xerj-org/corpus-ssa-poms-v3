F. Policy - Medicaid
In most states, eligibility for provisional payments also will allow the person to
be eligible for Medicaid benefits (see SI 01730.010). Unless reinstated, the person's eligibility for Medicaid benefits based on provisional
SSI payments ends when the provisional payments end.
