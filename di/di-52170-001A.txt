SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52170.001 - Paper Forms Used to Manually Compute Workers’ Compensation (WC)/Public Disability Benefit (PDB) Offset
Effective Dates: 03/10/2026 - Present | TN 7 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452170001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Paper WC/PDB computation forms
1. SSA-2455 (Offset worksheet – Disability insurance benefits)
Use the SSA-2455 to compute the amount of WC/PDB offset and the amounts payable to
the worker and auxiliaries. See DI 52170.010 - Form SSA-2455.
2. SSA-3643 (Offset worksheet – Triennial redetermination)
Use the SSA-3643 to compute increases in benefits as a result of a triennial redetermination.
See DI 52170.015 - SSA-3643.
3. SSA-2454 (Offset worksheet – Triennial redetermination previously applied)
Use the SSA-2454 to compute the amounts payable to the worker and auxiliaries after
offset when the WC/PDB rate changes after a redetermination increase. See DI 52170.020 - SSA-2454.
