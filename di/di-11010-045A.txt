SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.045 - Claimant Cannot Be Contacted or Whereabouts Unknown or Claimant Does Not Wish to Pursue Title II Claim
Effective Dates: 04/20/2026 - Present | TN 90 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
A disallowance is usually preferable to a withdrawal; this is particularly true in
disability cases.
1. Explanation - disallowance and withdrawal situations
The following subsections contain processing instructions for Title II disallowance
and withdrawal situations when the claimant cannot be contacted or does not wish to
pursue his/her claim or the claimants whereabouts are unknown.
2. Reference
See W/D vs. Disallowance - GN 00206.011 for a discussion of policy and procedures applicable to withdrawals.
