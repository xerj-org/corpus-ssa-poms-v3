F. Interpreter
Review the SSA-789 Request for Reconsideration-Disability Cessation Right to Appear,
or MSSICS generated printout, to determine the need for providing an interpreter.
See DI 23040.001 and DI 33010.030 for complete instructions on interpreters.
1.
Ensure that the DHU provides an interpreter when needed.
2.
Determine if there is any question regarding the qualifications of an interpreter
provided by the beneficiary (or recipient), and refer the folder to the SHO if there
is a question.
