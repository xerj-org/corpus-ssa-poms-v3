F. When a Title XVI claim is filed while an appeal is pending on a prior Title II disability
claim
When the Title II claim is pending at the reconsideration or hearing level and the
subsequent claim is filed under Title XVI, determine whether the claims share a common
issue (as described in DI 12045.005).
•
When a common issue exists, the subsequent claim may escalate to the same level as
the current appeal. For instructions on common issue cases, see DI 12045.010.
•
Escalate a subsequent claim to the hearing level only if the administrative law judge
(ALJ) agrees to join the claim with the pending appeal. For instructions on contacting
the hearing office to request to consolidate claims, see DI 12045.015.
NOTE: If there is no common issue, the claims remain separate. Process the Title XVI initial
claim as appropriate. The appeals process for single claims under the respective titles
will apply.
