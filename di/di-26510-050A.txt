SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.050 - Completing Item 23 (Medical List Number) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 02/03/2012 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Title II
When Item 15 is checked and the claimant's impairment meets or equals the level of
severity of the Listing of Impairments at the time of adjudication, enter up to 7
positions of the appropriate medical listing number. The first three positions (to
the left of the decimal point) must be numeric, with values of 001-114. Fill in with
leading zeroes if the medical listing number has fewer than 3 positions before the
decimal. Positions 4-5 (the first two positions to the right of the decimal point)
must be numeric; with values, 02-30 (must show leading zeroes). Position 6 (the third
position to the right of the decimal point) must be alpha with values A-O, or blank).
Position 7 (the last position) can have values A-O (alpha), 1-9 (numeric, no zeroes),
or be blank.
For example:
Medical listing “112.09” should be coded as “112.09”
Medical listing “4.02A2” should be coded as “004.02A2”
NOTE: These coding instructions coincide with the requirements for entering medical listing
codes to the National Disability Determination Services System.
