F. Concurrent Title II and Title XVI cessations
Follow the notice instructions in this section for Title II and Title XVI and add
disclaimer paragraph 842 on Title II notices and paragraph 841 on Title XVI notices.
NOTE: If you use form notices, add paragraphs (841 and 842) to the personalized explanations
of the determination.
