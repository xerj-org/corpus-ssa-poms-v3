C. Procedure for an unfavorable medical determination under medical improvement review
standard (MIRS)
If the individual has experienced medical improvement related to the ability to work
and the individual is capable of engaging in substantial gainful activity (SGA), determine
the individual is not eligible for EXR. Take the following actions:
1.
Complete an SSA-832-C3/U3 or an SSA-833-C3/U3 per DI 28057.020F.
2.
Prepare and send the denial notice. Include the personalized explanation with the
rationale for the finding.
3.
Close out the EXR in DCPS. NDDSS will show a System Override code of "N".
4.
Alert the FO using an electronic folder (EF) message in eView when a beneficiary is
concurrently entitled. The FO must initiate a medical CDR on the other title. For
paper cases, put all remarks on the routing slip.
5.
Scan all documentation in the electronic folder.
