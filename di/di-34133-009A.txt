A. What impairments do these listings cover?
We use these listings to evaluate all malignant neoplasms except certain neoplasms
associated with human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection. We use the criteria
in 14.08E to evaluate carcinoma of the cervix, Kaposi's sarcoma, lymphoma, and squamous
cell carcinoma of the anal canal and anal margin if you also have HIV infection.
