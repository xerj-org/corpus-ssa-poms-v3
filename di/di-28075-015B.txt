B. Procedure for rollback conversion cases
Prepare the Form SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal – Title XVI) as in any other Title XVI medical CDR determination (i.e.,
cases in which State plan criteria are not applicable).
