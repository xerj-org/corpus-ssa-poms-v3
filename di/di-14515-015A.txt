A. Procedure – Final Termination Notices for Title II and Title XVI
Prepare a title II termination notice on a form SSA-L951-U2, Social Security Notice
Letterhead. Prepare a title XVI termination notice on a form SSA-L8165-U2, Supplemental
Security Income Notice of Decision.
