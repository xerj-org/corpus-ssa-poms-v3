SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33015.022 - Disability Hearing Officer (DHO) Who Held Hearing Becomes Unavailable to Write Determination
Effective Dates: 09/18/1992 - Present | TN 12 (09-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433015022 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Actions required when DHO becomes unavailable
1. Fully favorable determination warranted
Have another DHO prepare the determination if the evidence supports a fully favorable
determination.
2. Unfavorable determination indicated
a.
Schedule another hearing per DI
33010.005.
b.
Modify the notice language in DI
33010.080 or DI
33010.085 to show the reason for scheduling another hearing. An example of such a modification
is in DI
33015.065.
c.
Place a copy of the notice in the claims folder if the folder is paper.
3. Beneficiary (or recipient) does not want another hearing
a.
Cancel the hearing as outlined in DI
33010.005.
b.
Document the claims folder to show that beneficiary (or recipient) declined the offer
of another hearing.
c.
Prepare an SSA-5002 Report of Contact to document the folder if the beneficiary (or
recipient) declined the hearing by a manner not in writing, e.g., telephone.
d.
A DHO prepares the determination based on the evidence of record.
