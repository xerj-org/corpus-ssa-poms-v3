SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33535.035 - Psychological Tests
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433535035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

M. The scales of independent behavior - (generally available since January 1984)
Assesses 14 critical areas of independent and adaptive behavior including self-care,
motor, socialization and community independence skills.
The potential evaluation consequences of all the above psychological tests is a more
accurate and objective adjudication based on standardized, validated instruments.
