B. Title XVI CDR notice
For Title XVI only continuance determination, the DDS is responsible for notifying
the recipient of the determination made in their case when a determination is a continuance.
The DDS will prepare and (if not subject to QA review) release a Title XVI continuation
notice on the form SSA-L1014 (Supplemental Security Income Disability Information), see NL 00701.430. Check the block or blocks when applicable for each specific case situation shown
in the chart in DI 28095.100 Stock Letters for Title II, Title XVI and Concurrent Title II and Title XVI Claims
Continuances. Check Block 6 in most situations.
The paper case is subject to a QA review
If the paper case is subject to a QA review:
•
Do not date the notice,
•
Fold it under the flap of the mailing envelope, and
•
Place it in file for release by OQR.
For cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) involving a manual notice,
see QR
04440.150 Processing Correct or Corrected Disability Cases, DI 81020.130 Electronic Case Closure and NL 00603.100 How to Prepare the Notice.
NOTE: For Title XVI child cases requiring a continuance notice, see NL 00705.356 and NL 00705.357.
