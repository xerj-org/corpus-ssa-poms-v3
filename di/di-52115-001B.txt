B. Federal Employees' Compensation Act (FECA)
The OWCP Division of Federal Employees' Compensation (DFEC) administers the FECA WC
program. FECA pays WC benefits to civilian officers and employees of the U.S. government
as well as others deemed to be federal employees for purposes of workers compensation.
To process FECA cases, see DI 52115.010.
