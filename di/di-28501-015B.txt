SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28501.015 - Reopening of Prior Favorable Initial Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 03/22/2023 - Present | TN 6 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428501015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Prior favorable CDR determination
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) may reopen a case if a prior favorable
CDR determination is incorrect and such action is not precluded by the rules of administrative
finality.
Reopen the case only when the prior favorable CDR determination is wholly unsupported
by the evidence, not because of a difference in judgment.
