DI 25020.001 Introduction - Functional Limitations
The effects of any particular type of limitation on a range(s) of work vary greatly
depending upon the degree of the limitation and thus, must be determined based upon
the facts of each individual case. This subchapter provides some general adjudicative guidelinesfor making these necessary individualized determinations.
