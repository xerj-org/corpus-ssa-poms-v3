B. Policy - applications filed before 7/65
For applications filed before 7/65 or where death occurred prior to 9/65, in addition
to the above requirements, an individual must:
•
Have been under the disability at the time the application was filed; and
•
Not have been entitled in a prior month to a monthly retirement benefit reduced because
he/she had not attained full retirement age (FRA).
