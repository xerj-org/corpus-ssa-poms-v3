C. Procedure – obtaining a “General Ticket Query”
1. Access the TKQY screen
To access the TKQY screen:
•
select 32, “Continuing Disability Review File” from SSA's main menu (MAIN);
•
select 11, “Ticket to Work,” from the CDR Selection Menu (MCDR) and enter the ticket
holder's SSN; and
•
select 1, “General Ticket Query,” from the Ticket Submenu (TMEN).
2. MSOM reference
MSOM CDR 004.003 provides information on how to read the fields on the TKQY screen.
