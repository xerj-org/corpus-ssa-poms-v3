DI 27536.005 Required Information for Reopening Initial Determinations
There is no need to duplicate information documented elsewhere in the file. Use the
tables below to locate the information required to reopen initial determinations;
and, what actions are necessary if the information is not already contained in the
file.
Required information for reopening an initial determination includes:
•
Allegations
•
Assessment of functional abilities
•
Basis for determination
•
Citation of jobs and incidence
•
Duration of impairment
•
Evidence of substantial gainful activity (SGA) findings
•
Evidence Sources
•
Interpretation of medical findings
•
Medical facts
•
Pain-related issues
•
Resolution of conflicting medical opinions
•
Resolution of inconsistencies material to the determination
•
Vocational rule used for medical-vocational determinations
