F. DDS- Evaluation of Blind cases
The DDS must make a blindness determination in both Title II and Title XVI cases if
the claimant alleges a visual impairment, and a period of a disability based on blindness
is possible. The FO will send the case to DDS for a blindness determination, regardless
of the SGA determination see DI 26005.001 and DI 26005.005.
