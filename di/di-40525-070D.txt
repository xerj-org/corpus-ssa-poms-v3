D. Procedure – document your efforts and findings
Inevitably there will be some cases that cannot be found. Once you have done a thorough
job looking for a file — DOCUMENT YOUR EFFORTS. Documentation is for follow-up purposes and to inform the next person/component who
handles the case not to spend time repeating the search effort. Use the SSA-5002 (see
DI 40525.125) to document your efforts. Circle/highlight pertinent data/information, e.g., on
the NUMI circle/highlight any name change. Keep all reports of telephone calls, and other pertinent material and queries in file.
