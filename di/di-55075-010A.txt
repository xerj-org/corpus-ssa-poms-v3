SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55075.010 - Disputes Between Beneficiaries/Recipients and State VR Agencies
Effective Dates: 07/23/2024 - Present | TN 2 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455075010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy – no SSA involvement
The dispute resolution procedures in the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended, apply
to any beneficiary/recipient who assigns a ticket to a State VR agency. Thus, SSA
will not be involved in resolving disputes between a beneficiary/recipient and a State
VR agency.
