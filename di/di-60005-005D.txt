D. National 800 Number Network (N8NN) Responsibilities
The responsibility of the National 800 number network (N8NN) will be limited to referrals.
If someone calls the N8NN to inquire about the demonstration, refer the caller to
the appropriate FO of jurisdiction. N8NN agents should document eLAS screens with
remarks indicating the claimant is a GIFTT participant.
