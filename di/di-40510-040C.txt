C. Procedure - how to complete the SSA-899-U2
Complete an SSA-899-U2 following DI 13010.175 in title II and concurrent title II/title XVI cases and a separate SSA-899-U2 in
dual entitlement cases (e.g., DIB/CDB) for each account except for the following changes:
•
HEADING: Change the SSA-899-U2 heading to read: “CONTINUING DISABILITY REVIEW-MEDICAL
ONLY”.
•
SECTION A: Delete the word “DISABILITY” and replace with the words “IMPAIRMENT SEVERITY”
when an SSA-833-U3 determination has been made that disability already ceased due
to SGA. Check block 1.
•
SECTION B: Check Block 10 in Part I and specify “Possible Medical Issue”. Check block
2 in Part II.
•
SECTION C: Check Block 2 and modify the description to read “IMPAIRMENT STILL DISABLING.”
•
REMARKS SECTION: Identify the case as an EPE case and indicate the reason why the
impairment is still disabling, e.g., “EPE/Extended Medicare Eligibility Case = Currently
disabled per DI 40510.040A.”
•
NOTIFICATION BLOCK: Enter SSA-L1013.
•
CATEGORY of CASE BLOCK: Indicate the type of case.
