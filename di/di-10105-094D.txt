D. Requests for assistance from the DDS
If the FO identified a prisoner case, and did not annotate Item 5 of the SSA-3367,
or the DDS identifies a prisoner case, the DDS will request FO assistance to determine
if a felony was committed.
If the FO does not respond to the DDS requests for assistance in obtaining the required
information, the DDS will process the title II claim as a “No Determination” (ND),
with the reason code “FO Determination Requested.”
