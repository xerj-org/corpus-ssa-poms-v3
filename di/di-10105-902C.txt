SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.902 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary or Survivor Applications Filed During the Benefit Continuation Period (BCP) - Disability Benefit Equals or Exceeds Auxiliary or Survivor Benefit
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105902 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Processing Center (PC) processing
The PC will:
1.
Establish control of the A101. The control should be annotated “Effectuate Auxiliary
(Survivor) Claim When Decision Reached on Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB) Cessation
- P.L. 98-460”. Cross-refer the claimant’s own SSN to the SSN on which the auxiliary
or survivor claims were filed.
2.
Upon notification of the final decision to either affirm or reverse the cessation,
forward the A101 to a claims authorizer (CA) for review and adjudication of the claim.
•
For affirmations in which benefit continuation payments have been terminated, amend
the A101 to correct the benefit amounts on the MBEN screen. Delete the entry in the
smaller actuarially reduced monthly benefit amount (“SAMBA”) on the MBEN screen and
the annotation in the remarks portion of the BCRN screen, and take the proper action
to effectuate the award and recover the benefit continuation overpayment.
•
For reversals to a continuance, process the auxiliary or survivor award as in DI 10105.901C.2. (note that it may be appropriate to contact the claimant to withdraw the application
if the auxiliary or survivor entitlement is disadvantageous).
