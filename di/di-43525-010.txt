DI 43525.010 Initial Claim - SGA Denials
All SGA denials on INTPSC jurisdiction claims, whether made by the domestic DO or
SGA specialist, will be reviewed by a disability examiner as described in DI 11010.140. The DO or SGA specialist will prepare an SSA-831-U5 and denial notice. The disability
examiner will release the notice and strip the SSA-831-U5.
