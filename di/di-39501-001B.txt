B. Description-chapter structure
The chapter is organized by functional areas and includes such topics as Quality Assurance,
Personnel, Training, Travel, Office Space, Equipment, Medical Services, Supplies,
etc.
The instructions and standards in all subchapters are mandatory except as noted otherwise.
Guides where noted are discretionary or advisory.
