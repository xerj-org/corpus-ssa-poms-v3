A. No surviving eligible spouse
Return the case to the field office (FO) unadjudicated if there is no surviving eligible
spouse and:
•
there are no unpaid medical expenses; or
•
the disability determination services (DDS) does not make Medicaid eligibility determinations
for the State.
If the FO later learns there is a surviving eligible spouse, the case will be returned
to the DDS for a determination. For instructions regarding Medicaid only cases, see
DI 23510.035D. in this section.
