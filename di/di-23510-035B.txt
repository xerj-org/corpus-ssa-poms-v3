B. Surviving eligible spouse
If there is a surviving eligible spouse, the DDS must make a disability determination.
Use the appropriate chart for instructions about
•
completing Form SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal),
•
preparing the notification (if needed), and
•
routing the case.
1. Allowance
Use this chart for fully favorable allowance determinations:
SSA-831
Notification
Special routing
Complete as usual with the following exceptions:
Item 17: Enter
•
“NXP” for the diary type,
•
“0000” for the diary date (may be 4 or 6 characters depending on DDS legacy system), and
•
“7” for the diary reason.
Item 29 (Ltr./Par. No.): Leave blank
Item 34 (Remarks): If claimant filed for self enter, “Claimant deceased (date of death).”
No DDS prepared notification needed for fully favorable allowance.
Route the case to the FO with a Special Handling flag (see, DI 81020.085 Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Flags).
Alert the FO via email or other electronic means that "SSI claimant deceased – claim
allowed but not input – please input for underpayment due spouse."
For electronic disability insurance benefit (eDib) cases, see DI 81020.090 Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Messages.
2. Partially favorable allowance
Use this chart for partially favorable allowance determinations:
SSA-831
Notification
Special Routing
Complete as usual with the following exceptions:
Item 17: Enter
•
“NXP” for the diary type,
•
“0000” for the diary date (may be 4 or 6 characters depending on DDS legacy system), and
•
“7” for the diary reason.
Item 29 (Ltr./Par. No.): Enter “DO D/L 1029, 1373”. (If the system is unable to generate
the proper disability determination notice and a manual notice must be prepared.)
Item 34 (Remarks) If claimant filed for self enter, “Claimant deceased (date of death).”
DDS prepares a personalized disability explanation in the third person.
Alert the FO via email or other electronic means to release the notice attachment
with the final notice prepared by the FO. For eDib cases see, DI 81020.130 Electronic Case Closure.
Do not prepare an interim notice SSA-1157-U2 (SI).
Route the case to the FO with a Special Handling flag (see, DI 81020.085 Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Flags).
Alert the FO via email or other electronic means that "SSI claimant deceased – claim
allowed but not input – please input for underpayment due spouse."
3. Denial
Use this chart for denial determinations (initial and reconsideration claims:
SSA-831
Notification
Special routing
Complete as usual with the following exceptions:
Item 34 (Remarks) If claimant filed for self enter, “Claimant deceased (date of death).”
DDS prepares a personalized disability explanation in the third person, and includes
the appropriate decision paragraph per DI 23510.040, Determination Paragraph, Death after filing - Title XVI (Chart). The DDS releases
the notice.
For eDib cases see, DI 81020.130 Electronic Case Closure.
Route the case to the FO with a Special Handling flag (see, DI 81020.085 Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Flags).
Alert the FO via email or other electronic means that, "SSI claimant deceased – claim
denied but not input."
For eDib cases, see DI 81020.090 Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Messages.
