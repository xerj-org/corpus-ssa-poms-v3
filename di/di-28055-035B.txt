B. Policy - Determination Notice
Written notice will be provided to persons who have completed 9 TWP months and who,
based solely on medical/vocational grounds (or failure to cooperate or whereabouts
unknown), are determined to have an impairment which remains of disabling severity
(EPE continuance) or which is no longer of disabling severity (EPE cessation) although
disability may have ceased previously due to SGA.
Written notice will be provided auxiliaries in EPE medical cessation
cases only.
