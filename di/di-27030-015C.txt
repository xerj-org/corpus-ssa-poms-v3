C. Affirmation of Closed Period and Unfavorable Onset Date Allowance
When a closed period or unfavorable onset date allowance is affirmed, the DDS prepares
and releases a modified notice on preprinted form SSA-1130-U2 with a personalized
explanation. The DDS must delete the phrase “denying your claim” from the third sentence
of the first paragraph. Include paragraph 841 (Title II disclaimer) on concurrent
Title II/Title XVI claims when the SSA-1130-U2 is not preprinted.
