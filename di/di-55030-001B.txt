B. Accepting earnings evidence from the TPM
Under the Ticket to Work program, the TPM annotates wage information it receives from
ENs and State VR agencies serving Ticketholders as ENs in iTOPSS. The TPM evaluates
the evidence of earnings it receives (see DI 55030.010B for further information).
