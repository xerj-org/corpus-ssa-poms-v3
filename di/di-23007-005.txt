DI 23007.005 Contacting the Claimant, Appointed Representative, or Third Party in Claims Involving
Failure to Cooperate and Insufficient Evidence
This section describes whom adjudicators must contact when a claimant does not comply
with an initial request for evidence or action, or an initial notice of a consultative
examination (CE) appointment. The instructions apply to initial and reconsideration
cases using the adult sequential evaluation process (see DI 22001.001A). For a Title XVI disabled child (DC) case, see DI 25205.020. For a continuing disability review (CDR) case, see DI 28075.005.
