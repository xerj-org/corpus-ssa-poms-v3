DI 11020.016 Childhood Disability Development and Determination
Instructions in the chapters on Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) and Obtaining Evidence
of Disability also apply to Childhood Disability Benefit (CDB) cases. Specifically,
for obtaining evidence of disability in CDB cases, see DI 11005.000 “Disability Interviews-Table of Contents” and for the instructions on SGA, see DI 10501.000 “General – SGA-Table of Contents”.
