SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26010.020 - Completing Items 16A (Primary Diagnosis) and 16B (Secondary Diagnosis) on the SSA-831 for Title II and Title XVI Blindness Cases
Effective Dates: 10/22/2018 - Present | TN 6 (04-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426010020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. “Statutory Blindness” is the primary diagnosis
When “Statutory Blindness” is the primary diagnosis:
•
In Item 16A (Primary Diagnosis), use the term “Statutory Blindness” as the primary diagnosis.
Enter body system “02” and impairment code “3694” in the blocks provided.
•
In Item 16B (Secondary Diagnosis), show the underlying cause of the blindness and the corresponding
diagnosis code.
