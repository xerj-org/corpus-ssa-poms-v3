Effective Dates: 06/28/2023 - Present | TN 3 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440505120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating policy for third party reports
If a third party reports that an individual is not disabled, is not following required
treatment, or has returned to work, and the report:
•
Is from someone in a position to know; and
•
Indicates a real possibility of cessation.
Then, we must investigate the situation.
