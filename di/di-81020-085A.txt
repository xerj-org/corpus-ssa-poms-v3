SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81020.085 - Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Flags
Effective Dates: 03/20/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481020085 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Electronic flags identify special case handling situations (e.g., Terminal Illness
(TERI). Case flags are displayed in eView by selecting “Flags” on the secondary title
bar (see Secondary Title Bar - DI 81005.035). Some Disability Determination Services (DDS) case processing systems may also display
case flags to their users.
