SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24501.004 - Medical Specialty Codes
Effective Dates: 05/05/2015 - Present | TN 35 (06-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424501004 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When medical specialty codes are required
Include the responsible medical or psychological consultant’s specialty code on each
of the following:
1. Medical assessment forms
The following are disability program medical assessment forms:
•
SSA-2506-BK (Psychiatric Review Technique)
•
SSA-4734-BK (Physical Residual Functional Capacity Assessment)
•
SSA-4734-F4-SUP (Mental Residual Functional Capacity Assessment)
•
SSA-538-F6 (Childhood Disability Evaluation Form) for Title XVI childhood disability
cases
•
SSA-416 (Medical Evaluation)
2. Disability determination forms
The following are agency disability determination forms:
•
SSA-831 C3/U3 (Disability Determination and Transmittal)
•
SSA-832 C3/U3 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and
Transmittal Title XVI), or
•
SSA-833 C3/U3 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and
Transmittal Title II).
