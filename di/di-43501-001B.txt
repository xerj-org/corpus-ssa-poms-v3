B. Policy - performs DDS functions
ODIO, OIO's foreign disability examiners (FDE's) perform the full range of DDS functions.
FDE's develop and adjudicate:
1. Develop and adjudicate
a.
Initial claims
b.
Continuing disability review (CDR) cases
c.
Reconsideration of initial denials
d.
Pre-hearing development of reconsidered cessations
e.
Certain claims based on totalized earnings
2. Process assistance requests
FDE's process assistance requests from:
a.
Department of State,
b.
ODAR, and
c.
Foreign agencies with which we have reciprocal agreements.
