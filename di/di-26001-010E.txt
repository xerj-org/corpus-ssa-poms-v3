E. Freeze-only blindness determinations
The field office will send a “freeze-only” DIB claim to the disability determination
services for a blindness determination (see DI 26001.015A.2.) if the claimant:
•
is performing SGA, and
•
alleges or shows evidence of blindness or a visual disorder.
