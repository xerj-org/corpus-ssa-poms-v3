SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.905 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary Applications Filed After the Benefit Continuation Election (BCE)
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105905 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Childhood Disability Beneficiary (CDB) claim filed after the Benefits Continuation
Election (BCE) on disabled Number Holder’s (NH's) Social Security Number (SSN)
Hold subsequent CDB claims in the FO until a final decision is reached on the disabled
NH's appeal.
•
If the cessation decision becomes final the FO will process the claim to a denial.
•
If the cessation is reversed, the FO will develop the claim in the normal manner before
sending it to Disability Determination Service (DDS) for the disability decision.
NOTE: Claims filed for mother's/fathers benefits at the time the claim is filed should
be processed as in DI 10105.901A.
