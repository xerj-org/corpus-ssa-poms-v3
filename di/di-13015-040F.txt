F. Procedure - Folder in the Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR), the
Office of the Inspector General (OIG), or the Office of the General Counsel (OGC)
Locations
If the folder(s) is in ODAR, OIG or OGC:
1.
Obtain a query (e.g., OHAQ) to confirm the folder is still in ODAR, OIG or OGC.
2.
If the case is still active in one of these locations, document on the SSA-5002 (see
DI 13015.250).
•
DO NOT REQUEST the return of the folder.
•
Close out the CDR on the DCF.
•
Annotate in the remarks of the DCF that the CDR is not being pursued pending return
of the folder.
•
Discard the Direct Release alert.
•
DO NOT tickle for return of the folder. Central Office monitors completion of all
CDRs - no additional actions are needed.
3.
If the query, i.e., OHAQ, shows the folder is not active in ODAR, OIG, or OGC, search
for the folder in the new location reflected on the query.
•
If unable to locate the folder(s), proceed to DI 13015.080.
•
Folder found: See DI 13005.030.
