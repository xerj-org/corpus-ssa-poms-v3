D. Disposition of the SSA-3643
If the SSA-3643 cannot be stored or recorded in the Modernized Claims System (MCS),
Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS), or the ICF, fax the form into the Evidence
Portal (EP) or the electronic disability (eDIB) folder (eView), as appropriate.
NOTE: If processing center (PC) action is required, the FO will also fax the form to the PC’s Paperless fax number.
