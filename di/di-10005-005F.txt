F. Updating a third-party address
When an office, general delivery, hospital, or institution address is given, obtain
the claimant's home mailing address and telephone number and record the information
in the application.
If the home address is unavailable, obtain the name, address, and telephone number
of a friend, relative, or neighbor who can contact the claimant.
