F. Pre-effectuation review (PER) and quality assurance (QA) sample cases
When the DDS is responsible for preparing a notice or personalized explanation and
PER or QA selects the case for review, the DDS will:
•
prepare a notice with an explanation without a date,
•
fold it for mailing,
•
place it under the flap of the appropriate envelope, and
•
clip the notice inside the right hand side of the folder for release by the reviewing
component.
NOTE: For cases involving a QA review see, DI 81020.135 and DI 81020.065. For cases in Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) involving a manual notice,
see QR
0440.150 Processing Correct or Corrected Disability Cases, DI 81020.130 Electronic Case Closure and NL 00603.100 How to Prepare the Notice.
