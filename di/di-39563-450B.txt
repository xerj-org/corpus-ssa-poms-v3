B. Clerical training
Support function staff are critical to the effective operation of the DDS. General
program orientation and specific task related training are needed to assure that support
staff members fully comprehend their roles and responsibilities and the impacts of
their actions. DDS's should assess and meet ongoing training requirements based on
programmatic, reporting and legislative changes as well as technological advances.
