D. Operating procedure
1. Review of IRDs
a.
Review for:
•
substance;
•
operational impact;
•
resource impact.
b.
Complete the eIRD. Written comments may also be submitted in a memorandum or directly
on the draft transmittal.
2. Submission of comments
a.
The eIRD site manager creates a specific page for the IRD (within three working days).
The system notifies the author via email when the eIRD SharePoint page is ready, which
includes the link to the page. The author checks all the documents within the created
page for accuracy and then sends the supplied link to stakeholders for comment.
b.
Reviewers upload their comments on the eIRD SharePoint page.
c.
Once the author receives all comments and the eIRD period ends, the request moves
to the "Post IRD Authoring" step of the PPS process. At the Post-IRD step, the author
reconciles all eIRD comments in PPS.
