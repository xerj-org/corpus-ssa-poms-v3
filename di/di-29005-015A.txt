A. Approved withdrawal
An approved withdrawal cancels the reconsideration request. The field office (FO)
must approve or disapprove all withdrawal requests. For information on approved withdrawals,
see DI 12026.025E.4.
NOTE: A withdrawal is different from a waiver of the right to appear at a disability hearing.
For information about a withdrawal request for reconsideration of a disability cessation,
see DI 33010.055.
