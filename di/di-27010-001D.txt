D. SSA-3441 not received from claimant
The FO processes the reconsideration claim according to the procedures in DI 81010.150E which, after required follow up attempts, permits transfer of the appeal without
completion of form SSA-3441. If the DDS receives a reconsideration claim without a
completed SSA-3441, the DDS must follow up with the claimant (see DI 22505.014A).
For more information on:
•
Circumstances where a claimant fails or refuses to complete form SSA-3441, see DI 12005.005D and DI 27001.001C.3.
•
DDS instructions for processing electronic reconsideration cases when an SSA-3441
was not received, see DI 81020.025A.
