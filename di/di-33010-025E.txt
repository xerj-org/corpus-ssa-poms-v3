Effective Dates: 06/06/2023 - Present | TN 6 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Issuing the Spanish notices
1.
Staple the Spanish notice on top of the English notice.
2.
Send scheduling and determination notices printed in both English and Spanish to the
beneficiary (or recipient), and representative, if any.
3.
Place both copies of both the Spanish and English notices in the claims folder.
NOTE: Spanish notices are not required in foreign claim cases.
