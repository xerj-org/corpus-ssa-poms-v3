H. Statutory benefit continuation payments for POD cases
POD participants were subject to regular medical CDRs. A POD participant who ceased
to have a disabling impairment no longer qualified for the POD participation period
and was not eligible for the benefit offset unless they received statutory benefit
continuation payments based on a medical cessation appeal. In that case, the beneficiary
may have received reduced benefit payments under the benefit offset during the appeal
if the beneficiary:
•
was within the POD participation period (For a definition of POD beneficiary, please
see DI 60075.035A); and
•
worked above the POD monthly threshold amount.
