C. Routing
For electronic cases, refer to DI 81010.085, (Transferring Cases in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)). For paper
cases use a SSA 636-U3 (Transmittal Notice – Hearing Case) to transmit the claim folder(s)
to the HO. Refer to GN 03103.110 for instructions on the use and completion of the SSA 636-U3.
