SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34001.034 - Cancer (Malignant Neoplastic Diseases)
Effective Dates: 01/13/2017 - Present | TN 22 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434001034 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What impairments do these listings cover? We use these listings to evaluate all cancers (malignant neoplastic diseases) except
certain cancers associated with human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection. We use
the criteria in 14.11B to evaluate primary central nervous system lymphoma, 14.11C
to evaluate primary effusion lymphoma, and 14.11E to evaluate pulmonary Kaposi sarcoma
if you also have HIV infection. We evaluate all other cancers associated with HIV
infection, for example, Hodgkin lymphoma or non-pulmonary Kaposi sarcoma, under this
body system or under 14.11F-I in the immune system disorders body system.
