SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25501.220 - Potential Onset Date (POD)
Effective Dates: 10/04/2023 - Present | TN 50 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425501220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Component roles regarding the POD
1. FO roles
The FO:
•
documents work activity on the SSA-821-BK or SSA-820-BK, including potential continued work or work after the AOD,
•
makes SGA determinations and records possible UWA months on the SSA-823 (unless the work was clearly not SGA). For detailed information to determine if work
is or is not SGA, see DI 10505.003,
•
must enter the POD for each Title II disability claim type on the SSA-3367 (see the
chart in DI 25501.220C.1.b.in this subsection), and
•
must enter the POD for Title XVI claims on the SSA-3367 if the POD is different from
the protective filing date or the filing date.
•
must alert the DDS if a prior period of disability ended in a termination due to failure
to cooperate. Do not assign a POD during a period of disability that ended in cessation
or termination.
REMINDER: For concurrent claims or for multiple Title II claims, the FO must provide the POD
separately for each disability claim type. For concurrent claims, the Title II POD
must always be provided but the Title XVI POD need only be provided if the POD is
different from the filing date or the protective filing date.
NOTE: Never assign a POD on a technical denial, for example, due to an SGA denial, because
there is no possibility of disability entitlement on the claim.

2. DDS roles
The DDS:
•
focuses all disability case development on the POD,
•
uses the POD when determining the earliest possible EOD, and
•
makes a final UWA determination, if applicable.
