SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25505.025 - Duration Requirement for Disability
Effective Dates: 04/24/2015 - Present | TN 5 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425505025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. 12 continuous months
Unless the impairment(s) is expected to result in death, to meet the 12-month duration
requirement, the impairment(s) must prevent the claimant from engaging in any substantial
gainful activity (SGA) for 12 months and must have lasted or be expected to last for
a continuous period of not less than 12 months.
REMINDER: The duration period can begin before the date first insured (DFI) in title II DIB
claims, before the date of a spouse’s death in title II DWB claims, and prior to the
filing date in title XVI claims.
