A. Requests for a Copy of a Disability Folder
When a claimant, beneficiary, or appointed representative requests a copy of the official
file folder in either paper or electronic format, all documents that make up the case
must be included. The official disability case folder consists of documentation per
GN 00301.285. Requests for certified copies of a CEF without a subpoena or court order must be
forwarded to the National Records Center to ^Folder Certification Request. Requests
for certified copies of a CEF with a subpoena or court order must be fowarded to ^LP
PFDLaw Regional Disclosure.
