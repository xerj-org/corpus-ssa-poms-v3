E. New TFB based on a new first-considered date of offset
Determine a new TFB when the following events establish a new first-considered date
of offset:
1.
A new period of DIB is established; or
2.
A new WC/PDB claim is awarded based on a new injury or illness.
EXAMPLE: WC entitlement from one injury ends and the NH sustains a new injury. The new WC claim
causes a new first-considered date of offset and a new TFB is established that month.
For information regarding a break in WC/PDB entitlement but no break in DIB entitlement,
see DI 52150.055C.1.
