B. Procedure to input a medical diary to the disability control file (DCF)
The SSA-832 or SSA-833 establishes a diary on the DCF. To establish or correct a diary
after the disability examiner has input the determination, contact the FO, per SM
01305.915D (Diary Requirements When MD Transmitted – MR Diary). Only the FO has the
ability to change the diary after DDS input.
