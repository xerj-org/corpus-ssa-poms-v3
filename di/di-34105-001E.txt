E. Digestive System
Specific evaluation criteria are now included for assessing malnutrition resulting
from disorders of the digestive system. New criteria for cirrhosis of the liver and
peptic ulcer are intended to remove the ambiguities and references to “permanency” not removed following the 1965 amendments.
