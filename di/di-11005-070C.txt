C. Visual impairment alleged
During the application process, it is important for the field office (FO) to be aware
that:
1.
The claimant may not specifically state that he or she is blind but may allege another
type of visual impairment.
2.
The claimant may request a special notice option (SNO); see SNO options in NL 01001.010.
3.
Certain visual impairments, based on severity, may meet the definition of blindness.
