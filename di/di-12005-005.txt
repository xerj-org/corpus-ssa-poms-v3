DI 12005.005 Processing a Reconsideration Request for a Medically Denied Initial Disability Claim
Handle assembly and routing of reconsideration cases in the same manner as initial
claims. Update disability report information for all levels of appeal up to the Appeals
Council review. Maintain this information in the official disability folder.
