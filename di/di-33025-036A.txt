A. Definition of evidence
The regulations on determining disability provide broad-based authority to consider
any kind of evidence bearing on the issue of disability. The regulations (20 CFR
404.1513(a) and 416.913(a)) specify that the term “evidence” means anything the individual or anyone else submits
or we obtain that relates to the claim.
