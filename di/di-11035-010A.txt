SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11035.010 - FO Processing of Medicare for Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) Disability Claims
Effective Dates: 09/02/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411035010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Action when the MQGE claimant has filed a claim with the Office of Personnel Management
(OPM)
A claimant who files an application for Medicare based on the worker's Federal employment
may have also filed an application with OPM (formerly the Civil Service Commission)
for a civil service annuity based on disability. When the claimant has filed with
OPM, alert the DDS by an entry in the remarks section of the SSA-3367and include the
civil service annuity claim number.
