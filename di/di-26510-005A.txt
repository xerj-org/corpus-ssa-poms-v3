SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.005 - Completing Items 11A and 11B (Presumptive Disability/Presumptive Blindness) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 08/10/2011 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The field office (FO) and Disability Determination Services (DDS) must complete entries
in Item 11, as applicable, when making a finding of presumptive disability (PD) or
presumptive blindness (PB) in a paper case. For instructions when the case is processed
electronically see DI 23535.020.
Complete this section if either the FO or the DDS makes the PD or PB decision.
NOTE: Do not make an entry in Item 11A or 11B for a fully electronic case.
