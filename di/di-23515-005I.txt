I. Disclosure to the Department of Homeland Security
SSA must disclose certain enumeration, identity and location information when requested
by the Secretary of DHS or a DHS official, duly authorized by him or her, in accordance
with section 290(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act. The local DHS offices
send their requests for information to the DHS, United States Citizen, and Immigration
Services District Director in Baltimore, who forwards them to the Office of Central
Operation for processing(see GN 03313.095).
