DI 81010.143 Processing Medical Reactivations
Medical Reactivation changes a claim that is in closed, transferred, or manually cleared
status in the electronic folder to pending or active again. A claim is Medically Reactivated
in eView so that the prior medical decision can be reopened. The policy regarding
reopening and revision has not changed. Follow the existing reopening policies and
procedures, DI 27501.005 - Reopening and Revisions, General; DI 27505.001 - Time limits and other conditions for Reopening a Final Determination or Decision;
DI 28500.000 - CDR Reopening.
