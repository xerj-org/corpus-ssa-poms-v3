Effective Dates: 09/22/2014 - Present | TN 2 (10-08)

B. Claimant is statutorily blind
1.
Item 16A (Primary Diagnosis)—show “Statutory Blindness” as the primary diagnosis per
DI 26510.015C.2.b.
2.
Complete the forms as normally required for an allowance or continuance for DWB and
CDB (other than issues of comparability) claimants.
NOTE: The FO effectuates an allowance or continuance, as usual.
