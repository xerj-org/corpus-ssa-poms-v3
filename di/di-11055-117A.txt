Effective Dates: 09/03/2009 - Present | TN 95 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411055117 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
SSA has jurisdiction for adjudicating Title XVI-only and concurrent Title II and Title
XVI disability claims filed by a career RR employee or dependent RR annuitant.
