B. Adverse medical reopenings (AMR)
•
When an initial determination is reopened and adversely revised based on medical factors,
as described above, the Disability Determination Services (DDS) sends the claimant
a notice containing reconsideration appeal rights that include the opportunity for
a disability hearing at the DHU.
•
When the DDS proposes an AMR of a reconsideration-level determination, the DDS will
not issue the unfavorable determination. The DDS will forward the case to the DHU.
The DHU sends a notice to the claimant advising them of the proposed revision and
their right to request a disability hearing before a revised determination is issued.
