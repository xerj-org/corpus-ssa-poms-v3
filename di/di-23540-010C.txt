SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23540.010 - Completing Form SSA-831 for Medicare Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) Cases
Effective Dates: 12/14/2011 - Present | TN 2 (09-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423540010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Item 29 – ltr/par no.
If a system-generated notice cannot be used, the DDS examiner designates “DDS D/L”
and the appropriate notice or rider in item 29 of the SSA-831. (For a list of MQGE
case notices and riders, see DI 23540.015.)
