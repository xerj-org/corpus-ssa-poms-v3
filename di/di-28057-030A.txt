A. Background for EXR appeals
A request for EXR involves two types of determinations: a provisional benefit determination
and an EXR determination.
1. Provisional benefits determinations are not subject to administrative and judicial
reviews (appeals), see DI 13050.025.
2. EXR technical and medical determinations are initial determinations subject to
the administrative and judicial(appeals) review process, see DI 13050.065, GN
03101.070 and SI
04010.010. For the policy for EXR appeals , see DI 13050.085.
