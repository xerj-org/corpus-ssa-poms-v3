A. Introduction to due process
The Fifth Amendment to the Constitution provides that no person shall be deprived of property without
due process of law.  An individual's interest in continuing to receive Social Security or Supplemental
Security Income benefits constitutes a statutorily created “property” interest. The
Fifth Amendment protects that interest.
