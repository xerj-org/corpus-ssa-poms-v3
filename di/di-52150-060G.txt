SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.060 - Prorating a Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Lump Sum Settlement
Effective Dates: 08/01/2023 - Present | TN 36 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Manually prorating the LS
Prorate the LS manually on the reverse side of the form SSA-2455 if the ICF #31 or
#32 cannot process the computation. Verify all of the following:
•
Gross amount of the LS
•
Start Date
•
Weekly rate at which to prorate
•
Excludable expenses
For manual processing, follow instructions outlined in DI 52170.030. After completing the manual proration, enter the results in ICF WC/PDB Function
#34 (OREO) and forward to the BA/BTE for input into MACADE and MBR update.
