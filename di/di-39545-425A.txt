SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.425 - Release of Consultative Examination (CE) Reports to Claimants By CE Providers
Effective Dates: 06/26/2023 - Present | TN 7 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545425 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a CE provider receives a request for information concerning an examination performed
under an agreement or contract with the Agency, the CE provider may not disclose this
information, except as permitted by SSA’s disclosure regulations. (See Privacy and
Disclosure of Official Records and Information, 20 CFR Part 401.20(b)(1)(iii).)
