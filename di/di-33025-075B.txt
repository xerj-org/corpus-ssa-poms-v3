B. Policy – Appealing the decision in an age 18 redetermination case
Recipients found not disabled in an age 18 redetermination, based on the application
of the adult disability criteria, may appeal the determination that they are not disabled.
Reconsiderations of the age 18 redetermination cases will be conducted under the regulations
that govern the disability hearing process. See 20 CFR §416.1414 through §416.1418.
