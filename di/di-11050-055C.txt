SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11050.055 - Transferring Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases to Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR)
Effective Dates: 10/01/2026 - Present | TN 7 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411050055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Completing the EDCS Screens
•
Add the flag: Railroad Disability Case - DCR 2A, along with any others that apply.
•
Verify the destination code V32 is on the Transfer screen.
•
Identify the claim as either CAREER RAILROAD or DEPENDENT RR ANNUITANT on the EDCS
routing form remarks section.
