D. Proof of age for the claimant
Proof of Age (POA) is not required unless one of the following applies:
1.
The claimant is possibly age 65 at ESRD onset and onset was prior to 10/ 78; or
2.
Fully insured status is an issue and the claimant is not insured by all of the dates
in file (e.g., age 22 involved).
When development of age is required under these rules, request the field office to
obtain POA following the usual POA guidelines in GN 00302.001. If the claimant's insured status is based on another person's earnings record, be
aware that POA may be needed for the WE.
