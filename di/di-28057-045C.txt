C. DDS procedures for case processing
When DDS receives the cases from the FO, process as follows:
•
Adjudicate the initial application or reconsideration following normal procedures
per DI 26500.000. Adjudicate the EXR request or reconsideration following DI 28057.020.
•
Print documentation that pertains solely to the excluded claim (for example, SSA-831
and determination notice), and associate these items in the MDF for FO adjudication
after making a determination.
•
Upload any documents that apply to both the electronic and the paper claim in eView.
•
Return the electronic and paper portions of the case to the FO.
