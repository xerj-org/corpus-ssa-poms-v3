A. Definition of blindness
Sections 216(i)(1) and 1614(a)(2) of the Social Security Act (Act) define blindness as central visual acuity of 20/200
or less in the better eye with the use of a correcting lens. We use the best-corrected
visual acuity for distance in the better eye to determine if the claimant meets this
definition.
The Act also provides that we consider an eye that has a visual field limitation such
that the widest diameter of the visual field subtends an angle no greater than 20
degrees as having a central visual acuity of 20/200 or less.
This statutory definition of blindness is in the Listing of Impairments (listings).
