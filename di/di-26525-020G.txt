G. Procedure - systems coding
Complete the diary block of Forms SSA-831-C3/U3 (item 17) and SSA-832-C3/U3 and SSA-833-C3/U3
(item 23) as follows:
1. Diary Type
Enter “MRN”.
2. Diary Date
See DI 26525.020D. for guidance. Enter “01” for the day. Set a date no later than the current month
plus 30 months, because of systems limitations.
3. Diary Reason
See DI 26525.025B. for MIE criteria and reason codes.
