DI 29005.020 Career Railroad and Railroad Annuitant Reconsideration Continuing Disability Review
(CDR) Requests
The requests for reconsideration filed by the following groups are not a part of the
disability determination services (DDS) jurisdiction:
•
career railroad (RR) employees
•
annuitants of deceased career RR workers
•
disabled dependents of deceased career RR workers
The DDS does not process requests for reconsideration cases from the previously cited
groups; therefore, forward the cases to the following address:
Great Lakes Program Service Center (GLPSC),
Disability Review Section
P.O. Box 87755
Chicago, IL 60680-0955
