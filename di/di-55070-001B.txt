B. Policy - change in referral authority - disability determination services (DDS) in
ticket States
The authority to refer individuals was repealed in November 2003, upon full implementation
of the Ticket to Work Program. The DDS does not make referrals to State VR agencies.
SSA has advised State VR agencies of the change in the referral process.
