F. How to submit a case for RPC
To submit a case for RPC, a DDS-authorized requester must access the RPC tool to initiate
and complete an on-line RPC template. The DDS inputs information obtained from the
CEF about issues related to the deficiency citation, including:
•
A brief summary of the case facts;
•
A discussion of the disagreement with the DQRs deficiency citation; and
•
Case-specific policy references.
The DDS is not required to undertake any additional development prior to submitting
the case for RPC.
The DDS must include all post-adjudicative evidence in the case record.
NOTE:
The RPC panel discussion members do not consider post-adjudicative evidence prior
to reaching a resolution on the RPC submission. The panel members only consider post-adjudicative
evidence after they reach a resolution, see DI
30007.145J.2.
