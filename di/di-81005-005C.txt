C. eView Case Screen
In eView, when users select a specific case to view, the Disability Case Process (View
Mode) screen will appear. This screen contains:
•
Primary and secondary title bars;
•
The following series of tabs:
–
Alerts & Messages;
–
Case Data;
–
Case Documents;
–
Status/History; and
–
Exhibit List (if one exists); and
•
Functional links and buttons.
The Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) and the Office of Appellate Operations (OAO)
can view additional case tabs including the Exhibit List tab.
NOTE:
eView will time out after 30 minutes due to lack of activity (i.e., moving from one
screen to another). Just entering data on the same screen will not prevent a time
out from occurring, and if a time out occurs, any changes to the data entered on that
screen will be lost.
After 27 minutes of inactivity, the user receives a warning that a time out will soon
occur. To continue the session for another 30 minutes select the “OK” button on the
warning dialogue box. There is no limit on how many times the user can extend the
session.
