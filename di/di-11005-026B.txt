SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.026 - Completing the SSA-3373-BK (Function Report – Adult)
Effective Dates: 06/20/2007 - Present | TN 11 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005026 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. When to Complete
The FO must complete an SSA-3373 only for someone who:
•
Is homeless; or
•
Will be difficult to contact by telephone; or
•
Will be unavailable for contact (e.g., may be on an extended trip).
2. When not to complete
The FO does not complete the SSA-3373 when SSA-3368 curtailment is applicable (see
DI 11005.020).
