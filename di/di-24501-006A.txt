A. When to use the SSA-416 (Medical Evaluation) and MA-416 (Medical Advisor Evaluation)
1. Medical consultant (MC) and psychological consultant (PC) medical evaluation
The MC/PC uses the SSA-416 (Medical Evaluation) to document medical evaluation of any of the following medical
findings for adult claims and some child claims*:
Medical Findings:
For Additional Policy Information, see:
Evidence does not establish (or is insufficient to establish) a severe physical medically
determinable impairment (MDI), or a combination of physical MDIs, that is severe and
meets the duration requirement
•
DI 24505.001 Individual Must Have a Medically Determinable Severe Impairment
•
DI 24505.005 Evaluation of Medical Impairments that are Not Severe
•
DI 25205.005 Evidence of a Medically Determinable Impairment
•
DI 25220.005 Determining If a Child Has a Severe Impairment(s)
•
DI 25505.030 Evaluation of the Duration Requirement for Disability
Impairment meets or medically equals a physical listing
•
DI 24508.005 Impairment Meets a Listing
•
DI 24508.010 Impairment or Combination of Impairments Equaling a Listing - Medical Equivalence
•
DI 25220.010 Meets or Medical Equals
Continuing disability review (CDR) medical evaluation
•
DI 28005.030 Step-by-Step Discussion of the Title XVI Child Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
Sequential Evaluation Process
•
DI 28010.015 Comparison of Symptoms, Signs, and Laboratory Findings When Evaluating Medical Improvement
(MI)
•
DI 28010.050 Adjudicator and Medical or Psychological Consultant (MC/PC) Roles in Considering

*NOTE: Title XVI Childhood Disability (DC) claims do not require the use of an SSA-416 or
MA-416, but these forms may be used for scenarios that do not require a domain evaluation
(see DI 25230.001).
2. Using the MA-416 for a Medical Advisor’s (MA) analysis
The MA will use the MA-416 for electronic and paper claims in the Disability Case
Processing System (DCPS) to enter his or her full medical analysis in the “Evaluations
and
Assessments” section on the “Impairment
Severity/Sign” page in DCPS.
For more information on:
•
The role of the MA in adult claims, see DI 24501.001B.6
•
The role of the MA in child claims, see DI 25230.005D.3
