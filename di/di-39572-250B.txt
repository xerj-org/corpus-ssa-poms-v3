B. Related policy Reporting responsibilities
1. DDS
a.
Keep the DA informed of any events or problems significantly affecting the decentralization
effort, and
b.
provide DA with two formal status reports, one at the end of 6 months after approval
of the plan and one after a full year of operation.
2. CO
Keep the DDS informed of any systems requirements or changes for the decentralized
locations.
