C. List of Questions
The FO is responsible for obtaining information about sources of evidence of the child's
functioning on the SSA-3820-F6. However, in those situations where the information
is not in the case record or additional information is needed, contact the applicant
and use the questions on either the SSA-3820-F6 or the SSA-3881-BK as guides. See
DI 11005.152 and OS 15020.430 for examples of the two forms.
NOTE: The questions on the two forms are not all-inclusive.
