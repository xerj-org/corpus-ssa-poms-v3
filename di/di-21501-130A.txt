A. Blindness—(Section 400.56, Compiled laws of 1948, as amended.)
1. RULE 400.10(5), ADMINISTRATIVE RULES:
“Eligibility for aid to the blind shall not be contingent upon the recipient's willingness
to submit to an operation recommended for the possible restoration of his sight.”
2. RULE 400.10(6), ADMINISTRATIVE RULES:
“Blindness as a basis for determining eligibility for aid to the blind shall exist
when an individual is found to have a visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the better
eye with correction, or is found to have a limitation of the fields of vision such
that the widest diameter of the visual field subtends an angular distance not greater
than 20 degrees, through an examination by an ophthalmologist or an optometrist licensed
to practice in Michigan.”
