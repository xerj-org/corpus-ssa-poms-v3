SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 32005.100 - Informal Remand Case Routing, Non-CDR
Effective Dates: 04/06/2018 - Present | TN 1 (09-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0432005100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Fully favorable revised disability determination
Following a fully favorable revised disability determination:
•
Route the claim(s) folder to the field office (FO) for action.
•
Add “Action Necessary - INFORMAL REMAND” to the SSA-408 (Route Slip) Remarks.
