C. Procedure - screen-in criteria
See DI 28087.010B.2. for basis of referral of title XVI child applicants.
The applicant age 15 or over who meets any of the following screen-in criteria should
be referred to a State VR agency (see DI 28087.030).
1.
All applicants, except those who are terminally ill, whose primary impairment is a
limited central visual acuity of 20/70 or less in the better eye with best correction,
or an equivalent loss of either field of vision or visual efficiency, or visual problem
so severe as to limit ability to engage in vocational or other daily activity; and
all applicants whose impairment meets, or may lead to meeting, the definition of statutory
blindness as defined in section 216(i)(1)(B) or 1614(a)(2) of the Social Security
Act; or
2.
File reflects applicant's desire to work or working despite impairment and obviously
underemployed; or
3.
Impairment can be corrected or substantially reduced with the aid of physical restoration
or other medical treatment; or
4.
File contains definitive evidence that applicant can benefit from vocational rehabilitation
services (e.g., statement from physician or consultant); or
5.
File indicates an SSI applicant has a plan for achieving self-support.
Proceed to DI 28087.020D. if screen-in criteria are not met.
