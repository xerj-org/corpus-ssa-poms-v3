SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39530.015 - Lease/Purchase of DDS Equipment
Effective Dates: 09/04/2026 - Present | TN 3 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439530015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
Lease/purchase agreements provide that some or all payments made for the use of equipment,over
a specified period of time, may be applied toward the purchase of the equipment.
This policy does not apply to IT equipment. IT equipment is different from general
DDS equipment.
