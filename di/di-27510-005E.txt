E. When a prior favorable Title II freeze determination or decision cannot be reopened
because the 4-Year period for reopening based on “Good Cause” expires
When a claimant files a subsequent claim for DIB:
•
Evaluate the evidence of record, and prepare a determination on the new claim on its
own merits; and
•
Consider the prior determination under the continuing disability procedures if the
freeze determination was incorrect.
NOTE: You can establish onset within the prior adjudicated period after the 4-year period
for reopening based on “good cause” has expired, and we base benefits on the current
application date.
