B. LS amounts not subject to offset
LS amounts not subject to offset (i.e., those amounts that do not qualify as excludable
expenses, but instead are deducted off the top from the gross LS amount) are rare,
and include:
•
LS discounts
•
Amounts to a spouse for inchoate rights (Massachusetts)
•
Assessed penalties
•
Accrued interest
Annotate these amounts in ICF as a SPECIAL AMOUNT DEDUCTED FROM THE GROSS on the WCLS
screen in ICF. ICF deducts these amounts before the LS award is prorated.
