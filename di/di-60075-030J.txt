J. Post-POD work CDRs
When POD participation ends, the beneficiary reverts to regular SSDI rules for work
the following month. We may need to process a work CDR following POD participation
in order to evaluate SGA level earnings. If processing a post-POD work CDR, follow
these steps:
1.
Initiate Work CDR: eWork will identify the beneficiary as a Prior POD Beneficiary;
2.
Develop earnings for the post-POD review period only: eWork will set a review start
date for the first month after POD participation ends. Do not develop or input wages
in the POD participation period; and
3.
Do not attempt to reopen any prior decisions: eWork will restrict the reopening of
any decision prior to POD.
NOTE: Excuse all earnings from the last recorded decision in eWork through the end of POD
participation from regular rules SSDI evaluation. It is important to evaluate earnings
within the new review period only in eWork.
