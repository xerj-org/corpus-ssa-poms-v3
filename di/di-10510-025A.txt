SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10510.025 - Documenting Self-Employment Cases Using the SSA-820-BK (Work Activity Report-Self-Employment) and the SSA-823 (Report of SGA Determination- For SSA Use Only)
Effective Dates: 01/08/2025 - Present | TN 23 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410510025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Purpose of the SSA-820-BK
The field office (FO) and processing center (PC) use the SSA-820-BK (Work Activity Report Self-Employment) to document work activity and to determine
if any work incentives apply prior to making a substantial gainful activity (SGA)
determination for initial claims, appeals, and continuing disability review (CDR)
cases. To document the SGA decision, use the SSA-823 (Report of SGA Determination – For SSA Use Only).
