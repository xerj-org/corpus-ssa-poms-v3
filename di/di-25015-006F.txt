F. Examples of factors that may impact the case
This subsection provides examples of case factor combinations that could support use
of the higher age category in a borderline age situation.
EXAMPLE 1:
The claimant was 54 years, 10 months old on the date their Title II insured status
expired (or the date of adjudication. or the date the DWB prescribed period or CDB
reentitlement period ended). The claimant has a light RFC, an eighth-grade education,
and no transferable skills from their semiskilled PRW. Medical-vocational rule 202.11
directs a denial, while medical-vocational rule 202.02 directs an allowance.
EXAMPLE 2:
The claimant is 49 years, 11 months old, and has a sedentary RFC, a fifth-grade education,
and unskilled PRW. Medical-vocational rule 201.18 directs a denial, while medical-vocational
rule 201.09 directs an allowance.
EXAMPLE 3:
The claimant is 54 years, six months old, and has a medium RFC, a fourth-grade education,
and no PRW. Medical-vocational rule 203.18 directs a denial, while medical-vocational
rule 203.10 directs an allowance.
