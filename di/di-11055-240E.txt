E. Procedure for initiating payment
Take the following actions if a financial emergency exists:
1.
Transmit PD/PB data on the Disability page;
2.
Initiate an EAP (DI 11055.245); and
3.
Process the EAP via the Emergency Advance Payment (UPEP) screen in MSSICS (MSOM BUSSR
003.008).
