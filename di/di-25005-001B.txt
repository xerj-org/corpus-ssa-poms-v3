SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25005.001 - Determination of Capacity for Past Relevant Work (PRW) —Basics of Step 4 of the Sequential Evaluation Process
Effective Dates: 07/08/2024 - Present | TN 20 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425005001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Availability of work at step 4
At step 4, do not consider whether PRW exists in significant numbers in the national
economy. Adjudicators must limit analysis at this step to a function-by-function comparison
of the claimant’s RFC with the demands of PRW as the claimant actually performed that
work or as workers generally perform that work in the national economy. Find the claimant
not disabled if their RFC is consistent with the demands of PRW.
