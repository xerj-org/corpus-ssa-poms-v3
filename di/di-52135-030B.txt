B. Policy – When receipt of SDI causes offset
1. DIB beneficiaries are subject to offset based on receipt of SDI benefits if paid to:
•
Private employees
•
State and local government employees if the SDI benefit is based on State or local
employment not covered under Section 218 of the Social Security Act
2. DIB beneficiaries are not subject to offset based on receipt of SDI benefits if paid
to:
State and local government employees whose SDI benefit is based on State or local
employment all or “substantially all” (i.e., 85 percent or more) of which is covered
under Section 218 for Social Security purposes. See DI 52125.001C.3. for a definition of “substantially all.”
