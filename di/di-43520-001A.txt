A. Use of SSA-423
The International Disability Unit (IDU) uses the SSA-423 (Request for Services and Reply (Foreign Disability Case)) to correspond with the
Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) regarding development in foreign disability cases as follows:
•
Development of medical evidence.
•
Development of nonmedical evidence.
•
Request for verification of a foreign medical source's qualifications,
•
Assistance to the claimant.
