DI 52150.045 Chart of States’ Maximum Workers’ Compensation (WC) Benefits
Each State, U.S. territory, and the District of Columbia (D.C.) has maximum weekly
WC amounts that represent the highest WC rate a worker can receive if injured on or
after a particular date. Use only the information in the charts without adding cost-of-living
increases.
