DI 13501.015 DO/BO Referral to VR for Postemployment Services
When the DO prepares a continuance determination on Form SSA-833-U5 based on a concluded
continuing disability investigation which showed that the individual was not working
or, if working, was not working at the SGA level and the individual had previously
received VR services the case should be considered for rereferral to the VR agency.
This referral would enable the VR agency to determine whether the individual might
benefit from postemployment VR services, i.e., services designed to assist the individual
in adjusting to or maintaining employment.
