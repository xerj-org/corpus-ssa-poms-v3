A. Continuing disability review (CDR) determination notices
The disability determination services (DDS) will prepare and release the applicable
notices, unless the case is selected for quality assurance (QA) or pre-effectuation
review (PER). For instructions on preparation of the notice, see NL 00603.100.
The two standard continuation notices are the SSA-L1013 (Social Security Notice Of
Continued Disability), and the SSA-L1014 (Supplemental Security Income Disability Information). The chart in DI 28095.100, provides information on completion of these notices in MINE or MINE-equivalent cases.
