SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.006 - When Not to Purchase a Consultative Examination (CE)
Effective Dates: 06/10/2026 - Present | TN 65 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Drug or alcohol testing
Do not purchase a CE for drug or alcohol testing to evaluate an issue of drug addiction
or alcoholism (DAA). A single drug or alcohol test is not sufficient to establish
DAA as a medically determinable impairment (MDI), nor does it provide pertinent information
that can help us determine whether DAA is material to a finding of disability. For
details on evaluating cases involving DAA, see SSR13-2p.
