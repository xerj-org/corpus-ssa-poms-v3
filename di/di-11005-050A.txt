SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.050 - Prescribed Period and Controlling Date
Effective Dates: 07/16/2015 - Present | TN 107 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Information used to establish the prescribed period for DWB (DI 10110.001) must be accurate. Incorrect information could affect the claimant's entitlement
to benefits because disability must begin before the end of the prescribed period.
A Prescribed Period has a Beginning Date, and an Ending Date, as defined below;
1. Beginning Date
The prescribed period begins with the latest of the following:
•
The month of the number holder’s death; or
•
The last month of previous entitlement to DWB that terminated because disability ceased;
or
•
The last month of entitlement to Mother’s or Father’s Benefits (“E” benefits).
NOTE:
“E” beneficiaries may file for deemed entitlement for Medicare (DI
11005.050B.1.a). Even though the prescribed period has not yet begun, onset can still be established.
2. Ending Date
The prescribed period ends with the earlier of:
•
84 months (7 years) after the prescribed period begins; or
•
The month before the month the widow(er) attains age 60 (or age 65 if filing for DWB-Medicare
(DI
25501.360).
EXAMPLE: The prescribed period began in 3/2002 and the widow attained age 60 in 5/2008, you
would choose the earlier of 3/2009 (the 84th month after the prescribed period began)
and 4/2008 (the month before the month the widow attained age 60). In this example,
the prescribed period would end in 4/2008.
