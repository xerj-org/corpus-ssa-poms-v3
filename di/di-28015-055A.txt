A. Definitions of a listing and listing section
The terms “listing” and “listing section” used in this subchapter refer to listing
headings such as Listing 5.05 (Chronic liver disease), including all subsections such
as 5.05A, 5.05B, 5.05C, 5.05D, 5.05E, 5.05F, and 5.05G.
