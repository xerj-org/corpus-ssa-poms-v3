E. Notification of disability determination
The DDS prepares the notices in all partially favorable or completely unfavorable
disability determinations, the DDS prepares a personalized disability explanation
(PDE) in the third person.
For more information about preparation and specific content of the notice, see:
•
“How to Prepare the Notice” per NL 00603.000,
•
“Electronic Case Closure” per DI 81020.130, and
•
“Personalized Disability Explanation in Initial Closed Period and Unfavorable Onset
Date Allowances” per DI 26530.015.
