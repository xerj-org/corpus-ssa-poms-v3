C. Jurisdiction disability determination needed
If a disability determination is necessary for a Disabled Widow(er) Benefits (DWB)
or Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB) under the 1981 Amendments to establish eligibility
to a disability benefit only so the survivor can collect the LSDP, the Disability
Determination Service (DDS) of the State in which the DWB or CDB resides has jurisdiction.
These cases can be identified by the remark For LSDP only. WE's date of death (date)
in item 5 of the SSA-3367 (Disability Report Field Office).
