Effective Dates: 05/01/2023 - Present | TN 85 (05-23)

C. Documenting the CEF when PD/PB findings are met
Prior to DDS case transfer, document a PD/PB finding by following the procedures:
1.
Check blind or low vision on the Disability Clearance page.
2.
Check Presumptive disability or blindness on the Disability Clearance page and enter
the date when PD/PB was established in the presumptive onset date field on the Disability
Clearance page. Examples to assist in determining the correct date for PD/PB are provided
in DI 23535.010C.
REMINDER:
Do not enter a PD or PB onset earlier than the month in which the claimant filed his
or her application.
3.
Select the PD/PB category on the SSA-3367 in EDCS.
•
Include payment status (i.e., non-medical eligibility factors met/not met).
•
If the FO determined that the claimant meets the non-medical eligibility factors,
initiate the PD/PB payment immediately.
•
If the FO has not yet determined if claimant meets the non-medical eligibility factors,
expedite the outstanding development and initiate the PD/PB payment upon completion,
if applicable.
4.
Add the “Presumptive Disability/Blind” or “Presumptive Disb” flag in EDCS and include
information about the PD/PB finding in the Remarks section provided.
NOTE:
Add a terminal illness (TERI) flag in addition to the Presumptive Disb flag if the
case meets the criteria listed in The Disability Interview – Identifying Terminal
Illness (TERI) Cases in DI 11005.601C.
