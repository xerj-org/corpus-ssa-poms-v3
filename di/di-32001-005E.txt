E. Duplicate claims or applications
Two or more disability claims or applications, for the same claimant, and the same benefit, payment, or period of disability, filed either:
•
before the release of the notice of an initial determination on the prior application;
OR
•
after the release of the notice of a favorable determination on the prior application
with disability established as alleged.
