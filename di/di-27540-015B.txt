B. Procedure for selecting notices
Prepare the notice on blank SSA-L8052-U2 letterhead using the model language indicated.
Do not include the italicized language from the model notices in the actual notice
that is being released because it is informational to the Disability Determination
(DDS) personnel and not meant for the beneficiary.
Use the notice indicated for the following situations:
1. Reopening and revision is fully favorable
No DDS notice is required.
2. Reopening and revision is less than fully favorable
•
Allowance to Closed Period – Title XVI – Reopening Notice 5 (NL 00705.221)
•
Denial to Partially Favorable Onset (Not AOD) – Title XVI – Reopening Notice 7 (NL 00705.231)
•
Denial to Closed Period – Title XVI – Reopening Notice 9 (NL 00705.241)
•
Fully or Partially Favorable Onset to a Later Onset – Title XVI – Reopening Notice
11 (NL 00705.251)
3. Reopening and revision is unfavorable
Allowance to Denial – Title XVI – Reopening Notice 2 (NL 00705.206)
4. Basis change only
Blind to Disabled – Title XVI – Reopening Notice 14 (NL 00705.266)
5. No change in basis or end result – claimant aware
•
No Change in Basis or End Result – Claimant Aware – Reopening Notice 12; or
•
Denial of Request to Reopen and Revise – Reopening Notice 12 (NL 00705.256)
6. No change in basis or end result – claimant unaware or denial of request to reopen
and revise
No DDS notice is required.
