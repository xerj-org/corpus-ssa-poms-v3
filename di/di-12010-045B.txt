SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.045 - Action Following An Unfavorable Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Hearing Decision
Effective Dates: 04/11/2018 - Present | TN 14 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If notice of an unfavorable decision or dismissal is received, associate the nonmedical
material retained in the FO with the claim folder(s) and input the decision or dismissal.
If the claimant files a HA 520-U5 (Request for Review of Hearing Decision/Order),
follow DI 12020.001 or DI 81010.150.
