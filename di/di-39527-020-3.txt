SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39527.020 - DDS Space in Publicly Owned Buildings
Effective Dates: 10/11/2023 - Present | TN 35 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

3. DDS space furnished and controlled by central State organization
a.
•
No matter who controls the space it should be direct billed based on square footage.
b.
•
No lease costs should be comingled with indirect cost. Whenever possible costs should
be direct billed.
