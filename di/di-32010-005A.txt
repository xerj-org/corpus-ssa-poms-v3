A. Procedures for routing an electronic CDR (eCDR) case
•
Create an undated notice or explanation of the determination in the Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF).
•
Forward the eCDR to the appropriate QA review component. See DI 81020.205 - Receipting an Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR).
