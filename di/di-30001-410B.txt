SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 30001.410 - Technical Review of Form SSA-831-U3 (Disability Determination and Transmittal)
Effective Dates: 11/23/1993 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0430001410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Items 1 - 14
a.
DDSs should verify the information in items 1-14 for accuracy as specified in DI 26510.001- DI 26510.005.
b.
If there is a significant error in items1-14, notify the FO of the error.
c.
Resolve discrepancies involving SSN, name, and type of claim before DDS systems input
is made.
2. Items 15 - 34 and item 36
a.
When reviewing items 15-34 and item 36, follow the guidelines in DI 26510.000 and DI 27015.000 for completing the SSA-831-U3.
b.
In item 34 (Remarks): For cases that are Certified Electronic Files (CEF), DDS QA
updates the DDS worksheet with an entry indicating that a claim was reviewed by the
QA unit. For non-electronic cases, verify whether “DDS QA” has been entered whenever
DDS QA has reviewed the case.
