A. Introduction
1. Purpose
The purpose of this subchapter is to assist the disability determination services
(DDSs) in  planning and budgeting for their space needs to carry out responsibilities
under section 221 of the Social Security Act, as amended.
2. Contents
Included in this subchapter are the space standards and guides DDSs are expected to adhere to in their space planning activities.
