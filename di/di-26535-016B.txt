B. Partially favorable allowances
In Title XVI claims, a partially favorable allowance with an unfavorable onset date
is one in which the date of onset is established after the protective filing date,
or after the date of filing, if there is no protective filing.
1. Title XVI unfavorable onset date
Title XVI allowance notices are computer generated based upon systems input. The DDS
will make no entry in item 29 of the SSA-831. The DDS prepares and releases the interim
notice according to the instructions in DI 26535.036 and alerts the FO that further processing is needed via EDCS, or by attaching an
SSA-408 (Route Slip) to the paper folder. If the case is selected for Quality Assurance
(QA) review, process according to the instructions in DI 26535.036.
2. Closed period allowance
The Disability Determination Service (DDS) prepares a personalized explanation with
an interim notice for initial Title II and Title XVI closed period and unfavorable
onset date allowances according to the instructions and procedures in the following:
•
DI 26530.005, Cases Requiring a Personalized Disability Explanation
•
DI 26530.015, Personalized Explanation in Initial Closed Period, Unfavorable Onset Date, and DWB
Allowances Where the Month Of Entitlement is Restricted to January 1991 Under P.L.
101-508
•
DI 81020.130, Electronic Case Closure
•
NL 00603.001, General Notice Policy – Title II and Title XVI
