D. Develop for a representative payee
If during the course of the interview, it appears that the claimant may be incapable
of managing his or her benefits:
•
Request Capability Development Needed on the SSA-3367 in EDCS, or on the MCS Exclusion
Information page in EDCS for the DDS to assess capability during their medical review.
•
Try to obtain the name, address, and telephone number of a close relative or friend
who may be interested in assisting the claimant during the application process.
•
Develop for a representative payee, when appropriate.
See GN 00502.000, Determining the Need for, Developing and Selecting a Representative Payee for more
information.
