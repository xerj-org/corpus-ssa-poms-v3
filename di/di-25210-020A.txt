A. Policy - initiating, sustaining and completing activities
We will consider how effectively you function by examining how independently you are
able to initiate, sustain, and complete your activities despite your impairment(s),
compared to other children your age who do not have impairments. We will consider:
1.
The range of activities you do;
2.
Your ability to do them independently, including any prompting you may need to begin,
carry through, and complete your activities;
3.
The pace at which you do your activities;
4.
How much effort you need to make to do your activities; and
5.
How long you are able to sustain your activities.
