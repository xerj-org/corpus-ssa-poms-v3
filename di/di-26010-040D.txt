D. Procedure for Title XVI allowances
Enter regulation basis code “A61” when statutory blindness is established for the
following types of claims:
•
Blind Individual (BI),
•
Blind Spouse (BS), or
•
Blind Child (BC).
NOTE: If the claimant was disabled in the month of filing and became statutorily blind
in a later month, see DI 26510.045B for the appropriate basis code entry.
