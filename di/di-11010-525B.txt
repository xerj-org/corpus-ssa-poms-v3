SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.525 - Assisting Claimants who are Unable to Provide Medical Evidence
Effective Dates: 12/22/2014 - Present | TN 55 (07-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010525 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Claimant can obtain medical evidence
If the claimant informs the FO that he or she will obtain additional evidence, the
FO will forward the following to the DDS:
•
the disability folder, and
•
any medical evidence, upon receipt.
NOTE: For information on receiving medical evidence in the FO, refer to DI 81010.125.
