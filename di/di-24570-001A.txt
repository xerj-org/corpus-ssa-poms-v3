A. Background of the publication of SSR 19-2p
SSA published Social Security Ruling (SSR) 19-2p, "Evaluating Cases Involving Obesity"
on May 20, 2019. This SSR provides guidance on how we establish that a person has
a medically determinable impairment of obesity and how we evaluate obesity in disability
claims under Titles II and XVI of the Social Security Act.
