B. Policy - effects of medications
We will consider the effects of medication on your symptoms, signs, laboratory findings,
and functioning. Although medications may control the most obvious manifestations
of your impairment(s), they may or may not affect the functional limitations imposed
by your impairment(s). If your symptoms or signs are reduced by medications, we will
consider:
1.
Any of your functional limitations that may nevertheless persist, even if there is
improvement from the medications;
2.
Whether your medications create any side effects that cause or contribute to your
functional limitations;
3.
The frequency of your need for medication;
4.
Changes in your medication or the way your medication is prescribed; and
5.
Any evidence over time of how medication helps or does not help you to function compared
to other children your age who do not have impairments.
