SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22501.007 - Using eView to Obtain an Electronically Signed Form SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration)
Effective Dates: 09/06/2013 - Present | TN 35 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422501007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When attestation is permitted
The SSA or DDS employee may use the attestation process in DI 22501.007E, in this section, to obtain a signed and dated Form SSA-827 under the following conditions:
1.
The claimant is one of the following:
•
an adult (age 18 or older) who is signing on their own behalf, or
•
a child (under age 18) who is signing on their own behalf, or
•
a child whose claim is being pursued by an individual with the authority to sign on
behalf of a child claimant (for example, a parent or guardian).
2.
The individual signing the Form SSA-827 chooses to sign using the attestation process
in one of the following situations:
•
During a FO disability interview (for example, in office, video conference, or teleclaim
interviews); or
•
Via face-to-face or telephone contact with an SSA or DDS employee (for example, Form
SSA-827 expired prior to adjudication).
