H. Quality assurance (QA) review
A quality review, required by SSA regulations, to measure DDS performance and accuracy.
Before effectuation, SSA selects a random sample of DDS determinations for QA review
by the Office of Quality Review (OQR); QA review evaluates decisional accuracy as
well as documentation, see QR 04440.005.
