SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12010.035 - Action Following a Favorable Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) Hearing Decision
Effective Dates: 09/22/2022 - Present | TN 18 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412010035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy when favorable decision is received
Effectuate ALJ and Attorney Advisor decisions immediately upon receipt of the folder
or the electronic alert.
NOTE: The ALJ decision will not be effectuated if the field office (FO) or processing
center (PC) is informed that the Appeals Council (AC) is reviewing the decision under
its authority in 20 CFR 404.969 and 416.1469. For instructions on paying interim disability benefits when the ALJs decision has
not been issued or effectuated within 110 days after the date of the ALJ decision
because the AC has taken own motion review, see DI 42010.205 (Interim Benefits in Cases of Delayed Final Decisions Title II) and SI 02007.001 (Interim Benefits in Cases of Delayed Final Decisions SSI).
