B. Summary of YTED Policy Waivers
We are waiving certain SSI program rules for YTED participants who receive SSI payments.
SSI payments for YTED participants with eearned income will be exempt from standard
SSI earned income counting rules, as defined in SI 00820.001. Normal income deeming rules are waived. For more information, see DI 60010.015.
