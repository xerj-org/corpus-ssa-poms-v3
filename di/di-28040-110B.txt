SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28040.110 - Attempts to Contact are Unsuccessful or Unproductive in Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) or MINE-Equivalent Cases
Effective Dates: 03/10/2022 - Present | TN 13 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428040110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Requesting FO assistance in MINE or MINE-equivalent Cases
The DDS may request assistance from the FO when there is a need to contact the beneficiary
or recipient, obtain completed forms, or gather other information. The DDS may complete
an assistance request (AR) to the FO using form SSA-883-U3 (Request for Evidence of Assistance (Disability Case)), or the electronic AR described
in DI 81020.100. On the AR:
•
Annotate MINE or MINE-equivalent Case -- Sensitive Handling Required.
•
Indicate DDS actions taken to contact the beneficiary or recipient representative,
or representative payee. Request that the FO make further attempts to contact the
beneficiary or recipient.
•
If applicable, indicate the impairment(s) and annotate the questions on the SSA-454-BK (Continuing Disability Review Report) that are relevant to the particular case. In
paper cases, attach the SSA-454. Request the FO to obtain answers to the annotated
questions, being sensitive to the nature of the impairment(s) and the age of the beneficiary
or recipient. For example, do not request answers to the work-related questions on
the SSA-454 if the beneficiary or recipient is a Title XVI child under age 15, unless
there are indications that the child may be working.
•
If applicable, request the FO to obtain a signature and date on the SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose Information to the Social Security Administration).
•
Indicate other assistance needed from the FO (for example, locate requests, third
party contacts, etc.).
Request the FO return all documentation to the DDS for processing within 15 days.
The DDS should create a diary for 20 days to follow up if the FO has not completed
the AR and returned the requested documentation.
