A. Introduction to ETTs
1. Listings in 4.00
The listings in the Cardiovascular System (4.00) provide for use of the ETT as primary
evidence in cases involving chronic heart failure and ischemic heart disease (listing
4.04A). However, the listings also provide for use of other types of evidence as the
basis for decisions when available evidence does not include acceptable ETT results.
