DI 55050.030 Handling Calls from the Ticket Program Manager (TPM)
TPM calls FO
The Office of Research, Demonstrations, and Employment Support (ORDES) in Central
Office is responsible for responding to questions and issues that TPM may have (see
DI 55005.005A.). If a TPM employee calls a Field component information about a beneficiary/recipient
or a specific ticket-related issue, tell the caller to go through their management
staff. (TPM's management staff will contact ORDES.)
