D. Procedure - Line Item Reporting
1. Total full-time personnel
The total full-time personnel should reflect the sum of the entries on lines 1 a through
l. The entries should represent only personnel who worked full-time on SSA disability
program work. (Temporary personnel--regardless of the number of hours worked--should
not be reported here.)
•
Line 1 a and b (Administrative Personnel and Supervisors)
The entries should represent: (1) the number of work hours chargeable to professional
disability program staff who spend full time on non-adjudicative functions such as
supervision, administration, organization and methods work, etc., and/or (2) the number
of such supervisory hours chargeable to those professional disability program staff
members who regularly divide their time between supervisory and adjudicative duties.
In the latter instance where actual time records of the time devoted to supervisory
duties are maintained, the entries should be made on the basis of such time records.
Otherwise, the best possible estimate of the actual division of time should be made,
with time-record confirmation of this division at regular intervals. These instructions
also apply to the holiday and leave time entries under column B.
•
Lines 1 c through l (Examiners, Hearing Officers, Clericals, Consultants, QA Personnel,
Vocational Specialists, Systems Specialists, etc.)
The entries should represent the number of hours chargeable to staff/consultants who
work full-time on SSA disability program work
2. Total part-time personnel
The total part-time personnel should reflect the sum of the entries on lines 2 a through
l.
Line 2 a through l (Administrative Personnel, Supervisors, Examiners, Hearing Officers,
Clericals, Consultants, QA Personnel, Vocational Specialists, Systems Specialists,
etc.)
The entries should represent the number of hours charged to persons working part-time
on the SSA disability program.
Where a medical consultant is being paid solely on the basis of the number of hours
actually worked, the entry will be on that basis. Where a medical consultant works
a predetermined number of hours a week, e.g., 10 hours per week, the entry for hours
worked should be an extension of these hours for the quarter. Where a medical consultant
is paid on a monthly stipend or case-cost basis, the entry for hours should be estimated
if other records are not available
3. Total temporary personnel
This line should reflect DDS personnel hired into temporary positions (full-time and
part-time). Do not include acquisition of contract services (e.g., Kelly Services,
etc.).
•
Line 3a (Full Time)
Enter the total hours charged to temporary personnel working full-time on the disability
program.
•
Line 3b (Part Time)
Enter the total hours charged to temporary personnel working part-time on the disability
program.
4. Total direct personnel services
This line should reflect the sum of the entries on lines 1 through 3 above (full-time,
part-time, and temporary).
5. Total FTE (calculation)
The line reflects the FTE calculation for regular time (line 4--for columns A and
C--divided by 520 hours) and overtime (line 4--for column D--divided by 438.57 hours).
