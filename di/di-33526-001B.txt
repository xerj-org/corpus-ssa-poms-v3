B. Using the tables of impairment codes
For the complete list of impairment codes in numerical order, including the body system
code, see DI 33526.100. For each of the 15 body systems in the Listings of Impairments, we provide:
•
A list of codes to be used when a specific Part A (adult) listing is met or equaled;
•
A list of codes to be used when a specific Part B (child) listing is met or medically
equaled;
•
A general list of codes to be used when the impairment does not meet or medically
equal a Part A or Part B listing;
•
Reminders for selecting codes within the body system.
For the immune body system, we also provide a list of codes to be used to indicate
the secondary impairment when the primary impairment is human immunodeficiency virus
(HIV) infection.
We also include a section for impairments or situations that do not align with a specific
body system as well as a section with all codes in numerical sequence.
