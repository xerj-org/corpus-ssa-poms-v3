SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23010.009 - Necessary Conditions to Make a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Determination
Effective Dates: 01/03/2019 - Present | TN 5 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423010009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Three conditions necessary before making a failure to follow prescribed treatment
(FTFPT) determination
Only make an FTFPT determination when all three of the following conditions are met:
•
The individual is found to be entitled to benefits based on disability or eligible
for blindness benefits;
•
The individual's own medical source prescribed treatment for the impairment(s) upon
which the disability finding was based; and
•
The individual did not follow the prescribed treatment.
