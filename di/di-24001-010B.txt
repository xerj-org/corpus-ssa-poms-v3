SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24001.010 - Effect of SGA on Disability Status
Effective Dates: 03/20/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Onset
Some onset factors are:
1.
Onset of disability cannot be established prior to the end of the last successful
engagement in SGA, except in Statutory Blindness cases and cases where there was no
SGA for at least 12 months.
2.
DDS examiners should be alert to the possible effect of a period of work on the date
of disability onset as well as the possibility of establishing a date of onset of
disability earlier than the alleged onset date if the evidence would support a medical
or medical-vocational allowance at an earlier date in the absence of SGA. In this
situation any information in file which indicates that one or more periods of work
prior to AOD may have been non-SGA or an unsuccessful work attempt should be developed
fully before final determination of the onset date.
