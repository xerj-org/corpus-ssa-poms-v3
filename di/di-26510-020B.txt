B. How to complete the diary entry (Item 17) on the SSA-831
NOTE: The Disability Determination and Transmittal, SSA-831 requires month and year entries
for diary dates. However, the NDDSS requires that a day entry of "01" also be entered
for diary dates. The diary date can contain four or six characters depending on the
Legacy system used by a particular State DDS; e.g., 08/05; 08/01/05; 08/2005.
