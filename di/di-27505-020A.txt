A. Change of Position
A change of position occurs when a policy or legal precedent previously used to adjudicate
cases, which was correct and reasonable when made, is changed as a result of amendments
to the Social Security Act, the regulations, the publication of Social Security Rulings,
or new policy considerations. See Reopenings – Change in Ruling or Legal Interpretation
– Change of Position (GN
04001.100).
