B. Item 24 – remarks
Use Item 24 to alert the Social Security personnel to any special circumstance or
situation in previous Items on the SSA-832 or SSA-833.
NOTE: This chart includes only a few examples of special situations that might be annotated
in Item 24. It is not a complete list.
