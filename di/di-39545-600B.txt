SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.600 - Fee Schedules
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 12 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure DDS responsibilities
•
Maintain documentation to support the rates of payment it uses.
•
Provide documentation and obtain Disability Adjudication (DA) approval when fee schedules
increase. The request could include documentation of the difficulties in obtaining
the necessary testing/source, specific proposed fees with an explanation of how those
fees were derived, and an analysis of projected change in annual medical costs.
