E. Standalone i3368
Any i3368 initiated by an IU prior to 01/25/2014 is a standalone i3368. We consider
any additional i3368 partials created after a standalone i3368 to be standalone i3368s,
if they are part of a set of records (DI 81007.005B.3.j.). The IU also may have created and submitted an associated iClaim, but a standalone
i3368 is not dependent on iClaim. The standalone i3368 may protect a filing date for
benefits. Follow existing procedures for determining the earliest possible protective
filing date. (See GN 00204.010.)
NOTE: Any i3368 started by submission of an iClaim after 01/24/2014 is not a standalone
i3368.
