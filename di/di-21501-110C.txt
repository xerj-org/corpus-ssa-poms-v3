C. Disqualifying conditions — ADC, DA and GA
Amputations, asthma, diabetes, epilepsy, fractures, hemorrhoids, hernia, menopausal
syndrome, mental deficiency, obesity, post-surgical complications, psychoneurosis,
uterine fibroid and varicose veins shall not be considered incapacitating under the
above definition unless:
1.
Certification is for temporary period for treatment and rehabilitation
OR
2.
The condition cannot be corrected or controlled by treatment without undue risk to
the client
OR
3.
The client cannot engage in a useful occupation because of the impairment.
