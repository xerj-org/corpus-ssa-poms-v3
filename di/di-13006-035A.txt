Effective Dates: 10/13/2021 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413006035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. An age-18 redetermination appeal
Any individual dissatisfied with the age-18 redetermination decision can file an appeal
following the normal continuing disability review (CDR) procedures. The individual
will be eligible for statutory benefit continuation (SBC) through the administrative
law judge (ALJ) level of appeals.
