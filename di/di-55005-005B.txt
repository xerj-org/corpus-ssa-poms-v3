Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455005005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Role of field components
Field components have the following three main responsibilities with regards to the
TPM:
1.
Contact the TPM with questions about earnings evidence recorded by the TPM.
2.
Redirect ticket-specific questions and issues from ticketholders and other parties
to the Ticket to Work Helpline (see DI 55050and DI 55005).
3.
Refer complaints received (or have) about the TPM to the Regional Center for Disability
Programs for referral to ORDES, such as:
•
the TPM's phone lines are always busy;
•
the TPM is unresponsive to inquiries; or
•
the TPM makes inappropriate referrals of beneficiaries to field components.
(For more information on handling complaints, see DI 55075)
