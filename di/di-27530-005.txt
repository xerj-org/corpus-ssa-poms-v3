DI 27530.005 Processing Adverse Reopenings for Paper Cases
NOTE: For instructions on processing electronic reopenings see DI 81020.115.
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) may be required to initiate adverse reopening
procedures to correct a prior determination. Prepare a new SSA-831 Disability Determination
and Transmittal for each reopened claim.
