E. Time limits for filing
•
For an initial claim: An injured worker must file a claim within 3 years from the date of the injury.
•
To petition for a hearing: The worker must petition for a hearing within 18 months after receiving notice that
their claim was denied.
•
To request a review: The worker is barred from receiving WC payments on the grounds of changed circumstances
if 4 or more years have passed since their WC was last paid or denied. If a request
for reinstatement of WC was successful, WC is only paid retroactive to the date the
hearing was requested.
