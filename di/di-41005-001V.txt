V. Item 24 - remarks
Enter any of the following remarks, if applicable. More than one remark may be entered.
(The following list is not all inclusive.)
1.
In DIB attainment - DIB allowed cases, show “Stat Blind - MOE to DIB (date).”
2.
If the DIB, CDB, or DWB individual is age 55 or over and is engaging in comparable
SGA, show “Beneficiary engaging in comparable SGA.”
3.
If the DIB, CDB, or DWB individual is age 55 or over, is entitled to a TWP and has
engaged in noncomparable SGA since the MOE, enter “Stat Blind Noncomparable SGA” and “Stat Blind WK - (Mo. &Yr. - On.” If work is not continuous, show each month and year of SGA.
4.
If the “began” date in item 9 (J) (1) involves the first of the month, show “Date stat blindness began (date).”
5.
If applicable, show the SSN on which the beneficiary has other entitlement, e.g.,
CDB or DWB.
6.
Use a clarifying remark to explain any apparent inconsistency.
7.
Where current evidence shows the individual is capable of handling funds and has a
payee or is found incapable, show “claimant (in)capable per Dr.'s report dated .” Also, see DI 23001.001.
8.
If the blind individual is legally competent, but has a representative payee enter
“Blind Individuals address.”
9.
When the beneficiary is represented by an attorney or other designated party, enter
the name and address of the representative. The file should contain a statement by
the beneficiary in all cases of approval of representative.
