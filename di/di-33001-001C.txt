C. Who may conduct a disability hearing
Experienced disability adjudicators who have completed Social Security Administration
(SSA) DHO training may conduct disability hearings. These individuals:
•
must have had no involvement in making the determination under appeal, i.e. the initial
continuing disability review (CDR) cessation or adverse reopening, or the pre-hearing
(PH) affirmation of the initial CDR cessation,
and
•
are part of an adjudicatory unit of a state agency or Federal component (DHU) other
than the adjudicatory unit that made the determination under appeal (DDS).
