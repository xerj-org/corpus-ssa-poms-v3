C. Case review does not warrant fully favorable determination
If, after developing and reviewing the evidence in the case, INTPSC does not find
that a fully favorable determination is warranted, INTPSC will realign the case folder,
prepare the Summary of Evidence and forward the claim under cover of an SSA-408 to
the appropriate DHO in accordance with DI 29025.000.
