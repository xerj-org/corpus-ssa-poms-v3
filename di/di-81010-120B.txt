B. Completing the SSA-820/821 After Case Transfer
•
Perform the attestation dialogue as specified in Alternative Signature Methods - GN 00201.015F.2.
•
Document the SSA-820/821 and MCS/MSSIC as previously instructed in DI 81010.120A. of this section.
•
Use DPS to generate the form attestation cover letter;
The notice is combined with the Attestation Cover Letter and is under the “Cover Letters”
menu on DPS.
•
Select “Print and Send to ORS” button to ensure the document for attestation (i.e.
attestation cover) is in the Online Retrieval System (ORS);
•
Give or mail the claimant a copy of the attestation cover notice and the SSA 820/821;
and
•
Fax a copy of the attestation cover notice into section E. Disability Related Development
(blue) of the CEF using document type 1030 for the SSA-820 and 1040 for the SSA-821.
