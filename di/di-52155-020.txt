DI 52155.020 Disability Claims with a Prior Period of Disability with Workers' Compensation/Public
Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
If the number holder (NH) has multiple periods of disability, consider WC/PDB offset
for every period of entitlement. Reimposing offset from a prior period of disability
cannot be processed through MCS EC. These cases require A101 or EF101 processing.
