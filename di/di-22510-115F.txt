SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.115 - Adult Consultative Examination (CE) Report Content Guidelines for Speech and Language Impairments
Effective Dates: 01/28/2026 - Present | TN 63 (01-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. SLP Conclusions
The CE report must include the following:
1.
A statement of conclusions correlated with information from the history, clinical
observations, and formal assessment;
2.
A statement of whether test data are generally consistent with observations and impressions
about the claimant's speech and language skills, and that explains or comments on
any discrepancies;
3.
If the claimant uses a hearing aid or glasses but did not bring them to the examination,
a statement regarding the validity of the examination results;
4.
If a diagnosis is established, a statement that explains whether and to what extent
the identified speech or language impairment may reasonably be expected to affect
the claimant's ability to perform the demands of work activities over the next 12
months; and
5.
The CE provider's signature, educational degree, and certification or licensure credentials.
