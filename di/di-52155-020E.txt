SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52155.020 - Disability Claims with a Prior Period of Disability with Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452155020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. WC/PDB LS proration continues from prior period of DIB
If LS proration will continue past the new date of entitlement (DOE),
•
Establish new ACE and TFB.
•
Reimpose offset effective with the new DOE. Use the same proration method used to
compute offset in the prior DIB claim.
•
Terminate offset effective with the LS end date determined from the original LS proration
determination.
NOTE: Provisional payments and expedited reinstatement (EXR) awards do not represent a
new period of disability. See DI 52160.001 for EXR awards involving WC/PDB offset.
