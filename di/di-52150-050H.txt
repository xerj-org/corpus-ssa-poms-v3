SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.050 - Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) with Excludable Expenses
Effective Dates: 08/22/2016 - Present | TN 37 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Expenses that the NH did not pay
Carefully review any verification of excludable expenses. The NH must pay for or incur
the expense for the WC claim.
Excludable expenses do not include:
•
Prescription expenses for an illness not related to the WC claim or for other family
members.
•
Any medical expense that another health insurance policy or company paid for, or reimbursed.
This is especially important when establishing future medical expenses. Assume the
worker paid the medical expenses, unless there is evidence to the contrary.
