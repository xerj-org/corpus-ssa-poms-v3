D. Responding to an inquiry when the claim is in the DDS
The initial response may serve as a final reply in most claims, regardless of the
location of any related claims folder. For public officials that accept this form
of reply, use the telephone to provide the final reply when practical.
If you need additional information to reply to public official inquiries:
•
contact the field office by telephone; or
•
contact the regional office to address your concerns by telephone if they accept that
form of communication.
