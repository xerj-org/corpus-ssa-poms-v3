A. Coding the MCS/MSSICS Development Screens - Railroad (RR) Cases to DCR
•
Create the DDSDEC issue and set a 90-day tickle on the Development Worksheet for both
MCS and the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE).
•
Show the DDS code of V32 on the ADIB (MSSICS) and the Development Worksheet Remarks
(MCS).
•
Enter "Career RR Case" or Dependent RR Annuitant in the SSA-3367, Disability Report-Field
Office, Remarks section.
