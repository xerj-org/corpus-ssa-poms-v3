J. NY WC terms and abbreviations
The following chart explains the terms and abbreviations often used in NY WC forms
and documents.
ANCR
accident, notice, and causal relation
ASSESSMENT
fee for appeal to WC board
AWW
average weekly wage based on an average of the injured worker’s earnings for an entire
year prior to the injury.
ATTY
attorney
CCP
carrier continue payments
CLOSED
no further action will be taken on the claim and that no formal hearing will be held
unless the injured worker or any party of interest specifically requests it.
CLT
claimant; the injured worker
DBC
reimburse disability benefits carrier (NYS 26 week PDB)
DBL
disability benefits lien (NYS 26 week PDB)
DEFICIENCY COMP
date third-party settlement proration credit ends
DES
disability exceeds schedule
DLWP
disability less than waiting period
FROI
First report of injury
HIA
held in abeyance (decision postponed until a later date)
HOLIDAY
third-party settlement proration period
INCOME EXECUTION ORDER
garnishment
IME
independent medical exam
M.P.A.
modify prior award
NCLT
no compensable lost time
NFCRD
no further causally related disability
NLT
no lost time
NME
no medical evidence
NOD
notice of decision
ODNCR
occupational disease, notice, and causal relation
OLN
other lien
PAID IN FULL
all retro benefits paid; usually does not mean payments have stopped
PAY PERIOD
2 weeks
PP
partial permanent disability
PHP
protracted healing period with additional weeks of WC
PT
partial temporary disability; SSA normally refers to this type of WC as TP, or temporary
partial.
PWO
paid while out by employer
RATE PER WEEK
Rate per week for 100% disability is calculated at two-thirds of average weekly wage
not to exceed the maximum rate established by law at the time of the accident.
RE
reduced earnings, i.e., eligible to return to work with restrictions with WC payments
for 2/3 the difference between prior AWW and new wage.
REIMB EMP
reimburse employer
RTW
return to work
SLU
schedule loss of use
SROI
subsequent report of injury
TP
total permanent disability; SSA normally refers to this type of WC as PT, or permanent
total.
TR
tentative rate
TT
total temporary disability
WTH
withhold
15-8D
Section of NY WC law regarding special funds liability and apportionment
