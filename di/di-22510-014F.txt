F. CE report content for THCEs
Follow existing policy for:
•
CE report content guidelines that apply to all CEs in DI 22510.015.
•
CE report content guidelines that apply to certain examinations in DI 22510.048, DI 22510.060, DI 22510.102, or DI 22510.112, as appropriate.
In addition, the CE report must include:
•
A statement that the CE was conducted using video technology, and
•
The name of the video application used.
