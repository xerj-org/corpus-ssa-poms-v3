SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81020.250 - Closing and Routing Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 05/26/2021 - Present | TN 22 (05-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481020250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Closing an eCDR
1.
Ensure all non-essential and duplicate documents have been moved to the “Current Development/Temporary
Section” of the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF);
2.
Upload all case development and determination documents which were generated or created
in the electronic format, including scanned copies of evidence, eForms, and Disability
Determination Services (DDS) system-generated documents (e.g., determination form,
worksheet, accurately dated notices), and ensure they are successfully uploaded and
accessible through eView;
3.
Update the National Disability Determination Services System (NDDSS) with the determination
and closure data;
4.
Follow current procedures to close the case on the DDS system;
5.
Date and release notices (unless the case is selected for Office of Quality Review
[OQR] review);
6.
Transfer jurisdiction of the eCDR CEF and paper Comparison Point Decision (CPD) folder,
if applicable, to the appropriate office, including those cases selected for OQR review.
7.
Check the applicable block on the eCDR Paper CPD Folder Flag and mail the folder to
the appropriate office. To view image of the “eCDR Paper CPD Folder Flag” see Associating
the Comparison Point Decision (CDP) Folder with an eCDR – DI 81010.235B.
