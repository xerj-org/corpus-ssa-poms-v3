DI 10501.025 Clearly Not SGA
The field office (FO), and processing center (PC) normally resolves title II, title
XVI, and concurrent substantial gainful activity (SGA) work issues. There are certain,
limited situations when the disability determination services (DDS) can make an SGA
determination if the work is clearly not SGA.
