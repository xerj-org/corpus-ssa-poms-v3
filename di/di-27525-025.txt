DI 27525.025 Exhibits of Predetermination Notice Language
NOTE: Use the model letter(s) in DI 27525.025A and DI 27525.025B in this section, or similar language, before making a revised determination. Because
most adverse reopenings are a result of establishing a later onset date, the model
language pertains to adverse onset reopenings. Modify the language for reopenings
due to receipt of additional evidence or other causes as appropriate. For exhibits
on Pre-Determination Notice Language, see DI 27525.005.
