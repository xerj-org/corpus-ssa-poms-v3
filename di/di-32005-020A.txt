A. Definition of “no determination” cases
“No determination” (ND) cases are cases that the DDS does not do a formal determination.
The following list is not all-inclusive. Reasons for ND cases include:
•
Cases that are not in the DDS jurisdiction (for details of jurisdiction, see DI 20101.000); or
•
A Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB) or Disabled Widow(er) Benefit (DWB) case in which
the claimant dies within five months of the alleged onset date, and no earlier onset
is possible. (For details of death issues, see DI 23510.000); or
•
A Continuing Disability Review (CDR) case sent to the DDS for review when there is
no continuing disability issue involved (see DI 28001.015); or
•
A CDR case where the beneficiary has died since the last disability determination.
