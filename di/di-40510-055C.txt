SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40510.055 - Title II Medical or SGA Cessations - New Title XVI Application
Effective Dates: 04/22/2026 - Present | TN 32 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
To determine title XVI involvement get a current SSI-2 query which contains the PSY
field in accordance with DI 40505.210 if an SSI-2 query 30 days old or less is not in file.
Handle as follows when title XVI status is determined:
If title XVI is ...
Then ...
Not Yet Allowed
Telephone the FO that the title II benefits are being ceased, the effective date of
cessation, and the basis for cessation. Document the conversation in the title II
folder, make sure due process has been afforded, and process the determination.
Allowed/Title II SGA Cessation
Advise the FO that title II claim is being sent to them for preparation of the SGA
cessation and to take whatever action is warranted on the title XVI claim.
Allowed/Title II Medical Cessation
Follow the instructions provided above for the title XVI “Not Yet Allowed” situation.
​
NOTE: Work activity performed July 1, 1987, or later does not raise an issue of continuing
eligibility for title XVI payments. The TWP/EPE provisions do not apply and SGA is
no longer a basis for title XVI cessation.
