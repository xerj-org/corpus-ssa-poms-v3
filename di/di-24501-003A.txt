SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24501.003 - Corrective Action When an Unqualified Individual Acted as a Medical Consultant (MC) or Psychological Consultant (PC)
Effective Dates: 01/04/2021 - Present | TN 19 (01-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424501003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Overview
MCs and PCs are members of a team that makes disability determinations. The MC completes
the medical portion of the case review and medical assessments, including any applicable
residual functional capacity (RFC) assessment(s), for all physical impairments in
a claim. A PC completes the medical portion of the case review and medical assessments,
including any applicable RFC assessment(s), for all mental impairments in a claim.
MCs and PCs must meet the relevant qualifications described in DI 24501.001C.
For procedures to ensure an individual meets the relevant MC or PC qualifications,
see DI 39569.300C.2.
This section provides instructions for when an individual who did not meet the qualifications
of an MC or PC nevertheless completed the medical portion of case reviews and medical
assessments in the capacity of an MC or PC.
