A. Disability Determination Services (DDS) jurisdiction of a subsequent Title II claim
See DI 27516.001A. for the definition of res judicata.
The DDS has jurisdiction of a subsequent claim filed after a prior claim that was
denied through the date the non-disability requirements were last met and an exception
to field office (FO) res judicata processing applies. The FO exceptions are:
1.
Mental Incapacity.
2.
Misinformation.
3.
New fact or issue.
4.
Change in law or policy.
See DI 27516.001B2 for a complete explanation of the exceptions. If any one of the exceptions applies,
the DDS cannot apply res judicata.
