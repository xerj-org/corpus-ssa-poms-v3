Effective Dates: 03/24/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424503010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of objective medical evidence
Objective medical evidence means signs, laboratory findings, or both, from a medical
source. Objective medical evidence does not include symptoms, diagnoses, or medical
opinions. For more information, see DI 24503.005 Categories of Evidence.
