A. Blindness
Blind means that the individual is totally without or has impaired vision of not more
than 20/200 visual acuity in the better eye and for whom a diagnosis and medical findings
show that vision cannot be improved to better than 20/200; or who has loss of vision due wholly or in part to impairment of field vision or
to other factors which affect the usefulness of vision to a like degree.
