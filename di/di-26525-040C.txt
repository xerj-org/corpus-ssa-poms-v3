SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.040 - The MINE or MINE-Equivalent Diary - General
Effective Dates: 05/12/2009 - Present | TN 13 (05-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Complete the diary block of Forms SSA-831-C3/U3 (item 17) and SSA-832-C3/U3 SSA-833-C3/U3
(item 23) as follows:
1. Diary Type
Enter “MRP”.
2. Diary Date
Enter a two-digit code for the month, day, and year 84 months in the future, counting
the month of adjudication. Use “01” for the day (e.g., if this decision is in 4/02,
diary for 03/01/09).
3. Diary Reason
Enter “7”.
