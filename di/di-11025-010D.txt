D. Controlling a DMC claim
Since there is no control in MCS and Processing Center (PC) action may be required:
•
Fax a request into paperless to notify the PC or send them a Modernized Development
Worksheet (MDW). Tell the PC that a childhood determination is pending. Include in
the text “Handle per RS 00202.095B.2”. If the DMC determination is still pending when the child attains age 16, the PC
places the parent in suspense status. If the parent is in terminated status, the PC
will not reinstate the parent and place him or her in suspense. Benefits will not
be reinstated until the child is found to be disabled and in the parent’s care;
•
Create a special message on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) showing that a DMC
claim is pending and the date sent to DDS;
•
Control the case on MDW unless the claim is in MCS.
