SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27520.020 - Prior Favorable or Unfavorable Title II Determination or Decision - Felony Related Impairment
Effective Dates: 07/28/2008 - Present | TN 12 (07-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427520020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Allowed Determination or Decision
Reopen at any time to:
•
Exclude a felony-related impairment from consideration because the claimant has been
convicted of a felony; or
•
Consider an earlier onset if exclusion of a felony-related impairment resulted in
a later onset and the claimant's felony conviction has been overturned.
REMINDER: If revising the determination or decision to a denial or other unfavorable decision,
assure that appropriate due process predetermination notification is given.
