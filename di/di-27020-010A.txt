SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27020.010 - Rationale for Claims Escalated to the Reconsideration Level
Effective Dates: 03/04/2010 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427020010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Combined initial and reconsideration determinations
Combined initial and reconsideration determinations occur when a claimant files a
related claim (DIB-CDB, DIB-DWB, DWB-CDB, DIB-DI, etc.), involving the same issue,
while a reconsideration request is pending; escalate the newly filed claim to the reconsideration level. Combined determinations prepared under these instructions are reconsideration determinations.
