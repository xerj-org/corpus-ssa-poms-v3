B. Handling a request for withdrawal of the reconsideration request
•
In person or telephonic request for withdrawal
If the DDS receives a withdrawal request either in person or by telephone, advise
the individual that withdrawing the reconsideration request for reconsideration of a disability cessation may be disadvantageous based on the reasons cited on the form SSA-789 (Request for Reconsideration-Disability Cessation Right to Appear).
If the individual still wants to withdraw the request for reconsideration of a disability
cessation, advise them that they must submit a written request to the FO for approval.
If necessary, the DDS should telephone the disability hearing unit (DHU) and request
postponement of the hearing process, pending disposition of the withdrawal.
•
Written request for withdrawal
If the DDS receives a written request for withdrawal, place the request into the electronic
or paper file. Contact and advise the individual that withdrawing the request for
reconsideration of a disability cessation may be disadvantageous based on the reasons
cited on the form, SSA-789.
If the individual wants to continue withdrawal of the request for reconsideration
of a disability cessation, forward the case folder as a ‘No Determination’ (ND) to
the FO for approval or disapproval of the request. If necessary, telephone the DHU
and request postponement of the hearing process pending the disposition of the withdrawal.
