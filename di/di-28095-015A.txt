A. Notice designation
The office responsible for preparing the notice will use Item 29 on the form SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title
XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title
II). The disability examiner will designate the notice by using the following codes
in Item 29:
•
disability determination services (DDS) use “DDS/DL”;
•
program service center (PSC) use “PSC/DL”;
•
Office of Disability Operations (ODO) use “ODO/DL”; or
•
field office (FO) use “FO/DL”.
Use the appropriate code with the notice designation along with any required paragraphs
and fill-ins, in proper sequence. Designate the office that will prepare the notice
followed by the letter “S” in place of the notice instructions, if there is insufficient
space in Item 29. For suffix “S” instructions see DI 28095.015B in this section.
If the DDS will prepare the notice but the notice will be release by another component,
indicate “DDS/DL-S” in Item 29. In some special situations, the DDS examiner will
not specify the notice information but will only designate the component responsible
for the notice (for example, ODO/DL) in Item 29.
NOTE: For notice designation instructions, see NL 00603.025 Designating Notice Information on Disability Determination Form.
