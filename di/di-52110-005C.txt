C. Developing VR payments
Develop the existence of a specific VR plan. If the NH cannot provide proof of actual
participation in a plan:
1.
Contact the WC carrier or the WC attorney for verification.
NOTE: The WC attorney can assist in obtaining actual proof but oral or written statements
from the attorney do not constitute proof of WC or proof of participation in a VR
plan.
2.
Determine if VR payments are paid in addition to, or in lieu of WC.
3.
Verify the amount of any VR payments and related WC.
