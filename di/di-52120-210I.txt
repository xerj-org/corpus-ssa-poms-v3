I. Verifying WC
1. Carrier
Our best source of information is usually the WC carrier. Carrier contact information
can be obtained from the state. See the state link for contact information for Pennsylvania
carriers under ‘References’ in this section.
2. State
The Bureau of WC can be contacted for help in identifying the carrier.
