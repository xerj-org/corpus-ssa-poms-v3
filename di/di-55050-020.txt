DI 55050.020 Handling Program-Related Questions
Procedure
Field components should answer program-related questions from beneficiaries/recipients
(e.g., questions about how work and earnings may affect their benefits). TSCs should
refer beneficiaries/recipients with complex program-related questions to their servicing
FOs or to the Ticket Program Manager (TPM). See DI 55050.025A.
