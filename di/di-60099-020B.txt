B. April 2011 through August 2011
Abt notified 80,000 beneficiaries of their automatic eligibility in the BOND project
by sending a “good news” letter to each BOND participant in stage 1. The BOND work
CDR unit coded the DCF, indicating that the beneficiary is a BOND participant. For
a definition of a BOND participant, see DI 60099.015A.
