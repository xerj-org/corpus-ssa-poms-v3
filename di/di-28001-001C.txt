C. Policy for conducting CDRs
We will notify the individual of the nature of the review, why we are conducting the
review, and that the medical improvement (MI) standard will apply, for the definition
of MI, see DI
28010.001A. The individual or representative may submit medical and other evidence, as applicable,
for consideration during the review. The purpose of the review is to determine if
an individuals impairment(s) has improved since the most recent favorable determination,
called the comparison point decision (CPD). When appropriate, we also determine if
an exception to MI applies. During this review, the final decision could result in
termination of benefits. The termination date is two months after the cessation date.
