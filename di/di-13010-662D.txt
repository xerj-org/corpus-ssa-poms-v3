D. Procedure - Reading the QMMD Screen
The QMMD contains information relating to medical determinations made, including the
medical re-exam diary date. The data in the fields is propagated and cannot be changed
in query mode. The coding on this screen is the normal coding for the fields involved.
The second column on the QMMD shows the data the Office of Continuing Disability Reviews
Support has on file.
