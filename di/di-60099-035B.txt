B. Start and end date of BOND participation
1. Start date
The participation period begins with the first month after completion of the TWP or
the month after random assignment to a treatment group, whichever comes later.
If the beneficiary is currently dually entitled to disability benefits, (i.e., HA/CDB
or HA/DWB) the earliest TWP completion date between the two records is compared to
the random assignment date to determine the start date of participation for both entitlements.
2. End date
The participation period ends the last day of the 60th month from the start date of
participation. The participation period starting and ending dates are on the DEMO
screen in the Disability Control File (DCF).
