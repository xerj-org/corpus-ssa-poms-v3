DI 12095.001 General
This subchapter contains the forms used in the initial, postentitlement/ posteligibility
appeals process. The forms used follow the appeals process; (i.e., reconsideration,
hearing, appeals). Also included are forms relating to representation, model notices,
and benefit/payment continuation model letters.
