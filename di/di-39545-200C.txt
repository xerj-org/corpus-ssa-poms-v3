Effective Dates: 08/22/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - monitoring
DDSs should perform routine CE oversight in accordance with established DDS procedures.
A CE provider should not be used if the provider uses support staff to help perform
CEs who do not meet appropriate licensing or certification requirements of the State.
