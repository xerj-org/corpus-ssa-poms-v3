H. Time limitations for filing claims
1. Initial claims
Unless the individual gives notice to the employer within 21 days of the injury (or
the employer had knowledge of the injury), no WC is payable before the date notice
is given. For any compensation to be allowed, notice must be given within 120 days
of the injury.
2. Protests to denied or terminated claims
If the request for WC benefits was denied or WC benefits were paid but then stopped,
the individual has 3 years from the date of injury to file a claim petition or petition
to reinstate WC benefits.
3. Occupational disease cases
The injury/disability must occur within 300 weeks from the date of last employment
in an occupation in which the individual had exposure to a hazard, and a petition
must be filed no later than 3 years from the date of injury/disability.
If WC benefits were terminated, a petition to reinstate WC benefits must be filed
within 3 years after the date of the most recent WC check.
