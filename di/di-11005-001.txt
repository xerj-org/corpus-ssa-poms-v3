DI 11005.001 Field Office Disability Interviews
Interviewers should review all relevant queries before conducting the interview, e.g.,
the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR), Supplemental Security Income Display (SSID),
Disability Determination Services Query (DDSQ), Hearing and Appeals Case Processing
System (HACPS), or Appeals Review Processing System (ARPS).
