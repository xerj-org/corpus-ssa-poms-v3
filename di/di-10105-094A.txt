SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.094 - Field Offices Identifying and Documenting Prisoner Status for Title II Disability Benefits
Effective Dates: 07/10/2014 - Present | TN 2 (02-13)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105094 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Identify possible prisoner status
Be alert to:
•
Periods of unemployment due to incarceration prior to filing (review the Summary Earnings
Query (SEQY) or Detail Earnings Query (DEQY));
•
Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS) records;
•
Medical sources indicating prison involvement; and
•
Prisoner data on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR).
NOTE: You can locate prisoner information for a claimant on the Prisoner (PRISONER) or
Prisoner Suspension (PRSNDATA) data line.
