A. Payment of disability interim benefits (IB)
If the Appeals Council (AC) initiates review of an administrative law judge’s (ALJ)
favorable disability decision and the agency does not issue a final decision within
110 days from the ALJ’s decision, we will pay the claimant interim benefits.
NOTE: To process interim benefit payments for Title XVI Supplemental Security Income
(SSI) cases, see SI 02007.000.
