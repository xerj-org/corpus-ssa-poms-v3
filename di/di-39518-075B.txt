B. Operating procedure
1. Form SSA-4514
a. Recording hours
Record the number of part-time hours as well as hours worked by full-time employees
on Form SSA-4514.
b. Sending reports
Send
this form to the SSA RO
and SSA CO each quarter.
2. SAOR staffing reporting
Instructions for State Agency Operating Report (SAOR) reporting of staff are in the Management Information Manual, Part IV.
