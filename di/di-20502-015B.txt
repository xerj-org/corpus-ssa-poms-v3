B. SSA-820-F4/SSA-821-BK – Work Activity Report – Self Employed/Employee
Work issue cases sent to the DDS from the FO will contain a work issue determination
on an SSA-821 (Work Report -- Employee) or SSA-820 (Work Activity Report -- Self Employed
Person). (See DI 10510.025 and DI 10505.035 for instructions for completing reports. See GN 00201.015 for policy and procedure when signature proxy is involved.) See DI
81020.120 for electronic procedures.
