G. IDR sessions and lockout
An online session expires if the IU does not move from one IDR screen to another within
30 minutes. When a session expires due to inactivity, information on the current screen
is lost. The IDR presents a warning message about five minutes prior to expiration.
When an IU exits an IDR, there is a five-minute lockout period. The IU can reenter
the IDR after five minutes. The IDR system retains information through the last screen
on which the IU selected the “Next” button.
