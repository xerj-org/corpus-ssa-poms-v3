DI 60075.045 The Promoting Opportunity Demonstration (POD) Automated System (PAS)
The PAS was a stand-alone system that accepted database files from Abt Associate’s
(Abt’s) Information Data System (IDS). It contained the beneficiary’s report of gross
earnings and impairment related work expenses (IRWE) for a month. It also contained
a beneficiary’s request for withdrawal from the POD.
