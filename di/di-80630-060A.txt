Effective Dates: 09/18/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0480630060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Traditional workload
The traditional PC workloads such as FO and modular component work issue referrals,
earnings enforcement, Title II ALJ reversals, CDR mailers and non-responders will be processed in paper folders and the Paperless
Processing Center (PPC) system, according to existing instructions, until PPC and
PCACS both integrate with the EF.
