D. Considering medical opinions and prior administrative medical findings
Do not consider medical opinions or prior administrative medical findings when establishing
a medically determinable impairment (MDI). However, once an MDI is established, consider
prior administrative medical findings for all other findings in a claim. Because medical
opinions relate to functional abilities and limitations, consider medical opinions
for determining severity at step 2, at step 3, and for determining an adults residual
functional capacity (RFC).
