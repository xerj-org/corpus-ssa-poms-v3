E. Suspension and termination events
Suspend or terminate provisional benefits following normal benefit policies. Such
suspension or termination events include:
•
prisoner suspension,
•
alien suspension, and
•
marriage of a CDB
For a discussion of suspension and termination events, see GN 02602.005, RS 00203.030, RS 00203.035, and RS 00207.002. Provisional benefits will not resume when a suspension event continues beyond the
provisional benefit period ending month.
