DI 39542.020 Reporting State Contracts to DA - Operating Procedure
Submit reports of new contracts within 30 days of the date they are awarded. Exclude:
•
contracts for services and functions described in DI 39542.015; and
•
contracts for indirect services by another agency of the State.
Show
in the report of each contract the following:
•
name and address
of the contractor;
•
work
the contractor performs;
•
duration
of the contract including renewal options;
•
costs
of the contract.
