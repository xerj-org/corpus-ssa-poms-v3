SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28075.030 - Capability Issue Raised During a CDR
Effective Dates: 06/03/2009 - Present | TN 16 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428075030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Capability development is necessary when evidence obtained during a CDR raises a question
as to the individual's ability to manage benefits and a continuance is being processed.
CAUTION: Be alert to situations where a beneficiary with a representative payee now appears
capable and may no longer need a payee.
