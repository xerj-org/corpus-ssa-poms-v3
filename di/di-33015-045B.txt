SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33015.045 - Disqualification of Disability Hearing Officer (DHO) or Supervisory Hearing Officer (SHO)
Effective Dates: 06/08/2026 - Present | TN 26 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433015045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Actions for potential disqualification
1. DHO actions
a.
Immediately bring to the attention of the SHO any case from which you believe you
should be disqualified.
b.
If any travel cases present potential disqualification issues, consult the SHO about
those issues well in advance of your scheduled travel so that appropriate action can
be taken before you travel.
2. Beneficiary or recipient requests disqualification
a.
Note and deny a beneficiary's (or recipient's) request for disqualification if it
is made at the hearing and you see no valid reason for disqualification.
b.
Immediately attempt to consult with the SHO, if necessary by telephone, to determine
how to proceed if there is some reasonable basis for granting the request even if
it is not clear that the request should be granted.
c.
Recess the hearing, if necessary, until it is determined how to proceed.
3. Documentation
a.
The SHO or DHO prepares a brief memorandum for the claims folder that summarizes any
consultation between the DHO and the SHO on the question of whether the DHO should
be disqualified.
b.
The SHO notes in the claims folder any situation where the SHO disqualified the DHO
from the case.
NOTE: These notations will inform any subsequent reviewer of the case and the beneficiary
(or recipient) or appointed representative about the factors and reasons involved.
