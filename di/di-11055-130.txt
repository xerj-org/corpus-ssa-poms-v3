DI 11055.130 Field Office (FO) Completion of the SSA-831 for a Modernized Supplemental Security
Income Claims System (MSSICS) Limitation or Exclusion Case
If the Title XVI claim is a MSSICS limitation or exclusion, prepare the SSA-831 (Disability
Determination and Transmittal) to transmit the case to the Disability Determination
Services (DDS).
The following section contains FO procedures for completing the paper SSA-831 in disability
claims that are Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) exclusions or limitations
(see DI 81010.030).
