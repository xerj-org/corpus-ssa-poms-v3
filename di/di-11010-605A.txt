SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.605 - Requesting Evidence from the Office of Personnel Management (OPM)
Effective Dates: 03/29/2002 - Present | TN 41 (01-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010605 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
OPM will furnish medical evidence on former civil service employees who have filed
for civil service disability retirement. These records may include a letter from the
Treating sources, a Certificate of Medical Examination (with detailed clinical signs
and laboratory findings), a report from the employing agency describing job duties,
and the employee's ability to perform those duties.
