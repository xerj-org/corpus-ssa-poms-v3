SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.040 - Special Instructions for Medicare Only Cases
Effective Dates: 04/07/2006 - Present | TN 20 (09-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. SSA-833-U3
In item 24 remarks, add an appropriate remark such as “MQGE
Case” or “Medicare Only Case”.
2. Notices
Modify forms and letters used in conducting CDRs and in notifying of the decision so that
no reference is made to cash benefit payments.
