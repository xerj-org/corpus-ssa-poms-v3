B. Claimant's Consent
Do not send an SSA-827 when requesting a third party to complete the SSA-3380 (Function
Report - Adult Third Party), or any similar form, unless the DDS knows from past experience
that the source requires it. Although the Privacy Act permits disclosure of the SSN,
in the rare instance in which a third party requests an SSA-827, the DDS should effectively
black out the SSN(s) previously entered on the SSA-827.
