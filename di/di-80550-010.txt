DI 80550.010 CPMS Access
CPMS is a unified, user-friendly, web-based system that can be accessed by both HO
staff and case processing staff throughout the Social Security Administration, including
DDS. Users other than HO staff have limited, query/read only access. Users can obtain
the most current information on hearing cases. Access CPMS through the desktop OHO-CPMS link.
