SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28010.030 - Evaluating Listings with a Specified Timeframe at the Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
Effective Dates: 05/25/2023 - Present | TN 12 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428010030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of “listing with a specified timeframe”
A listing in the Listing of Impairments that indicates a specific length of time for
the disability is a “listing with a specified timeframe.” For example, listing 6.04
(“Chronic kidney disease, with kidney transplant”) is a listing with a specified timeframe
because the language of listing 6.04 indicates the impairment is disabling for 1-year
following the transplant. Some listings direct the number of months or years the impairment
meets the listing.
