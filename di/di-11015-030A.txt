Effective Dates: 10/29/2010 - Present | TN 32 (07-16)

A. Factors of entitlement not met
If the DWB claimant does not meet the non-medical factors of entitlement (for example,
age or relationship), deny the claim.
For entitlement factors, see:
•
RS 00207.000 – Widow(er)’s Benefits Subchapter Table of Contents
