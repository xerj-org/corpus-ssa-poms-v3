SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55050.001 - Handling General Questions About SSA's Ticket To Work Program
Effective Dates: 05/02/2022 - Present | TN 2 (05-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455050001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure – answer general ticket questions
Field components should answer general questions about SSA's Ticket to Work program
that a beneficiary/recipient or a member of the general public asks. If the question
requires a detailed explanation about the Ticket to Work program, refer the caller
to SSA's Ticket Program Manager (TPM). See DI 55050.025.
