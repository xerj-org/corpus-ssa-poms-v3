C. Personalized Disability Notices
DDS must prepare and release a personalized disability notice (PDN) to a CDB reentitlement
claimant on all less than fully favorable disability determinations. The PDN consists
of a form notice (SSA-L443) and a personalized disability explanation (PDE) containing
the required information. For complete instructions on completing notices, see NL 00603.100 and DI 81020.130. For complete PDE instructions, see DI 26530.040.
