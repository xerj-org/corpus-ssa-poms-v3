SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52155.025 - A101 Processing Claims with Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452155025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. MCS EC provides the WC/PDB offset determination
•
If MCS EC screens provide computations, WC/PDB data will propagate to the A101.
•
Verify that the results are correct and the benefit amounts on the MBEN correctly
reflect the AMT PAYABLE AFT OFFSET.
•
No further action is necessary for WC/PDB processing and the A101 can be completed.
