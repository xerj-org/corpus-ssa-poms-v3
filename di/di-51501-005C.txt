SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 51501.005 - Claimant Requests to File a New Disability Application
Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present | TN 18 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0451501005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Improper lead established
If we improperly establish a lead for a new application in eLAS while the claimant
has a pending appeal, make only one attempt to contact the claimant by phone.
1. Contact successful
If the contact is successful, explain that he or she must make a choice to either
continue the appeal or request to withdraw the appeal and file a new application.
•
Document the claimants choice on an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) in the pending EDCS
claim.
•
Management must delete the improper lead using the instructions in MS 00601.004.
2. Contact unsuccessful
If the contact is unsuccessful, explain at the time of the claimants appointment that
he or she must make a choice to either continue the appeal or request to withdraw
the appeal and file a new application.
•
Document the claimants choice on an SSA-5002 in the pending EDCS claim.
•
Management must delete the improper lead using the instructions in MS 00601.004.
If the claimant does not keep his or her appointment:
•
Send an SSA-2708 (Field Office Call Come In Request); and
•
Management must delete the improper lead using the instructions in MS 00601.004.
