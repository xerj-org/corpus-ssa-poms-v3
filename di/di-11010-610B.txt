SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.610 - Field Office (FO) Receives Additional Evidence
Effective Dates: 01/28/2016 - Present | TN 56 (01-16)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010610 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disability claim folders pending in the Disability Quality Branch (DQB) / Disability
Case Review (DCR)
If the FO receives additional medical evidence while the disability claim is pending
in DQB/DCR, FAX the evidence to DQB/DCR as appropriate. The fax number of the appropriate
DQB/DCR is located in the Detailed Office/Organization Resources System (DOORS).
