SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52155.040 - Processing Disability Claims with a Federal Public Disability Benefit (PDB)
Effective Dates: 09/09/2025 - Present | TN 3 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452155040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Processing initial claims involving Federal PDB
The Modernized Claims System (MCS) application screen Claimant Military Retirement/
Federal Benefits (CLMR) and the Windfall Elimination Provision Common screens (which
are accessed from the MCS path when the month of entitlement (MOE) is prior to January
2024) collect data on federal pensions and the coverage status of the work on which
the pension is based.
If an MCS claim cannot be taken and the claimant's MOE is prior to January 2024, ask
the individual to complete and return the Form SSA-150, Modified Benefit Formula Questionnaire.
See form exhibit in OS 15010.130 - Form SSA-150.
