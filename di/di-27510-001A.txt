SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27510.001 - Period Covered by a Final Determination or Decision: “Period Adjudicated”
Effective Dates: 10/05/2015 - Present | TN 2 (06-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427510001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating Policy
Where there has been a prior final determination or decision on the issue of disability,
the following apply when adjudicating post-entitlement issues or a subsequent claim
of the same individual:
•
The “period adjudicated” refers to that period covered by a determination or decision
which has become administratively final and binding upon both the claimant and SSA unless a new determination or decision is made on
appeal or reopening. See Final Determination or Decision (DI 27501.001A.3.) and Reopening and Revising a Determination or Decision (DI 27501.005).
•
On subsequent claims, adjudicators will limit their consideration to the period after
the prior period adjudicated, except in certain circumstances (see Subsequent Applications
– Adjudicators Will Limit Consideration on Subsequent Claims (DI 27510.005A.).
