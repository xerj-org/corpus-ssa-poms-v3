A. Policy for End Stage Renal Disease Medicare only entitlement of a dependent adult
child using a parent's insured status
As explained in 24 CFR 406.13, The Social Security Act provides coverage under Medicare's hospital insurance (HI)
and supplemental medical insurance (SMI) based on End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) for
a dependent adult child if the claimant:
•
is determined to have ESRD that requires a regular course of dialysis or a kidney
transplant, and
•
is a dependent adult child of an individual who is either entitled to monthly title
II benefits or is fully or currently insured (including fully or currently insured
by employment under the Railroad Retirement Act), and
•
files an application for ESRD benefits as a dependent adult child.
