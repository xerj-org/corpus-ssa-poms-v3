SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 51501.015 - Claimant Submits Additional Evidence
Effective Dates: 03/05/2026 - Present | TN 16 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0451501015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Additional evidence
Additional evidence includes, but is not limited to, medical evidence, statements
by the claimant about his or her impairment and restrictions, and disability reports;
e.g., SSA-3368 (Disability Report – Adult), Internet SSA-3368 (i3368), SSA-3367 (Disability
Report – Field Office).
1. Prior claim pending at the initial, reconsideration, or hearing level
•
Fax the evidence into the electronic folder (EF).
•
For paper folders, mail the evidence to the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
or hearing office (HO).
2. Prior claim pending at the Appeals Council (AC) level
•
Fax the evidence into the EF.
•
Use the UAT function to alert the AC that there is additional evidence in file.
•
For paper cases, email ^DA ApplsReqReview Generalwith attachments of the evidence and an SSA-5002 (report of Contact) to notify the
AC of the additional evidence.
If the evidence indicates a new critical or disabling condition (defined in DI 51501.001B), follow the instructions in DI 51501.015C in this section.
