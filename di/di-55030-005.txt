DI 55030.005 Employment Network (EN) Responsibilities for Reporting Alleged Earnings to the Ticket
Program Manager (TPM)
ENs and State vocational rehabilitation (VR) agencies serving Ticketholders as ENs
submit evidence of Ticketholder earnings to the TPM as part of their request to be
paid for the work-related milestones and outcomes achieved by the Ticketholders they
serve. See DI 55060.005 for an explanation of EN payment systems under the Ticket to Work program.
NOTE: While ENs and State VR agencies must report earnings when they request payment,
they may report work or earnings more frequently to the TPM.
