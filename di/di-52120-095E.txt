E. Time limitations for filing claims
•
INJURY: The individual must file a written claim with the employer within 200 days of the
date of injury (or receipt of last payment of WC or medical treatment).
•
OCCUPATIONAL DISEASE: The individual must file a written claim with the employer within one year of onset
of disability from an occupational disease
•
APPEAL: The individual must file for a hearing within 3 years of injury, or within 2 years
of last payment of WC or medical treatment.
