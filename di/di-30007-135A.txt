A. Receiving the RPC submission
When a DDS requests an RPC, the RPC tool automatically:
1.
Assigns a case number;
2.
Enters the date of the submission; and
3.
Adds the case to the Workload Management screen.
DDS has the option to request a panel discussion or have their claim expedited. They
should select the appropriate box when submitting their claim to RPC. Cases that do
not specify an option will be processed in a way which is most advantageous to all
parties involved.
