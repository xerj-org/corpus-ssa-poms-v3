E. Time limitations for filing claims
The individual must file a claim within 1 year from the date of injury, or within
1 year after the last payment of WC. However, the individual has up to 3 years after
the termination of nonscheduled benefits to reopen their case due to changes in condition
[Statute: 32-1505(b)].
