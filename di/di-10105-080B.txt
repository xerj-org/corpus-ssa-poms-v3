B. Age 50 requirement for benefits prior to 11/60
Prior to 11/60 a NH must have attained age 50 in order to qualify for DIB. NH’s under
50 could become entitled to a freeze but not to monthly benefits. This requirement
was dropped by the 1960 amendments for cash benefits beginning 11/60, based on applications
filed after 8/60.
