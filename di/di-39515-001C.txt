SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39515.001 - Guidelines for Providing a QA Function
Effective Dates: 08/12/2025 - Present | TN 1 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439515001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Operating procedure
1. Budgeting
a. Workpower analysis
Indicate in your analysis, the QA staff and workyears needed (see DI 39503.220).
b. QA plan
Submit as part of your budget request, a QA plan that includes:
•
A complete explanation with support rationale of the staff and functions used for
QA.
•
Justification for supporting the full range of QA activities if you plan to intensify
or expand your QA activities.
2. Reporting
a.
No specific reports are required other than your budget submittal, State Agency Operations
Report and the State Agency Work Measurement System.
b.
SSA may require local reporting.
