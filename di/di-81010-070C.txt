C. SSA-831 Disability Determination and Transmittal
EDCS can also print an SSA-831 to document SGA or technical denials (see Processing
Field Office (FO) Determinations - DI 81010.140). If an FO determination is made in EDCS:
•
The print screen will enable the 831 check box; and
•
Provide a list of claims in that case that have FO Determinations; and
•
The SSA-831 information generated from EDCS by the FO Determination function will
be available to print.
