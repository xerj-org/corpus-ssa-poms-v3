SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22505.027 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Adjudicative Team Member Furnishes Evidence
Effective Dates: 02/28/2001 - Present | TN 3 (02-01)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422505027 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
DDS medical and psychological consultants (MC/PCs) will not participate in the review or determination of the case when they have prior knowledge of the claimant, e.g., when the claimant was a patient.
