E. Appeal filed on a Medically Reactivated claim
If an appeal is filed at the FO while a medical reactivation (including a medical
reactivation involving a possible reopening or a subsequent claim medical reactivation)
is pending at the DDS, the FO provides notification of the appeal to the DDS via an
SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) and a telephone call. The DDS:
•
Closes the claim with a No Determination with reason No Decision Change (NODC). See
Processing No Determination (ND) Claims - DI 81020.127; and
•
Sends the claim to case closure.
