SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26525.030 - MIE Examples
Effective Dates: 01/27/2017 - Present | TN 21 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426525030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Judgment required
Use sound adjudicative judgment in setting the MIE diary.
•
Do not infer that the conditions in DI 26525.030C. are all inclusive.
•
Do not set a MIE diary only because an impairment is listed in DI 26525.030C.
2. Case evaluation
Consider all the case facts in determining whether to set a MIE diary (e.g., the presence
of other chronic or progressive impairments, adverse vocational factors, and active
participation in a therapeutic or treatment regime).
•
For adults, set the MIE diary only when there is good reason to expect cessation under
the MIRS (see DI 28010.000 for MIRS guidelines).
•
For children, set the MIE diary with greater flexibility, since children may have
a greater potential for improvement than do adults.
