B. Effect of the EPE
The EPE does not change the definition of disability. A beneficiary is eligible for
payments as long as they continue to meet the definition of disability. The EPE permits
re-instatement of benefits when the beneficiary benefits have ceased due to SGA, and
their earnings fall below the SGA levels.
