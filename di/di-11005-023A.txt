A. Introduction
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) use the information on the SSA-3368-BK (Disability Report – Adult) to address certain technical and non-medical aspects
of a disability claim, such as establishing the correct onset date, identifying unsuccessful
work attempts, and obtaining information about substantial gainful activity (SGA).
The DDS also uses the SSA-3368-BK to develop medical and other evidence that can be
used to assess the individual’s alleged impairments in conjunction with non-medical
factors, such as education and work history.
The following instructions are for completing the paper SSA-3368-BK.
