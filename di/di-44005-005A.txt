SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 44005.005 - Joint Freeze Processing
Effective Dates: 06/22/2010 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0444005005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) actions
RRB makes their disability decision and prepares a joint freeze SSA-831(Disability
Determination and Transmittal), allowing or denying the freeze. They then forward
the paper file to the Great Lakes Program Service Center-Disability case review (GLPSC-DCR).
