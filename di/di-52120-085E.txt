E. Important dates
Dates
07/01/1988 - 07/01/1991
For dates of injury that fall within this period, if Temporary Total (TT) is paid
for over 78 weeks, the employer/carrier is eligible for a credit against any award
of Permanent Partial Impairment (PPI)
07/01/1989 - 06/30/1991
For dates of injury that fall between this period,
Permanent Total (PT) is payable in
addition to TT payments for up to 125 weeks,
subject to the maximum compensation limits.
Refer to Indiana Workers’ Compensation Handbook maximum compensation limits (see References below)
07/01/1991
Permanent Total (PT) are payable in addition to TT payments of up to 125 weeks, subject
to the maximum compensation limits. Also, the employer/carrier is eligible for a credit
against any award of PPI if TT is paid for over 125 weeks
07/01/1997
Awards for any combination of PT, TT and TP are limited to a total of 500 weeks
