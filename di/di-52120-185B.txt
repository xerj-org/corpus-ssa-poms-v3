B. Cost-of-living Adjustments (COLA)
WSI provides long-term TP disability inflation adjustment to workers with a disability
date or date of successful reapplication, occurring after 06/30/1991. A worker is
eligible beginning on the first day of July, after the fifth full year and every year
thereafter.
