A. Policy fee schedule rates
Each State determines its rates of payment for purchasing medical or other services
necessary to make determinations of disability.
•
Rates may not exceed the highest rate paid by Federal or other agencies in the State
for the same or similar type services.
•
States must adhere to the cost principles and standards in:
–
Cost Principles for State Governments, Regulations
2 CFR
225
https://www2.ntia.doc.gov/files/BTOP_OMB_2cfr225.pdf
–
DDS Financial Management, DI 39506.001.
