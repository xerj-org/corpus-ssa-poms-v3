DI 52135.130 Minnesota Public Disability Benefits (PDB)
Under Minnesota law, a person employed as a police officer or firefighter who becomes
disabled from an injury or illness arising out of or in the course of the line of
duty shall be entitled to a disability benefit (i.e., PDB) if they are not entitled
to a service pension (i.e., retirement benefit). To determine if the PDB will cause
offset, see DI 52135.130A. below. For a definition and information about service pensions, see DI 52135.130B. below.
