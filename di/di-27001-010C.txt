C. Sufficiency of evidence at the reconsideration level
Sufficiency of evidence must be considered during the reconsideration process. If
the evidence contains inconsistencies or insufficiencies that are material to the
determination, the DDS must complete supplemental development to resolve the inconsistencies
or insufficiencies.
For more information on:
•
Inconsistent and insufficient evidence, see DI
24501.016B.
•
Supplemental development, see DI 22505.008.
