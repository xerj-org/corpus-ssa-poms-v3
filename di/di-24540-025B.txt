B. Policy Sections of SSR 06-01p
Two short sections explain the purpose of the SSR and provide the pertinent history.
Policy interpretation is in question-and-answer format. The two questions answered
in the SSR are:
I.
What Types of Impairments May Result From Exposure to Tremolite Asbestos?
II.
How Do We Consider Impairments Related to Tremolite Asbestos Exposure in The Sequential
Evaluation Process?
