D. Policy - referral of denied title xvi applicants
Section 1615 of the Act requires only that recipients of payments based on blindness
or disability who have not attained age 65 be referred to the State vocational rehabilitation
agency. However, it is the Social Security Administration's (SSA's) policy that denied
title XVI applicants be screened and referred in the same manner as denied title II
applicants.
