B. Procedure - Medicare language in denial notices
When the FO prepares the denial notice, they will include the appropriate Medicare
language, based on the type of denial and type of Medicare coverage. The chart below
shows the appropriate notice language (known as a UTI in DPS) to use when constructing
the denial notice. In some situations, there won’t be Medicare language inserted.
Examples of these notices are contained in DI 13050.105, Exhibits 6, 7 and 8.
