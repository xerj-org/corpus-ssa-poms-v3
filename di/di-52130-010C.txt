C. Determining whether covered service exclusions are met
Review the earnings history on the Detailed Earnings Query (DEQY):
1.
If there is no non-covered (CSRS) employment, stop. The 85 percent covered service
exception is met. Do not offset.
2.
If there is some CSRS employment and it appears that the pension may be offsettable
but cannot be determined from the DEQY, develop for proof per DI 52130.001 in this section.
3.
If the covered-service exclusions in DI 52130.001 do not apply, verify the FERS benefit amount and offset the gross benefit amount
before any deductions.
4.
Input the case via the Modernized Claims System (MCS) or the Interactive Computations
Facility (ICF), and show the WC/PDB type as “FM”. Code the CSPDB indicator per the
instructions in DI 52130.025.
