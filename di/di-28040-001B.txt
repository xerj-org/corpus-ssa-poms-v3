B. MINE review frequency
Sections 221(i)(1) and 1614(a)(3)(H)(ii)(I) of the Social Security Act, as amended, require the Social Security Administration
to review these cases on a schedule to be determined by the Commissioner. The review
schedule for MINE or MINE-equivalent cases is established in the regulations as between
5 and 7 years.
