B. Policy Principle
1. Insurance
With proper budgetary approval, the DDS may incur expenses for various employee insurance programs.
2. Bonds
The cost of premiums
on bonds covering State employees is allowable.
3. Types, Extent, and Cost of Liability Insurance
a.
The types, extent and cost of coverage will be in accordance with general State or local government policy and sound business practice.
b.
Contributions to a reserve for a self-insurance program approved by the Federal grant
or agency are allowable to the extent that the types of coverage, extent
of
rates,
and premiums would have been purchased to cover the risks.
