C. Legislative extension of the Title II re-entitlement period
1. Public law (P.L.) 100-203 (The Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987)
This provision lengthened the re-entitlement period under the EPE provision from 15
months to 36 months.
2. Effective date
The 36-month re-entitlement period under EPE became effective 01/01/1988.
3. Applies to
This provision applies to beneficiaries who:
•
are entitled to Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) benefits for any month
after 12/1987; or
•
were entitled to SSDI for any month before 01/01/1988, and whose 15-month re-entitlement
period under the prior law had not elapsed as of 01/01/1988.
