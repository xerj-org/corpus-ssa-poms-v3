C. SSA-3367 Disability Report – Field Office
The FO uses this report to record information that does not involve the claimant’s
input. The DDS uses this information to determine the appropriate time period for
developing medical evidence, the technical factors that must be considered in establishing
the onset date of disability, the history of claimants’ prior case actions, claimants’
insured status, and to review observations about claimants recorded during contacts
with the FO. (See DI 11005.045.)
