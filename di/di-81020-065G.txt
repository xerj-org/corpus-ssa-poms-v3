G. EDCS Routing Forms and Associated Paper Folders
1. Storing the EDCS Routing Form
If the official folder is a paper MDF, but an EDCS case has been created, (e.g., EDCS
exclusion claim), attach the EDCS routing form to the paper MDF. DDS must:
•
Remove the EDCS routing form from the front of the paper folder; and
•
File the EDCS routing form in the blue section of the paper MDF on top of all other
forms.
This alerts subsequent reviewing components to review the CEF since associating the
EDCS claim with the paper folder.
2. Disability Reports and Imaged Documents
The EDCS routing form lists:
•
Which disability reports (e.g., SSA-3368 (Disability Report – Adult), SSA-3367 [Disability
Report - Field Office]) are available through eView; and
•
Any imaged documents that were added to the electronic folder by the Field Office
(FO).
