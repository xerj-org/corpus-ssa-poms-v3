C. Explanation - EC
Prior to the Title II Redesign Release in July 1999, the MCS back-end process (the
EC process) identified most processing limitations before EC produced an insured status
determination and an E/R Changes in the Redesign Release moved many of the processing
limitations and exceptions further back in the EC process.
