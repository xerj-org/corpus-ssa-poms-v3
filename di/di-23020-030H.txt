H. Removal of dire need flag
The DDS may remove the dire need flag whenever there is evidence which establishes
the claimant's alleged circumstance(s) does not meet the definition of dire need.
The adjudicator must clearly document the reason for removal of the dire need flag
in the official case file. When documenting in DCPS, the adjudicator should document
the reason for removing the dire need flag in the case notes section of DCPS.
