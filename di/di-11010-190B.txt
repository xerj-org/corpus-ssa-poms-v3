B. Policy -- Using the SSA-831
Reminders when using the SSA-831 include:
•
Use an SSA-831 to transmit cases to the Disability Case Review (DCR) at the Program
Service Center (PSC) or Disability Determination Services (DDS) when the case is an
EDCS exclusion. See Certified Electronic folder (CEF) Exclusions and Limitations -
DI 81010.030.
•
Access the SSA-831 as an eForm through the Form Selector website
•
Complete a separate SSA-831 for each claim filed.
EXAMPLE: An individual files a DIB claim on his/her own SSN and also files a Childhood Disability
Benefits (CDB) or Disabled Widow(er)s (DWB) claim on another SSN, complete an SSA-831
for each claim.
•
Print three copies (folder copy, Vocational Rehabilitation copy, and State Agency
copy) and place in the inside front of the paper folder or fax to the electronic folder
per Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes - DI 81010.090.
