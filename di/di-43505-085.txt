DI 43505.085 Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Jurisdiction for CDR determinations is governed by the residence of the primary beneficiary
using the guidelines for initial claims. (See DI 43501.001 and DI 43505.015.) The following types of continuing cases will be forwarded to an examiner for review
and appropriate action. (See DI 43510.080 for development instructions.)
A.
Scheduled medical improvement expected cases.
B.
Periodic review cases
C.
Voluntary or Vocational Rehabilitation Report of Return to Work
D.
Anonymous Report of Work Activity
E.
Report of medical improvement
F.
CDR Enforcements
