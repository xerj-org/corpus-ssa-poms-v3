D. Procedure - SSA-5666, Request for Administrative Information
1. SSA-5666 Is Required Form
a.
The SSA-5666, Request for Administrative Information, is the only form approved by
SSA and the OMB for requesting records from all educational sources, e.g., schools,
records administrators, counselors.
b.
Such records comprise copies of educational tests or comprehensive evaluations, including
Individualized Education Programs, triennial assessments, psychological or speech/language
testing, teacher/therapist progress notes, and all such records that provide information
about an individual’s functioning.
2. Provide DDS Identification Information
a.
DDS name
b.
DDS address
3. Attach Label or Type Claimant’s Identification Information
a.
Name
b.
SSN
