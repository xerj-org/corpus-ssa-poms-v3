D. Attorney fees
Attorney fees of 25 percent allowed.
If a worker prevails on appeal, in addition to the 25 percent, the maximum fee on
appeals to the full commission from a decision of the administration law judge is
$500.00. The maximum fee on appeals to the Court of Appeals or Supreme Court from
a decision of the commission is $1,000.00.
