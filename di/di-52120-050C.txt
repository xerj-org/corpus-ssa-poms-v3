C. Attorney fees
Attorney fees are approved on a case-by-case basis but are limited by law to 20 percent
of the actual benefit secured through the efforts of the attorney [Statute: 32-1530
(f)]. Upon approval of a fee, it becomes a lien against the WC awarded.
