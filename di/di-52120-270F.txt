F. Time limitations for filing claims
1. Initial Claims
All injury claims must be filed within six months from the date of injury, or the
claim may be barred. Claims for WC based on an occupational disease must be filed
within 3 years.
2. Claims Reopened for PT WC
Claims for PT must generally be filed within 5 years of the date of prior closure
of the claim.
