SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.001 - CDR Considerations for Individuals Convicted of a Felony
Effective Dates: 12/11/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. CDR required
A CDR is necessary if any of the following conditions apply:
•
A medical (MIE) diary matures.
•
The case is selected for MIP, MINE or sample review.
•
An event raises a disability issue even though the individual is not in pay status
because of confinement.
