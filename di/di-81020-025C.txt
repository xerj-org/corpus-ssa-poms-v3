C. Appeals of less than fully favorable (LTFF) initial determinations
An LTFF determination appeal occurs when the claimant files an appeal based on one
or more of the following:
•
Closed period (CP),
•
Adverse established onset date, or
•
Medical diagnosis.
If the DDS receives a reconsideration claim of an LTFF determination, follow current
procedures for processing reconsideration cases. If an appeal is filed while a medically
reactivated claim is pending in the DDS, follow procedures in DI 81020.117 Processing Medical Reactivations.
