SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 55075.020 - Disputes Involving Contract Issues Between SSA and ENs or SSA and the TPM
Effective Dates: 02/18/2022 - Present | TN 1 (02-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0455075020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy – contract language covers dispute resolution
ENs operate under agreements with SSA that contain dispute resolution processes. Similarly,
the TPM operates under a contract with SSA which contains dispute resolution procedures.
SSA will follow these procedures to resolve a dispute that arises between the agency
and an EN or the TPM.
