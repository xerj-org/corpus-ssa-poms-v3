C. Procedure – handling beneficiary/recipient dispute-related questions
If a beneficiary/recipient who has assigned a ticket to a State VR agency contacts
a field component to complain about the services the State VR agency is providing,
advise the individual to contact their State VR agency or a CAP specialist in their
State's P&A system.
