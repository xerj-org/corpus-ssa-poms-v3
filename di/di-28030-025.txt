DI 28030.025 Simultaneous Development in Work Activity Situations
If the disability determination services (DDS) discovers new work activity during
development of a case, and this activity is not likely to result in a cessation but
the work issue needs clarification, the DDS should refer the work issue to the field
office for resolution, but retain the folder and continue development. For information
on assistance requests, see DI 28030.015B.
