Effective Dates: 03/14/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure – integrity issues
1.
Check medical source reimbursements on a random basis to determine whether there is
corresponding and appropriate evidence in the claims file and an indication of its
receipt in the DDS.
2.
Verify reimbursement was the lower of the amount billed by the source or the maximum
allowed under the fee schedule.
3.
Investigate any incidence of missing evidence and take action to review the basis
for the decision.
