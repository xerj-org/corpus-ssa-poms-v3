I. Additional requirements for intellectual disability
The CE report must describe the following:
1.
Current documentation of IQ score by a standardized test of general intelligence.
Acceptable instruments (for example, the Wechsler scales) are those that have a representative
normative sample, a mean of approximately 100 and standard deviation of approximately
15 in the general population, and cover a broad range of cognitive and perceptual-motor
functions;
2.
Summary of composite scores (for example, Full Scale IQ, Verbal Comprehension Index,
Perceptual Reasoning Index) together with the individual subtest scores;
3.
Interpretation of the scores and assessment of the validity of the obtained scores,
indicating any factors that may have influenced the results, such as the child's attitude
and degree of cooperation, the presence of visual, hearing, or other physical problems,
and recent prior exposure to the same or similar test; and
4.
Consistency of the obtained test results with the child’s education and social adjustment,
especially in the area of personal self-sufficiency.
