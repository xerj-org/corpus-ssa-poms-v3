SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.020 - Guidelines for Reviewing and Obtaining Corrected Consultative Examination Reports
Effective Dates: 01/06/2025 - Present | TN 52 (01-25)

B. Procedure for processing cases with unsigned or improperly signed CE reports as evidence
There may be situations when the DDS receives a CE report that is unsigned or improperly
signed by the CE source but the content of the CE report, along with the other evidence
in file, is sufficient for a determination. Follow the procedures below to process
these types of cases. These procedures apply to both written and electronic signatures.
For more information on signature requirements for CE reports see DI 22510.015C.
1. Unsigned or improperly signed CE report and the determination is not fully favorable
If the CE report is unsigned or improperly signed and the determination is not fully
favorable:
a.
Do not adjudicate or close the case. The DDS must wait until they receive a properly
signed CE report.
b.
Document the attempts to obtain a properly signed report in a Case Note in DCPS and
"write to" the CDW.
c.
If a properly signed report cannot be obtained from the CE source who originally performed
the CE for any reason, the DDS must reschedule the CE with another qualified CE source
(see DI 22510.010).
d.
The DDS includes the unsigned or improperly signed CE report in the disability folder
(see DI 81020.070C).
2. Unsigned or improperly signed CE report and the determination is fully favorable
If the CE report is unsigned or improperly signed and the determination is fully favorable:
a.
Document the attempts to obtain a properly signed CE report in a Case Note in DCPS
and "write to" the CDW.
b.
Adjudicate and close the case.
c.
The DDS includes the properly signed CE report, obtained post adjudication, in Section
F (Medical Records (Yellow Back)) of the disability folder.
NOTE: In the rare scenario when the DDS cannot obtain a properly signed CE report after
the case has been adjudicated (e.g., the CE source is deceased or incarcerated, fraud
or similar fault is involved, etc.), contact the Regional Office for instructions.
