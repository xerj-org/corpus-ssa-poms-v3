SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25501.470 - Determining Onset for the Established Onset Date (EOD) When the Claimant has an Exacerbating and Remitting Condition
Effective Dates: 11/19/2012 - Present | TN 11 (11-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425501470 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Component roles for determining onset when a claimant has an exacerbating and remitting
condition
1. Field office (FO)
The field office does not have a role in administering this policy.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
When determining onset in cases where there is an exacerbating and remitting impairment,
the adjudicator considers:
•
the history of the disease process, including whether the condition has developed
a specific course, and
•
the length of the exacerbations and remissions and the severity of the symptoms and
signs, which occur in both phases that could affect onset.
