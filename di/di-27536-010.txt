DI 27536.010 Required Information for Reopening Continuing Disability Review Determinations
There is no need to duplicate information documented elsewhere in the file. Use the
tables below to locate the information required to reopen continuing disability review
(CDR) determinations; and, what actions are necessary if the information is not already
contained in the file.
Required information for reopening a CDR determination includes:
•
Assessment of functional abilities
•
Basis for determination
•
Basis for the most recent prior favorable medical determination, i.e., comparison
point decision (CPD)
•
Beneficiary's reason for continued entitlement or cessation
•
Citation of jobs and incidence
•
Date of CPD
•
Discussion of comparison of medical findings under medical improvement review standards
(MIRS)
•
Evidence sources
•
Medical facts
•
Pain-related issues
•
Reason for current review
•
Resolution of conflicting medical opinions
•
Resolution of inconsistencies material to the determination
•
Statement of nonmedical issues
•
Vocational rule used for medical-vocational determinations
