A. Alerts Field
The Alerts field contains a list of system generated electronic notifications created
when a field office (FO) completes an Update-After-Transfer (UAT) action after the
case transfers jurisdiction from the FO. For instance, when a claim specialist uses
the UAT utility, Electronic Disability Collect system (EDCS) generates an alert to
the office with jurisdiction. For details on the FO UAT utility, see DI 81010.095. For details on Alerts, see DI 81020.080.
