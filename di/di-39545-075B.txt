SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.075 - Management of the Consultative Examination (CE) Process
Effective Dates: 03/14/2014 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
DDSs at a minimum must provide procedures for:
•
Monitoring referrals and purchasing of CEs to ensure only required examinations and
tests are purchased;
•
Performing medical license verifications to ensure only qualified providers perform
CEs for disability determination services;
•
Ensuring close attention is given to specific evaluation issues involved in each case;
•
CE scheduling intervals;
•
Ensuring timely completion and submission of CE reports;
•
Handling claimant complaints;
•
Reviewing CE reports;
•
Conducting annual onsite reviews of key providers;
•
Conducting ongoing recruitment, orientation and training for CE providers.
