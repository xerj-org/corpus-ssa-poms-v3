A. Policy on documenting capability
When developing capability, DDS will document the folder to indicate whether capability
is resolved or unresolved.
To find capability resolved, the DDS must use evidence from an acceptable medical
source. When capability is unresolved, the DDS provide a capability opinion using
information from third-party sources to support the opinion. For additional information
on medical and other evidence of an individual’s impairment(s). See DI 22505.003.
