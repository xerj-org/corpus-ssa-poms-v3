H. Procedure - Disability cases routed directly to the PC
The FO has the primary responsibility for disability denial notices for DIB-RIB, DWB,
and CDB claims. This includes MCS EC automated notices and manual notices. However,
the PC must review the folder to determine if a copy of the manual notice is in the
folder.
If the FO did not send a notice, refer to item 29 of the SSA-831 to determine if the
PC should send one of the notices listed in DI
11010.345A, DI 11010.345B or DI 11010.345G (in this section).
