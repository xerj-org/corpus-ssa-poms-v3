SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28010.040 - Evaluating Listings with Age-Based Periods of Disability at the Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 17 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428010040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition of “listings with an age-based period of disability”
A listing with an age-based period of disability is a listing that indicates an age
as a time period for which the impairment meets the listing. Listings that designate
an age-based period of disability will have varied lengths of the diary based on the
child’s age at the time of adjudication and the age criteria designated in the listing.
We refer to the diary criteria in these listings as “listings with an age-based period
of disability.”
Some listings designate evaluation criteria for a specific age within the listing
criteria, for example, listing 100.04. Listings specifying age as part of the evaluation
criteria for the listing itself are not discussed in this section, as these listings
do not specify an age as a time period for which the impairment should be considered
disabling.
Listings that have an age-based period of disability will designate specific criteria:
•
Indicates a certain age as a time period for which the impairment meets the listing
and includes a subsection once the child has obtained that age (i.e., Listing 102.11),
or
•
Indicates an age that an impairment should be disabling until (i.e., Listing 105.10,
which indicates we consider a child that requires supplemental daily enteral feeding
via a gastrostomy, duodenostomy, or jejunostomy under a disability until they have
attained age 3).
