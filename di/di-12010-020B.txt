B. Procedure for remand to the disability determination services (DDS) or processing
center (PC)
1.
Forward the case to the DDS or PC that prepared the previous unfavorable determination.
2.
Notify the Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) by sending a photocopy of the HA 501-U5
(Request for Hearing), annotated per DI 12010.015C.1.a.
