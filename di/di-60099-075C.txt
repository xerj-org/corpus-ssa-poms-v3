C. FO procedure to process BOND reconsideration requests
The BOND work CDR unit (L5R) OFFICE L5R NO
LONGER OPERATING is the only office with jurisdiction to make a decision on BOND overpayment reconsideration
requests.
Forward all requests for reconsideration to L5R OFFICE L5R NO
LONGER OPERATING by:
1.
keying the reconsideration request in DMS;
2.
faxing the SSA-561-U2 and any accompanying evidence into the Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF) or Non-Disability Repository for Evidentiary Document (NDRED) as appropriate;
3.
sending an MDW to L5R OFFICE L5R NO
LONGER OPERATING notifying them of the pending reconsideration request.
For additional information about reconsideration requests, see DI 60099.075A.1.b. in this section.
