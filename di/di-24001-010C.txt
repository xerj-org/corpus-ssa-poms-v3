SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24001.010 - Effect of SGA on Disability Status
Effective Dates: 03/20/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Duration
When a beneficiary returns to work at the SGA level less than one year after onset,
a question is raised as to whether the 12 month duration requirement for disability
is met, (see DI 25505.025) and whether the disability should be ceased immediately or continued subject to
a trial work period, or whether the prior favorable determination should be reopened
and revised to a denial or a later onset date. For a discussion of “Return to Work” issues, refer to DI 13010.105 and DI 13010.110.
