SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.305 - Routing a Non-disability Claim after Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB) Claim Forwarded for Determination
Effective Dates: 11/10/2008 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disability determination not received by the FO
If the FO has not received the disability file or notice of disability determination
when the number holder’s (NHs) non-disability claim is completed and the NH is alive,
forward the Retirement Survivors Insurance (RSI) file to the PC. If the DIB or DIB-Retirement
Insurance Benefits (RIB) claimant dies, follow Sending Initial Disability Insurance
Benefits (DIB), Freeze Claims to the Disability Determination Services (DDS) - DI 11010.255. For claims entered into the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) see DI 81010.020.
