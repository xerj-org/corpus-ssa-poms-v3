SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.075 - Work Deductions and Offset of Auxiliary Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/23/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. DMAX involved
1. Benefits payable 07/1980 — 12/1986
Where a DMAX was involved, the law stated SSA should impose work deductions before
applying WC/PDB. (See GN 02603.040.)
2. Benefits payable 01/1987 – and later
If a DMAX is involved, compute WC/PDB offset before applying auxiliary deductions.
(NOTE: In order to prevent delays in processing, SSA revised the above policy.) It
is not necessary to obtain work deduction information before applying offset as of
01/01/1987.
