B. Cost-Of-Living adjustments (COLA)
A COLA is only provided for recipients of PT benefits. To qualify, the individual’s
case must have been closed by a decision. Beginning in the second year after the award
was issued, the recipient will receive an amount from the Commission’s Rate Adjustment
Fund (RAF) that reflects the increase in the State-wide average weekly wage during
the preceding year. These payments are made monthly and are payments made separately
and in addition to the regular PT benefit. The monthly payments are adjusted July
15th of each year. These payments should be included when calculating offset.
