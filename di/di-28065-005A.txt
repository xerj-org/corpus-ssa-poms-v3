SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.005 - Identifying Prisoner/Felon Cases
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DO Identification
The district office (DO) will identify cases that may be affected by these provisions
when, in the course of their development, they are able to determine that the claimant
has been charged with or convicted of a felony or has an impairment connected with
confinement. They will prepare a Report of Contact containing information regarding
the status of any criminal case (e.g., trial pending, conviction obtained, etc.).
