A. Time frame for issuing the RPC resolution
RPCs goal is to issue a resolution as quickly as possible upon receipt of the Disability
Determination Services' (DDSs) RPC submission. A variety of factors may affect this
goal, including current caseloads, available staff, the size of the RPC case file,
and whether there is a need for additional medical, vocational, or policy input.
