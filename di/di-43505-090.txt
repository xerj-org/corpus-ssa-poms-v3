DI 43505.090 Reconsideration Cases
INTPSC reconsideration examiners will review timely filed requests for reconsideration,
including requests filed on initial or continuing claims. Jurisdictional guidelines
for reconsideration cases are the same as those for initial processing. (See DI 43505.015 through DI
43505.075.)
