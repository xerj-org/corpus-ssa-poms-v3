D. Regulation basis code
Any time the claimant has not cooperated with providing evidence, taking an action,
or attending a CE and that evidence has the potential to change the determination,
use the “Insufficient Evidence Furnished” regulation basis code in accordance with
DI 26510.045. Do not use the failure or refusal to attend a CE regulation basis code.
Use the “Insufficient Evidence Furnished” Personalized Disability Explanation (PDE)
language, do not use the Failure or Refusal to Attend a Consultative Examination PDE.
For instructions on the PDE, see DI 26530.020D.
