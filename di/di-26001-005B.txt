B. Title XVI evidence of statutory blindness
For Title XVI claims:
1.
We need an eye examination by a physician (medical or osteopathic doctor) skilled
in diseases of the eye or by an optometrist.
2.
We do not need documentation of the cause of the blindness.
3.
For additional information about evidence needed to evaluate visual disorders and
statutory blindness under Title XVI, see “How do we evaluate visual disorders?” in
DI 34001.012A. and DI 34005.102A.
