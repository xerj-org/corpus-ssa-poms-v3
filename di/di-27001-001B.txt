B. Claimant's rights during the reconsideration process
The reconsideration process provides the claimant:
•
An opportunity to submit additional evidence; or make new allegations;
•
A de novo (i.e., new) review of the evidence considered in making the initial medical
determination and any other evidence submitted or obtained;
•
A reconsidered determination based on all evidence of record.
