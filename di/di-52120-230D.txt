D. Retirement insurance benefit (RIB) considerations
For injuries occurring on or after 07/01/ 1993, PT benefits are limited to the difference
between 150 percent of PT weekly benefits and amounts received for SSA RIB. However,
the difference cannot exceed the PT weekly benefit. This reduction does not apply
to a worker who is entitled to SSA RIB at the time of the injury.
For example: Figuring PT when a worker receives SSA RIB. Assume the worker receives
WC checks weekly.
X = unadjusted PT weekly check
Y = SSA RIB monthly benefit
(1.5X) x (52/12) Y = adjusted PT check
