SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34124.011 - Cardiovascular Listings from 05/24/02 to 04/12/06
Effective Dates: 05/19/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434124011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Cardiovascular impairment results from one or more of four consequences of heart disease:
1. Chronic heart failure or ventricular dysfunction.
2. Discomfort or pain due to myocardial ischemia, with or without necrosis of heart
muscle.
3. Syncope, or near syncope, due to inadequate cerebral perfusion from any cardiac
cause such as obstruction of flow or disturbance in rhythm or conduction resulting
in inadequate cardiac output.
4. Central cyanosis due to right-to-left shunt, arterial desaturation, or pulmonary
vascular disease.
Impairment from diseases of arteries and veins may result from disorders of the vasculature
in the central nervous system (11.04A, B), eyes (2.02-2.04), kidney (6.02), and other
organs.
