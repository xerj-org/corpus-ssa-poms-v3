DI 81010.230 Creating an EDCS Record for an Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR)
EDCS includes screens to collect and display information for processing electronic
CDRs (eCDRs). The EDCS files created are similar to the abbreviated records currently
created for cases excluded from the Modernized Claim System (MCS). Data is propagated
from the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) and Supplemental Security Record (SSR) to
the eCDR in EDCS.
