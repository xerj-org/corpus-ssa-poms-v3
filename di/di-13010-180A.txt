A. Procedure - Continuances
Show the rationale for a continuance in the SSA 4268. For example: work activity after
the trial work period (TWP) did not represent substantial gainful activity (SGA);
or although work was at a SGA level, it ended within 3 months of the end of the TWP
due to the impairment.
