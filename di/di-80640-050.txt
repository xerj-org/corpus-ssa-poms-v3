DI 80640.050 Routing Evidence
NOTE: As of January 2004 the PSCs, FOs, ODAR and OQA have the ability to create a bar
code for documents. However,
at this time you should not create bar
codes. You should:
•
Forward any medical evidence to the appropriate DDS if the claim is pending in DDS,
OQA, or if the claim has been adjudicated.
•
Forward any medical evidence to the appropriate ODAR office if the claim is pending
there.
•
Forward any non-medical evidence for placement in the MDF, since the paper folder
is still the official folder.
