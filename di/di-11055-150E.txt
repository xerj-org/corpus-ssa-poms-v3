E. Identifying delayed cases
The field office (FO) considers a case delayed when any initial disability claim is
still pending in the FO 70 days after the filing of the application.
To alert the Disability Determination Services (DDS) of a delayed case:
•
Add the Aged Flag in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) prior to transferring
the case; and
•
Include an explanation in the space provided.
When a delayed claim is excluded from electronic processing, attach Form SSA-408 (Route
Slip) to the upper edge of the front face of the paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF).
Annotate on Form SSA-408 “Expedite – Delayed Case” (see OS 15020.035).
