D. Reminders for choosing an impairment code for digestive disorders
•
Do not use 5799 if the person is over age three at the time of adjudication.
•
Do not use 4750 for favorable determinations more than one year after the date of the transplant.
Use the code for the residual impairment that is the basis for the allowance.
