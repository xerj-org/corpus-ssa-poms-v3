C. First and second tier return cases
Processing time usually continues to accrue on returned cases; therefore, DDS's should
process these cases expeditiously. The DDS should give priority processing status
to cases returned by the DDS Quality Assurance Unit, and those returned by the regional
Disability Analysis Branch or the Office of Disability Operations on a decisional
or documentational deficiency basis.
