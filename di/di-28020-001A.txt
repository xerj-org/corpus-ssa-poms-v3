A. Intent of Group I and Group II exceptions to MI
The exceptions to MI permit finding that disability ceased in limited situations where,
even though there has been no MI or MI is not related to the ability to work, evidence
shows that the person should no longer be considered disabled or never should have
been considered disabled.
