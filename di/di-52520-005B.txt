B. Title XVI pre-2007 Martinez cases that require a DDS disability determination
The previously ineligible fugitive felon recipient may file a new disability application
with a protective filing date of April 1, 2009. These claims require a disability
determination from the DDS. These claims may have one of the following case characteristics:
•
No subsequent claim filed after 4/2009; or
•
Subsequent claim filed after 4/2009 with a disability determination or decision; or
•
Subsequent technically denied claim filed after 4/2009; or
•
Title XVI claim with a title II disability denial determination or decision for the
same period or later; or
•
Title XVI claim with a title II disability allowance determination or decision, with
an established onset date later than the title XVI application date of 4/1/2009.
NOTE: The listed case characteristics are the most common. However, the FO may refer other
types of claims to the DDS, as appropriate.
