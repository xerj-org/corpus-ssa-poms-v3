C. Case routing
When you obtain all necessary evidence and make a medical determination, complete
the:
•
processing,
•
disposition, and
•
route the case (For routing instructions see DI 32005.025).
Ensure that the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Indicator is correct and that you
forward any paper folders associated with the CEF to the next location.
NOTE: If the Office of Quality Review (OQR) selects this case for Federal quality review
and the electronic file also has a paper folder, the DDS must mail the folder to the
reviewing office.
