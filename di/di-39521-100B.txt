SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39521.100 - Training and Staff Development - General
Effective Dates: 03/21/2002 - Present | TN 19 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439521100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. State responsibilities
•
The DDS administrator has primary responsibility for managing the DDS training and
staff development process.
•
The DDS administrator has maximum management flexibility to plan such a program and
apply it in a manner that most efficiently and effectively meets the needs of the
agency.
•
The State will ensure that all employees have an acceptable level of competence.
•
The DDS will request SSA provided training materials with the description and quantity
of material required. (For instructions on requesting SSA material and supplies, see
DI 39539.001.)
2. SSA responsibilities
a. Administration
SSA will foster a program of training and staff-development designed to provide maximum
proficiency in performing the varied duties of the organization.
SSA will ensure uniformity and effectiveness in the administration of the disability
program by providing training assistance to facilitate basic and advanced technical
proficiency of disability staff.
b. Training
SSA will provide training and other instructional materials to facilitate basic and
advanced technical proficiency of disability staff. If a DDS fails to meet minimum
standards of performance, SSA will specify and provide training where appropriate
to improve performance.
Where SSA concludes the training materials are complex or that uniform training is
needed for national program policy purposes, SSA will discuss the initiative with
DDS representatives prior to implementation.
