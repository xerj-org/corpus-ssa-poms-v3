SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26530.020 - Personalized Disability Explanation in Initial Denials
Effective Dates: 01/12/2022 - Present | TN 8 (01-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426530020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Policy -- Special situations
1. Insufficient evidence and does not wish to continue development of claim
The DDS will prepare the personalized disability explanation based on the instructions
in DI 26530.020B. (items l-5 and 9). The explanation should also include:
a.
A brief description of the evidence needed to adjudicate the claim; and
b.
A brief description of the attempts to obtain evidence and the response received (if
any).
2. Failure to follow prescribed treatment
The DDS will prepare the personalized disability explanation based on the instructions
in DI 26530.020B. (items l-5 and 9). The explanation should also include:
a.
A description of vocational limitations (e.g., age, education) if vocational factors
are considered; and
b.
A description of the prescribed treatment the claimant refuses to follow and what
the treatment will allegedly accomplish.
3. Special handling
When the DDS prepares the personalized disability explanation in special handling
cases, it should be prepared based on the instructions in DI 26530.020B. It is clipped inside the folder for release with the appropriate notice.
