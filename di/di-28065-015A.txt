SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28065.015 - Disability Determinations - Individual Convicted But Not Confined
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428065015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
A CDR will be conducted in the customary manner taking into account information developed
by the DO on any newly discovered felony case. Evidence will be secured and evaluated
in accordance with DI 28005.001. Prepare a determination of continuance or cessation, as appropriate.
