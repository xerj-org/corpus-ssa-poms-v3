E. Time limitations for filing claims
A worker has 6 months from the date of injury to file a WC claim. A worker may only
pursue a claim after the 6 month limit if they can show that the employer/carrier
had knowledge of the accident or was not prejudiced by the delay. In no event may
proceedings be started more than 6 years from the date of injury.
