A. Related procedure
Budget procedures for performance management are included in the subchapter on DDS
Budget Procedures, DI 39503.001).
Disability Adjudication (DA) leadership, the Disability Program Administrator and
the budget specialist are available to assist the DDS with performance management
budget considerations.
