F. Commutation of periodic WC payments
1.
A disabled worker, dependents or employer may apply to have periodic benefit payments
commuted to one or more lump sum payments. Such payments, if they fully discharge
the employer’s WC liability, meet SSA’s definition of a lump sum for proration purposes.
2.
The portion of the periodic benefit payments commuted to one or more lump sum payments
are computed at 4 percent true discount compounded annually. This mandatory 4 percent
lump sum discount is not offsettable per DI 52150.060B. - LS amounts which are not offset.
