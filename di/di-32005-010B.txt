B. Destination Field Office (FO)
Enter FO on the To line when the routing chart indicates the FO as the case destination.
Do not enter the FO address unless the FO that sent the case to the DDS requested
that you forward the case to another FO because the claimant moved. In this situation,
enter the address of the new FO.
In initial cases, check the box Associate with material and strike out the words previously
transmitted on (date) .
