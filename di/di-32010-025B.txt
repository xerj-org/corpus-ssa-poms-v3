B. Routing CDR cessation determinations
The Disability Determination Services (DDS) may receive cases where eligibility has
previously terminated for a non-disability reason, for example, a termination could
occur following a suspension period of 12 consecutive calendar months. Further, State
conversion reentitlement cases are processed by the DDS.
•
If the cessation determination is a paper CDR folder, include a remark on an SSA-408 (Route Slip), indicating the type of case and a remark stating what cessation or
termination action was taken or may be necessary.
•
If the cessation determination is an electronic folder or needs to be converted to
an electronic folder, please see DI 81010.020 Creating Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Cases.
