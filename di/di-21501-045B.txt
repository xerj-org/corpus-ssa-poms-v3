B. Permanent and total disability — permanent and total disability defined (119-6-3)
Permanent and total disability shall mean a permanent physical or mental impairment
which is disabling, and which, because of other factors such as age, training, experience,
and social setting, substantially precludes a person from engaging in useful occupations,
as a homemaker or as a wage earner in employment which exists in the community, and for which occupations the person has competence.
