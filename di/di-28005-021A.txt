SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28005.021 - Title XVI: Determining Continuing Disability at Step 2 and 3 of the Medical Improvement Review Standard Sequential Evaluation Process for Children Under Age 18 - Functional Equivalence
Effective Dates: 03/20/2026 - Present | TN 26 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428005021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
On March 23, 2026, SSA published SSR
26-1p, “Title XVI: Determining Continuing Disability at Step 2 and 3 of the Medical Improvement
Review Standard Sequential Evaluation Process for Children Under Age 18 – Functional
Equivalence.”
This SSR provides the process for determining continuing disability at steps 2 and
3 of the medical improvement review standard (MIRS) for children under age 18 who
receive Supplemental Security Income (SSI) under Title XVI of the Social Security
Act and how to apply the functional equivalence rule at steps 2 and 3.
