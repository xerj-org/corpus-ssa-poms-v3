DI 22510.055 Pediatric Consultative Examination (CE) Report Content Guidelines for Speech and Language
Impairments in Children from Birth to Attainment of Age 3
Use the following guidelines to provide the minimum content in a CE report for a child
with a speech or language impairment. Each Disability Determination Service (DDS)
will notify medical sources of any additional requirements.
