E. Adding an Escalated Claim to a Case
Use the UAT utility to add an escalated claim to a case if there is at least one claim
at the reconsideration or hearing level, and a new claim needs to be escalated to
that same level.
To add an escalated claim:
•
Open the case in EDCS;
•
Select Transfer/Updates on the toolbar at the top of the EDCS screen path;
•
Select the Transfer Claims tab;
•
Select the claim pending at the field office; and
•
Click the OK button to send the escalated claim to DDS or OHO.
NOTE: Never route an “escalated” claim to OQR, even if there is a pending claim in OQR.
