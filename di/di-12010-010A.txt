A. Policy for assembly and routing folders for an ALJ hearing
The field office assembles all required forms and evidence in the proper folder order
and transmits the claim folder(s) on a priority basis to the hearing office (HO).
For information on transferring electronic cases see, Transferring Cases in Electronic
Disability Collect System (EDCS), DI 81010.085.
