E. Follow-up questions
Although the RPC resolution is final, components may submit questions to RPC for further
clarification of an RPC resolution. Components should send follow-up questions to
RPC (include the RPC case number in the subject line) using the mailbox ^LP DP RPC
Controls
RPC will respond to the inquirer via email, and cc Disability Adjudication (DA), DQR
and DC. RPC will also attach the question and response as an additional document to
the case in the RPC Tool. In rare situations, a follow-up question indicates program
instructions do not support the resolution. If this occurs, RPC will refer the case
to the appropriate DP policy component for guidance. If the policy component determines
that program instructions do not support the resolution, RPC will respond to the question,
correct the RPC resolution in its response to the question and, if necessary, advise
the DQR to reinstate or rescind the deficiency citation.
NOTE: RPC must receive follow-up questions within 7 business days of the date of the RPC
resolution.
