SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39527.050 - DDS Office Space Allocation Guide
Effective Dates: 07/08/2026 - Present | TN 39 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Definitions
1. Nonusable space
Nonusable space is space not conducive to a working environment. It includes but is
not limited to:
•
restrooms,
•
stairways,
•
elevator shafts,
•
heating and air conditioning space, and
•
interoffice space (e.g., hallways and building corridors).
Nonusable space must be sufficient to provide unobstructed access.
2. Net office space
Net office space consists of the gross space available minus the nonusable space.
3. Administrative support space
Administrative support space is space used for:
•
storage of forms, supplies, publications, etc.,
•
multipurpose areas, and
•
dedicated scanners and computers.
4. Multipurpose areas
Multipurpose areas have various uses. These include but are not limited to space for:
•
specialized equipment including scanners and computer servers,
•
filing cabinets, confidential shredding bins, and shredding machines,
•
mail activities,
•
employee lunch and break periods,
•
computer training room,
•
a reception area,
•
an interviewing area, and
•
video service delivery equipment.
5. Intra office movement space
Intra office movement space is the amount of space in excess of the basic DDS space
requirements.
6. Employee
The term, employee, means all personnel working in the DDS, including full-time and
part-time personnel, contracted personnel, and any other personnel permanently assigned
to the DDS (e.g., personnel working in the DDS but paid through indirect costs).
