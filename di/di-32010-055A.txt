A. Procedure for transfers of MQGE cases
Individuals who qualify as MQGE are subject to the same conditions and reviews that
apply to regular Title II disabled individuals (e.g., extended period of eligibility
(EPE), medical and trial work period (TWP).
•
If the folder is electronic, insert: 'Attn: Disability Examiner. See Form SSA-833
in file' on the Alerts and Messages screen.
•
If folder is paper, attach an SSA-408 (Route slip) annotated with 'Attn: Disability Examiner. See Form SSA-833 in file.'
•
Include messages to explain what action is necessary, if any, for both the paper and
electronic folder.
