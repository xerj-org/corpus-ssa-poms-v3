DI 52120.265 Washington (WA) Workers Compensation (WC)
CITATIONS:
SSR 94-6 , Workers Compensation OffsetExcluding Legal Expenses Incurred in Connection with
Initial Award of Workers Compensation BenefitsWashington
Washington State reduces its WC benefits if the disabled worker also receives Social
Security disability insurance benefits (DIB). Since WC benefits are reduced (offset),
rather than DIB, this is referred to as reverse offset or reverse jurisdiction (RJ).
