SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12005.015 - Late Filing of a Request for Reconsideration
Effective Dates: 10/02/2009 - Present | TN 10 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412005015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. If the claimant wishes to re-file
If it is determined that the claimant did not have good cause for late filing and
he or she expresses intent to file again:
1.
Take a new application.
2.
Develop and process the new application as an initial claim.
3.
If the prior claim folder(s) is an MDF and is not in the FO, secure and associate
the prior claim folder(s) before sending the new claim to the DDS.
4.
Create a message in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) or eView stating
Late-filed reconsideration, good cause not met. New application for processing and
reopening consideration. If the official folder is paper, annotate the SSA-3367 Disability
Report Field Office, as above.
5.
Transfer the claim to the DDS for an initial determination and reopening consideration.
