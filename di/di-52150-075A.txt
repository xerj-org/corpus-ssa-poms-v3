SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52150.075 - Work Deductions and Offset of Auxiliary Benefits
Effective Dates: 03/23/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452150075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DMAX not involved
If a DMAX is not involved, compute workers’ compensation/public disability benefit (WC/PDB) offset
before applying auxiliary work deductions.
NOTE: The WC offset amount is taxable for the worker and reported on the SSA-1099 even
if full work deductions apply to the auxiliary benefits.
