F. Procedure - case evaluation
To set a timely MIE diary date, consider:
•
age at MIE diary maturity
•
clinical findings
•
past response to therapy
•
type of treatment
•
duration
•
expected clinical course of the impairment
•
vocational factors at diary maturity.
