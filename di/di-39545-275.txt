DI 39545.275 Missed Consultative Examination (CE) Appointments
Policy
SSA has a no-pay policy for missed CE appointments.
•
DDSs may pay a nominal fee to compensate the consultative examiner who reviewed background
medical records prior to the missed CE.
•
On a case-by-case basis, DDSs may request an exemption to the no-pay policy (e.g.,
another agency within the State allows a payment for missed CEs, inability to recruit
or retain a certain type of provider, etc.). To obtain an exemption, the DDS should
work with Disability Adjudication (DA) to document the situation.
•
DDSs will forward the request, along with supporting documentation, to DA for approval.
