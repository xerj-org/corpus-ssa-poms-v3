Effective Dates: 04/15/2013 - Present | TN 2 (07-26)

F. Responding to an inquiry when there is no record of a paper claim
If you cannot find a record of a paper claim after a thorough search inform the inquirer
you are sending the inquiry to the FO for reply.
If you cannot determine the servicing FO:
•
complete an SSA-408, annotated with the remark: Please respond to inquiry, no record
of a claim in our office, and
•
attach the SSA-408 to the inquiry send the inquiry and a copy of the acknowledgement
to the parallel FO (in the locality of the DDS) for forwarding to the proper FO.
