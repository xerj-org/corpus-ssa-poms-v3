SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10105.905 - Taking and Processing Auxiliary Applications Filed After the Benefit Continuation Election (BCE)
Effective Dates: 06/15/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410105905 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Field Office (FO) role
The FO will:
1.
Develop the auxiliary claim in the normal manner.
2.
Prepare an A101 Automated Determination of Award , using data from the Master Beneficiary
Record (MBR). Enter a remark on the A101-“Child (spouse, mother, father) claim-Hold
pending disability decision.”
3.
Advise the applicant in writing that the decision as to whether benefits are payable
will be made only after a final decision is made on the number holder’s (NH's) disability.
4.
Adjudicate the A101. Modernized Claims System (MCS) will establish a control and transfer
ownership to the PC (See DI
10105.905B. for exception).
