DI 52155.030 EF101 Processing of Claims with Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
A101 processing limitations may require using the Electronic Form 101 (EF101) to process
claims. The EF101 is also used to process amended awards which could involve WC/PDB
offset.
