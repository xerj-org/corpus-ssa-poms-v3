C. No formal reopening
If the claimant is aware of additional development and new evidence does not result
in a formal reopening:
•
Prepare a notice to advise the claimant according to the instructions in NL 00705.256—Reopening 12 – No Change in Basis of End Result- Claimant Aware or Denial of Request
to Reopen and Revise – Title II or Title XVI; and
•
Place the notice in the file for release by the review component.
