SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.360 - Handling Trailer Material for Paper Folders
Effective Dates: 06/22/2012 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010360 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure - benefit payments affected
For Certified Electronic Folders (CEFs) follow the procedures in Processing Trailer
Material for the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) - DI 81020.140. The following instructions apply when the official folder is paper. If subsequent
material, to be associated with the claim, requires immediate action and field office
(FO) direct input is not possible:
•
Fax the material directly to the Paperless Processing Center (PPC) making the benefit
payments, even if the disability case is temporarily in another office.
EXAMPLE: The case may be in the Office of Disability Operations (ODO) or the Disability Determination
Services (DDS), but a Processing Center (PC) is making the payments, or the case is
in the Disability Determination Services (DDS), but ODO is paying the benefits.
•
Examples are requests for suspension of benefits and statements of recent work activity.
For these cases the component receiving the information should handle the case. The
case is not sent to the PC.
•
If the case is in the DDS, fax the material to the PC and notify the DDS about the
notice of death or change of address.
•
When faxing material to the PC, indicate the DDS has been notified.
•
If the case is in ODO, and ODO is not the paying office, fax the material to the Program
Service Center (PSC) for immediate action and notify ODO of notice of death, change
of address.
