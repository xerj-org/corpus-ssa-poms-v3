H. Process after receiving policy guidance
Once the DP panel member receives the requested guidance, the panel member will share
it with the original panel (or a representative if an original panel member is unavailable).
DP, DA, and QR will decide whether the guidance results in a resolution or merits
reconvening the panel for discussion.
