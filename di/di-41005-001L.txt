L. Item 11 - reason for cessation and Item 12 - reason for continuance
In item 11, enter the appropriate code from the list in DI 41005.035, Exhibit 1. Codes from this list also apply to EPE cases where benefits are stopped
(suspended) during an EPE.
In item 12, enter appropriate code from the list in DI 41005.035, Exhibit 2. Codes from this list also apply to EPE cases where benefits are reinstated
during an EPE.
The medical listing number is completed only in DIB attainment cases when DIB is allowed or denied.
