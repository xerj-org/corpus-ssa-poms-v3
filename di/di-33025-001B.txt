B. Hearing procedures
Process the case per usual hearing procedures. Indicate in the notice attached to
the decision that we will notify the beneficiary or recipient separately if they are
eligible for Medicare. For a systematic approach on how to conduct disability hearings
see, DI 33001.000 Disability Hearing Procedures.
