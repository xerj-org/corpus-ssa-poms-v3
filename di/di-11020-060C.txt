C. Disability determination for CDB to enable a survivor to collect the Lump Sum Death
Payment (LSDP)
If a CDB disability determination is necessary under the 1981 Amendments to establish
eligibility to a disability benefit only so the survivor can collect the LSDP, forward
the case to the DDS of the State in which the CDB claimant resides unless otherwise
excluded by procedures in this subchapter.
