D. The Workload Summary screen
This screen lists RPCs that the adjudicating components have submitted to RPC, but
are not yet resolved.
Managers in QR, DA, and DCs are able to request access to the Workload Summary screen.
Access enables these units to review the pending RPC cases in eView and to see when
RPC panel discussions are scheduled.
