B. DDS identifies a contagious disease that the FO is unaware of
If after following the procedure outlined above, the DDS determines that a claimant
does have a contagious disease that requires an alert to the FO, take the following
steps:
1.
DDS staff must immediately notify DDS management.
2.
DDS management must immediately notify the regional office Center for Disability (CD).
3.
CD staff must immediately notify management in the FO that took the claim.
4.
FO management must immediately determine whether employee contact occurred with the
claimant and, when appropriate, ensure medical testing for FO staff. (See GN 00203.010 Conducting Interviewing under Difficult Situations.)
NOTE: Notify by telephone, email, fax, electronically through messages in eView or any
combination of these options.
