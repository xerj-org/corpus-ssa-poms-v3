DI 81010.137 Receiving Documents Submitted through iAppeals
Starting March 14, 2015, iAppeals users have the option to submit (upload) forms and
other evidence with their Internet appeal requests. In addition to reconsideration
and hearing requests, the i3441, and an e827, the iAppeals attachment utility will
allow Internet users (IU) to submit up to 10 documents to associate with the appeal.
