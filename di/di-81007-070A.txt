A. The Internet query facility (IQF)
The IQF is a set of queries available to field office personnel via the Shared Processes
menu on the SSA mainframe. Claims technicians use it to access information in the
Internet report database and to control Internet workloads.
This subchapter briefly describes the IQF. Full details are available in MSOM MS Internet
001-003.
