DI 43510.080 Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Development
When review of an alerted folder reveals that a CDR is necessary (see DI 40505.000 for events which raise a CDR issue and screening instructions), the FDE's development
actions are determined by the residence of the primary beneficiary.
