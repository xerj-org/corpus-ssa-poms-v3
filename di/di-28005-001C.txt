SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28005.001 - Legal Standard for Determining if Disability Continues
Effective Dates: 08/08/2022 - Present | TN 13 (11-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428005001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. When the MIRS applies
MIRS applies in the following cases:
•
All CDRs after October 8, 1984.
•
Any determination or decision with an adjudicative date on or after December 6, 1985,
apply MIRS to establish that a closed period of disability ended. See DI 25510.001C for additional information.
•
Reopenings to a cessation or denial with an adjudicative date of October 8, 1984,
or later, based on a revised determination or decision concerning the individual's
disability, except as noted in DI 28005.001F below.
