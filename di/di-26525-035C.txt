C. Procedure - systems coding
Complete the diary block of forms SSA-831-C3/U3 (item 17) and SSA-832-C3/U3 SSA-833-C3/U3
(item 23) as follows:
1. Diary Type
Enter “MRN”.
2. Diary Date
Enter a two-digit code for month, day, and year for 36 months in the future, counting
the month of adjudication. Use “01” for the day (e.g., if the month of this decision
is 3/02, diary for 02/01/05).
3. Diary Reason
Enter “3”.
