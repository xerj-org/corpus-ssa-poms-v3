SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 33010.005 - Scheduling, Postponing, Canceling, or Changing Place of the Disability Hearing
Effective Dates: 07/01/2026 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Handling requests for postponements and changes in place of the disability hearing
1. Changes requested by the beneficiary (or recipient)
a.
If the beneficiary (or recipient) is unable to travel or has some other reason why
they cannot attend the hearing at the scheduled time or place, they should request,
at the earliest possible date, that the time or place be changed.
b.
A beneficiary (or recipient) may request a change using form SSA-769 Request for Change
in Time/Place of Disability Hearing. The beneficiary (or recipient) may submit the
request directly to the DHU or to the FO, which will send it to the DHU. See DI 12095.055 for information about form SSA-769 Request for Change in Time/Place of Disability
Hearing.
c.
Only a DHO, supervisory hearing officer (SHO), or Disability Adjudication (DA) has
the authority to grant or deny a request for change in time or place of the hearing.
d.
The DHO, SHO, or DA will change the time or place if there is good cause for doing
so, taking into account any impact on the expeditious handling of the hearing.
e.
Consider contacting the beneficiary (or recipient) in some circumstances to further
explore the reason(s) for the request.
2. Beneficiary's (or recipient's) request denied
a.
Deny the request in writing, include a brief explanation of the decision, and send
a copy to the beneficiary (or recipient), appointed representative, and representative
payee, if any, and
b.
Prepare an SSA-5002 Report of Contact and annotate actions taken and a brief reason
for the denial.
3. Beneficiary's (or recipient's) request granted
a. If the request does not change the DHU:
1.
Send a new scheduling notice,
2.
Prepare an SSA-5002 Report of Contact and annotate actions taken and a brief reason
for the approval, and
3.
Update the case control system.
b. If the request changes the DHU:
1.
Update the case control system, and
2.
Transfer the case to the new DHU, if applicable. The new DHU will schedule the hearing.
See DI
33010.040.
4. Postponement requested by DDS or DHU
a.
In some situations, the DDS may request the DHU to postpone a hearing because it is
unable to complete its action on the case within its expected time frame.
b.
In some situations, a DHO may postpone a hearing (e.g., when the DHO returns a case
to the DDS for additional development).
c.
Postpone the scheduled hearing and reschedule it, if necessary.
5. Procedure to postpone or cancel the hearing
a.
Revise the hearing schedule.
b.
Notify the beneficiary (or recipient) and appointed representative of the postponement
or cancelation and any subsequently rescheduled hearing.
c.
Prepare the notice as above in DI
33010.005D.
d.
The 20-day advance notice applies to rescheduled hearings.
e.
See DI
33010.090 for notice language. See DI
33095.059 for the Spanish translation of that language.
