Effective Dates: 07/08/2026 - Present | TN 41 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439527025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples
1. Repairs and maintenance
Repairs and maintenance include, but are not limited to:
•
painting,
•
repairs to plaster,
•
patching roofs, and
•
minor repairs to doors, elevators, electrical equipment, etc.
2. Major repairs and replacements
Major repairs and replacements include, but are not limited to:
•
structural changes to buildings,
•
new roofs,
•
new heating systems, etc.
