A. Disability Interview - General Responsibilities
The disability interview process is based on the following general areas of responsibility.
1. Field Office (FO)
•
SSA-3368-BK (Disability Report - Adult), or SSA-3820-BK (Disability Report - Child);
•
SSA-3375-BK through SSA-3379-BK (Function Report - Child), as applicable by the claimant's
age;
•
SSA-3367 (Disability Report - Field Office); and
•
SSA-3369-BK (Work History Report), when the claimant had more than one job in the
five-year period before they stopped working due to their medical condition(s) and
the FO has reason to believe the claimant may be difficult to recontact (e.g., due
to homelessness).
NOTE: When requested by the claimant, the FO will mail a Disability Starter Kit with an
appointment letter.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
•
SSA-3369-BK (Work History Report);
•
SSA-3373-BK (Function Report-Adult);
•
SSA-3380-BK (Function Report-Adult-Third Party).
When the FO has curtailed completion of the forms listed in DI 11005.018A.1. in this section, the DDS is responsible for obtaining any additional information
that is necessary, based on the case facts. For additional information on curtailment
scenarios, see DI 11005.020.
