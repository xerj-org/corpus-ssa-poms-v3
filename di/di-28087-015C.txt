C. Procedure - when screening performed by state vr agency personnel
The screening and referral of disability cases is usually a concomitant function of
the DDS when making a disability or blindness determination. However, in those agencies
where the DDS is not a part of the State VR agency, the State VR agency may elect
to perform the screening and referral functions. In the latter situation, the DDS
may be asked to participate in the development of the screening criteria, and should
obtain for its records, copies of the criteria that are adopted.
