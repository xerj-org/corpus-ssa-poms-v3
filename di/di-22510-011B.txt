SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.011 - Use of Pain Specialists and/or Pain Centers (Clinics) As CE Sources
Effective Dates: 05/06/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Evidence Of Record
In evaluating pain, give consideration to:
a.
All available medical evidence,
b.
Statement(s) of:
•
The claimant,
•
Treating or examining physicians, and
•
Others.
2. CE
Evidence from CEs, when applicable.
