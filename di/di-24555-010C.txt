C. Exceptions to using the CMS-2728-U3 to meet listing 6.03 or 106.03
Do not use the CMS-2728-U3 as the only evidence to establish that the impairment meets
listing 6.03 or 106.03 if:
•
The allowance based on meeting listing 6.03 or 106.03 is not fully favorable, or
•
The ICD-9 (International Classification of Diseases, Ninth Revision) or ICD-10 (International
Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision) diagnosis code in Item 17 indicates that
the renal disease or kidney failure may be acute or may improve. See DI 24555.010C.1. and DI 24555.010C.2. in this section for ICD-10 and ICD-9 codes that may indicate that dialysis may be
acute or may not last for a period of 12 months. If one of these diagnosis codes is
present in Item 17, we need additional evidence to show that the renal disease is
chronic and will meet the duration requirement.
