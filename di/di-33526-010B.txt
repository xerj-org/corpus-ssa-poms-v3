B. When a special senses or speech impairment meets or medically equals a Part B listing
Use the appropriate impairment code from the table below if the special senses or
speech impairment meets or medically equals a Part B listing. When the impairment
meets listing 102.02 or 102.03A, see DI 26510.015C.
