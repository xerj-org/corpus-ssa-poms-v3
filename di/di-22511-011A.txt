SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22511.011 - Contacting Claimants and Collateral Sources
Effective Dates: 10/18/2011 - Present | TN 4 (03-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422511011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Many factors can make an impact on the method selected for developing claimant and
collateral evidence (e.g., geographical location, access to telephone, work schedules,
etc.). The manner in which such evidence is obtained must be left to the adjudicator's
discretion.
