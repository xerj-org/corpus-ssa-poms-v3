A. Introduction
The Function Report - Child (FRC) forms (SSA-3375 through SSA-3379) are used in child cases to obtain functional
information from the applicant, and from other caregivers.
The FRC forms replace all other forms used to obtain similar information from sources
such as a child's parents.
