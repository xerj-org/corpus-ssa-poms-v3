E. Completing items 26- 29 of SSA-831-U3
The process for completing items 26- 29 on the SSA-831-U3 at the reconsideration level
is generally the same for initial claims. However the disability examiner and medical
consultant or psychological consultant must be different from the reviewers who made
the initial decision. For instructions, see DI 26510.000 – Completion of Form SSA-831-C3/U3 – Title II, Title XVI and Concurrent Claims –
Table of Contents and DI 27001.001E.2 — The Reconsideration Process.
