D. Possible Reopening Situation
If a reopening and reversal to a denial is necessary the beneficiary must be given
written notice of the proposed action (see DI 23501.075). A reopening and reversal to a denial is necessary where a felony-related impairment
must be excluded from consideration and its exclusion results in a determination that
the individual is not disabled.
The DO will also diary a case in which an appeal is pending. When a conviction is
overturned by an appeal, a reopening and reversal to an allowance may be permitted.
