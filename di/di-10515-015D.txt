SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10515.015 - Work Comparability Provisions for Blind Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), and Disabled Widow(er) Benefits (DWB) Claimants, Age 55 and Older
Effective Dates: 06/21/2012 - Present | TN 9 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410515015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Comparability decisions: age-55-or-older DIB, CDB, and DWB claims
1.
Determination as to disability under this provision requires a comparison of the skills
and abilities utilized by the individual in the period before, and in the period after, the later of either the onset of blindness or the attainment of age 55.
2.
Work-comparability decisions under the special definition of blindness for these DIB,
CDB, and DWB claimants age 55 or older will be made by the Disability Determination
Services (DDS) in initial and reconsideration cases, and in continuing disability
cases where there is a medical issue; Office of Disability Operations/Payment Service
Center (ODO/PSC) will make these decisions in continuing disability cases without
medical issues.
3.
In order to determine a work-comparability issue, the DDS must know whether the two
periods of work under comparison are SGA. Since the work in either one or both periods
will generally have been performed over several years, a determination of comparability
may involve some year(s) in which the pre-1978 SGA test applies and other year(s)
in which the SGA test specifically for blind persons applies. The use of one test
or the other, or perhaps both tests, may be necessary in evaluating a single period
of gainful work, and may necessitate DO preparation of more than one SGA determination
in such cases.
4.
When the issue of work comparability occurs, develop and prepare determinations as
to SGA (using SSA-820-F4 or SSA-821-BK as appropriate) for:
a.
each job which:
1.
lasted 12 months or longer (not necessarily 12 consecutive months)...
2.
in the 10 years prior to the later of either...the onset of blindness or the attainment of age 55.
Also, develop and prepare SGA determinations for:
b.
each period of work after the later of:
1.
the onset of blindness, or
2.
the attainment of age 55.
Apply the earnings guideline that is appropriate for the year in which the work was
performed (see DI 10501.015).
