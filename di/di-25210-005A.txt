SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25210.005 - Factors We Consider When We Evaluate The Effects Of Your Impairment(s) On Your Functioning (Section 416.924a(b)(1)-(2))
Effective Dates: 08/12/2024 - Present | TN 2 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425210005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
We must consider your functioning when we decide whether your impairment(s) is "severe"
and when we decide whether your impairment(s) functionally equals the listings. We
will also consider your functioning when we decide whether your impairment(s) meets
or medically equals a listing if the listing we are considering includes functioning
among its criteria.
