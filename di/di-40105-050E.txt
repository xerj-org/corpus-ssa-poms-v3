E. Processing the claim when administrative res judicata applies
When res judicata applies, a Form SSA-831 (Disability Determination and Transmittal)
is not necessary.
The Disability Examiner/Disability Processing Specialist (DE/DPS) annotates the certified
electronic folder per DI 27516.005D.1explaining that res judicata applies and request that the FO input denial code S1.
For instructions on annotating the CEF, see DI 81020.090.
