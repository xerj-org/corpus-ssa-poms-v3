A. Introduction
There may be instances when the FO returns a development which indicates an earnings
discrepancy exists (e.g., the disabled individual disclaims work activity and is unable
to explain posted earnings to their SSN). Follow these instructions when a suspected
or verified earnings discrepancy issue exists.
