SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.305 - Routing a Non-disability Claim after Disability Insurance Benefits (DIB) Claim Forwarded for Determination
Effective Dates: 11/10/2008 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010305 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Disability determination received by the field office (FO)
Send the non-disability claim to the Program Service Center (PSC). If the disability
determination is favorable, when the official folder is paper, also send it to the
PC if action is needed, if not, send the folder to National Record Center (NRC). For
Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) cases, no action is needed.
