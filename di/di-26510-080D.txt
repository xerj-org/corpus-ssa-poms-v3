D. Completing Item 28 in DWB claims
1.
Do not check Block A in DWB claims.
2.
Check Block B for DWB “closed period” allowances.
3.
Block C
•
Check this block for DWB claimants found disabled on or before the date the prescribed
period requirement is last met.
•
Enter, on the line following Block C, the date shown in Item 15A in DWB claims.
4.
In DWB closed period claims, check Block E, and enter the last day of the second month
after the month disability ceased (the date shown in Item 15B).
