A. Purpose of the Form SSA-2455
Use the SSA-2455 to compute the amount of workers’ compensation/public disability
benefits (WC/PDB) offset. You may use the e2455+, a new tool for calculating amounts payable after WC/PDB offset, instead of the paper
Form SSA-2455. The e2455+ can propagate offset rates into OREO (see DI 52165.020).
