A. Folder Documentation
The adjudicator will:
•
Place copies of all pre-determination notices in the official case folder.
•
Document all subsequent contacts with the individual or auxiliaries on an SSA-5002,
Report of Contact, or similar document.
•
For electronic processing instructions, see DI 81010.090 and DI 81020.050.
