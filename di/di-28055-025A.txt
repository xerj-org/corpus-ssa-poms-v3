SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.025 - DDS Evaluation and Processing of EPE Cases
Effective Dates: 11/27/1990 - Present | TN 20 (09-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The DDS will perform a medical/vocational CDR on EPE cases referred by ODIO /PSC-DRS
through the FO or directly by the FO. A person will be considered to have a disabling
impairment until a formal determination is made that the person is not disabled, with
written notice of the determination and right to reconsideration.
NOTE: The FO or ODIO/PSC-DRS will not  delay reinstatement / suspension actions (i.e., due to change in earnings) pending
completion of a DDS medical review or by requesting the folder. The FO can reinstate/
suspend benefits without the folder via teletype request to ODIO/PSC-DRS.
