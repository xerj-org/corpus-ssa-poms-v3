SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28055.005 - How the EPE Works
Effective Dates: 04/11/2011 - Present | TN 20 (09-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428055005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples
SETTING: A disabled wage-earner completes a 9-month TWP in 12/88. The EPE begins 1/89.
1. Example 1
Disability ceases 1/89 due to SGA and SGA continues. Payments are placed in suspense
4/89. The DDS finds that the person does not have a disabling impairment as of 10/89.
The reinstatement period ends 9/89, i.e, the month before the first month the impairment
is not disabling. The EPE ends 12/89, i.e., the second month after the month the person's
impairment is not disabling. The BTM is 1/90, i.e., the third month after the month
the person's impairment is not disabling.
2. Example 2
Disability ceases 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are placed in suspense 4/89. The DDS finds
impairment severity continues. The worker continues to do SGA in all months. The reinstatement
period ends 12/91, i.e., the last day of the 36th month after the TWP. The EPE also
ends 12/91, i.e., the month before the first month of SGA after the 36th month. The
BTM is 1/92, i.e., the first month of SGA after the 36th month.
3. Example 3
Disability ceases 1/89 due to SGA. Payments are placed in suspense 4/89. The DDS finds
impairment severity continues. The worker performs SGA thru 5/ 91. Payments are reinstated
6/91. The worker returns to SGA in 6/94. The reinstatement period ends 12/91, i.e,
the last day of the 36th month after the TWP. The EPE and payments end 5/94,i.e.,
the month before the first month of SGA after the 36th month. The BTM is 6/94, i.e.,
the first month of SGA after the 36th month.
4. Example 4
The DDS finds that disability continues after the TWP is completed and the work is
not SGA; therefore, payments continue uninterrupted. The worker does not perform SGA
until 6/93. The reinstatement period ends 12/91, i.e, the last day of the 36th month
after the TWP. The EPE and payments end 8/93, i.e., the second month after the month
disability ceased due to SGA. The BTM is 9/93, i.e., the third month after the month
disability ceased due to SGA.
