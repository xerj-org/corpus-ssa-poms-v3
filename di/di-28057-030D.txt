D. Hearings and Appeals Council (AC) review
If the individual disagrees with the reconsideration determination, the FO processes
the hearing request per DI 13050.085D and DI 12010.001. If the individual disagrees with the determination made by an ALJ, the FO processes
the request for AC review per DI 13050.085E and DI 12020.000.
