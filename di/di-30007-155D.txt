D. DQR actions after RPC resolution
1.
If there are actions for the DQR, the DQR will immediately take the action(s) specified
in the RPC resolution and finish processing the case. The DQR will manage the case
per their normal business processes.
2.
The DQR will ensure that the DDS has completed all the actions identified in the RPC
resolution. If the corrected determination is not consistent with the RPC resolution,
DQR will email notification to RPC (include the RPC case number in the subject line)
at ^LP DP RPC Controls to the attention of the Director.
