SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13510.001 - Payment to States for Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) Services Which Result in a Continuous Period of Substantial Gainful Activity
Effective Dates: 05/12/1999 - Present | TN 1 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413510001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. State VR Agency Claims for Reimbursement
If a State VR agency believes its services have resulted (or soon will result) in
a beneficiary or recipient performing SGA for a continuous period of 9 months and
it wants to be reimbursed for those services, it must file a claim for reimbursement.
The State must file a claim within 12 months following the ninth month of SGA. Each
claim must provide SSA with identifying information about the individual served. SSA
will verify that the person was a beneficiary /recipient. SSA also will determine
whether the person engaged in SGA for a continuous period of 9 months (VR continuous
period of SGA).
