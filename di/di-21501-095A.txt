A. Blindness
An applicant or recipient must have no vision, or, not more than 20/200 central vision
acuity in the better eye with correcting glasses, or a field defect in which the peripheral
field has contracted to an extent that the widest diameter of visual field subtends
at an angular distance of no greater than twenty (20) degrees.
