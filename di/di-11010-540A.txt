SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.540 - Medical Evidence Received From a Foreign Source
Effective Dates: 12/22/2014 - Present | TN 54 (12-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010540 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Evidence received from sources within the U.S., Canada or Mexico
The field office (FO) will do the following:
•
Examine the application and evidence for completeness.
•
Contact the claimant or his or her alternate contact for any additional necessary
information.
The FO will not:
•
Try to determine if a foreign medical report is valid or if a foreign medical source
is professionally competent.
•
Directly contact any physicians or medical institutions located in foreign countries.
