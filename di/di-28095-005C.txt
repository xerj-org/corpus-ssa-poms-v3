C. When to prepare a manual notice
The disability determination service (DDS) will prepare a manual notice when:
1.
The DDS ceased the CDR case. (Personalized paragraphs cannot be merged with the paragraphs
on SSA computer system); or
2.
The DDS continues the CDR case and the computer notice is inadequate because the appropriate
notice language has not been programmed; or
3.
The DDS continues the CDR case and they cannot generate a computer notice because
successful transmission of the data to trigger the notice cannot be accomplished.
This is termed a “systems limitation” case and DDS will identify this case on the
SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
– Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
–Title II).
Systems limitations occur through:
•
Input exceptions due to missing or invalid codes or data. When this occurs, make a
correction and correct data transmitted to trigger the notice.
•
Processing exceptions due to data inconsistent with what is already on the record.
If correction is possible, transmit correct data to trigger the notice.
•
Processing alerts due to prior information on the record, which may require additional
development or processing.
