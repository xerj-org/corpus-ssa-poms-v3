D. Additional Requirements for Processing QDD Cases with an EDCS Exclusion
In concurrent claims, when one of the claims is an EDCS exclusion, follow the procedures
in Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions and Limitations (DI 81010.030). In addition, the FO will:
1.
Complete all actions in DI 11005.603C. in this section;
2.
Place signed, dated, and witnessed SSA-827s, along with any claimant-provided medical
evidence, in the paper Modular Disability Folder (MDF); and
3.
Attach an EDCS routing form to the front of the folder and forward it to the DDS after
transferring the electronic portion of the claim.
