Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510014 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Agreement requirement to participate in a THCE
The claimant (parent of a minor child or legal guardian) must agree in advance to
the claimant’s participation in a THCE. Disability Determinations Services (DDS) will
not schedule the THCE unless:
•
The appropriate individual(s) per DI 22510.014B in this section agrees to participate, and
•
The agreement is documented per DI 22510.014C in this section.
DDS will schedule an in-person consultative examination (CE) in the absence of such
agreement.
