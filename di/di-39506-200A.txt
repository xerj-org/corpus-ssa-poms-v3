A. Introduction
The following sections set forth standards and definitions for State agency use in
recording and reporting obligations incurred for SSA disability program operations.
Also presented are instructions for the preparation of fiscal reports: Forms SSA-4513
(State Agency Report of Obligations for SSA Disability Programs), and SSA-4514 (Time
Report of Personnel Services for Disability Determination Services).
