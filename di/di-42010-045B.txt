SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.045 - Erroneous Payment or Notice After an Administrative Law Judge Decision
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | TN 16 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Processing center (PC) mistakenly handles a closed period award as ongoing entitlement
1. Error found and SSA is within the time for reopening
If the ALJ issues a closed period award and the processing center erroneously establishes
ongoing payments and the error is found within the time limits for reopening, take
the following actions before stopping current payments and establishing an overpayment:
a.
Send a due process notice as explained in GN 03001.005 and GN 03001.015.
b.
After releasing the due process notice, diary the case for 15 days (10 days plus 5
days for mailing) for possible rebuttal before SSA takes the adverse action.
c.
After the rebuttal period ends, the claimant must receive a final notice for all events
that result in an adverse action after the action is taken. The notice must provide
sufficient information for the beneficiary to make an informed appeal decision, such
as the:
1.
action taken,
2.
basis for the action,
3.
appeal rights, and
4.
waiver provision if the adverse action results in an overpayment.
2. Error found and SSA is not within the time for reopening
If the ALJ issues a closed period award and the PC erroneously establishes ongoing
payments and the error is not found within the time limits for reopening, forward
the case to the field office (FO) using the Modernized Development Worksheet to initiate
a continuing disability review.
