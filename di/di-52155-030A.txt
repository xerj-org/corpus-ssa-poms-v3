A. Preparing the WC/PDB offset calculation
Data from MCS or A101 will not propagate into the EF101.
Before starting the EF101 complete the WC/PDB offset determination in the T2 Interactive
Computation Facility (ICF) #32WC/PDB: ADJUDICATE CLAIM or #34 WC/PDB: ONLINE RECORD
ENTRY. For detailed instructions, see DI 52155.025B.
