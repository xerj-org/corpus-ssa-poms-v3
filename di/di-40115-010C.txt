Effective Dates: 03/07/2011 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440115010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Notice and routing Instructions
1. Notice
•
Enter the letter and paragraph number block of the SSA-833 NLN;
•
Enter S in item 29 of the SSA-831; and
•
Prepare paragraph for the claims authorizer/claims technical examiner (CA/CTE) on
an SSA-573 (Special Typing Instructions) to show dates of old and new periods of disability.
Also, show months for which checks are due.
2. Routing
•
Route the folder to a CA/CTE.
•
Explain the effects of both determinations, and
•
Ask the CA/CTE to prepare a new award and forward it to a benefit authorizer/benefit
technical examiner for processing.
