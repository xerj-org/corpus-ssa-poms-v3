Effective Dates: 07/01/2026 - Present | TN 9 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0433010005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Scheduling notice
The notice of the time and place of the hearing must be mailed
at least 20 calendar days before the hearing. See DI
33010.005A for information on waiver of the advance notice.
1.
Use:
•
an SSA-L951-U2/C2/SP letterhead for Title II and concurrent Title II/XVI cases,
•
an SSA-L8052-U2/C2 letterhead for Title XVI cases, or
•
a State letterhead.
2.
Type Important Information as the third line of the letterhead when three line letterhead
versions are used.
3.
See DI
33010.080 for language, which must be contained in the scheduling notice for domestic claims,
and DI
33010.085 for the language, which must be contained in foreign claim cases.
4.
Send the notice to the beneficiary (or recipient) and their representative payee,
when a legally competent adult has a representative payee.
5.
Send a copy of the notice to the beneficiary's (or recipient's) appointed representative,
if applicable.
NOTE: The language referenced above is required regardless of whether the distance from
the beneficiary's (or recipient's) residence (or place of entry into the U.S.) to
the hearing site exceeds 75 miles.
