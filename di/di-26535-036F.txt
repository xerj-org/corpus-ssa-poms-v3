F. Title II and Title XVI special notice options for the blind or visually impaired
Effective June 30, 2008, special notice options are available to any applicant, beneficiary,
claimant or recipient who applies for benefits under title II or title XVI if they
are blind or tell us they need assistance because of a visual impairment. The policies
and instructions on the preparation of special notification to the blind or visually
impaired are located in NL 01000.000ff. Special Notice Options for the Blind or Visually
Impaired.
