SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29501.020 - Returning Requested Development to the Administrative Law Judge
Effective Dates: 08/11/2011 - Present | TN 1 (08-11)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429501020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Paper cases only
For paper cases:
•
Forward the completed development, reports, or forms to the requesting ALJ.
•
If the requested development was a consultative exam (CE), attach a copy of the appointment
letter or DDS authorization form to the medical report forwarded to the ALJ.
•
Annotate the routing form with “Administrative Law Judge's Direct Request” in the
remarks section.
