A. When the new evidence related to the prior determination or decision error exception
applies
This category of exception applies if:
•
new evidence that relates to the prior determination or decision refutes the conclusions
that were based on the prior evidence; and
•
evidence shows the claim would not have been allowed or continued if the new evidence
had been considered at the time of the prior decision.
In deciding whether this category of error applies, carefully consider whether any
new evidence provides useful medical information about the status of the impairment(s)
at the time of the prior decision.
