4. Effect of Electronic Certification
Once a State received electronic certification, the MDF was no longer prepared for
EDCS cases transferred to the DDS (except for exclusions). Adjudicative documentation
is stored only in CEFs, and CEFs, are the official agency records for electronic cases.
(See DI
81010.030 for cases that are CEF exclusions, EDCS Exclusions and Limitations.)
After certification, CEF procedures apply to EDCS cases transferred from FOs to the
DDS on or after the certification date.
