A. Procedure for routing 'no determination' case folders
•
Place a remark in the 'Alerts' and 'Messages' section of the Certified Electronic
Folder (CEF) in eView using the chart below.
•
For paper cases, add the remarks to form SSA-408 (Route Slip). See DI 81020.127 Processing “No Determination” (ND) Claims for more examples of, 'No Determination/Decision'
cases.
•
Send the electronic CDR (eCDR) and the CPD to the same location.
