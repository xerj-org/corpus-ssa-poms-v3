A. Policy - general
We consider all relevant information (i.e., evidence) in your case record. The evidence
in your case record may include information from medical sources, such as your pediatrician,
other physician, psychologist, or qualified speech-language pathologist; other medical
sources not listed in DI 22505.003A., such as physical, occupational, and rehabilitation therapists; and nonmedical sources,
such as your parents, teachers, and other people who know you.
