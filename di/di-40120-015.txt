DI 40120.015 Processing Center (PC) Instructions for Duplicate Applications
A duplicate application is an unnecessary application filed for the same type of benefit
or the same period of disability. Processing Center instructions and the type of notification
generally used for duplicate applications are located in GN 00204.028.
