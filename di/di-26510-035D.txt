SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.035 - Completing Item 20 (Vocational Background) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 09/06/2012 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Title XVI
If the claimant is age 18 or older at the time of adjudication, complete item 20 per
DI 26510.035A . If the claimant has no work history, enter ”None”. If the claimant has not attained
age 18 at the time of adjudication, do not complete item 20.
