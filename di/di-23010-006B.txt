B. Making a Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment (FTFPT) determination
We may make an FTFPT determination at steps 3 and 5 of the sequential evaluation process
and during a CDR. For more information, see DI 23010.007 When to Apply Failure to Follow Prescribed Treatment Policy.
