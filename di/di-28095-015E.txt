E. Return of document requested for a “C25” suffix
When a beneficiary or recipient request the return of a document they submitted, enter
“C25” in Item 29 of the SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal-Title XVI or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability
or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title II) for example, 443-C25. (Paragraph
C25 reads, “Enclosure: Document(s)”). If the beneficiary or recipient expressed concern
about the loss of the document or requested special handling, use the “S” suffix and
use registered or certified mail for notice and document on the SSA-865 (Special Instruction
Notice-Disability).
Identify the returning document by marking it lightly with an “X” in pencil in the
upper right corner. Place this document on top of all materials on the right side
of the folder.
