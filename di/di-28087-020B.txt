B. Procedure - screen-out criteria
Any disability/blindness applicant, other than one with a visual impairment, who meets
any of the following criteria should not be referred. Follow guidelines in DI 28087.045 for those cases.
1.
Illness in terminal stage--irreversible or irremediable; or
2.
Severe or rapidly progressive physical or mental impairment not responding to treatment
so that training or employment would be precluded; or
3.
No demonstrated mental or physical impairment (for not severe impairment, see Screen-In);
or
4.
Acute medical or post-surgical condition which is expected to improve so that return
to usual work may be anticipated; or
5.
Organ or system impairment so severe that when considering non-medical factors, potential
to do sustained work is doubtful; or
6.
The presence of a significant impairment and either a sparse work record or a record
of performing arduous unskilled labor for 35 years or more and having no more than
a marginal education and no transferable skills; or
7.
Applicant under age 15 unless the individual circumstances indicate a special readiness
for beginning VR services.
If screen-out criteria are not met, proceed to DI 28087.020C.
