C. Establish the CPD date
To establish the CPD date, the adjudicator will determine if the DDS:
•
Made the initial determination within the last 3 years. If there is no CDR date on
the MBR, the allowance determination date is likely the CPD date.
•
Made the initial determination more than 3 years ago, or 7 years in medical improvement
not expected (MINE) cases. The later decision should be the CPD date. However, due
to delays in CDR processing, the absence of CDR data on the MBR may indicate that
the initial decision is the CPD date.
