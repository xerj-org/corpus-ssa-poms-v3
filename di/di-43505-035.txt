DI 43505.035 Closed Period/Freeze Determination Where Disability Ceased More than 14 Months Before
Application
Occasionally claims may be received in INTPSC where the claimant's disability ceased
more than 14 months before the application was filed. It may be necessary to determine
if the late filing was the result of a mental or physical impairment. (See DI 40105.025 for SSA-831-U5 completion instructions.)
