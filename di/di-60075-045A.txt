A. How the PAS worked
The PAS worked as follows:
1.
Received monthly earnings files containing earnings and IRWE from Abt;
2.
Calculated monthly offset amount using earnings files from the contractor;
3.
Deducted offset amount from the POD participant’s corresponding Monthly Benefit Amount
(MBA);
4.
Created a manual adjustment credit and award process (MADCAP) action that updates
the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR);
5.
Provided universal text indicator (UTI) information as part of the MADCAP action;
6.
Created (after the previous action) a special message on the MBR indicating that this
is a POD beneficiary; and
7.
Added the “DP” demonstration code and the start and end date of participation to the
HISTDEMO field on the MBR.
