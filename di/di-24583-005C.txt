C. How do we determine whether the person has a medically determinable mental impairment?
We evaluate the pertinent symptoms, signs, and laboratory findings to determine whether
the person has a medically determinable mental impairment. We must specify the symptoms,
signs, and laboratory findings that substantiate the presence of the impairment(s)
on a standard document. For more information about the standard document, see DI 24583.005G.
