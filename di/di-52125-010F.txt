Effective Dates: 06/09/2009 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452125010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Folder documentation
WC/PDB verification often involves complex, State-specific forms and documents that
routinely involve a certain degree of interpretation. Therefore, all WC/PDB proofs
must be permanently retained in file to provide the most comprehensive documentation
(see DI 52145.015 Retention of Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Proofs).
