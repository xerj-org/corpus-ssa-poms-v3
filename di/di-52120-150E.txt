E. Time limitations for filing claims
There is normally a 6 month time limit for filing a WC claim, or an appeal to the
denial of a claim. Claims are generally barred unless the parties agree on the WC
payable or one of the parties files a petition within two years after the date of
injury (or within two years of the last WC payment made).
