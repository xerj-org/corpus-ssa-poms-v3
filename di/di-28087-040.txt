DI 28087.040 Advising Designated State Agency About Title XVI Childhood Cessations
PROCEDURE
When the DDS determines that a child under age 16 is no longer blind or disabled for
title XVI purposes, forward a copy of Form SSA-832-U5, Cessation or Continuance of
Disability, to the State agency designated to receive referrals of allowed title XVI
children (see DI 28087.010A.2.).
