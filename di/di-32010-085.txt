DI 32010.085 Continuing Disability Review (CDR) 'Adverse Reopening' Cases
Always send the paper comparison point decision (CPD) folder to the same location
where the certified electronic folder (CEF) is transferred. Include messages to explain
what action is necessary, if any, for both the paper and electronic folder.
