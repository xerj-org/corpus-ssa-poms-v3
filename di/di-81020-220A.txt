SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81020.220 - Electronic Continuing Disability Review (eCDR) Development
Effective Dates: 07/17/2018 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481020220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Requesting eCDR evidence
•
Develop all necessary evidence (e.g., medical evidence of record, work history, beneficiary
and/or third party questionnaires), using the Disability Determination Services (DDS)
case processing system;
•
Obtain Consultative Examination(s) (CE), if necessary;
•
Develop necessary evidence if the beneficiary/recipient is participating in a vocational
rehabilitation program
•
Ensure all folder documents are saved to the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF).
NOTE: The Field Office (FO) sends any medical evidence received directly from the beneficiary
to the DDS for scanning into the CEF.
