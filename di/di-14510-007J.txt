J. Past due VP diaries
ODO follows up on matured VP diaries and no FO action is required. However, if you
discover a past due diary during an RZ or PE event, check eView or EP for recent development
requests and denial or allowance notices. If there is no recent case activity, send
an email to OCO.ODO.OSB.SECTION.301@ssa.gov alerting ODO of the past due VP diary.
