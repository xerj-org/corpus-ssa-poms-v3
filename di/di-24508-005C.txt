C. Can an impairment(s) meet a listing based only on a diagnosis?
To meet a listing, the MDI(s) must satisfy all of the criteria in the listing. Although
we have a few listings that only require a claimant have a particular condition (for
example, listing 11.10 for Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis), we must have clinical findings,
laboratory findings, or both that establish the condition(s). An impairment cannot
meet a listing based on a diagnosis only.
