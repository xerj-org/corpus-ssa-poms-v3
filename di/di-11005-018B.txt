SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11005.018 - The Disability Interview Process
Effective Dates: 06/21/2024 - Present | TN 114 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411005018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Disability Interview Process - Details
1. Self-help
The claimant completes a Medical and Job Worksheet for adult claims or a Medical Information
and School Worksheet for child claims, and the appropriate child Function Report (SSA-3375
through SSA-3379).
The FO will conduct the disability interview, enter the claimant's information into
the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS), and prepare an EDCS-3367 (Disability
Report – Field Office). Additionally, the FO will complete the SSA-820 (Work Activity
Report - Self-Employment), SSA-821 (Work Activity Report - Employee), and SSA-823
(Report of SGA Determination), as applicable, when there are work issues, obtain an
application, and obtain a signed, dated, and witnessed SSA-827 (Authorization to Disclose
Information to the Social Security Administration). For more information on using
the SSA-820 and SSA-823, see DI 10510.025. For more information on using the SSA-821 and SSA-823, see DI 10505.035.
The FO will then transfer the case to the DDS.
2. Non-Self-Help
The FO will conduct the disability interview, enter the claimant's information into
EDCS, and prepare an EDCS-3367. Additionally, the FO will complete the SSA-820 (Work
Activity Report - Self-Employment), SSA-821 (Work Activity Report - Employee), and
SSA-823 (Report of SGA Determination), as applicable, when there are work issues,
obtain an application, and obtain a signed, dated, and witnessed SSA-827. For more
information on using the SSA-820 and SSA-823, see DI 10510.025. For more information on using the SSA-821 and SSA-823, see DI 10505.035.
When the FO believes recontact with the claimant will be difficult (as discussed in
DI 11005.018A.1., in this section), the claimant asks to have the forms completed by an interviewer
or demonstrates concern about their ability to complete the forms, or the FO interviewer
suspects the claimant will be unable to complete the forms properly, the FO will complete
the SSA-3373-BK and the SSA- 3369-BK (if the claimant had more than one job in the
five-year period before they stopped working due to their medical condition(s)).
The FO will then transfer the case to the DDS.
3. DDS Procedures
The DDS will use the forms in the Disability Case Processing System (DCPS) to collect
information about the claimant's symptoms, the SSA-3373-BK to collect information
about the claimant's ability to perform activities of daily living, and the SSA-3380-BK
to collect information from third parties familiar with the claimant's daily activities
and functioning (e.g., the claimant's spouse, relatives, neighbors, friends, clergy,
or teachers).
Neither the SSA-3373-BK or the SSA-3380-BK is required in all cases. The DDS will
use them if they determine that functional evidence is needed. The DDS will not send
the SSA-827 to a third party unless the DDS knows from past experience that the source
requires it to complete the SSA-3380-BK. For more information on completing the SSA-827,
see DI 11005.055. The DDS may also decide to obtain the information requested on the SSA-3373-BK or
SSA-3380-BK by telephone and document it on an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) or in
a Case Note that is copied to the case development worksheet (CDW) in DCPS.
