Effective Dates: 07/13/2023 - Present | TN 16 (07-23)

C. Procedure for vacating a medical cessation determination on a paper folder CDR
If you have a paper folder:
1.
Receipt the case as a reopening.
2.
Document the folder by adding an SSA-5002 containing the following language:
“Inappropriate medical CDR cessation determination was initiated or processed. The
disabled person had CDR protection based on their _________ (example: Ticket to Work)
status. Do not use any documentation contained in this file related to this CDR. Do
not consider any medical evidence in file obtained for this CDR for future CDR purposes.”
3.
Annotate the Form(s) SSA-832/833: “DO NOT PROCESS – CDR vacated.”
4.
Prepare and send the notice of the vacated medical cessation determination to the
disabled person. See exhibit in DI 28075.310 to be consistent with the information in electronic processes. Include a copy of
the notice in the paper folder.
5.
Close the case in NDDSS as a “No Determination -- No CDR Issue.”
6.
Transfer jurisdiction of the CDR to the FO.
