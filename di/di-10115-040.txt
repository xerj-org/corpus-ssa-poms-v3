DI 10115.040 Childhood Disability Benefit (CDB) Reentitlement & Trial Work Period (TWP) Issues
Policy
A CDB who becomes reentitled to CDB benefits which began before age 22 or within 84
months following the month in which the most recent entitlement to CDB terminated
is again entitled to a TWP.
NOTE: Effective 10/1/04, P.L. 108-203 allows re-entitlement to childhood disability benefits
after the 84 months period if the beneficiary's previous entitlement to disability
terminated because of Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA).
