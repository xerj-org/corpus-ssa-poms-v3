G. Routing of continuing disability review (CDR) death or no issue case
When the DDS receives a CDR case and the DDS discovers that the beneficiary is deceased
or there is, in fact, no continuing disability issue, send the file to ODO or National
Records Center (NRC), depending on which component is the final repository for the
claim.
