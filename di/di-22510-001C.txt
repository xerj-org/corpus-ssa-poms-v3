C. Payment for CEs
•
SSA pays for any CE ordered for the claimant; and
•
SSA does not pay for any medical examination arranged by the claimant or the claimant's
appointed representative unless it is approved in advance.
For more information on purchasing CEs, see DI 39545.000.
