C. Procedure for evaluation
1. Determination instructions
Follow these instructions:
a.
Apply Federal criteria, including the Medical Improvement Review Standard (MIRS).
See DI 28005.001 through DI 28005.030.
b.
If the adjudicator finds a cessation under the Federal criteria, apply State criteria
and include another consideration of MIRS.
c.
For a discussion of the Comparison Point Decision (CPD), see DI 28010.020
d.
When it is difficult or impossible to locate the evidence considered in making the
most recent prior decision under the State plan, follow DI 28035.025.
e.
For breaks in Title XVI eligibility, follow DI 28075.020.
2. State plan material
When we must make a determination as to whether disability or blindness under a State
plan continues to exist, follow DI 21501.001 through DI 21501.270.
Consider the entire State plan definition in making the determination.
Especially consider those written State plan definitions of disability and blindness
that are clearly more liberal. The more liberal definitions are italicized.
3. Supplemental SWA instructions
Follow these instructions:
a.
Obtain written supplemental manual instructions to provide clarification of State
plan requirements.
b.
Consider these instructions as part of the State plan definition.
c.
Forward a copy of instructions to the DCR.
d.
If used for the adjudication of a conversion case, identify and describe the relevant
supplemental manual instructions.
e.
Explain the effect of these instructions on the determination.
f.
Refer any questions to the DCR.
