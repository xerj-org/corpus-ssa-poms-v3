F. Mental status examination for children age 12 and older
The case facts emphasized will determine the specific areas of mental status during
the examination, but the report for children age 12 and older must include the information
in DI 22510.048 E and a detailed description of the child's:
1.
Judgment and insight;
2.
Impulse control;
3.
Orientation;
4.
Memory; and
5.
Homicidal and suicidal ideation.
Note:
The description of the child's mental status must not be an enumeration of the symptoms
reported by the child (or other source); rather the description must be the examining
source's description of the above items.
