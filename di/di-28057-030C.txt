C. Procedure for paper case clearance
Take the following steps to clear a paper EXR reconsideration case:
1.
If the determination is favorable, fax the EXR approval to the FO, so the FO can initiate
necessary non-medical development, see DI
11010.340. If the determination is unfavorable see DI
28057.020.
2.
Forward the folder to the FO for any appropriate action.
