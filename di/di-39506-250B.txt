B. How to file Form SSA-871
We require submission of the Form SSA-871 only if the Disability Determination Service
(DDS) purchased equipment during the quarter reported.
Submit copies as follows:
•
Send the original (with signature) to Disability Adjudication (DA).
•
Send to the SSA Disability Adjudication (DA) State Disability Resource Management
Division mail box ^DA SDRM Funding.
•
Copies due on the 25th of the month after the quarter ends. For example, for the quarter
that ends 9/30/2025, reports are due by 10/25/2025.
