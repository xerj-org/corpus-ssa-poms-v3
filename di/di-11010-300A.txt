SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.300 - Routing Concurrent Disability Insurance Benefits Retirement and Survivor Insurance (DIB-RSI) Claims
Effective Dates: 05/21/2009 - Present | TN 49 (08-08)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Modernized Claim System Earnings Computation (MCS/ EC) processes concurrent Retirement
Insurance Benefits/Disability Insurance Benefits (RIB/DIB) claims when a disability
claim is a denial and there is an active Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) for A Beneficiary
Identification Code (BIC). MCS/ EC processes a DIB allowance over a Retirement Insurance
Benefit (RIB) allowance as long as the DIB Month of Entitlement (MOE) is the same
or prior to the RIB MOE. The MCS claim creates segments allowing the Claims Representative
(CR) to process both claims completely.
In concurrent RIB/DIB claims where the DIB MOE is later than the RIB MOE:
•
Process the RIB claim through EC and the disability claim segments.
•
After the RIB and DIB claims have segmented, process the RIB only.
•
Go back to the DW01 (on the RIB segment) and XXXXXX out any issues pertaining to the
DIB claim only.
•
Process the disability award using the Automated Determination of Award (A101); and
if an exclusion exists, process an EF101.
