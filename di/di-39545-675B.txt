B. Obtain a Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) Codebook
•
DDS should obtain a copy of the CPT and purchase updates each year. The AMA publishes
the CPT Codebook annually.
•
CMS bases Medicare fees upon services and procedures using CPT coding system.
•
The CPT Codebook is available from the AMA at the AMA Store
