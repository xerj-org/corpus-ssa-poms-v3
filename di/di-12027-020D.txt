D. Policy – SBC Payment Month Overpayments
•
SBC payments are not overpayments until the medical cessation decision is final. The
medical cessation decision becomes final when the individual does not request further
review within the appeal period (or has good cause for a untimely filed appeal) or
has exhausted all levels of appeal. See GN
02201.009C.
•
By law, the individual must appeal the medical cessation in good faith to be offered
waiver consideration. If the individual does not appeal in good faith, do not offer
waiver consideration. The FO must consider any physical, mental, educational, or linguistic
limitations in determining good faith.
•
For instructions on processing requests for waiver of recovery of overpayments in
disability cessation appeal cases with SBC involvement, see Payment Continuation Waiver
Title II, XVI, GN 02250.036.
