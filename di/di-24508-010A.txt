SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24508.010 - Impairment or Combination of Impairments Equaling a Listing – Medical Equivalence
Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 3 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424508010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What is medically equals?
An impairment(s) is medically equal to a listed impairment in the Listing of Impairments
(Listings) if it is at least equal in severity and duration to the criteria of any
listed impairment.
