SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10520.020 - Correlating Alleged IRWE with Person's Disability
Effective Dates: 10/25/2024 - Present | TN 24 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410520020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure - If Unable to Determine Whether IRWE Correlates With DDS Established Diagnosis
If the beneficiary/claimant alleges IRWE for an impairment that has not been established
by DDS as a primary or secondary diagnosis, or the technician cannot determine the
relationship of the requested IRWE to the impairment, a correlation issue can still
be established if:
•
the person is being treated by a physician or health care provider for the alleged
impairment; and
•
the alleged IRWE is needed to enable the person to work.
1. Correlation established by medical file
Obtain and review the medical file for any medical records or statements from a treating
physician or health care provider that will confirm:
•
the existence of the alleged impairment; and
•
the person is being treated by a physician or health care provider for the impairment;
and
•
the identity of the physician or health care provider.
If correlation is found, follow procedures in DI 10520.020B and DI 10520.020C for adjudicating initial claims. For continuing disability reviews, follow procedure
in DI 10520.020D.
2. No correlation established by medical records
When the beneficiary’s or claimant’s alleged IRWE does not correlate with either the
DDS diagnosis or medical records in the medical file, ask the beneficiary to provide
medical records or a statement from the treating physician or health care provider
that establishes correlation.
If the beneficiary is unable to provide documentation, contact the physician or health
care provider directly to determine if the person is being treated for the alleged
impairment and that the alleged IRWE is needed due to the impairment.
•
Obtain a signed SSA-827 from the beneficiary.
•
Include customized language that fits the person’s case.
For example: “(Beneficiary name) has reported that they (take/use item or service) due to (impairment(s))
in order to work. We need your assistance to determine if we can deduct the cost of
these items from their countable earnings. Attached is a signed release of information.
1) Are you currently treating (beneficiary name) for (impairment(s)? (Yes or no checkbox.)
2) Are you prescribing or providing the following due to this/these impairment(s)?
List items or services. (Yes or no checkbox.)”
If no medical records or statements are provided to establish correlation, consult
the regional office before disallowing the IRWE. If the regional office should require
any assistance in making the IRWE determination, it may contact DCRDP, ORDES, Employment
Policy Team.
