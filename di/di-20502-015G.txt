G. SSA-3371 (Pain Report – Child)
An SSA-3371 must be completed when an SSA-3820 is required and pain must be developed
(Section 3, Question C. on the SSA-3820 is answered “Yes”).
NOTE: The DDS will determine when pain must be developed and will collect the information.
