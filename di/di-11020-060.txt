DI 11020.060 Disability Determination Services (DDS) Jurisdiction and Routing
To determine whether to send a case to a DDS for a determination, follow guidelines
in DI 11010.255.
To determine which DDS should receive the case, see DI 11010.255.
For electronic disability routing and transfer, see DI 81010.085.
NOTE: The GLPSC-Disability Case Review (DCR) location code V32 has jurisdiction of Title
II disability determinations in career railroad and surviving dependent railroad annuitant
claims (see DI 11050.005C).
