DI 25501.430 Medical Consultant, Psychological Consultant, and Disability Examiner Roles in Determining
Onset for the Established Onset Date
This section explains our policy regarding the medical consultant (MC), psychological
consultant (PC), and the disability examiner (DE) roles in determining the established
onset date (EOD).
REMINDERS:
•
The onset date is the first day the claimant meets the definition of disability or
statutory blindness as defined in the Act and regulations.
•
The EOD is the date that a claimant meets the definition of disability and all the
relevant entitlement or eligibility factors.
