C. Policy - step 2 -you must have a medically determinable impairment(s) that is severe
(section 416.924(c))
If you do not have a medically determinable impairment, or your impairment(s) is a
slight abnormality or a combination of slight abnormalities that causes no more than
minimal functional limitations, we will find that you do not have a severe impairment(s)
and are, therefore, not disabled.
