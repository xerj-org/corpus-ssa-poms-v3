B. Procedure – How to get to the CDRI Screen
To get to the CDRI screen.
•
Select 32, DISABILITY CONTROL FILE, from SSA's main menu (MAIN).
•
Select 10, OFFICE ACTIONS, from the CDR SELECTION MENU (MCDR) and enter the beneficiary's/recipient's
BOAN, or press the PF10 key while on another screen.
