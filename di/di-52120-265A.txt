A. WA WC types
There are four types of WC benefits payable under Washington State law.
SSA WC Type
State WC Type
Temporary Total (TT)
Time Loss
Option 1 Vocational Plan Payments
Temporary Partial (TP)
Loss of Earning Power (LEP)
Permanent Total (PT)
Pension
Permanent Partial (PP)
Permanent Partial Disability
Washington State reduces (offsets) its Time Loss and Pension benefits if the disabled
worker is also receiving Social Security retirement insurance benefit (RIB) or DIB.
The State offset also applies to Time Loss or Pension benefits paid to the spouse
and children living in the same household as the disabled worker.
State law does not provide for offset of PP for the receipt of Social Security RIB
or DIB. A permanent partial lump sum (LS) will cause offset of Social Security DIB.
