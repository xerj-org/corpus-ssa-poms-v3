D. Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR)
The ACR reflects the relationship between the urine albumin to the urine creatinine.
Albumin is one of the proteins measured in the UPCR. We may use the ACR to evaluate
medical equivalence under listing 6.06. For purposes of medical equivalence, an ACR
value that is equal to or greater than 3.5 is of equal medical significance to a UPCR
of 3.5 or greater. We accept ACR values calculated from random (spot) urine samples
or timed collections (including 24-hour collections).
