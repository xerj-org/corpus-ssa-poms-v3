F. Example of determining CPD date
A CDR alert was generated 10/2018. The FO and the DDS were unable to locate the prior
paper folder. The DDSQ shows 02/13/15 as the Title XVI onset date, with a 3-year medical
re-exam diary. The case was closed (SCD) 06/07/15. The SSR shows current folder location
L47 (Folder destroyed). There is no indication that SSA conducted a CDR between onset
date and current CDR alert date. The established CPD is 06/07/15 – the date DDS made
the last favorable determination.
