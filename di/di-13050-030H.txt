H. Policy - Appeals
Actions to pay, adjust or terminate provisional payments are not initial determinations that are subject to the administrative and judicial review
(appeals) process. However, overpayment decisions related to provisional payments
are subject to appeal. See DI 13050.085 for a discussion about appeals.
