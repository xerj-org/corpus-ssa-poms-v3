Effective Dates: 01/13/2017 - Present | TN 1 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434233013 (fetched 2026-10-04)

J. Can we establish the existence of a disabling impairment prior to the date of the
evidence that shows the cancer satisfies the criteria of a listing?
Yes. We will consider factors such as:
1. The type of cancer and its location.
2. The extent of involvement when the cancer was first demonstrated.
3. Your symptoms.
