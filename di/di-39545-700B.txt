Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545700 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure maintaining fee schedules
•
Submit to Disability Adjudication (DA) a copy of the DDS fee schedule.
•
Submit to DA on a flow basis copies of changes to an existing fee schedule with any
issuances and instructions pertinent to their application. If only portions or specific
items were changed, submit only those changes. Allow sufficient time for DA review
before implementing any changes.
•
Review the DDS fee schedule with DA on a regular basis to determine whether the fee
schedule is adequate and cost effective.
•
If the DDS uses a Federal fee schedule or a fee schedule used by another agency in
that State, ensure that any changes to that fee schedule are reflected, when appropriate,
in the rates paid by the DDS.
