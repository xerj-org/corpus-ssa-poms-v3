SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28015.200 - Title II Beneficiary Now Over Age 18, but Comparison Point Decision (CPD) Used Part B Listing
Effective Dates: 11/02/2021 - Present | TN 15 (11-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428015200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Relating medical improvement (MI) to the ability to work
1. Use Part B listings in the current CDR
Consider the previously used listing to determine if MI is related to the ability
to work, even though Part B ordinarily does not apply after age 18.
2. Use Part B after age 18
a. Current findings
Consider current findings, including recipient’s age.
b. Age 18 and Listing is no longer met
Consider the prior Listings of Impairments as instructed in DI 34100.000. Do not use the beneficiary’s age by itself for finding the listing is no longer
met.
c. Different criteria by age
When considering listing sections that contain different criteria broken down by age,
apply the criteria that apply just before age 18 (even though the beneficiary is now
over 18).
