E. Program Service Center- Disability Case Review (PSC/DCR)
The DPB responsibilities include:
1.
Has the same responsibilities as DDS examiners (DI 13001.010B. in this section).
2.
Has the same responsibilities as ODO examiners (DI 13001.010D. in this section).
3.
Review PSC jurisdiction cases, e.g., DIB age 53 and over, disabled widow(er) benefits
(DWB), and childhood disability benefits (CDB).
4.
Review and process Title II and Title XVI CDR Mailer Processing Center review cases.
5.
Great Lakes PSC-DCR has jurisdiction of Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) cases, see
DI 44001.140.
