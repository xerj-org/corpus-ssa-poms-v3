C. CSRS Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA)
Apply offset to CSRS COLAs. The COLA is limited for the first year a federal CS pension
is received. The individual receives one-twelfth of the COLA for each month of the
calendar year that the individual was entitled to the pension. COLAs are paid in full
in succeeding years.
