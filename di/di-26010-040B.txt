B. Procedure for Title II — fully insured for blindness only — denial
Enter regulation basis code “F1” when the claimant:
•
Meets only the fully insured requirement for blindness at the alleged onset date or
later,
•
Alleges a visual impairment, and
•
Has an impairment that does not meet the definition of statutory blindness.
