A. General CPD information
The CPD is the most recent favorable medical decision that the individual was disabled or continued to be disabled.
The most recent favorable medical decision is the latest final determination or decision
involving a consideration of the medical evidence and whether the individual was disabled
or continued to be disabled.
The CPD date is the date that the adjudicator, Administrative Law Judge (ALJ), or
Administrative Appeals Judge (AAJ) signed on the most recent favorable SSA-831, SSA-832/833,
or decision.
