I. What do these terms in the listings mean?
1. Persistent:
Failure to achieve a complete remission.
2. Progressive:
The malignancy became more extensive after treatment.
3. Recurrent, relapse:
A malignancy that had been in complete remission or entirely removed by surgery has
returned.
