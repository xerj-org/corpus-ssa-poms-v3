SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28005.030 - Step-by-Step Discussion of the Title XVI Child Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Sequential Evaluation Process
Effective Dates: 03/24/2026 - Present | TN 31 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428005030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The discussion provides an overview of the Title XVI child CDR process. The 3-step
sequential evaluation process for Title XVI child CDRs is outlined in subsection DI 28005.030C and corresponds to the summary chart provided in DI 28005.025. Additionally, the use of the SSA-538 (Child Disability Evaluation Form or "CDEF")
or the Childhood Disability Evaluation (CDE) is discussed in subsection DI 28005.030D.
