D. Additional considerations for the prior error exception
1. Any prior medical determination or decision
The prior error exception can apply to any medical determination or decision on the
issue of disability, not just the comparison point decision (CPD).
2. No unnecessary development or reviews
While the CPD and supporting evidence must be reviewed in some detail, only develop
the prior error exception if there is substantial evidence that suggests a decisional
error was made on a previous claim. Do not begin to review the CPD (or any other prior
determination or decision) with the intent of finding an error.
