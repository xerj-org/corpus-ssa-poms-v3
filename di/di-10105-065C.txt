C. Prisoner provisions
On October 19, 1980, H.R. 5295 (P.L. 96-473) was signed into law and contained provisions
which imposed restrictions on Title II disability determinations and payments to prisoners
who are convicted of felonies. A summary of these prisoner provisions can be found
in GN 02607.000, DI 10105.100, and DI 10105.105. (These provisions do not apply to Title XVI.)
