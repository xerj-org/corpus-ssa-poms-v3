C. Procedure on Death after filing a title II application
1. Alleged onset date (AOD) more than five months prior to death
a.
All Title II claims in which onset is alleged at least five full months before the
month of death require a disability determination. When a Title II claimant dies before
a disability determination is made, prepare a disability determination.
b.
For cases in which the earliest possible established onset date results in a finding
of death in the waiting period, see DI 23510.001C.2. below.
c. For denial cases in which the impairment did not last 12 months nor was it the
cause of death, complete the claim in the usual manner with the exceptions as outlined
in the chart, DI 23510.020, Death after Filing - Title II (Chart).
NOTE:
The Established Onset Date (EOD) is the first day of the claimant's five month waiting
period. See DI 25501.300A.4.
2. Death in the waiting period
This situation applies only to disability insurance benefit (DIB) and disabled widow(er)
benefit (DWB) claims, when the waiting period applies. There is no waiting period
for childhood disability benefit (CDB) claimants, DIB claimants with amyotrophic lateral
sclerosis (ALS) approved on or after July 23, 2020, or for a DIB or DWB claimant who
had a recent prior period of disability (e.g., previous closed period). For these
cases where the waiting period does not apply, make a disability determination.
For DIB claims see, DI 10105.075A. When the Five Month Waiting Period is not Required
For DWB claims see, DI 10110.001G.1. Requirements for Disabled Widow(er)'s Benefits (DWB)
If the DDS determines there is no possibility of establishing an earlier onset date
(e.g., substantial gainful activity (SGA) stopped at the potential onset date (POD)
due to a traumatic impairment), then process the case as a no determination case according
to instruction on Death After Filing for Title II as outlined in DI 23510.020. For electronic disability insurance benefit (eDib) cases also see, DI 81020.127, Processing “No Determination” Claims.
