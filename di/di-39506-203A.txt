SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39506.203 - Updating and Reconciling Unliquidated Obligations
Effective Dates: 03/12/2002 - Present | TN 30 (03-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439506203 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - current year
Valid unliquidated obligations should be supported by documents/records that describe
the nature of the obligations and support the amounts recorded. It is particularly
important that changes in CE and MER authorizations (e.g., cancellation or modification)
are reflected in the unliquidated obligations reported by the agency. State agencies
should review unliquidated obligations at least once each month to cancel those no
longer valid—and screen CE authorizations to determine whether the unliquidated obligation
represents an authorization still in effect.
