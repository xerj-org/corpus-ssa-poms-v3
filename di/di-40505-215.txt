DI 40505.215 Operating Policy for Title XVI Clear-cut Cessation Cases
The Office of Disability Operations (ODO) does not cease Title XVI cases due to work.
However, a clear-cut cessation applies if all of the clear-cut cessation criteria
in DI 28030.035 meet (Cessation Without Full Medical Development – Clear-Cut Cessations).
