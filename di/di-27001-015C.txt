C. The reconsideration determination
When making the reconsideration determination, we either affirm or reverse the initial
determination.
For information on:
•
The reconsideration SSA-831, see DI 27015.001G.
•
The reconsideration personalized disability explanation (PDE), see DI 27025.000.
•
The reconsideration rationale, see DI 27020.000.
