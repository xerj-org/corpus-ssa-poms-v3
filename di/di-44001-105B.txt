SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 44001.105 - Jurisdiction of Dual Eligibility Claims
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 10 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0444001105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. DCR jurisdiction of dual eligibility claims
DCR has jurisdiction of the following RR related claims:
1. Career railroad case
A disability claim filed by a RR worker who has at least 60 months of creditable RR
service earned after 12/31/95 (or 120 months earned at any time).
2. Dependent RR survivor disability annuitant case
A disability claim filed with SSA by an individual who is also receiving or filing
for a RR disability annuity with RRB on the account of a deceased RR employee. The
disability claim with SSA must be filed on a different Social Security number (SSN) than that of the deceased RR worker.
EXAMPLE:
An individual who is receiving a disabled widows annuity from the RRB files with SSA
for disability insurance benefits (DIB) on her own account, and Supplemental Security
Income (SSI) benefits.
NOTE:
If the spouse or child of a living career RR worker files for DIB or SSI on his or her own SSN, DCR does not have jurisdiction
of the claim. Route the claim to the DDS in the claimant's state of residence, per
DI 11050.010.
3. Exceptions
•
Foreign Address
If the claimant has a foreign address, transfer the claim and all material to the
Office of International Operations, location code V22, for processing. The foreign
address takes precedence over the RR issue in determining jurisdiction. For more information,
refer to DI 43505.075.
•
End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) only
These cases are within the jurisdiction of the Office of Disability Operations PC7.
For more information about ESRD refer to DI 45001.005. For more information about Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Exclusions and Limitations
refer to DI 81010.030.
If DCR receives a claim that is not within its jurisdiction, transfer the claim back
to the FO for proper disposition.
