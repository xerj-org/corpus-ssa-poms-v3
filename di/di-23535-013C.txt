SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23535.013 - Using the Certification of Low Birth Weight for Supplemental Security Income Eligibility (Form SSA-3830)
Effective Dates: 06/11/2015 - Present | TN 33 (11-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423535013 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. DDSs must not substitute other forms for the SSA-3830
Use Form SSA-3830 to obtain information from hospitals in LBW claims. Do not delete,
modify, reword, reorganize, add to, alter, or change the questions in this OMB-approved
form.
