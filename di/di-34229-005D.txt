Effective Dates: 07/21/2015 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434229005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Hyperfunction of the adrenal cortex. Evidence of growth retardation must be documented as described in 100.00. Elevated
blood or urinary free cortisol levels are not acceptable in lieu of urinary 17-hydroxycorticosteroid
excretion for the diagnosis of adrenal cortical hyperfunction.
