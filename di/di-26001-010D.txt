D. No duration requirement for a disability freeze
The duration requirement for statutory blindness under Title II DIB is different for
a disability freeze and cash benefits.
1. Cash benefits
The 12-month duration requirement applies for cash benefits.
2. Disability freeze
There is no duration requirement for a disability freeze. However, because a disability
freeze is also a period of disability, there is a separate requirement that a period
of disability must last at least 5 full calendar months.
