DI 26010.045 Completing Item 23 (Medical Listing No.) on the SSA-831 for Title II and Title XVI
Enter the appropriate medical listing number including any letter and number subsection
(for example, “002.02” or “002.03A”) when:
•
Item 15A, Item 15B, or Item 18B is checked; and
•
The claimant’s impairment meets the level of severity in the Listing of Impairments
at the time of adjudication.
