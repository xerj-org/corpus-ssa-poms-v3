SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34133.013 - Cancer Listings from 7/20/15 to 1/16/17
Effective Dates: 01/13/2017 - Present | TN 1 (01-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434133013 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. What impairments do these listings cover?
We use these listings to evaluate all cancers (malignant neoplastic diseases), except
certain cancers associated with human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection. If you
have HIV infection, we use the criteria in 14.08E to evaluate carcinoma of the cervix,
Kaposi sarcoma, lymphoma, and squamous cell carcinoma of the anal canal and anal margin.
