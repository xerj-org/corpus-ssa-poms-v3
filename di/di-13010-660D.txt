D. Procedure - Reading the QMID Screen
The QMID contains information relating to medical determinations made and the medical
re-exam diary date. The data in the fields is propagated. The coding on this screen
is the normal coding for the fields involved. The second column on the QMID shows
the data the Office of Disability has on file.
