SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24598.003 - Low Birth Weight (LBW)
Effective Dates: 06/21/2022 - Present | TN 4 (06-22)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424598003 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. When is it appropriate to establish LBW as an MDI?
Establish LBW when we have the following documentation recording a qualifying birth
weight:
•
an original or certified copy of the infant’s birth certificate; or
•
a medical record signed by an acceptable medical source.
1. Listing 100.04
Evaluate LBW under listing 100.04 in infants from birth to attainment of age l. If
the infant's MDI meets the requirements for 100.04A and 100.04B, see DI 25235.006 Medical Diary Criteria for Low Birth Weight (LBW) Infants under Title XVI.
Note: Values in the table in 100.04B represent the weight considered small for GA (that
is, less than the 3rd percentile). The table does not include a value for GAs below 31 weeks because the
small for GA value for 31 weeks or below is less than 1200 grams, which is the criterion
in 100.04A. The table also does not include values for GAs above 40 weeks because
the small for GA value is above 2000 grams, which is the maximum birth weight considered
under 100.04B.
2. Evaluating LBW under listing 100.04B with a GA reported in weeks and days
a.
The table in listing 100.04B does not describe GA in weeks and days; therefore, a
GA in weeks and days, and an accompanying birth weight that is less than the 3rd percentile for that GA, is a finding of equivalent severity and duration to the listing
requirement, which means the impairment medically equals the listing.
