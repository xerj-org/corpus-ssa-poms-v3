B. How to apply the error on the face of record exception
In deciding whether this error category applies, only consider evidence on the record
at the time of the prior determination or decision. For additional reminders about
consideration of prior error, see DI 28020.350C through DI 28020.350E, Group I Exception - Prior Error Overview.
