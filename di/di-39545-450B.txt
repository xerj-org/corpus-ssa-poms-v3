SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.450 - Independent Consultative Examination (CE) Report Review System
Effective Dates: 04/20/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
•
Develop specific instructions for review of the types of CE reports outlined in DI 39545.450A.
•
Provide feedback to the CE provider whose report was found deficient.
•
Provide ways to correct the problem.
•
Ensure review for a reasonable period until the problem is corrected, or until the
DDS is sure proper notice has been given. If the problem is not corrected, the CE
provider should be removed from the panel per State procedures.
