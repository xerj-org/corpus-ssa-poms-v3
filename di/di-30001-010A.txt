SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 30001.010 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Quality Assurance (QA) Authority and Responsibilities
Effective Dates: 11/23/1993 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0430001010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Authority
The DDS Administrator has the authority and flexibility to design a QA function best
suited to the needs of the agency and assigns roles accordingly. For additional information
on the guidelines for providing a QA function, see DI 39515.001.
