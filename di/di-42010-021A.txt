SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 42010.021 - Completing Form SSA-833 in Favorable Administrative Law Judge Decisions (Continuing Disability Review)
Effective Dates: 11/28/2023 - Present | TN 24 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0442010021 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Effectuating reversals of cessations
When an administrative law judge (ALJ) issues a fully or partially favorable Continuing
Disability Review (CDR) decision, effectuate the decision using Form SSA-833. The
processing center (PC) completes Form SSA-833 using information in the decision and
in our electronic systems which include eView, Evidence Portal (EP), Modernized Claims
System (MCS), Case Processing and Management System (CPMS), and Hearings and Appeals
Case Processing System (HACPS).
