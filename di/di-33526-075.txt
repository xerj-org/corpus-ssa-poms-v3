DI 33526.075 Impairment Codes for Low Birth Weight and Failure to Thrive
Use the impairment codes in this section to identify Low Birth Weight (LBW) and Failure
to Thrive (FTT). See the reminders in DI 33526.075C for codes with an asterisk (*).
NOTE: Low Birth Weight and Failure to Thrive does not have any listings in Part A.
