A. Policy for repairs and alterations for DDS office space
1. Rate for service and maintenance
Repairs and maintenance may be included in the rate for service and maintenance.
If privately owned, it is either included in the lease or direct billed by the landlord.
If this is publicly owned, it is either direct billed or indirect costs.
2. Amortization for repairs and replacements
Major repairs and replacements may be amortized
over a period of years provided the total costs
for space on an annual basis do not exceed comparable rental costs.
3. Alterations
Depending on the terms of the lease, SSA funds will be authorized to pay for necessary
alterations when the:
a.
Landlord is unwilling to pay for necessary alterations;
b.
Improvements are not the landlord's responsibility under terms of the lease; and
c.
Alterations are needed to:
•
improve utilization of space,
•
meet environmental or security specifications, or
•
house special equipment.
EXAMPLE:Computer or word processing equipment.
CAUTION:Electronic data processing (EDP) equipment often has special environmental or structural
needs. To lessen alteration costs, select buildings which can reasonably be expected
to meet EDP needs without major alterations.
4. Disability Adjudication (DA) prior approval
a.
DA
approval must be obtained before the DDS can enter into agreements to:
•
amortize
the cost of alterations by agreeing to an increase in rent for a stipulated period
of time; or
•
pay for
repairs or alterations in order to obtain lower rental rates.
b.
DA approval is not needed
for minor alterations not exceeding $1,000 providing:
•
such alterations are not the responsibility of the landlord; and
•
sufficient obligational authority is available.
