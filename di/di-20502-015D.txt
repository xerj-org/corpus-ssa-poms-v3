D. SSA-3368 (Disability Report - Adult)
This report is used for all adult Title II initial claims Disability Insurance Benefits
(DIB), Disabled Widow’s Benefits (DWB), Childhood Disability Benefits (CDB), including
Disabled Minor Child (DMC) and all adult Title XVI initial disability or blindness
claims. (See DI
11005.023.) This report is also used for Title XVI age 18 redeterminations.
