E. Employers’ Reinsurance Fund (ERF)
1. Types of payments made by ERF
•
PT payments in excess of 312 weeks for injuries occurring before 07/01/1994
•
Second injury PT payments in excess of 156 weeks for injuries occurring from 07/01/1988
to 06/30/1994.
•
ERF payments are offsettable
2. Amount and frequency of ERF payments
•
Prior to 01/01/2000: ERF payments were paid every 28 days.
•
Effective 01/01/2000: ERF payments are paid on a monthly basis.
•
ERF payment for December 1999: This payment was adjusted to reflect any additional
monies due because of the conversion from a 28-day payment cycle to a monthly payment
cycle. The payment included the additional days due from the end of the 28-day cycle
to December 31st.
EXAMPLE: If the weekly WC rate was $225.00, the $900.00 every 28 day payments would switch
to $975.00 per month payments as of January 2000 (which would not be a rate change
- even though it might look like one on a payment history printout).
•
Payments to a worker receiving the minimum ERF PT payment may be readjusted each July
1 based on changes in the State average weekly wage.
