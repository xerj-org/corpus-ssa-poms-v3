SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 41001.020 - Work Comparability Provisions for Blind DIB, CDB, or DWB Individuals, Age 55 and Older
Effective Dates: 07/11/2012 - Present | TN 1 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0441001020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Applications
A blind DIB, CDB, or DWB individual entitled to disability benefit status under the
special work comparability provisions whose benefits are suspended due to SGA need
not file a new application when they cease SGA in order to receive disability benefits.
(This contrasts with the requirement for blind workers in regular “freeze” status only [CDB and DWB claimants are not eligible for “freeze” ], who have been denied for cash benefit purposes because of SGA, and who must, therefore,
file a new application when SGA ceases in order to receive disability benefits.)
