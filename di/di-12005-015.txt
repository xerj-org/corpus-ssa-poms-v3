DI 12005.015 Late Filing of a Request for Reconsideration
The field office (FO) makes a good cause determination when a request for reconsideration
in a disability case is filed late (see Time Limit for Filing an Appeal GN 03101.010). Consider a request for reconsideration to have been filed timely if the claimant
can show that he or she had good cause for missing the 60-day time limit.
