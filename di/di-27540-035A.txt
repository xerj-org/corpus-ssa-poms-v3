SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 27540.035 - Concurrent Title II/Title XVI – Statutory Benefit Continuation Does Not Apply
Effective Dates: 02/17/2011 - Present | TN 3 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0427540035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Goldberg/Kelly automatic payment continuation governs the following types of reopenings
and revisions:
•
Failure to follow prescribed treatment; and
•
Fraud or similar fault.
For information on the cases where the Disability Determination Services (DDS) sends
a predetermination due process notice, see Policy – Due Process for Adverse Reopenings
DI 27525.001C.

DI 27525.010 Providing Due Process in Adverse Reopenings Involving Title XVI and Concurrent Title

•

•

NOTE: Goldberg/Kelly procedures do not apply to Title XVI whereabouts unknown cases because
WU (S06) is considered an administrative suspension and does not result in a loss
of eligibility.
