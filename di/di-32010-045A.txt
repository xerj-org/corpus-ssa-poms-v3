A. Procedure for handling folder routing
When an individual is participating in a Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) or similar
program, eView will display a 'Potential Section 301 Case.' A 'Potential Section 301
Case' flag generates an alert to the field office (FO) via the Electronic Disability
Collect System (EDCS) Actions Page: 'CDR Cessation Added - Potential 301 Benefit Continuation.'
•
If the case is a certified electronic folder (CEF) routed from the FO to the Disability
Determination Services (DDS), add a 'Potential Section 301 Case' flag in the EDCS
to identify that the beneficiary is participating in a VR or similar program. See
DI 81020.085 Certified Electronic Folder Flags (CEF), and DI 14510.010 Procedures for Cases Involving Participation in Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) or
Similar Program.
•
For paper cases, the DDS alerts the FO of the cessation with the remark on an SSA-408
(Route Slip), attached to the outside of the folder annotated with a 'Potential Section
301 Benefits flag - handle per DI 14510.003C.'
