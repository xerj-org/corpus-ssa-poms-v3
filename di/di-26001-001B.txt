B. Meeting a listing for statutory blindness
A claimant has statutory blindness only if the visual disorder meets the criteria
of listings:
•
2.02 or 2.03A for adults, or
•
102.02A, 102.02B, or 102.03A for children.
A claimant does not have statutory blindness if the visual disorder:
•
medically equals the criteria of listings 2.02, 2.03A, 102.02A, 102.02B, or 102.03A;
or
•
meets or medically equals the criteria of 2.03B, 2.03C, 2.04A, 2.04B, 102.03B, 102.03C,
102.04A, or 102.04B.
