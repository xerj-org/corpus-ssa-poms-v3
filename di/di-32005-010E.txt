E. Destination Disability Quality Branch (DQB)
Enter DQB on the To line followed by the City name for the DQB in the SSA region where
your State is located.
When preparing the SSA-408 to route the review case to the DQB, always include a remark
that specifies the type of review that the case was selected for, e.g., PER CASE.
