SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 41005.015 - Work Issue CDR: All Statutorily Blind Individuals Except DIB, CDB, and DWB Persons Age 55 or Older
Effective Dates: 07/11/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0441005015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
The TWP and EPE provisions of the law that apply to nonblind individuals also apply
to these persons. Use the monetary guides in DI 10501.015 and the evaluation guides in DI 41001.010 and DI 41001.015 for assessing SGA.
