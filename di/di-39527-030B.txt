B. Procedure for office space budgeting
1. Identify space locations
Follow these guidelines for identifiable space locations:
a.
Submit an analysis of base year expenditures and the budget estimate.
b.
Include the following elements for each location:
•
total rental costs,
•
number of square feet of space,
•
cost-per-square foot, and
•
services included in rental cost.
2. Identify major items of expense for office maintenance
a.
Break out the major items of expense in the analysis of base year expenditures and the budget
estimate for office maintenance.
EXAMPLE: Light, heat, and janitor service.
b.
Specify whether office maintenance, in whole or in part, is included
in the agency's rental contract.
c.
Specify in the budget submittal if office maintenance is not included.
