C. Concurrent cases - no additional action required
Route both cases to the NRC (Office code N88) at the address shown above code. In paper cases, if a Form SSA-4387 (SSI Folder
Return) flag is on top of the folder, DO NOT REMOVE. If another flag or form must be placed on top of the folder (for example, an SSA-408 (Route Slip), attach the additional flag or form on top of the SSA-4387.
