B. Routing other disability determination services (DDS) jurisdiction cases
If the DDS receives a case not in its jurisdiction, redirect the case to the appropriate
office, as follows:
1.
If the case is addressed to another DDS, forward the case immediately;
2.
If the case is addressed to the receiving DDS, but another DDS has jurisdiction:
a.
Change all copies of the transmittal to the appropriate DDS,
b.
Notify the FO of the transfer, and
c.
Forward the case to the appropriate DDS, via an SSA-408.
3.
If the receiving DDS cannot determine the appropriate DDS of jurisdiction, telephone
the Regional Office for resolution of the jurisdictional question.
4.
If the FO discovers a case is not in the DDS jurisdiction, the FO will telephone the
DDS and request you to return the file, or provide forwarding instructions.
