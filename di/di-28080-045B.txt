B. Diary Controls and Final Actions
The adjudicator will:
•
Set appropriate diary controls and take action to finalize the determination.
•
Not process the case before 15 days (10 days, plus five days for mailing time) expires
unless the adjudicator receives a written statement from the individual stating that
he or she will not submit additional evidence.
•
Complete the determination if there is no response in 15 days. The DDS does not follow
up with an individual who fails to respond to the letter.
•
Transfer electronic folders to the field office (FO) of record.
•
Send paper folders to the Office of Disability Operations (ODO) or the appropriate
program service center (PSC). For CDR routing instructions, see DI 32010..001 through
DI 32010.085.
