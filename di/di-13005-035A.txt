SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13005.035 - Exceptions to the Personal Contact
Effective Dates: 08/03/2023 - Present | TN 18 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413005035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A personal contact is recommended, but not required, in each CDR case to help ensure
that beneficiaries have a full opportunity to present their cases. The contact should
be made with:
•
the beneficiary; if there is no mental impairment; or either
•
the beneficiary only or both the beneficiary and the payee, if there is one, when
the beneficiary has a physical impairment only; or
•
the payee only, if there is one, when the beneficiary has a mental impairment.
