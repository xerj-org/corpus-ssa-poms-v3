DI 52170.025 Rounding Rules for Offset Computations
There are rounding rules to consider when manually computing benefit amounts in workers’
compensation (WC) and public disability benefit (PDB) offset cases. Different rules
apply to periods before 06/1982 and periods beginning 06/1982. It is critical to use
the rounding rules correctly to ensure accurate benefit amounts. For more information,
see RS 00601.020 - Rounding of Benefits.
