A. Background – Childhood disability eligibility at attainment of age 18
Pursuant to Public Law (P.L.) 104-193, all title XVI childhood disability recipients
who attained age 18 on or after August 22, 1996, and were eligible as children in
the month before the month in which they attained age 18, must have their eligibility
redetermined under the adult standards for initial claims.
Use the adult disability criteria for initial claims to make disability redeterminations,
excluding the first step of the sequential evaluation, process, substantial gainful
activity (SGA).
NOTE: The medical improvement review standard (MIRS) does not apply in Age 18 redetermination
cases.
