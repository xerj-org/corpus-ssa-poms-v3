B. When the individual is working and the work has not been addressed
When the DDS identifies the individual as working and the work has not been addressed:
•
Send an assistance request (AR) to the field office (FO) requesting substantial gainful
activity (SGA) development. The DDS may continue evidence development; however, the
DDS adjudicator cannot make a favorable determination until the work issue is resolved.
•
If the DDS evidence development is complete and the evidence does not support a fully
favorable determination, the DDS does not hold the case until FO completes the work
development. The DDS forwards the case to the DHU following instructions in DI 29025.001.
