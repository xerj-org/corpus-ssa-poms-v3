Effective Dates: 04/22/2026 - Present | TN 32 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440510055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A currently entitled title II beneficiary may file a title XVI claim. The FO is instructed
to adopt the title II allowance. Therefore, there must be coordination between the
reviewer and the FO so that the title II allowance is not adopted to the title XVI
application when the reviewer is preparing to cease title II benefits.
