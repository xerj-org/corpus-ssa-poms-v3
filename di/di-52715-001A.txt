Effective Dates: 09/18/2012 - Present |

A. Introduction
This subchapter provides field office (FO), disability determination services (DDS),
and processing center (PC) case processing instructions to be applied in implementing
AR 00-1(4) acquiescing in the Fourth Circuit Court of Appeals" decision in Albright v. Commissioner of the Social Security Administration.
The Albright AR differs from the Lively AR in that under the Albright AR, findings contained in a final decision by an Administrative Law Judge or the
Appeals Council on a prior disability claim are to be treated as evidence and given
appropriate weight in light of all relevant facts and circumstances when determining
disability with respect to an unadjudicated period involved in a subsequent disability
claim. In determining the weight to be given to a prior finding, the adjudicator will
consider, among other factors, whether the fact on which a prior finding was based
is subject to change with the passage of time. This differs significantly from the
requirements of the Lively AR which provided, in general, that the adjudicator of a subsequent claim must adopt
a prior finding regarding residual functional capacity or other finding required at
a step of the sequential evaluation process absent new and material evidence.
