E. Lump sum payments
Any interested party may apply for WC benefits to be paid in one or more lump sum
payments. If the LS payment fully discharges the employer’s WC liability, the payment
meets SSA’s definition of a lump sum for proration purposes. For more information,
see DI 52150.060 - Prorating a Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefits (WC/PDB) Lump Sum.
