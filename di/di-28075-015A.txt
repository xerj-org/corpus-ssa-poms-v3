SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28075.015 - Rollback Conversion Cases
Effective Dates: 10/20/2015 - Present | TN 7 (10-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428075015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for rollback conversion cases
Evaluate solely under the Federal definition of disability and blindness those individuals
who were eligible for State Aid to the Permanently and Totally Disabled or Aged or
Blind in 12/1973, but who did not receive aid for at least 1 month prior to 07/1973,
i.e., rollback cases.
