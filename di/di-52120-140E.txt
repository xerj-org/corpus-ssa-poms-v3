E. Time limitations for filing claims
The DWC must receive claims within:
•
Two years after the date of the injury or within two years after the individual receives
his or her last WC payment;
•
Two years after an occupational disease was reasonably discoverable; or
•
Three years after the date of the injury or the last WC payment made, if the employer
did not timely report the injury or illness to the Division.
