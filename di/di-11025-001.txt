DI 11025.001 Introduction to Disability Determinations for Entitlement to a Non-Disability Payment
Some Title II entitlements require a disability allowance before paying a non-disability
benefit to a Retirement and Survivors Insurance (RSI) beneficiary. We discuss three
situations in this section.
