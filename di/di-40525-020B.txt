B. Policy
The Processing Center (PC) is responsible for obtaining/securing the title II or the
title II portion of a concurrent title II/XVI medical folder(s) so the continuing
disability review (CDR) can be initiated. The field office (FO) is responsible for
obtaining/securing the title XVI medical folder(s).
Studies have shown when every effort is made to find a lost folder, in most cases
the folder is found.
The instructions in this subchapter discuss the PC actions in this folder recall process.
