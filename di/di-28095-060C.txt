C. Concurrent Title II and Title XVI CDR notice
1. Title XVI in current pay status
The DDS is responsible for notifying the recipient of the determinations made on both
cases. In concurrent Title II and Title XVI continuing disability cases in which,
Title XVI payments are in current pay status and there is a determination of continuance,
complete Item 29 on the form SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
– Title XVI). Complete Item 29 of the form SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
–Title II). For the Title XVI portion of the case, follow the instructions in DI 28095.060B in this section. For the Title II portion of the case, follow the instruction in
DI 28095.060A in this section.
2. Title XVI in non-pay status
Concurrent Title II and Title XVI cases in which the Title XVI case is in non-pay
status, the Title II case requires a notice but not for the Title XVI case, see DI 28095.060A in this section . Enter “NLN” in Item 29 of Form SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance
of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal-Title XVI).
NOTE: If a continuance implies a change from “blind” to “disabled”, see DI 28080.065.
