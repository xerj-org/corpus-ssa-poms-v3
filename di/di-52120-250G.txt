G. WC Forms
Once approved by the WC Division, Vermont WC forms awarding payments become binding
agreements and (absent evidence of fraud or material mistake of fact) the parties
are deemed to have waived their right to contest them. A claimant's failure or refusal
to sign a compensation agreement offered by the employer does not preclude their right
to continuing compensation benefits.
1.
Form 2, Denial of Workers’ Compensation Benefits by Employer or
Carrier: This form is sent to the worker when their claim is denied, and can be used to appeal
that denial.
2.
Form
10,
Certificate of Dependency and
Concurrent
Employment:
This form must be filed by any worker claiming WC indemnity payments, even when they
have no dependents. It is used to determine his right to the additional weekly WC
of $10.00 for each dependent child under 21.
3.
Form 13, Report of Benefits and Related Expenses Paid: This form is filed on about July 1 each year, and also within 60 days after the final
payment of medical or indemnity benefits. It specifically states the WC amounts paid
to date.
4.
Form 16, Compromise
Agreement: This form is used to settle disputes over the compensability of a claim or the extent
of benefits due. Once executed by the parties and approved by the WC commissioner,
it relieves the employer of all further liability for WC benefits related to the injury.
5.
Form 22, Agreement for Permanent Partial
or Permanent Total
Disability
Compensation: Used when the worker is deemed to have suffered a permanent impairment as a result
of their work-related injury or occupational disease.
6.
Form 25M, Memorandum of Payment: This form must be filed whenever a worker receives more than 90 calendar days of continuous
TT.
7.
Form 27,
Employer's
Notice of Intention to Discontinue Payments: Once the carrier determines that the worker has reached maximum medical improvement
and so is no longer entitled to either TT or TP WC, they are required by law to notify
both the worker and the WC Department of their intention to discontinue weekly temporary
benefits by filing this form. Temporary WC cannot be terminated until this form is
received by both the WC commissioner and the claimant. If the claimant is represented
by counsel, a copy of the notice must also be sent to their attorney. A determination
must then be made within 45 days as to whether the claimant has any permanent impairment
as a result of the work injury.
8.
Form
28, Notice of
Change in Compensation Rate for Injuries after 7/1/1986 and Form
28A,
Notice of Change in Compensation
Rate for Injuries
Prior to 7/1/1986: These forms are issued each year for the COLA increases in the weekly WC rates beginning
July 1. Form 28 is used for injuries after 7/1/1986 and Form 28A is used for injuries
before 7/1/1986. Form 28A is only available by request by contacting Trudy Smith at
(802) 828-2991 or via email at trudy.smith@vermont.gov.
9.
Form 32, Agreement
for Temporary
Compensation: In all cases in which temporary total and/or temporary partial disability benefits
are owed, the employer or insurance carrier shall enter into an Agreement for Temporary
Compensation (Form 32) with the injured worker, and shall begin paying benefits immediately.
Once executed by the parties, the completed Agreement shall be filed with the Commissioner
for review and approval.
