A. Intake and clearance
The disability determination services (DDS) must record the intake to and clearance
from the DDS for each case. Use the systems facility in the DDS to transmit data to
the appropriate SSA system after the examiner has completed the determination (or
no determination).
Disability examiner involvement in systems processing varies from state-to-state.
