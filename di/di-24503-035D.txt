SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 24503.035 - Evaluation and Articulation Requirements for Medical Opinions, Opinions, and Prior Administrative Medical Findings - Claims Filed before March 27, 2017
Effective Dates: 03/24/2017 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424503035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. When we evaluate this evidence
Do not consider medical opinions, opinions, or prior administrative medical findings
when establishing a medically determinable impairment (MDI). However, once an MDI
is established, consider medical opinions, opinions, and prior administrative medical
findings for all other findings in a claim, as appropriate.
