D. Part II — Disability Status
It is important that the information about the disability status be provided to enable
us to determine if reimbursement to a VR agency is proper.
1. Continuing Block
Check this block if the disability is continuing.
2. Ceased Block
Show the month and the year of cessation (not termination).
3. Basis For Cessation
a. Medical—Medical Reexamination Diary Set
Check this block if the evidence shows the case was ceased on a medical basis. The
“Yes” block means there was a diary set. The “No” block means there was no diary.
b. Other
If the basis for the cessation was for a reason other than a Medical cessation, check
the “other” block and indicate the basis for cessation and the month benefits/payments stopped.
