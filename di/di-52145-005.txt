DI 52145.005 Obtaining Verification/Proof from Outside Sources
This section explains how to identify possible sources of WC/PDB information and how
to obtain the necessary information from WC/PDB carriers and other outside sources
if the number holder (NH) or attorney cannot or will not provide verification.
