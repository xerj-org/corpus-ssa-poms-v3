SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11015.015 - Field Office (FO) Processing of a Disabled Widow(er)s Claim for an Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Exclusion or Limitation Case
Effective Dates: 04/09/2012 - Present | TN 31 (10-10)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411015015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Transfer the claim to the office of jurisdiction for a medical determination
Transfer material to the DDS promptly for a medical determination.
In concurrently filed MCS- and EDCS-exclusion cases, when you develop and forward
both claims simultaneously (e.g., DIB-DWB), prepare a paper SSA-831 for each claim
and transfer for a medical determination.
If you do not transfer both claims simultaneously:
•
release the other case immediately to the DDS, and
•
include an RPOC in each folder regarding the development status of the other claim.
