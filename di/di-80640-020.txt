DI 80640.020 Electronic Folder (EF)
The EF is SSA's electronic disability repository that stores the claimant’s disability
file information. Once the electronic disability process has been implemented nationally,
all case processing components (e.g., FOs, DDS, DPBs, DQBs, ODAR, etc.) will have
the capability to access, view and exchange case related information with the EF.
At the present, the EF will be accompanied by a paper MDF. All documents that are
stored in the EF must also be filed in the MDF.
