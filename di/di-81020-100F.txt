F. Receiving AR
DDS, ODAR or OQR offices make an electronic AR by electronically completing and transmitting
a:
•
SSA-883 (Request for Evidence or Assistance);
•
HA-4488 (Request for DDS Assistance in Obtaining Reports);
•
HA-4489 (Request for DDS Assistance in Obtaining CE); or
•
SSA-5526 (Request for Assistance)
To process an electronic AR:
•
Review the Alerts and Messages tab of eView to determine if any Messages were added
by the initiating office that paper documentation is associated with the AR;
•
Review the CEF for the SSA-883, HA-4488, or HA-4489;
•
Receipt and assign the assistance request;
NOTE: If associating paper documentation with the AR, do NOT receipt the AR until receiving the paper documentation;
•
Take the appropriate action needed per the SSA-883, HA-4488, or HA-4489;
•
If needing an SSA-827 for development, print a copy of the SSA-827 stored in the CEF;
•
If scheduling a CE for ODAR or another DDS, make sure to categorize the appropriate
background material; and
•
Upload all development and documentation to the CEF which sends an alert to the initiating
office indicating that documents were added to the case.
NOTE: Because the receiving office only has LAC of the CEF, they are not notified if new
documents are added to the CEF or if messages are added to the CEF. In addition, the
receiving office is not notified that the AR has been canceled by the initiating office
unless both the initiating and receiving office have the full AR capability.
