B. Policy – disputes from a State VR agency about a cost-reimbursement payment issue
related to a beneficiary/recipient with a ticket
SSA has in place dispute resolution mechanisms for issues involving:
•
disputes on the amount that SSA pays to a State VR agency for serving a beneficiary/recipient
who completes a continuous period of substantial gainful activity (SGA); and
•
disputes on whether there was a continuous period of SGA and whether the services
the State VR agency provided contributed to the continuous period.
ORDES is the SSA component that has the lead on these issues.
