B. Exhibit 2 - Disallowance Notice - 1619(b) Medicaid determination for aged individual
Disallowance notice language
You asked us to see if you are   (1) under our rules so that we can make your Supplemental Security Income (SSI) case active
again.
We are writing to tell you that you are not   (1)   under our rules. Therefore, we cannot make your SSI case active again.
This decision is based on a review of your health problems. We have enclosed a page
that gives you more details on how we made our decision.
Although we cannot make your SSI case active again, you may still qualify for Medicaid.
If you have any questions about Medicaid, you should contact (name of Medicaid agency).
Fill-ins:
(1)
Choice 1 - blind
Choice 2 - disabled
