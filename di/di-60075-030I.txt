I. Fraud issues and the POD
Normal processing procedures apply for all fraud cases. A POD participant will remain
in the POD during the investigation and processing of fraud allegations. However,
if the decision involves removing earnings that affected the offset decision, we will
modify our decisions accordingly. For more information on handling fraud, see DI 11006.025.
