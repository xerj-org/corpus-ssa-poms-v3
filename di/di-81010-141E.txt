E. Work issues
Limited work development is necessary if work issues that would impact the period
of eligibility are found after the DDS medical determination. Review the earnings
record, NDNH, SEQY and/or DEQY.
1. Work determined not SGA
If work is determined not to be SGA, process the determinations per DI 12010.025 – Action Following a Favorable Determination by the Disability Determination Service
(DDS) in Informal Remand Cases.
2. Work determined SGA
If work is determined to be SGA:
•
Document the CEF with the findings using eForms SSA-5002, and
•
Fax a copy of the SSA-5002 to the originating HO. This is the only way the HO will
know they must follow existing procedures to process the hearing.
