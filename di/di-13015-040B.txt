Effective Dates: 12/09/2013 - Present | TN 1 (01-01)

B. Procedure – Previous SSANRC or FRCs Search
DO NOT CONTACT SSANRC if the DCF has already directly requested the folder and this request
resulted in the:
1.
Posting of an L50/L51 (unable to locate) folder location; or.
2.
Receipt in the FO of alert message "Folder/PE Recall Requested Unable to Locate";
or.
3.
Receipt in the FO of a post eligibility (PE) material folder (no medical evidence),
since SSANRC has already searched for and sent all folders and related PE material
that they and the FRCs could find within their offices under the requested SSN.
4.
Follow DI 13015.035B. and DI 13015.080.
