D. Policy - Indirect Costs
Indirect costs include obligations for expenses incurred under a written Indirect
Cost Negotiation Agreement (ICNA) approval, or an approved State-wide Cost Allocation
Plan (SWCAP)—also known as Central Services Cost Allocation Plan (CSCAP). (See DI 39506.300 for additional instructions regarding State Indirect Cost Agreements.)
