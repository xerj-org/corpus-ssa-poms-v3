B. Pre-hearing processing of a request for reconsideration with a new application
IF...
THEN...
A fully favorable reconsideration determination is not issued, but a new period of
disability is established based on the new claim and the new claim is fully favorable.
•
The pre-hearing (PH) unit will process the new claim independent of the reconsideration
case, using initial case instructions,
•
Document the case folder to show the period of disability established based on the
new application, and
•
Prepare the reconsideration for transmittal to the disability hearing unit (DHU).
Be sure the folder sent to the DHU has copies of the evidence in the new case.
A fully favorable reconsideration determination is not issued, and a new period of
disability cannot be established on the new claim or the new claim is not fully favorable.
Prepare both the reconsideration case and the new claim for transmittal to the DHU.
A fully favorable reconsideration determination is issued, and a new period of disability
cannot be established on the new claim or the new claim is not fully favorable.
This is potential collateral estoppel. The PH unit will issue the reconsideration
determination and complete a No Determination (ND) on the new claim with the remark
"FO determination requested." See DI 27515.001 for additional instructions on message or SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) completion.
A fully favorable reconsideration determination is issued, and the new claim is fully
favorable.
•
The PH unit will issue both determinations.
•
Exception: If the cases are the same type and the same benefit and both determinations
are fully favorable, this is considered a duplicate claim. The PH unit will issue
the reconsideration determination and complete a No Determination (ND) on the new
claim with the remark "Not processed- duplicate claim." Forward the cases to the field
office (FO) following normal operating instructions.
