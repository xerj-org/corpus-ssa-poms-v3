H. Verifying WC
Follow verification procedures in POMS subchapter DI 52145.001F.3. If the Kentucky Division of Workers’ Compensation Funds (formerly the Special Fund)
is involved, fax a request to them (see link to this Fund under DI 52120.100I. References, below). The fax number is located on their website and there is no charge
for records. If the Kentucky Department of Workers’ Claims is involved, there is a
charge of up to $.50 per page for documents. Develop through the servicing field office.
(There is no longer an agreement between field office #441 in Frankfort, Kentucky,
and the Kentucky Department of Workers’ Claims to obtain WC verification free of charge.)
