A. Disability Determination Services (DDS) jurisdiction
Jurisdiction is with the DDS in the State of the wage earner’s permanent residence
immediately before his/her death. If the wage earner did not have a permanent residence,
or a permanent residence is not obvious, determine DDS jurisdiction by the residence
of the applicant.
