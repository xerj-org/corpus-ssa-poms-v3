SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52145.005 - Obtaining Verification/Proof from Outside Sources
Effective Dates: 04/04/2018 - Present | TN 7 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452145005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Federal WC
Obtain verification for Federal WC from:
1.
The Office of Workers’ Compensation Payments (part of DOL) servicing the State in
which the NH is receiving payments for Federal Employees Compensation Act (FECA) See
DI 52115.010.
2.
The Office of Workers’ Compensation Payments or the insurance carrier for the Longshoreman
and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act (LHWCA). See DI 52115.005.
