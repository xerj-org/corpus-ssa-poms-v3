SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52155.020 - Disability Claims with a Prior Period of Disability with Workers' Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB)
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452155020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. WC/PDB periodic payments continue from prior period of DIB
•
Redetermine the first possible month of offset for a new period of DIB. For an explanation
of the first possible month of offset, see DI 52150.020.
•
Establish a new Total Family Benefit (TFB) based on the new period of entitlement.
For complete information about the TFB, see DI 52150.005.
•
Establish a new Average Current Earnings (ACE) based on the year of onset of the new
DIB period. For instructions on determining the ACE, see DI 52150.010.
•
Use the year in which WC/PDB offset is imposed in the new period of DIB to determine
the year of a triennial redetermination of the ACE. For rules on triennial redetermination
of the ACE, see DI 52150.080.
