SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28087.035 - Preparation and Routing of Title XVI Child Referrals to the Designated State Agency
Effective Dates: 06/04/2024 - Present | TN 2 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428087035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - material to be attached
Attach legible copies of the following material:
1.
Form SSA-1994, Cover Sheet (Confidential Information).
2.
Form SSA-831-U5, The Disability Determination.
3.
Form SSA-3820, “Medical History and Disability Report -- Widow, Widower, Surviving
Divorced Wife, or Disabled Child”.
4.
Current Medical Reports
5.
Any other information considered useful for social, developmental, educational, medical
and rehabilitative services.
