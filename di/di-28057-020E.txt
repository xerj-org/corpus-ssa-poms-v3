E. Procedure for failure to cooperate
If the individual does not cooperate, follow the Failure to Cooperate procedures in
DI 28075.005 and take the following actions:
1. Complete the SSA-832-C3/U3 or an SSA-833-C3/U3, per DI 28057.020F; and
2. Prepare and send the denial notice. Include the personalized explanation with the
rationale for the finding.
3. Close out the EXR in DCPS. NDDSS will show a System Override code of "N".
4. Scan all documentation in the electronic folder.
