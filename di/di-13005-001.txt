DI 13005.001 Field Office (FO) Procedures for Medical Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
When the Social Security Administration (SSA) determines a review of an individuals
medical condition is necessary, we establish a medical issue for the CDR on the Disability
Control File (DCF).
When conducting a CDR, the FO will:
•
identify and process possible medical recovery issues raised at the FO level,
•
obtain the prior paper folder(s) (if any) that contain the most recent favorable medical
decision also known as the comparison point decision (CPD) to associate with the current
CDR,
•
conduct a personal interview (when necessary)
•
establish and transfer an electronic continuing disability review (eCDR) in the electronic
disability collect system (EDCS) unless an exclusion applies, and
•
secure information and development requests by other components (Disability Determination
Services (DDS), Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR), Office of Disability
Operations (ODO), Program Service Center-Disability Case Review, (PSC-DCR), and the
Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO)).
NOTE: For the FO procedures involving personal contact and exceptions to personal contact
see, DI 13005.030. For procedures on creating an eCDR, see DI 81010.230. For cases in EDCS with electronic exclusions and limitations see, DI 81010.030.
