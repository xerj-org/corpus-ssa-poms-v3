B. Lump Sum (LS) payments
WC Commission's decision is final unless a petition for review is filed within 20
days. The Commission's decision upon review is final unless either party files for
judicial review within 30 days. In addition, for the period of one year, the WC Commission
can review a compensation order but only for a change in condition or mistake in fact.
