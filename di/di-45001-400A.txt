A. General
On December 27, 2020, the President signed the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021
(CAA 2021) that includes Medicare enrollment changes. Section 402 of the Extended
Months of Coverage of Immunosuppressive Drugs for Kidney Transplant Patients and Other
Renal Dialysis Provisions (Part B-ID), allows a person who does not have certain other
health insurance coverage that includes immunosuppressive drugs to enroll in Part
B-ID. Enrollment in Medicare Parts A and B for people who have End-Stage Renal Disease
(ESRD) generally ends 36 months after a successful kidney transplant. This provision
allows Part B-ID beyond the original 36 months. Coverage begins January 1, 2023.
