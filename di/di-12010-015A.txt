A. Policy for informal remand of medical/vocational determinations
Informal remand is a procedure that provides a prehearing review by the adjudicating
component (i.e. the DDS) if there is a strong likelihood of reversal of the reconsideration
determination. The field office (FO) may determine whether informal remand criteria
apply.
