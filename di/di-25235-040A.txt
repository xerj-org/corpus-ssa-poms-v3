A. Referral of title XVI child applicants
The disability determination service (DDS) will refer all allowed blind and disabled
children under age 16 to the agency administering the State program under Title V
of the Social Security Act or other agency designated by the State for social, developmental,
educational, medical, and rehabilitative services.
