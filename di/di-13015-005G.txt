SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 13015.005 - Failure to Cooperate-Insufficient Evidence Decision (FTC) Suspension Procedures for Continuing Disability Reviews (CDR)- Field Office (FO)
Effective Dates: 10/21/2024 - Present | TN 26 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0413015005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. FTC- medical and work issues combined
When both work and medical issues are involved, prepare a work issue determination
whenever possible based on available evidence. If the work issue determination does
not result in a termination of benefits, proceed with the medical CDR. If the beneficiary
does not cooperate with the FO's request for information, take the necessary steps
to place the beneficiary in suspense following the procedures in subsection B in this
section.
