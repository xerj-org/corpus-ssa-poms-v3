DI 28065.030 Disability Determinations - Insufficient Evidence
If sufficient evidence on which to base a disability decision cannot be secured because
the Title II beneficiary is confined to a penal institution, prepare an SSA-833-U5
to continue the period of disability and enter in item 24: “Prisoner Case – Resolution
of CD issue required upon reinstatement of benefits.”
