C. Process – FO actions in securing the Title II folder(s)
NOTE: FOs do not consider a folder "missing" until a duplicate folder is received from
OCO/PSC and the record is updated to the Disability Control File (DCF).
FOs are responsible for obtaining a title II folder in the following situations:
•
the folder received from the Megasite/Caves/PSC is not a current medical folder ;
OR,
•
the folder received from the Megasite/Caves/PSC is for the wrong claimant, i.e. HA
folder received and the CDB is due the CDR; OR,
•
the folder received has the wrong account number (WAN); OR,
•
the title II folder is in an FO.
