E. Entering the diary for Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE)
1. Diary type
Enter "MRP."
2. Diary date
•
Enter a four-digit code for month and year for 84 months (7 years) in the future;
count the month of adjudication (e.g., if adjudication occurs in 11/99, diary for
10/06).
•
In all claims, establish the diary date without regard to the claimant's actual age
(even if the diary will not mature until after the person attains full retirement
age).
3. Diary reason
Enter "7."
4. Reference
•
DI 26525.045B, MINE or MINE Equivalent Criteria – Procedure
