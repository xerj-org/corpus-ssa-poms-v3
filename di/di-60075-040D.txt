D. Monthly offset calculation
A POD participant’s benefits were offset $1 for every $2 earned above the POD monthly
threshold. The threshold was the greater of either the current year TWP level ($840
in 2017) or the total approved monthly amount of IRWE. The threshold could not exceed
the current year SGA/Blind SGA rate even if a beneficiary’s IRWE were higher.
POD participants were required to report their earnings and IRWE, if any, to Abt monthly.
For each monthly report, we calculated a new offset for the corresponding benefit
month. Earning reports carried forward to the next month if a beneficiary failed to
report. However, IRWE did not carry forward to the following month(s). If IRWE increased
the threshold in an offset calculation for a given month and the beneficiary failed
to report earnings or IRWE the following month, the offset was recalculated using
the last reported earnings and the TWP amount as the threshold.
Example of a monthly offset calculation
A beneficiary volunteered to enroll in the POD in November 2017. Abt sent the beneficiary
a letter informing them of their random assignment to treatment group 1 and that their
offset eligibility starts in December 2017. Abt also sent the beneficiary a welcome
package identifying their work incentive counselor (WIC).
The beneficiary contacted their WIC in early December 2017 to report their November
2017 earnings. They reported to their WIC that they earned $2000 in November 2017
and had $300 in medication co-pays. The beneficiary uploaded their paystubs via the
POD website. The beneficiary's monthly benefit amount (MBA) is $1200. The WIC enters
the following information from them in the POD Information Data System (IDS):
•
Date of report: 12/05/2017;
•
Earnings Month: 11/2017;
•
Earnings Report Amount: $2000;
•
Impairment Related Work Expenses: $300;
•
Monthly Benefit Amount: $1200
Use the following chart to determine the monthly offset amount:

Step
Description
Outcome
Step 1

$2,000

Step 4
Determine threshold used to calculate offset amount
*Greater of TWP amount or actual IRWE but IRWE amount can’t exceed yearly SGA amount
$840
Step 5
Subtract threshold determined in Step 3 from Monthly Earnings Report in Step 1.
$2000 - $840 = $1160
Step 6
Divide the amount found in Step 5 by 2. The result is the offset amount.
$1160/2 = $580
Step 7
Subtract the offset amount found in Step 5 from the Monthly Benefit Amount (MBA) for
the report month. The result is the offset monthly benefit amount.
$1200 - $580 = $620
When applying the benefit offset, POD reduced the beneficiary's monthly benefit amount
by $580. Their new POD offset monthly benefit amount is now $620.
