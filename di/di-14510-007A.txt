A. Identifying a Section 301 determination
You can identify Section 301 cases in several ways:
•
Remarks on the Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) and Supplemental Security Income Record
(SSR);
•
A “Y” in the 301 Indicator of the Disability Determination Section Query (DDSQ);
•
A Disability Control File (DCF) event indicating “301 case” on the query CDR screen
(QCDR) or the CDR Entitlement (ECDR) screen (Title II cases) and Development Worksheet
(Title XVI cases);
•
A copy of the Section 301 decision in eView or the Evidence Portal (EP).
