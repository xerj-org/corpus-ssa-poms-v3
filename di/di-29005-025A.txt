SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 29005.025 - Individual Dies before a Determination is made on a Request for Reconsideration Continuing Disability Review (CDR)
Effective Dates: 09/23/2016 - Present | TN 4 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429005025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. An individual dies before the reconsideration CDR determination
1. The disability determination services’ (DDS) actions
•
When an individual dies while the case is with the pre-hearing reviewer, the DDS does
the following actions:
•
notifies the FO for development of a qualified substitute party.
•
may place the case on hold pending the FO resolution of a substitute party.
Upon completion of the development, the FO sends a SSA-770-U4 (Notice Regarding Substitution of Party Upon Death of Claimant Reconsideration of
Disability Cessation) for association with the case folder.
•
For more information on FO procedures regarding the death of an individual, see DI 12026.025E.
2. A substitute party is located
The hearing process proceeds with a substitute party based on the information provided
on the SSA-770-U4.
a. The substitute party wishes to continue with the hearing
When the substitute party wishes to continue with the hearing, the DDS and the pre-hearing
(PH) process proceeds based on the information contained on the SSA-770-U4. The DDS
notifies the substitute party that a hearing may not be necessary pending a favorable
determination based on available evidence.
b. The substitute party wishes to withdraw the request for reconsideration
When the SSA-770-U4 indicates the substitute party wishes to withdraw the request
for reconsideration, the DDS does the following:
•
Notify the substitute party that a hearing may not be necessary pending a favorable
determination based on available evidence.
•
Process the determination if a favorable determination is warranted or
•
Return the case to the FO for approval of the withdrawal. The FO will send a letter
of approval to the substitute party notifying them.
3. A substitute party is not located
When a substitute party is not located, the FO notifies the DDS and the disability
hearing unit (DHU) by completing a SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) documenting that the substitute party could not be located and
includes the name and address of any known next of kin of the individual.
The PH reviewer proceeds with the PH case processing. If a continuance determination
is warranted, the PH reviewer will:
•
complete the form(s), SSA-832 or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal).
For additional information on completing the forms, SSA-832 and SSA-833, see DI 28084.000
•
send a notice of the reconsidered determination to the name and address of the ‘Next
of Kin of [name of deceased]’.
If a fully favorable determination is not warranted, the PH reviewer will send the
case to the DHU. For information on DHU handling of deceased individuals, see DI 33010.060.
