B. Arthrogram studies (generally available since January 1978)
These are x ray studies performed by injecting radiopaque material within specific
joints such as shoulder, knee, hip, elbow, wrist, and ankle to more accurately delineate
various pathological conditions and, because of this, is an improved technique.
