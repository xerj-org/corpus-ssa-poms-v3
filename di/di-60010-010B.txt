SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 60010.010 - Youth Transition Exploration Demonstration (YTED) Participants
Effective Dates: 02/06/2026 - Present | TN 6 (02-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0460010010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedures
1.
For YTED participants who apply for benefits
The waiver will not affect initial claims, and SSA will consider income and resources
as it normally does. The YTED participant list will include only individuals with
current entitlement to SSI benefits, not those filing an initial claim.
FO technicians input the project participation information into CCE during the initial
claim.
a.
Create Report of Contact (DROC)
Document the DROC with the following information:
•
YTED participant
•
YTED start date (date of YTED enrollment)
•
YTED evidence submitted by claimant
•
YTED POMS DI 60010.000
b.
SSR Special Message Page Inputs
•
These procedures are only for participants coded as YTED.
•
Update YTED MMYY in the Case Characteristics Code (CG) field (where MMYY is the start
month of YTED enrollment). For example, YTED 0925 would indicate that the claimant
enrolled in YTED in September 2025. The CG field must be 4 characters.
NOTE:
Every YTED participant must have this entry. It is important for the demonstration
research that the CG field is not overlaid by a subsequent input.
•
Update the following in the Remarks field, "YTED waiver case - special income exclusions
– See DROC - retain this remark until 12/31/2028."
2.
For SSI recipients who participate in YTED
On a monthly basis, the FOP contact sends the project participation list to FO technicians.
They should input the information into the SSR Special Message page and DCF. Follow
the SSR Special Message page instructions listed in DI
60010.010B.1.b and DCF input instructions below:
DCF Input
•
Select Option 32 from SSA Main Menu in PCOM.
•
Select Option 16 from CDR Selection Menu (MCDR).
•
Input DY in the Demo Indicator Field. DY is the demonstration code for YTED.
•
Input the MMDDYYYY in the Participation Start Date field where the MMDDYYYY is the
month, day, and year the individual enrolled in the YTED.
•
Input the MMDDYYYY in the Participation Stop Date field where the MMDDYYYY is the
month, day, and year the participant is no longer eligible for the YTED waivers. All
participations terminate on December 31, 2028. Exceptions may include voluntary withdrawal
or death. Mathematica will notify the SSA when it learns of withdrawals.
