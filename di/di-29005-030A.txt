A. Returns for additional development (RADS)
The supervisory hearing officer may return a case to the disability determination
services (DDS) for additional development at any time before the disability hearing
officer (DHO) makes the reconsidered determination.
The Office of Quality Review (OQR) may return a case selected for quality review to
the disability hearing officer (DHO) to obtain additional development. The DHO may
then prepare a development request and forward it with the case folder to the DDS.
