SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 12045.030 - Same Title Claims – New Claim Filed, Prior Claim at Reconsideration Level
Effective Dates: 07/20/2012 - Present | TN 1 (11-02)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0412045030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Duplicate Claim
A claim is a duplicate if an individual filed a previous application for the same
benefits and there has not been a final decision on that claim. See GN 00204.028A.
2. Related Claim
Disability claims filed by or for the same individual under the same title (DIB, DWB,
CDB) are related if they share the same deciding issue, whether medical or substantial
gainful activity (SGA).
