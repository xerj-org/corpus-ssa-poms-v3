A. Introduction to the medical improvement review standard (MIRS)
Under the continuing disability review (CDR) instructions in effect before October
9, 1984, adjudicators only considered the individual's current ability to engage in
substantial gainful activity (SGA) and did not consider medical improvement (MI) in
deciding if disability continued. The “Social Security Disability Benefits Reform
Act of 1984” (P.L. 98-460), provided the MIRS in CDR cases. The MIRS is a series of steps in the CDR sequential
evaluation process.
