C. When IB begin and end
1. When IB begin
We pay interim benefits under Title II beginning the month before the month in which
the 110th day falls.
EXAMPLE:
If the 110th day after the ALJ’s favorable disability decision is October 5, 2015,
IB begin September 2015. Begin processing IB once the start order is received from
the Office of Appellate Operations (OAO).
2. When IB end
Terminate Title II IB effective the month before the month of the final decision.
For guidance on when a decision becomes final, refer to GN 03101.060. Do not consider IB as an overpayment even if SSA stopped IB late, unless fraud is
involved. For more information see DI 42010.205G.
EXAMPLE: The Commissioner issues a final decision on December 15, 2015 after AC's own motion
review. IB would terminate November 2015.
REMINDER: The date on the ALJ decision becomes the final decision date if the claimant does
not appeal or the AC does not initiates its own motion review. The Office of Appellate
Operations (OAO) sends a stop order once the decision becomes final.
