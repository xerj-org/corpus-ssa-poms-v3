DI 28040.220 Completion of the SSA-832 or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness
Determination and Transmittal) in Medical Improvement Not Expected (MINE) or MINE-Equivalent
Cases
Adjudicators will prepare a Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal (SSA-832 for Title XVI, SSA-833 for Title II) for all continuing disability review (CDR) determinations. For detailed
instructions on completing these forms, see DI 28084.000.
If a continuance is indicated on a case where MINE or MINE-equivalent procedures are
applicable, the adjudicator will annotate “MINE or MINE-equivalent CASE” in item 24
(Remarks) of the SSA-832 or SSA-833.
If the MINE or MINE-equivalent criteria no longer applies, the adjudicator will process
the SSA-832 or SSA-833 as a “regular” CDR determination.
