DI 43525.030 Continuing Disability - CDR Enforcements
Alerts will be generated for disabled individuals who have substantial earnings posted
to their earnings records. For claimants using a foreign address, an SSA-423-U6/SSA-5526-U3
will be used for development to the FSP/VARO.
