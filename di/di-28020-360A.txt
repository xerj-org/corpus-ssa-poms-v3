A. When the required and material evidence was missing error exception applies
This category of error applies if:
•
at the time of the prior determination or decision, required and
material evidence of the severity of the impairment(s) was missing; and
•
the evidence in question becomes available, and upon review, it now shows that if
the evidence had been present before, we would not have found the individual disabled.
