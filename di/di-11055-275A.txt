A. Disability Determination Services (DDS) notification
When the DDS transmits a disability or blindness determination, the Field Office (FO)
receives notification via the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) Actions
Page. In addition to the EDCS Actions Page, the ways in which the DDS alerts the FO
of the determination vary depending on whether the claim is an allowance or a denial.
1. Allowances
The FO initiates all outstanding non-medical development when:
•
The FO receives notification on the EDCS Actions Page and the CR Action List; or
•
The servicing DDS notifies the FO prior to returning jurisdiction to the FO.
For more information about the ways the DDS may notify the FO, see SI 00603.020C.
For more information about the EDCS Actions Page and CR Action List, see DI 81010.100 and MSOM T16WMI 001.012.
For more information about the deferred development application process, see SI 00603.003.
REMINDER: The FO must initiate and complete a Preeffectuation Review Contact (PERC) before
finally adjudicating deferred Title XVI claims or non-deferred Title XVI claims, processed
more than 120 days after the last completed non-medical development.
2. Denials
When the FO receives notice of the DDS medical denial prior to completion of the development
of the non-medical factors, the FO discontinues non-medical development.
