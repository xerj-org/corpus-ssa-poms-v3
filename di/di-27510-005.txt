DI 27510.005 Subsequent Claim Filed
NOTE: If a claimant files a new disability application and has a prior disability claim
for the same title and benefit type pending at any level of administrative review, see DI 51501.001 – Procedural Change for Subsequent Disability Applications Effective July 28, 2011.
