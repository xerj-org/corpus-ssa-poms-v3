SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 26510.030 - Completing Item 19 (Claimant Not Disabled) on the SSA-831
Effective Dates: 04/22/2009 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. DWB Medicare only
Check block B in DWB Medicare-only cases when:
•
the evidence establishes that the onset of disability was at age 62 years and 7 months
or later.
•
AOD is at age 62 and 7 months or later and there is no reason to question the allegation
of the onset of disability.
NOTE: Since a disabled widow(er) must be disabled for 29 months (which includes the waiting
period plus the assumed 24 months entitlement to disability benefits) before the individual
can become entitled to hospital insurance benefits, there is no need to make a disability
determination through the current date, if a disability is alleged at or after age
62 years and 7 months. The disabled widow(er) will automatically become entitled to
hospital insurance benefits upon attainment of age 65 regardless of whether or not
she/he is disabled. In both of the above situations, insert “Age 62 years and 7 months.”
If your legacy system does not allow insertion of this comment in Item 19B, go to
Item 34 (Remarks), enter Item number, and add the comment there.
