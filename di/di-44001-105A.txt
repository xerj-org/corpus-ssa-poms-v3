A. Jurisdiction of Railroad (RR) Claims
Branch 2A of Disability Case Review (DCR), within Risk and Quality, has jurisdiction
of dual eligibility claims filed by career railroad (RR) employees and certain dependent
RR survivor disability annuitants. The State disability determination services (DDS)
does not have jurisdiction.
These cases are processed by DCR, location code V32, to facilitate the coordination
of medical evidence and disability determinations with the Railroad Retirement Board
(RRB), which is also located in Chicago, Illinois.
