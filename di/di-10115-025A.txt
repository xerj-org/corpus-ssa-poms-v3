Effective Dates: 01/18/2007 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410115025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy – MOET Deceased Wage Earner (WE)
An individual entitled to surviving child's benefits will be entitled (or reentitled)
to a monthly benefit effective with the first month in which all of the requirements
in RS 00203.001 are met during any part of that month.
