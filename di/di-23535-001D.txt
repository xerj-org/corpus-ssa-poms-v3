SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23535.001 - Presumptive Disability/Presumptive Blindness (PD/PB) Eligibility, Authority, and Payment Issues
Effective Dates: 02/17/2026 - Present | TN 37 (02-26)

D. PD/PB payment issues
1.
Prior to the final determination of disability or blindness, an individual may receive
up to six months of payments based on a PD/PB finding, if he or she meets all other
eligibility requirements.
2.
Payments based on a PD/PB finding are not considered overpayments if it is later determined
that the claimant is not disabled or blind unless:
•
The claim is disallowed due to ineligibility based on factors other than disability
or blindness; or
•
It is subsequently determined that the claimant had excess resources or that the amount
of payment was computed in error. In such cases, only the incorrect payment amount
is considered an overpayment.
3.
If a claimant eligible for PD/PB benefits does not receive the payment(s), or it/they
were returned, and the claimant subsequently receives a denial decision from the DDS,
SSA may re-issue the PD/PB benefit(s) via one-time payment (OTP).
