C. Congressional inquiry for Presumptive Disability/Blindness determinations
If you receive an inquiry on a claim when the FO previously made a presumptive disability/blindness
determination, but DDS had not made a final disability determination, take the follow
actions:
•
call and advise DA of the inquiry;
•
provide the data necessary for DA to respond to the inquiry; and
•
if necessary, diary the claim in accordance with the instructions in DI 31005.020A.
