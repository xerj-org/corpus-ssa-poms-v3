SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 81010.145 - Processing Reconsideration Requests of Field Office (FO) Determinations
Effective Dates: 08/04/2026 - Present | TN 92 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0481010145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS)
If the technically denied disability claim was previously established on EDCS, see
Processing Field Office (FO) Technical Reactivations - DI 81010.110 for Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) cases to determine whether the reestablished
disability claim being sent to the Disability Determination Service (DDS) can be processed
by reactivating the prior EDCS record.
If the case cannot be processed as an EDCS reactivation, it must be processed as an
EDCS exclusion per CEF Exclusions and Limitations - DI 81010.030C.
NOTE: If an EDCS record was established for a technically denied claim, that EDCS record
MUST be closed following instructions in Creating EDCS Cases - DI 81010.020D.3. and Deleting and Manually Clearing EDCS Cases - DI 81010.105.
NOTE: Do not attempt to use an open EDCS record for a technically denied claim to send a reconsideration
action to the DDS.
