B. Procedures for handling prior comparison point decision (CPD) paper folders
•
Paper folders are excluded from Disability Quality Branch (DQB) reviews. Scan all
relevant medical or other pertinent documents pertaining to the prior CPD paper folder
into the CEF.
Create an undated explanation of the determination
•
Forward the eCDR and CEF to the QA reviewing component. See DI 81020.010 Paper Folder Indicators (PFI) and DI 81020.255 Processing a Reconsideration Appeal of an Electronic Continuing Disability Review
(eCDR) Cessation.
