E. Other productivity (DI 39503.810 C.6.)
1. Nonrecurring decentralization (DI 39503.810 C.6.a.)
The State agency began planning its decentralization in fiscal year 1974 at which
time 1.75 work-years was expended. A total of 3.25 work-years is estimated to be expended
in fiscal year 1975.
FY 1975 = +1.50 work-years
3.25 FY 1975 - 1.75 FY 1974 = 1.50 work-years
2. Forced productivity (DI 39503.810 C.6.b.)
In order to reduce the number of pending cases, the State agency decided to take some
expedient measures which saved 29.5 minutes processing time per case. This was begun
in fiscal year 1975 and affected 55,000 cases.
FY 1975 = -15.47 work-years
55,000 cases x 29.5 minutes = 1,622,500 minutes ÷60 = 27,041 work-years ÷1,750 = -15.45
work-years
3. IBM sorter experiment (DI 39503.810 C.6.c.)
The State agency experienced with an IBM paper sorting machine during fiscal year
1975 for future implementation possibilities. A total of 1,500 cases were used in
this experiment which required 50 minutes each additional processing time.
FY 1975 = +.71 work-years
1,500 cases ×50 minutes = 75,000 minutes
75,000 minutes ÷60 = 1,250 hours
1,250 hours ÷1,750 = +.71 work-years
