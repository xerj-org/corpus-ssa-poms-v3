SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 28075.025 - Processing Medicare for Qualified Government Employment (MQGE) Cases
Effective Dates: 07/11/2007 - Present | TN 7 (10-15)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0428075025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Cessation cases
Follow these instructions for cessation cases:
a.
Prepare a Form SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination
and Transmittal – Title II). Enter “MQGE only” in item 24.
b.
Prepare a notice to the individual using appropriate paragraphs as instructed in DI 28095.105.
c.
In EPE cases prepare a notice to the individual using appropriate paragraphs as instructed
in DI 13010.215A.
d.
Route folder to FO.
2. Continuance cases
Follow these instructions for continuance cases:
a.
Prepare a Form SSA-833 determination and enter “MQGE only” in item 24.
b.
Send an SSA-L270-U2 (Social Security Disability Information) to the individual. For
an exhibit of SSA-L270-U2, see DI 31515.201.
c.
Route folder to FO, as appropriate. If the folder is electronic, insert “Attn: Disability
Examiner. See Form SSA-833 in file” on the Alerts and Messages screen. If folder is
paper, attach SSA-408 annotated with “Attn: Disability Examiner. See Form SSA-833
in file.”
