SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 22510.055 - Pediatric Consultative Examination (CE) Report Content Guidelines for Speech and Language Impairments in Children from Birth to Attainment of Age 3
Effective Dates: 01/28/2026 - Present | TN 62 (01-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0422510055 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. SLP conclusions
The CE report must include the following:
1.
A statement of conclusions correlated with information from the history, clinical
observations, and formal assessment;
2.
A statement that indicates whether direct assessment data are generally consistent
with observations and impressions about the child's conversational skills (e.g., oral
language or social interaction) and school language skills (e.g., narrative discourse),
and that explains or comments on any discrepancies;
3.
If the child uses a hearing aid or glasses but did not bring them to the examination,
a statement regarding the validity of the examination results;
4.
If a diagnosis is established, a statement that explains whether and to what extent
the identified speech or language impairment may reasonably be expected to affect
the child’s functioning (specifically the child's ability to acquire and use information
and to interact and relate with others over the next 12 months); and
5.
The CE provider's signature, educational degree, and certification or licensure credentials.
