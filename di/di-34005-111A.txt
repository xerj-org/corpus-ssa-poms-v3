SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 34005.111 - Neurological Disorders
Effective Dates: 09/28/2016 - Present | TN 20 (09-16)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0434005111 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Which neurological disorders do we evaluate under these listings? We evaluate epilepsy, coma or persistent vegetative state (PVS), and neurological
disorders that cause disorganization of motor function, bulbar and neuromuscular dysfunction,
or communication impairment. Under this body system, we evaluate the limitations resulting
from the impact of the neurological disease process itself. If you have a neurological
disorder(s) that affects your physical and mental functioning, we will evaluate your
impairments under the rules we use to determine functional equivalence. If your neurological
disorder results in only mental impairment or if you have a co-occurring mental condition
that is not caused by your neurological disorder (for example, Autism spectrum disorder),
we will evaluate your mental impairment under the mental disorders body system, 112.00.
