DI 40505.115 Voluntary Report of Medical Improvement
An individual may voluntarily report improvement or recovery. Unless the report clearly
raises no issue of medical improvement, we will initiate a CDR. If the diary date
does not show in the DCF, refer to MS CDR 001.017.
