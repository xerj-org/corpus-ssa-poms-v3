C. Joint freeze cases
Joint freeze cases are claims for disability filed with RRB by a career RR worker.
An application for a disability annuity with the RRB is deemed a freeze application
(joint freeze) with SSA. The number holder does not need to contact SSA for a freeze
determination. The RRB makes its determination based on SSA disability program policy
and procedures and sends the file to DCR for review and processing of the SSA freeze.
