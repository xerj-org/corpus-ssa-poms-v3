SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 40501.015 - Processing Center (PC) Responsibilities for Continuing Disability Review (CDR) Cases
Effective Dates: 01/23/1990 - Present | TN 2 (08-16)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0440501015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating policies of CDR adjudication
1. Alert processing
When the Social Security Administration (SSA) receives a medical diary alert:
a.
Locate and associate the folder(s);
b.
Review the case(s) to ensure that a CDR is appropriate; and
c.
Forward the case(s) to the field office (FO) for processing. For information on processing
medical diary alerts, see DI 40505.001 through DI 40505.005.
2. Suicidal or homicidal behavior cases
Alert the FO if the file indicates the beneficiary demonstrates potential suicidal
or homicidal behavior. Before processing cases involving suicide or homicide see DI 40505.001D.3.
3. Section 1619 CDR cases
Based on a review of the record for Section 1619 CDR cases, the FO determines if the
beneficiary needs a CDR. For information on processing Section 1619 CDR cases, see
DI 40540.001.
4. Foreign cases
The Office of Earnings and International Operations (OEIO) process all foreign CDR
cases.
5. Railroad CDR cases
Great Lakes Program Service Center (GLPSC) coordinates railroad CDRs with the Railroad
Retirement Board, and makes the CDR determination(s) for career railroad and dependent
annuitant cases. Also, see DI 44001.140 Continuing Disability Reviews for Railroad Cases.
6. Diary coding on continuances
To establish case diaries, use the guidelines in DI 26525.001 Scheduling Continuing Disability Reviews (CDRs) – General Policies.
Record the diaries on the SSA-832 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
– Title XVI) or SSA-833 (Cessation or Continuance of Disability or Blindness Determination and Transmittal
– Title II). For further information, see DI 40505.300 Diary Coding of Continuance Determinations.
7. Pre-effectuation review (PER)
PCs perform PER on a percentage of Title II continuance cases in cases involving disability
determination services (DDS) determinations. This can occur before or after the disability
examiner (DE) takes action to make payment or notifies the beneficiary.

DI 40115.001 Processing Centers (PCs) Ability to Change Initial Disability Determination Services

8. Other CDRs
Whenever a CDR issue rises, initiate CDR processing, regardless of whether a diary
matured. See, DI 40505.100 Medical Improvement Expected (MIE) Cases with Work Activity.
