B. Updating Certain MCS or MSSICS Changes in EDCS
Once an EDCS case is transferred, EDCS locks and no changes can be made directly to
the disability report forms. Limited data can be repropagated into EDCS (e.g., claimant
name, address, and telephone number) using the UAT procedure. See DI 81010.045 for more information.
To update MCS or MSSICS information on a case after transfer of jurisdiction of an
EDCS case:
•
Correct the information in MCS or MSSICS;
•
Open the case in EDCS;
•
Select Transfer/Updates tab on the toolbar at the top of the EDCS screen path;
•
Review the changes on the Summary of Updates screen on the Mainframe Updates tab;
and
•
Click the OK button to send update to DDS or OHO.
NOTE: For UATs on the Mainframe Updates and Other Updates tab, EDCS will automatically
determine the office that currently has jurisdiction and notify the office of the
update. For UATs on Transfer Claims tab, EDCS allows users to choose which office
the claim(s) should be transferred to from a drop-down list, or it will allow them
to manually enter an office destination.
