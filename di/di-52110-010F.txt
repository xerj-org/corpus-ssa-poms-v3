SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 52110.010 - Workers’ Compensation/Public Disability Benefit (WC/PDB) Payments Made to Employer
Effective Dates: 06/20/2023 - Present | TN 1 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452110010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Post entitlement action - WC/PDB paid directly to the employer
Take the following actions when WC/PDB is paid directly to the employer:
1.
Verify the WC/PDB amount via direct contact.
2.
Contact the WC/PDB payer or the employer to verify the amount of WC/PDB paid to the
employer.
3.
Offset any WC/PDB that the individual is allowed to keep. If all WC/PDB is paid to
the employer, do not enter WC/PDB into ICF.
4.
Develop the date the payments will no longer be turned over or paid to the employer
and set a diary for re-contact.
