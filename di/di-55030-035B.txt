B. How to get to the QERN screen
To get to the QERN screen:
•
select 32, “Continuing Disability Review File,” from SSA's main menu (MAIN) screen;
•
select 12, “Query Earnings,” from the CDR selection menu (MCDR) screen and enter the
beneficiary/recipient's SSN and the earrings beginning date; and
•
select the correct entitlement by placing an "X” in the appropriate "SEL" field of
the entitlement selection (ESEL) screen.
