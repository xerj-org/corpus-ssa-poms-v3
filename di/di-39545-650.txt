DI 39545.650 Using the Medicare Fee Schedule and Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) Codes
DDSs may elect to use the Medicare fee schedule as the benchmark for establishing
the maximum rates of payment. Medicare uses the AMA CPT coding system.
