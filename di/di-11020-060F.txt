SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11020.060 - Disability Determination Services (DDS) Jurisdiction and Routing
Effective Dates: 04/20/2026 - Present | TN 7 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411020060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Child receiving (or applying for) student or auxiliary benefits is over age 18 and
alleges disability
Separate the material for the award of auxiliary or student benefits, and forward
disability-related material to the appropriate DDS. Annotate both sets of claims material
regarding the whereabouts of the other material.
