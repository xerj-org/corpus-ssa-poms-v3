Effective Dates: 08/10/2011 - Present | TN 10 (04-09)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0426510005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. How to complete Items 11A and 11B
Complete Items 11A and 11B as follows:
•
If the FO made the decision to process the case under the PD/PB procedures and did
not complete item 11, check block 11A, enter code 01 in the lined space and enter
in 11B, the applicable PD/PB category number from the chart in DI 11055.231.
•
If the DDS makes the decision to process the case under the PD/PB procedures, check
block 11A, enter code 02 in the lined space and make no entry in 11B.
