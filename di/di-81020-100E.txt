E. Cancelling AR
If, prior to completion of the AR, the requesting DDS determines that the electronic
AR is no longer necessary:
•
Create a cancellation request to the CEF through the Electronic AR function; and
•
Include a reason for the cancellation within the cancellation request.
NOTE: Not all DDS Legacy systems support the cancel function of the Electronic AR. For
those offices that do not have the cancel function, make a cancellation of an electronic
AR by contacting the other office.
