SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39557.005 - DDS Quality Performance Support
Effective Dates: 09/22/2025 - Present | TN 2 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439557005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Types of Support:
1.
Optional Support - We may offer, or a DDS may request, performance support at any time the regular
monitoring and review process reveals support could enhance performance. The State
does not have to be below the initial performance accuracy rate of 90.6 percent to
receive performance support. Support will be offered, or granted upon request, based
on available resources.
2.
Mandatory Support - We will provide a DDS with performance support if regular monitoring and review
reveal quality performance standards are not met for two consecutive calendar quarters.
