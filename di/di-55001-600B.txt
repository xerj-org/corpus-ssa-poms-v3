B. Policy - VR refusal sanctions eliminated
Effective January 1, 2001, the law requires SSA to end title II benefit deduction
and title XVI benefit suspension actions due to refusal of State VR services. Effective
January 1, 2001, no sanction actions will be taken due to refusal of VR services.
