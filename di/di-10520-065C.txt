C. Process - Notices
Appropriate notices for the following specific circumstances will be found in DI 13095.140 and NL 00804.175. Notices are produced by eWork if they are used to adjudicate work issue CDR decisions.
1. Allowances Or Continuances
Advise a person to report any changes in earnings or IRWE when:
a.
A claim is allowed or benefits continue because the deduction of IRWE results in earnings
below the SGA level, or
b.
SSI payments are affected because of the deduction of IRWE.
2. Denials Or Title II Cessations
Notify a person whose claim is denied or whose benefits are ceased when:
a.
IRWE are not allowed as claimed, or
b.
The amount of such expenses is insufficient to reduce the person's earnings below
SGA, or to make the person eligible for SSI based on income.
