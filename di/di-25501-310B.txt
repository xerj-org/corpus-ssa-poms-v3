SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 25501.310 - The Established Onset Date (EOD) for Disability Insurance Benefit (DIB) Claims and Date First Insured (DFI)
Effective Dates: 07/06/2015 - Present | TN 58 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0425501310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Component roles concerning using DFI as a factor in establishing the EOD
1. Field office (FO)
The FO calculates and establishes the DFI. The FO may consider DFI when determining
a POD. If the claimant has performed SGA after DFI, POD will likely be after DFI.
2. Disability Determination Services (DDS)
The DDS will not establish the EOD or blindness prior to the DFI.
