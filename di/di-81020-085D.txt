D. Deleting a Flag
Flags are not automatically deleted in the CEF after case closure or case transfer.
However, flags may be manually deleted from eView. Do not delete flags unless they
are incorrect or no longer applicable. If a flag is no longer applicable, the office
with jurisdiction of the CEF is responsible for deleting the flag.
To delete a flag from eView:
•
Click on the “Edit (Lock Case)” link to access “edit mode”;
•
Click on the “Flags (View Detail and Edit” on the secondary title bar;
•
Click on “Delete”;
•
Click on “Cancel “ to exit the screen; and
•
Click on the “Edit (Lock Case)” link to exit “edit mode”.
