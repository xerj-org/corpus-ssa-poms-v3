Effective Dates: 03/06/2024 - Present | TN 29 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0424501001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction to the roles and responsibilities of the disability examiner (DE), medical
consultant (MC), psychological consultant (PC), and medical advisor (MA)
A disability determination requires the DE, MC, and PC to collaborate as the adjudicative
team. Not all team members participate in the development and evaluation of all claims,
as outlined in DI 24501.001B in this section.
All team members have training in the disability process. MAs are not part of the
adjudicative team but can provide analysis of medical issues.
