A. General – Defining informal remands
The Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR) Hearing Office may return
selected claims to the Disability Determination Services (DDS) as Informal Remands
(IR) per DI 27520.001. DDSs that have the necessary upgrade to their case processing software can now process
ODAR IRs electronically.
