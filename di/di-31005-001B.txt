Effective Dates: 04/15/2013 - Present |

B. Inquiries from appointed representatives
If you receive an inquiry or a request for status directly from the claimant's appointed
representative, the type of response you provide depends on the type of inquiry and
the status of the claim at the time you received the inquiry. You may release some
types of information without claimant authorization. Please see additional information
regarding the policy and procedures that govern the release of information in GN 03300.000,
and GN 03910.001 thru GN 03910.090.
NOTE: Do not disclose disability claim determination information to an appointed representative
until claim processing is complete as there may be a possibility of reversal by the
Disability Quality Branch.
