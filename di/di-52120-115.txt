DI 52120.115 Maryland Workers' Compensation (WC)
The Maryland Workers Compensation Commission does not make benefit payments to injured workers. The Commission's role is to process
and adjudicate claims. Once claims determinations have been made, the insurance carriers
and self-insured employers make WC payments as required by the Commission's awards
and orders.
