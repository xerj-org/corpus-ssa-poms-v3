Effective Dates: 07/27/2023 - Present | TN 5 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0452170040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Sources of notice
1.
Applicant, spouse, representative payee
2.
Outside source, e.g., attorney, employer, employer’s insurance company, or the appropriate
State agency.
3.
SSA-2514 (Record of Claimant’s Intent to File) (or its electronic equivalent) can
be used only when the SSA-2514 is annotated with a statement regarding WC entitlement.
NOTE: Contact the worker and/or appropriate agency if there is reason to believe the worker
is receiving WC or an increase in WC but notice was not received.
