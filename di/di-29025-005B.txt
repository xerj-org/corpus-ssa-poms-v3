Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0429025005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definition - adverse effect
Adverse effect can occur if direct access to a medical report by an individual is
expected to:
•
Disrupt the doctor – patient relationship;
•
Interfere with the patients’ medical management;
•
In some way prove detrimental to the health, safety, and well being of the subject
individual and/or members of their family.
