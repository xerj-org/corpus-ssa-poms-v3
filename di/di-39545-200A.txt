SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 39545.200 - Monitoring Qualifications of Consultative Examination (CE) Provider Support Staff
Effective Dates: 08/22/2012 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0439545200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
CE providers may use support staff to help perform the CE. Any such support staff
(e.g., X-ray technician, nurse, etc.) must meet appropriate licensing or certification
requirements of the State. CE providers must certify that their support staff meets
State requirements and have not been sanctioned.
