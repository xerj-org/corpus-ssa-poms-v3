A. Operating policy Review process
a.
Disability Adjudication (DA) will conduct at least one review of the decentralized
operation within the first year of its operation.
b.
DA will initiate the special review after reasonable notice is given and not be part
of any regular visit program.
•
The review will be initiated whether or not the DDS is meeting regulated performance
standards, to ascertain how the new operation is functioning and what overall progress
has been made.
•
Areas of concern will be much the same as those included in the State's proposal.
c.
After the initial review, all subsequent reviews of the decentralized operation will be part of DA's regular or special reviews
and visit program of the DDS.
