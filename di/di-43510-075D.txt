D. Travel expenses for foreign CE
Foreign Service Manual instructions provide that when travel in connection with a
proposed examination would entail substantial costs, the foreign service post will
consider all alternative means of obtaining the information and will usually proceed
by the least expensive method. If the FSP needs advice on the method to be used, a
report will be made by operations memorandum before the post will proceed with the
examination.
Evaluate the alternative methods of obtaining the information and instruct the post
by means of an SSA-423A or direct teletype to use the least expensive method which
will secure the evidence needed for adjudication. Do not check any items in “Services Requested.” Enter the necessary instructions in the Remarks section of the SSA-423A. Use the
proper diary code for follow-up requests to the FSP.
