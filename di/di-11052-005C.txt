C. Obtain proof of age (POA)
POA is required whenever attainment of age 22 or 62 affects fully insured status of
an insured number holder who does not have 40 quarters of coverage. The FO should
obtain POA following the usual POA guidelines in GN 00302.030. In addition, if the
claimant is a child of the insured, POA is required to determine dependency requirements
as explained in DI 11052.005D, in this section.
