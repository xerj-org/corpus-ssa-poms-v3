SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 23022.075 - Appeals Processing of Compassionate Allowances (CAL) Cases
Effective Dates: 06/28/2013 - Present | TN 24 (02-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0423022075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Initial claim was not CAL and the Disability Determination Services (DDS) identifies
CAL at the reconsideration level
If the DDS identifies a CAL condition at the reconsideration level, manually update
the case to CAL: Y (refer to adding a case to CAL processing in DI 23022.055B) and process using expedited procedures.
It may be appropriate to manually update the case even when the CAL condition was
present at the initial level. Use judgment and consider the issues in DI 23022.055E to decide whether to again identify the case for CAL processing.
