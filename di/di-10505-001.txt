DI 10505.001 Evaluation and Development of Employment
This section provides instructions for evaluating and developing work issues in employment
situations for non-blind persons only.
For cases with self-employment, see DI 10510.000
For title II blindness cases, see DI 10515.000
For general information on the meaning of substantial gainful activity (SGA), see
DI 10501.001
