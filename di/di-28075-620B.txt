B. Form SSA-833 for Title II determination
The items are as follows:
1.
Item 20 - Why Review Was Made (WRM): Enter Code 26, (1619 CDR - Concurrent Title II Determination).
2.
Item 23 - Diary Information: In continuances, enter an MIE, MIP, or MINE diary, as appropriate, based on the
instructions in DI 26525.000, unless a TWP diary is needed.
3.
Item 24 – Remarks: Enter “1619 CDR - Concurrent Title II Determination.”
4.
Item 29 - Letter/Paragraph Number: In continuances, enter “1013”; in cessations, show “S”. For notice instructions,
see DI 28075.625.
