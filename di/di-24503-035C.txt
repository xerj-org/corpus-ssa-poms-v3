C. Sources of medical opinions, opinions, and prior administrative medical findings
Acceptable medical sources (AMSs) can make evidence we categorize as medical opinions.
Medical consultants (MC) and psychological consultants (PC) can make evidence we categorize
as prior administrative medical findings. Medical sources who are not AMSs and nonmedical
sources can make evidence we categorize as opinions.
