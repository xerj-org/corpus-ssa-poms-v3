SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 10115.022 - Work Activity and Childhood Disability Benefit (CDB) Entitlement
Effective Dates: 06/11/2008 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0410115022 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - Work Activity by the Wage Earner (WE)
Work deductions incurred by the WE will have the same effect on a CDB as on any other
auxiliary. If a CDB marries a WE entitled under the RSI provisions, and is dually
entitled as a B and a CDB, he/she is subject to deductions occasioned by the WE’s
work, all deductions being computed and applied to the B portion of the monthly benefit
amount.
