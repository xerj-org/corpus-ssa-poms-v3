B. When the advances in medical or vocational therapy or technology exception applies
Consider this exception if all of the following criteria are satisfied:
•
MI has NOT occurred, or MI is NOT related to the ability to work;
•
evidence shows that the individual has benefited from services that reflect advances
in medical or vocational therapy or technology; and
•
these advances have decreased the severity of the impairment(s) or increased the individual's
is ability to do basic work activities.
