SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - DI 11010.540 - Medical Evidence Received From a Foreign Source
Effective Dates: 12/22/2014 - Present | TN 54 (12-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010540 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Evidence received from sources outside of the U.S., Canada, or Mexico
The FO must examine the foreign application and the evidence from a foreign applicant
for completeness.
•
If the disability or non-disability evidence is not complete and the source of additional
development is outside of the U.S., Canada, or Mexico, do not ask the source for the
additional evidence.
•
Forward the materials to OCO/OIO with a notation concerning any additional development
that might be necessary in the case. For information on sending cases to OCO/OIO refer
to DI 11010.275.
