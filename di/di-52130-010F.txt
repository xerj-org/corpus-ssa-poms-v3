Effective Dates: 11/04/2008 - Present | TN 1 (07-23)

F. Age 62 FERS recomputation and the retirement insurance benefit (RIB) option
Recompute the offset at age 62 if the FERS disability is offsettable. This is because
OPM recomputes the FERS disability benefit when the individual attains age 62, possibly
resulting in a higher FERS benefit. The processing center (PC) should take the following
step:
1.
Verify the recomputed FERS benefit amount.
2.
Adjust the offset accordingly.
3.
Consider the RIB option per DI 52150.030 if the RIB will be higher than the DIB amount payable after offset.
