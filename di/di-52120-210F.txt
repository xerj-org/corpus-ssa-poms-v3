F. Attorney fees
Although the individual may represent himself in WC proceedings, other layperson representation
is not allowed. Any attorney fee agreement must be approved by a WC judge or the Workers’
Compensation Appeal Board, and approved fees become liens against awards. Fees are
limited by statute to 20 percent of any past due or ongoing wage loss benefits.
