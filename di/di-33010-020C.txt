C. Requesting additional development
1. Return of case to development unit
a.
A RAD must be approved by SHO.
b.
Prepare an SSA-5002 Report of Contact that includes the following information:
1.
the name of the DHO,
2.
whether the hearing has occurred or whether the beneficiary (or recipient) waived
their appearance, and
3.
what additional development is needed.
c.
Document the DHU folder.
d.
Route the claim folder to the DDS or appropriate unit.
e.
Notify the beneficiary (or recipient) and appointed representative of the action taken,
and that there will be a delay pending further development.
2. If the case is returned to the DHU
Give the beneficiary (or recipient) and appointed representative the opportunity to
review and comment on any evidence obtained by the DHU after a hearing before issuing
an unfavorable determination. See DI
33015.035 for additional instructions.
