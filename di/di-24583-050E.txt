E. How do we consider psychological tests that do not satisfy program requirements?
The record may contain reports of psychological tests that do not satisfy program
requirements. Consider psychological tests that do not satisfy program requirements
for the information they provide. For example, if the record contains the results
of the California Achievement Test, which is administered in a group setting typically
by a teacher, consider those results to determine if additional development such as
identification of treatment records, a CE, or an intelligence test is necessary.
