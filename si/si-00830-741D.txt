D. Documenting EEOICP
1. EEOICP documentation
Use the Evidence (EVID) screen, a Report of Contact page and lock it or an SSA-5002,
Report of Contact, to record the determination that the EEOICP payment is excluded
from income and resources. Include the following information from the EEOICP document:
•
The amount of the payment;
•
The date the payment was issued; and
•
That it represents payment under the EEOICP.
Alternately, fax the EEOICP document and the SSA-5002 documenting the income and resource
determination into the Non-Disability Repository of Evidentiary Documents (NDRED)
or the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate.
For the EEOICP resource policy, see SI 01130.690.
2. Contact with DOL
If the claimant does not have written notification of the EEOICP payment and a phone
contact was made with DOL, document as follows:
a. SSI consolidated claims experience (CCE)
For CCE cases, complete a Report of Contact page and lock it or summarize on EVID
the date of the DOL contact, contact person’s name, title, telephone number, whether
the payment amount is from the EEIOCP, the amount and date the payment was made.
b. Non MSSICS
For non MSSICS cases, document the information in SI 00830.741D.2.a., in this section, on EVID or an SSA-5002, Report of Contact, and fax it into NDRED
or CEF, as appropriate.
