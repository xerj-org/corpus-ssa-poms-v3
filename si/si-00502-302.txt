SI 00502.302 Aliens Whose 7-year Eligibility Period Ended August 22, 1996, through October, 1,
2008, and who SSA Manually Reinstated
To determine if the alien is eligible for the benefit extension as provided in P.L.
110-328, follow these procedures if SSA reinstated the alien’s payments, per SI 00502.301D.1.
See Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Extension for Elderly and Disabled Refugees
Act, SI 00502.301.
