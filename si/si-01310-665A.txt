A. Procedure
Explain the question and the items owned by the individual which must be considered.
If the answer to 9(a) is “Yes,” complete 9(b) and follow the development steps in the appropriate POMS instructions
(see B. below).
If the answer to all assets in question 9 is “No,” do not develop further. Go to question 10.
