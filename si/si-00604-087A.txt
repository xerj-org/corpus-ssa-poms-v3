Effective Dates: 03/28/2012 - Present | TN 5 (03-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604087 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Question 51 alerts interviewers to the possibility that we are withholding part of
another benefit payment to recover a previous overpayment. This information prevents
"double counting" that income when computing the Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
payment.
