G. Procedure for IGRA trust amendments
If the tribe makes any substantive amendments to the master trust agreement, the FO
must email the amended document to the RTL for referral back to the RCC for an additional
review and opinion. The RTL will then update the precedent file in the SSITMS SharePoint
Repository for Precedents.
