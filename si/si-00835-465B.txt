B. Policy — household operating expenses and inside ISM
The household's operating expenses (i.e., actual cash expenditures for food and shelter)
are used in determining the existence and amount of inside ISM without regard to the current market value (CMV) of the food and shelter provided.
NOTE: For purposes of determining household operating expenses, it is immaterial whether
the members of the household have pooled their funds to pay for the food and shelter
items or individual members make payments for particular expenses directly to the
vendors.
The following are not household operating expenses:
•
unpaid portions of food and shelter bills (i.e, arrearages) until they are paid;
•
the cost of food consumed by individuals who separately consume or separately purchase
their food;
•
the value of food purchased with food stamps.
