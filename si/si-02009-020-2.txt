Throughout the fourth quarter of 1981 each member of the couple would continue to
be due $10 per month. Ef­fective January 1982 it is necessary to determine whether
the couple is living together in the same room in the facili­ty. If they were not
living together, the computation for the first quarter of 1982 would follow the rules
in SI 02009.025. Beginning in January, Mary would be due $25 a month and John would no longer be
eligible since John's countable income ($30/month) exceeds the monthly FBR of $25.
