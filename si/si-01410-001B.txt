B. Procedure - Field Office Responsibilities
FO: Use the following criteria to determine eligibility for SSP.
•
identify individuals converted from the State-administered predecessor programs as
indicated on the supplemental security record ( SSR) in the (CONV) field (as described in SM 01601.625);
•
determine and post to the SSR the State and county of residence for all claimants
and recipients following the rules in this subchapter; and
•
determine and maintain the correct OSS information on the SSR in federally-administered
States.
