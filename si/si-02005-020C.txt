C. Policy — computation when there is oss involvement
1. The Eligibility Test
a.
Use the CI in the CM. Compare it to the FBR (not the FLA D Federal payment limit)
and the OSS level for FLA D.
b.
If CI is less than or equal to the FBR, the person is federally eligible and OSS eligible.
c.
If CI is greater than the FBR, and the excess income is less than the OSS level, the
person is federally ineligible (no Federal SSI is due) but OSS eligible.
d.
If CI is greater than the FBR and the excess income is equal to or greater than the
OSS level, the person is federally ineligible (no Federal SSI due) and OSS ineligible
(no OSS payment due).
2. The Payment Computation
a.
Use the CI in the BM (adjusted by COLA coordination). Compare it to the FLA D Federal
payment limit and the OSS level for FLA D.
b.
If CI is less than the Federal payment limit, the difference is the Federal SSI payment
due (subject to proration for months before 9/1/96) and the full OSS level is the
OSS payment due (subject to proration for months before 9/1/96).
c.
If CI is equal to the Federal payment limit, no Federal SSI payment is due, but the
full OSS level is the OSS payment due (subject to proration for months before 9/1/96).
See SI 02005.020D. and SM 01305.001N. for payment status E01.)
d.
If CI is greater than the Federal payment limit and the excess income (the CI in excess
of the payment limit) is less than the OSS level, no Federal SSI payment is due. The
difference between the excess income and the OSS level is the OSS payment due (subject
to proration for months before 9/1/96).
e.
If CI is greater than the Federal payment limit and the excess income is equal to
or greater than the OSS level, no Federal SSI payment is due and no OSS payment is
due.
