C. Verifying the payment
Verify that the payment is from the EEOICP by:
•
Reviewing DOL documents in the claimant’s possession, or
•
Contacting the local DOL district office by telephone. A list of the DOL district
offices and telephone numbers is included on the DOL website at:
http;//www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/contact/local-offices
Click on the state on the U.S map or scroll down for the state to obtain the contact
information.
