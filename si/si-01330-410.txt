SI 01330.410 Deeming - Meeting Resource Eligibility Requirement - Policy
CITATIONS:
Sec. 211 of Public Law 93-66, as amended.
Regulations 20 CFR 416.1203, 20 CFR 416.1205
The countable resources of a qualified individual whose SSI payment includes an essential
person increment cannot exceed the resource limitation of:
•
an eligible individual, if the qualified individual has no spouse; or
•
a couple, if the qualified individual has a spouse. If the qualified individual's
spouse is the essential person, the resource limit for a couple applies.
