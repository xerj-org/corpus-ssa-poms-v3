SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02220.011 - Reconciliation of Overpayment Debt on Terminated Records
Effective Dates: 06/03/2021 - Present | TN 69 (06-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
The failure to close out an overpayment on a terminated record and transfer it to
the current record results in a lost opportunity to recover the overpayment. Adjustment
of ongoing payments is the most effective method of overpayment recovery. Yet, many
times overpayment data are not balanced and closed out on a terminated record and
then transferred for recovery on the current record. Using the automated reconciliation
process to the fullest extent possible will greatly increase SSA's ability to recover
SSI overpayments.
