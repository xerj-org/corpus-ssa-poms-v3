C. Procedure - Subsequent Changes
When a subsequent change occurs that may affect continuing benefits before they have
been paid, follow these steps:
STEP
ACTION
1
Determine if the change results in an adverse action. Do this by manually recomputing
benefits and comparing the new monthly amount with the proper amount reflected in
the appeal decision, not with the last payment on the record.
2
Issue the appropriate manual notice.
