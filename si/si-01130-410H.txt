H. Procedure for Use of Burial Funds
Use the following chart to determine if a redesignation of burial funds is necessary.
STEP
ACTION
1
Following the rule that non-excluded funds are used before excluded funds, as explained
in SI 01130.410C.5.f. in this section, compare the amount remaining in the fund (exclusive of interest)
with the amount originally designated.
2
Is the amount remaining less than the originally designated amount?
•
If No, go to Step 3.
•
If Yes, go to Step 4.
3
Does the individual wish to add money to the burial fund?
•
If No, go to Step 7.
•
If Yes, go to Step 6.
NOTE: Additional funds can only be excluded until the funds reach $1,500 unless a reduction
applies, per SI 01130.410C.1.b. in this section. Funds added in excess of this amount are not excluded.
4
Does the individual intend to add money to replenish the fund?
•
If No, go to Step 6.
•
If Yes, go to Step 5.
NOTE: Excluded funds can be replenished up to $1,500 unless a reduction applies, per SI 01130.410C.1.b. in this section. Funds added in excess of this amount are not excluded.
5
After funds are added, is the amount of replenished funds now different from the amount
originally designated?
•
If No, go to Step 7.
•
If Yes, go to Step 6.
6
Revise the designation of burial funds to reflect the new amount of burial funds or
other changes.
NOTE: This can be done in the remarks section on a redetermination using a paper form, the
value screen and File Documentation Notes section in the SSI claim system or otherwise
on an SSA-795. A new SSA-4169 - Burial Resource Statement is not required. For more
information on the SSA-4169, see SI 01130.414.
Go to SI 01130.410I. in this section.
7
No redesignation required. Go to SI 01130.410I. in this section.
