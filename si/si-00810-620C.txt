C. Example “worst
month” computation
Roberta Packard is the ineligible spouse of an eligible individual. Neither the eligible
individual nor their ineligible child has any income. Roberta reports expected monthly
earned income of $150 in January through April 2024, $400 in May through July, and
$587 in August and continuing. The FO does a “worst month” computation using earned income of $587 and a $472 allocation for the ineligible
child. The income remaining after this allocation is less than the $472 difference
between the FBR for a couple and that for an individual. Therefore, the FO places
the “worst month” computation in file and enters on the eligible individual's record continuing earned
income of $587 for Roberta beginning in January.
