B. Completing an RZ with a KB Diary
For cases already in the SSI Claims system, conduct the RZ in the system. For cases
that cannot be processed within the SSI Claims system, conduct the RZ using Form SSA-8203-BK
(Statement for Determining Continuing Eligibility for Supplemental Security Income
Payments). For additional information on when to complete a paper or electronic RZ,
see SI 02305.032.
Cover the following issues during the RZ:
•
Determine if the child meets the definition of a student per SI 00501.020B.
•
Determine if the child is eligible for Title II or other benefits (e.g., disabled
adult child benefits, a private pension, or government pension benefits based on child's
relationship to parent).
–
If the child works, they may have special insured status for Title II disability.
Refer the child to a Claims Specialist to take a Title II disability claim.
–
If the child receives Title II child's benefits and has not yet filed a disabled adult
child's claim, refer the child to a Claims Specialist to take the claim as required,
see RS 00203.080B.
NOTE: If Title II child's benefits terminate due to attainment of age 18, the system will
set a 2F diary on the record per SM 02001.207.
•
Consider direct payment of benefits.
•
Develop the correct living arrangement(s).
Monitor the record for compliance to the Filing of Other Benefits policy, see SI 00510.025.
