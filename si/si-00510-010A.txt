Effective Dates: 11/08/2017 - Present | TN 13 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500510010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure — VA Benefits — General
Explore potential eligibility for a VA payment if the claimant/recipient is a:
•
Veteran; or
•
Surviving child (see SI 00830.314B.6.) or an unmarried widow or widower of a veteran or service person (individual who
died while in service); or
•
Dependent parent (see SI 00830.314C.9.) of a disabled veteran or service person; or
•
Surviving parent (see SI 00830.314C.10.) of a veteran or service person who died from a service-connected cause; and
•
The criteria in SI 00510.010B. or SI 00510.010C. of this section are met.
REMINDER: When referring an individual to the VA, advise them to telephone first to obtain information
on filing requirements and proofs they may need to bring.
