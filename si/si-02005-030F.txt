SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02005.030 - Effect of Changes in Couple’s Status on SSI Payment Computations
Effective Dates: 05/09/2012 - Present | TN 24 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502005030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Special couple’s computation when one member already receives SSI and the spouse becomes
eligible
We pay an eligible couple the couple’s FBR minus the couple’s countable income. Generally,
each member of the couple receives a payment equal to one-half of the amount due the
couple. However, each member does not receive one-half of the amounts due the eligible
couple in the following situation.
Assume that one of the members received Title XVI payments, and subsequently the other
spouse becomes eligible for Title XVI benefits for some months for which we already
paid Title XVI benefits. In this situation, the newly eligible spouse is due an amount
for the overlapping months that is equal to the difference between the amount the
couple would be due minus what we paid the one spouse as an individual.
The Social Security Act only provides for the Social Security Administration (SSA)
to pay Title XVI benefits to an eligible couple. It does not provide that each member
be eligible to half of the couple FBR. SSA made a decision to pay each member of an
eligible couple half of what the couple is due. However, if we already paid one member
of the couple for a month, then we only can pay the other member an amount so that
the total paid equals the amount due the couple. For couple computation rules, see
SI 00501.155.
