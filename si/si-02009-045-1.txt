SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02009.045 - Both Members of an Eligible Couple Change to Different Living Arrangements Within a Quarter
Effective Dates: 04/24/2024 - Present | TN 2 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502009045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. One Member of a couple Goes From L/A A to L/A B, the Other Member Goes from L/A A
to L/A D Within a Quar­ter. There is No Month Where the Computation in SI 02009.030 Applies. - (Example 1)
Mr. and Mrs. Bertho are an eligible couple. Mr. Bertho receives a pension payable
the third month of each quar­ter in the amount of $540. On November 18, 1981 Mr. Bertho
enters a nursing facility and Medicaid pays over 50 percent of all costs. On November
23, 1981, Mrs. Bertho goes to stay with theiradult child while Mr. Bertho remains
in the nursing home. Mrs. Bertho makes no con­tribution to their family member's household
expenses (i.e., re­ceives in-kind support and maintenance). Mr. Bertho remains in
the nursing facility throughout December 1981.
