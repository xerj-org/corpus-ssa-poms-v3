SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.143 - Monthly Wage Reporting
Effective Dates: 09/26/2013 - Present | TN 91 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820143 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Glossary of terms for SSI wage reporting
1. Alleged
An “alleged” monthly wage amount is any:
•
monthly wage report an individual submits without evidence that does not meet the
criteria for us to consider it “reported;” or
•
wage report submitted without evidence for a month other than the current month, or
the month immediately preceding the current month.
2. Automated wage reporting methods
Automated wage reporting methods are electronic tools or systems that recipients,
deemors, and representative payees may use to report monthly wages. These methods
of wage reporting:
•
eliminate the need for field office (FO) intervention in processing most monthly wage
reports; and
•
post wages to the SSI claim system (i.e., MSSICS or CCE) and the Supplemental Security
Record (SSR) as “reported” wages.
The SSA Mobile Wage Reporting application (SSAMWR), the SSI Telephone Wage Reporting
system (SSITWR), and the myWageReport application (myWR) are all automated wage-reporting
methods.
3. Employer Identification Number (EIN)
EINs are unique identification numbers assigned to employers by the Internal Revenue
Service (IRS) for tax purposes. SSA uses EINs to record employer wage reports and
associate an individual’s earnings with a particular employer. EINs are present on
W-2 forms and may be included on pay stubs. Additionally, SSA records EINs on wage-related
queries like the Detailed Earnings Query (DEQY) and New Hire Data Query (NDNH). Using
the Alpha Access to the Employment Information File (AEQY), technicians may locate
EINs using employer information.
EINs are becoming increasingly important for automation in SSI wage reporting. For
example, myWR requires an EIN match in the SSI claim system or eWork in order to display
eligible employers to users. If the EIN field is not completed, myWR users will not
be able to report wages for that employer. For this reason, technicians are encouraged
to complete EIN fields on all Wages pages in the SSI Claim system (i.e., CCE) when
establishing new employers and updating information for existing employers.
See MS 08114.027 for more information about the Wages page.
4. J3 diary
The J3 diary detects discrepancies between the Master Earnings File (MEF) and the
SSR where a recipient or deemor’s wage amount on the SSR is more than what the MEF reflects.
The system generates an alert for over-tolerance discrepancies of more than $1,000
annually.
Refer to development instructions in SI 02310.056.
5. J5 diary
The J5 diary detects discrepancies between the MEF and the SSR where a deemor’s wage amount on the SSR is more than what the MEF reflects.
The system generates an alert for over-tolerance discrepancies of more than $2,000
annually.
Refer to development instructions in SI 02310.056.
6. Reported
A “reported” monthly wage amount is submitted by a recipient, representative payee
or deemor and is derived from pay stub wage information; the individual making the
report need not submit the underlying pay stub(s) at the time of the report. The reported
wage amount must be:
•
posted to the SSI claim system or the SSR before the recurring payment tape cutoff
for the subsequent month; or
•
submitted using myWR, SSAMWR, or SSITWR on any day during the current reporting month.
To consider wages “reported,” the submitted wage amount must include the total gross
wages received for the current month or the month immediately preceding the month
in which we receive the report.
7. S7 diary
The S7 diary detects discrepancies between the Office of Child Support Enforcement
(OCSE) database and the SSR where a recipient or deemor’s wage amount on the SSR is
more than what the OCSE reflects. The system generates an alert for over-tolerance discrepancies
of more than $250 per quarter for recipients and $500 per quarter for deemors.
8. SSI Monthly Wage Verification Application (SSIMWV)
SSIMWV is an Intranet-based application that automates SSA staff's input of monthly
wage reports submitted with pay stubs for electronic cases and cases outside of the
SSI claim system (i.e., non-MSSICS or non-CCE).
9. Verified
A “verified” monthly wage amount is accompanied by evidence of wages (e.g., pay stubs).
See SI 00820.130 for more information on wage evidence and SI 00820.135 for procedures on verifying wages.
