The State cannot recover any money for January and February since interim assistance
and SSI were not both paid for these months. The State can recover $280.00 for April
through June, but can only recover $153.55 for March, the prorated month. A State
cannot recover more than the prorated amount for March even if more SSI is available.
The recipient is eligible to receive $312.26 of the retroactive payment ($1,305.81
minus $993.55).
