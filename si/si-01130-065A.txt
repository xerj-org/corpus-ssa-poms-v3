A. Background
The NFIP is a Federal program established under the National Flood Insurance Act of
1968. Its purpose is to reduce future flood damage through community floodplain management
ordinances and to provide insurance coverage for property owners against potential
losses. The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) administers the NFIP.
