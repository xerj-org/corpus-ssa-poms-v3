A. Introduction
The purpose of the FS-SSA/APP and FS-Referred blocks is to indicate if we completed
a Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) application, formerly called food
stamps (FS), or if we referred a claimant to the local SNAP office. We use this information
to bill the Food and Nutrition Service (FNS) for the number of SNAP applications we
complete.
