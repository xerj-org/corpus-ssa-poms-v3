E. Policy - Witnessed Signature
When a paper or printout RZ is filed with a signature and the SSI individual has not
previously signed the application or another RZ by attestation, an Agency employee
will annotate CCE by receipting in the ATTEST issue in the Development Worksheet page..
This documents that the employee received the RZ form containing a signature. The
electronic annotation is deemed equivalent to a signature. Once the annotation is
made, the signed paper form is not retained in the RZ folder. The form is returned
to the individual with the appropriate cover notice.
