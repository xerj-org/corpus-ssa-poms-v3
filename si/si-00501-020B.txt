B. Definition of a student for purposes of the SEIE
A student for purposes of the SEIE is a recipient (including a deemor or member of
a couple) who is:
•
under age 22; and
•
regularly attending school, college, or training designed to prepare them for a paying
job as described in SI 00501.020C.1 in this section.
For information related to the student earned income exclusion, see SI 00820.510.
