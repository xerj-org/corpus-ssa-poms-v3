SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.116 - “Deemed Qualified Alien” Status Based On Battery Or Extreme Cruelty By A Family Member
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502116 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy -- General
1. Deemed Qualified Aliens
Alien claimants filing for federal benefits may be eligible as "deemed qualified aliens"
based on battery or extreme cruelty. The alien claimant must meet the following four
requirements to be considered "deemed qualified aliens" based on battery or extreme
cruelty:
a.
the alien, the alien's child, or the parent of an alien child must have been battered
or subjected to extreme cruelty by a spouse, parent, or other member of the family
living in the same household as the alien, and in the case of an alien whose child
is abused, the alien did not participate in the abuse, AND
b.
there is a substantial connection between the abuse and the need for the benefits
sought, AND
c.
the abusive act(s) must have taken place while the alien was in the United States,
but the battered alien must not be currently residing with the batterer, AND
d.
the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) or the Executive Office for Immigration
Review (EOIR) has approved the alien's petition, or has found that the alien's pending
petition sets forth a prima facie case, under one of the provisions of the Immigration
and Naturalization Act (INA) listed under SI 00502.116B.2 in this section.
2. Immigration Statuses Required For Eligibility
a.
Status as an Immediate Relative of a U.S. Citizen under section 204(a)(1)(A)(i) of the INA based on a petition filed by a U.S. citizen
for their spouse or child, OR
b.
Status as an Immediate Relative of a U.S. Citizen under section 204(a)(1)(A)(ii) of the INA based on a petition filed by a widow(er)
of a U.S. citizen to whom the alien had been married for at least two years at the
time of the citizen's death, OR
c.
Status as an Immediate Relative of a U.S. Citizen under section 204(a)(1)(A)(iii) of the INA based on a petition filed by an alien
spouse of a U.S. citizen with whom they have resided in the U.S., and the alien or
a child of the alien has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty by the citizen
spouse, OR
d.
Status as an Immediate Relative of a U.S. Citizen under section 204(a)(1)(A)(iv) of the INA based on a petition filed by a child of
a U.S. citizen with whom they resided in the U.S., and who were subjected to battery
or extreme cruelty by such parent during the period of residence, OR
e.
Classification to Immigrant Status as the Spouse or Child of a lawfully admitted for
permanent residence
(LPR) alien under section 204(a)(1)(B)(i) of the INA based on a petition filed by a LPR
for their spouse or child, OR
f.
Classification to Immigrant Status as the Spouse of a
LPR Alien
under section 204(a)(1)(B)(ii) of the INA based on a petition filed by the alien spouse
of the LPR alien with whom they have resided in the U.S., and the alien or a child
of the alien have been subjected to battery or extreme cruelty by the LPR alien with
whom they have resided in the U.S., OR
g.
Classification to Immigrant Status as the Child of a
LPR Alien
under section 204(a)(1)(B)(iii) of the INA based on a petition filed by the child
of the LPR alien with whom they resided in the U.S., and the alien child had been
subjected to battery or extreme cruelty by the LPR alien, OR
h.
Suspension of Deportation and Adjustment to
LPR
status under section 244(a)(3) of the INA as in effect prior to 4/1/97, based on a petition
filed by an alien who has been a continuous U.S. resident for at least three years,
and who (or whose child) has been subjected to battery or extreme cruelty by a spouse
or parent who is a U.S. citizen/LPR alien, OR
i.
Cancellation of Removal and Adjustment to
LPR
Status under section 240A(b)(2) of the INA, based on a petition filed by an alien who has
been a continuous U.S. resident for at least three years, and who has been subjected
to battery or extreme cruelty by a spouse or parent who is a U.S. citizen or LPR alien.
NOTE: Alien claimants who have filed a petition under SI 00502.116B.2.c, SI 00502.116B.2.d, and SI 00502.116B.2.f through SI 00502.116B.2.i (i.e., self-petition cases) may be considered "deemed qualified aliens" based on
battery or extreme cruelty if they have been granted a prima facie determination from
DHS or EOIR, as applicable, and meet all other requirements.
3. SSI Eligible Exception Categories of Battered Aliens
Alien claimants who are “deemed qualified aliens” based solely on battery or extreme cruelty may be eligible for SSI if, in addition
to meeting the other SSI eligibility requirements, they also meet one of the following
categories:
a.
Veteran (SI 00502.140C).
b.
Active duty military (SI 00502.140D).
c.
Spouse of a veteran or active duty military personnel
(SI 00502.140E.1).
d.
Child of a veteran or active duty military personnel
(SI 00502.140E.2).
e.
Aliens who are blind or disabled and were lawfully residing in the U.S. on
8/22/96 (SI 00502.142B).
