A. Introduction to PMV rule
When a claimant or couple receives in-kind support and maintenance (ISM), but does
not receive both food and shelter from the household in which the claimant or couple lives, the value
of the one-third reduction (VTR) rule does not apply and the ISM is valued under the
PMV rule.
This section describes the PMV rule and explains when to use it.
