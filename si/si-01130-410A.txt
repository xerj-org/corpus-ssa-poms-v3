A. Evolution of our burial funds policy
Burial funds first became excludable from resources on November 1, 1982. From that
date until April 1, 1988, we could exclude burial funds only for those months in which
resources would have otherwise exceeded the limit. Effective April 1, 1988, the exclusion
can apply whether or not the individual would have excess resources without it. Effective
August 1, 1990, other changes (as discussed below) were made to burial funds policy.
