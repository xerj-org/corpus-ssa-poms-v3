Effective Dates: 11/19/1996 - Present | TN 3 (11-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501410010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - general
Use your knowledge of your service area to overkey the prefilled State and county
code when appropriate.
NOTE: The Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System ( MSSICS) will prefill a State and county code based on the residence ZIP code. ZIP codes
reflect the State accurately but may cross county lines.
