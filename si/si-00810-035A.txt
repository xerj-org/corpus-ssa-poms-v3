A. Definition of unstated income
Unstated income is income not reported or otherwise known to the Social Security Administration
(SSA) but determined to exist because an individual’s (or couple’s) living expenses
exceed income from known sources. Claimants, recipients, and deemors may be found
to receive unstated income.
