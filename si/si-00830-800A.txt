A. Definition
Bureau of Indian Affairs General Assistance (BIA GA) is a federally funded program
administered by the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) through its local agency or a tribe.
The program makes periodic payments to needy Indians.
The administering agency determines need according to the standards used by State
welfare agencies for Aid to Families with Dependent Children (AFDC).
