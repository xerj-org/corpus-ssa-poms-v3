SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.745 - Department of Defense (DOD) Payments to Certain Persons Captured and Interned by North Vietnam
Effective Dates: 06/29/2004 - Present | TN 93 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830745 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
1. Statutory Authority
Section 606 of Public Law (P.L.) 105-78 provides that payments to certain individuals
who were captured and interned by North Vietnam are excluded from income.
2. What Agency Makes the Payments
Such payments are made by the Department of Defense pursuant to section 657 of the
National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1997 (P.L. 104-201).
3. Who Receives the Payments When the Captured and Interned Individual is Deceased
If the individual who was captured and interned by North Vietnam is deceased, payments
may be made to the surviving spouse or, if none, to surviving children (including
biological and adopted children) of the decedent, in equal shares.
4. How Much Money Does the Individual Receive
The amount payable for each captured and interned individual is a one-time payment
of $40,000. If the individual's confinement or internment exceeded 20 years, the payment
may include an additional $2,000 for each full year in excess of 20 (and a proportionate
amount for a partial year), not to exceed a one-time payment of $50,000 in total.
