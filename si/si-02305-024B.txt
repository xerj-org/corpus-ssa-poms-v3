B. Background
In 1935, Title IV-A of the Social Security Act established the Aid to Families with
Dependent Children (AFDC) program, which provided financial assistance to children
whose families had little to no income. In 1996, The Personal Responsibility and Work
Opportunity Reconciliation Act (PRWORA) replaced the AFDC program with the Temporary
Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) program. TANF allocates federal cash block grants
to the States for the purpose of providing assistance to families with children and
little to no income. All States were to implement TANF by July 1997.
