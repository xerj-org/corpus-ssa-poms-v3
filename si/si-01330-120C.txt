C. Procedure for pension funds
1.
Accept the allegation if an ineligible spouse alleges having money in a pension fund.
2.
Treat the withdrawal as a conversion of a resource per SI 01110.600B.4., if an ineligible spouse withdraws monies they had in a pension fund.
