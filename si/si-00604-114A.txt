A. When and how to complete question 3
Complete when the claimant's residence address differs from their mailing address
or when there is a representative payee. Enter the state and county. We need this
information to determine the correct amount of state payments and to establish categorical
Medicaid eligibility. When the state of residence is not clear, see SI 01410.001 Eligibility for State Supplementary Payments, through SI 01410.050 Public Contact About State and County of Residence.
NOTE: If the claimant owns a home in another state and we are excluding that home as the
principal place of residence because of intent to return, use the state where that
home is located.
