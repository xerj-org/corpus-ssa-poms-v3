SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00520.141 - Temporary Institutionalization (TI) Benefits - Notices
Effective Dates: 06/01/2009 - Present | TN 57 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500520141 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure — Manual Notice Situations
See the exceptions listed in SI 00520.140 D.2. for the corresponding letters used to identify particular situations.
1. Exceptions a, b, c
When notifying members of an eligible couple, use the appropriate paragraphs listed
in B.2. and B.3. below for both notices. However, modify the language for the notice
to the spouse not in the institution. For example, in paragraph 1922, instead of saying
“you entered a medical facility,” say “your spouse entered a medical facility.”
2. Exception d
Send a manual notice using the appropriate paragraphs listed in B.2. and B.3. below.
3. Exception e
Send a manual notice using the appropriate paragraphs listed in B.2. and B.3. below
if you identify this situation before initial input and you are not changing the living
arrangement. If the living arrangement is changing, also include either paragraph
2161 or paragraph 2162.
NOTE: If you identify this situation after initial input, and the living arrangement is
not changing, no manual notice is necessary.
If you are changing the living arrangement, send a manual notice and include paragraph
2161 or 2162. Also, when deleting the continued benefit eligibility information from
the record, suppress the notice. See SM 01305.760E.4.
4. Exception f
Suppress the automated notice and send a manual notice using the appropriate paragraphs
listed in B.3.c. below. Send the notice in the month the individual is placed in LA
D or NO2 status. Include in the denial notice either:
•
paragraph INTM50, fill-in 20, choice 37, 38, or 39; or
•
paragraph INTM51, fill-in 25, choice 43, 44, or 45.
5. Eligible Couple — Eligible for TI Benefits
When one or both members of an eligible couple are eligible to receive TI benefits,
you must prepare manual notices to each member of the couple if you can notify them
before the individual(s) leaves the institution.
NOTE: Manual notices are needed because the system can generate notices only after a month
of resolution (MOR) is input. (See SM 01305.750 and SM 01005.750.)
Send the notice(s) of eligibility as soon as you determine eligibility. Do not wait
until you make input to the system. (For example, one member of an eligible couple
is eligible for TI benefits in May 1993, but no MOR has been determined yet. Send
manual notices of eligibility in May 1993.)
6. Reversal of Denial of TI Benefits
When you are reversing a denial of TI benefits, send a manual notice. See B.3.d. below
for paragraphs to be used.
