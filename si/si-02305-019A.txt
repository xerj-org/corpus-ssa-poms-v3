A. Introduction
We use the Supplemental Security Income Notice of Appointment
to schedule post-eligibility personal contact interviews with SSI recipients and/or
representative payees.
Use the following notices for scheduling post-eligibility personal contact interview
appointments:
•
SSAL-3074-U2 - used for recipients (or their representative payee) who are other than
1619(b) eligible; and
•
SSAL-3075-U2 - used for 1619(b) recipients or their representative payees.
Both notices are located in the Document Processing System.
The notices are available in both English and Spanish.
