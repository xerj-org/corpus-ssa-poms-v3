SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00601.009 - Application Effective Date
Effective Dates: 01/04/2024 - Present | TN 78 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Examples
1. Aged Claim Application Filing Date is Before Attainment of Age 65 But Within the Same
Month
Elliot Smith files an SSI application in the field office (FO) on 9/15. He will attain
age 65 on 9/29 and the FO determines that he meets all other factors of eligibility
for September. His first payment will be on 10/1.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 9/29 in EFFECTIVE FILING DATE on Supplemental Security Income
Application page and 9/15 in RECEIVED on the Development Worksheet page for application.
For non-MSSICS inputs, enter on the SSA-450-SI, 9/29 in AP and 9/15 in Date of Filing
(DF).
If Elliot Smith had excess resources in September and spent down in September, he
would be eligible for SSI benefits 10/1. Assuming he meets all other factors of eligibility
for October, his first payment would be on 11/1.
For MSSICS inputs enter 9/29 in EFFECTIVE FILING DATE on Supplemental Security Income
Application page and enter 9/15 in RECEIVED on Development Worksheet page for application.
The Adjudicative Decision (DADJ) screen should reflect the payment status (PS) codes:
N04, EFFECTIVE DATE 09/01 and C01, EFFECTIVE DATE 10/01.
For non-MSSICS inputs on the SSA-450-SI, enter AP 9/29, DF 9/15, PS N04 09/01 and
C01 10/01.
NOTE: The system will overlay C01 with E02 for 10/1.
2. Aged Claim Application Filing Date is Before Attainment of Age 65 But Attainment is
in a Later Month
Robert Bowman files an SSI application in the FO on 9/5. He will attain age 65 on
10/19 and the FO determines that he meets all other factors of eligibility for October.
The E02 month is October and his first payment will be on 11/1.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 10/19 in EFFECTIVE FILING DATE on Supplemental Security Income
Application page and 9/5 in RECEIVED on Development Worksheet page for application.
For non-MSSICS inputs, enter 10/19 in AP and 9/5 in DF on the SSA-450-SI.
3. Disability Claim - Protective Filing Date and Receipt Date are in Different Months
Barb Jones contacts the Teleservice Center (TSC) on 8/18. She appears eligible for
SSI in August and September. The teleservice representative (TSR) schedules a teleclaim
appointment for 9/12. An SSI application is taken that day. The FO receives her signed
application 10/12. The DDS determines that Barb Jones is disabled as of 10/3. Since
she first meets all of the eligibility requirements on 10/3, her E02 month is October
and her first payment will be for 11/1. The system will derive the correct date payment
will begin based on the FO input and the DDS input of the onset date determination.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 8/18 in PROTECTIVE FILING DATE and EFFECTIVE FILING DATE
on Supplemental Security Income Application page and 10/12 in RECEIVED on Development
Worksheet page for application.
For non-MSSICS inputs enter 8/18 in AP and 10/12 in DF on the SSA-450-SI.
4. Aged Claim - Protective Filing Date, Receipt Date and the Date of Attainment of Age
65 Are Within the Same Month But the Receipt Date is Later Than the Date of Attainment
Laura Harris contacts the TSC on 5/10. She will attain age 65 on 5/12. She appears
eligible for May and June. The TSR schedules a teleclaim appointment for 5/15. The
application is taken that date and mailed to the claimant for signature. The application
is received in the FO on 5/31. Her E02 month is May and her first payment month is
June.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 5/10 in PROTECTIVE FILING DATE and EFFECTIVE FILING DATE
on Supplemental Security Income Application page. When the application is received,
enter 5/31 in EFFECTIVE FILING DATE and 5/31 in RECEIVED on Development Worksheet
page.
For non-MSSICS inputs, enter 5/31 in AP on the SSA-450-SI. No DF entry is required.
5. Aged Claim - Protective Filing Date, Receipt Date and the Date of Attainment of Age
65 are Within the Same Month But the Receipt Date is Earlier than the Date of Attainment
Donald Filer contacts the TSC on 2/3. He attains age 65 on 2/15. The TSR documents
the protective filing date in eLAS and schedules a teleclaim appointment for 2/5.
The teleclaim is taken that day and sent to Donald Filer for his signature. He signs
and mails the application to the FO the same day and it is received in the FO on 2/9.
His E02 month is February and his first payment month is March.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 2/3 in PROTECTIVE FILING DATE and EFFECTIVE FILING DATE.
When the application is received on 2/9, enter 2/15 (date of age 65 attainment) in
EFFECTIVE FILING DATE and 2/9 in RECEIVED on Development Worksheet page.
For non-MSSICS inputs, enter 2/15 in AP and 2/9 in the DF field on the SSA-450-SI.
6. Aged Claim - Protective Filing Date and Receipt in the Same Month (Age 65 Exception
Does Not Apply)
Wally Paddington, who is 68 years old, contacts the TSC on 5/10. He appears eligible
for May and June. The TSR schedules a teleclaim appointment for 5/25. The application
is taken that date and mailed to the claimant for his signature. The application is
received in the FO on 5/31.
For MSSICS inputs, enter 5/10 in PROTECTIVE FILING DATE and 5/10 in EFFECTIVE FILING
DATE on Supplemental Security Income Application page.
For non-MSSICS inputs, enter 5/10 in AP and 531 in the DF field on the SSA-450-SI.
7. Disability Claim Disabled Child Attains Age 18 in Filing Date Month and Deeming Applies
Alan Davidson files SSI disability application in the FO on 10/15. He attains age
18 on 10/24. The DDS determines that Alan Davidson is disabled as of October. Since
his parents' resources are deemed to him in the month of attainment of age 18, he
is ineligible for SSI for October and eligible for November as a disabled individual
when deeming no longer applies. His E02 month is November and his first payment month
is December.
For MSSICS inputs enter 10/15 in EFFECTIVE FILING on Supplemental Security Income
Application page and on Development Worksheet page in RECEIVED. Include the parental
information on the appropriate MSSICS screens so that the system can compute the correct
payment month.
For non-MSSICS inputs first establish the disabled child's claim entering 10/15 in
AP, PS N04 for 10/01 and C01 for 11/01 on the SSA-450-SI. No DF entry is required.
The system will overlay C01 with E02 for 11/01. Once the disabled child's record is
established, follow SM 01305.770 for changing the record composition to disabled individual
for November. NOTE: Please refer to POMS SI 00515.010 for Processing Certain Financial Permission Cases When a Deemor Refuses Permission
and the Claimant Turns Age 18.
