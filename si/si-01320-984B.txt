B. Individual Regains Eligibility Under the Usual Deeming Rules
If an individual regains SSI eligibility using the usual deeming rules, pay SSI in
accordance with the usual procedures. If the individual again becomes ineligible under
the usual rules, call the APS. Do not reapply the special deeming rules unless the
Board's approval of the waiver specifically addresses the new period of ineligibility.
After discussion with OSSI, IDB, the RO, APS will explain how to proceed.
