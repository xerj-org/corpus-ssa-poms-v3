C. Procedure for manual IAR notices IC and PC
Follow these instructions to prepare a manual IAR notice:
•
Add paragraph SSI009 (formerly #1046) to the SSA-L8025. For IAR notice language, see
NL 00804.125; and
•
Send the individual an SSA-L8165 (SSI Important Information) to inform the claimant
that the retroactive check goes to the State when the individual's recurring SSI check
precedes the retroactive check.
NOTES:
•
The SSA-L8165 does not include appeal rights because it is not an initial determination
subject to appeal.
•
If you send the notice to a claimant, beneficiary, or representative payee who alleges
being blind or visually impaired, follow the special notice procedures in NL 01001.010.
