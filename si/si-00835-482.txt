NOTE: For living arrangement periods effective 09/30/2024 or later, use form SSA-5064 to document a claimant or recipient’s statement about receiving a loan to cover shelter
cost; and use form SSA-L5065 to document a statement about covering shelter cost for a claimant or recipient.
These forms no longer require a signature.
NOTE: Prior to 09/30/2024, continue to use form SSA-5062 and SSA-L5063 to document receipt of a loan, or covering the cost of shelter on behalf of a claimant
or recipient via a loan. These forms no longer require a signature.
