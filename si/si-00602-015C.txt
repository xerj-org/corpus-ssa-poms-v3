SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00602.015 - Completing the SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)) as an Abbreviated Application (ABAP)
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 8 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500602015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Additional items to complete when using the SSA-8000-BK as an ABAP
1. Paper Application
Complete any item(s) that pertain(s) to the allegation (or evidence presented) that
causes ineligibility. If the reason for denial is:
a. Part II, Living Arrangements
Complete the questions in Part II that pertain to the living arrangement and in-kind
support and maintenance issue(s) that cause ineligibility. Also, complete question
38. Document the file per the instructions on living arrangements and in-kind support
and maintenance in SI 00835.000.
b. Resident of a Public Institution
Complete questions 20 and 21. Document the form per the instructions on institutionalization
in SI 00520.000. An ABAP is not appropriate if the claimant meets the requirements for a prerelease
case as explained in SI 00520.900.
c. Part III, Resources
Complete the questions in Part III that pertain to the allegation or evidence which
causes ineligibility and question 49.
d. Part IV, Income
Complete the items in Part IV pertaining to earned and unearned income as follows.
For:
•
unearned income only, complete questions 50 and 52.
•
earned income only, complete questions 53, 54, and 55. Also, complete question 57
if filing as a child.
•
unearned and earned income, complete all questions in Part IV.
2. SSI Claims System instructions for the ABAP
Enter the SSI Claims System and select Abbreviated from the Claim Type drop-down menu
on the Supplemental Security Income Application page.
The abbreviated application reason selected on the Supplemental Security Income Application
page determines which other pages are included in the path. Complete the pages necessary
to support the denial. For income denials, answer all questions on the Income selection
page. For resource denials, answer all questions on the Resource Selection page.
