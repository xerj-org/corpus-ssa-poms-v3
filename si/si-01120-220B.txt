SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.220 - Cash Loans
Effective Dates: 05/28/2024 - Present | TN 101 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Loan
A loan is a transaction whereby one party advances money to, or on behalf of another
party, who promises to repay the lender in full, with or without interest. The loan
agreement may be written or oral, and must be enforceable under State law.
2. Negotiable agreement
A negotiable agreement is (e.g., a loan) where the owner of the agreement itself can
transfer it from one person to another to include the whole amount of money expressed
on its face.
3. Bona fide agreement
A bona fide agreement is legally valid under the applicable State’s law and made in
good faith.
