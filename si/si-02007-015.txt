SI 02007.015 Nondisability Notices for Interim Benefits - SSI
PROCEDURE
The notice language for interim benefits is on the Document Processing System (DPS).
Send a manual notice to the claimant (or the representative payee) and any attorney
or nonattorney representative when:
•
You start interim benefits. This is the notice “SSI Important Information”;
•
You determine that interim benefits are not payable. This is the notice “SSI Important Information”;
•
A change occurs that increases the amount of the interim benefits. This is the “Notice of Change in Payment”; or
•
A change occurs that will decrease or stop interim benefits. This is the “Notice of Planned Action”.
