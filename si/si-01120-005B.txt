SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.005 - Distinguishing Resources from Income
Effective Dates: 08/01/2024 - Present | TN 91 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy principles
1. Income - Counting Rules
Items received in cash or in kind during a month are evaluated under the income-counting
rules.
2. Resource - Counting Rules
Items retained as of the first moment of the month following receipt are subject to
evaluation under resource-counting rules.
