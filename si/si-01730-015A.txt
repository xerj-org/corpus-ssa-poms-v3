SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01730.015 - Disability Determinations for Deceased Claimants to Establish Medicaid Eligibility
Effective Dates: 06/10/1996 - Present | TN 10 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501730015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When a claimant dies without a spouse or parent eligible for an underpayment after
filing an SSI disability or blindness application, a medical evaluation for initial
claims and reconsideration by the Disability Determination Services (DDS) is still
necessary when all of the following conditions are present:
•
The State has entered into an agreement for Federal determinations of Medicaid eligibility;
and
•
There are unpaid medical expenses (UMEs); and
•
The State Medicaid agency requests the medical evaluation.
