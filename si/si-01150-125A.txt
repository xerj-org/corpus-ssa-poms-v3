A. Policy — Transfer for purpose other than to obtain SSI
Section 206 of P.L. 106-169 (Foster Care Independence Act of 1999) provides an exception
to the period of ineligibility if the individual transferred the resource exclusively
for a purpose other than to obtain SSI benefits.
