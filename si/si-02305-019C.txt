C. Procedure for completing the fill-in spaces on Notices SSA-L3074-U2 and SSA-L3075-U2
Complete the fill-in spaces on the SSA-L-3074-U2 and the SSA-L3075-U2, as follows:
STEP
ACTION
1
•
Review the Supplemental Security Record (SSR) and the Post Entitlement Operational
Data Store (PE ODS) listing to determine whether a face-to-face or telephone interview
is appropriate.
EXAMPLE: ZH09 coding in the CG field of the SSR indicates that the recipient is deaf and uses
sign language. Therefore, a telephone interview is not appropriate.
NOTE: The presence of a diary may indicate that we require evidence. Additionally, the
recipient or representative payee may need to bring evidence to a face-to-face interview
subsequent to a telephone interview.
For more information on when to conduct a face-to-face interview, see SI 02305.019D.
2
Tear off the perforated tab at the top of the notice to separate pages 1 and 2.
3
On page 1, fill in the following information:
•
Recipient's name and mailing address or use a preprinted label;
•
Office address;
•
Recipient's social security number;
•
Date you complete the appointment notice;
•
Date and time of the scheduled interview.
4
Check the appropriate blocks on page 1 of the form and fill in:
•
The telephone number the recipient or representative payee must call and the name
of the person the recipient or representative payee must ask for if scheduling a call-in
interview; or
•
The telephone number you will call if you will initiate the telephone interview; or
•
The field office address and the person the recipient or representative payee must
ask for if scheduling a face-to-face interview.
5
Continue on page 1 and fill in the month/day/year the recipient needs to contact you
to avoid suspension (i.e., 30 days from the date of the notice).
6
On page 2, fill in the reference date. Use the proposed appointment date to determine
the reference date. (See SI 02305.034C. for instructions on determining the reference date.)
7
Check the appropriate item(s) the recipient or representative payee must:
•
Have available for telephone interviews; or
•
Bring with them for face-to-face interviews.
Include the name of the recipient or deemor for whom you need the item(s).
NOTE: For the third item, enter the earliest date (month/year) for which you need to verify
earnings.
In alien cases, use the other fill-in to indicate that, if the recipient's alien status
has changed since the last time they had contact with SSA, they must bring in evidence
showing their new status.
EXAMPLE: If you are not considered a U.S. citizen for SSI purposes and your immigration status
has changed since the last time we contacted you, please have available the necessary
papers that show your current alien status.
8
Enter the FO phone number and the person to ask for if recipients have any questions.
9
Enter the FO manager's name and title.
10
Enclose a copy of the SSA-3073 (or SSA-3073-SP for recipients who receive Spanish
notices ), Important Messages from Your Social Security Office.
NOTE: SSA is required to inform recipients about computer matching, Supplemental Nutrition
Assistance Program (SNAP), and Medicaid transfer of resources rules during the RZ
process. Enclosing the SSA-3073 with the appointment notice fulfills this requirement.
11
Retain a dated copy of the notice to document the beginning of the 30-day closeout
period.
