A. Procedure
Obtain answers in Part V for the time period specified in the questions .
NOTE: Several States have Combined Application Projects (CAP) or supplemental nutrition
assistance program (SNAP) waiver projects. In these States, the SSI application becomes
the SNAP application for some applicants. Refer to regional instructions if your State
has a SNAP waiver project.
