B. Procedure for FS-SSA/APP and FS-Referred blocks
After you complete question 58, mark one block, if applicable. For question 58, see
SI 00604.096.
NOTE: Do not mark the "FS-SSA/APP" block unless you complete a SNAP (Food Stamp) application.
