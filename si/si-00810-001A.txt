A. Background
The following sections explain how to treat income in the Supplemental Security Income
(SSI) program. These rules apply to the Federal benefit and to any federally administered
optional State supplement.
This chapter explains how we count income. For guidance on retrospective monthly accounting
computation, refer to SI 02005.001
