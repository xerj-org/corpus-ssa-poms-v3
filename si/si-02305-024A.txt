SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.024 - Limited Issues (LIs) with Continuing Type "F" Income
Effective Dates: 11/02/2006 - Present | TN 100 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305024 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Cases with a redetermination (RZ) profile code of "X" and a selection indicator of
"F" are selected for LI development because the record indicates continuing type "F"
income. However, if one of the following codes appear in the UMI field of the unearned
income data (UMIH) segment of the Supplemental Security Record (SSR), these cases
are not selected for LI development:
•
IRFG, IBIA, IOIA, or IIBN is in the first four positions of the UMI field of the UMIH
segment of the SSR; or
•
TANFC or MULTI is in the first five positions of the UMI field of the UMIH segment
of the SSR.
