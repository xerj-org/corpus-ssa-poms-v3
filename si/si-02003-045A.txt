A. Policy for when IAR may be appealed
1. Individual's right to appeal to the State
•
An individual has the opportunity for a State hearing if they disagree with the State's
actions during the IAR process.
•
An individual is not entitled to a Federal appeal on the State's actions regarding
repayment of interim assistance.
2. Individual right to appeal to SSA
An individual has a right to appeal to SSA if disagree with SSA’s determination of
the:
•
amount of IAR paid to the State;
•
months in the IAR period; or
•
remaining amount of SSI due the individual.
