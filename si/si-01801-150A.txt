SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01801.150 - Expedited Service for Purposes of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Benefits
Effective Dates: 01/04/2024 - Present | TN 31 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501801150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Conditions a household must meet for expedited service
Households in “immediate need” are those in which the household:
•
has less than $150 in monthly gross income (earned or unearned) and $100 or less in
liquid resources, e.g., cash or money in a checking or savings account (this includes
a one-person household);
•
has combined monthly gross income and liquid resources that are less than the household's
monthly rent or mortgage and utilities; or
•
is a migrant or seasonal farmworker with little or no income at the time of filing
for SNAP benefits (even though they may receive income at some other time during the
month of application).
