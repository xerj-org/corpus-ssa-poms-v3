C. Development and documentation
When it is necessary to develop resources which allegedly include HEA/SMA, obtain
the individual's statement either signed or recorded on a Report of Contact identifying
(if not already documented in file):
•
amount of HEA/SMA funds received;
•
when received and from what source; and
•
amount of remaining funds.
