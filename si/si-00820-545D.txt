D. Policy — Threshold for 1619(b) eligibility
When eligibility under section 1619(b) is being determined for an individual, BWE
and IRWE are used in the individualized threshold calculation described in SI 02302.050. (See SI 00820.540C for pre-12/1/90 requirements.)
