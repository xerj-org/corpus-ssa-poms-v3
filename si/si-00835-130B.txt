SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.130 - Public Assistance Households
Effective Dates: 09/24/2024 - Present | TN 129 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
A PA household is defined as:
•
Effective
09/30/2024, one that has both a Supplemental Security Income (SSI) applicant or recipient, and
at least one other household member who receives one or more of the listed PA payments.
•
Prior
to 09/30/2024, one in which every member of the household received a PA payment.
NOTE:
Prior to 09/30/2024, a SNAP benefit was not included as a PA payment.
