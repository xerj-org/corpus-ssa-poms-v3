SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01210.610 - Special Blind Income Disregards - Recipient With Nonblind or <Quote>Nonconverted</Quote> Eligible Spouse
Effective Dates: 11/06/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501210610 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
We never apply State plan disregards to the income of an individual who does not qualify
for the alternative income counting provision. When calculating countable income for
a “mixed” couple under the State plan, we apply State plan disregards to the income of the
blind converted recipient, and no disregards to the income of the nonconverted spouse.
