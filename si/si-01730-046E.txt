E. Policy — New Transfer of Assets Trust Exceptions
As described above, the revised transfer of assets provision also applies to certain
trusts. However, the new linkage between transfer of assets and trusts also contains
new specific trust exceptions which could be a benefit for recipients of large retroactive
SSI payments. (See SI 01730.048 for more information.)
