B. Process for automated IAR notices for IC and PE cases
Unless we suppress the notice, the system automatically generates an SSA-L8025 (Award
Notice) along with paragraph SSI009 (formerly #1046) to a claimant who qualifies for
SSI payments and whose retroactive check is sent to the State that provided interim
assistance in initial claims (IC) and post-entitlement (PE) situations.
