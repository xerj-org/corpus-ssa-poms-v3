C. How to handle expedited SNAP applications
Advise an applicant who appears to meet the expedited service criteria that they may
apply for SNAP benefits at SSA or the local SNAP office.
1. Applicant prefers to apply for benefits at the SNAP office
If the applicant prefers to apply for benefits at the local SNAP office, refer the
applicant to the nearest SNAP office. Be sure to give them the address and telephone
number of the local SNAP office. Then annotate “SNAP - Dire Need Referral” in the
Remarks section on the Supplemental Security Record (SSR).
2. Applicant prefers to apply for benefits at the FO
If the applicant prefers to apply for benefits at the FO, complete the expedited service
questions on the SNAP application (see SI 01801.150A) and complete the SSA-4233 (Social Security Administration Transmittal for Supplemental
Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Applications).
Be sure to:
•
check the “YES” box for “Expedited Service Requested” and write in bold “EXPEDITED
PROCESSING” across the top of first page of the SNAP application.
NOTE: If you mail the paper application to the SNAP office, write “EXPEDITED PROCESSING”
in red ink.
•
fax the application and all available or relevant documentation to the local SNAP
office to process via the SSA-4233; and
•
annotate “SNAP – Dire Need Referral” in the Remarks section on the SSR.
IMPORTANT: Immediately send the application to the SNAP office. Do not hold the application in
the office.
