E. How to develop the amount of excluded FVECF funds
When conducting a resource determination, accept the individual's allegation about
the amount of excluded FVECF funds still retained and exclude that amount. If the
individual is unable to provide an allegation about the amount of FVECF funds still
retained, develop the excluded amount using the instructions for commingled funds
in SI 01130.700.
