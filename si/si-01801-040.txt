SI 01801.040 Living Arrangements that May Affect Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)
Eligibility
Generally, a Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipient is not eligible for SNAP benefits if they live in:
•
an institution; or
•
a commercial boarding house.
IMPORTANT:
SSI recipients who reside in an institution or a commercial boarding house still have
the right to apply for SNAP benefits. Refer the applicant or recipient to the local
SNAP office to file an application, if they meet the criteria listed above. The SNAP
office will make an eligibility determination.
These instructions provide guidance about the prerelease provision under which a resident
of a public institution may apply for SNAP benefits at the same time that they apply
for SSI benefits, if the resident expects to live in a pure SSI household upon release.
