B. Procedure
1. ALJ Decision — Appellant Contacts FO And Is Not Represented
Assist the appellant in preparing his/her objections if the ALJ rendered a recommended
decision, or exceptions if the ALJ issued a final decision. Promptly forward them
to the Office of Appellate Operations, OHO. Do not prepare Form HA-520-U5.
2. If the Appellant Is Represented By Counsel
Ordinarily, do not deal with the appellant directly. Where either an ALJ or the AC
requests development in a remanded case, undertake development through counsel, unless
the appellant's attorney, the ALJ or the AC specifically requests direct contact with
the appellant.
