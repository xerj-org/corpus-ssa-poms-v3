SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.530 - Work-Related Unearned Income
Effective Dates: 02/18/2009 - Present | TN 147 (07-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830530 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The following work-related payments are unearned income:
•
Certain in-kind items provided as remuneration for employment (SI 00820.010A., SI 00835.390 and INTRANETSSI 014.064 for the SSI claim system input instructions).
•
Money paid to a resident of a public institution when no employer/ employee relationship
exists (SI 00520.010).
•
Tips under $20 per month.
•
Jury fees (i.e., fees paid for services, not expense money). (See SI 00830.100 if expense money is provided.)
•
Certain shelter provided to members of the Uniformed Services and their families (See
SI 00820.400).
•
Indian fishing rights income (SI 00830.880).
NOTE: Work-related unearned income, other than in-kind items, is recorded on the SSI claim
system IOTH Other Income page. See INTRANETSSI 014.064 for the input instructions.
