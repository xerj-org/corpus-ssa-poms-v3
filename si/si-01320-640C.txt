C. Income denial
A denial based solely on excess income will not always require a new deeming computation.
For example, in the situation described in SI 01320.640B.1.a. in this section, if Crystal were denied on the basis of deemed income, only
the income in excess of what is needed to reduce Crystal's benefit to zero is deemed
to Steven, per SI 01320.620B.6. In this case, a disability determination for Crystal is not obtained and the original
computation to determine Steven's benefit would stand as long as the deemed income
remained sufficient to deny Crystal or until some other change occurs that requires
a recomputation (e.g., Crystal spouse’s income decreases or Crystal’s record terminates).
NOTE:  Applications should not be solicited for the purpose of obtaining a denial based on
income. The parent should, however, be provided an explanation of the deeming policies,
including the effect of their decision regarding whether to file an application, whenever
the parent alleges being aged, blind, or disabled.
