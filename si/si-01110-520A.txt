SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01110.520 - Property Rights Without Ownership of the Property
Effective Dates: 09/13/2024 - Present | TN 52 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501110520 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
An individual may have certain rights with respect to property without also having
the right to dispose of the property. However, the individual may have the right to
sell their right or interest, i.e., the right to use or possess the property.
