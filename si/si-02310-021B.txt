B. Process - verification
1. Step 1
Obtain evidence from the individual's own records, if available.
2. Step 2
If the individual does not have sufficient evidence, contact the appropriate Military
Finance Center; see SI 02310.021B.3. Provide the following information:
•
The service member’s given name, middle initial and surname;
•
The service member’s service identification number (if available);
•
The service member’s SSN;
•
The annuitant’s or allottee’s name;
•
The annuitant’s or allottee’s SSN;
•
The period for which payment information is needed; and
•
A copy of the DOD extract (SI 02310.021C).
