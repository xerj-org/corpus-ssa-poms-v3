SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.450 - Replacement of Income Already Received
Effective Dates: 10/28/2024 - Present | TN 15 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy for replacement of income already received
•
If an individual's income is lost, stolen, or destroyed and the individual receives
a replacement, the replacement is not income.
•
Once a payment has been issued and treated as income in determining an individual's
eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI), the reissuance of that same payment
is not income. For instance, if one member of a couple receives income and dies before
the check is cashed, the reissued check is not income to the surviving spouse.
On the other hand, if the original payment is not used to determine the surviving
spouse's eligibility (e.g., because the couple is separated), the reissued check is
income to the surviving spouse. See SI 00830.545 for the treatment of death benefits.
