B. Procedure
If a potential criminal violation is discovered while conducting an RZ take the following
steps:
•
Complete the redetermination; and
•
Follow instructions in GN 04110 ff. for reporting potential violations and GN 04110
ff. for evidence requirements ; then
•
Follow recovery instructions in SI 02201.005D.3 and SI 02220.010B.3.b.
