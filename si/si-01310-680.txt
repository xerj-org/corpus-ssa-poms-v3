SI 01310.680 SSA-8010-BK - Burial Spaces - Question 12
Procedure
Complete 12(a) for all deemors (and their spouses, if applicable).
When completing 12(b) use the “Relationship to You or Your Spouse” column when the burial space item is not for the person making the statement (or
for their spouse).
