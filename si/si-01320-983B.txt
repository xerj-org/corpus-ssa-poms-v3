SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.983 - Effect of Federal Benefit Rate and/or State Supplementation Payment Increases on Special Deeming Cases — Operating Procedure (for Cases Approved or Pending Before January 1, 1985)
Effective Dates: 12/02/2024 - Present | TN 60 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320983 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Examples
1. Payment is $1 prior to FBR increase
Carol is a 9-year old child who is living with their parents and receiving $1 in SSI
benefits based on Board approval of the State Medicaid agency's application for waiver
of the usual deeming rules. Carol's payments are as follows:
November 1988 - $1.00
December 1988 - $1.00
January 1989 and continuing - $1.00
2. First month payment is reduced to $1 is month of FBR increase
John, who is 12 years old, was receiving $25 in SSI each month John was in the hospital.
The Board approved their State Medicaid agency's application and John returned home
to live with John's parents on October 15, 1984. John has no income.. There is no
State supplement. John's SSI payments are as follows:
October 1984 - $314.00 (Full FBR; no FCI in August)
November 1984 - $314.00 (Full FBR; no FCI in September)
December 1984 - $314.00 (Full FBR; no FCI in October)
January 1985 and continuing - $1.00 (Special deeming rules apply in payment computation)
3. FIRST MONTH PAYMENT IS REDUCED TO $1 IS THE MONTH AFTER THE FBR INCREASE
Elizabeth Johnson, a disabled adult, was receiving $25 in SSI each month while they
were in the hospital. The State Medicaid agency's application of November 4, 1984,
was approved by the Board and Elizabeth returned home to live with their (ineligible)
spouse on November 20, 1984. Elizabeth has no income.. There is no State supplement.
Elizabeth's SSI payments are as follows:
November 1984 - $314.00 (Full FBR, no FCI in September)
December 1984 - $314.00 (Full FBR, no FCI in October)
January 1985 - -$325.00 (Full FBR; no FCI in November)
February 1985 and continuing - $1.00 (Special deeming rules apply in payment computation)
4. FIRST MONTH PAYMENT IS REDUCED TO $1 IS MONNTH AFTER FBR AND STATE SUPPLEMENT INCREASE
Jane, who is 3 years old, was receiving $25 in SSI each month Jane was in the hospital.
The Board approved the State Medicaid agency's application on November 18, 1984, and
Jane returned home to live with their parents on November 21, 1984. The State supplement
of $26 will increase to $30 January 1, 1985.
Jane's SSI payments are as follows:
November 1984 - $340.00 (Full FBR +State supplement; no FCI in September)
December 1984 - $26.00 (Full State supplement only; see SI 02005.005 B.2.)
January 1985 - $30.00 (Full State supplement only (1/85 rate); see SI 02005.005 B.2.)
February 1985 and continuing - $1.00 (Special deeming rules apply in payment computation.)
