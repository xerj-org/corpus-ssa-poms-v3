C. Unrecoverable Debt Policy
1. Definition of Unrecoverable Debt
SSA considers a Title XVI debt to be unrecoverable if it meets all the following conditions:
•
The RECOOP billing status is past due, or the debt has been written off as uncollectible
(SSI overpayment sequence with a "DB" or "DM" TAC code, see SM 01311.200);
•
SSA has not entered into an installment agreement with the debtor within the past
30 days, or the debtor has failed to make any payment for forty five days;
•
No appeal or waiver is pending;
•
The debtor is a former recipient.
2. When a debt is unrecoverable
An SSI debt is unrecoverable when it meets the definition in SI
02220.013B.1. For purposes of the administrative offset program, a specific unrecoverable date
is not required.
