SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.112 - Initial Claims with Period of Ineligibility and Disability Determination Needed
Effective Dates: 01/25/2010 - Present | TN 43 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501150112 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — Determining allowance or denial
1. Claim was not sent to the DDS
If the claim was not sent to the DDS because the period of ineligibility does not
end and the individual does not meet the undue hardship requirements within the applicable
3-month period, process the claim as an N04 denial. See SI 01150.112D. for input instructions.
2. The DDS determines individual is not disabled
If the DDS determines that the individual is not disabled, process the medical denial.
It is not necessary to adjudicate the transfer of resources issue. However, the file
should be documented with the transfer information in the event that the individual
files a reconsideration on the medical denial.
3. The DDS determines individual is disabled
Conduct your normal pre-effectuation review of the claim. Determine for each month
starting with the month of filing whether the individual is eligible for SSI based
on undue hardship. As needed, determine if the individual is eligible for any months
because the period of ineligibility has ended.
a. Individual is eligible for at least 1 month
If the individual is eligible for at least 1 month of benefits, process the case as
an allowance (assuming all other eligibility requirements are met).
Example: Wilson transferred a resource on 3/5/00 and filed for SSI on 4/20/00. Due to the
resource transfer Wilson will be subject to a period of ineligibility from 4/01/00-9/30/02.
At the point that Wilson filed, it appeared to the CR that Wilson would qualify for
benefits based on undue hardship within the 3-month period, so the CR sent the case
to the DDS. The DDS determined that Wilson is disabled and returned the case to the
field office on 7/21/00.
The CR conducts the pre-effectuation review and determines that Wilson qualifies for
payments based on undue hardship starting in 6/00. When processing the allowance,
the CR inputs N04 for 4/00 and 5/00 because Wilson did not qualify for benefits based
on undue hardship. The CR inputs C01 starting in 6/00. The CR documents the file that
Wilson alleges undue hardship for future months and that it appears that Wilson will
meet the requirements based on an estimate of Wilson's total available funds expected
in the future months. Then, the CR tickles the case using an RT diary per SI 01150.112D.3. to develop whether Wilson will continue to meet the requirements for payments
based on undue hardship.
b. Individual is not eligible for an SSI payment
If the individual is not eligible for any SSI payment since the month of filing, process
the case as a denial.
Example: Jones filed for SSI on 5/11/00. In 3/00 Jones gave away $15,000 cash to a relative
so Jones is subject to a period of ineligibility. At the point Jones filed their claim,
it appeared to the CR that Jones was likely to qualify for benefits based on undue
hardship within the 3-month period, so the CR sent the case to DDS. The DDS determined
that Jones is disabled and returned the case to the field office on 9/7/00. The CR
conducts a pre-effectuation review on 9/15/00 and determines that, due to Jones' total
available funds, Jones did not qualify for benefits based on undue hardship from the
month of filing through 9/00. Therefore, the CR processes the claim as a denial and
issues a manual notice to Jones on 9/15/00. On 11/3/00 Jones comes back to the office
and requests benefits based on undue hardship as of 10/00. Since Jones is within the
appeal period, the CR processes their request as a reconsideration of the 5/11/00
initial claim.
