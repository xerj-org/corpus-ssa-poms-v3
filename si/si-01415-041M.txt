M. Description of supplements - Pennsylvania
1. Definitions of State living arrangement variations
Code
Definition
C
Living with an EP -- Recipients who are not living in the household of another or
in a foster care home for adults and who have one or more EP’s as defined under the
Federal rules.
D
Living in the Household of Another with an EP -- Recipients who live in the household
of another and have one or more EP’s as defined under the Federal rules.
G
Living in a Domiciliary Care Facility -- Adult persons (age 18 and over) certified
by the State to be residing in non-medical residential care facilities.
H
Living in a Personal Care Boarding Home -- Adult Persons (age 18 and over) certified
by the State to be residing in non-medical residential care facilities.
Y
Optional Supplementation Waived -- Individual is eligible for, but waived their right
to receive, an optional supplement.
Z
No Supplement Cases -- Includes all recipients who are residing in a medical facility
in which Title XIX is paying more than 50 percent of the cost of care and recipients
in FLA-A or FLA-B who receive a State-administered supplement.
