SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.110 - Loss of Essential Person Status - SSI
Effective Dates: 01/05/2024 - Present | TN 78 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principles
1. EP Criteria Not Met
Individual loses EP status permanently if for any month after December 1973 does not
meet each of the criteria in SI 00501.100B.2.
2. EP Becomes Eligible for SSI
No longer an EP beginning the month they become eligible for SSI.
3. Qualified Individual Chooses to Delete EP
•
A qualified individual may request in writing that SSA determine the FBR without the
addition of an EP increment even if the deeming of income and resources from the EP
does not cause a reduced payment or ineligibility.
•
SSA must explain to the individual how the EP increment works (i.e., it may either
lower or increase the monthly payment depending upon what income the EP has).
•
The request is irrevocable and results in the permanent loss of EP status for any
person excluded by the request.
•
The loss of EP status is effective the month following the month of the written request.
4. Temporary Absences from the Home
a.
EP remains an EP if:
•
Intends to return; and
•
Facts support intention; and
•
There is a likelihood of return; and
•
Absence does not exceed 90 days.
b.
If a qualified individual is temporarily absent from their home (e.g., hospitalization), the EP increment continues if:
•
Intends to return; and
•
Facts support intention; and
•
There is a likelihood of return; and
•
Absence does not exceed 6 months.
5. EP's Income and Resources Cause Loss of Eligibility
Determine eligibility without consideration of that EP. The EP loses that status permanently
and the increment no longer applies.
