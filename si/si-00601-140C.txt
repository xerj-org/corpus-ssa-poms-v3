SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00601.140 - Final Request - Failure to Pursue Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Eligibility (N17)--Initial Claims
Effective Dates: 09/15/2023 - Present | TN 42 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Obtain Explanation
Ask the claimant/potential representative payee why they do not wish to pursue the
SSI claim.
a.
Provide assistance if the reason is because of difficulty in obtaining the information/evidence.
b.
Provide assistance if the claimant has difficulty communicating, e.g., language barrier.
2. Confirm Decision
a.
Attempt to confirm the decision by contacting the claimant if the statement is made
by the potential representative payee, who is other than the legal guardian or parent
with custody of a minor child.
b.
Develop for a new payee if the claimant does not confirm the potential representative
payee's statement.
3. Documentation
a. Claimant/Representative Payee in Field Office (FO)
Obtain the individual's signed statement that they no longer wish to pursue the claim.
b. Claimant/Representative Payee Not in FO
Record the individual's telephone statement that they no longer wishes to pursue the
claim on the Report of Contact screen in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE)
Development Menu, if the claim was taken in the CCE, or on a Report of Contact (RC)
form. If a RC was used, fax the RC into the Evidence Portal (EP). (See SI 00601.080 for documentation requirements.)
