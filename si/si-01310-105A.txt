SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.105 - Deeming Concept - Alien
Effective Dates: 04/13/1999 - Present | TN 6 (04-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
An alien (for purposes of deeming) is an individual lawfully admitted for permanent
residence in the U.S. Sponsored aliens who meet the criteria in SI 01320.915 are considered lawfully admitted as permanent residents, and may be subject to sponsor-to-alien
deeming.
