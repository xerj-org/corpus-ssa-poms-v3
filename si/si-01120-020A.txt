Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
An eligible individual (EI) or deemor may have an agent to act on their behalf or
may serve as an agent for someone else. When an agency relationship exists, it is
important to distinguish an agent's actions on their own behalf from those on behalf
of the person for whom they serve as agent.
