A. Policy for completing question 13
If a qualified alien meets the requirements in SI 00502.140A, they meet the military service condition for eligibility. In general, a qualified
alien is potentially eligible under this condition if the alien, their spouse, or
parent (if the alien is under 18) is on active duty in the U.S. armed forces or is
an honorably discharged U.S. veteran.
