SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.705 - Refunds of Taxes Paid on Real Property or Food
Effective Dates: 09/30/1991 - Present | TN 44 (10-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830705 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Accept an allegation that a refund of this nature has been received and exclude the
income without further development unless you have reason to question the allegation
(e.g., the program making the refund is unknown, the amount of the refund appears
inordinate, etc.).
