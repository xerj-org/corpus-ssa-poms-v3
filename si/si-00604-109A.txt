A. Introduction
Two of the alternative signature methods described in GN 00201.015 apply to Supplemental Security Income (SSI) applications, attestation and witnessed
signature (EXCEPTION: MSSICS exclusions). When the claimant files an SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental
Security Income) with a pen-and-ink signature, follow the procedures below.
