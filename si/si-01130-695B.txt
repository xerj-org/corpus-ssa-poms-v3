Effective Dates: 06/30/2004 - Present | TN 127 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130695 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The following payments, regardless of when received, are excluded from income and
resources for both SSI and Medicaid purposes:
•
Payments from the Ricky Ray Hemophilia Fund;
•
Payments made from any fund established pursuant to a class settlement in the case
of Susan Walker v. Bayer Corporation, et al., 96-C-5024 (N.D. Ill); and
•
Payments made pursuant to a release of all claims in a case that is entered into in
lieu of the class settlement mentioned above and that is signed by all parties in
the case on or before the later of (1) December 31, 1997, or (2) the date that is
270 days after the date on which such release is first sent to persons (or the legal
representative of such persons) to whom the payment is to be made.
There is no time limit for this resource exclusion.
Prior to July 1, 2004, any interest earned on retained funds is not excluded from
income or resources under this ACT for SSI purposes. For SSI benefits payable on or
after July 1, 2004, interest earned on retained funds is excluded from income for
SSI purposes. (See SI 00830.500C.)
