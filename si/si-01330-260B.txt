B. Policy — resource limit
A child's countable resources are compared with the resource limit for an individual
with no spouse. If the resources do not exceed the limit as of the first moment of
the month, the child meets the resource eligibility requirement. If countable resources
exceed the limit as of the first moment of the month, the child is ineligible because
of excess resources.
