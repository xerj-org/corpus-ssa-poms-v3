A. Introduction
For SSI purposes, many Federal statutes provide for the exclusion from income and
resources of certain payments made to members of Indian tribes and groups. Some statutes
pertain to specific tribes or Indian groups while others apply to certain types of
payments. Some statutes that predate the SSI program provide that some payments made
under those acts shall not be considered as income or resources when determining eligibility
for assistance under the Social Security Act.
