A. Appeals council (AC) review policy
The AC considers Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) decisions and orders of dismissal
at the request of the claimant or on its own motion. When the AC receives a request
to review an ALJ action, the AC may deny or dismiss the request for review, or grant
the request and issue a decision or remand the case to the ALJ.
