SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.130 - Filing For Medicaid Based on SSI
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 75 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
In states where SSA makes the title XIX determination, inform the applicant that the
SSI application is also an application for Medicaid and that there is no need to file another application.
NOTE: If unpaid medical expenses
exist for the period prior to the month of the SSI application, the State may require a
separate Medicaid application.
