E. Procedure - Federal Means-Tested Public Benefits
If earnings are posted to the worker's (the LAPR alien and/or their parent(s) and/or
spouse(s)) E/R beginning 1/1/1997, or alleged by the worker beginning 1/1/1997, ask
if any Federal means-tested public benefits were received by the LAPR alien or the
worker in the same quarter the earnings were paid. (See SI 00502.135B.3.)
Accept the allegation regarding the Federal means-tested benefit absent evidence to
the contrary. If verification is required, contact the appropriate State agency administering
the benefit and obtain the date(s) the individual was eligible.
