B. When To Use Deferred Development
Use deferred development in all claims that do not meet the requirements for simultaneous
development in SI 00603.002C.
(Deferred development systems procedures are located in SM 01005.650 and SM 01010.055.
For MSSICS cases, see MSOM MSSICS 008.003 .
