SI 00835.042 Temporary Absence of a Child from a Federal Living Arrangement Due to School Attendance
CITATIONS: Regulations 20 CFR 416.1148(b)(2); 20 CFR 416.1149(c)(2)
Use this section to determine in-kind support and maintenance (ISM) for a child under
age 22 who is temporarily absent from the permanent living arrangement due to school
attendance. For an eligible individual age 22 or older, there is no provision for
an extended temporary absence due to school attendance.
