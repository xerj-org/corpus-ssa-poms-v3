C. Policy — cost of living adjustment (cola) notice
A COLA is an initial determination in that the claimant can question our calculation.
Direct depositors receive a printed notice. For nondirect depositors who get a check
stuffer, use the first check issued in the increased amount as the notice of the COLA
for counting the period for appeal purposes.
NOTE: See GN 04030.060 for information on COLA's and administrative finality.
