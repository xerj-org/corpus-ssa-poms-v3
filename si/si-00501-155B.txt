B. Policy - Couple Computation Rules
1. Couple Computation Criteria
The couple computation criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a., SI 00501.154A.1.b., , SI 00501.154A.1.c. and SI 02005.030 continue to apply.
2. Application Filed on or After August 22, 1996
For applications filed on or after August 22, 1996, the following rules apply to couple
computations:
a. Month of Application
Couple computation rules apply only if the criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a., SI 00501.154A.1.b., and SI 00501.154A.1.c. are met as of the first day of the month following the date the application is filed.
b. Month of Request for Reinstatement of Benefits
Couple computation rules apply only if the criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a., SI 00501.154A.1.b., and SI 00501.154A.1.c. are met as of the date a request for reinstatement is filed.
c. Separate Applications or Requests in a Month
•
If, in a month, the members of a couple file separate applications, couple computation
rules apply only if the criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a., SI 00501.154A.1.b., and SI 00501.154A.1.c. are met as of the first day of the month following the month the separate applications
are filed.
•
If, in a month, the members file separate requests for reinstatement of benefits,
couple computation rules apply only if the criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a., SI 00501.154A.1.b., and SI 00501.154A.1.c. are met as of the latest request date.
•
If, in a month, one member files an application and the other a request for reinstatement,
couple computation rules apply only if the criteria in SI 00501.154A.1.a, SI 00501.154A.1.b., and SI 00501.154A.1.c. are met as of the first day of the month following the date the application is filed.
NOTE: The proration of benefits policy in SI 02005.007A. will no longer apply to initial applications filed on or after 08/22/96. For reinstatement
of benefits, the proration rules in SI 02005.007E. continue to apply.
