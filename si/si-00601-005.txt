SI 00601.005 Introduction to Applications Policy
The basic application policies common to title II and title XVI are in GN 00201.000 - GN 00210.000. Specific supplemental security income (SSI) policies and procedures are discussed
in SI 00601.008 - SI 00601.140. For instructions regarding SSI disability claims, see DI 11005.001 - DI 11005.605 and DI 11055.001 - DI 11055.415.
