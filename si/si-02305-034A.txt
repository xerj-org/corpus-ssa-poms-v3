SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.034 - Period of Review Redeterminations (RZs) and Limited Issues (LIs)
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 110 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305034 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions
1. Period of Review
The period of review is the time period that the RZ or LI covers,The RZ period of review covers eligibility
factors both prospectively and retroactively. The beginning month of the period of
review cannot exceed 2 years (plus 2 months for cases where the retrospective monthly
accounting computation is used for income received in those months as budget months)
retroactively from the initiation date of the current RZ or LI. The period of review
for development ends with the last day of the month before the current RZ or LI initiation
date.
When RZs are completed in the SSI Claims system, the system determines the begin date
of the period of review.
Prior LI case completion has no effect on the current RZ period of review.
2. Reference Date
The reference date is the date the period of review begins. The reference date is the latest date of
the following:
•
The month the last RZ was initiated (the RZI field of the CRZD segment of a SSID,
as described in SM 01601.460);
•
The month of application (use the date in the AP field of the PRSN segment of a SSID,
as described in SM 01601.475);
•
The month the PERC was completed (the PC field of the CRZD segment of a SSID, as described
in SM 01601.460);
•
The first month of eligibility (the DOE field of the PRSN segment of a SSID, as described
in SM 01601.460); or
•
2 years (plus 2 months for cases where the retrospective monthly accounting computation
is used for income received in those months as budget months) retroactively from the
initiation date of the current RZ or LI.
3. Initiation Date for FO RZs
The RZ initiation date (RZI) is the date you obtain allegations on the RZ forms. It is the date:
•
A redetermination event is opened in the SSI Claims system.
•
An RZ form is completed and signed at a face-to-face interview.
•
An RZ is completed by telephone interview.
The initiation date is important because it serves as protective filing for recipients
who are entitled to receive Title II benefits on the date the RZ is initiated. (See
GN 00204.015 for a discussion of RZs as protective filings).
See SI 02305.034E. for a discussion of the importance of transmitting the RZI as soon as
possible after the RZ interview.
