A. Introduction to health flexible spending arrangements (FSA)
Health flexible spending arrangements (FSAs), also known as flexible spending accounts,
are an employer-established benefit plan used to reimburse employees for qualified
medical expenses. An employee’s voluntary salary reduction agreement with the employer
usually funds health FSAs. The employer may also contribute to the health FSA. Self-employed
individuals are not eligible for health FSAs.
See information on salary reduction agreement in RS 01402.010 and cafeteria benefit plans in SI 00820.102.
