A. Policy — Exception for non-home transfers to certain family members
The period of ineligibility for transferring a non-home resource at less than fair
market value does not apply if the resource was transferred to:
•
the transferor's spouse (including a separated spouse); or
•
another person for the sole benefit of the transferor's spouse; or
•
the transferor's child of any age who is blind or disabled.
NOTE: The period of ineligibility also does not apply if the resource is first transferred
to the transferor's spouse and the spouse subsequently transfers it to another individual
for the sole benefit of the spouse.
