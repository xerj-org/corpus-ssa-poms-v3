3. Individual's SSI Payment is Less Than the 10 Percent Rate
Don Acuna is due a Federal SSI payment of $50.00 for April 2026 based on the budget
month's countable income of $944.00. Because 10 percent of the sum of the countable
income and the amount due exceeds the SSI payment, the proposed reduction rate will
be $50.00 (the entire SSI payment).
