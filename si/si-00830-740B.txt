SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.740 - Radiation Exposure Compensation Trust Fund and Radiation Exposure Compensation Act Payments
Effective Dates: 05/19/2026 - Present | TN 281 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830740 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. RECTF and RECA Payments
Payments from the RECTF and RECA are excluded from income.
2. Interest on Unspent RECTF and RECA Funds
Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent RECTF funds is counted as
income. For SSI benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent RECTF
and RECA funds are excluded from income. (See SI 00830.500C.)
