B. How to complete questions 22 and 23
Check the block that describes the noninstitutional care situation in question 22.
Questions 23(a) and (b) collect identifying information about the facility and placement
agency. The purpose of (c) is to determine the value of any in-kind support and maintenance
(ISM) the claimant may be receiving.
