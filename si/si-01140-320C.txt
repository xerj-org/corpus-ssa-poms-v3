C. Policy—USDA Contract As a Resource
The USDA contract for the installment payments is a resource as of the month following
the notification date month shown in item 2 on the CCC-960, “Tobacco Transaction Payment
Notification of Contract Approval and Appeal Rights”, because the contract can be
converted to cash by entering into an assignment or successor-in-interest contract.
See SI 01140.320F. for determining the cash value of the USDA contract.
