B. Definitions used in foreclosure for SSI purposes
Foreclosure - a process through which a lender or mortgagee seeks to regain the property because
the borrower has failed to comply with an agreement between the two parties, most
commonly by defaulting on payments.
Home - an individual’s home is property in which they have an ownership interest and that
serves as their principal place of residence.
Real Property - land and the improvements made on it, for instance buildings, wells, dams, and
other additions as well as what lies beneath it (e.g. oil or minerals).
Short Sale – the sale of the property for less than the amount of the secured loans or mortgage
on the property. Short sale is usually made to avoid foreclosure. The lender generally
has to approve a short sale in order for the sale to go to settlement. This usually
requires a market analysis to show the market has declined and the property cannot
currently sell for the amount still owed.
