SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01801.040 - Living Arrangements that May Affect Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Eligibility
Effective Dates: 06/07/2018 - Present | TN 34 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501801040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Residents of commercial boarding houses
Generally, residents of a commercial boarding house are not eligible for SNAP benefits. For SNAP eligibility purposes, a commercial boarding
house is an establishment licensed by the state or a local jurisdiction to offer meals
and lodging for compensation. In the absence of state or local licensing requirements,
a commercial boarding house is a commercial establishment that offers meals and lodging
for compensation with the intent to make a profit.
If a resident of a commercial boarding house inquires about SNAP benefits, take the
following actions:
•
explain that they may not be eligible for SNAP because of the rules concerning residents
of a commercial boarding house;
•
offer informational materials about SNAP benefits and the address and phone number
of the local SNAP office; and
•
explain that they can file an application at the SNAP office for a SNAP eligibility
determination.
NOTE:
Residents of a non-commercial boarding house are usually considered an “individual
household” for SNAP eligibility purposes. For more information about non-commercial
boarders, see SI 01801.060A.2.
