A. Procedure for when to use Form SSA-L5061
Use Form SSA-L5061 to develop rental subsidy whenever you request rental information
from a landlord and it is not feasible to document the issue solely within the SSI
Claim System. Instructions for preparing the paper document for mailing and documenting
the electronic file are provided below.
