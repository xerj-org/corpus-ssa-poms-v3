A. Background on resource exclusions for household goods and personal effects
A change in the regulations, effective March 9, 2005, establishes that the resource
exclusion for household goods and personal effects no longer has a dollar limit. As
a result, beginning with resource determinations for April 2005, SSA no longer counts
household goods and personal effects as resources to decide a person’s eligibility
to receive Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits.
