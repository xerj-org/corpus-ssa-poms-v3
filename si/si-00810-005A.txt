SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.005 - What is Income
Effective Dates: 07/28/2023 - Present | TN 66 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions
1. Definition of income beginning 03/09/2005
Income is any item an individual receives in cash or in-kind that can be used to meet
their need for food or shelter.
Income includes, for the purposes of title XVI, the receipt of any item which can
be applied, either directly or by sale or conversion, to meet basic needs of food
or shelter.
NOTE: See SI 00835.400 for in-kind support and maintenance policy and procedures pertaining to this regulation
change.
2. Definition of income before 03/09/2005
Income is any item an individual receives in cash or in kind that can be used to meet
their need for food, clothing, or shelter.
Income includes the receipt of any item which can be applied, either directly or by
sale or conversion, to meet basic needs of food, clothing, or shelter.
3. In-kind income beginning 03/09/2005
In-kind income is not cash, but is actually food, or shelter, or some item that can
be used to get one of these basic need items.
4. In-kind income before 03/09/2005
In-kind income is not cash, but is actually food, clothing, or shelter, or some item that can be used to get one of these basic need items.
