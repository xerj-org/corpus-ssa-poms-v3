E. Procedure
Develop allegations of ownership of real property, as follows:
1. If recipients allege no ownership of real property and other information in SSA records
agrees
If recipients allege no ownership of real property and other information in SSA records
agrees, do not develop ownership of real property any further.
2. If recipients allege no ownership of real property and other information in SSA records
conflicts
If recipients allege no ownership of real property and other information in SSA records
conflicts, did the recipient dispose of a resource which we have on record?
•
If so, develop as appropriate for an increase/decrease in the value of resources per SI 01110.600. If it appears the resource was disposed of for less than the fair market value,
see SI 01150.110.
•
If not, develop per SI 01140.100 and SI 01130.140.
3. If recipients allege ownership of real property
If recipients allege ownership of real property, develop per SI 01140.100 and SI 01130.130.
