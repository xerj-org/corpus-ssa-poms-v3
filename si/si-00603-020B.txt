B. Definitions
There are two separate procedures established for early notification.
1. DDS early alert notification
The DDS Early Alert Notification procedure is used when the disability reviewer has
determined that the claimant is disabled. (This procedure is a regional option.)
2. Representative payee alert
The Representative Payee Alert is the early alert of an opinion or potential opinion
of incapability. (This procedure is described in DI 23001.001.)
