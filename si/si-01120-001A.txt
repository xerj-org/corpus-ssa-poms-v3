A. Introduction
This subchapter deals with the process of applying the basic principles in subchapter
SI 01110 in determining whether property (an asset) is a resource. If it is a resource,subchapters
SI 01130 and SI 01150 provide guidance on possible exclusions. If a resource is not
excludable, see subchapter SI 01140. These guidelines apply to both initial claims
and to posteligibility situations.
