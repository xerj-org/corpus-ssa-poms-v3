C. Procedure exclusions
Consider exclusions listed in SI 00810.410, SI 00830.050 through SI 00830.099, SI 00820.500 and SI 00830.060 which may reduce the amount of countable income. For income excluded under a PASS,
develop for compliance with the plan per SI 00870.055, so it can be determined whether the exclusion should be continued, be adjusted,
or stopped.
