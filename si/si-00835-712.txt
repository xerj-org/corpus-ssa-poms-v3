SI 00835.712 Exclusion of In-Kind Support and Maintenance for Residents in Private Nonprofit Residential
Care Institutions
CITATIONS: Social Security Act, as amended, section
1612(a)(2)(A); Regulations, 20 CFR 416.1144
This section applies only when the eligible individual lives in a private nonprofit
residential care institution.
When the private nonprofit institution pays for some or all of the individual's food
and shelter, it is necessary to determine whether the food and shelter counts as In-kind
Support and Maintenance (ISM). This section provides the instructions to make that
determination.
