SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.800 - Fugitive Felon Status for Residents of Connecticut, New York, and Vermont—<Italic>Fowlkes</Italic> Court Case
Effective Dates: 07/18/2007 - Present | TN 12 (06-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530800 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Examples
1. Example - The Fowlkes AR applies to a previous determination on fugitive felon status
a. Fully Favorable Relief on Reconsideration
John lives in Vermont. John's SSI benefits were suspended effective May 2005, because
of an unsatisfied felony warrant. John's suspension notice was dated 10/04/05. John
filed a timely request for reconsideration, which was denied on 12/20/05. John did
not appeal the reconsideration determination. On 09/06/06, John requested that the
Fowlkes AR be applied to John's claim. John meets the requirements for Fowlkes relief upon readjudication because John was living in a Second Circuit State when
John's request for reconsideration was denied, and the reconsideration notice is dated
after 12/06/05 (the date of court decision). John's benefits may be reinstated retroactive
to May 2005, the first month of N25.
b. Fully Favorable Readjudication
Jane lives in New York. Jane filed for SSI benefits on 11/22/05. Jane's claim was
denied because of an unsatisfied felony warrant. Jane's denial notice was dated 01/04/06.
Jane did not appeal the denial. On 05/17/06, Jane requested that the Fowlkes AR be applied to Jane's claim. Jane meets the requirements for Fowlkes relief upon readjudication because Jane was living in a Second Circuit State when
Jane's SSI claim was denied, the denial notice is dated after 12/06/05 (the date of
the court decision), and Jane is living in the Second Circuit when Jane's claim is
readjudicated. Jane's claim is developed for all factors of eligibility.
2. Example - The Fowlkes AR does not apply to a previous determination on fugitive felon status that became
final before 12/06/05
Mary lives in New York. Mary's claim for SSI benefits was denied because of an unsatisfied
felony warrant. Mary's denial notice was dated 08/23/05. Mary did not appeal the denial.
On 10/04/06, Mary requested mandatory good cause for not satisfying the felony warrant.
Mary's request for good cause was denied on 05/25/06. The Fowlkes AR does not apply to Mary's claim, because no determination or decision was made
on the issue of fugitive status on or after 12/06/05. A determination on the good
cause exception is separate from the determination that a person is or was a fugitive
felon.
Although the Fowlkes AR does not apply to the previous determination, if Mary files a new application
while residing in the Second Circuit, the AR would apply to the new determination
on fugitive felon status.
3. Example - The Recipient Moves Out of the Second Circuit—Partially Favorable Relief
Tom was living in New York when Tom's SSI benefits were suspended effective August
2005, because of an unsatisfied felony warrant. Tom's suspension notice was dated
01/20/06. Tom did not appeal the suspension. On 04/18/06, Tom requested readjudication
of Tom's claim under the Fowlkes AR. Tom met the requirements for Fowlkes relief upon readjudication because Tom was living in a Second Circuit State when
Tom's SSI benefits were suspended, the suspension notice was dated after 12/06/05
(the date of court decision), and Tom was still living in the Second Circuit when
Tom's case was readjudicated. Tom's benefits were reinstated retroactive to August
2005, the first month of N25.
On 06/24/06, Tom moved to Florida. The Fowlkes AR no longer applies to Tom because Tom no longer resides in the Second Circuit.
Tom still has an unsatisfied felony warrant, and Tom cannot establish good cause for
not satisfying the warrant. Tom's benefits are suspended effective June 2006, the
month in which Tom moved out of the Second Circuit.
