SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.008 - Filing Date Block
Effective Dates: 09/18/2026 - Present | TN 83 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604008 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure-When/How to Complete
Enter the filing date.
•
Receipt:
Mark this block if the receipt date is the filing date.
•
Protective Filing: Mark this block if there is a protective filing date.
NOTE: If a closeout notice was previously issued for the protective filing date and the
application form is received after the protective filing closeout period, the protective
filing date cannot be used as the filing date. However, if an open protective filing
exists, use that date. Otherwise, use the receipt date as the filing date. For establishing
protective filings refer to SI 00601.020. For protective writings and oral inquiries refer to SI 00601.025. For close out procedures refer to SI 00601.037B.
