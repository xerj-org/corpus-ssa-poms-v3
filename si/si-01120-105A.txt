SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.105 - Certain Stock in Alaska Regional or Village Corporations
Effective Dates: 01/21/1993 - Present | TN 29 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120105 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy principles
1. Stock Not Transferable
The term “resources” does not include shares of stock held by a native Alaskan in a regional or village
corporation during the 20-year period in which the stock is inalienable (nontransferable)
under sections 7(h) and 8(c) of the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act (ANCSA).
2. Stock Becomes Transferable
Effective January 1, 1992, this stock becomes transferable and will then meet the
definition of a resource. However, a statutory exclusion applies (SI 00830.830).
