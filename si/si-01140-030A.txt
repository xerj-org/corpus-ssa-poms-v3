A. Operating policy — liquid resources
1. Assumption
For presumably liquid resources (SI 01110.305), assume that the person whose name is shown as owner owns the entire resource. If
more than one owner is shown, assume that each has an equal ownership interest.
2. Exceptions: Checking/Savings Accounts and Time Deposits
See SI 01140.200 and SI 01140.205 for checking and savings accounts. See SI 01140.210 for time deposits.
