A. When to use SSA-L8009-U3
Use the SSA-L8009-U3 in initial claims situations to request information or evidence.
For reference purposes, SI 02306.020 contains the notices used in post-eligibility situations. For additional failure
to cooperate information, see DI 11018.000.
