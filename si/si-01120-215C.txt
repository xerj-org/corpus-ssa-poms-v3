C. Example of unprobated estate
1. Situation
An SSI recipient lives in a house owned by their parent. The recipient buys their
own food but pays no rent and is charged with rent-free shelter at the presumed maximum
value (PMV). In March, their parent dies and the house is part of the parent's estate
pending probate. The will is uncontested and the recipient is the sole heir. Regional
instructions confirm that, under state law, the property conveys to the recipient
at the time of the parent's death and that there is no legal prohibition against immediate
sale.
2. Analysis
February is the last month the recipient received rent-free shelter valued at the
PMV. The house in which the recipient lives is inheritance income in March and is
valued at the PMV. If the recipient retains ownership of the property, it is subject
to resource-counting rules beginning in April. Assuming the recipient continues to
live in the house, it is an excluded resource under SI 01130.100.
