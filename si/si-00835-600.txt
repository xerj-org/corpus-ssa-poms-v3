SI 00835.600 SSA-8006-F4 — Statement of Living Arrangements, In-Kind Support and Maintenance
NOTE:
For living arrangement (LA) periods effective 09/30/2024 or later, use form SSA-8007 to document a claimant or recipient’s statement about their LA and in-kind support
and maintenance (ISM).
NOTE: Prior to 09/30/2024, continue to use form SSA-8006 to document a claimant or recipient’s statement about their LA and ISM.
