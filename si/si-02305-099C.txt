C. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 15
Question 15 on the SSA-8203-BK - Since the date on page 1, have you (or your spouse living with you) received or do you expect to receive in
the next 14 months, any of the income listed below:
a.
Private pensions, annuities (other than Social Security, SSI, or SNAP)?
b.
Unemployment or worker's compensation?
c.
TANF or State or local assistance based on need?
d.
Veterans Administration benefits (based on need, not based on need, education)?
e.
Rental/lease income?
f.
Alimony or child support?
g.
Dividends or royalties?
h.
Interest earned on money in bank accounts (including interest on checking accounts)?
i.
Money from a trust fund?
j.
Money from any other person or organization?
NOTE:
Information about cash income to a household, outside In-kind Support and Maintenance
(ISM), or cash income from within the household is recorded in Question 9.g., and
Question 10 of the form. Income already recorded in Question 9.g and Question 10 should
not be recorded in item j. of Question 15.
