SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.250 - Rebates and Refunds
Effective Dates: 08/30/2024 - Present | TN 12 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Example
Rose Woods, an elderly Supplemental Security Income recipient, pays property taxes
on the home they live in. Because of Rose's low income, the city government returns
part of Rose's property taxes in the form of a check. This return of money already
paid by Rose is not income.
