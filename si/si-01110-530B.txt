SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01110.530 - Whose Resources to Consider
Effective Dates: 09/13/2024 - Present | TN 52 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501110530 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Spouse of Adult Individual
The resources of an individual include those of a spouse, and the applicable resource
limit is that for a couple, provided that the spouse:
•
if eligible, lives in the same household as the individual as of the first moment of the month
for which resources are being determined (prior to October 1, 1990, if eligible,  had not been separated from the individual for more than 6 full calendar months).
•
if ineligible, lives in the same household as the individual as of the first moment of the month
for which resources are being determined.
SI 00501.154 contains policy that applies when the members of a couple separate in the month for
which resources are being determined, as well as additional considerations pertaining
to couple status.
2. Parent(s) of Child Under Age 18
The resources of an individual who is a child under the age of 18 include those of
their ineligible parent(s) or spouse of parent(s).
SI 01330.200 ff. contains policy and procedures concerning parent-to-child deeming. The applicable
resources limit for a child is always that for an individual.
3. Essential person of qualified individual
The resources of a qualified individual include those of their essential person(s).
The applicable resources limit depends solely on whether or not the qualified individual
has a spouse. See SI 00501.100 for qualified individuals and essential persons and SI 01330.420 on deeming resources from an essential person.
4. Sponsor of an alien
For 3 years following an alien's admission into the U.S. for permanent residence,
the alien's resources may include those of their sponsor and of the sponsor's spouse.
The applicable resources limit depends solely on whether or not the alien has a spouse.
See SI 01330.500 through SI 01330.550 on deeming resources from a sponsor.
