E. Procedure — Foreign Work or Service Benefits
If an individual alleges working in or serving in the military of a foreign country,
apply the guidelines in GN 01701.005 to obtain the necessary information to determine if the individual might qualify
for benefits payable by the foreign country or by SSA as a result of a totalization
agreement.
If there is no totalization agreement, refer to the Social
Security in Other Countries website, and RS 02610.015 for additional guidelines.
