SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.410 - Burial Funds Exclusion - August 1, 1990 and Continuing
Effective Dates: 11/14/2013 - Present | TN 138 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure Posteligibility
1. Verify Funds Already Excluded
a. General verification of funds already excluded
If the SSR shows excluded burial funds, verify the current amount unless the tolerance
in SI 01140.010B.2. applies. Remember that increases due to appreciation or accumulated interest are
excludable even if they cause the total burial funds exclusion to exceed $1,500. If
the funds have decreased, see SI 01130.410G. in this section.
Also, inquire whether designated burial funds continue to meet the definition of burial
funds in SI 01130.410B.1. in this section and are being maintained separately from non-burial-related assets,
per SI 01130.410C.3. in this section.
•
If funds do meet the above requirements, no documentation is necessary. However, inform or remind
the individual of the requirements for maintaining the burial funds exclusion.
•
If funds do not meet the above requirements, document the changes that are required and see SI 01130.410F.1.b. or SI 01130.410F.1.c. in this section, as applicable.
b. First Field Office-Initiated Redetermination On or After 8/1/90
If this is the first field office-initiated redetermination on or after 8/1/90, and
the funds do not meet the definition of a burial fund or are commingled with non-burial-related
assets, take the following steps:
•
Tell the individual that they must convert the funds to meet the current definition
or separate them from all other non-burial-related assets before the first of the
month after the next month. Advise the individual of the current amount of the burial
fund (as determined above) that should be separated.
•
If the individual protests the need to convert or separate, tell the individual that
they can present evidence of why the funds cannot be converted or separated. Document
the file if the individual provides evidence that meets the exception in SI 01130.410C.4.b in this section.
•
If no impediment to conversion or separation applies, diary the case to follow-up
with the recipient in the month that is two months after the initiation of the redetermination.
If the necessary conversion or separation has not occurred by the required time, terminate
the burial funds exclusion effective with the first of the month that is two months
following the month of the initiation of the redetermination and inform the individual
what steps they can take to reestablish the exclusion. Offer conditional benefits
if appropriate. For more information about conditional benefits, see SI 01150.200.
The diary date above applies to personal contact redeterminations. For the diary date
for other types of redeterminations, see SI 01130.410C.4. in this section.
c. Subsequent Redeterminations
If this is not the first field office initiated redetermination on or after 8/1/90,
take the following steps:
•
Terminate the burial funds exclusion effective with the first of the month following
the month in which the burial funds fail to meet either the conversion or the separation
requirement.
•
Tell the individual why the exclusion no longer applies and what steps they can take
to reestablish the exclusion. Offer conditional benefits if appropriate. For more
information about conditional benefits, see SI 01150.200.
2. Recipient Wishes To Designate Funds
If an individual wishes to designate funds for burial, proceed as you would for an
initial claim. This applies whether no funds are currently excluded or less than $1,500
(excluding appreciation or accumulated interest) is currently excluded.
3. Apply Burial Funds-Related Income/Resources Exclusions
See SI 01130.410J. in this section.
