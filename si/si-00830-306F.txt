SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.306 - Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Educational Benefits
Effective Dates: 08/05/2009 - Present | TN 263 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830306 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Acceptable evidence
Accept the following, in the order listed, as documentation of type, amount, and frequency
of payments:
•
A VA award letter or comparable document in the veteran or dependent’s possession;
or
•
An SSA-L1103-U3, Supplemental Security Income Request for Information, from the Veterans
Affairs Regional Office (VARO).
For more information about the SSA-L1103-U3, see SI 00830.320.
