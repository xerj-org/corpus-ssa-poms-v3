B. Policy — Time Limit for Resumption of Use
1. 12-Month Rule
Resumption of use must be expected within 12 months of last use. For example, if property
was last used in October, resumption of use must reasonably be expected to occur before
the end of the following October.
2. 12-Month Extension
The 12-month period can be extended for an additional 12 months if nonuse is due to
a disabling condition (see D. below).
