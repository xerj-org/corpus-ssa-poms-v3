SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.681 - Payments to Veterans’ Children with Certain Birth Defects
Effective Dates: 09/22/2004 - Present | TN 51 (09-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130681 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Individuals who apply for SSI benefits or receive SSI benefits may have documents
from VA. Use documents such as letters or notices from VA to verify the type of payment.
1.
If documents from VA show the type of the payment, use the documents as proof of the
type of payment.
2.
If documents from VA do not show the type of the payment or if no documents are available
to verify the type of payment, contact VA. Follow instructions in SI 00830.320. Ask VA if they consider the payment to be a benefit for children of Vietnam or Korea
service veterans with spina bifida or a benefit for children of women Vietnam veterans
with certain birth defects. If you use form SSA-L1103-U3, use the “Remarks” section
of the form to request the specific type of payment.
NOTE: While individuals receiving these payments are children of veterans, many would not
meet the definition of “child” for SSI. They may be SSI applicants or recipients and
may have spouses or children who are SSI applicants or recipients.
