C. Process for verification of presidential declaration
A declaration by the President of a major disaster will be public information, i.e.,
newspaper, television, radio, and printing in the Federal Register .   The Office
of the Assistant Regional Commissioner, Programs for the area is responsible for confirming
a presidential declaration of a major disaster and the geographic area involved and
communicating this information to the field offices involved.  (This may be done by
means of a regional precedent.)
