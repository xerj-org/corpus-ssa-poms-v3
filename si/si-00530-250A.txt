SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.250 - Efforts to Locate Unsuccessful
Effective Dates: 08/05/2022 - Present | TN 15 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use
Use these instructions together with the instructions covering systems input in SI 00530.230 when the OIG OI-5D indicates that the SSI recipient was positively identified as
the wanted individual but the law enforcement agency reported that the individual
was not located. OIG will provide applicable warrant information.
