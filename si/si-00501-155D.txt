SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.155 - Couple Computation Rules for Applications Filed On or After August 22, 1996
Effective Dates: 08/05/2004 - Present | TN 71 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501155 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Examples
EXAMPLE 1: Month of Application
Lisa Askins is receiving SSI benefits. John Askins files for SSI on 08/28/96; they
will attain age 65 on 09/03/96. Couple computation rules do not apply for September
because John was not eligible for benefits on September 1, the first day of the month
following the date the application was filed. John and Lisa are an eligible couple
beginning 10/01/96, the first day of the month following the date John meets all factors
of eligibility. (For the months of August and September, Lisa is an eligible individual
with an ineligible spouse and deeming would apply.)
EXAMPLE 2: Month of Application
Robert Bishop has been receiving SSI since 07/92. Their spouse Wilma, who had not
been receiving SSI, files for SSI on 08/29/96 because Wilma attained age 65 on 08/27/96.
Couple computation rules apply beginning 09/01/96, because the requirements in SI 00501.154A.1. are met on first day of the month following the date Wilma's application was filed.
In the same example, Robert and Wilma draw SSI as a couple from 09/96 - 12/96, but
become resource ineligible effective 01/97. On 03/10/97 the couple files a request
for reinstatement of benefits because their resources dropped below the limit on 2/28/97.
They will be eligible as a couple with couple computation rules applying for March
since they meet couple criteria on the date their request for reinstatement is filed
and they meet the resources criteria as of the first moment of the month.
EXAMPLE 3: Couple Filing in Same Month
Donna Reynolds is over age 65 and files for SSI on 8/24/96. Donna informs the CR that
Alex Reynolds is ill, but will come in on 8/29/96 to file for SSI. Alex applies on
8/30/96, and will attain age 65 on 9/2/96. Couple computation rules do not apply for
September because Alex was not eligible on September 1. Donna, however, will be paid
as of 9/1/96, (the first day of the month following the date Donna filed) as an individual
with an ineligible spouse. They will be paid as a couple on 10/01/96.
EXAMPLE 4: Separate Application and Request for Reinstatement in A Month
Gloria Altman has been receiving SSI since 1/95. Their benefits were suspended from
1/96-8/96 because of excess income. Gloria's spouse John retired in 7/96 and filed
for SSI on 8/25/96 (John became age 65 in May). On 8/30/96, Gloria requested reinstatement
of their own benefits, since Gloria's income dropped beginning in August. John is
eligible for benefits 09/01/96, the first day of the month following the date their
own application is filed. Since Gloria no longer has excess income and requested reinstatement
on 8/30/96, Gloria is eligible for August as an individual with an ineligible spouse,
as John is not eligible for payment until September. Beginning September 1, they can
be paid as an eligible couple.
