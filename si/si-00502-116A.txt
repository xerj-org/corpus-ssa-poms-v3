SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.116 - “Deemed Qualified Alien” Status Based On Battery Or Extreme Cruelty By A Family Member
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502116 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
P.L. 104-193, as amended, provides an exception to the rules which restrict eligibility for public
benefits for certain aliens who were battered or subjected to extreme cruelty (also
referred to as "abused") by a spouse or by a member of the household where the alien
was residing when the abuse took place. Under this provision, “deemed qualified alien” status can be established for certain battered or abused aliens provided they meet
additional conditions. See SI 00502.200 for changes in sponsor-to-noncitizen deeming rules that apply to alien claimants
who have been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty.
