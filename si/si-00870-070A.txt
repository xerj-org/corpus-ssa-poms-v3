A. Suspension
The PASS expert will suspend a PASS when the PASS participant fails to meet the criteria
to extend the PASS in SI 00870.055. The PASS Specialist should also keep the Control and Information System (CIS) updated so it can be used to obtain current status on individual PASS participants.
