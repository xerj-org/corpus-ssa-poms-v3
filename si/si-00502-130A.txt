SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.130 - Documentary Evidence of Qualified Alien Status
Effective Dates: 09/15/2026 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
Use the following documentary evidence procedures to establish qualified alien status.
The documentation and current immigration status must be verified via the Department
of Homeland Security's (DHS) online Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements
(SAVE) program in all cases, including initial claims prior to approval, redeterminations, and immigration status
changes.
In addition to the general documentation policies found in GN 00301.286 and GN 00301.288, technicians must electronically store in the electronic folder (e.g., Evidence Portal,
eView) legible copies of supporting original immigration document(s) that provide
evidence of immigration status. Technicians must scan legible copies of both sides
of the immigration document. If the immigration documentation is a passport, then
store an image of the passport cover, the photo or data page, and a Visa page (if
applicable). If the document includes an Alien Registration Number (ARN or A#), enter
the ARN in Person Information. Return the immigration document to the claimant. After
receiving the SAVE response, update the status code on the record as appropriate and
store a copy of the SAVE response in the electronic folder. Once the immigration status
has been verified via SAVE, continue developing other eligibility requirements, as
appropriate.
If the individual indicates they do not have a current immigration document, provide
the SAVE fact sheet, "SAVE Records: Fast Facts for Benefit Applicants," which provides information on contacting DHS to update, correct, or replace immigration
documents.
