SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.043 - Temporary Absence from a Federal LA Due to Confinement in a Medicaid Facility
Effective Dates: 10/31/2023 - Present | TN 113 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835043 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to Use
Use the instructions in this section to determine temporary absence from a Federal
LA unless the individual qualifies for continuation of benefits because of temporary
institutionalization (SI 00520.140) for any portion of the same confinement.
