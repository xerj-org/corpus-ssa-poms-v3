SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00510.020 - Use of the SSA-L8050-U3 to Refer Individuals for Program Benefits Other than Title II
Effective Dates: 05/20/2015 - Present | TN 17 (01-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500510020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — Routing the SSA-L8050-U3 (For Non-Title II Benefits)
1.
Remove the carbons (if not MSSICS generated) and complete Part 2 of the last page,
as explained in SI 00510.020 B.4.b.in this section. Be sure the claimant/recipient signs and dates Part 1.
2.
Route the completed pages as follows:
•
Give or send to the SSI claimant/recipient pages 1, 3, and 4 of the SSA-L8050-U3.
NOTE: The claimant/recipient may elect to take or send page 3 to the source of the potential
other benefits. It is their responsibility to apply for the other benefit within the
specified time limit (30 or 35 days as appropriate), and provide the FO with documentation
of filing, such as a SSA-L8050–U3 signed and completed by the benefit source or a
receipt for an online benefit application.
•
Consider attaching form SSA-L1103 (SSI Request for Information) to the SSA-L8050-U3
when referring an individual to the VA and unusual medical expenses (see SI 00830.312), augmentation (see SI 00830.314), or aid and attendance or a housebound allowance (see SI 00830.308) is involved. When following this procedure, annotate the SSA-L1103 “to be used if
application approved.” See SI 00830.320 for instructions on the completion of the SSA-L1103).
•
Give or mail the SSA-L8050-U3 to the individual and, as appropriate a return envelope
along with the “Claim Information Notice” to deliver to the organization or agency
directly if they wish.
•
Retain the “FO Copy” for control purposes.
•
Follow procedures in SI 00510.025C and D to monitor compliance and document determination.
