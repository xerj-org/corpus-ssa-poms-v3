C. Policy — Deeming
In determining how much of an ineligible spouse's or parent's income is subject to
deeming, earnings which are used to meet work expenses are not counted, if the ineligible
spouse or parent is blind or disabled.
See SI 01320.170 and SI 01320.190 to determine whether the spouse or parent is blind or disabled. Work expenses should
be documented and verified according to SI 00820.550.
