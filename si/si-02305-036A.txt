A. Policy - Blind Recipients
All blind SSI claimants and recipients may choose how they receive notices of any
determination or other action taken or proposed that can affect their SSI benefits
(see NL 01001.010). They may choose to receive:
•
A supplementary telephone call within 5 working days after the notice is mailed; or
•
certified mail; or
•
regular mail.
