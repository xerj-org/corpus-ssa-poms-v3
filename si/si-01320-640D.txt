D. Other denials
If denials other than disability (SI 01320.640B. in this section) are possible (e.g., excess resources, etc.) in multiple deeming
cases, those reasons for denial should take priority over denials for income. For
example, using the same family situation as Example l in SI 01320.640B. in this section, if Crystal could be denied on the basis of both excess income and
resources, the denial would be based on the excess resources and the parent-to-child
deeming procedures in SI 01320.500 would be used to determine Steven's eligibility and payment amount.
