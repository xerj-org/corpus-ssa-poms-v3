A. Overview of self-employment plans
Self-employment is a desirable PASS goal for many individuals for a variety of reasons.
An individual can schedule their own hours and work around the limitations of their
impairments. If an individual is considering a self-employment goal, the PASS Specialist
must discuss with the individual why self-employment is preferred over working for
an employer in the chosen field. A self-employment goal will most likely require additional
time, dedication, and effort on the part of the individual. A self-employment goal
has limitations, both financial and circumstantial, that will not work for everyone.
Ongoing family and community support will be key in determining the success of the
business. An individual with a self-employment goal should seek out the expertise
of a third party that is familiar with the chosen field. In many instances, Vocational
Rehabilitation Services may assist individuals in establishing a business and paying
for some start-up expenses. An individual may want to seek out the assistance of a
Work Incentive Planning and Assistance (WIPA) agency or a benefits planner.
