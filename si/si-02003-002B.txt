B. Policy on what are basic needs for IA purposes
IA payments are for basic needs. We define basic needs as essential items for everyday
living that cannot wait until DDS makes an SSI eligibility determination. Basic needs
include:
•
food;
•
clothing;
•
shelter;
•
personal hygiene items;
•
grooming items;
•
transportation to obtain basic needs; and
•
emergency medical needs that are not reimbursable under another Federal program.
We can consider other items not on this list as basic needs if they meet the definition
of basic needs and the assistance provided by the State is cash or a vendor payment
and financed wholly from State or local funds.
NOTE: Funeral expenses are not a basic need for IAR purposes.
