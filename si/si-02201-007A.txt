SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02201.007 - Supplemental Security Income (SSI): Overpayment Fraud
Effective Dates: 03/29/2010 - Present | TN 27 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502201007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. If you suspect the overpayment is the result of fraud
1. If you suspect that an overpayment was the result of fraud, stop or suspend all recovery
efforts, including sending the initial overpayment notice, until:
•
You no longer suspect fraud, or
•
Your investigation does not result in a referral to the Office of the Inspector General
(OIG).
2. If you refer the case to OIG for review and:
a. OIG returns the case within the 60-day review period without prosecuting
The field office (FO) will determine if fraud or similar fault exists as described
in SI 04070.020. If fraud or similar fault exists, complete SSA-553 Special Determination
and fax it into NDRed using the Evidence Portal (EP) at View Evidence-Evidence Portal
(ssa.gov); Issue an overpayment notice to the recipient or representative payee. The
notice will include an explanation that fraud or similar fault exists and describe
the evidence we used to make the determination. The notice will also include appeal
rights.
b. OIG provides no response to the case referral within 60-days
The FO will prepare and issue the notice of overpayment on the 61st day. Include an
explanation that we have determined fraud or similar fault and describe the evidence
we used to make our determination.
