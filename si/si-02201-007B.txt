SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02201.007 - Supplemental Security Income (SSI): Overpayment Fraud
Effective Dates: 03/29/2010 - Present | TN 27 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502201007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Possibility of fraud discovered after the expiration of the period for reopening
1. If the possibility of fraud as a cause of the overpayment is first discovered after
the expiration of the period for reopening:
•
Limit the overpayment period and recovery efforts to the period that has been reopened
under the rules of administrative finality. For information on administrative finality,
refer to SI 04070.000.
•
Do not send the initial notice of overpayment until one of the conditions are met
in SI
02201.007A.
•
When one or more of the conditions are met in SI
02201.007A, send an initial overpayment notice showing the full period and amount of the overpayment.
•
Resume recovery efforts.
2. If OIG accepts your referral
If the OIG accepts your referral, do not begin or resume any recovery action until
the OIG completes its processing.
3. If OIG has declined prosecution
If the OIG has declined prosecution, a revised determination of overpayment is required.
Send a revised initial overpayment notice showing the full period of the overpayment
and the full amount of the overpayment.
