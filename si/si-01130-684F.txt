F. How to document excluded FVECF funds
Document the amount of the FVECF payment, date it was received and the determination
that the payment is from the FVECF and is excluded from resources by following the
procedures in GN 00301.000 General Evidentiary Standards sections GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289.
