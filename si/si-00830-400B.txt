B. Definition
For SSI purposes, Aid to Families with Dependent Children (AFDC) refers to the payments
made under the former Part A of Title IV of the Social Security Act except for payments
made under the former section 406(e) of Part A. (Payments under section 406(e) are
emergency assistance payments and the applicable instructions appear at SI 00830.405.)
