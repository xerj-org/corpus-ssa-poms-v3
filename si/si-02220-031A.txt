Effective Dates: 09/01/2026 - Present | TN 141 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220031 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
An overpaid individual may offer to pay an amount that is less than the total overpayment
to settle the overpayment. This is a compromise offer. This section provides instructions
for field office (FO) processing and referral of compromise offers to the Assistant
Regional Commissioner for Management and Operations Support (ARC MOS).
