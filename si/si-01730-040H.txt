H. List of automatic assignment states
As of the date of this transmittal, the automatic assignment section 1634 States are:
Arkansas
North Carolina
Louisiana
Tennessee
Michigan
Texas
Ohio
Wisconsin
Automatic assignment procedures result from a State notifying the Centers for Medicare
& Medicaid Services (CMS) that it wishes the signed AOR statement to be waived because of State law. CMS then
notifies SSA when a section 1634 State no longer requires a signed AOR statement.
