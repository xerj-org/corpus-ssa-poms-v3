H. Maine Indians Funds
Exclusion of revenues from the Maine Indians Claims Settlement Fund and Land Acquisition
Fund paid under section 5 of Public Law 96-420 (see SI 00830.830D.11.). This applies to the income of sponsors of aliens only if the alien lives in the sponsor's household. Prior to April 1, 1987, this exclusion
applied regardless of the alien's residence.
