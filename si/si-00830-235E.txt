We looked at your Supplemental Security Income (SSI) case again to see if we owe you
money because of the Tennant court case. This case is about how we figure SSI eligibility and payment amount for
some people who receive or did receive workers' compensation (WC) payments that include
money paid to you for past, current, or future expenses related to the WC claim.
Our review showed that we had counted as unearned income the part of your WC payments
that was paid to you for expenses related to your claim. Therefore, we refigured your
SSI eligibility and payment amount without counting this income and found you are
due   (1)   .
Because you are due   (2)   , we are sending you another letter soon telling you:
•
how much SSI you will get and for what months;
•
when to expect your SSI money;
•
the new amount of the WC benefit which we used to figure your SSI; and
•
what to do if you disagree with our decision about the amount we owe you.
