F. Procedure - documenting allegations
Document information as required by POMS instructions for the issues listed in E,
above in this section.
•
SSI Claims System - Document material information on the Household Composition page.
For instructions on completing the page, see MS 08110.011.
•
SSA-8203-BK - Complete the fill-in spaces for this question on the form. Record any
additional relevant information in the REMARKS section of the form.
•
SSA-8006-F4/SSA-8007 - If you are completing an RZ on paper and you need more information
about the living arrangement to make a living arrangement or In-Kind Support and Maintenance
(ISM) decision, complete an SSA-8006-F4 or SSA-8007 per SI 00835.600.
