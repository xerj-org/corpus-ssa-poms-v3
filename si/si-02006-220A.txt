SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02006.220 - Examples of Processing Representative Fee Adjustments of Title II Income
Effective Dates: 03/07/2005 - Present | TN 14 (03-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502006220 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. PROCEDURE - SUMMARY
•
Determine the title XVI portion of the fee per GN 03940.050.
•
Add the title II portion and title XVI portion of the fee to determine the total fee.
•
Use the SSA-2894, “Offset Case Authorized
Fee Worksheet.” Follow steps in SI 02006.210.
