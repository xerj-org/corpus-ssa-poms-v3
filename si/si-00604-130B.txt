SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.130 - Filing For Medicaid Based on SSI
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 75 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604130 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. SSA Makes Medicaid Determination
The SSI application is also an application for Medicaid when SSA has an agreement
to make Medicaid eligibility determinations on behalf of a State. SSA's application
date policy applies to this Medicaid application.
2. SSA Does Not Make Medicaid Determination
When SSA does not have an agreement to make determinations for the State, an individual
must file an application with the State/local Medicaid agency.
