F. Example — move from own household to a title xix facility, OSS involved
1. Facts
Sally is an SSI recipient who lives in their own household. The FBR is $512 and the
OSS level is $32. On 9/15/2000, Sally begins residence (a permanent living arrangement
change) in a title XIX facility. The Federal payment limit is $30, and the OSS level
is $10. In every month, their income is a $58 private pension.
2. Federal Eligibility Test
The CI of $38 ($58-$20 general exclusion) does not exceed the FBR, so they are federally
eligible every month.
