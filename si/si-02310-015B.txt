SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02310.015 - Savings Security Instrument Match (SB Diary)
Effective Dates: 09/18/2026 - Present | TN 76 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502310015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The individual(s) whose name(s) appears on the instrument is/are the owner(s) regardless
of the Social Security Number (SSN) shown on the instrument. (See SI 01140.240A.)
Do not release copies of the savings security alert to individuals unless ownership
has been verified by BFS or the SSI recipient.
