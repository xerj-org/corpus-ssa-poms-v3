SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02220.011 - Reconciliation of Overpayment Debt on Terminated Records
Effective Dates: 06/03/2021 - Present | TN 69 (06-21)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The field office (FO) is responsible for recognizing the need to transfer an overpayment
from a terminated record to the latest record. The correct amount of the overpayment
to be transferred must be determined. Then the terminated record must be balanced
and closed out. Finally, the overpayment information must be posted to the latest
record. Beginning 07/24/99, this process is performed automatically in most cases
through the automated reconciliation process. However, the FO must take actions in
cases that require manual processing.
