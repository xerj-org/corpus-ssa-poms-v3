SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.045 - The Threshold Test for Section 1619(b) Eligibility
Effective Dates: 03/29/2006 - Present | TN 34 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Follow this procedure each time a threshold determination is made:
Step
Action
1
Calculate the individual's annual gross earned income per SI 00820.150 and place the calculation in the folder.
2
If the individual's annual gross earned income is less than the appropriate threshold
in SI 02302.200, transmit eligibility under the sufficiency of earnings test per SM 01305.975.
3
If the individual is not eligible using the threshold chart and has any of the expenses
listed in SI 02302.050 C.1, follow the instructions in SI 02302.050 to determine if the individual would be eligible under an individualized threshold
calculation.
