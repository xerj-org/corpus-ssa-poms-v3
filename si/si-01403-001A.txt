A. Introduction to pass-along provisions
Congress approved pass-along legislation because some States reduced their supplementary
payment levels when Congress enacted cost-of-living adjustments (COLAs) in the Federal Benefit Rate (FBR). As a result, some Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients often did not receive the full amount of the COLA increase.
