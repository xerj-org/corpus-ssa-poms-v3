SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.086 - Developing Institutionalization - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 08/03/2026 - Present | TN 101 (08-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305086 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - General
Develop allegations about institutionalization as follows:
1.
If the recipient alleges no institutionalization and other information in SSA records agrees, do not develop institutionalization
further.
2.
If the recipient alleges institutionalization and other information in SSA records indicates that the institution is the individual's
permanent residence, do not develop institutionalization further.
3.
If the recipient alleges institutionalization and no FLA-D or PSY-NO2 is on the SSR and other information in SSA records indicates that this was a temporary
institutionalization (TI) case, do not develop unless the period of confinement was
4 or more full months. In such cases, develop per POMS in SI 00520.140.
4.
If the recipient alleges institutionalization and no FLA-D or PSY-N02 is on the SSR and no other information in SSA records indicates that this was a TI
case (regardless of allegation about receiving a TI letter), contact FO parallel to
the institution to determine if this was a 9115 case and develop, as necessary, per
POMS in SI 00520.140.
5.
If the recipient alleges institutionalization and FLA-D or PSY-N02 is on the SSR, document the name and address of the institution and the dates of confinement
in spaces provided. If the recipient lived in a public institution for more than a
month, consider proration for the month of release (see SI 02005.007F).
6.
If the recipient alleges institutionalization or no institutionalization and other information in SSA records conflicts, develop, as necessary, per SI 00520.140.
