SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.474 - Conversions — Converting to Monthly Amounts
Effective Dates: 12/13/2023 - Present | TN 117 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835474 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
For purposes of ISM determinations, household operating expenses and the CMV of household
operating costs must be expressed as monthly amounts. Bills for food and shelter items
which are not paid or billed monthly (e.g., weekly, quarterly, annually, etc.) must
be converted to monthly amounts.
