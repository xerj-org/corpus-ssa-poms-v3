A. Introduction
Section 204 of the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act
of 1996, Public Law (P.L.) 104-193 provides that an SSI application is effective on
the later of the first day of the month following the date the application is filed
or the first day of the month following the date the individual becomes eligible for
such benefits with respect to such application. It also modifies the definition of
“eligible spouse” to comply with that rule.
