SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02220.018 - Debtor Reactions to Collection of Title XVI Overpayments by Administrative Wage Garnishment (AWG)
Effective Dates: 01/04/2023 - Present | TN 113 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
While several types of debtor reactions to AWG are the same as those regarding TRO
and TOP, the debtor may make requests and allegations that are unique to the AWG program.
For example, the debtor may request hardship or may allege having been fired by the
employer. Further, the requests and allegations unique to AWG can be made at any time
during the AWG process, from the 60-day due process notice through the final garnishment
payments.
Therefore, this section is organized according to the following four logical groups
of debtor requests and allegations:
•
Due Process Requests (SI 02220.018B.)
•
Debtor Offers to Repay (SI 02220.018C.)
•
Debtor Allegations about Employment (SI 02220.018D.)
•
Other Reports that could Affect AWG (SI 02220.018E.)
