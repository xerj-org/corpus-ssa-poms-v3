SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00603.015 - Processing Nondisability Aspects of a Disability Appeal
Effective Dates: 05/18/1998 - Present | TN 11 (05-98)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500603015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - hearing
When a claimant files a hearing request on the issue of disability, follow the instructions
below regarding the nondisability factors.
1. Consider simultaneous development
If the claim is deferred development, consider whether the claimant's medical condition
has changed so that the claim now meets criteria for simultaneous development per
SI 00603.002C. If so, see SI 00603.004C. for documentation and systems input instructions.
2. Check address
Check that our record has the claimant's current mailing and residence addresses and
telephone number. Change the record if any of these has changed.
3. Changes reported - eligibility not effected
Follow the procedures below if the claimant reports any changes that do not affect
eligibility for all months.
a. Deferred Development Claim
Do not develop the alleged changes but record them on the system if possible.
b. Simultaneous Development Claim
Develop alleged changes and record them on the system.
4. Eligibility questionable
If eligibility is questionable for all months in the life of the application, pursue
development to resolve the issues. However, do not delay sending the file to the hearing
office (HO).
a. Claimant Is Eligible
If you determine that the claimant is eligible for any month in the life of the application,
make any appropriate systems changes.
b. Claimant Is Ineligible
If you determine the claimant is ineligible for all months, explain the denial issue
on an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) and send the SSA-5002 and any evidence to the HO
with an SSA-408. Write the claimant's name and social security number on the SSA-408
and annotate it with the following:
“Associate with pending hearing request on disability issue. Claimant does not meet
nondisability eligibility requirement(s). ALJ may wish to consider both issues.”
5. Simultaneous development incomplete
If the claim was simultaneous development and development was incomplete, continue
to develop the other nondisability issues.
