C. Amount and frequency of mortgage payment
Enter the amount of the mortgage and the frequency of the mortgage payment (monthly,
bi-monthly, or other appropriate period) in 28(c).
1. Home paid for--no mortgage
Enter "paid for" in "Amount" block.
2. No mortgage payment
Enter "see remarks" in "Amount" block and explain in Remarks why there is no mortgage
payment and the owner has not paid for the home.
