D. Policy – 209(b) states
1619(a) or (b) eligibles who reside in 209(b) States must have been eligible for Medicaid
in the month immediately prior to becoming eligible for 1619 status to retain their
Medicaid eligibility. The 209(b) States are: Connecticut; Hawaii; Illinois; Minnesota;
Missouri; New Hampshire; North Dakota; and Virginia (SI 01715.010).
