D. Procedure for determining participation in a WIOA program
Accept the applicant, recipient, deemor, or representative payee's allegation of participation
in a WIOA program and the receipt of supportive services such as childcare, transportation,
medical care, meals, and other reasonable expenses, provided in cash or in-kind, unless
there is reason to question the information.
