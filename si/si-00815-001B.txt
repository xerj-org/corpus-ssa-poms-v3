B. Policy - What is not income - beginning 03/09/2005
An item received is not income if:
•
it is neither food nor shelter; or
•
it cannot be used to obtain food or shelter.
REMINDER: Even when an item is not income, it subject to evaluation as a resource when it is
retained as of the first moment of the month after the month of receipt (See SI 01110.600.).
NOTE: See SI 00835.400 for in-kind support and maintenance policy and procedures pertaining to this regulation
change.
