SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00602.005 - Abbreviated Application (ABAP) Obtained Inappropriately
Effective Dates: 05/20/2011 - Present | TN 4 (03-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500602005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. ABAP taken on paper
a. Before Adjudication
If a full application is appropriate, obtain the necessary additional information
per GN 00205.020A. and complete a supplemental SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental Security Income).
Write "SUPPLEMENTAL" at the top of the SSA-8000-BK.
b. After Adjudication
Reopen the determination under the rules of administrative finality per SI 04070.010, or process a reconsideration per SI 00602.025, if appropriate.
c. Systems Input
For the specific action(s) necessary to correct the situation see SM 01010.015.
2. ABAP taken in the Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System (MSSICS)
For instructions on changing MSSICS application type before the interviewer receipts-in
the application on the DWO1, see MSOM MSSICS 001.004. For instructions on changes
after the interviewer receipts-in the application on the DWO1, see MSOM MSSICS 022.004
O-14.
