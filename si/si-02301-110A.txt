SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.110 - Penalty Notices and Collection Actions
Effective Dates: 09/13/2001 - Present | TN 62 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
FOs notify individuals of all penalty decisions either orally or in a manually prepared
written notice. A decision to collect a penalty always requires a written notice and
systems input even when current circumstances do not permit collection.
