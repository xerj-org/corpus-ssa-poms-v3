A. Definition of domestic and non-domestic travel
1. Domestic travel
Domestic travel is travel in or between the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto
Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands.
2. Non-domestic travel
Non-domestic travel is travel to any place other than those listed in SI 00830.521A.1. in this section.
