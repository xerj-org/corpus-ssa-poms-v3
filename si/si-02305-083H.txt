H. Procedure - Spouse enters a Medicaid institution
If the SSI recipient's spouse enters an institution and the absence is not
temporary, explain that allocations of the institutionalized spouse's income to the SSI recipient
as part of the Medicaid determination must be reported. See SI 00810.120D.5. for income-counting rules which apply when an individual absent from the household
provides money for the household.
