SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.110 - Certain Cash to Purchase Medical or Social Services
Effective Dates: 08/02/2024 - Present | TN 92 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. INTRODUCTION
An individual cannot always disburse in the month of receipt cash given them to purchase
approved medical or social services. To permit use of such funds in the manner intended,
it is reasonable to assume, for a limited time, that the individual will use them
to pay for the approved services and, therefore, that they are not available for their
support and maintenance.
