Effective Dates: 10/11/2011 - Present | TN 23 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500520730 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure - general
The FO will:
•
maintain ongoing contact with all appropriate institutions in the service area;
•
use regular visits as a means to encourage cooperation;
•
establish procedures for the institutions to report timely on events which affect
eligibility and payment determinations (e.g., admissions, discharges, changes of income,
death).
