F. Life Expectancy table
SSA's Office of the Chief Actuary maintains yearly life expectancy tables on the agency's public-facing website. Use the most recent table on that website
to determine the value of compensation of services for life and ISM for life. After
you determine the yearly value of services (or ISM), multiply it by the “life expectancy”
(which means the average years of life remaining) for the year corresponding to the
individual's age and sex.
