A. Introduction
Property of any kind, including cash,cannot be a resource in a month unless,as of
the first moment of that month, it meets all three criteria in B. below. However,
it is not unusual for a nonresource to become a resource or vice versa; i.e., a key
factor can materialize or go out of existence.
