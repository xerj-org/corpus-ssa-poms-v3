D. Policy — period of eligibility
1. Eligibility stops
Eligibility for the PESH exception stops effective with the 7th month throughout which
the individual resides in a shelter in any 9-month period.
2. Ineligibility continues
Ineligibility while in a PESH continues for as long as the person has received benefits
for 6 months during the applicable rolling 9-month period.
