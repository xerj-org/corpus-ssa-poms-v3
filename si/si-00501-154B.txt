SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.154 - Determining When Couple Computation Rules Apply
Effective Dates: 08/18/2011 - Present | TN 71 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501154 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definition
A household is common living quarters and facilities under domestic arrangements that create one
economic unit. Sharing a room in an institution is not living in the same household.
The temporary absence of a couple member from a household does not affect the applicability
of couple computation rules.
