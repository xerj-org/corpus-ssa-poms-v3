D. Determining the months a State will not receive reimbursement by IAR
We will not reimburse a State for any month:
•
prior to the month the IAR period began;
•
that does not include a dollar payment amount for both SSI and IA;
•
after the release of the recipient’s first payment of retroactive SSI benefits (unless
the exception applies in SI 02003.003C.4. in this section);
•
after the release of the recipient’s first PE payment of retroactive SSI benefits
(unless the exception applies in subsection SI 02003.003C.4. in this section); or,
•
for any month not reported to us by the State.
