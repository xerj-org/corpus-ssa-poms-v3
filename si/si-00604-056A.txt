SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.056 - Rental Liability: Question 29
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 25 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604056 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When and how to complete question 29
Complete 29(a) when questions 28(a) and (b) are "NO." Complete 29(b), (c), and (d)
as appropriate.
1. Question 29(c) is answered “YES”
If the claimant answers 29(c) "YES," enter the name of the person with rental liability
and answer question 30.
2. Question 29(c) is answered “NO”
If the claimant answers 29(c) "NO," enter the name of the person who has home ownership
and answer question 31.
3. Question 29(d)
To identify the amount and frequency of the rent payment, question 29(d) requires
a response for all claimants who answer "YES" to 29(a) or (b).
4. Leads to unstated income
If it appears that the claimant does not have sufficient income to cover stated expenses,
consider the amount of the rent along with any other information about living expenses
as a lead to unstated income.
