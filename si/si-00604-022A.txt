Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 5 (03-12)

A. When to complete questions 8 through 10
Complete questions 8 and 9 for all claimants and question 10 as applicable. The information
in question 8 is required for the determination of citizenship and can be useful when
we need proof of age documentation.
Do not provide information for a spouse who is not filing.
