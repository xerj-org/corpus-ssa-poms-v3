E. Procedures for developing pandemic-related disaster assistance on or after August
21, 2026
Effective August 21, 2026, special development procedures described in SI 00830.623B-C and subsection C above no longer apply to pandemic-related disaster assistance;
technicians no longer need to specifically ask about and document the receipt of pandemic-related
assistance on a Report of Contact.
For any new allegations of pandemic-related disaster assistance obtained on or after
August 21, 2026:
•
Follow general disaster assistance instructions in SI 00830.620 to develop allegations of disaster assistance for income purposes.
•
Follow general instructions in SI 01130.620 to develop resource exclusions of retained pandemic-related disaster assistance only
when an individual alleges the receipt of excludable disaster assistance and the exclusion
would permit eligibility for SSI benefits.
For cases in which an individual has previously alleged retaining pandemic-related
disaster assistance and we have documented the continued exclusion according to subsection
C above:
•
Continue to apply instructions in subsection C above. Ask about the retention of pandemic-related
disaster assistance payments for the current review period and update allegations
about previously documented assistance until no retained assistance remains, following
instructions in this section.
