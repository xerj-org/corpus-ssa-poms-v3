SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.950 - Deeming of Income From a Sponsor of a Non-Citizen
Effective Dates: 11/14/2024 - Present | TN 59 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320950 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Sponsor and Sponsor's Spouse Separate — Operating Procedures
1.
If a sponsor and their spouse (who is not a co-sponsor) separate, do not deem the
spouse's income to the non-citizen beginning with the month after the month of separation.
2.
Discontinue the allocation for the spouse beginning with the month after the month
of separation. (If the spouse then qualifies as a dependent of the sponsor, a dependent
allocation is given for the spouse beginning with the month following the month of
separation.)
