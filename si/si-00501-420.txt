Citations:

Use the information in this section when you need to make a Permanent Residence Under
Color of Law (PRUCOL) determination under the pre-1996 immigration reform Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) eligibility rules. See information on “SSI eligibility for nonqualified aliens who were
receiving SSI on 8/22/96 - 1998 “Grandfathering” legislation
in SI 00502.153.
