SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02101.065 - Notices
Effective Dates: 08/01/2025 - Present | TN 39 (08-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502101065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Automated Notice
Automated notices pertaining to underpayments issued
by the system.
2. Manual Notice
Prepare a manual notice in the following situations:
•
Manually computed or manually issued payments, i.e., M01 and OTP's;
•
Cases which require manual dedicated accounts (i.e., optional);
•
Cases which are an exception to the installment process;
•
Cases in which the recipient has a representative who is not their representative
payee; and
•
Cases in which there is netting of excess payments against additional amounts due.
