J. Procedure - Rental Subsidy
Develop allegations related to a possible rental subsidy, as follows:
1.
If recipients allege that their residence is rented but no one in the household is
the
landlord's parent, child, or spouse and other information in SSA records agrees, do not develop rental subsidy.
2.
If recipients allege that their residence is rented and they or someone else living
with
them is the landlord's parent, child, or spouse:
•
Document the name of the household member who is related.
•
Develop rental subsidy per SI 00835.380 and SI 00835.380 and SI 00835.520.
NOTE: See SI 02305.091E.2. for RZs being conducted for residents of Texas, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, Connecticut,
New York and Vermont.
3.
If the recipients allege that their residence is rented and no one else living with
them
is the landlord's parent, child, or spouse and other information in SSA records conflicts:
•
Document the name of the household member who is related.
•
Develop rental subsidy per SI 00835.380 and SI 00835.520.
NOTE: See SI 02305.091E.2. for RZs being conducted for residents of Texas, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, Connecticut,
New York and Vermont.
