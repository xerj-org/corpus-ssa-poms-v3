A. Introduction — Transfer of resources
In order to determine eligibility for SSI, SSA must determine the value of an individual's
resources at the first moment of the month. Transferring ownership of a resource may
affect the value of an individual's countable resources which in turn can affect SSI
eligibility. As of 12/14/99, transferring ownership of a resource for less than fair
market value can result in a period of ineligibility for SSI. Therefore, when an individual
alleges that a resource has been transferred it is necessary to develop this issue
in order to determine the effect on SSI eligibility. SSA must also notify State Medicaid
agencies about resource transfers, regardless of when the transfer occurred.
