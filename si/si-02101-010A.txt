SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02101.010 - Past-Due Benefits Payable - Individual Alive Under Age 18 with Representative Payee - Dedicated Account Required
Effective Dates: 04/13/2026 - Present | TN 40 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502101010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction - dedicated accounts
Effective August 1996, after any Interim Assistance (IA) reimbursement payment and
direct payment of representative fees, we must pay any remaining past-due payment
that exceeds six times the Federal Benefit Rate (FBR) plus any federally-administered
Optional State Supplement (OSS) that we would normally pay to the representative payee
(payee) of an eligible individual under age 18, into a dedicated account at a financial
institution. For dedicated account requirements, refer to GN 00603.025. For information regarding collective savings and checking accounts, refer to GN 00603.020.
