SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01140.320 - Tobacco Transition Payment Program (TTPP) or The Tobacco Buyout
Effective Dates: 08/17/2007 - Present | TN 71 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501140320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Example
Jones, a quota holder, submits a CCC-960 indicating their notification date as 05/17/2005
and ten annual installment payments for a total of $7500 due. On 05/20/2005, Jones
went to State Bank to negotiate obtaining cash for their USDA contract. State Bank
agreed to pay Jones $6300 for their contract. On 05/25/2005 Jones completed the required
CCC form at their local USDA office to assign their 10 installment payments to State
Bank. The USDA office approved their request and State Bank paid Jones $6300 in 05/2005
in exchange for the installment payments. Jones submitted a copy of the agreement
they entered into with State Bank which verified they received a discounted cash payment
in exchange for the installment payments. The $6300 check Jones received is considered
a conversion of a resource in 05/2005 for fair market value. Since Jones retained
the money into June 2005, they are ineligible for SSI benefits effective 06/2005 based
on excess resources.
If Jones is a quota producer, the $6300 payment they received from State Bank in 05/2005
would be considered NESE for 2005.
