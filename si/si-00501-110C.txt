SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.110 - Loss of Essential Person Status - SSI
Effective Dates: 01/05/2024 - Present | TN 78 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Operating Procedure
1. Absence of Qualified Individual
a.
Contact the qualified individual and the EP or authorized representative to see whether the
qualified individual intends to return to reside with the EP. (Use telephone contact
whenever possible.)
b.
Record the information on a report of contact (RC).
c.
Control the case via diary or listing procedures (see SM 01301.200 ff.) for recontact with
the individual prior to the expiration of the time period (i.e., 6 months for the
qualified individual and 90 days for the EP).
d.
Recontact the individual before the expiration of the time period (see SI 00501.110B.). If the absence will exceed the time period, document the file and make input to
end the EP increment the following month. (See SM 01301.525F.4.)
2. Absence of EP
The same procedures explained above apply.
3. EP Becomes Eligible Spouse (ES)
a.
Any month or months the qualified individual receives an EP increment and we subsequently determine the EP is an ES is not an overpayment.
b.
No underpayment is due the newly determined ES.
c.
Any payment made to the eligible (qualified) individual for the month represents payment to both
the individual and eligible spouse.
d.
Make payments to the eligible couple in accordance with SI 02001.005.
CAUTION: An overpayment may exist in a. above if we otherwise pay the qualified individual
too much.
4. Notice Requirement
When a person is no longer an EP, notify the qualified individual or the representative
of the proposed reduction in the payment amount. Suppress the systems-generated notice
following SM 01301.675 and send an SSA-L8155-U2 (see NL 00803.030).
