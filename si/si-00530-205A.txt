A. Background
The Fugitive Felon Automation Program (FFAP) is designed to automate the suspension
action and notices for most SSI fugitive felon (FF) cases. Some cases will be excluded
from the process and will still require FO action to process the N25 suspension action,
post remarks to the SSR, and send the manual notice.
