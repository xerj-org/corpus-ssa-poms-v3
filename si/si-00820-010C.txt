SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.010 - In-Kind Items Provided as Remuneration for Employment
Effective Dates: 05/29/2024 - Present | TN 91 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — Situations Other Than Those Listed In SI 00820.010B.
If food, clothing and/or shelter is provided under circumstances other than those
listed in (SI 00820.010B), use the following chart to determine how much and what type of income is chargeable.
When shelter is received for services rendered by a household member other than the
claimant/recipient, see (SI 00835.390C.1.).
STEP
ACTION
1
Has a wage determination been made on this issue for Title II purposes, or does an
FO precedent exist for this employer?
If yes, go to Step 2.
If no, go to Step 3.
2
Document the Title II wage determination on EVID, or a Report of Contact and lock
it. For non SSI Claims system (i.e. non-MSSICS) situations, document on an SSA-5002
(Report of Contact) and store it in the Non-Disability Repository (NDRED) using the
Evidence Portal (EP) or the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate, and,
Charge the appropriate income.
STOP.
3
Ask the employer whether the food and/or shelter is furnished for their convenience,
on their premises, and, in the case of shelter, as a condition of employment.
If yes to all, go to Step 4.
If no to any, go to Step 5.
REMINDER: If an employer/employee (ER/EE) relationship has not yet been established, but it
appears that food and/or shelter is furnished for the “employer's convenience,” determine
if, in fact, the latter is true before developing the ER/EE relationship. If it is,
do not develop for an ER/EE relationship unless additional remuneration is involved.
4
Determine the value of the food and/or shelter as ISM (unearned income) subject to
the presumed maximum value (PMV) rules in (SI 00835.300) and charge the income. (Food provided by an employer may be ISM to one person or
outside the household ISM; shelter provided by an employer is always outside ISM (For
more information related to computing ISM in employment situations see SI 00835.350).)
NOTE: Effective March 9, 2005, clothing can never be ISM.
Go to Step 6.
5
Charge the current market value (CMV) of the food, clothing and/or shelter as wages.
Go to Step 6.
6
Document the employer’s statement and the basis for the determination on an EVID screen,
or a Report of Contact and lock it. For non SSI Claims system situations document
the employer’s statement on a SSA-5002, and store it in NDRED via EP or the CEF, as
appropriate.
