A. When and how to complete question 61
If the applicant is filing on behalf on someone else, complete question 61. The information
requested in 61(a) supports the proper applicant policy which allows a third party
to sign an application as explained in SI 00601.012A.2.
A “YES” response to (b) alerts the interviewer to access the Representative Payee
System (RPS) to take the SSA-11 (Request to be Selected Representative Payee). A “NO”
response requires an explanation in the remarks section and indicates that representative
payee development is needed.
