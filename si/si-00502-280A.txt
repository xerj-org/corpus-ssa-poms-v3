SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.280 - Indigence Exception to Sponsor Deeming
Effective Dates: 09/04/2025 - Present | TN 59 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502280 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Generally, if an alien’s sponsor signed a new version affidavit of support, the income
and resources of the sponsor (and the sponsor's spouse if living in the same household)
are deemed to the alien for purposes of determining Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
eligibility and payment amount. However, under the provision described in this section,
deeming is suspended for certain "indigent" aliens.
NOTE: References throughout these instructions to "sponsor" also include the sponsor’s
spouse who lives in the same household as the sponsor.
