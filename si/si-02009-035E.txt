E. Policy -- diaries
Diary the case for the sixth month after the eligible couple began living apart due
to the medical confinement of one. When the diary matures, develop to see whether
they are still separated and, if so, redetermine the eligibility status of each member
of the couple as an eligi­ble individual. (See SI 02305.000ff.)
