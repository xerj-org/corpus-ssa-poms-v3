F. When the marital relationship applies to eligibility and computation of payments
For SSI purposes, two individuals are married for a month if they meet the criteria
in SI 00501.150B in this section as of the first moment of the month. For the exceptions to the “first
moment of the month” rule, see SI 00501.154, Determining When Couple Computation Rules Apply.
