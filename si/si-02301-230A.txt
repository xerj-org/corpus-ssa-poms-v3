SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.230 - Voluntary Termination (N19)
Effective Dates: 08/30/2023 - Present | TN 78 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301230 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Process
FOs input N19 in the PS field on the SSR to terminate benefits at the request of a
recipient, their legal guardian or representative payee. See SM 01305.001 and SM 01301.450
for coding the PS field in non-MSSICS cases and MS 08125.008 for coding the Payment
Status page for MSSICS cases.
NOTE: N19 is an exception to systems GK processing. This means that FOs can input N19 through
the day of the monthly recurring payment tape cutoff to stop the following month's
benefit and have the system automatically issue the notice. See SI 02301.301 about systems processing based on certain monthly cutoff dates.
