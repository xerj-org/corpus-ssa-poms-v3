Effective Dates: 11/06/2013 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501210600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Example
A blind grandfathered recipient living in State X who continues to meet the conditions
in SI 01210.100 has the following income:
•
$220 wages
•
$15 blind work expenses (BWE) under both Federal and State plan rules
•
$135 RSDI benefit
The State plan income disregards used in the chart below are typical. Actual disregards
vary by State.
