B. Background
Title XIX of the Social Security Act (the Act), known as Medicaid in most States (some States have State-specific Medicaid program names such as Medi-Cal in California; in Arizona, it is called AHCCS, the Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System), establishes a joint state-federal
partnership in which federal financial participation is provided for each State's
medical assistance program. The Federal government pays 50 percent of Medicaid administrative
costs and between 50 and 83 percent of program costs following a statutory formula.
The Medicaid statute requires coverage of mandatory groups and gives States the option
to provide Medicaid to various other groups.
