SI 00604.105 Completing Part VII of the SSA-8000-BK (Remarks)
Procedure
Use Part VII to explain answers or to provide other information. Indicate to whom
the explanation applies and the question number, if applicable. For claims taken through
the SSI Claims system, refer to MSOM MS 08108.001C for completing the Person Remarks
(Printed) field.
