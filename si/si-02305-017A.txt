A. Policy - Required Use of the RZ WIZ with Limited Issue Cases
The RZ WIZ must be used with all Limited Issues (LIs). The RZ WIZ must also be used
with all RZs, LI and wage-related diary interviews, and wage-related diaries. See
Completing RZs in the SSI Claims System or on the Paper Form SSA-8203-BK, (SI 02305.071).
