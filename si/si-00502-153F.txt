F. Exhibit notices
Send the appropriate exhibit notice in SI 00502.160 - SI 00502.170 when the redetermination of a grandfathered nonqualified alien results in a determination
of ineligibility based on citizenship/alien status. (No notice is necessary if the alien continues
to meet PRUCOL.)
•
Recipient in Current Pay: Send EXHIBIT Notice in SI 00502.160
•
Recipient in Non-Pay Status: Send EXHIBIT Notice in SI 00502.165
•
Recipient in 1619(B) Status: Send EXHIBIT Notice in SI 00502.170
