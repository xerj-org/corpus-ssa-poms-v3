A. Background
In 1993, the National and Community Service Trust Act (NCSTA) established the Corporation
for National and Community Service (CNCS) by combining two formerly independent agencies:
the Commission on National Service and ACTION.
ACTION consisted of a number of volunteer services programs including:
•
Volunteers in Service to America (VISTA) (now AmeriCorps*VISTA)
•
University Year for ACTION (UYA)
•
Special and Demonstration Volunteer Programs
•
Retired Senior Volunteer Program (RSVP)
•
Foster Grandparent Program
•
Senior Companion Program.
