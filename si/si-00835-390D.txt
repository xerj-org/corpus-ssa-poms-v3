SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.390 - Food, or Shelter That is Remuneration for Work But is not Wages
Effective Dates: 09/14/2023 - Present | TN 109 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835390 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure — Developing CMV/CMRV
1. Shelter
Obtain the CMRV of shelter for purposes of this section as you would for a rental
subsidy (see SI 00835.380C.3.). Do not develop for a rental subsidy if an individual receives shelter in return
for work.
2. Food
a.
If food benefits the household, follow SI 00835.350 for determining the value of outside ISM.
b.
If food is ISM to one, follow SI 00835.400 for determining its value.
