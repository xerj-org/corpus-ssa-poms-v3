SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.390 - Food, or Shelter That is Remuneration for Work But is not Wages
Effective Dates: 09/14/2023 - Present | TN 109 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835390 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Food or shelter that is unearned income is ISM. It may be income only to one person or to a household and is valued under
the VTR or the PMV rule, as appropriate. It makes no difference that the income was
received in return for work.
