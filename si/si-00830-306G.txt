Effective Dates: 08/05/2009 - Present | TN 263 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830306 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Non-contributory or contributory programs
1. Service began prior to January 1, 1977 or after June 30, 1985
If there is evidence in file that the veteran’s active service began prior to January
1, 1977 or after June 30, 1985, assume that the educational benefits are paid under
a non-contributory program.
A signed statement from the veteran or the veteran’s surviving spouse or child is
sufficient evidence of service dates.
2. Service began between January 1, 1977 and June 30, 1985
If there is evidence that the veteran’s active duty service began between January
1, 1977 and June 30, 1985, assume that the educational benefits (other than Chapter
31 benefits) are paid to a veteran under a contributory program.
A signed statement from the veteran or the veteran’s surviving spouse or child is
sufficient evidence of service dates.
Verify the portion of any VA educational benefit that is a withdrawal from the veteran’s
contributions to the fund.
Use Form SSA-L1103-U3 to verify the information with the VARO and add the following
in the remarks section of Part I:
“For each periodic payment of educational benefits listed in II.C, also provide the
dollar amount representing a return of the veteran’s own contribution.”
