B. Procedure if individual requests appeal of IAR
1. Individual protests payment of SSI to IAR agency
Explain to the individual that they signed the reimbursement authorization and can
appeal this issue only to the State. Refer the individual to the appropriate state
agency.
2. Recipient protests the amount of SSI paid to the State
Offer the individual the opportunity to file an appeal through the regular SSI appeals
procedures. For information about appeals, see SI 04005.010, Overview of the Administrative Review (Appeals) Process for SSI.
