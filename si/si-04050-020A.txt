SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 04050.020 - Action While SSI Litigation is Pending
Effective Dates: 02/20/2024 - Present | TN 5 (02-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0504050020 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
You must be careful to obtain approval as required in this section before taking any
action on a claim involved in any way in pending litigation. During the time an action
is pending in court for review of the final administrative decision, thecase is not under SSA's jurisdiction.
Adjudicative action may not be taken that directly affects a claim before the court
unless the case is remanded to the Commissioner. This is true even if the appellants
file a Complaint in the District Court while a request for administrative review is
pending.
