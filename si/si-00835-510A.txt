A. Definition of breakpoints
A breakpoint is an event which signals a possible change in the value of inside or
outside in-kind support and maintenance (ISM). It may signal a change in the way inside
ISM is valued: value of the one-third reduction (VTR) vs. presumed maximum value (PMV)
or it may mean that ISM has started or stopped. Further investigation is necessary
to determine if the event actually affects the living arrangement (LA) or ISM.
