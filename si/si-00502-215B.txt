B. Policy--determining sponsorship and type of affidavit of support
DHS will certify whether an alien has a sponsor and, if so, what kind of affidavit
the sponsor signed. Determining which affidavit of support was signed is critical
since deeming will apply differently.
1. Old Version Affidavits of Support
All affidavits signed prior to 12/19/97 are old version affidavits of support. This
includes cases where the applicant may have entered the country AFTER 12/19/97, but
applied for an immigrant visa BEFORE 12/19/97. Old version affidavits of support will
also be used with cases of sponsored aliens other than those described in SI 00502.215A.2.
2. New Version Affidavits of Support
Generally, in family-based immigration situations, an affidavit of support signed
on or after 12/19/97 will be a new version affidavit of support (SI 00502.215A.2.). However, if the alien had an official interview with an immigration officer prior
to 12/19/97, a new version affidavit may not be required. Since we are unable to determine
when such interviews have occurred, verification with DHS is needed. (See SI 00502.240 for verification procedure, and SI 00502.215C. for a list of immigration codes that may indicate the alien was sponsored under a
new version affidavit.)
