B. Policy for payments to victims of Nazi persecution
1. Payments to victims of Nazi persecution
Effective on or after August 1, 1994, exclude from income and resources payments received
from any source before, on, or after that date by individuals because of their status as victims of Nazi persecution.
NOTE: Prior to August 1, 1994, we excluded from income and resources payments made to victims of Nazi persecution from the German,
Austrian, or Dutch governments
2. Interest exclusion
Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent payments to victims of Nazi persecution
is not excluded. For SSI benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest earned
on unspent payment to victims of Nazi persecution is excluded from income, see SI 00830.500C.
