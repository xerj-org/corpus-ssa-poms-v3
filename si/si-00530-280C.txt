C. Procedure — suspension action not appropriate
If you review the evidence submitted by the OIG and determine that no action is appropriate
based on the referral (e.g. because benefits were already terminated for the period
covered by the warrant or there was no SSI eligibility):
1.
Complete the feedback form with an explanation of your determination,
2.
Make two copies of the Investigative Summary and the feedback from. Retain the original
and send one copy to the OIG (at the address provided on the form) and one copy to
your Regional Office, ATTN: REGIONAL FUGITIVE COORDINATOR.
3.
Return feedback form to OIG within 10 days of your determination.
