B. Limits for Coverdell ESAs
1. Limit for annual contributions
•
Total annual contributions for the designated beneficiary, excluding any rollover
contributions, cannot exceed $2,000 in any year.
•
An individual or organization cannot contribute more than $2,000 for any one designated
beneficiary for a year.
•
No contributions can be made after the designated beneficiary reaches age 18, unless
the beneficiary is a special needs beneficiary.
2. Limit for number of Coverdell ESAs
There is no limit on the number of separate Coverdell ESAs that can be established
for a designated beneficiary.
