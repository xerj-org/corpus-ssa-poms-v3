C. Witnessed Signature
Witnessed signature is the action taken by an Agency employee of annotating CCE that
they received a paper redetermination or amendment with a signature, and the SSI individual
had not previously signed the RZ by attestation. Once the annotation is made, the
signed paper form is not retained in the RZ file. Instead, it is returned to the individual
with the appropriate cover notice.
