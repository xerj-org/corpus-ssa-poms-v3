SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.040 - The Medicaid Use Test for Section 1619(b) Eligibility
Effective Dates: 09/06/2024 - Present | TN 35 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Follow this procedure each time a Medicaid use determination is made:
Step
Action
1
Ask the individual the following questions:
•
“Have you used any medical care or services in the past 12 months that was paid for
by Medicaid (or Medi-Cal, etc.)?”
•
“Do you expect to receive any medical care or services in the next 12 months that will
be paid for by Medicaid (or Medi-Cal, etc.)?”
•
“Without Medicaid (Medi-Cal, etc.), would you be unable to pay your medical bills if
you become ill or injured in the next 12 months?”
Rephrase the questions, as necessary, to fit the individual's understanding.
2
If the individual answers “yes” to any  of the questions in Step 1:
a.
Record the individual’s allegation regarding use of Medicaid on a Report of Contact,
Person Statement or Report of Contact page.
b.
Transmit a finding that the individual meets the use test to the system per SM 01305.975.
c.
Proceed with development and documentation of sufficiency of earnings (i.e., the threshold
test), per SI 02302.045.
3
If the individual answers “no” to all  of the questions in step 1:
a.
Be sure the response is well-founded given the individual's situation.   A negative
response is appropriate only when there are sufficient alternate sources available
to the individual to pay for their medical care (e.g., comprehensive medical coverage
through health insurance or membership in a health plan, access to other health programs).
b.
Record the allegation on a Report of Contact, Person Statement or Report of Contact
page. To avoid potential misunderstandings, include the name and type of the individual’s
other medical coverage, e.g., “Cummings, Inc. employee health plan – Kaiser HMO.”
c.
Transmit the finding of ineligibility to the SSR per
SM 01305.975.
