C. UTMA provisions
Under the UTMA legislation:
•
a donor makes an irrevocable transfer of money or other property to a minor;
•
the transfer, plus any income it generates, is under the control of a custodian until
the minor reaches the age of majority established by State law;
•
the minor does not have the right to liquidate property until they reach the age of
majority established by State law;
•
the custodian has discretion to provide to the minor or spend for the minor's use and benefit as much of the assets
as they deem equitable; and
•
the minor automatically receives control of the assets upon attainment of the age of majority established by State law.
