D. Procedure - SSA-8203-BK
Document the recipient's residence by:
•
Checking the block which best describes the recipient's (and their spouse's) place
of residence.
•
Indicating “commercial” (COMM) or “residential” (RES) if a person lives in a room.
•
Indicating “recipient” (REC) or “spouse” next to places of residence if the couple's residences differ.
