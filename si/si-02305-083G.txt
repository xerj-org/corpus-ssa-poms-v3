G. Procedure Divorce or Separation
If an eligible individual and their spouse or child recipient's parents divorce or
separate:
•
Document the separated spouse's (parent's) income and resources from the first day
of the period of review through the month of separation (the separated person's income
and resources are deemed to the recipient only through the month of separation).
•
Develop separation/divorce per SI 00501.150.
•
Correct the record composition on the SSR.
