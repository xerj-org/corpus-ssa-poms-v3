SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.800 - Deeming of Income from Essential Persons
Effective Dates: 12/21/1994 - Present | TN 47 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320800 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Examples
1. Essential Person With Earned Income
Edna Jones is an aged individual who has received SSI since January 1974. Edna has
no income, and lives in their own home with their niece, Helen, who is Edna's essential
person. Helen works part-time, and was paid $1,334 in January 2024, and $1,025 in
February and March. To determine Edna's eligibility for January, first deem Helen's
income ($1,334) to be Edna's unearned income. Then subtract the $20 general income
exclusion, leaving $1,314. Since the countable income is less than the 2024 FBR plus
the essential person increment ($943 + $472 = $1415), Edna remains eligible. To determine
Edna's payment amount, use Edna's countable income (including deemed income from Edna's
essential person) in November. Edna's payment amount for March is $101 ($1,314 countable
income in January subtracted from the FBR plus essential person increment for March).
2. Essential Person Dies
Roger. Murray is a disabled SSI recipient whose spouse, Dana, is Roger's essential
person. Roger has no income. On May 15, 2024, Dana dies. Dana had been receiving an
annuity of $1,355 per month. The FO determines that the essential person increment
no longer applies to Roger beginning with June 2024. Since Roger's own income (without
deeming) is zero for all budget months, Roger receives an SSI payment of $943 ($943
FBR minus zero countable income) beginning with June, which is the first month for
which the essential person increment no longer applies.
