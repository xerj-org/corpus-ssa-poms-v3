C. Policy
1. Calculation of UP “At Risk”
The UP is only “at risk” to the extent that it exceeds the title II offset amount. We risk an OP by eliminating
the review. The amount “at risk” is the UP minus the potential title II offset for the underpayment period.
SSI UP
- Offset amount in UP period
Amount at risk
2. Exclusion Decision
Exclude a case from UP review if the amount “at risk” is less than the review threshold.
3. Relationship to Other Case Review Requirements
a.
Exclusion of a case from UP review does not remove the UP period from subsequent review
and correction by the FO. For example, the FO must review that period during the next
redetermination under the rules and selection criteria in SI 02305.001.
b.
There are two reasons for this:
•
the FO may discover that the amount at risk is in fact an overpaid amount; or
•
an additional UP may exist.
c.
If any factor used to compute the UP and the title II offset amount are incorrect,
see
SI 02006.010 for computing the true OP or UP.
