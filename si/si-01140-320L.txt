SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01140.320 - Tobacco Transition Payment Program (TTPP) or The Tobacco Buyout
Effective Dates: 08/17/2007 - Present | TN 71 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501140320 (fetched 2026-10-04)

L. Referrals
If the claimant has questions about the Tobacco Buyout or wants a list of approved
financial institutions, refer the individual to the local USDA/FSA office or its Internet
web site at www.fsa.usda.gov/tobacco.
NOTE: The local USDA office handles questions regarding assignments and the NTPC handles
questions regarding successor-in-interest contracts.
