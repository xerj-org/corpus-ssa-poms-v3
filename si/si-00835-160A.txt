A. Policy for sharing and the value of the one-third reduction VTR
A sharing arrangement exists when an individual's contribution equals or exceeds their
pro rata share of the household operating expenses, provided the household expenses
include both food and shelter.
An individual is not subject to the value of the one-third reduction (VTR) when sharing
exists. This is because the individual is paying the pro rata share for the food and
shelter they receive from the household.
