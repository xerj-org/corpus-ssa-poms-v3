Effective Dates: 10/31/2023 - Present | TN 113 (10-23)

B. Policy— When an Absence is Temporary
1. Conditions for a Temporary Absence
A temporary absence exists, without time limit, if an individual:
•
has been in a permanent living arrangement for at least 1 full calendar month prior
to an absence;
•
intends to return to the permanent living arrangement; and
•
throughout each calendar month of absence is subject to the $30 payment limit because
the individual is in a medical care facility where Medicaid pays more than 50 percent
of the cost of the individual's care. This also includes situations where the payment
limit applies when Medicaid does not pay because of a transfer of resources.
2. ISM During a Temporary Absence
We value ISM as if an individual were physically in their permanent living arrangement
for the month of entry into the facility and the month in which the temporary absence
ends. However, we do not charge food or shelter as income for any month the $30 payment
limit applies.
