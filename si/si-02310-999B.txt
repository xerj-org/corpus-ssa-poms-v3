LEGEND:
1- Date of RRB Run
2- Housed - under number
3- Type record: Individual, Eligible Spouse, Ineligible Spouse or Parent 1, Parent
2 (points to the person on the SSIRD for Analysis; not identifying information)
4- Beneficiary
5- RRB Claim Number
6- RRB BIC: RA, RH-railroad employee  JA-joint award or dual entitlement
MA, MH-spouse     PA, PD, PH - parent
WA, WD, WH-widow(er)  CA, CD, CH-consolidated family award
7-Beneficiary's SSN
8- RR Rate: This is the basic railroad benefit which the recipient is entitled to
after SMIB is deducted.
9- SMIB Ded: This is the amount of the SMIB premium being deducted.
10 - Supp Annu Amt: This is an extra RR benefit which a small number of RR beneficiaries
receive.
