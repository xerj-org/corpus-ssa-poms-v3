SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.141 - Alternative Wage Verification – Periodic Reports With Evidence
Effective Dates: 08/15/2022 - Present | TN 89 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820141 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Some FOs have made arrangements with employers (usually sheltered workshops) to report
earnings on a periodic basis for all of the SSI recipients they employ.
Additionally, some FOs have made arrangements with recipients/deemors to report and
provide proof of earnings on a periodic basis.
