SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02201.025 - Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Overpayment: Notifying Overpaid Individuals
Effective Dates: 12/12/2024 - Present | TN 23 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502201025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
If we issue more Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments to an eligible individual
than the amount they are due for one or more months, the individual is overpaid. When
we overpay individuals we must notify them of the overpayment.
