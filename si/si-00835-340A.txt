A. Introduction to computing In-Kind Support and Maintenance within a household
The purpose of this in-kind support and maintenance (ISM) computation is to determine
the actual value (AV) of food, shelter, or both that other household members provide
to a claimant when the value of the one-third reduction (VTR) does not apply. There
are two computations for ISM from within a household: claimant only and eligible couple.
