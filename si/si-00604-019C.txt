C. Procedure for documenting prior marriages
When completing a full SSI application (i.e., not deferred or abbreviated), document
marital relationships that began:
•
Prior to the Effective Filing Date and ended in death (regardless of duration) or
ended in divorce, if married 10 years or longer; or
•
On or after the Effective Filing Date, to ensure we capture all deeming situations.
1. Information about prior marriages
Document the SSI application with the former spouse's:
•
Name (including maiden name);
•
Birthdate (in MM/DD/YYYY format);
•
Social Security number (if available);
•
Date of marriage (in MM/DD/YYYY format);
•
Date Marriage Ended; and
•
How marriage ended (for example, death, divorce, annulment,).
2. Claimant potentially entitled to Title II benefits
If the claimant was previously married and is potentially eligible for Title II benefits
on the earnings record of a former spouse, to obtain a separate Title II application to meet the filing for other benefits
requirement follow SI 00510.001D.
