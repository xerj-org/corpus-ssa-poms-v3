A. Introduction
Form SSA-3991 and form SSA-3992 guide the user through the calculation of monthly
earned and unearned income using a leave and earnings statement (LES), as explained
in SI 00830.540G. Use of these worksheets is optional.
The worksheet (or PC program) is not needed when the service member is paid only once
a month.
