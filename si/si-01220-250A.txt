SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01220.250 - Death, Divorce, or Separation After December 1973—Policy
Effective Dates: 02/15/1997 - Present | TN 1 (11-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501220250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. DEATH
Where only one person, either the eligible individual or the eligible spouse, meets
the conditions specified in SI 01220.100 and that person dies after December 1973, only the SSI Federal resource limits and
exclusions will be applied for the now eligible individual.
