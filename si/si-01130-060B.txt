B. Procedure -- new or questionable exclusion
1. Contact Regional Office
Contact the regional office (RO) if there is reason to believe an exclusion exists
for a program with Federal involvement (which is not addressed in POMS), or if you
learn of a new exclusion.
2. Helpful Information
The name of the local program or agency may not be sufficient information to make
a determination. If possible, provide the RO with the following information:
•
The name of the program and what it does;
•
The public law (name and number) which authorizes the program;
•
The section number(s) in the public law which pertain to the program; and
•
The Federal agency which is responsible for Federal involvement in the program.
