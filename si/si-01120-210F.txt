SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.210 - Retirement Funds
Effective Dates: 12/04/2012 - Present | TN 29 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120210 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Example
1. Situation
Jeff Grant currently works 3 days a week for a company where he has been employed
full-time for 20 years. Under his employer's pension plan, Mr. Grant has a $4,000
retirement fund. The CR confirms that Mr. Grant could withdraw the funds now, but
there would be a penalty for early withdrawal and he would forfeit eligibility for
an annuity when he stopped working.
2. Analysis
Since Mr. Grant can withdraw the retirement funds without terminating employment,
they are a resource in the amount available after penalty deduction. This is true
despite the fact Mr. Grant forfeits eligibility for periodic annuity payments in the
future. Since SSI is a current needs program, all sources of available support (unless
otherwise excluded) are considered in determining eligibility.
