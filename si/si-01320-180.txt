SI 01320.180 Deeming - Home Energy Assistance and Support and Maintenance Assistance - Policy Principle
CITATIONS:
Regulations 20 CFR 416.1161(a)(17), (b), (c), (d)
Certain home energy assistance and support and maintenance assistance provided under
the programs described in SI 00830.605 is excluded from the income of a spouse, essential person, parent, or sponsor of
an alien for deeming purposes. This assistance is also excluded when determining the
amount of the allocation for an ineligible child or an eligible alien (sponsored by
an ineligible spouse or parent).
