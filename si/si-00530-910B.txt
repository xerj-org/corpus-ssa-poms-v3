B. List of violent, drug related, and nonviolent and not drug related offense codes
See GN 02613.900B. for a list of criminal offense codes that the Department of Justice has determined
to be violent, drug related, or nonviolent and not drug related. For cases with missing
warrant information on the Fugitive Felon System Control File (FFSCF), see GN 02613.175, Obtaining Missing Warrant Information or Incorrect Identity on Warrants.
