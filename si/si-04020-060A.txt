A. Policy — Notice of reconsideration determination
The notice must be mailed to the claimant's last known address, and it must include
specific reasons for the determination and administrative law judge (ALJ) hearing
rights. If appropriate, it must also include an explanation of how to use Expedited
Appeals Process (EAP). For EAP information, see SI 04060.000.
