B. Procedure for completing question 56
Ask if the ineligible spouse, ineligible parent, ineligible child or eligible alien
(sponsored by the ineligible spouse or parent) whose income is subject to deeming,
makes support payments. For development of the exclusion from deemed income of support
payments made under a court order or enforced under title IV-D, see SI 01320.145.
