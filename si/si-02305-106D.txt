D. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 22
Question 22 on the SSA-8203-BK is a two-part question, as follows:
1.
Question “22.a.” Burial Spaces—Do you (or your spouse living with you) own any headstones or markers,
or cemetery lots, crypts, urns, mausoleums, or other repositories for burial?
2.
Question “22.b.” Burial Funds —Do you (or your spouse living with you) have any money or other assets, such as,
burial contracts, trusts, insurance policies, agreements, or anything else you intend
to use for your burial expenses? (Include assets listed in items 16 through 21.)
