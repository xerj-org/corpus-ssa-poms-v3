3. Limit With Spouse
For a qualified individual with a spouse, the qualified individual's countable resources
are compared with the resource limit for an individual with a spouse. If the resources
do not exceed the limit, the qualified individual meets the resource eligibility requirement.
(If countable resources exceed the limit, see SI 01150.200ff.)
