SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.679 - Individual Development Accounts (IDAs) -- Demonstration Project
Effective Dates: 09/01/2011 - Present | TN 127 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130679 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Policy - Effective 01/01/01
1. Contributions
An individual's contributions that are deposited in a Demonstration Project IDA are
excluded from resources.
2. Matching Funds
Any matching funds that are deposited in a Demonstration Project IDA are excluded
from resources.
3. Interest
Any interest earned on the individual's own contributions and on the matching funds
that are deposited in a Demonstration Project IDA is excluded from resources.
