SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.300 - Department of Veterans Affairs Payments
Effective Dates: 11/04/2014 - Present | TN 244 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Explore the possibility of receipt of, or potential eligibility for, a VA payment,
whenever it comes to your attention that a claimant or recipient is:
•
A veteran;
•
The child or spouse of a disabled or deceased service person or veteran;
•
An unmarried spouse of a deceased service person or veteran;
•
The dependent parent of a disabled service person or veteran, see SI 00830.314C.9;
•
The surviving parent of a service person or veteran who died from a service-connected
cause, see SI 00830.314C.10.
NOTE:
The Social Security Act requires that an applicant or recipient who is potentially
eligible for some VA benefits must apply for those benefits. For more information,
see SI 00510.001, Overview of the Filing for Other Program Benefits Requirement.
