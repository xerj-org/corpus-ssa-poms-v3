F. Procedure - Disabled or Blind Recipient
If the recipient is disabled and has NESE, develop IRWE per SI 02305.097.
If the recipient is blind and has NESE, develop blind work expenses per SI 00820.535.
Discuss the possibility of establishing a Plan for Achieving Self-Support. If the
person has a PASS, develop for compliance with their plan per SI 00870.020.
