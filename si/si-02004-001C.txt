C. Policy - maximum emergency advance payment and immediate payment amounts payable
The EAP is based on the amount an individual requests for the emergency, and the total
amount of SSI they are currently due. The maximum EAP amount is the most we can pay
an individual for one EAP. When issuing an EAP, we will pay the lower of these amounts:
•
The FBR plus the federally administered SSP. The SSP includes mandatory minimum State
supplementary (MMSS) and optional State supplementary (OSS) payments. For more information
on State Supplementation, refer to SI 01401.001; or
NOTE:
If we issue the EAP before the first month that payment is due, use the FBR and the
SSP for the first month payment is due. Otherwise, use the FBR and SSP for the month
in which we issue the EAP.
•
The total amount of payments due from the first month that payment is due through
the month in which we pay the EAP; or
•
The total amount the individual requested for the financial emergency.
The IP amount is based on the individual requests for the emergency, but not more
than $2,000. The maximum Title XVI IP amount is $2,000.00 for an individual or each
member of an eligible couple. The maximum concurrent (Title XVI/Title II) IP amount
is $5,000.00 for an individual or each member of a couple. Do not exceed this limit. If more benefits are due, use another method of payment
(e.g., A-OTP). When issuing an IP, pay the smallest of these amounts.
•
The maximum IP amount, $2,000, for a Title XVI eligible individual or each member
of a Title XVI eligible couple; or
•
The maximum IP, $5,000, for an individual receiving concurrent benefits (Title II
and Title XVI); or
•
The total unpaid benefits due at the time the FO makes the IP; or
•
The requested amount for the emergency, which may be less than the total of unpaid
benefits due or $2,000 for an individual receiving Title XVI only, or $5,000 for an
individual receiving concurrent benefits.
