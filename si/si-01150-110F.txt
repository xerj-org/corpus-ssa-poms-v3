Effective Dates: 10/30/2006 - Present | TN 44 (12-24)

F. Policy—transfer by a co-owner
1. General Rule
This provision applies to transfers by co-owners only when the transfer by the co-owner
reduces or eliminates the individual's ownership interest in the resource.
2. Real Property
Generally, transfer of real property by a co-owner will not affect the individual's
ownership of the property. The individual would retain their ownership interest when
a co-owner sells their interest in the property.
3. Jointly-Owned Bank Accounts
In joint bank account situations, it is necessary to develop the ownership of the
funds using the instructions in SI 01140.205 before determining whether there has been a transfer of resources. If an eligible
individual is a joint-owner of a bank account with an ineligible individual, we assume
that the eligible individual owns all of the funds in the account. If the eligible
individual owns all of the funds, withdrawal of funds by the co-owner would be a transfer
of resources for less than FMV. If the eligible individual owns the funds and withdraws
them, it will be necessary to determine whether the individual spent the funds and
received FMV (SI 01150.007). In addition, if the eligible individual takes their name off the account, this
could also be a transfer of resources unless all the funds belonged to the co-owner.
