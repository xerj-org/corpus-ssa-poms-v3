SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.093 - Developing Other Forms of In-Kind Income - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 109 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305093 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
Develop allegations about other forms of in-kind income as follows:
•
If recipients allege they did not receive any other ISM and there is no information
in our records to indicate otherwise, do not develop other forms of in-kind income
any further.
•
If recipients allege they received ISM or allege they did not receive ISM but information
in our records indicate otherwise, develop, as necessary, per SI 00835.310 and SI 00835.400.
