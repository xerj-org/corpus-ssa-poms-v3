Effective Dates: 08/20/2015 - Present | TN 7 (06-23)

C. Servicing FO’s procedure for obtaining a folder
When the folder is in another FO or PC, or no folder exists, take one of the following
steps:
1. Folder is in another FO or PC
Request the folder and follow-up on the folder request per the instructions in GN 03103.080E through GN 03103.080K. When you receive the folder, forward it to OHO and control it per the procedures
in SI 04030.030B.1.g. in this section.
2. Electronically stored documents and destroyed or lost folder
a.
Documents stored electronically - If a folder does not exist because the records are stored electronically, follow
the procedures for electronically stored documents in GN 03103.080E.
b.
Destroyed or lost folder - If a folder is destroyed or lost, follow the procedure for a destroyed or lost
folder in GN 03103.080E.
When you have a folder, send it to the servicing HO. Control the folder per SI 04030.030B.1.g. in this section.
