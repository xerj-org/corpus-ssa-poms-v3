B. Policy — Decision to Collect a Penalty
1. Appeal Rights
A decision to collect a penalty is an initial determination with appeal rights, but
there is no right to payment continuation under GK due process procedures.
NOTE: Collection of a penalty may proceed when the recipient appeals it
unless the recipient also appeals the overpayment determination.
2. Notice Requirements
The collect decision always requires a written notice.
The notice must explain the reason for the penalty deduction, the amount of the deduction
and how to file for a reconsideration.
