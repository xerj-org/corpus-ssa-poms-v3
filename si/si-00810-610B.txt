SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.610 - How to Estimate Income
Effective Dates: 08/07/2023 - Present | TN 70 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810610 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Operating procedure
NOTE:
There are income projection programs available on the personal computer which can
be
used in estimating future wages IRWE, BWE, NESE, and PASS exclusions.
1. When a change occurs
An anticipated change in income occurs when you expect an individual's income to start,
to stop, or to come in at a different rate in the future.
2. How to develop a change
When you anticipate an increase in income, use only that income which the individual
is reasonably sure he will receive.
Except in self-employment situations (SI 00820.230), do not compute on the basis of an anticipated decrease in or termination of income
unless that decrease or termination can be verified. Instead, tell the individual
what income has been used in the computation and that he should report immediately
when a decrease or termination can actually be verified so that appropriate adjustment
can be made.
3. Examples
a. Anticipated Decrease in Income
Danny Kelp, a student child, receives support payments from an absent parent. These
payments are $160 a month. In March, Danny's father begins a new job which pays less
money. Danny notifies SSA that, based on their father's decrease in salary, Danny
expects their support payments to decrease to $125 a month. The FO includes $160 unearned
income in Danny's countable income computation, and tells Danny to report immediately
when it can be verified that the payments have decreased.
b. Change in Number of Paydays Is Not an Anticipated Change in Income
Gretchen Alden, a blind SSI recipient, receives weekly unemployment compensation of
$60 on Wednesdays. Because there are five Wednesdays in August 2023, Gretchen receives
five checks during this month. The FO does not consider a factor such as the number
of paydays in a month to be a change in income since Gretchen is still receiving the
income at the same weekly rate.
