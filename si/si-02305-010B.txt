Effective Dates: 09/05/2019 - Present | TN 95 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Obtain the pending listing of scheduled RZs from the MI Central Page. The instructions
for RZs and LIs are available in the Redeterminations/Limited Issues (RZ/LI) User
Guide on the MI Central Page.
Scheduled RZs will also be controlled on the Workload Management System (WMS). See
MSOM T16WMS 002.001 through MSOM T16WMS 008.001 for detailed information about WMS.
FOs can also retrieve a list of cases through StaRZ and StriPEs The Next Generation
(SSTING). However, do not use SSTNG for workload control purposes.
