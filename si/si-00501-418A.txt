A. Introduction - Systems Requirements
With minor changes (see SI 00501.418B. and SI 00501.418C.), most current systems instructions apply to these cases. These changes will allow
the system to process payment in these cases and route all output correctly. Follow
these instructions to ensure that the correct address and servicing FO codes are posted
to the record. The purpose of the sequence of the address inputs is to reflect the
actual residence and mailing addresses of the child while allowing the Cumberland
FO to retain jurisdiction of the case.
