SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01110.600 - First-of-the-Month (FOM) Rule for Making Resource Determinations
Effective Dates: 09/13/2024 - Present | TN 52 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501110600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. EXAMPLE — Receipt of a resource considered as income and exchanged in same month
An SSI recipient received a $350 unemployment insurance benefit on January 10 at which
time it was unearned income. On January 18, the individual used the $350 to purchase
several shares of stock; i.e., they exchanged one resource (cash) for another resource
(stock). We never counted the $350 cash payment as a resource because the individual
exchanged it for stock in the month of receipt. The stock is not income; it is a different
form of resource. Since a resource is not countable until the first moment of the
month following its receipt, we first count the stock in the resources determination
made as of February 1.
