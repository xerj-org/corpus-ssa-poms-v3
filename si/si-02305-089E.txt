E. Procedure - developing allegations
Develop allegations about current household composition as follows:
1. Recipient alleges no other people live with them
If the recipient alleges no other people live with them and other information in SSA
records agrees , do not develop current household composition further.
2. Recipient alleges that someone else lives in the household or no one else lives in
the household
If the recipient alleges that someone else lives in the household or no one else lives
in the household and other information in SSA records conflicts, develop any changes
per instructions in F, in this section.
3. Determine if a protective filing applies for other individuals
Determine if a protective filing applies for other individuals who live in the household
such as the ineligible spouse (i.e., they turned 65, or became blind or disabled)
refer to GN 00204.010 and SI 00601.025.
