B. Background
The law provides for excluding from income earnings which are used to pay for blind work expenses (BWE) and impairment-related work expenses  (IRWE).
The legislative history of the SSI program shows that Congress wanted to provide every
opportunity and encouragement to people who are blind or disabled to return to or
begin gainful employment. Congress intended that the work expense provisions be liberally
construed, if necessary, to accomplish these objectives.
