E. When to complete question 59(c)
Consult regional instructions to determine in which States to ask question 59(c).
For these states, complete question (c) when the claimant answers:
•
(a) "YES" or in automatic assignment of rights states; and
•
(b) (regardless of response).
NOTE: We send the answers to 59(a), (b), and (c) to the state via the SDX and serve as
the basis for us to bill the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services.
