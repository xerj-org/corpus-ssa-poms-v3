Effective Dates: 08/29/2019 - Present | TN 12 (08-19)

B. Policy
1. Electronic Transfer of Funds
a.
States should arrange with their commercial bank(s) to transfer funds through the
Federal Reserve System to the SSA deposit fund account. ROs must initiate the required
monthly transfer of funds by telephone or by email to the State. In return, the State
requests its commercial bank to transmit the required funds to the local Federal Reserve
Bank or Branch by telephone or by email.
b.
The State should advise its commercial bank to include the following information in
the transfer request:
•
Amount: $ ;
•
For Deposit to Account: Social Security Administration - SSI - Account Symbol 28X6504;
•
For Account: State of ;
•
Agency Location Code - 28040001;
•
Month for which payment is made; and
•
Transfer to the Federal Reserve Bank of New York.
2. Transfer of State Funds by Check
States that transfer funds by check should forward their checks to:
Social Security Administration
Chief Financial Officer - Office of Finance
P.O. Box 17042
Baltimore, Maryland 21235-0001
3. Timing the Funds Transfer
a.
States must deposit funds in the SSA account on the business day before payments are
released to recipients.
b.
Recipients receive payments on the first day of each month or, when the first day
of the month falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday, the last workday of the preceding
month.
4. State Problems
States should direct problems or questions concerning the procedures for transfer
of funds to the ARC-MOS, or designee.
