C. Procedure—documenting whether an exception applies
•
If an exception may apply, follow the procedures in the appropriate section pertaining
to the exception in order to make a determination and document accordingly. (See SI 01150.127D.)
•
If no exceptions apply, document the file with your determination that all the exceptions
were considered and that none apply to the individual .
•
On systems, use the Report of Contact page or the Remarks field on the Property/Cash
Given or Sold page to document your determination that no exceptions apply. On paper
claims, use a Report of Contact.
NOTE: It is not necessary record in the file the answers to the questions in SI 01150.127B.
