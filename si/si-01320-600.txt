SI 01320.600 Multiple Deeming — General
When more than one eligible individual lives in the same household and there is a
parent-child relationship, a multiple deeming situation may exist. The following sections
provide the rules to follow in parent-child multiple deeming situations. When this
type of deeming is involved, it may or may not be necessary to recompute eligibility
and payment amounts, depending on the situation. (See SI 01320.640.) See SI 01320.900 for the rules that apply to aliens subject to deeming from a sponsor and a spouse
or parent, or from multiple sponsors.
