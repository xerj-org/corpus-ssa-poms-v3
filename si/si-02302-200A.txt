SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.200 - Charted Threshold Amounts
Effective Dates: 01/20/2026 - Present | TN 38 (01-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. The threshold amounts in the following charts are computed in the following manner:
2 x the annual State (individual living independently) supplementation rate (if any)
+
2 x the FBR + 85 x 12 = the base amount (the annual amount of earned income it takes
to reduce the annual SSI Federal Benefit to zero)
+
Average per capita Medicaid expenses by State
=
Threshold Amount
