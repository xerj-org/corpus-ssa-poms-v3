D. Policy - general
1. Income Exclusion
See SI 00830.730.
2. Interest
Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned by conserved Agent Orange settlement payments
is not excluded from income by this provision. (See SI 00830.500 for development.) For SSI benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest earned
by conserved Agent Orange settlement payments is excluded from income. (See SI 00830.500C.)
3. Commingled Funds
See SI 01130.700.
