SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02205.001 - Responsibility for Repayment in SSI Sponsor/Alien Caused Overpayments
Effective Dates: 07/10/2012 - Present | TN 2 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502205001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principles
1. General
a.
A sponsor is “jointly and severally liable”
with the alien for the repayment of any SSI overpayment made to the alien during the
3-year period after their admission for permanent residence in the United States that
resulted from the sponsor's failure to provide correct information regarding their
own income and resources and the income and resources of their living with spouse,
if any.
“Jointly and severally liable” means that the alien and the alien's sponsor are responsible together and individually
for the repayment of the overpayment.
b.
In addition, relieve a sponsor of repayment liability if good cause existed for
their
failureto provide information regarding their income and resources.
c.
Find good causewith respect to the sponsor to exist when the failure to supply correct information
was not willful.
2. Adjustment of Title II and/or Title XVI Payments
When refund has not been made, adjust future title II or title XVI benefits due either
the sponsor or the alien to recover any outstanding sponsor-caused overpayment (assuming
waiver of recovery has not been approved).
