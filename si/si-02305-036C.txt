SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.036 - Special Notices to Recipients - RZs
Effective Dates: 11/02/2006 - Present | TN 54 (11-06)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305036 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Personal Contact Interviews - Blind Recipients
Following are the procedures to use with blind individuals.
a.
Explain that while the Agency is currently unable to offer Braille notices, the Agency
is establishing a database of persons who would like to receive Braille notices.
b.
Offer the options listed in SI 02305.036A.
c.
If the individual prefers to receive notices in Braille (when the Agency is able to
provide these notices):
•
Record the preference by posting a code of “4” in the “MAIL CHOICE” field on ADDR in MSSICS.
•
Record the individual's second preference in the Remarks Screen (CRMK) in MSSICS.
NOTE: The notification preference can also be input using a 1719B.
d.
In a non-MSSICS case, document the file per NL 01001.010 (non-MSSICS case).
e.
In MSSICS cases, document the choice on the ADDR screen per MSOM MSSICS 008.005 and
NL 01001.010.
2. Personal Contact Interviews - Foreign Language Preference
Record the recipient's choice in the Foreign Language notice (LPW) field of the SSR.
You can use a SSA-1719B input per SM 01005.545 and NL 00801.025B.2.a using the FV field. In MSSICS cases use the Client Language (CLLG) screen. In non-MSSICS
cases, complete the FV field on the SSA-1719b (see SM 01005.545 and NL 00801.025B.2.a.).
3. Personal Contact Interviews - Recipients with Spanish Surnames
Following are the procedures to use with recipients who have Spanish surnames.
•
Explain and offer the options in SI 02305.036B.
•
Document the file per NL 00801.025 if the recipient makes the choice during a RZ completed on paper forms. In MSSICS,
document the choice on the CLLG screen per MSOM ICD 002.004.
•
Record the recipient's preference on the CLLG for MSSICS cases or via the FV field
for non-MSSICS cases.
4. Personal Contact Interviews - Other Foreign Languages
Following are the procedures to use with recipients who would like to communicate
with SSA in languages other than English or Spanish.
•
Document the file per NL 00801.025 if the recipient makes the choice during a RZ completed on paper forms. In MSSICS,
document the choice on the CLLG screen per MSOM ICD 002.004.
•
Record the recipient's preference on the CLLG for MSSICS cases or via the FV field
for non-MSSICS cases.
