A. Why we document claims
We document a claim for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits to show that an
individual meets all of the eligibility requirements. We require additional documentation
for an eligible individual who is subject to deeming from an ineligible spouse, parent,
essential person, or sponsor.
