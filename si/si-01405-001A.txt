A. Introduction
This section discusses factors which must be considered as to whether the Federal
or State government should administer a State's Mandatory Minimum State Supplementary
(MMSS) and/or Optional State Supplementary (OSS) payments programs. See SI 01401.001 for general information on State supplementation payments. See SI 01415.010 for listing of States and administration of supplementation programs.
