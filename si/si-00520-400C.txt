C. Policy for residency status for ED/VOC consideration
1. Who is not a resident (ED/VOC)
Do not apply the status of “resident of a public institution” if:
a.
a recipient:
•
lives in a public institution; but
•
is there primarily to receive ED/VOC training; and
•
is participating in an approved ED/VOC training program; and
b.
the approved program’s purpose is to prepare the recipient for gainful employment.
2. Further considerations
Apply the following considerations to determine ED/VOC status.
a.
There is no age limitation and there is no requirement to meet the definitions of
“student,” see SI 00501.020C for definition of student. For definition of “child”, see SI 00501.010.
b.
The exception to resident status based on ED/VOC training does not apply to residents
of penal institutions.
c.
All programs that have been determined to be “educational institutions” for the purpose
of title II student child benefit entitlement meet the requirement of being an approved
program.
d.
Generally, ED/VOC designs the training that is part of a rehabilitation plan formulated
by the State vocational rehabilitation (VR) agency to prepare the recipient for gainful
employment. However, this is not the case if the only vocational objective of a VR plan is the attainment or improvement of
functional self-sufficiency such as homemaking capabilities.
