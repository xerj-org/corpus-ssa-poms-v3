F. Procedure for how to document federal tax refunds and advance tax credits for SSI
income purposes
Accept the individual’s allegation of the amount attributable to the federal refundable
and advance tax credit and the month received the tax refund. Document the allegation
on the Report of Contact page and lock it, or fax the report of contact into the Non-Disability
Repository for Evidentiary Documents (NDRED) for non-MSSICS cases, according to the
procedures in GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289. Do not retain the paper form after you document the issue electronically.
