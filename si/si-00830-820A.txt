A. Introduction
No special policy applies to Individual Indian Money (IIM) accounts. Regular income
and resources rules concerning restricted and unrestricted accounts apply. The following
material is provided for informational purposes only.
IIM accounts are similar to regular bank accounts. Funds retained in an IIM account
may earn interest. The BIA area office or agency on the reservation administers these
accounts which are either restricted or unrestricted. A restricted account may be
converted to an unrestricted account or vice versa, but only with BIA approval.
