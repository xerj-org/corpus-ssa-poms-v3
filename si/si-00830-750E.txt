E. Procedure for verifying tax-exempt status of donor organizations
A gift from a tax-exempt organization requires proof of the organizations current
tax-exempt status from the IRS.
1. Precedent exists for donor organization
•
Determine if the non-profit donor is an IRS-approved tax-exempt organization by entering
the name of the organization and searching the
State and Local Income Precedent and Resource Center
(SLR)
•
If a precedent exists, and the provisions of this section apply to the gift, create
a Report of Contact page noting the SLR precedent as proof of the organizations tax-exempt
status and lock it. For non-SSI Claims system cases, use the paper SSA-5002 (Report
of Contact) and store electronically using the EP.
2. To create a precedent
If no precedent exists, contact the donor organization and request that they fax or
send a copy of its state nonprofit certification or its determination letter from
the IRS.
When evidence is received:
•
note the date the state nonprofit certification or determination letter from the IRS
was received on a Report of Contact page and lock it;
•
for non-SSI Claims system cases, use paper SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) and store
electronically using the EP;
•
store the evidence electronically using the EP; and
•
go to the SLR intranet site and follow the steps listed there to create a national
precedent. To create the precedent, categorize the contact type as "other" and annotate
in remarks the IRS tax-exempt certification along with any other relevant information.
3. Questionable tax-exempt status
a. Donor organization's tax-exempt status revoked
(1)
If evidence indicates the donor organizations tax-exempt status has been revoked,
go to the IRS web site and review both the current lists of organizations from which
the IRS has revoked tax-exempt status at:
Tax Exempt Organization
Search | Internal Revenue Service.
(2)
If the donor organization appears on the IRS Revocations listing, note the date the
IRS revoked the tax-exempt status on a Report of Contact page and take steps described
in SI 00830.750E.3.b. in this section, and lock it.
b. Donor organization lost its tax-exempt status
(1)
If the organization lost its tax-exempt status, do not apply the income exclusion
described in this section to the gift. Count the gift as unearned income in the month
of receipt and as a resource in the following month. For policy and procedures regarding
gifts from any organization that is not tax-exempt, see SI 00830.520 and for more on the resource exclusion for Gifts to Children with Life-Threatening
Conditions see SI 01130.689.
(2)
Document on a Report of Contact page:
•
the date the donor organization was added to the IRS Revocations list;
•
the source of this information;
•
that the gifts to children with life-threatening conditions income exclusion does
not apply because the donor organization is not tax-exempt per IRS records; and
•
lock the Report of Contact page.
NOTE:
For non-SSI Claims system cases, document the required information in steps 1 and
2 using the paper SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) and store electronically using the
EP.
