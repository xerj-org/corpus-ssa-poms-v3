C. Procedure for returned IAR checks
1. IAR Check Returned to SSA
•
The State may return an IAR check to the FO for various reasons (e.g., the check was
misdirected or is not negotiable).
•
The returned IAR amount may be in the form of the SSI check or a check issued by the
State agency.
•
Treat both types of returned IAR checks as a refund in accordance with SM 01315.075
and SM 01315.100 if it is reissued to a State agency.
•
If the State IAR office returns an IAR check to the FO because it was erroneously
sent to the State, reissue the returned check to the recipient or their representative
payee or legal guardian in accordance with current SSI payment procedures. NOTE: Do not reissue the check to another State for any reason.
2. Returned IAR check due the recipient
If the recipient mistakenly returns the residual IAR check to SSA, follow the returned
check procedure in SM 01315.020 and SM 01315.042.
