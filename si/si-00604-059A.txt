SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.059 - Separate Consumption and Separate Purchase of Food: Question 32
Effective Dates: 12/27/2023 - Present | TN 52 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604059 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to complete question 32
When the claimant eats all meals outside the household, answer question 32(a) "YES."
If the claimant does not pay for food consumed outside the household, they may be
getting in-kind support and maintenance (ISM).
When the claimant shops for their own food or gives money to someone else to shop
for the food, answer (b) "YES."
