D. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK — Question 24
Question 24 on the SSA-8203-BK — Since the date on page 1, have you (or your spouse living with you) had any change in health insurance coverage
or other insurance that pays for medical bills? (Do not include Medicare, but do include
insurance such as accident, automobile, or casualty if it covers medical bills for
any reason).
