SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.120 - Rental Liability as LA Basis
Effective Dates: 08/08/2023 - Present | TN 111 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure — curtailed development of rental liability
1. Initial Claim — Signed Statement Only
Accept an individual’s statement, signed or on a Report of Contact page, that the
individual or living-with spouse has rental liability if:
•
the individual lives alone; or
•
the only other household member(s) is the individual's spouse, their deemor, or any
child (whether or not related).
2. Initial Claim — Continuous Rental Liability
If SI 00835.120C.1. does not apply, accept the individual’s statement, signed or on a Report of Contact
page, that the individual or living-with spouse has rental liability in the following
situations.
a.
The individual or their living-with spouse has had continuous rental liability since
the current period of residence began and every other household member either:
•
falls into one of the categories listed in SI 00835.120C.1.; or
•
met the definition of “child” at the beginning of the current period of residence (SI 00501.010).
b.
Document on a Report of Contact page or on a signed SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) paper
form the rationale for the determination that rental liability exists based on continuous
rental liability.
3. Initial Claim — Evaluating Household Member Status as a Child
a.
Accept an allegation of a child's past or present school attendance in applying SI 00835.120C.1. or SI 00835.120C.2.
b.
Use the full development procedure below (SI 00835.120D.) if the individual alleging rental liability is a child.
4. Posteligibility (PE)
a.
In PE situations, redevelop rental liability only if the individual's allegation is
inconsistent with the information on the SSR or the SSI Claim pending file.
NOTE:
Be alert to situations where rental liability may need to be developed for the month
after an eligible child turns 18.
b.
Follow SI 00835.120C.1., SI 00835.120C.2., or SI 00835.120C.3. in this section as appropriate if redevelopment of rental liability is needed.
