SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.071 - Completing Redeterminations (RZs) in the SSI Claims system or on the paper form SSA-8203-BK
Effective Dates: 09/29/2023 - Present | TN 105 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305071 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure when using CCE
1. Preparing for a the SSI Claims System RZ
Follow the instructions in SI 02305.071A in this section.
2. Conducting the SSI Claims System interview
Take these steps:
a.
Open an RZ event.
b.
Complete the RZ on the chosen path.
c.
Review the Privacy Act/Paperwork Act Notice, Computer Matching and Privacy Protection
Act provisions, and transfer of resources provisions with the recipient or representative
payee, if applicable.
d.
Review the affirmation of statement requirement with the recipient or representative
payee, if applicable.
e.
Review recipient reporting responsibilities with the recipient or representative payee,
if applicable.
f.
Obtain the following on the printout of the RZ form:
•
the recipients signature using alternative signature methods for cases processed through
the SSI Claims System (see SI 02305.003); and
•
signatures of witness(es), if necessary.
