SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.040 - The Medicaid Use Test for Section 1619(b) Eligibility
Effective Dates: 09/06/2024 - Present | TN 35 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. General
To be eligible for 1619(b), an individual must depend on Medicaid coverage to continue
working.
An individual depends on Medicaid coverage if they:
a.
Used Medicaid coverage within the past 12 months; or
b.
Expect to use Medicaid coverage in the next 12 months; or
c.
Would be unable to pay unexpected medical bills in the next 12 months without Medicaid
coverage.
2. When to Make Determination
a. Initial Determination
The initial Medicaid use determination is made at the time the individual reports
earnings which will cause ineligibility for cash payment.
EXCEPTION: If this would require an additional contact with the recipient (e.g., the earnings
were reported by mail), the determination is deferred until the next scheduled redetermination.
b. Subsequent Determinations
Subsequent Medicaid use determinations are made at each scheduled 1619 redetermination.
