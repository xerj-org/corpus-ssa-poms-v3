SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.045 - Scheduled Redeterminations (RZs) for Recipients Residing in Medical Institutions
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 110 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Scheduled Federal Living Arrangement “D” RZs
Federal living arrangement “D” (FLA-D) cases are selected for RZ only if they meet one of the following selection
criteria:
•
UC profile RZs;
•
PE HEP, 1619(b) or critical birthday RZs; or
•
Limited issue cases.
2. Method of Completing the Redetermination for Scheduled FLA-D RZs
Abbreviated procedures may only be used for FLA-D cases with scheduled RZs.
Generally, RZs are completed in the SSI Claims system or on a SSA-8203-BK However,
if all the following conditions are met, an abbreviated RZ procedure may be used:
•
The case was selected for a scheduled RZ;
•
The recipient does not have an active iteration in the SSI Claims system;
•
The recipient continually resided in the institution for the entire period of review
and is expected to continue to reside there;
•
Medicaid continues to pay more than 50 percent of the recipient's cost of care at
the institution, or for a child under age 18, Medicaid and/or private health insurance
continues to pay more than half of the cost of care;
•
The recipient's record indicates the individual does not have earned income of more
than $65 in any month of the period of review;
•
The recipient does not receive a total amount of monthly unearned income that exceeds
the unearned income exclusion amount of $20 per month regardless of whether the amount
agrees with the amount on our records (ignore state supplementary payments (SI 01410.001) when determining if the case meets this criteria);
•
The recipient's record does not indicate an excludable resource; and
•
The recipient does not have conserved funds at the institution or in a financial establishment
that exceed the statutory resource limit considering applicable exclusions.
3. Unscheduled FLA-D Redeterminations
All unscheduled FLA-D RZs should be completed in the SSI Claims system or on an SSA-8203-BK.
