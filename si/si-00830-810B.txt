SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.810 - Bureau of Indian Affairs Adult Custodial Care and Child Welfare Assistance Payments
Effective Dates: 04/12/1995 - Present | TN 67 (04-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830810 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Non-institutionalized recipients
BIA ACC and CWA payments (other than foster care assistance) made to noninstitutionalized
individuals are federally funded income based on need and, therefore, count as income
dollar for dollar.
2. Institutionalized recipients
The presumed maximum value rule applies to income resulting from BIA ACC and CWA payments
made on behalf of institutionalized individuals.
3. Foster care
BIA foster care assistance is considered a social service and, therefore, is not income
for SSI purposes.
