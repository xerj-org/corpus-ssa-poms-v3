SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.672 - Manual Notices - Victims' Compensation Payments and State or Local Relocation Assistance
Effective Dates: 05/15/2009 - Present | TN 127 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130672 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. When to Issue a Notice
Issue a manual notice only in cases where:
•
receipt of a victims' compensation payment or State or local relocation assistance
payment is established and developed per SI 01130.665 or SI 01130.670, respectively; and
•
there is at least 1 month remaining in the exclusion period.
2. How to Issue a Notice
a.
Issue the manual notice on a Form SSA-L8166-U2.
b.
See NL 00801.010 E. on sending manual notices and NL 00804.100 ff. for the appropriate standard notice paragraphs.
c.
Include the appropriate language in B. below.
NOTE: If the individual is blind or visually impaired, see instructions at NL 01001.010 for more information on the special blind or visually impaired notice options.
