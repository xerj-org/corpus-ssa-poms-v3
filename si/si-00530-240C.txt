SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.240 - Individual Identified and Taken into Custody
Effective Dates: 08/05/2022 - Present | TN 15 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530240 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Examples
1. Example 1: individual fleeing to avoid confinement after conviction for a felony and
incarcerated-administrative finality involved
Joe Blue applied for, and was awarded, SSI benefits in January 1996. Joe has never
been selected for redetermination. The OIG OI-5D shows that Joe has a felony conviction
and a warrant was issued on June 29, 1997 for Joe's arrest. A computer match identified
the warrant in September 2001. On September 22, 2001, Joe was taken into custody and
incarcerated. The FO receives the OIG referral on October 10, 2001.
Payments are suspended, payment status code N25 for October 1999 through September
2001, and payment status code N22 beginning October 2001.
Explanation: SSA first identified Joe Blue as a fugitive felon in the September 2001 computer
match. Since Joe was never notified about the fugitive provisions of the law, fraud
or similar fault is not found, and the rules of administrative finality will only
allow benefits to be suspended a maximum of 2 years in the past.
2. Example 2: individual fleeing to avoid prosecution; apprehended and held pending bail
hearing
Jane Redd missed a trial date for prosecution of a felony. OIG’s OI-5D shows that
a warrant for Jane's arrest was issued on January 25, 2002. Jane is apprehended by
the police on September 30, 2002 and held in the county jail pending a bail hearing.
On October 4, 2002, a judge allows Jane to be released on bail.
Payment status code N25 is input for the period from January 2002 through September
2002. Payments resume in October 2002.
Explanation: Jane Redd’s warrant is no longer active as of the date Jane is apprehended, therefore,
N25 no longer applies beginning October 2002. If Jane had remained incarcerated, payment
status code N22 would have been applicable beginning October 2002. However, since
Jane was released on bail, payments resume in October 2002.
