B. Operating Policy
1. Burden of proof
Applicants and recipients (or their representative payees) are responsible for providing
SSA with proof of income and for reporting any changes in income.
2. Additional verification requirements
See the instructions for the particular type of income involved for additional verification
requirements.
3. Initial claims versus posteligibility situations
Unless instructions dealing with particular types of income state otherwise, verification
requirements for initial claims also apply in posteligibility situations.
