C. Description of The OCSE Match
SSA matches the SSR against the OCSE wage database, looking for significant wage discrepancies
for all SSI recipients and deemors in all States and D.C. When the comparison shows
significant unreported or underreported wages, alerts are generated to FOs and diaries
are posted to the SSR for FOs to resolve discrepancies, update the SSR, determine
past and continuing SSI eligibility, and pursue overpayment recovery.
