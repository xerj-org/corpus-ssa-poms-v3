C. Procedure - FO responsibilities/COA reported after move overseas
If the child is already overseas when the COA is reported, use the following procedure:
•
Input the COA as in SI
00501.418C;
•
Send the Cumberland FO the MDW as illustrated in SI
00501.419A, Exhibit 1;
•
Make photocopies of any available proofs documented in this section SI 00501.416B.1.; and
•
Forward the entire COA report to the Cumberland FO by attaching the completed COA
input, copy of MDW, proofs and any documentation to the completed route slip in SI
00501.419B, Exhibit 2.
