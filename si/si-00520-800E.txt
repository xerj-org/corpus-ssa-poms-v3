E. Procedure — Documentation Requirements
1. Contents of Precedent
The precedent should:
•
describe the facility's total program and potential variations for individual residents;
•
explain how the residents who are in one type of program are distinguished from those
in another; and
•
Note in the remarks section other pertinent information, including allegations from
the facility on controversial issues.
2. Documenting Claims File
Use the Report of Contact page to document the claims file when there is already a
precedent for the facility on the SEANET
8045 website.
