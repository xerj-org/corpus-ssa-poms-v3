B. How to treat an NFIP payment for SSI purposes
Assistance provided under the NFIP for flood mitigation activities with respect to
property (e.g. mitigation payments or grants) is not considered income or a resource
of the property owner when determining Supplemental Security Income (SSI) eligibility
or payment amount. There is no time limit on the resource exclusion of an NFIP payment.
