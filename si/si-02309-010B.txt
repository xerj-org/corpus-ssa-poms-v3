SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02309.010 - Processing the Insured Status (KZ) Diary - Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
Effective Dates: 09/26/2023 - Present | TN 30 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502309010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Exceptions to taking a Title II application
You are required to take a Title II application to process the KZ diary, except when:
1.
The recipient receives a higher Title II benefit than the potential retirement insurance
benefits (RIB) or disability insurance benefits (DIB) computation, and filing for
a lesser RIB or DIB amount is not advantageous to the recipient (see SI 00510.001C);
2.
The current payment status has a non-pay code, including payment status code E01,
suspense, or terminated status code (unless the case is in 1619(b) status); or
3.
A Title II application for the recipients only potential Title II eligibility is pending
at the reconsideration, hearing, or Appeals Council Review level of appeal.
4.
The SSR indicates a technician correctly developed and documented a KZ diary, and
the diary resets based on a semi-annual selection process. Ensure LAG earnings have
been considered (DISCO, ICERS, etc.) and the DLI has not changed since the last Title
II application.
(Do not redevelop the KZ diary. Confirm the file is documented properly using Step 5 in SI 02309.010D in this section.)
If any of the above exceptions applies, annotate the exception on the DDKZ screen
(see MS 04422.012). For example, input Title II application not taken per SI 02309.010B. The
recipient receives a higher Title II benefit than the potential retirement insurance
benefits (RIB)
or disability insurance benefits (DIB) computation, and filing for a lesser RIB or
DIB amount is not
advantageous to the recipient.
CAUTION: You must consider whether RIB benefits were previously considered (beginning at age
61 years and 9 months, or older) before taking no action to re-develop or clearing
the reset diary.
