A. Background on the SSA-L1103-U3 (SSI Request for Information)
Form SSA-L1103-U3 (SSI Request for Information) is used to obtain benefit verification
from the VA. Depending on the type of VA benefit the individual is receiving, the
SSA-L1103-U3 request is sent to either the appropriate Veterans Affairs Regional Office
(VARO) or VA Pension Management Center (PMC).
The VA also maintains call centers throughout the United States that provide certain
current benefit amounts for veterans and surviving spouses. Information about payment
history, augmentation, unusual medical expenses, aid and attendance, housebound allowances
and educational benefits is not provided by phone.
