Example 2:
Marcel Proust filed an SSI disability application on July 20.1981. A presumptive disability
finding was made on August 23. Marcel receives a $125 employment related pension in
the first month of each quarter and $50 in sup­port each month from their parent.
On December 3, 1981, Marcel's claim is finally allowed effective July 198 1.
Originally, a monthly computation was made in the initial quarter of eligibility based
on an August 1981 deemed initial month of eligibility. The computation will involve
a quarterly computation for the period July 1981 to September 1981.

Example 3:
Same as above example except that the claim was filed September 2 and the PD determination
is made October 8. Originally Mr. Proust is paid for October-December 1981; i.e.,
a quarterly computation is used. When the final allowance is received, payment will
be made for Septem­ber 1981 based on a monthly computation.
