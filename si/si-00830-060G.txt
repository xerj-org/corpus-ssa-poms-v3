G. Policy for how to treat the MWP tax credit
IMPORTANT: The MWP tax credit ended with the 2011 tax year, beginning after December 31, 2010.
1. Worker receives MWP tax credit in their wages
Normal income counting rules apply if the individual receives a tax credit in wages.
The MWP tax credit modifies only the individual’s net pay; therefore, it does not affect gross wages. For more information about determining
countable wages, see SI 00820.100.
2. Worker receives MWP tax credit in the form of a tax refund
The amount attributable to the MWP tax credit is not countable income if the individual
or couple receives the tax credit in the form of a federal tax refund.
