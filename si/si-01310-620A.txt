SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.620 - SSA-8010-BK - Public Income Maintenance Payments - Question 1
Effective Dates: 10/18/2024 - Present | TN 38 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310620 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. Ask About Receipt of PIM Payments
Ask the person(s) making the statement whether they have received any public income
maintenance (PIM) payments in the past, including the period from the first moment
of the filing month, or expects to receive any this month or in the next 14 months.
(PIM payments are listed in 1(b) on the form.)
NOTE: Do not list as State and local income maintenance assistance those items which specifically
do not meet the definition of income, such as social services (see SI 00810.010), or assistance excluded by other Federal statutes.
2. PIM Payments Received or Expected
a.
Complete 1(b) if question 1(a) is answered “Yes.”
b.
If the person's share of a family assistance grant (e.g., AFDC) is unknown, show the
total family grant under “AMOUNT.”
c.
Go to question 2.
3. No PIM Payments
If question 1(a) is answered "No," go to question 3.
