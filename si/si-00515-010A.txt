SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00515.010 - Processing Certain Financial Permission Cases When a Deemor Refuses Permission and the Claimant Turns Age 18
Effective Dates: 05/27/2026 - Present | TN 12 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500515010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits requires that deemor(s)
give permission for SSA to request any financial records that financial institutions
may have for the deemor (i.e., financial permission). If a deemor does not give us
financial permission and we cannot establish good cause, the claimant cannot receive
SSI benefits.
Specific processing is required in initial claims when a parent deemor will not give
SSA financial permission and the claimant will attain age 18, causing the deeming
relationship to terminate.
