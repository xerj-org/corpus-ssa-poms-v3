A. When to use these instructions to determine the existence of marital relationships
for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) purposes
Use the instructions in this section to determine the existence of a marital relationship
for SSI purposes.
For instructions about same-sex couples, also see GN 00210.800 Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Same-Sex Marriages and Same-Sex Couples.
