A. Background
The purpose of the special benefits institutionalized for 1619 eligibles is to provide
continued benefits so that the recipient can maintain their living arrangement through
short periods of institutionalization. In order to protect the SSI payment for this
purpose, the law requires assurance that the benefit will not be required for payment
of the costs of institutional care.
