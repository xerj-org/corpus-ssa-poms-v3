SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.150 - Interests of Individual Indians in Trust or Restricted Lands
Effective Dates: 04/12/1995 - Present | TN 35 (04-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If an individual Indian alleges an interest in trust or restricted land:
•
obtain for the file a copy of any document or documents that might identify it as
such; and/or
•
verify the allegation with the appropriate Indian agency.
If verification is by phone, document the file with an RC. Prepare a determination
on the basis of the evidence.
