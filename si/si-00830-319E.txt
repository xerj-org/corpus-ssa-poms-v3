E. Documenting the payment is from the FVECF
Document the amount of the FVECF payment, date it was received and the determination
that the payment is from the FVECF and is excluded from income by following the procedures
in GN 00301.000 General Evidentiary Standards, see sections GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289.
