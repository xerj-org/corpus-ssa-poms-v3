A. Introduction
The interviewer must complete the SSA-8010-BK Statement of Income and Resources. Make
sure any individual signing the form understands
•
the purpose of the interview,
•
the uses of the information,
•
the penalties for giving false information.
The following sections are the general requirements for completing the SSA-8010-BK.
