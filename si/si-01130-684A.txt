A. Background on FVECF payments
On February 17, 2009, the President signed the American Recovery and Reinvestment
Act of 2009 (ARRA). Section 1002 of the ARRA established the FVECF to issue one-time
payments to eligible Filipino veterans who aided American troops during World War
II. An eligible veteran can receive the payment by filing an application with the
United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) within one year of enactment (from
02/17/09 through 02/16/10) of the ARRA. A surviving spouse may receive the FVECF payment
if the eligible veteran filed for it but did not receive the payment prior to death.
Filipino veterans who are United States citizens are eligible for a one-time payment
of $15,000, and veterans who are non-citizens are eligible for a one-time payment
of $9,000.
