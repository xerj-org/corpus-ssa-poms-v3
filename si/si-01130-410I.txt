I. Procedure for Use of Burial Funds
Use the following chart to determine if it is necessary to document the ratio of funds
and interest.
STEP
ACTION
1
Does the burial fund contain both excluded and non-excluded amounts?
•
If No, go to Step 2.
•
If Yes, go to Step 3.
2
Is the amount of excluded interest different than before funds were spent?
•
If No, it is not necessary to annotate the file. STOP
•
If Yes, go to Step 4.
3
Calculate the ratio of excluded funds to non-excluded funds per SI 00830.501. Compare the new ratio to the prior ratio before spending. Are they the same?
•
If No, go to Step 4.
•
If Yes, it is not necessary to annotate the file. STOP
4
Annotate the new amount of interest or appreciation in the file. If excluded and non-excluded
funds are commingled, record the ratio of excluded to non-excluded funds in the file.
STOP.
