Effective Dates: 05/18/2020 - Present | TN 34 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604077 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to complete question 45
Complete 45(a) and (c) for all claimants. Complete items (b) and (d) as appropriate.
1. How to complete 45(c)
a.
Do not record an answer until you determine, based on the claimant's response, whether the item represents income or a change
in value or countability of a resource and its effect on eligibility.
b.
If there was no change or if the increase/decrease does not affect eligibility, answer
"NO."
NOTE: If a new "resource" is really "income" not previously reported, make the appropriate
entry in Part IV.
2. Converted resources
Consider the term "acquired" to include "converted" in this instance.
