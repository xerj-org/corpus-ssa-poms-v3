C. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 10
Question 10 on the SSA-8203-BK Since the date on page 1, did anyone not living with you:
•
Give you a free place to live?
•
Help you pay the mortgage, rent, property insurance, property taxes, and sewerage
charges?
•
Give you or help you pay for gas, electricity, heating fuel, water, and garbage collection
services? Starting September 30, 2024, we will not ask you if anyone gives you help
or helps you pay for food.
