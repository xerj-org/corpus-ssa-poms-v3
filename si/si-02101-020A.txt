Effective Dates: 01/26/2024 - Present | TN 35 (08-24)

A. Introduction
Effective May 22, 2006, we enacted changes to the installment formula for Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) past-due payments. This section describes when we must issue
past-due payments by installments.
