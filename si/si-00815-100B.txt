SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.100 - Food and Shelter Received During a Medical Confinement
Effective Dates: 03/22/1994 - Present | TN 1 (04-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
The file must be documented to show the place and dates of the inpatient stay. When
a multiple-purpose facility is involved, the file must also be documented to show
the medical purpose of the confinement.
NOTE: See SI 00835.704 when in-kind support and maintenance received in an institution cannot be disregarded
using this section. See SI 00520.001 for required documentation of other aspects of an institutional stay.
