SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.675 - SSA-8010-BK - Burial Funds - Question 11
Effective Dates: 04/22/1997 - Present | TN 5 (04-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310675 (fetched 2026-10-04)

1. When/How to Complete Question 11
a.
Complete 11(a) for all deemors (and their spouses, if applicable) whether or not alleged countable resources exceed the statutory resource limit.
b.
If the person alleges having burial funds, complete 11(b).
c.
Complete 11(c) only when interest earned or appreciation in value will not remain in the burial fund.
NOTE: Do not confuse the burial fund exclusion with the burial space exclusion, covered
in question 12.
