A. Background on the Tribal Trust Accounting and Management Settlement
American Indian tribes filed lawsuits alleging that the Federal Government mismanaged
monetary assets and natural resources held in trust by the United States for the benefit
of the tribes. On April 11, 2012, several tribes agreed to settle their cases with
the Government.
Under these agreements, the Government will pay about $1 billion to the tribes in
total. The amount paid to each tribe varies considerably. Under the agreements, each
tribe may decide how to use the settlement funds and whether to distribute these funds
as per capita payments to individual tribe members. Per capita payments are payments
made according to the number of individuals in a specific group and in which each
individual shares equally.
