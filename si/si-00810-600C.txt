SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.600 - Period for Which an Estimate is Required
Effective Dates: 05/07/2012 - Present | TN 71 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Examples
1. Initial claim—deferred development
Sam Polk files for SSI in March based on disability. Sam's only income consists of
rental income which varies from month-to-month. The FO obtains estimates of Sam's
net rental income for the period from the month of effective filing until a review
of income will be completed (based on FO experience). Once the medical allowance is
received, the FO verifies the income for the month of effective filing through the
month prior to the month of the recontact. The FO then enters income estimates for
the month of the recontact through the month the next redetermination or review of
income will be completed, with a continuing code of “C” as part of the last entry.
2. Review of income in less than 15 months
An aged individual undergoes a redetermination in October. The individual's only income
consists of fluctuating wages from a part-time seasonal job; the monthly amount exceeds
$65. Since the individual's job will end in January, the FO documents that and diaries
the case for a review of income at that time. The FO then obtains and enters income
estimates for October through January, and enters a continuing code of “C” as part of the last entry.
