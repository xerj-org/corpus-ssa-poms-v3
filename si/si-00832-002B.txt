SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00832.002 - Processing Self-Identified Anderson Cases - Initial Reviews
Effective Dates: 11/19/1996 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500832002 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure — Screening Inquiries
Obtain the inquirer's Social Security number and current address. Also follow the
screening guide below, until a response indicates you need not continue. Record all
of the individual's allegations on a Form SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other
Person), another signed statement, or an SSA-5002 (Report of Contact). Document the
file with any needed system queries.
1.
Did the inquirer apply for or receive SSI benefits at any time after October 1981?
(Verify with queries, including STALE queries, if needed.)
•
If yes, or uncertain (at the time of the interview), go to 2.
•
If no, no underpayment is due. Issue notice per C.3.d. below, and prepare a report
per C.5. below.
2.
Was the inquirer eligible for SSI benefits or in suspense because of excess income,
or was an initial claim denied because of excess income for any months after October
1981?
•
If yes or uncertain (at the time of the interview), go to 3.
•
If no, no underpayment is due. Issue notice per C.3.d. below, and prepare a report
per C.5. below.
3.
Did the inquirer receive a VA protected pension at any time after October 1981?
(Assume yes if the case appeared on an Anderson case listing or if the inquirer received
the Anderson notice to members of eligible couples described in SI 00832.001 C., and skip to 5. Otherwise, ask the individual.)
•
If yes or uncertain, go to 4.
•
If no, no underpayment is due. Issue notice per C.3.d. below, and prepare a report
per C.5. below.
4.
Did VA recognize one or more dependents at any time after October 1981?
•
If yes or uncertain, go to 5.
•
If no, no underpayment is due. Issue notice per C.3.d. below, and prepare a report
per C.5. below.
5.
Did the inquirer have an SSI eligible spouse at any time after October 1981? (Verify
with queries.)
•
If yes or uncertain (at the time of the interview), go to 6.
•
If no, follow the review steps beginning at C.1. below.
6.
At the time the inquirer had an SSI eligible spouse, did VA recognize the eligible
spouse as a dependent?
•
If yes or uncertain, go to 7.
EXCEPTION: If the inquirer resides in Alaska, Arizona, California, Hawaii, Idaho, Montana, Nevada,
Northern Mariana Islands, Oregon, or Washington, see SI 00832.316 for necessary actions.
•
If no, follow the review steps beginning at C.1. below.
7.
At the time VA recognized the eligible spouse as a dependent, did it recognize any
other dependents?
•
If yes or uncertain, follow the review steps beginning at C.1. below.
•
If no, no underpayment is due for this time period. Go to 8.
8.
At any time after October 1981, did the inquirer not have an eligible spouse, or did
VA not recognize the eligible spouse as a dependent, or did VA recognize a dependent
other than the spouse?
•
If yes or uncertain, follow the review steps beginning at C.1. below for the relevant
time period.
•
If no, no underpayment is due. Issue notice per C.3.d. below, and prepare a report
per C.5. below.
