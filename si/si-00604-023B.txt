B. When to complete question 11
Complete question 11 when the claimant is neither a U.S. citizen by birth nor a naturalized
U.S. citizen. Follow the "Go To" instructions.
•
For evidence of qualified alien status see SI 00502.130 and for verifying DHS documents, see SI 00502.115.
•
If the alien alleges being an American Indian born in Canada, verify status per SI 00502.105B.1.
•
If the alien alleges being a member of a federally recognized Indian tribe, verify
status per SI 00502.105B.2.
