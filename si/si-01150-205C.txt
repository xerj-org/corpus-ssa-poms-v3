SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.205 - Field Office Responsibilities During a Conditional Benefits Period
Effective Dates: 12/11/2024 - Present | TN 51 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501150205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Examples - Efforts to Sell Real Property
1. FIRST CONTACT— REASONABLE EFFORTS EXIST
Darwin, an SSI recipient, signed an SSA-8060-U3 on April 11, 1989, agreeing to sell
recently inherited nonhome real property valued at $8,000. When contacted on May 16,
1989, Darwin stated that the property had been listed with a realtor at $8,000 since
April 24. Darwin submits a copy of the listing for the file and the FO determines
that Darwin is making reasonable efforts to sell. Therefore, the conditional exclusion
of the property remains in effect.
2. SECOND CONTACT— REASONABLE EFFORTS HAVE STOPPED
When the FO recontacts Darwin on September 19, 1989, Darwin states that Darwin had
just refused an offer of $6,000 as being too low. Darwin says they have no proof that
$6,000 was unreasonable; it was Darwin's opinion that it was too low. The FO determines
that the offer was reasonable ($6,000 is more than two-thirds of $8,000) and that
Darwin is no longer making reasonable efforts to sell. As of October 1, conditional
benefits are no longer payable and the FO issues a notice of planned action on the
basis of excess resources.
