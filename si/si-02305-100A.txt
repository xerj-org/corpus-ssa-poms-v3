A. Policy
The following section provides general rules for developing allegations of recipient
resources during a redetermination (RZ) interview.
When using paper forms to conduct RZs, “YES” responses to questions designed to elicit
information about resources require an explanation because the Supplemental Security
Record (SSR) does not contain detailed information about resources.
NOTE: Effective 11/18/2006 permission to contact financial institutions must be requested at all
RZs initiated by field offices (FOs).
Open a RZ with recaps in MSSICS and use the appropriate screens (including the AFIP
screen) to complete the RZ.
