D. Procedure for determining a program's educational or vocational status
•
If the recipient is participating in a training program as a VR client, contact the
VR agency to verify the occupational goal of the recipient's plan. Whenever possible,
obtain the information from the VR agency by telephone and record the reply on a report
of contact. When telephone contact is not possible, request the information in writing.
•
If the recipient is not a VR client, evaluate the training program with regard to
whether it meets all requirements for ED/VOC.
Document the file with a written assessment of the program and copies of any appropriate
supporting documents.
