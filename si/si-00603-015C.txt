C. Procedure - appeals council (AC) review
If a claimant requests an Appeals Council (AC) review of an ALJ decision, process
the request per instructions in SI 04040.020. Check that our record has the claimant's current mailing and residence addresses
and telephone number. Change the record if any of these has changed. Do not develop
any nondisability eligibility issues.
