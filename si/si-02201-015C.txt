SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02201.015 - SSI Netting/Offsetting Overpayments Against Underpayments
Effective Dates: 10/18/2023 - Present | TN 16 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502201015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - offsetting
When there is a collect decision, the system reduces the outstanding balance of an
overpayment by the amount of an underpayment.
When the underpayment amount exceeds the overpayment balance, the remainder is paid
to the individual.
