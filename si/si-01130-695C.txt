SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.695 - Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund Payments
Effective Dates: 06/30/2004 - Present | TN 127 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130695 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Documentation
If any unspent funds from a Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund payment or unspent private
funds paid pursuant to a hemophilia-clotting-factor suit are retained into the month
following the month of receipt (or deposited in a financial institution account),
complete the appropriate resource page (e.g., RFIA). Annotate the Excluded Amount.
Select other as the exclusion reason and indicate “Ricky Ray Fund” as the reason.
Also, document on the EVID screen that the money is from such fund, the amount received,
date received, and amount in a financial institution account(s). For example, “Ricky
Ray Fund, Received 11/11/11 $$$,$$$.cc, Amount of Funds in Account No. xxxxxxxxx on
11/11/11, $$$,$$$.cc.”
To identify excluded funds that have been commingled with nonexcluded funds, see SI 01130.700.
2. Obtaining evidence
If an individual applies in a month after they received a Ricky Ray payment, obtain
evidence of the payment.
If evidence is not available from the individual:
•
For private payments, contact Metropolitan Life at 1-800-638-8787 for verification
of the type and amount of the private payment. Document the EVID screen (or document
the file) with the type and amount of the payment.
•
For payments from the Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund, contact the Ricky Ray Program
Office at 1-888-496-0338 for verification of the type and amount of the Ricky Ray
Hemophilia Relief Fund payment. Document the EVID screen (or document the file) with
the type and amount of the payment.
3. Reopening prior determinations
If a case comes to your attention where SSA previously counted as income or resources
a payment as described in SI 01130.695B, reopen and revise the earliest determination possible under the rules of administrative
finality (SI 04070.000).
