SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.084 - Developing Recipient Residence - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 09/10/2026 - Present | TN 102 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305084 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
Develop allegations about recipient(s)' residences as follows:
1. If the recipient alleges no change in residence address
If the recipient alleges no change in residence address, ask the recipient what their address is then do the following:
•
If the address is on the SSR, do not develop change of address (COA) further.
•
If the address is not on the SSR, determine whether there was an unreported COA during the period of review.
•
IF YES, develop per instruction SI 02305.084E, if necessary.
•
IF NO, develop “temporary absence” per SI 00835.040.
2. If the recipient alleges a change in residence address
If the recipient alleges a change in residence address, ask the recipient what their address is then do the following:
•
If the address is on the SSR, do not develop COA further.
NOTE: To determine living arrangements and in-kind support and maintenance (LA/ISM) issues
for cases with a COA reported since a prior determination, the period of review for
LA/ISM begins with the first day of the month the COA was previously reported. Absent
evidence to the contrary, assume that breakpoints (SI 00835.510) were developed back to the prior determination.
•
If the address is not on the SSR, determine whether there was an unreported COA during the period of review.
•
IF NO, develop “temporary absence” per SI 00835.040.
•
Document the new address and dates of move.
•
Develop changes in LA ISM - SI 00835.500.
•
Document on a SSA-8006/F4, SSA-8007, or a SSA-8008 or the appropriate SSI Claims System
screens, if necessary. (In the SSI Claims System, a breakpoint requires a separate
LA iteration.)
•
Develop residence for Medicaid and State supplementary payments per SI 01410.001.
•
Document appropriate optional State supplement variations per regional instructions.
