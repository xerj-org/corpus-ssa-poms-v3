SI 02003.023 Processing eIAR Exception Cases
The electronic Interim Assistance Reimbursement system (eIAR) and the FO intervention
process cannot process a small number of complex reimbursement cases. These eIAR exception
cases require manual processing similar to the processing required for IAR payment
pending cases before implementation of eIAR.
