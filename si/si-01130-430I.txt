I. Procedure for resource exclusion for months prior to April 2005
1. Items to exclude regardless of value
a. Wedding ring and engagement ring
Exclude one wedding ring and one engagement ring per individual, regardless of value.
b. Items a person’s physical condition may require
•
Prosthetic devices;
•
Wheelchairs;
•
Hospital beds;
•
Dialysis machines; and
•
Other items required by a person's physical condition, regardless of value, if other
members of the household do not primarily use them extensively.
2. Exclusion of up to $2,000 equity of other items
A general exclusion of up to $2,000 applies to the total equity value of household
goods and personal effects, other than those excluded, regardless of value. Do not
exclude any portion of the total equity in excess of $2,000 under this provision.
