A. Introduction
The policy and procedures in this section apply only to members of religious orders
who have taken a vow of poverty. No other special provisions apply to SSI eligibility
benefit determinations for members of religious orders.
The existence of a vow of poverty is a factor in determining whether cash or in-kind
earnings are considered wages or net earnings from self-employment, per RS 01901.600, RS 01901.620, and RS 01901.640. The existence of a vow of poverty is also a factor in determining
if payments made by a member to the order can be considered contributions for food,
or shelter.
