D. Periods of nonattendance
1. School break
A recipient remains a student when classes end if they attended classes regularly
just before the school closed for school break and;
•
tells us that they intend to resume attending regularly when school reopens; or
•
resumes attending regularly when school reopens.
NOTE: If a student changes intent to return to school, determine that the recipient is
no longer a student effective the month the intent changed.
Example:
Jane is a 19-year-old student who enrolled in a vocational course. In June 2014, they
completed one-half of the courses, and reported their intent to return to school for
the second half in late August 2014. In October 2014, you discovered that Jane did
not return to school. Jane lost their student status effective August 1, 2014.
2. Absence due to a recommendation of a teacher or counselor
A student's counselor or teacher may believe the student needs to stay out of class
for a short time to enable them to continue studying or training. Consider the recipient
to be a student regularly attending school, college, or training that prepares them
for a paying job. The training courses must prepare the student for work or for a
job that will meet the student’s specific needs.
3. Attendance in the last month of school
A recipient is a student regularly attending school for the month in which they complete
or stop the course of study or training.
