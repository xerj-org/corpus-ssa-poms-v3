B. When to verify alien status with DHS
All noncitizens applying for or receiving SSI must have their immigration status and
documentation verified with DHS through the SAVE program. This requirement applies
to all initial claims prior to approval, immigration status changes (e.g., noncitizen to
naturalized U.S. citizen), and redeterminations.
