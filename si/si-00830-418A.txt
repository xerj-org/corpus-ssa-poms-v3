A. Definition of alimony and spousal support
Alimony or spousal support (sometimes called “maintenance”) is an allowance for support
made by a court from the funds of one spouse to the other spouse in connection with
a suit for separation or divorce.
Alimony and spousal support payments are cash or in-kind contributions to meet some
or all of a person's needs for food and shelter. Payments may be court-ordered or
voluntary.
