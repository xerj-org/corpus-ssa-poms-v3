Effective Dates: 04/12/2018 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)

A. Introduction
On 8/22/96, P.L. 104-193, the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation
Act of 1996 (PRWORA) allowed qualified aliens who were lawfully residing in the U.S.
on 8/22/96, and who are blind or disabled, to be eligible for Supplemental Security
Income (SSI) benefits, provided all other eligibility requirements are met. The alien's
immigration status and document must be verified with DHS via an online Systematic
Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) request in all cases, including initial claims prior to approval, redeterminations, and immigration status
changes. Follow SI 00502.110 for general alien development. For verification of status with DHS via SAVE, see
SI 00502.115.
IMPORTANT: If a qualified alien is eligible for SSI benefits based on another exception condition
without a time limit (e.g., Lawful Permanent Resident (LPR) with 40 QQs or the military
service conditions), then it is not
necessary to develop eligibility under the provision described in this section.
