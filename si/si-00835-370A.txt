A. When to use rent-free shelter instructions
Use this section to develop the issue of rent-free shelter in both initial claims
and post-eligibility situations if:
•
sequential development of living arrangements (LA) shows that the value of one-third
reduction (VTR) does not apply; and
•
there is an allegation that no one in the household has any ownership interest in,
or rental liability for, the place of residence.
