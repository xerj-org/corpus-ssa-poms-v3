3. Individual - Month of Ineligibility in the Quarter
Example 1:
Charles Carlson, an aged individual, was living alone in their own home until August
18, 1981 when Charles was rushed to the VA hospital for treatment. The VA hospital
is a public institution receiving Medicaid payments on Charles's behalf. Charles was
ineligible for September. Charles re­mained in the hospital until October 5.1981,
when Charles re­turned home. Charles had earned income of $150 in the September quarter
and unearned income of $600 result­ing in countable income of $540 ($180 per month) for the quarter. The computation of the payment amount is:
