SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.086 - Income: Question 50
Effective Dates: 05/11/2020 - Present | TN 57 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604086 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When and how to complete question 50
Complete question 50(a) for all claimants. A "YES" or "NO" indication is required
for each item in (a). For any "YES" answer to (a), complete (b).
1. Policy for completing “Other Income or Support” section
Record cash gifts, foreign pensions, interest, and other income not previously captured
under "Other income."
NOTE: We cover wages, sick pay, and self-employment income in questions 53 and 54.
2. Policy for completing the “Frequency of Income” block
a.
Enter the frequency that the claimant receives income. They may not receive all income
monthly.
b.
If income is expected to continue, write "Continuing" in the "To" portion of the "Dates
Expected or Received" block.
3. Policy for completing the “Identifying Number” block
In the "Identifying Number" block enter the appropriate number or write Unknown, or
N/A (not applicable).
