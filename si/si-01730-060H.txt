H. Region VIII Denver
1. State of Colorado
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Colorado Department of Human Resources (CDHS)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients. The State makes determinations for all
other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and third party
coverage on the SSI application, and at all redeterminations.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the local county Department of Human Services.
2. State of Montana
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Montana Department of Public Health and Human Services (DPHHS)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of the mandatory State
supplement. The State makes determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the question regarding third party coverage and at all redeterminations.
SSA does not obtain answers to the question regarding unpaid medical expenses.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the local county Office of Public Assistance for a Medicaid eligibility
determination. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
3. State of North Dakota
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
North Dakota Department of Human Services
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients utilizing more restrictive criteria
than title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local county Social Services Office for determination of
eligibility for Medicaid. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral
instructions.
