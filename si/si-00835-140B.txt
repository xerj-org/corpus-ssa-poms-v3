B. Policy — separate consumption
As a step in the sequence of household living arrangement (LA) development, consider
separate consumption when:
1.
The individual lives in a household with at least one person other than a spouse,
child (as defined in SI 00501.010), or person whose income is deemable to the individual; and
2.
The individual does not have ownership interest (SI 00835.110) or rental liability
(SI 00835.120); and
3.
A public assistance household does not exist (SI 00835.130); and
4.
The individual or at least one member of an eligible couple alleges eating no meals
in the household during a month.
