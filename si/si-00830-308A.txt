A. Introduction for VA aid and attendance and housebound allowances
The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) pays an allowance to veterans, spouses of
disabled veterans, surviving spouses, and surviving parents who are in regular need
of the aid and attendance of another person or who are housebound. This allowance
is combined with the individual's pension or compensation payment.
