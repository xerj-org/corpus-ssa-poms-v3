SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.074 - Abridged RZ For T30/Start Date Cases When An Ineligible Spouse Becomes Eligible
Effective Dates: 09/17/2003 - Present | TN 44 (09-03)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305074 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
T30/T33 Start Date cases when an ineligible spouse becomes eligible require an unscheduled
RZ. Interviewers are only required to ask 13 questions on the SSA-8203-BK (Statement
for Determining Continuing Eligibility for Supplemental Security Income Payments)
if the interview is documented on paper. MSSICS may be used for these RZs, but there
is no abridged path. The questions asked during an abridged RZ for these cases focus
on the documentation required for the RZ period of review. All of the questions begin
with “Since the date above.”
