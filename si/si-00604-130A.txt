A. Introduction
The SSA-8000-BK, Application for Supplemental Security Income, is worded to protect
claimants' rights to Medicaid under Title XIX. The scope of the application with respect
to medical assistance depends upon the agreements made between SSA and each State.
SSI applications are applications for Medicaid under Title XIX of the Act where
applicable.
