F. Procedure — contacting Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
When verifying sponsorship with DHS, ask them to check their central case control.
Since individuals' records are sometimes interfiled with the records of family members,
if you know that the claimant entered the U.S. with other family members, give this
information to the DHS to assist them in their search.
For addresses of DHS offices to contact for verification, see RM 10214.200.
