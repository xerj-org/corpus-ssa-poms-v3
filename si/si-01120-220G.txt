G. Description Forms SSA-2854 and SSA-2855
To document informal loans, you may use two locally reproducible forms.
•
Form SSA-2854 (Statement of Funds You Provided to Another) has two parts. The first
part is a cover letter to the lender, which identifies the borrower and explains why
SSA needs the information. The second part is a questionnaire that the lender completes.
•
Form SSA-2855 (Statement of Funds You Received) has one part that the borrower completes.
