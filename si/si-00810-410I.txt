SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.410 - Infrequent or Irregular Income Exclusion
Effective Dates: 07/28/2023 - Present | TN 77 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

I. Procedure – general
1. Initial claims
a. Infrequent
If income is regular but may qualify for exclusion as infrequent, evaluate its receipt
over the quarter of filing, including any months prior to filing.
b. Irregular
If income may qualify for exclusion as irregular, evaluate the predictability of its
receipt beginning with the month of application.
2. All situations
a. Individual’s allegation
Obtain a statement signed or recorded on the Report of Contact page concerning the
type, amount, frequency, or predictability of income. The statement, or similar information
on the SSI application or redetermination form, is sufficient documentation. Absent
evidence to the contrary, accept the individual's allegation.
b. Evidence disagrees with allegation
If there is evidence which disagrees with the individual's allegations, develop and
document under the appropriate income rules.
