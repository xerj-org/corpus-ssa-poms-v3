B. Clamant reporting responsibilities
1.
Provide an explanation to all claimants.
2.
Emphasize the importance of promptly reporting any changes since they often affect
eligibility and payment amount. For example, a delay in reporting may result in an
overpayment requiring repayment.
3.
Point out that the claimant must also report any changes in the income and resources
of the deemor if we deem income and resources to the claimant.
4.
Explain that failure to report events or facts affecting eligibility or payment may
constitute a crime under Federal or State law or both. For information regarding penalties,
see SI 02301.100 Assessing Penalties and GN 04105.015 Administrative Penalties.
