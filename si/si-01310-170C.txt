C. Procedure for absence from a deeming household due to military service - OCTOBER 1,
1993
1. October 24, 1990, through September 30, 1993
Do not deny, suspend, or reduce SSI payments to an individual due to the military
assignment of the individual's ineligible spouse or parent.
2. Prior to October 24, 1990
Follow the instructions in SI 01310.165 to determine whether the deemor's absence from the household was temporary. If the
absence was not temporary, discontinue deeming effective with the month following
the month the deemor left the household.
