SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.142 - Qualified Aliens Who Are Blind Or Disabled And Were Lawfully Residing In The U.S. On 8/22/96
Effective Dates: 04/12/2018 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502142 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure - Developing Lawfully Residing in the U.S.
1. Obtain Allegation of U.S. Residence
Ask the individual if they were lawfully residing in the U.S. on 8/22/96. If so, request
DHS documents showing the individual's immigration status for that date. Verify the
DHS document via SAVE. Develop residence under SI 00502.142B.2.a.
We base the determination of whether an alien meets the lawful presence requirements
on documents issued by DHS and verified by an online SAVE request. Follow the procedures
in the next two subsections to verify lawful presence on or before 8/22/96.
2. Verifying Lawful Presence Based on Individual's Immigration Documents
If a qualified alien was a U.S. resident on 8/22/96 and has current (unexpired) immigration
documents that were effective on or before 8/22/96, the alien was lawfully present in the U.S. on 8/22/96 and meets the "lawfully residing
in the U.S. on 8/22/96" requirement. Reverify the individual's immigration status
via on online SAVE request.
A qualified alien who was a U.S. resident on 8/22/96, and whose current alien status
was granted after 8/22/96, may be able to provide a document (for example, a passport with an I-94
stamp) that shows that:
•
the alien was in an earlier status in one of the categories listed in SI 00502.142B.2; and
•
the earlier status was granted before 8/22/96.
If such a document conforms to the description in RS 00204.025B and appears authentic, it establishes lawful presence. Reverify the immigration document
via an online SAVE request.
See RM 10214.100 through RM 10214.170 and SI 00502.115 for immigration information available through the SAVE program.
