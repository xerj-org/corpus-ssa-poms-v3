5. The OSS Eligibility Test
The couple OSS payment level for code A, living independently is $150. The couple
OSS payment level for code B, in the household of another is $140; the individual
OSS payment level for code B is $120 and the individual OSS payment level for code
A is $125.
For March 1989 through September 1989: Both members of couple are eligible for OSS
because they are eligible for Federal benefits.
