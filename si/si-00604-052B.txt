B. When and how to complete question 26
When a claimant lives with someone other than a spouse (i.e., question
25
is answered "NO"), complete 26(a). Complete all blocks for each claimant listed. If the claimant lives
with an unrelated member of the opposite sex, see SI 00501.152 for possible holding out development.
EXCEPTION: Complete the "Under Age 22" portion only for claimants who are under age 22.
1. Public assistance
This documents if the claimant lives in a public assistance household.
2. Birth dates
Consider dates of birth for everyone in the household because a child's age can be
a factor in determining inside in-kind support and maintenance (ISM) development and
deeming allocations.
3. Child status
Use the information in the "Under Age 22" portion of this question to help determine
if others in the household meet the Supplemental Security Income (SSI) definition
of child.
NOTE: Student status and the student earned income exclusions for the claimant are covered
in question 57 (For more information on school attendance, see SI 00604.093).
