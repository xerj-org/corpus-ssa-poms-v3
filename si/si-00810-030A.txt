SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.030 - When Income Is Counted
Effective Dates: 07/28/2023 - Present | TN 64 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principle
Generally, we count income at the earliest of the following points:
•
when it is received; or,
•
when it is credited to an individual's account; or
•
when it is set aside for an individual's use.
We determine income monthly and count it the month it is received.
