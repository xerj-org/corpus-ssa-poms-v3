SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02005.025 - Prospective Accounting for Certain Income Based on Need
Effective Dates: 09/08/2023 - Present | TN 24 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502005025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure — countable income (CI) for payment computation
Use the amount of income in SI 02005.025C that is received in the computation month (CM). Do not use the amount of income in
SI 02005.025C that is received in the BM.
Use normal computation rules for other kinds of income.
NOTE:
The income listed in SI 02005.025C is identified by code “F” in the SSI system. Other types of income are identified
as “F” but they are not subject to prospective accounting. If this would cause an
incorrect payment, the FO must intervene (e.g., issue a one-time payment if additional
money is due).
