SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01210.620 - Special Blind Income Disregards - Recipient With Ineligible Spouse
Effective Dates: 10/30/1995 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501210620 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Example
A blind grandfathered recipient living in State X has an ineligible spouse and one
minor ineligible child, who has no income. The blind recipient has the following income:
•
$250.00 wages
•
$35.00 BWE under State plan rules
•
$75.00 BWE under Federal SSI rules
•
$60.00 union pension
The ineligible spouse has $375.00 in wages.
The State plan income disregards used in the Example are typical. Actual disregards
vary by State.
