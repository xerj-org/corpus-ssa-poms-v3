SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.088 - Developing Changes in Household Composition - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 08/03/2026 - Present | TN 102 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305088 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure - Developing Allegations
Developing allegations about changes in household composition as follows:
1. Recipient alleges no change in Household composition
If the recipient alleges no change in household composition and other information
in SSA records agrees, do not develop changes in household composition further.
2. Recipient alleges a change in household composition or no change
If the recipient alleges a change in household composition or no change and other
information in SSA records conflicts, develop, as necessary per:
•
Marital status (SI 02305.083)
•
Deeming of income and resources per SI 01320.000, SI 01330.000
•
LA/ISM (SI 00835.001 - SI 00835.600)
•
State supplementary payments per SI 01410.001
•
Child/student-child status per SI 00501.010 and SI 00501.020
NOTE: Accept allegations of ineligible child status, unless there is reason for doubt, and
ineligible child's student status, unless the student child is under 21 and alleges
earnings.
Consider possible changes in ineligible child allocations.
