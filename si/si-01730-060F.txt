Effective Dates: 04/03/2017 - Present | TN 12 (05-14)

F. Region VI Dallas
1. State of Arkansas
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Arkansas Medicaid, Department of Human Services (DHS)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of the mandatory State
supplement. The State makes determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and third party
coverage on the SSI application, and at all redeterminations.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the Department of Human Services, Arkansas Division of Medical Services
for a Medicaid eligibility determination. Refer to regional supplements for any additional
referral instructions.

4. State of Oklahoma
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Oklahoma Department of Human Services (OKDHS), SoonerCare,
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients utilizing more restrictive criteria
than title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and third
party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local OKDHS Human Services Center for a Medicaid eligibility
determination. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
