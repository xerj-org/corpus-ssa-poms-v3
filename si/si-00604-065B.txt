Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 82 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to complete question 38
Complete 38 for all claimants. Explain changes in "Remarks."
1. Effect of changes in residence on payment
Consider changes in residence as well as any other change in the living arrangement
or other breakpoints (e.g., change in household composition). For breakpoints, see
SI 00835.510.
NOTE: A breakpoint may affect the LA and in-kind support and maintenance (ISM) determinations,
which in turn, may affect payment or eligibility.
2. Documentation of previous LA
If there is a need to document the previous living arrangement, use an SSA-8006-F4
(Statement of Living Arrangements, In-Kind Support and Maintenance) or an SSA-8007
(Statement of Living Arrangements).
