D. Procedure – Netting Notices
Manual Notices
Use the netting paragraph found in DPS when preparing a manual netting notice if the
underpayment is being paid by A-OTP or if the automated notice has been suppressed.
Use the appropriate notice--an SSA-L8025, SSA-L8151, SSA-L8100 or SSA-8166.
