C. Operating procedures
Determine the nature of the assistance and apply the appropriate operating instructions
pertaining to income and income exclusions. Related sections include:
SI 00815.050 (Medical and Social Services)
SI 00830.605 (Home Energy Assistance and Support and Maintenance Assistance)
SI 00835.300 ff. (Presumed Maximum Value)
SI 00810.420 ($20 General Income Exclusion)
NOTE: Assistance involving FEMA funds is most often provided in kind by private nonprofit
organizations and with State certification will qualify for exclusion as HEA/SMA (see
SI 00830.605).
