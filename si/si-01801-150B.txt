SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01801.150 - Expedited Service for Purposes of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Benefits
Effective Dates: 01/04/2024 - Present | TN 31 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501801150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Refer qualifying households to the SNAP office for expedited service
Advise an applicant that they may receive faster service by applying for SNAP benefits
at the local SNAP office, instead of the Social Security Administration (SSA), only when the applicant:
•
appears to qualify for expedited service; or
•
raises an unsolicited question during the course of a telephone interview (i.e., initial
claim or redetermination), which reasonably requires you to refer the applicant to
the SNAP office for faster service.
