E. Augmented benefits
The VA typically considers the existence of dependents when determining a veteran's
eligibility for educational benefits. An augmented benefit is an increase in benefit
payment that a veteran receives because of a dependent. For a discussion of augmentation,
see SI 00830.314.
For SSI purposes, only that portion of an educational payment that is income to the
veteran obtaining the education is subject to the educational expenses exclusion as
described in SI 00830.455. The augmented portion, which is income to the dependent, is not subject to the educational
expenses exclusion.
