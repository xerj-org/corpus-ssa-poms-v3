Effective Dates: 07/28/2025 - Present | TN 35 (07-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502003040 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Pages 4, 5, and 6 of the retroactive amount due summary
Enter the months covered by the retroactive check and the dollar amounts per month
equal to the retroactive check. When the amount for consecutive months are equal,
show the beginning date of the first consecutive month (MM/DD/YY) and the ending date
of the last consecutive month (MM/DD/YY) plus the per month dollar amount ($000).
Enter any prorated dates for a PE case on a separate line.
