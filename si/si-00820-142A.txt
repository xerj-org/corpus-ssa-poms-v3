SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.142 - Alternative Wage Verification - Sheltered Workshop Pays $65 or Less Per Month to All
Effective Dates: 11/20/2023 - Present | TN 91 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820142 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A sheltered workshop/work activities center may not have paid any of its program participants
more than $65 a month for a particular past period. Once you become aware of such
an occurrence, you may set up a precedent file to that effect and use it to verify
wages.
