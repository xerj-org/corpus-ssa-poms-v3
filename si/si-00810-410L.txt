SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.410 - Infrequent or Irregular Income Exclusion
Effective Dates: 07/28/2023 - Present | TN 77 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

L. Examples – benefit payments beginning July 1, 2004
1. Infrequent/Irregular income — quarterly income only
Situation: A recipient receives a birthday gift of $100 cash in January and wins $25 in March
playing the State lottery.
Analysis: Since the income received is no more than once a quarter, its receipt is infrequent.
Since neither could be anticipated, both are also irregular. The $60 infrequent or
irregular income exclusion reduces the $100 to $40 since it is the first infrequent
or irregular income received in that quarter. The $25 received in March is not affected
by the infrequent or irregular income exclusion.
2. Infrequent/Irregular income – monthly and quarterly income
Situation:
One member of an eligible couple receives a one-time wage payment of $85 for a single
day of work in September. This member of the couple also receives in September 2023:
an unexpected birthday gift of $30 in cash from the couple's daughter and $15 in winnings
playing Bingo. The other member of the couple receives a title II check of $150 each
month.
Analysis: The $30 gift is both infrequent and irregular unearned income. The $15 Bingo winnings
are both infrequent and irregular unearned income. The $45 of unearned income in September
from these sources may be excluded. The title II is received each month. Therefore,
it is not excludable as infrequent or irregular. The wage payment is infrequent earned
income and the first $30, of the $85 received, is excludable. The remaining $55 is
subject to the other earned income exclusions (i.e., $65 plus one-half the remainder).
3. Infrequent/Irregular income – amount exceeds limit
Situation:
A claimant files for SSI in September
2023. In that month, the claimant receives a birthday gift of $25 cash from a friend on
September 15. Also on September 2, the claimant's sister, who lives elsewhere, pays
the claimant's $500 mortgage payment directly to the mortgage company “just to help
my sibling out this month.”
Analysis: Both sources of income are infrequent and irregular. However, since the $500 payment
was received first, the $60 exclusion reduces the amount to $440. This $440 is subject
to the $324.66 PMV (2023 rate), so in September, we count ISM of $324.66 and cash
of $25. NOTE: If the cash birthday gift had been received first, the $25 would have
been excluded as infrequent or irregular income and the $500 mortgage payment would
have been reduced to $465 by the remaining $35 of the infrequent or irregular income
exclusion. The $465 remaining mortgage payment would then be subject to the $324.66
PMV.
4. ISM from an institution
Situation: An individual lived in an institution to receive vocational training from
August 31,
2023
through December 20,
2023. The individual received food and shelter from the institution during the months
September through December. The individual did not receive any ISM and did not have
any other unearned income before or after the period of institutionalization.
Analysis: The CR correctly values the food and shelter received from the institution at the
PMV for all months September through December. The first $60 of the actual value of
ISM received in September is excluded as infrequent income. The remaining amount after
the $60 is deducted is subject to the PMV. The ISM received in the months October
through December is not irregular because the individual could have reasonably expected
to receive the food and shelter. The ISM received in the months October through December
is also not infrequent income because the individual received the ISM in more than
one month of the calendar quarter.
5. Child support received from absent parent
Situation: An SSA eligible child received ISM in May 2023 from an absent parent as part of
the child's support agreement. The actual value of the ISM was $600. The absent parent
did not provide ISM in any other months during the calendar quarter.
Analysis: For the purpose of applying the infrequent or irregular income exclusion, the CR
correctly treats the ISM as if it was child support and determines that the infrequent
or irregular income exclusion does not apply because the child support is subject
to another income exclusion. The CR then excludes from income $200 (one-third of the
$600 in child support). The remaining $400 of ISM is subject to the PMV. The 2023
PMV is $324.66, which the CR charges as income in the month of May.
