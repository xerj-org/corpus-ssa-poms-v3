SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.681 - Payments to Veterans’ Children with Certain Birth Defects
Effective Dates: 09/22/2004 - Present | TN 51 (09-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130681 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
The following types of VA benefits are excluded from income and resources for SSI
purposes:
•
VA payments made to or on behalf of certain Vietnam veterans' natural children regardless
of their age or marital status, for any disability resulting from spina bifida suffered
by such children;
•
VA payments made to or on behalf of certain Korea service veterans' natural children
regardless of their age or marital status, for any disability resulting from spina
bifida suffered by such children; and
•
VA payments made to or on behalf of natural children of women Vietnam veterans regardless
of their age or marital status, for certain birth defects.
While individuals receiving these payments are children of veterans, many would not
meet the definition of “child” for SSI. They may be SSI applicants or recipients and
may have spouses or children who are SSI applicants or recipients.
NOTE: For benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest and dividends earned on unspent
payments are excluded from income. See SI 00830.500. For benefits payable prior to July 1, 2004, interest and dividends earned on unspent
payments are not excluded from income.
