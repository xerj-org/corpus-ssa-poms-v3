B. Description — Types of Ownership
1. SOLE VS. SHARED OWNERSHIP
An individual may have sole ownership of a property or may share its ownership with
others. See SI 01110.510.
2. FEE SIMPLE OWNERSHIP
Fee simple ownership, which relates only to real property, is completely free of conditions
imposed by others. See SI 01110.515 A.1.
3. LESS THAN FEE SIMPLE OWNERSHIP
a.
A life estate interest conveys ownership of limited duration. See SI 01110.515 A.2. and B.
b.
Equitable ownership can occur when an individual does not have legal title to property.
See SI 01110.515 A.2.b. and C.
4. PROPERTY RIGHTS WITHOUT OWNERSHIP
a.
A leasehold conveys a time-limited control of property but not ownership of it. See
SI 01110.520 B.1.
b.
An incorporeal interest in property is a right to use the property but without any
right to possess it or sell it. See SI 01110.520 B.2.
