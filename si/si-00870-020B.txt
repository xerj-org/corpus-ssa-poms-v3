B. Responsibility for developing a PASS
Each region has a PASS Cadre with one or more PASS Specialist(s) or Area Work Incentive
Coordinator(s) (AWIC). In most instances, a PASS Specialist or AWIC will be the Social
Security Administration (SSA) employee who assists the individual to develop a PASS.
Field office (FO) staff who receive inquiries about a PASS or identify a likely PASS
candidate will share information with the individual, accept documentation and forward
information to the PASS Cadre as outlined in this section.
