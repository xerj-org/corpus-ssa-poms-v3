D. Procedure - developmental tolerance for nonliquid resources
Do not redevelop the CMV of nonliquid resources alleged during a UC RZ if the following
conditions exist:
•
The resource in question was reported previously and is on the SSR (CG field), on
the Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System (MSSICS) screens, or otherwise
documented in the initial claims file still in the office;
•
The UC RZ is initiated within 12 months of the month of application, the month of
first eligibility, or the date of the “Review statement Summary for Supplemental Security
Income” (a PERC is the “Review statement Summary for Supplemental Security Income”
spell out PERC) or supplemental SSA-8000-BK (APPLICATION FOR SUPPLERMENTAL SECURITY
INCOME (SSI)), whichever is latest; and
•
The value of the nonliquid resource is not expected to affect eligibility.
Document that these conditions are met if CMV is not redeveloped.
