SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.207 - Verifying Medicaid Eligibility Under a State Home Care Plan
Effective Dates: 09/27/2024 - Present | TN 24 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310207 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. When to Verify
Verify Medicaid eligibility under a State home care plan when:
•
a child is ineligible for SSI (or will be ineligible when they return home from a
medical institution) because of deemed parental income or resources; or a child is eligible (or would be eligible) for less SSI under the parental deeming
rules than they would receive under the deeming waiver provision; and
•
the disabled child received an SSI personal needs allowance while institutionalized;
and
•
the payee alleges the child is eligible for Medicaid under a State home care plan,
or does not know, or there is any other evidence that the child may be covered under
such a plan.
2. How to Verify
See regional instructions to determine whether a State has a home care plan that may
allow waiver of the SSI deeming rules, and for instructions on verifying eligibility
under a State home care plan.
3. What to Verify
Verify that the child is covered by a Medicaid State home care plan under either section
1915(c) or section 1902(e)(3) of the Social Security Act and the date coverage began.
Also verify whether there have been any changes in eligibility under the State home
care plan during the review period, or whether any changes are expected in the near
future.
