A. Policy for IAR notices sent to State
SSA sends a notice about the status of the individual's pending claim to the State
agency providing interim assistance when:
•
An GR code is posted to a claimant's record on the SSR; and
•
The individual is found to be eligible or ineligible for an SSI initial payment; or
•
We reinstate the individual after a period of suspense or termination.
NOTE: We send a notice in post eligibility situations only when we reinstate the individual's
benefits after a period of suspense or termination.
