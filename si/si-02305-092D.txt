SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.092 - Developing ISM and Cash Contributions from Outside the Household - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 109 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305092 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
Develop allegations of ISM and cash contributions from outside the household as follows:
•
If recipients state they did not receive ISM or cash contributions from outside the
household and other information in SSA records corroborate this, do not develop outside
ISM any further.
•
If recipients allege receipt of ISM or cash contributions from outside the household
or no receipt and other information in SSA records conflicts, develop per SI 00835.350C. and SI 00835.360, SI 00835.370 or SI 00835.450C., as necessary.
