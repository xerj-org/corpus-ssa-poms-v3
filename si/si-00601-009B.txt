Effective Dates: 01/04/2024 - Present | TN 78 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601009 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
SSI payments (including Presumptive Disability or Presumptive Blindness (PD/PB) payments)
cannot begin before the application effective date. See SI 02004.005A. for emergency advance payments made before the first month of eligibility for payment.
For applications filed on or after 8/22/96, the earliest possible date for which benefits
can begin is 9/1/96. Contact your regional office for instructions on processing claims
filed before 8/22/96.
The application effective date is the first day of the month following the later of
the date the:
•
application is filed; or
•
individual becomes eligible for such benefits.
