SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.175 - Deeming - In-Home Supportive Services Payments
Effective Dates: 10/01/2024 - Present | TN 35 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320175 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy Principle
Payments provided under title XX or other Federal, State, or local governmental programs
to an eligible individual and paid by the individual to their ineligible spouse, parent,
or ineligible child living in the same household in return for in-home supportive
(chore, attendant, homemaker) services, are excluded from income for deeming purposes.
These payments are also excluded from income for deeming purposes for other eligible
individuals in the same household.
•
Such payments, made directly to the ineligible spouse, parent or child to provide
the services to the eligible individual, are also excluded from income for deeming
purposes. The payments may be in the form of wages if the provider/deemor is an employee
of the agency.
•
Such payments to an eligible noncitizen in return for in-home supportive services
to an eligible individual are excluded from income for purposes of reducing the eligible
noncitizen allocation when the eligible individual's ineligible spouse or parent is
the noncitizen's sponsor.
•
Such payments to an essential person or a sponsor of noncitizen are income subject
to deeming.
NOTE: Qualifying in-home supportive service payments disbursed by a non-governmental agency
follow general medical or social services policy found at SI 00815.050D.1. Payments from a governmental medical or social services program that are disbursed
by a non-governmental agency are not income for SSI purposes and are not included
in the deeming computation.
