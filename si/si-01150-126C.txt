C. Policy — When to consider undue hardship
On an initial claim or a posteligibility case, undue hardship always should be considered
if the individual has transferred a resource for less than fair market value and none of the other exceptions to the period of ineligibility apply. Before denying a claim or stopping benefits, always consider whether benefits may
be payable under the undue hardship exception. Explain to the individual that benefits
may be payable based on undue hardship. If the individual alleges undue hardship,
make a determination and document the file. If the individual does not allege undue
hardship, a determination is not required, but document the file (SI 01150.126E.).
