SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.180 - Other Assistance Involving Government Funds
Effective Dates: 01/28/1997 - Present | TN 68 (04-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830180 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Assistance which is neither IBON nor ABON, but which involves government funds, is
subject to the general rules pertaining to income and income exclusions.
NOTE: See sections SI 00830.625, SI 00830.615 and SI 00830.405 for examples of this type of assistance.
