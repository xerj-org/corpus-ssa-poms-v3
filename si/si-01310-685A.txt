A. Introduction
We need information about sponsorship of a noncitizen when the person making the statement
is:
•
The sponsor of a noncitizen and completes the SSA-8010-BK on behalf of the noncitizen;
or
•
An ineligible parent and completes the SSA-8010-BK on behalf of their child, and also
sponsors an noncitizen who is eligible for SSI benefits.
The information is used to determine the proper allocation (dependent allocation for
sponsor-to-alien deeming or eligible alien allocation for parent-to-child deeming).
