D. Procedures for developing pandemic-related disaster assistance on or after August
21, 2026
Effective August 21, 2026, special income and resource development procedures described
in subsection B-C above and SI 01130.623C no longer apply to pandemic-related disaster assistance.
Technicians are no longer required to routinely ask SSI individuals about receipt
and retention of pandemic-related disaster payments received March 2020 or later or
specifically document the responses on a Report of Contact in CCE or on an SSA-5002
when developing income during:
•
initial claims and RZs;
•
resource-related limited issues in which an Access to Financial Institutions (AFI)
request is required; and
•
Post-eligibility (PE) events involving disaster assistance.
For allegations of pandemic-related assistance obtained on or after August 21, 2026,
technicians must follow general disaster assistance instructions to develop allegations
of disaster assistance for income purposes in SI 00830.620.
For instructions on developing resource exclusions of pandemic-related disaster assistance
on or after August 21, 2026, see SI 01130.623E.
