Effective Dates: 06/28/2012 - Present | TN 5 (04-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310670 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
Obtain a “Yes” or “No” answer for each printed item in 10(a).
For each “Yes” answer, complete 10(b) and ask whether more than one similar asset exists. If so,
complete a separate line in 10(b) for each item, or use the “Remarks” section.
If the answer is “No” to all questions in 10(a) go to question 11.
