SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.007 - Developmental Rules in Posteligibility Situations
Effective Dates: 02/21/1995 - Present | TN 66 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830007 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. General
Unless specific instructions indicate otherwise, the general rules for developing
unearned income found in SI 00830.005 also apply in PE situations.
2. Developmental tolerances
When completing a redetermination, follow the specific developmental tolerances which
apply for certain interfaced income (SI 02305.099).
