SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.080 - Burial Space: Question 48
Effective Dates: 03/28/2012 - Present | TN 35 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604080 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When and how to complete question 48
Complete 48(a) for all claimants. When the claimant answers (a) "YES," complete (b).
Otherwise, go to question 49.
NOTE: Do not confuse the burial space exclusion with the burial fund exclusion covered
in question 47 per SI 00604.079.
Use the "Relationship to You or Your Spouse" column to record the relationship between
the person for whom the burial space is held and the claimant or spouse.
