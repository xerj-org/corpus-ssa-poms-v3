E. Examples of deeming to eligible child(ren)
1. Deeming to an eligible child from a parent who is ineligible because of excess income
Clifford Day, age 65, and their child, Tom, apply for SSI benefits on October 1, 2025.
John, an ineligible child, also lives in the household. Clifford has unearned income
in October of $380 and earned income of $1,853. John has unearned income of $290.
Tom has no income. There is no State supplement.

income
