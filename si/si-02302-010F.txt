F. Policy – Medical facilities
If a 1619 eligible enters a public medical or psychiatric facility or a Medicaid facility
(as described in SI 00520.001), they may be eligible to receive a benefit based on the full Federal Benefit Rate
(FBR), plus appropriate State supplement, for up to 2 months. For a detailed discussion
of this provision, see SI 00520.130.
