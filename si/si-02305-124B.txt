SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.124 - Developing RZs With Multiple Rep Payees
Effective Dates: 11/30/2023 - Present | TN 88 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305124 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
A pre-RZ SSID may indicate change(s) of rep payee during the RZ period of review.
The RZ will be conducted with the appropriate rep payee for the period which they
served. If the pre-RZ SSID indicates that a RZ was conducted with the change of payee
(the RZD date in the CRZD segment and the selection date in the REPY segment agree),
the development need not be duplicated.
If the case is a concurrent title II/title XVI case, it is preferable to have one
payee for both programs.
