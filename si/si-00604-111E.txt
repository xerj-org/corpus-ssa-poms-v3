E. Applicant filing on behalf of a claimant
Apply the Proper Applicant rules in SI 00601.012 and have the appropriate person sign the application.
NOTE: Enter a telephone number where we may reach the applicant during the day. If the
claimant, spouse or applicant have different telephone numbers, enter the respective
numbers in "Remarks".
