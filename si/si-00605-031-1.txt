1. Miscellaneous
Complete question 33 only if someone files on behalf of the claimant(s). (See SI 00601.012 for proper applicant policy.) The applicant data (APLC) screen (MSOM MSSICS 008.006)
records the name and relationship information for an applicant who is other than the
claimant for applications taken in MSSICS.
