SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.983 - Effect of Federal Benefit Rate and/or State Supplementation Payment Increases on Special Deeming Cases — Operating Procedure (for Cases Approved or Pending Before January 1, 1985)
Effective Dates: 12/02/2024 - Present | TN 60 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320983 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General
A cost-of-living increase in the Federal benefit rate (FBR) for individuals should
not increase the $1 payment made in special deeming cases. If the individual continues
to be ineligible under the usual deeming rules and would have received a $1 payment
under the special deeming rules if there was no change in the Federal benefit rate
or optional supplement payment, they will continue to receive $1. Follow manual pay
procedures to insure that the correct payment is made.
