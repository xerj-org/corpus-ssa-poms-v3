F. Procedure - documenting the resources of others
NOTE: Effective 11/18/2006 permission to contact financial institutions must be requested at all RZs
initiated by FOs.
Open a RZ with recaps in MSSICS and use the appropriate screens (including the AFIP
screen) to complete the RZ.
Documentation instructions for MSSICS cases are in SI 00515.001D.1. Documentation instructions for non-MSSICS cases are in SI 00515.001D.2.
Document the resources of the following people on an SSA-8010-BK (Statement of Income
and Resources), SSA-795 (Statement of Claimant or Other Person), the REMARKS section
of an SSA-8203-BK or SSA-8202-BK, or on the appropriate MSSICS screen:
•
An eligible child's parent(s) and the parent's spouse living in the same household
(SI 01330.200);
•
The resources of the eligible child's parent(s) are documented in Question 13 and
Question 14 of the SSA-3989-OCR-SM;
•
Sponsors of aliens and the sponsor's spouse (SI 01330.500); and/or
•
Essential persons (SI 01330.400);
•
Record the effective dates of and reasons for any changes in the REMARKS section of
paper forms or on appropriate MSSICS screens.
