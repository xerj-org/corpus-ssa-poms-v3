B. Procedure for rebates and refunds
1. General
Unless you have reason to question the situation, obtain the individual's statement
either signed or recorded on the Report of Contact page that a rebate or refund of
money is a return of money already paid and do not count it as income.
2. Questionable situation
In questionable situations, obtain any documents in the individual's possession, and
contact the source of the payment, etc., to verify that the payment is a return of
money already paid. Document the file per GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289.
