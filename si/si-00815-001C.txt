C. Policy – What is not income - before 03/09/2005
Prior to a regulation change on 03/09/2005, the SSI definition of what is not income
included “clothing” as follows:
An item received is not income if it is not food, clothing, or shelter or it cannot be used to obtain food, clothing, or shelter.
