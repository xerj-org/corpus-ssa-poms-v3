B. Procedure for which notice to use
Use SSA-L3074-U2 when scheduling redetermination (RZ) and/or limited issue personal
contact interviews.
EXCEPTION: Use SSA-L3075-U2 to schedule interviews with 1619(b) eligible recipients.
Conduct a telephone interview whenever possible, especially if recipients and representative
payees work. Earnings posted to the record may indicate that these individuals are
working and a face-to-face interview may be inconvenient.
