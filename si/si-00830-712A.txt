A. Introduction
The Victims of Nazi Persecution Act of 1994 (P.L. 103-286) provides that any payments
made to individuals because of their status as victims of Nazi persecution must be
disregarded in determining eligibility for and the amount of benefits provided under
any needs-based federal program. Thus, these payments are excluded from both income
and resources for purposes of Supplemental Security Income (SSI). The statute does
not specifically address the treatment of reparation payments inherited upon the death
of the victim of persecution.
