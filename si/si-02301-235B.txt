B. Policy on when not to suspend SSI - N20
We do not suspend SSI using N20 when:
•
the U.S. Postal Service (USPS) returns an initial written request as undeliverable
and you cannot locate the person (SI 02301.240 (S06)),
•
the recipient may need a representative payee (GN 00502.020) or change in payee (GN 00504.100 (S08))
•
a third party not listed in SI 02305.235A such as a neighbor, household member, employer or financial institution fails to
comply, or
•
there is sufficient information to establish current SSI eligibility and benefit amount.
NOTE: When you are unable to confirm eligibility for a past nonpay period (that is, not
payment issued), suspend SSI for the closed period using payment status N05 (SI 02301.220).
