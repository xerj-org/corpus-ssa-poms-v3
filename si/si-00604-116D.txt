D. Direct deposit not elected
Follow this procedure:
•
If the claimant does not have a bank account, discuss the Direct Express® debit card
and its advantages as stated in GN 02402.007.
•
If the claimant does not agree to electronic payment, tell the claimant, “We will
temporarily pay you by check and Treasury will contact you at a later time to discuss
how you will receive your benefit payments in the future.”
•
Mark either the "No Account" block or the "Enroll in Direct Express" block.
•
Explain that the claimant must promptly report any change in residence or mailing
address to a Social Security office
