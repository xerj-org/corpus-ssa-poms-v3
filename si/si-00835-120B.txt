Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835120 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Landlord
A landlord is a party who provides living quarters in return for rent. For purposes of LA and
ISM, a landlord and their tenant(s) cannot be members of the same household.
2. Rent
Rent is a consideration which consists of cash only and which is due to the landlord or
their authorized agent under a rental agreement. Services provided in return for shelter
do not constitute rent. See SI 00835.020 for further discussion of the term “rent.”
