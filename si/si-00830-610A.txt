A. Background
The National and Community Service Trust Act of 1993 (NCSTA) (P.L. 103-82) established
the Corporation for National and Community Service (CNCS) by combining two formerly
independent agencies: the Commission on National Service and ACTION.
ACTION consisted of a number of volunteer service programs including:
•
Volunteers in Service to America (VISTA) (now AmeriCorps*VISTA);
•
University Year for ACTION (UYA);
•
Special and Demonstration Volunteer Programs;
•
Retired Senior Volunteer Program (RSVP);
•
Foster Grandparent Program;
•
Senior Companion Program.
