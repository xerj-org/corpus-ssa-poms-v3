A. Policy on PRUCOL
An alien who has entered the U.S. and continuously resided here before January 1,
1972 meets (PRUCOL), unless there is evidence DHS lawfully admitted the individual
as a nonimmigrant. See information on when an alien is lawfully admitted but not for
permanent residence in GN 00303.450.
