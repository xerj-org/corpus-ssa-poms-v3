O. Description of supplements for Utah
1. Definitions of State living arrangement variations for Utah
Code
Definition
A
Living Alone or With Others – Apply this OS code to recipients who are in Federal
codes A and C.
B
Living in the Household of Another – Apply this OS code to recipients who are “living
in the household of another” for Federal purposes. Children under age 18 are included
in this living arrangement variation.
Z
No Supplement Cases – Apply this OS code to all recipients who are not included in
OS codes A or B.
