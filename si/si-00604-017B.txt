SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.017 - Other Names and SSNs and Identifying Information - Question 1(b) - (d)
Effective Dates: 10/31/2011 - Present | TN 4 (04-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604017 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. When/How To Complete
Complete 1(b) for all claimants. If answered "YES," complete (c) and list any other
names and SSNs used by the claimant. Complete (d) unless the claimant is also filing for Title II benefits. SI 00601.062 has procedures for
verifying claimant identity.
2. Multiple SSNs
When reviewing the numident query, check for multiple SSNs. If the claimant has more
than one SSN, also obtain Master Beneficiary Record (MBR) and SSR queries under the other SSNs. Retain the queries in the file.
