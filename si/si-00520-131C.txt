C. Procedure — general
1. Notify Institution
Use appropriate model forms at Exhibit E.1. through Exhibit E.4., reproduced locally,
to notify institutions about the provision, to obtain an agreement when needed, and
to notify the institution regarding specific cases and benefit months which are affected
by the agreement.
2. Do Not Alter Forms
Do not alter these forms without the permission of the Office of Income Security Programs
(OISP) obtained through the RO.
