B. Operating Policy
1. Loss of EP Increment Due to Absences
A qualified individual loses the EP increment(s) effective with the earlier of the
month following the month in which:
•
Qualified individual's absence exceeds 6 months; or
•
EP's absence exceeds 90 days; or
•
EP or qualified individual changes intent and the absence becomes permanent; or
•
EP or qualified individual indicates prior to leaving the home they will not return
to it.
2. Qualified Individual Loses Eligibility
If a qualified individual becomes ineligible (e.g., because of their own excess income
or resources) and subsequently reestablishes eligibility, EP status may resume as
well, if they meet all requirements.
