SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.060 - Quarterly Verification of Earnings
Effective Dates: 12/22/2009 - Present | TN 14 (09-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Verify Earnings
In addition to the annual redetermination required for section 1619(a) and (b) cases,
earned income and exclusions from earned income must be verified at least quarterly
(more frequently at local option).
2. When to Verify
Earned income and exclusions from earned income must be verified no later than the
beginning of the second month of each quarter.
EXCEPTION: Where a redetermination is conducted in the quarter being determined, the earnings
verification performed as part of that redetermination also satisfies this requirement.
Earnings are verified for section 1619 cases for the period beginning with the month
after the last month for which earnings have been previously verified and ending with
the last full calendar month prior to the current month.
3. Threshold Determinations
Eligibility is not redetermined under the threshold test each quarter.
