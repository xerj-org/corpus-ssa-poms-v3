Effective Dates: 10/06/2023 - Present | TN 55 (12-23)

A. Introduction
This section describes the policy and procedures for withdrawal of an SSI application,
cancellation of the withdrawal request, and situations where consent is involved.
NOTE:
The systems payment status code for a withdrawn claim is N12.
