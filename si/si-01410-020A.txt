A. Introduction
Below is a chart you may use to help you develop the State and county of residence
of a claimant or recipient when needed. The chart is general and will not resolve
all issues. See SI 01410.030 for policy.
NOTE: Some terms used in the chart, such as “capable” and “incapable” have a special meaning for residency purposes. See SI 01410.030A. for the definitions of these terms.
