C. When a new IAR authorization is required
When an IAR authorization is no longer effective, SSA will not reimburse the State
unless it obtains a new authorization. A new authorization is required if:
•
the State does not submit the signed authorization within 30 calendar days;
•
the individual does not file for SSI within 12 months of the date the authorization
was received by the State;
•
the individual files a new SSI application; or
•
the individual requests a review or appeal of their suspension or termination and
the individual will receive IA payments while awaiting a decision by SSA to reverses
the suspension or termination.
