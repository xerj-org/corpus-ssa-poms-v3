SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01140.044 - Resources With Zero Value
Effective Dates: 03/01/1999 - Present | TN 7 (03-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501140044 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Reasonable Efforts to Sell
For the effect of reasonable but unsuccessful efforts to sell real property, see SI 01130.140.
2. Conversion of a Resource
Should property that has been determined to have no CMV be sold, the proceeds of the
sale represent the conversion of a resource, not income (SI 01110.600 B.4.).
