D. Procedure — Expenses Paid After Work Stops
1. Expense Paid Before Earned Income Stops
Deduct a work expense that is paid in a month after work has stopped from earned income
received in a month after work has stopped only when:
•
the income is based on work activity (e.g., not income received as a silent partner
in a business); and
•
the work activity was performed in a period when the individual required the item
or service as explained in DI 10520.030E.2.b.
2. Expense Paid After Earned Income Stops
Deduct the work expense from the After Earned earned income received in the last Income
Stops month of work when:
•
the work expense is paid in a month after the individual last worked and received
earned income; and
•
the payment was for an item or service used while working.
