D. Procedure for developing flexible spending arrangements
1. Evidence indicates account is an FSA
Develop for a health FSA when an individual alleges owning an employer-established
health benefit account. Request evidence that supports the account is an FSA. You
may request one of the following:
•
an FSA account statement,
•
a current pay stub that shows the salary deduction going into the FSA, or
•
an FSA debit card that shows the printed certification on the back of the card.
Once you verify the account is a health FSA, accept the allegation of the health FSA
balance. No further development is necessary. Record the health FSA in the Other Resources
(ROTH) page in MSSICS and exclude the balance. For non-MSSICS cases, enter remarks
stating the following: “Balance in health FSA is excluded.”
2. Evidence suggests the account is not an FSA
If the evidence shows the account is not an FSA, verify what type of account it is
and process accordingly. For information on Health Saving Accounts (HSAs) and Medical
Savings Accounts (MSAs), see SI 01120.235. For information on checking and savings accounts, see SI 01140.200. For other common investment vehicles, such as stocks and bonds, see SI 01140.220 through SI 01140.300.
