B. Description of RZs completed on MSSICS screens
Opening a “REDETERMINATION with or without RECAPs” event creates a Modernized Supplemental
Security Income Claims System (MSSICS) “path” which guides the interviewer to the
appropriate screens to document allegations and evidence relating to liquid resources,
as necessary.
