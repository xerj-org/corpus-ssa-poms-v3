C. Description of Form SSA-8202-BK - Question 10
Question 10 on the SSA-8202-BK — Do you or your spouse living with you, own any land or buildings
or does your name appear on a deed or mortgage of any land or building where YOU DO
NOT LIVE?
This includes inherited property, property outside the United States and/or any property
your name is on with other members of your family.
