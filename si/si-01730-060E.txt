SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01730.060 - Medicaid Information by Regions
Effective Dates: 04/03/2017 - Present | TN 12 (05-14)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501730060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Region V — Chicago
1. State of Illinois
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services (HFS)
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients utilizing more restrictive criteria
than title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local Department of Human Services office for determination
of eligibility for Medicaid. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral
instructions.
2. State of Indiana
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Indiana Family & Social Services Administration (FSSA)
•
Medicaid Determination
Effective June 1, 2014, SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients. The State makes
determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
Effective June 1, 2014, SSA obtains answers to the question regarding third party
coverage on the application and at all redeterminations. SSA does not obtain answers
to the question regarding unpaid medical expenses.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the local county Division of Family Resources (DFR) for a Medicaid eligibility
determination. Refer to regional Supplements for any additional referral instructions.
3. State of Michigan
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Michigan Department of Community Health (MDCH)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory and optional
State supplements. The State makes determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the question regarding third party coverage on the SSI application
and at all redeterminations.
SSA does not obtain answers to the question regarding unpaid medical expenses.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the local Department of Community Health office for determination of
Medicaid eligibility. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
