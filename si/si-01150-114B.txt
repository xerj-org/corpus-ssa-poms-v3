Effective Dates: 09/27/2002 - Present | TN 48 (12-24)

B. Procedure — Applying the period of ineligibility
Follow this procedure to determine which months are affected by the period of ineligibility.
Based on when the transfer occurred and the number of months in the period of ineligibility,
the months affected can be:
a.
All past months;
b.
Past and future months;
c.
Future months only.
Example 1: All Past Months During a redetermination for Howard conducted on 7/2/00, it is learned that they
transferred a resource on 12/22/99 that results in a period of ineligibility of 5
months. The period of ineligibility begins as of 1/00 (the month after the transfer)
and continues through 5/00.
Example 2: Past and Future Months During a redetermination for Fine conducted on 7/2/00, it is learned that they transferred
a resource on 12/22/99 that results in a period of ineligibility of 16 months. The
period of ineligibility begins as of 1/00 (the month after the transfer) and continues
through 4/01.
Example 3: All Future Months Johnson who is eligible for SSI comes into the field office on 7/12/00 to report
that they inherited stocks on 7/3/00 and gave them to their daughter on 7/5/00. The
value of the stocks results in a period of ineligibility of 4 months. The period of
ineligibility begins in 8/00 (the month after the transfer) and continues through
11/00. Johnson would be eligible again for SSI in 12/00.
