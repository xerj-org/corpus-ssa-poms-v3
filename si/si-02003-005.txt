SI 02003.005 Requirements for Valid IAR Authorizations
An IAR authorization is an individual's written permission for SSA to withhold the
individual's initial claim or post-entitlement SSI payment and send it to the State
to reimburse the State for interim assistance provided to the individual.
NOTE: The eIAR system has not changed the authorization process.
