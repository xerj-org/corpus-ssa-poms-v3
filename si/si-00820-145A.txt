SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.145 - Form SSA-L4201-BK (Letter to Employer Requesting Wage Information)
Effective Dates: 11/20/2023 - Present | TN 91 (05-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820145 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When To Use SSA-L4201-BK
Use the SSA-L4201-BK to:
•
verify past wage information (including blind work expenses (BWE), impairment-related
work expenses (IRWE), cafeteria plan deductions, and child support deductions, when
needed) when you cannot obtain the information by other means (see SI 00820.130), or
•
obtain current wage information to estimate future wages when you cannot establish
an estimate otherwise (see SI 00820.150).
