A. Operating Policy—Follow-ups
During a conditional benefits period, we make followup contacts by telephone whenever
possible.
1. FREQUENCY OF CONTACTS
a.
Personal Property - We make contact every 30 days during the initial 3-month period
as well as during any extension period.
b.
Real Property - We make contact:
•
35 days following the date of the conditional benefits agreement; and
•
every 60 days thereafter until the end of the 9-month period.
2. PURPOSE OF CONTACTS
We contact the individual in order to:
•
remind them of the responsibility for selling the property and the time remaining
in the disposal period;
•
verify the efforts being made to accomplish a sale;
•
verify if there has been an offer to buy since the prior contact; and
•
verify good cause in the absence of reasonable efforts to sell.
