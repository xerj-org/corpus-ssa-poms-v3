SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.640 - Programs for Older Americans
Effective Dates: 03/17/2011 - Present | TN 41 (03-91)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830640 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Verify Program
Use documents in the individual's possession, contact with the provider or a local
council on aging, or a precedent to verify that the program is funded by the Federal
Government under chapter 35 of “The Older Americans Act ” and whether a wage or salary is paid.
2. Wage or Salary
See SI 00820.100 ff.
3. Not a Wage Or Salary-Accept Allegation
Accept the individual's allegation of receipt of anything other than a wage or salary
and exclude it without further development unless you have reason to question the
allegation.
