A. Introduction
This section explains how a recipient can become eligible for supplemental security
income (SSI) benefits under one category for Federal payments and a different category
for optional State supplementation (OSS). For Federal payments this could mean issuing
payments under one category while developing potential eligibility under another.
We make these considerations, not only at the time of the initial application, but
also in any posteligibility situation where a potential other category exists which
will benefit the recipient.
