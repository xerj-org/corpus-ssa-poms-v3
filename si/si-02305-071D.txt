Effective Dates: 09/29/2023 - Present | TN 105 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305071 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Entering the responses
Use the following steps to enter responses on the SSI Claims System screens:
1.
Review the information on the SSI Claims System screens with the recipient;
2.
Complete any necessary development see SI 02305.082 through SI 02305.124; and
3.
Resolve any discrepancies following appropriate instructions, unless a tolerance applies.
