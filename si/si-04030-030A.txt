A. Preparing for the hearing
The following are field office (FO) instructions for sending the Form HA-501-U5 (Request
for Hearing by Administrative Law Judge) (HA-501) and folder to the servicing hearing
office (HO). In these instructions, we use the words hearing request and HA-501 interchangeably.
These instructions presume the:
•
claimant received a reconsideration determination; and
•
technician completed the HA-501 per GN 03103.020 and when applicable, completed the Electronic Non-disability Summary Sheet (eNDSS)
per GN 03103.130.
Refer to the following references for situations that you may encounter when preparing
a hearing request and folder.
1.
Disability: See DI 12010.010 for detailed instructions on preparing and forwarding the folder to the servicing
HO. General instructions for preparing and forwarding the HA-501 and folder are located
in SI 04030.030B.2.
2.
Information about the hearing process: For information about the hearing process, see SI 04030.010 and SI 04030.020.
3.
Late hearing request: When a claimant submits a hearing request late, see GN 03101.020.
4.
Hearing request filed in an FO other than the servicing FO:
When the claimant submits the hearing request somewhere other than their servicing
FO, see GN 03103.090.
5.
No reconsideration determination: When the claimant submits a hearing request, but we have not made a reconsideration
determination, see SI 04030.020B.4.
6.
Respond to a folder request: For instructions on how to respond to a folder request from another office, see GN 03103.080L through GN 03103.080M.
