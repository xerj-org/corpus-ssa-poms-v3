Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830730 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Agent Orange settlement payments made in connection with the case of In re Agent Orange Product Liability Litigation come from a fund created by manufacturers of Agent Orange who agreed to pay into a
settlement fund. Payments began in March 1989. Qualifying veterans will receive at
least one payment a year for the life of the program. Qualifying survivors of deceased
veterans will receive a single lump sum payment.
