SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01110.510 - Sole vs. Shared Ownership
Effective Dates: 09/13/2024 - Present | TN 52 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501110510 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Sole ownership
Sole ownership of (real or personal) property means that only one person may sell,
transfer or otherwise dispose of the property. However, sole ownership may be subject
to conditions imposed by others as, for example, sole ownership of a remainder interest
in property, see SI 01110.515.
2. Shared ownership
Shared ownership of (real or personal) property means that two or more people own
it concurrently. For additional information regarding different types of shared ownership,
see SI 01110.510C.
