B. Procedure - Determining Whether an Activity Constitutes Self-Employment
Consider all factors in the case when an individual alleges participating in an activity
that produces income to determine if it constitutes self-employment. If a determination
is made that the activity does not result in wages or NESE, then the income derived
by the activity is unearned income. Sometimes, just one different factor in two similar
cases can result in each case being treated differently. The examples in SI 00820.200C. illustrate how specific factors of a case may result in a different conclusion. They
are not provided as generalized guidance on specific types of activities.
