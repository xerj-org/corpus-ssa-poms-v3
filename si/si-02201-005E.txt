E. Applying the rules of administrative finality
1.
Do not apply the rules of Administrative Finality to cases involving fraud.
2.
Make any revision to a prior overpayment determination only under the rules of administrative
finality. These rules permit the recovery of overpayments which occurred more than
2 years in the past as long as the determination was made timely.
3.
If the rules of administrative finality permit the determination of an overpayment
more than 24 months in the past, post this overpayment to the Supplemental Security
Record (SSR).
4.
If the SSR shows an overpayment, assume that any changes made to the system which
resulted in the overpayment being computed were properly made within the rules of
administrative finality. For more information about administrative finality, see SI 04070.001.
5.
To generate an automated overpayment notice (AONS), follow the instructions in SM
01301.365.
