B. Procedure - Question 31 – Child Support
Ask if the ineligible spouse, ineligible parent, ineligible child or eligible alien
(sponsored by the ineligible spouse or parent) whose income is subject to deeming,
makes support payments. See SI 01320.145 for developing the exclusion from deemed income of support payments made under a
court order or enforced under Title IV-D.
This information is collected on the Income Selection page (MS08114.003) for MSSICS
applications.
