SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.110 - When Home Ownership is the Living Arrangement (LA) Basis

A. Policy on home ownership and in-kind support and maintenance (ISM)
1. ISM
The Value of the One-Third Reduction (VTR) never applies when a beneficiary is in
their own household. Any ISM the beneficiary receives is subject to the Presumed Maximum
Value (PMV) rule.
2. Home ownership for beneficiary living in own household
A beneficiary is living in their own household when any form of ownership of the home belongs to:
•
the beneficiary;
•
their living-with eligible spouse; or
•
any person whose income may be deemed to them.
