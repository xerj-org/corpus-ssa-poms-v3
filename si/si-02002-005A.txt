A. Policy for regional office interim assistance reimbursement accounting
The regional office (RO) is responsible for conducting a sample review every two to
three years (depending on RO workloads) of the State's accounting records of monthly
interim assistance reimbursement (IAR) payments.
ROs may modify the review procedures outlined in this chapter to conform to State
variations and establish procedures to ensure accurate and timely IAR accounting.
