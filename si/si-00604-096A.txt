A. Policy for SNAP questions
We are responsible for asking question 58 to determine if the claimant is already
receiving or wants to enroll in SNAP (formerly known as food stamps). For a complete
discussion of our role in SNAP, see SI 01801.005 Overview of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Benefits through SI 01801.310 How to Complete Form SSA-4233, Social Security Administration Transmittal for Supplemental
Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Applications.
