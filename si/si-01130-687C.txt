SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.687 - Corporation for National and Community Service (CNCS) (Formerly ACTION) Programs
Effective Dates: 06/30/2004 - Present | TN 50 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130687 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Assumption
Assume that all payments made by CNCS under former ACTION programs are excluded from
resources.
2. Verify Program
Use documents in the individual's possession, contact with the program or agency involved
or a precedent to verify that a program is one of those listed in SI 01130.687A. or is otherwise funded by or according to agreement with the Federal Government under
a former ACTION program.
3. Accept Allegation
Accept an individual's allegation of participation in a former ACTION program and
exclude any payments from resources without further development or documentation.
