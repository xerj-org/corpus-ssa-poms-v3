B. Policy - Offering Notices in Spanish
In compliance with the case of Cruz v. Califano (U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Pennsylvania (2/79)), Spanish language
notices will be provided to those who meet certain criteria and choose to receive
Spanish notices or those who request Spanish language notices. (See NL 00801.025.)
