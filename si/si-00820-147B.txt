B. Types of service from wage verification companies
Wage verification companies may provide different types of service. Some provide SSA
with immediate online access; others may fax information to SSA within a few days.
Requirements for accessing wage verifications differ among the various companies.
Some wage verification companies may ask SSA to register with the company or have
specific requirements for a wage earner’s signed authorization to release the information.
