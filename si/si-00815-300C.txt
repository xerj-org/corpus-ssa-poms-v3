SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.300 - Credit Life or Credit Disability Insurance Payments
Effective Dates: 08/29/2024 - Present | TN 11 (08-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815300 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Example
Frank Fritz, an Supplemental Security Income recipient, purchased credit disability
insurance when Frank bought their home. Subsequently Frank was in a car accident and
became totally disabled. Because of Frank's disability, the insurance company pays
off the home mortgage. Neither the payment nor the increased equity in the home is
income to Frank.
