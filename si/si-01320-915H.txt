H. Procedure— no proof of sponsorship in Department of Homeland Security (DHS) file
Do not apply sponsor-to-noncitizen deeming if the development results in no evidence
of a sponsor in SI 01320.915G.
Document the SSI file with DHS' response showing that there is no evidence of sponsorship
in the DHS file (i.e., a copy of the OF-230, any correspondences or a Report of Contact).
