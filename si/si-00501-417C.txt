SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.417 - Blind or Disabled Children of Military Personnel Stationed Overseas - Informing Recipients of Changes
Effective Dates: 01/05/2024 - Present | TN 77 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501417 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure - Giving Appropriate Notice
1. Adverse Actions
Use the form SSA-L8155-U2, Notice of Planned Action, to inform the recipient of any
adverse changes. In addition to the more usual types of adverse actions (e.g., suspension
of benefits, benefit reduction, termination of benefits), be sure to cover the points
below as appropriate:
•
SSI payments are suspended for failure to confirm eligibility (N05).
•
The child's SSI benefits are being reduced because they no longer live in a State
which pays a supplement to SSI recipients.
•
The child's eligibility for Medicaid may be affected because they no longer live in
a State which provides coverage. For further information, contact the Department of
Social Services/Public Welfare in the State of last residence.
•
The child is not a United States citizen and, therefore, not eligible to receive SSI
while overseas.
•
The child's SSI benefits are being stopped because they are no longer living with
a parent who is a member of the armed forces, and the child is still outside the United
States.
•
The child's SSI benefits are being stopped because the parent is no longer serving
on permanent duty ashore overseas in the armed forces, and the child is still outside
the United States.
•
The child's SSI benefits are being stopped because they are no longer a “child.”
2. Other Situations Requiring Notification
a. Initial Claims Allowances
Use the SSA-8025-U2, Notice of Payment Decision, and language from NL 00804.174.
b. No Payments Are Due
Use the SSA-L8166-U2, SSI Notice, and language from NL 00804.174.
c. Only Retroactive Payments Being Made
Use the SSA-L8100-U2, SSI Notice of Revised Determination, and language from NL 00804.174.
d. Other Changes
Select stock language as appropriate from NL 00804.174 and use the applicable form below to notify recipients of other changes:
•
SSA-L8151-U2, Notice of Change;
•
SSA-L8165-U2, Notice of Decision.
e. Points Appropriate to the Case
As appropriate to the individual case, cover the points in SI 00501.417C.1. in any notice you send.
