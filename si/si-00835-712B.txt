SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.712 - Exclusion of In-Kind Support and Maintenance for Residents in Private Nonprofit Residential Care Institutions
Effective Dates: 11/08/2023 - Present | TN 126 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835712 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure for determining if the “Church Amendment” applies
Determine that the “Church Amendment” ISM exclusion applies when the case meets all three criteria:
a.
Neither the individual nor the institution (or its controlling organization) have
an express obligation to provide full support and maintenance;
b.
The institution has tax-exempt status; and
c.
The institution does not receive payment to provide support and maintenance.
1. No express obligation to provide full support and maintenance
Determine whether an individual or a representative of the institution has an express
obligation to provide support and maintenance using the following criteria.
a.
There is an express obligation to provide full support and maintenance because of
either:
•
a lump-sum payment, or
•
membership in an organization.
b.
If the contact alleges that an express obligation exists, accept the allegation and
determine that the exclusion does not apply.
c.
If the contact alleges that no such obligation exists, verify the arrangement by obtaining
a copy of an appropriate document (e.g., membership certificate, entrance agreement,
organizational constitution, bylaws, etc.).
d.
If the document establishes that an express obligation exists, the ISM exclusion does
not apply. ISM provided by the institution is countable unless otherwise excluded (e.g.,
under the SMA exclusion in SI 00830.605).
e.
If you obtain a document to verify the express obligation issue, put a copy in the
file Evidence Portal (EP) or Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) or record the pertinent
information on a Report of Contact page in the SSI Claims System.
f.
If the development establishes that there is no express obligation, the ISM exclusion
may apply. Develop for the institution's tax-exempt status.
2. Determine whether the private nonprofit institution or the controlling organization
is tax-exempt
a. If a SEANET 8045 precedent exists for the institution
If a SEANET 8045 exists:
•
Review the 8045 precedent to determine whether the institution or its controlling
organization is tax-exempt under section 501(a) of the Internal Revenue Code and is
of the kind described in sections 501(c) and 501(d) of that code. NOTE: This includes organizations covered under section 501(c)(3).
•
Document the file by recording the information pertinent to the tax-exempt status
on a Report of Contact page (SSA-5002 for non-MSSICS cases).
•
If the institution is tax-exempt, then the ISM exclusion may apply. Develop whether
the institution is paid to provide the eligible individual's support and maintenance
per SI 00835.712B.3.in this section.
Note: The 8045 precedent can be also accessed via the Facility Precedent Resource Center
(FPR) module the in National Precedent Repository.
b. If no SEANET 8045 precedent exists
If no precedent exists:
•
Request the institution to fax or send a copy of its State nonprofit certification
or its determination letter from the IRS. Verify the tax-exempt status and document
the file by recording information pertinent to the tax-exempt status on a Report of
Contact page (SSA-5002 for non-MSSICS cases). If provided, fax the certification or
IRS letter into the appropriate electronic folder.
•
If the institution or controlling organization is not tax-exempt, determine that the
ISM exclusion does not apply and count the food and shelter provided by the institution
as ISM unless it is otherwise excluded (e.g., under the SMA exclusion). For more information
on the SMA exclusion, see SI 00830.605).
•
If the institution or controlling organization is tax-exempt, then the ISM exclusion
may apply. Develop whether the institution is paid to provide the eligible individual's
support and maintenance.
c. Verifying questionable tax-exempt status
Use the following steps to verify the organization’s tax exempt if there is an indication
that the IRS revoked its tax-exempt status:
•
If evidence indicates the IRS may have revoked the organization’s tax-exempt status,
check the following IRS websites: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/revocations-of-501c3-determinations and https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/automatic-revocation-of-exemption.
•
If the donor organization appears on the site, we can conclude that the Church amendment
exclusion does not apply. Document the date the IRS revoked the tax-exempt status
on a Report of Contact page in the SSI Claims System or on an SSA-5002 for non-MSSICS
cases.
d. Documenting that an organization lost its tax-exempt status
If the organization lost its tax-exempt status, do not apply the ISM exclusion. Document
the following on a Report of Contact within the SSI Claims System or on an SSA-5002
for non-MSSICS cases:
•
The date the organization was added to the IRS “Revocations” list;
•
The source of this information; and
•
“ISM exclusion does not apply.”
NOTE: For additional information on verifying an organization’s tax-exempt status, please
see SI 00830.750E.
3. Documenting whether the Institution received payment to provide support and maintenance
Use the following steps to document whether the institution received payment to provide
support and maintenance:
a.
Contact the institution to determine if the institution provides support and maintenance
for which it does not receive payment.
b.
Document the file with the response of the institution on a Report of Contact page
within the SSI Claims System or an SSA-5002 for a non-MSSICS case.
c.
Determine that the exclusion does not apply if the institution does not provide at least some support for which it does
not receive payment.
d.
Determine that the exclusion applies if the institution provides at least some support
for which it does not receive payment and meets the two other criteria.
NOTE: If the individual or a third party makes some payment for the individual's food and
shelter, the unpaid portion provided by the institution is still excludable if the
institution meets all three requirements listed in SI 00835.712B.1. through SI 00835.712B.3. in this section.
EXAMPLE: Individual makes a partial payment
Henry , an eligible individual, resides in St. Joseph's Retirement Home, a nonprofit
residential care institution. A charitable organization operates the home and the
organization is tax-exempt, and a precedent is on file with the FO. St. Joseph's provides Henry with support and maintenance.
In return, Henry agreed to turn over all current assets and to surrender all or part
of any additional income or property Henry may receive in the future. The CS obtains
a copy of the agreement from the institution.
The CS determines that the institution provides some support and maintenance
without payment because the value of the support and maintenance exceeds what Henry
can pay. The support and maintenance provided is not due to membership in an organization
nor due to a lump-sum prepayment given to St. Joseph's to provide lifetime care. Henry,
in accordance with the written agreement, must make further payments should Henry
become financially able to do so in the future. The CS determines that St. Joseph's
does not have an express
obligation to provide support and maintenance. Therefore, because the institution meets all
three requirements, the CS determines that the support and maintenance provided by
the institution is not ISM because the “Church amendment” exclusion applies.
