SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.450 - Replacement of Income Already Received
Effective Dates: 10/28/2024 - Present | TN 15 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Example
Bob Akers, an SSI recipient, received a replacement Title II check after their regular
monthly Title II check was damaged in the mail. The replacement check is not income
to Bob. Bob is charged with income for the month the regular Title II check was paid.
