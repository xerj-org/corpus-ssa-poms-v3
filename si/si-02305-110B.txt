B. Description of RZs Completed on MSSICS Screens
Opening a REDETERMINATION with or without RECAPs event in the SSI Claims System creates
a path that will guide the interviewer to the appropriate screens to document allegations
and evidence relating to potential eligibility for Title II benefits.
