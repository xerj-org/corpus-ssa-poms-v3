D. Description of System Capabilities
See SM 01320.600. For most cases, the SSI system will compute the true OP or UP. It
will do this by using the information from the payment history (PMTH) segment (the
amount paid) and the WCMP segment (the offset amount and the amount that would have
been due had we paid the title II income on time).
