G. Procedure - Documenting Allegations
Document information as required by POMS instructions for the issues listed in SI 02305.088J.
•
SSA-8203-BK - complete the fill-in spaces on the form. If necessary, use the REMARKS
section of the form, too.
•
SSA-8006-F4/SSA-8007 - Complete an SSA-8006-F4 or SSA-8007 for RZs completed on paper
forms, if necessary per SI 00835.600.
•
SSI Claims System - Document material information on the Household Expenses and Contributions
page. See MS 08110.013 for instructions on completing the screen.
