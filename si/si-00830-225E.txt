E. Procedure - unemployment, sickness, and strike benefits
Obtain evidence of unemployment, sickness, and strike benefits from the individual's
own records, such as an award letter or actual check. If this evidence is unavailable,
contact RRB headquarters in Chicago at:
Railroad Retirement Board
844 Rush Street
Chicago, IL 60611
Local RRB offices do not maintain this information.
