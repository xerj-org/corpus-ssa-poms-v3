B. Procedure— Preparing Manual Notices
Follow the instructions below whenever you must send a manual notice.
NOTE: Follow the special notice procedures in NL 01001.000 if the notice will be sent to a claimant, beneficiary, or representative payee who
alleges being blind or visually impaired.
1. Forms to Use
Use the following forms:
a.
no change in payment amount— SSA-L8166-U2.
b.
reduction of payment amount, suspension or denial—SSA-L8155-U2.
c.
change to retroactive payments only— SSA-L8100-U2.
