A. Introduction
Questions 26(b) and (c) aid in determining whether a deeming allocation applies and
whether we should reduce the allocation by any income.
NOTE: Do not confuse questions 26(b) and (c) with questions 57(a) through (d), which cover
the student child earned income exclusion.
