SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01330.200 - Deeming of Resources
Effective Dates: 10/07/2024 - Present | TN 9 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501330200 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
If there is more than one SSI eligible child under age 18 in the household, equally
divide the value of the deemed resources among those children.
If an eligible child is later determined ineligible for any reason or is no longer
subject to deeming (e.g., after attainment of age 18), divide the value of the deemed
resources among the remaining eligible children, effective with the first month the
child is ineligible or no longer subject to deeming.
