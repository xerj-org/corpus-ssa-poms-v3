A. Introduction
This section provides resource policies that apply to an individual who is a creditor
and, therefore, owns an agreement such as a promissory note or a property agreement.
A creditor is a seller of property.
See SI 01120.220 for the procedures for developing the resource value of cash loans. See SI 00835.482 for developing loans of in-kind support and maintenance.
