SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00870.006 - Elements of a PASS
Effective Dates: 05/02/2014 - Present | TN 43 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500870006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Policy for miscellaneous items
1. Multiple PASS plans
An individual can have only one active PASS at a time, but once a plan ends, an individual
can apply for another. Before we can approve a subsequent PASS:
•
complete a final accounting for the prior PASS (see SI 00870.070D); and
•
the individual must show that they can either no longer work at or obtain work in
the prior work goal.
NOTE:
An overpayment that stems from a final accounting does not preclude the evaluation
of a subsequent PASS on its own merits. However, the repayment of an overpayment related
to one PASS is not an allowable expense for purposes of another PASS.
2. Aged individuals
Approve a PASS for an aged individual only if they received SSI benefits based on
blindness or disability (or State aid based on disability) for the month before turning
age 65.
3. Written plan
A PASS must be submitted in writing, preferably on Form SSA-545-BK (Plan to Achieve
Self-Support (PASS)), signed or attested by the individual and, if applicable, the
representative payee.
4. Funds for pursuing the PASS
Approval of a PASS permits the exclusion of countable income and resources in determining
eligibility or payment amount for SSI. An individual must have countable income or
resources to be excluded and must meet SSA’s disability or blindness criteria.
5. Third-party assistance
A PASS applicant or participant may authorize a third party to act on their behalf
in matters pertaining to the PASS by submitting to SSA a signed statement that identifies
the third party and the limits (including the length) of the authorization.
For example, the statement, “I authorize (agency or individual) to act on my behalf
in all matters pertaining to my PASS for the life of my PASS” would permit SSA to
communicate openly with the third party about all matters pertaining to the PASS.
This statement does not have to be on a particular form. For more information about
the issues of consent and access, see GN 03305.001 and GN 03340.001.
If the individual wishes to provide consent to SSA to disclose information and records
regarding the PASS to the third party, the individual should identify this third party
on the SSA-545-BK in sections G and I.
