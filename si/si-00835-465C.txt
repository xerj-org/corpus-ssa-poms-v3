C. Policy — CMV of household costs and outside ISM
When a person who is not a household member pays a vendor directly for any of the
household's costs or provides the household with food or shelter items for less than the CMV, outside ISM exists.
It is necessary to establish the CMV of the food or shelter item provided when determining
the value of outside ISM.
NOTE: The CMV of household costs is usually the vendor charge. When there is no vendor involved,
the CMV is whatever the item would sell for on the open market. (See SI 00835.020B.6. for the definition of CMV.)
