SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02006.100 - Excluding Title II Offset Cases from Underpayment Review
Effective Dates: 04/15/2024 - Present | TN 20 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502006100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Procedure
1. Excluding a Case From Review
The FO compares the UP to the potential offset amount for the months in the UP period.
If the amount at risk is below the review threshold, it ceases its review and releases
the UP as appropriate given any installment or dedicated account requirements.
2. Documenting the Exclusion
Do the exclusion computation on an SSA-5002 (“Report
of Contact”). If you can determine that there is no money at risk, or that the risk is low, without
writing down a month-by-month computation, state why you are excluding the case from
review on an SSA-5002. In either case, put the SSA-5002 in file.
