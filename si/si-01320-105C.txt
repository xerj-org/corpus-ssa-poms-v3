C. Receipts by Indian Tribes - Lands in Trust
Exclusion under section 6 of Public Law 94-114 of receipts from lands held in trust
by the Federal Government, and distributed to members of certain Indian tribes (see
SI 00830.830.). This applies to the income of sponsors of aliens only if the alien lives in the sponsor's household.
