SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.670 - Individual Development Accounts (IDAs) -- Demonstration Project
Effective Dates: 06/18/2001 - Present | TN 246 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830670 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure – effective 01-01-01
1. How to verify demonstration project IDAs
In both initial claims and redeterminations, use documents in the individual's possession
to verify that the account is a Demonstration Project IDA. If such documents are not
available, contact the Demonstration Project agency to verify that the account is
a Demonstration Project IDA.
2. Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE) input
•
On the Wages page, input the gross monthly earned income in the "Alleged Amount" or
"Verified Amount" field, as appropriate. Input any amounts deposited into a Demonstration
Project IDA in that month in the "Other Deduction Amount" field. Enter "Demonstration
Project IDA" and the applicable months in the "Other Deduction Amount Reason " field.
•
Use the Interest page to input all interest received on the account each month. Input
the total interest in the "Alleged Amount" or "Verified Amount" field and the "Other
Deduction Amount" field. Enter "Demonstration Project IDA" and the applicable months
in the "Other Deduction Amount Reason " field.
•
On the Resource Selection page, answer “yes” in the “Financial Institution Accounts”
field.
•
On the Financial Institution Account page, identify the type of account and then complete
the screen as normally. In the “Alleged Value” or “Verified Value” field, input the
balance in the account. In the “Excluded Amount” field, also input the balance in
the account. In the “Exclusion Reason” field, list “TANF IDA” or “AFIA IDA”.
3. Non-MSSICS input
•
Enter in the EN field on the SSA-1719-B, the SSI PE update screens, or the SSA-450-SI,
the individual's gross wages minus any contributions to a Demonstration Project IDA
in that month.
•
Do not input any matching funds or interest earned on the Demonstration Project IDA.
•
Enter "Demonstration Project IDA" and the applicable months in the SSR Remarks field.
4. Responding to inquiries about demonstration project IDAs
If an individual asks about the availability of Demonstration Project IDAs in their
area, or about the IDA program's rules, check the listing of grantees at ACF's website
at http://www.acf.hhs.gov/assetbuilding/states.html .
