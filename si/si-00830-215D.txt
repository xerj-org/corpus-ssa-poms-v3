D. Reduction of BL benefits
Part B BL benefits are not workers’ compensation (WC) for offset purposes. Part B
benefits may be offset as a public disability benefit.
Part C BL benefits are subject to offsets (e.g., WC) and can be reduced due to the
recovery of an overpayment.
Part C benefits may be reduced because of liens imposed by other Federal agencies
(such as the Internal Revenue Service).
