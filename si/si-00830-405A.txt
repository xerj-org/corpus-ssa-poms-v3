A. Introduction
1. Emergency Assistance Defined
Emergency Assistance is the term for a variety of programs offered by States and tribes
that provide payments to families to meet emergency needs. States and Indian tribes
have the option to tailor their EA programs to fit their needs.
2. Funding Source and Income Test
Some States' EA programs are funded with Federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families
(TANF) money while others are not, and eligibility for some State's programs may be
based on income while others are not. (See SI 00830.403 for information on TANF.)
