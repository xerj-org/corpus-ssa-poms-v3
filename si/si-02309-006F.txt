F. Recipient is outside of the country
You may discover that a recipient is out of the country. Immediately obtain the complete
foreign address and telephone number. If the reporter alleges they do not know how
to contact the recipient, initiate unable to locate procedures found in SI 02301.240. It may also be an indication of death. For death cases, see SI 02309.006G.
If the recipient is residing outside of the country and the foreign address is available,
follow procedures found in SI 02301.225.
