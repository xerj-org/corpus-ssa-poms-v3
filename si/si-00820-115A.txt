SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.115 - Wage Advances and Deferred Wages
Effective Dates: 11/20/2023 - Present | TN 89 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions
1. Wage Advances
Advances are payments by an employer to an individual for work to be done in the future.
2. Deferred Wages
Wages are considered “deferred” if they are received later than their normal payment
date. Types of wage payments which may be deferred include vacation pay, dismissal
and severance pay, back pay, bonuses, etc.
