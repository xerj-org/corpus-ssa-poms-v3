SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00602.015 - Completing the SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)) as an Abbreviated Application (ABAP)
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 8 (05-20)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500602015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. General procedure for completing the SSA-8000-BK as an ABAP
Partially complete the SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental Security Income (SSI))
only for the following situations:
1.
You discover ineligibility after partially completing an SSA-8000-BK; or
2.
You decide the SSA-8000-BK can better document the denial (e.g., deeming is involved); or
3.
An SSA-8001-BK is unavailable at the time of the interview (e.g., contact station interview).
For information on ABAP and paper application forms, see SI 00602.001C.4.
