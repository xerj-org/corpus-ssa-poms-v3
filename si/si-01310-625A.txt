SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.625 - SSA-8010-BK - Other Income Received While Receiving PIM Payments - Question 2
Effective Dates: 10/16/2024 - Present | TN 36 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310625 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. Receipt of Other Income
Ask the person making the statement whether they received any other income while receiving
the PIM payments shown in question 1.
2. Is More Information About Other Income Needed?
a.
If the answer to question 2(a) is “No,” do not complete questions 3-5. Go to question 6 (except for an ineligible child;
skip to question 17).
b.
If the answer to question 2(a) is “Yes,” and
THE PERSON MAKING THE
STATEMENT IS
THEN
The sponsor of a noncitizen or the sponsor's spouse; an essential person
Always complete questions 3, 4, and 5 about other income
A parent or the spouse of a parent; an ineligible child
Complete questions 3, 4, and 5 only for periods when no PIM payments were received or are expected to be received.
c.
When the person received no PIM payments for some months in the period being covered,
and alleges no other income, explain in the “Remarks” section. Unstated income may be an issue.
d.
If the person making the statement does not expect the PIM payments to continue, explain
in“Remarks” section and show any expected income for the next 14 months in questions 3, 4, and
5. Remind the person to report when their income actually changes.
