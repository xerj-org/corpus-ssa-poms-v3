SI 02305.014 RZs and Records Meeting Automated T32/Start Date Criteria
Procedure - After Clearing Profile E and M RZs
Automated record termination occurs if;
•
A case meets the criteria outlined in SM 01802.002A.; and
•
A RZ was initiated/completed within 8 months prior to the computation run.
If this criterion is not met, the system will set another profile E or profile M RZ,
as appropriate.
After completing the profile E or M RZ, check the SSR following the next computation
run. If the record was not selected for the automated T32/Start Date process, you
should T33/Start Date the record. This will prevent the case from being reselected
as an E or M profile RZ in a subsequent RZ merge run.
