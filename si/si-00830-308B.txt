B. Policy for the income treatment of aid and attendance and housebound allowances
Follow these guidelines:
•
VA aid and attendance and housebound allowances are not income for SSI purposes. See
SI 00815.050 for rules on medical and social services payments.
•
If the veteran pays their own spouse or deemor to provide medical or social services,
the payment is not income to the eligible spouse or deemor. See SI 01320.175 for a discussion of in-home supportive services payments.
•
If a veteran without a dependent spouse or child is covered by a Medicaid plan for
services furnished to them by a nursing facility, the maximum pension that can be
paid to or for the veteran for any month after the month of admission to such nursing
facility is $90. This reduced pension is an aid and attendance allowance in all cases,
and not income.
•
If a surviving spouse without a dependent child is covered by a Medicaid plan for
services furnished to them by a nursing facility, the maximum pension that can be
paid to or for the surviving spouse for any month after the month of admission to
such nursing facility is $90. This reduced pension is an aid and attendance allowance
in all cases, and not income.
