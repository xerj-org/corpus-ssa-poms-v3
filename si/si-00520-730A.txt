Effective Dates: 10/11/2011 - Present | TN 23 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500520730 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
There is a potential for overpayments, delays in reinstatement, and delays in adjustment
of payment for SSI claimants and recipients who are admitted to or discharged from
public institutions, or facilities where Medicaid may be paying more than 50 percent
of the cost of care. It is essential that all FOs work closely with the administration
and staff of these institutions to facilitate the flow of information regarding the
SSI population.
