SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.683 - Japanese-American and Aleutian Restitution Payments
Effective Dates: 11/17/2023 - Present | TN 111 (11-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130683 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Restitution payments made by the U.S. Government to individual Japanese-Americans
or the spouse or parent of an individual of Japanese ancestry (or, if deceased, to
their survivors) and Aleuts who were interned or relocated during World War II are
excluded from resources.
Restitution payments from the Canadian government to individual Japanese-Canadians
who were interned or relocated during World War II are excluded from resources.
Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent Japanese-American, Aleutian, and
Japanese-Canadian restitution payments is not excluded from income and resources by
this provision. For SSI benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest earned
on unspent Japanese- American, Aleutian, and Japanese-Canadian restitution payment
is excluded from income. (See SI 00830.500C.)
