SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.755 - Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund Payments
Effective Dates: 06/29/2004 - Present | TN 93 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830755 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
NOTE: FOs are not required to take extra measures to identify affected individuals. However,
FOs should consider the possibility of receipt of these payments when processing claims
for individuals with HIV.
1. Evidence
a. Documentation of Evidence
Complete the appropriate income screen (e.g., IOTH). Annotate the type of income as
“14” and show “Ricky Ray Fund” in the Explanation. Also annotate the “ALLEGED AMOUNT”
or “VERIFIED AMOUNT,”
“AMT NOT COUNTED,” and “POSTED AMOUNT.” Indicate “Ricky Ray Fund” in “REASON NOT
COUNTED.”
Document the EVID screen (or document the file) with information extracted from documents
in the individual's possession (e.g., award letter) to verify the type and amount
of the payment.
b. Obtaining Evidence
If evidence is not available from the individual:
•
For private payments, contact Metropolitan Life at 1-800-638-8787 for verification
of the type and amount of the private payment. Document the EVID screen (or document
the file) with the type and amount of the payment.
•
For payments from the Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund, contact the Ricky Ray Program
Office at 1-888-496-0338 for verification of the type and amount of the Ricky Ray
Hemophilia Relief Fund payment. Document the EVID screen (or document the file) with
the type and amount of the payment.
2. Reopening Prior Determination
If a case comes to your attention where SSA previously counted as income or resources
a payment as described in SI 00830.755B., reopen and revise the earliest determination possible under the rules of administrative
finality (SI 04070.000).
