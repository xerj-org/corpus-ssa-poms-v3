A. Introduction
Workers' compensation (WC) payments are awarded to an injured employee or their survivor(s)
under federal and state WC laws, such as the Longshoremen and Harbor Workers' Compensation
Act. The payments may be made by a federal or state agency, an insurance company,
or an employer.
