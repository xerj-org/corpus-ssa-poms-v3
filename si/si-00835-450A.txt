SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.450 - Cash Income from Within and from Outside Households
Effective Dates: 12/13/2023 - Present | TN 117 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835450 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
A claimant, who is the householder, may receive cash income as well as ISM from other
members of their household. Also, a claimant may receive cash income as well as ISM
from sources outside the household. The following section provides instructions for
distinguishing between ISM and cash income and for calculating the amount of cash
income.
