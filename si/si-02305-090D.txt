SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.090 - Developing Public Assistance Household - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 109 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305090 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure Form SSA-8203-BK-Question 8
Skip Question 8 of the SSA-8203-BK if the recipient's permanent residence is an institution or they
live alone.
Develop allegations about public assistance households, as follows:
1. If recipient alleges no change in public assistance
If the recipient alleges no change in public assistance household status and the record
does not indicate otherwise, do not develop PA household further.
If someone other than a spouse, child (as defined in SI 00501.010), or person whose income may be deemed lives with the recipient and it is not a PA
household:
•
Develop LA and In-Kind Support and Maintenance (ISM) (see SI 00835.100 - SI 00835.340 and SI 00835.450B.);
•
Complete the SSI Claims system screens, Part II of a SSA-8006 for living arrangement
prior to September 30, 2024, or Part II of a SSA-8007 for living arrangement beginning
on or after September 30, 2024, as appropriate, see SI 00835.600.
EXCEPTIONS:
For a list of situations when inside ISM development is not required, such as the
recipient lives in a public assistance household, see SI 00835.340B.2.b.
For a list of situations when further documentation of the evidence for inside ISM
is not required, such as when the alleged value (AV) of the ISM is greater than the
presumed maximum valued (PMV), see SI 00835.340D.2.
2. If recipient alleges a change in public assistance
If the recipient alleges a change in public assistance household status or no change
and the information conflicts, develop PA household as necessary per SI 00835.130.
