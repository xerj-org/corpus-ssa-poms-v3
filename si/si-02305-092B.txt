B. Description of RZs Completed in the SSI Claims System
Opening a “REDETERMINATION” event in the SSI Claims System creates a “path” which guides the interviewer to the appropriate screens to document allegations and
evidence relating to ISM and cash contributions from outside the household, as necessary.
