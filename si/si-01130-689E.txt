E. Procedure for verifying tax-exempt status of donor organizations
A gift from a tax-exempt organization requires proof of the organizations current
tax-exempt status from the IRS.
1. Precedent exists for donor organization
•
Determine if the donor is an IRS approved tax-exempt organization by entering the
name of the organization and searching the
State and Local Income Precedent and Resource Center
(SLR).
•
If a precedent exists, and the provisions of this section apply to the gift, create
a Report of Contact page or SSA-5002 (Report of Contact) as applicable, noting SLR
precedent as proof of the organizations tax-exempt status and lock it.
2. To create a precedent
If no precedent exists, contact the donor organization and request that they fax or
send a copy of its State non-profit certification or its determination letter from
the IRS.
When evidence is in hand:
•
Verify the non-profit status and dates status was granted or changed,
•
Annotate the file documentation notes or a Report of Contact with the date of non-profit
status,
•
Store the evidence electronically using the Evidence Portal, including the State non-profit
certification or determination letter from the IRS, and,
•
Go to the SLR intranet site and follow the steps listed there to create a national
precedent. To create the precedent, categorize the contact type as non-profit organization
and the precedent type as IRS tax-exempt certification.
3. Verify the organizations tax-exempt status if it is questionable
a. Confirm tax-exempt status with the IRS
1.
If evidence indicates the organizations tax-exempt status may have been revoked, go
to the IRS web site and review both of the current lists of organizations from which
the IRS has recently revoked tax-exempt status at: Tax Exempt Organization Search
Internal Revenue Service.
2.
If the donor organization is listed on the IRS Revocations listing, note the date
the tax exempt status was revoked on a Report of Contact page and take the steps described
in SI 01130.689E.3.b. this section.
b. Organization has lost its tax-exempt status
1.
Do not apply the resource exclusion described in this section to the gift. Count the
gift as unearned income in the month of receipt and as a resource in the following
month. For income policy and procedures regarding gifts from any organization that
is not tax-exempt, see SI 00830.520.
2.
Document on a Report of Contact page or SSA-5002 (Report of Contact), as applicable:
•
The date the organization was added to the IRS Revocations list;
•
The source of this information; and
•
That the gifts to children with life-threatening conditions resource exclusion does
not apply because the donor organization is not tax-exempt, per IRS records, and lock
the Report of Contact page.
