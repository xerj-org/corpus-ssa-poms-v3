A. Introduction
These questions satisfy Medicaid eligibility requirements where the SSA-8000-BK (Application
for Supplemental Security Income) is an application for Medicaid. The claimant must
agree to assign their rights to third party payments for services covered under the
State Medicaid plan and provide third party information.
