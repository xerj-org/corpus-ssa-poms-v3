SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02310.015 - Savings Security Instrument Match (SB Diary)
Effective Dates: 09/18/2026 - Present | TN 76 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502310015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Description of match
This match detects ownership of savings security instruments (including U.S. Saving
Bonds) by SSI recipients and deemors. This change in terminology is consistent with
that now used by the Bureau of the Fiscal Service (BFS). Savings securities can be
either definitive records (paper bonds) or book entry (electronic records).
1. Comparison
The match compares SSI records in current pay and force due status with a payment
greater than zero against BFS's savings security registration file. The match identifies
currently owned series E and EE instruments issued after December 1973 and series
I instruments which were first issued in September 1998.
2. Frequency of Match
For several years, SSA conducted a yearly computer match with BFS. The match was conducted
in August of each year with alerts posted to the supplemental security record (SSR)
in September and set on the Post Entitlement Operational Data Store (PE ODS) system
in October.
Beginning in 1998, a second match was conducted in February of each year, with alerts
posted to the SSR in March and set on PE ODS in April. Having the savings security
match conducted every 6 months corresponds to BFS's schedule of updating the redemption
value for savings security instruments (May through October and November through April)
and provides SSA with more current data.
3. Generation of Alerts
The system generates an SB alert, controlled by PE ODS, for records in which the total
redemption amount for all individuals on the record (eligibles and ineligibles) equals
or exceeds $1,000 and was not alerted in the prior two matches.
