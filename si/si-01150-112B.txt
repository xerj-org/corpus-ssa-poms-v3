SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.112 - Initial Claims with Period of Ineligibility and Disability Determination Needed
Effective Dates: 01/25/2010 - Present | TN 43 (12-24)

B. Procedure—Determining whether to send case to the DDS
Use this procedure for an initial claim that requires a medical decision from the
DDS.
1. Determine whether undue hardship exists within 2 months of filing month
Follow the instructions in SI 01150.126 to determine whether the individual meets the requirements to receive SSI based on
undue hardship for the month of filing or the following 2 months.
a.
Send the case to the DDS if the individual meets or is likely to meet the undue hardship
requirements for any month of this 3-month period.
b.
Do not send the case to the DDS if the individual will not meet the undue hardship
requirements for any month of the 3-month period.
NOTE: The determination of whether the individual is likely to meet the undue hardship
requirements in the 3-month period is based on the claims representative's analysis
of the facts in the specific case. These procedures are based on an understanding
that an SSI applicant's financial situation (undue hardship) is subject to change
during the life of the application.
2. Period of ineligibility ends within 2 months of filing month
Send the case to the DDS for a medical decision if the individual's period of ineligibility
will end in the month of filing or in the following 2 months. This rule applies whether
the individual appears to meet the undue hardship requirement or not.
Example: Smith files for SSI on 5/15/00 and it is determined that their resource transfer
period of ineligibility will end in 7/00. Since the period of ineligibility ends within
2 months of the filing month, the field office sends the case to the DDS for a medical
determination.
