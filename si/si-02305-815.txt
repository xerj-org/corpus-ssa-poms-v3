This is a very important letter about keeping your Supplemental Security Income (SSI)

You Have An Appointment
Although you don't get SSI now because of your earnings, we keep your SSI case active.
This means that if your earnings go down, we can restart your SSI faster. And if you
have Medicaid, you can usually keep it while you work.
To keep your case active, we must review it every year. We need information from you
to make sure you still meet the SSI rules.
