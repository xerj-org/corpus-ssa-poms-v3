SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01330.160 - Deeming - Countable Resources
Effective Dates: 11/01/1995 - Present | TN 6 (08-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501330160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Total countable resources are the combination of the resources of the eligible individual
and the ineligible spouse after all applicable resource exclusions are applied.
Total countable resources are compared with the resource limitation for a couple.
If the amount of the resources does not exceed the limit, the applicant /recipient
meets the resource eligibility requirement. If countable resources exceed the limit,
the applicant/recipient is ineligible.
