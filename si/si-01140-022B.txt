SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01140.022 - High/Low Development Option for Liquid Resources
Effective Dates: 09/27/1990 - Present | TN 3 (07-90)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501140022 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Operating policy
1. More Than One Countable Liquid Resource
When an individual has more than one countable liquid resource, you may use:
•
the highest verified value for any or all of them if doing so permits eligibility for all months in the period of review; or
•
the lowest verified value for any or all of them if doing so results in ineligibility for all months in the period of review.
2. Single Countable Liquid Resource
If there is only one countable liquid resource, you may use the high /low option to establish eligibility
or ineligibility for any month within the period of review. Determine resources for
other months within the period of review in the usual manner.
