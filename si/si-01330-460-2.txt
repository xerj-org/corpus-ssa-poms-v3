2. Limit With No Spouse
For a qualified individual with no spouse, the qualified individual's countable resources
are compared with the resource limit for an individual. If the resources do not exceed
the limit, the qualified individual meets the resource eligibility requirement. (If
countable resources exceed the limit, see SI 01150.200ff.)
