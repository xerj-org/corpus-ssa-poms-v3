SI 01210.450 Special Blind Income Provisions - Effect of Termination of Marital Relationship
Policy
If a marital relationship ends between individuals who both meet the requirements
of SI 01210.100, the members of the couple retain their right to use the alternative income counting
provision as individuals.
See SI 00501.150 C.2.b. for events that terminate a marital relationship.
