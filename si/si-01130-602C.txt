C. Procedure - When to Develop the Exclusion
Develop the exclusion only if it would affect eligibility for regular or conditional
SSI benefits, in that order.
REMINDER: Send a notice to the affected beneficiary/new payee explaining the restitution payment
and the 9-month exclusion from resources (See NL 00703.213 - Repayment of Misused Funds – Notice to Beneficiary/New Payee.)
NOTE: If the individual is blind or visually impaired, see instructions at NL 01001.010 for more information on the special blind or visually impaired notice options.
