SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.600 - Retroactive Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and Retirement, Survivors and Disability (RSDI) Payments
Effective Dates: 12/02/2020 - Present | TN 127 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130600 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions
1. Retroactive SSI benefits
Retroactive SSI benefits — which include any federally administered State supplementation — are SSI benefits
issued in any month after the calendar month for which they are paid. Thus, benefits for January that are issued in February are retroactive.
2. Retroactive RSDI benefits
Retroactive RSDI benefits are those issued in any month that is more than a month after the calendar month for which they are paid. Thus, RSDI benefits for January
that are issued in February are not retroactive, but RSDI benefits for January that
are issued in March are retroactive.
