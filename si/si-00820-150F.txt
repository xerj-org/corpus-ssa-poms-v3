F. Procedure for when the wage earner anticipates a decrease in their wages
Before decreasing a previously developed wage estimate, consider whether available
evidence supports the current allegation that a wage earner’s wages decreased. If
evidence to the contrary exists, contact the employer to verify the anticipated decrease.
Document your determination on a Report of Contact and lock it, or on an SSA-5002
in non-SSI Claims system cases.
Example of a wage earner anticipating a decrease in wages
You complete a redetermination and obtain primary wage evidence for the previous 24-month
review period of the disabled child’s parent. The parent grossed $600 biweekly, except
December to March when the parent worked overtime and grossed $1000 biweekly. The
child’s payee (the other parent) tells you they expects their spouse to work less
overtime this winter and wants you to post a $600 biweekly gross estimate for the
working parent year round. As evidence to the contrary exists, you contact the employer
and verify that they do not want the employee to work overtime this winter before
reducing the wage estimate.
NOTE: Document your conversations with the wage reporter and the employer as well as show
how you made your determination on separate Reports of Contact and lock them, or on
an SSA-5002 in non-SSI Claims system cases.
