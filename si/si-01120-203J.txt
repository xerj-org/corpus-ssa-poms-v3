J. Procedure to verify nonprofit associations when evaluating pooled trusts
When a trust is alleged to be established through the actions of a nonprofit or a
tax-exempt organization, consult the pooled trust precedent in SSITMS. If none exists, follow policy and procedure for verifying the tax-exempt status
of organizations found at SI 01130.689E. “Gifts to children with life-threatening conditions.”
