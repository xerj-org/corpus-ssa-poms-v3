D. Teleclaim
1. Pen-and-ink signature
If we take an SSA-8000-BK (Application for Supplemental Security Income) during a
telephone interview and the proper applicant or claimant prefer to sign with pen-and-ink,
mail the application to the claimant for signature. Refer to GN
00201.015C.
