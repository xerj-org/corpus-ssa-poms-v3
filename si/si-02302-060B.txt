SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.060 - Quarterly Verification of Earnings
Effective Dates: 12/22/2009 - Present | TN 14 (09-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Recipient Contact
a.
Since the recipients are usually working, do not attempt face-to-face contact in the
FO unless required by other POMS instructions or other methods of obtaining earnings
information are not successful.
b.
Coordinate earnings verification, redetermination, and CDR contacts to the extent
possible.
c.
Request wage information for each month not previously verified prior to the current
month.
d.
Request an estimate of earnings for the current month and the following months up
to the next scheduled verification of earnings.
e.
If the recipient does not respond to SSA's requests for the necessary information,
see SI 02301.235.
2. Earnings Verification
a.
Verify earnings per SI 00820.135 ff. Be extremely sensitive to any indications that the employer will object to verification
on a quarterly basis (e.g., the employer has been uncooperative in the past, the recipient
indicates the employer will object, etc.).
b.
If the FO has indications that there will be problems with the employer, obtain documentation
solely from the recipient.
c.
If the recipient cannot provide verification of all earnings (e.g., acceptable payslips),
estimate the amount from whatever documentation the recipient can provide and input
that amount as unverified.
d.
If quarterly verification cannot be secured, record attempts to obtain the information
on an SSA-5002.
e.
When the information becomes available, or at the time of a redetermination, whichever
is earlier, verify any unverified earnings per SI 00820.130 through the month prior to the recipient contact or redetermination.
3. Changes to Exclusions
When reviewing verified earnings, determine whether any changes have occurred regarding
any earned income exclusions that might apply such as IRWE, PASS, or BWE. Document
the excluded amounts per
SI 00820.500 ff. (IRWE and BWE) or SI 00870.001 ff. (PASS).
4. Earnings Estimates
In estimating future earnings, the amounts earned in the past few months are often
the best guide. However, consider any indication given by the recipient that a change
in earnings is anticipated. (See SI 00820.150 for additional information on estimating future wages.)
5. Systems Input
Transmit the new earnings estimate to the system. See SM 01305.101 ff. for instructions
on systems input.
