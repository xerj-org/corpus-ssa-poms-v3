SI 01320.120 Deeming - Foster Care Payments for an Ineligible Child - Policy Principle
Authority: Regulations 20 CFR 416.1161
(a)(5), (b), (c), (d)
Any payments received for providing foster care to an ineligible child do not represent
income for deeming purposes, nor do the payments represent income to an ineligible
child for purposes of determining the allocation. Any foster care payments received
by an essential person or a sponsor of an alien are subject to deeming.
See SI 00830.410 for definitions of foster care and foster care payment and for the policy on foster
care payments for an eligible child.
