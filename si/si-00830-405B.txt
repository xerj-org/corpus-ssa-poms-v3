B. Policy
Payments are not income when they are paid from an EA program whose primary purpose
is to provide social or medical services (SI 00815.050). All other EA payments are unearned income.
EA payments that are unearned income may be income based on need (IBON, see SI 00830.170), assistance based on need (ABON, see SI 00830.175), or other unearned income.
