A. Introduction
Title XVI of the Social Security Act specifies who is eligible to receive Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) benefits, the amount of cash payments, and the conditions under
which payments can be made. A person who applies for SSI and meets the conditions
in the law is eligible for benefits. The material in this subchapter is an introduction
to the SSI eligibility requirements. Subsequent sections explain these requirements
in detail.
