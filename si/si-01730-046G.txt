G. Procedure — Developing and Documenting
If an individual alleges a transfer of assets or the existence of a trust which does
not affect SSI eligibility but might affect Medicaid eligibility, it must be documented.
Obtain an allegation of a transfer on a signed SSA-795 whenever possible (and on a
Report of Contact when the individual will not sign). Copy any trust information provided.
Follow the guidelines in SI 01150.110 and regional and local procedures.
