SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02302.045 - The Threshold Test for Section 1619(b) Eligibility
Effective Dates: 03/29/2006 - Present | TN 34 (07-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502302045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process - Thresholds
1. Charted Thresholds
a.
Threshold amounts for each State are shown in the charts in SI 02302.200. These thresholds are used for most situations but, where special needs exist an
individualized calculation is made. (See 2., below.)
b.
The charted threshold is calculated for each State by adding:
•
the annualized gross earned income amount which would reduce SSI cash benefits to
zero for an individual with no other income (taking into account the State's supplementary
payment level, if any); and
•
the State's average annual per capita Medicaid expenditures.
c.
If the individual's gross earned income for the 12-month period being determined is
equal to or less than the threshold amount shown on the chart, they meet this requirement.
2. Individualized Threshold
If the individual is not eligible using the charted threshold amount, the FO will
determine whether the individual would be eligible under an individualized threshold
calculation per SI 02302.050.
