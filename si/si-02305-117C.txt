SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.117 - Informing Recipients of their Reporting Responsibilities - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 107 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305117 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
1. Face-To-Face Interviews
Review recipient reporting responsibilities and provide the recipient with a tear-off
sheet at the end of a face-to-face interview, as follows:
•
When using paper forms detach the tear-off sheet and print the recipient's name/SSN
and the date on the tear-off sheet. The SSI Claims system prints this information
on the first page of the reporting responsibilities pages of the RZ printout.
•
Review the reporting responsibilities. See instructions in SI 02301.005D.
•
When using paper forms print the FO number (or “800” number) and the FO address on the tear-off sheet. The SSI Claims system prints this
information on the first page of the reporting responsibilities pages of the RZ printout.
•
Explain how the recipient may report changes.
•
Give the recipient the tear-off sheet from the paper forms or the SSI Claims system
reporting responsibilities pages of the RZ printout.
2. Telephone Interviews
Review recipient reporting responsibilities and provide the recipient with a tear-off
sheet after a telephone interview, as follows:
•
When using paper forms detach the tear-off sheet and print the recipient's name/SSN
and the date on the tear-off sheet. The SSI Claims system prints this information
on the first page of the reporting responsibilities pages of the RZ printout.
•
Review the reporting responsibilities. See instructions in SI 02301.005D.
•
When using paper forms print the FO number (or “800” number) and the FO address on the tear-off sheet. The SSI Claims system prints this
information on the first page of the reporting responsibilities pages of the RZ printout.
•
Explain how the recipient may report changes.
•
Mail the tear-off sheet with the SSI Claims system RZ printout or SSA-8203-BK when
they are sent to recipients for signature. Include a brief covering note explaining
what the tear-off sheet is.
EXAMPLE: You are responsible for reporting changes in your circumstances that may affect the
amount of your SSI payment. The enclosed form lists the things you have to report.
If any of these things change, call or write your Social Security office. The address
and phone number are written on the form.
