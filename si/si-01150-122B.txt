B. Policy — Residing in the transferor's home
For the purpose of determining whether the individual qualifies for the transfer of
a home exception, the home must have been transferred to a person who resided in the
transferor's home. A person resides in the transferor's home if it is that person's
primary place of residence. We follow the same criteria used to determine an eligible
individual's place of residence when determining the FLA and ISM, SI 00835.020.
