C. Anderson case — process
Individuals who see an Anderson poster or who received a July 1990 Anderson notice
issued to former augmented protected pension recipients may inquire about relief.
Members of SSI eligible couples who received an April 1990 Anderson notice may also
inquire about relief. FO's will review the case of any individual requesting a review
before the time limit expires (D.8. below).
NOTE: Many July 1990 notice recipients are not class members because they never applied
for or received SSI benefits.
