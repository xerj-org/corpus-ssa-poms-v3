C. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 26
Question 14 on the SSA-8203-BK Please answer the following questions:
a.
Are you age 62 or older?
b.
If you are age 50 or older, are you a widow (er)?
c.
If you are age 50 or older and divorced, is your divorced spouse deceased?
d.
If you were disabled before age 22, do you have a parent who is age 62 or older or
disabled, or deceased?
