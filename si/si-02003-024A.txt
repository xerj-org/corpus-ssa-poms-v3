A. Background on eIAR proration cases
SSA no longer prorates SSI benefits for the first month of SSI eligibility on initial
claims. We do prorate SSI benefits following certain suspensions, which can occur
while an initial claim is pending or when we do a PE reinstatement following a period
of suspension. For a list of the suspensions that cause prorated SSI benefits, see
SI 02005.008, Proration after Period of Ineligibility.
