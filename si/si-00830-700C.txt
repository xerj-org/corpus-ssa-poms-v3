C. Procedure for home produce for personal consumption
1. Assumption about use
Assume that any home produce which an individual alleges will be used for personal
or household consumption will be so used.
2. Amount of home produce traded or sold is small
If the produce is basically raised for home consumption rather than as a business
and the amount of produce traded or sold is small (e.g., extra eggs, home-canned beans,
etc.), assume that the production costs equaled the value of what was received. No
income is derived from such a trade or sale.
3. Accept allegation
Accept an individual's allegations concerning the raising, catching, and consuming
of home produce unless you have reason to question the allegation.
4. Documentation
If you apply an assumption from SI 00830.700C.1 or SI 00830.700C.2 in this section, document the allegation only. No further development or documentation
is needed.
