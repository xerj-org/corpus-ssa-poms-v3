B. When and how to complete question 26(c)
Complete question 26(c) when a claimant listed in 26(a) receives income and meets
the definition of an ineligible child for deeming purposes.
Follow developmental instructions in SI 01320.185 if any ineligible child listed in 26(c) receives earned income.
