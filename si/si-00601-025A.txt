A. Policy - oral inquiry
An oral inquiry is when a potential claimant or third party asks about the potential
claimant's eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payments. When an oral
inquiry is received, it must be documented to establish a protective filing. If a
protective filing already exists and the closeout period has not expired, then no
further action is needed.
We will use the date of an oral inquiry about SSI payments as the filing date of an
application for SSI payments only if:
•
Use of that date will result in SSI eligibility,
•
The potential claimant is alive at the time the inquiry is made, and
•
A proper applicant files a valid application within 60 days from the date of the protective
filing closeout notification.
