SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.685 - Food Programs with Federal Involvement
Effective Dates: 03/14/2000 - Present | TN 39 (03-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130685 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. Assumption
Assume that any of the following have Federal involvement and are excludable under
other Federal statutes:
•
Meals and milk provided free or at reduced rates to children in schools and service
facilities (e.g., day care centers, settlement houses, or recreation centers);
•
U.S.D.A. food commodities distributed by any program (private or governmental);
•
Food stamps, and cash which has been verified as the value of the food stamps provided
in lieu of the stamps;
•
WIC distributions to pregnant women, infants, and children.
2. Assumption Applies--Verification
Accept an individual's allegation of receipt of anything to which the assumption in
SI 01130.685B.1. applies unless you have reason to question it.
Use a precedent, documents in the individual's possession, or contact with the provider
to verify the substitution of cash for food stamps.
Exclude the item(s) from resources without further development or documentation.
3. Assumption Does Not Apply--Verification
Use documents in the individual's possession, contact with the provider, etc., to
determine the nature of the program. (See SI 00830.165.)
