SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.095 - Developing Earned Income Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 10/02/2026 - Present | TN 108 (10-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305095 (fetched 2026-10-04)

H. Procedure disabled or blind recipient
If the recipient is disabled and has earned income, develop IRWE per SI 02305.097. If the recipient is blind and has earned income, develop blind work expenses per
SI 00820.535.
Discuss the possibility of establishing a Plan for Achieving Self Support per SI 00870.000. Information on the PASS can also be found on SSA's official web site at https://www.ssa.gov/disabilityresearch/wi/pass.htmSSA
