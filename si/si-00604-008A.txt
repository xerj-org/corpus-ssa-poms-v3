SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.008 - Filing Date Block
Effective Dates: 09/18/2026 - Present | TN 83 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604008 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The filing date will be either the date the application is received or the protective
filing date, whichever is earlier. For determining the filing date see, GN 00204.007. When an individual is filing on the basis of age, if the application date is before
attainment of age 65, the effective filing date is the date the claimant becomes 65,
see GN 00201.005C.2.
