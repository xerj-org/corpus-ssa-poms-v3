Effective Dates: 12/28/2023 - Present | TN 61 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604075 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to complete question 41
Complete 41(a) for all claimants. When the claimant answers (a) "YES," complete (b).
Otherwise, go to question 42.
1. Procedure for reporting property in question 41
a.
Report all property whether it is excluded or countable.
b.
In addition to the current residence, there may be residential property, a mobile
home that they own, or are paying for at the time of application. Record the information
needed to decide the issue of principal place of residence pertinent to the home exclusion
in these cases.
2. Encumbered property
If the claimant has a loan on any of the items they report, record the name and address
(telephone number, if available) of the creditors in 41(b).
