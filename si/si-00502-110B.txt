SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.110 - General Alien Development
Effective Dates: 12/05/2023 - Present | TN 73 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
Use the following procedure to verify the alien's immigration status:
1.
Ask the alien claimant/recipient for their current immigration status with DHS.
NOTE: If a LPR has been in qualified alien status for less than seven years and might be
eligible based on prior status as a refugee, asylee, an alien whose deportation or
removal has been withheld, or a Cuban/Haitian entrant per SI 00502.108B, also ask about the prior status.
NOTE: For information about the 1998 "Grandfathering" legislation that affected SSI eligibility
for nonqualified aliens who were receiving SSI 00502.153.
NOTE: American Indians born in Canada and members of federally-recognized Indian tribes
who were born outside the U.S. do not have to be "qualified aliens." For documentation
requirements for American Indians, see SI 00502.105.
2.
Scan legible copies of both sides of the immigration document into the electronic
folder (e.g., Evidence Portal, eView). If the document includes an Alien Registration
Number (ARN or A#), enter the ARN in Person Information. Return the immigration document
to the claimant.
If the individual indicates they do not have a DHS-issued document, provide the SAVE
fact sheet, "SAVE Records: Fast Facts for Benefit Applicants," which provides information on contacting DHS to update, correct, or replace immigration
documents.
If the individual does not ultimately provide the required DHS evidence, follow usual
closeout procedures in SI 00601.110 to deny the claim (N18).Verify the immigration status via an online SAVE request.
Access SAVE via the I-MAIN website. See GN 00303.630 for submitting and interpreting
a SAVE response.
3.
Verify the immigration status via an online SAVE request. Access SAVE via the I-MAIN
website. See GN 00303.630 for submitting and interpreting a SAVE response. After receiving
the SAVE response, update the status code on the record as appropriate and store a
copy of the SAVE response in the electronic folder. Once the immigration status has
been verified via SAVE, continue developing other eligibility requirements, as appropriate.
a.
If SAVE confirms a potentially SSI-eligible alien status, proceed with alien eligibility
development, including development of other alien eligibility factors in SI 00502.100A.3, (e.g., 40 qualifying quarters, veteran or military status, or spouse, widow(er)
or child relationship to a veteran or military service member, or blind/disabled and
lawfully residing in the U.S. on 8/22/96).
i.
If the qualified alien meets one of the other eligibility factors in SI 00502.100A.3, follow notice procedure in SI 00502.410A. Establish an AT diary to reverify immigration status if needed based on the granted
immigration status (e.g., parolee or conditional LPR).
ii.
If the qualified alien does not meet one of the other eligibility factors in SI 00502.100A.3, deny the claim (N13). Follow notice procedure in SI 00502.410B.
b.
If SAVE indicates the individual does not have lawful immigration status or the individual
has a lawful immigration status but is not an SSI-eligible alien, the claimant is
not eligible for SSI benefits.
c.
If SAVE returns a message to "Institute Additional Verification" or "Resubmit Doc
(Need Copy Original)," follow the appropriate next steps in the SAVE process, as outlined
in GN 00303.630.
4.
Annotate the SAVE verification results in the Report of Contact (DROC) page and adjudicate
the claim as appropriate in the Consolidated Claims Experience (CCE).
5.
A pending decision (e.g., asylum application, adjustment of status pending) does not
establish eligibility unless SAVE confirms a current qualified alien status. If an
alien attains eligible status while the claim is pending, find SSI eligibility as
of the first day in which all factors of eligibility (including citizenship/eligible
alien status) are met. Apply the application effective date rules in SI 00601.009 to initial claims filed 8/22/96 or later where the individual attains eligible alien
status or naturalizes while the application is pending.
6.
If the individual has a sponsor or if sponsor deeming rules apply, refer to SI 00502.240 for procedures on verifying affidavits of support and sponsor information.
