B. Procedure
In determining the CMV of outside ISM, deduct the amount still owed from the bill
or statement. Count towards the CMV only the amount actually paid to the vendor or
provided to the household.
In determining household operating expenses for inside ISM, do not include amounts
the household still owes. Count only the actual cash expenditures of the household
toward the food and shelter items.
