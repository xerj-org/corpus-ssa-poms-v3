3. State Funding
a.
Each month, the State is required to transfer funds to SSA to cover its estimated
monthly liability. Electronic transfer of funds is the preferred method of funds transfer
(see SI 02901.510 B).
b.
Transfer of funds are required to be made timely to ensure that monies are deposited
to SSA on the fifth business day after payments are to be made to the recipients.
States subject to the provisions of the Cash Management Improvement Act of 1990, will
be charged an interest penalty for late payments.
