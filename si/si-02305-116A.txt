A. Introduction
Spaces for the following signatures are provided on the SSI Claims system RZ printout
Form SSA-8203-BK.
•
Recipient and eligible spouse;
•
Witnesses; and
•
Representative Payee.
NOTE: Most RZs processed through SSI Claims system will use alternative signature methods,
see SI 02305.003. The signature block may be crossed out if the RZ form was provided to the individual.
