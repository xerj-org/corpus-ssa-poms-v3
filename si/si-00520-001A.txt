A. Introduction to residence in an institution
This subchapter defines terms and provides the policies and procedures for determining
the effect of institutionalization on Supplemental Security Income (SSI) eligibility
and payment.
1. Residence in an institution
Residence in an institution (as defined for SSI purposes) can affect an SSI applicant's
or recipient's eligibility, payment amount, or both. For example:
•
Residents of public institutions generally are ineligible for SSI; and
•
Residents of medical treatment facilities (public or private) may be eligible when
Medicaid is paying more than 50% of the cost but are limited to a maximum Federal
payment of $30 a month.

3. Non-institutional Care Situations
If an individual is not a resident of an institution, the individual may be living
in a non-institutional care situation, which would require us to calculate any ISM
the individual receives. For more information, see SI
00835.790.
