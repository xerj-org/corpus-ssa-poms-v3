SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.122 - RZs Involving Essential Person(s)
Effective Dates: 06/02/2026 - Present | TN 99 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305122 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1. General
Determine continuing EP status. If the recipient receives an EP increment and the
EP:
•
Continues to meet EP criteria outlined in SI 00501.100C., document continuing EP status on an SSA-8010-BK, or the REMARKS section of the SSA-8203-BK,
or on a signed SSA-795.
•
Loses EP status per one of the conditions outlined in SI 00501.110, document the reason the EP status was lost and the date it was lost on an SSA-8010-BK,
or the REMARKS section of the SSA-8203-BK or on a signed SSA-795.
2. EP Dies During Period of Review
If the EP dies during the period of review:
•
Secure proof of death;
•
Document the date of death and any changes in countable income and resources, including
the amount deemed to the recipient, on a SSA-795, SSA-8010-BK, or the REMARKS section
of the SSA-8203-BK.
NOTE: Document the EP's income and resources, from the first day of the period of review,
through the month of death. The EP's income and resources are deemed to the recipient
through the month of death per SI 01320.800C.
•
Develop for rep payee, if appropriate per GN 00502.001.
IMPORTANT: If you do not transmit the RZ initiation date with the death input, the system will:
•
Generate a D6 alert; and
•
Delete the case from Post Entitlement Operational Data Store (PE ODS); and
•
Reselect the case as a profile D RZ during the next merge.
