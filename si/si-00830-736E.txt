E. How to document the income exclusion
1. Documenting payments in the SSI claim system
To post payments in the SSI claim system:
•
Select “Yes” for “Other Income” as the appropriate income type on the Income Selection
page;
•
Document all income information on the Other Income (IOTH) page in the SSI claims
system; then
•
Select “settlements or awards” as the income type from the ‘TYPE” drop-down list;
•
Enter payment amount and date information by completing the income monthly values
table.
For more information on how to complete the Other Income (IOTH) page, see MS 08114.064.
2. Non-SSI claim systems documentation
On the SSA-1719B (Supplemental Security Income Post-Entitlement Input) or SSA-450-SI
(SSI Data Input and Determination) enter:
•
“SG” for “settlement or awards” as the unearned income type; and
•
the date and amount of the payment and any other appropriate fields.
For a discussion on how to code the UM claim identification field for Initial Claims,
see SM 01005.181 General Input Requirements – UM. For Post Eligibility Claims, see
SM 01305.200.
