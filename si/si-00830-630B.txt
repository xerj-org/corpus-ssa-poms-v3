B. Policy
1. Exclusion
The value of any assistance paid with respect to a dwelling unit is excluded from
income and resources if paid under:
•
the United States Housing Act of 1937 (section 1437 et seq. of 42 U.S.C.)
•
the National Housing Act (section 1701 et seq. of 12 U.S.C.)
•
section 101 of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1965 (section 1701s of 12
U.S.C., section 1451 of 42 U.S.C.);
•
title V of the Housing Act of 1949 (section 1471 et seq. of 42 U.S.C.); or
•
section 202(h) of the Housing Act of 1959.
