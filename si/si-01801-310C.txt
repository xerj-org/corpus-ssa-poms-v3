Effective Dates: 08/23/2017 - Present | TN 19 (08-17)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501801310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. How to prepare and send the SSA-4233 transmittal form
Follow regional or local office procedures to route completed SNAP application packages
or any subsequently submitted SNAP-related evidence. If no specific local procedures
exist:
•
place the documents in the following order: SSA-4233 on top, supporting evidence or
documents next, and, if applicable, the SNAP application; and
•
fax the SNAP application package or evidence to the local SNAP office.
IMPORTANT: Send the completed SSA-4233 transmittal form, along with the supporting documentation
or SNAP application, to the SNAP office within one federal workday after you receive
a signed application.
