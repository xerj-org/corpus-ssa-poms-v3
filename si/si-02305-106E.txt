E. Procedure - Burial Spaces
Develop allegations of burial spaces, as follows:
1.
If recipients allege no burial spaces and other information in SSA
records agrees, do not develop burial spaces any further.
2.
If recipients allege no burial spaces and other information in SSA
records conflicts, did the recipient dispose of a resource which we have on record?
a.
If so, develop as appropriate for an increase/decrease in the value of resources per SI 01110.600. If it appears the resource was disposed of for less than the fair market value,
see SI 01150.110.
b.
If not, develop as in SI 02305.106F.3.
3.
If recipients allege burial spaces, develop, as necessary, per SI 01130.400.
