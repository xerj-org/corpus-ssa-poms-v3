SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00601.011 - Filing Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Applications for Disabled Youth Transitioning out of Foster Care
Effective Dates: 10/04/2010 - Present | TN 72 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background for SSI applications from disabled youth transitioning out of foster care
Disabled youth receiving Title IV-E federal foster care benefits usually cannot become
eligible for SSI until foster care payments have stopped. Eligibility for foster care
payments in most States ends at age 18 attainment. These youth need the income support
and health services that result from SSI eligibility to ease the transition to independent
living. To help with this transition, SSA may accept an SSI application from a youth
up to 180 days before their foster care eligibility will end. This is an exception
to the general rule of accepting an SSI application in the month before the month
of eligibility, as described in GN
00201.005C.2.
NOTE: If the foster care payments do not preclude SSI eligibility, the FO should follow
normal claims taking procedures.
