B. Policy for documenting prior marriages
Filing for other benefits is an eligibility requirement for SSI. We collect information
about prior marriages to identify potential eligibility to Title II benefits for a:
•
Divorced Spouse;
•
Independently Entitled Divorced Spouse;
•
Surviving Spouse;
•
Surviving Divorced Spouse; or
•
Surviving Divorced Mother/Father.
