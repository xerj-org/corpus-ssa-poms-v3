A. Introduction
Section 206 of P.L. 106-169 (Foster Care Independence Act of 1999) provides for an
exception to the period of ineligibility if not getting SSI benefits would create
undue hardship for the individual. If none of the other exceptions apply, it is necessary
to determine whether benefits are payable based on undue hardship before applying
the period of ineligibility to the individual.
