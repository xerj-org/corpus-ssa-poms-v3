A. Introduction
Questions 12(a)-(c) document whether sponsor-to-alien deeming applies. We need the
information in (d) because prior alien status may have a bearing on current eligibility.
Answers to (e) through (f) determine whether a claimant in this category has or can
be credited with 40 qualifying quarters (QQs), as defined in SI 00502.135.
