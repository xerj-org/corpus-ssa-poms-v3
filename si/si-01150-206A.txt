SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.206 - Field Office Responsibilities When a Conditional Benefits Period Ends
Effective Dates: 12/02/2024 - Present | TN 42 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501150206 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Operating procedure for property sold
1. Overpayment development
Post the overpayment to the social security record (SSR) using the P TAC instructions
in SM 01311.300.
2. Eligibility and notice development
Determine eligibility for regular (not conditional) benefits based on sale proceeds
remaining after refund. To issue the appropriate manual notice see SI 01150.210.
