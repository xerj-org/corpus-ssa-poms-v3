B. Procedure
Follow these steps to issue a manual GK notice:
STEP
ACTION
1
Use the Document Processing System (DPS) to complete an SSA-L8155-U2 (or another standard
notice form as appropriate) using appropriate captions and paragraphs from NL 00804.105. Follow instructions in NL 00803.020.
2
Date the notice the day you mail it (or hand it to the recipient in the FO).
3
Mail the notice at least 15 days (or hand it to the recipient at least 10 days) before
the first day of the adverse action month.
4
Place a copy of the notice in the recipient's file or ensure a copy is retained in
the Online Retrieval System (ORS). When applicable, annotate the copy with the date
it was handed to the recipient.
5
Suppress the systems notice for the situation, if appropriate.
NOTE: To suppress the systems GK notice during the GK temporary period, input required DPD
entries (see SM 01305.530). Input of NP1000 in the Notice Suppression (NP) field alone
without the appropriate DPD entries will not suppress the notice.
