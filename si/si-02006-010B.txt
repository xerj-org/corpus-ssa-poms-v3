B. Operating Policy - Formula for Computation of True OP/UP
1. Net Payment
a.
The “net payment”
takes into consideration that we recovered SSI payments.
SSI paid
- Offset amount
Net payment
b.
If the offset is zero,
then the SSI paid = the net payment.
c.
If the offset amount is negative, subtract the negative amount from the SSI paid; e.g., $100 SSI paid minus negative
$10 = $110.
2. Net Amount Due
This is the amount of SSI the person would have been due had we paid the title II
income on time, based on the correct SSI information. It takes into consideration
the SSI payments we should have recovered.
3. True OP/UP
Net payment
- Net amount due
True OP (If positive)
True UP (If negative)
