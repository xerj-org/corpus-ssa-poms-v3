A. Explanation of Form SSA-8008
Use Form SSA-8008 to:
•
summarize the determination(s) of a claimant’s living arrangements (LA),
•
identify chargeable inside or outside in-kind support maintenance (ISM), and
•
document any other ISM received.
This section describes the five parts of the SSA-8008. The SSA-8008 is an internal
form, not for public use.
NOTE: The SSA-8008 is a developmental tool for adjudicators. It is not required in the
file as documentation of the living arrangement.
