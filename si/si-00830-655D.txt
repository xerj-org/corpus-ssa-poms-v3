SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.655 - Relocation Assistance
Effective Dates: 09/27/2023 - Present | TN 206 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830655 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
Verify that the project which displaces the individual has governmental involvement.
This can be done by using documents in the individual's possession, contacting the
provider or entity involved in the project, or using a precedent. Once verified, obtain
the individual's statement either signed or recorded on a Report of Contact page of
the assistance without further development or documentation.
NOTE: If the individual retains relocation assistance beyond the month of receipt, see
SI 01130.670B.
