H. Procedure - record documentation
1. Recipient abroad
Use the remarks (RMKS) segment of the SSR to record information necessary to determine
when the recipient left the U.S., where they are temporarily residing abroad (if not
entered in mailing address), and the U.S. contact.
2. Past period as a student abroad
Document all months the individual is eligible under this provision after a period
of absence in the RMKS segment of the SSR as follows:
ELIGIBLE AS STUDENT OUTSIDE U.S. MM/YY-MM/YY
