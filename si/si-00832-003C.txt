C. Anderson Underpayment Determinations — Process
RO's will notify FO's of cases which require second reviews by sending SSIRD's for
cases in the FO's service area. In each case, the FO will write to the VARO for correct
VA income information. VA will recompute the correct VA income amount based on procedures
developed as the result of the January 28, 1992 order and respond to the FO. SSA will
then make income correction inputs, if necessary, and pay any underpayments due. FO's
will also take steps to resolve any SSI overpayments that result when verified VA
income exceeds the amount of posted VA income.
