SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02220.005 - Documenting the Supplemental Security Income Overpayment
Effective Dates: 02/14/2025 - Present | TN 133 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
This section provides guidance on documenting the disposition decisions of Supplemental
Security Income (SSI) overpayments, with the exception of waiver determinations. For
waiver policies and procedures, refer to GN 02250.000.
