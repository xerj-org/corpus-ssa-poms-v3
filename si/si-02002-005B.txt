B. Procedure for RO IAR Sample Reviews
Use the IAR Sample Review Worksheet in SI 02002.060 Exhibit IV, or a regionally developed substitute form to verify the accuracy of the
State’s completion of:
•
the IAR authorization forms;
•
the eIAR computer screens;
•
Form SSAL-8125-F6's (Supplemental Security Income Notice of Interim Assistance).
Also, use the IAR Sample Review Worksheet to ensure that the State is claiming reimbursement
for the correct dollar amount and the appropriate months and verify that the State
is meeting IAR apportionment notice requirements.
NOTE: The Office of Income Security Programs (OISP) must approve any regionally developed
Sample Review Worksheets. Refer to SI 02002.040 for further details.
