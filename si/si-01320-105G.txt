G. Per Capita Funds - Warm Springs Reservation Tribes
Exclusion under section 4 of Public Law 97-436 of per capita judgment funds paid to
the Confederated Tribes of the Warm Springs Reservation (see SI 00830.830D.19.). This applies to the income of sponsors of aliens only if the alien lives in the sponsor's household. Prior to April 1, 1987, this exclusion
applied regardless of the alien's residence.
