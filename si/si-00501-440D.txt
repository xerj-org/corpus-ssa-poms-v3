D. Policy principles
1. Prucol based on LTR
Aliens granted LTR status under IRCA can meet the PRUCOL criteria discussed in SI 00501.420 and, therefore, may be eligible for SSI.
2. Agricultural workers
SAW's and, beginning fiscal year 1990, the additional agricultural workers are subject
to the SSI rules governing LAPR's during the period they have LTR status. (See GN 00303.440.)
