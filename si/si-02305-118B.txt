B. Procedure
Completion of the “FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY” block is self-explanatory except for the following:
Check The Ones That Apply
Check off all of the following that apply to the case:
•
C - change in eligibility or payment amount.
•
NC - no change in eligibility or payment amount.
•
M - face-to-face interview conducted (SSA-8203-BK).
•
N - telephone interview conducted (SSA-8203-BK).
•
FS-APP - food stamp application taken.
•
FS-REF - food stamp referral.
