F. Procedure – Systems input
1. Non-SSI Claims system Cases
For Non-SSI Claims system cases, enter amounts excluded from resources and the beginning
and ending dates for the 9-month exclusion period in the “RMKS” segment of the SSR.
If appropriate, enter amounts of interest and dividends in the “UMIH” segment of the
SSR.
2. SSI Claims system Cases
For SSI Claims system cases:
•
Use the Other Income page to enter income amounts as “Alleged Amount” or “Verified
Amount”, as appropriate, and “Amt Not Counted”.
•
Use the Other Resource page to enter resource amounts as “Alleged Value” or “Verified
Value”, as appropriate, and “Excluded Amount.”
•
Use the Interest page to enter interest amounts as “Alleged Amount” or “Verified Amount”,
as appropriate, and “Amt Not Counted”.
•
Use the Dividend page to enter dividend amounts as “Alleged Amount” or “Verified Amount”,
as appropriate, and “Amt Not Counted”.
