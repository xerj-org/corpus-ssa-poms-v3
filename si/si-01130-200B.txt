B. Definitions for automobile exclusion
1. Automobile
For Supplemental Security Income (SSI) purposes, “automobile” means any registered or unregistered vehicle used for transportation. Vehicles used for transportation include but are not limited to cars, trucks, motorcycles,
boats, snowmobiles, animal-drawn vehicles, and even animals.
A temporarily broken down vehicle normally used for transportation meets the definition
of an automobile. For more on inoperable automobiles see SI 01130.200E.3.
2. Current Market Value (CMV)
The CMV of an automobile is the average price an automobile of that particular year,
make, model and condition will sell for on the open market (to a private recipient)
in the particular geographic area involved.
3. Equity Value (EV)
The EV of an automobile is the price it can sell for on the open market (to a private
recipient) in the particular geographic area involved, minus any encumbrances. For
the definition of an encumbrance see SI 01110.400A.1.c. and to determine equity value see SI 01140.042.
