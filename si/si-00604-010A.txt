SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.010 - Procedure for Type of Claim Section
Effective Dates: 09/10/2026 - Present | TN 82 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to complete type of claim section
Complete the type of claim section for each application. At the beginning of the interview,
decide whether the type of claim will be "Individual," "Individual with Ineligible
Spouse," "Couple," "Child," or "Child with Parent(s)." Mark the appropriate block.
