SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00815.400 - Bills Paid By a Third Party
Effective Dates: 03/08/2005 - Present | TN 2 (05-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500815400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Payment of an individual's bills (including supplementary medical insurance under
title XVIII or other medical insurance premiums) by a third party directly to the
supplier is not income. However, anything received in kind as a result of the payment
is income if it is food or shelter.
