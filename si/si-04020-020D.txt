D. Procedure -- Third party requests
1. Inquirer calls at the FO
If the inquirer calls the FO to request a reconsideration on behalf of a claimant
and the caller is other than an appointed representative or representative payee:
a.
Explain the requirements regarding disclosure and representation of claimants.
b.
Give the inquirer a form SSA-1696 and a form SSA-561-U2, and a copy of the pamphlet
“Your Right to Representation.”
c.
Fill in only the claimant’s name on the first two forms.
d.
Instruct the inquirer to contact the claimant or recipient and have the forms completed
and returned to the FO within 15 working days.
e.
If the appeal is being established in MSSICS, complete the ANCR (Non-Claimant Requestor)
screen and RPOC (Report of Contact) screen describing the contact.
f.
Follow up in 15 days with the claimant or recipient, not the inquirer.
g.
Manually prepare a notice that states the following:
"We are writing to tell you that we learned from (1) that you want to appeal your
Supplemental Security Income (SSI) case.
"What We Will Need
"We gave (2) two forms for you to fill out, and return to us. These forms are:

3. Inquiry from a Member of Congress (MC)
Ordinarily, a congressional inquiry is merely a request for information or an explanation.
However, in some cases, the inquiry or the claimant’s letter (a copy of which may
be enclosed with the congressional inquiry) constitutes an expressed or implied request
for reconsideration. You will need to decide whether the inquiry constitutes an implied
request based on the wording of the claimant’s letter. Resolve doubts in favor of
finding it to be an implied request in which case, complete the SSA-561-U2 and attach
a copy of the congressional inquiry.
a. Notice to the Claimant
In such cases, send a letter to the claimant advising that the congressional inquiry
is being accepted as a request for reconsideration. In the notice, state the following:
"We are writing to you because (1) asked about our determination dated (2) on (3)
Supplemental Security Income (SSI) case.
"We will use (4) letter as (5) request for an appeal. Please tell us how you want
to appeal. We have listed the ways to appeal below. These are the same ways to appeal
that we listed in the letter dated (6).

" (7).

we can review this case, you must fill out and return the SSA-561-U2. This form tells

Enclose SSA-561-U2, pamphlet, and a preaddressed envelope.
•
Give the claimant 15 days from receipt of the letter to choose the type of reconsideration
they prefer. If you do not receive a response within that time, follow up with the
claimant by telephone if possible. If you cannot contact the claimant by telephone,
send a follow-up letter.
•
If you receive no response either by telephone or letter within 15 days (remember
to allow 5 days mailing time), promptly conduct a reconsideration by means of a case
review.
•
Complete a form SSA-561-U2 indicating the choice of case review, but do not sign the
form.
•
Attach a copy of the correspondence received from the MC (including enclosures, if
any) and a report of contact describing the attempts to contact the claimant.
Complete the ANCR (Non-Claimant Requestor) screen with the necessary information.
b. Notice to MC
1.
If the reconsideration determination is the same as the initial determination
Give the MC a complete explanation separate from the claimant's SSA-L8456-U2, a copy
of which should be enclosed with the response to the MC. Include a reminder to the
claimant on the SSA-L8456-U2 that the reconsideration was requested on their behalf
by the MC. Have the district manager or the branch manager sign the letter.
2.
If the reconsidered determination is different from the initial determination
Explain in the letter to the MC how the changed determination affects the claimant
in terms of the specific complaint.
Upon completion of the reconsideration, effectuate the determination by normal procedures.
