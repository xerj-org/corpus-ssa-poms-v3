SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.315 - Erroneous Payments and GK Payment Continuation
Effective Dates: 06/11/2013 - Present | TN 60 (07-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301315 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Examples
1. Example 1
Multiple Payment Case/Payment Over the Payment Ceiling
In July 1993, the FO discovers that Randy Yates is receiving SSI payments on three
different SSNs. They are receiving monthly payments of $74, $434 (FBR), and a payment
of $1000 which is over the payment ceiling. The FO determines that Randy Yates is
entitled only to the monthly payment of $74 which they are receiving on the correct
SSN. On July 10, the FO inputs T20 to the incorrect records to terminate the erroneous
payments for the following month, and issues a manual SSA-L8165-U2. The notice explains
that SSA will send only one monthly payment of $74 beginning August and offers payment
continuation at $434.
NOTE: The FO offers payment continuation at $434 since it is the highest payment within
the payment ceiling. Since it is higher than the correct amount on the record, the
FO would have to pay by A-OTP an additional $360 each month to provide payment continuation
until the appeal decision. For a sample notice, see NL 00804.700, notice number 4.
2. Example 2
Payment Ceiling Case
In July 1993, the FO discovers continuing SSI payments over the payment ceiling for
Lamar Candy of $1289.34 since March 1993. The FO attempted to pay $289.34 beginning
March, but a transmission error resulted in the erroneous payments on the M02 record.
Lamar Candy is due $289.34 for March and $254 for April and continuing. On July 5,
the FO inputs to reduce the payment and issues a manual SSA-L8165-U2. The notice explains
the reduction to $254 beginning August and offers payment continuation at $289.34.
NOTE: The FO must offer payment continuation at $289.34 because it is the amount that the
FO attempted to pay for the first month of erroneous payments and because it is higher
than the correct amount due. For a sample notice, see NL 00804.700, notice number 5.
