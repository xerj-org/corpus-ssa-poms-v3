G. Procedure for Use of Burial Funds For Another Purpose
Use the following chart to determine if the penalty for use of burial funds for another
purpose applies.
STEP
ACTION
1
Is there some indication that burial funds were used for a non-burial purpose?
•
If No, stop.
•
If Yes, go to Step 2.
2
Was the individual eligible in the month that funds were used?
•
If No, go to Step 9.
•
If Yes, go to Step 3.
3
If you add the amount of excluded burial funds (including any amount spent) to the
individual's countable resources in the month the funds were used, does the individual
have excess resources?
•
If No, go to Step 9.
•
If Yes, go to Step 4.
4
•
Obtain the individual's signed statement as to whether any of the funds were used
for a non-burial purpose and, if so, the amount used.
•
Obtain any pertinent evidence, including signed statements from other individuals
who may know about the funds in question.
Does the evidence indicate burial funds were used for a non-burial purpose?
•
If No, go to Step 9.
•
If Yes, go to Step 5.
5
Does the burial fund contain only excluded burial funds?
•
If No, go to Step 6.
•
If Yes, go to Step 7.
6
Assuming that non-excluded funds are used before excluded funds, did the individual
spend excluded funds?
•
If No, go to Step 9.
•
If Yes, go to Step 7.
7
A penalty applies in the amount of the excluded funds used. Prepare a special determination
documenting your decision about the use of funds and the amount used, following instructions
in GN 01010.360.
Go to Step 8.
8
Prepare a manual Notice of Planned Action (SSA-L8155) by suppressing the automated
notice at the time of the determination. In addition to the appropriate standard notice
paragraphs in NL 00804.100, include the language in SI 01130.410L.1. or SI 01130.410L.2. in this section as appropriate.
NOTE: If the individual is blind or visually impaired, see instructions at NL 01001.010 for more information on the special blind or visually impaired notice options.
(See SM 01311.310 on systems input for check adjustment.)
Go to SI 01130.410H. in this section.
9
No penalty applies. Go to SI 01130.410H. in this section.
