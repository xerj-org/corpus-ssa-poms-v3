B. Policy for alimony and spousal support
Alimony and spousal support payments are unearned income to the spouse.
1. Alimony and spousal support and deeming
Exclude the income used to make court-ordered or Title IV-D support payments by an
ineligible spouse, ineligible parent, ineligible child, or eligible alien from the
deemor's income.
For information on deeming income used to comply with a Court Order for support, see
SI 01320.145.
