SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.019 - Prior Marriage(s)
Effective Dates: 06/24/2026 - Present | TN 76 (06-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604019 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Effective August 16, 2019, as is currently done in Title II claims, technicians taking
Supplemental Security Income claims will document only prior marriages that could
result in entitlement to Title II benefits. For the Title II marriage documentation
policy refer to GN 00205.070.
