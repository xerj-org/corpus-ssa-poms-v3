H. Procedure for documenting the wage estimate
Take the following steps to document:
1.
Document your determination of the estimate using the Wages page and a Report of Contact
and lock it, or on an SSA-5002 in non-SSI Claims system cases.
2.
Store any paper evidence in NDRED via the EP or eView, as appropriate.
3.
View the documents stored in NDRED and eView for readability by using EP. Do not retain
after storing the documents. For more information on storing forms electronically,
refer to GN 00301.322.
