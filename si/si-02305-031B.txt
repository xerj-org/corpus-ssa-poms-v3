B. Procedure
Capture the contact method in the SSI Claims System on the Post Eligibility Menu screen,
MS 08104.008.
For RZs that cannot be processed in the SSI Claims System see disposition codes, which
indicate the method of contact when making 1719B input, SM 01501.003.
