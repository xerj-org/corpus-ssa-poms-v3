SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02009.030 - One Member of an Eligible Couple Living in the Household of Another and Other Member in Own Household
Effective Dates: 11/27/2002 - Present | TN 2 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502009030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy -- special quarterly computation – general
When one member of an eligible couple is receiving in-kind support and maintenance
while living in the house­hold of another throughout a month (see SI 00835.000 ff.). A special quarterly computation is required. All quarterly income received
by the couple in the quarter is used in computing the couple payment amount. However,
the full couple payment is apportioned in a special way.
