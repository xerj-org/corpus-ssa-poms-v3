2. Alert Contents
a.
The first line of the alert displays:
•
The housed-under number (HUN) for the record;
•
Total redemption value;
•
A code that indicates whether a CG field entry of RE21 (savings security instruments)
appears on the SSR. The code is Y for yes or blank for no; and
•
The FO code.
b.
The second line displays:
•
The SSN;
•
The State code; and
•
The name of the individual.
c.
The third line displays:
•
Denom - Shows the denomination of the instrument.
•
Serial No. - The serial number of the instrument.
•
Series - Shows whether the series is E, or EE, or I.
•
Issue Date - The year and month the instrument was issued.
•
Value - The redemption value of the instrument as of the date in the Return Date field.
•
Book Account The account number of a book entry security.
•
Return Date - The year and month in which the redemption value was calculated.
