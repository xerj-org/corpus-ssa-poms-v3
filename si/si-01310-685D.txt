D. Procedure — question 15
•
Complete question 15 for individuals who answer question 14.
•
Emphasize question 15 because it acknowledges a sponsor's liability for any overpayment
made to a noncitizen that results from the sponsor's failure to provide correct information
regarding deemable income and resources.
•
Explain that payment cannot be made to the claimant/recipient without the sponsor's
agreement to report changes.
