G. Policy – CDR and Medical Folder Reviews
1. Medical Folder Reviews (MFR)
Prior to December 1999, an MFR was required on 1619(a) and 1619(b) cases to determine
whether a medical CDR was needed.
2. CDR
The system will identify 1619(a) and 1619(b) cases for review with an A1 or an A2
diary, see DI 13005.016. To determine when a CDR is required based on the A1 or A2 diary follow instructions
in DI 13005.018.
NOTE: Do not conduct a CDR on a 1619 case more than once in any 12-month period.
