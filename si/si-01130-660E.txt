E. Procedure - initial claims
If an individual alleges that their resources include unspent Agent Orange settlement
payments:
•
verify the date(s) and amount(s) of such payment(s) in accordance with SI 00830.730; and
•
obtain a statement as to the date(s) and amount(s) of any account deposits corresponding
to the payments.
Absent evidence to the contrary, accept the allegation regarding deposits.
