C. Permission to Contact Financial Institutions
Permission to contact financial institutions must be requested from all recipients
and deemors with all RZs initiated in a FO. See SI 00515.001B. and SI 00515.001D. Also, completion of either the AFIP screen or the paper permission must be completed
for any pending open RZ event initiated prior to 11/18/2006.
