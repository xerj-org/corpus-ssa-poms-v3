C. What is an early termination provision?
An early termination provision allows a trust to terminate before the death of the
beneficiary. Commonly, such provisions provide for termination of the trust when,
for example, the beneficiary is no longer disabled or otherwise becomes ineligible
for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and Medicaid, or when the trust no longer contains
enough assets to justify its continued administration.
