B. When to develop rental subsidy
Develop for a possible rental subsidy only if:
•
sequential living arrangement (LA) development shows that someone in the household
has rental liability and theVTR does not apply; and
•
someone in the household is related as a parent or child to the landlord or landlord's
spouse.
