A. Introduction to VA educational benefits
The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) provides educational assistance through different
programs, including vocational rehabilitation. The Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
policies on income and resources depend on the nature of the VA program. The SSI interviewer
determines from which program (called “chapters” for VA purposes) the veteran draws
benefits because VA chapters provide different benefits at different times.
