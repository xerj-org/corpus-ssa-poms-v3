D. Procedure - Verifying income
NOTE:
Permission to contact financial institutions must be requested at all RZs initiated
by FOs. See SI 00515.001B. and SI 00515.001D.
Assume that permission has previously been collected if:
•
The initial claim was filed 11/18/2006 or later, or
•
A redetermination has been completed by the field office 11/18/2006 or later. (See
SM 01501.002C.8. for a description of the redetermination disposition codes found
in the RZD segment of the SSR.)
Always verify fluctuating income (for example, child support payments).
Verify income per instructions in SI 02305.094B.1. unless:
•
Unearned income alleged agrees with the SSR and other information does not conflict;
OR
•
A developmental tolerance applies. (See SI 00820.140B.)
