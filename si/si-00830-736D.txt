D. Developing and verifying payments made from a State eugenics compensation program
1. When to develop
Develop this exclusion only when an individual alleges the receipt of eugenics compensation
payments excludable under this provision and the exclusion allows eligibility for
Supplement Security Income (SSI) benefits.
2. How to develop
a. Individual has documentation
When the individual has documentation of payments:
•
Use documents in the individual’s possession to verify the date and amount of payment
from a State eugenics compensation program.
•
Obtain the individual’s statement if not evident from the documents, and record on
a Report of Contact page the amount and date of payments.
b. Individual does not have documentation
If the individual does not have documentation or there is a reason to question the
source of the payments:
•
Obtain the SSI recipient’s signed statement regarding the amount and date of receipt
of payment on a Report of Contact page or SSA-5002, and
•
Verify the information with the appropriate State compensation agency.
For more information regarding evidentiary requirements, see GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289.
