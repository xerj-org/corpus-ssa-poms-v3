SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.250 - Death of a Recipient (T01)
Effective Dates: 01/04/2016 - Present | TN 15 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Process
This is what happens when SSA receives a death report for a recipient and terminates
benefits.
STAGE
DESCRIPTION
1
SSA receives a death report from a first or third party. The source could be, but
is not limited to, one of these:
•
a representative payee, relative, friend, neighbor, or an unidentified individual,
•
a returned check in the FO,
•
a death report from the U.S. Treasury Department,
•
an MBR interface, or
•
a computer match with various outside agencies.
2
FOs carefully evaluate death reports, verify reports when required and review death
inputs in the death (DH) field on the SSR. See GN 02602.050 through GN 02602.080 on processing death reports.
NOTE: FOs also input death for eligible and ineligible persons on a recipient's record in
the DH field and determine if the event affects the recipient's eligibility and payment
amount. See redetermination requirements in SI 02305.022 and SI 02305.083B.
3
The system terminates benefits the next month it can based on the monthly recurring
payment tape cutoff and generates T01 on the record based on:
•
FO input of the recipient's date of death in the DH field on the SSR,
•
FO input of a returned check in the (CH) field on the SSR,
•
an MBR interface, or
•
a death report from the U.S. Treasury Department.
NOTE: T01 is an exception to advance notice and systems GK processing described in SI 02301.300.
4
The system automatically issues a notice (Form SSA-L8166) with appeal rights to a
payee on a deceased recipient's record.
