E. Procedure - exclusions
Consider the following exclusions:
•
Property essential for self-support (see SI 01130.500 for an explanation);
•
Plan to Achieve Self-Support (PASS) (see SI 00870.001 for an explanation);
NOTE: If a resource is being excluded for an existing PASS, develop for compliance with
the plan. Determine whether the exclusion should be continued, be adjusted, or stopped.
•
Pension funds (including individual retirement accounts) owned by certain deemors
(SI 01330.120 and SI 01330.220);
•
Retroactive SSI and Retirement, Survivors Disability Insurance (RSDI) payments (SI 01130.600);
•
Jointly owned real property which cannot be sold without undue hardship (due to loss
of housing) to the other owner(s) (SI 01130.130);
•
Real property for so long as the owner's reasonable efforts to sell it are unsuccessful
(SI 01130.140);
•
State plan exclusions for “converted” recipients (SI 01220.001);
•
Radiation Exposure Compensation Trust Fund Payments (SI 01130.680); and/or
•
Any other applicable resource exclusion listed in SI 01110.210.
Count the value of partially excludable resources in excess of the amount that can
be excluded.
