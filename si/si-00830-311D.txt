Effective Dates: 10/24/2024 - Present | TN 266 (10-24)

D. Verifying, determining, and documenting IT and CWT participation
1. Verification of IT, CWT or SE phase of CWT participation
Ask the claimant if they have a letter from the VA confirming their participation
in the IT, CWT or SE phase of CWT program. If the claimant does not have a VA letter
readily available, to verify participation in IT, CWT or SE phase of CWT, follow the
procedures in SI 00830.320. If the claimant participates in the SE phase of CWT, verify their wages per normal
wage development and verification procedures. For wage verification, see SI 00820.133.
2. Determination
a. IT or CWT participation
If the individual is participating in the IT or CWT program, exclude the income they
receive from the Fund.
b. SE phase of CWT participation
If the individual is participating in the SE phase of the CWT program, evaluate the
income received from the employer under normal earned income development policy.
3. Documentation
a. Paper evidence
Use the Report of Contact page and lock it or Evidence (EVID) screen to record any
paper documentation received or fax the paper into the Non-Disability Repository for
Evidentiary Documents (NDRED) or the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate.
In non-MSSICS situations, record the information on an SSA-5002, Report of Contact,
and fax it into NDRED or CEF, as appropriate.
b. Telephone contact
Use the Report of Contact page and lock it or EVID screen to record the contact person’s
name, phone number, date of contact and type of program participation. In non-MSSICS
situations, record the information on an SSA-5002 and fax it into NDRED or CEF, as
appropriate.
c. Wages
If the claimant is participating in the SE phase of CWT, document the wages per normal
earned income documentation procedures.
