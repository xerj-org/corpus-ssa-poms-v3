G. Request for hearing processing instructions
If the individual wishes to appeal an unfavorable PASS reconsideration decision, forward
the HA-501-U5, along with any material submitted, to the servicing PASS Cadre via WorkTrack. Advise
the individual that a PASS Specialist or AWIC, who did not make the reconsideration
decision, will establish the request for the hearing, to be decided by an Administrative
Law Judge (ALJ).
