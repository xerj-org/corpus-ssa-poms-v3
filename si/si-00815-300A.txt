A. Definition of credit life and credit disability insurance
Credit life and credit disability insurance policies are issued to or on behalf of
borrowers, to cover payments on loans, mortgages, etc. in the event of death or disability.
These insurance payments are made directly to loan or mortgage companies, etc. and
are not available to the individual.
