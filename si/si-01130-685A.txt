SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.685 - Food Programs with Federal Involvement
Effective Dates: 03/14/2000 - Present | TN 39 (03-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130685 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Food Stamp Program
The value of the food under the food stamp program to any household is excluded from
resources.
2. School Lunch Programs
The value of any assistance to children under chapter 13 of title 42 of the U.S. Code,
School Lunch Programs, is excluded from resources.
3. Child Nutrition Programs
The value of any assistance to children (e.g., school breakfasts, WIC Program, Milk
Programs) under chapter 13A of title 42 of the U.S. Code, Child Nutrition, is excluded
from resources.
