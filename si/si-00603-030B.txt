B. Policy - General
An FO must conduct a PERC when required by SI 00603.031 through SI 00603.032 in order to pay a claim which required a disability determination. The file is not
sufficiently documented without documentation of the PERC. Closeout procedures apply
when the claimant does not respond to Form SSA-L8009-U3 (Request for Information/Evidence)
to come in, call in, or return the signed PERC document. (See SI
00601.110 and SI 00601.120 for a further explanation of closeout procedures.)
