SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01210.500 - Special Blind Income Disregards - Applying Specific October 1972 State Plan Rules
Effective Dates: 01/06/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501210500 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
We use SSI definitions of earned and unearned income when applying State income disregards.
EXCEPTION: For residents of Pennsylvania and California, regional instructions provide the definition of income to use when applying State
plan income disregards.
