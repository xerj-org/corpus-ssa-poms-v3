D. Procedure for posteligibility development
If, after SSI eligibility is established, an individual receives real property (e.g.
as an inheritance or gift) which may be excludable as their home, apply the policy
and procedures in SI 01130.100B. and SI 01130.100C. to determine whether the home exclusion applies.
Redevelop the exclusion from resources of an individual's home only if something raises
a question about the correctness of the original determination or indicates that the
exclusion may no longer apply (e.g., change of address).
If the individual has left their home but intends to return to it, see SI 01130.100E.
