SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02101.006 - SSI Underpayment Due - Individual Deceased - Surviving Spouse Not Member of an Eligible Couple
Effective Dates: 09/22/2023 - Present | TN 26 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502101006 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy - General
1. To Whom Payable
If the deceased individual had a spouse for the month of death who was not an eligible
spouse, that spouse may receive the underpayment if they were living in the same household
(LISH) any time in the month of death or any time during the preceding 6 months.
2. Underpayment Limit
The amount payable to a spouse who is not the surviving member of an eligible couple
is limited by law to SSI benefits due for June 1986 and later months. (See SI 02101.006C.4.)
3. Time Limit for Requesting Payment
The surviving spouse has 24 months after the month of death to request any underpayment
which may be payable on the deceased individual's record.
