I. Procedure for documenting loans of ISM
1. Electronic documentation
Document the borrower’s allegation of the loan of food or shelter or both under the
Remarks section on the Household Expenses and Contribution page in the SSI Claim System.
Document the lender’s verification of the loan on a Report of Contact page.
REMINDER: The beginning date of the loan is not necessarily the date that the CS records the
borrower’s allegations on the Remarks section or Report of Contact page.
2. Use the SSA-5062 to document the claimant's statement about loan of food or shelter
or both
If you cannot obtain the information in person or by telephone, you may send the paper
form SSA-5062 to the borrower to document allegations of the loan of ISM.
REMINDER: The beginning date of the loan is not necessarily the date that the claimant signed
the SSA-5062.
When the FO receives the paper form SSA-5062, document:
•
SSI Claim cases by recording the information on a Report of Contact page;
•
Non-MSSICS cases by faxing the completed SSA-5062 into the appropriate electronic
folder; and
•
Do not retain the paper form after you document the issue electronically.
To view this form, go to SSA-5062.
3. Use the SSA-L5063-F3 to document the lender statement about food or shelter or both
provided to another
If you cannot obtain the information in person or by telephone, send the paper form
SSA-L5063-F3 to the lender to document allegations of the loan of ISM. Form SSA-L5063-F3
has two parts. The first part is a cover letter to the lender that explains why SSA
needs the information. The second part is a questionnaire used to document the lender’s
statement. REMINDER: The beginning date of the loan is not necessarily the date that the lender signed
the SSA-L5063-F3.
When the paper form SSA-L5063-F3 is received in the FO, document:
•
SSI Claim cases by recording the information on a Report of Contact page;
•
Non-MSSICS cases by faxing the completed SSA-L5063-F3 into the appropriate electronic
folder; and
•
Do not retain the paper form after you document the issue electronically.
For more information on storing forms electronically refer to GN 00301.322 “Retention of Paper Material After Faxing into Either the eDIB folder or Claims Folder
using NDRed”.
To view this form, go to SSA-L5063–F3.
4. Oral Summarized Privacy Act Statement for the SSA-5062 and the SSA-L5063-F3
During telephone interviews the CS is responsible for reading the following summarized
privacy act statement before documenting information obtained verbally from the SSA-L5062
and the SSA-L5063-F3 within a case file:
Sections 1612(a) and 1631(e) of the Social Security Act allows us to collect your
information, which we will use to determine your Supplemental Security Income payment
amount.
Providing this information is voluntary, but not providing the information could affect
your
Supplemental Security Income monthly federal benefit payment. We may disclose your
information as
law permits, including to employers, our contractors, and others listed in routine
uses in System of
Records Notice 60-0089 and 60-0103; available at www.ssa.gov/privacy. Your information
may also be
used in computer matching programs to establish or verify eligibility for Federal
benefit programs
and debts under these programs.
After reading the summarized statement the CS is responsible for mailing the form
with the complete privacy act statement to the recipient, or directing the recipient
to www.ssa.gov/privacy to review the full statement electronically. Document on a
Report of Contact the following statement, Oral Summarized Privacy Act Statement Read and include the recipient's name or the lender's name (whichever applies) and the
date of the action. In addition, document if you mailed the privacy act statement
or directed the recipient to the website to review an electronic version.
