Do not consider the value of assistance as income, in the deeming computation for
an individual, who received assistance from another person, or directly from the Supplemental
Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP).
In addition, if the Department of Agriculture (e.g., supplemental food programs) donates
food to an individual, for deeming purposes, do not consider the value of that food
as income.
This exclusion applies to the income of
•
a spouse,
•
parent(s),
•
an essential person,
(For Essential Person definition, see SI 00501.100)
•
the sponsor of an alien, and
•
an ineligible child living in the household
For purposes of determining the amount of the allocation, this exclusion also applies
to the income of an eligible alien that an ineligible spouse or parent sponsored.
