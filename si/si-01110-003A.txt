A. Policy principles
1. Excess resources cause ineligibility
An individual (or couple) with countable resources in excess of the statutory limit
is not eligible for Federal SSI or federally- administered State supplementary payments.
However, conditional benefits provisions may apply see SI 01150.200.

3. Change in marital status
A change in marital status will result in a change to the applicable resource limit.
The resource limit change is effective with the month that we begin treating both
members of a couple as individuals, or vice versa.
For example, separation from a spouse will reduce the limit from $3,000 to $2,000.
Refer to our policy for the spouse of adult individuals SI 01110.530 B.
