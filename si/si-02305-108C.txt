C. Description of Form SSA-8202-BK — Question 12
Question 12 on the SSA-8202-BK — Since the date on page 1, have you or your spouse had any change in health insurance coverage or other insurance
that pays for medical bills? (DO NOT INCLUDE — Medicare. DO INCLUDE-
-Insurance, such as accident, automobile, or casualty if it covers medical bills for
any reason.)
