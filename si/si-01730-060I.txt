I. Region IX San Francisco
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
1. State of Arizona
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System (AHCCCS )
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients. The State makes determinations for all
other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the questions regarding third party coverage on the SSI application
and at all redeterminations. Effective February 1, 2014, we also obtain answers to
the question regarding unpaid medical expenses.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the AHCCCS agency for determination of eligibility for Medicaid. Refer
to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
2. State of California
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Department of Health Care Services, Medi-Cal
•
Medicaid (Medi-Cal) Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory minimum and
optional State supplementary payments. The State makes determinations for all other
groups.
•
Additional Medicaid (Medi-Cal) Questions
SSA obtains answers to the question regarding third party coverage on the SSI application
and at all redeterminations.
SSA does not obtain answers to the question regarding unpaid medical expenses. (See
“Medicaid (Medi-Cal) Referrals” below.)
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid (Medi-Cal) Referrals
While SSA is not required to ask the question regarding unpaid medical expenses on
behalf of the State, SSA should tell claimants that the Medicaid (Medi-Cal) program
may pay for unpaid medical expenses and refer them to the Medicaid (Medi-Cal) office.
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the Department of Health Care Services office for determination of eligibility
for Medicaid (Medi-Cal). Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral
instructions.
3. State of Hawaii
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Hawaii Department of Human Services, Med-Quest Division
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory minimum
and optional State supplementary payments utilizing more restrictive criteria than
title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local Department of Human Services for determination of
eligibility for Medicaid. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral
instructions.
4. State of Nevada
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Nevada Department of Health and Human Services
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory and
optional State supplementary payments utilizing title XVI criteria.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local Department of Health and Human Services office for
determination of eligibility for Medicaid. Refer to regional supplements for any additional
referral instructions.
