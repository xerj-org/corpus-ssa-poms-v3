A. Introduction
The procedures in this section are for conducting a full preeffectuation review contact
(PERC) upon receipt of the first notice of disability allowance (see SI 00603.030 for the definition of a notice of disability allowance) on a simultaneous development
claim. The following procedures are divided into instructions for non-MSSICS processing
and processing via MSSICS.
