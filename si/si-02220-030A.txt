Effective Dates: 04/13/2026 - Present | TN 139 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
A compromise settlement offer occurs when an overpaid individual offers to pay a lesser
amount than they currently owe. This section provides guidance on compromise offers
and when the agency can accept them to compromise an overpayment.
