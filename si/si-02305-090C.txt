C. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 8
Question 8 on Form SSA-8203-BK
a.
Prior to September 30, 2024, do all of the people who live with you receive public
assistance payments? (For example, TANF, VA pension, SSI, general assistance)
b.
Starting September 30, 2024, does any other person who lives with you receive public
assistance payments? (For example, SNAP, TANF, VA pension, SSI, general assistance)
