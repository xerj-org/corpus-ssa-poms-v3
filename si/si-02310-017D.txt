Effective Dates: 03/20/2009 - Present | TN 67 (05-24)

D. Procedures - Clearance
1. Documentation
•
For MSSICS cases, document information as required by MSOM MSSICS 022.012 , Development Documentation (DDOC).
•
For non-MSSICS cases, record development in the “remarks” section of the SSR.
2. Clear the Diary
Delete the diary on the Update Diaries (UDIA) screen and complete the Build Transaction
SSR process. See SM 01301.215 (List of Diaries and Alerts), MSOM BUSSR 002.019 (UDIA) and MSOM BUSSR 001.002 (BTS).
