D. Procedure - verification
If a person challenges the income amount we will use to compute the SSI benefits,
ask him/her for verification of payments. If his/her records are insufficient, proceed
as described below.
1. VA
a.
See SI 00830.300 for types of VA payments. Obtain verification of VA benefit data from the VA Regional
Office per SI
00830.320.
b.
VA pension and compensation benefits can be paid under the same VA claim number (although
not simultaneously). Consider the possibility of a change in type of payment and whether
the $20 monthly exclusion applies.
2. RRB
Verify RRB payments per SI 00830.225.
3. OPM
See SI 00830.220.
