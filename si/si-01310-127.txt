SI 01310.127 Deeming Concept - Deemor
Definition
A deemor is an individual whose income and resources are subject to deeming, as defined
in SI 01310.001B. Such individuals include:
•
Ineligible parents;
•
Sponsors of noncitizens;
•
Ineligible spouses; and
•
Essential persons.
It does not matter whether these individuals have sufficient income or resources to
deem; they are still considered to be deemors.
The type of income such an individual receives (e.g., public income-maintenance payments
such as Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, VA pensions based on need) does not
exclude them from this definition.
