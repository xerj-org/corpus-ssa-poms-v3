SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.005 - How Do We Identify SSI Recipients Who are Fugitives or Probation or Parole Violators?
Effective Dates: 01/25/2024 - Present | TN 19 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Preview
Information regarding an SSI recipient's fugitive status or probation/parole violations
may come from a first party report, a report from the Office of the Inspector General
(OIG) or from a third party report. The development necessary for a case is determined
by the source of the report.
