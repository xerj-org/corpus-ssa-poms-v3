A. Background – SGA
SGA determinations are required to establish initial disability for SSI but are not
required for continuing SSI eligibility purposes. However, in concurrent cases, SGA
determinations are necessary for continuing eligibility of Title II benefits (DI 13010.025A). SGA determinations are also required for purposes of determining reimbursement
for services to vocational rehabilitation agencies (DI 13510.001) and employment networks (DI 55030.001). These determinations will in no way affect whether an individual is eligible for
regular or 1619 benefits.
