F. Procedure – Continuation of payments in PE cases
Take whatever action is necessary to effect payment continuation if:
•
In medical cessation cases, a request for reconsideration and a request for payment
continuation are received within 10 days after receipt of the disability determination,
see DI 12027.008 — DI 12027.020, and
•
In Goldberg Kelly cases, the appeal request is filed within 60 days after receipt
of the notice, see SI 02301.301.
NOTE: The 10 and 60 day timeframes can be extended if good cause for late filing is established.
