A. Introduction
The general rules on estimating income apply in deeming cases. However, when the income
of an ineligible spouse or parent is expected to result in no deemed income because
of income exclusions or allocations (or both), the field office (FO) may eliminate
some development or simplify entry of income data into the Supplemental Security Record.
The simplified development and entry rules are explained in subsections B. and C.
