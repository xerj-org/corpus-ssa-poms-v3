F. Procedure - Cumberland FO/initial claims
Process initial claims for blind or disabled children of military personnel stationed
overseas as follows:
1.
Determine whether the child meets the exception in SI
00501.415C.1.;
2.
Document the file using the procedure in this section SI
00501.416B.1.;
3.
Obtain a full application. (i.e., MSSICS Full Application);
4.
Create an EDCS Case. Include paper copies of the disability reports in the file;
5.
Attach the completed designated flag to the front of the folder (see SI
00501.419C, Exhibit 3 - Cumberland Flag);
6.
Establish the SSI record following the criteria in SI
00501.418B; and
7.
Send the case to OIO (see in this section SI 00501.416A.1.c. – OIO's Address) for a medical decision.
