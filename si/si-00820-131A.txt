A. Introduction
The “Income Summary” worksheets (SSA-5124) in SI 00820.131D may be reproduced and completed by the certifier to summarize and certify verified
earned and unearned income data from pay stubs or other documents, in lieu of photocopying
the documents for the file. A separate summary worksheet should be completed for each
type of income. Use of these worksheets is optional.
Electronic versions of the SSA-5124 can be found on the ChiNet Webpage and the InForm Library website.
