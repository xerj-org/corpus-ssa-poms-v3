SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.235 - Failure to Provide Information (N20)
Effective Dates: 04/24/2025 - Present | TN 81 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301235 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Procedure during personal followup contacts
During each telephone or face-to-face contact involving a request for needed information:
•
Remind the person of the information we need by the due date.
•
Explain to the recipient/payee that the recipient may lose other benefits based on
receiving SSI such as Medicaid, food stamps, State Medicare buy-in (SMIB) and public
housing if he or she does not comply with the request.
•
Consider the need for a payee (GN 00502.020) if the recipient does not understand the request or the possibility of losing SSI
eligibility.
•
Arrange for an interpreter if the person has limited English proficiency or is hearing-impaired,
refer to GN 00203.012.
•
Offer to help obtain the information, refer to SI 02301.200.
•
Grant more time to comply with the request if needed.
•
Discuss voluntary termination if the person does not want to pursue SSI eligibility,
refer to (SI 02301.230).
•
Stop and mail a follow-up notice if the person is hostile or abusive, refer to GN 00203.013.
