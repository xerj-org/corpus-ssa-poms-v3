SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02220.045 - Supplemental Security Income Overpayment Recovery from an Estate
Effective Dates: 12/14/2023 - Present | TN 134 (05-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502220045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
We must pursue recovery of Supplemental Security Income (SSI) overpayments from an
individual's estate, or any other liable individual, after the overpaid individual
has died. These instructions provide guidance on overpayment recovery from an estate.
