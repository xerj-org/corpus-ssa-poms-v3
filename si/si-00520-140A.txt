A. Introduction to Temporary Institutionalization (TI) benefits
Section 1611(e)(1)(G) of the Social Security Act provides that Supplemental Security
Income (SSI) recipients, who are temporarily institutionalized for medical care (and
who would otherwise receive a reduced benefit or none at all), can get benefits during
the first three full months of institutionalization to pay expenses to maintain their
home or living arrangement where they may return upon discharge. We refer to benefits
paid under section 1611(e)(1)(G) as temporary institutionalization (TI) benefits.
