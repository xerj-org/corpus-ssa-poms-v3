F. Procedures for clearing the RZ before completing CDR or disability redetermination
If you complete the non-medical RZ development, clear the RZ or close the RZ event.
Non-medical RZs do not require completion of the CDR or disability redetermination
and resolution of diaries associated with CDRs or disability redeterminations for
clearance.
