E. Procedure - other allowable expenses apply in obtaining title II
If a claimant incurs other allowable expenses besides attorney fees in obtaining the
title II income, add the amount of these expenses to the amount of authorized fees.
(See SI 02006.200A.1. above.) Use that sum of expenses to adjust the title II income as explained in
SI 02006.200.C.1. above.
