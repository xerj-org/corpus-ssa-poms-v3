SI 02301.001 SSI Posteligibility Changes
Overview
Changes in recipients’ circumstances can affect their eligibility and payment amounts.
This subchapter provides instructions on:
•
Recipients’ responsibilities for reporting changes that may affect SSI benefits,
•
Field office responsibilities for acting on reports or change and correcting benefits,
•
Assessing and collecting penalties for recipients’ failures to report changes on time
which adversely affect their SSI,
•
Reducing, suspending and terminating SSI benefits,
•
Reinstating SSI benefits,
•
Providing Goldberg/Kelly (GK) due process protections before reducing, suspending
or terminating SSI benefits.
