SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.018 - Marital Status: Question 5
Effective Dates: 03/28/2012 - Present | TN 58 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When to complete question 5
Complete 5(a) for all claimants. Follow the "Go To" instructions as appropriate.
1. Spouse is 65 or older or will attain age 65 within the evaluation period or is blind
or disabled
For determining if a protective filing exists when the claimant names a spouse on
a title XVI application, follow the instructions in SI 00601.020. For the protective filing closeout instructions, see SI 00601.037.
2. Spouse's Social Security number (SSN) is unknown
If the spouse's SSN is required but unknown, follow instructions for other names and
SSNs.
3. Other names and SSNs
If the spouse has used other names or SSNs, refer to SI 00604.017.
