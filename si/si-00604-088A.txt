SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.088 - In-Kind Support and Maintenance (ISM) to One Person or Other In-Kind Income: Question 52
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 5 (03-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604088 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to complete question 52
Complete for all claimants. Explain "YES" answers in "Remarks" by describing the item,
source, (name, address, street, city, State, ZIP code, and phone number), date received,
and value. (This type of income is not subject to the 14-month projection.) If "NO",
go to question 53.
NOTE: Do not confuse question 52 with question 38. Question 52 elicits information to clarify
whether the claimant (not the entire household) receives ISM or some other type of in-kind income that
benefits only the claimant. Question 38 elicits outside ISM that benefits all members of the household.
