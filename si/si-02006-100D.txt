SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02006.100 - Excluding Title II Offset Cases from Underpayment Review
Effective Dates: 04/15/2024 - Present | TN 20 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502006100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Examples
1. Case Excluded From Review - Amount At Risk = 0
a. Facts: SSI UP Computation
•
UP period = February 2023- July 2024;
•
UP for February - December 2023 = $914 per month;
•
UP for January 2024 - July 2024 = $943
•
Total UP = $16,655;
•
FO decides to issue SSI benefits prospectively and withhold the UP pending completion
of its review. SSI benefits for August 2024 are issued.
b. Facts: Offset
•
Title II benefit starting January 2023: $950;
•
Benefits due $950 title II income in February -December 2023, $979 January- July 2024;
•
Retroactive title II benefits are not paid until the FO resolves the SSI UP.
•
If we had paid the title II income due each month on time, they would have been due
$0 SSI. The offset amount would be $914 from February 2023 - December 2023 and $943
from January 2024 - July 2024. .

2. Case Excluded From Review - Amount At Risk is Less Than Review Threshold
a. Facts: SSI UP computation
•
UP period = February 2023 -July 2024;
•
UP for February - December 2023 - $914 per month;
•
UP for January - July 2024 - $943 per month;
•
Total UP = $16,655.00;
•
FO decides to issue benefits prospectively and withhold the UP pending completion
of its review. SSI benefits for August 2024 are issued.
b. Facts : Offset
•
First title II benefit: $320 in January 2023;
•
Due $320 title II income in February -December 2023;
•
Due $340.00 title II income January 2024- July 2024;
•
Retroactive title II benefits are not paid until the FO resolves the SSI UP;
•
If we had paid the title II income due in February 2023 through July 2024 on time,
they would have been due $614.00 per month for February through December and 634.00
from January 2024 on. The offset amount would be $300 per month for March on.
