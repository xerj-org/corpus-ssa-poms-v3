D. Policy -- qualified individual institutionalized – not LA
When a qualified individual continues to be eligible for SSI while institutionalized,
the addition of the essential person increment(s) follows the same rules as described
in SI 02009.050C. . If the qualified individual­ is ineligible for payment because they are an inmate
of a public institution, the essential person increment may be permanently lost depending
on the length of stay, (see SI 00501.110).
