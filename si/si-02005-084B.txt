B. Definitions
1. Federal Eligibility Amount
The Federal eligibility amount is the difference between the CM FBR and the CM FCI.
2. Value Of The One-Third Reduction (VTR)
The Value of the One-Third Reduction (VTR) is the income value assigned (for Federal
purposes) to in-kind support and maintenance received by an SSI recipient when living
in the household of another during the CM. (See SI 00835.200 ff. and SI 02005.084D.2.)
3. In-Kind Income
In-kind income is the value assigned to in-kind support and maintenance for MMSS purposes
only. It may or may not be the same amount as the VTR.
