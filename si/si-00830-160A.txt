SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.160 - Annuities, Pensions, Retirement, or Disability Payments
Effective Dates: 08/07/2024 - Present | TN 261 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830160 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions
1. Annuity
An annuity is a sum paid yearly or at other specific times in return for the payment
of a fixed sum. Annuities may be purchased by an individual or by an employer.
2. Pensions and retirement benefits
Pensions and retirement benefits are payments to a worker after retirement from employment.
These payments may be paid directly by a former employer, by a trust fund, an insurance
company, or other entity.
3. Disability benefits
Disability benefits are payments made because of injury or other disability.
