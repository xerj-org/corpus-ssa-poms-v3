Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502006100 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Process - Interaction of Underpayment and Offset
1. Case Under Review
When a case is under review, and SSI benefits are issued prospectively while SSI benefits
for prior months are withheld, the system posts UP's for prior months.
2. Title II Offset Computation
The system is able to perform the title II offset computation when there is an SSI
UP as long as there is an SSI payment due for a month in the windfall period.
