A. Introduction
SSI recipients depend on their benefits to meet critical financial needs. Our objective
is to pay recipients' benefits accurately and on time. We are also responsible for
taking timely actions that adversely affect SSI eligibility and payments. Overpayments
can cause additional hardship for recipients.
