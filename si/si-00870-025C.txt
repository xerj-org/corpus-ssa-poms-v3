C. PASS Checklists
To ensure the accuracy and consistency of PASS determinations, when processing each
SSA-545-BK and the supporting documentation complete the PASS Initial Application
Checklist.
Upon every PASS Review, complete the PASS Progress Review Checklist.
Once completed, record each Checklist in the Electronic Viewer (eView) or Evidence
Portal (EP)).
