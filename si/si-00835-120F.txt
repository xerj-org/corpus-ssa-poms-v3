F. Procedure — shelter for services
Follow these steps if the individual alleges that the landlord accepts services in
lieu of cash payment for rent.
•
Determine that the individual does not have rental liability if the individual alleges that the required rent is services
only (SI 00835.390).
•
Determine that the individual has rental liability if the alleged rent payment consists
partly of services and partly of cash.
•
Document that rental liability exists using curtailed or full development as appropriate.
