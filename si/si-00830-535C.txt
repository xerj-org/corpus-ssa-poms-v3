C. Policy for payments received under the WIOA
Any payments received under the WIOA are subject to the general rules pertaining to
income and income exclusions. Income received through WIOA programs are in the form
of wages, stipends, and incentive payments.  Some support received under the WIOA
are in the form of supportive services such as childcare, transportation, medical
care, meals, and other reasonable expenses, provided in cash or in-kind.
