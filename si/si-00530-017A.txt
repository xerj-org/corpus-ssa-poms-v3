A. Introduction
As discussed in earlier sections, warrant and SSR information are screened to determine
if good cause can be granted. If so, no suspension action on the warrant and no notice
are necessary. If not, we will send recipients a tailored Notice of Planned Action
that explains that their payments will be suspended and what specific documentation
they must provide to establish that either mandatory or discretionary good cause exists.
