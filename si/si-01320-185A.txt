SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.185 - Deeming - Earned Income of an Ineligible Child Who is a Student
Effective Dates: 05/07/2002 - Present | TN 39 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320185 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
If an ineligible child is a student (SI 00501.020), the child's earned income up to a monthly and yearly maximum does not reduce the
allocation for the ineligible child. The excluded amounts are the same as those applied
to earnings of eligible students and are subject to change annually. See SI 00820.510B. for a table reflecting Student Earned Income Exclusion (SEIE) amounts.
