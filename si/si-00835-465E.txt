E. Procedures — documenting household costs
1. Inside ISM
When the issue is inside ISM, document household operating expenses following the
instructions in SI 00835.340D.
2. Outside ISM
When the issue is outside ISM, document the CMV of household costs following the instructions
in SI 00835.350C.
3. Curtailment
For both inside and outside ISM, if the tax, mortgage, or rental cost of a residence
include land as well as shelter, do not undertake development to value the shelter
separately from the land.
