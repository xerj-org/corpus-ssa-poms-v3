A. Background on financial permission
In 1999, Congress amended the Social Security Act to grant the Commissioner authority
to verify financial accounts. In 2003, the Commissioner exercised this authority by
promulgating regulations to make giving permission to contact financial institutions
an eligibility requirement for Supplemental Security Income (SSI).
