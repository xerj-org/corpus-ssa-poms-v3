A. Introduction
The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) has numerous programs which make payments
to Supplemental Security Income (SSI) applicants, recipients, and their families.
For SSI purposes, the treatment of those VA payments depends on the nature of the
payments. The most common types of VA payments are explained in the following sections:
•
Pension, SI 00830.302
•
Compensation, SI 00830.304
•
Educational Assistance, SI 00830.306
•
Aid and Attendance Allowance, SI 00830.308
•
Housebound Allowance, SI 00830.308
•
Clothing Allowance, SI 00830.310
•
Payment Adjustment For Unusual Medical Expenses, SI 00830.312
•
Payments to Vietnam Veterans' Children with Spina Bifida, SI 00830.318
•
Insurance Payments, SI 00830.160 (for disability insurance) and SI 00830.545 (for life insurance).
