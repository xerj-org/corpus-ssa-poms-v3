C. What is not income
1. Vocational rehabilitation
Payments made as part of a VA program of vocational rehabilitation are not income
(SI 00815.050C), including any augmentation for dependents.
EXCEPTION: Subsistence allowances received during vocational rehabilitation may be augmented, but the dependent’s portion is not income.
2. Withdrawal of contributions
Any portion of a VA educational benefit that is a withdrawal of the veteran’s own
contribution is conversion of a resource and is not income. However, any portion of
the withdrawal that is retained into the month following the month of receipt is a
countable resource.
