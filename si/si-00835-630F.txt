F. Part V Summary
Use the summary section to total the actual values of ISM subject to the PMV rule.
Additional Information
Use this section to record State supplement calculations, State LA determinations,
or complex ISM calculations, such as those involving conversions. For information
on ISM and Household Conversions, see SI 00835.470.
