7. Summary
a.
The true OP period is March through May. The true OP is $204.
b.
The true OP of $204 is less than the OP that would have existed ignoring offset.
•
Ignoring offset, the OP period would have been May, June, and July, because the SSI
benefits for those months would have been the SSI benefits affected by VTR in March,
April, and May.
•
The amount paid was $1104 ($368 x 3), the amount due was $736.02 ($245.34 x 3), and
the OP would have been the difference: $367.98.
