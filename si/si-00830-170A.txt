SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.170 - Income Based on Need (IBON)
Effective Dates: 12/13/2007 - Present | TN 80 (04-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830170 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
Income based on need (IBON) is assistance:
1.
provided under a program which uses income as a factor of eligibility; and
2.
funded wholly or partially by the Federal government or a nongovernmental agency (e.g.,
Catholic Charities or the Salvation Army, the funds are provided specifically for
a formalized program whose general purpose is similar to that of the SSI program).
EXCEPTION: State supplementary payments (as defined in SI 01401.001 ff. made to refugees are not IBON (see SI
00830.175), despite involvement of Federal funds.
