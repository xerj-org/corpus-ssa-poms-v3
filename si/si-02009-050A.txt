SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02009.050 - Eligible Individual With Essential Person(s)
Effective Dates: 11/27/2002 - Present | TN 2 (04-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502009050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy -- general
When a qualified individual (see SI 00501.100) with­out an eligible spouse has an essential person(s), the FBR is increased by
an increment for each essential person (see SI 02001.020 for incremental amount). The qualified ­individual will continue to be entitled to
the essential per­son increment until there is a loss of the essential person status
(SI 00501.110).
