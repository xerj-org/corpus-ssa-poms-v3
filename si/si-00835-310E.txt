SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.310 - Distinguishing Between In-Kind Support and Maintenance (ISM) and Other Unearned In-Kind Income
Effective Dates: 09/14/2023 - Present | TN 109 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835310 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Examples distinguishing between different in-kind support and maintenance and other
unearned in-kind income
1. In-kind item which is not income
William's adult child Amber purchased a furnace for William's home when the old furnace
stopped working. Amber bought the furnace from the local utility company and paid
for it in monthly installments on a utility bill. The utility company confirmed that
William cannot return the furnace for cash since Amber made the purchase and it was
purchased on credit. Therefore, the gift of the furnace is not income to William.
2. ISM in the form of a mobile home
Victoria is an aged beneficiary living in an apartment. The landlord raises the rent.
Victoria's sibling, Alfred, decides to buy Victoria a mobile home to live in so that
Victoria can move from the apartment. Alfred puts the mobile home in both their names.
Alfred makes a down payment of $4,000 in April 2011, and loan payments of $400 each
month thereafter. Victoria moves into the mobile home in April, and lives there alone.
The sibling's down payment of $4,000 in April is ISM to Victoria. Alfred's loan payment
of $400 beginning in May is a shelter payment and is outside ISM. Since the actual
value of each source of ISM exceeds the PMV, we charge the PMV for April 2011 and
continuing. For more information on ISM to one person, see SI 00835.400A. For more information on ISM from outside household, see SI 00835.350.
3. ISM from outright gift of a home
The facts are the same as in Example 2 in SI 00835.310E.2., in this section, except that Victoria's sibling buys the mobile home and pays for
it in full, and registers the home in Victoria's 's name only. The purchase of the
home as a gift is ISM to Victoria in April, charged at the PMV. Beginning in May,
there is no chargeable ISM to Victoria, because Victoria owns the home, and there
is no mortgage payment.
