SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.060 - Exclusions Under Other Federal Statutes
Effective Dates: 03/14/2000 - Present | TN 39 (03-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130060 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
1. General
Federal statutes other than the Social Security Act sometimes provide that the SSI
program must exclude from resources (or income and resources) assistance provided
under those statutes. See SI 01130.050 for a list of exclusions and a guide to instructions about exclusions, including
those specified by other Federal statutes.
2. New Exclusions
Be aware that new or different assistance programs may have Federal involvement and
be subject to an exclusion. (Central Office does not always immediately know when
exclusions are enacted if they are handled by another agency.) Follow the guidelines
in this section when a program is questionable or someone alleges a new exclusion
or type of assistance.
