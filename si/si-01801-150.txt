SI 01801.150 Expedited Service for Purposes of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)
Benefits
A household in immediate need of food assistance may be entitled to what the Food
and Nutrition Service (FNS) calls “expedited service.” For a household entitled to
expedited service, the state SNAP office can issue coupons or an authorization-to-participate
(ATP) card to the recipient no later than seven days following the application date.
