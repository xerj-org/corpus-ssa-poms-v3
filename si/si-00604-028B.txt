B. Developing military service
Develop military service if:
•
the claimant is a qualified alien, and
•
appears to meet the military service requirements in SI 00502.140, and
•
does not meet another condition that permits eligibility (To found out when a qualified
alien can be eligible for SSI, see SI 00502.100A.3) or is in a time-limited category (for more information on the time-limited eligibility
aliens, see SI 00502.106).
