E. When to use Part III of the SSA-8006-F4
This part refers to the individual's responsibility to notify SSA of any changes that
could affect their benefit amount.
It authorizes sources to release information to SSA needed to verify allegations,
and it informs the individual, through a penalty clause, that failure to report any
changes could result in a penalty. There is also space for the signature of the individual
and space for witnesses, should their signatures be required. Part III must be completed whenever
the SSA-8006-F4 is completed in whole or in part.
