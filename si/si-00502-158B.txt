SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00502.158 - Individuals Who Received Informal Denials Based On Alien Eligibility Law In Effect Prior To 8/5/97
Effective Dates: 07/20/2026 - Present | TN 71 (07-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500502158 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedures
Apply the policy in SI 00502.158 to an SSI claim filed by an individual:
•
who submits evidence of a prior informal denial based on alien status; or
•
for whom acceptable evidence of a protective filing date can be established per GN 00204.010
Adjudicate the claim to award or denial following current POMS policies and procedures.
