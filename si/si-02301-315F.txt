F. Process — continuing payments during appeals
This chart describes how recipients receive payment continuation until the appeal
decision in a multiple payment or payment ceiling case.
When the payment continuation amount is...
then...
the correct amount on a C01 record
the system will continue to pay the correct amount without FO input.
an amount higher than the correct amount on a C01 record
the FO must use an A-OTP each month to pay the additional amount due (see SM 01901.001).
an amount higher than the correct amount on an M02 record
the FO must use force due to pay higher amount (see SM 01701.001).
