SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.045 - Completing Part II (Living Arrangements) of the SSA-8000-BK: General
Effective Dates: 09/10/2026 - Present | TN 82 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Time period for questions and answers
We designed the questions in Part II to document living arrangements as of the day
the claimant signs the form. Any changes in living arrangements since the first moment
of the filing date month should be recorded in question 39 and explained in Remarks.
1. Additional information needed
If there is a need to document a change in living arrangements that occurs after the
claimant files the claim and before adjudication, complete SSA-8006-F4 (Statement
of Living Arrangements, In-Kind Support and Maintenance) or the SSA-8007 (Statement
of Living Arrangements). For instructions on completing the SSA-8006-F4 or SSA-8007,
see SI 00835.600.
