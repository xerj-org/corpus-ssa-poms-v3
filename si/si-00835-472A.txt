SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.472 - Conversions — More than One Household Uses the Same Shelter Item
Effective Dates: 12/13/2023 - Present | TN 117 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835472 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
More than one household may use a shelter item in common. A shelter item used in common
is one used jointly by two or more households. For example, two households living
under one roof may use electricity that is billed on only one meter. Conversion of
such a bill includes determining the value of the shelter item to the eligible individual's
household and establishing who pays for the item.
