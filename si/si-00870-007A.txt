A. Policy for selecting the month to start a PASS
The Social Security Administration (SSA) makes a PASS effective with any month of
eligibility for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) or any month of potential eligibility,
assuming approval of the PASS, subject to the rules of administrative finality in
SI 04070.001.
