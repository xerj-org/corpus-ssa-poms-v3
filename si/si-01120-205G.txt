SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.205 - Uniform Transfers to Minors Act
Effective Dates: 01/31/2008 - Present | TN 90 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

G. Example
1. Situation
During a prior redetermination, the FO established that G. Smith irrevocably transferred
under the UTMA, the sum of $10,000 to their grandchild, E. Smith, a 14-year-old SSI
beneficiary. At the current redetermination, E.'s parent, who is the custodian of
the account, volunteers that E.'s parent withdrew $1,000 from the account to pay for
a 3-week vacation for E. at a summer camp with special facilities for disabled children.
2. Analysis
Regional instructions confirm that there are no legal prohibitions in the State that
preclude UTMA withdrawals for a minor's benefit. The CR determines that the withdrawal
is not income to E. because the custodian did not disburse it to E. nor does it represent
a third party vendor payment of food or shelter. The CR updates the CG field to show
the new UTMA balance.
