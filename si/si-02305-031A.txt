SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.031 - Type of Redetermination (RZ) Interview - SSI Claims System and Paper Forms
Effective Dates: 09/16/2026 - Present | TN 106 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305031 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The following is the policy on what type of RZ interview to conduct.
1. SSI Claims System
RZs should be completed using the SSI Claims System whenever possible.
RZs must only be conducted during face-to-face or telephone interviews, not through
the mail.
Use telephone interviews whenever possible so the recipient, payee, or any deemors
do not miss work or medical appointments. Subsection A.3 below explains when face-to-face
interviews are required.
2. SSA-8203-BK
If an RZ cannot be processed in the SSI Claims System, conduct a face-to-face or telephone
interview using form SSA-8203-BK.
The SSA-8203-BK is not a self-help form. Assist the recipient or payee with completing the form.
If a recipient or payee requests a blank form, do not refuse to provide one; however,
inform the individual that he or she will still need to complete a face-to-face or
telephone interview to complete the RZ.
3. Face-to-Face interview generally required
In the following situations, face-to-face RZ interviews are generally required.
a.
Representative payee suitability development, see SI 02305.123.
b.
Disability redeterminations, see DI 11070.001 and DI 25201.010.
c.
A qualified alien with a 7-year time limit, see SI 02305.082.
4. Group interviews
Group RZ interviews are not allowed.
