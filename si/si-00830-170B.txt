SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.170 - Income Based on Need (IBON)
Effective Dates: 12/13/2007 - Present | TN 80 (04-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830170 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy Principle
Income based on need is counted as income dollar for dollar, unless it is totally
excluded by statute (e.g., food stamps) or excluded under a PASS (SI 00870.001 ff.). The $20 general income exclusion (SI 00810.420) does not apply to IBON.
NOTE: If a nongovernmental agency is involved, consider whether the assistance qualifies
for exclusion as HEA/SMA (SI 00830.605) or meets the definition of a social service (SI 00815.050).
