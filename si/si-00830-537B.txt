B. Policy - AmeriCorps State and National and AmeriCorps NCCC payments effective with
benefits payable on or after September 1, 2008
1. Policy - exclusion
Effective with benefits payable on or after September 1, 2008, cash or in-kind payments
provided by AmeriCorps State and National or AmeriCorps NCCC are excluded from income
for SSI purposes, even if they meet the definition of wages.
Such payments include, but are not limited to:
•
Living allowance payments
•
Stipends
•
Food and shelter
•
Clothing allowance
•
Educational awards
•
Payments in lieu of educational awards.
2. Policy - living arrangements/temporary absence
Participants are considered to be in LA/A. Temporary absence rules do not apply for
living arrangement purposes.
