D. Procedure for developing the resource exclusion for payments from a State eugenics
compensation program
1. When to develop
Develop this exclusion only when an individual alleges the receipt of eugenics compensation
payments excludable under this provision and the exclusion allows eligibility for
Supplement Security Income (SSI) benefits.
2. How to develop
Determine if the alleged or verified eugenics compensation amount was excludable from
income under SI 00830.736, Payments to Victims from a State Eugenics Compensation Programs.
•
If the income exclusion applied, exclude the unspent portion of the compensation amount
retained the month after receipt as a resource.
•
If the income exclusion did not apply, count as a resource the unspent portion of
the payment.
