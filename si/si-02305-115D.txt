SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.115 - Residence Address - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 107 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
1. Documentation - the SSI Claims system
a.
Document the residence address on the Residence Address and Jurisdiction (LRES) page
under the Living Arrangements tab in the SSI Claims system, see MS 08110.007.
b.
Document the mailing address on the Personal Information page (APID) under the General
ID tab.
2. Documentation - Paper Form SSA-8203-BK
Document the residence address in the space provided or in the REMARKS section of
the paper RZ form.
If the mailing address is different than the residential address, document the mailing
address in the REMARKS section of the paper RZ form.
3. Representative Payee Involvement
For proper input of residence address see SM 01301.485C. and MSOM RPS 002.006.
