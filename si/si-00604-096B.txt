SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.096 - Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) (Formerly Known as Food Stamps): Question 58
Effective Dates: 04/08/2019 - Present | TN 8 (04-19)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604096 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When and how to complete question 58
1.
Complete 58(a) for all claimants.
2.
The "Go To" instructions in (a) will lead you through the appropriate questions.
3.
Remember to ask if you may take a SNAP application if the claimant has received a
SNAP recertification notice in the last 30 days.
4.
See SI 01801.010 for detailed instructions on completing the SNAP questions.
