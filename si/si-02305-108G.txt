G. Procedure - Developing Allegations of TPL
Develop allegations about third party liability, as follows:
•
If recipients allege no change in TPL and other information in SSA
records agrees, do not develop TPL any further.
•
If recipients allege a change in TPL or no change in TPL and other
information in SSA records conflicts, develop per SI 01730.045.
•
If recipients refuse to provide TPL information, follow instructions in SI 01730.045.
