E. Development and documentation — personal property used by an employee
1. Individual's Statement
If an individual alleges owning items that are used in their work as an employee;
or, for months of eligibility before May 1, 1990, that their employer required they
provide as a condition of employment, obtain their statement either signed or recorded
on a Report of Contact page which includes:
•
the name, address, and telephone number of the employer;
•
a general description of the items;
•
a general description of their duties; and
•
whether the items are currently being used.
If the individual is temporarily not working (e.g., job loss, seasonal employment),
or the property is not otherwise in current use, see SI 01130.504.
2. Supporting Evidence
Absent evidence to the contrary, accept the individual's statement.
