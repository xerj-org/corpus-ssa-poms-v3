A. Introduction
The Federal Government through the Administration on Aging is involved in a variety
of programs for older Americans. The programs may be operated by State or local governments
or community organizations. Some types of programs are:
•
health services;
•
nutrition services (see SI 00830.635);
•
legal assistance; and
•
community service employment, such as Senior Community Service Employment Program
(SCSEP).
