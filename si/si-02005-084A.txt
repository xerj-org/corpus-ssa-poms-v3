SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02005.084 - MMSS Eligibility and Payment Computations Under RMA
Effective Dates: 09/08/2023 - Present | TN 24 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502005084 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
MMSS payments are always computed using countable income received in the CM. To be
eligible for MMSS, an individual's MIL must be greater than the sum of:
•
CM FCI/SCI;
•
In-kind income in the CM, if any; and
•
Federal eligibility amount.
