SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.135 - Deeming Concept - Essential Person
Effective Dates: 04/13/1999 - Present | TN 6 (04-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310135 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
An essential person is someone who:
•
has continuously lived in the individual's home since December 1973; and
•
was not eligible for State assistance in December 1973; and
•
has never been eligible for SSI benefits as an eligible individual or as an eligible
spouse; and
•
State records show that, under a State plan in effect for June 1973, the State took
that person's needs into account in determining the qualified individual's need for
State assistance for December 1973.
