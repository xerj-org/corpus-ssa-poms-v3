SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.250 - Efforts to Locate Unsuccessful
Effective Dates: 08/05/2022 - Present | TN 15 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530250 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
1.
Review all information provided by OIG and, if applicable, suspend SSI benefits effective
with the first day of whichever of the following months is earlier:
•
The month in which the warrant is issued, or in which the court order or decision,
or order or decision by an appropriate agency in the United States (e.g. parole board)
is issued which finds that the individual is wanted for prosecution, or custody or
confinement after conviction for a felony, or is violating (or violated) a condition
of their parole or probation, or
•
The first month during which the individual fled to avoid such prosecution, custody
or conviction, or violated a condition of parole or probation, if indicated on the
warrant, court order or decision.
2.
Annotate appropriate Remarks on SSR. For example, RECIPIENT IS A FUGITIVE FELON WANTED
FOR EMBEZZLEMENT. JURISDICTION OF NEW YORK CITY POLICE DEPT 555-555-5555. LE UNABLE
TO LOCATE; NEW ADDRESS INFORMATION SHOULD BE REPORTED TO OIG, JOE FRIDAY 444-444-4444.
3.
Per instruction in SI 00530.017, prepare a Notice of Planned Action (SSA-L8155-U2) using the appropriate Document
Processing System (DPS) notice.
NOTE: If the individual did not answer fugitive questions on an SSI application or redetermination
(forms completed before October 23, 2000), the rules of Administrative Finality should
be taken into consideration (See SI 00530.500 through SI 00530.510).
