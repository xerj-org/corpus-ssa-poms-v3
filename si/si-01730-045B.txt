Effective Dates: 07/27/2016 - Present | TN 10 (06-96)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501730045 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy — TPL sources
1. Definition of Third Party
Third Party — A third party is any source, including health insurance, which is legally liable
to pay medical bills before money from the Medicaid program, a welfare program or
needs-based program (such as SSI) is spent.
2. Examples of Third Party
Examples of third parties include, but are not limited to:
•
Employer, private purchase and union membership based health insurance, sheltered
workshops, continuation of health insurance coverage under statute (“COBRA continuation”) and coverage available from an employer under the Employee Retirement Income Security
Act (ERISA)
•
Medical support derived from noncustodial parents
•
Armed Forces and the Public Health Service
•
Pending lawsuits or no-fault clauses or State laws covering accidents, product liability,
and Workers' Compensation
•
Employee conversion/extension rights
•
Fraternal and benefit societies and churches and church groups
•
Insurance purchased or endowed as part of a college fee
•
Membership in a health maintenance organization (except for those with a contract
under Medicare/Medicaid)
•
See SI 01730.050E., Exhibit 5, “Types of Insurance”
