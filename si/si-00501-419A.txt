A. Exhibit 1 - Modernized Development Worksheet (MDW) to Cumberland
1.
Send an MDW to the Cumberland FO (See MSOM MDW 001.002 - Instructions on Accessing the MDW).
2.
Complete the entire MDW and use the following information when completing the following
fields.
•
Show “275” as the FO;
•
Show “DC” as the ID;
•
Complete applicant's contact information (e.g., name, foreign address and phone number,
etc.);
•
If reporting a COA, in MISC show “Attention Military Children Overseas Coordinator”, date expected to join military
parent overseas MM/DD/YY, and date case file mailed MM/DD/YY;
•
If reporting an Inquiry or Appeal Request, in MISC show “Attention Military Children Overseas Coordinator;
•
Show “MIL CHILD” as the ISSUE;
•
Show “T16CG” as the CATEGORY;
•
Document REMARKS with any available information in SI 00501.416B.1.;
•
If a protective writing is received, show the protective filing receipt date in REMARKS; and
•
If reporting an Appeal Request, show the date the Appeal Request was received.
NOTE: See MSOM MDW 001.003 for a description of the fields in MDWscreens.
