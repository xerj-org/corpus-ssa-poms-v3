A. Introduction
The SSA-8006-F4 is an interviewing and development tool for LA and ISM. The answers
to the questions on the SSA-8006-F4 document the individual's allegations regarding:
•
LA basis (SI 00835.001);
•
ISM subject to the value of the one-third reduction (VTR) (SI 00835.200 through SI 00835.210) or the presumed maximum value (PMV) rule (SI 00835.300 through SI 00835.320); and
•
cash income from within and outside a household (SI 00835.450).
