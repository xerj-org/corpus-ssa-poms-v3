Effective Dates: 09/22/2026 - Present | TN 82 (09-26)

B. Documenting oral inquiries
SSA technicians will use SSA systems (e.g., Modernized Claims System (MCS), Consolidated
Claims Experience (CCE), Appointment Scheduling Calendar (ASC), Enhanced Leads and
Appointment System (eLAS)) to document the protective filing based on the oral inquiry.
