B. Policy
The Supplemental Security Income Request for Information is a general-purpose letter
we use to request eligibility information and schedule appointments with individuals
in posteligibility (PE) situations.
The two versions of the Supplemental Security Income Request for Information letter
are:
SSA-L8003-U2 for individuals other than 1619(b) eligibles; and
SSA-L8004-U2 for 1619(b) eligibles only.
These letters are available in English and Spanish in the Document Processing System
(DPS), under the national and PE tabs.
