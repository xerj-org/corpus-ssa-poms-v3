G. Procedure - developing allegations of liquid resources
This section provides procedures for developing recipient responses to resource(s)
questions during the RZ interview.
NOTE: Effective November 18, 2006 permission to contact financial institutions must be requested at all RZs initiated
by FOs.
Open a RZ with recaps in MSSICS and use the appropriate screens (including the AFIP
screen) to complete the RZ.
Documentation instructions for MSSICS and non-MSSICS cases are in SM 01501.250B.
Develop allegations about liquid resources, as follows:
1. If recipients allege no resources and other information in SSA records agrees.
If recipients allege no resources and other information in SSA records agrees, do
not develop that item any further.
2. If recipients allege no resources and other information in SSA records conflicts
If recipients allege no resources and other information in SSA records conflicts,
determine whether they disposed of the resource that we have on record.
If so, develop per SI 01150.110.
If not, develop per SI 02305.101.
3. If recipients allege resources
If recipients allege resources, develop, as necessary, per SI 02305.101H considering the liquid resource verification tolerance (SI 01140.010B.2.).
