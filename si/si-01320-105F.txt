F. Per Capita Funds - Certain Tribes or Groups (P.L. 93-134)
Exclusion of per capita judgment funds to tribes or groups under section 7 of Public
Law 93-134, as amended (see SI 00830.830C.1.). This applies to the income of sponsors of aliens only if the alien lives in the sponsor's household. Prior to April 1, 1987, this exclusion
applied regardless of the alien's residence.
