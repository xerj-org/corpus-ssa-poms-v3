E. Letter to advise rebuttal rights
Use DPS to send a rebuttal letter.
•
For initial claims, the “General” folder contains the “Rebuttal Rights Notification”
letter.
•
For post entitlement events (PE), the “Post Entitlement” folder contains the SSI “Rebuttal
Rights” letter.
NOTE: Allow 30 calendar days for a reply if a letter is sent to the individual explaining
their rebuttal rights. If no reply is received by close of business of the 30th calendar
day, and the individual has not indicated a need for assistance, charge the PMV.
