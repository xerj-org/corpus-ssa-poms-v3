K. Procedure for property in the Northern Mariana Islands
For property in the Northern Mariana Islands, use an SSA-562-U3 (Request for Assistance
Certification) to ask the Resident Representative in Saipan to obtain evidence that
we cannot obtain locally (see GN 00904.080).
