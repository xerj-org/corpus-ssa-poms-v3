A. Introduction
Frequently, the mailing address and the residence address of claimants/ recipients
are the same. Ordinarily, there is no reason to doubt a claimant's or recipient's
allegation as to residence. However, recipients do change address, reside in institutions
or allege a State/county of residence that seems improbable. This subsection provides
some guidelines. Usually the State and county of residence is clear from other information
in the file or on the Supplemental Security Income Record Display (SSIRD).
