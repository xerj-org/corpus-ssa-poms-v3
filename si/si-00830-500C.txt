C. Policy – Income Treatment–Benefits Payable Beginning July 1, 2004
Section 430 of P.L. 108-203, Social Security Protection Act of 2004, provides that
dividends or interest earned on resources not excluded under §1613(a) of the Social
Security Act or excluded under other Federal statutes are excluded income for SSI
benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004.
The following describes when dividends or interest are considered income or excluded
income:
