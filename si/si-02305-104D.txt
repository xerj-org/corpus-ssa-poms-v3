D. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK - Question 20
Question 20 on the SSA-8203-BK — Do you (or your spouse living with you) own or are you buying
any real estate (land or buildings or other structures on the land)? (Include property
outside the U.S., inherited property, and life estates. Do not include your home.)
