B. Policy for the income exclusion from a State eugenics compensation program
Effective October 7, 2016, exclude from countable income all eugenics compensations
payments paid by a State eugenics compensation program. See resource exclusion development
in Payments from a State Eugenics Compensation Program SI 01130.736.
