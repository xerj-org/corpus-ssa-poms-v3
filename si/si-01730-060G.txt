G. Region VII Kansas City
1. State of Iowa
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Iowa Department of Human Services, Iowa Medicaid Enterprise (IME)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory and optional
State supplements. The State makes determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and third party
coverage on the SSI application, and at all redeterminations.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the local Human Services Office for a Medicaid eligibility determination.
Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
2. State of Kansas
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Kansas Department of Health and Environment
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of the mandatory
State supplement utilizing title XVI criteria.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the local medical assistance office for a Medicaid eligibility
determination. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral instructions.
3. State of Missouri
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Missouri Department of Social Services, MO HealthNet Division
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients utilizing more restrictive criteria
than title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and
third party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referral
Refer SSI claimants to the local Department of Social Services office for a Medicaid
eligibility determination. Refer to regional supplements for any additional referral
instructions.

•
Name of Administering State Agency
Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes determinations for SSI recipients utilizing more restrictive criteria

•
Additional Medicaid Questions

•
Medicaid Referral
