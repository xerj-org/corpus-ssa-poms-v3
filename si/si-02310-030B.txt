SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02310.030 - E2 Diary - Potential Rental Subsidy Development Needed
Effective Dates: 05/26/2026 - Present | TN 74 (05-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502310030 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. E2 Diary
1. E2 diary set annually
During the annual systems COLA run, our system will set an E2 diary on a SSR when:
•
the rental subsidy amount is greater than zero for the most recent LA period, and
•
the current payment status on the SSR is C01, M02, or N01.
2. E2 diary processing instructions
a. E2 diaries in the 2025 COLA run
For E2 diaries in the 2025 COLA run, insert a new living arrangement period effective
September 30, 2024 when:
•
the SSI recipient's current living arrangement requires a rental subsidy decision,
and
•
the SSI recipient was not living in New York, Connecticut, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin,
Vermont, or Texas on September 30, 2024.
b. E2 diaries in the annual COLA run
To clear an E2 diary, follow the instructions in SI 00835.380 to determine if rental subsidy development is necessary for January 1st of the current
year. Even if there are no changes to the amount of the rental subsidy after the rental
amount and CMRV are verified, create a new LA effective January 1st of the year in
question (12/31/CCYY) and update the rental amount and CMRV.
c. Unable to obtain the information
If you followed the instructions in SI 008353.380, you are unable to obtain the information
from the claimant or landlord after two attempts, and 30 days have passed, then document
all attempts on a Report of Contact and clear the diary via the UDIA screen in the
Direct SSR update prior to closing the Limited Issue (LI) or redetermination (RZ).
