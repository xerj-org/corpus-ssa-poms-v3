SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.150 - Deeming Concept - Qualified Individual
Effective Dates: 04/13/1999 - Present | TN 6 (04-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definition
A qualified individual is one who:
•
was a recipient of aid or assistance for December 1973 under a State plan approved
under title I, X, XIV, or XVI of the SSACT; and
•
the State took into account the needs of another person in deciding need for State
assistance for December 1973; and
•
the other person was living in the home of the recipient in December 1973; and
•
the other person was not eligible for State assistance for December 1973.
