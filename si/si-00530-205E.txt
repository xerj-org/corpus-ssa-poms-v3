E. Procedure -- completing the OI-5E
When processing fugitive felon cases, field office personnel are required to complete
an electronic OI-5E feedback form to advise OIG of the results of the fugitive felon
referral so that clearance data is transmitted by OIG to FFMI.
If payments are suspended, complete the OI-5E with the following information:
•
payments were suspended on (mm/dd/yy) – insert date that OI-5E was completed;
•
monthly payment at time of suspension;
•
total overpayment; and
•
name, title and telephone number of person completing form.
If payments are not suspended, the OI-5E should be completed and submitted to explain
why no action is required. (Refer to SI 00530.015C.7.). Otherwise, the case will remain pending on the FO FFMI listing.
Refer to SI 00530.025 “Deferral of Overpayment Recovery”
