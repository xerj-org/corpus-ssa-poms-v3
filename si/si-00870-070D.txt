SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00870.070 - Suspension, Termination Or Resumption Of A Plan to Achieve Self-Support (PASS)
Effective Dates: 12/16/2024 - Present | TN 52 (04-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500870070 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Effective Dates
1. Income Excluded Under PASS
A PASS is suspended or terminated effective the month the PASS expert issues a notice
of suspension or termination. Set-aside income will count for eligibility and payment
calculations as of the suspension or termination month. The individual must account
for any set-aside income throughout the month of the suspension or termination event;
this includes any months affected by Retrospective Monthly Accounting (RMA) (see SI 02005.001).
2. Resources Only Excluded Under PASS
A PASS, under which only resources are set aside, is suspended or terminated effective
with the month the PASS expert issues notice of suspension or termination. Previously
set-aside resources count as of the first moment of the month following the suspension
or termination letter unless otherwise excluded (see SI 01130.000 ff.). It does not matter whether the individual plans to use these funds for the
planned expenditures after the PASS expert suspends or terminates the plan.
