Effective Dates: 01/03/2024 - Present | TN 16 (09-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500510025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure — Document the Determination
Record the FO determination for failure to pursue the other program benefit and document
the claims file. For MSSICS, document the Report of Contact page.
Explain in the determination:
•
What step(s) the individual failed to take, and
•
What assistance, if any, SSA provided to the individual.
REMINDER: Before finding failure to take all appropriate steps, consider the individual's and/or
their representative's physical and mental ability to take the necessary steps.
