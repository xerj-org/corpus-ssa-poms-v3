H. Procedure to document NHRP ownership
Follow the steps in this section to document NHRP ownership in the SSI Claims system.
1. Sole ownership
Absent evidence to the contrary, accept a claimant, recipient, or deemors allegation
of sole ownership of property.
Document the allegation either via a signed Person Statement (or SSA-795) or on a
Report of Contact (or SSA-5002).
2. Shared ownership
Document an allegation of shared ownership with any of the following evidence:
•
a tax assessment notice or bill;
•
a current mortgage statement;
•
a deed;
•
a report of title search; or
•
a will, court record, or other documentation of inheritance.
If the claimant, recipient, or deemor alleges owning anything other than an equal
share of the property (e.g., alleges having a 25 percent ownership interest where
there are only two owners), the evidence must support the specific ownership level
alleged. Upload any associated evidence into the electronic folder using the EP.
