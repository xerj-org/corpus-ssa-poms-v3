SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02005.025 - Prospective Accounting for Certain Income Based on Need
Effective Dates: 09/08/2023 - Present | TN 24 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502005025 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Definitions
1. Retrospective Monthly Accounting
Retrospective monthly accounting (RMA) means that the SSI benefit for a month is computed
using income from a prior month.
2. Prospective Accounting
Prospective accounting means that the SSI benefit for a month is computed using income
received in that same month.
