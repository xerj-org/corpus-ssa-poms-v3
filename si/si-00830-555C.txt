SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.555 - Trade Readjustment Act Payments
Effective Dates: 03/30/2000 - Present | TN 81 (03-00)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830555 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Procedure
Use documents in the individual's possession (such as an award letter) to verify the
nature and amount of these payments. If such documents are not available, use locally
established procedures to contact the State unemployment office to obtain this information.
Document the verification on the Evidence (EVID) screen or a report of contact with
the State unemployment office.
