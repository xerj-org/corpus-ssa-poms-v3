SI 02009.060 Presumptive Disability/Blindness Involvement In Computations
POLICY
The month a presumptive disability/blindness (PDIPB) finding is made is considered
to be the initial month of eligibility for computing the PD/PB payment. (See DI 11055.230). The month an application is filed (if earlier than the month the PD/PB determination
is made) is not controlling. In a situation in which the effec­tive filing date is
in the first month of a calendar quarter but the PD/PB determination is made in the
second or third month of the quarter, the computation of PD/PB payments in the initial
quarter of eligibility is monthly; (i.e., the effective filing date is deemed to be
the first day of the month in which the PD/PB determination is made). This monthly
computation is mandated by Regu­lations 20 CFR 416.952. A quarterly computation will be used to compute PD/PB payment in a subsequent quar­ter. Even though
only one or two months of PD/PB pay­ments are made in the subsequent quarter income
for the entire quarter is considered and apportioned over the 3 months. Systems input
and force due/pay requirements are given in SM 01701.000 ff. All other computation
rules in SI 02009.001-SI 02009.055 apply. See DI 11055.230 for PD/PB requirements. See SI 02009.025 for couple status change computations in situations where one member of a couple
bas been deter­mined eligible and the other member starts or stops receiving PD/PB
payments in the second or third month of a quarter. Short quarter computations will
be used in the latter situations.
The payment amount will be recomputed when a final Allowance determination is made.
A payment that was initially computed monthly may require a quarterly re-computation
