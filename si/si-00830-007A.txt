A. Introduction
Some types of income and income exclusions have special rules that apply in posteligibility
(PE) situations. For example, in the section on rental income, if an SSI recipient
with rental income reports a new expense, the remaining income estimates may need
to be recalculated.
When no specific instructions exist, however, the general rules in this subsection
apply.
