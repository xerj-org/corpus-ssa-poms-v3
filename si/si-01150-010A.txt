SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.010 - Notifying Individuals About the Effect of Resource Transfers
Effective Dates: 05/12/2009 - Present | TN 60 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501150010 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Section 206 of P.L. 106-169 (Foster Care Independence Act of 1999) provides for a
period of ineligibility for SSI (up to 36 months) for an individual who disposes of
resources for less than FMV. This applies to resource transfers made on or after 12/14/99.
SSA is required to notify the public about this new penalty provision as well as the
potential effect of a transfer on Medicaid eligibility.
Note:
The period of ineligibility for transfers at less than fair market value does not
apply to transfers made by a deemor unless the deemor co-owns the resource with the
SSI applicant or recipient, or is the spouse of an SSI applicant or recipient. For
example, the provision does not apply to a resource transfer made by a parent who
is a deemor (unless the eligible child and parent are co-owners of the resource).
