The State can recover the $200 paid for May, June, August, and September. The State
cannot recover any money for April since the claimant was not eligible to receive
an SSI payment in April (E02 month). The State cannot recover any money for July since
the claimant was not eligible for an SSI payment. The recipient is eligible to receive
$286.00 of the retroactive SSI payment ($1,086 minus the $800 reimbursed to the State).
