A. Introduction
Some items received by an individual are not income because they do not meet the definition of income in SI 00810.005. Other items are income by definition but are excluded from an individual’s income by Federal statute (see SI 00830.099 – Guide to Unearned Income Exclusions).
The adjudicator must distinguish between an item which is not income by definition
and an item which is excluded from income by Federal statute. The field office can
determine if an item does not meet the definition of income using the policy below.
Only those items specifically listed in the law and regulations as income exclusions
can be excluded from income.
