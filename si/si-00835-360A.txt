SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.360 - When to Charge In-Kind Support and Maintenance (ISM) from Third Party Vendor Payments
Effective Dates: 10/31/2023 - Present | TN 113 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835360 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Definitions of a third party, vendor, and third party vendor payments
1. Third party
A third party is any person or entity other than the eligible individual, couple,
or the vendor.
2. Vendor
A vendor is a:
•
seller of goods or services (e.g., a merchant, retailer, contractor); or
•
person or entity who takes the place of the vendor in a transaction by paying the
vendor for goods or services (e.g., a credit card company, bank, or other creditor).
3. Third party vendor payment (TPVP)
A third party vendor payment (TPVP) is a payment made directly to a vendor by a third
party for goods or services the vendor provided to an eligible individual, or couple.
