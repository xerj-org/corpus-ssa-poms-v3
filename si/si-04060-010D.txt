D. Procedure
If the claimant disputes the facts of the determination or decision, even because of a constitutional issue, the claimant
must use the regular administrative appeals process. Be sure the claimant understands
that once all parties have signed the EAP agreement, the claimant will have no further
appeal rights within the administrative appeals process.
1. FO EAP responsibilities
Follow the FO procedures in GN 03107.150.
2. RO EAP responsibilities
Follow the RO procedures in GN 03107.250.
