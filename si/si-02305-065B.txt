SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.065 - RZs as Part of Effectuation of an Appellate Decision
Effective Dates: 12/07/2005 - Present | TN 53 (12-05)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305065 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
An unscheduled RZ is required after all appellate reversals EXCEPT when all of the
following conditions are met.
•
The ALJ reverses an unfavorable CDR determination; and,
•
The individual is receiving Statutory Benefit Continuation (SBC) payments; and,
•
There has been a RZ during the time period beginning with the first month of SBC payment
and ending with the month of the appellate determination.
NOTE: SBC payments are not made for appeals beyond the ALJ level.
