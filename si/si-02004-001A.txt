A. Introduction
This section provides information and guidance on emergency advance payments (EAP)
and Title XVI Immediate Payments (IP). The EAP and IP are two methods of expedited
payments made only in the field office (FO) within the Third Party Payment System (TPPS). Effective
02/09/2026, we issue these payments via a United States Debit Card (USDC). For more
information about a USDC, refer to TPPS and GN 02402.021.
