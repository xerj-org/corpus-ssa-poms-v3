A. Introduction
The Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996, Public
Law (P.L.) 104-193, establishes a new point at which supplemental security income
(SSI) payments can begin. We are calling this date the application effective date.
The change in law is effective for applications filed on or after 8/22/96.
