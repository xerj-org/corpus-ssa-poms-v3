Effective Dates: 01/04/2008 - Present | TN 15 (01-24)

C. Procedure - Routing the SSA-L8051-U3
1.
Give or send the first page of the SSA-L8051-U3 to the Title XVI claimant/recipient.
2.
Keep the FO copy for control purposes.
3.
The Title II adjudicator will complete Page 3 of the form and return it to the Title
XVI adjudicator when Title II status is obtained.
