SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02301.005 - SSI Posteligibility - Recipient Reporting
Effective Dates: 03/11/2026 - Present | TN 86 (03-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502301005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
Recipients or their representative payees must report changes on time that can affect
SSI benefits. SSA employees explain these responsibilities to recipients and payees
during each interview. SSA also provides written explanations of reporting responsibilities.
