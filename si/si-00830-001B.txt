B. Description of the subchapter
The instructions in this subchapter  apply to unearned income and unearned income
exclusions.
The subchapter is organized so that payments which are similar in nature are grouped
together. Unearned income exclusions and counting rules are not all in one place.
Those related to a specific type of payment are discussed in sections about the payment
itself.
