E. Procedure to maintain an electronic PASS library
Effective December 17, 2019, maintain an electronic library of approved and disapproved
PASS cases. Before you store documentation for a PASS, first determine whether the
document needs to be stored electronically according to instructions in GN 00301.000. Record PASS documentation in the certified electronic folder in eView or EP.
Use the material in this library for reviews of PASS changes, progress checks and
reviews, expense evaluations, and as templates for new plans. While it is not necessary
to retain all documents associated with each case, you must retain the SSA-545-BK,
all notices, appeal requests, verification of savings and expenditures, completed
checklists per SI 00870.025C, and final accounting in all cases.
Follow national folder retention policy to determine destruction dates for all PASS
folders regardless of the creation date, see SM 01201.910A.
Situation
Destruction Period
PASS Denials
Two years after the final decision date.
PASS Terminations (Regardless of the reason for the termination.)
Six years after the PASS termination notice date of the most recently awarded PASS.
NOTE: To determine when a PASS terminates follow the instructions in SI 00870.070.
Before destroying a folder, consider the following:
1.
If the PASS suspension results in an overpayment, delay destruction of the folder
until 10 years after collection actions have terminated.
2.
At the discretion of the PASS Cadres, the PASS Specialist may choose to retain non-electronic
PASS folders longer than the periods described above when the PASS is complex and
warrants retention of the folder.
3.
Do not destroy any records that are subject to an Office of General Counsel (OGC)
litigation hold until OGC instructs you that the hold is no longer in place, consistent
with the Agency Litigation Preservation Policy, AIMS GAM 14.17.
