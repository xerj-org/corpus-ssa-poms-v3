B. Policy for resource exclusion of payments for clinical trial participation
Effective May 1, 2011, exclude from countable resources the first $2,000 of compensation
received per calendar year by an SSI beneficiary, spouse, or deemor for participating
in a clinical trial for a rare disease or condition that meets the income exclusion
criteria listed in SI 00830.735, Payments for Clinical Trials Participation.
IMPORTANT: Excluded clinical trial compensation payments do not need to be separately identifiable.
For more information on how to identify commingled funds (excluded with non-excluded
funds), see SI 01130.700.
