SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.018 - Blind and Disabled Child SSI Recipients Overseas - RZs
Effective Dates: 10/04/2007 - Present | TN 56 (10-07)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
Effective April 1, 1990, blind or disabled children who are living with a parent who
is a member of the United States (U.S.) Armed Forces assigned to permanent duty ashore
outside the United States, Puerto Rico, and the territories and possessions of the
U.S. may continue to receive SSI payments.
