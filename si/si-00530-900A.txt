SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.900 - What Security Measures Should Field Offices Take When Interviewing an Individual Identified as a Fugitive or a Parole or Probation Violator?
Effective Dates: 07/29/2022 - Present | TN 14 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530900 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Generally, the field office manager is in the best position to decide how to handle
sensitive interviews in their office. Factors such as the severity of the crime and
the history of prior interviews with the individual should be considered in decisions
about the level of security needed.
