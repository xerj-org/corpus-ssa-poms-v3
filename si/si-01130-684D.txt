D. How to verify receipt of an FVECF payment
Verify that the payment is from the FVECF by:
•
Reviewing VA documents provided by the claimant;
•
Contacting the VA at their toll-free number (1-800-827-1000); or
•
Faxing a request for information to the appropriate VA regional office. Use form SSA-L1103-U3,
Supplemental Security Income Request for Information.
