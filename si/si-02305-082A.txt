A. Introduction
The Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996, P.L.
104-93, revised noncitizen eligibility for SSI payments. There are no questions on
the RZ forms about the alien status since it does not apply to a majority of the general
SSI population. However, the RZ interview provides the perfect opportunity to be certain
our records show noncitizen recipients' current alien status codes. This is particularly
important for recipients whose eligibility is time-limited.
This section provides information on the different alien statuses and changes that
may affect the alien status codes.
The alien's status must be verified with the Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
in all cases, including redeterminations and immigration status changes. Any DHS issued
document, including the Certificate of Naturalization, must be verified by an online
Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) request. Refer to SI 00502.110 for general alien development. For verification of status with DHS via SAVE, see
SI 00502.115. Follow standard SAVE verification procedures in GN 00303.610 through GN 00303.640.
