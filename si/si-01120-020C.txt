C. Operating Policies — Agent Holds Assets
1. Actions By Agent
For purposes of this section, an action by someone in their capacity as an agent is
equivalent to an action by the ward for whom they act. For example, RSI funds held
by a representative payee for a title II beneficiary are the same as funds held by
the beneficiary.
2. Status of Assets Held For Ward
Unless there is a legal restriction on the agent's access to assets held for a ward
or against their use for the ward's support and maintenance, the assets are the ward's
resources. They are not the agent's resources since the agent has no ownership interest
in them and often is not legally free to use them for their own support and maintenance.
3. Property Title Must Show Ownership
An agent holding property of any kind for a ward must keep it in a form that clearly
shows ownership by the ward.
