SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02310.018 - DHS Match -- Removal From the United States (3D Diary)
Effective Dates: 11/13/2012 - Present | TN 64 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502310018 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedures - Clearance
1. Documentation
For MSSICS cases, document the development of a 3D diary on a Report of Contact page.
For non-MSSICS cases, record development in the “remarks” section of the SSR.
2. Clear the Diary
Delete the diary on the UDIA screen and build transaction SSR. See SM 01301.215 for
a list of diaries and alerts, and MSOM BUSSR 002.019, Update Diaries (UDIA).
