SI 01801.300 Completing the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Application
Each state designs its own Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) application
form. Refer to your regional or local instructions for supplemental information related
to each state, if applicable.
NOTE::
The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Food and Nutrition Service (FNS)
does not maintain a national SNAP application form.
