SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.152 - Determining Whether Two Individuals Are Holding Themselves Out as a Married Couple
Effective Dates: 12/23/2025 - Present | TN 85 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501152 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. When to use these instructions to determine whether two individuals are holding themselves
out as a married couple
Use these instructions to determine whether two unrelated individuals who live in
the same household are considered married for supplemental security income (SSI) purposes
because they hold themselves out as a married couple to the community in which they
live. This means both individuals lead people in the community in which they reside
to believe they are a married couple. Examples of how they can do so include telling
others they are married, using the terms “spouse” or “husband” or “wife” to describe
each other verbally or in writing, or adopting the use of the same last name. Terms
such as “partner” or “girl/boyfriend” or “fiancé" may be an indication there is no
holding out.
The instructions in this section also apply to same-sex couples. For more information
about same-sex couples, see GN 00210.800 Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Same-Sex Marriages and Same-Sex Couples.
