C. EXAMPLE — First Month of TCC Includes Nonrecurring Income
1. FBR/Information
a.
An aged couple.
b.
Date of filing is May 1, 2005
c.
FBR is $869
d.
One member of the couple receives Title II of $420.00 each month. That member also
received a one-time pension amount of $300.00 in June 2005.
2. CI
a.
The CI in May 2005 = $400 (Title II income) – E02 month (no payment due)
b.
The CI in June 2005 = $700 per month (includes CI of $400 for Title II income and
CI income of $300 for one-time pension amount)
c.
The CI for July 2005 on = $400 (Title II income)
3. Eligibility Test
a.
May 2005 -- Couple meets all factors of eligibility – E02 month.
b.
June 2005 on -- Couple meets all factors of eligibility – June is first month of payment.
4. Payment Computation
The payment computation is as follows:
a. May 2005
E02 month – no payment due.
b. June 2005
$869                  FBR in June
-700                  CI in June
$169                  Payment for couple
$84.50               Payment for each member
c. July 2005
$869                  FBR in July
-400                  CI for June (excludes nonrecurring income of $300 used in June only)
$469                  Payment for couple
$234.50               Payment for each member
d. August 2005
$869                  FBR in August
-400                  CI in June (excludes nonrecurring income of $300 used in June only)
$469                  Payment for couple
$234.50              Payment for each member
