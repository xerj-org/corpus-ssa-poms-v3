B. Procedure — question 13
Complete for all deemors. If the answer is “Yes” and the person making the statement is:
•
the sponsor of a noncitizen reporting the sponsor's income and resources on behalf
of that noncitizen, then go to question 14.
•
an ineligible parent reporting the ineligible parent's income and resources on behalf
of their eligible child, then go to question 16.
If the answer is “No,” then go to item 17 (Signature).
