A. When to Use
The following instructions are used only for determining the dependent's portion of
augmented benefits paid from July 1, 1990 through November 16, 1994 by the Department
of Veterans Affairs (VA) in the Ninth Federal Judicial Circuit in the Western United
States. See SI
00830.314 for general instructions regarding the treatment of augmented VA benefits.
