A. Background
P.L. 105-306 enacted on 10/28/98 supersedes prior welfare and immigration reform legislation
regarding alien/citizenship status and SSI eligibility criteria for nonqualified aliens
who were receiving benefits on 8/22/96. Under the prior legislation, SSI benefits
for nonqualified aliens were to be suspended effective 10/1/98 unless the aliens adjusted
to U.S. citizens, nationals, certain American Indian status, or qualified alien status.
Under P.L. 105-306, nonqualified aliens receiving SSI benefits on 8/22/96 were grandfathered.
On 12/3/98 CO sent notices to approximately 9,000 SSI recipients who are affected
by the 1998 legislative change informing them that their benefits would not automatically
be suspended effective 10/1/98.
