B. Procedure for determining monthly amounts
Use the following formulas to determine monthly amounts for future months if the Title
XVI rate increase and rate chart has not been updated. For past and current FBR rates,
see SI 02001.020.
•
2 FBR + $85 = monthly earned income break-even amount.
•
FBR + $20 = monthly unearned income break-even amount.
NOTE: For individuals or couples in FLA-B, include the value of the one-third reduction
(VTR), to determine the appropriate break-even points. That is, in the above formulas,
substitute “FBR minus VTR” for “FBR.”
