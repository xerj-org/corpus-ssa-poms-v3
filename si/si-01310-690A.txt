A. When to Use
Use this space to record explanations of any of the items listed elsewhere on the
form. Enter the item number before each explanation. Also use this space to record
information that is essential to properly deem income and/or resources, but is not
covered elsewhere on the SSA-8010-BK, the SSA-8000-BK, or the SSA-8203-BK.
