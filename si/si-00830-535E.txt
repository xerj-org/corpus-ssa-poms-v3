E. Procedure for determining countable income
Determine the type, amount, and frequency of the income the applicant, recipient,
or deemor receives. Income received through WIOA programs, such as wages, stipends,
incentive payments, etc., should be counted under the general rules of earned income.
Disregard any payments that represent supportive services without further development or documentation
since supportive services such as those described in SI 00830.535A. of this section are assumed to be social services and not income. For more information
on the exclusion of social services from income, see SI 00815.050D and SI 00815.050E.
