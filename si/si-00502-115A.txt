A. Introduction
All noncitizens must submit acceptable documentation of their immigration status (e.g.,
as a qualified alien, etc.). To ensure program integrity, we must verify the immigration
status of all noncitizens who are applying for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
(prior to adjudicating the claim), are subject to a redetermination, or whose immigration
status has changed. This applies regardless of the appearance or type (e.g., Certificates
of Naturalization) of immigration document. To verify nearly all immigration documents,
we use the Department of Homeland Security's (DHS) Systematic Alien Verification for
Entitlements (SAVE) program. This tool provides timely, authoritative information
to support eligibility determinations.
