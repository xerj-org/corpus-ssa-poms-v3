D. Related policies
1. Income deeming
For detailed computation rules and examples, see SI 02001.015 and for deeming of income, see SI 01320.000.
2. Resources deeming
For resources deeming, see SI 01330.000.
3. Manner of payment
We pay the qualified individual and not the EP because the EP is not an SSI recipient.
For benefit rates and payment determinations, see SI 02001.015 Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Essential Person Increment and SI 01320.800 Deeming of Income from Essential Persons.
