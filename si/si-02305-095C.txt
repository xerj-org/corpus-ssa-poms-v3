C. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK Question 12
Question 12 on the SSA-8203-BK is a two-part question:
Since the date on page 1, have you, or your spouse living with you, worked OR do you expect to work in the next
14 months? If “yes,” please give:
•
Amounts for Past Months
•
Estimates for Current and Future Months
