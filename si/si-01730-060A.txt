A. Medicaid Information for Region I Boston
1. State of Connecticut
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
HUSKY Health Program - Connecticut
•
Medicaid Determinations
The State makes Medicaid determinations for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients
utilizing more restrictive criteria than title XVI.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA does not obtain answers to the questions on unpaid medical expenses and third
party coverage.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Refer SSI claimants to the nearest Connecticut State Department of Social Services
office for determination of eligibility for Medicaid per local procedures.
2. State of Maine
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Maine Department of Health and Human Services, Office of MaineCare Services (OMS)
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes Medicaid determinations for SSI recipients. The State makes Medicaid determinations
for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses and third party
coverage at the initial SSI application, and at all redeterminations.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
1.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
2.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
3.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
When SSA does not make determinations of eligibility for Medicaid, refer those SSI
claimants to the Office of MaineCare Services for determination of eligibility for
Medicaid per local procedures.
3. State of Massachusetts
The following describes the State’s Medicaid program:
•
Name of Administering State Agency
Massachusetts’ Executive Office of Health and Human Services (EOHHS), MassHealth
•
Medicaid Determinations
SSA makes Medicaid determinations for SSI recipients and recipients of mandatory and
optional State supplements. The State makes determinations for all other groups.
•
Additional Medicaid Questions
SSA obtains answers to the questions regarding unpaid medical expenses at the initial
application and third party coverage at the initial application and at all redeterminations.
•
Field Office Instructions
Provide the following information to the SSI claimants:
a.
Their SSI applications serve also as applications for Medicaid;
b.
They will receive notice regarding their Medicaid eligibility from the State; and
c.
A denial of their SSI claim does not necessarily preclude Medicaid eligibility if
they can meet the requirements of any Medicaid coverage group.
•
Medicaid Referrals
Prepare and fax the Massachusetts Medicaid Referral form (MA-R-3/2017) to the MA State Supplement Program (SSP) for all those individuals
who require immediate medical assistance and for those whom SSA does not make determinations
of eligibility for Medicaid. Fax the Massachusetts Medicaid Referral form (MA-R-1 3/2017) to fax number: 617-886-8198.
