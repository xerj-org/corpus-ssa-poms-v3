SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02305.117 - Informing Recipients of their Reporting Responsibilities - Redeterminations (RZs)
Effective Dates: 09/17/2026 - Present | TN 107 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502305117 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
Recipients are required to report changes in their circumstances that may affect their
eligibility for and the amount of their SSI payments.
SSA personnel discuss reporting responsibilities during personal contact interviews.
Written instructions are provided for RZs mailed by the FO.
SSA provides written instructions on several paper forms and SSI Claims system printouts.
