Effective Dates: 09/15/2023 - Present | TN 42 (09-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601140 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
An SSI claim can be denied for failure to pursue eligibility if the claimant or potential
representative payee, who is either the legal guardian or parent with custody of a
minor child, states that they no longer wish to pursue the SSI claim.
A SSI claim cannot be denied for failure to pursue eligibility based solely on the
statement of a potential representative payee who is not either the legal guardian
or parent with custody of a minor child.
