SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01110.115 - Assets That Are Not Resources
Effective Dates: 01/21/1993 - Present | TN 51 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501110115 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy Principle — General Rule
Assets of any kind are not resources if the individual does not have:
•
any ownership interest; or
•
the legal right, authority, or power to liquidate them (provided they are not already
in cash); or
•
the legal right to use the assets for their support and maintenance.
EXAMPLE: An individual owns a block of stock jointly with their sibling. Although the form
of ownership is one which would permit either to sell the property without the other's
consent, the siblings have a legally binding agreement that one will not sell without
consent of the other. The individual's sibling refuses to provide consent, thereby
making the stock not a resource for the individual. However, if the sibling should
give consent, the stock would be subject to evaluation under the resources-counting
rules beginning with the month following the month of consent.
The value of the stock would not be counted as income to the individual in the month consent is given.
