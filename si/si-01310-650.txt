SI 01310.650 SSA-8010-BK - Other Property - Questions 7-12
Procedure
Complete questions 7-12 for anyone other than an ineligible child. Obtain a “yes” or “no” answer to each of the questions 7-9, 11 and 12. Obtain a “yes” or “no” answer to each item printed in question 10. Remind the individual to report an item even if they share
ownership with someone else.
