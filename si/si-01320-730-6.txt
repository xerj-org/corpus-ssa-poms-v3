6. Determinations
a.
Enter the deemed income. Always treat deemed income as unearned income.
b.
To a. above, add the individual's own income.
c.
From the total of a. and b. above, subtract the general income exclusion ($20).
d.
To the total from c. above, add any countable earned income the child has after applying
the $65 earned income exclusion, any IRWEs, 1/2 the remainder, and any BWEs.
e.
From the total of d. above, subtract any amount approved for a plan for achieving
self-support.
f.
Add the value of the one-third reduction (VTR) to the result of e. above.
g.
Subtract the result of f. above from the FBR including State supplement (where applicable).
This is the payment amount.
NOTE: The “Determinations” section of the SSA-8017 is not all-inclusive. For more complete instructions on payment
computations for an eligible child once you have determined the amount of deemed income,
see SI 02005.090.
