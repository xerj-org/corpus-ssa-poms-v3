SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.035 - Alien Allocation: Question 17
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 5 (03-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604035 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. When and how to complete
1. Complete 17(a)
Complete 17(a) only when a spouse is not filing for SSI and:
•
The claimant and spouse are living together; or
•
The claimant and spouse separated since the first moment of the filing date month.
2. Complete 17(b)
When the claimant answers “YES” to (a), complete 17(b).
3. Separation prior to reference date month
If the claimant was separated prior to the first moment of the filing date month, do not complete (a). The alien allocation
does not apply in this situation. Therefore, skip 17(a) and go to question 18.
