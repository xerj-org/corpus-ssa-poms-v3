Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502005095 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Procedure
To use the proration chart, first find the effective day of filing in the left hand
column. Then, select the correct month of filing column to the right of the effective
filing day column. (The fraction shown in the month column gives the number of benefits
days and the number of days in the month.)
