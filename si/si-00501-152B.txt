SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00501.152 - Determining Whether Two Individuals Are Holding Themselves Out as a Married Couple
Effective Dates: 12/23/2025 - Present | TN 85 (12-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500501152 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Obtaining holding out allegation
Always obtain the claimant's/recipient's signed statement of their allegation about
holding out in the application or redetermination path unless there is an obvious
reason for the individuals to live in the same household, other than as a couple.
The following are examples.
•
They are in a family relationship (e.g., siblings).
•
They have an employer-employee relationship such as for housekeeping or child care.
•
Their household arrangement is only for the purpose of sharing expenses.
For treatment of allegations, see SI
00501.152C in this section.
