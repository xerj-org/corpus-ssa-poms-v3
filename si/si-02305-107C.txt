C. Description of Form SSA-8202-BK Question 11
Question 11 on the SSA-8202-BK – Since the date on page 1,
have you (or your spouse living with you) sold, transferred title, disposed of, or
given away any money, or other property, including money or property in foreign countries?
If “yes,” give the following information:
What you sold, transferred title, disposed of, or gave away and the value of the property.
Fill in blocks are below the question to be used to enter the information.
