A. Policy
1. General
A limited issue (LI) case requires development of a specific issue or event without
conducting an RZ. When developing LIs, if a deemor's income caused the diary to be
set, exclusions and allocations for the recipient and all the members of the deeming
unit should be reviewed for accuracy and updated if necessary.
The LI case profile code is X.
2. Using RZwiz to conduct RZ and LI interviews
NOTE:
Use RZwiz with all LIs. Also use RZwiz with all RZs, LI and wage-related diary interviews,
and wage-related diaries. See SI 02305.071.
