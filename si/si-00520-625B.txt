SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00520.625 - Safe Havens Demonstration Program
Effective Dates: 12/08/1994 - Present | TN 20 (12-94)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500520625 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. POLICY
All SSI provisions apply to participants in the safe havens demonstration program,
except that no individual living in a safe haven will:
•
be considered a resident of a public institution (SI 00520.001 B.4.); or
•
be charged ISM, to the extent that such support and maintenance is provided as a result
of the safe havens demonstration project.
