SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.682 - Department of Defense (DOD) Payments to Certain Persons Captured and Interned by North Vietnam
Effective Dates: 06/30/2004 - Present | TN 50 (06-04)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130682 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Payments to a Captured and Interned Individual
Payments made to an individual who was captured and interned by the Democratic Republic
of North Vietnam as a result of that person's participation in certain military operations
(known as OPLAN 34A or its predecessor, or OPLAN 35) are excluded from resources.
2. Payments to a Surviving Spouse or Child
Payments to such an individual's surviving spouse or child of any age are excluded
from resources.
3. Interest Received on Unspent Payments
Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent payments is not excluded from income and resources by this provision. For SSI benefits payable on
or after July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent payments is excluded income. (See
SI 00830.500C.)
