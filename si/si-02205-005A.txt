SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02205.005 - Sponsor Questions Responsibility for SSI Overpayment
Effective Dates: 10/19/2023 - Present | TN 1 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502205005 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy principles
A sponsor is “jointly and severally liable” with the alien for the repayment of any SSI overpayment made to the alien (during
the 3-year period after their entry to the United Sates) that resulted from the sponsor's
failure to provide correct information regarding their own income and resources and
the income and resources of their living with spouse, if any.
However, if the failure to report was not willful, make a without fault finding relieving
the sponsor of the responsibility to repay the overpayment.
