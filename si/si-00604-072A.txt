A. When and how to complete question 44
Complete 44(a) for all claimants. If the claimant answers (a) "YES," complete (b)
only. Otherwise, go to question 45.
Record all life insurance policies regardless of face value or type of policy to determine whether we may exclude any policy. If the claimant does not have the
information, write "Will provide."
