SI 01330.300 Multiple Deeming - Introduction
When more than one eligible individual lives in the same household and there is a
parent-child relationship, a multiple deeming situation may exist. Section SI 01330.310 provides the rules to follow in parent-child multiple deeming situations. (See SI 01330.500 for the rules that apply to aliens subject to deeming from a sponsor and a spouse
or parent, or from multiple sponsors.)
