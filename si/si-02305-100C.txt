C. Procedure - developmental tolerance for liquid resources
Verify alleged countable liquid resources totaling $400 or more (SI 01140.010B.).
NOTE: Cases with Internal Revenue Service (IRS) alerts must be developed unless the SSR
or FO files/records clearly indicate that a particular transaction on the IRS alert
was previously developed and the transaction previously developed belongs to the recipient
(or deemor) or represents excluded income or resources per SI 02310.047A.
See SI 01140.010B.2. for instructions on applying the liquid resource verification tolerance to excludable
burial funds.
