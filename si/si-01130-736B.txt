B. Policy for the resource exclusion of payments from a State eugenics compensation program
Effective October 7, 2016, exclude from countable resources all eugenics compensation
payments paid by a State eugenics compensation program and retained the month after
receipt. For payments received prior to October 7, 2016 and retained, apply the resource
exclusion effective with the November 2016 resource determination. See income exclusion
development in Payments from a State eugenics compensation program SI 00830.736.
