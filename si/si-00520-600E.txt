E. Policy — absence from a PESH
1. 14-Day temporary absence not applicable
The 14-day temporary absence rule (SI 00520.001B.7.) does not apply in determining residency throughout a month. Any period of a day
or more during which the individual is not a resident of the PESH, breaks the continuity
of stay for that month, so that the individual can be eligible for that month and
the month does not count toward the 6-month payment limit.
2. Leaving a PESH during a period of ineligibility
If an individual leaves a PESH during a period of ineligibility, any SSI payment for
a month in which they reattain eligibility is prorated.
