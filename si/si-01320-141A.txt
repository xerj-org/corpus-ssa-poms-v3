A. Policy on what is excluded from deeming
We exclude from deemed income any public income-maintenance (PIM) payment and any
income used to compute such payment, as discussed in SI 01320.141B. in this section.
This exclusion applies to the income of an ineligible spouse or parent when used to
compute a PIM payment regardless of who receives that payment.
This exclusion does not apply to individuals who receive Medicaid while working under
the Section 1619(b) provisions. The exclusion does not apply because they are considered
individuals with disabilities who are receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
payments only for the purpose of eligibility for Medicaid.
