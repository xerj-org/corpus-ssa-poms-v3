SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.150 - Gifts of Domestic Travel Tickets
Effective Dates: 08/04/1999 - Present | TN 34 (08-99)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120150 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
1. Domestic Travel Tickets
The value of a ticket for domestic travel received by an individual (or spouse) is
not a resource if the ticket is:
•
received as a gift;
•
not converted to cash; and
•
excluded from income per SI 00830.521.
2. Non-Domestic Travel Tickets
The value of tickets for non-domestic travel received by an individual are a resource.
EXCEPTION: If the ticket cannot be sold or converted to cash due to restrictions placed on the
ticket, it is not a resource.
