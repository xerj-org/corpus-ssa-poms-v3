SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00820.560 - Allocating Work Expenses
Effective Dates: 04/09/2018 - Present | TN 93 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500820560 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy — Effective Dates
1. IRWE
For IRWE, the instructions in this section are effective for payments made after November
30, 1980.
2. BWE
a.
For BWE, the instructions below that pertain to the proration of expenses paid prior
to starting work or after stopping work are effective for payments made on or after
July 1, 1987.
b.
Prior to July 1, 1987, expenses were deducted only when paid. Expenses paid prior
to starting work or after stopping work were not deductible.
