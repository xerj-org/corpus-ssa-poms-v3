I. Procedure - Landlord Information
Documentation of landlord information is only required when rental liability is alleged
and the recipient or anyone else in the household is the landlord's parent, child,
or spouse.
Document the landlord's:
•
Name
•
Address
•
Phone number
•
Monthly Rent
