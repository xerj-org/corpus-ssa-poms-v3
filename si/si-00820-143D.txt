D. Recruitment for automated wage reporting methods (myWR, SSAMWR, and SSITWR)
1. Recruitment discussions are mandatory
Recruitment discussions are mandatory. Recruit all recipients, their representative
payees, and deemors to report monthly wage amounts using automated reporting tools
(e.g., myWR, SSAMWR, and SSITWR). Individuals may choose their reporting method and
are not required to report by using an automated reporting method. Do not refuse to
accept monthly wage reports submitted using a method other than an automated wage
reporting method (e.g., mailing pay stubs).
For specific instructions on how to recruit wage reporters to use automated reporting
methods and case characteristics that may influence the choice of reporting method,
see SI 00820.144C.
2. Disclaimer language provided in the electronic reporting methods to encourage reports
during the first 6 days of the month
To be considered in time to process a particular month’s payment, SSI recipients or
their representative payees generally must report income changes within the first
ten days of the month following the month of change, as described in SI 02301.005. While myWR, SSAMWR, and SSITWR accept wage reports on any day during the current
reporting month, regular reporting prior to the Goldberg Kelly (GK) cutoff can affect
payment in time to prevent improper payments. Therefore, while recruiting participants
to use electronic wage reporting methods, emphasize the importance of reporting wages
during the first 6 days of the month to avoid incorrect payments.
myWR
To encourage submission of SSI and concurrent wage reports within the first six days
of the reporting month, myWR displays informational messages in the wage-reporting
path. For example, individuals reporting May’s wages in June see the following message
on the Wage Report Confirmation screen as they are submitting their report: "Please report your wages for June in the first
six days of July to avoid incorrect payments.”
SSAMWR
and
SSITWR
If the reporter makes a successful wage report after the GK systems cutoff date, SSAMWR
or SSITWR present the following language: "Because we received the report after (Automated fill
in of the current month and GK cutoff day), the wages you reported today might not
be reflected in a
future SSI payment. We will mail a notice if there are changes to the payment amount
explaining
these changes and your right to appeal.”
For more information on GK processing, see SI 02301.301.
