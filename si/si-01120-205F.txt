SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01120.205 - Uniform Transfers to Minors Act
Effective Dates: 01/31/2008 - Present | TN 90 (06-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501120205 (fetched 2026-10-04)

F. Development and documentation
1. Initial claims
During an initial claim, UTMA accounts should be processed as follows:
•
Request a written document from the custodian or donor (e.g., a deed, appropriate
titling on a certificate of deposit, savings passbook, etc., reflecting it is being
held by an adult custodian under a State Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, other transfer
document referring to an adult custodian with reference to the State UTMA or other
documentation that satisfies your State UTMA statutory requirements) to confirm an
allegation of an UTMA transfer.
•
If there is no document designating an UTMA transfer, do not develop further. Instead,
proceed as if there had been no allegation of an UTMA transfer.
•
If there is evidence that questions the validity of an UTMA account, follow regional
instructions regarding legality of gifts or transfers. In the absence of such instructions,
consult with your Regional Office (RO) program staff. If necessary, the RO staff will
request the documentation needed for possible submission to the Office of the General
Counsel.
•
If there is a valid UTMA account and the minor is an SSI recipient, see regional instructions
to confirm the State's legal age of majority. Also, input “UTMA” to the CG field,
followed by the account balance and the month and year in which the minor will attain
majority. For example, in a State where the legal age of majority is 21, a $6,000
UTMA transfer to a child born June 10, 1990 would read: “UTMA,6000,0611.”
2. Posteligibility
a. Develop UTMA only in the following situations
•
there is an allegation of a new UTMA transfer since the last redetermination;
•
there has been a withdrawal from an existing UTMA account; or
•
the minor has attained (or is about to attain) the age of majority.
b. UTMA transfer in CG field
If the CG field already reflects existence of an UTMA transfer, develop and document
the situations in a. above only when material. If the current allegation is that no
UTMA transfer exists, resolve the discrepancy.
c. No UTMA transfer in CG field
If the CG field does not reflect existence of an UTMA transfer, follow 1. above to
develop an UTMA transfer that is newly alleged or discovered.
NOTE: Routine development of increases in an established UTMA account is discretionary.
Such increases do not affect eligibility or payment, but you may wish to update information
in the CG field or the Remarks section of the SSR.
