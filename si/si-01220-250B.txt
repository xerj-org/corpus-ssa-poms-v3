B. DIVORCE OR SEPARATION
Where a recipient who meets the conditions in SI 01220.100 is divorced or is living apart for more than 6 months from an eligible spouse who
does not meet the requirements of SI 01220.100, the spouse or previous spouse not meeting those requirements may not have the State
plan resource limits and exclusions applied. However, if both the eligible individual
and the divorced or separated spouse meet the conditions in SI 01220.100, the State plan resource limits and exclusions continue to apply.
