A. Introduction
Section 435 of The Social Security Protection Act of 2004, Public Law 108-203, provides
a 9-month resource exclusion for grants, scholarships, fellowships, and gifts used
to pay for tuition, fees, and other necessary educational expenses at any educational
institution, including vocational and technical education.
