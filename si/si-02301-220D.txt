D. Procedure – Removing N05 for a Past Period
Remove an N05 determination on a record and reinstate benefits for a past period subject
to the rules of administrative finality (SI 04070.000) when:
•
the recipient appeals the N05 determination and provides new information establishing
eligibility/payment amount, or
•
the FO receives new information establishing eligibility/payment amount.
