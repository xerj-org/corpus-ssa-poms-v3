SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01130.602 - Restitution Payments for Misused Benefits
Effective Dates: 05/15/2009 - Present | TN 53 (02-06)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501130602 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
1. Restitution
Payment to the beneficiary or the beneficiary’s representative payee in the amount
equal to the benefits misused in the following situations:
a.
The misuse resulted from the Social Security Administration’s negligent failure to
investigate or monitor a representative payee; or
b.
An organizational or individual payee misused the benefits, without regard to whether
SSA was negligent.
2. 9-month Exclusion
The unspent portion of restitution (See SI 01130.602B.1.) made on or after March 2, 2004 is excluded from resources for the 9 calendar months following the month in which
the restitution is received.
This exclusion applies to an eligible individual/couple and to any person (e.g., spouse,
parents, essential persons, and alien sponsors) whose income is deemed to be included
in the individual’s/couple’s income for SSI purposes.
