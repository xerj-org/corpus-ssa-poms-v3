SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01320.950 - Deeming of Income From a Sponsor of a Non-Citizen
Effective Dates: 11/14/2024 - Present | TN 59 (11-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501320950 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. More Than One Non-Citizen With the Same Sponsor — Operating Procedures
1.
Do not divide the sponsor's income among the non-citizens.
2.
When both members of an eligible couple have the same sponsor, deem the sponsor's
income to each member. The couple's countable income includes the sum of their deemed
income amounts.
