SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00601.011 - Filing Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Applications for Disabled Youth Transitioning out of Foster Care
Effective Dates: 10/04/2010 - Present | TN 72 (04-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500601011 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure for taking a SSI application from disabled youth age 18 and older transitioning
out of foster care.
Use the following MSSICS coding for a claimant in foster care who is age 18 or older
as of the application filing date. For policy on MSSICS inputs, see SI
00601.009.C.2.
1. SSR Remarks (CRMK)
Add the remark “YOUTH TRANSITIONING OUT OF FOSTER CARE” to the SSR.
