Effective Dates: 01/05/2024 - Present | TN 119 (01-24)

C. POLICY - INTENDED USE
1. During First 9 Months
What the individual intends to do with the funds does not affect their treatment for
the first 9 months.
2. Role in Extension for Good Cause
An individual cannot qualify for an extension of the initial 9-month period unless
they intend to use the funds for their designated purpose, i.e., repair or replacement
of excluded resources.
3. Change of Intent During Extension
The good cause extension will terminate as of the date of the change of intent. The
funds previously not treated as resources will be taken into account in determining
resources for the following month.
