F. Income trusts established under section 1917(d)(4)(B) of the Act
Income trusts, sometimes called Miller trusts (named after a court case), established under section 1917(d)(4)(B) of the
Act are not considered exceptions to trust rules for SSI purposes. However, some States may exclude
these trusts from counting as a resource for Medicaid purposes. This type of trust
is composed only of pension, Social Security, and other income to the individual (and
accumulated income in the trust).
