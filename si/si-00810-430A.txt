SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00810.430 - Plan to Achieve Self-Support (PASS) as an Income Exclusion
Effective Dates: 02/23/2009 - Present | TN 73 (08-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810430 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
Income, whether earned or unearned, of a blind or disabled recipient may be excluded
if such income is needed to fulfill a plan to achieving self-support (PASS).
This exclusion does not apply to a blind or disabled individual age 65 or older, unless
the individual was receiving SSI or State disability or blind payments for the month
before attaining age 65.
