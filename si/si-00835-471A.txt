SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.471 - Conversions — Distinguishing Between Household Costs and Non-Household Costs
Effective Dates: 12/13/2023 - Present | TN 117 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835471 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
When determining household costs for ISM determinations, it may be necessary to distinguish
between actual household costs and non-household costs (i.e., costs associated with
operating a business or agricultural operation in the home). Business or agricultural
expenses are not household costs for ISM determinations.
