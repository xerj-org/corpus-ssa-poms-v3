C. Comparing records and computer matching
1.
Provide an explanation to all claimants of what comparing records and computer matching
means.
2.
Point out that we will check the claimant’s statements using computer-matching programs
by comparing our records to those of other agencies including the Internal Revenue
Service and we may check financial institution records.
