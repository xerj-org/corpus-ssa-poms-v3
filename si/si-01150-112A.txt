SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01150.112 - Initial Claims with Period of Ineligibility and Disability Determination Needed
Effective Dates: 01/25/2010 - Present | TN 43 (12-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501150112 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
The procedures in this section apply only to initial claims if:
a.
A transfer of resources occurred on or after 12/14/99;
b.
A period of ineligibility exists based on a transfer of resources;
c.
The individual does not meet one of the transfer exceptions listed in SI 01150.121 through SI 01150.125; and
d.
A disability determination by the disability determination services (DDS) is needed.
