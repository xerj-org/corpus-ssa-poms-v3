A. Procedure - determining resources of parent(s)
If a child under age 18 lives in the same household with a parent(s) applying for
SSI or an eligible parent(s), determine the countable resources of the parent(s) by
following the guidelines in SI 01110.001 ff. and SI 01330.100 ff., as applicable.
