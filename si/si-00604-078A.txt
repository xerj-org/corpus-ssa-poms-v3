A. Transfer of resources policy
Public Law (P.L.) 100-169 (Foster Care Independence Act of 1999) requires us to determine
if a claimant is ineligible for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) due to transfer
of resources for less than fair market value within 36 months prior to the filing
date month. Section 1917(c) of the Social Security Act requires us to obtain transfer
of resource information needed for State Medicaid eligibility determinations. (For
required development, see SI 01150.001.)
