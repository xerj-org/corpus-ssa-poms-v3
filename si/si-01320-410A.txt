A. Background
Several States have introduced family payment caps for certain Aid to Families with
Dependent Children (AFDC) family grant amounts. AFDC family payment caps may be imposed
throughout a State or only in certain counties. When a payment cap is imposed, the
family's AFDC grant is not increased when a new child is born into the household.
Under the Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) provisions, a family cap
is also allowable. Each state will make its own decision about applying such a cap.
