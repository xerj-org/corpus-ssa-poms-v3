D. RZ procedure for age 18 cases
Use the following procedures when developing RZs for age 18 cases (profile K):
1.
Contact the recipient for a face-to-face interview for both the non-medical RZ and
the disability redetermination. Face-to-face contact is preferred for disability redeterminations.
For FO personal contact see, see DI 13005.030. For exceptions to personal contact, see DI 13005.035.
2.
To help prepare the recipient for the interview explain that you will conduct the
following two types of redetermination interviews.
•
Inform the recipient that we conduct a non-medical redetermination interview and a
medical redetermination.
•
Advise the recipient to bring information and documentation pertaining to their living
arrangement, income, and resources.
•
Advise the recipient to be prepared to provide relevant medical evidence.
3.
For instructions on age 18 disability redeterminations, see DI 11070.001-040.
4.
Attempt to schedule both the non-medical redetermination and the disability redetermination
interviews at the same time.
5.
Consider capability development and direct payment to the recipient, if appropriate.
6.
Follow instructions in SI 02309.003 to complete the RZ.
