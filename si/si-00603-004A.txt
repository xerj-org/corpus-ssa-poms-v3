A. Introduction
The instructions on processing simultaneous development claims in this section are
divided into two parts:
•
initial simultaneous development, meaning the claim has been simultaneous development
from the start of the application process; and
•
switch to simultaneous development, meaning the claim was deferred initially and then
switched to simultaneous development before receipt of a disability determination.
