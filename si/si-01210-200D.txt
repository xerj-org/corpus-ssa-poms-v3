D. Process
WHEN...
THEN...
the field office (FO) inputs gross income to the SSR
the system applies SSI exclusions to calculate CI/FR.
the FO also inputs type K income to the SSR
the system reads the amount as BCI (not as unearned income), and compares it with
CI/FR.
the system compares BCI with CI/FR
the system treats the lower amount as CI and computes eligibility and payment accordingly.
