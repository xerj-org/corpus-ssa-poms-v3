A. Background
In section 1634 States (see SI 01730.050F., Exhibit 6), an SSI redetermination is also a redetermination of Medicaid eligibility.
Continued Medicaid eligibility can depend upon continued current payment status for
SSI, federally administered SSP's, or 1619(b) participation.
