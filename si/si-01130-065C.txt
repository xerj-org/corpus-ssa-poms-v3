C. How to treat interest earned on an unspent NFIP payment
Interest earned on an unspent NFIP payment is excluded from income (for the policy
on Income Treatment–Benefits Payable Beginning July 1, 2004, see SI 00830.500C). However, any interest earned on an NFIP payment that is retained into the month
following the month of receipt, is a countable resource.
