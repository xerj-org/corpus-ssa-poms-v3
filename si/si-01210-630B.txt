SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01210.630 - Special Blind Income Disregards - Recipient With Essential Person
Effective Dates: 01/06/1999 - Present | 
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501210630 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. PROCEDURE
1. Example
A blind grandfathered recipient living in State X has an essential person. The blind
recipient has the following income:
•
$120 wages
•
$10 BWE under both Federal and State plan rules
The essential person has income of $100.00 wages.
The State plan income disregards used in the chart below are typical. Actual disregards
vary by State.
2. Chart: Determining CI
STEP
1
Compute BCI.
Apply the applicable State plan income disregards (SI 01210.500) to the income of the recipient only.
BCI of Blind Recipient Only
$120.00  wages
-  7.50   general disregard
$112.50
- 85.00   earned income disregard
$ 27.50
÷   2   disregard of one-half of remainder
$ 13.75
- 10.00   BWE
$  3.75  BCI (recipient only)
​
​
Add the essential person's income. Do not apply disregards.
BCI for Blind Recipient and Essential Person
$   3.75  BCI of blind recipient only
+100.00   income deemed from essential
person
$103.75  BCI (recipient and essential person)
2
Compute CI/FR.
Apply the SSI exclusions to the combined income of the recipient and the essential
person.
Unearned Income
$100.00   income deemed from essential person
- 20.00   general exclusion
$ 80.00  countable unearned income
​
​
The system performs this computation, except for cases (such as force pay) requiring
a manual computation.
Earned Income
$120.00  wages
- 65.00   earned income exclusion
$ 55.00
÷   2   exclusion of one-half of remainder
$ 27.50
- 10.00   BWE
$ 17.50  countable earned income
​
​
​
CI/FR
$ 80.00  countable unearned income
+ 17.50   countable earned income
$ 97.50  CI/FR
3
Determine CI.
Compare BCI from step 1 with CI/FR from step 2. Use the lower amount of income as CI.
$ 97.50  CI (CI/FR from step 2)
