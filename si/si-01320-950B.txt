B. Determining Payment — Policy Principles
The procedure in A.1-4. above is followed to determine the SSI payment amount for
a non-citizen, using the non-citizen's income, including the sponsor's (and living-with
spouse's, if any) deemable income, from the budget month. The budget month is 2 months
prior to the computation month
except when determining the payment amount for the first and second months of eligibility
or after a period of ineligibility:
1.
In the first month of eligibility (or reeligibility), the non-citizen's income, including
the sponsor's deemable income, received in the current month, is used to determine
both eligibility and payment amount.
2.
In the second month, the non-citizen's income, including the sponsor's deemable income,
received in that month is used to determine eligibility, but the non-citizen's countable
income (including deemed income) received in the first month is used to determine
the payment amount.
