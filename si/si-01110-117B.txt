B. Procedure for documentation
When an individual alleges having been unaware of ownership of an asset, obtain a
statement either signed or recorded on a Report of Contact from the individual. Also
obtain any available supporting documentation, including (but not limited to) signed
statements from other individuals who are familiar with the individual's situation.
Document the file with your determination regarding the alleged “unknown” resource. (An SSA-5002, Report of Contact, may be used for this purpose.)
