C. Procedure for coordination with other pending posteligibility (PE) actions
You must coordinate D8 diary development with scheduled field office (FO) redeterminations
(RZs) or other pending PE actions (e.g., other interface alerts, continuing disability
reviews, or representative payee accounting).
