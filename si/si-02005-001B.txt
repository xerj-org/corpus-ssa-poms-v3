B. Policy — Retrospective Monthly Accounting (RMA)
Effective April 1982, we base payment for a month on known circumstances for a closed
month. This is RMA.
It has two elements:
•
the eligibility test, which is based on the individual's (or couple's) income, resources
and other factors in a month; and
•
the payment computation, which is generally based on the income in the second month
before the month for which payment is being computed (the “budget month” (BM)).
