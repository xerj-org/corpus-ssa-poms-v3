2. Couples Record
Example:
A disability claim is filed in November 1981 by an in­dividual married to an eligible
person. The DO makes a presumptive finding of disability effective with the month
of filing. A short quarter computation combin­ing all of the couple's eligibility
and benefit criteria for November and December is performed. Both mem­bers of the
couple will receive equal monthly benefits for November and December. (See SI 02009.025.) A regular quarterly computation will be performed in January 1982. The disability
determination is still pending after the three-month period for payment of presumptive
benefits has elapsed.
