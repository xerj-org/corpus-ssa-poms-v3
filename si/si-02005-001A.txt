A. Background
Quarterly Computation
From January 1974 through March 1982, we used a quarterly computation to compute eligibility
and payment for Federal supplemental security income (SSI) benefits and federally
administered optional State supplementary (OSS) payments. We based these computations
on circumstances during the quarter.
“For information on quarterly computations, see SI 02009.000.”
