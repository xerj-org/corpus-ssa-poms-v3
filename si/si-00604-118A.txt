SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00604.118 - Blind or Visually Impaired Notice Options: Question 64
Effective Dates: 04/11/2007 - Present | TN 5 (03-12)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500604118 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Introduction
We automatically approve requests for the following currently available formats:
•
standard print notice by certified mail;
•
standard print notice by first-class mail and a follow-up telephone call to read the
notice within 5 business days from the date of the notice;
•
standard print and Braille notice by first-class mail; or
•
standard print notice and a data compact disc (CD) in Microsoft Word format by first-class
mail.
NOTE: The data CD requires a computer with software that can open Microsoft Word files.
•
standard print notice and a large print notice (18 point font); and
•
standard print notice and an audio compact disc (CD).
