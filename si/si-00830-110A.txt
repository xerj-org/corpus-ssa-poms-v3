A. Definitions
1. Overpayment
The total amount an individual received for any period that exceeds the total amount
that should have been paid to the individual for that period. For purposes of this
section, an overpayment is defined by the entity paying the benefit and may include
overpayments made to someone other than the individual whose benefits are withheld.
2. Double counting
Counting unearned income twice.
