SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.316 - Augmented VA Benefits in the Ninth Circuit
Effective Dates: 10/22/2004 - Present | TN 76 (12-97)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830316 (fetched 2026-10-04)

D. Procedure
1. Identification of Affected Cases
Verify the amount of a VA dependent's portion for all SSI claims filed or pending
on or after November 17, 1994, including denied claims under appeal.
Verify the amount of a VA dependent's portion for posteligibility cases at the first
redetermination or other income review completed on or after November 17, 1994.
NOTE: Previous POMS instructions provided that, in the Ninth Circuit, FOs would input a
VA dependent's portion to the SSR under the appropriate income code and in a continuing
amount of zero (except when the dependent's portion was countable per B.3. or B.4.
above). FOs also were to have entered a CG code of “9THX CIRC AUGM VAXX.” Develop
cases with such entries if a VA dependent appears to be involved.
2. Effective Date of Input
a. Initial Claims and Denied Claims Under Appeal
Input the verified amount of the VA dependent's portion paid to a designated beneficiary
as VA income to the dependent effective with the first month of SSI eligibility or
the first month in which VA income was received, but not earlier than November 17,
1994.
b. Posteligibility Cases Requiring Reposting of VA Dependent's Amounts
For cases in which the VA dependent's portion had been reduced to a continuing amount
of zero because of Paxton v.Secretary,input the verified amount of the VA dependent's portion paid to a designated beneficiary
as VA income to the dependent effective with the month in which the verification is received. (This implementation method will usually eliminate the need to make an overpayment
determination for months after the policy effective date). Do not charge a dependent's
portion greater than zero for any months before the month in which verification was
received, unless:
•
the benefit was paid to the dependent by apportionment, or
•
the dependent's portion is countable per C.3. above.
If verification of the dependent's portion is already in file when the redetermination
or income review is initiated, input the verified amount as VA income to the dependent
effective with the current calendar month at the time of the input.
Delete any CG code related to the prior Ninth Circuit policy.
3. Manual Notice When Restoring Dependent's Portions to PE Cases
In the same systems input used to restore VA income to the record, suppress the automated
notice. Use the Field Office Notice Software (FONS) to generate an SSI Notice of Planned
Action (SSA-L8155-U2).
Prepare a dictated paragraph to explain that:
•
beginning (month/year), we consider part of (the designated beneficiary's) VA benefit
to be (the dependent's) unearned income, and
•
we now use that income to decide the amount of the SSI payment.
NOTE: A manual notice is required only when initially restoring the amounts of VA dependent's
portions for cases in which the VA dependent's portion had been reduced to a continuing
amount of zero because of Paxton v. Secretary.
