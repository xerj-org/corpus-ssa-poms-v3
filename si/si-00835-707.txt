SI 00835.707 Procedures For Determining ISM Provided To Residents of Institutions
The following general procedures are used for determining ISM received by residents
of all types of institutions. However, see the following sections for additional instructions
applicable to specific institutions:
•
Private for-profit residential institutions, SI 00835.708;
•
Private nonprofit residential institutions, SI 00835.710 and SI 00835.712;
•
Educational/vocational facilities, SI 00835.714;
•
Public institutions, SI 00835.716.
