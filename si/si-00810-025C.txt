C. Policy for earned, unearned income, deeming, and court-ordered payments
1. Earned income
Wages are what an individual receives (before any deductions) for working as someone
else's employee. For information on wages, see SI 00820.100.
2. Unearned income
For instructions on determining the amount of unearned income, if it involves garnishment
or other withholding, see SI 00830.115.
3. Deeming and court ordered support payments
When the court orders garnishment of income of an ineligible spouse, parent, ineligible
child, or eligible alien (sponsored by an ineligible spouse or parent) to pay court-ordered
or title IV-D enforced support payments, for guidance see SI 01320.145.
