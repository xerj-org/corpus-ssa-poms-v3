K. Procedure -- effectuating the withdrawal through the CCE System
You may process the withdrawal through the CCE System by entering a denial code of
N12 on the Determination screen in the CCE Clearance Menu.
NOTE:
You must have a pending SSI Claims system file with a Pending File Begin Date equal
to the effective filing date to properly effectuate the withdrawal.
