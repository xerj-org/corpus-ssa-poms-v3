SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00835.400 - In-Kind Support and Maintenance (ISM) only to the<EndLine/> Claimant
Effective Dates: 09/26/2023 - Present | TN 117 (12-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500835400 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy – Beginning March 9, 2005
1. General Rules
The general rules for ISM only to the claimant are:
a
ISM only to the claimant is ISM that benefits only one member of the household such
as a gift of food to an eligible individual. ISM only to the claimant is ISM that
is not considered to benefit all members of the household.
b
ISM only to the claimant is neither inside ISM nor outside ISM for computational purposes
because its value is not determined based on the number of household members.
c
ISM only to the claimant can be provided by a person who lives inside or outside the
household.
d
ISM only to the claimant counts as income in the month it is received. For example,
a gift of food given to the eligible individual on 05/15 counts as ISM in May.
e
ISM only to the claimant is valued under the presumed maximum value (PMV) rule.
f
ISM provided to a household of one is not classified as ISM only to the claimant.
ISM classified as outside ISM (See SI 00835.350) should be treated as outside ISM even when the eligible individual is the only household
member. Outside ISM is ISM which could benefit all household members if there were
more than one household member.
NOTE: Clothing received before March 9, 2005 is counted as ISM. Clothing received on or after March 9, 2005 is not counted as ISM.
2. Examples of ISM only to the Claimant
The following are examples of ISM only to the claimant:
a
The individual receives ISM in the form of food outside a household setting such as
a gift of meals at a relative's home.
b
Food received by an individual and consumed inside a household may constitute ISM
only to the claimant. For example, if an individual who is on a special diet receives
a gift of food, such food may constitute ISM only to the claimant.
c
An individual acquires ownership interest in the dwelling, which constitutes their
shelter in the month they receive it. This acquisition benefits only the individual
and not other household members (See SI
00835.310).
d
An eligible child receives food while temporarily absent to attend school when the
child is subject to deeming and someone other than the deemor pays for the food (See
SI 00835.042).
e
An eligible child receives shelter while temporarily absent to attend school when
the child is subject to deeming and someone other than the deemor pays for the shelter
at school (See SI
00835.042).
NOTE: Remember that the infrequent or irregular income exclusion may apply to ISM only to
the claimant, e.g., when the individual gets an occasional meal or shelter item from
a relative or friend. (See SI
00810.410).
