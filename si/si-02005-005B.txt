B. EXAMPLE — First Month of TCC Does Not Include Nonrecurring Income
1. FBR/Information
a.
An aged couple
b.
Date of filing is May 1, 2005
c.
FBR is $869.00
d.
One member of the couple receives $225 from a private pension beginning May 2005 and
continuing.
2. CI
a.
The CI in June 2005 = $ 205
b.
The CI from July 2005 on = $205 per month.
3. Eligibility Test
a.
May 2005 = Couple meets all factors of eligibility – E02 month
b.
June 2005 on = Couple meets all factors of eligibility and June is the first month
the couple will be paid.
4. Payment Computation
The payment computation is as follows:
a. May 2005
E02 month – no payment due.
b. June 2005
$869                  FBR in June
-205                  CI in June
$664                  Payment for couple
$332                  Payment for each member
c. July 2005
$869                  FBR in July
-205                  CI in June
$664                  Payment for couple
$332                  Payment for each member
d. August 2005
$869                  FBR in August
-205                  CI in June
$664                  Payment for couple
$332                  Payment for each member
