B. Policy for EEOICP
1. Supplemental Security Income (SSI) income exclusion
Exclude from income for SSI purposes, lump sum payments made under the EEOICP, including
reimbursement for medical expenses.
NOTE: Individuals who are eligible under Section 5 of the Radiation Exposure Compensation
Program (RECP) may also be eligible for compensation and paid medical expenses under
the EEOICP.
See SI 00830.740 which states that RECP payments are also excluded from income and SI 01130.680 which states that RECP payments are excluded from resources.
2. Interest on unspent EEOICP payments
Effective July 1, 2004, interest earned on unspent EEOICP payments is excluded from
income for SSI purposes. See SI 00830.500C, for the policy on dividends and interest.
