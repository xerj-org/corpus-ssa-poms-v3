F. Procedure - Rental Liability - Recipient
Develop allegations related to recipient rental liability as follows:
1. Recipient alleges rental liability or no rental liability and SSA Records Agree
If a recipient alleges rental liability or no rental liability and other information
in SSA records agrees, do not develop recipient rental liability.
2. Recipient alleges rental liability or no rental liability and SSA Records Conflict
If a recipient alleges rental liability or no rental liability and other information
in SSA records conflicts, develop rental liability following instructions in SI 00835.120.
