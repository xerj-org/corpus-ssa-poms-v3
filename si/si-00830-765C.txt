C. Procedure for verifying incentives for prevention of chronic diseases in Medicaid
Field offices should use documents in the individual's possession to verify:
•
that the payment is from the IPCDM program, and
•
the amount and date of receipt of the payment.
Obtain the individual's statement either signed or recorded on a Report of Contact
page of the type, amount, and date of receipt if the above information is not evident
from the documents. For more information on electronic documentation, see GN 00301.285 through GN 00301.289.
