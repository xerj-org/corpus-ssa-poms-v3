SI 02007.020 Follow ups and Controls on Continuing Title XVI Interim Benefits
Section 1631(a)(8) of the Social Security Act requires us to pay monthly Federal benefits
prospectively if the Commissioner of Social Security (COSS) does not make a final
decision within 110 calendar days after the date of a favorable administrative law
judges (ALJ) disability or blindness decision. These interim benefits start with the
month in which the 110th day falls, and end with the month in which we make a final
decision.
