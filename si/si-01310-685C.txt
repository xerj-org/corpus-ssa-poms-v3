C. Procedure — question 14
•
Complete 14(a). List the sponsor's dependents in 14(b) if (a) is “Yes.” If the sponsor alleges no dependents, go to question 15.
If the dependent is also an SSI claimant/recipient, consider the need to update other
cases in multiple noncitizen deeming situations.
