SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02201.023 - Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Overpayment: Recovery From A Representative Payee
Effective Dates: 10/27/2023 - Present | TN 24 (01-25)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502201023 (fetched 2026-10-04)

C. Recovery of the overpayment from a representative payee’s own title II or title XVI
benefits
1. Use of cross-program recovery to recover an SSI overpayment from the representative
payee’s title II benefits
If the representative payee is:
a.
Liable (either individually or jointly) for repayment of an overpayment, and
b.
They are eligible for title II benefits on their own earnings, and
c.
Recovery is waived or otherwise unavailable on the SSI individual's own record:
•
Recover the overpayment from the representative payee's title II payments.
•
Use the UOPD screen to select “13” RECOVERS FROM TITLE II BENEFITS – see MSOM BUSSR
004.008, SM 00616.030 and SM 01311.255 for instructions.
EXAMPLE: An SSI beneficiary, is also the representative payee for another recipient. who was
overpaid $300 in March 2008. Social Security granted a waiver recovery from their
record. The FO should use the P TAC process to transfer the overpayment to payee's
record for recovery.
NOTE:
Cross-program recovery is available in all States except those select cases in New
York that fall within the Ellender class action suit.
For criteria for inclusion in the Ellender class, see GN 02210.214.
2. Use of the representative payee's title XVI payment
a.
If the liable representative payee is receiving SSI payments, recover the recipient’s
overpayment from the representative payees’ title XVI payments.
b.
Use the P TAC/Q TAC process to transfer the overpayment to the representative payees’
record. For information on the P TAC/ Q TAC process, see SM 01311.300 and SM 01311.350.
