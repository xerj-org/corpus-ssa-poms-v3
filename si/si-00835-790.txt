SI 00835.790 ISM and Non-institutional Care Situations
CITATIONS:
Regulations, 20 CFR 416.1143
If an individual is not a resident of an institution, the individual may be living
in a non-institutional care situation. Some examples of non-institutional care include
foster care and family care that meet certain requirements as described in SI
00835.790A.1, in this section.
NOTE:
we provide instructions on determining the effect of institutionalization on SSI eligibility
and payment, see SI 00520.001.
