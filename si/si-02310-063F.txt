F. Procedures for clearance of an S7 alert/diary
1. S7 alert/diary wage data
See Wages – General, SI 00820.100 and How to Treat Fluctuating Earned Income Which Never Exceeds $65/Month, SI 00820.160.
The wage data represent the amount received in a quarter. Obtain a monthly amount
for SSI purposes if the wages constitute countable income.
2. Clear the S7 alert/diary
a.
Documentation — SSI Claims system -- Individual Has Earned Income
•
Document wage development on the Wages page (MS 08114.027) ;
•
Document sheltered workshop payment development on the Wages page (MS 08114.027);
•
Document sick pay development on the Sick Pay (Earned) page (MS 08114.036).
•
Record the source and amount of wages/sick pay on Wages/Sick Pay (Earned) pages (MS
08114.036) and document proof used on a Report of Contact or EVID screen per GN 00301.286C.4
b.
Documentation — non-SSI Claims system -- Individual Has Earned Income
See SI 00820.100 through SI 00820.160.
c.
Diary Clearance Input
•
SSI Claims system – Complete the Over Reported Wage Development page (DORW) (MS 04422.028)
and build transaction SSR for S7 diaries
•
Non-SSI Claims system - Transmit earned income to the SSR. (See SM 01305.101.)
Transmit diary deletion to the SSR via SSA-1719B input. (See SM 01301.220.)
