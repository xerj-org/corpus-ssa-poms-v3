A. Introduction to payments for clinical trial participation
On October 5, 2010, the President signed into law the “Improving Access to Clinical Trials Act.” This Act provides for the income and resource exclusion of compensation received
for participating in clinical trials researching and testing treatment of rare diseases
or conditions as defined in Section 5(b)(2) of the “Orphan Drug Act”. The income exclusion applies to the first $2,000 per calendar year received by
an SSI beneficiary, spouse, or deemor as compensation for participation in clinical
trials that meet the criteria detailed in this section.
