F. Rule for rollover and transfer of Coverdell ESA funds
Funds in a Coverdell ESA may be transferred or “rolled over” to a member of the beneficiary’s
family. When a designated beneficiary “rolls over” funds in a Coverdell ESA to a family
member, the rollover is a transfer of a resource for SSI purposes.
