SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01310.630 - SSA-8010-BK - Employment/Wages - Question 3
Effective Dates: 10/31/1995 - Present | TN 4 (02-95)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310630 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Procedure
1. Receipt of Wages in Past Months
If the answer to question 3(a) is “Yes,” complete 3(b) and (c). If the answer to 3(a) is “No,” go to 3(d).
2. Estimating Future
If 3(d) is answered “Yes,” complete 3(e), if applicable, and show the following information in 3(f):
•
rate of pay
•
amount (e.g., 35 hours) worked per pay period
•
how often paid
•
pay day (e.g., “paid every Friday” or “paid on 1st and 15th of each month”)
•
date last paid
3. Changes in Wages
If the individual expects any change in wages (question 3(g)), explain in (h).
4. No Wages Alleged
If questions 3(a) and (d) are both answered “No,” go to question 4.
