D. Description of Form SSA-8203-BK Question 23
Question 23on the SSA-8203-BK is a two-part question:
•
Since the date on page 1, have you, or your spouse living with you sold, transferred title, disposed of or given
away any money, or other property, including money or property in foreign countries?
•
If you co-owned property with another person(s), did you or any co-owner sell, transfer,
or give away any co-owned money or property?
If “yes” to a. or b. go on to c. If no to both, go to 24.
