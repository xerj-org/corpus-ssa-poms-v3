SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02310.015 - Savings Security Instrument Match (SB Diary)
Effective Dates: 09/18/2026 - Present | TN 76 (09-26)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502310015 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Procedure - clearance
For purposes of these instructions, the term savings securities includes both paperless
book entry securities and paper U.S. savings bonds.
1. Identify Records
Identify records with SB diaries by accessing the PE ODS system. Obtain an SSID to
determine to whom the savings security data apply.
2. Contact Individual
a. Confirm Ownership
Contact the individual (i.e., recipient, payee, or deemor) according to the instructions
in SI 02310.007B. to confirm instrument ownership.
b. Individual Agrees with Savings Security Data
Determine whether the recipient's total countable resources exceed the applicable
limit. See SI 02310.015E.5. Do not contact BFS.
c. Individual Denies Ownership
If the value of the securities combined with other resources does not affect eligibility,
take no action. If the value of the securities and other resources is material to
eligibility, verify ownership with BFS per SI 02310.015E.3.
3. Contact BFS
a.
Contact BFS for savings security instrument ownership information via an SSA-562-U3
to FO 675 Minneapolis, MN. (See SI 02310.049B.6.e. for information to include on the SSA-562-U3.) BFS expects turnaround time for responding
to a verification request to take between 2 and 3 weeks from the date they receive
the request.
b.
Also send a dated SSA-8510 with a signature of the owner as it appears on the alert
and a copy of the savings security interface alert.
c.
In deeming situations, obtain an SSA-8510 for each individual (except minor children)
on the record.
d.
When a representative payee signs the SSA-8510, add the statement SSA records confirm
that the person that signed this form is a representative payee for the beneficiary,
and as such, is permitted to authorize disclosure.
e.
When the recipient is deceased, have the survivor sign the SSA-8510, and add the statement
SSA records confirm that the person that signed this form is the [show relationship,
such as widow or widower], and as such, is permitted to authorize disclosure.
4. Receive BFS Response
a.
Accept the BFS record as evidence of ownership. (If the individual's name appears
on the microfilm record, BFS will provide a copy of the record). See SI 02310.015B. regarding possession.
b.
If BFS reports that it has no record of security ownership for the individual, discontinue
development of the alert. (See SI 02310.015E.6.b.)
5. Verify Savings Security Values
Determine retroactive monthly values by using the procedures in SI 01140.240 and any instructions from your regional office staff.
6. Clear the Diary
Record all ownership verification results to avoid contacting BFS more than once for
verification of the same savings securities/savings bonds.
a.
Documentation - Individual Owns Savings Securities/Savings Bonds
•
Modernized Supplemental Security Income Claims System (MSSICS) Document information
required on the Stock, Bond, or Mutual Fund (MS 08113.020) and DSAB (MS 04422.015
Savings Bond Interface Development (DSAB)) screens. Input RE21 in the CG field via
the CMSC screen (MS 04423.006 Miscellaneous Data (CMSC)).
•
Non-MSSICS Transmit ownership information and current redemption value in the REMARKS
field of the SSR. (See SM 01301.841, SM 01601.910 and SI 01140.240C.5.). Input RE21 in the CG field (See SM 01301.785 SM 01301.820).
EXAMPLE 1: RECIPIENT OWNS BOOK ENTRY SAVINGS SECURITIES - REDEMPTION VALUE $$$$.
EXAMPLE 2: RECIPIENT OWNS PAPER U.S. SAVINGS BONDS REDEMPTION VALUE $$$$.
b.
Documentation - Individual Does Not Own and/or Does Not Have Access to Saving Securities/Savings
Bonds
•
MSSICS - Document information required on the DSAB (MS 04422.015 Savings Bond Interface
Development (DSAB)) screen.
•
Non-MSSICS Record your development in the remarks section of the SSR
c.
Individual Is/Was Ineligible
•
MSSICS - The system will post N04 to the record. MSSICS will look at the pending file
and post applicable payment status codes based on the information contained in the
pending file.
•
Non-MSSICS - Transmit payment status code N04 for the appropriate months to the SSR
via SSA-1719B input. (See SM 01305.001.)
d.
Diary Clearance Input
•
MSSICS - Complete the DSAB screen and build transaction SSR. See SM 01301.205.
•
Non-MSSICS - Transmit diary deletion to the SSR via Direct SSR Update. See SM 01301.210
and MS 00302.019 Update Diaries (UDIA).
