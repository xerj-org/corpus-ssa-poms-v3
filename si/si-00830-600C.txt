C. Procedure
Use documents in the individual's possession, contact with the provider or agency
involved, or a precedent to verify that assistance from a particular program is provided
under the Federal Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program or “LIHEAP” . Once this is verified, no further development or documentation is necessary.
