SI 01330.100 Deeming of Resources - Policy
Authority: Act, Sec. 1614(f), as amended. Regulations 20 CFR 416.1202
The resources of an individual who is living with an ineligible spouse include the
resources of the spouse, whether or not they are available to the applicant /recipient.
