C. List of reasons for N19
These are some of the reasons why recipients may choose to terminate SSI eligibility:
•
SSI eligibility prevents them from receiving more money (or benefits) from other programs
such as Aid to Families with Dependent Children (AFDC) and private pensions
•
They have political or religious objections to receiving public assistance funds
•
They no longer wish to comply with requests for information and pursue continuing
SSI eligibility for a small SSI payment or for personal reasons
