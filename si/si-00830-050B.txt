SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00830.050 - Overview of Unearned Income Exclusions
Effective Dates: 09/27/2010 - Present | TN 235 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500830050 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Policy
•
Exclusions never reduce unearned income below zero.
•
Except for the $20 general exclusion (SI 00810.420), no unused unearned income exclusion may be applied to earned income.
•
The infrequent or irregular income exclusion does not apply to unearned income that
is subject to other exclusions, see SI 00810.410H. For example, the infrequent or irregular income exclusion does not apply to child
support that is subject to the one-third child support exclusion.
