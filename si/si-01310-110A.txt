Effective Dates: 09/26/2024 - Present | TN 21 (09-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501310110 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Background
It would not be equitable to deem the entire amount of the ineligible spouse's, parent's,
or sponsor's income to the individual or child without some provision to permit the
deemor to meet the deemor's own needs and those of ineligible children in the household.
