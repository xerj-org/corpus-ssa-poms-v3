C. Procedure
1. To Compute the SSI Payment for January
a.
Subtract the Title II income in January’s BM (November or December) from the January
Title II income.
b.
Add the difference to the BM income to determine the countable income for January.
2. To Compute the SSI Payment for February
a.
Subtract the Title II income in February's BM (December) from the February Title II
income.
b.
Add the difference to the BM income to determine the countable income for February.
