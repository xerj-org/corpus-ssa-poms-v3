SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 00530.410 - Can I Use Information From a Law Enforcement Officer to Suspend or Deny Payments?
Effective Dates: 08/05/2022 - Present | TN 14 (01-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500530410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. Policy
The information a law enforcement officer provides with their request for assistance
in locating an SSI recipient may lead to the identification of an SSI claimant or
recipient as being a person who is a fugitive felon or a parole or probation violator.
While a request from LE must be in writing for SSA to provide information to LE, a
verbal request may be used as a lead to develop a possible FF suspension action.
Do not contact the SSI claimant or recipient to verify third party reports that an
individual is fleeing to avoid prosecution, or custody or confinement after conviction,
for a crime that is a felony, or is a probation or parole violator.
