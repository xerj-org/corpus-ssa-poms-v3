Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0500810410 (fetched 2026-10-04)

E. Policy – benefit payments prior to July 1, 2004
We exclude income which is received either infrequently or irregularly provided the
total of such income does not exceed:
•
$10 per month of earned income; and
•
$20 per month of unearned income.
NOTE:
The definition of “infrequent” during this time period is the same as in SI 00830.420D.
