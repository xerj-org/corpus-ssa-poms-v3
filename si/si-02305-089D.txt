D. Procedure - SSA-8203-BK
Skip question 7 on the SSA-8203-BK if the recipient's permanent residence is an institution.
If the recipient has an ineligible spouse, list them in the spaces provided.
Do not re-list the individuals documented in the fill-in spaces for question 6.
