SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 02205.001 - Responsibility for Repayment in SSI Sponsor/Alien Caused Overpayments
Effective Dates: 07/10/2012 - Present | TN 2 (10-23)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0502205001 (fetched 2026-10-04)

B. Operating Policy
1. Sponsor is Alien's Representative Payee
When the sponsor is also the alien's representative payee, follow SI 02201.021 for overpayments caused by factors other than deeming of the sponsor's income and
resources.
2. Sponsor is not Responsible
a.
When the failureto provide correct information was not willful.
b.
Willful failuremeans the sponsor intentionally failed to supply complete and correct information
regarding their income and resources. If willful failure exists, fraud development
may be appropriate (GN 04110.010). If the sponsor questions their liability see SI 02205.005.
3. Multiple Cause Overpayments
When more than one event has contributed to the creation of an overpayment, the alien
will be liable for the repayment of the entire overpaid amount. The sponsor will be
liable for only the portion resulting from their failure to provide information regarding
their own income and resources. If the sponsor is also the alien's representative
payee, see B.1, above.
4. Recovery Priority
Recovery priority is determined by the SSI payment status of the alien.
a. Current Eligibility
When the overpaid alien remains in current payment status (PSY = C01), first attempt
to recover the entire overpayment from them.
Attempt recovery from the sponsor only when a waiver of recovery is approved with
regard to the alien, and the sponsor is liable.
b. No Current Eligibility
When the overpaid alien is in nonpayment status, attempt recovery from both the alien
and their sponsor, if liable.
