A. Policy for completing part 5
We are responsible for asking claimants living in a pure SSI household these questions
to determine if the claimant is receiving SNAP or wants to apply for SNAP. For a complete
discussion of our role in SNAP, see SI 01801.005 through SI 01801.310.
