SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) - SI 01330.550 - Sponsor-to-Noncitizen Resources Deeming - Computation Rules
Effective Dates: 10/08/2024 - Present | TN 11 (10-24)
Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0501330550 (fetched 2026-10-04)

A. POLICY
1. Excluded Sponsor Resources
The resource exclusions that apply to a sponsor are the same exclusions that apply
to an eligible individual.
See SI 01130.100 ff. for excluded resources.
2. Amount of Resources Deemed From Sponsor to Noncitizen
a. Sponsor Does Not Live With a Spouse
The nonexcluded resources of the sponsor are deemed to the noncitizen to the extent
that those resources exceed the resource limit for an individual.
EXAMPLE: After applying the resource exclusions in SI 01130.100 ff., the adjudicator determines that the sponsor has $3,200 in countable resources.
The current resource limit for an individual is $2,000.
$1,200 ($3,200-2,000) of the sponsor's resources is deemed to the noncitizen.
b. Sponsor Lives With a Spouse — Spouse is Not Noncitizen's Sponsor
The nonexcluded resources of the sponsor and their spouse are deemed to the noncitizen
to the extent that those resources exceed the resource limit for a couple.
EXAMPLE: After applying the resource exclusions in SI 01130.100 ff., the adjudicator determines that the sponsor and sponsor's spouse have combined
countable resources of $3,500. The current resource limit for a couple is $3,000.
$500 ($3,500-3,000) of the sponsor's and sponsor's spouse's resources is deemed to
the noncitizen.
c. Sponsor Lives With a Spouse — Spouse is Also a Sponsor of the Noncitizen
The nonexcluded resources of the sponsor and their spouse are deemed to the noncitizen
to the extent that those resources exceed twice the resource limit for an individual.
EXAMPLE: After applying the resource exclusions in SI 01130.100 ff., the adjudicator determines that the sponsor and sponsor's spouse have combined
countable resources of $3,500. The current resource limit for an individual is $2,000.
None of the sponsor's and sponsor's spouse's resources are deemed to the noncitizen,
since their value is under $4,000 (twice the individual resource limit of $2,000).
